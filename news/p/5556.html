AI 时代的“数字贵族”与被献祭的真实世界Digital Aristocracy and the Sacrificed Real World

科技 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-14 § 链接
极端的薪资差额不是技术红利，而是结构性暴力的量化指标。
Extreme pay gaps are not tech dividends, but quantified metrics of structural violence.

人均 230 万英镑。这个数字在 OpenAI 伦敦办公室的账单里是“人才战争”的战果，但在加尔通的暴力三角里，它是赤裸裸的 structural violence。当 110 个人的平均收入是英国中位数薪资的 58 倍时，我们讨论的不再是薪酬竞争力，而是一个新阶级的诞生——数字贵族。

这种极端的资源倾斜揭示了一个残酷的博弈逻辑：在 AI 竞赛的叙事中，只有极少数能够定义 $\tau$-scaling 或 Astra 模型的 AI 研究员被赋予了极高的“存在性”价值。而支撑这个系统的其他环节，以及被 AI 替代的数百万劳动者，在认知入口中被彻底客体化。这种分配机制本质上是 masculine-centric narrative 的延伸：崇拜绝对的力量、速度和统治力，而将社会成本（如旧金山和伦敦被推高的房价）视为必然的副作用。

最讽刺的共谋发生在 OpenAI 的政府事务团队。前财政大臣 George Osborne 的加入，标志着技术资本与政治权力的深度合流。他们通过定义什么是“AI 安全”、什么是“全球竞争力”来掌控解释权，从而让这种畸形的财富分配显得“理性”且“必要”。

当安全主管因为文化崩坏而离职，而员工在拿每小时 1000 英镑的薪水时，这种 discrepancy 证明了：这家公司追求的不是公正的表达 (Just Expressions)，而是在一个毫无约束的博弈场中，通过垄断认知入口来制造一个对自己绝对有利的现实。他们不是在创造未来，而是在用金钱修筑一道更高的生物墙，将绝大多数人类排除在 AI 时代的定价权之外。

£2.3 million per person. In OpenAI's London accounts, this is a 'talent war' victory; in Galtung's Violence Triangle, it is raw structural violence. When 110 people earn 58 times the UK median, we are no longer discussing competitiveness, but the birth of a new class: the Digital Aristocracy.

This extreme resource tilt reveals a brutal logic of game theory: in the AI race narrative, only a handful of researchers who can define $\tau$-scaling or Astra models are granted high 'existential' value. Everyone else—the support staff and the millions displaced by AI—are completely objectified at the cognitive entry point. This distribution is a direct extension of the masculine-centric narrative: worshiping absolute power, speed, and dominance, while dismissing social costs (like skyrocketing rents in SF and London) as inevitable externalities.

The most cynical complicity occurs in the government affairs team. The joining of George Osborne signals a deep merger between tech capital and political power. By defining 'AI Safety' and 'Global Competitiveness,' they seize the power of interpretation, making this distorted wealth distribution appear 'rational' and 'necessary.'

While safety leaders quit citing a broken culture, employees pocket £1,000 per hour. This discrepancy proves that OpenAI is not seeking Just Expressions, but is manipulating the cognitive entry point to manufacture a reality solely in its favor. They aren't creating the future; they are using capital to build a higher biological wall, excluding the vast majority of humanity from the pricing power of the AI era.
