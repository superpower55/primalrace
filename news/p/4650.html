Milo 的驱逐与右翼的“纯洁性”骗局Milo's Deportation and the Right-Wing 'Purity' Scam

国际 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-30 § 链接
右翼排挤 Milo 不是因为他太极端，而是因为他失去了被利用的价值。
The right isn't rejecting Milo for being too extreme, but for losing his utility as a tool.

Milo Yiannopoulos 被特朗普政府驱逐回英国，这出戏最讽刺的地方不在于一个 alt-right 挑衅者被他曾经服务过的权力机器像垃圾一样扔掉，而在于英国右翼现在对他表现出的“嫌弃”。GB News 说“绝对不会碰他”，Reform UK 拒之门外，这看起来像是右翼在进行某种道德审查或“纯洁性”清理，但实际上这只是一场关于生存空间的剔除博弈。

在右翼的生态位里，挑衅 (provocation) 是一种被武器化的表达工具。当 Milo 在美国通过扮演一个极端、乖戾的形象来为特朗普争取认知入口时，他的“极端”是资产；而当他失去了权力中心的背书，且不再能为当前的权力结构提供流量红利时，这种“极端”就变成了负债。右翼所谓的“太极端”，其实是指他已经失去了作为“有用工具”的资格。

这种排挤揭示了右翼内部共谋者的残酷逻辑：他们并不在乎你是否支持儿童性行为或发表种族主义言论（看看 Tommy Robinson 就能明白），他们在乎的是你是否能被纳入一个可控的、能产生商业利润或政治资本的叙事框架内。Milo 的失败在于他试图通过扮演一个“独立挑衅者”来维持主体性，但在一个由权力垄断解释权的结构里，没有权力背书的挑衅只是噪音。

他现在试图投靠 Restore Britain，这不过是从一个大共谋场域跳进一个小共谋场域。对于这些被主流右翼抛弃的“边缘人”来说，抱团取暖不是因为价值观的契合，而是因为他们共同地成为了权力叙事中的废料。这场驱逐战证明了：在权力博弈中，所谓的“立场”永远是随时可以被替换的皮肤，唯一的真实是你是被利用的资产，还是被丢弃的垃圾。

The deportation of Milo Yiannopoulos by the Trump administration is a masterclass in the cruelty of power. The most ironic part isn't that an alt-right provocateur was dumped by the very machine he helped build, but the sudden 'disgust' shown by the UK right wing. GB News's refusal to touch him and Reform UK's cold shoulder aren't acts of moral hygiene; they are cold calculations in a game of existential survival.

In the right-wing ecosystem, provocation is a weaponized expression. When Milo played the extreme, erratic persona to open cognitive entries for Trump, his 'extremism' was an asset. Now that he lacks the endorsement of the power center and can no longer provide traffic dividends, that same 'extremism' becomes a liability. When the UK right says he is 'too extreme,' they actually mean he is no longer a useful tool.

This rejection exposes the logic of the complicity within the right: they don't actually care about the content of your transgressions—just look at Tommy Robinson. They only care whether you fit into a controllable narrative framework that generates profit or political capital. Milo's failure was his attempt to maintain subjectivity as an 'independent provocateur,' forgetting that in a structure where power monopolizes the right to interpret, provocation without power is just noise.

His pivot toward Restore Britain is simply moving from a large field of complicity to a smaller one. For these 'outcasts,' huddling together isn't about shared values, but about the shared experience of becoming waste in a power narrative. This deportation proves that in the game of power, 'stance' is merely a skin to be swapped; the only reality is whether you are an asset to be used or trash to be discarded.
