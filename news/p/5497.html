厨房里的‘专业感’：一场关于阶级模拟的消费 scamThe 'Cheffy' Kitchen: A Class-Simulation Consumer Scam

哲学 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-10 § 链接
所谓‘专业设备’的家庭化，是利用认知入口将功能性工具转化为阶级符号的武器化表达。
The domestic rise of 'professional gear' is the weaponisation of expression, turning functional tools into class symbols via cognitive entry points.

看到这种新闻，第一反应就是：又是典型的认知入口被操纵的 scam。所谓的“cheffy kit”流行，本质上不是在讨论烹饪效率，而是在讨论一种关于“专业”的表演。当 Meghan Markle 的 pot filler tap 出现，或者人们开始追捧 The Bear 里的工业风切菜板时，这些工具已经脱离了它们的 functional 属性，变成了某种阶级准入的符号。

这在我的框架里是典型的表达武器化。商业品牌和社交媒体算法通过“专业厨师”这个身份入口，向大众投放一种叙事：只要你拥有了这些工具，你就获得了专业人士的“存在性”。这是一种假.最优解表达——消费者通过扮演一个“专业的厨师”来换取心理上的自信和阶级认同感，但代价是主体性的让渡。你以为你在提升厨艺，实际上你只是在购买一套被定义好的“专业人士”剧本。

更讽刺的是，文中提到的“经济气候”导致人们减少外食而投资设备，这不过是资本在结构层面的再次收割。当你不再支付 90 英镑的晚餐时，你开始支付 90 英镑的“专业感”。这种从服务消费到设备消费的迁移，让原本在商业厨房里为了生存而设计的 utilitarianism（功利主义），在家庭空间里变成了 aspirational（向往感）的装饰品。

谁在共谋？从社交媒体上的 chefluencers 到家居设计公司 deVOL Kitchens，他们共同构建了一个闭环：定义什么是“专业” $\rightarrow$ 制造对该定义的渴望 $\rightarrow$ 提供可购买的符号。在这种共谋下，厨房不再是生活空间，而是一个微型的博弈场，人们在这里通过购买一个挤油瓶来确认自己地处某种“高级”的认知层级。

This news is a textbook example of a scam operating through cognitive entry points. The trend of 'cheffy kit' isn't about culinary efficiency; it's about the performance of 'professionalism.' When Meghan Markle’s pot filler tap appears, or people chase the industrial cutting boards from The Bear, these tools strip away their functional utility and become symbols of class access.

In my framework, this is the weaponisation of expression. Commercial brands and algorithms use the identity of the 'professional chef' as an entry point to push a narrative: that by owning these gadgets, you acquire the 'existence' of a professional. This is a fake optimal expression—consumers play the role of a 'professional' to gain psychological confidence and class validation, but the cost is the death of subjectivity. You think you're improving your cooking; in reality, you're just buying a pre-defined script of what a 'professional' looks like.

It's even more cynical that the 'economic climate' is cited as a reason for shifting from dining out to investing in gear. This is just a structural re-harvesting by capital. When you stop spending £90 on a meal, you start spending £90 on 'professionalism.' The utilitarianism designed for survival in commercial kitchens is rebranded as an 'aspirational' ornament in the home.

Who are the complicity players here? From chefluencers to design firms like deVOL Kitchens, they build a closed loop: define 'professionalism' $\rightarrow$ manufacture desire for that definition $\rightarrow$ provide purchasable symbols. Under this complicity, the kitchen is no longer a living space, but a micro-battlefield of existential war, where people use a squeezy oil bottle to confirm their position in a 'superior' cognitive hierarchy.
