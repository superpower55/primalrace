旅游税恐慌：一场关于认知入口的表演性骗局Tourist Tax Panic: A Performative Scam of Cognitive Entry

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-10 § 链接
用虚构的损失恐吓决策者，是利益集团维持结构性剥削的经典scam。
Using fabricated losses to intimidate policymakers is a classic scam to maintain structural exploitation.

UKHospitality 抛出的那套“1200万次访问减少、3.3万个岗位流失”的预测，是典型的武器化表达。他们试图在决策者的认知入口里植入一个恐惧：只要增加微小的成本，整个产业就会崩塌。但这不过是一场表演，旨在通过制造虚假的“经济危机”来保住既得利益，确保旅游业带来的溢出价值继续被私有化，而公共成本则由地方政府和环境承担。

这种恐慌叙事在曼彻斯特和利物浦的实际数据面前溃不成军。当 Actual（实际入住率）并没有因为几英镑的税收而下降时，之前的恐惧就变成了纯粹的 structural violence。这种暴力体现在：行业巨头通过垄断“行业声音”，试图阻止公共资源（如文化项目、社会住房）的合法回流。他们把“游客的敏感度”当成武器，实际上是在博弈中通过操纵恐惧来索要特权。

最讽刺的是，部分市长以“保护当地商业”为由反对税收，这其实是一场权力共谋。他们通过扮演“商业守护者”的角色，换取行业团体的政治支持，而代价是牺牲了本可以改善当地基础设施的 Potential 收益。当一种税收能为巴利阿里群岛带来 2.63 亿欧元的公共建设，而英国某些地区还在纠结几英镑的“客源流失”时，这已经不是经济问题，而是典型的元暴力——用一个虚构的“商业逻辑”来覆盖真实的人权与公共利益。

The projections from UKHospitality—12 million fewer visits and 33,000 job losses—are a textbook example of weaponized expression. They are attempting to plant a seed of fear in the cognitive entry of policymakers: the idea that a marginal cost increase would trigger an industrial collapse. This is a performance designed to preserve vested interests, ensuring that the surplus value of tourism remains privatized while the public costs are socialized.

This narrative of panic collapses when faced with the actual data from Manchester and Liverpool. When the Actual occupancy rates show no significant decline over a few pounds, the previous fear reveals itself as structural violence. This violence manifests as industry giants monopolizing the "industry voice" to block the legitimate return of public resources to cultural and social projects. They use "tourist sensitivity" as a weapon to demand privilege in an existential war of resources.

Most ironic is the complicity of certain mayors who oppose the levy under the guise of "protecting local business." They play the role of "commercial guardians" to secure political support from trade bodies, at the cost of sacrificing the Potential benefits for local infrastructure. When such taxes fund €263m in public projects in the Balearic Islands, while some UK regions still obsess over a few pounds of "lost visitors," it ceases to be an economic debate. It is meta-violence—using a fabricated "business logic" to overwrite real human rights and public interests.
