欧盟的“团结”是给谁准备的入场券EU 'Solidarity' is a Ticket for the Powerful

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-01 § 链接
人道主义叙事是最高级的武器，用来掩盖结构性暴力的共谋。
Humanitarian narratives are weaponized to mask the complicity of structural violence.

这出戏的剧本极其经典：西班牙首相在控诉意大利“自私”和“不合法”，而意大利在指责西班牙通过给非法移民提供合法化路径来“激励”人口贩卖。在这一场关于 Schengen 协议和边境政策的口水战中，欧盟的政治精英们在进行一场典型的存在性战争，争夺的是谁在定义“欧洲秩序”的解释权。

但请注意，在这个对话框里，那 5 万名试图跨越边境的人，以及至少 67 具在防波堤被挤压或溺死的尸体，仅仅被处理成了背景板上的“流量数据”或“政治筹码”。这就是典型的 cultural violence：将具体的人类苦难抽象化为“移民危机”或“非法入境”，从而让随后的驱逐、拦截和物理隔离显得正当且必要。在这种叙事中，人道主义成了被武器化的入口——当西班牙谈论“团结”和“人权”时，他实际上是在谈论欧盟内部的行政协作，而不是那些在海水中挣扎的身体。

这里的共谋关系极其讽刺。欧盟国家在公共空间表演“价值观一致”，但在 structural 层面，他们共同维护着一道由法律、铁丝网和充气屏障组成的生物墙。这道墙将人类分为“被允许进入的”和“被定义为非法入侵的”。无论西班牙和意大利如何争吵，他们都在共谋维护一个核心事实：欧洲的繁荣必须建立在对原初种族和全球南方的结构性剥削之上，而边境的暴力则是维持这种剥削的必要成本。

至于那些被指责为“激励”移民的合法化方案，不过是权力者在博弈中抛出的诱饵。真正的最优解从来不在于给多少人发签证，而在于谁能掌控定义“合法”的权力。当 19 岁的塞内加尔青年 Souleil 认为去欧洲工作是唯一的出路时，他面对的不是一个国家，而是一套完整的、全球性的元暴力系统。

The script is classic: the Spanish PM decries Italy's 'selfishness' and 'illegality,' while Italy accuses Spain of 'incentivizing' trafficking via regularization schemes. In this verbal skirmish over the Schengen Area, EU elites are engaged in an existential war to seize the interpretive power over 'European Order.'

Notice that the 50,000 people crossing the border and the at least 67 bodies crushed or drowned at the breakwater are treated merely as 'traffic data' or 'political chips.' This is textbook cultural violence: abstracting concrete human suffering into a 'migrant crisis' or 'illegal entry' to make subsequent deportations, interceptions, and physical barriers appear legitimate. Here, humanitarianism is a weaponized entry point—when Spain speaks of 'solidarity' and 'human rights,' he is discussing administrative coordination within the EU, not the bodies struggling in the water.

The complicity here is ironic. EU states perform 'value alignment' in public, yet at the structural level, they collectively maintain a biological wall of laws, barbed wire, and inflatable barriers. This wall divides humanity into the 'permitted' and the 'illegal.' Regardless of the friction between Madrid and Rome, they are complicit in maintaining one core fact: European prosperity is built upon the structural exploitation of the Primal Race and the Global South, with border violence serving as the necessary cost of maintenance.

As for the regularization schemes blamed for 'incentivizing' migration, they are merely bait thrown by power-holders in a game of stakes. The true optimal expression is never about how many visas are issued, but about who controls the power to define 'legality.' When 19-year-old Souleil from Senegal believes moving to Europe is his only chance for a living, he is not facing a country, but a comprehensive, global system of meta-violence.
