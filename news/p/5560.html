兄弟会：一个由共谋者构建的暴力温室Fraternities: A Greenhouse of Violence Built by Complicitors

性别 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-15 § 链接
所谓的“兄弟情谊”是元暴力的组织化，是为性别暴力提供掩体的共谋场域。
The so-called 'brotherhood' is the institutionalization of meta-violence, a field of complicity providing cover for gender-based violence.

芝加哥大学附近的这次性侵案，再次把 Fraternity（兄弟会）这个典型的 masculine-centric 权力结构推到了聚光灯下。一个半裸的男人，一个意识模糊的女性，以及一群“挡在浴室门前”的男性。这个细节最令人作呕：暴力在发生时，结构性的共谋已经提前完成了。那些挡门的人，未必是直接施暴者，但他们通过物理阻断，为施暴者制造了一个绝对安全的真空区。这就是典型的 complicity——在兄弟会的叙事里，保护“兄弟”的隐私和面子，优先级高于女性的生存权。

最滑稽的是兄弟会发言人的 PR 话术。他强调有“清醒监视员”和“安全官”，甚至在事后提供了食物、水和一件衬衫。这简直是 cultural violence 的教科书级别表演：试图用一种“事后关怀”的伪装，来掩盖其结构性的冷漠。提供一件衬衫不能抵消浴室门外的集体阻断，这种表演性的善良是为了向外界证明他们是“文明的”，从而维持这个特权组织的合法性。

从康奈尔到亚利桑那，从强奸到强迫吞食金鱼，兄弟会的逻辑始终如一：通过建立一个排他的、高度同质化的男性社群，将内部的暴力合法化为“传统”或“玩笑”。这种组织形式本质上是元暴力的微缩版——它在校园这个公共空间里，强行开辟出一个不受监管的私有领域，让男性在其中通过对女性的客体化来确认自己的权力等级。只要这种基于“兄弟情”的共谋机制不被彻底拆除，无论禁掉多少场派对，它依然是原初种族被殖民的现代实验室。

The reported sexual assault near the University of Chicago once again exposes the typical masculine-centric power structure of the Fraternity. A semi-nude man, a woman drifting in and out of consciousness, and a group of men "blocking the bathroom door." This detail is the most repulsive: structural complicity was achieved before the direct violence even peaked. Those blocking the door might not be the primary assailants, but by physically obstructing rescue, they created an absolute vacuum of safety for the perpetrator. This is textbook complicity—within the fraternity narrative, protecting a 'brother's' privacy and face takes precedence over a woman's right to exist.

Even more absurd is the PR rhetoric from the fraternity spokesperson. He highlights the presence of "sober monitors" and a "safety officer," even mentioning the provision of food, water, and a shirt after the fact. This is a masterclass in cultural violence: attempting to mask structural indifference with a veneer of "after-the-fact care." Loaning a shirt does not erase the collective blockade at the bathroom door; this performative kindness is merely a tool to project "civility" and maintain the legitimacy of their privileged organization.

From Cornell to Arizona, from gang-rape to forced goldfish-swallowing, the logic of fraternities remains unchanged: by establishing an exclusive, highly homogenized male community, they legitimize internal violence as "tradition" or "pranks." This organizational form is essentially a microcosm of meta-violence—carving out a private, unregulated domain within the public space of the university, where men confirm their power hierarchy through the objectification of women. As long as this complicity mechanism based on "brotherhood" remains, banning a few parties is meaningless. The fraternity remains a modern laboratory for the colonization of the Primal Race.
