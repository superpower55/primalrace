SpaceX 的月球“意外”：一次关于权力溢出的空间垃圾表演SpaceX's Lunar 'Accident': A Performance of Power Overflow and Space Junk

科技 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-31 § 链接
当资本垄断了认知入口，失控的暴力也被包装成科学的观测机会。
When capital monopolizes the cognitive entrance, uncontrolled violence is rebranded as a scientific observation opportunity.

一次典型的 structural violence 及其随后的 cultural 掩盖。SpaceX 的火箭残骸在太空中漂流一年后撞击月球，这在技术上被定义为“意外”，但在权力逻辑上是极致的傲慢。如果一个普通个体在公共空间制造了同等规模的垃圾并造成破坏，他会被定义为罪犯；但当这个行为由掌控了认知入口的巨头实施时，它变成了科学家和天文爱好者“渴望观察”的学术样本。

请注意这种叙事的 weaponization：将一次由于缺乏责任心（未将残骸推入太阳轨道）而导致的碰撞，转化为一个关于“研究未来宇航员面临风险”的正面叙事。这是一种极其熟练的共谋。SpaceX 制造了空间垃圾（暴力），而科学界和媒体则通过将其定义为“观测机会”，完成了对这种失职的合法化。在这种 masculine-centric 的技术叙事中，对自然的侵占和破坏被简化为一种“探索”的副产品。

这种“意外”的本质是权力溢出。当一个组织拥有了定义“什么是进步”的权力，它就拥有了定义“什么是意外”的权力。月球表面被刻下的一道深坑，不是科学的注脚，而是资本在无监管状态下对公共资源进行的一次暴力涂鸦。而我们，作为观众，正被引导着在望远镜后扮演一个好奇的共谋者。

A textbook case of structural violence followed by cultural masking. A SpaceX rocket stage drifting for a year before slamming into the moon is technically labeled an 'accident,' but in the logic of power, it is pure arrogance. If an ordinary individual created debris of this scale in a public space, they would be a criminal; yet, when the actor is a giant controlling the cognitive entrance, the event is transformed into a 'coveted' academic sample for scientists and stargazers.

Observe the weaponisation of this narrative: a collision caused by a lack of accountability—failing to nudge the stage into a solar orbit—is pivoted into a positive story about 'understanding hazards for future astronauts.' This is a sophisticated form of complicity. SpaceX produced space junk (violence), and the scientific community and media, by defining it as an 'observation opportunity,' legitimized this negligence. In this masculine-centric technical narrative, the occupation and destruction of nature are reduced to a mere byproduct of 'exploration.'

This 'accident' is essentially a power overflow. When an organization possesses the power to define 'what is progress,' it possesses the power to define 'what is an accident.' The crater carved into the lunar surface is not a scientific footnote, but a violent graffiti of capital on public resources under a zero-regulation regime. And we, the audience, are being guided to play the role of a curious co-conspirator behind the telescope.
