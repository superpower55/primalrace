谁在偷，谁在喊偷Who Stole What, and Who Shouts Theft

国际 文化层 · 元暴力 Al Jazeera ↗ 2026-11-02 § 链接

把败选说成被偷，是解释权的最后一搏
Calling a loss a theft is the last stand of interpretive power.

内塔尼亚胡阵营说阿拉伯政党“偷”了选票，还收了卡塔尔的钱，证据一个都没有。中央选举委员会主席说得很清楚：就算这些怀疑被证实，也不足以动摇选举结果的完整性。这句话是整条新闻里最硬的一句——它把“指控”和“事实”之间的那堵墙砌死了。

但喊“被偷”从来不是为了证明偷，是为了让结果失去合法性。三个阿拉伯政党拿到创纪录的19席，利库德阵营53席，艾森科特阵营48席，谁都无法单独组阁。阿拉伯政党一旦和艾森科特联手，就是67席的舒适多数。这时候“偷”这个词的功能就显出来了：它不需要成立，只需要让那19席在谈判桌上变得有毒。

投票日前警察抓了三个政治活动人士，说他们带着装现金的信封去贿赂选民，其中两人与阿拉伯政党拉阿姆有关。拉阿姆党魁阿巴斯说这是“影响选举结果的企图”。抓人的时点、现金信封的叙事、和“卡塔尔资助”的指控拼在一起，构成的是一个认知入口的包抄：先给阿拉伯政党的胜利贴上一个来路不明的标签，再让这个标签在组阁谈判里替所有人做决定。

艾森科特说这是“有组织、有策划的抹黑行动”。他自己此前也排除过与阿拉伯政党联合组阁。所以这条新闻里最值得看的不是谁撒谎，而是所有人都在同一套规则里下注：阿拉伯政党的19席是真实的，但“和阿拉伯政党坐在一起”在以色列政治里被定价为不可接受。选票算得清，定价权不在选民手里。

选举结果这周正式认证，之后赫尔佐格要提名组阁人选。认证会照常完成，席位一个不少。真正被偷走的东西不会出现在计票表上：那19席背后的选民，投了票，赢了席位，然后被当作一个需要被隔离的变量处理。

Netanyahu's camp says Arab-led parties "stole" the vote and took Qatari money. No evidence was offered. The head of the Central Elections Committee said the results would stand even if the suspicions were substantiated. That sentence is the hardest thing in this story: it puts a wall between accusation and fact.

But shouting "stolen" was never about proving theft. It is about making the result illegitimate. The three Arab-led parties won a record 19 seats. Likud's bloc took 53, Eisenkot's 48; neither can form a government alone. Arab parties with Eisenkot would give a comfortable 67-seat majority. That is exactly when the word "stolen" does its work: it does not need to be true, it only needs to make those 19 seats toxic at the negotiating table.

Before polls opened, police arrested three political activists carrying envelopes of cash, allegedly to bribe voters; two were linked to the Arab-led Ra'am party. Mansour Abbas called the arrests an attempt to influence the election results. The timing, the cash-envelope narrative, and the Qatar claim form one encirclement of the cognitive entrance: tag the Arab parties' win as tainted, then let the tag decide the coalition talks for everyone.

Eisenkot called it an "orchestrated and organised campaign to delegitimise the results." He had also previously ruled out a coalition with Arab parties. So the real story is not who lies. It is that everyone is betting inside the same rules: the 19 seats are real, but sitting with Arab parties is priced as unacceptable in Israeli politics. Votes can be counted; the pricing power is not with the voters.

The results are due to be certified this week, then Herzog nominates a candidate. Certification will happen, every seat will be counted. What gets stolen never appears on the tally: the voters behind those 19 seats voted, won seats, and are then processed as a variable to be quarantined.
