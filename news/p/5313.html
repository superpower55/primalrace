星舰入轨：一场关于“可能性”的昂贵投喂Starship in Orbit: An Expensive Feeding of 'Possibilities'

科技 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-28 § 链接
技术里程碑是事实，但将其包装成“人类跨越”是典型的武器化叙事。
Technical milestones are facts; framing them as 'human leaps' is weaponized narrative.

SpaceX 的 Starship 入轨了，工程师们在控制中心欢呼，资本市场在 IPO 的波动中寻找支撑。从技术维度看，这确实是一个 milestone，Actual 终于向 Potential 靠近了一步。但我们要问的是：这个“可能性”是被谁定义的？又是为了谁制造的？

在 Musk 的叙事里，这是通往火星的阶梯，是人类成为多行星物种的壮举。这套浪漫主义叙事是极佳的 weaponized expression，它通过制造一种“人类共同命运”的认知入口，掩盖了其背后极其纯粹的资本逻辑——通过垄断低成本入轨能力，夺取太空时代的定价权。当一个公司能定义什么是“可行的航线”时，它就定义了未来的事实。

有趣的是，报道中提到之前的失败：在巴哈马散落的碎片、失控的爆炸。在传统的科研叙事里，这是风险；但在 Musk 的博弈模型里，这是快速迭代的成本。这种“允许失败”的特权，本身就是一种结构性优势。而大众被引导去关注那句“Starship is in orbit”，而非思考一个私人公司掌控全球轨道资产时，会对公共空间的解释权产生怎样的侵蚀。

这不是好新闻，因为这次差额的缩小并没有让 Potential 变得更公正，它只是让一个已经拥有 meta-violence 能力的个体，在物理空间上又扩张了一次。真正的里程碑不应该是火箭飞多高，而应该是谁在决定火箭飞向哪里。

SpaceX's Starship has reached orbit. Engineers cheer, and the stock market seeks stability post-IPO. Technically, it is a milestone—the Actual moving toward the Potential. But we must ask: Who defines this 'possibility,' and for whom is it manufactured?

In Musk's narrative, this is a ladder to Mars, a leap for humanity to become a multi-planetary species. This romanticized narrative is a textbook example of weaponized expression. By creating a cognitive entry point of 'common human destiny,' it masks a raw capital logic: seizing the pricing power of the space age by monopolizing low-cost orbital access. When one company defines what a 'viable trajectory' is, it defines the facts of the future.

Notably, the report mentions previous failures—debris in the Bahamas, uncontrolled explosions. In traditional research, these are risks; in Musk's game, they are the cost of rapid iteration. This privilege to fail is a structural advantage. While the public is led to focus on the phrase 'Starship is in orbit,' we ignore how a private entity controlling global orbital assets erodes the interpretative power of public space.

This is not good_news. The reduction of the gap here does not make the Potential more just; it simply allows an entity already possessing meta-violence to expand its reach physically. A true milestone is not how high a rocket flies, but who decides where it goes.
