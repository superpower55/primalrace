用“撤离”掩盖“驱逐”：一场关于共谋的暴力表演Evacuation as a Mask for Expulsion: A Performance of Complicity

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-13 § 链接
所谓的“无能”是最高级的共谋，它将结构性暴力伪装成执行失误。
So-called 'impotence' is the highest form of complicity, masking structural violence as operational failure.

这起事件最荒诞的剧本在于：一名高级将领亲自承诺要“撤离”定居者，结果第二天士兵直接把原住民赶出了家门。这种反差不是由于军队的“无能”或“缺乏意志”，而是一场极其精准的 cultural violence 演出。在元暴力的逻辑里，承诺是给国际压力看的 PR 掩体，而驱逐才是实际的 structural 目标。

请注意细节：士兵在驱逐原住民后，竟然和定居者在被抢占的露台上一起踢足球。这个画面是整场博弈中最诚实的表达。它揭露了 IDF 与定居者之间深层的 complicity（共谋）——他们不是在对抗，而是在共享一个关于“谁才是这片土地主人”的男性中心叙事。在这种叙事中，巴勒斯坦人的家不是家，而是可以被随意处置的资源，而原住民的痛苦被定义为可以被忽视的“噪音”。

最令人作呕的是那些所谓的“国内批评者”，他们指责军队在流氓面前显得“无能”。这种批评实际上在帮凶掩盖罪行：它试图将一个系统性的 ethnic cleansing（种族清洗）过程，简化为几个“坏警察”或“弱将领”的执行失误。当杀人者几乎完全享有 impunity（豁免权）时，任何关于“缺乏意志”的讨论都是 scam。

这本质上是一场原初种族的殖民复刻。通过切断水电、拦截救护车、撕毁古兰经，施暴者在物理层（direct）和文化层（cultural）同步地剥夺对方的主体性。当士兵和定居者在露台上踢球时，他们完成了一次关于权力定价权的确认：在这里，定义“事实”和“正义”的权力，只属于那些持有枪支和殖民叙事的人。

The most absurd part of this script is that a top commander personally promised to 'evacuate' settlers, only for soldiers to force the original residents out of their homes the very next day. This contradiction isn't a result of military 'impotence' or 'lack of will'; it is a precisely executed performance of cultural violence. In the logic of meta-violence, the promise is a PR shield for international pressure, while the expulsion is the actual structural objective.

Observe the detail: soldiers, after displacing the Palestinians, were filmed playing football with the settlers on the seized terrace. This image is the most honest expression in the entire game. It exposes the deep complicity between the IDF and the settlers—they are not opposing each other; they are sharing a masculine-centric narrative about 'who owns the land.' In this narrative, a Palestinian home is not a home, but a resource to be disposed of, and the suffering of the residents is defined as 'noise' to be ignored.

Most nauseating are the 'domestic critics' who describe the army as 'impotent' in the face of bullies. This critique actually helps the accomplices: it attempts to simplify a systematic process of ethnic cleansing into a few 'bad cops' or 'weak generals' failing in execution. When killers enjoy near-total impunity, any discussion about 'lack of will' is a scam.

This is essentially a reproduction of the colonization of the Primal Race. By cutting off water, blocking ambulances, and tearing the Qur'an, the aggressors simultaneously strip the victims of their subjectivity at both the direct and cultural layers. When soldiers and settlers play football on that terrace, they are confirming the pricing power of authority: here, the power to define 'fact' and 'justice' belongs solely to those who hold the guns and the colonial narrative.
