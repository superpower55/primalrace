以“安全”之名，在西岸制造原初种族的殖民蓝图Colonizing the Primal Race: The Blueprint of Terror in the West Bank

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-12 § 链接
暴力不是事件，而是将他人客体化为“可消耗资源”的结构性共谋。
Violence is not an event, but a structural complicity that objectifies others as disposable resources.

这次在 Qusra 村的围困不是什么“定居者冲突”，而是一场教科书式的 structural violence。当定居者切断水电、封锁救护车，而身着制服的士兵在旁边搭建帐篷或维持“封闭军事区”时，这已经不是个体的恶意，而是一套完整的、由国家机器背书的殖民机制。Violence = Potential − Actual，这些巴勒斯坦家庭本应拥有的生存权、医疗权被强行扣除，差额就是这场恐怖主义。

最令人作呕的是那种典型的 masculine-centric narrative 的表演。将军到场，承诺“严肃对待”，却在撤走帐篷后留下床垫，让施暴者继续留在门口。这种“表演性让步”是文化层面的武器化：它向国际社会输出一种“我们在管理”的假象，实则在 meta 层面上完成了对暴力的合法化。军队在这里不是秩序的维护者，而是定居者暴行的共谋者 (complicit)，他们通过定义谁是“骚乱者”来掌控解释权，从而在事实上推动种族清洗。

这种将特定人群定义为“非人”或“次等公民”并剥夺其主体性的逻辑，正是原初种族被殖民的通用蓝图。无论是通过身体的直接暴力，还是通过法律和制度的 structural violence，其核心目的只有一个：夺取土地，抹除对方的存在性。当 1100 多人被杀而仅有一例起诉时，这种 impunity（豁免权）本身就是最深层的元暴力——它在告诉世界，某些人的生命在当前的叙事中是没有定价权的。

The siege in Qusra is not a 'settler conflict'; it is a textbook case of structural violence. When settlers cut off water and power and block ambulances while uniformed soldiers set up tents or maintain 'closed military zones,' we are seeing a colonial mechanism endorsed by the state. Violence = Potential − Actual; the basic rights to survival and healthcare are forcibly subtracted from these families, and the deficit is the terror they endure.

Most repulsive is the performance of the masculine-centric narrative. A general visits, promises 'seriousness,' and removes a tent but leaves the bedding, allowing the attackers to remain at the doorstep. This 'performative concession' is a weaponized cultural narrative: it projects an image of 'management' to the international community while legitimizing violence at the meta level. The army is not the maintainer of order but a complicit partner in settler terror, controlling the interpretation of 'disturbances' to facilitate ethnic cleansing.

This logic of defining a group as 'subhuman' or 'second-class' to strip them of their subjectivity is the universal blueprint for the colonization of the Primal Race. Whether through direct violence or structural violence embedded in law, the goal is the same: seize land and erase the other's existence. When over 1,100 people are killed with only one indictment, this impunity is the ultimate meta-violence—it signals that in the current narrative, certain lives simply have no price.
