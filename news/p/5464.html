JD Vance 的“美国工人”叙事与现代契约奴隶制JD Vance's 'American Worker' Narrative and Modern Contract Slavery

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-08 § 链接
用“爱国主义”包装的劳动力清洗，本质是权力对个体生存权的再次定价。
A labor purge wrapped in patriotism is essentially a re-pricing of survival by power.

JD Vance 把 H-1B 员工称为“外国契约奴隶” (foreign indentured servants)，这真是一次极其讽刺的表达。他试图用一个关于“保护美国工人”的浪漫叙事来掩盖一个残酷的 structural violence：在 H-1B 体系中，个体的生存权被彻底地与雇主绑定。正如移民倡议组织所言，这些高技术工人因为签证绑定而失去了议价能力，这才是真正的现代契约奴隶制。

这场博弈的本质不是“美国人 vs 外国人”，而是“权力中心 vs 依附者”。Vance 扮演的是一个正义的保护者，但他的逻辑是典型的 masculine-centric narrative：通过制造敌我对立，将复杂的劳动力市场简化为一场关于“纯洁性”和“忠诚度”的战争。他口中的“游戏” (the game) 实际上是政府在通过操控认知入口，重新定义谁才是“合格的”美国贡献者。

微软和 Adobe 的被禁，不是因为它们“滥用”了系统，而是因为它们成了此次政治表演的 sacrificial lambs。当政府宣布“我们要雇佣伟大的美国工人”时，它并没有提供任何提升底层工人 Potential 的 structural 方案，而只是通过削减另一群弱势者的 Actual 权利来制造一种“正义”的幻觉。在这种共谋中，美国本土工人被当作了攻击移民的工具，而移民则成了权力博弈中的 disposable assets。

最令人作呕的是这种叙事的 weaponization。它把经济层面的薪资压制，包装成一种民族主义的胜利。在这种逻辑下，只要你被定义为“外来者”，你所有的专业能力和主体性都将被抹除，只剩下一个可以被随时切断的签证编号。这根本不是在保护工人，而是在向所有企业发出信号：只要你们配合政府的政治叙事，你们依然可以继续在更隐蔽的维度上压榨劳动力。

JD Vance calling H-1B workers "foreign indentured servants" is a piece of peak irony. He attempts to mask a brutal structural violence with a romantic narrative of "protecting American workers." The reality of the H-1B system is that an individual's right to exist in a country is entirely tethered to an employer. As advocacy groups noted, these high-skilled workers lose all bargaining power because their visas are tied to a company—that is the actual modern contract slavery.

This game is not "Americans vs. Foreigners," but "Power Centers vs. Dependents." Vance performs the role of the righteous protector, employing a classic masculine-centric narrative: simplifying a complex labor market into a war of "purity" and "loyalty" by creating an us-vs-them dichotomy. The "game" he refers to is actually the government using its control over cognitive entries to redefine who qualifies as a "legitimate" contributor to America.

The suspensions of Microsoft and Adobe are not about "abusing" a system; these companies are merely sacrificial lambs for a political performance. When the government proclaims "hire great American workers," it offers no structural solution to increase the Potential of local workers. Instead, it creates an illusion of justice by slashing the Actual rights of another vulnerable group. In this complicity, native workers are used as tools to attack immigrants, while immigrants are treated as disposable assets in a power struggle.

Most repulsive is the weaponisation of this narrative. It packages economic wage suppression as a nationalist victory. Under this logic, once you are labeled an "outsider," your professional merit and subjectivity are erased, replaced by a visa number that can be revoked at will. This isn't about protecting workers; it's a signal to corporations: as long as you align with the political narrative, you can continue to exploit labor in more invisible dimensions.
