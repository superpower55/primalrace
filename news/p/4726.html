用廉价导弹对冲“经济D-Day”：一场关于定义权的存在性战争Hedging 'Economic D-Day' with Cheap Missiles: An Existential War of Definition

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-03 § 链接
战争的胜负不在于拦截率，而在于谁在定义“绝望”与“崩溃”。
Victory in war is not about interception rates, but about who defines 'despair' and 'collapse'.

特朗普政府把新制裁称为“经济D-Day”，试图通过定义一个决定性的时间节点来制造一种“必然崩溃”的叙事。这在第三章的逻辑里是典型的 weaponized expression：通过掌控认知入口，将经济压力等同于政权死刑，从而在国际舆论中预设伊朗的失败。

但现实的博弈是物质性的。伊朗用 Kheibar Shekan 这种廉价且快速部署的导弹，在结构层面上完成了一次极其高效的对冲。当美国昂贵的拦截系统在为几枚廉价导弹买单时，这场战争的成本结构本身就成了伊朗的武器。这不仅是军事博弈，更是一场关于“谁在被消耗”的存在性战争。伊朗通过持续的打击能力，在物理层面否决了美国试图通过制裁强加给它的“绝望”定义。

最讽刺的共谋发生在以色列身上。以色列国防部长一方面承认制裁有效，另一方面警告这会逼迫德黑兰采取“绝望之举”。这种叙事陷阱非常精巧：它将伊朗的进攻定义为“绝望者的垂死挣扎”，从而为未来的全面打击（将对方推回石器时代）预设道德合法性。这是一种典型的元暴力（meta violence）操作——无论伊朗怎么反击，都被预先定义为“崩溃前兆”。

这场冲突没有所谓的“文明”或“人道”底色，只有不同层级的暴力三角在联动。美国在 structural 层通过制裁施压，伊朗在 direct 层通过导弹反击，而以色列则在 cultural 层通过定义“绝望”来为未来的暴力清场。所谓的“绝望步骤”不是客观事实，而是进攻者为了夺取解释权而制造的叙事诱饵。

The Trump administration labeled the new sanctions an “Economic D-Day,” attempting to manufacture a narrative of inevitable collapse by defining a decisive temporal node. In terms of weaponized expression, this is a calculated move to seize the cognitive entry point and equate economic pressure with a political death sentence.

However, the actual game is material. By utilizing the Kheibar Shekan—cheap, rapid-deployment missiles—Iran has executed a highly efficient hedge at the structural level. While expensive US interceptors pay the bill for low-cost projectiles, the cost structure of the war itself becomes Iran's weapon. This is not merely military tactics; it is an existential war over "who is being consumed." Iran is physically negating the definition of "despair" that the US is trying to impose.

The most cynical complicity comes from Israel. The Defense Minister simultaneously acknowledges the sanctions' effectiveness while warning they may drive Tehran to "desperate steps." This is a sophisticated narrative trap: by framing Iranian aggression as the "death throes of the desperate," it pre-establishes the moral legitimacy for a total strike to push the enemy "back to the stone age." This is meta violence in action—regardless of Iran's response, it is pre-defined as a symptom of collapse.

There is no "civilized" or "humanitarian" backdrop here, only the linkage of the Violence Triangle. The US exerts pressure at the structural level, Iran retaliates at the direct level, and Israel operates at the cultural level by defining "despair" to clear the way for future violence. "Desperate steps" are not an objective fact, but a narrative bait used by the aggressor to seize the power of interpretation.
