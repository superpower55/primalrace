用末日叙事掩盖财务破产：AI 巨头的认知操纵Apocalypse as a Hedge: The AI Giants' Cognitive Scam

科技 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-20 § 链接
当产品无法盈利时，最好的掩护就是宣布它可能毁灭人类。
When the business model fails, the best cover is claiming the product might destroy humanity.

这套逻辑简直是教科书级的 weaponized expression。硅谷的 AI 巨头们最近在集体表演一种极其诡异的戏码：一边投入数千亿美金堆算力，一边突然忧心忡忡地警告 AI 可能会毁灭人类，并呼吁政府介入监管。这种叙事转换的本质不是良心发现，而是一次精准的认知入口争夺。

在元暴力的逻辑里，定义“什么是风险”的人就掌握了定价权。通过制造“AI 毁灭世界”的末日叙事，这些巨头成功将公众的注意力从“商业模式是否成立”这个具体问题，转移到了“人类生存危机”这个宏大叙事上。这是一种极高明的 scam：如果一个产品强大到能威胁人类，那么它的高昂成本和不可理喻的亏损就变成了某种“必要的代价”，而监管则成了他们用来构建 regulatory moat（监管护城河）的武器，旨在通过提高准入门槛，把那些没钱买算力的竞争对手挡在门外。

但事实是，这层文化包装之下的 structural violence 已经到了临界点。从 Bloomberg 的数据看，AI 的 token 价格在暴跌，而构建成本却在攀升。这就是典型的“现实不配合就重新定义现实”——当 unit economics 无法闭环时，他们就发明一套“秘密数学”来计算 adjusted operating income，试图在财务崩塌前通过杠杆和 take-or-pay 合约制造出繁荣的假象。

这种模式与 2008 年的次贷危机如出一辙：用极其复杂的金融衍生品（这次是 compute commencement wall）掩盖底层的信用缺失。当这些巨头在公共空间表演“拯救人类”的圣徒时，他们在私下共谋的是一场巨大的财务博弈。他们赌的是在泡沫破裂前，能通过操纵叙事让国家机器为他们的债务买单。

This is a textbook case of weaponized expression. Silicon Valley's AI titans are performing a bizarre play: sinking hundreds of billions into compute while simultaneously warning that AI might obliterate the human race, calling for urgent government regulation. This shift in narrative isn't a moral awakening; it's a strategic seizure of the cognitive entry point.

Under the logic of meta-violence, those who define "risk" hold the pricing power. By manufacturing an apocalyptic narrative, these firms shift public attention from the concrete question of "does the business model work?" to the grand narrative of "human survival." It's a sophisticated scam: if a product is powerful enough to threaten existence, its astronomical costs and irrational losses are framed as "necessary sacrifices," and regulation becomes a tool to build a regulatory moat, locking out competitors who cannot afford the entry fee.

Beneath this cultural facade, the structural violence is reaching a breaking point. As Bloomberg data shows, token prices are collapsing while construction costs soar. This is the classic mechanism of redefining reality when the facts refuse to cooperate—when unit economics fail to close, they invent a "secret mathematics" to calculate adjusted operating income, using leverage and take-or-pay contracts to simulate prosperity.

This mirrors the 2008 subprime crisis: using complex financial instruments (this time, the "compute commencement wall") to mask a fundamental lack of credit. While these giants perform the role of saints saving humanity in public, they are engaged in a private complicity of financial gambling. They are betting that before the bubble bursts, they can manipulate the narrative enough to make the state foot the bill for their debts.
