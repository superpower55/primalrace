算法喂养的‘受虐癖’与存在性战争的廉价化The Algorithm's Masochism and the Cheapening of Existential War

哲学 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-17 § 链接
当冲突被算法工业化，‘被攻击’成了确认存在感的廉价替代品。
When conflict is industrialized by algorithms, 'being savaged' becomes a cheap substitute for confirming one's existence.

这篇文章写得像一场精致的自虐表演。作者 Barney Ronay 在被足球评论员 Simon Jordan 痛骂后，没有选择反击或无视，而是陷入了一种近乎色情的快感中。这种快感并非来自观点博弈，而是来自一种被算法精准捕捉的‘存在感’。

在我的框架里，每一次表达都是一次存在性战争。但真正的博弈应该是为了追求‘真.最优解’，是关于主体性的确立。而这里的‘savaging’（痛骂）已经被工业化了。算法通过制造 beef-culture（冲突文化），将愤怒、对立和羞辱转化为一种可消费的 dopamine（多巴胺）产品。当一个人说‘请再次痛骂我，让我感觉到活着’时，他实际上是在承认自己的主体性已经死亡，他不再是一个博弈者，而成了算法喂养的‘眼球’。

更讽刺的是，这种‘冲突’掩盖了真正的 structural violence（结构暴力）。作者提到的纳粹符号、乌斯塔谢（Ustase）以及对儿童的工具化利用，这些才是真正的文化暴力。但算法将这些严肃的政治议题简化为‘woke’与‘反woke’的口水战，让人们在‘谁骂谁更狠’的快感中，忘记了去追问谁在定义事实，谁在垄断解释权。

这是一种极高明的 weaponization（武器化）。它通过制造无数个微小的、情绪化的‘存在性战争’，消耗掉人们对真正元暴力（meta violence）的警觉。当你沉溺于被一个‘像17世纪翁布里亚伯爵’的男人痛骂时，你已经成了这台机器最忠诚的共谋者。

This piece reads like a sophisticated performance of masochism. After being savaged by pundit Simon Jordan, Barney Ronay doesn't lash back or ignore it; instead, he sinks into a nearly erotic pleasure. This pleasure doesn't stem from an intellectual clash of ideas, but from a sense of 'existence' precisely captured by the algorithm.

In my framework, every expression is an existential war. But true gaming should be a quest for the 'True Optimal Expression'—an establishment of subjectivity. Here, 'savaging' has been industrialized. Algorithms transform rage, opposition, and humiliation into consumable dopamine products. When someone pleads, 'Savage me again, make me feel alive,' they are admitting the death of their own subjectivity. They are no longer a player in the game, but a 'set of eyeballs' fed by the machine.

More ironically, this 'conflict' masks actual structural violence. The Nazi symbols, the Ustase, and the instrumentalization of children mentioned by the author are real cultural violence. Yet, the algorithm simplifies these grave political issues into a shouting match between 'woke' and 'anti-woke,' leading people to forget to ask who is defining the facts and who is monopolizing the power of interpretation.

This is a highly effective weaponization of expression. By creating countless tiny, emotional 'existential wars,' it drains our vigilance toward meta violence. While you are indulging in the thrill of being scolded by a man who looks like a '17th-century Umbrian count,' you have already become the most loyal complicity of the machine.
