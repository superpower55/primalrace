特使的定价权：当外交官变成家族的私产The Price of Loyalty: Ambassadorships as Family Assets

国际 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-15 § 链接
外交席位被武器化为家族利益的兑换券，这不仅是腐败，更是元暴力的权力分赃。
Weaponizing diplomatic seats as loyalty vouchers is not just corruption, but the spoils of meta-violence.

把外交大使的席位当成给捐款者和亲信的‘赏金’，这在美式政治里不是新鲜事，但 Trump 把这套逻辑推向了极致：他不需要政策专家，只需要 loyalists。在这种叙事里，大使不再是国家利益的代表，而是 Trump 个人意志的延伸，是他在全球范围内安插的‘家奴’。

最精彩的莫过于 Kimberly Guilfoyle。在与 Trump Jr. 订婚失败后，她迅速被‘安置’在希腊大使的位置上。这简直是教科书级的‘补偿性表达’：当一个女性在家族博弈中失去‘准儿媳’的身份票时，通过一个大使席位来维持她的社会阶级和存在感。但结果显而易见，这种缺乏专业能力的 appointment 必然导致 structural violence —— 她利用公权力为特定建筑公司牟利，甚至在确认听证会前要求盟友帮她还 10 万美元的信用卡账单。这已经不是腐败，而是一种极度的傲慢：在这些 loyalists 眼里，国家资源就是 Trump 家族的私产。

从被赦免的税务诈骗犯 Charles Kushner 到选举否认者 Kari Lake，这套任命逻辑的核心是‘共谋’ (complicity)。Trump 通过赋予这些毫无资质的人以权力，让他们在制度内成为自己的共谋者。而那些被空置的席位（如乌克兰、古巴）则揭示了残酷的真相：当‘忠诚’高于‘能力’时，国家真正的战略需求被排在了家族分赃的后面。这是一种典型的 meta violence，它通过重新定义‘合格官员’的标准，将公共职能彻底私有化，让外交变成了权力者的私人俱乐部。

Rewarding donors and allies with plum ambassadorships is a long-standing American tradition, but Trump has pushed this logic to its extreme: he doesn't want policy experts; he wants loyalists. In this narrative, an ambassador is no longer a representative of national interest, but an extension of Trump's personal will—a 'household servant' stationed globally.

Kimberly Guilfoyle is the most vivid example. After her engagement to Trump Jr. collapsed, she was swiftly 'settled' as the ambassador to Greece. This is a textbook case of 'compensatory expression': when a woman loses her 'ticket' as a prospective daughter-in-law in a family game, a diplomatic seat is used to maintain her social class and sense of existence. The result was inevitable; such a lightweight appointment leads directly to structural violence. Promoting specific construction firms and asking allies to pay off a $100,000 Amex bill isn't just corruption—it's extreme arrogance. To these loyalists, state resources are merely the Trump family's private assets.

From the pardoned tax evader Charles Kushner to the election denier Kari Lake, the core of this appointment logic is complicity. By granting power to the unqualified, Trump creates a network of accomplices within the system. The half-empty list of unfilled posts—including critical ones like Ukraine—reveals the grim truth: when 'loyalty' outweighs 'competence,' genuine strategic needs are sidelined for family spoils. This is meta-violence in action, redefining the criteria for 'qualified officials' to privatize public functions and turn diplomacy into a private club for the powerful.
