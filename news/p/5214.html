真相的定价权：谁在定义“虚假信息”？The Pricing Power of Truth: Who Defines 'Disinformation'?

国际 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-23 § 链接
反虚假信息的机构，本质上是国家在争夺“什么是事实”的制造权。
Anti-disinformation units are essentially the state seizing the power to manufacture 'what is factual'.

英国政府成立这个所谓的 National Centre for Information Defence，表面上是在对抗俄罗斯的 bots 和 information warfare，但本质上这是一次典型的认知入口争夺战。当政府宣布要帮助民众“区分事实与虚构” (separate fact from fiction) 时，它实际上是在宣布：由我们来定义什么是“事实”，而除此之外的叙事统统是“虚构”。

这是一个极其危险的武器化逻辑。在加尔通的暴力三角里，这属于典型的 cultural violence。通过建立一个法定机构来垄断对“真实”的解释权，政府实际上是在制造一种新的 meta violence。Farage 把它比作奥威尔的“真理部”虽然带有强烈的政治表演色彩，但逻辑上抓住了重点：谁掌握了定义“ disinformation”的权力，谁就掌握了清除异见、定义敌友的权力。

最讽刺的共谋在于，跨党派的支持让这次权力扩张显得“公正”。当劳工党、保守党和自民党在“国家安全”这个宏大叙事下达成共识，他们实际上是在共谋建立一套新的审查机制。他们告诉公众，为了防御外部敌人的“信息战”，我们必须交出内部的解释权。这种以“保护”为名的控制，正是所有武器化叙事的标准模版。

所谓的“不干涉国内政治异见”在结构性权力面前毫无意义。因为一旦“事实”的定义权被收拢，任何有效的政治异见都可以被轻而易举地贴上“受外国势力操纵”或“传播虚假信息”的标签，从而在 cultural 层面上被合法地抹除。这不再是关于真假的博弈，而是关于谁能决定真相的定价权。

The UK government's establishment of the National Centre for Information Defence is framed as a defense against Russian bots and information warfare, but in essence, it is a strategic struggle for the cognitive entry point. When the government claims it will help the public “separate fact from fiction,” it is actually declaring: we define the 'fact,' and any narrative outside our perimeter is 'fiction.'

This is a dangerous weaponization of expression. In Galtung's Violence Triangle, this is textbook cultural violence. By monopolizing the interpretative power over 'truth' through a statutory body, the state is constructing a new form of meta-violence. While Nigel Farage’s comparison to Orwell’s Ministry of Truth is high-performance political theater, it hits the mark: whoever holds the power to define 'disinformation' holds the power to erase dissent and designate enemies.

The most cynical part is the complicity. The cross-party backing makes this expansion of power look 'just.' When Labour, the Tories, and the Lib Dems align under the grand narrative of 'national security,' they are conspiring to build a new censorship apparatus. They are selling the public a scam: that to defend against external information war, we must surrender internal interpretative autonomy.

Promises not to target 'domestic political dissent' are meaningless in the face of structural power. Once the definition of 'fact' is centralized, any effective political dissent can be easily labeled as 'foreign-influenced' or 'misinformation,' thereby being legitimately erased at the cultural layer. This is no longer a game of truth versus lies; it is a battle for the pricing power of reality.
