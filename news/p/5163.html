专用法庭：是削减暴力的手术刀，还是昂贵的创口贴？Specialist Courtrooms: Surgical Tool or Expensive Band-Aid?

性别 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-20 § 链接
用硬件升级掩盖结构性失效，是典型的 PR 式正义。
Using hardware upgrades to mask structural failure is classic PR justice.

英格兰和威尔士决定搞“专用法庭”来优先处理强奸案。提供私人入口、远程观察、固定开庭日期。从加尔通的暴力三角来看，这确实在 attempt 缩小 direct 层的暴力差额——让受害者在追求正义的过程中不再被二次伤害，这在 physical 层面是 progress。

但我们必须追问：这真的是 structural 层的胜利吗？一个法庭的物理空间被优化了，但背后的司法逻辑变了吗？Claire Waxman 已经点破了真相：积压的案件量在激增。当成千上万的受害者在排队时，几个“高级房间”不过是给极少数幸运儿提供的特权，而绝大多数人依然在 structural violence 的泥潭里等待。如果司法系统的整体效能不提升，专用法庭就是一种典型的 gimmick，是用一个可见的硬件升级来掩盖不可见的系统性崩塌。

最讽刺的是，这种“进步”的叙事往往依赖于个别女性（如 Charlotte Nichols）通过放弃匿名权、用自己的创伤作为筹码来换取。这在存在性战争中是一种极其沉重的博弈：女性必须通过 a form of self-sacrifice 来证明这个系统的失效，而系统则通过赞美她们的“勇敢”来完成一次公关闭环。这种“勇敢”叙事本身就是一种 cultural violence，它将制度的失能转化为个体的道德光环，从而减轻了立法者的愧疚感。

好新闻应该是 Potential 和 Actual 的差额被实质性缩小。如果专用法庭不能带动定罪率的实质提升，而仅仅是让受害者在被拒绝正义时坐得更舒服，那么这依然是一场关于“体面”的共谋，而非关于“正义”的革命。

England and Wales are introducing "specialist courtrooms" for rape cases, featuring private entrances and fixed trial dates. In terms of the Violence Triangle, this is an attempt to reduce the gap in the direct layer—preventing survivors from being re-traumatized during the legal process. On a physical level, this is progress.

But we must ask: is this a structural victory? Optimizing the physical space of a courtroom does not fix the underlying judicial logic. Claire Waxman hit the nail on the head: the backlog is worsening. When tens of thousands are waiting, a few "luxury rooms" are merely privileges for a lucky few, while the masses remain trapped in structural violence. Without a systemic increase in efficiency, these courts are a gimmick—a visible hardware patch for an invisible systemic collapse.

The irony is that this "progress" relies on individual women, like Charlotte Nichols, waiving their anonymity and using their trauma as leverage. In the existential war, this is a brutal trade: women must perform a form of self-sacrifice to prove the system's failure, while the system uses the narrative of their "bravery" to close a PR loop. This "bravery" narrative is itself a form of cultural violence; it transforms institutional failure into individual moral glory, easing the guilt of the legislators.

Good news is defined by the actual narrowing of the gap between Potential and Actual. If these courtrooms don't lead to a substantive rise in conviction rates, but merely make survivors more comfortable while they are denied justice, then this is just a complicity of "decency," not a revolution of justice.
