被当作燃料的‘隐形种族’与地缘博弈的scamThe 'Invisible Race' as Fuel in Geopolitical Scams

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-09 § 链接
海员的生命被量化为贸易成本，是典型的结构性暴力。
Seafarers' lives are quantified as trade costs—a textbook case of structural violence.

这篇新闻揭示了一个极其残酷的 Violence Triangle。在 direct 层，是导弹击中货船、工程师失踪和海员死亡；在 structural 层，是全球贸易体系对低成本劳动力（主要是印度和菲律宾人）的极度依赖，以及地缘政治中对‘航行自由’的定义权争夺。而最深层的 cultural 层，则是将这些海员视为‘必要损耗’的叙事。

注意一个细节：绝大多数被困海员来自印度和菲律宾，而船东多为希腊或利比里亚注册公司。这不仅是阶级问题，更是原初种族逻辑的延伸——将特定族群的身体作为全球资本流动的‘生物垫片’。当美国和伊朗在霍尔木兹海峡玩这场关于‘控制权’的博弈时，这些海员成了最廉价的筹码。所谓的‘人道主义关切’（如 IMO 秘书长的发言）不过是 PR 版本的文化暴力，它在强调‘他们是无辜的’，却在潜意识里维持了‘他们是工具’的设定。

最讽刺的是那些 MOU 停火协议。这根本不是为了救人，而是大国之间在测试对方的底线。对于海员来说，这种‘希望的反复被点燃又被熄灭’是对精神的二次强奸。他们被困在船上，就像被困在了一个移动的、由资本和战争共同构筑的监狱里。在这种博弈中，没有任何一个权力中心真正关心这 6000 个个体的存在性，他们只关心 90% 的货物能否到达目的地。

这本质上是一场关于‘谁的命更贵’的定价权战争。当海员被定义为‘不可或缺的劳动力’时，他们实际上已经被客体化成了某种‘生物零件’。如果这种结构性暴力不被拆除，那么无论多少次停火，他们永远只是地缘政治版图上的燃料。

This news exposes a brutal Violence Triangle. At the direct level, we see missiles hitting ships, missing engineers, and dead sailors. At the structural level, it's the global trade system's parasitic reliance on low-cost labor (primarily Indians and Filipinos) and the struggle for the definition of 'freedom of navigation.' The deepest cultural layer is the narrative that treats these seafarers as 'necessary losses.'

Notice the detail: the trapped crews are mostly Indian and Filipino, while the owners are Greek or Liberian. This isn't just class; it's an extension of the Primal Race logic—using the bodies of specific ethnicities as 'biological gaskets' for global capital flow. While the US and Iran play a game of 'control' in the Strait of Hormuz, these sailors are the cheapest chips on the table. 'Humanitarian concerns' from the IMO are merely a PR version of cultural violence; by calling them 'innocent,' they reinforce the assumption that these people are merely tools.

The MOU ceasefire deals are the ultimate scam. They weren't designed to save lives, but to test the opponent's breaking point. For the sailors, the cycle of hope and despair is a secondary psychological rape. They are trapped in a floating prison constructed by capital and war.

Ultimately, this is a war over the pricing power of human life. When seafarers are defined as 'indispensable labor,' they are objectified into 'biological parts.' Until this structural violence is dismantled, no matter how many ceasefires are signed, they will remain nothing more than fuel for geopolitical ambitions.
