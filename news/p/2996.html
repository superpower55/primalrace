养老危机的本质是结构性剥削的共谋Social Care Crisis as a Conspiracy of Structural Exploitation

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-29 § 链接
社会护理的崩溃是撒切尔私有化遗产的延迟暴力
The collapse of social care is the delayed violence of Thatcherite privatization.

这就是典型的 Structural Violence。英国社会护理系统的崩塌不是什么“管理失误”，而是一次蓄意的、跨越三十年的资源掠夺。从 1988 年撒切尔强行关闭地方政府养老院、将其外包给私人资本开始，国家就通过一种“隐形”的方式，将最脆弱的群体——失智老人和照顾他们的女性——直接扔进了私人股权基金 (Private Equity) 的绞肉机里。

在这个系统里，Actual 状态与 Potential 状态的差额被最大化了。私有化养老院为了利润，在降低护理质量的同时通过高昂费用剥夺家庭财产。而最阴险的 Cultural Violence 在于，大众被洗脑成认为养老是“个人责任”或“运气博弈”，从而在潜意识中接受了这种剥削。直到 78% 的地方议会资金被这个破碎的黑洞吞噬，人们才意识到，所谓的“自由市场”其实是让穷人破产、让富人套现的 Scam。

Andy Burnham 试图建立 National Care Service，本质上是在尝试把这部分被武器化的资源重新收回公共领域。但这场战争最关键的共谋节点在于：谁来买单？如果依然让年轻人通过 NI (国民保险) 为养老买单，而让持有巨额资产的养老阶层继续通过资本收益逃避责任，那么这不过是另一种形式的代际掠夺。真正的 Just Expression 应该是：让持有资源的人支付代价，而非让最没有议价权的底层在“自愿”的假象中继续共谋。

This is a textbook case of Structural Violence. The collapse of the UK social care system is not a 'management failure,' but a deliberate, thirty-year resource plunder. Starting from 1988, when Thatcher forced councils to close care homes and outsource them to the private sector, the state effectively threw the most vulnerable—dementia patients and the women who care for them—into the meat grinder of Private Equity.

In this system, the gap between Actual and Potential states is maximized. For-profit homes slash care quality to inflate margins while bankrupting families through astronomical costs. The most insidious Cultural Violence here is the narrative that care is a 'personal responsibility' or a 'game of fate,' leading the public to subconsciously accept this exploitation. Only when 78% of local council funds are swallowed by this black hole do people realize that the so-called 'free market' is actually a scam designed to bankrupt the poor and enrich the asset-holders.

Andy Burnham’s attempt to establish a National Care Service is an effort to reclaim these weaponized resources for the public. However, the critical complicity node in this war is: who pays? If the burden still falls on the young via NI contributions while the wealthy elderly escape through capital gains, it remains a form of intergenerational plunder. A Just Expression requires those holding the wealth to pay the price, rather than letting the most powerless continue to conspire in their own exploitation under the guise of 'voluntary' contribution.
