以“价值平等”为名的工业级人力资源精准喂养Industrial-Grade Human Resource Feeding Masked as 'Value Equality'

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-27 § 链接
将教育适配于当地商业需求，本质是将学生从“人”降格为“零件”。
Adapting education to local business needs is the act of degrading students from 'humans' to 'components'.

Burnham 试图用一套极其浪漫的叙事——“让安全帽与学位帽同样受尊重”——来掩盖一个冷酷的结构性陷阱。这根本不是在赋予技术教育以尊严，而是在通过 structural violence 预先定义一个人的存在性上限。当一个 14 岁的孩子被告知他的课程需要“响应当地就业市场”时，他其实是被告知：你的生命价值由当地企业的 HR 决定，你的认知入口将被限定在某个特定工业区的需求之内。

这套逻辑是典型的武器化表达。它把“就业率”和“削减福利开支”这两个政府的 KPI，包装成对年轻人的“关怀”。在这种叙事里，教育不再是主体性的觉醒，而成了一个精准的人力资源适配插件。所谓的 parity（平等），其实是让更多人心甘情愿地进入一个被预设好的、低流动性的底层赛道。如果一个地区的经济支柱是低端制造业，那么这里的孩子就应该在 14 岁起就被训练成合格的螺丝钉，而不需要通过 academic route 去质疑这个结构的合理性。

最令人作呕的共谋在于，这种计划被描述为“权力下放”和“去中心化”。实际上，权力并没有交给学生，而是从 Whitehall（白厅）转移到了 local businesses（当地企业）手中。这只是将元暴力的解释权从国家层面下放到资本层面。当教育被直接挂钩于 local jobs market，学校就成了企业的预科班，而学生则成了被提前定价的商品。这种“最优解表达”在政客看来是解决了失业危机，但在个体看来，这不过是提前在生物墙之外又筑起了一道认知的墙。

Burnham is employing a romanticized narrative—"valuing the hard hat as much as the graduation cap"—to camouflage a cold, structural trap. This is not about granting dignity to vocational education; it is about pre-defining a person's existential ceiling through structural violence. When a 14-year-old is told their curriculum must be "responsive to the local jobs market," they are effectively being told that their life's value is determined by a local HR manager, and their cognitive entry points will be confined to the needs of a specific industrial zone.

This logic is a textbook example of weaponized expression. It packages government KPIs, such as "employment rates" and "cutting the benefits bill," as "care" for the youth. In this narrative, education is no longer about the awakening of subjectivity, but about becoming a precision-engineered plugin for human resource needs. The so-called "parity" is actually a mechanism to funnel more people into pre-determined, low-mobility bottom-tier tracks. If a region's economic pillar is low-end manufacturing, children there should be trained as compliant screws from age 14, rather than using an academic route to question the legitimacy of this structure.

The most repulsive complicity lies in describing this as "devolution" and "decentralization." In reality, power has not been handed to the students; it has merely shifted from Whitehall to local businesses. This is a transfer of the meta-violence's interpretive power from the state to capital. When education is directly pegged to the local jobs market, schools become corporate preparatory classes, and students become pre-priced commodities. This "optimal expression" may solve the unemployment crisis for politicians, but for the individual, it is simply the construction of a cognitive wall atop the biological wall.
