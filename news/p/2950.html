以药费为饵，用疫苗为祭的生存博弈Prescription Costs as Bait, Vaccines as Sacrifice

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-28 § 链接
权力不关心生命，它只关心生命被定价后的政治价值。
Power doesn't care about life; it only cares about the political value of life once priced.

特朗普政府计划砍掉 36 亿美元的 Medicare 补贴，本质上是一次精准的 structural violence。通过提高 2500 万老年人的药费，权力将生存资源从最脆弱的群体手中抽离。最讽刺的是，这种剥夺被精准地排期在期中选举之后——这说明在权力的计算中，弱者的生命权只是一个可以随时调整的 PR 变量，只有在影响选票时才具有暂时的“价值”。

与此同时，RFK Jr. 对疫苗计划的激进削减，则是一场典型的 weaponized expression。将“缓解自闭症”这种伪科学叙事包装成政治遗产（legacy），实际上是在利用认知入口篡改公共卫生事实。当权力通过定义“什么是健康”来干预生物学事实时，它在制造一种新的生物墙：让那些无法获得有效疫苗保护的底层人口，在未来的流行病中成为被筛选掉的冗余。

而在这场权力游戏中，最令人作呕的是那种典型的 masculine-centric narrative。在林赛·格雷厄姆的葬礼上，特朗普将其描述为“战士”和“爱战争的人”，将大规模杀戮和战争机器的运转浪漫化为一种“爱国主义”的激情。这种元暴力将人类的身体降格为可消耗的工具，而那些在战争中死去的人，不过是这个男性中心叙事中被抹掉的注脚。

从药费补贴到疫苗削减，再到战争崇拜，这套逻辑高度统一：剥夺弱者的主体性，将生存权转化为权力者的筹码。在这个系统中，没有所谓的“良政”，只有在不同层级的共谋中，权力如何更高效地收割那些被定义为“次要”的生命。

The Trump administration's plan to slash $3.6bn in Medicare subsidies is a textbook case of structural violence. By hiking drug costs for 25 million seniors, power strips essential resources from the most vulnerable. The most cynical part is the timing—scheduled for after the midterms—proving that in the eyes of the state, the right to survive is merely a PR variable, possessing value only when it affects votes.

Simultaneously, RFK Jr.’s aggressive cuts to vaccine schedules represent the weaponisation of expression. Packaging the pseudo-scientific narrative of "alleviating autism" as a political legacy is an attempt to seize the cognitive entrance and rewrite public health facts. When power defines "what is healthy" to override biological reality, it constructs a new biological wall, leaving marginalized populations as redundancies to be filtered out in the next pandemic.

Overlapping this is the pervasive masculine-centric narrative. At Lindsey Graham's funeral, Trump lauded him as a "warrior" who "never saw a war he didn't like," romanticizing mass killing and the war machine as "patriotism." This meta-violence reduces human bodies to disposable tools, where those killed in war are merely erased footnotes in a male-centric script.

From drug subsidies to vaccine cuts and the glorification of war, the logic is seamless: strip the subject of their agency and convert the right to exist into a bargaining chip for the powerful. In this system, there is no such thing as "good governance," only a series of complicity where power more efficiently harvests those defined as "secondary" lives.
