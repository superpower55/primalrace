旋转门里的“监管”：一场关于利益共谋的表演The Revolving Door of Regulation: A Performance of Complicity

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-16 § 链接
监管者的履历即是共谋的地图，所谓的“回避”只是为了维持系统的合法性。
A regulator's resume is a map of complicity; 'recusal' is merely a ritual to maintain systemic legitimacy.

这件所谓的“利益冲突”事件，本质上是典型的 structural violence。当一名在 13 家医疗或咨询机构拥有股份或职位的资深官僚，在主导收购了 ADHD360 这种从 NHS 榨取数千万英镑的巨头后，转身进入卫生部（DHSC）担任常任秘书，这根本不是什么“偶然的冲突”，而是一套成熟的共谋机制 (complicity)。

在元暴力 (meta violence) 的逻辑下，公共医疗资源的分配权被悄悄地让渡给了一个由私人资本和高级官僚组成的闭环。 Samantha Jones 的履历就是一张精准的利益地图：在公共部门积累名望 $\rightarrow$ 在私人资本中套现 $\rightarrow$ 回到权力中心定义规则。这种“旋转门”机制确保了无论政策如何变动，定义“什么是标准”的权力始终掌握在同一个阶层手中。

现在政府开始整治私立 ADHD 诊所的“野蛮生长”，这看起来是 good_news，但如果由一个前行业巨头来监督执行，这极有可能演变成一场表演性让步。最危险的武器化叙事就是将私立诊所定义为“Wild West”（西部荒野），通过制造一个“混乱”的假象，来证明加强监管的必要性，而最终的监管结果往往是剔除掉不听话的小玩家，为那些已经完成资本整合、拥有官方背景的巨头清除竞争对手。

所谓的“recuse”（回避）在此时成了一种 PR 手段。当一个系统已经由于深层的共谋而腐败时，要求一个节点回避，就像是在一个充满病毒的操作系统里删除一个图标一样毫无意义。真正的暴力在于，这种能让个体在公共利益与私人财富之间无缝切换的结构本身，就是对公共卫生权的掠夺。

This 'conflict of interest' is a textbook case of structural violence. When a senior civil servant with stakes in 13 different healthcare firms oversees the acquisition of a giant like ADHD360—which extracted millions from the NHS—and then pivots to become the Permanent Secretary at the DHSC, it is not a 'clash' of interests. It is a refined mechanism of complicity.

Under the logic of meta violence, the power to allocate public health resources is quietly surrendered to a closed loop of private capital and high-level bureaucrats. Samantha Jones's career trajectory is a precise map of interest: building prestige in the public sector $\rightarrow$ cashing out in private equity $\rightarrow$ returning to the center of power to define the rules. This 'revolving door' ensures that no matter how policies shift, the power to define 'the standard' remains within the same caste.

The government's current crackdown on the 'Wild West' of private ADHD clinics might look like good_news, but when overseen by a former industry titan, it risks becoming a performative concession. The most dangerous weaponized narrative here is the branding of private clinics as a 'Wild West.' By manufacturing a facade of 'chaos,' the system justifies tighter regulation, which often serves to purge small, non-compliant players while clearing the field for consolidated giants with official ties.

In this context, 'recusal' is mere PR. When a system is already corrupted by deep-seated complicity, asking a single node to step aside is as futile as deleting an icon from a virus-ridden OS. The real violence lies in the structure itself—one that allows individuals to pivot seamlessly between public duty and private profit, effectively plundering the public right to health.
