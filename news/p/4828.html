防火墙的失效与“德国精神”的武器化The Failure of the Firewall and the Weaponization of 'Germanness'

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-06 § 链接
当主流叙事失去定价权，极右翼便通过制造“原初恐惧”夺取认知入口。
When mainstream narratives lose pricing power, the far-right seizes cognitive entry points by manufacturing primal fear.

AfD 在萨克森-安哈尔特州的胜利不是一次简单的选举意外，而是一场典型的存在性战争。主流政党所谓的 Brandmauer（防火墙）在 structural 层面上已经崩塌。当 CDU 这种传统权力机器无法在经济和安全上提供实际的 actual 救济时，它所维护的“民主”和“责任”就成了毫无意义的文化装饰，变成了元暴力的空壳。

Siegmund 的操作是教科书级的表达武器化。他骑着东德时期的摩托车，利用“Ostalgie”（东德情结）这个认知入口，精准地将经济挫败感转化为对移民的仇恨。他试图将州格言从 “Think Modern” 改为 “Think German”，这本质上是在重新定义“事实”的制造权：他要通过剔除多样性，将“德国人”这个身份重新定义为一个排他的、纯净的、基于生物墙的种族概念。在这种叙事里，任何不符合他定义的个体都被客体化为“需要被清理的垃圾”。

最讽刺的是，AfD 宣称要“建立桥梁”，但其具体计划——禁掉学校的彩虹旗、大规模遣返——正是要拆除所有公正表达的可能性。这是一种极端的 masculine-centric narrative：通过制造一个外部敌人（移民/少数群体），来赋予其支持者一种虚假的、基于压迫他人的“强者”身份。在这种共谋中，选民用自己的主体性交换了一张通往所谓“纯净德国”的入场券。

这场胜利揭示了一个残酷的事实：当文明的掩体无法覆盖生存的焦虑，人们会毫不犹豫地选择最原始的暴力叙事。防火墙挡不住饥饿和恐惧，它只能挡住那些还在幻想着靠“礼貌讨论”就能拯救民主的 naive 理想主义者。

The AfD's victory in Saxony-Anhalt is not a mere electoral fluke, but a textbook existential war. The so-called Brandmauer (firewall) of mainstream parties has collapsed at the structural level. When traditional power machines like the CDU fail to provide actual relief in economy and security, the 'democracy' and 'responsibility' they uphold become meaningless cultural decorations—empty shells of meta-violence.

Siegmund's strategy is a masterclass in the weaponisation of expression. Riding an East German moped and leveraging 'Ostalgie' as a cognitive entry point, he precisely converts economic frustration into hatred for immigrants. His attempt to change the state slogan from 'Think Modern' to 'Think German' is essentially an attempt to seize the power to manufacture facts: he seeks to redefine 'German' as an exclusive, pure racial concept based on the biological wall.

The irony is that while the AfD claims to 'build bridges,' its actual agenda—banning rainbow flags in schools and mass deportations—is designed to demolish all possibilities of just expressions. This is an extreme masculine-centric narrative: by creating an external enemy, it grants its supporters a fake 'strongman' identity based on the oppression of others. In this complicity, voters exchange their subjectivity for a ticket to a so-called 'pure Germany.'

This victory reveals a brutal truth: when the shelter of 'civilization' fails to cover survival anxiety, people will unhesitatingly embrace the most primitive narratives of violence. Firewalls cannot block hunger or fear; they only block the naive idealists who believe democracy can be saved through 'polite discussion.'
