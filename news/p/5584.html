用低薪者的血肉，喂养所谓的“就业率”Feeding 'Employment Rates' with the Flesh of the Poor

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-16 § 链接
将生存权的削减包装成机会的增加，是典型的结构性暴力叙事。
Packaging the erosion of survival rights as the creation of opportunity is textbook structural violence.

保守党这套逻辑简直是教科书级别的 scam。他们把“冻结最低工资”包装成解决青年失业的良药，潜台词极其残忍：为了让年轻人能找到工作，我们得让他们变得足够廉价，廉价到雇主愿意为了省钱而雇佣他们。

这在加尔通的暴力三角里是标准的 structural violence。它不直接杀人，但通过制度性地剥夺低薪者的 purchasing power（五年损失近 9000 英镑），将这部分人群强行推向贫困线。在这种叙事中，低薪劳动者不再是具有主体性的“人”，而成了一个可以被随意调低定价的“成本项”。

最令人作呕的是这种 weaponized 叙事：用“就业率”这个宏大指标来掩盖个体生存空间的萎缩。增加 27 万个就业岗位，代价是让所有低薪者在通胀中被洗劫。这根本不是在创造机会，而是在通过降低生存标准来制造一种“繁荣”的假象。这种“最优解”只属于雇主，而对于劳动者来说，这是一种主体性的死亡——你被允许存在，但前提是你必须接受被剥削的定价。

而工党虽然在口头批判，但其内部对于是否兑现“同工同酬”承诺的犹豫，本身就是一种共谋。当政治博弈的筹码变成了底层人的饭碗，所谓的“经济理性”不过是元暴力在劳动力市场上的延伸：定义谁该牺牲，由谁来定义，而牺牲者永远没有解释权。

The Tory logic here is a textbook scam. By framing a minimum wage freeze as a cure for youth unemployment, their subtext is brutal: to help young people find work, we must make them cheap enough that employers hire them simply to save money.

In the Violence Triangle, this is pure structural violence. It doesn't kill directly, but by institutionally stripping the purchasing power of low-paid workers—a loss of nearly £9,000 over five years—it forcibly pushes a segment of the population toward the poverty line. In this narrative, the low-paid worker is no longer a human with agency, but a 'cost item' whose price can be arbitrarily lowered.

What's most repulsive is the weaponised narrative: using the grand metric of 'employment rates' to mask the shrinkage of individual survival space. Adding 270,000 jobs at the cost of letting all low-wage earners be plundered by inflation is not creating opportunity; it is manufacturing a facade of prosperity by lowering the baseline of existence. This 'optimal solution' serves only the employers; for the workers, it is the death of subjectivity—you are permitted to exist only if you accept the price of exploitation.

Even Labour's hesitation over fulfilling its promise of equal pay, despite their public criticism, is a form of complicity. When the bargaining chips of political games are the bowls of the poor, so-called 'economic rationality' is merely the extension of meta-violence in the labour market: deciding who must be sacrificed, and by whom, while the sacrificed are denied any right to explain their existence.
