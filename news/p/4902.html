权力下放的真相：一场名为“旅游税”的共谋分赃The Truth of Devolution: A Complicit Looting via 'Tourist Tax'

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-10 § 链接
所谓的“权力下放”往往是风险下放，而收益则在共谋者之间重新分配。
Devolution is often just the delegation of risk, while the spoils are redistributed among complicit elites.

把收税权交给市长，这在政治叙事里被包装成“权力下放” (devolution) 的进步。但剥开这层皮，这就是一次典型的结构性共谋：中央政府在预算压力下甩掉锅，把增加成本的“恶名”交给地方官员，而市长们则在期待一个可以随意支配的现金池。

这种 levy 的设计极其阴险。虽然 Angela Rayner 试图用“按比例收费”来伪装成对预算旅行者的保护，但本质上这依然是 structural violence。成本最终会通过酒店和 Airbnb 的定价机制，由最底层的服务人员（绝大多数是女性和移民）以及预算有限的游客承担。而这些钱被投向所谓的“公共服务”和“基础设施”，在实际操作中往往变成了政客们刷政绩的景观工程。

UKHospitality 的抗议只是另一种博弈。他们不是在担心游客的钱包，而是在担心自己的利润率被侵蚀。他们提出的“度假奖金” (holiday bonus) 方案更像是一个 scam：希望中央政府继续出钱，而地方政府只需要通过吸引游客来领赏。这不过是两拨共谋者在争夺谁来定义这个“钱袋子”的入口。

当权力被碎片化地交给地方市长，且缺乏透明的上限监管时，这种“权力”就变成了武器。它不再是为了改善公共生活，而成了地方门阀在认知入口上制造“城市竞争力”的筹码。一个城市的生长，不应该建立在对临时访客的随机抽税之上。

Handing taxing power to mayors is packaged in political narratives as the progress of devolution. But strip away the veneer, and it is a classic structural complicity: the central government sheds the burden of budget pressure by delegating the 'villainy' of raising costs to local officials, while mayors anticipate a slush fund they can manipulate at will.

The design of this levy is insidious. Although Angela Rayner attempts to disguise it as protection for budget travelers by using a percentage-based fee, it remains structural violence. The cost will ultimately be borne by the lowest-level service workers—mostly women and migrants—and budget-constrained tourists, through the pricing mechanisms of hotels and Airbnbs. Meanwhile, the revenue invested in 'public services' and 'infrastructure' often transforms into vanity projects for politicians to boost their profiles.

UKHospitality's protest is simply another game. They aren't worried about the tourists' wallets, but about the erosion of their own profit margins. Their proposed 'holiday bonus' is a scam: they want the central government to keep paying while local authorities simply collect rewards for attracting visitors. This is just two groups of complicitors fighting over who controls the entry point of the 'money bag'.

When power is fragmented and handed to mayors without transparent caps, this 'power' becomes a weapon. It is no longer about improving public life, but a chip for local elites to manufacture 'city competitiveness' at the cognitive entry. The growth of a city should not be built on the random taxing of temporary visitors.
