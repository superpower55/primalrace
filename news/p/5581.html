讣告里的足球赛与消失的受害者Football Matches in Obituaries and the Vanishing Victims

哲学 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-16 § 链接
用个体温情掩盖结构暴力，是典型的文化共谋。
Using individual warmth to mask structural violence is a classic form of cultural complicity.

这是一篇典型的、充满温情的精英阶层共谋样本。在《卫报》给 Lord Laming 的讣告信件里，我们看到了什么？一个严谨的导师，一场充满活力的足球赛，以及一个“凶猛且不拿黄牌”的后卫。这种叙事将一个掌握司法资源、决定罪犯命运的权力者，简化为一个在绿茵场上奔跑的、具有人格魅力的个体。

但在 Laming 这种人定义的“细节”和“一致性” (consistency) 背后，是无数被定格在结构性暴力中的受试者。在 probation service（缓刑服务）这个权力场域里， supervisor 的一个决定就意味着一个人的自由或囚禁。然而在讣告的叙事入口中，权力的傲慢被转化为“职业操守”，而他所面对的那些被定义为 offenders 的人群，在这一场温情的回忆录里彻底消失了。他们不是人，只是 Laming 职业生涯中用来证明自己“严谨”的背景板。

最讽刺的是那场 1-0 的胜利。这种“不让任何东西通过”的凶猛，在足球场上是美德，在司法权力运作中则是绝对的掌控。这种将权力运作浪漫化、个体化的表达，正是 meta violence 的运作方式：它通过制造一个“好人”的形象，让人们忘记这个“好人”所代表的制度本身就是一套剥削与规训的机器。共谋者们在信件中互相确认对方的优秀，而这封信本身就是一块遮羞布，掩盖了权力在执行过程中必然产生的暴力差额。

This is a textbook sample of elite complicity wrapped in warmth. In this letter to Lord Laming's obituary in The Guardian, what do we see? A meticulous mentor, a spirited football match, and a 'fierce full back' who never received a yellow card. This narrative reduces a power-holder who controlled judicial resources and decided the fates of offenders to a charismatic individual running on a green pitch.

Yet, behind the 'attention to detail' and 'consistency' defined by men like Laming, lie countless subjects frozen in structural violence. In the power field of the probation service, a supervisor's decision can mean the difference between freedom and imprisonment. However, in the narrative entrance of this obituary, the arrogance of power is converted into 'professionalism,' while the people defined as offenders vanish completely. They are not humans here; they are merely backgrounds used to prove Laming's 'rigor.'

The irony peaks with that 1-0 victory. This 'fierceness' that lets nothing pass is a virtue on the football field, but in the operation of judicial power, it is absolute control. Romanticizing the exercise of power in this way is exactly how meta violence works: it creates the image of a 'good man' to make us forget that the system this 'good man' represents is a machine of exploitation and discipline. The co-conspirators confirm each other's excellence in the letter, and the letter itself serves as a veil, covering the violence gap inevitably produced by the operation of power.
