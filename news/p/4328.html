自然不是救世主，而是被我们阉割后的残存Nature is Not a Savior, But a Survivor of Our Castration

好消息 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-21 § 链接
所谓“自然解决方案”的成功，本质上是对人类长期实施的结构性暴力的部分撤回。
The success of 'nature-based solutions' is essentially a partial withdrawal of long-term structural violence against the land.

看到这种新闻，不要 naive 地庆祝自然在“拯救”我们。Holnicote 庄园之所以在干旱中保持翠绿，不是因为自然突然变得慷慨，而是因为人们停止了对土地的阉割。所谓的 rewilding（再野化），其实就是把之前被强行拉直的河流还给它，把被抽干的湿地还给它。这在加尔通的暴力三角里，是 structural violence 的一次局部撤回。

几代人以来，人类的逻辑是“快速排水”，这种将自然客体化、工具化的思维，就是典型的 masculine-centric narrative（男性中心叙事）：认为自然是需要被征服、被修剪、被高效管理的资源。我们把河流拉直，是为了更高效地服务于农业和工业，这种对自然“表达”的强行修正，最终导致了整体生态系统的 Potential 与 Actual 之间出现了巨大的差额，而这个差额在气候危机面前，就变成了毁灭性的暴力。

有趣的是，右翼评论员试图将 rewilding 武器化，将其描述为野火的诱因。这是一种典型的认知入口争夺战：通过制造恐惧，试图维持那种“掌控一切”的旧权力结构。而 National Trust 的回应虽然正确，但依然在用“基础设施”这种工业词汇来描述自然。这说明即使在进步的叙事里，我们依然在潜意识地共谋，试图将自然重新定义为一种“有用的工具”，而不是一个具有主体性的存在。

好新闻的机制在于，它证明了当结构性暴力减轻时，系统能够自我修复。但我们要警惕的是，这种“绿色奇迹”是否会变成一种新的 PR 包装，让权力者认为只要做几个 rewilding 样板房，就可以继续在其他地方肆无忌惮地掠夺。如果解释权依然掌握在那些习惯于“管理”自然的人手中，那么这次的翠绿，不过是暴政在崩溃前的一次温柔表演。

Do not be naive and celebrate nature 'saving' us. The lush greenery of the Holnicote estate is not a gift of nature's generosity, but a result of humans ceasing the castration of the land. Rewilding is simply returning the rivers that were forcibly straightened and the wetlands that were drained. In terms of Galtung's Violence Triangle, this is a localized withdrawal of structural violence.

For generations, the dominant logic has been 'rapid drainage'—a masculine-centric narrative that views nature as a resource to be conquered, pruned, and efficiently managed. By straightening rivers for agricultural and industrial efficiency, we forcibly corrected nature's expression. The resulting gap between Potential and Actual has now manifested as destructive violence in the face of the climate crisis.

It is telling that right-wing commentators attempt to weaponize rewilding by labeling it a cause of wildfires. This is a classic battle over cognitive entry points: using fear to maintain an obsolete power structure of 'total control.' While the National Trust's response is correct, they still describe nature as 'essential infrastructure.' This reveals a lingering complicity; even in progressive narratives, we subconsciously try to redefine nature as a 'useful tool' rather than a subject with its own agency.

This is good_news only because it proves the system can heal when structural violence is reduced. However, we must remain vigilant. We cannot allow this 'green miracle' to become a PR scam, letting those in power believe that a few rewilding showrooms justify continued plunder elsewhere. As long as the power of interpretation remains with those who seek to 'manage' nature, this greenery is nothing more than a tender performance before the inevitable collapse.
