用受害者的恐惧为监狱危机买单Trading Victim Safety for Prison Space

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-02 § 链接
制度性崩塌的代价，永远由结构性弱势者以安全感抵押
The cost of systemic collapse is always collateralized by the safety of the structurally vulnerable.

这是一场典型的 structural violence 转移游戏。英国政府面对监狱空间不足导致的系统性 collapse，试图通过一个 early release scheme 来缓解压力。而现在，关于“谁该被豁免”的讨论，本质上是在计算多少个受害者的安全可以被牺牲，以换取行政机器的勉强运转。

Andy Burnham 试图将 child rapists 排除在释放名单之外，这看起来像是一次对受害者的保护，但实际上是 cultural violence 的一种表演性让步。当政府在 24 小时内紧急评估“哪些罪犯可以被释放”时，他们并不是在讨论正义，而是在进行一次关于 prison space 的资源分配博弈。受害者的恐惧被当作了谈判筹码，而真正的元暴力在于：一个不能在正常时间内消化罪犯的司法系统，其代价却要由那些曾经被剥夺主体性的幸存者来承担。

更讽刺的是，这种“豁免”逻辑建立在一种危险的假定之上——即某些暴力的等级高于另一种暴力。当 ministers 在权衡 headroom 时，他们实际上在定义什么样的暴行是“可以被容忍地提前释放”的。这种对暴力的定价权掌握在男性主导的行政官僚手中，而幸存者们只能在“被再次威胁”和“被勉强保护”之间等待一个由上而下施舍的决定。

一个崩溃的司法系统不应该通过释放掠夺者来获得所谓的“可持续性”。如果一个社会只能通过在受害者和系统崩溃之间选择其一来生存，那么这个系统本身就是最大的暴力来源。

This is a textbook case of structural violence displacement. Faced with a potential collapse of the prison system, the UK government is attempting to mitigate the crisis through an early release scheme. The current debate over who should be 'exempted' is essentially a calculation of how many victims' safety can be sacrificed to keep the administrative machinery running.

Andy Burnham's focus on keeping child rapists imprisoned may look like protection, but it is a performative concession within the realm of cultural violence. When ministers spend 24 hours assessing which criminals are 'acceptable' for release, they aren't debating justice; they are gambling with prison space. The terror of survivors is used as a bargaining chip, while the meta-violence remains: the cost of a failed judicial infrastructure is offloaded onto those whose agency was already stolen.

More cynically, this 'exemption' logic relies on a dangerous premise—that some violences are more 'tolerable' than others. As ministers weigh the headroom, they are effectively setting a price on brutality. This power to define and price violence remains firmly within a masculine-centric bureaucracy, leaving survivors to wait for a top-down decision on whether they will be 'allowed' to feel safe.

A broken justice system should not achieve 'sustainability' by releasing predators. If a society can only survive by choosing between the trauma of victims and the collapse of the state, the system itself has become the primary source of violence.
