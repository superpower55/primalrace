理性的入场券与被遗忘的生物墙The Ticket to Reason and the Forgotten Biological Wall

哲学 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-21 § 链接
科学叙事是最高效的认知入口，但它往往在剔除神灵的同时剔除了性别
Scientific narrative is the most efficient cognitive entry, yet it often erases gender while erasing gods.

Alice Roberts 的阅读史是一个典型的“认知入口”迁移过程：从童年的人文温情，到 Dawkins 带来的生物学视角，最终通过《自私的基因》完成对宗教信仰的 annhilation。这看起来是一场关于“理智”的胜利，但从存在性战争的角度看，这其实是她拿到了一张进入男性中心叙事 (masculine-centric narrative) 顶层俱乐部的入场券。

Dawkins 和 Gould 代表的是一种极端的、去情境化的理性。这种理性在拆穿“神圣创造”这个 scam 时极其有力，因为它用生物学事实取代了文化暴力的解释权。但问题在于，这种被奉为金科玉律的“科学客观”，在历史上长期地与元暴力共谋。它在定义“自然过程”时，习惯性地将女性的生物墙（如生育、激素、生理周期）简化为某种需要被解释的“生物特性”，而非一种结构性的压迫基础。

Roberts 庆祝自己成为了一个 humanist，但这种 humanism 往往默认是以“男性”为原型的通用人类。当她通过阅读 Dawkins 摆脱了 Anglican faith 的规训时，她可能并未意识到，她进入的是另一个由男性学者定义、书写并垄断解释权的认知场域。在这种场域里，理性被武器化为一种筛选机制：只有能够剥离情感、剥离身体经验、进入纯粹逻辑博弈的人，才能被视为“真正理智”的。

一个生物学家能够通过阅读意识到神不存在，这是 good_news，因为这削减了 cultural violence 的一层。但如果这种理性在面对性别不平等时，依然习惯性地用“生物学差异”来合理化结构性暴力，那么这种理性就成了另一种高级的共谋。

Alice Roberts' reading history is a classic migration of cognitive entries: from childhood warmth to the biological perspective of Dawkins, finally completing the annihilation of faith through *The Selfish Gene*. This appears to be a victory for 'reason,' but in the context of an existential war, it is actually her acquiring a ticket into the top-tier club of the masculine-centric narrative.

Dawkins and Gould represent an extreme, decontextualized rationality. This reason is incredibly powerful in exposing the scam of 'divine creation' because it replaces the interpretative power of cultural violence with biological facts. However, this 'scientific objectivity' has historically been in complicity with meta-violence. In defining 'natural processes,' it habitually simplifies the biological wall of women—such as reproduction and hormones—into mere 'biological traits' rather than the foundation of structural oppression.

Roberts celebrates becoming a humanist, but this humanism often defaults to a universal human prototyped as 'male.' While she escaped the discipline of the Anglican faith through Dawkins, she may not have realized she entered another cognitive field defined, written, and monopolized by male scholars. In this field, reason is weaponized as a screening mechanism: only those who can strip away emotion and bodily experience to enter pure logical gaming are seen as 'truly rational.'

It is good_news that a biologist realizes God does not exist, as it reduces a layer of cultural violence. But if this same reason habitually uses 'biological difference' to justify structural violence when facing gender inequality, then this reason becomes another sophisticated form of complicity.
