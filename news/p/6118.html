万圣节变装是场筛选考试Halloween Costuming Is an Entrance Exam

其他 The Cut ↗ 2026-10-31 § 链接
新闻摘要万圣节前夕，美国各地名人纷纷打造精致造型。海蒂·克鲁姆继续发布神秘预告，金·卡戴珊则装扮成13岁女儿诺斯，诺斯本人则扮成真人秀明星娜塔莉·纳恩。贾奈尔·梦奈、帕丽斯·希尔顿、柯柯·帕尔默等人也各有造型。
The newsAhead of Halloween, celebrities across the US unveiled elaborate costumes. Heidi Klum teased her look, while Kim Kardashian dressed as her 13-year-old daughter North, who went as reality star Natalie Nunn. Janelle Monáe, Paris Hilton and Keke Palmer also shared their costumes.
变装越冷门，越是在给圈层定价
The more niche the costume, the more it prices the circle.

万圣节变装不是玩，是一次公开的表达考试。考的科目叫“你懂不懂”。

The Cut 这篇清单里，明星们扮的都不是“女巫”“公主”这类公共符号，而是 Bad Girls Club 的 Natalie Nunn、Dandadan 的 Seiko Ayase、Family Guy 的 Hot Meg、White Chicks、Ellen 采访 Dakota Johnson 那段名场面。每一个名字都是一道门槛：认得出来的人自动进圈，认不出来的被礼貌地留在门外。这就是审美作为认知入口在干活——品味不区分好坏，只区分谁是自己人。

有意思的是 Kim Kardashian 扮的是自己 13 岁的女儿 North West，而 North 拒绝扮妈妈，去扮了 Natalie Nunn，还配了假下巴。母女互为素材，这在表达上是把家人也变成了可调用的符号。North 选 Nunn 而不是选母亲，未必是叛逆，更像是在这个年纪已经学会了：要被人看见，得挑一个“懂的人才懂”的坐标。

更值得看的是那套后台。文章开头就写了，明星们集结了最精英的化妆师、特效假体专家、造型师、美甲师。所谓“冷门”，是团队用钱和专业堆出来的。普通人模仿不了，不是因为不懂梗，是因为请不起那支队伍。冷门成了新的奢侈品：logo 越小越贵，这个逻辑换到万圣节就是梗越偏越高级。

Paris Hilton 特意声明孩子们“自己想扮这些角色”。这句话本身就很说明问题——在一个连孩子扮茶杯都要被公开审视的场合，“自愿”需要被提前辩护。至于 Keke Palmer 和 3 岁儿子扮 Michael 和 Janet Jackson，温情是真的，但孩子在这场表达博弈里没有投票权也是真的。

所以这份清单里，谁在付代价？没有血，没有伤，代价是隐形的：一整个晚上，人们用别人的形象确认自己的位置，而定义“什么算酷”的权力，始终在那些请得起团队、发得出 teaser 的人手里。

真正没被拍进照片的，是那些照着清单买了同款假发、第二天上班还要装作不在意的人。

Halloween dressing-up is not play. It is a public expression exam, and the subject is: do you get it.

In The Cut's roundup, almost nobody is a witch or a princess. They are Natalie Nunn from Bad Girls Club, Seiko Ayase from Dandadan, Hot Meg from Family Guy, White Chicks, the Ellen-Dakota Johnson interview. Every name is a threshold: those who recognize it enter the circle, those who don't are politely left outside. This is taste working as a cognitive entry point — taste doesn't separate good from bad, it separates us from them.

Kim Kardashian dressed as her own 13-year-old daughter, North West. North refused to dress as her mother and went as Natalie Nunn, prosthetic chin included. Mother and daughter become each other's material — expression turning family into a callable symbol. North picking Nunn over Mom may not be rebellion; it looks more like a kid who has already learned that to be seen, you pick a coordinate only insiders can read.

Look at the backstage. The piece opens by noting that celebrities assembled elite crews of make-up artists, prosthetics experts, stylists, nail technicians. The "niche" is built with money and professional labor. Ordinary people can't copy it — not because they don't know the reference, but because they can't afford the crew. Niche is the new luxury: the smaller the logo the more expensive, and transplanted to Halloween, the more obscure the meme the higher the tier.

Paris Hilton made a point of promising her kids wanted those characters. That sentence is telling: in a setting where even a child dressed as a teacup gets publicly audited, consent has to be pre-defended. Keke Palmer and her 3-year-old as Michael and Janet Jackson is genuinely warm, and it is also true that the child had no vote in this expression game.

So who pays? No blood, no wound. The cost is invisible: for one night people confirm their position through someone else's image, while the power to define what counts as cool stays with those who can hire the team and post the teaser.

What never makes the photos: the people who bought the same wig off the list and have to pretend not to care at work the next morning.
