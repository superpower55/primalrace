所谓“秘密配方”，不过是把人当人看The 'Secret Sauce' is Simply Treating Humans as Humans

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-07 § 链接
结构性暴力不是因为缺乏“配方”，而是因为故意切断了连接。
Structural violence isn't a lack of 'recipes'; it's a deliberate severance of connections.

前内阁大臣 Milburn 带着一群官员去都柏林寻找解决青年失业的“secret sauce”。这个词用得极其精准，它把一个结构性的政治失败，包装成了一场寻找某种神秘烹饪技巧的探索。在这种叙事里，英国青年的困境被简化成了“方法论”的问题，而不是一个关于资源剥夺和共谋的政治问题。

根据加尔通的暴力三角，英国 100 万 NEET 青年的现状就是典型的 structural violence。从 2010 年代开始的 austerity（紧缩政策）直接砍掉了 73% 的青年服务经费，这就是在物理层面上切断了年轻人与社会的连接。当公共青年俱乐部被关闭，贫困家庭孩子的成绩下降 12%，这不是“缺乏配方”，而是系统性地制造了 Potential 与 Actual 之间的巨大差额。这种差额就是暴力。

而爱尔兰的所谓“成功”，很大程度上是经济周期回弹和对技术教育（technical education）的实质性投入。最关键的 meta 层进步在于，他们开始承认“拿到工作比上大学更重要”。这在本质上是在挑战一种长期的 cultural violence：即用“学术成就”作为唯一的智能筛选标准，将技术工人定义为“不够聪明”的次等公民。这种叙事本质上是精英阶层为了维持自身的定价权而制造的认知入口。

最讽刺的细节是，英国人习惯用“hard to reach”（难以触及）来形容这些年轻人。这是一个完美的 weaponized concept，它通过语言操纵，将系统性的抛弃伪装成了受害者的个体缺陷。正如 Mind 的负责人所说，不是孩子难以触及，而是权力者把自己变成了不可触及的堡垒。这种叙事闭环让共谋者们可以心安理得地在 Paul Smith 的 Polo 衫下，一边烤着小蛋糕，一边讨论如何“拯救”被他们亲手抛弃的一代人。

Former cabinet minister Alan Milburn leads a delegation to Dublin searching for a 'secret sauce' to solve the youth jobs crisis. The term is tellingly precise; it packages a structural political failure as a quest for some mystical culinary technique. In this narrative, the plight of British youth is reduced to a problem of 'methodology' rather than a political issue of resource deprivation and complicity.

Applying Galtung's Violence Triangle, the status of over a million NEETs in the UK is textbook structural violence. The austerity drive of the 2010s, which slashed youth service spending by 73%, was a physical severance of the connection between young people and society. When youth clubs vanish and the grades of poor children drop by 12%, it's not a lack of 'recipes'—it's the intentional manufacturing of a gap between Potential and Actual. That gap is violence.

Ireland's 'success' is largely a mix of economic rebound and substantive investment in technical education. The critical meta-level progress is the admission that getting a job is more important than going to university. This challenges a long-standing cultural violence: using 'academic achievement' as the sole proxy for intelligence, thereby defining technical workers as 'not smart enough' second-class citizens. This narrative is an entry point created by the elite to maintain their pricing power over social status.

The most cynical detail is the UK's use of the phrase 'hard to reach' to describe these youths. This is a perfectly weaponized concept, using linguistic manipulation to disguise systemic abandonment as an individual flaw of the victim. As the head of Mind noted, the kids aren't hard to reach; the authorities have made themselves unreachable. This narrative loop allows the complicitors to bake fairy cakes in their Paul Smith polos while discussing how to 'save' a generation they personally discarded.
