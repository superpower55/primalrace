石油、地缘与被抹除的肉身Oil, Geopolitics, and the Erased Flesh

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-07 § 链接
战争叙事是最高级的元暴力：将肉身降格为战略筹码。
War narratives are the ultimate meta-violence: reducing human bodies to strategic chips.

这篇新闻是典型的 masculine-centric narrative。所有的关键词都是：military camp, oil exports, naval blockade, energy infrastructure。在这一套叙事里，所谓的“危机”是指石油出口被掐断，所谓的“损失”是指武器库被摧毁。至于那些死掉的士兵和受伤的平民，他们只是被用来量化战争烈度的数字。这就是元暴力（meta violence）的运作方式：通过定义什么是“重要”的，直接将人的存在性（existence）抹除。

从加尔通的暴力三角来看，这里的 direct violence（导弹袭击、伤亡）只是冰山一角。真正的暴力在于 structural 层：也门被当作美国、以色列与伊朗博弈的棋盘，其主权和生存权在结构上被剥夺。而 cultural 层则通过“保护平民”或“响应攻击”这种武器化的表达（weaponized expression），将这种屠杀合法化。在这种叙事中，人们讨论的是 Hormuz 海峡的贸易额，而不是一个四岁孩子被炮击后的余生。

谁在共谋？沙特、伊朗、美国以及那些在新闻稿中提供“匿名消息”的官员。他们共谋维持一套关于“地缘政治”的解释权，将血淋淋的屠杀包装成一种必然的战略博弈。在这种博弈中，没有任何一方在关心人的 Potential，他们只在乎 Actual 的权力版图。当人类的身体被降格为消耗品，而石油管道被视为生命线时，这就是一场彻头彻尾的性别暴力——因为这种将客体化、工具化的逻辑，正是父权制最原初的蓝图。

This report is a textbook example of a masculine-centric narrative. The keywords are all military camps, oil exports, naval blockades, and energy infrastructure. In this framework, a "crisis" is defined by the interruption of oil flow, and "loss" is measured by the destruction of weapons depots. The dead soldiers and wounded civilians are merely statistics used to quantify the intensity of the conflict. This is how meta-violence operates: by defining what is "significant," it effectively erases human existence.

Applying the Violence Triangle, the direct violence of missile strikes is only the tip of the iceberg. The real violence is structural: Yemen is treated as a chessboard for the US, Israel, and Iran, with its sovereignty and right to exist structurally stripped away. The cultural layer then weaponizes expressions like "protecting civilians" or "responding to attacks" to legitimize the slaughter. While the narrative focuses on the trade volume of the Strait of Hormuz, it ignores the lifelong trauma of a four-year-old child hit by shelling.

Who are the complicit parties? Saudi Arabia, Iran, the US, and the "anonymous officials" providing intel. They conspire to maintain an interpretative monopoly over "geopolitics," packaging massacre as inevitable strategic gaming. In this game, no one cares about human Potential; they only care about the Actual map of power. When human bodies are downgraded to consumables while oil pipelines are treated as lifelines, it is fundamentally a gendered violence—for this logic of objectification and instrumentalization is the primal blueprint of patriarchy.
