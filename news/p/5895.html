数据丢了，人没丢The Data Is Gone, the Women Aren't

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-31 § 链接
连流产都要丢进统计黑洞，这就是结构暴力。
When even miscarriages fall into a statistical black hole, that is structural violence.

ICE把怀孕被拘留者的流产数弄丢了。不是漏报，是换了医疗账单供应商之后，从2025年10月3日到2026年6月，一整年的数据不存在。官方先对《卫报》说在月度报告里追踪，后来改口承认没有。这个顺序本身就是全部的答案：先给一个听起来在管事的说法，被追问到墙角，再退到文件里已经写好的事实。

结构暴力最省力的形态不是不给资源，是让资源消失得没有记录。2021年ICE自己的指令写得清楚：除非法律禁止释放或存在例外情况，应完全避免拘留怀孕、产后和哺乳期移民；即便拘留，也要密切监测并记录健康状况。指令在，执行不在，记录也不在。没有记录，就没有可被追责的伤害；没有数字，就没有可以被起诉的对象。加尔通的公式在这里是字面意义上的：Potential 减去 Actual，差额被删掉了，账面上就不存在暴力。

报道里说，被拘留的孕妇难以获得常规和紧急医疗，专家说流产前后照护的缺口会把可治疗的状况变成危及生命的急症。这些女人经历的事情，只以“18”这个数字存在了九个月，然后连数字都没有了。她们的身体是唯一还在记录的地方。

值得看的是谁在补这个洞。三名民主党众议员联名六十四人写信，给ICE代理局长定下11月6日的回复期限，问的是“领导层何时首次知晓追踪缺陷”。这是structural层的一次具体施压，不是表演。但它到目前为止仍然只是要求，不是结果：DHS没有确认收到信，发言人只回了一句“说怀孕被拘留者获得次等照护的说法是假的”。立法者能做的上限是索取数据，而数据恰恰是被弄丢的那样东西。

还有一层没人问：这些怀孕的女人为什么会在拘留中。ICE的指令说避免拘留，实际拘留人数却创了纪录。谁决定把一个孕妇关起来，谁在文件上签的字，谁从“她自愿”“程序合规”这类说法里脱身——报道没写，报道只写了数据没了。名字缺席的地方，正是解释权被垄断的地方。

《卫报》和记者委员会为这些数字打了信息公开官司才拿到一部分，说明这套系统不是不能记录，是不想被记录。等到11月6日，ICE可以交出一份漂亮的回复，也可以什么都不交。两种情况下，那些在拘留中失去妊娠的女人，身体里那笔账都不会被系统认领。

ICE lost count of miscarriages among detained pregnant immigrants. Not undercounted—lost. After switching medical billing providers in October, the agency has no data from 3 October 2025 through June 2026. It first told the Guardian it tracked miscarriages in monthly reports, then confirmed what its internal documents already said: it does not. That sequence is the whole answer. Offer a version that sounds like governance, get pushed to the wall, retreat to the fact already written in the file.

Structural violence is at its most efficient not when it withholds resources but when it makes them vanish without a record. ICE's own 2021 directive is explicit: avoid detaining pregnant, postpartum and nursing immigrants unless release is prohibited by law or exceptional circumstances exist; and if detained, monitor and document their health closely. The directive exists. The execution does not. The records do not. No records, no accountable harm; no numbers, no defendant. Galtung's formula turns literal here: Potential minus Actual, and the difference has been deleted, so on the books there is no violence.

Detained pregnant women told the Guardian they struggled to access routine and emergency care; experts said gaps during pregnancy and around miscarriage can turn treatable conditions into life-threatening emergencies. What these women went through existed as the number 18 for nine months, and then not even as a number. Their bodies are the only place still keeping the record.

Worth watching is who is plugging the hole. Three Democratic representatives and sixty-four cosigners sent a letter to ICE's acting director with a 6 November deadline, asking when leadership first knew about the tracking deficiencies. That is concrete structural pressure, not performance. But so far it is a request, not a result: DHS did not confirm receipt, and a spokesperson offered only that claims of subprime care are FALSE. The ceiling of what legislators can do is demand data—the very thing that was lost.

One layer nobody asks about: why these pregnant women are in detention at all. The directive says avoid it; the detention numbers hit a record. Who decided to lock up a pregnant woman, who signed the form, who walks away behind phrases like she consented or procedure was followed—the report doesn't say. It only says the data is gone. Where names are absent is exactly where the monopoly on explanation sits.

The Guardian and the Reporters Committee had to sue under FOIA to get even part of these numbers. The system can record. It chooses not to be recorded. By 6 November ICE can file a polished reply or file nothing. Either way, the account those women carry in their bodies will not be claimed by the system.
