内塔尼亚胡的“核弹”：一种典型的认知入口武器化Netanyahu's 'Nuclear' Trope: The Weaponisation of Cognitive Entry Points

国际 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-14 § 链接
将国家定义为“伊斯兰共和国”并非描述事实，而是通过制造虚假现实夺取解释权。
Defining a nation as an 'Islamic Republic' is not a description of fact, but a seizure of the right to interpret reality.

内塔尼亚胡把英国称为“第一个拥有核武器的伊斯兰共和国”，这在逻辑上是个 scam，但在政治表达上是一次精准的武器化操作。他并不在乎英国是否真的变成了伊斯兰共和国——事实上，巴基斯坦早就在这个定义上捷足先登——他在意的是通过一个极端的、带有种族主义色彩的 trope，直接接管关于英国“身份”的认知入口。

这是一个典型的 cultural violence 案例。通过将英国这个传统盟友“他者化”为敌对的宗教政体，他试图在认知层面上抹除英国现任政府在加沙问题上的道德立场。当对方被定义为“伊斯兰共和国”时，对方的所有批评就不再是基于人权的公正表达，而变成了某种“宗教渗透”的产物。这让内塔尼亚胡能够迅速地将外交失败转化为一种“文明对抗”的叙事，从而在极右翼共谋者的圈子里获得存在性胜利。

最令人作呕的是这种叙事的联动性：从特朗普到 JD Vance，再到内塔尼亚胡，他们共享同一套武器库。他们通过攻击伦敦市长 Sadiq Khan 等特定个体，将生物墙（种族与宗教）转化为政治攻击的靶点。这种操作的本质是夺取“什么是事实”的制造权：只要我定义你为伊斯兰国家，我就获得了在结构层面对你实施制裁或在文化层面对你进行羞辱的合法性。

外交失败被包装成文化战争，这正是元暴力的运作方式。内塔尼亚胡在用一种自毁式的表达来掩盖他在结构层面的孤立。他试图把全世界都拉入这场关于“身份”的存在性战争，因为在事实层面的博弈中，他已经输了。

Benjamin Netanyahu calling the UK the 'first Islamic republic to get a nuclear weapon' is a logical scam, but a precise weaponisation of expression. He doesn't care whether the UK has actually become an Islamic republic—Pakistan already holds that title—what he cares about is seizing the cognitive entry point of Britain's 'identity' through a far-right, racialised trope.

This is a textbook case of cultural violence. By 'othering' a traditional ally as a hostile religious regime, he attempts to erase the moral standing of the current UK government regarding Gaza. Once the opponent is defined as an 'Islamic republic,' their criticisms are no longer Just Expressions based on human rights, but products of 'religious infiltration.' This allows Netanyahu to pivot a diplomatic failure into a narrative of 'civilizational clash,' securing an existential win among his far-right complicity network.

What's most repulsive is the synergy of this narrative: from Trump to JD Vance and now Netanyahu, they share the same arsenal. By targeting individuals like Sadiq Khan, they weaponise the biological wall (race and religion) into political targets. The essence of this is the seizure of the power to manufacture 'what is fact': if I can define you as an Islamist state, I gain the legitimacy to sanction you structurally or shame you culturally.

Diplomatic failure rebranded as a culture war is exactly how meta-violence operates. Netanyahu uses self-destructive expression to mask his structural isolation. He is trying to drag the world into an existential war of identities because, in the game of actual facts, he has already lost.
