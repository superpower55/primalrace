救救那个所谓的“和平计划”？别逗了Saving the 'Peace Plan'? Don't Make Me Laugh

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-17 § 链接
和平计划不是为了终结暴力，而是为了重新定价暴力。
Peace plans don't end violence; they merely reprice it.

看到“救救（saved）”这个词我就想笑。在加沙的废墟上，所谓的 peace plan 从来不是为了把 Actual 状态推向 Potential 的公正，而是一场关于“谁能定义和平”的 weaponized 叙事博弈。特朗普的女婿 Jared Kushner 穿梭在哈马斯和内塔尼亚胡之间，这根本不是外交，而是一次典型的商业谈判——把人命作为筹码，在认知入口处制造一种“美国在努力”的假象。

内塔尼亚胡的“罕见决裂”不过是博弈中的一种表达策略。在 masculine-centric narrative 的逻辑里，和平只有在能最大化自身权力时才有价值。当 15 点计划不能满足其对领土和绝对控制的贪婪时，这份计划就成了可以随意丢弃的草稿。这种所谓的“和平”，本质上是 structural violence 的升级版：它试图通过一套被西方定义的规则，将被殖民者的生存权重新打包成一种可以交易的资产。

最讽刺的是，这种叙事总是把加沙的女性和儿童作为背景板，甚至作为开战或停战的道德装饰。在这个元暴力的闭环里，谁在共谋？是那些把“外交努力”等同于“人道主义”的媒体，也是那些试图在权力席位上分一杯羹的调停者。他们共谋掩盖了一个事实：只要解释权依然被垄断在这些所谓的“强人”手中，任何计划都只是在给暴力换一套更文明的皮。

The word 'saved' is a joke. In the ruins of Gaza, a 'peace plan' is never about pushing the Actual state toward Potential justice; it is a weaponized narrative game over 'who defines peace.' Jared Kushner's diplomatic mission is not diplomacy—it is a business negotiation, using human lives as chips to manufacture a facade of 'American effort' at the cognitive entry point.

Netanyahu's 'rare break' is simply an expression strategy in a game of power. In the masculine-centric narrative, peace only has value when it maximizes one's own dominance. When a 15-point plan fails to satisfy the greed for territory and absolute control, the plan becomes a disposable draft. This 'peace' is essentially an upgrade of structural violence: it attempts to repackage the survival rights of the colonized as a tradable asset under Western-defined rules.

The irony is that women and children in Gaza are always treated as backdrops or moral ornaments for starting or stopping wars. In this loop of meta-violence, who are the complicit? The media that equates 'diplomatic effort' with 'humanitarianism,' and the mediators seeking a seat at the table of power. They conspire to hide one fact: as long as the power of interpretation remains monopolized by these so-called 'strongmen,' any plan is just giving violence a more 'civilized' skin.
