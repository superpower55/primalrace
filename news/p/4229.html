杰森·斯坦森的肌肉循环与被物化的“功能性女性”Jason Statham's Muscle Loop and the Functional Objectification of Women

性别 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-20 § 链接
动作片的公式化不仅是剧本的懒惰，更是元暴力的肌肉记忆。
Action movie formulas aren't just lazy writing; they are the muscle memory of meta-violence.

看这部《Mutiny》的影评，最让我感到厌倦的不是电影本身的 a-list 还是 d-list，而是那种被视为“安全、抚慰”的 Jason Statham vehicle。这种“车辆”本质上是一套高度标准化的 masculine-centric narrative：一个身体强健、情绪缺失的男性主体，在封闭空间里通过物理暴力解决问题。这种叙事之所以被认为“稳健”，是因为它精准地复刻了元暴力的逻辑——世界被简化为强弱之分，而强者的定义就是拥有施加直接暴力的能力。

最令人作呕的 scam 是片中对女性角色的处理。Annabelle Wallis 扮演的女性被定义为“单身母亲”且是“泼辣的三等航海员”，还自带一个“废物前夫”和“世界上最可爱的女儿”。这简直是教科书级别的 weaponized 叙事：给女性角色贴上“母亲”和“受害者”的标签，是为了在逻辑上将其置于需要被拯救或被辅助的地位，即便她被设定为“泼辣”，其最终功能依然是作为男主角肌肉力量的陪衬和情感点缀。这种设定让女性在电影中成为了一个 functional object，而不是一个拥有主体性的 person。

至于电影里夹带的“拯救泰国难民”情节，不过是给这套暴力机器贴的一层人道主义金箔，用以在 cultural layer 上合法化男主角的杀戮。这种“为了正义而暴力”的公式，让观众在消费肌肉快感的同时，还能获得一种廉价的道德优越感。这根本不是电影，这是一次关于“强权即正义”的潜意识规训。当评论者说这种公式“不矫揉造作”时，他其实是在共谋一种对陈旧父权结构的温情维护。

Reading the review of 'Mutiny', what exhausts me isn't whether the film is A-list or D-list, but the 'safe, soothing' quality of the 'Jason Statham vehicle'. This 'vehicle' is essentially a highly standardized masculine-centric narrative: a physically dominant, emotionally void male subject solving problems through direct violence. This narrative feels 'stable' because it precisely replicates the logic of meta-violence—the world is simplified into a binary of strong vs. weak, where strength is defined solely by the capacity to inflict violence.

The most offensive scam here is the treatment of the female lead. Annabelle Wallis is cast as a 'single mother' and a 'feisty third mate' with a 'deadbeat ex' and a 'wonderful daughter'. This is a textbook example of weaponized narrative: by labeling the woman as a 'mother' and a 'victim', the script structurally positions her as someone to be rescued or assisted. Even her 'feistiness' serves only as a foil to the protagonist's muscle. She is a functional object, not a person with subjectivity.

The subplot of 'saving Thai refugees' is merely a layer of humanitarian gold leaf applied to this violence machine to legitimize the protagonist's killing at the cultural layer. This formula of 'violence for the sake of goodness' allows the audience to consume the thrill of muscle while gaining a cheap sense of moral superiority. This isn't cinema; it's a subconscious conditioning of 'might makes right'. When the critic calls this formula 'unpretentious', he is complicit in maintaining a nostalgic affection for the old patriarchal structure.
