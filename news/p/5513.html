生乳热潮：一场关于“怀疑”的认知收割 scamThe Raw Milk Craze: A Cognitive Harvest Scam Built on Skepticism

哲学 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-11 § 链接
当对制度的怀疑被武器化，生物学常识就成了收割韭菜的认知入口。
When skepticism of institutions is weaponized, biological common sense becomes a cognitive entry point for harvesting the naive.

生乳（raw milk）在美利坚的复兴，本质上不是一次健康回归，而是一场典型的表达武器化。它精准地捕捉了后疫情时代一个极其危险的认知入口：对政府监管的 skepticism。当 RFK Jr. 这种级别的政治人物将 FDA 的公共卫生标准定义为“战争”和“压制”时，他实际上是在通过重新定义“事实”，为一种高风险的消费行为提供政治合法性。

这套叙事极其阴险。它把“喝生乳”包装成一种反抗权威、夺回身体主权的存在性战争。对于消费者来说，购买生乳不再仅仅是饮食选择，而是一次身份投票——我是一个“觉醒的”、不被体制洗脑的独立思考者。这种身份认同感掩盖了最基础的生物墙事实：人类的免疫系统，尤其是幼儿的免疫系统，在面对大肠杆菌等病原体时没有任何所谓的“自然抵抗力”。

最讽刺的是，生乳生产者利用一个所谓的“herd share”法律漏洞（loophole）来逃避监管，这本身就是一种结构性共谋。他们一方面宣称产品安全，另一方面却拒绝公开第三方实验室的名字，仅靠 Google 上的五星好评来构建信任。这种用“情感叙事”替代“科学验证”的手段，是典型的文化暴力——它通过制造一种“纯真、家庭、自然”的审美伪装，让消费者在潜意识中将“未经处理”等同于“天然健康”，从而心甘情愿地接受 Potential（潜在健康）与 Actual（实际致病）之间的巨大差额。

RFK Jr. 在就职后迅速停止倡导生乳，转而推广全脂奶，这简直是教科书级别的政治博弈。在他需要通过“反建制”人设积累筹码时，生乳是绝佳的武器；而当他真正进入权力中心，需要维护公共卫生基本盘时，这把武器就被随手丢弃了。对于那些因为他的煽动而让孩子陷入 E. coli 感染的父母来说，这种“最优解表达”的切换，代价是真实的血泪。

The resurgence of raw milk in the US is not a return to health, but a textbook case of the weaponisation of expression. It precisely captures a dangerous cognitive entry point in the post-pandemic era: a systemic skepticism toward government regulation. When a political figure like RFK Jr. defines public health standards as a "war" or "suppression," he is effectively redefining "facts" to provide political legitimacy for a high-risk consumption behavior.

This narrative is insidious. It packages raw milk consumption as an existential war—a way to resist authority and reclaim bodily autonomy. For the consumer, buying raw milk is no longer just a dietary choice; it is an identity vote. They are signaling that they are "awakened," independent thinkers who refuse to be brainwashed by the system. This identity politics masks the biological wall: the human immune system, especially in infants, has no so-called "natural resistance" against pathogens like E. coli.

Most ironic is how raw milk producers use the "herd share" loophole to evade regulation—a clear form of structural complicity. They claim safety while refusing to name their third-party labs, relying instead on five-star Google reviews to build trust. This substitution of "emotional narrative" for "scientific verification" is a form of cultural violence. By manufacturing an aesthetic of "purity, family, and nature," they trick consumers into equating "unprocessed" with "natural health," making them willingly accept the gap between Potential and Actual health.

RFK Jr.'s swift pivot from raw milk to whole milk upon taking office is a masterclass in political gaming. While he needed an "anti-establishment" persona to gain leverage, raw milk was a perfect weapon. Once inside the center of power, where maintaining the public health baseline became necessary, the weapon was discarded. For parents whose children suffered E. coli infections due to his rhetoric, this shift in "optimal expression" comes at the cost of real blood and tears.
