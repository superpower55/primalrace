用命换Like：一场关于“男性气概”的低劣博弈Trading Life for Likes: A Low-End Game of Masculinity

性别 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-25 § 链接
当现实世界失去肯定，暴力表达便成了唯一的认知入口。
When reality offers no affirmation, violent expression becomes the only entry point for existence.

这根本不是什么“追求刺激”的亚文化，而是一场典型的存在性战争。在结构性失权的背景下，这群年轻男性在现实世界中找不到能够证明自身价值的路径，于是他们转向一个极其廉价且危险的认知入口：通过在物理世界制造直接暴力（Dangerous Driving），来换取虚拟世界中的身份确证（Clout and Glory）。

这就是典型的“假.最优解表达”。他们扮演着一个被父权制定义为“勇敢”、“掌控”且“叛逆”的男性角色，试图通过这种表演来获得同类群体的肯定。但代价是主体性的彻底死亡——他们的价值不再取决于他们是谁，而取决于算法给出的 View 数量。在这种博弈中，身体被降格为一种消耗品，而死亡则成了这场低劣表演的最终注脚。

更讽刺的是，这种行为背后存在着一套完整的共谋机制。从拍摄、剪辑的“clippers”到在评论区狂欢的观众，他们共同构建了一个将暴力美学化的文化层。这种文化暴力让直接的谋杀（如撞死警察和无辜者）在叙事中被稀释为“娱乐目的”，从而使结构性的绝望被掩盖在速度的快感之下。

政府要求平台删帖，这只是在 structural 层做简单的补丁。只要元暴力——即那种将“破坏力”等同于“男性力量”的中心叙事——依然垄断着年轻男性的认知，他们就会在下一个 #hashtag 之后，继续用生命去填补那个名为“肯定”的无底洞。

This isn't some 'thrill-seeking' subculture; it is a textbook existential war. Against a backdrop of structural disempowerment, these young men find no path to validate their worth in the physical world. Consequently, they pivot to a cheap and lethal cognitive entry point: manufacturing direct violence in the offline world to trade for identity confirmation (clout and glory) in the virtual one.

This is a classic 'False Optimal Expression.' They perform a role defined by the masculine-centric narrative—one of 'bravery,' 'control,' and 'rebellion'—to win the approval of their peers. The cost is the death of subjectivity: their value is no longer about who they are, but about the number of views dictated by an algorithm. In this game, the body is downgraded to a consumable, and death becomes the final footnote to a pathetic performance.

More ironically, a complete system of complicity supports this. From the 'clippers' who edit the footage to the audience cheering in the comments, they collectively build a cultural layer that aestheticizes violence. This cultural violence dilutes direct murder—such as killing police officers and innocents—into 'entertainment,' masking structural hopelessness beneath the rush of speed.

Government demands for content removal are merely structural patches. As long as the meta-violence—the narrative that equates 'destructive power' with 'masculine strength'—continues to monopolize the cognition of young men, they will simply find the next #hashtag to fill the bottomless pit of 'affirmation' with their lives.
