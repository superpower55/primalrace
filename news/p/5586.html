诊断书不是救赎，而是结构性暴力的确认函Diagnosis is Not Salvation, But a Confirmation Letter of Structural Violence

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-16 § 链接
标签(Label)不等于救济，它往往只是为了管理而制造的认知入口。
A label is not relief; it is often just a cognitive entry point created for management.

这几封读者来信揭示了一个残酷的真相：在医疗系统中，诊断(Diagnosis)被异化成了一种纯粹的分类学游戏。对于 Rebecca 来说，51 岁才拿到的自闭症诊断书仅仅是“解释了生活”，却没能“帮助生活”。这就是典型的 structural violence——系统通过给予一个 label 让你觉得问题被“看见”了，从而在心理上完成一次廉价的抚慰，但实际的资源分配（Actual）依然停留在原地。Potential 与 Actual 之间的差额，就是这群神经多样性人群被系统长期剥削的暴力成本。

更深层的共谋在于，医疗体系试图将所有问题“医疗化”。John Bryant 问得极其精准：如果问题源于社会变迁，为什么它被定义为医疗问题？当社会将不符合“标准生产力”的个体定义为“障碍(Disorder)”时，它实际上是在通过定义“正常”来实施元暴力。这种叙事将个体与环境的冲突，转化为个体大脑的病理缺陷。这样一来，政府和权力阶层就无需面对超加工食品(UPFs)的产业垄断，无需面对残酷的就业市场，只需要给受害者贴上一个标签，然后告诉他们：“你病了，请在等待名单里耐心等待。”

这种“诊断-等待-无果”的闭环是一场极其高效的管理 scam。它让弱势者在追求“身份确证”的过程中耗尽生命，而共谋者们——无论是官僚化的 NHS 还是定义标准的医学界——则在维持一套名为“专业”的解释权垄断。当一个 70 岁女性在绝经后才被意识到可能患有 ADHD 时，她失去的不是一个诊断，而是整个主体性被结构性抹除的几十年。

These letters reveal a brutal truth: in the healthcare system, diagnosis has been alienated into a pure game of taxonomy. For Rebecca, receiving an autism diagnosis at 51 merely "explained her life" without "helping her live it." This is classic structural violence—the system provides a label to make the individual feel "seen," offering a cheap psychological consolation while actual resource allocation remains stagnant. The gap between Potential and Actual is the violence cost of long-term exploitation of neurodivergent people.

There is a deeper complicity in the attempt to "medicalize" everything. John Bryant's question is piercing: if the problems are tied to social changes, why is it primarily a healthcare issue? When society defines those who don't fit "standard productivity" as having a "disorder," it is exercising meta-violence by defining "normalcy." This narrative transforms the conflict between the individual and the environment into a pathological defect of the brain. Consequently, governments and power structures avoid addressing the monopoly of ultra-processed foods (UPFs) or the cruelty of the job market; they simply slap a label on the victim and tell them: "You are ill, please wait patiently in the queue."

This "diagnosis-waiting-void" loop is a highly efficient management scam. It exhausts the weak in their pursuit of identity confirmation, while the complicitors—from the bureaucratic NHS to the medical establishment—maintain a monopoly over the right of interpretation under the guise of "professionalism." When a 70-year-old woman is only recognized as potentially having ADHD post-menopause, what she lost was not just a diagnosis, but decades of her subjectivity being structurally erased.
