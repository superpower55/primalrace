检方替他数了五千个The Prosecutor Counted His Five Thousand

性别 直接层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-30 § 链接
他管女人叫消遣，检方只能证明她们不是自愿的消遣。
He called women amusements. The court can only prove they weren't willing ones.

检方在结案陈词里替受害者说了一句话：她们没什么可图的。不图名，没上那部纪录片，没申请赔偿，唯一可能的好处是closure。这句话本身就是在回答辩方最省力的那条路——六个互不相识的女人，被一部2023年的电视纪录片施了"magic force"，集体把旧事重编了一遍。

值得盯住的是：这套辩护逻辑之所以成立，靠的不是证据，是元暴力的默认设置。一个女人隔了十几年才开口，她的记忆天然可疑；一部纪录片播出之后她开口，她的动机天然可疑。男被告的"demeanour changed"需要被证明，女原告的"vivid recollections"需要被辩护。谁的解释权被默认握在手里，一目了然。

检方描述的作案模式是同一套动作：先好，然后"a pivot"，眼睛变了，从迷人切到可怕和刻薄，说停不停，继续拉、推、扯、顶。这不是六个孤立的意外，是一个人对另一类人反复执行的同一道程序。而事后他还去要电话和邮箱——检方管这叫gaslighting用得"good effect"。要联系方式不是留念，是把受害者重新标记成"自愿的参与者"，让她自己都不确定刚才发生了什么。这是最经济的一种武器化：不用封口，让她怀疑自己的嘴。

然后是那个数字。五千个。检方说最年轻的证人"只是又一个数字，加进他正在记的五千个 notch 里"。一个人能把性接触记成计分板，前提是他从来没把对方当人，只当"a means to an end"、"the amusements"。他自己2017年就这么说了，在采访里，公开的。这不是被挖出来的黑料，是他一直摆在台面上的自我描述，只是当时没人愿意把它读成供词。

辩方的结案陈词周一才来。在那之前，能确定的只有一件事：这六个人赢不回那十年，最多赢回一句"她们不是自愿的"。而五千这个数字，无论判决如何，都还在那儿。

The prosecutor's closing speech answered the defence's cheapest route with one line: these women had nothing to gain. No fame, no part in the documentary, no compensation claim — perhaps some closure. That sentence exists because the alternative is easier to sell: six women who don't know each other, hypnotised by a 2023 Dispatches film into collectively rewriting their own pasts.

What's worth watching is why that defence works at all. Not evidence — default settings. A woman who speaks years later has a suspect memory; a woman who speaks after a documentary has a suspect motive. The male defendant's "demeanour" must be proven; the complainants' "vivid recollections" must be defended. Whose account arrives pre-authorized is not a mystery.

The pattern described is one procedure run six times: nice, then "a pivot," the eyes change, charming snaps to frightening and nasty, no means nothing, keep pulling, pushing, tugging, thrusting. Not six accidents — one man executing the same program on a category of people. And afterwards he asked for their numbers and emails. The prosecution calls it gaslighting used to "good effect." Asking for contact details isn't sentiment; it re-labels the victim as a willing participant, so she stops being sure what just happened. The cheapest weaponisation there is: don't silence her, make her doubt her own mouth.

Then the number. Five thousand. The youngest witness was "just another number to add to his 5,000 notches." A man can keep a scoreboard only if the other side was never people — "a means to an end," "the amusements." He said so himself, in a 2017 interview, in public. Not a leak. A self-description left on the table, which at the time nobody chose to read as a confession.

The defence speaks Monday. Until then, one thing is settled: these six cannot win back the decade. At best they win the sentence "they did not consent." And the five thousand stays on the board either way.
