十六个人换来一次换政府Sixteen Dead, One Government Out

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 Al Jazeera ↗ 2026-11-01 § 链接
新闻摘要2024年11月1日，塞尔维亚诺维萨德火车站顶棚坍塌，共致16人死亡。事故引发学生主导的抗议并演变为政治运动，学生名单反对派运动于上周上台执政。
The newsOn November 1, 2024, a canopy collapse at Novi Sad railway station in Serbia killed 16 people. The accident sparked student-led protests that grew into a political campaign, bringing the Student List opposition movement to power last week.
问责被换成了换届，账还没算。
Accountability got swapped for an election. The bill is still open.

十六个人死了，两年，换了一个政府。这不是正义，这是把正义的账目挪到了另一个科目下。

看时间线就清楚了。2024年11月1日，诺维萨德火车站混凝土雨棚塌下来，现场十四人死，三人重伤送医，其中两人后来死亡，总数十六人。四天后数千人上街，几十人被捕，市政厅被砸。观察者注意到一件事：对示威者的司法反应快得像闪电，对事故本身的调查却卡在原地。这就是结构暴力的标准姿势——机器能动，只是选择不动。

11月21日，三周压力之后，检方宣布逮捕十一人，包括前建设部长韦西奇。12月30日起诉十三人。然后高等法院把韦西奇和塞尔维亚铁路基础设施代理总经理塔纳斯科维奇的羁押改成软禁。管辖权争议、案卷转移，程序透明度跟着一起塌。到2025年1月15日，贝尔格莱德有组织犯罪检察署另开一条线，查重建期间的资金诈骗、腐败和洗钱，涉及韦西奇、前贸易部长莫米罗维奇和另外十二人。两条线，两年，没有一个名字被定罪。

真正在动的是另一条线。2025年3月11日，诺维萨德逮捕十三名活动人士和学生，罪名是破坏宪法秩序和暴力行为。学生被起诉，部长被软禁。这个对比不需要任何理论加工，它自己会说话。

所以当2026年9月27日武契奇辞去总统职务、在最后一天赦免所有被起诉的学生和活动人士时，那不是良心，那是选举前的清场。赦免令结束的是“破坏宪法秩序”的诉讼，让流亡的人能回来投票——它结束的不是那十六个人的案子。10月25日学生名单党赢下选举，投票率是三十多年来最高。武契奇承认败选。

这场胜利是真的：一个由学生、教授、法律专家组成的非正式运动，在两年里把自己变成了执政力量，这是元层的一次换手——解释权从旧机器手里被拿走了。但换手不等于清算。报道说得很清楚，诺维萨德事故的问责在选举结果公布后仍是核心议题。“仍是”这两个字，就是全部的差额。

加尔通的公式在这里是干净的：潜力是一个能追责的政府，实际是一个换了届但还没追责的政府，差额就是暴力。塌下来的雨棚是直接层，腐败和采购不透明是结构层，把抗议者告上法庭、把部长放回家是文化层对前两层的合法化。三层联动，两年，动的是最上面那一层。

还有一件事报道没写：那十六个人是谁。名字、年龄、职业，一个都没有。他们是这场政治更替的起点，却不是它的主体。学生赢了席位，政府换了主人，而死者的家属拿到的是什么——报道没有说，这就是答案的一部分。

雨棚塌之前，195份文件锁在八个文件夹里；雨棚塌之后，它们被拿出来一部分，被称作让步。现在政府换了，那些文件夹还在。

Sixteen people died. Two years later, a government changed. That is not justice; that is justice moved to a different line item.

The timeline says it plainly. On November 1, 2024, a concrete canopy collapsed at the renovated Novi Sad railway station: fourteen dead at the scene, three taken to hospital in critical condition, two of whom later died, in November 2024 and March 2025, bringing the total to sixteen. Four days later, thousands gathered in Novi Sad; dozens were arrested and City Hall was damaged. Observers noted the swift judicial response to the protesters while the investigation into the accident itself stalled. That is structural violence in its standard posture: the machine works, it simply chooses not to.

On November 21, after three weeks of pressure, prosecutors announced eleven arrests, including former Construction Minister Goran Vesic. On December 30, thirteen people were indicted. Then the higher courts overturned the detention of Vesic and Jelena Tanaskovic, acting general director of Serbia Railways Infrastructure, placing them under house arrest. A jurisdiction dispute and the transfer of case files further clouded the proceedings. By January 15, 2025, Belgrade's organized crime prosecutor opened a separate investigation into financial fraud, corruption and money laundering during the reconstruction, involving Vesic, former Trade Minister Tomislav Momirovic and twelve others. Two tracks, two years, not one name convicted.

The other track moved. On March 11, 2025, thirteen activists and students were arrested in Novi Sad, charged with undermining the constitutional order and violent behaviour. Students indicted, ministers under house arrest. That contrast needs no theoretical scaffolding. It speaks on its own.

So when Vucic resigned as president on September 27, 2026, and on his last day pardoned all prosecuted students and activists, that was not conscience. That was pre-election cleanup. The pardon ended the proceedings for 'undermining the constitutional order' and let exiles return in time to vote. It did not end the case of the sixteen. On October 25, the Student List won, with turnout the highest in more than thirty years. Vucic conceded.

The win is real: an informal student-led movement turned itself into a governing force in two years. That is a handover at the meta level, the power to explain taken from the old machine. But a handover is not an accounting. The report is explicit that accountability for the Novi Sad tragedy remains a central issue after the results. 'Remains' is the whole gap.

Galtung's formula is clean here: the potential is a government that holds someone responsible; the actual is a government that changed hands and has not. The gap is the violence. The canopy is the direct layer, corruption and opaque procurement the structural layer, prosecuting protesters while releasing ministers the cultural layer that legitimizes both. Three layers, two years, and only the top one moved.

One thing the report does not give: who the sixteen were. No names, no ages, no occupations. They are the starting point of this political turnover, not its subject. Students won seats, the government changed owners, and what the victims' families received, the report does not say. That silence is part of the answer.

Before the canopy fell, 195 documents sat in eight binders. After it fell, some were produced and called a concession. The government has changed now. The binders are still there.
