把党派当公司经营，是典型的元暴力共谋Running a Party Like a Firm: A Textbook Case of Meta-Violence Complicity

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-20 § 链接
将政治表达定义为“董事会讨论”，是夺取解释权的权力游戏。
Defining political expression as 'boardroom discussion' is a power play to seize the right of interpretation.

Richard Tice 的逻辑极其粗暴：内部的分歧应该留在“闭门会议”里，对外则要统一“销售信息”。请注意这个用词——boardroom, brainstorming, sell a message。他不是在经营一个政治政党，而是在经营一家公司。在公司结构里，员工对 CEO 的批评被定义为“不专业”或“违规”，而在政治结构里，这叫扼杀监督。

这是一种典型的 meta violence。当 Farage 成为这个组织的绝对权力中心，任何对他个人行为（比如缺席计票）的质疑都被转化为对“组织纪律”的挑衅。Tice 试图通过将政治空间“企业化”，把公共讨论的权利降格为员工的内部抱怨，从而在结构层（structural layer）合法地清除异己。这种“闭门讨论”的叙事，本质上是权力者为了维护自身形象而制造的认知入口，目的是让外界只看到被修剪过的、统一的“产品”。

而 Suella Braverman 对“性别研究”等学位的攻击，则是这场权力游戏的绝佳注脚。她通过定义什么是“Mickey Mouse degrees”来筛选敌我，将那些挑战传统男本位叙事的学术领域直接贴上“无用”的标签。这不仅是教育政策的博弈，更是文化层（cultural layer）的武器化：通过剥夺这些研究的经济资源，从根源上削弱那些试图拆穿元暴力的认知工具。

这场戏里最讽刺的是，他们一边宣称自己是给选民提供“更多选择”的竞争者，一边在内部通过 suspension 抹除所有不符合其口味的表达。这种“对外竞争，对内服从”的共谋，不过是父权制管理模式在政治领域的复刻。

Richard Tice's logic is brutally simplistic: internal disagreements stay 'behind closed doors,' and externally, the party 'sells a message.' Notice the lexicon—boardroom, brainstorming, sell. He isn't leading a political party; he's managing a corporation. In a corporate structure, criticizing the CEO is 'unprofessional'; in a political structure, it's the death of oversight.

This is a classic manifestation of meta violence. With Farage as the absolute center of power, any questioning of his personal conduct is transformed into a provocation against 'organizational discipline.' By 'corporatizing' the political space, Tice degrades the right to public discourse into mere employee grievances, thereby legitimizing the purging of dissent at the structural layer.

Suella Braverman's attack on 'gender studies' and other degrees serves as the perfect coda to this power play. By labeling these as 'Mickey Mouse degrees,' she filters allies from enemies and marks academic fields that challenge masculine-centric narratives as 'useless.' This isn't just an education policy; it's the weaponization of the cultural layer: stripping economic resources from the very tools used to dismantle meta violence.

The irony is peak: they claim to offer voters 'more choice' while using suspensions to erase any expression that doesn't fit their brand. This complicity—'competition' outward, 'obedience' inward—is nothing more than the reproduction of patriarchal management in the political arena.
