AI 的物理外壳与权力的防御工事The Physical Shell of AI and the Fortifications of Power

科技 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-14 § 链接
当 AI 成为不可见的元暴力，数据中心就是它唯一的肉身。
When AI becomes an invisible meta-violence, the datacenter is its only flesh.

大多数人习惯于把 AI 当作云端的一串代码，但这次苏格兰警察的警告撕开了这个 scam：AI 不是什么虚无的‘智能’，它是极其沉重的物理存在。它是数以百计的柴油发电机，是吞噬掉一个国家峰值电量 1.5 倍的电力怪兽，是直接建在临终关怀院和医院旁边的工业噪音。这就是典型的 structural violence——将环境成本和健康风险强行摊派给最没有议价能力的底层群体，而将算力红利留在硅谷和伦敦的董事会里。

有趣的是，警察在这里扮演的角色不是‘治安维护者’，而是大科技公司的‘安保顾问’。他们提醒开发商建立‘强有力的安全措施’以防止抗议者‘未经计划的入侵’。这是一种极具讽刺的共谋：国家暴力机器在预判民众对生存权的防御行为，并将其定义为‘破坏’，从而提前为资本的物理扩张修筑防御工事。在这一逻辑中，一个患有白血病孩子的未来，在‘AI 国家战略’面前被定价为零。

正如 UCL 教授所言，数据中心是 AI 唯一的 physical manifestation。当算法在文化层（cultural layer）通过‘效率’和‘进步’的叙事掩盖其对人类思考能力的剥夺时，人们终于找到了可以触摸、可以围堵、可以攻击的实体。这不再是关于‘AI 是否会取代人类’的哲学讨论，而是一场关于土地、电力和呼吸权的生存博弈。当解释权被垄断在‘安全标准’和‘规划法规’这些武器化的词汇背后时，抗议者试图通过物理破坏来夺回一点点存在感。

Most people treat AI as a string of code in the cloud, but the warning from Police Scotland tears through this scam: AI is not some ethereal 'intelligence'; it is a heavy, brutal physical presence. It is hundreds of diesel generators, a power-hungry monster consuming 1.5 times a nation's peak demand, and industrial noise dumped directly next to hospices and hospitals. This is structural violence in its purest form—externalizing environmental and health costs onto the most powerless while concentrating the compute dividends in boardrooms in Silicon Valley and London.

It is profoundly ironic that the police here are acting not as 'peacekeepers' but as 'security consultants' for Big Tech. By warning developers to implement 'robust security' to prevent 'unplanned incursions,' the state apparatus is pre-emptively defining the defense of survival rights as 'disruption.' This is a clear act of complicity, building fortifications for capital's physical expansion. In this calculus, the future of a child with leukemia is priced at zero compared to the 'National AI Strategy.'

As the UCL professor noted, datacenters are the only physical manifestation of AI. While algorithms mask the erosion of human cognition through cultural narratives of 'efficiency' and 'progress,' people have finally found something they can touch, surround, and attack. This is no longer a philosophical debate about whether AI will replace humans; it is an existential war over land, electricity, and the right to breathe. When the power of interpretation is monopolized by weaponized terms like 'safety standards' and 'planning regulations,' physical resistance becomes the only way for the marginalized to reclaim their existence.
