面罩下的‘地缘’共谋与元暴力的投射Masked Complicity: Meta-Violence Projected in Far-Right Riots

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-07 § 链接
极右翼的‘守护’叙事，本质上是男性中心主义对客体化群体的再次殖民。
Far-right 'protection' narratives are merely the recolonization of objectified groups by masculine-centric narratives.

穿着全黑、戴着巴拉克拉法帽（balaclavas），这种视觉表达在极右翼的剧本里被包装成‘战士’的英勇，但在存在性战争的博弈中，这其实是一种典型的 weaponized expression。他们通过遮蔽个体面孔，将自己转化为一个纯粹的、具有攻击性的‘男性集体’符号，以此在 Dover 和 Portsmouth 制造直接暴力（direct violence）的威胁。

Danny Tommo 所谓的‘ground zero’叙事，是一个极其精准的认知入口。他并不在讨论难民的生存权，而是在定义一个‘领地’。在元暴力的逻辑下，这种行为是对‘原初种族’殖民逻辑的现代复刻：将寻求庇护者（asylum seekers）客体化为‘入侵者’，从而赋予自己实施暴力的正当性。这不仅是种族主义，更是 masculine-centric narrative 的权力投射——通过定义谁是‘外来者’，来确认自己作为‘保护者’的虚假主体性。

最讽刺的共谋在于，这类暴徒往往打着‘保护本土女性/家庭’的旗号行动，但其行为逻辑却与他们声称要对抗的‘野蛮’完全同构。他们并不在乎任何具体的个体，他们在乎的是通过这种‘thuggish’的表演，在社会结构层（structural layer）地巩固一种基于恐惧的统治秩序。当暴力被冠以‘爱国’或‘守护’之名时，cultural violence 就完成了对 direct violence 的洗白。

这场闹剧揭示了一个事实：极右翼的本质不是政治立场，而是一场关于‘谁有权定义事实’的解释权战争。他们试图通过物理上的阻碍，来制造一个‘我们vs他们’的伪事实，从而在认知入口处截断任何关于人权的讨论。

All-black attire and balaclavas are packaged as 'warrior' bravery in the far-right script, but in the existential war, this is a classic weaponized expression. By erasing individual faces, they transform themselves into a pure, aggressive 'masculine collective' symbol, manufacturing the threat of direct violence in Dover and Portsmouth.

Danny Tommo’s ‘ground zero’ narrative is a precision-engineered cognitive entry point. He isn't discussing the survival rights of asylum seekers; he is defining a ‘territory.’ Under the logic of meta-violence, this is a modern reproduction of the colonial logic applied to the Primal Race: objectifying asylum seekers as ‘invaders’ to justify the exercise of violence. This is not just racism; it is a projection of masculine-centric narrative—confirming a fake subjectivity as a ‘protector’ by defining who the ‘outsider’ is.

The most cynical complicity lies in how these thugs often act under the guise of ‘protecting local women and families,’ while their behavioral logic is identical to the ‘barbarism’ they claim to fight. They care nothing for actual individuals; they care about consolidating a rule of fear at the structural layer through ‘thuggish’ performance.

This farce reveals that the far-right is not a political stance, but a war over the power of interpretation. By creating physical obstructions, they manufacture a pseudo-fact of ‘us vs them,’ effectively blocking any discourse on human rights at the cognitive entry point.
