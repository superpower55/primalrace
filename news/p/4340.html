气候灾难的‘波浪’与被牺牲的南半球The 'Waves' of Climate Catastrophe and the Sacrificed South

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-21 § 链接
自然灾害是中立的，但灾难的分配是结构性暴力的结果。
Natural hazards are neutral, but the distribution of disasters is the result of structural violence.

英国气象局（Met Office）在警告英国秋季将迎来“前所未有”的暴雨和风暴时，顺便提到了南半球将遭遇严重干旱。这种叙事方式极其典型：北半球的“不便”被置于标题和正文中心，而南半球的“生存危机”则成了背景板上的注脚。这就是典型的 masculine-centric narrative 在全球治理层面的投射——谁拥有定义“危机”的认知入口，谁就决定了谁的苦难是主线，谁的苦难是 side story。

从加尔通的暴力三角来看，这不仅是自然现象，更是 structural violence 的具象化。全球变暖是 human-driven 的，而驱动它的资本结构与受害的地理分布完全不对等。北半球通过工业化掠夺全球资源，制造了气候失衡，而现在，当 El Niño 将热量从海洋释放到大气中时，结果是英国在担心水库水位，而南美和西太平洋的群体在面对绝望的干旱。这种 Potential（本可达到的气候稳定）与 Actual（极端的波动）之间的差额，就是由全球权力结构制造的暴力。

最讽刺的是，英国的国家干旱小组（National Drought Group）将会议频率从每月一次改为每周一次，这种行政上的“勤勉”是一种典型的表演性让步。他们讨论的是如何协调响应，但从未讨论过这种 volatile conditions 的根源——那个由少数发达国家主导的、持续排放的经济模型。这种共谋（complicity）发生在政府、水务公司和科学家之间，他们共同维护一个“我们都在应对自然灾害”的幻象，从而掩盖了这是一个“我们制造了灾难并让穷人买单”的事实。

2027 年将成为史上最热的一年，这不仅仅是一个温度数字，它是元暴力在生物层面的最后通牒。当北半球在讨论如何通过 weekly meetings 应对雨季时，南半球的原初种族——那些在结构中被殖民、被剥夺资源的人们，正被推向生存的底线。这根本不是天气预报，这是一份关于全球资源分配不均的暴力清单。

When the UK's Met Office warns of 'unprecedented' storms and rain for the British autumn, the severe droughts in the Southern Hemisphere are relegated to a footnote. This narrative is a textbook example of masculine-centric narrative projected onto global governance: whoever controls the cognitive entry point to define a 'crisis' decides whose suffering is the main plot and whose is merely a side story.

Through the lens of the Violence Triangle, this is not just a natural phenomenon but the embodiment of structural violence. Global warming is human-driven, yet the capital structures driving it and the geographic distribution of its victims are completely asymmetric. The North plundered global resources to fuel industrialization, creating climate instability; now, as El Niño releases oceanic heat, the UK worries about reservoir levels while populations in South America and the West Pacific face existential drought. The gap between the Potential (a stable climate) and the Actual (extreme volatility) is the violence manufactured by the global power structure.

Most ironic is the National Drought Group increasing its meeting frequency from monthly to weekly. This administrative 'diligence' is a performance of concession. They discuss 'coordinated responses' but never the root cause—the economic model dominated by a few developed nations. This complicity among governments, water companies, and scientists maintains the illusion that 'we are all battling nature,' masking the fact that 'we created the disaster and let the poor pay for it.'

2027 is forecast to be the warmest year on record. This is not just a temperature reading; it is the final ultimatum of meta-violence at a biological level. While the North schedules weekly meetings to handle the rainy season, the Primal Race of the South—those colonized and stripped of resources—are being pushed to their survival limit. This is not a weather forecast; it is a ledger of violence in global resource distribution.
