末日叙事：权力者的终极免责声明The Apocalypse Narrative: The Ultimate Disclaimer of Power

国际 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-02 § 链接
用“神意”垄断解释权，是权力者将结构暴力合法化的终极武器。
Monopolizing interpretation through 'Divine Will' is the ultimate weapon for legitimizing structural violence.

JD Vance 的这套说辞是典型的 weaponized expression。当一个掌握国家权力的人开始讨论“末日”（end times）和“神的工作”时，他实际上是在构建一个超越世俗法律和伦理的认知入口。如果你的行为导致了世界的崩溃，但那是“神的旨意”，那么所有的 structural violence 就变成了神圣的必然，而他自己则从一个政治决策者变成了神谕的执行者。

这种叙事最阴险的地方在于它提供了一种绝对的免责机制：如果结果是好的，那是他的功劳；如果结果是灾难性的，那是末日将至。在这种逻辑下，任何对他权力的质疑都被简化为对“神”的亵渎。这是一种极高明的 meta violence，通过垄断精神世界的解释权，将现实中的政治博弈偷换成神学预言。

至于他口中 AI 婚姻咨询是“撒旦”的论调，不过是权力者在感受到技术可能解构传统男本位家庭结构时的应激反应。他恐惧的不是 AI 缺乏灵魂，而是 AI 可能提供一套不依赖于父权共谋的、公正的表达方式。而他为 Trump 将自己比作耶稣的 meme 辩护，则揭露了这套宗教叙事在他们内部的真实定位：宗教不是信仰，而是用来装点权力、筛选共谋者的装饰品。他们并不敬畏神，他们只是在利用神的皮囊来扩充自己的存在性。

JD Vance’s rhetoric is a textbook case of weaponized expression. When a man wielding state power begins discussing the 'end times' and 'God’s work,' he is constructing a cognitive entry point that transcends secular law and ethics. If his actions lead to societal collapse, but it is framed as 'God's will,' then all structural violence is transformed into divine necessity, and he shifts from a political actor to a mere executor of prophecy.

The most insidious part of this narrative is the absolute disclaimer it provides: if the outcome is positive, it is his achievement; if it is catastrophic, it is simply the apocalypse. Under this logic, any critique of his power is reduced to blasphemy. This is a sophisticated form of meta violence, substituting political accountability with theological prophecy by monopolizing the interpretation of the spiritual realm.

His claim that AI marriage counseling is 'satanic' is merely a stress response from a power-holder sensing that technology might deconstruct the masculine-centric family structure. He doesn't fear a lack of soul in AI; he fears that AI might offer a form of Just Expressions that doesn't rely on patriarchal complicity. His defense of Trump’s AI meme as Jesus further reveals the true function of this religious narrative: religion is not faith, but an ornament for power and a tool for filtering co-conspirators. They do not revere God; they simply wear His skin to expand their own existence.
