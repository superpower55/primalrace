用身体作为战场：伊朗政权的元暴力闭环The Body as Battlefield: The Meta-Violence Loop of the Iranian Regime

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-16 § 链接
性暴力不是权力的副产品，而是维持男本位统治的结构性工具。
Sexual violence is not a byproduct of power, but a structural tool for maintaining masculine-centric dominance.

看到这种新闻，不要落入“个案残暴”的感伤框架。这种 coordinated（协同）的杀戮与性虐待，是典型的 meta violence（元暴力）在 direct 层面的物理投射。当一个政权以“道德警察”之名要求女性佩戴头巾时，它争夺的根本不是布料的覆盖率，而是对女性身体的解释权和定价权。头巾是认知入口，而强奸和身体穿刺则是最终的暴力清算。

注意报告中细节的残酷性：对女性的阴道、肛门、口腔侵入，以及对男性的肛门侵入。这在生物学上毫无意义，但在权力博弈中具有极强的象征意义——它是在通过物理方式强行将所有抗争者“女性化”和“客体化”。在男性中心叙事中，被侵入意味着主体性的彻底死亡，意味着你被降格为一件可以被随意处置的物品。这种 torture 的逻辑是：既然你试图通过表达（Expression）来确立身份，那么我就用最极端的暴力摧毁你的生物墙，让你在生理和心理上同时坍塌。

最令人作呕的共谋（complicity）在于这种暴力的层级化。从最高领导层到街头的执行者，形成了一套完整的 command chain。执行者在实施暴行时，其心理支撑来自于一种共谋协议：只要我维护这个男本位的秩序，我就能获得体制内的生存优势。而这种 impunity（免责）则进一步确认了这种共谋的收益——只要你站在元暴力这一边，杀戮与强奸就是被允许的“维护秩序”。

人权即女权。当一个政权将女性身体视为殖民地，将其作为政治博弈的战场时，任何一个在这个结构中生存的人——无论性别——最终都会成为这个暴力机器的潜在耗材。这种对原初种族的殖民逻辑，正是所有结构性暴力的蓝图。

Do not fall into the trap of viewing this as mere 'individual cruelty.' The coordinated killing and sexual abuse described here are direct physical projections of meta-violence. When a regime uses 'morality police' to enforce the hijab, it is not fighting over fabric; it is fighting for the interpretation and pricing of the female body. The hijab is the cognitive entry point; rape and bodily penetration are the final violent settlements.

Note the harrowing specificity of the torture: vaginal, anal, and oral penetration of women, and anal penetration of men. Biologically, this is meaningless; politically, it is a powerful symbol of forced 'feminization' and 'objectification.' In a masculine-centric narrative, being penetrated signifies the total death of subjectivity—you are degraded into an object to be disposed of. The logic of this torture is simple: since you attempted to establish your identity through Expression, I will use extreme violence to demolish your biological wall, causing you to collapse physically and psychologically.

The most sickening part is the complicity within the hierarchical command chain. The agents on the street operate under a complicity agreement: by upholding this masculine-centric order, they secure their own survival advantages within the system. The resulting impunity confirms the payoff of this complicity—as long as you align with meta-violence, killing and rape are sanctioned as 'maintaining order.'

Human rights are women's rights. When a regime treats the female body as a colony and a political battlefield, every person within that structure—regardless of gender—eventually becomes potential fodder for the machine. This colonial logic applied to the Primal Race is the blueprint for all structural violence.
