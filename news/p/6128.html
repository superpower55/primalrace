公主还在，剧没了The Princess Stays, the Series Doesn't

文娱 结构层 · 文化层 A.V. Club ↗ 2026-10-31 § 链接
先砍掉剧集，再宣布特辑，这叫止损不叫兑现
Cancel the show, announce a special: that's damage control, not a promise kept

迪士尼砍掉《公主与青蛙》剧集一年后，宣布特辑仍在制作。这不是好消息，是把砍掉的东西换了个名字再卖一遍。

2020年高调宣布的Tiana剧集，在Disney+的排期里漂了几年，去年被确认取消。主演Anika Noni Rose公开表达了不满——报道写的是"very public unhappiness"。现在华特迪士尼动画工作室CCO Jared Bush确认，有一个Tiana特辑在"active production"，原班配音、新歌、完整故事。听起来像兑现承诺。但Bush自己说"I'm hesitant to give you the specifics"，连上线时间都没有。一个连时间表都不敢给的项目，"soon"这个词是用来安抚人的，不是用来交付的。

这里动的不是direct层，是structural层和cultural层的联动。Disney+整体收缩原创内容，报道说得很清楚：除了Marvel、Star Wars、Percy Jackson，其他原创基本被放弃。长片动画不再为流媒体做——这是结构性的撤退。Tiana剧集是这场撤退里的一个数字。然后cultural层补上：把"取消"重新包装成"特辑即将到来"，让取消这件事在公众记忆里变得模糊。观众记住的是"还有新内容"，不是"剧集被砍了"。

更值得看的是Tiana这个角色在迪士尼体系里的位置。报道提到Splash Mountain被重新主题化，用Tiana替换掉Song of the South的品牌——这是在处理一段种族主义遗产，用一位Black American公主做清洁剂。Tiana同时留在公主品牌线里，靠Anika Noni Rose的表演维持存在感。角色被保留，作品被削减。品牌需要她，流媒体不需要她的剧集。

Colman Domingo七月提到想写一个真人版衍生项目，报道说《公主与青蛙》是迪士尼真人翻拍机器还没碰过的少数动画片之一。这句话读起来像夸奖，其实是排队通知。真人翻拍是迪士尼最成熟的变现路径，Tiana迟早会被放进去。到那时候，今天这个特辑就更像是一次过渡期的占位——让角色别凉，等真人版来收钱。

谁付了代价？Joyce Sherri，原定剧集的showrunner，报道说她参与了这个特辑的工作。她的剧集没了，她的名字被挪到一个没定档的特辑上。Anika Noni Rose公开不满过，现在被请回来唱新歌。两个人都被留在了体系里，但都不是按她们原本要的方式。

特辑如果真出来，会是好内容——原班配音、新歌、完整故事，这些条件摆在那里。但它缩小的差额，比剧集原本能缩小的小得多。一部以Tiana为中心的剧集，是一个Black American女性角色持续占据叙事中心的位置；一个特辑，是一次性的露出。前者是结构，后者是事件。迪士尼选了后者，然后让前者在新闻里看起来像是还在。

Bush说"It is pretty spectacular"。一个连上线时间都不肯给的人，说它spectacular。这句话不是说给观众听的，是说给还在等的人听的。

A year after Disney scrapped its Princess And The Frog series, it confirms a special is still coming. This isn't good news. It's the cancelled thing renamed and resold.

The Tiana series was announced with fanfare in 2020, drifted around Disney+'s schedule for years, and was confirmed dead last year. Anika Noni Rose made her "very public unhappiness" known. Now Walt Disney Animation Studios CCO Jared Bush confirms a Tiana special is in "active production"—original cast, new songs, a full story. It sounds like a promise kept. But Bush himself says "I'm hesitant to give you the specifics," and there's no release date. A project that won't name a date is using "soon" to soothe, not to deliver.

What's moving here isn't the direct layer. It's structural and cultural, working together. Disney+ is retreating from original content across the board—the report is explicit that anything that isn't Marvel, Star Wars, or Percy Jackson is basically abandoned. Long-form animation for streaming is over. The Tiana series was a number in that retreat. Then the cultural layer patches it: cancellation gets repackaged as a special on the way, and the cancellation blurs in public memory. What people remember is "there's still new content," not "the series was killed."

More telling is Tiana's position inside the Disney machine. Splash Mountain was re-themed with Tiana and her bayou buddies, replacing the Song Of The South branding with Black American characters "a bit less likely to get it into public tussles with the NAACP." Tiana stays in the princess line, kept visible by Anika Noni Rose's performance. The character is retained; the work is cut. The brand needs her. Streaming doesn't need her series.

Colman Domingo floated writing some live-action spin-off in July, and the report notes that Princess And The Frog is one of the few animated Disney films the live-action remake machine hasn't come for yet. That reads like a compliment. It's a queue ticket. Live-action remakes are Disney's most reliable monetization path, and Tiana will eventually be fed into it. Which makes today's special look like a placeholder—keep the character warm until the remake arrives to collect.

Who paid? Joyce Sherri, the would-be showrunner of the cancelled series, is attached to this special, per the report. Her series is gone; her name is moved onto an undated special. Anika Noni Rose was publicly unhappy, and now she's back to sing new songs. Both are kept inside the system, but not in the form they were originally promised.

If the special arrives, it will probably be good—original cast, new songs, a full story. But the gap it closes is much smaller than the one the series would have closed. A series centred on Tiana is a Black American woman holding narrative centre on an ongoing basis. A special is a one-time appearance. The first is structure. The second is an event. Disney chose the event, then let the news make the first look like it's still alive.

Bush says "It is pretty spectacular." A man who won't give a release date calling it spectacular isn't talking to the audience. He's talking to the people still waiting.
