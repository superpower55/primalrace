平台不是中介，而是暴力的结构性掩体Platforms Aren't Intermediaries; They Are Structural Shields for Violence

科技 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-01 § 链接
用“非合同方”定义自身，是平台将结构暴力合法化的最高级 scam。
Defining oneself as a 'non-contractual party' is the ultimate scam to legitimize structural violence.

Booking.com 和 Airbnb 玩的是一套极其卑劣的叙事武器化游戏：在获客端，它们通过算法、评价体系和品牌背书，把自己包装成一个掌控质量的“信任中心”；但在履约端，它们迅速切换身份，通过 Terms & Conditions 将自己定义为单纯的“ conduit（管道）”，宣称自己不是合同方。

这就是典型的 structural violence。平台通过定义“灰区”，在认知入口上制造了巨大的不对称。用户购买的是平台的“exceptional”信用背书，但面对风险时，平台却要求用户去面对一个可能并不理性的个体房东。这种“权力在，责任不在”的结构，本质上是平台在掠夺用户的安全感并将其转化为利润，而将所有的风险成本转嫁给个体。

更令人作呕的是那些所谓的“补偿方案”——用 travel credits（旅行积分）代替现金退款。这不仅是经济上的克扣，更是一种文化层面的规训：它试图通过一种“恩赐”的姿态，强迫受害者在未来的交易中继续与这个施暴系统绑定。这不是在解决问题，而是在通过一种低成本的表演性让步，掩盖其对解释权的绝对垄断。

在这种共谋结构中，平台与不合格的房东达成了某种默契：房东提供劣质产品，平台提供流量并掩盖风险，双方共同通过一个名为“自由市场”的叙事，将消费者的不安定义为“合同纠纷”。

Booking.com and Airbnb are playing a despicable game of weaponized expression. On the acquisition end, they position themselves as 'trust centers' through algorithms and brand endorsement. But on the fulfillment end, they swiftly pivot, using their T&Cs to define themselves as a mere 'conduit'—claiming they are not a contractual party.

This is classic structural violence. By defining a 'grey area,' the platform creates a massive asymmetry at the cognitive entry point. Users buy the platform's 'exceptional' credibility, but when risks materialize, the platform forces them to deal with an irrational individual host. This structure—where power exists but responsibility is denied—is essentially the platform harvesting user security for profit while offloading all risk onto the individual.

Even more repulsive are the so-called 'compensation' schemes: offering travel credits instead of cash refunds. This is not just financial skimming; it is cultural regulation. It attempts to use a gesture of 'grace' to force the victim into a continued bond with the oppressive system. This isn't a resolution; it's a performative concession designed to mask the absolute monopoly over the power of interpretation.

In this complicity, platforms and substandard hosts reach a tacit agreement: the host provides a defective product, the platform provides traffic and masks the risk, and both use the narrative of the 'free market' to redefine consumer anxiety as a mere 'contractual dispute.'
