外交驱逐战：用外交官做筹码的权力游戏Diplomatic Expulsions: The Power Game of Human Chess

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-05 § 链接
外交上的 '对等报复' 是结构暴力在文化层面的表演性共谋。
Reciprocal diplomatic retaliation is a performative complicity of structural violence at the cultural layer.

以色列关闭东耶路撒冷领事馆，英国威胁以 1:1 的比例驱逐外交官。这场典型的 tit-for-tat 博弈在新闻叙事中被包装成“外交关系最低点”，但本质上是一场关于认知入口的权力战争。外交官在此时不再是沟通的工具，而是被量化的筹码。当权力者在讨论“一个换一个”时，他们实际上是在共谋一套名为“外交礼仪”的游戏规则，而这套规则恰恰掩盖了最底层的 structural violence。

真正的暴力不在于几个外交官是否被驱逐，而在于那个被关闭的领事馆所覆盖的区域——东耶路撒冷、西岸和加沙。那里正在发生的是 Miliband 所说的 ethnic cleansing（种族清洗）。在这种元暴力（meta violence）面前，外交官的进出只是文化层面的表演。以色列通过关闭领事馆来惩罚英国的贸易禁令，试图夺取对“非法定居点”这一事实的解释权，将国际法定义的非法行为重新定义为“道德扭曲”。

最讽刺的是，即便是在这种对抗中，叙事依然被男性中心主义的权力逻辑垄断。从 Netanyahu 到 Miliband，这场博弈是典型的“强人政治”表演。Powell 担心禁令会给 Netanyahu 在选举中提供便利，这揭示了国际政治的残酷真相：受害者的生存权（Potential）与实际状态（Actual）之间的差额，在政治家眼中仅仅是选举筹码的一部分。这种将人类苦难 weaponized 的艺术，才是这场外交闹剧背后最深层的暴力。

Israel closes the East Jerusalem consulate; the UK threatens a 1:1 expulsion of diplomats. This classic tit-for-tat game is framed as the 'lowest point in relations,' but it is actually a war over cognitive entry points. In this context, diplomats are no longer tools for communication, but quantified chips. When powers discuss 'one for one,' they are complicit in a game called 'diplomatic protocol,' which serves as a screen to mask the underlying structural violence.

The real violence is not whether a few diplomats are expelled, but the reality of the territory that consulate covered—East Jerusalem, the West Bank, and Gaza. What is happening there is, as Miliband stated, ethnic cleansing. In the face of such meta violence, the movement of diplomats is mere performative theater at the cultural layer. By closing the consulate to punish trade bans, Israel attempts to seize the interpretation of 'illegal settlements,' rebranding a violation of international law as 'morally distorted.'

The irony is that even in this conflict, the narrative is monopolized by a masculine-centric power logic. From Netanyahu to Miliband, this is a performance of 'strongman politics.' Powell's concern that sanctions might benefit Netanyahu's election campaign reveals the brutal truth of international politics: the gap between the victims' potential and their actual state is treated merely as a variable in an election strategy. This art of weaponizing human suffering is the deepest violence behind this diplomatic farce.
