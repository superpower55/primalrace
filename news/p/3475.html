DPD的“合规”外壳与底层劳工的消失DPD's 'Compliance' Shell and the Erasure of Precarious Labor

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-09 § 链接
结构性剥削的最高境界是利用法律主体性的差额进行共谋。
The pinnacle of structural exploitation is conspiring through the gap in legal subjectivity.

DPD 这场关于病假工资和养老金的闹剧，是典型的 structural violence。在加尔通的暴力三角里，这不只是钱的问题，而是通过一个精心设计的“主体性差额”将暴力合法化：DPD 支付 charge rate 给中介，中介雇佣临时工。当权利缺失时，DPD 迅速启动防御机制——“法律上，中介才是雇主”。

这就是典型的共谋 (complicity)。DPD 和招聘机构之间达成了一场心照不宣的博弈：DPD 压低采购成本，中介通过剥削工人来维持利润。双方在合同里写满“要求合作伙伴遵守法律”，但这只是文化层面的 PR 掩体。真正的最优解表达是：只要我不直接成为法律意义上的“雇主”，我就能心安理得地享受廉价劳动力带来的红利，而无需为他们的病痛和养老买单。

最残酷的细节在于那些被刻意剔除的 spreadsheet。为了避开 12 周后的养老金缴纳，工人被在临界点前“移走”或更换。这不仅是经济掠夺，更是对人作为主体性的抹除——工人被简化为一种可随时替换的、没有生命周期（不需要养老和病假）的生物插件。在这种 masculine-centric 的商业叙事中，效率是唯一的真理，而底层的生物需求被定义为“成本干扰项”。

不要被 DPD 所谓的“道德责任”所欺骗。在这种结构里，所谓的“合规”就是一种武器化的表达，用来掩盖 Potential（法定权利）与 Actual（实际所得）之间巨大的暴力差额。这场博弈的赢家是那些掌控定价权的资本，而输家则是那些在法律主体性缝隙中被榨干的临时工。

The DPD scandal over sick pay and pensions is a textbook case of structural violence. In the Violence Triangle, this isn't just about missing money; it's about legitimizing violence through a carefully engineered gap in subjectivity: DPD pays a charge rate to agencies, and agencies hire temps. When entitlements vanish, DPD triggers its defense mechanism: "Legally, the agency is the employer."

This is pure complicity. DPD and recruitment firms have reached a tacit agreement: DPD suppresses procurement costs, and agencies maintain margins by exploiting workers. They fill their contracts with clauses demanding "compliance with the law," but this is merely a cultural PR shield. The fake optimal expression here is simple: as long as I am not the "employer" in the eyes of the law, I can enjoy the dividends of cheap labor without paying for their illness or old age.

The most brutal detail lies in the spreadsheets. To avoid pension contributions due after 12 weeks, workers are "moved on" or replaced just before the threshold. This is more than economic plunder; it is the erasure of the worker's subjectivity. Humans are reduced to biological plugins—transient assets with no life cycle, no need for rest or retirement.

Do not be fooled by DPD's "ethical responsibilities." In this structure, "compliance" is a weaponized expression used to mask the massive gap between Potential (legal rights) and Actual (realized pay). The winners of this game are those who control the pricing power, while the losers are the temporary workers being drained in the cracks of legal subjectivity.
