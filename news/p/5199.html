被“挫败”的恐怖袭击与结构性暴力的循环Foiled Attacks and the Cycle of Structural Violence

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-22 § 链接
直接暴力的消失不等于安全的到来，它只是结构性暴力的暂时休止符。
The absence of direct violence is not safety; it is merely a pause in structural violence.

警方宣布挫败了一场针对曼彻斯特犹太社区的“迫在眉睫”的袭击，这种叙事在新闻业中被定义为 good_news。但如果套用加尔通的暴力三角，我们会发现，direct violence（直接暴力）虽然被拦截，但 structural violence（结构暴力）和 cultural violence（文化暴力）依然在全速运转。

这场袭击的背景是去年的血案和当前的宗教节日，这意味着袭击者选择的时机本身就是一种 weaponized expression（武器化表达）——利用特定群体的恐惧节点来最大化其政治影响力。而警方在逮捕后对动机的模糊处理，以及对“伊斯兰极端主义”或“国家链接”的含糊推测，恰恰揭示了认知入口的博弈：在反恐叙事中，定义“谁是恐怖分子”本身就是一种权力行使，它决定了接下来的暴力将被正当化为“防御”还是被定义为“压迫”。

最令人不安的共谋（complicity）在于，在这种“挫败袭击-产生恐慌-加强监控-再次被袭击”的循环中，安全感被异化成了一种可以通过增加监控和限制自由来购买的商品。犹太社区在 CST 的提醒下保持“冷静与警觉”，这种状态并非真正的安全，而是一种在 meta violence（元暴力）笼罩下的生存策略：他们必须承认自己是被标记的、脆弱的客体，才能在体制的保护下获得暂时的生存权。

这次拦截缩小了 Potential 与 Actual 之间的差额，但这只是在 direct 层面的修补。只要文化层面对特定族群的非人化（dehumanization）依然存在，只要结构层面的资源分配和地缘仇恨不被拆除，下一次“迫在眉睫”的袭击只是时间问题。

Police claim to have foiled an 'imminent' attack on Manchester's Jewish community, a narrative framed as good_news. However, applying the Violence Triangle reveals that while direct violence was intercepted, structural and cultural violence remain in full operation.

The timing of the plot—aligned with religious holidays and the anniversary of a previous massacre—is a form of weaponized expression, designed to maximize psychological impact by exploiting a community's trauma. The police's ambiguity regarding motives, oscillating between 'Islamist extremism' and 'state links,' highlights the battle for the cognitive entry point: in counter-terrorism, the power to define 'who is a terrorist' is the power to legitimize subsequent violence as 'defense' or 'oppression.'

The most chilling complicity lies in the loop of 'intercept-panic-surveil-attack.' Safety is fetishized as a commodity purchasable through increased surveillance and restricted liberties. The Jewish community, urged to remain 'calm and alert,' is not experiencing true safety, but a survival strategy under meta violence: they must accept their status as marked, vulnerable objects to secure temporary protection from the system.

This intervention reduced the gap between Potential and Actual in the direct layer, but it is a mere patch. As long as the cultural layer continues the dehumanization of specific groups and the structural layer maintains geopolitical hatred, the next 'imminent' attack is inevitable.
