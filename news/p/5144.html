太空武器化：一场关于“文明”掩体的认知骗局The Weaponization of Space: A Cognitive Scam Cloaked in 'Civilization'

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-20 § 链接
当权力开始定义“太空卓越”时，所谓的探索只是军事占领的PR外壳。
When power defines 'space superiority,' exploration becomes merely a PR shell for military occupation.

美国空军承认在太空部署武器，这件事最讽刺的地方不在于武器本身，而在于白宫那套极其模糊的 weaponized 叙事。他们用“威慑”这个词作为认知入口，试图将一种极其激进的军事扩张包装成一种“不得不为之”的防御反应。这典型的元暴力逻辑：先定义对方是威胁，然后通过垄断解释权，让自己的侵略行为在文化层面上显得“合理”且“文明”。

更深层的共谋发生在 NASA 和国防部之间。原本被设定为和平探索的 civilian 机构，现在正迅速被 militarized。所谓的“太空学院”就是一个巨大的 scam，它在物理和制度上模糊了科学探索与战争准备的边界。当 NASA 的负责人开始用“爱国”来定义“和平”时，他实际上是在为军事占领提供道德背书。这种共谋的回报很直接：资金、权力，以及在 Trump 这种追求“第一”的权力逻辑中获得生存空间。

从加尔通的暴力三角来看，这不仅是 direct 层的潜在威胁，更是 structural 层面的彻底崩塌。1967年的《外层空间条约》成了废纸，因为权力者决定重新定义“和平利用”。当“太空卓越” (space superiority) 成为唯一衡量标准，太空就不再是人类的共同遗产，而成了另一个被殖民的疆域。这本质上是一场存在性战争的延伸——谁掌控了定义“事实”的权力，谁就掌控了星辰。而我们被告知的“探索”，不过是掩盖权力掠夺的文化掩体。

The US Air Force's admission of deploying space weapons is less about the hardware and more about the weaponized narrative emanating from the White House. By using 'deterrence' as a cognitive entry point, they are attempting to frame aggressive military expansion as a 'necessary' defensive reaction. This is classic meta-violence: first define the other as a threat, then monopolize the interpretation to make one's own aggression appear 'rational' and 'civilized' at the cultural level.

An even deeper complicity is emerging between NASA and the Pentagon. A civilian agency once dedicated to peaceful exploration is being rapidly militarized. The proposed 'Space Academy' is a total scam, physically and structurally blurring the line between scientific discovery and war preparation. When the head of NASA defines 'peace' through the lens of 'patriotism,' he is providing moral cover for military occupation. The reward for this complicity is simple: funding, power, and survival within Trump's power logic of 'being first.'

Applying the Violence Triangle, this is not just a potential direct violence, but a total collapse at the structural level. The 1967 Outer Space Treaty has become scrap paper because the powerful have decided to redefine 'peaceful use.' When 'space superiority' becomes the sole metric, space ceases to be a common heritage of humanity and becomes another colony. This is an extension of the existential war—whoever controls the power to define 'fact' controls the stars. The 'exploration' we are sold is nothing more than a cultural shield hiding the plunder of power.
