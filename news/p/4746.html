米莱的‘变革之风’：一场关于主权叙事的廉价套利Milei's 'Winds of Change': Cheap Arbitrage of Sovereignty Narratives

国际 文化层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-04 § 链接
主权在政治博弈中常被武器化为掩盖内部结构性崩塌的烟雾弹。
Sovereignty is often weaponized as a smokescreen to mask internal structural collapse.

米莱把马尔维纳斯群岛（福克兰群岛）搬上台面，绝不是因为他突然发现了某种‘正义’，而是一次典型的表达武器化。一个自称撒切尔仰慕者的自由意志主义者，在支持率跌至谷底、失业率攀升时，突然通过‘主权’这个认知入口来收割民族主义情绪。这在存在性战争中是极低成本的套利：用一个宏大的外部敌手，来抵消内部对‘电锯’紧缩政策的愤怒。

这场博弈的本质是两个‘强人’叙事的共谋。特朗普将领土主权作为惩罚北约盟友的筹码，而米莱则试图将自己包装成美国在南美‘可靠的伙伴’。在这种 masculine-centric narrative 中，领土被简化为可以交易的资产，而岛民 99.8% 的自决权被轻描淡写地视为‘不重要’。当米莱引用世界杯冠军横幅上的口号时，他实际上是在利用文化层面的共情，将复杂的国际法问题简化为一场体育比赛般的胜负欲。

最讽刺的是，米莱在电视演说中让所有部长站在一起营造气势，结果却没拿到任何实质性的美国背书。这种‘表演性表达’试图制造一种‘可能性’的假象，但实际上，他只是在用一种元暴力的逻辑——即强者决定事实——来试图取代另一种强者的逻辑。当他宣布增加国防预算建设海军基地时，他增加的不是国家安全，而是结构性暴力在边境的物理投影。

Milei bringing the Malvinas claim back to the forefront is not a discovery of 'justice,' but a textbook weaponisation of expression. A self-styled libertarian and admirer of Margaret Thatcher suddenly pivots to nationalist fervor exactly when his popularity hits rock bottom and unemployment spikes. In the existential war of politics, this is low-cost arbitrage: using a grand external enemy to offset internal rage against his 'chainsaw' austerity.

This is a complicity between two 'strongman' narratives. Trump treats territorial sovereignty as a bargaining chip to punish NATO allies, while Milei attempts to brand himself as a 'reliable partner' for the US in South America. In this masculine-centric narrative, territory is reduced to a tradable asset, and the 99.8% self-determination of the islanders is dismissed as irrelevant. By echoing World Cup slogans, Milei leverages cultural empathy to flatten a complex legal dispute into a simple win-loss game.

The irony lies in the performance. Summoning all his ministers for a televised address only to deliver a few decrees without actual US endorsement proves this was a performative expression designed to manufacture a fake 'possibility.' He is simply attempting to replace one logic of meta-violence—where the strong define reality—with another. When he increases the defense budget for a naval base, he isn't enhancing security; he is merely projecting structural violence onto the physical border.
