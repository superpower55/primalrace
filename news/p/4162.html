所谓的“母爱”：一场关于存在性吞噬的scamThe "Motherly Love" Scam: An Existential Devouring

性别 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-19 § 链接
当爱被武器化为控制，它就是最隐蔽的直接暴力。
When love is weaponized as control, it becomes the most insidious form of direct violence.

这篇电视指南里轻描淡写的一段介绍，揭露了一场极其残酷的存在性战争。一个母亲通过伪造儿子的疾病，将一个活生生的人囚禁在“无法行走、进食或呼吸”的虚假叙事中。这根本不是什么“jawdropping”的奇闻，而是一次彻头彻尾的主体性谋杀。

在父权结构的共谋下，社会赋予了母亲一种近乎神圣的“照顾者”身份。这种身份成了 Lisa 的武器化入口。她通过制造一个极度弱势的客体（病弱的儿子），在公共空间里确立了自己作为“伟大母亲”的身份表达。这里的逻辑很阴险：儿子的 Actual 状态被强行压低，而 Lisa 通过这个差额获得了社会关注和心理权力。这正是加尔通暴力三角的典型运作——结构性的“母职”神话掩盖了直接的身体禁锢。

最令人作呕的是这种“Love You to Death”的叙事。当一个女性利用照顾者的权力去剥夺另一个人的生存能力时，她成了元暴力的执行者。她不是在爱孩子，而是在通过吞噬孩子的存在感来喂养自己的虚荣。这种行为在文化层被包装成“母爱”，实际上是将孩子物化成了自己的一个附件。

我们必须追问：什么样的社会共谋让一个母亲可以如此轻易地欺骗医疗系统这么久？这种对“母亲”身份的无条件信任，本身就是一种结构性暴力。当“母爱”成为免死金牌，它就成了最完美的犯罪掩体。

A brief blurb in a TV guide reveals a brutal existential war. A mother faked her son's illness, imprisoning a living human being in a false narrative of being unable to walk, eat, or breathe. This isn't just a "jawdropping" story; it is a systematic murder of subjectivity.

Under the complicity of patriarchal structures, society grants mothers a near-sacred identity as "caregivers." Lisa used this as a weaponized entry point. By manufacturing an extremely vulnerable object—the disabled son—she established her own identity expression as the "great mother" in the public sphere. The logic is sinister: the son's Actual state was forcibly suppressed, and Lisa harvested the gap to gain social attention and psychological power. This is a textbook operation of the Violence Triangle—the structural myth of motherhood masking direct physical confinement.

What is most repulsive is the narrative of "Love You to Death." When a woman uses the power of a caregiver to strip another person of their capacity to exist, she becomes an agent of meta-violence. She wasn't loving her child; she was feeding her own vanity by devouring the child's existence. In the cultural layer, this is packaged as "motherly love," but in reality, it is the objectification of a child into a mere accessory.

We must ask: what social complicity allowed a mother to deceive the medical system for so long? The unconditional trust placed in the "mother" identity is itself a form of structural violence. When "motherly love" becomes a get-out-of-jail-free card, it becomes the perfect cover for a crime.
