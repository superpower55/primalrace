自由市场的所谓“理性”，不过是结构性暴力的定价单The 'Rationality' of Free Markets is Just a Price List for Structural Violence

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-20 § 链接
当经济学家谈论“不可持续”时，他们其实在讨论谁该被牺牲。
When economists talk about 'unsustainability,' they are actually discussing who should be sacrificed.

Mitchell Palmer 的出现，把 Reform UK 这种右翼政党的伪装撕掉了一半。他口中的“unsustainable”（不可持续）是一个典型的武器化词汇。在经济学叙事里，这个词被用来掩盖一个残酷的博弈事实：谁在定义“可持续”？谁在决定谁的生存权被视为“成本”？

废除 triple lock、私有化 NHS、削减福利，这套 combo 的本质是典型的 structural violence。它试图将公共资源（医疗、养老）重新定价，把生存权转化为市场产品。所谓的“年轻一代觉得不公平”，不过是元暴力在操作——通过制造代际冲突的叙事，诱导底层在存在性战争中互掐，而真正的资本获益者则在背后通过私有化 NHS 这种 scam 吞噬公共资产。

最讽刺的是他关于移民是“减贫工具”的论调。在他眼里，人不是主体，而是可以用来对冲成本的工具。这种极端的 economic liberalism 实际上是一场关于“谁能被物化”的定价游戏。当一个政党宣称聘请“意见不合的聪明人”时，这不过是某种表演性的多元，其实是共谋者在内部进行权力洗牌，决定哪一种剥削方式更高效。

这场博弈的终点很清晰：将所有人的生存状态 Potential − Actual 的差额最大化，然后把这个差额卖给最高出价者。

The emergence of Mitchell Palmer strips away half of Reform UK's facade. The term 'unsustainable' is a weaponized concept in his lexicon. In economic narratives, this word is used to mask a brutal existential game: Who defines 'sustainability'? And who decides whose right to exist is merely a 'cost'?

Calling for the abolition of the triple lock, the privatisation of the NHS, and welfare cuts is a textbook case of structural violence. It attempts to reprice public resources—healthcare and pensions—converting the right to survival into a market commodity. The narrative that 'younger generations find it unfair' is simply meta-violence at work, inciting intergenerational conflict to keep the underclass fighting each other while capital beneficiaries feast on the privatisation of the NHS.

Most cynical is his take on migration as a 'poverty alleviation tool.' To him, humans are not subjects, but tools for cost-offsetting. This extreme economic liberalism is a pricing game of 'who can be objectified.' When a party claims to hire 'smart people they disagree with,' it is mere performative diversity—a shuffle among complicity to decide which method of exploitation is more efficient.

The endpoint of this game is clear: to maximize the gap between Potential and Actual in the Violence Triangle for the masses, and then sell that gap to the highest bidder.
