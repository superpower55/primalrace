床上的权力博弈：当“情夫”决定在婚姻里退休Power Play in Bed: When the 'Lover' Retires in Marriage

性别 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-09 § 链接
性快感的分配不均，本质上是男性中心叙事在私域的权力垄断。
The unequal distribution of sexual pleasure is a manifestation of masculine-centric narrative in the private sphere.

这是一个典型的关于“最优解表达”被异化，并最终导致主体性死亡的案例。在这段关系里，性不是为了连接，而是一场关于谁需要为谁提供服务的博弈。男方在年轻时扮演的“情夫”角色，本质上是一种无需承担责任、仅提取快感的 weaponized 表达。当他进入婚姻，将这种“提取模式”惯性地迁移到妻子身上时，他实际上在执行一种潜意识的元暴力：我的快感是优先的，而你的快感是可以通过玩具或外部男性来“外包”的。

最令人作呕的 scam 是他建议妻子去寻找其他男人，只要“不要告诉他”。这看似是宽容，实则是最高级的控制——他通过定义规则，将自己从“提供快感”的义务中彻底解脱，同时依然保留对婚姻结构的掌控权。他试图把妻子变成一个在身体上被外包、在情感上依然对他忠诚的客体。这根本不是什么“年龄增长”或“睾酮降低”的生物学问题，而是他拒绝将妻子视为一个具有同等主体性的存在。

所谓的 couples therapy 或“重新设计性菜单”，在面对一个已经决定在婚姻中“退休”的掠夺者时，往往只是表演性的让步。当一个男人告诉你他曾经是很多女性的 lover，但现在对你没有好奇心时，他其实已经完成了他的表达：你只是他生活中的一个功能性组件（生育者、照顾者），而非一个需要被探索的生命。在这种 structural violence 下，任何试图通过“沟通”来挽回的努力，都只是在帮共谋者修补囚笼。

This is a classic case of 'Optimal Expression' being alienated, leading to the death of subjectivity. In this relationship, sex is not about connection, but a game of who serves whom. The 'lover' role the husband played in his youth was a weaponized expression—extracting pleasure without responsibility. By migrating this 'extraction mode' into marriage, he executes a subconscious meta-violence: his pleasure is priority, while hers is a task to be 'outsourced' to toys or other men.

The most disgusting scam is his suggestion that she sleep with others, provided she 'doesn't tell him.' This is not tolerance; it is a high-level form of control. He defines the rules to liberate himself from the obligation of providing pleasure while maintaining structural dominance over the marriage. He seeks to turn his wife into an object—physically outsourced, yet emotionally tethered.

Attributing this to 'ageing' or 'low testosterone' is a distraction. It is not a biological failure, but a refusal to recognize the wife as a subject with equal agency. When a man tells you he was a lover to many but has no curiosity about you, he has made his expression clear: you are a functional component (procreator, caregiver), not a life to be explored. In the face of such structural violence, 'couples therapy' is often just a performative concession, helping the complicity of the oppressor to patch up the cage.
