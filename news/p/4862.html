英国的谴责：一场结构性暴力的PR修补UK Condemnation: A PR Patch for Structural Violence

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-08 § 链接
谴责不等于削减暴力，除非它能改变Potential与Actual的差额。
Condemnation is not reduction of violence unless it narrows the gap between Potential and Actual.

英国政府这次把 'ethnic cleansing' (种族清洗) 这种词摆在台面上，看起来是外交辞令的升级，但本质上依然是 cultural layer 的表演性让步。根据加尔通的暴力三角，Violence = Potential − Actual。在西岸和加沙，Actual 的状态是数万名儿童的死亡和系统性的土地掠夺，而 Potential 的状态应该是基本人权的保障。英国的 'strongest condemnation' 能让这个差额缩小多少？

这种叙事是典型的武器化表达。西方国家习惯于在实施 structural violence (结构性暴力) 的过程中，通过偶尔的、高分贝的谴责来维持其 '文明' 与 '人道' 的认知入口。这种机制让外界产生一种错觉：只要谴责得足够强烈，就意味着正义在运作。但事实上，制裁贸易与地面上的杀戮之间存在着巨大的时差和脱节。只要以色列政府在元暴力 (meta violence) 的逻辑下继续将巴勒斯坦人定义为 '非人' 或 '威胁'，这种定义权的垄断才是所有直接暴力 (direct violence) 的根源。

我们需要追问的是：这次谴责是被谁赢出来的？是底层抗争的压力，还是地缘政治博弈中的一次筹码交换？如果制裁没有强制执行机制，没有具体到个体的救济，那么这次 '最强谴责' 仅仅是另一种形式的 cultural violence —— 它用一种 '我们已经在关注' 的姿态，掩盖了结构性共谋的惯性。

The UK government bringing 'ethnic cleansing' into its official vocabulary looks like an escalation of diplomatic rhetoric, but it remains a performative concession at the cultural layer. According to Galtung's Violence Triangle, Violence = Potential − Actual. In the West Bank and Gaza, the Actual is the death of tens of thousands of children and systematic land theft, while the Potential is the guarantee of basic human rights. How much does the 'strongest condemnation' actually narrow this gap?

This is a classic weaponisation of expression. Western powers often maintain their cognitive entry points of 'civilization' and 'humanity' by deploying high-decibel condemnations while structural violence continues. This mechanism creates an illusion: that as long as the condemnation is loud enough, justice is functioning. In reality, there is a massive disconnect between trade sanctions and the slaughter on the ground. As long as the Israeli government continues to define Palestinians as 'sub-human' or 'threats' under the logic of meta violence, this monopoly on interpretation remains the root of all direct violence.

We must ask: who won this condemnation? Was it the result of grassroots resistance, or a chip exchanged in a geopolitical game? Without enforcement mechanisms or concrete individual relief, this 'strongest condemnation' is merely another form of cultural violence — using the posture of 'we are watching' to mask the inertia of structural complicity.
