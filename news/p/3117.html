冷冻血样与科技棉条：一场精心包装的生物殖民Frozen Blood and Techno-Tampons: A Masterclass in Biological Colonization

性别 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-01 § 链接
将女性身体数据化为产品的过程，本质上是元暴力的商业升级。
Converting the female body into data products is simply a commercial upgrade of meta-violence.

Bryan Johnson 把女友的经血冻在 -80°C 冰箱里，并称其为“被低估的资源”。这听起来像是个前卫的 biohacking 实验，但剥开“科学”的外壳，这不过是典型的男性中心叙事 (masculine-centric narrative) 在生物学层面的延伸：将女性身体客体化为可观测、可量化、可提取的资源，而解释权依然牢牢掌握在男性手中。

Kate Tolo 试图用“为了全人类女性”这种宏大叙事来为自己的配合正名。这正是典型的“假.最优解表达”——在父权结构中，女性通过扮演“科学贡献者”或“被研究的客体”来换取某种认同感或医疗救济。当她面对 100 多个每日任务和“科技棉条”时，她不是在被“看见”，而是在被“采集”。这种采集的终点不是医疗正义，而是 Johnson 旗下公司 Immortals 即将推出的女性产品线。这就是一个标准的商业 scam：用“打破医疗误区”作为认知入口，将女性身体作为免费的 R&D 实验室，最后将结果包装成产品反向卖给女性。

最令人作呕的共谋在于，这种行为被冠以“女性健康”之名。事实上，真正的医疗正义应该是结构性的资源重新分配，而不是一个科技富豪把女友当成私人宠物实验。从给儿子抽血到冷冻女友经血，Johnson 在践行一种极端的、武器化的表达：通过定义什么是“生物测量”，他试图在存在性战争中通过掌控他人的生物数据来获得某种神性。在这种叙事里，女性不再是主体，而是一个带有 1400 万个数据点的生物样本。

Bryan Johnson freezing his girlfriend’s menstrual blood at -80°C is framed as a biohacking experiment. In reality, it is a textbook extension of masculine-centric narrative into biology: objectifying the female body as a resource to be measured, quantified, and extracted, while the power of interpretation remains exclusively male.

Kate Tolo justifies her participation through the grand narrative of "the good of womankind." This is a classic False Optimal Expression. Within a patriarchal structure, women often play the role of the "scientific contributor" or "observed object" to gain a sense of validation or medical relief. As she submits to 100+ daily tasks and "techno-tampons," she is not being "seen"—she is being harvested. The destination of this harvest isn't medical justice, but the product line of Johnson’s company, Immortals. It is a commercial scam: using the "medical misogyny" narrative as a cognitive entry point to turn the female body into a free R&D lab, only to sell the results back to women as products.

The most disturbing complicity here is the branding of this as "women's health." True medical justice requires structural redistribution of resources, not a tech mogul treating his partner as a private lab pet. From draining his son's plasma to freezing his girlfriend's blood, Johnson is employing a weaponized expression: by defining what constitutes "biological measurement," he seeks a god-like dominance in the existential war. In this narrative, the woman ceases to be a subject and becomes a biological specimen with 14 million data points.
