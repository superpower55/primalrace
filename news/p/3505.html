在模拟战争中扮演民主，在真实暴力中交出选票Simulating Democracy in War Games While Surrendering Ballots to Real Violence

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-09 § 链接
当权力试图重新定义事实，模拟演习只是在既定剧本里扮演受害者。
When power redefines facts, wargaming is merely performing the role of the victim within a pre-written script.

民主党人在会议室里玩 wargame，模拟 deepfake、武装民兵和火bombing。这看起来像是在防守，但本质上是一场关于“认知入口”的博弈失守。当 Trump 能够通过一次电视讲话就将“中国窃取选民信息”这种毫无根据的 scam 变成一个政治议题，他已经夺取了定义事实的制造权。在这种 meta violence 面前，法律专家讨论宪法条款就像是用一把生锈的剪刀试图剪断钢缆。

注意这个逻辑：Trump 并不需要 deepfake 真的能骗过专家，他只需要通过 amplify 这些谎言，制造出一种“混乱”的氛围。这种 confusion 本身就是一种武器化的表达。当武装人员出现在投票站，或者 DHS 代理人被部署到投票点，这不再是 structural violence 的潜在可能，而是 direct violence 的预演。而民主党的应对方案——“all-hands-on-deck”——在机制上依然是 reactive 的。他们试图在对方已经通过元暴力重塑现实后，用传统的法律程序去“修复”现实。

最讽刺的是，这种“模拟演习”本身也成了一种表演性的表达。它给进步派选民提供了一种“我们在行动”的心理安慰，但它没有触及核心：一个能够威胁撤销 OMB 资金、威胁 FCC 执照的行政权力，已经将国家机器变成了一个巨大的共谋场域。当法律被工具化为攻击投票权的武器，所谓的“民主韧性”不过是掩盖结构性坍塌的遮羞布。这场战争的胜负不在于谁的模拟演习更真实，而在于谁能定义什么是“合法的选举”。

Democrats are playing wargames in conference rooms, simulating deepfakes, armed vigilantes, and firebombings. It looks like defense, but it's actually a total surrender of the "cognitive entry points." When Trump can turn a baseless scam about "China stealing voter data" into a national political agenda via a single TV address, he has already seized the power to manufacture facts. In the face of such meta violence, legal experts debating constitutional clauses are like people trying to cut steel cables with rusty scissors.

Observe the logic: Trump doesn't need deepfakes to actually fool the experts; he only needs to amplify the lies to create an atmosphere of "chaos." This confusion itself is a weaponised expression. When armed men appear at polls or DHS agents are deployed, we are no longer talking about the potential of structural violence, but the rehearsal of direct violence. The Democratic response—"all-hands-on-deck"—remains fundamentally reactive. They are trying to "repair" reality using traditional legal procedures after the opponent has already reshaped reality through meta violence.

The irony is that these "tabletop exercises" have become a form of performative expression. They offer progressive voters a psychological sedative—the feeling that "something is being done"—while ignoring the core issue: an executive power capable of threatening OMB funding and FCC licenses has turned the state apparatus into a massive field of complicity. When the law is weaponised to attack voting rights, "democratic resilience" is just a veil covering a structural collapse. The outcome of this war isn't decided by whose simulation is more realistic, but by who defines what constitutes a "legitimate election."
