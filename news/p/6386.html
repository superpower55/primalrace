秋天是用来谈恋爱的Autumn Is for Falling in Love

文娱 文化层 Entertainment Weekly ↗ 2026-11-01 § 链接
二十六部 cozy 片单，卖的是同一种浪漫爱
A 26-film fall list selling one product: romantic love.

一份 26 部电影的秋季片单，从《Moonstruck》排到《You've Got Mail》，几乎每一部的落点都是同一个：两个人终于在一起。这不是选片口味，是投放。

《Practical Magic》最能说明问题。两个女巫，会法术，有诅咒，头发漂亮，阿姨古怪——然后呢？影评自己写了，她们爱的男人是纸板剪影，死了也不心疼。一部关于女巫的电影，被拍成了一部关于读书会的电影，只是蜡烛多一点。女巫的力量被抽走，剩下的用途是谈恋爱。这就是武器化的入口：先给你一个看起来有主体性的壳，再把壳里的东西换成浪漫爱。

《All That Heaven Allows》和它对它的翻拍《Far From Heaven》倒是诚实，直接把阶级、年龄、种族的越界摆到台面上，让观众看见一个社区如何用体面把人围死。可片单把它们和《When Harry Met Sally》并列成同一种“cozy”，等于把拆穿结构的电影重新包装成结构本身。看一部控诉体面的电影，然后感到温暖——这个转化本身就是共谋。

《Juno》更直接。16 岁，怀孕，决定生下来送人，然后被写成“值得反复回看的 comfort watch”。一个少女的身体被当作剧情装置，她的选择被当作温馨。片单没有问这个选择是在什么条件下做出的，因为一问，cozy 就没了。

《Love Story》那条自己都写了：主角会死，但我们还是要推荐。死的是她，被记住的是他。这套模板此后每一部秋季爱情片都在复制。

所以这份片单真正在卖的不是电影，是一种季节性的情感规训：秋天到了，你该想恋爱了，该有个人了，该在壁炉前裹着毛衣看别人相爱了。它把“浪漫爱”当成空气一样自然的东西发给你，你收下的时候不会觉得那是被投放的。

二十六部里，真正没有把女性写成等待被爱的容器的，大概只有《The Trouble With Harry》——一具尸体躺在山坡上，几个镇民忙着处理，谁都不太伤心。那才是秋天该有的温度。

A list of 26 fall movies, running from Moonstruck to You've Got Mail, and nearly every one lands on the same point: two people finally get together. That isn't taste in programming. That's distribution.

Practical Magic makes the mechanism visible. Two witches, actual spells, a curse, great hair, daffy aunts — and then what? The review itself says it: the men they love are cardboard cutouts, and their deaths don't really hurt. A movie about witches gets mistaken for a movie about a book club, with more candles. The witches' power is drained out, and what's left is for romance. That's the weaponized entry point: hand over a shell that looks like agency, then swap the contents for romantic love.

All That Heaven Allows and its descendant Far From Heaven are at least honest — class, age, race, a community closing ranks with good manners. But the list files them next to When Harry Met Sally as the same kind of cozy, which repackages a film that exposes the structure as the structure itself. Watch a movie indict class respectability, then feel warm. That conversion is the complicity.

Juno is blunter. Sixteen, pregnant, decides to carry and give the baby away, and it's sold as a comfort watch worth revisiting. A girl's body becomes a plot device; her choice becomes coziness. The list never asks under what conditions that choice was made, because asking would end the cozy.

Love Story, the list admits, kills its lead and recommends it anyway. She dies; he's remembered. Every fall romance since has copied the template.

So what the list actually sells isn't movies. It's seasonal emotional training: autumn is here, you should want romance, you should want someone, you should curl up by the fire in a sweater and watch other people fall in love. It hands you romantic love as if it were air, and you don't notice it was placed there.

Of the 26, the only one that doesn't write women as containers waiting to be loved is probably The Trouble With Harry — a corpse on a hillside, a town busy disposing of it, nobody especially sad. That's the temperature autumn should have.
