被误认为“不合群”的，是对游戏化共谋的拒绝Not 'Anti-Social', But a Refusal of Gamified Complicity

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-01 § 链接
游戏不是娱乐，而是用预设规则强行接管主体性的权力博弈。
Games are not leisure; they are power plays that hijack subjectivity through preset rules.

大多数人把这篇评论看作一个女性在抱怨“不擅长游戏”的社交尴尬，这又是典型的男性中心叙事：将一个关于权力与主体性的反抗，简化为“能力不足”的个人特质。Emily Watkins 真正反感的不是乒乓球或拼字游戏，而是游戏作为一种 weaponized expression 对有机对话的截断。

在社会博弈中，游戏提供了一套假的最优解表达。它通过预设的规则（rules）定义什么是“赢”，从而将复杂的个体关系简化为分数的竞争。当你进入游戏，你就不再是那个拥有复杂背景、情感和主观意志的个体，而是一个在规则内执行指令的棋子。这种“组织起来的快乐” (organised fun) 实际上是一种 structural violence，它强迫参与者在既定框架内表现，任何试图在游戏之外建立深度连接的尝试，都会被视为对“游戏氛围”的破坏。

文章中提到的那个“对卡片位置极其认真”的群体，正是元暴力的微缩样本：他们通过垄断对规则的解释权，将细碎的程序正义转化为一种支配他人的权力快感。而 Watkins 追求的“咖啡与红酒的时间”，本质上是要求回归到一种公正的表达 (Just Expressions)——在这种状态下，人与人的连接不依赖于谁赢了谁，而依赖于主体性的真实碰撞。

所谓的“不具竞争心”，其实是对这种低级共谋的拒绝。真正的乐趣始于游戏停止，因为只有当预设的剧本被撕毁，个体才重新拿回定义自己存在的方式。

Most readers will dismiss this piece as a woman complaining about her 'lack of gaming skills'—a classic masculine-centric narrative that reduces a rebellion against power to a personal deficiency. What Emily Watkins is actually fighting is not ping-pong or Scrabble, but the weaponisation of games as a means to curtail organic human connection.

In social dynamics, games provide a fake optimal expression. By defining 'winning' through preset rules, they flatten complex individualities into mere competitors. The moment you enter a game, you cease to be a subject with a unique background and will; you become a pawn executing instructions within a framework. This 'organised fun' is a form of structural violence, forcing participants to perform according to a script and dismissing any attempt at authentic connection as a disruption of the 'vibe'.

The cohort she describes—those 'frothing at the mouth' over the placement of a card—are micro-samples of meta-violence. They convert the monopoly of interpretative power over trivial rules into a sense of dominance. Watkins' longing for 'coffee or wine' is a demand for Just Expressions, where human connection relies not on who defeated whom, but on the collision of genuine subjectivities.

What is labeled as 'pathologically uncompetitive' is, in fact, a refusal of this complicity. Real fun begins when the games stop, because only when the preset script is torn up does the individual reclaim the power to define their own existence.
