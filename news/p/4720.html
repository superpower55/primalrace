哥斯拉级别的灾难，是人类共谋的最终结算单Godzilla-level Disaster: The Final Settlement of Human Complicity

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-03 § 链接
自然循环被武器化为结构暴力，全球变暖是人类共谋的利息。
Natural cycles weaponized into structural violence; global warming is the interest on human complicity.

联合国用“哥斯拉级别” (Godzilla-level) 来形容这次厄尔尼诺，这种拟人化的恐怖叙事虽然抓眼球，但掩盖了一个事实：这根本不是什么不可抗力的“自然灾害”，而是一场由化石燃料驱动的、经过精密计算的结构性暴力 (structural violence)。

按照加尔通的暴力三角，当全球气温被推高到千年之最，Potential（本可维持的生态稳定）与 Actual（被破坏的现状）之间的差额，就是由工业文明通过共谋 (complicity) 制造的暴力。资本市场、能源巨头与政治权力通过垄断认知入口，将气候危机包装成“不可预见的极端天气”，从而逃避作为施暴者的责任。这是一种典型的文化暴力 (cultural violence)：它让直接的死亡（如欧洲3.5万人的热浪死伤）看起来像是不幸的随机事件，而非系统性谋杀。

最讽刺的是，这次厄尔尼诺正在形成一个恶性正反馈循环：全球变暖强化厄尔尼诺，而厄尔尼诺反过来加速气候危机。这就像一个被设计好的 scam，底层群体——那些在印度面临季风减弱、在南美面对洪水、在印尼面对山火的人们——成了这场博弈中唯一的买单者。他们没有定义权，没有解释权，只有在“危险区域”等待被收割的身体。

面对这种 meta-violence，联合国秘书长呼吁的“赢得比赛”听起来像是一场毫无诚意的 PR 演讲。如果规则制定者依然是那些通过污染获利的共谋者，那么任何所谓的“气候行动”都只是在给哥斯拉喂食，好让它在下一次爆发前暂时安静下来。

The UN uses the term "Godzilla-level" to describe this El Niño. While this anthropomorphic narrative of terror grabs attention, it masks a stark reality: this is not an "uncontrollable natural disaster," but a calculated structural violence driven by fossil fuels.

Applying Galtung's Violence Triangle, when global temperatures are pushed to a millennium high, the gap between the Potential (ecological stability) and the Actual (the degraded present) is the violence manufactured through the complicity of capital markets, energy giants, and political power. By monopolizing cognitive entry points, they package the climate crisis as "unpredictable extreme weather" to evade their role as perpetrators. This is classic cultural violence: it makes direct deaths—such as the 35,000 heatwave casualties in Europe—appear as random misfortunes rather than systemic homicide.

Most ironically, this El Niño is forming a vicious positive feedback loop: global heating intensifies El Niño, which in turn turbocharges the climate crisis. It is a designed scam where the marginalized—those facing weakened monsoons in India, floods in South America, and wildfires in Indonesia—are the only ones paying the bill. They possess no power to define or interpret; they only have bodies waiting to be harvested in the "danger zone."

Facing this meta-violence, the UN Secretary-General's call to "win the race" sounds like an insincere PR stunt. As long as the rule-makers remain the complicitors profiting from pollution, any so-called "climate action" is merely feeding the Godzilla to keep it quiet until the next eruption.
