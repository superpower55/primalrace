海洋热浪：一场关于碳排出的共谋账单Marine Heatwaves: The Bill for a Carbon Complicity

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-19 § 链接
气候崩溃不是自然灾害，而是工业文明在元暴力驱动下的共谋结果。
Climate breakdown is not a natural disaster, but the result of complicity driven by meta-violence.

海洋热浪被描述为一种“现象”，但按照加尔通的暴力三角，这本质上是 structural violence 的具象化。碳污染导致海水升温 6°C，这种差额就是暴力：海洋生物本可处于的生存状态与实际被灼烧状态之间的鸿沟，是由化石燃料工业及其背后的权力结构强行制造的。

最讽刺的共谋在于欧洲的姿态：一方面在学术报告中承认气候崩溃导致了 2/3 的超额热量，另一方面却在现实中继续扩张化石燃料基础设施。这是一种典型的 weaponized narrative——用“科学认知”来扮演负责任的文明角色，同时用“经济增长”来维持对能源垄断的解释权。这种“一手承认，一手扩张”的逻辑，正是元暴力的运作方式：定义规则的人，永远在为自己的掠夺寻找合理化出口。

海洋生物的大规模死亡、沿海社区生计的崩溃，被包装成“生态挑战”，但它们其实是这场全球性共谋的代价。当欧洲作为全球污染贡献最大且最快的洲，试图用 PR 版本的环保政策来对冲其实际的碳排放时，它在制造一种假象：认为气候危机是可以通过“管理”来解决的，而不是必须通过“权力让渡”和“结构拆除”来终结的 scam。

Marine heatwaves are framed as a 'phenomenon,' but through the lens of the Violence Triangle, this is structural violence materialized. Carbon pollution pushing waters 6°C above normal is a gap of violence: the difference between the potential survival state of marine life and the actual scorched reality, forcibly created by the fossil fuel industry and its power structures.

The most cynical part of this complicity is Europe's posture: acknowledging in research that climate breakdown caused two-thirds of the excess heat, while simultaneously expanding fossil fuel infrastructure. This is a weaponized narrative—performing the role of a 'responsible civilization' through scientific discourse, while maintaining the monopoly over the explanation of economic growth. This 'acknowledge with one hand, expand with the other' logic is exactly how meta-violence operates: those who define the rules always find a way to rationalize their plunder.

The mass mortality of marine species and the collapse of coastal livelihoods are packaged as 'ecological challenges,' but they are the costs of this global complicity. As the fastest-warming continent and a disproportionate polluter, Europe's attempt to offset its emissions with PR-version environmental policies creates a scam: the illusion that the climate crisis is a 'management' problem rather than one requiring the dismantling of structural power.
