用和平作诱饵的博弈陷阱The Peace Bait: A Game of Existential Trap

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-29 § 链接
当保护仅基于政令而非法律，缴械即是交付生命定价权的自杀。
When protection rests on decrees rather than law, disarmament is a suicide of pricing power.

这 99 个哥伦比亚游击队员在选举前三天缴械，在博弈论中这叫典型的“信息不对称”下的惨败。前总统 Petro 的“全面和平”计划（Total Peace）本质上是一场政治 PR 秀，他试图用这 99 个人作为 sample 来制造一种“和平可行”的叙事，以此在选举前为自己刷存在感。而这 99 个人被告知他们是“开路先锋”，实际上他们是被推到前台的 sacrificial lambs。

注意这个 ZUT（临时安置区）的性质：它不是由法律保障的避风港，而是由总统 decree（政令）维持的临时笼子。在结构层（structural layer），这意味着他们的生存权不依赖于公正的法律框架，而依赖于某个具体政治人物的意志。当右翼总统 De la Espriella 上台，这种基于个人意志的保护瞬间失效。对于新总统来说，这 99 个人不是和平的希望，而是前任留下的、可以随时被定义为“罪犯”并用来彰显强硬立场的政治资产。

最讽刺的是，这些人中大多数人将游击队称为“公司”（the company），这意味着他们的参与动机是经济性的而非意识形态的。但在这次博弈中，他们失去了唯一的筹码——武器。现在他们处于一种极端的 legal limbo（法律真空）状态：没有武器，没有资金，没有法律保障，只有对面一英里外盯着他们的士兵。这种从 Potential（拥有武装的博弈能力）到 Actual（赤手空拳的囚徒）的剧烈跌落，就是最直接的结构性暴力。

那个 19 岁少年说如果计划失败他会重新拿起武器，这是一种绝望的本能反应。但现实是，当解释权被新政府垄断，他们甚至没有机会重新选择。这场“和平”是一场 scam，它向结构性弱势者兜售一个虚假的最优解，代价是让他们在权力交替的瞬间，彻底失去对自己生命的定价权。

The disarmament of these 99 Colombian guerrillas three days before the election is a textbook case of failure under information asymmetry. Petro’s “Total Peace” was essentially a political PR stunt, using these 99 individuals as a sample to manufacture a narrative of feasibility to boost his image before the runoff. They were told they were 'pioneers'; in reality, they were sacrificial lambs.

Look at the nature of the ZUT: it is not a sanctuary guaranteed by law, but a temporary cage maintained by presidential decree. On a structural layer, their survival depends not on a just legal framework, but on the whim of a specific political actor. With the rise of the far-right De la Espriella, this personal protection vanished instantly. To the new president, these people are not hopes for peace, but political assets—criminals-in-waiting who can be jailed or killed to perform 'strength'.

Ironically, most of these combatants viewed their guerrilla group as 'the company,' indicating economic rather than ideological motives. Yet, in this existential war, they surrendered their only bargaining chip: their weapons. They are now in a legal limbo, stripped of power, funds, and legal standing, with soldiers watching from a mile away. This collapse from Potential (armed bargaining power) to Actual (unarmed prisoners) is the essence of structural violence.

The 19-year-old's claim that he would take up arms again is a desperate instinct. But when the power of interpretation is monopolized by the new regime, they may not even get the chance to choose. This 'peace' was a scam—selling a fake optimal expression to the structurally weak, at the cost of their own life-pricing power during a power transition.
