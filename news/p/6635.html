裁员名单上，没有「执行」The Layoff List Has No "Execution" on It

文娱 结构层 · 文化层 The Hollywood Reporter ↗ 2026-11-02 § 链接
新闻摘要Netflix 10月中旬在全球裁员约1000人后，其伦敦办公室约2%至3%的员工受到影响。负责英国及欧洲、中东和非洲本地语言内容制作的副总裁安娜·马莱特在受影响之列。
The newsAfter Netflix laid off about 1,000 employees globally in mid-October, roughly 2 to 3 percent of staff at its London office were affected. Among them was Anna Mallett, VP of physical production for the U.K. and local-language content across EMEA.
「执行不够好」是裁掉做执行的人，不是裁掉下命令的人
"Execution isn't good enough" fires the people who execute, not the ones who order it.

Netflix 说「美国需要更好的执行」，然后伦敦办公室走了百分之二到三的人。

十月已经在美国裁了约一千人，占员工总数百分之六，是 2022 年以来最大的一次。现在轮到英国。伦敦几百号人里，安娜·马莱特（Anna Mallett）在名单上——她管英国和 EMEA 的实体制作，做过《驯鹿宝贝》《青春期》，《纸钞屋》后几季也有她。一个把「执行」做到能出爆款的人，被「执行不够好」这句话裁掉了。

这就是「让叙事看起来像事实」的经典操作：先定义问题——「内容没有本垒打，是因为执行不到位」——再用这个定义去分配代价。萨兰多斯在财报电话上说业务飞轮在转，战略不需要根本性调整，只是美国跑得不够快。翻译一下：结构没问题，是人的问题。于是结构不动，人走。

注意他说的是「其他地区指望这些片子成为本垒打，好在全世界流动」。也就是说，英国、欧洲、中东和非洲的制作团队，职责是为美国总部的全球片库供货。供货方被裁，需求方的战略被宣布「没问题」。谁在定义什么叫「执行」，谁就不用被「执行」考核。

裁员从来不是中性的成本优化。它是一次表达权的重新分配：留下的人接手离开的人的判断，被裁的人连解释自己为什么被裁的位置都没有。马莱特从 ITN 的 CEO、BBC Studios 的制作董事总经理做到 Netflix 的副总裁，履历本身就是一份「执行」的证明，而这份证明在这套叙事里不作数。

报道没写的是：伦敦这几百人里，走的是谁，留下的是谁，那百分之二到三的构成是什么。通讯岗位走了，制作岗位走了，比例背后的名单没人公布。不公布本身就是一种表达——它让「执行不够好」这句话无法被检验，只能被相信。

「本垒打」这个词用得很好。打球的人被换下场，看台上的人说球队需要更好的击球。

Netflix says the U.S. needs better execution, and then 2 to 3 percent of the London office walks.

About 1,000 people, 6 percent of the workforce, were cut in the U.S. in October — the largest round since 2022. Now it's the U.K.'s turn. Anna Mallett is on the list: vp of physical production for the U.K. and local-language content across EMEA, with Baby Reindeer, Adolescence and later seasons of Money Heist on her record. Someone whose execution produced hits got cut by the sentence "execution isn't good enough."

This is the standard move: define the problem first — no home runs because execution is weak — then use that definition to allocate the cost. Sarandos told the earnings call the flywheel is working, no fundamental shift in strategy is needed, the U.S. just isn't growing as fast as it could. Translate: the structure is fine, the people are the problem. So the structure stays and the people go.

He also said other territories count on those titles becoming home runs so they travel the world. Which means the U.K., Europe, Middle East and Africa production teams exist to supply the global library. The suppliers get laid off; the demand side's strategy is declared sound. Whoever defines what "execution" means never gets evaluated by it.

A layoff is never neutral cost optimization. It reassigns the right to speak: those who stay absorb the judgment of those who leave, and those who leave have no position from which to explain why. Mallett went from CEO of ITN and managing director of production at BBC Studios to vp at Netflix — a résumé that is itself proof of execution, and proof that doesn't count in this narrative.

What the report doesn't say: who left among those several hundred in London, who stayed, what the 2 to 3 percent was made of. Communications roles went, production roles went, the list behind the percentage was not published. Not publishing is itself an expression — it makes "execution isn't good enough" impossible to check and therefore possible to believe.

"Home runs" is the right word. The players get pulled; the stands announce the team needs better hitting.
