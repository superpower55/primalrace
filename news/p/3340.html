用 23 万套房制造的敌意幻象The Illusion of 230,000 Homes Built on Hatred

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-07 § 链接
结构性匮乏被伪装成身份战争，用驱逐弱者来掩盖治理能力的破产。
Structural scarcity disguised as an identity war to mask the bankruptcy of governance.

典型的 weaponized 叙事。保守党试图通过一个简单的算术题——将 23 万套社会住房从“外国国民”手中夺回给“英国本土家庭”——来制造一种资源被窃取的假象。这在本质上是一场关于认知入口的争夺战：它将住房危机的 structural violence（结构性暴力）转化为一场针对特定身份的身份政治战争。

事实上，根据 English Housing Survey，非英爱国民的持房率仅为 11.5%。这意味着，即便真的把这部分人全部驱逐，也无法解决住房危机这个巨大的 systemic gap。但这正是这套 scam 的高明之处：它不需要真的解决问题，只需要给本土底层一个可以投射仇恨的 target。通过定义谁是“free ride”的入侵者，统治阶层成功地让底层在一个虚假的“最优解表达”中内卷，而掩盖了他们长期不建设新住房的治理失能。

最令人作呕的是这种暴力在 direct 层面的残酷性：六个月搬离，无论你是否有孩子，无论你是否是支撑医疗系统的护理人员。这是一种赤裸裸的 meta violence（元暴力）——在“英国优先”的宏大叙事下，人的基本生存权被降格为一种可撤销的特权。当住房被定义为一种需要“earned”的奖赏而非人权时，所有人其实都进入了一个随时可能被剔除的博弈场。这次是外国国民，下次是谁？

这场共谋的逻辑很简单：右翼政客提供敌意，底层在恐惧中买单，而真正的权力持有者在混乱中继续垄断资源。所谓的“divisive”不仅是分歧，而是一种精心设计的切割，旨在确保 Potential 和 Actual 之间的差额永远无法被填平。

A textbook case of weaponized narrative. The Conservatives are attempting to frame a simple arithmetic problem—reclaiming 230,000 social homes from "foreign nationals" for "British families"—as a correction of theft. This is a battle for the cognitive entry point: transforming the structural violence of the housing crisis into an identity war against a specific group.

In reality, with non-UK/Irish nationals holding only 11.5% of social housing according to the English Housing Survey, mass eviction would do nothing to close the systemic gap of the housing crisis. But that is the core of the scam. They don't need to solve the problem; they only need to provide the local underclass with a target for their resentment. By defining some as "free riders," the ruling class lures the poor into a fake optimal expression of hatred, effectively masking their own failure to build new homes.

The brutality at the direct level is staggering: a six-month eviction notice regardless of children or professional contribution to the NHS. This is meta-violence in its purest form—under the narrative of "Britain First," the basic right to shelter is downgraded to a revocable privilege. When housing is framed as something to be "earned" rather than a human right, everyone enters a game of precariousness. Today it is foreign nationals; who is next?

The complicity here is clear: right-wing politicians provide the animosity, the underclass pays with their fear, and the real power holders continue to monopolize resources amidst the chaos. The "divisiveness" is not an accident; it is a calculated cleavage designed to ensure the gap between Potential and Actual remains wide open.
