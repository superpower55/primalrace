特朗普及共和党的共谋：一场关于主体性死亡的自杀式博弈The Complicity of Trump and the GOP: A Suicidal Game of Subjectivity Death

国际 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-04 § 链接
当权力不再依赖制度而依赖人格，政党就成了权力的耗材。
When power shifts from institutions to personality, the party becomes mere disposable fuel.

很多人在好奇特朗普为什么在期中选举前疯狂地自毁——挑起伊朗战争、得罪加拿大、强推数据中心。这种分析太 naive 了，还在用传统的“政党利益”逻辑去揣度一个已经完成权力异化的 autocrat。

从加尔通的暴力三角来看，这里发生的是一场典型的 structural violence。共和党这个组织，已经从一个拥有独立议程的政治实体，退化成了特朗普个人表达的 appendage。在元暴力的逻辑下，特朗普不需要一个高效的国会来作为 rubber stamp，因为他已经通过最高法院获得了近乎绝对的 immunity。当制度性的制衡失效，国会的权力就成了虚设的 writs，而他则掌握了实际的暴力机器和赦免权。

这就是一场极其残酷的共谋 (complicity)。共和党的精英们在潜意识中达成了一种协议：放弃主体性，扮演特朗普的追随者，以换取在权力核心边缘的生存空间。这种“假.最优解表达”让他们在短期内获得了权力的分红，但代价是主体性的彻底死亡。现在，特朗普正在执行他的“真.最优解表达”：通过制造共和党的失败，来证明这个党如果没有他就是废物，从而完成从“党魁”到“唯一神”的认知升级。

这种 narcissism 背后是极端的权力逻辑：他不在乎共和党赢不赢，他在乎的是共和党必须意识到，他们只能通过依附他才能赢。把队友变成耗材，然后指责耗材没用，这是典型的 masculine-centric narrative 权力游戏。共和党现在不是在面对 blue wave，而是在面对一个把他们当成认知入口实验品的操盘手。

Many are puzzled by Trump's apparent self-sabotage ahead of the midterms—the Iran war, the Canada feud, the data center push. This analysis is naive; it attempts to use traditional "party interest" logic to decipher an autocrat who has already completed a power mutation.

Through the lens of the Violence Triangle, we are witnessing structural violence. The GOP has devolved from a political entity with an independent agenda into a mere appendage of Trump's personal expression. Under the logic of meta-violence, Trump no longer needs a functional Congress as a rubber stamp because the Supreme Court has granted him near-absolute immunity. When institutional checks fail, Congressional power becomes a set of hollow writs, while he controls the actual machinery of violence and the power of pardon.

This is a brutal form of complicity. GOP elites have subconsciously agreed to surrender their subjectivity and play the role of followers in exchange for survival on the fringes of power. This "fake optimal expression" gave them short-term dividends, but the cost was the death of their subjectivity. Now, Trump is executing his "true optimal expression": by manufacturing a GOP defeat, he proves the party is useless without him, completing his cognitive upgrade from "party leader" to "sole deity."

Behind this narcissism lies an extreme power logic: he doesn't care if the GOP wins; he cares that the GOP realizes they can only win by clinging to him. Turning teammates into disposable fuel and then mocking their uselessness is a classic masculine-centric narrative power game. The GOP isn't facing a blue wave; they are facing a puppeteer using them as a test case for his cognitive entrance.
