互助的浪漫化是结构性暴力的麻醉剂Romanticizing Mutual Aid as an Anesthetic for Structural Violence

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-08 § 链接
用个体偶然的互助掩盖系统性的压迫，是典型的文化暴力。
Replacing systemic critique with accidental kindness is a textbook case of cultural violence.

这部剧的结构被赞美为“优雅的8字形”，但在我看来，这种结构恰恰完成了对结构性暴力的某种消解。剧本试图讨论社会压力如何摧毁精神健康，但它给出的解法竟然是两个陌生人在绝望时刻的“偶然交汇”与“互助”。这是一种极其危险的叙事陷阱：它将一个关于资源匮乏、阶级焦虑和系统性压迫的问题，简化为了一个关于“善意”的个体博弈问题。

注意剧中的角色设定。Esme在艺术学院被孤立，Harris在米其林餐厅面对暴政般的主厨。这些不是单纯的“压力”，而是典型的结构性暴力（structural violence）。米其林餐厅的等级森严、艺术教育中的权力不对等，这些都是由男性中心叙事（masculine-centric narrative）构建的权力场域。然而，剧作通过一种“轻盈的触感”将这些残酷的压迫转化为一种关于“情绪脆弱性”的讨论，仿佛抑郁症只是某种随机的生物性运气不好，而非被环境物理性地改写了意志。

当评论员庆祝这种“互助的力量”时，他们实际上在共谋一种文化暴力（cultural violence）：它告诉受害者，你不需要去挑战那个暴政主厨，不需要去拆除那个孤立你的制度，你只需要等待一个陌生人的偶然出现。这种“互助”在 Potential − Actual 的差额面前微不足道。它没有削减任何一层的暴力，只是在文化层给受害者打了一剂麻醉药，让人们在赞美“人性光辉”的同时，心安理得地维持那个制造抑郁的结构。真正的最优解表达应该是对压迫机制的揭露与反抗，而不是在绝望的深渊里互相取暖，然后告诉观众这很“优雅”。

The play's structure is praised as an 'elegant figure of eight,' but to me, this symmetry serves to neutralize the reality of structural violence. While the play claims to explore how social pressures erode mental health, its resolution relies on the 'chance encounter' and 'mutual support' between two strangers. This is a dangerous narrative trap: it reduces a systemic issue of resource scarcity and class anxiety to a game of individual kindness.

Look at the characters. Esme is isolated in an art academy; Harris is crushed by a tyrannical head chef in a Michelin restaurant. These aren't just 'stresses'—they are manifestations of structural violence. The rigid hierarchy of fine dining and the power imbalances in art education are power fields constructed by a masculine-centric narrative. Yet, the play uses a 'lightness of touch' to frame this as a study of 'emotional fragility,' as if depression were a random biological glitch rather than a will physically rewritten by a hostile environment.

When critics celebrate the 'power of mutual support,' they are complicit in a form of cultural violence. The message is clear: you don't need to challenge the tyrannical chef or dismantle the isolating institution; you just need to wait for a stranger to cross your path. In the equation of Violence = Potential − Actual, this 'mutual aid' does nothing to close the gap. It doesn't erode any layer of violence; it merely applies a cultural anesthetic, allowing the audience to admire 'humanity' while the structures that produce depression remain untouched. A true optimal expression would be the exposure and dismantling of the oppressive mechanism, not warming each other in the abyss and calling it 'elegant.'
