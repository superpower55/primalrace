用“自然”掩盖“人造”，是右翼最擅长的叙事scamUsing 'Nature' to Mask 'Man-made': The Right-Wing Narrative Scam

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-09 § 链接
将气候危机归咎于生态修复，是典型的用文化暴力掩盖结构暴力。
Blaming ecological restoration for climate crises is a classic use of cultural violence to mask structural violence.

这场火灾最讽刺的不是森林被烧毁，而是右翼媒体在火还没熄灭时，就迅速启动了一套名为“再野化 (rewilding)”的武器化叙事。他们试图把一个由全球变暖驱动的结构性灾难，通过定义“过度生长”的植物是罪魁祸首，将其转化为一个关于“管理不善”的文化议题。这套逻辑极其阴险：它把气候危机这个巨大的 structural violence 缩小为几个环保主义者的“审美偏好”问题，从而让真正的责任方——那些驱动全球升温的工业体系——在认知入口处被悄悄抹除。

这本质上是一场关于“解释权”的博弈。右翼试图定义什么是“正确的自然”：在他们看来，只有被人类修剪得整整齐齐、符合 20 世纪管理逻辑的景观才是安全的。而任何试图恢复生态自愈能力的尝试，都被贴上“危险”的标签。这种叙事通过制造恐惧，试图夺取对自然定义权的定价权，将“自然”本身客体化为一种需要被人类绝对掌控的资源，而非一个共生的系统。

好在这次博弈中，事实本身成了反击的武器。专家指出，起火的正是那些被“传统管理”了上百年的区域，而真正具有生态多样性的区域反而起到了缓冲作用。这证明了所谓的“管理”有时只是另一种形式的共谋——我们共谋维持一种看似有序的假象，直到气候崩溃的现实撕破这张皮。当人们意识到“人造火灾”才是本质时，右翼的这套文化掩体就失效了。

但我们不能 naive 地庆祝。即使叙事被拆穿，那个被暴露出的二战未爆弹依然埋在土里。这才是最精准的隐喻：在文化层面的口水战之下，结构层面的危机（气候崩溃）和历史层面的暴力（战争遗留）依然在潜伏。下次火灾来临前，我们能建立起真正的 fire-resilient 体系，还是继续在“再野化”还是“传统管理”的伪命题里内耗？

The most ironic part of this fire isn't the devastation of the heath, but how right-wing media launched a weaponized narrative of 'rewilding' before the flames were even out. They attempted to pivot a structural disaster driven by global warming into a cultural debate about 'mismanagement' and 'overgrown vegetation.' This is a sinister logic: it shrinks the massive structural violence of climate change into a question of environmentalists' 'aesthetic preferences,' effectively erasing the actual culprits—the industrial systems driving global heating—from the cognitive entry point.

This is essentially a game for the power of interpretation. The right wing seeks to define the 'correct nature': in their view, only landscapes manicured to fit 20th-century management logic are safe. Any attempt to restore ecological resilience is labeled 'dangerous.' By manufacturing fear, they attempt to seize the pricing power over the definition of nature, objectifying it as a resource to be absolutely controlled by humans rather than a symbiotic system.

Fortunately, in this bout, the facts served as the weapon of resistance. Experts pointed out that the fire raged precisely in areas under 'traditional management' for centuries, while biodiverse areas actually acted as buffers. This proves that so-called 'management' is often just another form of complicity—we conspire to maintain an illusion of order until the reality of climate collapse tears through the veil. Once people realize these are 'human fires,' the right-wing's cultural shield fails.

But we shouldn't be naive. Even if the narrative is debunked, the unexploded WWII ordnance remains buried in the soil. This is the most precise metaphor: beneath the cultural bickering, structural crises (climate collapse) and historical violence (war remnants) are still lurking. Before the next fire, will we build a truly fire-resilient system, or continue to waste energy on the false dichotomy of 'rewilding' versus 'traditional management'?
