法奥奇的“罪名”：一场关于解释权的政治狩猎The 'Crimes' of Fauci: A Political Hunt for Interpretive Power

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-10 § 链接
正义的辩论被武器化的司法取代，这是元暴力的典型表演。
When legitimate debate is replaced by weaponized justice, it is a performance of meta-violence.

法奥奇（Fauci）被共和党主导的委员会定义为“罪犯”，这根本不是一场关于公共卫生失误的审计，而是一次教科书级别的表达武器化（weaponization of expression）。在这场博弈中，事实（Fact）已经不重要了，重要的是谁拥有定义“事实”的解释权。

共和党人通过将法奥奇塑造为“超级反派”（supervillain），成功地在文化层（cultural layer）建立了一套叙事：一个傲慢的精英在欺骗大众。当这种叙事被内化为政治正确，随之而来的结构性暴力（structural violence）就顺理成章——利用参议院委员会这个权力席位，将法律程序转化为羞辱工具。法奥奇在听证会上100多次行使第五修正案，这在法律上是最优解表达，但在元暴力（meta violence）的叙事逻辑里，这被对方直接翻译成了“心虚”和“掩盖真相”。

最讽刺的是，这种“司法武器化”在共和党内部是高度共谋的。他们并不在乎法奥奇是否真的资助了病毒研究，他们在乎的是通过这场表演，向选民证明他们能够“惩罚”那些代表建制派、代表“精英知识分子”的符号。这是一种典型的男性中心叙事下的权力游戏：通过制造一个敌人并将其摧毁，来确立自身的强力形象。

当司法部被转化为政治工具，当听证会变成断头台的预演，我们看到的不是法治的胜利，而是解释权的绝对垄断。法奥奇的处境提醒我们，在一个元暴力主导的系统中，只要你处于对方定义的“敌人”阵营，任何合理的防御都会被解读为罪证。

The attempt by the Republican-led committee to define Anthony Fauci as a 'criminal' is not a public health audit; it is a textbook case of the weaponization of expression. In this game, facts are irrelevant; what matters is who holds the power to define those facts.

By casting Fauci as a 'supervillain,' Republicans have successfully constructed a narrative at the cultural layer: the arrogant elite deceiving the masses. Once this narrative is internalized, structural violence follows naturally—using the power of a Senate committee to transform legal proceedings into tools of humiliation. Fauci's invocation of the Fifth Amendment over 100 times was his optimal expression for survival, yet within the logic of meta-violence, it is instantly translated as 'guilt' and 'cover-up.'

Most ironically, this 'weaponized justice' is a product of deep complicity. They do not care whether Fauci actually funded virus research; they care about the performance—proving to their base that they can 'punish' symbols of the establishment and the intellectual elite. This is a classic power game of masculine-centric narrative: establishing one's own strength by manufacturing and destroying an enemy.

When the Justice Department becomes a political tool and hearings become rehearsals for the guillotine, we are witnessing not the victory of the rule of law, but the absolute monopoly of interpretive power. Fauci's plight reminds us that in a system governed by meta-violence, any reasonable defense is interpreted as evidence of a crime as long as you are categorized as the 'enemy.'
