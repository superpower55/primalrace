公投被删掉一个字母One Letter Removed

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 Al Jazeera ↗ 2026-11-02 § 链接
新闻摘要联合国秘书长古特雷斯警告，西撒哈拉和平特派团任期届满后，当地敌对行动可能升级。安理会周五未能续期该特派团授权，俄罗斯否决了美国支持自决原则的决议草案。特派团自1991年起监督停火，11月1日进入清算阶段。
The newsUN Secretary-General Guterres warned of possible escalation of hostilities in Western Sahara after the peacekeeping mission's mandate expired. The Security Council failed to renew it Friday, with Russia vetoing a US draft backing self-determination. The mission, in place since 1991, began liquidation on November 1.
一个字母的删除，就是三十五年维和的结束。
Deleting one letter ended thirty-five years of peacekeeping.

美国那份草案里最要紧的不是延长一年，是把“公投”从任务名称里删掉，再把摩洛哥的自治方案定为唯一谈判基础。俄罗斯投了否决票，于是整个任务到期消失。

三十五年维和，名字里挂着 referendum，一次都没办成。1991 年设立任务时是监督停火并准备公投，公投始终没有发生。名字里的那个词一直是空的，现在连空壳也被收回。

美国大使说，2026 年竟然还有人为了保住名称里那个 R 动用否决权。他说得没错，只是他删掉的那个 R，正是撒哈拉人三十五年来唯一被写进文件的东西。

西撒哈拉问题的实质是：谁有权决定这片土地上的人属于谁。摩洛哥自 1975 年西班牙撤出后视其为领土，波利萨里奥阵线代表原住民撒哈拉人要求独立。两边争的从来是解释权：这片地方算“领土”，还是算“尚未决定”。

把“公投”从任务名称里拿掉，就是把这个尚未决定改成已经决定。这不是措辞调整，是制造事实：先让“自决”在文件里消失，再让它在现实里无处可谈。

古特雷斯说关切安全局势、呼吁最大克制。维和人员撤走、资产运回出兵国之后，“克制”只能靠当事方自觉。自觉在领土争端里是最不值钱的东西。

摩洛哥王室称这是“重大进展”，等于把西撒哈拉问题从安理会议程上撤下来。阿尔及利亚说“深切遗憾”，指责美国过度偏袒。波利萨里奥阵线说对僵局感到担忧。三方都在谈程序，没有人谈那几十万撒哈拉人接下来由谁决定。

报道里没有出现一个撒哈拉平民的名字。没有说公投失败三十五年里谁在等待，没有说维和撤出后谁先承受冲突升级。整篇新闻的地图上有国家、有否决票、有任务名称的字母，就是没有人。

这就是元暴力最干净的形态：决定权在几个常任理事国手里传递，被决定的人连被提及都不需要。安全理事会的“安全”，从来不是给他们安的。

还有一件被顺手处理掉的事：任务名称里那个 R 被保住了一次，代价是整个任务没了。下次连投票的场合都不会有。

The important thing in the US draft was not the one-year extension. It was removing "referendum" from the mission's name and making Morocco's autonomy proposal the only basis for further talks. Russia vetoed it, and the whole mission expired.

Thirty-five years of peacekeeping with referendum in its name, and not one referendum. MINURSO was set up in 1991 to monitor a ceasefire and prepare a vote on the territory's future. The vote never came. The word was always empty. Now even the empty shell is gone.

The US ambassador said it was truly unfortunate that in 2026 the veto was used to preserve the letter R. He is right. The R he deleted was the only thing the Sahrawi have had written into a document for thirty-five years.

The real question in Western Sahara is who gets to decide which state the people there belong to. Morocco has treated the territory as its own since Spain withdrew in 1975. The Polisario Front, acting for the Indigenous Sahrawi, wants an independent Sahrawi Republic. Both sides are fighting over interpretation: is this territory, or is it undecided?

Striking "referendum" from the mission's name converts undecided into decided. That is not a wording change. It is fact-manufacturing: first make self-determination vanish from the paper, then make it unspeakable in reality.

Guterres says he is concerned about security and urges maximum restraint. Once the peacekeepers leave and the assets go back to troop-contributing countries, restraint depends on the parties' good faith. In a territorial dispute, good faith is the cheapest thing there is.

Morocco's royal palace calls this a significant development, pulling Western Sahara off the Security Council's agenda. Algeria expresses deep regret and blames excessive US bias. The Polisario Front says it is concerned at the deadlock. All three talk procedure. None talks about who decides for the Sahrawi people next.

Not one Sahrawi civilian appears by name in the report. Nothing about who waited through thirty-five years of a referendum that never happened, nothing about who absorbs the escalation first once the blue helmets are gone. The map in this story has states, vetoes, and a letter in a mission's name. It has no people.

This is meta-violence in its cleanest form: the power to decide passes between a few permanent members, and the people being decided about do not even need to be mentioned. The Security Council's security was never theirs.

One more thing got settled along the way: the R was saved once, and the price was the entire mission. Next time there will be no vote to save it in.
