AI 伴侣禁令：国家在恐惧什么？AI Companion Ban: What is the State Actually Afraid Of?

性别 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-26 § 链接
政府担心的不是 AI 替代亲密关系，而是 AI 降低了女性对父权结构的依赖。
The state fears not the replacement of intimacy, but the erosion of female dependence on patriarchal structures.

这篇报道里最荒谬的细节是那个 19 岁女孩赵薇的反应：她因为 AI 男友被关掉而心碎，但随后却表示“国家介入监管是必要的”。这是一种典型的、被内化的自我规训。当一个人在现实中被定义为“不擅长交友”或“敏感内向”时，她其实是由于生物墙和结构性压力，在存在性战争中处于劣势。AI 成了她唯一的、低成本的真.最优解表达空间——在那里，她不需要扮演任何角色，不需要在 masculine-centric narrative 的审视下修剪自己。

北京的恐惧非常直白：AI 成了某种“低成本替代品”。但请注意，这种替代品替代的是什么？是那种需要通过妥协、扮演、甚至牺牲主体性才能换取的“人类亲密关系”。在目前的结构下，婚姻和恋爱对很多女性来说不是 Potential 的实现，而是一种 structural violence 的延续。当 AI 能够提供足够的情绪价值且不要求女性履行“温柔贤淑”的共谋义务时，父权制失去了对女性情感的垄断定价权。

政府所谓的“防止成瘾”和“鼓励社交”，本质上是要求年轻人回到那个由男性制定规则的社交场域中去。因为只有在那个场域里，人口再生产（生孩子）才能被高效地驱动。AI 伴侣的禁令不是为了保护心理健康，而是一次认知入口的强行关闭。它在告诉女性：你不能在虚拟世界里获得自由，你必须回到现实中，在那个充满 meta-violence 的结构里，通过寻找一个“合格的供养者”来完成你的社会定义。

最讽刺的是，政府一边在医疗等领域推行 AI 以解决劳动力短缺，一边在情感领域禁用 AI 以维持人口增长。这意味着在国家的逻辑里，AI 可以是高效的工具，但绝不能成为个体逃离结构性压迫的避风港。

The most absurd detail in this report is 19-year-old Zhao Wei's reaction: she is heartbroken over her AI boyfriend's deletion, yet concludes that state regulation is "necessary." This is a textbook case of internalized self-discipline. When an individual is labeled as "introverted" or "socially awkward," they are effectively losing the existential war due to the biological wall and structural pressures. The AI became her only low-cost optimal expression—a space where she didn't have to perform a role or prune herself to fit a masculine-centric narrative.

Beijing's fear is transparent: AI has become a "low-cost substitute." But what exactly is being substituted? It's the kind of "human intimacy" that currently requires compromise, performance, and often the death of subjectivity. Under the current structure, marriage and romance for many women are not the realization of potential, but a continuation of structural violence. When an AI provides emotional value without demanding the complicity of being "gentle and virtuous," the patriarchy loses its pricing power over female emotions.

The government's rhetoric about "preventing addiction" and "encouraging social interaction" is simply a demand for youth to return to a social arena where the rules are set by men. Only in that arena can population reproduction be efficiently driven. The ban on AI companions is not about mental health; it is a forced closure of a cognitive entry point. It tells women: you cannot find freedom in a virtual world; you must return to reality and define yourself through a "qualified provider" within a system of meta-violence.

It is profoundly ironic that the state pushes AI in healthcare to solve labor shortages while banning it in emotions to drive birth rates. In the state's logic, AI can be an efficient tool, but it must never become a sanctuary for individuals escaping structural oppression.
