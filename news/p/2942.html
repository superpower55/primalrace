用“猎杀者”的扮演掩盖无能的表演Masking Incompetence Through the 'Hunter-Killer' Performance

国际 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-28 § 链接
政治表演的本质是抢夺认知入口，将臆想的威胁转化为权力的合法性。
Political performance is the seizure of cognitive entries, transforming imagined threats into a legitimacy of power.

这篇文章用讽刺的手法撕开了保守派政治家 Chris Philp 的表演面具。一个曾经在内政部通过把移民安置在酒店来“解决”问题的人，现在给自己贴上 “Hunter-Killer” 的标签，试图通过一种极端的、军事化的表达来确立自己的身份。这在我的框架里叫“假.最优解表达”：他并不真的拥有拦截小船的能力，所以他必须扮演一个被敌国军队威胁、在生死边缘徘徊的“孤胆英雄”，以此来向选民兑现某种虚假的强悍。

最荒谬的在于他将“法军向海中开枪”这一无关紧要的客观事实，通过自己的认知过滤器，武器化为一场针对他个人的“存在性战争”。他不仅在欺骗外界，甚至在通过这种自我催眠来完成一种主体性的重塑——从一个平庸的官僚，变成一个被马克龙忌惮的战略棋子。这种叙事逻辑极其典型：当 Actual（实际能力）极低时，通过制造一个巨大的 Potential 威胁，来掩盖两者之间巨大的差额，而这个差额就是他通过表演试图填补的政治资本。

而 Kemi 的反应则揭示了这种共谋的生态：在男本位的权力叙事中，即便对方在表演，只要这种表演符合“强权”的审美，共谋者就会迅速跟进，甚至将这种臆想升级为“核打击”的快感。这不再是关于边境安全的讨论，而是一场关于谁能更像“强者”的 Cosplay 比赛。这种将暴力美学化、将恐惧叙事化的过程，正是文化暴力在政治领域的最高级应用——它让真实的受害者（那些在橡皮艇上的移民）在叙事中彻底消失，只剩下两个政治演员在名为“国家安全”的舞台上互演。

This piece uses satire to strip away the mask of Conservative politician Chris Philp. A man who once 'solved' immigration by housing arrivals in hotels now adopts the 'Hunter-Killer' persona, attempting to establish his identity through an extreme, militarized expression. In my framework, this is a 'False Optimal Expression': lacking the actual capacity to stop the boats, he performs the role of a brave soldier threatened by foreign navies to sell a facade of strength to the electorate.

The absurdity peaks as he weaponizes the objective fact of French sailors firing into the sea—a non-event—into an 'existential war' targeting him personally. He is not just deceiving the public; he is attempting a reconstruction of his subjecthood, transforming from a mediocre bureaucrat into a strategic pawn feared by Macron. This logic is classic: when the Actual capacity is abysmal, one manufactures a massive Potential threat to bridge the gap, using that void as political capital.

Kemi's reaction reveals the ecology of this complicity. Within the masculine-centric narrative, as long as the performance aligns with the aesthetic of 'strongman' power, collaborators will amplify it, even escalating the fantasy to the thrill of 'tactical nuclear strikes.' This is no longer a debate on border security, but a Cosplay competition of who can appear more dominant. This process of aestheticizing violence and narrating fear is the peak of cultural violence in politics—it ensures the actual victims (the migrants in rubber dinghies) vanish from the narrative, leaving only two political actors performing 'national security' on a stage.
