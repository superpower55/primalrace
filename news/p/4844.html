用贸易禁令兑换的“人道主义”入场券Humanitarian Tickets Bought with Trade Bans

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-07 § 链接
结构性暴力的削减不能靠表演性让步，而靠权力解释权的让渡。
Reduction of structural violence requires the transfer of interpretive power, not performative concessions.

这次英国政府所谓的“关系重置” (reset)，本质上是一次典型的 structural violence 削减尝试，但其力度依然被精准地控制在不触动核心利益的范围内。禁止非法定居点贸易，是将此前长期被 Foreign Office 掩盖的“无法执行” (impossible to implement) 这一文化谎言拆穿，把一个原本属于 rhetorics 的支持转变为实际的 economic cost。从加尔通的暴力三角来看，这在 structural 层面上确实缩小了 Potential 与 Actual 的差额，因为定居点的扩张直接决定了巴勒斯坦生存空间的坍缩。

但我们需要警惕这种“进步”背后的 weaponized 叙事。Miliband 选择了国际法和 ICJ 作为认知入口，这在文化层面上是对元暴力的一次微小反击——尝试用普世规则替代单边霸权。然而，这种反击是极其谨慎的：它避开了“种族灭绝” (genocide) 这种触及本质的定义，也避开了对加沙援助限制的实质性制裁。这是一种“安全”的进步，一种在不激怒美国和以色列核心权力层的前提下，通过牺牲部分贸易利益来安抚国内民意（尤其是应对绿党压力）的博弈最优解。

最讽刺的共谋在于，当英国试图通过贸易禁令来维护国际法时，外部环境的 meta-violence 正在迅速反噬。内塔尼亚胡之子关于马尔维纳斯群岛的言论以及特朗普的威胁，揭示了国际政治中一种粗暴的逻辑：如果你试图用“人权”和“法律”来约束我，我就用“主权”和“安全”来勒索你。这场博弈证明了，只要解释权依然被强权垄断，任何基于良心的 structural 调整都可能被对方转化为新的直接暴力威胁。

The UK government's so-called "reset" is a typical attempt to reduce structural violence, yet its intensity is precisely calibrated to avoid disturbing core interests. Banning trade from illegal settlements dismantles the cultural lie of "impossibility to implement" long maintained by the Foreign Office, converting rhetorical support into actual economic cost. In terms of Galtung's Violence Triangle, this narrows the gap between Potential and Actual at the structural level, as settlement expansion directly dictates the collapse of Palestinian living space.

However, we must remain vigilant about the weaponized narrative behind this "progress." Miliband uses international law and the ICJ as cognitive entries, a minor counter-attack against meta-violence at the cultural level—attempting to replace unilateral hegemony with universal rules. Yet, this counter-attack is overly cautious: it avoids the essential definition of "genocide" and shuns substantive sanctions over aid restrictions in Gaza. This is a "safe" progression, an optimal expression designed to appease domestic pressure (specifically from the Greens) without alienating the core power structures of the US and Israel.

The most poignant complicity lies in how the meta-violence of the external environment swiftly retaliates. The comments from Netanyahu's son regarding the Falkland Islands and Trump's threats reveal a crude logic in international politics: if you attempt to constrain me with "human rights" and "law," I will blackmail you with "sovereignty" and "security." This game proves that as long as the power of interpretation remains monopolized by the hegemon, any structural adjustment based on conscience can be converted by the opponent into new threats of direct violence.
