用死者的账单，掩盖战争贩子的空手而归Using the Dead's Bill to Mask a Warmonger's Empty Hands

国际 文化层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-10 § 链接
赔偿金是战争叙事的遮羞布，用死者的定价权掩盖博弈的失败。
Reparations are a smokescreen for war narratives, using the pricing of death to mask strategic failure.

Trump 的这套逻辑是典型的 weaponized expression。当他在 Hormuz 海峡的谈判陷入 impasse，无法通过实际的权力交易达成协议时，他迅速切换到了“道德与账单”的叙事入口。把数十年来的士兵死亡和伊朗平民的血债转化为一个具体的 compensation 数字，本质上是将复杂的 structural violence 简化为一场商业讨价还价。这种做法最阴险的地方在于，他试图通过定义“谁欠谁钱”来夺取这场冲突的解释权。

这种叙事极其讽刺。一个将战争视为筹码、将地缘政治当成房地产交易的商人，现在突然开始关心“受害者的赔偿”。这不过是一次拙劣的 PR 操纵，旨在为 11 月的中期选举制造一种“强硬”的姿态。他提到的那些死在路边炸弹下的士兵，以及被伊朗政权杀害的抗议者，在这一刻被彻底客体化为他谈判桌上的筹码。死者的生命被赋予了一个价格，而这个价格不是为了正义，而是为了在谈判陷入僵局时，给自己找一个能够“愤怒”的理由。

而 Netanyahu 的公开决裂则揭示了这场共谋的真相：在 masculine-centric narrative 的世界里，所谓的“和平路线图”永远是次要的，而“绝对安全”和“彻底摧毁”才是男权政治的真.最优解。Trump 和 Netanyahu 之间那种“关系很好”的表演，不过是两个权力博弈者在面对不同利益节点时的暂时妥协。他们共谋地将中东的女性和儿童作为战争的背景板，而真正的博弈始终在几个男人之间关于“谁更强硬”的自尊心战争中进行。

最终，所有的赔偿要求都将变成一种文化暴力。它让人们误以为战争的伤痛可以通过金钱结算，从而掩盖了这种暴力最深层的元暴力——即认为某些地区的生命是可以被定价、被交换、被作为谈判筹码的殖民者逻辑。

Trump's logic is a textbook case of weaponized expression. As negotiations over the Strait of Hormuz hit an impasse and power trades fail, he swiftly pivots to a narrative of "morality and billing." By converting decades of soldier deaths and Iranian civilian casualties into a specific compensation figure, he reduces complex structural violence to a commercial haggle. The sinister core here is the attempt to seize the interpretation of the conflict by defining "who owes whom."

The irony is palpable. A man who treats war as a chip and geopolitics as a real estate deal suddenly pretends to care about "victim reparations." This is nothing more than a clumsy PR stunt to project "strength" ahead of the November midterms. The soldiers killed by roadside bombs and the protesters slaughtered by the Iranian regime are completely objectified as bargaining chips. Their lives are assigned a price—not for justice, but to provide Trump with a reason to be "outraged" when his deals fail.

Netanyahu's public break reveals the truth of this complicity: in a masculine-centric narrative, "peace roadmaps" are always secondary. The true optimal expression for these men is "absolute security" and "total destruction." The performance of a "very good" relationship between Trump and Netanyahu is merely a temporary truce between two power players. They are co-conspirators in treating the women and children of the Middle East as mere backdrops for a vanity war over who is "tougher."

Ultimately, these demands for reparations function as cultural violence. They trick the public into believing that the trauma of war can be settled with a check, masking the meta-violence: the colonial logic that certain lives are priceable, exchangeable, and disposable assets on a negotiation table.
