贸易关税是元暴力的粗鄙注脚Tariffs as a Crude Footnote to Meta-Violence

国际 文化层 · 结构层 · 直接层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-24 § 链接
关税威胁不是贸易博弈，而是强权对解释权的暴力强拆。
Tariff threats are not trade games, but a violent demolition of the right to interpret.

特朗普把欧盟对科技巨头的反垄断罚款定义为“抢劫”，这不仅仅是修辞，而是一次典型的武器化表达。他试图通过将“法律监管”重新定义为“刑事抢劫”，夺取对事实的解释权。在这种叙事里，Google等公司的垄断行为被抹除，取而代之的是一个被欺负的“受害者”形象。这正是元暴力的运作方式：用一种更强力的暴力（关税威胁）去覆盖另一种制度性的正义尝试。

Google对此的“欢迎”态度极其讽刺。一个在欧洲通过操纵搜索结果、扼杀竞争来获利的垄断者，在面对一个用暴力威胁来保护它的强权时，竟然表现出了感恩。这就是典型的共谋。科技巨头通过让渡一部分政治忠诚，换取强权在结构层面对其垄断地位的背书。在这种共谋中，所谓的“美国纳税人”成了最廉价的挡箭牌，而实际获益的是那些掌控认知入口的资本巨头。

从加尔通的暴力三角来看，欧盟的Digital Markets Act试图在structural层减少科技巨头对市场的暴力，而特朗普则在cultural层通过“猪猪储蓄罐”这种低幼化的叙事将其污名化，并准备在direct层通过关税实施物理打击。这本质上是一场关于“谁有权制定规则”的存在性战争。当强权决定用关税来代替法律讨论时，它在宣告：在这个世界上，只要你足够强，你就可以定义什么是“公正”，而所有不符合你利益的规则，统统都是“抢劫”。

Trump defining the EU's antitrust fines as 'ROBBING' is not mere rhetoric; it is a textbook example of the weaponisation of expression. By redefining 'legal regulation' as 'criminal robbery,' he attempts to seize the interpretative power over facts. In this narrative, the monopolistic behavior of Google and others is erased, replaced by the image of a persecuted 'victim.' This is exactly how meta-violence operates: using a more blunt force (tariff threats) to overwrite a systemic attempt at justice.

Google's 'appreciation' of this intervention is profoundly ironic. A monopolist that profits by manipulating search results and stifling competition in Europe suddenly feels grateful when a powerhouse uses violent threats to protect it. This is pure complicity. Tech giants trade a portion of their political loyalty for the strongman's endorsement of their structural dominance. In this complicity, the 'American Taxpayer' serves as a cheap shield, while the actual winners are the capital giants controlling the cognitive entries.

Applying Galtung's Violence Triangle, the EU's Digital Markets Act seeks to reduce structural violence against the market. Trump, however, uses cultural violence—via the infantile 'PIGGYBANK' narrative—to stigmatize this effort, preparing to deliver direct violence through tariffs. This is an existential war over 'who defines the rules.' When power decides to replace legal discourse with tariffs, it declares that as long as you are strong enough, you can define 'justice,' and any rule that contradicts your interest is simply 'robbery.'
