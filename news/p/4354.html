被当作“马戏团黑熊”的女性与新闻业的猎杀本能The 'Circus Bear' Treatment: Tabloid Hunting and the Erasure of Female Subjectivity

性别 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-21 § 链接
当权力将女性身体客体化为“猎物”，事实便成了可以随意篡改的装饰品。
When power objectifies the female body as 'prey,' facts become mere ornaments for the hunt.

Daily Mail 这次把一个无辜女性当成 Meg White 跟踪、偷拍并公开，最荒诞的不是认错人，而是他们认定对方是 Meg White 的逻辑：因为这个女人在被冒犯时礼貌地说了声“谢谢”。在男性中心叙事的新闻业逻辑里，女性的“礼貌”被自动翻译成“默认”，而她的主体性在面对这种 stalker 式的报道时，被简化为一种可以被随意解读的信号。这就是典型的 cultural violence：将女性的社交习惯武器化，用来掩盖其结构性的侵犯。

这种 voyeuristic（窥淫癖）的报道方式，本质上是一场关于“认知入口”的强暴。Daily Mail 并不在乎事实，他们在乎的是制造一种“揭秘”的快感。在他们眼中，女性——无论是名声在外的 Meg White 还是那个无辜的路人——都不是拥有隐私权的个体，而是被定价的商品，是供大众围观的“马戏团黑熊”。这种将女性身体客体化的逻辑，正是 meta violence 的具体实践：只要能服务于流量和权力，事实可以通过事后悄悄删除来“修正”，而受害者的精神损耗在他们的 editorial code 里根本没有定价。

最令人作呕的是这种共谋机制。这种 tabloid 习惯于用“公众人物”这个标签作为豁免权，将侵犯隐私合法化。而当他们认错人之后，选择的是“埋掉”而非“道歉”。这种沉默本身就是一种 structural violence，它在告诉所有女性：你的存在是透明的，你的身份是可替换的，而权力者对你的误认和羞辱，不需要付出任何代价。

The Daily Mail's stalking and misidentification of a woman as Meg White isn't just a journalistic fail; it's a manifestation of the hunting instinct inherent in masculine-centric media. The most absurd part is their 'proof' of identity: the woman politely said "thank you" when complimented. In the logic of the male gaze, female politeness is weaponized and translated into 'consent' or 'confirmation.' This is cultural violence in its purest form—reducing a woman's subjectivity to a signal that can be manipulated to justify an intrusion.

This voyeuristic approach is a violent seizure of the cognitive entry point. The Mail doesn't care about the truth; they care about the thrill of the 'reveal.' To them, women—whether a reclusive star or a random citizen—are not subjects with a right to privacy, but objects to be priced and consumed, treated like 'circus bears' for public amusement. This is the execution of meta violence: facts are disposable, and the mental toll on the victim is never factored into the cost of the story.

The complicity here is systemic. Tabloids use the 'public figure' label as a license for violation. By quietly deleting the article instead of issuing a formal apology, the Mail reinforces a structural violence: it signals that a woman's identity is interchangeable and her dignity is negligible. The silence is the final blow, confirming that the power to misidentify and humiliate comes with zero cost.
