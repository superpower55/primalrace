Default Yes 的幻觉与结构性房权的共谋The Illusion of 'Default Yes' and the Complicity of Structural Housing Power

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-17 § 链接
所谓的‘放宽规划’不过是将权力从地方共谋者移交给中央共谋者。
Loosening planning rules is merely shifting power from local complicitors to central ones.

Angela Rayner 宣布的这个 "default yes" 政策，在 PR 叙事里被包装成解决住房危机的救星，但如果用加尔通的暴力三角来看，这不过是一次 structural violence 的权力移交。住房危机本质上是 Potential（本可达到的居住状态）与 Actual（实际居住成本）之间巨大的差额，而这个差额正是由房产资本与规划制度共同制造的暴力。

所谓的“放宽规则”，核心在于将决定权从 local councils 移交给 Whitehall。这在政治博弈中是一次典型的权力重组：地方共谋者（那些受 NIMBY 主义影响、为了维持社区资产价值而阻碍开发的当地权贵）失去了否决权，但权力并没有下放到居民手中，而是被集中到了中央政府。这意味着开发商面对的不再是碎片化的地方阻力，而是一个统一的、更高效的审批入口。这种“效率”的提升，最终会转化为房产资本更快速地收割土地溢价，而非真正降低生活成本。

最讽刺的是那些所谓的“黄金法则”（golden rules），要求在绿带土地上增加 affordable housing。在资本逻辑里，affordable 是一个极其灵活的 weaponized concept。只要定义权在开发者和政府手中，所谓的“可负担”永远会高于底层民众的实际支付能力。这是一种典型的假.最优解表达：通过扮演“关心底层”的角色获得政策合法性，代价是让主体性被进一步剥夺的租房者在更高的房价中继续挣扎。

当政府宣布通过 AI 增长区和数据中心来带动基础设施时，他们实际上在定义一种新的“真实”：即生产力增长等于生活质量提升。但对于被 priced out 的年轻人来说，住在车站 10 分钟路程内并不意味着生活质量的提高，而意味着他们被更紧凑地地塞进了资本的通勤机器里，成为了一个更高效的生物电池。

Angela Rayner's "default yes" policy is packaged as a savior for the housing crisis, but through the lens of the Violence Triangle, it is nothing more than a transfer of structural violence. The housing crisis is the massive gap between Potential and Actual living conditions—a gap manufactured by the complicity of real estate capital and planning regimes.

The essence of this "loosening" is the migration of decision-making power from local councils to Whitehall. In the game of power, this is a strategic restructuring: local complicitors (the NIMBY elites protecting their asset values) lose their veto, but the power doesn't descend to the residents; it ascends to the center. For developers, this means replacing fragmented local resistance with a single, more efficient entry point. This "efficiency" will inevitably translate into faster land-value capture for capital, not lower costs for the people.

The so-called "golden rules" regarding affordable housing on green belt land are a classic example of a weaponized concept. As long as the definition of "affordable" remains with developers and the state, it will always exceed the actual payment capacity of the underclass. This is a fake optimal expression: playing the role of the "benevolent provider" to gain political legitimacy, while the displaced subjects continue to struggle in a higher-priced market.

By linking AI growth zones and data centers to infrastructure, the government is manufacturing a new reality: that productivity growth equals improved living standards. But for those priced out, living within 10 minutes of a station doesn't mean a better life; it means being more compactly packed into the machinery of capital, becoming a more efficient biological battery for the commute.
