用“事实”的定义权完成最后一次清场The Final Clearing: Monopolizing the Definition of Truth

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-18 § 链接
权力不通过杀死记者来消灭真相，而通过垄断“事实”的定义权来制造真实。
Power doesn't kill journalists to erase truth; it monopolizes the definition of 'fact' to manufacture reality.

特朗普这次禁令的本质不是在打击“Fake News”，而是在执行一次教科书级别的认知入口（Cognitive Entry）清理。当一个统治者开始定义什么是“小说” (Fiction) 和“谎言” (Lies) 时，他实际上是在宣布：在这个物理空间内，只有我的表达才是唯一合法的事实，其余的一切表达都是对现实的篡改。

这是一种典型的武器化表达。通过将 CNN、Politico 等媒体标记为“虚假”，他不仅在物理上切断了这些机构的访问权，更在文化层面上为未来的审查建立了合法性。注意那个细节：美联社因为使用了“墨西哥湾”而非“美国湾”而被禁。这根本不是地理之争，而是一次关于“命名权”的权力博弈。谁拥有定义地理、定义事实的权力，谁就拥有了对现实的定价权。

这背后是极其稳固的共谋机制。当总统在 Truth Social 上大喊“Fake News”时，他是在向其支持者发送信号：所有不符合我们叙事的真相都是敌人的武器。这种元暴力（Meta Violence）将复杂的政治博弈简化为“忠诚 vs 谎言”的二元对立，使得任何试图维持客观性的尝试都被自动归类为“共谋地狱”的一部分。

禁令本身只是 structural violence 的表象，真正的暴力发生在 cultural 层：当公众习惯于通过统治者的心情来判断新闻的真伪时，真相就从一个客观存在变成了权力的附属品。这一次，他不是在禁掉几个媒体，而是在禁掉“独立观察”这种可能性。

Trump's latest ban is not a crusade against 'Fake News,' but a textbook execution of Cognitive Entry clearance. When a ruler begins defining what constitutes 'Fiction' and 'Lies,' he is effectively declaring that within this physical and political space, only his expression is the legitimate fact, and all other expressions are distortions of reality.

This is the weaponisation of expression in its purest form. By labeling CNN and Politico as 'fake,' he isn't just cutting off physical access; he is establishing the cultural legitimacy for future censorship. The detail about the Associated Press being banned for using 'Gulf of Mexico' instead of 'Gulf of America' is telling. This isn't about geography; it's a power struggle over the right to name. Whoever controls the naming of the world controls the pricing of reality.

Underlying this is a rigid mechanism of complicity. When the president screams 'Fake News' on Truth Social, he signals to his base that any truth contradicting the narrative is a weapon of the enemy. This meta-violence simplifies complex political discourse into a binary of 'Loyalty vs. Lies,' automatically categorizing any attempt at objectivity as part of a hostile conspiracy.

The ban is merely the surface of structural violence. The real violence occurs at the cultural layer: when the public becomes accustomed to judging the truth of news based on the ruler's mood, truth ceases to be an objective existence and becomes a subsidiary of power. He isn't just banning outlets; he is banning the very possibility of independent observation.
