合作模式没了，童话还在Fable Drops Co-op, and the Fairy Tale Gets Smaller

文娱 结构层 Polygon.com ↗ 2026-11-01 § 链接
新闻摘要Xbox 即将推出的《神鬼寓言》重启版将由 Playground Games 开发，定于明年二月发售，官方 FAQ 确认本作为纯单人游戏，不再包含系列前作《神鬼寓言 2》《神鬼寓言 3》的合作模式。
The newsXbox's upcoming Fable reboot, developed by Playground Games and due next February, will be a purely single-player game, per an official FAQ, dropping the co-op mode featured in Fable 2 and Fable 3.
砍掉本地双人，就是把游戏的表达收窄成一个人的独白
Cutting local co-op turns a shared story into a solo monologue.

《Fable》重启版砍掉了本地合作模式，这条新闻最值得看的不是“少了一个功能”，而是这个系列最初靠什么成立。原作《Fable》的卖点是道德系统：你做了什么，长相会变，NPC 对你的反应也会变。到了《Fable 2》和《Fable 3》，合作模式让第二个人进入这个世界，两个玩家一起承担选择的后果。《Fable 3》后半段那个“Weight of the World”，一个人玩就是自己拍板，两个人玩就变成真正的道德辩论——要不要恢复童工、要不要把避难所改成妓院换钱，这些选择在有人跟你争的时候才有重量。

所以这不是“少了一个便利功能”，是少了一个让人被迫和另一个人协商的入口。Polygon 那篇文章写得很清楚：作者和一位朋友在疫情初期靠《Fable 2》《Fable 3》撑过一段日子，存档被 Xbox One 抹掉一次，第二个周六从头再来。这段关系后来延伸到现实——那位朋友明年要主持他的婚礼。合作模式在这里不是卖点，是两个人共同经历一件事的载体。

Playground Games 在 FAQ 里说这作是纯单人。这话本身没问题，问题在于它发生在本地合作被 AAA 工作室整体降级的时间点上。同一篇文章里提到，The Coalition 说《Gears of War: E-Day》的本地合作是“不可谈判”的，《Halo: Campaign Evolved》也带着本地合作发售。也就是说，不是技术做不到，是优先级排到了后面。

这里没有谁在施暴，但结构层在动：一个让两个人在同一张沙发上争论“要不要恢复童工”的入口被关掉了，换成一个更干净、更容易控制节奏的单人体验。玩家不会因此少玩一个游戏，但会少一次被迫和另一个人协商的机会。而协商这件事，恰恰是《Fable》系列最初那套道德系统真正在训练的东西。

那篇文章的作者说，他和朋友从 2020 年公布重启时就等着一起玩，现在只能接受。但他补了一句：那位朋友明年要主持他的婚礼。童话里的“happily ever after”从来不是一个人写完的。

The Fable reboot dropping local co-op isn't a story about a missing feature. It's a story about what the series was built on. The original Fable's hook was its morality system: your actions changed your appearance, and the citizens of Albion reacted accordingly. Fable 2 and Fable 3 added co-op, and that let a second player step into the same world and share the consequences. In Fable 3's "Weight of the World," playing solo meant you just made the choices you wanted. Playing with a friend turned it into an actual moral debate.

So this isn't a convenience being removed. It's an entry point for forced negotiation with another person being closed. The Polygon piece lays it out: the author and a friend leaned on Fable 2 and Fable 3 during the early pandemic, lost a save file to Xbox One's fickle cloud data, and started over the next Saturday. That friendship now extends to the friend officiating his wedding next year. Co-op wasn't a selling point there. It was the container for a shared experience.

Playground Games said in an FAQ that this entry is purely single-player. That's fine on its own. What makes it stand out is the timing: local co-op is being deprioritized across AAA. The same piece notes that The Coalition called local co-op non-negotiable for Gears of War: E-Day, and Halo: Campaign Evolved launched with it. So it's not that the technology can't do it. It's that the priority sits lower.

No one is being harmed here, but the structural layer is moving: an entry point that made two people on one couch argue about whether to reinstate child labor is being closed, replaced by a cleaner, more controlled solo experience. Players won't play one fewer game. They'll have one fewer occasion to be forced into negotiation with another person. And negotiation is exactly what Fable's original morality system was training.

The author says he and his friend had been waiting to play the reboot together since it was revealed in 2020, and now they're bummed. But he adds: the friend is officiating his wedding next year. The happily ever after in a fairy tale was never written by one person.
