用文化战争掩盖结构性掠夺的 scamA Scam of Culture War Masking Structural Plunder

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-05 § 链接
气候否认论不是科学争论，而是通过制造认知混乱来维持掠夺权的武器化叙事。
Climate denial is not a scientific debate, but a weaponized narrative designed to maintain plunder by manufacturing cognitive chaos.

Reform UK 的这场大会是一次典型的 weaponized expression 现场。Richard Tice 和 Farage 们在台上贩卖“Net Stupid Zero”的笑话，试图将气候科学这个客观事实转化为一场 Culture War。这套操作的本质是夺取解释权：通过把科学共识定义为“精英的谎言”或“政治正确”，他们成功地为支持者建立了一套认知入口，让人们在嘲笑“亚特兰蒂斯”的同时，心安理得地忽略正在发生的结构性暴力。

最讽刺的共谋发生在支持者内部。这些 delegate 们一边用毫无科学根据的论点（比如冰河时代二氧化碳更高）来否认气候危机，一边又在抱怨政府没有提供足够的空调、没有保护农民、没有改善新房的通风。这是一种极其扭曲的逻辑：他们承认 Actual（实际生活质量）在下降，但拒绝承认导致这个差额的 Potential（气候科学）原因。他们想要 adaptation（适应），却反对 mitigation（减排），这在逻辑上是 incoherent 的，但在政治博弈中却是最优解——因为这样他们既能维持对“体制”的愤怒，又不需要放弃由化石燃料支撑的既得利益结构。

这种认知混乱不是偶然，而是被精心制造的。当右翼政党将气候议题转化为身份政治的一部分时，他们实际上是在通过文化层面的暴力（cultural violence）来合法化结构层面的掠夺。他们让支持者相信，承认气候变化等同于背叛自己的阶级或身份。在这种叙事下，科学变成了“敌方”的武器，而否认则成了某种“觉醒”的表达。这不仅是对地球的暴力，更是对底层民众认知能力的殖民。

The Reform UK conference is a textbook display of weaponized expression. Richard Tice and Farage are peddling jokes about "Net Stupid Zero," attempting to transmute climate science—an objective fact—into a Culture War. The essence of this operation is the seizure of the right to interpret: by defining scientific consensus as "elite lies" or "political correctness," they establish a cognitive entry point for their supporters, allowing them to mock "Atlantis" while blissfully ignoring the ongoing structural violence.

The most ironic complicity occurs among the supporters. These delegates use scientifically baseless arguments (such as CO2 levels during ice ages) to deny the climate crisis, while simultaneously complaining that the government fails to provide enough air conditioning or protect farmers. This is a distorted logic: they acknowledge that the Actual (quality of life) is declining, but refuse to acknowledge the Potential (climate science) cause of this gap. They demand adaptation but oppose mitigation—incoherent logically, yet an optimal expression in political gaming, as it allows them to maintain anger toward "the system" without relinquishing the vested interests supported by fossil fuels.

This cognitive chaos is not accidental; it is manufactured. When right-wing parties turn climate issues into identity politics, they use cultural violence to legitimize structural plunder. They convince supporters that acknowledging climate change is equivalent to betraying their class or identity. In this narrative, science is framed as the enemy's weapon, and denial becomes a form of "awakening." This is not only violence against the planet but a colonization of the cognitive capacity of the working class.
