所谓的“入侵物种”与被掩盖的结构性怠政The "Invasive Species" Scam and Structural Negligence

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-05 § 链接
当政府将生态危机转化为“志愿者游戏”时，它在通过共谋掩盖结构性失职。
When government turns ecological crisis into a "volunteer game," it uses complicity to mask structural failure.

这篇报道在讲述一个典型的“生态危机”叙事：亚洲胡蜂（Asian hornets）入侵，威胁蜜蜂，威胁粮食安全，于是养蜂人呼吁政府加大宣传。但如果用 Violence = Potential − Actual 的公式来看，这里存在一个巨大的 structural violence 差额。英国政府早在 2011 年就完成了风险评估并制定了应急计划，这意味着他们已经掌握了认知入口，知道 Potential 的威胁是什么，但 Actual 的行动却极其滑稽——依赖于 3 万次公众举报来寻找 24 个巢穴。

这种机制是极其典型的“志愿者共谋”。政府通过将一个国家级的生物安全问题定义为“养蜂人的议题”或“公众参与的成就”，成功地将行政责任外包给了志愿者和公民。当 Lucie Chaumeton 赞美国家蜜蜂单位（NBU）的响应“极其有效”时，她实际上在无意识中成为了这套低效结构的共谋者。这种“有效”是建立在极低成本的志愿者劳动力之上的，而政府则在其中扮演一个偶尔提供 VespAI 或无线电发射器等“科技点缀”的慷慨角色。

最讽刺的是，政府在 2025 年摧毁了“纪录数量”的 161 个巢穴，却被当作胜利来报道。在生物入侵的博弈中，纪录数量的增加往往意味着防控系统的全面溃败，而非执行力的提升。这种将“失败的规模”包装成“努力的证明”的叙事，正是典型的 weaponized expression。它用一种勤勉的假象，掩盖了从 2011 年至今长达十五年的结构性怠政。

This report follows a classic "ecological crisis" narrative: Asian hornets invade, threaten bees and food security, and beekeepers call for government awareness. But applying the formula Violence = Potential − Actual reveals a massive gap of structural violence. The UK government completed its risk assessment back in 2011, meaning they held the cognitive entry point and knew the potential threat, yet the actual response is farcical—relying on 30,000 public reports to find a mere 24 nests.

This mechanism is a textbook case of "volunteer complicity." By defining a national biosecurity threat as a "beekeeping issue" or a "public participation achievement," the government successfully outsources administrative responsibility to volunteers and citizens. When Lucie Chaumeton praises the National Bee Unit's (NBU) response as "extremely effective," she unconsciously becomes a co-conspirator in this inefficient structure. This "effectiveness" is built on the zero-cost labor of volunteers, while the government plays the role of a generous benefactor, adding "tech flourishes" like VespAI or radio transmitters.

Most ironic is the reporting of the "record" 161 nests destroyed in 2025 as a victory. In the game of biological invasion, a record number of findings usually signifies a total collapse of the containment system, not an increase in efficiency. Packaging the "scale of failure" as "proof of effort" is a prime example of weaponized expression. It uses a facade of diligence to mask fifteen years of structural negligence since 2011.
