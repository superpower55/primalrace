国家安全法：一个名为“保护”的认知入口National Security Act: A Cognitive Entry Point Named 'Protection'

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-09 § 链接
所有的“国家安全”叙事，本质上都是权力在定义谁是敌我。
Every 'National Security' narrative is essentially power defining the boundary between friend and enemy.

这又是一次典型的 weaponized 表达。一个 31 岁的男人因为涉嫌协助 GRU 被捕，新闻的重点被刻意引导向了 2023 年出台的《国家安全法》（National Security Act）。在这种叙事里，法律被描绘成一个为了“保护”公民免受间谍和破坏活动侵害的盾牌。但我们需要追问的是：谁在定义什么是“威胁”？谁在掌控这个认知入口？

从加尔通的暴力三角来看，这属于典型的 structural violence。法律不是中立的工具，而是权力用来划定边界的武器。当一个国家宣布通过新法来“增加执法措施”时，它实际上是在扩大其解释权——将原本模糊的“干扰”或“影响”定义为刑事犯罪。这是一种元暴力的延伸：通过垄断对“安全”的定义，国家得以合法地在结构层面上清除异己或制造恐慌。

最讽刺的是 CPS 检察官那段标准的 PR 话术：强调“公共利益”并警告不要在网上分享可能影响审判的信息。这不仅是在维护法治，更是在构建一个封闭的认知黑盒。当“国家安全”成为最高优先级，任何试图拆解这套叙事的尝试都会被标记为“危害安全”。

我们看到的不是一个简单的间谍案，而是一次关于“什么是事实”的制造权演习。当权力通过法律将特定行为定义为 sabotage，它就完成了从文化层到结构层的暴力闭环。在这种闭环里，个体的存在性被简化为一个被指控的标签，而真正的博弈发生在那些制定规则的人手中。

Another classic case of weaponized expression. A 31-year-old man is arrested for alleged GRU ties, but the narrative is carefully steered toward the 2023 National Security Act. In this framing, the law is depicted as a shield 'protecting' citizens from espionage and sabotage. But the real question is: who defines the 'threat'? Who controls this cognitive entry point?

Through the lens of the Violence Triangle, this is structural violence. The law is not a neutral tool; it is a weapon used by power to draw boundaries. When a state claims to 'increase law enforcement measures,' it is actually expanding its monopoly on interpretation—redefining vague 'interference' or 'influence' as criminal acts. This is an extension of meta-violence: by monopolizing the definition of 'security,' the state legitimizes the removal of dissent or the manufacture of fear at a structural level.

Most ironic is the standard PR script from the CPS: emphasizing 'public interest' and warning against online commentary. This isn't just about due process; it's about constructing a closed cognitive black box. When 'National Security' becomes the ultimate priority, any attempt to dismantle the narrative is branded as a threat to safety.

What we are witnessing is not a simple spy case, but an exercise in the manufacture of reality. When power uses the law to define certain behaviors as sabotage, it completes the loop of violence from the cultural to the structural layer. In this loop, an individual's existence is reduced to a charged label, while the real game is played by those who write the rules.
