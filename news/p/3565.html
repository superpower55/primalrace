用“审查”掩盖的结构性谋杀Structural Murder Masked as 'Review'

哲学 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-10 § 链接
当制度将“不确定性”武器化，审查就成了维持暴力的共谋手段。
When the state weaponizes uncertainty, 'reviews' become mere tools of complicity to sustain violence.

IPP（公众保护监禁）不是一种法律判决，而是一种纯粹的心理武器。它最阴毒的地方在于制造了一个没有终点的 Potential，让 Actual 永远无法抵达。一个人因为偷花盆或偷车而被无限期囚禁，这种“不确定性”本身就是一种 direct violence，它通过剥夺时间的主权，直接将囚犯推向自杀。

最令人作呕的是 Andy Burnham 提出的所谓“新审查”（new review）。在法律界、人权组织甚至该制度的发明者都承认这是个 scam 之后，政府依然在玩弄“审查”这个认知入口。在权力博弈中，审查往往不是为了寻找答案，而是为了延迟执行。每一次 review 都是在给 structural violence 刷新的漆，用一种“理性的、程序正义的”伪装，掩盖其本质上的残酷：政治人物害怕被贴上“对犯罪软弱”的标签。

这是一个典型的共谋场域。政客们在公共空间表演“关注”，在私下里通过阻挠立法来确保自己的政治资本不缩水。他们共谋维持一个即便被定义为“污点”的制度，因为这个污点能给他们带来选票。在这种元暴力的逻辑下，囚犯的生命被量化成了政治博弈中的筹码，而那些被牺牲的生命，在权力者的叙事里只是“风险管理”的代价。

IPP is not a legal sentence; it is a pure psychological weapon. Its most sinister mechanism is the manufacturing of an unreachable Potential, ensuring the Actual never arrives. Indefinite detention for stealing a flowerpot or a car is direct violence—it seizes the sovereignty of time and pushes the prisoner toward suicide.

What is truly nauseating is Andy Burnham's proposed 'new review.' After the legal community, human rights groups, and even the architect of the system admitted it was a scam, the government continues to manipulate the 'review' as a cognitive entry point. In the game of power, a review is rarely about finding answers; it is about delaying execution. Every review is simply a fresh coat of paint on structural violence, using a facade of 'rationality' and 'due process' to mask a brutal truth: politicians are terrified of appearing 'soft on crime.'

This is a classic field of complicity. Politicians perform 'concern' in the public square while privately blocking legislation to protect their political capital. They conspire to maintain a system acknowledged as a 'stain' because that stain buys them votes. Under this meta-violence, human lives are quantified as chips in a political gamble, and the resulting deaths are dismissed as the mere cost of 'risk management.'
