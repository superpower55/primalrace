法拉奇的“移民烟雾弹”：权力的低级掩护术Farage's Immigration Smoke Screen: The Low-Tier Art of Power Cover-ups

国际 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-03 § 链接
用外部敌人的恐惧，掩盖内部共谋的崩塌。
Using the fear of external enemies to mask the collapse of internal complicity.

法拉奇（Nigel Farage）试图用一套重新包装的移民计划来转移视线，这在政治博弈中是极其典型的“认知入口”操纵。当 Reform UK 内部陷入权力斗争，当副手 Tice 被监管机构调查时，他迅速启动了那套最廉价的武器化叙事：制造一个外部的、非我族类的“威胁”，从而强行把公众的注意力从内部的烂摊子拉向一个虚构的战场。

这不仅是政治操弄，更是对文化暴力（cultural violence）的惯用手段。通过定义谁是“入侵者”，谁是“异类”，法拉奇在试图重建一种极端的身份政治。他不需要移民计划真的有效，他只需要这个计划能够激起受众的恐惧与排他本能，从而让支持者在情绪波动中忽略掉权力顶层的共谋崩塌。

这种叙事逻辑极其卑劣：将复杂的结构性社会问题简化为“我们 vs 他们”的二元对立。在这种逻辑里，任何对法拉奇内部混乱的质疑都会被贴上“不爱国”或“被建制派洗脑”的标签。这本质上是一场关于解释权的战争，他试图通过制造一个更大的“混乱”假象，来掩盖他自己才是那个制造混乱的源头。

Nigel Farage's attempt to deflect attention with a rehashed immigration plan is a textbook manipulation of the cognitive entry point. As Reform UK dissolves into internal strife and Richard Tice faces watchdog investigations, Farage activates the cheapest weaponized narrative available: manufacturing an external, 'alien' threat to drag the public's gaze away from the internal wreckage.

This is more than mere political maneuvering; it is a deployment of cultural violence. By defining who the 'invaders' and 'others' are, Farage is attempting to reconstruct an extreme form of identity politics. He doesn't need the immigration plan to be functional; he only needs it to trigger the fear and exclusionary instincts of his base, ensuring that supporters ignore the collapse of complicity at the top of the party.

The logic is predatory: reducing complex structural social issues to a binary 'Us vs Them'. In this framework, any critique of Farage's internal chaos is easily dismissed as 'unpatriotic' or 'establishment propaganda'. It is a war over the right of interpretation—he creates a fake macro-chaos to hide the fact that he is the primary source of the actual chaos.
