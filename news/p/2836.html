酒精特权与被抹除的社区真实Alcohol Privilege and the Erasure of Community Truth

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-24 § 链接
所谓的“社区支持”不过是权力者在定义谁才配叫社区。
So-called "community support" is just the powerful defining who deserves to be called a community.

这就是典型的 structural violence。Andy Burnham 宣布的 1 亿英镑减税计划，精准地将资源投喂给了酒吧、社交俱乐部和现场音乐场馆，而把咖啡馆排除在外。在权力者的逻辑里，只有售卖酒精的场所才被定义为“社区枢纽”。这种 artificial distinction 并不是基于经济压力，而是一场关于“什么是正统社交空间”的定义权博弈。

最讽刺的是，像 Dialogue Cafe 这样通过雇佣听障人士来减轻政府社会负担的 social enterprise，在权力者眼中竟然不具备被救济的价值。这种 exclusion 揭示了一个残酷的事实：政府所谓的“支持社区”，本质上是在支持一种符合男性中心叙事的、以酒精和喧闹为标志的公共空间。咖啡馆作为一种更安静、更包容、且往往承载更多女性与边缘群体社交需求的场所，在元暴力的筛选机制下被判定为“次要”的 retail business。

这种资源分配的偏差，实际上是在通过经济杠杆强行修剪社区的生态。当酒吧因为补贴而能够 undercut 咖啡馆的价格时，政府实际上在通过structural层面的操纵，逼迫人们离开包容性的空间，回归到那个由酒精定义、由男性权力逻辑主导的旧式枢纽中。这根本不是什么经济决策，而是一次潜意识的文化清洗。

This is a textbook case of structural violence. Andy Burnham’s £100m business rates cut precisely feeds pubs, social clubs, and music venues, while excluding cafes. In the logic of power, only venues selling alcohol are defined as "community hubs." This artificial distinction isn't based on commercial pressure, but is a game of defining the "legitimate" social space.

The irony is that social enterprises like Dialogue Cafe, which actively reduce government burdens by employing deaf baristas, are deemed unworthy of relief. This exclusion reveals a grim truth: the government's "support for communities" is actually support for a masculine-centric narrative of public space—one marked by alcohol and noise. Cafes, which are quieter, more inclusive, and often host the social needs of women and marginalized groups, are categorized as "secondary" retail businesses under the filter of meta-violence.

This skewed resource allocation is an attempt to prune the community ecosystem via economic levers. When pubs can undercut cafes due to subsidies, the state is structurally manipulating people away from inclusive spaces and back into old-fashioned hubs governed by alcohol and masculine power logic. This is not an economic decision; it is a subconscious cultural purge.
