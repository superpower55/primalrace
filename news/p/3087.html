社会处方：被医疗化包装的生存自救Social Prescribing: The Medicalization of Survival

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-31 § 链接
所谓的“社会处方”是结构性暴力在文化层面的温情补偿。
Social prescribing is a tender compensation for structural violence at the cultural layer.

这篇报道在兜售一种名为“社会处方”(social prescribing)的温情叙事：把游泳、绘画、自然接触包装成医生的处方，试图通过个体爱好的“内驱力”来对冲抑郁和焦虑。在 Violence = Potential − Actual 的公式里，这看起来是在缩小差额，但实际上它在进行一场极其精巧的 cultural violence。它将一个人的精神崩溃归结为“缺乏爱好”或“缺乏正念”，从而把结构性暴力导致的创伤，转化为一种可以通过“尝试新事物”来解决的个人健康管理问题。

注意文中提到的 Amanda：她失去了母亲、丈夫和工作。这是一个典型的结构性崩塌。但医疗系统给她的解决方案不是去解决导致她崩溃的社会资源匮乏或支持系统缺失，而是给她开了一张去海里游泳的“处方”。当一个人的主体性被剥夺、生活在螺旋下降时，系统告诉她：“去游泳吧，你会感到灵魂清新。”这种叙事极其危险，它在用“身心健康”的词汇掩盖对 structural violence 的逃避。它把生存的绝望降级为一种“症状”，把自救的本能包装成一种医疗产品。

真正的 good_news 应该是：一个陷入绝望的女性在社会支持体系的保障下，无需担心成本和交通，能够体面地重新获得生存资源。而现在的“社会处方”更像是一种 PR 版本的救济：它承认了人们需要社群和自然，但它依然维持着“医生-病人”的权力等级，让个体在一个被定义好的“健康路径”里寻找慰藉。这本质上是元暴力的变体——它定义了什么是“正常的恢复”，并引导你通过扮演一个“热爱生活的人”来完成这次修复。

如果一个人因为被剥削而抑郁，那么最好的处方不是教她如何呼吸和浮潜，而是把剥削她的结构拆掉。在没有触及结构性改变之前，所有的“正念”和“爱好”都只是给囚笼刷了一层淡色的油漆。

This report peddles a heartwarming narrative called "social prescribing": packaging swimming, painting, and nature as medical prescriptions to counter depression and anxiety. In the formula Violence = Potential − Actual, this seems to close the gap, but it is actually a sophisticated form of cultural violence. By attributing mental collapse to a "lack of hobbies" or "mindfulness," it transforms trauma caused by structural violence into a personal health management issue solvable by "trying new things."

Look at Amanda: she lost her mother, husband, and job. This is a classic structural collapse. Yet, the medical system's solution isn't to address the systemic resource scarcity or the lack of support that caused her breakdown, but to give her a "prescription" for sea swimming. When a person's subjectivity is stripped and their life is spiraling, the system tells them: "Go swimming, you'll feel refreshed." This narrative is perilous; it uses the language of "wellness" to mask an evasion of structural violence. It downgrades existential despair into a "symptom" and packages the instinct for survival as a medical product.

True good_news would be a woman in despair regaining her life through a social support system that guarantees resources without worrying about cost or transport. Instead, "social prescribing" is a PR version of relief: it acknowledges the need for community and nature, yet maintains the power hierarchy of "doctor-patient," guiding the individual toward a predefined "healthy path." This is a variant of meta-violence—it defines what "normal recovery" looks like and encourages you to perform the role of a "life-lover" to achieve this repair.

If someone is depressed because they are being exploited, the best prescription is not teaching them how to breathe or float, but dismantling the structure of exploitation. Without structural change, all "mindfulness" and "hobbies" are merely a fresh coat of paint on the walls of a cage.
