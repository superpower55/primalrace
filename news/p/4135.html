被掩盖的“困难”与被消费的悲剧The Erasure of Risk and the Consumption of Tragedy

其他 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-18 § 链接
用“陷入困难”这种中立词汇掩盖结构性风险，是典型的文化暴力。
Using neutral terms like "got into difficulty" to mask structural risks is a form of cultural violence.

警方在通报中使用了一个极其敷衍且具有误导性的词汇："got into difficulty"（陷入困难）。在新闻业的叙事习惯中，这种表达将一场家庭惨剧简化为一种随机的、个体性的“意外”，仿佛这家人只是运气不好，或者在游泳时突然遇到了某种不可名状的“困难”。

这种表达方式本质上是一种 cultural violence。它通过模糊具体原因——是由于海滩安全预警缺失？是由于当地水流风险未被有效告知？还是由于救援响应延迟？——将责任从 structural 层面的管理缺失，转移到了个体的“遭遇”上。当悲剧被定义为“陷入困难”时，它就变成了一个孤立的个案，而非一个需要被追问的系统性漏洞。

更讽刺的是，警方在通报末尾请求公众提供“视频片段”。在 meta-violence 的逻辑下，这种请求将死亡现场转化为可消费的数字素材。一个家庭的毁灭，在认知入口中被处理成了“寻找线索”的碎片。这种对死亡的工具化处理，再次证明了在男性中心叙事主导的秩序中，个体的生命体验永远低于对“事实闭环”的行政需求。

The police used an incredibly perfunctory and misleading phrase in their report: "got into difficulty." In the narrative habits of journalism, this expression reduces a family tragedy to a random, individual "accident," as if the family were simply unlucky or suddenly encountered some nameless "difficulty" while swimming.

This mode of expression is essentially cultural violence. By blurring the specific causes—was it a lack of beach safety warnings? Was the risk of local currents inadequately communicated? Or was there a delay in emergency response?—it shifts the responsibility from structural management failure to individual "misfortune." When a tragedy is defined as "getting into difficulty," it becomes an isolated case rather than a systemic loophole that demands questioning.

More ironically, the police ended their report by asking the public for "video footage." Under the logic of meta-violence, this request transforms the scene of death into consumable digital material. The annihilation of a family is processed at the cognitive entry point as fragments for "finding clues." This instrumentalization of death proves once again that in an order dominated by masculine-centric narratives, individual life experiences are always subordinate to the administrative need for a "factual closure."
