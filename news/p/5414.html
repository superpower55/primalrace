肉丸的碎尸案与中产阶级的表演性劳动The Mutilation of Meatballs and Middle-Class Performative Labor

哲学 文化层 The New York Times ↗ 2026-10-04 § 链接
无意义的复杂化是权力对主体性的模拟，也是一种文化暴力。
Meaningless complexity is a simulation of power and a form of cultural violence.

NYT 这篇 Lasagna 食谱里藏着一个极其荒诞的逻辑：要求你先费劲地把肉揉成肉丸，煎好，然后再把它们切碎放入面片之间。这在烹饪逻辑上是完全冗余的，但在文化表达上却是极其精准的。这种“为了碎而圆”的步骤，就是典型的表演性劳动 (performative labor)。

在很多中产阶级的认知入口里，一件事情的“价值”不取决于结果的 Actual 状态，而取决于你投入了多少看似“专业”且“繁琐”的 Process。这种对复杂性的崇拜，本质上是一种对阶级品味的筛选。它在暗示：真正的“高级”不是高效地获得美味，而是拥有能够浪费在无意义步骤上的时间与精力。这就是一种武器化的审美，通过制造不必要的门槛来区分谁才是那个“懂得生活”的精英。

更讽刺的是，评论区里有人在追问 authentic（正宗）。但在这个被资本和媒体定义的事实制造权面前，所谓的“正宗”早已成了可被随意修改的标签。当你习惯于在 NYT 这种权力机构提供的食谱里寻找“正确”的表达方式时，你其实已经进入了一场关于“谁定义了美味”的共谋。你投入的每一分钟冗余劳动，都是在为这种元暴力的叙事投票：即认为“复杂”等同于“高级”，而“高效”等同于“低俗”。

这不仅仅是一盘面，这是一场关于认知入口的 scam。它让你在自我感动的劳作中，完成了对一种虚假品味的内化。

There is a profoundly absurd logic hidden in this NYT lasagna recipe: it asks you to laboriously shape meat into meatballs, brown them, and then chop them up to layer them. In culinary logic, this is entirely redundant; in cultural expression, it is surgically precise. This "rounding only to shred" process is a textbook example of performative labor.

In the cognitive entrance of the middle class, the "value" of an act is not determined by the actual result, but by how much "professional" and "tedious" process is invested. This worship of complexity is essentially a filter for class taste. It suggests that true "sophistication" is not about efficiently achieving flavor, but about possessing the time and energy to waste on meaningless steps. This is a weaponized aesthetic, using unnecessary thresholds to distinguish who the "connoisseurs" of life truly are.

More ironically, commenters are chasing the "authentic." But in the face of the power to manufacture facts held by capital and media, "authenticity" has become a label to be manipulated. When you seek the "correct" expression in a recipe provided by a power structure like the NYT, you have entered a complicity regarding "who defines taste." Every minute of redundant labor you invest is a vote for a meta-violence narrative: the belief that "complexity" equals "high-class," while "efficiency" equals "vulgarity."

This is not just a pasta dish; it is a scam of the cognitive entrance. It leads you to internalize a fake taste through a process of self-indulgent toil.
