ビーチの照片与权力的猎场Beach Photos and the Hunting Ground of Power

性别 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-15 § 链接
用‘自愿’掩盖剥削，是元暴力最常见的PR手段
Using 'consent' to mask exploitation is the most common PR tactic of meta-violence

Matt Gaetz 的回应简直是一次教科书级的 meta violence 演示。面对人口贩卖和性剥削的指控，他没有在事实层面进行有效辩护，而是甩出一张海滩照片，用 #beers 和 #babes 这种典型的 masculine-centric 叙事试图将一场权力的掠夺重构为一场‘自愿的派对’。

这正是典型的武器化表达：通过截取一个碎片化的瞬间（照片），试图覆盖掉整个结构性的压迫过程。在 Gaetz 的逻辑里，只要受害者在社交媒体上表现得‘开心’，那么之前的经济胁迫、药物控制和权力不对等就全部消失了。这是一种极其恶劣的 scam，它试图告诉世界：只要女性在被消费时笑了，这种消费就是公正的。

事实上，这起案件精准地揭示了结构暴力 (structural violence) 是如何运作的。被告利用公职、财富和权力，精准猎杀那些刚高中毕业、没有储蓄、处于经济极度脆弱状态的年轻女性。这种‘经济漏洞’就是猎人的入口。当一个掌握国家权力的男人告诉一个无产阶级女孩他能‘解决她的财务问题’时，这根本不是什么‘交易’，而是一次基于生存焦虑的强迫。

最讽刺的是，Gaetz 试图通过质疑受害者的‘叙事一致性’来洗白自己。但我们要追问的是：在一个由男性垄断解释权的结构中，一个被剥削的女性在社交媒体上维持‘体面’的假象，往往是她为了生存而采取的假.最优解表达。而现在，这个假象被 Gaetz 拿来当作攻击她的武器。这不仅是法律上的博弈，更是存在性战争的残酷延续——施暴者在利用受害者的生存策略来完成最后的闭环共谋。

Matt Gaetz's response is a textbook demonstration of meta-violence. Facing allegations of sex trafficking and exploitation, he avoids factual defense and instead throws out a beach photo, using a typical masculine-centric narrative of #beers and #babes to reconstruct a predatory power grab as a 'voluntary party.'

This is the weaponisation of expression: using a fragmented moment (a photo) to overwrite a structural process of oppression. In Gaetz's logic, as long as the victims appeared 'happy' on social media, the prior economic coercion, drug control, and power imbalance vanish. It is a vile scam, attempting to convince the world that consumption is just, provided the woman smiles while being consumed.

In reality, this case precisely reveals how structural violence operates. The defendants used public office, wealth, and power to prey on young women—recent high school graduates with no savings and extreme economic vulnerability. This 'economic gap' is the hunter's entry point. When a man holding state power tells a proletarian girl he can 'solve her financial problems,' it is not a 'transaction,' but a coercion based on survival anxiety.

Most ironically, Gaetz attempts to whitewash himself by questioning the 'consistency' of the victims' narratives. But the real question is: in a structure where the masculine-centric narrative monopolizes the right to interpret, a victim maintaining a facade of 'decency' on social media is often a fake optimal expression adopted for survival. Now, this facade is weaponised by Gaetz to attack her. This is not just a legal battle; it is a brutal continuation of the existential war—the abuser using the victim's survival strategy to complete the final loop of complicity.
