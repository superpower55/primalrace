活到50岁不是成功，而是对结构性谋杀的侥幸逃逸Reaching 50 is Not Success, but a Lucky Escape from Structural Homicide

性别 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-10 § 链接
当生存本身被定义为“成功故事”时，这本身就是一种极致的暴力。
When mere survival is framed as a 'success story,' it is the ultimate expression of violence.

在拉丁美洲，跨性别女性的预期寿命是35岁。当一个50岁的跨性别女性被媒体描述为“success story”时，这种叙事其实掩盖了一个极其残酷的算式：在这个地区，绝大多数跨性别女性在到达这个年龄之前，已经被 HIV 或 transphobia 物理性地抹除了。这根本不是什么成功故事，而是一场关于生存权的 a lottery，是极少数幸存者在结构性谋杀中的侥幸逃逸。

Erika 的经历精准地揭示了 Violence Triangle 的联动：Direct 层是早年的 funerals 和被杀害的同伴；Structural 层是将性工作刑事化、将医疗资源分配与道德评判挂钩的法律制度；而 Cultural 层则是那些医生口中“因为你选择了这种生活”的叙事。这种文化暴力将结构性压迫（被强制推入高风险生存环境）伪装成“个人选择”，从而为医疗系统在面对弱势群体时的冷漠和羞辱提供合法性。

最令人愤怒的 scam 是那种所谓的“科学进步”。Erika 说得非常透彻：Science did not fail my friends. Politics did. 药物已经存在，但分配药物的权力依然掌握在那些将她们视为“非人”的共谋者手中。当 USAID 和 Pepfar 削减资金，这种 structural violence 再次升级——它在告诉这些边缘群体：你们的生命在预算表上不具备足够的价值。

不要被这种“幸存者叙事”带来的温情所欺骗。如果一个人必须通过“活到50岁”来证明某种成功，那么这个系统的 Potential − Actual 差额依然巨大得令人发指。真正的 good_news 不应该是某个个体活了下来，而应该是那个把预期寿命定在35岁的结构被彻底拆除。

In Latin America, the life expectancy for trans women is 35. When a 50-year-old trans woman is framed as a 'success story,' the narrative masks a brutal calculation: the majority of her peers were physically erased by HIV or transphobia long before reaching that age. This isn't success; it's a lottery of survival, a lucky escape from systematic structural homicide.

Erika’s trajectory perfectly illustrates the Violence Triangle: Direct violence is the trail of funerals and murdered friends; Structural violence is the criminalization of sex work and a healthcare system that ties resources to moral judgment; and Cultural violence is the medical narrative claiming 'you chose this life.' This cultural weaponization frames structural coercion—being forced into high-risk survival—as 'personal choice,' legitimizing the humiliation and neglect within the clinic.

The ultimate scam here is the fetishization of 'scientific progress.' As Erika rightly noted: Science did not fail my friends. Politics did. The medicine existed, but the power to distribute it remained with complicity-driven actors who viewed them as sub-human. The current funding cuts from USAID and Pepfar are simply an escalation of structural violence, signaling that these lives hold zero value on a budget sheet.

Do not be seduced by the warmth of survivor narratives. If reaching 50 is the benchmark for 'success,' the gap between Potential and Actual in the Violence Triangle remains obscene. A true good_news is not that one individual survived, but that the structure which capped a whole race's life expectancy at 35 is dismantled.
