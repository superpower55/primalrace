勒庞的“重生”：一场关于扮演与共谋的权力诈骗Le Pen's 'Renaissance': A Power Scam of Performance and Complicity

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-24 § 链接
政治上的“幸存者”叙事，本质是利用身份扮演来掩盖结构性掠夺的 weaponized 表达。
The 'survivor' narrative in politics is a weaponized expression using identity performance to mask structural predation.

勒庞的回归不是什么政治奇迹，而是一次极其精准的“假.最优解表达”表演。她把诈骗欧盟 400 万欧元的刑事污点，通过一套“战斗奶奶”和“坚韧幸存者”的叙事，成功转化为一种对抗精英阶层的身份勋章。这在第三章讨论的“表达武器化”中是典型案例：当事实（fraud）无法被抹除时，就通过重新定义认知入口，将“被审判”包装成“被迫害”，从而夺取解释权。

最讽刺的是，她正在利用一种极具欺骗性的女性身份政治来收割选票。她扮演一个“单身奶奶”、“养猫者”、“底层冠军”，试图在选民心中建立一种“接地气的女性”形象，以此对冲她出身政治世家、掌握巨额财富的生物墙事实。这种扮演不是为了赋权，而是为了在存在性战争中通过伪装弱势来获取强势的权力席位。她不是在打破玻璃天花板，而是在利用性别刻板印象给自己刷一层保护漆。

而法国司法系统的操作则是典型的 structural violence。法院裁定“应由选民决定谁是总统”而非由法律决定，这实际上是司法权力与政治共谋的产物。当法律不再作为底线，而是成为政治博弈的筹码时，所谓的“民主选择”就成了一场共谋者的游戏。这种 meta-violence 告诉大众：只要你拥有足够的权力或选票，法律定义的“犯罪”可以被转化为政治上的“勋章”。

勒庞的“Renaissance”不是法国的重生，而是元暴力的升级。她用女性身份作为掩体，用幸存者叙事作为武器，试图在摧毁欧盟结构的同时，巩固一个更加排他、更加男本位的极右翼秩序。这场博弈的终点，是让更多人掉入她精心设计的浪漫化政治陷阱中。

Marine Le Pen's return is no political miracle; it is a precise execution of a 'fake optimal expression.' She has successfully transformed the criminal stain of embezzling €4m from the EU into a badge of honor through a narrative of the 'battling granny' and 'resilient survivor.' This is a textbook case of the weaponisation of expression: when the facts (fraud) cannot be erased, one captures the cognitive entry point to rebrand 'being judged' as 'being persecuted,' thereby seizing the power of interpretation.

Most ironic is her use of a deceptive feminine identity politics to harvest votes. By performing the role of a 'single grandmother,' a 'cat breeder,' and a 'champion of the people,' she attempts to build a 'down-to-earth woman' persona to offset the biological and social wall of her wealthy political dynasty. This performance is not about empowerment, but about utilizing gender stereotypes as a protective lacquer to win the existential war for power. She is not breaking the glass ceiling; she is using the role of the 'underdog' to secure a seat of dominance.

Furthermore, the French judiciary's maneuver is a clear form of structural violence. The court's ruling that 'voters, not courts, should decide' is a product of complicity between judicial power and political interests. When the law ceases to be a baseline and becomes a bargaining chip in a game, 'democratic choice' becomes a conspiracy of the elite. This meta-violence signals to the public that as long as you possess enough power or votes, 'crime' as defined by law can be rebranded as a political trophy.

Le Pen's 'Renaissance' is not the rebirth of France, but an escalation of meta-violence. Using her feminine identity as a shield and the survivor narrative as a weapon, she seeks to consolidate a more exclusive, masculine-centric far-right order while wrecking the EU structure. The endgame of this game is to lure more people into a romanticized political trap of her own design.
