死刑、穆夫提与被献祭的“正义”表演Death Penalties, Muftis, and the Performance of Sacrificial Justice

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-05 § 链接
法律的死刑判决往往是元暴力在结构层面的最后一次收割。
Capital punishment is the final harvest of meta-violence operating at the structural level.

一个主持犯罪类节目的女主持人，最终被判处死刑。这种讽刺的戏剧感正是国家机器最擅长的 weaponized 叙事：通过处决一个曾经在公共空间定义“正义”的人，来完成一次关于“法律至高无上”的表演。在埃及这种政教合一的 residue 中，死刑判决前必须咨询 Grand Mufti（大穆夫提）的宗教意见，这本质上是 meta violence 的闭环——世俗法律与宗教解释权共谋，共同定义谁该死，谁该活。

Sarah Khalifa 的身份很有意思：电视主持人、医美诊所老板、派对公司创办者。她试图通过多种表达（Expression）在社会博弈中寻找最优解，试图跨越阶级和权力的生物墙。但当她成为权力结构需要丢弃的弃子时，这些身份全部变成了加重判决的素材。在男性中心叙事的司法系统里，一个拥有资源、试图掌控局面的女性，其“野心”往往会被解读为更严重的罪恶，从而被赋予最极端的暴力——绞刑。

2025 年 541 个死刑判决，这个数字揭示了埃及 structural violence 的常态。死刑在这里不是为了消除犯罪，而是作为一种认知入口，向民众广播恐惧。当国家通过杀戮来维持“秩序”时，这种秩序本身就是最大的暴力。Potential（一个公正的司法体系）与 Actual（大面积死刑判决）之间的差额，就是这个国家对公民施加的元暴力。

A female TV presenter who spent her career defining 'justice' on screen is now sentenced to death. This ironic theatricality is exactly how the state machine handles weaponized narratives: by executing someone who once held the power of definition, the state performs a ritual of 'absolute law.' In Egypt's regime, where the Grand Mufti's religious opinion is required for capital cases, we see a closed loop of meta-violence—secular law and religious authority in complicity to decide who is disposable.

Sarah Khalifa's identity is telling: TV host, cosmetic surgery clinic owner, event planner. She attempted to find an optimal expression across multiple social strata to scale the biological and class walls. However, once she became a pawn the structure needed to sacrifice, these identities were weaponized against her. In a masculine-centric judicial system, a woman with resources and agency is often viewed not just as a criminal, but as a threat to the order, justifying the most extreme direct violence—hanging.

541 death sentences in 2025. This statistic reveals the normalization of structural violence in Egypt. The death penalty here isn't about crime prevention; it's a cognitive entry point designed to broadcast fear. When a state maintains 'order' through slaughter, the order itself is the violence. The gap between the Potential of a just legal system and the Actual reality of mass executions is the precise measure of the meta-violence exerted upon its people.
