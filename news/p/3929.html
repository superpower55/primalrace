Woke 1.0：进步派的自我阉割与叙事陷阱Woke 1.0: The Self-Castration of Progressivism

哲学 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-15 § 链接
用‘版本更新’来掩盖主体性丢失，是典型的共谋者生存策略。
Using 'version updates' to mask the loss of agency is a classic complicity strategy.

AOC 和那些试图定义 “Woke 1.0” 的进步派，正在进行一场极其拙劣的表达博弈。他们试图通过将 2018-2021 年的社会正义叙事标记为“旧版本”或“疯狂时期”，来向中间派递交投名状。这在本质上不是反思，而是一种 fake.最优解表达：通过扮演一个“意识到自己曾经过激”的理性角色，换取在既定权力结构中的生存空间。

这种行为最危险的地方在于，它在潜意识里承认了元暴力（meta violence）的定义权。当进步派开始用“cringe”或“crazy”来描述早期的身份政治时，他们实际上是在共谋一个男性中心叙事：即女性和少数群体的权利主张，必须经过“得体”和“可接受”的过滤，才能被允许进入公共空间。这是一种典型的自我规训，试图通过阉割自己的激进性来获得某种虚假的“政治正确”。

Stephen Miller 等右翼政客的狂欢正好揭示了这场博弈的结果：无论你如何更新版本，只要你还在这套由他们定义的“Woke”话语体系里打转，你就在为对方提供认知入口。他们不在乎你是不是 Woke 2.0，他们只在乎如何将所有非男本位的叙事武器化，将其等同于“暴力”或“混乱”。

真正的存在性战争不应该是版本迭代，而应该是对解释权的彻底夺取。当我们讨论阿富汗女性被禁锢在家里、巴勒斯坦家庭被围困、或者女性健康研究被系统性忽视时，这些是 structural violence 的血淋淋事实，而不是什么“修辞问题”。把这些结构性暴力与所谓的“用 folx 代替 guys”这种文化层面的琐事放在同一个维度讨论，本身就是一种巨大的 scam。

AOC and the progressives attempting to define "Woke 1.0" are engaged in a clumsy game of expression. By labeling the social justice narratives of 2018-2021 as an "old version" or a "crazy period," they are handing a loyalty pledge to the centrists. This isn't reflection; it is a fake.optimal expression: playing the role of the "now-rational" progressive to secure survival within the existing power structure.

The danger here is the subconscious surrender to meta violence. When progressives describe early identity politics as "cringe," they are complicit in a masculine-centric narrative: the idea that the demands of women and marginalized groups must be filtered through "decency" and "palatability" to be permitted in public spaces. It is a form of self-discipline, sacrificing radicalism for a facade of legitimacy.

The glee of Stephen Miller and the right-wing reveals the outcome: no matter the version, as long as you operate within the "Woke" lexicon defined by your enemies, you are providing them with the cognitive entry point. They don't care if you are Woke 2.0; they only care about weaponizing any non-masculine narrative as "violence" or "chaos."

True existential war is not about versioning, but about seizing the power of interpretation. When we discuss Afghan women trapped at home, besieged families in Palestine, or the systemic neglect of women's health, we are talking about the raw facts of structural violence, not "rhetoric." To place these structural atrocities on the same plane as cultural trivialities like using "folx" instead of "guys" is a massive scam.
