用 7700 万英镑买断地球的呼吸权Buying the Right to Choke the Planet for £77 Million

科技 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-21 § 链接
当碳排放被定价为可支付的罚金，环保就成了权力的赎罪券。
When carbon emissions are priced as fines, environmentalism becomes a mere indulgence for the powerful.

Digital Reef 所谓的“未来可持续数据中心”是一个典型的语义 scam。在规划文件中，他们坦承这个项目不符合 1.5°C 的气候目标，但随之而来的解决方案竟然是：如果达不到零碳，就给地方政府交一笔 7760 万英镑的“现金补偿”。

这在结构层 (structural layer) 是一次极其恶劣的资源掠夺。一个数据中心每年消耗 100 万户家庭的电力，排放相当于 2.7 万次纽约往返航线的二氧化碳，而开发者试图用一笔在 147 亿英镑总投资面前微不足道的现金，就买断了对绿带土地的破坏权和对气候目标的背叛权。这本质上是将环境成本“外部化”给全体公民，而将算力红利“内部化”给全球最富有的 AI 公司。

更深层的元暴力 (meta violence) 在于英国政府将数据中心定义为“关键国家基础设施”，并修改立法让他们可以绕过地方政府直接向中央申请。这意味着，一个由资本驱动的 AI 基础设施，在政治位阶上被等同于水资源和应急服务。当“算力”被定义为国家生存的必需品时，真实的生物生存空间——农田、野生动物栖息地以及可呼吸的空气——就变成了可以被牺牲的“次要成本”。

这种叙事武器化 (weaponization) 的逻辑极其简单：用“AI 竞赛”的宏大叙事掩盖具体的生态屠杀。如果一个项目需要通过给政府塞钱来抵消它对地球的破坏，那么它不是在创造未来，而是在用未来的生存权为现在的算力买单。

Digital Reef’s so-called “sustainable datacenter of the future” is a textbook semantic scam. In their own planning documents, they admit the project fails to align with the 1.5°C climate trajectory, yet their solution is a “cash in lieu” contribution of £77.6 million to a carbon offset fund.

On a structural layer, this is a predatory resource grab. A facility that drains electricity equivalent to 1 million households and emits as much CO2 as 27,000 New York flights is attempting to purchase the right to destroy green belt land with a sum that is negligible compared to its £14.7bn investment. It is the ultimate externalization of environmental costs onto the public to internalize compute dividends for the world's wealthiest AI firms.

The underlying meta violence is revealed in the UK government’s designation of datacentres as “critical national infrastructure,” coupled with legislation allowing them to bypass local authorities. By elevating “compute” to the same political status as water and emergency services, the biological necessity of survival—farmland, habitats, and breathable air—is rebranded as a “secondary cost” to be managed.

This is the weaponisation of narrative: using the “AI race” to mask a concrete ecological massacre. If a project requires paying a bribe to the state to offset its destruction of the planet, it isn't building the future; it is mortgaging our biological existence to fuel a server farm.
