用数学掩盖的共谋：谁在定义“工作”的定价权The Complicity of Math: Who Owns the Pricing Power of Life

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-20 § 链接
所谓的“成本数学”是结构暴力的伪装，旨在维持对生命时间的垄断。
The so-called 'math of cost' is a disguise for structural violence, designed to maintain the monopoly over human time.

这篇文章是典型的 masculine-centric narrative。作者试图用一套看似中立的“数学” (math) 来解构伯尼·桑德斯的提案，但这种数学本身就是一种 weaponized expression。他把员工的时间简化为成本项，把雇主的利润定义为不可触碰的基准线。在这种逻辑里，员工被客体化为生产线上的零件，而雇主的财务压力被塑造成了某种不可抗拒的自然规律。

最阴险的共谋在于作者对 AI 的叙事。他一方面警告桑德斯的法律会给 AI 自动化提供财务动力，另一方面又暗示这才是“合理的”演进。这其实是在恐吓劳动者：如果你要求更公正的时间分配，那么 AI 将成为取代你的工具。这种叙事将 structural violence 包装成技术进步，试图让劳动者在恐惧中接受更低的标准。

作者最后提出的“灵活性”和“心理收益”更是一个巨大的 scam。他建议通过“激励”而非“强制”来提供时间，这本质上是将生存权变成了雇主的“恩赐”。当一个人被定义为“被当作完整的人对待”需要依赖于雇主的善意时，这本身就是一种极端的 meta violence。这种叙事试图用文化层面的“温馨感”来掩盖结构层面的剥削。

真正的博弈不在于 32 小时还是 40 小时，而在于谁拥有对生命时间的定价权。如果 Actual 状态（被榨干的 40 小时）远低于 Potential 状态（人类本可拥有的自由时间），那么这个差额就是暴力。任何试图用“数学”来证明这个差额“合理”的尝试，都是在为共谋者递刀子。

This piece is a textbook example of masculine-centric narrative. The author attempts to dismantle Bernie Sanders' proposal using 'math' as a neutral tool, but this math is itself a form of weaponized expression. By reducing employees to cost items and treating employer profit as an immutable baseline, the worker is objectified as a mere part in a machine, while the employer's financial stress is framed as an inevitable law of nature.

The most insidious complicity lies in the narrative regarding AI. The author warns that Sanders' law would provide a financial incentive for automation, implying that this is the 'rational' evolution. This is a direct threat to the worker: demand a just distribution of time, and AI will be the tool used to replace you. It packages structural violence as technological progress, coercing workers into accepting lower standards out of fear.

The 'flexibility' and 'psychological benefits' proposed at the end are a total scam. Suggesting 'incentives' rather than 'mandates' for time off transforms a fundamental right into a corporate 'favor.' When being treated as a 'complete human being' depends on the benevolence of an employer, it is a manifestation of meta violence. This narrative uses cultural 'warmth' to mask structural exploitation.

The real game isn't about 32 versus 40 hours; it's about who holds the pricing power over human life. If the Actual state (being drained for 40 hours) is far below the Potential state (the freedom humans could actually have), that gap is violence. Any attempt to use 'math' to justify this gap is simply handing a knife to the co-conspirators.
