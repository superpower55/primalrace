当票房不再能买单，捕食者的“神格”才开始崩塌When Box Office Fails, the Predator's 'Divinity' Finally Collapses

性别 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-29 § 链接
权力对真相的掩盖，本质上是共谋者在计算资本的盈亏。
The suppression of truth is simply the complicity of those calculating the profit and loss of capital.

Jared Leto 的所谓“怪才”人设，不过是男性中心叙事为捕食者量身定制的掩体。在好莱坞这种极致的权力场里，一个男人发送死猪、用避孕套恶作剧被解读为“艺术家的古怪”，而他系统性地诱骗、性侵未成年少女却被当作“公开的秘密”而被集体噤声。这种认知偏差不是因为人们看不见，而是因为在 Meta Violence 的运作下，这种行为被内化为某种权力顶层的“特权表达”。

最令人作呕的不是 Leto 本人的暴行，而是这套高效运转的共谋机制 (complicity)。从 2005 年的八卦小报到 2018 年同行间的调侃，再到去年 Air Mail 的深度报道，真相一直在敲门，但门后的共谋者们——制片人、经纪人、公关团队，甚至部分媒体——在计算一个-1.3 亿美元票房亏损之前的盈亏平衡点。只要 Leto 还是那个能带来流量和票房的“资产”，他的罪行就是可以被共谋掩盖的成本。

这再次证明了，在父权结构的资本逻辑里，女性（尤其是未成年女性）的身体被物化为最廉价的消耗品。她们被 procurement 团队像采购零件一样运送到阴暗的酒店房间，而这种结构性暴力 (structural violence) 居然能通过“好莱坞文化”这种文化暴力 (cultural violence) 维持数十年之久。所谓的“勇敢发声”，在本质上是对这套共谋机器的一次强行拆解。

讽刺的是，正义的降临竟然取决于票房的崩盘。当 Leto 不再是票房保证，他就不再是那个需要被保护的“神”，而变成了一个可以被抛弃的累赘。这说明好莱坞的所谓“觉醒”根本不是基于人权的回归，而是一次精明的资产核算。在这种逻辑下，受害者的创伤被量化成了资本的贬值率。

Jared Leto's so-called 'eccentric genius' persona is nothing more than a shield tailored for predators within a masculine-centric narrative. In the power-driven ecosystem of Hollywood, a man sending dead pigs and used condoms to castmates is interpreted as 'artistic quirkiness,' while his systematic grooming and rape of underage girls are treated as an 'open secret' and collectively silenced. This cognitive dissonance exists not because people are blind, but because under the operation of meta-violence, such behavior is internalized as a 'privileged expression' of the elite.

What is truly sickening is not Leto's atrocities, but the efficiency of the complicity machine. From 2005 gossip columns to 2018 industry jokes and last year's Air Mail report, the truth has been knocking for decades. Yet the co-conspirators—producers, agents, PR teams, and even media outlets—were merely calculating the break-even point until a $130 million box office flop occurred. As long as Leto remained a 'profitable asset,' his crimes were just acceptable costs of doing business.

This confirms that in the capitalist logic of the patriarchy, female bodies—especially those of minors—are objectified as the cheapest consumables. They were procured like industrial parts and delivered to dingy hotel rooms, while this structural violence was sustained for decades by the cultural violence of 'Hollywood culture.' The so-called 'bravery' of the victims is, in essence, a forced dismantling of this complicity machinery.

It is profoundly ironic that justice arrives only when the box office crashes. Once Leto ceased to be a guarantee of profit, he stopped being a 'god' to be protected and became a liability to be discarded. Hollywood's 'awakening' is not a return to human rights, but a cold asset revaluation. In this logic, the trauma of victims is merely quantified as the depreciation rate of capital.
