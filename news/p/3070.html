把拦截导弹当成筹码：特朗普及他的“交易型”元暴力Patriot Missiles as Bargaining Chips: Trump and His 'Transactional' Meta-Violence

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-31 § 链接
将生存防御权定价为政治筹码，是最高级的结构性暴力。
Pricing the right to survive as a political chip is the ultimate form of structural violence.

特朗普及他的内阁在戴维营的这场聚会，本质上是一次关于“解释权”的权力分赃。最令人作呕的部分在于他对待乌克兰爱国者导弹的态度：他把一种能够决定数万平民生死的防御性技术，定义为一种可以被“背叛”的风险资产。在特朗普及他的叙事里，乌克兰的生存需求被简化为一场关于“技术泄露”和“未来忠诚度”的商业博弈。这就是典型的元暴力——由男性中心叙事主导的权力逻辑，将具体的生命个体客体化为筹码，从而在宏大叙事中获得掌控感。

与此同时，他的内阁成员在讨论“剔除 DEI”和攻击“极左恐怖主义”。这套操作的底层逻辑是一致的：通过制造一个虚构的敌人，来掩盖其对结构性弱势者的系统性剥夺。所谓的“回归功绩制 (Merit)”，其实就是一种武器化的表达，旨在通过重新定义“能力”来合法化既有的权力垄断。当他们把多样性、公平性和包容性定义为“社会工程”时，他们实际上是在清除所有可能挑战元暴力的认知入口。

最讽刺的是特朗普及他的共谋者们，一边在国际上通过战争和威胁来定义“胜利”，一边在内部通过设立所谓的“政治武器化补偿基金”来表演受害者。这种将权力顶端的人包装成被压迫者的叙事，是最高级的 scam。他通过定义谁是“被猎杀的爱国者”，成功地将法律正义转化为一种基于忠诚度的赏赐。在这种逻辑下，公正的表达彻底死亡，取而代之的是一个完全由权力定价的、毫无底线的交易市场。

The gathering of Trump and his cabinet at Camp David is essentially a spoils-sharing session over the 'right to interpret.' The most repulsive part is his stance on Patriot missiles for Ukraine: he defines a defensive technology that determines the lives of tens of thousands as a risky asset subject to 'betrayal.' In Trump's narrative, Ukraine's existential need is reduced to a commercial gamble over 'tech leaks' and 'future loyalty.' This is classic meta-violence—a masculine-centric power logic that objectifies living beings as chips to gain a sense of control within a grand narrative.

Simultaneously, his cabinet members discuss 'ripping out DEI' and attacking 'far-left terrorism.' The underlying logic is identical: manufacturing a fictional enemy to mask the systemic deprivation of the structurally disadvantaged. The so-called return to 'merit' is a weaponized expression designed to legitimize existing power monopolies by redefining 'ability.' By labeling diversity, equity, and inclusion as 'social engineering,' they are effectively clearing all cognitive entries that might challenge the meta-violence.

Most ironic is how Trump and his complicity circle define 'victory' through war and threats internationally, while performing as victims internally via a 'political weaponization' slush fund. This narrative, which packages the most powerful men as the oppressed, is the ultimate scam. By defining who the 'hunted patriots' are, he transforms legal justice into a reward based on loyalty. In this framework, Just Expressions are dead, replaced by a bottomless transactional market where everything is priced by power.
