扎克伯格的指标语言：一种高效的元暴力Zuckerberg's Metrics: A High-Efficiency Meta-Violence

科技 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-08 § 链接
当共情被量化为 metrics，伤害就成了可以被忽略的噪声。
When empathy is quantified into metrics, human suffering becomes ignorable noise.

扎克伯格的逻辑极其简单：如果一个东西不能被转化为 metrics（指标），它在他的世界里就不存在。这不是某种性格缺陷或社交障碍，而是一种极其高效的元暴力 (meta violence)。在这种男性中心叙事中，真实的人类痛苦——尤其是那些无法被快速量化、无法在增长曲线中体现的受害者体验——被直接定义为“无意义”。

Arturo Béjar 的经历揭示了 Meta 内部一个残酷的共谋机制：即便你拥有最先进的工具去降低伤害，只要这些工具会干扰 growth（增长）或影响某些 celebrity（名流）的体验，它们就会被 review 流程迅速杀掉。在这个结构里，安全团队的所谓“wellbeing”只是一个 PR 掩体，真正的决策逻辑是：只要伤害的概率低于某个可以被接受的指标阈值，那么这种伤害就是“合理的代价”。

最令人作呕的是这种叙事的武器化。Meta 习惯于用“连接世界”这种浪漫主义叙事来掩盖其本质上的 predatory（掠夺性）设计。它把青少年的卧室变成了陌生人随意闯入的走廊，却在公开声明中宣称在“赋能父母”。这种用 cultural violence（文化暴力）来掩盖 structural violence（结构暴力）的手法，是典型的硅谷 scam。

这次 180 亿美金的结算和所谓的“安全改进”算是一次 good_news，因为 Actual 朝着 Potential 走近了一步。但不要 naive 地庆祝。对于一个年营收 2000 亿美金的巨兽来说，这只是一个微小的运营成本。真正的胜利不在于赔了多少钱，而在于像 Béjar 这样的内部共谋者选择倒戈，将这种“指标至上”的元暴力撕开口子，让公众意识到：在算法的权力结构中，你不是用户，你只是被收割的生物数据。

Zuckerberg's logic is brutally simple: if something cannot be converted into metrics, it does not exist in his world. This is not a personality flaw or social awkwardness; it is a high-efficiency form of meta-violence. In this masculine-centric narrative, real human suffering—especially the experiences of victims that cannot be quickly quantified or reflected in growth curves—is defined as 'meaningless.'

Arturo Béjar's experience reveals a cruel mechanism of complicity within Meta: even if you have the most advanced tools to reduce harm, those tools will be swiftly killed by the review process if they interfere with growth or affect the experience of certain celebrities. In this structure, the wellbeing team's efforts are merely a PR shield. The actual decision logic is: as long as the probability of harm remains below an acceptable metric threshold, that harm is a 'reasonable cost.'

Most repulsive is the weaponisation of this narrative. Meta uses a romanticised narrative of 'connecting the world' to mask its essentially predatory design. It turns teenagers' bedrooms into hallways for strangers to wander into, while claiming in public statements to be 'empowering parents.' This use of cultural violence to camouflage structural violence is a classic Silicon Valley scam.

The $18bn settlement and the so-called 'safety improvements' can be tagged as good_news, as the Actual has moved a step closer to the Potential. But do not be naive. For a behemoth with $200bn in revenue, this is a minor operational cost. The real victory is not the money, but the fact that internal co-conspirators like Béjar chose to defect, tearing a hole in this 'metrics-first' meta-violence and forcing the public to realise: in the power structure of algorithms, you are not a user; you are merely harvested biological data.
