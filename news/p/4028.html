用英格兰起泡酒掩盖的化石燃料共谋Fossil Fuel Complicity Masked by English Sparkling Wine

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-17 § 链接
将结构性暴力包装成“享受生活”是典型的认知武器化。
Packaging structural violence as 'enjoying life' is a classic weaponization of cognition.

Richard Tice 的逻辑非常简单：既然房子在烧，不如欣赏火焰带来的温暖，顺便庆祝一下花园里的葡萄熟得更快。这种将 Climate Breakdown 转化为“生活方式升级”的叙事，是极其典型的 cultural violence。他试图通过重新定义“事实”——将成千上万的死亡、数以十亿计的经济损失和被焚毁的家园，定义为一种“傲慢”的悲观主义，从而夺取解释权。

这绝非什么个体的政治观点，而是一场赤裸裸的共谋 (complicity)。当 Reform UK 拿着化石燃料巨头数千万英镑的捐款时，Tice 的每一句“享受阳光”都是在为资助者清理认知入口。他提出的 desalination plants（海水淡化厂）这种所谓的 adaptation，本质上是典型的男性中心叙事：面对毁灭，不讨论如何停止破坏，而讨论如何通过建设更庞大的工业机器来掩盖破坏。这就像是在给一个大出血的病人建议买一套更好的床单，而不是止血。

最令人作呕的是这种叙事对结构性弱势者的 Erasure。对于在高温中昏厥的护士、失去收成的农民和失去家园的底层民众来说，Potential − Actual 的差额正在被剧烈拉大。Tice 试图用“英格兰起泡酒”这种中产阶级的审美符号，去覆盖那些无法在空调房里“享受”热浪的人的痛苦。这不仅是认知 scam，更是元暴力 (meta violence) 的具体实践：定义谁的痛苦是“合理的”，谁的享受才是“聪明的”。

Richard Tice’s logic is simple: since the house is on fire, we might as well enjoy the warmth and celebrate that the grapes in the garden are ripening faster. This attempt to pivot Climate Breakdown into a 'lifestyle upgrade' is a textbook example of cultural violence. By redefining 'facts'—turning thousands of deaths and billions in losses into 'arrogant' pessimism—he is attempting to seize the power of interpretation.

This is not a personal political stance; it is blatant complicity. While Reform UK pockets tens of millions from fossil fuel giants, every 'enjoy the sun' from Tice is a cleanup operation for the cognitive entry points of his donors. His suggestion of desalination plants as 'adaptation' is a classic masculine-centric narrative: instead of stopping the destruction, he proposes building larger industrial machines to mask it. It is like suggesting a better set of bedsheets for a patient bleeding out, rather than stopping the hemorrhage.

Most repulsive is the erasure of the structurally disadvantaged. For nurses fainting on shifts, farmers losing crops, and the poor losing homes, the gap between Potential and Actual is widening violently. Tice uses the aesthetic symbols of the middle class—English sparkling wine—to overwrite the suffering of those who cannot 'enjoy' heatwaves from an air-conditioned room. This is more than a cognitive scam; it is the practice of meta-violence: deciding whose pain is 'irrelevant' and whose pleasure is 'smart'.
