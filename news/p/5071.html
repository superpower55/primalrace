执法者的“免死金牌”与共谋者的护航The Law Enforcement 'Get Out of Jail Free' Card and the Art of Complicity

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 Reuters ↗ 2026-09-17 § 链接
暴力在制度化之后，其本质就是一种被法律背书的特权博弈。
Violence, once institutionalized, becomes a game of privilege backed by the law.

这不仅仅是一起关于滥用职权或谎言的刑事案件，而是一次典型的 structural violence（结构性暴力）在运作。一个 ICE 探员在有孩子在场的家中，隔着门向一名委内瑞拉男子腿部开火，随后用“被扫帚攻击”这种拙劣的谎言试图掩盖事实。这在 direct 层面上是残暴的，但在 structural 层面上则是必然的：当一个人被赋予了“驱逐”和“清理”的权力，他面对的不再是同类，而是被客体化的“目标”。

最令人作呕的是这场关于“逃亡”的政治博弈。德州州长 Abbott 拒绝签署引渡文件，试图用一种法律上的 technicality（技术细节）为施暴者提供庇护。这正是典型的 complicity（共谋）。在这种共谋中，施暴者、政治赞助者和部分司法体制达成了一致：只要你是在执行“大规模驱逐”这种符合某种政治叙事的任务，你的暴力就是正当的，你的谎言就是“合理的误判”，而你的逃避则是“岗位调整”。

这次逮捕之所以发生，是因为联邦大陪审团的起诉打破了原有的共谋闭环。但不要 naive 地认为正义得到了伸张。这只是一个极端的 case 触碰了法律底线。对于绝大多数在 Operation Metro Surge 这种行动中被剥夺主体性的人来说，他们的 Potential − Actual 差额依然巨大，而那个差额，就是被制度化了的暴力。

This is not merely a criminal case of abuse of power or lying; it is a textbook display of structural violence in action. An ICE agent firing into a man's leg through a door with children present, then fabricating a story about being attacked by a broom, is direct violence. But at the structural level, it is inevitable: when an individual is granted the power to 'deport' and 'purge,' the other is no longer a human, but a dehumanized target.

The most repulsive part is the political gambit over 'fugitive' status. Governor Abbott’s refusal to sign extradition papers is pure complicity. In this partnership, the perpetrator, the political patron, and parts of the judicial system agree on one thing: as long as the violence serves a specific political narrative—like mass deportation—the brutality is justified, the lies are 'reasonable mistakes,' and the evasion is merely a 'reassignment.'

This arrest happened only because a federal indictment broke the loop of complicity. Do not be naive enough to celebrate this as a victory for justice. This is just one extreme case that finally crossed a legal line. For the vast majority of those whose subjectivity was stripped during Operation Metro Surge, the gap between Potential and Actual remains vast. That gap is exactly what institutionalized violence looks like.
