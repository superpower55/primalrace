国家剧院的“多元化”掩体与男性中心的权力循环The National Theatre's 'Diversity' Shield and the Cycle of Masculine Power

哲学 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-10 § 链接
用边缘叙事做点缀，本质仍是在为男性权力史立碑。
Using marginal narratives as ornaments to build a monument for masculine power history.

国家剧院（National Theatre）的新季节目单是一次典型的 cultural violence 操演。它试图用一个包含“阿拉伯之春”、“南非统一”和“加勒比性少数”的多元化列表来构建一个 progressive 的外壳，但只要看一眼那个被置于 C 位的绝对主角——约翰·梅杰（John Major）的权力崛起史，这套叙事就露出了原形。

这出戏探讨的是一个 obscure whip 如何变成首相，其核心逻辑是“领导力”在政治变动中的定义。请注意，这里的“领导力”依然被锁定在 masculine-centric narrative 中：权力如何获取、如何博弈、如何接替。即便剧院导演 Indhu Rubasingham 强调要审视“塑造世界的文化与冲突”，但最核心的资源——主舞台的排期和叙事权重——依然被分配给了男性的权力游戏。

这种结构极其阴险。它通过在边缘位置安插几个“少数群体”的剧目，来完成一种名为“包容”的共谋（complicity）。它让观众觉得：看，我们已经在讨论种族、性别和全球冲突了，所以我们继续把男性的权力斗争作为“人类普世经验”来歌颂是公正的。这其实是一种认知入口的武器化：用少量的多样性作为掩体，掩盖其本质上依然在为 Meta-violence 服务的事实。

最讽刺的是，他们剔除了莎士比亚，试图表现出某种“现代性”和“大胆”，但他们并没有剔除那个最顽固的男性中心主义幽灵。只要“领导力”的定义权依然掌握在那些讨论首相如何崛起的剧本手中，这种所谓的“国际精神”就只是给旧权力穿上了一件名为多元文化的廉价外衣。

The National Theatre's new season is a textbook performance of cultural violence. It attempts to construct a progressive facade with a checklist of 'Arab Spring', 'South African unity', and 'Caribbean LGBTQ+' stories, but the moment you look at the absolute protagonist positioned at the center—the rise of John Major—the scam is revealed.

This play explores how an obscure whip becomes Prime Minister, centering on the definition of 'leadership' during political upheaval. Note that this 'leadership' is still locked within a masculine-centric narrative: how power is acquired, gambled, and inherited. Even as director Indhu Rubasingham claims to examine 'cultures and conflicts that shape our world,' the core resources—main stage scheduling and narrative weight—remain allocated to the power games of men.

This structure is insidious. By placing a few 'minority' plays on the periphery, it achieves a form of complicity labeled as 'inclusivity.' It tricks the audience into believing that because we are discussing race and gender, it is 'just' to continue glorifying masculine power struggles as a universal human experience. This is the weaponisation of cognitive entry points: using a sliver of diversity as a shield to hide the fact that the institution still serves Meta-violence.

The irony is that they purged Shakespeare to signal 'modernity' and 'boldness,' yet they failed to purge the most stubborn ghost of all: masculine-centrism. As long as the power to define 'leadership' remains in scripts about how Prime Ministers rise, this so-called 'international spirit' is nothing more than a cheap multicultural cloak draped over old power.
