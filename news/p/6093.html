一个人从厨房摔死，写成了一则脚注A Man Died Slipping in His Kitchen. It Got Written as a Footnote.

文娱 结构层 Deadline ↗ 2026-10-31 § 链接
死者的年龄有两个版本，死因只有一个版本
Two versions of his age. One version of how he died.

Sanjay Gupta 死了。57 岁，或者 59 岁，报道自己也没定下来。死因倒是很清楚：在孟买家里的厨房滑倒，头部重伤，送医后不治。警方登记为意外死亡，没有他杀嫌疑。

一个人在自己家的厨房里滑倒，然后死了。这句话本身就是全部的信息量，剩下的都是装饰。装饰里最响的那一块，是 Tarantino 2007 年的一段话：他看过 Kaante，觉得棒极了，说印度人跑到美国抢美国银行，多酷，而且演这些人的都是宝莱坞的传奇。

这段话被放在讣告里，功能是给死者加冕。但它同时是一份口供：Kaante 和 Reservoir Dogs 的相似，是 Tarantino 自己看出来的，报道也写了，这给 Gupta 带来了抄袭的质疑。一个导演一生最被引用的一句评价，来自被他借用的人，说的是“我被抄袭得很荣幸”。

这不是要在一个死人身上算账。Gupta 自己说过，他愿意从任何媒介里“乞讨、借用、偷窃”。这句话很诚实，甚至有点好看。问题在于，这句话在宝莱坞的产业里能成立，是因为有一个更大的结构在替他兜底：印度的犯罪片、黑帮片、neo-noir，长期把香港、好莱坞、韩国的东西拆开重组，再卖给本土观众。拆得好的人被叫大师，拆得不好的人被叫抄袭，界线由谁划定、由谁定价，从来不在这部电影里。

报道写了他的妻子 Anuradha Lekhi Gupta，儿子 Shivaansh，女儿 Dalai。写了他生于孟买，10 月 23 日生日，死在 10 月 30 日，中间隔了七天。写了他和 Sanjay Dutt 长期合作。这些都对，都是事实。

只是这份事实清单里，那个厨房没有被写进去。一个 57 岁的男人，在自己家里，滑了一跤。意外死亡报告是一份文件，不是一个解释。谁家的厨房地砖是滑的，谁家的房子是为谁设计的，谁在深夜独自站在灶台前——这些都不在讣告的射程里。

Tarantino 说他“真正感到荣幸”。Gupta 听不到了。

Sanjay Gupta is dead. Fifty-seven, or fifty-nine — the report itself can't settle it. The cause is clear enough: he slipped in the kitchen of his Mumbai home, suffered a serious head injury, and later died. Police registered an accidental death report and said no foul play is suspected.

A man slipped in his own kitchen and died. That sentence is the entire information content. The rest is decoration. The loudest piece of decoration is a 2007 quote from Tarantino: he saw Kaante, thought it was fabulous, said Indian guys coming to America to rob an American bank — how cool is that, and the guys are played by legends of Bollywood.

That quote sits in the obituary to crown the dead man. It also functions as a confession: the resemblance between Kaante and Reservoir Dogs was noticed by Tarantino himself, the report says so, and it raised questions of plagiarism. The most-quoted line of a director's career comes from the man he borrowed from, saying he was honored to be ripped off.

This is not about settling accounts with a dead man. Gupta said himself he was willing to beg, borrow and steal from any medium. That's an honest line, even a handsome one. The point is that the line could stand in Bollywood because a larger structure had his back: Indian crime and gangster cinema has long taken Hong Kong, Hollywood, Korean material apart and reassembled it for a domestic audience. Those who reassemble well are called masters; those who don't are called plagiarists. Who draws that line, and who prices it, was never inside the film.

The report names his wife, Anuradha Lekhi Gupta, his son Shivaansh, his daughter Dalai. It notes he was born in Mumbai on October 23, 1969, died on October 30 — seven days apart. It notes his long collaboration with Sanjay Dutt. All true. All facts.

The kitchen is not in that list of facts. A 57-year-old man, in his own home, slipped. An accidental death report is a document, not an explanation. Whose kitchen floor is slippery, whose house is designed for whom, who stands alone at the stove late at night — none of that is in the obituary's range.

Tarantino said he was truly honored. Gupta can't hear it now.
