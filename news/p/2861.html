从“国防部”到“战争部”：一场关于男性气概的血腥 scamFrom 'Defense' to 'War': A Bloody Scam of Masculine Bravado

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-25 § 链接
用“睾酮”掩盖伤亡，是用生物学神话为结构性暴力背书的典型 weaponization。
Using 'testosterone' to mask casualties is a textbook weaponization of biological myths to justify structural violence.

把 Pentagon 重新命名为“战争部”(Department of War)，这不是简单的称呼变更，而是一次极其精准的认知入口抢夺。它通过将“战争”从一种国家防御的结构性无奈，直接定义为一种进攻性的、纯粹的男性权力表达，完成了对元暴力的赤裸升级。在这种叙事下，战争不再关乎地缘政治或人权，而是一场关于“真男人”和“战士”的存在性战争。

最令人作呕的细节在于，当战争陷入泥潭、伤亡数据被操纵时，国防部长 Hegseth 竟然抛出了一套“睾酮缺乏症”(testosterone deficiency) 的筛查计划。这简直是 weaponized expression 的教科书级案例：当 structural violence（伤亡人数、战争失败）无法被掩盖时，他试图通过转移注意力到生物学层面的“男性气概”上，将士兵的战斗力与激素水平挂钩。这是一种极其卑劣的逻辑——把政治失败伪装成生物学缺陷，试图用一种虚假的“生物墙”来掩盖真实的血腥成本。

这就是典型的 masculine-centric narrative：在开战之初，用“不留情面”的强权叙事吸引共谋者；在进入 quagmire（泥潭）之后，则通过封锁媒体、操纵数据，将公众从 stakeholders 降级为 adversaries。所谓的“数据中断”和“分类机密”，不过是文化暴力在结构层面的执行，旨在确保只有被筛选过的、符合特定审美和立场的“友好媒体”能进入认知通道。

这场战争的本质是权力的傲慢，而它的包装则是对男性气概的病态崇拜。当一个政权开始用“睾酮”来定义军队的效能时，它实际上已经承认了其在政治和战略上的彻底破产。这种将生物性工具化以掩盖暴力差额的行为，正是元暴力最狰狞的模样。

Renaming the Pentagon to the 'Department of War' is not a mere semantic change; it is a precise seizure of the cognitive entry point. By redefining war from a structural necessity of national defense into an offensive, purely masculine expression of power, the administration has executed a naked upgrade of meta-violence. In this narrative, war is no longer about geopolitics or human rights, but an existential war over who qualifies as a 'real warrior.'

The most repulsive detail is Secretary Hegseth’s introduction of a 'testosterone deficiency' screening program just as the war spirals into a quagmire and casualty data is being manipulated. This is a textbook example of weaponized expression: when structural violence—the death toll and military failure—can no longer be hidden, he attempts to pivot the discourse toward a biological myth of 'masculinity.' It is a vile logic—masking political bankruptcy as a biological deficiency, using a fake 'biological wall' to obscure the actual cost of blood.

This is the essence of a masculine-centric narrative: attracting complicity through a narrative of 'no mercy' at the start, then transforming the public from stakeholders into adversaries through media blackouts and data manipulation. The so-called 'data disruptions' and 'classified spaces' are simply the execution of cultural violence at the structural level, ensuring that only 'friendly' media, filtered for specific aesthetics and loyalty, can access the narrative channel.

This war is a manifestation of power's arrogance, wrapped in a pathological cult of masculinity. When a regime begins to define military efficacy through 'testosterone,' it admits total bankruptcy in politics and strategy. This instrumentalization of biology to hide the gap in the Violence Triangle is exactly how meta-violence operates in its most hideous form.
