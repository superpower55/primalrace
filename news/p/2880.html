FIFA的“爱与和平”是最高级的暴力掩体FIFA’s 'Love and Peace' is the Ultimate Violence Shield

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Athletic ↗ 2026-07-27 § 链接
用“仇恨”定义批评，是用文化暴力掩盖结构性剥夺的典型scam。
Defining criticism as 'hate' is a classic scam using cultural violence to mask structural deprivation.

Infantino 这次在 Instagram 上的 15 页表演，是教科书级的表达武器化。他试图通过定义“仇恨” (hate) 来夺取解释权：在这个叙事里，所有对 FIFA 权力运作的质疑都被打包成一种“负能量”，而他则扮演那个在前线创造“欢乐与团结”的救世主。这不仅是 PR 话术，而是一种典型的文化暴力 (cultural violence) —— 通过重新定义词汇，让真正的暴力在“爱与和平”的掩体下变得不可见。

看这套逻辑的 structural 层：FIFA 面对的是签证歧视、政治干预红牌、以及对特定族群（如伊朗、塞内加尔）的系统性排斥。但 Infantino 并不在这些事实维度上博弈，他直接跳到了 meta 层，通过一种 masculine-centric 的傲慢，要求批评者去“冥想、祈祷或看球”。这意味着在他的世界观里，规则的制定者拥有绝对的定义权，而受害者或质疑者的表达被贬低为情绪化的“杂音”。

最令人作呕的是他将“全球名流的出席”作为成功指标。这正是共谋者理论 (complicity) 的现场：当资本、权力与名望在 VIP 席位上达成共识，他们共同构建了一个“世界之最”的幻象，而那些因为签证被拒、在看台外徘徊的人，以及在庆祝中窒息而死的底层，被简化为“极少数的个案”。

这种“现实不配合就重新定义现实”的机制，与某些科技巨头宣布新定律来掩盖芯片短板如出一辙。当 Infantino 宣布赛事“100% 安全且只有快乐”时，他不是在描述事实，而是在制造一个名为“真实”的产品。在这种元暴力的统治下，真相不再是客观的，而是由权力决定谁被允许进入这个“欢乐”的叙事。

Infantino’s 15-slide performance on Instagram is a textbook case of the weaponisation of expression. By labeling all scrutiny as 'hate,' he attempts to seize the power of interpretation: in this narrative, any critique of FIFA’s power dynamics is dismissed as 'negative energy,' while he casts himself as the savior delivering 'joy and togetherness.' This is not mere PR; it is a manifestation of cultural violence—redefining vocabulary to make actual violence invisible behind a shield of 'love and peace.'

Look at the structural layer: FIFA is facing visa discrimination, political interference in red cards, and the systemic exclusion of specific groups like Iranians and Senegalese. Yet, Infantino doesn't engage with these facts. He jumps straight to the meta-level, employing a masculine-centric arrogance by telling critics to 'meditate, pray, or watch a match.' In his worldview, the rule-makers possess absolute definition power, while the expressions of victims are reduced to emotional 'noise.'

Most repulsive is his use of 'global celebrity attendance' as a metric of success. This is the theory of complicity in action: when capital, power, and fame reach a consensus in the VIP boxes, they construct an illusion of a 'world-best' event. Meanwhile, those denied visas or those who died of asphyxiation in the streets are erased or dismissed as 'isolated incidents.'

This mechanism of 'redefining reality when reality doesn't comply' is identical to tech giants announcing new 'laws' to hide chip failures. When Infantino claims the tournament had '100% safety and only joy,' he isn't describing a fact; he is manufacturing a product called 'Truth.' Under this meta-violence, truth is no longer objective—it is whatever the power center decides is 'joyful.'
