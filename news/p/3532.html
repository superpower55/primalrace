内塔尼亚胡的博弈：用巴勒斯坦的血续命Netanyahu's Gambit: Sustaining Power with Palestinian Blood

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-09 § 链接
和平方案是政治筹码，而死亡是唯一真实的货币。
Peace plans are political chips; death is the only real currency.

所谓的“15点和平计划”从来不是为了结束暴力，而是两个面临选举压力的人在进行一次关于“谁更强硬”的表达博弈。特朗普需要一个“和平缔造者”的 statesman 标签来压低油价，而内塔尼亚胡需要一个“以色列捍卫者”的人设来在10月的选举中活下来。在这场存在性战争中，和平计划只是被武器化的叙事工具，其目的不是达成共识，而是通过“拒绝”来向国内强硬派递交投名状。

内塔尼亚胡要求的“完全解除武装”是一个典型的逻辑 scam。在结构性暴力（structural violence）中，强者永远要求弱者先交出所有防御手段，然后才承诺给予所谓的“和平”。这种不对称的博弈本质上是殖民逻辑的延续：只有当对方彻底失去主体性，所谓的“和平”才会被施舍。这种叙事将暴力合法化，把屠杀包装成“安全需求”，让直接暴力（direct violence）在文化层面上显得自然且正确。

最令人作呕的是这种共谋关系。美国历届政府在公共空间表演“关切人道主义”，但在结构层面上通过持续的军事援助为暴力背书。这种共谋的回报是地缘政治的控制权。无论谁在白宫，巴勒斯坦人的身体始终是被定价的资产，他们的死亡被量化为油价的波动或选票的增减。当内塔尼亚胡问“谁才是超级大国”时，他其实在确认一种元暴力（meta violence）：在这个男本位的全球权力结构中，只有通过展现更极致的破坏力，才能获得被尊重的解释权。

The so-called "15-point peace plan" was never about ending violence; it is an existential war of expression between two men facing elections, fighting over who can appear "tougher." Trump needs the statesman label of a "peacemaker" to lower oil prices, while Netanyahu needs the persona of the "defender of Israel" to survive the October elections. In this game, the peace plan is a weaponized narrative used not to reach consensus, but to signal loyalty to hardliners through the act of rejection.

Netanyahu's demand for "full disarmament" is a classic logical scam. In the realm of structural violence, the oppressor always demands the oppressed surrender all means of defense before promising a hypothetical "peace." This asymmetric gamble is a continuation of colonial logic: peace is only granted once the other's subjectivity is completely erased. This narrative legitimizes direct violence, packaging slaughter as a "security necessity" and making it appear natural at the cultural level.

Most repulsive is the complicity involved. Successive US administrations perform "humanitarian concern" in public while providing the structural backing for violence through military aid. The return on this complicity is geopolitical control. Regardless of who sits in the White House, Palestinian bodies remain priced assets, their deaths quantified by oil price fluctuations or vote counts. When Netanyahu asks "Who is the fucking superpower here?", he is confirming a meta-violence: in this masculine-centric global power structure, only the demonstration of extreme destructiveness earns the right to define reality.
