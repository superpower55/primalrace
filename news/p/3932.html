用罚单修补的教育，是结构性暴力的PR版本Fines as a Patch for Systemic Failure: The PR Version of Structural Violence

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-15 § 链接
当制度无法提供安全与支持时，强制出勤就成了对弱势者的二次收割。
When systems fail to provide safety, mandatory attendance becomes a secondary harvest of the vulnerable.

这场在马盖特海滩边上演的审判，本质上是一场关于“出勤率”的数字博弈。教育部门在焦虑地盯着一个数据：从疫情前的 4.7% 回升到现在的 6.93%。在官僚系统的逻辑里，只要把缺勤的父母拽进法庭，用罚金和监禁的威胁把孩子“赶”回教室，Actual 就能向 Potential 靠拢。但这根本不是在解决教育问题，而是在用 direct violence（罚款、起诉）来掩盖 structural violence（结构性暴力）。

最讽刺的对比在于法庭上的两类被告。一类是利用 term-time 差价出国旅游的父母，他们试图在制度的缝隙里寻找经济最优解，结果被告知“便宜的旅费不能作为辩护理由”。而另一类则触及了元暴力的核心：那个因为被校园霸凌到肋骨骨折、被美工刀威胁而不敢上学的 14 岁男孩，以及他那个疲惫不堪、患有阅读障碍的母亲。当学校无法提供基本的安全保障，当 ADHD 和自闭症孩子在体制内被撕碎时，母亲将孩子留在家里保护其生命安全，成了法律意义上的“犯罪”。

在这种叙事中，学校和地方议会成了所谓的“共谋者”——他们一边通过法律手段强制出勤，一边对校园霸凌和特殊教育需求的匮乏视而不见。他们定义了什么是“有效的教育”，却拒绝承认：一个恐惧被攻击的孩子，在家里学烘焙和理财，可能比在充满暴力的教室里坐满 6 小时更接近“公正的表达”。

教育部长 Paul Waugh 观察到周五的缺勤率最高，并将其归结为“参与度不足”。这种典型的 masculine-centric narrative（男性中心叙事）将系统性的崩坏简化为个体的态度问题。他们不关心孩子为什么不快乐，只关心数据是否好看。这种将“出勤”等同于“受教育”的 scam，正把无数处于边缘的家庭推向法律的对立面。

This trial by the Margate beach is essentially a numerical game over 'attendance rates.' The bureaucracy is obsessed with a single metric: returning from the post-pandemic 6.93% to the pre-pandemic 4.7%. In their logic, dragging parents into court and threatening them with fines or prison is the way to push Actual toward Potential. But this isn't solving education; it's using direct violence to mask structural violence.

The contrast between the defendants is stark. Some parents seek an economic optimal expression by traveling during term-time for cheaper rates, only to be told that 'cheap travel is not a defence.' Then there is the core of meta-violence: a 14-year-old boy with broken ribs from bullying and a mother struggling with dyslexia. When the school fails to provide basic safety, keeping a child home to save their life is rebranded as a 'crime.'

Here, the school and local council act as complicity partners. They enforce attendance through the law while ignoring the systemic failure to handle bullying and Special Educational Needs (SEN). They define 'effective education' by seat-time, ignoring that for a traumatized child, learning to bake or manage money at home is a more Just Expression than enduring six hours of terror in a classroom.

Education Minister Paul Waugh notes higher absences on Fridays and attributes it to 'lack of engagement.' This is a classic masculine-centric narrative, reducing systemic collapse to a matter of individual attitude. They don't care why children are miserable; they only care if the data looks good. This scam, equating 'attendance' with 'education,' is simply pushing marginalized families further into the crosshairs of the state.
