鲨鱼需要PR，而科学家需要打破“专业”的伪装Sharks Need PR, and Scientists Need to Strip the Professional Mask

好消息 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-06 § 链接
真正的最优解表达，是拒绝将主体性碎片化为单一的职业标签。
The true optimal expression is refusing to fragment one's subjectivity into a single professional label.

Jasmin Graham 说鲨鱼需要更好的 PR，这本身就是一个有趣的 meta 隐喻。在大多数人的认知入口里，鲨鱼被武器化为“怪物”以维持某种恐惧叙事；而科学家则被结构化为“严谨、枯燥、单一”的专业机器。但 Graham 展现的是一种真.最优解表达：她既是操刀生物检样的科学家，又是玩空中绸吊的 aerialist，还是在 Blockbusters 主题酒吧玩音乐宾果的普通人。

大多数人在社会博弈中为了获得“专业”的票价，会选择假.最优解表达——扮演一个被职业身份吞噬的角色，通过抹除生活中的“杂质”来换取体制内的认可。这种扮演本质上是主体性的死亡。而 Graham 将自己的生物墙（女性身份）与多元的表达（艺术、科学、亚文化）无缝缝合，她不需要在“科学家”和“个人”之间做零和博弈，而是直接定义了自己的存在性。

这种好新闻的本质在于，它在 cultural 层面上削弱了那种“专业主义”的元暴力。它告诉受众，一个能给鲨鱼缝合发射器的女性，也可以在深夜观看傻瓜视频并坚持睡够八小时。当一个人不再为了适配某种社会角色而进行自我规训，她就从共谋者的队列中脱身，夺回了定义自己“事实”的权力。

Jasmin Graham claims sharks need better PR, which is a fascinating meta-metaphor. In the common cognitive entry points, sharks are weaponized as 'monsters' to maintain a narrative of fear; similarly, scientists are structurally cast as 'rigorous, dull, and singular' professional machines. Graham, however, demonstrates a true optimal expression: she is a scientist performing muscle biopsies, an aerialist mastering silks, and a regular person playing music bingo at a Blockbusters-themed bar.

Most people, in the existential war for 'professional' credibility, opt for a fake optimal expression—playing a role consumed by their career, erasing the 'impurities' of life to gain institutional approval. This performance is, in essence, the death of subjectivity. Graham seamlessly sutures her biological wall (feminine identity) with diverse expressions (art, science, subculture). She refuses the zero-sum game between being a 'scientist' and being a 'person,' instead reclaiming the power to define her own existence.

This is good_news because it weakens the meta-violence of 'professionalism' at the cultural layer. It reveals that a woman who can suture transmitters into sharks can also watch silly videos and prioritize eight hours of sleep. When an individual stops self-disciplining to fit a social role, she breaks away from the ranks of complicity and seizes the right to manufacture her own facts.
