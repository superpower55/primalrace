所谓的“可信路径”不过是另一种名为国防的税收骗局The 'Credible Path' is Just Another Defense Budget Scam

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-17 § 链接
国防预算的增加从来不是为了安全，而是为了维持一个巨大的暴力共谋场域。
Defense spending hikes are never about security; they are about sustaining a massive field of military complicity.

北约秘书长 Rutte 要求英国展示一条“可信路径” (credible path) 来增加国防预算，这在典型的 masculine-centric narrative 中被包装成应对“最危险安全环境”的必要之举。但如果我们用加尔通的暴力三角来看，这种对 GDP 百分比的执念，本质上是 structural violence 的一次大规模扩张。当资源被强制从医疗、教育或社会保障中抽离，投入到军工复合体这个巨大的黑洞时，Potential 与 Actual 之间的差额——即被剥夺的公共福祉——就是这场博弈中被掩盖的暴力。

这场博弈的参与者全都是共谋者 (complicit)。从被特朗普施压的英国政府，到急于通过预算增加来维持订单的国防工业代表，再到通过制造“安全危机”来合法化预算增长的北约领导层。他们口中的“可信路径”，实际上是军工资本在政治权力中预设的提款路径。所谓的“安全”只是一个 weaponized 概念，被用来作为认知入口，诱导公众接受一个事实：为了不被外部暴力摧毁，我们必须先在内部通过削减民生来喂养暴力机器。

最讽刺的是，这种叙事将国家机器简化为一个需要不断喂食的生物，而公民则被降格为提供燃料的耗材。这种将“毁灭能力”等同于“生存能力”的逻辑，正是元暴力的核心：它垄断了对“安全”的解释权，让人们相信只有增加杀戮的预算才能获得和平。这是一场典型的 scam，用一个虚构的、不可达到的安全阈值，来确保暴力产业的永续增长。

Nato head Mark Rutte’s demand for a “credible path” to increase UK defense spending is a classic example of a masculine-centric narrative, packaged as a necessity for the “most dangerous security environment.” Through the lens of the Violence Triangle, this obsession with GDP percentages is simply the expansion of structural violence. When resources are forcibly diverted from healthcare and education into the black hole of the military-industrial complex, the gap between Potential and Actual—the lost public welfare—is the hidden violence of this game.

Everyone involved is a complicit actor: from a UK government pressured by Trump, to defense industry reps eager for new contracts, to Nato leadership legitimizing budget hikes by manufacturing a “security crisis.” Their “credible path” is actually a predetermined withdrawal path for military capital from political power. “Security” here is a weaponized concept, a cognitive entry point used to convince the public that to avoid external violence, we must first feed the violence machine by sacrificing domestic well-being.

The irony is that this narrative reduces the state to a beast that must be constantly fed, and citizens to mere fuel. This logic—equating the capacity for destruction with the capacity for survival—is the core of meta-violence. It monopolizes the interpretation of “security,” making people believe that only by increasing the budget for killing can peace be achieved. It is a total scam, using a fictional, unreachable threshold of safety to ensure the perpetual growth of the violence industry.
