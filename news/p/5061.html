关键词禁令：一种极其低端的元暴力实践Keyword Bans: A Crude Practice of Meta-Violence

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-16 § 链接
用关键词过滤科研经费，是试图通过掌控认知入口来抹除事实的拙劣尝试。
Filtering research grants via keywords is a clumsy attempt to erase facts by seizing the cognitive entry points.

这起针对 NIH 的诉讼揭露了一个极其典型的 weaponized 表达过程：特朗普政府试图通过一个包含 235 个关键词的“黑名单”，将联邦科研经费变成一种意识形态的投名状。当“gender”、“equity”或“sexual harassment”这些词被标记为禁区，这不再是学术优先级的调整，而是一次精准的 structural violence。它在告诉所有研究者：你的主体性必须让位于权力的审美，否则你的生存资源将被切断。

最讽刺的是，这种操作本质上是对元暴力 (meta violence) 的低端复刻。男性中心叙事向来习惯于定义什么是“客观”和“理性”，而将涉及性别、种族和权力的讨论打上“政治化”的标签从而将其排除在科学之外。这次禁令只是把这种潜意识的共谋变成了明文的行政指令——通过删除词汇来删除现实。如果一个关于阿尔茨海默症的研究因为关注“高风险社区”而被砍掉，那么被牺牲的不仅是数据，更是那些被原初种族逻辑长期殖民、在医疗资源分配中处于 Potential − Actual 差额底端的人群。

科研经费的 peer review 机制原本是某种程度上的公正表达，但现在被强行插入了一层 ideological review。这种对认知入口的强行拦截，试图制造一种“这些问题不存在”的假象。但事实是，当权力试图通过 scrub research 来净化现实时，它恰恰证明了这些被禁的词汇具有多么巨大的威胁力。因为只有真正能动摇共谋结构的真相，才需要被如此粗暴地噤声。

This lawsuit against the NIH exposes a textbook case of weaponized expression: the Trump administration is attempting to turn federal research funding into a loyalty pledge through a 'blacklist' of 235 keywords. When terms like 'gender,' 'equity,' or 'sexual harassment' become forbidden zones, it is no longer a shift in academic priority, but a precise act of structural violence. It signals to researchers that their subjectivity must yield to the aesthetics of power, or their survival resources will be severed.

Ironically, this operation is a crude replication of meta-violence. The masculine-centric narrative has always been adept at defining what is 'objective' and 'rational' while labeling discussions of gender, race, and power as 'political' to exclude them from science. This ban simply transforms that subconscious complicity into an explicit administrative order—erasing reality by deleting vocabulary. When an Alzheimer’s study is axed because it focuses on 'high-risk communities,' what is sacrificed is not just data, but the people at the bottom of the Potential − Actual gap, those long colonized by the logic of the Primal Race in healthcare distribution.

The peer review process for grants was once a form of Just Expression, but it has now been forcibly overlaid with an ideological review. This aggressive interception of cognitive entry points attempts to manufacture a reality where 'these problems do not exist.' Yet, the fact that power feels the need to scrub research to purify reality proves exactly how threatening these forbidden words are. Only truths capable of shaking the structure of complicity require such brutal silencing.
