把地缘政治当成房地产：特朗普的“领土”幻想Geopolitics as Real Estate: Trump's Territorial Fantasy

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-14 § 链接
将国际公海定义为私产，是典型的男性中心叙事对现实的强行篡改。
Defining international waters as private property is a classic masculine-centric narrative overriding reality.

特朗普把霍尔木兹海峡（Strait of Hormuz）视为可以随时宣布为“美国领土”的资产，这种逻辑不是外交，而是典型的房地产思维。在元暴力（meta violence）的框架下，这是一种极端的男性中心叙事：我拥有定义权，所以我拥有所有权。他试图通过控制认知入口，将“事实上的封锁”直接跳跃到“法理上的领土”，这种对现实的强行篡改是典型的武器化表达。

这场战争的叙事入口被精准地设定为“对抗邪恶国家”和“防止核武”，用来掩盖结构层面的暴力——全球贸易节点的瘫痪和底层民众被转嫁的油价成本。当他要求美国民众接受油价上涨是“正确的事”时，他实际上是在要求人们共谋，用个体的生活质量去交换他个人在存在性战争中的“胜利感”。

最荒谬的共谋在于，这种“强人”表达被包装成一种保护。但事实上，无论是伊朗的平民还是美国加油站前的司机，都成了这场男性权力博弈中的客体。在这种叙事中，领土、海峡和人口不再是具有主体性的存在，而变成了棋盘上的筹码。所谓的“胜利”不过是定义权的一次粗暴扩张：因为我能封锁，所以这里就是我的。

Trump treating the Strait of Hormuz as an asset to be declared 'US territory' isn't diplomacy; it's real estate logic. Within the framework of meta violence, this is an extreme masculine-centric narrative: I hold the power to define, therefore I hold the power to own. He attempts to bypass the 'de facto blockade' and jump straight to 'de jure territory' by weaponising expression to rewrite reality.

The narrative entry point of this war is precisely calibrated as 'fighting an evil state' and 'preventing nuclear weapons' to mask the structural violence—the paralysis of global trade and the cost shifted onto the working class via fuel prices. When he tells Americans that paying more is 'the right thing,' he is recruiting them as complicit actors, asking them to trade their quality of life for his personal sense of victory in an existential war.

The most absurd complicity is how this 'strongman' expression is packaged as protection. In reality, both Iranian civilians and American drivers are mere objects in a masculine power struggle. In this narrative, territories, straits, and populations lose their subjectivity and become chips on a gambling table. This 'victory' is nothing more than a crude expansion of the power to define: because I can blockade it, it belongs to me.
