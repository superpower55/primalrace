精英的自救指南：用“工人阶级”做遮羞布的叙事游戏The Elite's Survival Guide: Using the 'Working Class' as a Narrative Shield

哲学 文化层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-07 § 链接
当精英开始讨论如何“修复”系统时，他们讨论的是如何通过更新补丁来维持自己的解释权。
When elites discuss 'fixing' the system, they are actually discussing how to patch the software to maintain their monopoly on interpretation.

Daron Acemoğlu 这本书在玩一个典型的精英博弈游戏：通过谴责一部分精英（进步派），来试图挽救整个精英阶层（自由主义者）的合法性。他把自由民主的危机归结为左翼放弃了经济阶级而转向 identity politics，这在叙事上极其顺滑，因为它把结构性暴力简化成了“话术错误”。

从加尔通的暴力三角来看，这完全是 cultural layer 的修补尝试。Acemoğlu 提出的所谓“工人阶级自由民主”新契约，本质上是试图通过微调 structural layer（比如降低理发师的准入门槛、推行 worker AI）来掩盖元暴力的存在。他试图用一种“自上而下”的善意，去解决一个由“自上而下”的垄断所制造的问题。最讽刺的是，一个诺贝尔奖得主在书里抱怨精英的顶层讨论是徒劳的，而他本身正在进行一场最高规格的顶层讨论。

这种叙事逻辑与 Peter Thiel 的 Libertarian 逻辑其实是同一种 weaponized expression 的两面：Thiel 认为女性投票权破坏了资本主义民主，而 Acemoğlu 认为对身份政治的执着破坏了自由主义。两者都在试图定义什么是“正确的”民主，但都没有意识到，只要解释权依然被掌握在极少数“聪明人”手中，这种所谓的修复就只是在给原有的权力结构刷漆。所谓的 shared prosperity 从来不是被“设计”出来的，而是被底层抗争和共谋者倒戈逼出来的。指望一个经济学家通过一套新契约来“救”工人阶级，这本身就是一个巨大的 scam。

Daron Acemoğlu is playing a classic elite game: attempting to salvage the legitimacy of the entire elite class (liberals) by denouncing a subset of them (progressives). By attributing the crisis of liberal democracy to the left's shift from economic class to identity politics, he smoothly reduces structural violence to a mere 'rhetorical error.'

Applying Galtung's Violence Triangle, this is a transparent attempt at a cultural layer patch. The proposed 'working-class liberal democracy' is an effort to mask meta-violence by tweaking the structural layer—such as lowering regulatory barriers for hairdressers or promoting 'worker AI.' He seeks to solve a problem created by top-down monopoly with a top-down benevolence. The irony is peak: a Nobel laureate complaining that top-down elite debates are sterile while engaging in the most prestigious top-down debate possible.

This logic is simply the flip side of Peter Thiel's Libertarian weaponized expression. While Thiel claims women's suffrage ruined capitalist democracy, Acemoğlu claims identity politics ruined liberalism. Both are fighting over the right to define 'correct' democracy, ignoring that as long as the power of interpretation remains monopolized by a few 'learned philosophers,' any 'fix' is just a fresh coat of paint on an old power structure. Shared prosperity is never 'designed' by elites; it is forced by grassroots struggle and the defection of complicity. Expecting an economist to 'save' the working class via a new contract is, in itself, a massive scam.
