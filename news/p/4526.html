AI 购物车：用“效率”掩盖的体力掠夺与认知入口AI Trolleys: Physical Plunder and Cognitive Entry Masked as 'Efficiency'

科技 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-25 § 链接
技术升级如果只改变支付方式而不改变体力分配，就是一种结构性暴力。
Tech upgrades that only change payment while ignoring physical labor are a form of structural violence.

把 AI 装在购物车里，然后告诉消费者“生活变得更简单了”，这是一个典型的 weaponized 叙事。在这种叙事中，“简单”被定义为：不需要排队，不需要手动扫描。但请注意，AI 改变的是支付的流程，而不是购物的本质——即一个人类个体在物理空间中推着沉重金属筐进行劳作的过程。

新闻里那个叫 Debbie Files 的女性精准地戳破了这个 scam：她抱怨购物车太重，难以转弯，甚至觉得自己需要“驾驶培训”。这是一个极其重要的生物墙信号。当技术开发者（通常是男性中心叙事下的工程师）设计这款产品时，他们预设的“标准用户”是一个拥有足够肌肉量、能够轻松操控沉重设备的生物个体。对于体力上限较低的女性或老人，这种所谓的“升级”实际上增加了他们的物理负担。这就是 structural violence：资源和技术的分配在潜意识里排除了非标准身体。

更深层的陷阱在于 Caper 购物车的“推荐产品”功能。这不再是简单的购物，而是一个实时监控的认知入口。AI 通过追踪你的每一个动作，在你的消费决策瞬间植入引导。你以为你在掌控预算，实际上你是在一个被算法精密计算的闭环里进行博弈。这种对解释权的垄断，让消费者从“购买者”变成了“被喂养者”。

这种“好新闻”的本质是：企业通过将结账环节的劳动力转移给 AI，同时将物理推行和认知被引导的代价全部转嫁给用户。这不是 Actual 朝 Potential 走近，而是一次极其高效的成本转移共谋。

Putting AI in a shopping trolley and claiming 'life is much easier' is a textbook example of weaponized narrative. Here, 'easier' is narrowly defined as the absence of queues and manual scanning. However, AI only alters the payment flow, not the essence of shopping: the physical labor of a human pushing a heavy metal cage through a store.

Debbie Files, mentioned in the news, accurately exposes the scam. She complains the trolley is too heavy and hard to turn, feeling she needs 'driving lessons.' This is a critical signal of the biological wall. When engineers—typically operating within a masculine-centric narrative—designed this product, their 'standard user' was a biological entity with sufficient muscle mass to handle heavy equipment. For women or the elderly with lower physical ceilings, this 'upgrade' actually increases their physical burden. This is structural violence: the distribution of technology subconsciously excludes non-standard bodies.

There is a deeper trap in the 'related product recommendations.' This is no longer just shopping; it is a real-time cognitive entry. By tracking every move, the AI implants guidance at the exact moment of decision. You think you are managing a budget, but you are actually gambling within a closed loop precisely calculated by algorithms. This monopoly over the interpretation of 'need' transforms the consumer from a 'buyer' into a 'fed subject.'

The essence of this 'good news' is a complicity of cost-shifting: the company replaces checkout labor with AI while transferring the physical strain and cognitive manipulation entirely onto the user. This is not Actual moving toward Potential; it is a highly efficient redistribution of exploitation.
