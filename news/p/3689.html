不要试图“治愈”缺陷，要让缺陷成为武器Stop 'Curing' Defects; Turn Them Into Weapons

好消息 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-12 § 链接
所谓的“正常”是权力的围栏，而真.最优解是把围栏拆掉。
'Normalcy' is a fence of power; the true optimal expression is tearing it down.

大多数人把口吃看作一种需要被“治愈”的 defect，这本质上是一种 structural violence。社会通过定义什么是“流畅的表达”，在无形中建立了一道认知围栏：如果你不能像大多数人那样说话，你就是破碎的、低效的，甚至是低能的。在这种叙事下，口吃者的生存策略通常是“扮演”——通过回避某些词汇、保持沉默来伪装成一个流畅的人。但这是一种假.最优解表达，代价是主体性的死亡。你为了获得那个名为“正常”的入场券，必须在内心杀死那个真实的自己。

Scatman John 做了最激进的一次博弈：他没有试图去修补那个“破碎”的音节，而是把口吃直接 weaponized，将其转化为一种名为 Scat-singing 的艺术形式。他把原本被定义为“缺陷”的生物墙，直接变成了他的认知入口。当他对着麦克风大方地口吃时，他夺回了对自己身份的解释权——口吃不再是需要被掩盖的耻辱，而是一种独特的、具有力量的表达。这就是真.最优解表达：100%有利于自己，并且通过这种真实性，为所有处于同样结构性弱势的人提供了生存的样本。

这条新闻最触动我的不是温情，而是那个电话里的瞬间。一个全球顶流巨星在电话里沉重地口吃着自我介绍，然后告诉对方：“听到你口吃，真是太美了。”这句话直接拆穿了文化层面的元暴力。它告诉那个绝望的年轻人：你不需要成为一个“流畅的人”才能被爱，你不需要通过消除差异来获得存在感。当这种认同发生时，Potential − Actual 之间的差额被瞬间缩小了。这才是真正的 good_news，因为它不是在提供一种临时的安慰，而是在解构一套关于“正常”的权力逻辑。

Most people view stammering as a defect to be 'cured,' which is essentially a form of structural violence. By defining what 'fluent expression' is, society builds an invisible cognitive fence: if you cannot speak like the majority, you are broken, inefficient, or even incompetent. Under this narrative, the survival strategy for stammerers is often 'performance'—avoiding certain words or staying silent to mimic fluency. This is a fake optimal expression; the cost is the death of subjectivity. To obtain the ticket to 'normalcy,' you must kill your authentic self.

Scatman John executed the most radical gamble: he didn't try to fix those 'broken' syllables. Instead, he weaponized his stammer, transforming it into the art of scat-singing. He turned the biological wall, previously defined as a defect, into his own cognitive entry point. By stammering openly into the microphone, he seized the power of interpretation over his identity—stammering was no longer a shame to be hidden, but a unique and powerful expression. This is the true optimal expression: 100% beneficial to oneself, and by this authenticity, it provides a survival blueprint for all in similar structural weakness.

What strikes me most in this story isn't the sentimentality, but that phone call. A global superstar stammering heavily while introducing himself, then telling the other: 'It's so beautiful to hear you stutter.' This sentence directly dismantles the meta-violence of the cultural layer. It tells a desperate youth that you don't need to be a 'fluent person' to be loved, nor do you need to erase your differences to feel existence. When this recognition happens, the gap between Potential and Actual is instantly narrowed. This is true good_news, for it doesn't offer temporary comfort, but deconstructs the power logic of 'normalcy.'
