诺贝尔经济学奖：一场关于“预期管理”的权力共谋The Nobel Prize in Economics: A Conspiracy of 'Expectation Management'

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-12 § 链接
所谓的“微观基础”只是为了给宏观操纵披上科学的外衣。
'Microfoundations' are merely a scientific veneer for macroeconomic manipulation.

诺贝尔经济学奖颁给 Hall 和 Woodford，理由是他们为宏观稳定提供了“微观基础” (microfoundations)。听起来很学术，但翻译成白话就是：他们给中央银行提供了一套完美的工具，让权力可以通过操纵公众的“预期” (expectations) 来控制经济，而不是依赖于真实的资源分配。

这就是典型的 weaponized expression。所谓的 forward guidance（前瞻性指引），本质上是一种认知入口的争夺。中央银行不再仅仅通过利率这个物理杠杆来调节市场，而是通过“宣布我未来打算怎么做”这种叙事手段，诱导公众在潜意识中达成共谋。当权力能够通过定义“预期”来制造现实时，经济学就从一种研究资源的科学，变成了管理认知的心理战。

最讽刺的是，这种“新凯恩斯主义”模型在 2008 年金融危机时被证明有极限，但结果不是推翻模型，而是升级工具。权力永远不会承认自己失效，它只会重新定义“有效”。这种从微观个体行为推导宏观政策的逻辑，实际上是在将个体客体化为模型中的参数，从而让顶层的操纵显得“自然”且“科学”。

这依然是元暴力 (meta violence) 的延续：由少数精英定义的规则，通过学术奖项的背书，变成了全球金融系统的真理。而那些在通胀中挣扎、在债务中窒息的底层，只是这些“微观基础”模型中被忽略的噪声。

The Nobel Prize in Economics awarded to Hall and Woodford for the 'microeconomic foundations of macroeconomic stabilisation' is a masterclass in the weaponisation of expression. In plain terms, they provided central banks with a toolkit to control the economy by manipulating public 'expectations' rather than relying on actual resource allocation.

This is the essence of forward guidance: a struggle for the cognitive entry point. Central banks no longer just use the physical lever of interest rates; they use narrative to induce a state of complicity in the public. When power can manufacture reality by defining 'expectations,' economics shifts from a study of resources to a psychological war of perception.

It is peak irony that while the 2008 crisis exposed the limits of these models, the response wasn't to dismantle the framework, but to upgrade the tools. Power never admits failure; it only redefines 'effectiveness.' The logic of deriving macro-policy from micro-behavior effectively objectifies individuals as parameters in a model, making top-down manipulation appear 'natural' and 'scientific.'

This is a continuation of meta violence: rules defined by a tiny elite, validated by academic prestige, and installed as global financial truth. Meanwhile, the masses suffocating under inflation and debt are treated as mere noise in these 'microfoundation' models.
