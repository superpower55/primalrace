反恐叙事是权力夺取认知入口的万能遮羞布Counter-terrorism Narratives: The Universal Cloak for Seizing Cognitive Entry Points

国际 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-29 § 链接
当权力无法通过技术掌控现实时，它会通过定义“恐怖主义”来重塑现实。
When power cannot control reality through technology, it reshapes reality by defining 'terrorism'.

俄罗斯对 Durov 的指控是一次典型的认知入口抢夺战。在权力逻辑中，所谓“支持恐怖主义”从来不是一个法律事实，而是一个 weaponized concept（武器化概念）。当克里姆林宫无法在技术层面彻底封死 Telegram 的加密墙，且无法在 structural layer（结构层）通过行政手段将其收编为像 VK 那样的共谋工具时，它必须在 cultural layer（文化层）制造一个足以让任何中立者噤声的叙事：反恐。

这套逻辑极其卑劣且高效：它将“不配合监管”等同于“资助恐怖”，从而把一个关于 privacy（隐私）和 free speech（言论自由）的博弈，强行扭转为一场关于“国家安全”的道德审判。这种叙事武器化最阴险的地方在于，它利用了全球对恐怖主义的普遍恐惧，试图通过定义“什么是恐怖”，来夺取对数字通信解释权的绝对垄断。

有趣的是，这出戏里充满了典型的 masculine-centric narrative（男性中心叙事）的共谋痕迹。普京在“国际妇女节”上通过一名女军人的口，将 Telegram 定义为“敌对工具”。这里的女性角色被精准地塑造为一个 a-political（非政治的）传声筒，她的存在不是为了表达主体意志，而是为了给最高权力者的决定提供一种“温情”且“多元”的背书。这又是典型的元暴力：女性的表达被武器化，用来掩盖男性权力顶端的残酷博弈。

从 Renault 的 CEO 到 Telegram 的创始人，权力在不同时期的逻辑切换之快，足以证明其所谓的“法治”不过是随用随弃的 PR 包装。本质上，这依然是那场关于“谁能定义事实”的存在性战争。权力不在乎谁在恐怖主义，它只在乎谁在掌控那个能发送信息的按钮。

Russia's charges against Durov are a textbook case of a struggle for cognitive entry points. In the logic of power, 'aiding terrorism' is never a legal fact; it is a weaponized concept. When the Kremlin fails to breach Telegram's encryption at the technical level, or fails to co-opt it into a tool of complicity like VK at the structural layer, it must manufacture a narrative at the cultural layer that silences all neutrals: counter-terrorism.

This logic is as vile as it is efficient: it equates 'non-compliance' with 'funding terror,' forcibly pivoting a game of privacy and free speech into a moral trial of 'national security.' The most insidious part of this weaponization is how it exploits the global fear of terrorism to seize an absolute monopoly over the interpretation of digital communication.

Notably, this performance is riddled with the traces of masculine-centric narrative and complicity. Putin, during an 'International Women's Day' meeting, used a servicewoman to label Telegram an 'adversarial tool.' Here, the female character is precisely cast as an a-political mouthpiece. Her presence is not to express a subjective will, but to provide a 'soft' and 'diverse' endorsement for the decisions of the highest power. This is meta-violence in action: feminine expression is weaponized to mask the brutal gambles of male power at the top.

From the CEO of Renault to the founder of Telegram, the speed at which power switches its logic proves that its so-called 'rule of law' is nothing more than disposable PR packaging. At its core, this is still an existential war over 'who defines reality.' Power doesn't care who is actually a terrorist; it only cares who controls the button that sends the message.
