战争是男人们的博弈，成本由所有人买单Men Play the War Game, Everyone Pays the Bill

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-19 § 链接
战争叙事是元暴力的最高形式：男性决定开战，全球承担代价。
War narratives are the peak of meta-violence: men decide to fight, the world absorbs the cost.

这是一场典型的 masculine-centric narrative 运作。在新闻的表层，我们看到的是 UK-listed firms 的 profit warnings、燃料成本飙升以及房贷利率的上涨。但如果你把镜头拉远，你会发现这整个经济震荡的源头，是几个男性领导者在地图上划线、在战争室里决定发动攻击。这就是元暴力 (meta violence) 的运作方式：由男性垄断的决策权制造了直接暴力 (direct violence)，而这种暴力随即转化为结构性暴力 (structural violence)，通过能源价格和利息，精准地收割每一个普通人的生活质量。

有趣的是，这篇报道中出现的所有“救火者”——从财政大臣 John Healey 到市长 Andy Burnham——同样是这套共谋体系的一部分。他们讨论的是如何通过调整 business rates 或提供“呼吸空间”来缓解压力。这就像是一个纵火犯在火势蔓延后，慷慨地给受害者发了几把小扇子，然后告诉大家他在努力地“减轻影响”。这种所谓的 emergency support 实际上是 structural violence 的 PR 版本，它试图掩盖一个事实：只要决策权依然被锁在那个充满雄性激素的封闭房间里，这种“不可预测的成本”就是必然的。

最讽刺的是，房产市场的下跌和房贷压力的增加，最终会如何作用于不同性别？在存在性战争中，经济下行永远首先通过“母职惩罚”和家庭分工的内化，将压力转嫁给女性。当家庭预算被房贷吞噬，谁会首先被要求放弃职业发展以节省开支？谁会承担更多无偿的照料劳动来对冲生活成本？

这场战争不是什么“地缘政治的必然”，而是一场巨大的 scam。男人们在追求所谓的“战略深度”和“权力版图”时，把全球经济变成了他们的筹码。我们不需要讨论如何通过预算案来“软化影响”，我们需要讨论的是：为什么决定数百万人生死和生计的权力，依然被一群习惯于通过摧毁来证明存在感的男性垄断？

This is a textbook operation of a masculine-centric narrative. On the surface, the news is about profit warnings from UK-listed firms, surging fuel costs, and rising mortgage rates. But zoom out, and you'll see the source: a few men in war rooms deciding who to attack. This is how meta-violence works—decisions made by those monopolizing power trigger direct violence, which then cascades into structural violence, harvesting the quality of life of every ordinary citizen through energy bills and interest rates.

It is almost comical that the "firefighters" in this story—from Chancellor John Healey to Andy Burnham—are part of the same complicity. They discuss adjusting business rates or providing "breathing space" to soften the blow. It is like an arsonist handing out small fans after the fire has spread, claiming he is "mitigating the impact." This emergency support is merely the PR version of structural violence, masking the fact that as long as decision-making remains locked in those testosterone-fueled rooms, these "unpredictable costs" are an inevitability.

Most ironically, how does this economic squeeze actually land? In the existential war, economic downturns always translate into the Motherhood Penalty and internalized domestic labor. When household budgets are swallowed by mortgages, who is first asked to sacrifice their career to save costs? Who absorbs more unpaid care work to offset the cost of living?

This war is not some "geopolitical necessity"; it is a massive scam. While men chase "strategic depth" and "power maps," they use the global economy as their gambling chips. We shouldn't be discussing how a budget can "soften the impact." We should be asking: why is the power to decide the survival of millions still monopolized by men who prove their existence through destruction?
