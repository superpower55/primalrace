股东价值与生命价值的负相关律The Inverse Law of Shareholder Value and Human Life

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-07 § 链接
所谓的‘适量食用’是工业资本为延迟监管而制造的文化伪装。
The 'moderation' narrative is a cultural camouflage designed by industrial capital to delay regulation.

超加工食品（UPF）巨头在全世界发起的数百起诉讼，本质上是一场关于‘解释权’的绝望防御。它们试图通过法律手段将‘产品有害’这个事实，稀释为‘饮食习惯不健康’这个个体责任。这套逻辑极其阴险：只要我定义‘没有单一的垃圾食品，只有糟糕的整体饮食’，我就能把结构性暴力（structural violence）转化为个体的选择问题。

这是一种典型的武器化叙事（weaponized narrative）。就像烟草公司当年一样，它们利用疾病潜伏期的时滞，在文化层面上构建一套‘适量即可’的迷思，从而在认知入口处拦截公众的警觉。当可口可乐或百事可乐在法庭上主张‘商业表达自由’时，它们在共谋一个极其残酷的事实：股东的 dividend 必须建立在公众血管中糖分超标的基础上。这种‘增加的股东价值’与‘人类营养价值’成反比，是资本主义最诚实的数学公式。

最讽刺的共谋发生在政府层面。美国和欧盟长期补贴玉米、大豆和蔗糖，将这些廉价作物转化为工业原料，再通过地缘政治战略向全球倾销。这意味着，国家机器在结构层面上为 UPF 提供了最底层的成本优势，然后又在文化层面上扮演‘公共卫生守护者’。这种 schizophrenic 的状态，让 UPF 成了全球性的生物攻击。

好在 GLP-1 等减肥药的出现，从生物学维度强行打破了这种博弈。当人们的欲望被药物物理性地削减，工业资本通过‘浪漫化口味’和‘便利性’构建的认知入口失效了。这场存在性战争的胜负手不在于法律条文，而在于我们能否拆穿那个 scam：任何试图让你在‘毒药’中寻找‘适量’的建议，都是为了给资本争取更多收割时间的 PR 话术。

The hundreds of lawsuits launched by Ultra-Processed Food (UPF) giants globally are a desperate defense of the 'right to interpret'. They attempt to dilute the fact of 'harmful products' into the narrative of 'poor dietary habits', shifting structural violence onto individual responsibility. The logic is cynical: by claiming there is no such thing as a 'junk food' but only 'poor diets', they transform a systemic assault into a matter of personal choice.

This is a textbook case of weaponized narrative. Much like the tobacco industry, they exploit the latency of degenerative diseases to build a cultural myth of 'moderation', intercepting public vigilance at the cognitive entry point. When Coca-Cola or PepsiCo argue for 'freedom of commercial expression' in court, they are complicit in a brutal truth: shareholder dividends are predicated on the hyperglycemia of the masses. The 'added shareholder value' is in inverse proportion to human nutritional value—the most honest formula of capitalism.

The most profound complicity occurs at the state level. US and EU subsidies for corn, soy, and sugar provide the structural foundation for UPF's cost advantage, which is then exported via geopolitical strategies. The state machine provides the raw materials for the assault while simultaneously performing the role of 'public health guardian'. This schizophrenic state makes UPF a global biological attack.

Fortunately, the emergence of GLP-1 drugs has forcibly disrupted this game at the biological level. As appetite is physically reduced, the cognitive entry points built on 'flavor' and 'convenience' collapse. The turning point of this existential war is not found in legal texts, but in our ability to expose the scam: any advice suggesting you find 'moderation' within a poison is merely a PR tactic to buy more harvesting time for capital.
