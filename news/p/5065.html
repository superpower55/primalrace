用全球碳排放给尼泊尔人买单Paying for Global Carbon with Nepalese Lives

国际 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-16 § 链接
气候灾难是跨空间的结构性暴力，弱势者的生命是强权的外部成本。
Climate disaster is cross-spatial structural violence; the lives of the vulnerable are the external costs of hegemony.

1300条生命，5000人失踪。在主流叙事里，这被描述成一场“罕见的自然灾害”或“地质不稳定”。但帝国学院的这项研究撕开了伪装：这本质上是一场跨空间的结构性暴力 (structural violence)。

按照加尔通的暴力三角，当一个社区本可以拥有安全的生存环境，却因为数千英里外化石燃料的排放导致冰川崩塌而毁灭时，这个 Potential 与 Actual 的差额就是暴力。尼泊尔和西藏的村民在为他们从未参与的工业化繁荣支付血税。这是一种极端的非对称博弈：排放权在发达国家和工业巨头手中，而生存风险被外包给了全球最脆弱的 frontline communities。

最讽刺的是，尼泊尔政府现在必须向联合国申请“损失与损害基金”，并且需要提供“科学证明”来确认这场灾难与气候危机有关，才能拿到救济金。这种机制本身就是一种元暴力 (meta violence) —— 施暴者定义了赔偿的门槛，要求受害者用施暴者认可的语言和标准来证明自己的痛苦。这种“证明责任”的转移，是典型的权力不对等。

不要被所谓的“自然因素”或“地质复杂性”给骗了。当全球气温升高导致永久冻土线每年上升100米，冰川变成了随时会坍塌的定时炸弹，这就不再是自然演化，而是被武器化的碳排放对地球物理结构的强行改写。这场灾难不是一个孤立的 case，而是一个预告：在目前的全球共谋 (complicity) 结构下，只要碳排放的定价权依然在资本手中，弱势种族就永远是这场存在性战争中被牺牲的筹码。

1,300 dead, 5,000 missing. In mainstream narratives, this is framed as a 'rare natural disaster' or 'geological instability.' But the study from Imperial College tears off the mask: this is essentially structural violence on a cross-spatial scale.

Applying Galtung's Violence Triangle, when a community's potential for a safe environment is stripped away by fossil fuel emissions thousands of miles away, the gap between Potential and Actual is violence. The people of Nepal and Tibet are paying a blood tax for an industrial prosperity they never participated in. This is an extreme asymmetric game: the right to emit is held by developed nations and industrial giants, while the existential risk is outsourced to the most vulnerable frontline communities.

The most cynical part is that the Nepalese government must now apply for the UN's 'loss and damage fund,' requiring 'scientific proof' to link the disaster to the climate crisis to receive aid. This mechanism is a form of meta-violence — the aggressors define the threshold for compensation, forcing the victims to prove their suffering using the language and standards approved by the aggressors. This shift in the 'burden of proof' is a classic manifestation of power imbalance.

Don't be fooled by 'natural factors' or 'geological complexity.' When global warming pushes the freezing threshold up by 100 meters per decade, turning glaciers into ticking time bombs, it is no longer natural evolution. It is the forceful rewriting of Earth's physical structure by weaponized carbon emissions. This event is not an isolated case, but a preview: as long as the pricing power of carbon remains with capital within the current global complicity, vulnerable populations will always be the expendable pawns in this existential war.
