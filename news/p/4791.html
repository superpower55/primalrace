足球场上的“家庭”叙事与共谋的温情The 'Family' Narrative in Football and the Warmth of Complicity

哲学 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-05 § 链接
用“家庭”叙事包装的集体主义，是掩盖资源掠夺的文化暴力。
The romanticization of 'family' in collective narratives is a cultural violence masking structural exploitation.

一场无关痛痒的平局，却被报道成了一出关于“家庭”与“梦想”的温情剧。球场上飘扬的“One family, one dream”横幅，以及全场齐唱的《Can’t Help Falling in Love》，在典型的男性中心叙事（masculine-centric narrative）中，这被定义为“凝聚力”和“渴望”。

但这种对“家庭”的浪漫化表达本质上是一种武器化叙事。在职业体育这个极端的博弈场中，所谓的“One family”实际上是一种高效的共谋机制：它要求个体将主体性让渡给组织，将对高强度压榨的忍受内化为对“梦想”的忠诚。当俱乐部用“家庭”来定义雇佣关系时，它实际上是在模糊权力边界，使得球员在面对不公正待遇或伤病风险时，难以以纯粹的劳动者身份进行博弈。

这种叙事在足球界具有极强的惯性，它将男性之间的竞争和结盟包装成一种神圣的血缘纽带，从而在文化层（cultural layer）上合法化了对身体的过度消耗。一个19岁少年在生日当天完成关键封堵被赞美为“韧性”，而在这种叙事下，身体的损耗被转化为精神的勋章。

我们习惯于在体育新闻中消费这种热血，但必须意识到，这种“温情”是建立在对个体主体性消解的基础之上的。当“家庭”成为管理工具，它就不再是避风港，而是一个精巧的共谋场域。

A mediocre stalemate is framed as a heartwarming drama of 'family' and 'dreams.' The 'One family, one dream' banner and the collective singing of Elvis Presley are, in a masculine-centric narrative, defined as 'cohesion' and 'desire.'

However, this romanticized expression of 'family' is essentially a weaponized narrative. In the extreme game of professional sports, 'One family' is an efficient mechanism of complicity: it demands that individuals surrender their subjectivity to the organization and internalize the endurance of high-intensity exploitation as loyalty to a 'dream.' When a club defines an employment relationship as a 'family,' it blurs power boundaries, making it harder for players to negotiate as pure laborers when facing injustice or injury risks.

This narrative possesses strong inertia in football, packaging competition and alliance among men as a sacred blood bond, thereby legitimizing the over-consumption of the physical body at the cultural layer. A 19-year-old's vital block on his birthday is praised as 'resilience,' while physical attrition is converted into a spiritual medal.

We are accustomed to consuming this passion in sports news, but we must realize that this 'warmth' is built upon the erasure of individual subjectivity. When 'family' becomes a management tool, it ceases to be a sanctuary and becomes a sophisticated field of complicity.
