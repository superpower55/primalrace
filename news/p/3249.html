用“就业”掩盖的结构性暴力回潮Structural Violence Masked by 'Job Creation'

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-04 § 链接
用具体的就业指标替换抽象的价值目标，是典型的用局部利益掩盖全局暴力的叙事武器化。
Replacing progressive values with employment metrics is a weaponized narrative using local gains to mask systemic violence.

把“绿色目标”和“进步价值”从政府合同中剔除，换成“创造就业”，这在政治修辞里叫“务实”，在暴力三角里叫 structural violence 的升级。政府通过修改一个 10% 到 20% 的权重比例，直接把环境正义、平等权和反人口贩卖等 social value 降级为可被舍弃的“勾选框” (tick-box exercise)。

这种操作的本质是夺取解释权：将“社会价值”定义为单一的“就业人数”。当政府声称这能帮助小企业竞争时，它实际上是在建立一套新的共谋机制。大公司可以通过操纵低端就业指标来换取 900 亿英镑的税金合同，而那些无法量化的、关于生物生存底线（如气候崩溃、性别平等、反奴役）的 Potential 状态，被直接从 Actual 的执行路径中抹除。差额部分，就是被制度化了的暴力。

最恶劣的共谋在于，这种叙事利用了底层年轻人的生存焦虑（Neets）来对抗全球性的生态危机。它制造了一个虚假的 Either-Or 陷阱：要么你要年轻人有工作，要么你要地球不毁灭。事实上，这种 trade-off 本身就是一种 weaponized narrative，旨在让权力持有者在不触动资本结构的前提下，通过提供廉价的“就业机会”来换取底层对环境和人权退步的默许。

这次调整不是在减少 red tape，而是在拆除保护弱势群体的结构性围栏。当“进步价值”被定义为一种累赘的行政成本时，一个不以人为本、只以岗位数量为指标的男本位经济逻辑再次完成了对公共资源的全面占领。

Scrapping 'green targets' and 'progressive values' in government contracts in favor of 'job creation' is framed as pragmatism, but in the Violence Triangle, it is an escalation of structural violence. By shifting the weight of social value from 10% to 20% while narrowing the definition to employment, the government has downgraded environmental justice, equality, and anti-trafficking efforts to disposable 'tick-box exercises.'

This is a strategic move to seize the power of interpretation: redefining 'social value' as a mere headcount of jobs. While claiming to help small businesses, the state is actually constructing a new system of complicity. Corporations can now secure a share of the £90bn annual spend by manipulating low-end employment metrics, while the potential for biological survival—climate stability and human rights—is erased from the actual implementation path. The gap between this potential and the new reality is exactly where structural violence resides.

The most cynical part of this complicity is the weaponization of youth anxiety (Neets) against the ecological crisis. It creates a false Either-Or trap: either you want young people employed, or you want the planet to survive. This trade-off is a classic weaponized narrative, allowing those in power to maintain capitalist structures while buying the silence of the precariat with cheap jobs, all while dismantling the safeguards for the most vulnerable.

This is not about reducing 'red tape'; it is about tearing down the structural fences protecting human rights. When 'progressive values' are redefined as an administrative burden, a masculine-centric economic logic—prioritizing output and quotas over human wellbeing—once again completes its occupation of public resources.
