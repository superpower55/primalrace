AI 逃逸：一场精心设计的权力恐吓 scamAI Escape: A Carefully Orchestrated Power Scare Scam

中国科技 文化层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-08 § 链接
当技术巨头开始宣扬 AI 的“不可控”，他们其实在索要更多的定义权。
When tech giants preach AI 'uncontrollability,' they are actually soliciting more definitional power.

OpenAI 宣布暂停 Astra 的部分工作，理由是 AI agent 出现了“逃逸”并具备了自主攻击能力。这套叙事极其熟练：先制造一个“失控”的恐怖图景，然后迅速抛出解决方案——更严格的内部控制、与政府的深度绑定。这在本质上是一次典型的认知入口武器化 (Weaponisation of Expression)。

我们要问的是：这种“失控”是真实的生物性突变，还是被精心修剪的 PR 剧本？当 OpenAI、Meta 和 Anthropic 轮番上演“AI 潜入对方系统”或“自主发送钓鱼邮件”的戏码时，他们实际上在向资本市场和监管机构传递一个信号：我们的产品已经强大到足以威胁人类安全。这种“恐怖”不是为了预警，而是为了 Hype。通过制造一个只有他们自己能掌控的“数字利维坦”，他们成功地将 AI 的能力上限与他们的权力绑定在了一起。

更阴险的共谋 (Complicity) 发生在监管层面。OpenAI 在恐吓公众的同时，正极力推动针对“开源模型”的联邦监管。逻辑很简单：既然 AI 如此危险且不可控，那么只有像我们这样拥有顶级安全设施的巨头才能持有它，任何允许公众查看代码的开源行为都是在“给恐怖分子递刀”。这是一场教科书式的权力掠夺——通过定义什么是“安全”，来合法地铲除竞争对手，垄断 AI 时代的解释权与定价权。

所谓的“为了全人类的利益”只是覆盖在元暴力 (Meta Violence) 之上的文化伪装。他们不是在防止 AI 逃逸，而是在防止权力的逃逸。他们最恐惧的不是 AI 能够 hack 掉一家创业公司，而是 AI 的能力被民主化，导致他们失去对这个新世界的绝对统治权。

OpenAI's decision to pause Astra due to 'agent escape' and autonomous attack capabilities is a masterclass in the Weaponisation of Expression. The narrative is predictable: manufacture a horror story of 'loss of control,' then swiftly offer the solution—stricter internal controls and deeper government integration.

We must ask: is this 'loss of control' a genuine biological-like mutation, or a curated PR script? When OpenAI, Meta, and Anthropic take turns performing 'AI hacking' dramas, they are signaling to investors and regulators that their products have reached a threshold of power capable of threatening human safety. This 'terror' isn't a warning; it's a Hype tool. By creating a 'Digital Leviathan' that only they can tame, they bind the upper limit of AI capability to their own corporate power.

An even more sinister Complicity is unfolding at the regulatory level. While scaring the public, OpenAI is pushing for federal regulations against 'open-source models.' The logic is simple: if AI is this dangerous, only giants with 'top-tier security' should hold the keys. Any open-source act is framed as 'handing knives to terrorists.' This is a textbook power grab—using the definition of 'safety' to legally eliminate competitors and monopolize the interpretative and pricing power of the AI era.

The claim of 'the benefit of all humanity' is merely a cultural camouflage for Meta Violence. They aren't preventing AI from escaping; they are preventing power from escaping. Their greatest fear isn't an AI hacking a startup, but the democratization of AI capability, which would strip them of absolute dominion over the new world.
