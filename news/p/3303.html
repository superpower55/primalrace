拒绝扮演“中间派”：一场关于存在性的政治博弈Rejecting the 'Centrist' Masquerade: An Existential Political Gamble

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-06 § 链接
所谓的“可选举性”是建制派用以掩盖结构暴力的叙事陷阱。
The so-called 'electability' is a narrative trap used by the establishment to mask structural violence.

民主党建制派长期把持着一种名为“中道”的认知入口，告诉所有想要改变现状的人：如果你想赢，你必须扮演一个温和、模糊、不冒犯资本的角色。这本质上是一次关于主体性的 scam。在这种叙事下，候选人被要求在“可选举性” (electability) 的假面具下杀死自己的真.最优解表达，从而成为一个符合权力结构预期的客体。

Abdul El-Sayed 的这次初选胜利，实际上是撕开了这层文化暴力的伪装。他拒绝在“中间地带”进行表演，而是直接将博弈点拉回到 Actual 与 Potential 的差额上——全民医疗、反对种族灭绝。当他用 48.5% 的票数击败那个被数千万美元资本背书的“温和派”时，他证明了：在底层民众面对医疗成本激增和生活成本崩溃的 Structural Violence 时，真实的、敢于命名痛苦的表达，比精心修剪的“中立”更有力量。

但这还不是最终的胜利。共和党已经迅速启动了武器化叙事，用 AI 生成的特洛伊木马将他标签化为“共产主义”或“疯狂”。这是典型的元暴力操作：通过定义对方为“异类”来剥夺其解释权。El-Sayed 面对的不再仅仅是一个对手，而是一套习惯于将进步主义定义为“极端”的共谋机制。

这场战争的真正看点在于，如果他能在接下来的大选中获胜，他将彻底摧毁建制派关于“中间路线”的定价权。一个不扮演、不妥协的真.最优解表达如果能赢得权力，那么之前所有关于“必须温和才能获胜”的教条都将变成一个巨大的笑话。

The Democratic establishment has long monopolized a cognitive entrance called 'the center,' telling anyone seeking change that to win, they must perform as a moderate, vague, and corporate-friendly character. This is essentially a scam of subjectivity. Under this narrative, candidates are pressured to kill their true Optimal Expression behind a mask of 'electability,' becoming mere objects that fit the expectations of the power structure.

Abdul El-Sayed’s primary victory effectively tears through this veil of cultural violence. He refused to perform in the 'middle ground' and instead pulled the game back to the gap between Actual and Potential—universal healthcare and opposing genocide. By defeating a 'moderate' backed by tens of millions of dollars in corporate spending with 48.5% of the vote, he proved that when the working class faces the Structural Violence of healthcare and cost-of-living crises, a real expression that dares to name the pain is more powerful than a meticulously pruned 'neutrality.'

Yet, this is not the final victory. The Right has swiftly deployed weaponized narratives, using AI-generated Trojan horses to label him as 'communism' or 'crazy.' This is a classic operation of meta-violence: stripping away the right to interpret by defining the other as 'alien.' El-Sayed is no longer fighting just an opponent, but a complicity mechanism that habitually defines progressivism as 'extreme.'

The real stakes of this war lie in the fact that if he wins the general election, he will utterly destroy the establishment's pricing power over the 'centrist route.' If a true Optimal Expression—one that refuses to act or compromise—can seize power, then all previous dogmas about 'needing to be moderate to win' will be revealed as one giant scam.
