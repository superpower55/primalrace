Netflix 的新骗局：用“人性复杂”为暴力洗白Netflix's New Scam: Whitewashing Violence as 'Human Complexity'

性别 直接层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-13 § 链接
当暴力被包装成“特立独行”的叙事，受害者就成了背景板。
When violence is packaged as 'uniqueness', the victim becomes mere background noise.

Netflix 这部关于泰森的纪录片，是典型的 weaponized expression。它披着“人性深度”的外衣，实际上在进行一场极其恶劣的文化暴力 (cultural violence)。它把一个充满暴力前科的男人塑造成一个类似曼德拉的“幸存者”，通过展示他的贫穷、丧女之痛以及现在的药物幻觉，试图诱导观众产生共情，从而抵消他曾经对他人的直接暴力 (direct violence)。

最令人作呕的是对女性暴力的处理。面对家暴指控，泰森的回答是“我们打了起来”——这种典型的 masculine-centric narrative 将权力不对等地抹除，把施暴伪装成“冲突”。而面对强奸指控时的轻笑，则是对受害者主体性的二次抹杀。纪录片在结尾加上一个 survivors 资源网站的免责声明，这简直是最高级的 scam：它在同一部作品里既实施了暴力，又在表演“关心”，用一个 PR 动作掩盖结构性的残忍。

特朗普的出现则完成了这场共谋 (complicity) 的闭环。两个在权力结构顶端、习惯于通过定义“事实”来操纵现实的男性在屏幕上互认，他们将法律的裁决定义为“媒体的残酷”，将暴力定义为“真实”。这不再是纪录片，而是一次元暴力 (meta violence) 的权力展示：只要我足够“特殊”，我就可以定义什么是真理，而那些被我伤害的人，只是我通往“神圣”道路上的噪音。

Netflix's new Tyson docuseries is a textbook example of weaponized expression. Cloaked in the guise of 'human depth', it is actually performing a foul act of cultural violence. By showcasing his poverty, the tragedy of his daughter's death, and his current drug-induced hallucinations, the series attempts to engineer empathy to offset his history of direct violence.

Most repulsive is the handling of violence against women. Facing domestic abuse allegations, Tyson claims "we fought"—a classic masculine-centric narrative that erases power imbalances and disguises abuse as a mutual "conflict". His laughter when discussing his rape conviction is a secondary erasure of the victim's subjectivity. The disclaimer and link to survivors' resources at the end is the ultimate scam: the show commits the violence and then performs "care" in the same breath, using a PR gesture to mask structural cruelty.

Trump's cameo completes the loop of complicity. Two men at the top of the power structure, accustomed to manipulating reality by defining "facts", validate each other. They redefine legal convictions as "media cruelty" and violence as "truth". This isn't a documentary; it's a demonstration of meta violence: if I am "special" enough, I can define truth, and those I've harmed are merely noise on my path to "sanctity".
