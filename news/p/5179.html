Lauren Boebert 的表演：当权力在扮演“纯洁”Lauren Boebert's Performance: Power Playing the 'Pure' Card

权力者的否认不是事实之争，而是对解释权的防御性占领。
A powerful person's denial is not a factual dispute, but a defensive occupation of the right to interpret.

Lauren Boebert 的这次否认是一场典型的 existence war。面对关于与下属发生性关系并支付封口费的指控，她的第一反应不是法律层面的辩护，而是通过 verbose video rant 将指控者定义为 "hack"，将报道定义为 "scam"。这种策略的本质是试图通过夺取认知入口 (cognitive entry)，在事实被核实之前，先在文化层面上把对方定义为“政治敌手”而非“事实揭露者”。

最讽刺的在于她的身份表达。Boebert 长期将自己包装成 Christian values 的捍卫者，攻击 LGBTQ+ 议程，但在权力结构中，她涉嫌将下属转化为性客体并用金钱买断其沉默。这揭示了一个残酷的 structural violence：在权力的不对等面前，所谓的“自愿” (consent) 毫无意义。当一个议员能够决定员工的薪水、职业前景甚至将其变为 "ghost employee" 时，这种关系本质上是权力的掠夺。她通过扮演一个“纯洁的保守主义者”来获得政治票票价值，而私下里却利用这种权力进行最典型的 masculine-centric 掠夺。

她对记者的辱骂以及对“性别主义” (sexist stuff) 的指责，实际上是一种武器化的表达。她试图将自己置于“受害者”的位置，以掩盖她作为权力上位者的施暴者身份。这种共谋不仅发生在她与被收买的下属之间，更发生在她与那些只看重她的“保守标签”而选择性失明的支持者之间。无论她是否真的是 lesbian 或 bisexual，这在本质上不是性取向问题，而是权力滥用问题。当一个权力者试图用“我不是 X”来否认“我做了 Y”时，她已经在进行一场拙劣的叙事欺诈。

Lauren Boebert's denial is a textbook case of an existential war. Faced with allegations of sexual relationships with staff and hush-money payments, her first instinct wasn't a legal defense, but a verbose video rant labeling the accuser a "hack" and the reporting a "scam." This is a strategic attempt to seize the cognitive entry, defining the opponent as a "political enemy" rather than a "truth-teller" before the facts are even substantiated.

The irony lies in her expression of identity. Boebert has long branded herself as a defender of Christian values and a critic of the LGBTQ+ agenda, yet in the structural reality, she allegedly transformed subordinates into sexual objects and bought their silence. This reveals a stark structural violence: in the face of extreme power asymmetry, "consent" is a meaningless term. When a congresswoman controls a staffer's salary and career—even turning them into a "ghost employee"—the relationship is fundamentally one of predatory power. She plays the role of the "pure conservative" to increase her political value while practicing the most classic masculine-centric exploitation.

Her abuse of journalists and accusations of "sexist stuff" are weaponized expressions. She attempts to pivot into the role of the "victim" to mask her status as a predator in the power hierarchy. This complicity exists not only between her and the bought-off staff but also between her and the supporters who choose to be blind as long as her "conservative label" remains intact. Whether she is a lesbian or bisexual is irrelevant; this is not a question of sexual orientation, but of power abuse. When a power-holder tries to deny "I did Y" by claiming "I am not X," she is engaged in a clumsy narrative fraud.
