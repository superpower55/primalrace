用弱势者的血，喂养权力者的PlayStationFeeding Power's PlayStation with the Blood of the Vulnerable

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-16 § 链接
慈善工业的本质是权力对弱势资源的二次掠夺与共谋。
The charity industry is essentially a secondary predation and complicity of power over vulnerable resources.

这起丑闻最令人作呕的不是金额，而是那些 misappropriated 资金的去向：高端餐厅、夜总会，甚至是个人的 PlayStation 充值。这不仅仅是 wire fraud，这是一场极其典型的存在性战争中的掠夺。在加尔通的暴力三角里，这属于 structural violence 的顶端——那些本该用于缩小无家可归者 Potential 与 Actual 之间差额的公共资源，被转化为权力者用来维持其阶级表型 (Phenotype) 的消费符号。

所谓的 homelessness non-profits 在这里成了完美的 weaponized 认知入口。它们利用“救助弱势”的叙事掩盖内部的权力运作，将受助者客体化为获取 grant funding 的筹码。在这种结构中，无家可归者不是被救助的主体，而是被用来兑换现金的“资源”。这种共谋 (complicity) 发生在非营利组织内部，甚至延伸到政府监管的缺失，形成了一个封闭的利益环路。

而最讽刺的转折在于，这场清理行动被包装在 Trump administration 的政治叙事之下。当 JD Vance 被命名为 "fraud czar"，这种打击腐败的行为在本质上不再是为了保障人权，而是一次政治博弈中的认知战争。将“蓝色州”定义为 "crooked"，是通过定义对方的“不洁”来确立自身的“正义”。

在这种 meta-violence 的权力游戏中，无家可归者的生存困境依然是那个被牺牲的底色。无论是被非营利组织洗劫，还是被政治博弈当作攻击对方的弹药，弱势者的主体性在这一过程中被彻底抹除。他们只是这出权力剧本里的背景板，而那些真正的掠夺者，无论是在非营利组织里充值游戏卡的，还是在华盛顿操纵叙事的，都在共享同一套掠夺逻辑。

The most revolting part of this scandal isn't the amount of money, but where that misappropriated funding went: high-end restaurants, nightclubs, and even personal PlayStation charges. This is not merely wire fraud; it is a classic act of predation within an existential war. In Galtung's Violence Triangle, this is the peak of structural violence—public resources intended to close the gap between the Potential and Actual state of the homeless were converted into consumption symbols used by the powerful to maintain their Phenotype.

These so-called homelessness non-profits served as perfect weaponized cognitive entries. They used the narrative of "helping the vulnerable" to mask internal power dynamics, objectifying the homeless into mere chips for securing grant funding. In this structure, the homeless are not subjects to be helped, but resources to be cashed in. This complicity exists within the non-profits and extends to the failure of government oversight, forming a closed loop of mutual benefit.

The most ironic twist is that this cleanup is wrapped in the political narrative of the Trump administration. When JD Vance is named "fraud czar," the act of fighting corruption is no longer about safeguarding human rights, but a cognitive war within a political game. By defining "blue states" as "crooked," they establish their own "justice" by defining the other as "impure."

Under this meta-violence, the survival struggle of the homeless remains the sacrificed backdrop. Whether being plundered by non-profits or used as ammunition in political warfare, the subjectivity of the vulnerable is completely erased. They are merely background scenery in this power play, while the true predators—from those charging game cards in non-profits to those manipulating narratives in Washington—share the exact same logic of predation.
