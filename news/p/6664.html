投票前先制造混乱，这就是统治术Manufacture Chaos, Then Seize the Election

国际 结构层 · 文化层 ProPublica ↗ 2026-11-02 § 链接
先让选举看起来不可信，再以救世主姿态接管它
Make the vote look rigged, then arrive as its rescuer.

ProPublica 这篇报道里最该被拎出来的一句话，是那位被特朗普解职的选举协助委员会委员 Ben Hovland 说的：政府正在制造大量噪音、混乱和怀疑，结果是选民干脆不投了。这就是全部机制。不是舞弊，是让人相信有舞弊；不需要真的拦住谁投票，只需要让一部分人自己放弃。

加尔通的公式在这里算得很清楚：可达到的投票率与实际投票率之间的差额，就是暴力。这一差额由谁制造？由那些声称在保护选举的人制造。DHS 把选民名册和自己的移民数据、第三方数据混在一起，得出“疑似非公民”名单，加州那两万人的名单被该州称为“站不住脚”，洛杉矶县抽样分析后发现，很多人根本不在该县登记，或者从未投过票，还有些人在登记表上写着自己出生在美国。华盛顿州收到的四百人名单，州务卿直接说不可靠，里面有美国公民、有没登记的人、有重复条目。名单是假的，但名单带来的恐惧是真的。一个选民想的是：我别上那个名单，我不登记了，我不投了。

这就是第三章那套东西：夺取认知入口，制造“什么是事实”的权力。特朗普政府没有证据地宣称选举充斥舞弊，然后围绕这个虚构的事实部署资源、发传票、发威胁信。联邦法官把司法部对加州的要求称作“恐吓计划”，并下令阻止；另一名法官限制了 DHS 挨家挨户找疑似非公民选民的做法，说那是非法恐吓合法选民。法院一次次挡住，但挡不住那个已经投放出去的印象。木已成舟，舆论先跑完了。

佛罗里达 Leon County 那件事是个微缩模型：选举监督官 Mark Earley 得到过政府反复保证，不会有联邦探员出现在投票站，结果两辆标着海关与边境保护局的车停在该县最忙的提前投票站门口。他愤怒，上报 FBI，最后发现那两人只是路过想自己投票。Earley 说，考虑到媒体对联邦探员出现在投票站的负面报道，他们开着公务车来“显示出相当糟糕的判断力”。但更值得看的是他一开始为什么愤怒——因为他知道，车停在那里这件事本身，就是一次表达，一次投放，不管车里的人想干什么。

再看阿拉斯加那起诉讼。司法部要拦住该州认证参议员选举结果，理由是另一个也叫 Dan Sullivan 的人上了 ballot，会让选民困惑。特朗普说自己亲自下令提告。报道写明，联邦法律禁止政府干预正在进行的选举，挑战通常由败选者在投票结束后于州法院提出。联邦法官周日驳回了这起诉讼。但重点不在输赢，在于它演示了一件事：结果不合意时，联邦政府会动手。这就是给十一月之后准备的工具箱。

值得注意的是谁在共谋、回报是什么。共和党全国委员会说他们把投票监督的重点放在选民资格和名册准确性上，民主党放在恐吓和投票障碍上，双方都说自己扩大了监督规模。一边在制造怀疑，一边在准备应对怀疑，两边都在这个被加压的选举里找到了自己的位置。而被删掉的东西不在报道里：那些因为恐惧而没来投票的人不会上新闻，他们的名字不会出现在任何名单上，他们只是消失了。这才是这篇报道里最安静的一部分。

还有一层：CISA 被拆了。2024 年那批假炸弹威胁能迅速被澄清，靠的是 CISA 资助的一个信息板，通知选举官员 FBI 判定威胁不可信。现在这个机构没了，州和地方只能自己扛物理和网络攻击。Brennan Center 的 Derek Tisler 说，各州单干没有同样的力量。制造混乱的人和负责澄清混乱的人，是同一届政府。

报道最后停在华盛顿州总检察长 Nick Brown 的一句话上：他非常担心他们可能做什么。这句话是整篇的底色。法院挡住了大部分，但法院挡的是已经发生的事；真正在动的，是那些还没发生、也永远不会被记录下来的事——一个合格选民在周二决定待在家里。

The line in ProPublica's report that matters most is from Ben Hovland, the Election Assistance Commission commissioner Trump fired this year: the administration has been creating a lot of noise, confusion, and doubt, and he worries voters will just sit it out. That is the whole mechanism. Not fraud — the belief in fraud. You don't need to stop anyone from voting. You need a slice of them to stop themselves.

Run Galtung's formula here. The gap between the turnout that was possible and the turnout that happens is violence. Who produces that gap? The people claiming to protect the election. DHS blended public voter rolls with its own immigration data and third-party data to produce lists of likely noncitizens. California called the claim of 20,000 such voters specious; Los Angeles County sampled the names and found many weren't registered there or had never voted, and some had written on their forms that they were born in the United States. Washington's secretary of state called the government's list of 400 names not reliable — it included U.S. citizens, unregistered people, duplicate entries. The lists are false. The fear they generate is real. A voter thinks: I don't want to be on that list. I won't register. I won't vote.

This is the third-chapter move: seize the cognitive entrance, manufacture what counts as fact. The administration asserts, without evidence, that elections are beset by fraud, then deploys resources, subpoenas, and threat letters around the fiction. A federal judge called DOJ's demands to California an intimidation plan and blocked them; another judge limited DHS's door-to-door hunt for suspected noncitizen voters through Election Day, calling it illegal intimidation of legal voters. Courts keep blocking. They cannot block the impression already delivered.

Leon County, Florida, is the miniature. Election supervisor Mark Earley had been repeatedly assured agents would not park at polling places. Then two marked Customs and Border Protection vehicles sat outside his county's busiest early voting site. He was furious, reported it to the FBI, and learned the agents were passing through and had stopped to vote themselves. Earley said that given the negative coverage of federal agents at polls, showing up in official vehicles showed pretty bad judgment. But the more interesting fact is why he was furious before he knew: because the vehicles parked there were themselves an expression, a投放, regardless of what the men inside intended.

Then Alaska. DOJ sued to stop the state from certifying its Senate race because another man named Dan Sullivan was on the ballot and voters might be confused. Trump said he personally ordered the suit. Federal law bars the government from interfering with an active election, and challenges normally come from losing candidates in state court after voting ends. A federal judge threw it out on Sunday. The point isn't the loss. The point is the demonstration: when results displease, the federal government will move. That is a toolbox being assembled for after November.

Notice who is conspiring and what they get. The RNC says it is expanding poll watching around voter eligibility and roll accuracy; the DNC says it is expanding around intimidation and barriers. One side manufactures suspicion, the other prepares to meet it, and both find their footing in the pressurized midterm. What the report cannot show is the quiet part: the people who stay home on Tuesday will not appear in any story. They will not appear on any list. They will simply be missing.

And CISA is gone. In 2024, the false bomb threats were quickly defused partly because a message board funded by CISA told election officials the FBI deemed them not credible. Trump dismantled the agency. States and localities now absorb physical and cyber threats alone. Derek Tisler of the Brennan Center says states acting on their own just do not have the same power. The people generating the confusion and the people who would clear it were the same administration.

The report ends on Washington Attorney General Nick Brown saying he worries tremendously about what they might do. That is the ground tone. Courts have stopped most of it, but courts stop what has already happened. What is moving is the part that never gets recorded: an eligible voter deciding, on Tuesday, to stay home.
