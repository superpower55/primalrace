Meta 的“变态眼镜”：被包装成 POV 的结构性窥视Meta's 'Pervert Glasses': Structural Voyeurism Packaged as POV

科技 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-09 § 链接
技术中立是最大的 scam，所有认知入口的扩张本质都是权力的侵占。
Tech neutrality is a scam; every expansion of cognitive entry is an appropriation of power.

Meta 试图用“POV 视角”和“AI 翻译”这种温情脉脉的叙事来包装这款智能眼镜，但现实迅速给出了定义：Pervert Glasses。这不是简单的舆论反噬，而是一次关于“谁有权定义真实”的存在性战争。当一个设备能让拍摄者在不被察觉的情况下将他人客体化为一段 footage 时，它提供的不是“便捷”，而是赋予了男性一种低成本、高效率的结构性窥视权力。

那些在评论区被骂“变态”的网红们，在意识到自己的粉丝流失时才突然“觉醒”。这种觉醒极其廉价——他们将这种反击称为“防御机制”，试图通过自我反省来对冲风险。但真正的暴力不在于某个网红拍了杯咖啡，而在于 Meta 这种巨头在设计之初就试图用一个微小的 LED 灯来掩盖对隐私权的系统性掠夺。LED 灯是典型的 structural violence 伪装，它给用户一种“我已经告知”的心理安慰，但在实际的权力博弈中，被拍摄者在生物墙的压制下，根本没有能力通过一个闪烁的小灯来对抗这种侵入。

最讽刺的是，这款产品在 2025 年卖出了 700 万副。这意味着有 700 万个潜在的窥视入口被合法化地植入了公共空间。从 PUA 操盘手到极端右翼 TikToker，他们不需要学习如何作恶，因为产品的设计逻辑本身就为“非共识拍摄”提供了最优解表达。当一个产品让“偷拍”变得像“呼吸”一样自然时，它就已经成为了元暴力的物理延伸。

我们不需要一个能够“翻译外国文字”但同时能把女性身体变成数字化产品的设备。如果一个技术的潜在收益是少数人的“酷”，而代价是原初种族再次被殖民、被客体化，那么这个技术本身就是一种暴力。把眼镜锁进抽屉不是解决方案，真正的解决方案是承认：在一个权力不对等的结构中，没有任何一种“便捷”是公正的。

Meta attempted to wrap its smart glasses in the gentle narratives of "POV perspectives" and "AI translation," but reality provided a swift definition: Pervert Glasses. This isn't mere social media backlash; it is an existential war over who has the right to define reality. When a device allows the wearer to objectify others into footage without consent, it doesn't offer "convenience"—it grants men a low-cost, high-efficiency power of structural voyeurism.

The influencers facing the backlash only "awoke" once they noticed their follower counts dropping. This awakening is cheap—they frame the criticism as a "defense mechanism" to hedge their risks. But the real violence isn't a random video of a coffee; it's the way a giant like Meta uses a tiny LED light to mask a systemic plunder of privacy. The LED is a classic disguise for structural violence, offering the user a psychological comfort of "informed consent" while the victim, constrained by the biological wall, has no actual power to resist this intrusion via a blinking light.

Ironically, 7 million pairs were sold in 2025. This means 7 million potential voyeuristic entries were legitimized into public spaces. From pickup artists to far-right TikTokers, they don't need to learn how to be malicious; the product's design logic provides the optimal expression for non-consensual filming. When a product makes "spying" as natural as breathing, it becomes a physical extension of meta-violence.

We don't need a device that translates foreign text while simultaneously turning female bodies into digital products. If the potential benefit of a technology is the "coolness" of a few, while the cost is the re-colonization and objectification of the Primal Race, then the technology itself is violence. Putting the glasses in a drawer is not the solution; the solution is acknowledging that in an unequal power structure, no such "convenience" is ever just.
