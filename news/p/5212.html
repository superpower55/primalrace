用行政延迟制造死亡：一种文明的结构性屠杀Manufacturing Death via Administrative Delay: A Civilized Structural Slaughter

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-23 § 链接
当权力通过操纵资源分配来制造死亡，这就是结构性暴力。
When power manipulates resource distribution to cause death, it is structural violence.

这根本不是什么“行政延迟”或“最终决定”，这是一场教科书级别的 structural violence。根据加尔通的暴力三角，暴力不一定是直接的肢体冲突，当 Actual（实际获得的医疗保护）被权力人为地拉低，而 Potential（疫苗已研发且可用）依然存在，这中间的差额就是由政府制造的暴力。

最阴险的地方在于，这次攻击的入口极其精准：VFC（儿童疫苗计划）。被剥夺权利的是谁？是原住民、无保险者、依赖 Medicaid 的底层家庭。权力在通过这种方式筛选受害者——它不宣布禁止疫苗，因为那样会触动法律红线；它选择“延迟”和“不提供时间表”，用行政官僚主义的冗余作为掩体。这是一种“千刀万剐”式的剥夺，让最脆弱的群体在等待中被病毒收割。

RFK Jr. 的叙事是典型的 weaponized expression。他一边声称“没有剥夺任何人的权利”，一边在实际操作中切断供应。这种“说法”与“事实”的脱节，就是为了在文化层（cultural layer）制造一个“我们尊重选择”的假象，而实际上在结构层（structural layer）执行强制性的剥夺。这是一种极其卑劣的共谋：利用反疫苗的民粹叙事，在联邦机构内部建立一套将医疗资源作为政治筹码的机制。

这种“测试水温”的行为预示着一个极其危险的先例：如果 Covid 疫苗可以被这样延迟，那么乙肝、流感等所有基础免疫计划都可以被纳入这种“行政裁量权”中。当公共卫生被转化为权力博弈的筹码，底层孩子的身体就变成了这场存在性战争中的炮灰。

This is not a mere 'administrative delay' or 'pending decision'; it is a textbook case of structural violence. According to Galtung's Violence Triangle, violence is not only direct physical conflict. When the Actual (medical protection) is artificially lowered by power while the Potential (available vaccines) exists, the gap is violence manufactured by the state.

The most sinister part is the precision of the entry point: the VFC program. Who are the victims? Indigenous peoples, the uninsured, and low-income families relying on Medicaid. Power is filtering its targets—it doesn't announce a ban, which would trigger legal red lines; instead, it chooses 'delays' and 'lack of timelines,' using bureaucratic redundancy as a shield. This is 'death by a thousand cuts,' allowing the most vulnerable to be harvested by the virus during the wait.

RFK Jr.'s narrative is a classic example of weaponized expression. He claims 'no one's access has been taken away' while simultaneously severing the supply chain. This disconnect between narrative and fact is designed to create a facade of 'respecting choice' at the cultural layer, while executing forced deprivation at the structural layer. It is a vile complicity: using anti-vaccine populist narratives to establish a mechanism within federal agencies where medical resources are treated as political bargaining chips.

This 'testing the waters' behavior signals a dangerous precedent: if Covid vaccines can be delayed this way, then all basic immunization programs—Hepatitis B, flu, and others—can be subsumed under this 'administrative discretion.' When public health is converted into a pawn for power games, the bodies of underprivileged children become the fodder in this existential war.
