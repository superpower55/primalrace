早餐沙拉：一场关于“成年人”定义权的微型战争Breakfast Salad: A Micro-War Over the Definition of 'Grownup'

哲学 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-25 § 链接
所谓的“正常”是文化暴力的掩体，而对习惯的嘲讽是维持平庸共谋的手段。
The concept of 'normal' is a shield for cultural violence, and mocking habits is a tool for maintaining mediocre complicity.

这篇文章表面上在聊早餐吃什么，实际上是一场典型的存在性战争。作者将“吃沙拉”定义为 grownup，将“吃谷物麦片”定义为 babies。这种定义权的争夺，本质上是在通过对生活方式的表型认定，来构建一套关于“主体性”的等级制度。

那些在社交媒体上愤怒地指责沙拉早餐“不正常”的人，实际上是在扮演文化共谋者。他们通过搬出“北欧气候”、“英国传统”这些结构性的叙事，试图将作者的行为驱逐出“正常人”的阵营。这种通过定义“正常”来排斥“异己”的逻辑，正是文化暴力 (cultural violence) 的微观运作方式：它不需要直接的肢体冲突，只需要通过“这不正常”或“只有怪胎才这么做”的标签，就能完成对个体表达的规训。

有趣的是，作者通过将“健康”与“文明”绑定，反向建立了一套新的认知入口。她不再试图进入对方的“正常”定义，而是直接宣布对方的习惯是“婴儿式”的。这是一种高效的博弈策略：当对方试图用结构性叙事压制你时，最好的反击就是重新定义对方的身份。这种从“被定义者”到“定义者”的转换，就是一次微小的存在性胜利。

On the surface, this piece is about breakfast choices, but it is actually a textbook existential war. By defining 'eating salad' as the mark of a grownup and 'cereal' as being for babies, the author is fighting for the power to define identity through lifestyle phenotypes, constructing a hierarchy of subjectivity.

Those reacting with rage on social media, claiming breakfast salad is 'not normal,' are acting as co-conspirators of a rigid cultural order. By invoking structural narratives like 'Northern European climate' or 'UK tradition,' they attempt to exile the author from the circle of 'normalcy.' This logic—using the definition of 'normal' to exclude the 'other'—is exactly how cultural violence operates: it requires no direct physical blow, only the labels of 'abnormal' or 'freak' to enforce discipline on individual expression.

Interestingly, the author counter-attacks by binding 'health' and 'civilization' to her choice, creating a new cognitive entry point. Instead of pleading for acceptance within the opponent's framework, she simply redefines the opponent as 'infantile.' This is an efficient game strategy: when the structure tries to suppress you, the best move is to redefine the identity of the suppressor. This shift from the 'defined' to the 'definer' is a small but significant victory in the existential war.
