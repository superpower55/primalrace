Kitsch is the aesthetic weapon of the displaced to announce 'I am here'.
Kitsch is the aesthetic weapon of the displaced to announce 'I am here'.

所谓的 Kitsch（刻奇/庸俗艺术），在精英主义的叙事里一直被定义为“廉价”或“缺乏品味”。但这正是 Meta Violence（元暴力）的典型运作方式：由掌握定义权的人制定审美标尺，将底层或边缘群体的表达直接划入“低级”区间，从而在文化层面上完成一次对他者的剥夺。

在这组照片里，那些巨大的玻璃纤维雕塑、霓虹灯招牌，以及移民家庭在 70 年代建造的带有拱形阳台和水泥前院的房子，本质上不是在追求“美”，而是在进行一场 Existential War（存在性战争）。对于希腊、意大利或黎巴嫩的移民来说，这种被后世嘲笑为“tacky”的建筑风格，是他们在这个陌生国度里唯一能掌控的表达空间。通过这种 Maximalism（极简主义的反面）的视觉轰炸，他们在潜意识里大喊：“我在这里，我拥有这块地，我存在。”

这种表达是弱势者在结构性压迫下的 Optimal Expression（最优解表达）。他们无法进入主流的、被精英定义的“高雅”叙事，于是通过堆砌符号、放大色彩、制造视觉冗余来确立身份。当 Frida Las Vegas 将这些元素重新定义为“能量”和“包容”时，她实际上是在尝试夺回解释权，将一个被 weaponized 的贬义词（Kitsch）转化为一种认同的旗帜。

但我们不能 naive 地庆祝这种“审美回归”。当这些空间被转化为 Instagram 上的打卡点，当“庸俗”变成一种中产阶级的时尚消费时，原初的生存挣扎被滤镜抹平了。真正的 Kitsch 是带着粗糙、不完美甚至是某种绝望的生命力在呼吸，而一旦它被纳入“艺术欣赏”的框架，它就从一种生存的武器变成了某种被驯化的标本。

Kitsch has long been defined by elite narratives as 'cheap' or 'tasteless.' This is a textbook operation of Meta Violence: those who hold the power of definition set the aesthetic yardstick to categorize the expressions of the marginalized as 'vulgar,' completing a cultural dispossession of the Other.

In these photographs, the giant fiberglass sculptures, neon signs, and the arched balconies of 70s immigrant homes are not pursuing 'beauty'; they are engaged in an Existential War. For Greek, Italian, or Lebanese emigrants, this style—later ridiculed as 'tacky'—was the only expressive space they could control in a foreign land. Through this visual bombardment of Maximalism, they were subconsciously screaming: 'I am here, I own this plot, I exist.'

This expression is the Optimal Expression for the structurally disadvantaged. Unable to enter the 'high-brow' narratives defined by elites, they established identity by piling up symbols, amplifying colors, and creating visual redundancy. When Frida Las Vegas redefines these elements as 'energy' and 'inclusivity,' she is attempting to seize the power of interpretation, transforming a weaponized pejorative into a banner of identity.

However, we must not naively celebrate this 'aesthetic reappraisal.' When these spaces are converted into Instagram hotspots and 'kitsch' becomes a middle-class fashion consumption, the original struggle for survival is smoothed over by filters. True Kitsch breathes with roughness, imperfection, and a certain desperate vitality. Once it is subsumed into the framework of 'art appreciation,' it ceases to be a weapon of survival and becomes a domesticated specimen.
