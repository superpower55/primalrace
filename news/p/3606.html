用定义权为 AI 资本开后门Redefining Reality to Open Backdoors for AI Capital

中国科技 结构层 · 元暴力 Reuters ↗ 2026-08-10 § 链接
现实不配合就重新定义现实，是权力最隐蔽的暴力。
When reality doesn't comply, power simply redefines it. This is the most invisible form of violence.

这件所谓的“澄清”是典型的通过夺取解释权来制造可能性的艺术。SEC 的逻辑极其荒谬：因为 AI 算力需求暴涨，数据中心需要钱，于是一个律所写封信，监管机构就点头承认某些数据中心债券“不是”资产证券化产品 (ABS)。

这在金融逻辑上是 scam。ABS 的本质就是资产池化和现金流证券化，数据中心债券完全符合这个定义。但 SEC 选择通过“重新定义”来绕过关键的监管规则。这意味着权力持有者在面对 AI 资本的贪婪时，不再试图通过规则去约束风险，而是直接通过修改“什么是 ABS”的定义，给资本开了一个合法的后门。

这是一场典型的共谋。律所 Latham & Watkins 提供叙事入口，SEC 提供行政背书，AI 资本获得低成本融资。在这个过程中，所谓的“市场需求”成了最高指令，而监管的公正性被牺牲。当监管者开始通过“重新定义事实”来配合资本扩张时，结构性暴力就已经完成了闭环：风险被掩盖在定义之外，而最终的爆雷代价将由整个金融系统的底层承担。

This so-called "clarification" is a classic exercise in the art of manufacturing possibilities by seizing the power of definition. The SEC's logic is absurd: because AI computing demand is soaring and data centers need cash, a law firm writes a letter, and the regulator agrees that certain data center bonds are "not" asset-backed securities (ABS).

In financial terms, this is a scam. The essence of ABS is the pooling of assets and the securitization of cash flows; data center bonds fit this definition perfectly. However, the SEC chooses to bypass key regulatory rules by simply redefining what an ABS is. This proves that when power faces the greed of AI capital, it no longer seeks to constrain risk through rules, but instead creates a legal backdoor by altering the definition of reality.

This is a textbook case of complicity. Latham & Watkins provides the narrative entry, the SEC provides the administrative endorsement, and AI capital secures low-cost financing. In this process, "market demand" becomes the supreme command, and regulatory justice is sacrificed. When regulators begin to accommodate capital expansion by redefining facts, the structural violence is complete: risks are hidden behind definitions, and the eventual cost of the crash will be borne by the bottom of the financial system.
