用 OnlyFans 逻辑救赎：当创伤被定价为唯一的生存最优解Therapy via OnlyFans Logic: When Trauma Becomes the Only Optimal Trade

性别 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-09 § 链接
将创伤商品化是结构性暴力在文化层面的绝望自救。
Commodifying trauma is a desperate act of survival against structural violence.

这场名为《OnlyFans for Therapy》的演出精准地揭示了一个残酷的博弈：当一个女性面对终身创伤时，她发现传统的心理医疗系统是一道无法逾越的生物墙——不是生理上的，而是经济与制度上的。2599次咨询，每小时70英镑，这个数字不是医疗方案，而是一道封锁线。在 Potential（完全康复）与 Actual（破碎现状）之间，那个巨大的差额就是 structural violence。当正规渠道被定价为“不可承受”时，主体性被逼到了一个极端：将痛苦转化为可消费的 content。

这是一种典型的“假.最优解表达”。Verona Verbakel 试图通过模拟 OnlyFans 的逻辑——用私密性的暴露换取某种形式的认同或救赎——来对抗创伤。但这里存在一个极其阴险的 weaponized 逻辑：OnlyFans 的本质是物化身体以获取权力，而将 therapy 平台化，则是物化痛苦以获取生存。当一个女性必须在镜头前反复地、精准地表演自己的崩溃才能获得“被看见”的权利时，她实际上是在用另一种形式的共谋来换取生存空间。她扮演了一个“破碎的受害者”角色，以满足观众对真实痛苦的窥视欲。

最令人不安的不是这种表达的勇敢，而是这种勇敢背后的元暴力。一个社会允许女性在舞台上通过模拟色情平台的逻辑来处理童年被虐待的创伤，却不能提供一个让她们无需“表演”就能获得救济的医疗结构。这依然是 masculine-centric narrative 的胜利：它定义了什么是“勇敢的暴露”，却掩盖了它如何通过剥夺公共资源，逼迫弱势者在市场逻辑中自残式地寻找最优解。这场演出不是救赎，而是一次关于结构性失败的公开祭奠。

This performance, *OnlyFans for Therapy*, precisely exposes a brutal game: when a woman faces lifelong trauma, she finds the traditional psychiatric system to be an impassable wall—not biological, but economic and structural. 2,599 sessions at £70 each is not a medical plan; it is a blockade. The gap between Potential recovery and Actual brokenness is the definition of structural violence. When formal channels are priced out of reach, subjectivity is pushed to an extreme: converting pain into consumable content.

This is a textbook case of a "fake optimal expression." Verona Verbakel attempts to combat trauma by simulating the logic of OnlyFans—trading intimate exposure for a form of validation or redemption. However, there is an insidious weaponized logic here: while OnlyFans objectifies the body for power, "platforming" therapy objectifies suffering for survival. When a woman must perform her breakdown precisely for the camera to earn the right to be "seen," she is engaging in a form of complicity to secure a survival space. She plays the role of the "broken victim" to satisfy the audience's voyeurism of authentic pain.

What is truly unsettling is not the bravery of this expression, but the meta-violence supporting it. A society that allows a woman to process childhood abuse through the logic of a porn site, yet fails to provide a structural medical safety net where she doesn't have to "perform," is a society where the masculine-centric narrative still wins. It defines "brave exposure" while masking how it forces the marginalized into a self-destructive optimal expression within market logic. This show is not a redemption; it is a public requiem for structural failure.
