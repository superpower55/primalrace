FIFA的权力游戏：共谋者的倒戈与叙事换皮The FIFA Power Game: Defection of Complicitors and Narrative Skinning

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-11 § 链接
所谓的“治理改革”通常只是共谋者在利益分配不均后的重新站队。
So-called 'governance reform' is usually just a realignment of complicitors after unfair profit distribution.

FIFA这次的闹剧是典型的 structural violence 在顶层权力场中的投射。因凡蒂诺（Infantino）试图通过出售世界杯 21% 的权益来变现，触动了欧足联（UEFA）等区域性权力中心的利益。这根本不是什么关于“治理”或“独立审查”的道德追求，而是一场关于钱和定价权的 existential war。

看看这些反对者的词汇：“服务于足球而非指挥足球”、“领导力不是权力”。这种 weaponized 的叙事极其拙劣，试图将一次纯粹的利益分赃纠纷包装成某种文明的、公正的表达。实际上，UEFA、Concacaf 和 AFC 这些共谋者在过去十年里与 FIFA 的权力结构深度绑定，直到现在他们发现自己成了被收割的客体，才开始讨论所谓的“独立审查”。

最讽刺的是，这场权力博弈的筹码竟然是 U-20 女足世界杯。欧洲球队威胁要抵制比赛，这意味着女性球员的职业路径和表达空间再次被当作男性权力斗争的 disposable tools。在元暴力的逻辑下，女足赛事的成败在这些大佬眼中不过是用来敲诈对方的筹码，其主体性被彻底抹除。

如果这次所谓的“新框架”最终胜出，那也不是因为足球变得更公正了，而是因为一套新的共谋机制达成了。只要解释权依然垄断在少数几个男性官僚手中，这种“改革”就只是从一个 patronage system 切换到另一个更隐蔽的 patronage system 而已。

The current FIFA farce is a textbook projection of structural violence within the top-tier power arena. Infantino's attempt to monetize 21% of the World Cup's equity has triggered the interests of regional power centers like UEFA. This is not a moral quest for 'governance' or 'independent review'; it is an existential war over money and pricing power.

Look at the vocabulary of the opponents: 'leadership that serves football, not seeks to command it,' 'leadership is not about power.' This is a clumsy weaponisation of narrative, attempting to wrap a pure dispute over profit-sharing in the guise of a just expression. In reality, UEFA, Concacaf, and the AFC have been deeply bound in complicity with FIFA's power structure for a decade, only now discussing 'independent reviews' because they found themselves as the objects being harvested.

The most cynical part is that the bargaining chip for this power struggle is the U-20 Women's World Cup. European teams threaten a boycott, meaning the professional trajectories and expression of female players are once again treated as disposable tools for male power struggles. Under the logic of meta-violence, the success of a women's tournament is merely a leverage point for blackmail; their subjectivity is completely erased.

If this so-called 'new framework' eventually prevails, it won't be because football became more just, but because a new mechanism of complicity was reached. As long as the power of interpretation remains monopolised by a few male bureaucrats, this 'reform' is simply a switch from one patronage system to another, more covert one.
