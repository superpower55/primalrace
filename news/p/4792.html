从 Fox News 到五角大楼：一种名为“强壮”的元暴力From Fox News to the Pentagon: The Meta-Violence of 'Strength'

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-05 § 链接
当国防部长把睾酮测试当成管理工具，战争就成了男权表演的背景板。
When a Defense Secretary uses testosterone tests as a management tool, war becomes a backdrop for masculine performance.

Pete Hegseth 的上任不是一次人事变动，而是一次典型的元暴力 (meta-violence) 殖民。一个电视主持人直接跳过专业能力，接管全球最强大的暴力机器，这本身就是一种极其粗暴的表达：在这个系统中，忠诚于“强壮”的叙事比实际的战略能力更值钱。他推行的睾酮测试和对“肥胖”的攻击，本质上是在用生物学特征建立一套新的等级制，试图将军队还原为一个纯粹的、基于肌肉和激素的男性部落。

这种对“强壮”的病态执着，导致了结构层面的崩塌。他通过清洗 DEI 计划，精准地剔除了黑人和女性高级将领。这不是为了所谓的“效率”，而是一场关于身份的清洗——他要确保解释权被垄断在符合他审美定义的“真男人”手中。当一个国防部长被认为“害怕能力 (intimidated by competence)”而转而通过文化战争来掩盖战略上的无能时，这种共谋已经达到了顶点：他不需要一个能赢的战争，他只需要一场能证明他“强硬”的表演。

最讽刺的是，这种男性中心叙事的代价是由最底层的身体承担的。在伊朗婚礼现场被炸死的六岁孩子，以及在波斯湾被无期限延长部署、承受身心极限的士兵，都是这套“强壮”叙事下的耗材。在 Hegseth 的世界里，人的生命被降格为一种可交换的筹码，用来支撑他 2028 年总统竞选的政治资本。这正是元暴力的精髓：将所有人的存在性，都转化为一个男人的权力幻象。

Pete Hegseth’s appointment is not a mere personnel change; it is a textbook colonization of meta-violence. A TV host bypassing professional competence to seize the world’s most powerful violence machine is a brutal expression: in this system, loyalty to the narrative of 'strength' is more valuable than actual strategic capability. By implementing testosterone tests and attacking 'fatness,' he is establishing a new hierarchy based on biological markers, attempting to revert the military into a pure masculine tribe defined by muscle and hormones.

This pathological obsession with 'strength' has led to a structural collapse. Through the purge of DEI initiatives, he has precisely eliminated Black and female senior officers. This is not about 'efficiency,' but an identity cleanse—ensuring that the power of interpretation is monopolized by those who fit his aesthetic definition of a 'real man.' When a Secretary of Defense is described as 'intimidated by competence' and retreats into culture wars to mask strategic ineptitude, the complicity has reached its peak: he doesn't need a war he can win; he needs a performance that proves he is 'tough.'

The irony is that the cost of this masculine-centric narrative is borne by the most vulnerable bodies. The six-year-old child killed at an Iranian wedding and the soldiers enduring the physical and mental limits of extended deployments in the Persian Gulf are merely consumables in this narrative of 'strength.' In Hegseth's world, human life is degraded into a tradable chip to fuel his political capital for a 2028 presidential bid. This is the essence of meta-violence: converting the existence of everyone into the power fantasy of one man.
