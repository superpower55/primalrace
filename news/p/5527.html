所谓的“自愿”是结构性暴力的最高级伪装The 'Voluntary' Scam: The Ultimate Mask of Structural Violence

性别 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-12 § 链接
在权力不对等的结构中，“自愿”只是施暴者在元暴力掩护下的最优解表达。
In an unequal structure, 'voluntariness' is merely the optimal expression of the abuser shielded by meta-violence.

这篇信件里最令人不适的细节，是那位儿科医生提到的法庭结果：男孩认为那是 voluntary，女孩认为那是 rape。在法律的 binary 逻辑里，not guilty 意味着男孩的“真相”赢了。但事实上，这根本不是两个对等真相的碰撞，而是一次典型的 meta violence 运作。

当一个男孩在学校环境中对女孩实施性侵害时，他调用的是一套根深蒂固的 masculine-centric narrative。在很多男孩的认知入口里，性被定义为一种征服或获取，而女孩的身体被物化为可消费的资源。在这种叙事下，只要对方没有进行足以触发法律定义的“剧烈反抗”，施暴者就会迅速将行为定义为“自愿”。这种定义权本身就是一种暴力，它通过抹除受害者的主体性，将侵害包装成一种共识。

文中提到的“预防措施”——边界感、共情力、性教育——听起来很 nice，但如果不对抗那个底层的元暴力，这些都只是 structural violence 的 PR 版本。如果社会依然在潜意识里共谋，默认男性在性关系中拥有主导权，那么所谓的“同意” (consent) 永远会被扭曲成一种表演性的妥协。女孩在面对压力或权力不对等时选择的“不反抗”，在施暴者的逻辑里被自动转化为“自愿”，这正是最阴险的 scam。

法律的 not guilty 并没有给任何人带来救赎，它只是在 structural 层面上再次确认了：在男本位叙事中，女孩的痛苦是不被计入事实的。真正的 prevention 不是教孩子如何“礼貌”，而是拆掉那堵让男孩认为自己拥有定义他人身体权力的生物墙与文化墙。

The most disturbing detail in this letter is the pediatrician's mention of the court outcomes: the boys believed the sex was voluntary, while the girls believed they were raped. In the binary logic of criminal justice, 'not guilty' means the boys' 'truth' prevailed. But this is not a collision of two equal truths; it is a textbook operation of meta-violence.

When a boy sexually harms a girl in a school setting, he invokes a deeply embedded masculine-centric narrative. In the cognitive entry points of many boys, sex is defined as conquest or acquisition, and the female body is objectified as a consumable resource. Under this narrative, as long as the victim does not exhibit 'violent resistance' sufficient for legal thresholds, the abuser quickly defines the act as 'voluntary.' This power to define is itself a form of violence; it erases the victim's subjectivity and repackages assault as a consensus.

The 'preventative measures' mentioned—boundaries, empathy, sex education—sound pleasant, but without dismantling the underlying meta-violence, they are merely PR versions of structural violence. If society continues to be complicit in the subconscious assumption that males hold the dominant position in sexual relations, 'consent' will always be distorted into a performative compromise. The 'non-resistance' a girl chooses when facing pressure or power imbalance is automatically translated into 'voluntariness' in the abuser's logic—this is the most insidious scam.

A legal 'not guilty' brings salvation to no one. It merely confirms at a structural level that in a masculine-centric narrative, the pain of girls is not counted as a fact. True prevention is not about teaching children to be 'polite'; it is about tearing down the biological and cultural walls that allow boys to believe they possess the right to define another's body.
