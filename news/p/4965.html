特朗普及他的“解雇”艺术：一场关于定义权的元暴力表演Trump and the Art of Firing: A Performance of Meta-Violence over Definition

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-12 § 链接
解雇不是管理行为，而是一种夺取解释权并将其武器化的叙事表演。
Firing is not a managerial act, but a weaponized narrative to seize the power of definition.

这篇报道揭露了一个极其典型的 meta violence 运作机制：在特朗普的逻辑里，‘解雇’ (fire) 从不关乎绩效，而关乎谁拥有定义‘事实’的权力。无论是 15 年前在《学徒》中将一名金融学位持有者 framing 成‘不识字的有色人种’，还是在政府中通过推特单方面宣布解雇，其本质都是在进行一次存在性战争的暴力截断。

这种暴力在 structural 层面上表现为对制度惯例的践踏——比如直接无视独立机构的任职保障，在文化层面上则表现为一种极端的 masculine-centric narrative：强者定义弱者，定义权即是权力。他不需要事实，他只需要一个能让观众‘垂涎’的 line。当 Gene Folkes 被定义为‘愚蠢’，当 Rebecca Kelly Slaughter 被定义为‘不一致’，他们作为主体的表达被强行替换成了特朗普需要的‘客体标签’。

最令人作呕的是这种共谋机制。在早期的 Trump Organization，像 Barbara Res 这样的高管通过‘操纵’他的情绪来维持系统运转，这本身就是一种在元暴力结构下的生存最优解表达——通过扮演‘懂他’的人来换取执行力。而到了政治权力顶端，这种共谋变成了对‘忠诚’的绝对要求。不挑战他，就成了在这个 rigged system 中生存的唯一假.最优解。

这场表演最阴毒的地方在于，它将‘权力’与‘人格’完全等同。他不需要共情 (empathy)，因为在元暴力的视角下，共情是对权力的稀释。他将他人视为 product 而非 person，这正是原初种族殖民逻辑的现代变体：将对方客体化，然后随意处置其存在性。

This report exposes a classic mechanism of meta-violence: in Trump's logic, 'firing' is never about performance; it is about who owns the power to define 'fact'. Whether framing a finance degree holder as an 'illiterate person of colour' on The Apprentice 15 years ago, or unilaterally announcing dismissals via Twitter in government, the essence is a violent truncation of existence in an existential war.

This violence manifests as structural violence through the trampling of institutional norms—such as ignoring the tenure of independent agencies—and as cultural violence through an extreme masculine-centric narrative: the strong define the weak, and the power to define is the power itself. He doesn't need facts; he only needs a 'line' that makes the audience salivate. When Gene Folkes is defined as 'dumb' or Rebecca Kelly Slaughter as 'inconsistent', their subjective expression is forcibly replaced by 'object labels' required by Trump.

Most sickening is the mechanism of complicity. In the early Trump Organization, executives like Barbara Res maintained system stability by 'manipulating' his emotions—a typical fake optimal expression for survival under meta-violence: playing the role of the 'one who understands him' to gain executive power. At the peak of political power, this complicity evolves into an absolute demand for loyalty. Not challenging him becomes the only fake optimal expression in this rigged system.

The most insidious part of this performance is the total equation of 'power' with 'personality'. He has no need for empathy, because from the perspective of meta-violence, empathy dilutes power. By treating others as products rather than persons, he replicates the modern variant of the Primal Race colonial logic: objectify the other, then dispose of their existence at will.
