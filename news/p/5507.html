洛杉矶的蚊子与中产阶级的“美丽城镇”幻觉LA's Mosquitoes and the Middle-Class Illusion of 'Beautiful Towns'

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-11 § 链接
当结构性暴力被自然灾害激活，中产阶级的安稳只是昂贵的PR包装。
When structural violence is activated by natural disaster, middle-class stability is revealed as expensive PR.

洛杉矶被蚊子“占领”这件事，在新闻叙事里被包装成了一个气候变化的意外，但本质上这是一场关于 structural violence 的揭露。那些在 El Segundo 抱怨“投入大量金钱居住在美丽城镇”却被蚊子骚扰的居民，其实是在面对一个残酷的真相：你购买的不是安全，而是一种名为“中产生活”的 scam。

这种 scam 的运作逻辑是：通过高房价和社区隔离，制造一种远离自然风险、远离底层混乱的虚假真空。但生物墙 (biological wall) 是不认房产证的。当 El Niño 和热浪打破了这种人为的平衡，Culex 和 Aedes 蚊子成了最诚实的审计员，它们告诉这些居民：你们所谓的“美丽城镇”，在生态崩溃面前没有任何特权。那些试图用毛巾堵门缝、穿长袖睡觉的行为，本质上是主体性在面对不可控结构时的无力挣扎。

最讽刺的是，解决问题的路径依然被 masculine-centric narrative 掌控。政府在推行昂贵的 X 射线绝育计划，但却在面对一个简单的 structural 障碍——业主拒绝为提高税收买单。这是一种典型的共谋 (complicity)：中产阶级既希望享受公共卫生带来的安全，又拒绝承担维持这个系统的成本。他们宁愿买一个电蚊拍来获得某种“击杀”的快感（正如那个官员所说的 fulfilling activity），也不愿承认自己正身处一个失效的公共管理结构中。

这场“活地狱”不是蚊子制造的，而是由气候变暖、公共卫生投入不足以及中产阶级对“私有化安全”的迷信共同构成的。当一个城市的居民只能通过“习惯使用驱蚊剂”来适应生活时，这不叫适应，这叫在 structural violence 面前的慢性投降。

The 'takeover' of Los Angeles by mosquitoes is framed as a climatic accident, but it is fundamentally an exposure of structural violence. Residents of El Segundo complaining that they 'invested a lot of money' to live in a beautiful town only to be plagued by insects are facing a brutal truth: they didn't buy security, but a scam called 'middle-class lifestyle.'

This scam operates by using high real estate prices and community segregation to manufacture a fake vacuum, isolated from natural risks and systemic chaos. But the biological wall does not recognize property deeds. When El Niño and heatwaves shatter this artificial balance, the mosquitoes become the most honest auditors, proving that their 'beautiful towns' hold no privilege in the face of ecological collapse. The act of stuffing towels under doors or sleeping in long sleeves is simply the helplessness of subjectivity struggling against an uncontrollable structure.

Most ironic is that the solution remains trapped in a masculine-centric narrative. The government pushes expensive X-ray sterilization, yet hits a structural wall: property owners refuse to vote for tax hikes. This is a classic complicity: the middle class desires the security of public health but refuses to fund the system. They prefer the 'fulfilling activity' of using an electric swatter to feel a sense of control, rather than admitting they live within a failed public management structure.

This 'living hell' wasn't created by mosquitoes, but by the synergy of global warming, underfunded public health, and the middle-class delusion of 'privatized safety.' When a city's residents are told to simply 'make repellent part of their routine,' it is not adaptation—it is a chronic surrender to structural violence.
