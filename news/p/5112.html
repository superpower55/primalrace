燕麦奶布丁与“替代品”的客观性陷阱Oat Milk Pudding and the Trap of 'Substitute' Objectivity

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The New York Times ↗ 2026-09-18 § 链接
当一个替代方案被赋予五星好评，它往往在掩盖客观标准的消失。
When a substitute is rated five stars, it often masks the disappearance of objective standards.

纽约时报的一篇燕麦奶布丁食谱，在评论区里触发了一场关于“客观五星”的微型博弈。一个用户提出了一个极其尖锐的问题：这个五星好评，究竟是对一个独立产品的客观评价，还是因为它是“非奶制品替代品”而获得的宽容分？

这本质上是一场关于定价权的认知博弈。在传统的饮食叙事中，牛奶是 baseline，所有非奶制品（nondairy）都被定义为“替代品”（substitute）。一旦进入“替代品”这个认知入口，评价标准就发生了偏移：人们不再问“这是否是最好的布丁”，而是在问“作为一个替代品，它是不是足够像原版”。

这种叙事逻辑是一种隐形的 structural violence。它通过定义一个“原初标准”，将所有差异化的表达都置于“模仿”和“妥协”的地位。当你为燕麦奶布丁打五星时，你可能在潜意识里完成了一次共谋——你认可了“替代品”这个等级制度，承认了它只需要“足够好”就能获得最高分，而不需要在绝对的味觉维度上与原版竞争。

有趣的是，评论区里那些关于淀粉激活温度的争论，是真正的 Just Expression。它们在试图通过物理事实（boiling temp）来夺回对结果的控制权。而那个质疑评分标准的用户，则是在试图拆穿一个 cultural scam：我们习惯于给弱势的替代方案发放“鼓励奖”，却忘了这种温柔的宽容，恰恰是维持原初霸权最稳固的方式。

A simple oat milk pudding recipe from The New York Times triggers a micro-game of 'objective five stars' in the comments. One user asks a piercing question: is this rating an objective evaluation of the product, or a lenient score granted because it is a 'nondairy substitute'?

This is essentially a cognitive game over pricing power. In traditional culinary narratives, cow's milk is the baseline; all nondairy options are defined as 'substitutes'. Once you enter the cognitive entrance of 'substitution', the evaluation criteria shift: people stop asking 'Is this the best pudding?' and start asking 'As a substitute, is it close enough to the original?'

This narrative logic is a form of structural violence. By defining a 'primal standard', it places all divergent expressions in a position of 'imitation' and 'compromise'. When you give oat milk pudding five stars, you may be unconsciously engaging in complicity—you accept the hierarchy of substitutes and agree that being 'good enough' warrants a top score, without needing to compete with the original on an absolute sensory level.

Interestingly, the arguments over starch activation temperatures in the comments are true Just Expressions. They attempt to reclaim control over the outcome through physical facts (boiling temp). Meanwhile, the user questioning the rating is attempting to expose a cultural scam: we are used to giving 'encouragement awards' to disadvantaged alternatives, forgetting that this gentle leniency is exactly what maintains the primal hegemony most firmly.
