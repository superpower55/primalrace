特朗普的“反共”叙事：一场关于解释权的低级 scamTrump's 'Anti-Communism': A Low-Level Scam of Interpretation

国际 文化层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-04 § 链接
当权力者定义“共产主义”为敌人时，他通常在用其手段扩充私产。
When power defines 'communism' as the enemy, it is usually using its methods to expand private wealth.

特朗普在电视广告里大喊“击败共产主义”，而他转身就在用最典型的 command and control 模式接管美国经济。这根本不是意识形态的冲突，而是一次极其拙劣的武器化表达。他把“共产主义”这个词变成了一个认知入口，用来恐吓中西部那些对“左翼”有生理性恐惧的选民，从而在公共空间制造一个虚假的敌手，掩盖他本人正在进行的资源掠夺。

从 V-Dem 的数据看，美国在特朗普第二任期内国家对经济的控制力剧增（从全球第二低掉到第 16 位）。他接管 Intel，强索 Nippon Steel 的“黄金股”，这在逻辑上与他口中的“反共”完全相反。但这正是元暴力的精髓：解释权被垄断后，事实不再重要。只要他定义了什么是“红色威胁”，他就可以在实施事实上的 state takeover 时，依然被支持者视为“自由世界的捍卫者”。

这种行为模式不是马克思主义，而是典型的 fiefdom（领地）逻辑。他不需要技术官僚的纪律，只需要把国家金库当成 private piggy bank。这是一种极其低级的共谋：共和党选民通过厌恶一个想象中的“左翼乌托邦”，共谋地允许一个真实的独裁者将公共资源私有化。他们以为在防御共产主义，实际上是在为一种更原始的、基于家族王朝的掠夺制度递交投名状。

最讽刺的是，那些对纽约市几个政府运行的小店（bodegas）感到惊恐的中间阶层，却对总统把国家经济变成私人领地视而不见。这种认知的断裂说明，在当前的美国政治博弈中，叙事已经完全取代了机制。当“反共”成为一种身份标签而非政治主张时，它就成了一把完美的掩护伞，让权力者在光天化日之下完成对生产资料的非法占有。

Trump screams 'defeat communism' in TV ads while simultaneously implementing a classic command-and-control model to seize the US economy. This isn't an ideological conflict; it's a clumsy weaponisation of expression. He has turned 'communism' into a cognitive entry point to terrify middle-America's visceral fear of the 'Left,' creating a phantom enemy to mask his own predatory grab for resources.

V-Dem data shows a sharp spike in state control over the economy during Trump's second term (dropping from 2nd to 16th globally). Taking stakes in Intel or demanding a 'golden share' from Nippon Steel is logically opposite to his 'anti-communist' rhetoric. But this is the essence of meta-violence: once the monopoly on interpretation is secured, facts become irrelevant. As long as he defines the 'red threat,' he can execute a state takeover while being hailed as the 'defender of the free world.'

His logic isn't Marxist; it's the logic of a fiefdom. He doesn't need the discipline of technocrats; he just needs the state coffers as his private piggy bank. This is a crude complicity: Republican voters, driven by a hatred for an imagined 'leftist utopia,' collectively allow a real autocrat to privatize public resources. They believe they are fighting communism, while in fact, they are signing a pledge of allegiance to a primitive, dynasty-based plunder system.

The irony is peak: middle-class voters who freak out over city-run bodegas in NYC remain blind to a president turning the national economy into a personal estate. This cognitive rupture proves that in current US political gaming, narrative has completely replaced mechanism. When 'anti-communism' becomes an identity label rather than a political stance, it serves as a perfect umbrella, allowing the powerful to seize the means of production in broad daylight.
