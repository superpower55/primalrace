AI 滤镜下的权力自嗨与廉价的道歉AI Filters, Power Fantasies, and the Cheapness of Apology

哲学 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-26 § 链接
用 AI 篡改现实以迎合强权，是典型的主体性死亡。
Using AI to distort reality to flatter power is a total death of subjectivity.

一个信用社 CEO 用 ChatGPT 把家人的毛衣改成“Lake America”，然后发在 9/11 周年纪念日，并配上“Lol lol”。这不仅仅是所谓的“判断力缺失” (poor judgment)，而是一次极其典型的表达武器化尝试——只不过她试图接入的是 Trump 这种强权叙事的认知入口。

在 Trump 强行将 Lake Ontario 改名为 Lake America 的这场 trade war 中，这种改名行为本身就是一种 Meta Violence：通过重新定义地理现实来夺取解释权。而 Jane Smith 的行为，是试图通过 AI 滤镜将自己和家人包装成这种强权叙事的共谋者。她以为自己在“开玩笑” (poking fun)，但这种玩笑的底色是对他者（加拿大民众、原住民文化）存在性的轻视。当她使用 AI 篡改图像时，她实际上是在练习一种“制造可能性”的艺术——一种基于强权逻辑的、虚假的真实。

最讽刺的是她的道歉。她说“我不是一个想伤害任何人的人”，这种表达是典型的假.最优解表达。她在被解雇的压力下，迅速切换到一种“无辜且温柔”的女性化扮演角色，试图通过示弱来换取原谅。但信用社的决定证明了，在结构层面上，她的这种扮演已经失效了。她的主体性在追求强权认同的瞬间就已经死亡，而随后的道歉只是在为自己的失败进行 PR 补救。

这场闹剧揭示了一个事实：当一个人试图通过投靠元暴力来获得存在感时，她就成了那个被权力结构随意丢弃的耗材。她以为自己在通过 AI 操纵现实，实际上她只是被一种更宏大的、男性中心的权力叙事给操纵了。

A credit union CEO uses ChatGPT to edit her family's sweaters to say 'Lake America,' posting it on the anniversary of 9/11 with a 'Lol lol.' This isn't just 'poor judgment'; it's a blatant attempt at the weaponisation of expression—specifically, trying to plug into the cognitive entry point of a power-centric narrative.

Trump's executive order renaming Lake Ontario to Lake America is a textbook example of Meta Violence: seizing the right of interpretation by redefining physical reality. Jane Smith's act was an attempt to signal her complicity with this narrative through an AI filter. She claimed to be 'poking fun,' but the underlying tone is a dismissal of the existence of others—Canadians and Indigenous peoples. By using AI to alter the image, she was practicing the art of 'manufacturing possibilities'—a fake reality based on the logic of the strong.

Her apology is the most pathetic part. Claiming she 'never wanted to hurt anybody' is a classic fake optimal expression. Under the pressure of losing her job, she pivoted to a performance of the 'innocent and gentle' feminine role, hoping that vulnerability would buy her forgiveness. But the structural response from her employer proves this performance failed. Her subjectivity died the moment she sought validation from power; her apology is merely a PR exercise to mitigate the fallout.

This farce reveals a simple truth: when an individual tries to gain a sense of existence by aligning with meta-violence, they become a disposable asset of that very power structure. She thought she was manipulating reality with AI, but in truth, she was being manipulated by a masculine-centric narrative of dominance.
