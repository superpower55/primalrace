定义权的杀戮：当死亡被剔除出统计表The Slaughter of Definition: When Deaths are Erasured from the Tally

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-22 § 链接
掌控定义权就是掌控生存权，剔除统计数据是最高级的结构暴力。
Controlling the definition is controlling survival; erasing statistics is the highest form of structural violence.

CDC 剔除宾夕法尼亚州麻疹死亡病例的逻辑极其荒谬：因为没有一个完美的“定义”，所以死亡不存在。这在本质上是一场关于认知入口的武器化战争。当 RFK Jr. 这种反疫苗叙事的掌权者进入 HHS，他不需要直接下令禁止疫苗，他只需要通过操纵“什么是事实”的制造权，将 Actual（实际死亡）从 Potential（统计数据）中抹除。这就是典型的 structural violence —— 制度通过重新定义现实，让暴力变得不可见。

最令人作呕的是 CDC 局长的辩解。她用一个“被巴士撞死且患有新冠”的极端假设来掩盖统计延迟。这种叙事技巧是在试图将“技术性定义问题”与“政治性操纵”混淆。事实上，定义权本身就是政治。当宾州已经有了清晰定义而联邦政府拒绝采用时，这不再是科学讨论，而是共谋。权力层在共谋掩盖疫情的真实烈度，以维持某种特定的政治叙事：即疫苗并非必需，或者疫苗的副作用更值得关注。

最残酷的暴力发生在 meta 层：先天性麻疹（congenital measles）的出现。这些在子宫内就被感染的新生儿，是生物墙最脆弱的受害者。他们甚至没有机会在公共空间进行任何“表达”，就被结构性的认知缺失给杀死了。当权力者在争论死亡的定义时，真实的肉体正在因为这种定义权的缺失而消亡。这已经不是医疗危机，而是一场由权力驱动的、针对最弱势群体的存在性屠杀。

The CDC's logic for excluding measles deaths in Pennsylvania is absurd: because there is no perfect 'definition,' the deaths simply do not exist. This is essentially a weaponisation of the cognitive entry point. When an anti-vaccine narrative leader like RFK Jr. enters the HHS, he doesn't need to ban vaccines; he only needs to manipulate the power to manufacture 'what is a fact,' erasing the Actual deaths from the Potential statistics. This is classic structural violence — the system renders violence invisible by redefining reality.

The CDC director's defense is particularly nauseating. She uses a far-fetched scenario of someone 'hit by a bus while having Covid' to mask the reporting delay. This narrative tactic attempts to blur the line between 'technical definition' and 'political manipulation.' In reality, the power of definition is politics. When Pennsylvania has a clear definition and the federal government refuses to adopt it, it is no longer a scientific debate, but complicity. The power elite are conspiring to hide the true intensity of the outbreak to maintain a specific political narrative: that vaccines are unnecessary, or that their side effects deserve more attention.

The most brutal violence occurs at the meta level: the emergence of congenital measles. These newborns, infected in utero, are the most vulnerable victims of the biological wall. They have no chance for any 'expression' in the public space before being killed by a structural void of cognition. While the powerful argue over the definition of death, real bodies are perishing because of the absence of that definition. This is no longer a medical crisis; it is an existential slaughter of the most marginalized, driven by the will to power.
