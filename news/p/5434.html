乐高骗局与“撞车骗局”：男性的博弈成本与共谋代价Lego Scams and 'Crash for Cash': The Cost of Masculine Game Theory

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-06 § 链接
诈骗不是个案，而是男性在结构性优势中追求“低成本获利”的博弈结果。
Fraud is not an isolated case, but a result of masculine game theory seeking 'low-cost gains' within structural advantages.

一个用乐高套装骗保的男人被判刑，这在新闻叙事里被包装成一个“贪婪个体”的警示故事。但如果把视角拉高到 Violence Triangle，你会发现这不过是 structural violence 的一种衍生博弈。在英国 13.4 亿英镑的诈骗额度背后，是一个极其典型的男性化博弈逻辑：利用系统的漏洞，尝试用最低的成本（伪造发票、编造谎言）去套取最大的利益。这种对“捷径”的追求，本质上是男性中心叙事中对“掌控力”和“赢”的病态执着。

更值得关注的是文中提到的“撞车骗局” (crash for cash)——骗子通过在网上结识女性并诱导其参与伪造车祸来骗保。这才是真正的 meta violence。在这里，女性被彻底客体化为骗局中的“工具人”或“零件”，她们的身体和身份被用来作为骗保的掩体。这种共谋不是基于平等的利益交换，而是基于骗局制定者对认知入口的掌控。男性在设计规则，女性在被动扮演角色，最终的赔偿金流向了设计者，而风险则由整个保险系统的诚实消费者共同分担。

AI 伪造车祸现场的出现，标志着表达的武器化进入了新阶段。当“事实”可以被算法低成本制造时，保险公司所谓的“审核”就成了一场不对称的战争。这再次证明，所谓的“文明”和“理性”秩序，在面对追求最优解（哪怕是假.最优解）的掠夺者时，其防御机制是多么脆弱。所谓的“诚实消费者”在为谁买单？他们在为一群试图通过操纵现实来占便宜的博弈者支付溢价。

这场骗局的终点不是那个被判刑的乐高收藏家，而是那个依然认为“只要不涉及肉体杀戮就是文明生活”的共谋场域。只要解释权依然被掌握在那些定义“什么是成功博弈”的人手中，这种 scam 就会在不同的载体上无限循环。

A man imprisoned for a Lego insurance scam is framed as a cautionary tale of individual greed. However, through the lens of the Violence Triangle, this is a derivative of structural violence. Behind the £1.34bn fraud in the UK lies a quintessential masculine gaming logic: exploiting system loopholes to extract maximum profit with minimum effort (fake invoices, fabricated lies). This obsession with 'shortcuts' is a pathological manifestation of the desire for control and 'winning' inherent in masculine-centric narratives.

More telling is the 'crash for cash' scheme, where fraudsters recruit women online to stage accidents. This is pure meta-violence. Here, women are completely objectified as 'tools' or 'components' of the scam; their bodies and identities serve as shields for the fraud. This complicity is not an equal exchange of interests, but a result of the scammer's control over the cognitive entry points. Men design the rules, women perform the roles, and the payouts flow to the designers while the cost is socialized across all honest consumers.

The rise of AI-generated accident scenes marks a new phase in the weaponisation of expression. When 'facts' can be manufactured at low cost by algorithms, the insurance industry's 'audit' becomes an asymmetric war. It proves that the defenses of 'civilization' and 'rationality' are fragile against predators seeking a fake optimal expression.

The end of this story isn't the jailed Lego collector, but the complicity of a society that believes 'civilized life' is anything that avoids physical slaughter. As long as the power of interpretation remains with those who define 'successful gaming,' these scams will iterate infinitely across different mediums.
