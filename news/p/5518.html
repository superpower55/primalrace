电子脚镣的PR骗局与制度性虚构The PR Scam of Electronic Tags and Institutional Fiction

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-11 § 链接
当监管变成一种‘虚构’，法律就成了掩盖结构性失能的PR文案。
When supervision becomes a 'fiction', the law transforms into a PR brochure masking structural failure.

这篇报告揭露了一个极其荒诞的现实：在英格兰和威尔士，法庭宣判的刑期已经变成了一场‘fiction’（虚构）。5000多人该戴脚镣没戴，1600多人戴了却没信号。这意味着，所谓的‘电子监控’在实际运作中已经从一种 structural 约束退化成了一种心理安慰剂，或者说，是一场规模巨大的政府 PR scam。

从加尔通的暴力三角来看，这正是典型的 structural violence。政府在公共叙事中通过‘扩大电子监控’来向受害者和公众承诺安全，制造一种‘一切尽在掌控’的 cultural 假象，但实际的资源分配（staffing）却完全无法支撑这个叙事。这种 Potential（承诺的监管）与 Actual（实际的失能）之间的巨大差额，就是对受害者的一种二次暴力——你被告知对方被监控了，但实际上他处于真空状态。

最讽刺的是政府的回复：他们宣称在进行‘历史上最大规模的扩张’。在这种 masculine-centric 的管理逻辑中，‘规模’和‘数量’永远优先于‘效能’。增加脚镣的数量比增加 probation officers 的人数要容易得多，因为前者是可以通过采购合同完成的‘政绩’，而后者涉及长期的资源投入和人力成本。在这种共谋下，电子脚镣成了掩盖系统崩溃的遮羞布。

当法律的执行变成了概率游戏，‘刑期’这个词就失去了意义。一个六年的刑期被拆解为两年的监禁、两年的监管和两年的‘无人知晓’，这本质上是对法律解释权的私有化篡改。系统不再追求公正的表达，而是在追求一种‘看起来在运作’的表演性管理。

This report exposes a surreal reality: in England and Wales, court-imposed sentences have become essentially a 'fiction'. Over 5,000 offenders who should have been tagged weren't, and 1,600 had tags with no signal. Electronic monitoring has devolved from a structural constraint into a psychological placebo, or more accurately, a massive government PR scam.

Applying Galtung's Violence Triangle, this is textbook structural violence. The government utilizes a cultural narrative of 'expanding electronic monitoring' to promise safety to victims and the public, creating an illusion of control while the actual resource allocation—specifically staffing—is utterly insufficient. The vast gap between the Potential (promised supervision) and the Actual (systemic failure) is a form of secondary violence against victims, who are told the offender is monitored when they are actually in a vacuum.

The irony lies in the government's response: boasting about the 'biggest expansion in history'. In this masculine-centric management logic, 'scale' and 'quantity' always trump 'efficacy'. Increasing the number of tags is far easier than increasing the number of probation officers, as the former is a 'performance metric' achieved via procurement contracts, while the latter requires long-term investment. In this complicity, the electronic tag becomes a veil to hide a collapsing system.

When the enforcement of law becomes a game of probability, the term 'sentence' loses all meaning. A six-year sentence fragmented into two years of prison, two years of probation, and two years of 'nobody knows' is essentially a privatized hijacking of legal interpretation. The system is no longer seeking Just Expressions; it is pursuing a performative management that merely 'looks like it's working'.
