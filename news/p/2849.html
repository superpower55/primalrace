Karoline Leavitt 用了一个极其精准的词组来描述特朗普的演讲："unifying yet vicious"。在常识看来这是矛盾，但在存在性战争的博弈中，这正是最高效的武器化表达。所谓的 "unifying"（统一）并非指达成共识，而是通过制造一个共同的敌人，将追随者紧紧捆绑在某种身份认同之中；而 "vicious"（阴狠）则是直接对异己进行存在性的抹杀。

这场晚餐会的背景是之前的枪击事件。注意，当直接暴力 (direct violence) 发生后，权力者迅速将其转化为一种叙事入口。原本是媒体与权力者的博弈场，现在被重新定义为一次关于“安全”与“忠诚”的表演。特勤局局长说 "We do expect bad people to show up"，这里的 "bad people" 已经不再仅仅指那个开枪的疯子，而是一种随时可以被激活的标签，用来筛选谁是共谋者，谁是异己。

最讽刺的是，特朗普在起诉新闻机构、禁止美联社进入白宫的同时，依然选择在这个场合发表演讲。这不是在沟通，而是在通过占领认知入口来实施结构暴力 (structural violence)。他不需要通过事实来赢得辩论，他只需要通过定义什么是 "fake news"，就夺取了对事实的解释权。当一个权力者能同时定义 "serious" 和 "hilarious" 时，他实际上是在宣布：在这个空间里，逻辑不再起作用，只有我的意志是唯一的真实。

这场 gala 所谓的 "smaller and closer"，不过是权力在收紧控制范围。在元暴力的逻辑下，这种“亲近”意味着更高效的规训。当媒体被排除在真相之外，而演讲者在台上表演这种矛盾的魅力时，这本身就是一场关于主体性被剥夺的胜利演习。

Karoline Leavitt used a surgically precise phrase to describe Trump's speech: "unifying yet vicious." To the naive, this is a contradiction; in the game of Existential War, it is the most efficient weaponized expression. The "unifying" part is not about consensus, but about binding followers through the creation of a common enemy. The "vicious" part is the direct existential erasure of the other.

The backdrop of this gala is a prior shooting. Notice how, after direct violence occurs, the power structure immediately converts it into a narrative entry point. What was once a battlefield between the press and power is redefined as a performance of "security" and "loyalty." The Secret Service director's remark, "We do expect bad people to show up," reveals that "bad people" are no longer just the gunman, but a flexible label used to filter complicity and alienate dissent.

It is peak irony that Trump chooses to speak here while simultaneously suing news organizations and barring the AP from the White House. This is not communication; it is the occupation of cognitive entry points to exercise structural violence. He doesn't need facts to win a debate; he only needs to define what constitutes "fake news" to seize the power of interpretation. When a ruler can define both "serious" and "hilarious" simultaneously, he is announcing that logic is obsolete—only his will remains as the sole reality.

The so-called "smaller and closer" atmosphere of this gala is merely the tightening of the grip of power. Under the logic of meta-violence, this "closeness" translates to more efficient discipline. While the media is excluded from the truth, the performer on stage conducts a victory lap in the erasure of subjectivity.
