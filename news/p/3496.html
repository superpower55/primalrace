Luigi Mangione：被偶像化杀手与法庭表演的共谋Luigi Mangione: The Collusion of an Idolized Killer and Courtroom Theater

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-09 § 链接
当谋杀被包装成政治反抗，法庭就成了制造‘真实’的剧场。
When murder is packaged as political resistance, the courtroom becomes a theater for manufacturing 'truth'.

Luigi Mangione 这场所谓的‘世纪审判’，从一开始就不是关于法律的裁决，而是一场关于认知入口的争夺战。杀掉一个被广泛厌恶的医疗保险高管，让 Mangione 在社交媒体上迅速被 weaponized 成一个‘政治囚犯’或‘反抗英雄’。这种叙事极其危险，因为它将直接暴力 (direct violence) 浪漫化为一种正义的表达，试图通过一个极端的个体行为，来掩盖医疗体系结构性暴力 (structural violence) 的复杂性。

更有趣的是这场法庭上的‘闹剧’。辩方律师在媒体准入问题上大做文章，指责检方‘挑选记者’，结果被迅速 debunk。这种 drama 并不是法律策略，而是一种典型的 posturing。他们不需要真的赢得准入权，他们需要的是制造‘被不公正对待’的叙事，为未来的上诉制造 leverage，或者干脆为 Mangione 打造一个‘被体制迫害’的人设。

而那些涌入法庭的 influencers 则是这场 circus 的共谋者。他们并不关心 justice，他们关心的是 clicks 和 money。他们将严肃的司法程序转化为一种消费主义的 spectacle。当法律的威严被流量逻辑解构，法庭就不再是寻求事实的地方，而是一个制造‘真实’的工厂。在这种环境下，真相本身已经不重要了，重要的是谁能定义这个‘真实’，谁能掌控那个被神化的杀手形象。

这场审判最讽刺的地方在于：无论结果如何，Mangione 已经赢得了他想要的‘存在性’。他通过一次极端的暴力表达，强行在公共空间撕开了一个口子。但我们要警惕的是，如果这种‘暴力即正义’的叙事被内化，那么我们面对的将不是正义的回归，而是元暴力 (meta violence) 的升级——用另一种形式的杀戮来定义什么是‘正确’。

The so-called 'trial of the century' for Luigi Mangione is not about legal adjudication; it is a war over cognitive entry points. By killing a widely reviled healthcare executive, Mangione was rapidly weaponized on social media as a 'political prisoner' or a 'rebel hero.' This narrative is perilous because it romanticizes direct violence as a form of just expression, attempting to mask the complexities of structural violence within the healthcare system through a single extreme act.

Even more telling is the 'circus' unfolding in court. The defense's dramatic claims about press access, which were swiftly debunked, are not genuine legal strategies but a form of posturing. They don't need actual access; they need to manufacture a narrative of 'unfair treatment' to create leverage for future appeals or to solidify Mangione's persona as a victim of the system.

Meanwhile, the influencers flocking to the trial are complicit in this spectacle. They aren't seeking justice; they are seeking clicks and money. They transform a solemn judicial process into a consumerist spectacle. When legal dignity is dismantled by the logic of traffic, the court ceases to be a place for facts and becomes a factory for manufacturing 'truth.' In this environment, the actual truth is secondary to who can define the narrative and who can maintain the idolized image of the killer.

The ultimate irony is that Mangione has already won his 'existential' battle. Through an extreme act of violence, he forced a rupture in the public space. However, we must be vigilant: if the narrative of 'violence equals justice' is internalized, we are not witnessing a return to justice, but an escalation of meta-violence—using another form of slaughter to define what is 'right'.
