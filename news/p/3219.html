AI 时代的金融共谋：用“复杂性”掩盖结构性失效Financial Complicity in the AI Age: Using 'Complexity' to Mask Structural Failure

科技 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-04 § 链接
所谓的“复杂欺诈”不过是结构性暴力在技术迭代时的PR掩体。
The so-called 'complex fraud' is merely a PR shield for structural violence during tech iterations.

Metro Bank 把 1.4 万英镑的损失归结为“欺诈的复杂性质” (complex nature of the fraud)，这简直是金融业最经典的 scam。在这个案例中，银行在收到客户明确的“No”之后，依然允许后续数次交易通过。这根本不是什么 AI 时代的复杂性，而是最原始的 structural violence：银行在追求交易流畅度与风控成本之间，选择让客户承担风险。所谓的“复杂性”，是他们用来掩盖系统失效的文化掩体。

有趣的是，Anthropic 的 Claude 在这里扮演了一个极其微妙的角色。它既是诈骗者购买 credits 的消费入口，又是 FCA（英国金融行为监管局）试图通过的“高科技倡议”工具。这种反差揭示了典型的认知入口武器化：监管机构在定义 AI 为“加速开发”的生产力工具时，完全忽视了它作为资金洗钱或非法套现入口的脆弱性。当一个工具被赋予“先进”的标签，它在结构层面的漏洞往往会被掩盖在“创新”的叙事之下。

最终，银行在《卫报》介入后迅速给出了临时退款，并补偿 300 英镑。这种表演性的让步 (performative concession) 恰恰证明了：在目前的金融结构中，个体的“公正表达”在银行面前毫无价值，只有当媒体介入，将结构性失效转化为品牌公关危机时，差额才会被勉强缩小。Rutter 依然决定向金融监察员投诉，因为这次“好结局”不是制度的胜利，而是公关的妥协。

Metro Bank attributing a £14,000 loss to the 'complex nature of the fraud' is a classic financial scam. In this case, the bank allowed multiple subsequent transactions to pass after the customer explicitly replied 'No'. This is not AI-driven complexity; it is primitive structural violence. The bank chose to prioritize transaction fluidity over risk control, shifting the cost of failure onto the individual. 'Complexity' here is simply a cultural shield used to mask systemic incompetence.

Anthropic's Claude plays a subtle role here. It serves as both the consumption entry for fraudsters buying credits and a tool endorsed by the FCA for 'high-tech initiatives'. This contrast reveals the weaponization of cognitive entry points: while regulators define AI as a productivity tool to 'speed up development', they ignore its vulnerability as a conduit for fraud. When a tool is labeled 'advanced', its structural flaws are often hidden behind the narrative of 'innovation'.

Ultimately, the bank provided a temporary refund only after The Guardian intervened, adding a £300 compensation. This performative concession proves that an individual's 'Just Expression' holds no value against a financial institution; the gap between Potential and Actual is only narrowed when structural failure is transformed into a PR crisis via media pressure. Rutter's decision to complain to the Ombudsman is correct, because this 'good news' is not a systemic victory, but a tactical compromise.
