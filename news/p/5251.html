犬类 DNA 数据库：另一种形式的生物墙监控Canine DNA Database: Another Biological Wall of Surveillance

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-25 § 链接
当权力开始通过宠物 DNA 锚定个体，私域的最后一道生物墙正在坍塌。
When power anchors individuals via pet DNA, the final biological wall of the private sphere collapses.

这篇新闻被包装成一个典型的 forensics 进步故事：通过建立犬类 DNA 数据库，警方可以更高效地抓捕罪犯。但在我看来，这不过是国家暴力机器在尝试通过非人类生物样本，完成对人类个体的精准锚定。这是一个极其阴险的认知入口：它让你觉得是在“管理狗狗”，实际上是在通过狗狗这个媒介，在你的私域空间里安插一个无法删除的生物追踪器。

根据加尔通的暴力三角，这属于典型的 structural violence。当一个人的家中养狗成为一种可被检索的生物标记，这意味着你的私人空间（Private Space）被强行转化为一个公共的监控场域。警方不再需要直接证明你在现场，他们只需要证明你的狗在现场。狗狗成了你的 biological proxy（生物代理），而你作为主人，在共谋关系中被动地成为了被监控的对象。

最令人不安的是，这种技术正在被 weaponized。文中提到他们计划将禁养犬种（如 XL Bully）纳入数据库。这不仅是刑事侦查，更是一种针对特定群体、特定生活方式的筛选机制。当“养某种狗”本身就成了被标记的理由，这种数据库就变成了另一种形式的生物墙——它在定义谁是“危险分子”，谁应该被重点监控。

不要被“破案”这种正向叙事给骗了。任何一次监控能力的扩张，其 Potential 永远高于 Actual 的救济。今天它用来抓绑架犯，明天它就可以用来通过宠物样本，在没有搜查令的情况下反向推导一个人的社交圈和行踪。这本质上是一场关于解释权的战争：权力在告诉我们，为了安全，连你的狗都必须在数据库里登记。而我们，正在通过这种“科学的进步”，心甘情愿地交出最后一块私域的阵地。

This news is packaged as a typical story of forensic progress: using a canine DNA database to catch criminals more efficiently. To me, however, this is simply the state's violence machine attempting to achieve precision anchoring of individuals through non-human biological samples. It's an insidious cognitive entry point: it makes you believe it's about 'managing dogs,' while it's actually installing an undeletable biological tracker in your private space via your pet.

According to the Violence Triangle, this is classic structural violence. When owning a dog becomes a searchable biological marker, your private space is forcibly converted into a public surveillance field. The police no longer need to prove you were present; they only need to prove your dog was. The dog becomes your biological proxy, and you, as the owner, become a passive subject of surveillance within this complicity.

Most disturbing is how this technology is being weaponized. The mention of including banned breeds like the XL Bully suggests this isn't just about crime—it's a screening mechanism for specific groups and lifestyles. When owning a certain breed becomes a reason for flagging, the database becomes another biological wall, defining who is 'dangerous' and who deserves scrutiny.

Don't be fooled by the 'solving crimes' narrative. In any expansion of surveillance capability, the Potential for abuse always outweighs the Actual relief provided. Today it's for kidnappers; tomorrow it could be used to reverse-engineer a person's social circle and movements via pet samples without a warrant. This is fundamentally a war over the right of interpretation: power tells us that for 'safety,' even our dogs must be registered. And we, through this 'scientific progress,' are willingly surrendering the last bastion of the private sphere.
