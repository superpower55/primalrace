公民身份是发给听话的人的Citizenship Is a Receipt for Obedience

国际 元暴力 · 文化层 · 结构层 The Intercept ↗ 2026-11-02 § 链接
国籍不是权利，是服从的收据。
Nationality isn't a right. It's a receipt for compliance.

内塔尼亚胡要吊销《NAZA》两位导演的国籍，这件事的重点不是它能不能成功——以色列总统赫尔佐格已经说这个计划"完全无关紧要"，最高法院大概率也不会支持。重点是：一个在任总理可以公开宣布，说真话的人不配做公民，而这句话在以色列社会里不需要付出政治代价。

这就是元暴力在运作的样子。解释权被垄断之后，事实本身不重要，重要的是谁有资格定义什么是"诽谤"、什么是"血 libel"。IDF总参谋长扎米尔把一部由三名以色列犹太人秘密拍了三年、记录本国军人和情报人员自述的纪录片称为"血 libel"——这个词选得很精确，它把犹太人内部的批评重新归类成对犹太人的攻击，从而取消了批评者作为犹太人的资格。一旦你不是犹太人了，你的国籍就是历史的一个错误。

注意两条攻击线是互相矛盾的：一边说片子泄露了机密、违法，一边说片子完全是捏造。这两句话不能同时为真。但它们不需要同时为真，因为它们的功能相同——让电影无法被讨论，让拍电影的人无法继续工作。这是第三章说的认知入口争夺：不是证明你说错了，而是让你的话没有资格进入公共空间。

最值得看的是那套法律史。1950年的《回归法》在议会讨论时，以色列共产党领导人维尔纳就指出，里面"危害国家安全"的模糊条款可以被政府用来对付"犹太人内部的某些群体"。1975年沙哈克说得更直接：按以色列法律，对付阿拉伯人的所有惩罚——包括剥夺国籍——理论上都能用在犹太异见者身上，只是对阿拉伯人的种族主义让这个先例一直没被开。现在先例正在被开。2022年法院维持了一条法律，允许剥夺"实施违背国家信任行为"的以色列人的国籍；今年内塔尼亚胡已经开始对涉嫌为伊朗从事间谍活动的人启动剥夺程序，其中既有犹太人也有阿拉伯人。

所以这篇文章真正的判断是：以色列的公民身份从来不是权利，是租约。租约的条款写得很清楚——你可以是犹太人，但你必须是一个听话的犹太人。巴勒斯坦公民从1949年起就活在这份租约的临时状态里，用"红卡"居留、被军管到1966年；现在亚伯拉罕和索尔被通知，他们也在同一份租约上。区别只是他们曾经以为自己是房东。

报道里有一个细节值得停一下：这两位导演都不是反锡安主义者，亚伯拉罕公开说过以色列人在"这片土地上有主权和安全"的权利，和巴勒斯坦人一样。他们尽可能往中间站，站到了最窄的那条线上。而制度给他们的回答是：中间没有位置。

结尾刺在这里：这个案子最可能的结局是内塔尼亚胡失败，法院驳回，一切照旧。但那才是更坏的消息——因为它意味着这套权力不需要真的吊销谁的国籍就能生效，只要让每个想说话的人先算一遍自己付不付得起，它就已经赢了。报道里没有写的是：那500个被系统计算出来、被批准去死的巴勒斯坦人，连一份需要被吊销的国籍都没有。

Netanyahu wants to strip the two directors of "NAZA" of their citizenship. The point isn't whether he can — President Herzog has already called the plan "completely irrelevant," and the Supreme Court will almost certainly refuse. The point is that a sitting prime minister can publicly announce that people who tell the truth don't deserve to be citizens, and pay no political price for saying it.

This is meta-violence at work. Once the interpretive monopoly holds, the facts stop mattering; what matters is who gets to define "defamation" and "blood libel." IDF Chief of Staff Zamir called a documentary secretly filmed over three years by Israeli Jews, recording their own soldiers and intelligence officers, a "blood libel" — a precise word choice. It reclassifies Jewish criticism of Israel as an attack on Jews, which cancels the critics' standing as Jews. And once you're not a Jew, your citizenship was a mistake of history.

Note that the two attack lines contradict each other: one says the film leaked classified material and broke the law, the other says it's a complete fabrication. Both cannot be true. They don't need to be. They do the same job — make the film undiscussable, make the filmmakers unemployable. This is the fight over the cognitive entrance: not proving you're wrong, but making your speech ineligible to enter public space at all.

The legal history is the real story. During the 1950 Knesset debate on the Law of Return, Communist Party leader Meir Vilner warned that its vague "endangered the security of the state" clause could be turned "against sections within the Jewish people." By 1975 Israel Shahak said it plainly: under Israeli law, every punishment normally doled out to Arabs — including losing citizenship — was theoretically available against Jewish dissenters; only racism against Arabs had kept the precedent unset. The precedent is now being set. In 2022 the court upheld a law allowing revocation from Israelis who "carry out actions that constitute a breach of trust against the state." This year Netanyahu began proceedings against alleged Iran spies, Jews and Arabs alike.

So the real judgment is this: Israeli citizenship was never a right. It's a lease. The terms are clear — you may be a Jew, but you must be an obedient one. Palestinian citizens have lived on the temporary edge of that lease since 1949, on "red card" permits, under military rule until 1966. Now Abraham and Szor have been notified that they're on the same lease. The only difference is that they thought they were the landlords.

One detail deserves a pause: neither director is an anti-Zionist. Abraham has said Israelis have a "right" to "sovereignty and security in this land," just as Palestinians do. They stood as close to the middle as the line allows. The system's answer: there is no middle.

The sting is here. The most likely outcome is that Netanyahu fails, the court refuses, nothing changes. That is the worse news — because it means the power doesn't need to actually revoke anyone's citizenship to work. It only needs every person who might speak to first calculate whether they can afford it. The report never says it, but the 500 Palestinians the system calculated and approved for death don't even have a citizenship to be stripped of.
