足球帝国的权力共谋与定价权骗局The Power Complicity and Pricing Scam of Football's Empire

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-28 § 链接
所谓的管理危机，本质是共谋者之间关于‘分赃定价权’的博弈。
This is not a crisis of management, but a game of 'loot pricing' among co-conspirators.

把这次 UEFA 对 Infantino 的刑事指控看作‘正义对腐败的挑战’，就太 naive 了。在 FIFA 这种典型的权力黑盒里，不存在纯粹的‘清廉’，只存在‘定价权的争夺’。这根本不是一场关于法律的战争，而是一场关于谁能定义‘价值’的存在性战争。

Infantino 试图通过 FFE 计划将世界杯这个‘皇冠上的明珠’以 200 亿美金的低价贱卖给 Thrive Capital，这种 absurdly low 的定价本身就是一种结构性暴力。他不是在管理足球，而是在利用解释权制造一个 sweetheart deal，为自己预留一个年薪千万的退休金席位。这在元暴力（meta violence）的逻辑下非常顺畅：掌控者定义事实，事实服务于私利。

而 UEFA 的反击路径极其耐人寻味：先在 Women's World Cup 这种‘政治正确’的入口上做出让步，以此掩护在刑事指控上的强攻。这是一种典型的武器化表达——用表面的‘合作’来降低对方的防守阈值，然后迅速在法律层面对其进行精准打击。UEFA 并不在乎足球的纯洁，他们在乎的是：为什么这笔买卖的定价权不在我手里？

最讽刺的是那些‘等待证据才愿意倒戈’的会员协会。他们不是在等待正义，而是在计算博弈的最优解。在 FIFA 的 one-member, one-vote 伪民主叙事下，权力是通过利益分发来维持的。只要 Infantino 能持续通过 funding projects 购买忠诚，他就是这个共谋场域的中心。

这场闹剧揭示了一个残酷的事实：在顶级权力结构中，‘刑事指控’往往不是为了入狱，而是作为谈判桌上的筹码。当 Potential（足球的真实价值）与 Actual（贱卖价格）之间的差额大到让共谋者内部产生分歧时，暴力才会以‘法律’的形式出现。

Interpreting UEFA's criminal complaint against Infantino as 'justice fighting corruption' is painfully naive. In the black box of FIFA, there is no such thing as pure 'integrity'—only the struggle for the power to set the price. This is not a legal battle; it is an existential war over who defines 'value'.

Infantino's attempt to sell the World Cup—the crown jewel—to Thrive Capital for an absurdly low $20bn is a form of structural violence. He isn't managing football; he is using his monopoly on interpretation to manufacture a sweetheart deal, securing a multi-million dollar retirement for himself. This is the logic of meta-violence in action: the controller defines the facts, and the facts serve the private interest.

UEFA's counter-strategy is telling: they first make concessions on the 'politically correct' front of the Women's World Cup to mask their legal assault. This is the weaponisation of expression—using superficial 'cooperation' to lower the opponent's guard before striking with criminal charges. UEFA doesn't care about the purity of the game; they care that they were excluded from the pricing power.

Most ironic are the member associations 'waiting for evidence' before defecting. They aren't waiting for justice; they are calculating their optimal expression in this game. Under FIFA's pseudo-democratic narrative of 'one-member, one-vote', power is maintained through the distribution of loot. As long as Infantino can buy loyalty via funding projects, he remains the center of this complicity.

This farce reveals a brutal truth: in top-tier power structures, 'criminal complaints' are rarely about prison; they are bargaining chips. When the gap between Potential (the true value of football) and Actual (the fire-sale price) becomes large enough to fracture the internal complicity, violence manifests as 'the law'.
