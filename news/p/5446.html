废墟上的纪念碑与权力者的逃避Monuments on Ruins and the Escapism of Power

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-07 § 链接
创伤的 Actual 状态取决于权力者是否愿意支付真相的代价。
The Actual state of trauma depends on whether power is willing to pay the price for truth.

Violence = Potential − Actual。对于 10 月 7 日的幸存者来说，直接暴力 (direct violence) 虽然在物理上停止了，但结构性暴力 (structural violence) 依然在高效运转。这种暴力体现在一个极其残忍的差额中：受害者在废墟上挣扎着重建生活，而导致这场灾难的权力中心却在通过拒绝独立调查来维持其存在。

内塔尼亚胡的逻辑是典型的元暴力 (meta violence) 运作——他掌控着叙事权，将个体创伤转化为国家主义的燃料。当幸存者要求真相时，他通过政治操弄将这种诉求定义为“对国家安全的不忠”或“政治攻击”。这是一种极其卑劣的武器化表达：利用受害者的血泪来为更广泛的种族清洗和吞并领土提供合法性。在这种叙事中，死者变成了筹码，幸存者变成了装饰品。

最令人作呕的共谋 (complicity) 发生在权力阶层内部。那些希望通过战争掩盖自身腐败和无能的政客，与极端右翼达成利益站队。他们不需要真相，只需要一个永恒的敌人。当一个候选人诅咒幸存者“回到囚禁状态”时，这不再是个人的情绪失控，而是元暴力在文化层面的直接外溢——在权力者的逻辑里，只有能为权力服务的人才配拥有“人权”。

好新闻应该是差额的缩小，但这里只有差额的扩大。幸存者在纪念碑与新房之间徘徊，这种“不可能的选择”本质上是权力缺失后的精神内耗。真正的 healing 不是写一本书或建一个花园，而是让那个导致 1200 人死亡、数万人受创的系统被彻底审计。只要独立调查被拒绝，所有的纪念活动都只是在结构性暴力的掩盖下进行的表演性哀悼。

Violence = Potential − Actual. For the survivors of October 7, direct violence may have physically ceased, but structural violence continues to operate with high efficiency. This violence manifests as a cruel gap: while victims struggle to rebuild their lives amidst ruins, the power center responsible for the catastrophe maintains its existence by refusing an independent inquiry.

Netanyahu’s logic is a textbook operation of meta-violence—he monopolizes the narrative, converting individual trauma into fuel for nationalism. When survivors demand the truth, he weaponises expression to define such demands as 'disloyalty to national security' or 'political attacks.' It is a despicable strategy: using the blood and tears of victims to legitimize broader ethnic cleansing and annexation. In this narrative, the dead are reduced to bargaining chips, and the survivors to ornaments.

The most sickening complicity occurs within the power hierarchy. Politicians seeking to mask their own corruption and incompetence through war have aligned their interests with the far-right. They don't want truth; they want a perpetual enemy. When a candidate wishes a survivor were returned to captivity, it is no longer a personal emotional outburst, but a direct overflow of meta-violence at the cultural level—in the logic of power, only those who serve power deserve human rights.

Good news is the shrinking of the gap, but here the gap only widens. The survivors' hesitation between monuments and new homes is essentially a mental attrition caused by the absence of accountability. True healing is not writing a book or planting a garden; it is the thorough auditing of the system that allowed 1,200 deaths and traumatized tens of thousands. As long as an independent inquiry is rejected, all commemorations are merely performative mourning under the cover of structural violence.
