用“豁免权”给暴力背书的司法骗局The Judicial Scam of Using 'Immunity' to Endorse Violence

性别 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-21 § 链接
当制度用“豁免权”屏蔽责任，它就成了暴力的共谋者。
When a system uses 'immunity' to shield responsibility, it becomes a co-conspirator of violence.

这起案件揭露了一个极其恶劣的 Structural Violence：警方和检方在处理强奸与恐吓案时，如此随意地将受害者的住址泄露给施暴者，而法律竟然试图用“核心豁免权” (core immunity) 给这种低级失职穿上防弹衣。

所谓的“豁免权”本质上是一种 Meta Violence。它在定义谁是“不可触碰”的权力主体。在这种叙事里，司法机构的“程序稳定性”被置于受害者的“生存安全”之上。警方没标记保密，律师随口泄露，结果是受害者被迫搬家，而体制却在讨论如何合法地逃避赔偿。这哪是法律，这分明是权力阶层在通过定义“豁免”来合法化自己的傲慢。

Jess Phillips 提到的点很关键：民事诉讼不是为了那点钱，而是为了通过赔偿压力逼迫系统产生 Actual 的改变。如果没有这种 Accountability，警察和 CPS 就会在潜意识里继续把女性受害者视为“可消耗的程序零件”而非具有主体性的人。这种 Complicity 极其隐蔽——法官、律师、警察，他们通过维持一套彼此掩护的专业壁垒，共同构建了一个让受害者在寻求保护时反而被再次伤害的陷阱。

如果最高法院不推翻这个 Ruling，那么“保护女性”就成了一个纯粹的 PR Scam。一个不能被追责的保护机制，本质上就是一种变相的施暴。

This case exposes a vile form of Structural Violence: the police and CPS carelessly disclosed a survivor's address to her violent ex-partner, and the law is now attempting to wrap this gross negligence in the protective cloak of "core immunity."

This so-called "immunity" is essentially a form of Meta Violence. It defines who the "untouchable" subjects of power are. In this narrative, the "procedural stability" of judicial institutions is prioritized over the "existential security" of the survivor. The police failed to mark the address as confidential, the lawyer leaked it, the woman was forced to flee her home, and the system is now debating how to legally evade compensation. This isn't law; it is the power class using the definition of "immunity" to legitimize their own arrogance.

Jess Phillips hits the nail on the head: civil litigation isn't about the money; it's about using the pressure of damages to force an Actual change in the system. Without this Accountability, the police and CPS will subconsciously continue to treat female survivors as "expendable procedural parts" rather than human beings with agency. This Complicity is subtle—judges, lawyers, and police officers, by maintaining a professional barrier of mutual protection, collectively construct a trap where survivors are re-victimized while seeking protection.

If the Supreme Court fails to overturn this Ruling, "protecting women" becomes a pure PR Scam. A protection mechanism that cannot be held accountable is, in essence, just another form of violence.
