巴黎街头的橡胶子弹与政治指尖的月亮Rubber Bullets in Paris and the Political Finger Pointing at the Moon

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-08 § 链接
用暴力抹除需求，用叙事掩盖暴力，这是最典型的结构性共谋。
Erasure of needs via violence, masking of violence via narrative: a textbook case of structural complicity.

学生要求改善破败的教室、填补教师缺口，这在任何文明叙事中都是 reasonable。但法国政府给出的 response 是橡胶子弹和断手。这就是典型的 Violence = Potential − Actual：学生本可拥有正常的教育环境，而这个差额被转化为直接的身体伤害 (direct violence)。

更有趣的是文化层面的 weaponization。右翼政客 Marine Le Pen 迅速将焦点从“教育崩塌”转移到“反骚乱法”，这是极其熟练的认知入口操纵——通过放大极少数的暴力行为，将抗争者定义为“暴徒”，从而让结构性暴力 (structural violence) 变得合法且自然。正如文中提到的那个比喻：傻瓜在看手指，而月亮被刻意隐藏了。这里的“手指”就是被制造出来的骚乱叙事，而“月亮”是长期被剥夺的教育资源。

这场博弈的残酷之处在于，无论是左翼的 Mélenchon 还是右翼的 Le Pen，他们都在将这些年轻的 lycéens 视为 2027 年大选的“票仓”或“筹码”。这种政治共谋 (complicity) 将学生的主体性完全客体化。对于权力者来说，学生是否能坐在不漏水的教室里并不重要，重要的是这场骚乱能如何被定义，从而在接下来的存在性战争中为自己赢回解释权。

Students demand basic repairs for crumbling schools and a fix for teacher shortages—demands that are reasonable in any civilized narrative. Yet, the French government responds with rubber bullets and severed hands. This is a stark manifestation of Violence = Potential − Actual: the gap between the education students deserve and their reality is being filled with direct violence.

More insidious is the weaponisation of the cultural layer. Marine Le Pen swiftly shifts the focus from "educational collapse" to "anti-riot laws," a masterful manipulation of the cognitive entry point. By amplifying isolated incidents of unrest, she labels protesters as "thugs," making structural violence appear legitimate and natural. As the article suggests, the fool looks at the finger while the moon is hidden. The "finger" is the manufactured narrative of chaos; the "moon" is the long-term deprivation of resources.

The cruelty of this game lies in the complicity of the political class. Whether it is Mélenchon on the left or Le Pen on the right, these lycéens are treated as mere "votes" or "leverage" for the 2027 election. Their subjectivity is entirely objectified. For those in power, whether a student sits in a leak-free classroom is irrelevant; what matters is how the riot is defined to win the existential war for the right of interpretation.
