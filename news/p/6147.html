一部传记片，两个女人的名字A Biopic With Two Roles for Women

文娱 文化层 · 元暴力 Billboard ↗ 2026-10-31 § 链接
新闻摘要环球影业宣布，一部名为《Snoop》的Snoop Dogg传记片将于2027年8月6日在美国上映，由克雷格·布鲁尔执导、布莱恩·格雷泽制片。影片已公布选角，乔纳森·戴维斯饰演Snoop本人，迈尔斯·布洛克饰演Dr. Dre，卡姆伦·琼斯饰演2Pac。
The newsUniversal Pictures' Snoop Dogg biopic, titled Snoop, is set to hit U.S. theaters on Aug. 6, 2027, directed by Craig Brewer and produced by Brian Grazer. Casting has been announced, with Jonathan Daviss as Snoop, Myles Bullock as Dr. Dre and Camron Jones as 2Pac.
Snoop 的故事里，女人只有母亲和妻子两个格子
In Snoop's story, women get two slots: mother and wife.

这份演员表最该被盯着看的，不是谁演 Snoop，而是女性角色只有两个：母亲 Beverly Tate，妻子 Shante Broadus。一个生他，一个嫁他。中间那些撑起 Death Row 声音的女人——The Lady of Rage 出现在 Doggystyle 里，被列进来了，但她演的是"Death Row 家族的一员"，不是谁的故事。

这不是选角疏漏，是叙事结构。传记片是"一个人如何成为传奇"的机器，这台机器需要一个起源（母亲）和一个归宿（妻子），中间全是兄弟、合作者、对手、仇人。女人在这套模板里只能是坐标，不是路径。Lady of Rage 有名字、有作品、有位置，但她在这张表上的功能是"曾与 Snoop 同厂牌"，等于一个背景板。

Snoop 在 CinemaCon 说"轮到我讲我的故事了"，引的是 N.W.A 的 Straight Outta Compton。那部片子当年也被问过同样的问题：Dre 和 Cube 的故事讲完了，女人在哪里。十年过去，模板没变，只是换了一个主角。

Suge Knight 那条值得单独看。报道写他 2015 年肇事逃逸致死，认罪后正在服 28 年刑。这个人被列进演员表，是作为"把 Snoop 带进厂牌的人"，一个事业推手。他的暴力被压缩成一句履历，因为这部片子要讲的是崛起，不是代价。谁付了那个代价，报道没写，演员表里也没有那一栏。

所以这部片子会很好看，音乐会很好听，Snoop 会再一次被讲成一个从长滩走出来的传奇。而传奇的语法要求有些东西必须消失：那些没有成为母亲、没有成为妻子、也没有成为"家族一员"的女性，她们在这套叙事里连格子都没有。

传记片从来不是还原一个人的一生，是挑选一种讲法。这一次被挑选的讲法里，女人是入口和出口，路是男人走的。

The thing to stare at in this cast list isn't who plays Snoop. It's that there are exactly two women: his mother, Beverly Tate, and his wife, Shante Broadus. One births him, one marries him. The women who helped build the Death Row sound in between—The Lady of Rage appears on Doggystyle and is in the list—get filed as "part of the Death Row family," not as anyone's story.

This isn't a casting oversight. It's the structure of the genre. The biopic is a machine for turning a person into a legend, and the machine needs an origin (mother) and a destination (wife); everything between is brothers, collaborators, rivals, enemies. Women in this template are coordinates, not paths. Lady of Rage has a name, a catalog, a place—but her function on this list is "was on the same label as Snoop." That's a backdrop.

At CinemaCon, Snoop said it was his turn to tell his story, citing N.W.A's Straight Outta Compton. That film got the same question at the time: once Dre's and Cube's stories are told, where are the women. Ten years later the template hasn't changed. Only the lead has.

The Suge Knight entry deserves its own look. The report notes he's serving 28 years after pleading no contest to voluntary manslaughter in a fatal 2015 hit-and-run. He's in the cast list as "the man who brought Snoop into the label," a career catalyst. His violence gets compressed into one line of résumé, because this film is about the rise, not the cost. Who paid that cost isn't in the report, and there's no column for it in the cast list.

So the film will be good, the music will be great, and Snoop will once again be told as a legend who came out of Long Beach. The grammar of legend requires certain things to disappear: the women who didn't become mothers, didn't become wives, and didn't become "family members" don't even get a slot.

A biopic never restores a life. It selects a telling. In this telling, women are the entrance and the exit. The road is walked by men.
