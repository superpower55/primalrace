脱轨的不仅是火车，还有被掩盖的结构性暴力More Than a Derailment: The Hidden Structural Violence

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-13 § 链接
事故是直接暴力，但导致事故的资源匮乏是结构暴力。
Accidents are direct violence; the resource deprivation causing them is structural violence.

两节车厢侧翻，乘客站在顶棚，这种视觉上的冲击被迅速转化为一个“紧急响应”的公关叙事。交通大臣 Heidi Alexander 的推特在第一时间完成了表演：表达“深切关注”，感谢“紧急服务人员”。这套标准的政治剧本旨在将一件结构性灾难迅速地降级为一次偶然的“事故”。

按照加尔通的暴力三角，这次脱轨是 direct violence，但它绝非孤立事件。在英国铁路这种典型的 privatized 结构中，维护预算的削减、基础设施的陈旧以及对一线人员的压榨，构成了潜伏的 structural violence。当 Potential（本应安全运行的铁路）与 Actual（脱轨的现实）之间出现巨大差额，这个差额就是暴力。而这种暴力在平时被掩盖在“效率”和“成本控制”的文化叙事下，直到车厢翻覆，才被迫显现。

最令人作呕的是这种“响应式”的共谋。Network Rail 和 Southern Rail 的发言人用同样的模板化语言在维持秩序，而政治人物通过社交媒体在收割“负责任”的人设。他们共谋掩盖了一个事实：这种风险是可预见的，而这种预见被权力和资本为了短期利益而选择性忽略。在他们口中，人们应该“多预留 60 分钟行程”，但没人告诉乘客，他们每天在这些随时可能脱轨的铁轨上赌命，本身就是一种被内化的结构性暴力。

Two carriages on their side, passengers stranded on roofs—the visual shock is rapidly converted into a PR narrative of "rapid emergency response." Transport Secretary Heidi Alexander's tweet completes the performance: expressing "deep concern" and thanking "emergency services." This standard political script aims to downgrade a structural disaster into a mere "accident."

According to the Violence Triangle, this derailment is direct violence, but it is never an isolated event. In the privatized structure of British rail, budget cuts for maintenance, aging infrastructure, and the exploitation of frontline staff constitute a latent structural violence. When there is a gap between the Potential (a safely operating railway) and the Actual (the derailment), that gap is violence. This violence is usually masked by a cultural narrative of "efficiency" and "cost control" until the carriages flip, forcing it into view.

Most nauseating is this "responsive" complicity. Spokespeople from Network Rail and Southern Rail use template language to maintain order, while politicians harvest a "responsible" persona via social media. They conspire to hide one fact: this risk was foreseeable, and this foresight was selectively ignored by power and capital for short-term gain. They tell passengers to "allow 60 minutes extra," but no one tells them that gambling their lives daily on these precarious tracks is a form of internalized structural violence.
