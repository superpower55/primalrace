特朗普的“炸弹外交”：一场关于存在性的霸凌博弈Trump's 'Bomb Diplomacy': An Existential Bullying Game

国际 直接层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-17 § 链接
所谓的“和平谈判”只是强权在元暴力掩盖下的最后通牒。
The so-called 'peace negotiation' is merely an ultimatum delivered under the cover of meta-violence.

特朗普在 Fox News 上的发言不是外交辞令，而是一次典型的 weaponized expression。他将“结束战争”这个看似正面的叙事，包装成一个可以随意地通过直接暴力（direct violence）来兑现的筹码。在他的逻辑里，阿曼这个传统的 backchannel 角色不再是外交资产，而成了他追求“快速获胜”这一个人最优解表达的障碍。当现实不配合他的剧本时，他选择直接威胁摧毁对方，这本质上是元暴力（meta violence）的赤裸展示：定义谁是“阻碍”，然后赋予自己通过暴力清除阻碍的合法性。

最讽刺的是这种“不着急”与“炸掉你”的共存。他一方面声称没有时间表，另一方面却在 Truth Social 上划定红线。这是一种极端的博弈心理战，旨在通过制造不可预测的恐惧来夺取解释权。他试图将霍尔木兹海峡定义为“美国领土”，这已经不是在谈论地缘政治，而是在进行一场存在性战争（existential war）——通过抹除他国的主权表达，来确立美国绝对的、唯一的定义权。

这种叙事入口极其危险：它将战争简化为一场“扑克游戏”，将 IRGC 的领导层描述为“优秀的玩家但正在死去”。当国家机器将血腥的屠杀和领土吞并简化为赌桌上的胜负时，文化层面的暴力（cultural violence）就完成了闭环。在这个闭环里，平民的死亡被定义为“peanuts”，而霸凌被定义为“谈判技巧”。

Trump's remarks on Fox News are not diplomatic rhetoric, but a classic example of weaponized expression. He packages the seemingly positive narrative of 'ending the war' as a chip to be cashed in via direct violence. In his logic, Oman's role as a traditional backchannel is no longer a diplomatic asset, but an obstacle to his personal optimal expression of 'winning fast.' When reality fails to align with his script, he threatens total destruction—a naked display of meta-violence: defining who the 'obstacle' is and granting himself the legitimacy to remove it through force.

The most surreal part is the coexistence of 'no hurry' and 'bomb the shit out of them.' He claims to have no timeline while simultaneously drawing red lines on Truth Social. This is an extreme psychological game in an existential war, designed to seize the power of interpretation by manufacturing unpredictable terror. By suggesting the Strait of Hormuz become 'territory of the United States,' he is not discussing geopolitics; he is attempting to erase the expression of another nation's sovereignty to establish an absolute, singular definition of power.

This narrative entry point is perilous: it reduces war to a 'poker game' and describes the IRGC leadership as 'good players but dying.' When the state machine simplifies slaughter and annexation into wins and losses at a gambling table, the loop of cultural violence is closed. In this loop, civilian deaths are dismissed as 'peanuts,' and bullying is rebranded as 'negotiation tactics.'
