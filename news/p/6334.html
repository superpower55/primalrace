榜单把施暴者排第一The List Put the Abuser at the Top

文娱 文化层 · 元暴力 Billboard ↗ 2026-11-01 § 链接
一张年度榜单，就是一次定价权的行使。
A year-end list is an exercise of pricing power.

Billboard 的年度 R&B 榜单把 Chris Brown 排进了前十。他排第几不重要，重要的是他还在上面。2009 年之后他一直在上面。这份榜单的评选标准写得很清楚：vocal performance、musical and lyrical resonance、cultural buzz、touring success、business endeavors。没有一条问过，这些成就的代价是谁付的。

榜单本身是一次表达的武器化。谁被写进去、谁被漏掉、按什么排序，都是认知入口的投放。Billboard 说这不是纯按榜单数据排的——这句话是在强调，排序背后有人的判断，而判断就是权力。$300 million 的巡演收入、39 个城市、48 场演出、200 万观众（来源：Billboard），这些数字被当成纯粹的成就陈列出来，仿佛它们和那个被殴打的女人之间没有任何关系。

这不是个案问题。这是结构在正常运转。一个行业可以把一个施暴者重新包装成传奇，靠的不是掩盖，而是重新定义评价标准——把身体暴力从评价体系里删掉。删掉之后，剩下的全是“成就”。这就是文化暴力最标准的操作：让暴力看起来不像暴力，让成就看起来只是成就。

Chris Brown 拿到格莱美最佳 R&B 专辑，是他 13 年来的第二座。20 周年、$300 million 巡演、最高票房纪录——这份榜单给了他一整套加冕叙事。而那个被他打过的女人，在这篇报道里连名字都没有出现。她的缺席不是遗漏，是这套评价体系的构成部分。

Summer Walker 给 Chris Brown 的巡演开场。Kehlani、Bryson Tiller 也在他的巡演名单上。共谋不需要恶意，只需要继续工作、继续合作、继续把“和他同台”写进自己的履历。每个人都在自己的最优解里做选择，而这些选择叠起来，就是一个施暴者永远不会被行业真正放逐。

榜单里也有值得看的东西。kwn 的 queer perspective、Kehlani 给新人抬轿子的 generosity、Ravyn Lenae 从 2017 年给 SZA 开场一路走到 Hot 100 第五——这些是真实的劳动。但它们被放在同一张榜单上，和 Chris Brown 并列，共享同一个“2025 年 R&B 很强”的叙事。这个并列本身就是一次洗白。

Billboard 说“2025 年是 R&B 强劲的一年”。这句话没错。错的是，这份强劲里有一部分，是踩在一个女人身上换来的，而榜单假装没看见。

Billboard's year-end R&B list includes Chris Brown. His exact rank doesn't matter. What matters is that he's still on it. He's been on it since 2009. The criteria are spelled out: vocal performance, musical and lyrical resonance, cultural buzz, touring success, business endeavors. Not one criterion asks who paid for these achievements.

The list itself is a weaponized expression. Who gets written in, who gets left out, what order they're placed in — all of it is a cognitive entry point being deployed. Billboard says the picks weren't decided strictly by chart data. That sentence is an admission: human judgment shaped the ranking, and judgment is power. The $300 million tour gross, 39 cities, 48 shows, 2 million fans (source: Billboard) — these numbers are displayed as pure achievement, as if they have nothing to do with the woman he beat.

This isn't an individual case. This is a structure operating normally. An industry can repackage an abuser as a legend — not by covering things up, but by redefining the evaluation criteria. Delete physical violence from the rubric, and what's left is all "achievement." This is cultural violence in its standard form: making violence look like it isn't violence, making achievement look like it's just achievement.

Chris Brown won a Grammy for best R&B album — his second in 13 years. The 20th anniversary, the $300 million tour, the highest-grossing record — the list hands him a full coronation narrative. And the woman he beat doesn't even appear by name in this article. Her absence isn't an oversight. It's a structural component of the evaluation system.

Summer Walker opened for Chris Brown's tour. Kehlani and Bryson Tiller are on his tour bill too. Complicity doesn't require malice — just continuing to work, continuing to collaborate, continuing to write "shared a stage with him" into your résumé. Everyone makes their own optimal choice, and those choices stack up into an industry that will never actually exile an abuser.

There are things worth seeing on this list too. kwn's queer perspective, Kehlani's generosity in lifting rising stars, Ravyn Lenae's path from opening for SZA in 2017 to a Hot 100 top-five hit — that's real labor. But it's placed on the same list as Chris Brown, sharing the same "2025 was a strong year for R&B" narrative. The juxtaposition itself is a laundering operation.

Billboard says 2025 was a strong year for R&B. That's true. What's false is that part of this strength was bought by stepping on a woman — and the list pretends not to see it.
