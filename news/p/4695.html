自然灾害是掩体，结构性暴力才是凶手Natural Disasters as Cover, Structural Violence as the Killer

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-02 § 链接
所谓的“自然灾害”只是触发点，真正的暴力是资源分配的缺席。
A "natural calamity" is just a trigger; the real violence is the systemic absence of resources.

军队官员把这场悲剧称为“自然灾害” (natural calamity)，这是一个典型的文化暴力 (cultural violence) 陷阱。它通过将责任推给不可抗力的自然，成功地将政府在基础设施建设、预警机制以及灾后救援中的失能给“自然化”了。当 morgues 填满到必须用 DNA 来认尸的时候，这已经不是天灾，而是 structural violence 的集中爆发。

最令人作呕的细节在于，军队以“高风险”为由拒绝进入偏远村庄，而幸存者却在愤怒地质问：“如果高风险，就更应该采取行动”。这种叙事极其熟悉：权力中心通过定义“风险”来决定谁值得被救，谁应该被留在泥泞中等待死亡。在资源分配的博弈中，边陲地带的生命被定价为“可接受的损失”。

更深层的元暴力 (meta violence) 隐藏在救援的细节里。Save the Children 提供的所谓的“尊严包” (dignity kits) 包含额外的纱丽和卫生用品，这种对女性生理需求的补救虽然必要，但它掩盖了一个事实：在灾难发生前，这些女性就生活在极度的结构性匮乏中。而报道最后提到的“女孩人口贩卖”风险，揭示了该地区长期被殖民的原初种族状态——女性身体在和平时期是被消费的商品，在灾难时期则是被掠夺的猎物。

DNA 采样让 Prakrit 能够找到母亲，但这只是 direct 层面的微小救济。只要权力依然垄断着对“风险”的解释权，只要边陲的生命依然被视为次要的，这种所谓的“救援”就只是在结构性暴力之后的一次表演性收尾。

Army officials call this tragedy a "natural calamity," a classic trap of cultural violence. By attributing the disaster to an uncontrollable force of nature, the government effectively "naturalizes" its own failure in infrastructure, early warning systems, and rescue operations. When morgues are so full that DNA is the only way to identify the dead, this is no longer a natural disaster—it is the explosive manifestation of structural violence.

The most repulsive detail is the army's refusal to enter remote villages under the guise of "high risk," while survivors rightfully scream: "If it's high risk, you need to act." This narrative is a recurring scam: the center of power defines "risk" to decide who is worth saving and who is left to rot in the mud. In the game of resource allocation, lives in the periphery are priced as "acceptable losses."

Meta violence lurks even in the relief efforts. The so-called "dignity kits" provided by Save the Children, containing extra saris and hygiene products, are necessary but performative. They mask the fact that these women lived in structural deprivation long before the floods. The mention of child trafficking at the end reveals the Primal Race status of this region—female bodies are commodities for consumption in peace and prey for plunder in chaos.

DNA sampling may help Prakrit find her mother, but this is a mere relief at the direct level. As long as power monopolizes the interpretation of "risk" and peripheral lives remain secondary, this "rescue" is nothing more than a performative wrap-up after the structural violence has already done its work.
