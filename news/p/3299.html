FIFA的“金股”与伪善的看门人FIFA's 'Golden Share' and the Hypocrisy of the Gatekeeper

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-05 § 链接
所有通过扮演“守护者”来掩盖权力私有化的行为，都是典型的共谋scam。
Any act of playing the 'guardian' to mask the privatization of power is a classic complicity scam.

Infantino在2021年公开谴责欧超联赛（Super League）时的姿态，现在看来不过是一场拙劣的表演。这份被披露的term sheet揭露了一个极其肮脏的真相：FIFA不仅没有在抵制一个破坏足球生态的怪物，反而试图通过将自己重新品牌化为“Fifa Super League”来成为这个怪物的主人。所谓的“强烈反对”，不过是在博弈失败后的风险对冲，是典型的假.最优解表达——通过扮演一个维护秩序的“好人”来掩盖其对权力私有化的贪婪。

最令人作呕的细节在于那个“golden share”（金股）。FIFA试图在一个由私人资本控制的闭环联赛中获得特殊的治理权和政治权利。这哪里是在管理足球，这分明是在进行一次最高级别的共谋。Infantino试图在member associations（成员协会）被完全bypass的情况下，通过削减国际比赛窗口来为商业联赛腾空间。这种行为将足球从一项社会性运动降格为一种纯粹的corporate control（公司控制）产品。在这种叙事里，球迷和底层俱乐部只是被牺牲的耗材，而FIFA则通过操纵认知入口，将这种掠夺包装成“足球的进化”。

至于协议中提到的“承诺创建女性等效联赛”，这种典型的weaponized narrative（武器化叙事）简直是对女权的极致侮辱。在一个旨在剥削、垄断且由男性权力中心主导的结构性暴力方案中，夹带一个模糊的、没有具体日期的“女性计划”，不过是为了在道德层面上给自己刷一层绿漆，好让这个巨大的scam看起来更像是一次“进步”。

这就是典型的元暴力运作：男性中心叙事不仅垄断了球场的解释权，还垄断了关于“文明”和“秩序”的定义权。当Infantino自称不想与足球机构作对时，他其实是在说：只要我能拿到金股，我就是秩序本身。

Infantino's public condemnation of the Super League in 2021 was nothing more than a clumsy performance. The leaked term sheet reveals a filthy truth: FIFA wasn't fighting a monster that threatened the football ecosystem; it was trying to become the master of that monster by rebranding it as the 'Fifa Super League.' His 'strong disapproval' was merely a risk hedge after a failed gamble—a fake optimal expression, playing the 'good guy' to hide a greed for privatized power.

The most repulsive detail is the 'golden share.' FIFA sought special governance and political rights within a closed-loop league controlled by private capital. This isn't managing football; it is a high-level complicity. Infantino attempted to squeeze international windows to make room for commercial leagues while completely bypassing member associations. This transforms football from a social movement into a product of corporate control. In this narrative, fans and grassroots clubs are merely disposable consumables, while FIFA manipulates the cognitive entry to package this plunder as 'evolution.'

The promised 'women's equivalent' in the agreement is a textbook case of a weaponized narrative. Tucking a vague, undated 'women's plan' into a structural violence scheme dominated by a masculine-centric power center is an insult to feminism. It is merely a layer of moral paint used to make this massive scam look like 'progress.'

This is meta-violence in action: the masculine-centric narrative monopolizes not only the interpretation of the pitch but also the definition of 'civilization' and 'order.' When Infantino claimed he had no wish to work against football's institutions, he was actually saying: as long as I hold the golden share, I AM the institution.
