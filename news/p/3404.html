格陵兰的钻头与特朗普及他的“现代拓荒者”骗局Greenland's Drills and the 'Modern Wildcatter' Scam

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-08 § 链接
所谓的“拓荒”不过是帝国主义用商业掩盖的殖民预演。
So-called 'wildcatting' is merely a colonial rehearsal masked by commercial narratives.

这根本不是什么能源开发，而是一场教科书级别的 weaponized expression。特朗普及他的 Texas 资本在玩一个很简单的游戏：先用事实制造既成事实（fait accompli），再用叙事覆盖法律。在没有获得许可的情况下强行把钻井设备运上岸，然后把这种违规行为轻描淡写地称为“热情导致的沟通误会”——这在元暴力的逻辑里叫“强者定义现实”。

最令人作呕的是这套叙事的武器化路径：聘请 Dr. Phil 拍摄纪录片，将掠夺资源包装成“现代拓荒者”（modern-day wildcatters）的英雄主义冒险。这种浪漫化叙事试图将一个纯粹的资源抢劫案转化为一场关于“勇气”和“使命”的文化表演。当一个美国海军退役军官（且涉及导弹防御计划）进入董事会时，商业外壳已经薄得透明，底下露出的正是帝国主义的殖民钢骨。

格陵兰政府现在的处境就是典型的 structural violence。他们面对的不是一个商业谈判，而是一个生存博弈：要么违背环保承诺允许钻井，要么拒绝并给对方一个发动“帝国主义议程”的借口。这种“要么被买通，要么被吞噬”的选项，正是男性中心叙事在国际政治中的投射——强权通过制造威胁，强迫弱势方在两种形式的受损中选择其一。

不要被所谓的“建设性会议”骗了。当 300 个集装箱已经在路上时，所谓的“审批流程”不过是给殖民行为补办的入场券。这不再是关于石油，而是关于谁拥有对这片土地的解释权和定义权。

This is not energy development; it is a textbook case of weaponized expression. Trump and his Texas capital are playing a simple game: create a fait accompli with physical actions, then overwrite the law with a narrative. Landing drilling equipment without permission and then dismissing it as a 'communication confusion' caused by 'enthusiasm' is the meta-violence of the powerful defining reality.

Most repulsive is the weaponization of the narrative: hiring Dr. Phil to produce a documentary framing resource plunder as the heroic adventure of 'modern-day wildcatters.' This romanticized narrative attempts to transform a raw robbery into a cultural performance of 'courage' and 'mission.' When a US Navy veteran linked to missile defense joins the board, the commercial facade becomes transparent, revealing the colonial skeleton of imperialism.

The Greenland government is trapped in a classic state of structural violence. They aren't in a business negotiation, but an existential game: either betray environmental commitments by allowing drilling, or refuse and provide a pretext for an 'imperialist agenda.' This choice—between being bought or being devoured—is the projection of masculine-centric narratives in international politics, where power creates threats to force the weak into a lose-lose choice.

Do not be fooled by 'constructive meetings.' When 300 containers are already en route, the 'approval process' is merely a retroactive ticket for colonial entry. This is no longer about oil; it is about who holds the power of interpretation and definition over the land.
