禁掉游戏不能禁掉元暴力的子弹Banning Games Won't Stop Meta-Violence Bullets

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-18 § 链接
用禁止软件来应对枪击，是典型的用 PR 替代结构性治理的 scam。
Replacing structural governance with software bans is a classic PR scam.

菲律宾政府在面对校园枪击案时的反应极其滑稽：禁掉一款叫 Gorebox 的游戏。这套逻辑的潜台词是，暴力源于某种特定的“外部输入”，只要切断了认知入口，就能让学生们重新变回温顺的羔羊。这不仅是 naive，更是一种极其恶劣的 structural violence——它通过将问题“游戏化”和“个体化”，成功地掩盖了社会结构中真实的暴力差额。

Violence = Potential − Actual。一个 14 岁少年能持有高威力枪械并进入校园，这说明在 direct 层，枪支管控已经彻底失效；而在 structural 层，东南亚校园中弥漫的这种“copycat phenomenon”其实是元暴力的某种激进化表达。当一个社会将“强力”、“支配”和“毁灭”定义为男性化叙事中的某种极致表达时，枪击案就成了他们寻找“最优解表达”的畸形尝试。他们不是在模仿游戏，而是在模仿这个世界对“权力”的定义。

最讽刺的是校方的表态：“我们不希望这些事情发生”。这种 ritual 般的客套话掩盖了最核心的共谋：一个允许高威力武器在青少年手中流转的社会，本身就是一座巨大的暴力工厂。禁掉一个 App 只需要一纸公文，但要拆除支撑这种男性中心暴力叙事的元暴力，需要的是对资源分配、心理救济以及权力结构的彻底重构。可惜，政府更喜欢简单的 scam，因为那不需要他们面对真正的血腥。

The Philippine government's reaction to school shootings is farcical: banning a game called Gorebox. The underlying logic suggests that violence stems from a specific "external input," and by cutting off the cognitive entry point, students will return to being docile lambs. This is not just naive; it is a form of structural violence. By "gamifying" and "individualizing" the issue, they successfully mask the actual violence gap within the social structure.

Violence = Potential − Actual. The fact that a teenager could carry high-powered firearms into a school proves that at the direct level, gun control has utterly failed. At the structural level, the "copycat phenomenon" sweeping Southeast Asian campuses is an intensified expression of meta-violence. When a society defines "power," "dominance," and "destruction" as the pinnacle of masculine-centric narratives, shootings become a distorted attempt to find an "optimal expression." They aren't mimicking a game; they are mimicking the world's definition of power.

The university president's statement—"We do not want any of these things to happen"—is a ritualistic platitude that hides a deeper complicity: a society that allows high-powered weapons to circulate among youth is itself a massive violence factory. Banning an app takes a single memo, but dismantling the meta-violence that sustains this masculine-centric narrative requires a total reconstruction of resource allocation, psychological relief, and power structures. Unfortunately, governments prefer a simple scam, as it spares them from facing the actual blood.
