DeepMind 的“去神格化”与商业殖民The De-deification of DeepMind and Commercial Colonization

科技 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-08 § 链接
当科学家退场，AI 就从探索真理的工具变成了纯粹的商业武器。
When scientists exit, AI shifts from a tool for truth to a pure commercial weapon.

Demis Hassabis 的角色转移不是什么“为了关注大局”的体面退场，而是一次典型的结构性吞噬。DeepMind 曾经试图在 Google 的资本庇护下维持一个“独立演员”的幻象，用科学家的纯粹和伦理承诺作为认知入口，吸引全球顶尖大脑。但现在，这个幻象被撕碎了。从 charismatic founder 到一个 technical character 的接手，意味着 DeepMind 已经完成了从“研究机构”到“公司部门”的身份转换。

这不仅是管理权的更迭，更是元暴力的接管。当 science-driven agenda 给 way to cold, hard commercial reality，AI 的定义权就从“造福人类”转移到了“投资回报率”手中。更讽刺的是，那些曾经在内部抗议五角大楼军事合作、担忧加沙地带暴力的员工，现在面对的是一个彻底失去了伦理缓冲带的 Google。所谓的“人类监督”在数百亿美元的商业压力面前，不过是 PR 版本的文化掩体。

Jeff Dean 等核心研究员的离职进一步证明了这场存在性战争的结果：在商业逻辑的绝对统治下，科学家不再是定义方向的主体，而成了可被定价的资源。Google 试图用其庞大的生态系统（Search, YouTube）来对冲人才流失，但这本质上是用 structural violence（资源垄断）来掩盖其在 meta 层面上解释权的丧失。当一个实验室不再因为“这里有最伟大的研究者”而被选择，它就成了一个纯粹的加工厂。

Demis Hassabis's role shift is not a graceful exit for the 'big picture,' but a classic structural swallowing. DeepMind once attempted to maintain the illusion of an 'independent actor' under Google's capital, using scientific purity and ethical promises as a cognitive entry point to attract the world's top minds. Now, that illusion is shattered. Moving from a charismatic founder to a technical character signifies DeepMind's identity transition from a 'research institute' to a 'corporate subdivision.'

This is more than a leadership change; it is the takeover by meta-violence. As the science-driven agenda gives way to cold, hard commercial reality, the power to define AI shifts from 'benefiting humanity' to 'Return on Investment.' More ironically, employees who once protested Pentagon military contracts and voiced concerns over Gaza now face a Google that has completely lost its ethical buffer. The so-called 'human oversight' is merely a cultural shield in the PR version, powerless against the commercial pressure of hundreds of billions of dollars.

The departure of core researchers like Jeff Dean proves the outcome of this existential war: under the absolute reign of commercial logic, scientists are no longer subjects defining direction, but priced resources. Google tries to hedge talent loss with its massive ecosystem (Search, YouTube), but this is essentially using structural violence (resource monopoly) to mask the loss of its explanatory power at the meta level. When a lab is no longer chosen because 'the greatest researchers are here,' it becomes nothing more than a processing plant.
