红发：一个被误认作“多样性”的生物学巧合Ginger Hair: A Biological Coincidence Mistaken for Diversity

哲学 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-02 § 链接
用生物学上的罕见表型来掩盖结构性的种族撕裂，是典型的叙事武器化。
Using a rare phenotype to mask structural racial fractures is a classic weaponisation of narrative.

这组照片在视觉上很 an elegant，但逻辑上是个巨大的 scam。摄影师试图通过“红发”这个极小概率的表型 (Phenotype) 建立一个跨种族、跨阶级的连接点，试图以此推导出美国在撕裂中仍有“多样性”的统一。但这恰恰是典型的 cultural violence：用一个生物学上的随机巧合，去替代对 structural violence 的深刻讨论。

正文中出现了非裔、原住民、波斯裔和亚裔的红发者。这种“视觉奇观”给读者的错觉是：你看，红发可以出现在任何人身上，所以我们是统一的。但这完全是逻辑跳跃。一个原住民孩子拥有红发，并不能消弭他面对的殖民历史和被剥夺的土地；一个非裔女性被误认为保姆，这揭示的是根深蒂固的种族偏见，而不是红发带来的“有趣差异”。

最讽刺的是，文中有人提到“奴隶制让家族史变得模糊”，有人提到被棒球棍袭击的伤疤。这些才是真正的存在性战争 (Existential War) 的痕迹。然而，这些血淋淋的 structural 暴力，在“红发”这个浪漫化的认知入口面前，被稀释成了某种“独特的个人故事”。

当我们将关注点从“谁在被压迫”转移到“谁的发色很特别”时，我们实际上在参与一场共谋 (complicity)。我们用一种审美上的好奇心，掩盖了权力分配的不均。这不是在记录多样性，而是在通过制造一种“生物学上的普世感”，来给一个正在崩塌的社会打补丁。真正的统一不在于我们都有某种罕见的基因，而在于我们能否正视那些让彼此无法统一的元暴力。

Visually, this project is elegant; logically, it is a scam. The photographer uses 'ginger hair'—a low-probability Phenotype—as a bridge to suggest a unifying diversity across the US. This is a textbook example of cultural violence: replacing a rigorous analysis of structural violence with a biological fluke.

Seeing red hair in people of African, Indigenous, Persian, or Asian descent creates a visual spectacle. It tricks the viewer into thinking, 'Look, red hair can happen to anyone, so we are one.' This is a logical leap. An Indigenous child having red hair does not erase the colonial history or the stolen land; a Black woman being mistaken for a nanny reveals systemic racial bias, not a 'quirky difference' brought by hair color.

Most ironic are the mentions of family histories blurred by slavery or scars from baseball bat attacks. These are the actual marks of the Existential War. Yet, these structural violences are diluted into 'unique personal stories' through the romanticised cognitive entry of 'ginger hair.'

When we shift focus from 'who is being oppressed' to 'who has a special hair color,' we become part of the complicity. We use aesthetic curiosity to mask the inequality of power. This isn't documenting diversity; it's applying a biological patch to a collapsing society. True unity isn't found in sharing a rare gene, but in confronting the meta-violence that keeps us apart.
