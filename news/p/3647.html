用金融霸权为战争罪行买单Paying for War Crimes with Financial Hegemony

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-11 § 链接
所谓的“国际正义”在元暴力的金融墙面前只是个昂贵的 scam。
所谓的 'International Justice' is just an expensive scam facing the financial wall of meta-violence.

特朗普政府对 ICC 的制裁不是简单的政治博弈，而是一次教科书式的 structural violence。当美国利用其对全球金融和技术入口的垄断，通过冻结账户、封禁数字服务来瘫痪国际刑事法院时，它实际上是在定义什么是“合法”的司法。在这种逻辑下，追求正义的行为被定义为“犯罪”，而实施战争罪行的权力则被定义为“豁免”。

这正是典型的元暴力（meta violence）运作：通过掌控解释权，将“保护盟友”包装成“维护国家安全”，从而在文化层（cultural layer）将对战争罪行的纵容合法化。当法官、检察官甚至人权律师因为害怕被制裁而停止执业，这种暴力就从直接的肉体伤害升级为了对制度本身的阉割。这不仅仅是在保护内塔尼亚胡，而是在向全球宣告：只要你处于男性中心叙事所定义的“权力核心”，你就可以在不承担任何后果的情况下地狱化地统治他人。

那些被制裁的巴勒斯坦人权组织成了这场存在性战争中的牺牲品。他们的生物墙被进一步加固——不仅是身体上的被囚禁，更是经济和数字层面的被抹除。而那些在制裁压力下选择切断联系的“长期盟友”，则是这场共谋（complicity）的一部分。共谋的回报是生存，代价则是主体性的死亡。在这种结构下，人权被简化成了权力的附属品，而所谓的“法治”不过是强权用来筛选异己的武器。

The Trump administration's sanctions on the ICC are not mere political maneuvering, but a textbook display of structural violence. By leveraging its monopoly over global financial and technological gateways to paralyze the International Criminal Court, the US is effectively redefining 'legality.' In this logic, the pursuit of justice is criminalized, while the commission of war crimes is granted immunity.

This is exactly how meta-violence operates: by seizing the power of interpretation, 'protecting allies' is repackaged as 'national security,' legitimizing the tolerance of war crimes at the cultural layer. When judges, prosecutors, and human rights lawyers cease their practice for fear of sanctions, violence escalates from direct physical harm to the castration of the institution itself. This isn't just about shielding Netanyahu; it's a global announcement that as long as you reside in the 'core of power' defined by the masculine-centric narrative, you can rule others in a hellish manner without consequence.

Sanctioned Palestinian rights groups are the victims of this existential war. Their biological wall is further reinforced—not only through physical imprisonment but through economic and digital erasure. Those 'long-time allies' who cut ties under sanction pressure are part of the complicity. The reward for this complicity is survival; the cost is the death of subjectivity. In such a structure, human rights are reduced to appendages of power, and the 'rule of law' is nothing more than a weapon for the powerful to filter out the dissident.
