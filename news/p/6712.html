强奸犯不会留邮箱Rapists Don't Leave Their Email Addresses

性别 文化层 · 直接层 Vulture ↗ 2026-11-02 § 链接
新闻摘要英国喜剧演员拉塞尔·布兰德在伦敦受审，面临六名女性提出的七项指控，包括三项强奸、三项性侵和一项企图猥亵，时间跨度为1999年至2009年。他的辩护律师在结案陈词中称布兰德「无罪」，并称只有精神病态者才会一边强奸一边问对方是否享受，还称2023年第四频道纪录片「污染」了司法程序，使后续指控者的证词不可信。
The newsRussell Brand is on trial in the U.K. on seven charges from six women, including three counts of rape, three of sexual assault, and one of attempted indecent assault, spanning 1999 to 2009. In closing arguments his barrister said Brand is innocent, argued only a psychopath would rape someone while asking if they were enjoying it, and claimed a 2023 Channel Four documentary had "poisoned the well of justice" and contaminated the complainants.
辩方没在证明无罪，只在给「强奸犯」重新画像
The defense isn't proving innocence; it's redrawing the face of a rapist.

「强奸犯不会留下自己的邮箱。」这句话不是证据，是一幅画像。辩方整段结案陈词做的事只有一件：把「强奸犯」定义成精神病态者，然后拿布兰德不像精神病态者来证明他没有强奸。这套逻辑成立的前提，是强奸犯有一张固定的脸。没有。

留下邮箱、留下照片、走的时候还试着吻别，这些行为在辩方口中是清白者的签名。在一个男人对女人身体拥有默认接触权的结构里，这些恰恰是施暴者最常做的事：他不觉得自己在犯罪，所以他不躲。把「他不躲」读成「他没做」，等于把施暴者的自我认知当成了事实认定。

同一套辩护还顺手把指控者重新定了一次价。律师说，2023年那部纪录片「污染」了司法程序，让后来的女性站出来，所以她们的证词「从根本上被腐蚀」。翻译一下：女性彼此听见、彼此确认、决定一起开口，这被当成污染源。受害者之间产生联结，在辩方那里是一种程序瑕疵。

而那位说布兰德试图把她拖进洗手间的女性，辩方的说法是：他当时「像个疯子一样」，但她一拒绝，他就接受了，所以不构成犯罪。这句话自己就把指控坐实了一半：一个成年男人在公共场合试图把女人拖进洗手间，这被降格成「举止失常」，只要最后停手就算无事发生。停手不是同意，停手只是没做完。

这里动的是文化层，然后直接落到直接层。谁算「可信的受害者」、什么算「像强奸的强奸」，这套叙事在庭审之前就已经分配好了。六名女性、十年、七项指控，这些数字本身不构成判决，但它们说明一件事：单一指控可以被说成误会，六个互不相识的女人说出同一种模式，辩方只能靠「她们被污染了」来回应。

判决还没出来。但有一件事已经发生了：把「不像强奸犯」当成辩护理由，等于承认这个社会从来只惩罚长得像罪犯的人，而剩下的人，继续留他们的邮箱。

"Rapists do not leave their address." That is not evidence. That is a portrait. The whole closing speech does one thing: it defines "rapist" as psychopath, then argues Brand isn't a psychopath, therefore he didn't rape. The logic only works if rapists have a fixed face. They don't.

Leaving an email, leaving photos, trying to kiss her goodbye — the defense reads these as the signature of an innocent man. In a structure where a man has default access to a woman's body, these are exactly what a perpetrator does: he doesn't hide, because he doesn't think he's committing a crime. Reading "he didn't hide" as "he didn't do it" mistakes the perpetrator's self-image for a finding of fact.

The same defense re-prices the complainants. The 2023 documentary, the barrister says, "poisoned the well of justice" by prompting other women to come forward, so their testimony is "fundamentally corrupted." Translation: women hearing each other, confirming each other, deciding to speak together — that is the contamination. Victims forming a connection is, to the defense, a procedural defect.

And the woman who says Brand tried to drag her into a bathroom: the defense says he was "behaving like a crazy lunatic," but when she put her foot down he accepted it, so no crime. That sentence convicts him halfway on its own. An adult man trying to drag a woman into a bathroom in public is downgraded to "acting out," and stopping counts as nothing happened. Stopping is not consent. Stopping is just not finishing.

This moves at the cultural layer and lands on the direct one. Who counts as a credible victim, what counts as a rape that looks like rape — that ledger is settled long before the trial. Six women, a decade, seven charges: the numbers aren't a verdict, but they show that one accusation can be called a misunderstanding, while six strangers describing one pattern leaves the defense only "they were contaminated."

The verdict isn't in. But one thing already happened: arguing "he doesn't seem like a rapist" admits that this society only punishes people who look like criminals. The rest keep leaving their email addresses.
