承认暴力已经“正常化”，是最好的掩体Admitting Violence is "Normalised" is the Perfect Cover

国际 文化层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-20 § 链接
将结构性暴力定义为“常态”，本质上是在为不作为寻找合法性。
Defining structural violence as "normal" is simply a way to legitimize inaction.

平等部长的表态很有意思：她承认种族主义和性别歧视在英国已经“正常化” (normalised)。在加尔通的暴力三角里，当 cultural layer（文化层）开始将 structural violence（结构性暴力）定义为“正常”时，这正是暴力最危险的时刻——因为它不再被识别为暴力，而成了“现状”。

Phillipson 试图通过抨击社交媒体的“粪坑”和保守党政客的言论来展现姿态，但这不过是在 direct layer（直接层）做简单的对冲。真正的 meta violence（元暴力）在于，一个国家的权力机器在意识到暴力被正常化之后，第一反应竟然是将其作为一种“观察到的现象”来描述，而不是将其作为一种“必须被拆除的结构”去执行。这种叙事陷阱在于：一旦承认它是 normal，那么接下来的所有缓慢进展、低效改革，都可以被解释为“在面对如此根深蒂固的常态时，我们已经在努力了”。

最讽刺的共谋 (complicity) 发生在同一个政府内部：一边在播客里感叹女性暴力被轻视，一边为了缓解监狱压力计划提前释放 5000 名囚犯。这种逻辑极其典型的 masculine-centric narrative（男性中心叙事）——在宏大的“系统运行”面前，具体的女性受害者再次成为了可被牺牲的成本。所谓的“希望与能量”，不过是政治演员在权力交接后的情绪 PR，而 Potential 与 Actual 之间的暴力差额，在这次发言后没有缩小哪怕一毫米。

The equalities minister's statement is telling: she admits racism and sexism have become "normalised" in Britain. In Galtung's Violence Triangle, when the cultural layer begins to define structural violence as "normal," violence reaches its most dangerous state—it ceases to be recognized as violence and becomes the "status quo."

Phillipson attempts to perform by attacking the "cesspit" of social media and the rhetoric of Tory politicians, but this is merely a superficial hedge at the direct layer. The true meta-violence lies in the fact that when a state's power apparatus realizes violence has been normalised, its first instinct is to describe it as an "observed phenomenon" rather than an "architecture to be dismantled." The trap of this narrative is simple: once it is labelled as "normal," all subsequent sluggishness and inefficient reforms can be excused as "doing our best against such a deep-seated normality."

The most cynical complicity occurs within the government itself: lamenting the police's failure to take violence against women seriously in a podcast, while simultaneously planning to release 5,000 prisoners to ease prison overcrowding. This is a textbook masculine-centric narrative—where the specific victims of violence are once again treated as acceptable costs for the "functioning of the system." The so-called "energy and hope" is nothing more than emotional PR for political actors after a power shift; the gap between Potential and Actual violence hasn't shrunk by a single millimetre.
