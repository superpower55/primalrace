气候紧急状态下的表演性救火与结构性失效Performative Firefighting and Structural Failure in the Climate Emergency

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-28 § 链接
用战术性的救火掩盖战略性的气候共谋，是典型的结构性暴力。
Using tactical firefighting to mask strategic climate complicity is a textbook form of structural violence.

新闻里充斥着“赛跑”(race)、“关键日子”(crucial days)和“极其激烈”(extremely intense)这种充满临场感的词汇。法国总统马克龙在波尔多视察，西班牙首相桑切斯在气候会议上感叹。这种叙事将气候危机处理成了一场突发的、不可抗力的“自然灾害”，而政治人物则扮演着在灾难面前奋力抢救的英雄角色。但这正是典型的 weaponized narrative：通过强调“前所未有”的极端性，将结构性失效伪装成偶然的运气糟糕。

按照加尔通的暴力三角，这里的 structural violence 极其显著。欧盟官员承认欧洲根本没有为气候危机做好准备，这意味着在过去几十年的经济增长叙事中，对环境的掠夺被内化为一种“文明”的进步。这种掠夺是男性中心叙事下的工业文明共谋——为了维持一个由男性主导的、基于资源榨取和资本扩张的权力结构，他们心照不宣地忽略了生态系统的崩溃。现在的“救火”只是在 Actual 层面做补丁，而 Potential（一个可持续的生态环境）与现状之间的差额，正是由这些决策者在过去数十年通过共谋制造出来的暴力。

最讽刺的是，当 22 万人被撤离、森林被焚毁时，官方给出的解决方案是分发 150 万个 FFP2 口罩。这是一种极其卑微的、表演性的救济。口罩能过滤烟雾，但过滤不掉一个已经崩塌的制度。当“异常”变成“规则”，而规则制定者依然在用“应对紧急情况”的逻辑来处理一个系统性死局时，这种所谓的救助不过是给受害者发的一张止痛药，好让他们在接下来的热浪中更安静地承受结构性暴力的收割。

The news is saturated with high-tension vocabulary—"race," "crucial days," "extremely intense." President Macron visits Bordeaux; Prime Minister Sánchez laments at a climate conference. This narrative frames the climate crisis as a sudden, irresistible "natural disaster," casting politicians as heroes fighting against the odds. This is a classic weaponized narrative: by emphasizing the "unprecedented" nature of the fires, structural failure is camouflaged as a streak of bad luck.

In terms of the Violence Triangle, the structural violence here is glaring. EU officials admit Europe was simply not prepared. This means that within the narrative of economic growth over the last few decades, environmental plunder was internalized as "civilized" progress. This plunder is a complicity of industrial civilization under a masculine-centric narrative—sacrificing the ecosystem to maintain a power structure based on resource extraction and capital expansion. Current "firefighting" is merely a tactical patch on the Actual level, while the gap between the Potential (a sustainable ecosystem) and the current state is the very violence manufactured by these decision-makers through decades of complicity.

The irony peaks when, amidst the evacuation of 220,000 people and the incineration of forests, the official solution is the distribution of 1.5 million FFP2 masks. This is a pathetic, performative relief. Masks filter smoke, but they cannot filter a collapsed system. When the "exception" becomes the "rule," yet the rule-makers still apply "emergency response" logic to a systemic dead-end, such aid is nothing more than a painkiller, ensuring victims endure the harvest of structural violence more quietly during the next heatwave.
