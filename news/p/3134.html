Food Security is a Scam: The Complicity of SupermarketsFood Security is a Scam: The Complicity of Supermarkets

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-01 § 链接
Food security is not about nature's failure, but about the structural violence of a profit-first supply chain.
Food security is not a natural disaster, but a structural violence of a profit-first supply chain.

当英国超市巨头们写信给政客，用“粮食安全” (food security) 这种宏大叙事来呼吁国家优先级时，这就是一场典型的武器化表达。他们试图将气候危机带来的 crop failure 包装成一个需要政府买单的“国家危机”，从而掩盖一个事实：长期以来，这些零售巨头通过压低农民的利润空间，让农民在面对极端天气时毫无抗风险能力。所谓的“利润微薄” (tight margins) 不是自然现象，而是结构性暴力 (structural violence) 的结果。

在这种共谋机制中，超市、批发商与政府达成了一种默契：在气候平稳期，通过极低价压榨生产者以维持消费者的低价快感；而在危机期，迅速切换到“国家安全”模式，要求公共资源投入基础设施（如水库）来为私人资本的供应链止损。农民在其中扮演的是最可悲的客体——他们被告知要面对“最糟糕的年份”，却在申请规划许可的官僚体制中被拖死，最终只能看着作物 wilting on their feet。

这种叙事陷阱在于，它把一个分配正义问题伪装成了技术性问题。增加水库能解决短期缺水，但不能解决一个让生产者在面对 30°C 高温时只能“心碎且钱包破碎” (heartbreaking and wallet-breaking) 的经济模型。如果一个系统的 Potential 是在气候波动中生存，而 Actual 是依赖西班牙进口且农民破产，那么这个差额就是由资本共谋所制造的暴力。

When UK supermarket giants write to politicians using the grand narrative of "food security," it is a classic case of weaponized expression. They attempt to package crop failure caused by the climate crisis as a "national crisis" requiring government intervention, effectively masking the fact that these retailers have historically eroded farmers' resilience by squeezing their profit margins. The "tight margins" mentioned are not a natural occurrence, but a result of structural violence.

In this mechanism of complicity, supermarkets, wholesalers, and the government share a tacit agreement: during periods of climatic stability, they suppress producer prices to maintain the low-cost pleasure of consumers; during crises, they pivot to a "national security" mode, demanding public resources for infrastructure (like reservoirs) to hedge losses for private capital supply chains. Farmers play the most tragic role as objects—told they are facing the "worst year ever," while being strangled by the bureaucratic process of planning permissions, watching their crops wilt on their feet.

The scam of this narrative is that it disguises a problem of distributive justice as a technical issue. Building reservoirs might solve short-term water shortages, but it cannot fix an economic model that leaves producers "heartbreaking and wallet-breaking" when temperatures hit 30°C. If the Potential of a system is survival amid climatic volatility, and the Actual is a reliance on Spanish imports while domestic farmers go bankrupt, then that gap is the violence manufactured by capital complicity.
