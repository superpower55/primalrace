气候危机的“自然”叙事，是最高级的共谋The 'Natural' Narrative of Climate Crisis: The Ultimate Complicity

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-27 § 链接
将结构性毁灭包装成自然灾害，是文化暴力在抹除施暴者的主体性。
Packaging structural destruction as natural disaster is cultural violence erasing the perpetrator's agency.

这张漫画试图用讽刺来揭露南欧山火的荒诞，但我们得看穿这种讽刺背后的 meta violence。大众习惯将 wildfire 归类为“气候危机”或“自然灾害”，这种叙事本身就是一种 weaponization。它把一个由资本积累、资源掠夺、森林管理失能构成的 structural violence，简化为了一个不可抗力的“气候”概念。当人们在讨论“地球在发烧”时，真正地在通过剥削土地和劳动力获利的权力中心，成功地在认知入口处把自己洗成了旁观者。

这本质上是一场关于解释权的博弈。如果山火被定义为“自然灾害”，那么解决方案就是“减排”这种温和的 PR 词汇；但如果将其定义为“生态殖民的后果”，那么这就是一场存在性战争。目前的共谋机制非常高效：政府提供灾后救济，媒体报道惨状，环保组织呼吁捐款。在这个闭环里，所有人都在扮演一个“关心地球”的角色，而真正的施暴者——那些定义了现代工业文明、垄断了能源定价权的男性中心权力结构——在文化暴力层面上被完全隐形了。

不要被这种“讽刺”带来的快感欺骗。如果一件好事只是通过画一张讽刺漫画来获得关注，而没有触碰到权力分配的 structural 层，那么这不过是另一种形式的表演性让步。真正的 good_news 应该是：当受害者不再接受“自然灾害”这个定义，而是开始追究具体资本的法律责任时，Potential − Actual 的差额才真正开始缩小。

This cartoon attempts to use satire to expose the absurdity of southern Europe's wildfires, but we must see through the meta violence beneath the irony. The public is conditioned to categorize wildfires as 'climate crises' or 'natural disasters'—a narrative that is itself a form of weaponization. It reduces structural violence, built on capital accumulation, resource plunder, and systemic failure in forest management, into a passive concept of 'climate.' While the world discusses 'the planet running a fever,' the power centers profiting from the exploitation of land and labor successfully reposition themselves as mere bystanders at the cognitive entry point.

This is essentially a game for the right of interpretation. If wildfires are defined as 'natural disasters,' the solution becomes 'emission reduction'—a mild PR term. But if they are defined as 'the consequences of ecological colonialism,' it becomes an existential war. The current mechanism of complicity is highly efficient: governments provide relief, media report the tragedy, and NGOs call for donations. In this loop, everyone performs the role of 'caring for the Earth,' while the actual perpetrators—the masculine-centric power structures that defined modern industrial civilization and monopolize energy pricing—are completely erased via cultural violence.

Do not be deceived by the pleasure of satire. If a 'good' event is merely gaining attention through a cartoon without touching the structural layer of power distribution, it is just another form of performative concession. True good_news happens only when victims stop accepting the 'natural disaster' definition and start pursuing the legal liability of specific capital. Only then does the gap between Potential and Actual truly begin to close.
