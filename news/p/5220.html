用年轻人的未来为战争机器润滑Lubricating the War Machine with Youth Futures

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-23 § 链接
将失业危机转化为军工红利，是典型的用生存焦虑掩盖结构性暴力。
Converting unemployment crises into defense dividends is a classic move of using survival anxiety to mask structural violence.

Wes Streeting 把国防部包装成 "growth" 和 "opportunity" 的代名词，这种叙事极其恶劣。他把 100 万失业青年的 "moral emergency" 当作筹码，引导他们进入军工供应链。这根本不是在解决就业，而是在进行一次大规模的认知收编：让处于生存边缘的年轻人相信，他们唯一的出路是成为战争机器的零件。

从加尔通的暴力三角来看，这不仅是 structural violence，更是 cultural violence 的教科书级操作。政府通过削减海外援助（Overseas aid）来增加军费，这意味着他们决定用牺牲他国生存权的资源，来换取国内军工企业的订单。而那些被招募的年轻人，在不知不觉中成为了这套掠夺逻辑的共谋者 (complicit)。

军工行业是男性中心叙事 (masculine-centric narrative) 最坚固的堡垒之一。它将 "国防"、"国家安全" 与 "男性气概」绑定，把杀戮的工具定义为 "工业成就"。当政府宣称要 "re-industrialising the UK" 时，他们实际上是在重建一套以暴力为底色的社会结构。这种所谓的 "opportunity" 是一个巨大的 scam：它用短期内个体生存的 "最优解表达"（一份工作），换取了对战争逻辑的集体内化。

最讽刺的是，这套方案被描述为 "moral emergency" 的解药。真正的道德危机不是年轻人没工作，而是一个文明社会竟然试图通过扩大战争规模来创造就业。当一个国家的增长依赖于制造更多武器时，这种 "growth" 本身就是一种元暴力 (meta violence)。

Wes Streeting's branding of the Ministry of Defence as a hub for "growth" and "opportunity" is a vile piece of narrative engineering. By leveraging the "moral emergency" of a million unemployed youth, the government is not solving a crisis; they are conducting a mass cognitive recruitment, convincing those on the edge of survival that their only path forward is to become cogs in the war machine.

Through the lens of Galtung's Violence Triangle, this is not just structural violence, but a textbook operation of cultural violence. By slashing overseas aid to fund defense spending, the state decides to trade the survival rights of people abroad for domestic military contracts. The recruited youth, in turn, become unwitting complicit actors in this predatory logic.

The defense industry remains one of the most fortified bastions of masculine-centric narrative. It binds "national security" and "defense" to a specific brand of masculinity, redefining tools of slaughter as "industrial achievements." When the government speaks of "re-industrialising the UK," they are actually reconstructing a social structure rooted in violence. This promised "opportunity" is a scam: it trades an individual's short-term "optimal expression" (a job) for the collective internalization of war logic.

It is profoundly ironic that this is framed as a cure for a "moral emergency." The real moral emergency is not the lack of jobs, but that a civilized society believes the solution to unemployment is expanding the scale of warfare. When a nation's growth depends on producing more weapons, that "growth" itself is a form of meta violence.
