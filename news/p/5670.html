失业率下降的PR骗局与消失的岗位The Unemployment PR Scam and the Vanishing Vacancies

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-20 § 链接
数据上的“好转”往往是结构性暴力的掩体。
A 'positive' data point is often just a shield for structural violence.

失业率从4.9%降到4.7%，在政客眼中是Budget前的“利好”，在经济学家眼中是“好于预期”。但只要你把目光从这个单一数字移开，看向Vacancies（职位空缺）的崩塌，你就会发现这不过是一场典型的叙事武器化。职位空缺数跌破70万，创2021年以来新低，这意味着系统在停止制造可能性。

这是一个极其阴险的博弈：就业人数在增加，但招聘意愿在萎缩。这意味着现在的“就业”大概率是低质量的存量博弈，或者是被强行塞入低薪岗位的结果。当小企业因为劳动力成本上升而停止招聘时，个体在劳动力市场上的议价权被彻底剥夺。你虽然有份工作，但你失去了选择这份工作的权力，这就是典型的Structural Violence。

最讽刺的是，这种“好消息”被用来给财政大臣John Healey背书。这种将“失业率下降”等同于“经济健康”的逻辑，是典型的男性中心叙事（masculine-centric narrative）在经济领域的延伸——关注宏观的、数字的、权力层面的“胜利”，而完全无视微观个体在面对岗位消失时的存在性焦虑。当Potential（潜在机会）在快速缩减，Actual（实际状态）的微小提升不过是缓刑。

别被那个4.7%给骗了。一个不再创造岗位的市场，本质上是一个正在关闭入口的囚笼。

The unemployment rate dropping from 4.9% to 4.7% is a 'boost' for politicians and 'better than expected' for City economists. But move your gaze from this single digit to the collapse of Vacancies, and you'll see a classic weaponisation of narrative. Vacancies have plummeted below 700,000, a low since 2021. The system is simply stopping the manufacture of possibilities.

This is a sinister game: employment is up, but the will to hire is shrinking. This suggests that current 'employment' is likely a low-quality struggle over existing slots or people being forced into low-wage roles. As small firms stop recruiting due to rising labour costs, the individual's bargaining power in the labour market is stripped away. You may have a job, but you've lost the power to choose that job. This is textbook Structural Violence.

The irony is that this 'good news' is being used to endorse Chancellor John Healey. The logic that 'falling unemployment' equals 'economic health' is a projection of masculine-centric narrative into economics—prioritising macro-level, numerical 'victories' while ignoring the existential anxiety of individuals facing a vanishing job market. When Potential is shrinking rapidly, a slight uptick in Actual is merely a stayed execution.

Don't be fooled by that 4.7%. A market that stops creating vacancies is essentially a cage closing its entrance.
