道歉是廉价的，结构性掠夺才是真实的Apologies are Cheap, Structural Plunder is Real

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-14 § 链接
代际正义不是靠长辈的忏悔，而是靠停止对未来的预支。
Intergenerational justice is not achieved through elders' repentance, but by stopping the pre-emption of the future.

Polly Toynbee 的这篇文章在 perform 一种典型的“觉醒长辈”姿态。她意识到社会契约 broken 了，意识到 Gen Z 和 Millennials 在失业、低薪和房价面前被榨干了，于是她提出要 an apology。但问题在于，在存在性战争中，道歉是这个世界上最廉价的表达，因为它不涉及任何资源的重新分配。

我们要看的是 structural violence。所谓的“代际差距”，本质上是上一代人通过垄断资产（尤其是房产）和制定游戏规则，将未来的潜在价值提前套现，转化成了当下的生活质量。这是一种跨时间的掠夺。当 40% 的 25 岁年轻人不得不回父母家住时，这不再是“懒惰”或“雪花”，而是一个生物墙被制度化加固的结果：年轻人被剥夺了独立生存的物理空间，从而在心理和经济上被强制性地重新纳入父权/长辈的控制结构中。

这种 broken contract 不是意外，而是某种共谋的结果。上一代人享受了全球化和资产泡沫带来的红利，然后迅速通过立法、税收政策和市场准入，把门关上了。现在，当他们站在道德高地上说“我们欠你们一个道歉”时，这种叙事本身就是一种 weaponized expression——它试图用一种情感上的抚慰，来掩盖结构上的剥削，将一个政治经济问题转化为一个伦理问题。

真正的 good_news 不应该是长辈意识到自己错了，而应该是那些被垄断的资源被强制性地、结构性地释放出来。如果道歉之后，房价依然高企，工资依然停滞，那么这次“觉醒”不过是一场自我感动的 PR 秀。

Polly Toynbee is performing the typical role of the 'awakened elder.' She recognizes the social contract is broken and that Gen Z and Millennials are being drained by unemployment and ballooning house prices, so she offers an apology. But in the existential war, an apology is the cheapest form of expression because it involves zero redistribution of resources.

What we are seeing here is structural violence. The so-called 'generational gap' is essentially the previous generation cashing out the potential value of the future—through the monopoly of assets, especially real estate—to fund their own current quality of life. It is a temporal plunder. When 40% of 25-year-olds are forced back into their parents' homes, it's no longer about being 'lazy' or 'snowflakes'; it is the institutional reinforcement of a biological wall. Young people are stripped of the physical space for independence, forcing them back into a structure of control dominated by elders and patriarchy.

This broken contract is not an accident; it is the result of complicity. The older generation enjoyed the dividends of globalization and asset bubbles, then quickly shut the door through legislation, tax policies, and market barriers. Now, standing on a moral high ground saying 'we owe you an apology' is a form of weaponized expression—it attempts to substitute emotional solace for structural exploitation, transforming a political-economic crisis into an ethical one.

Actual good_news would not be elders realizing they were wrong, but the forced, structural release of monopolized resources. If the apology is followed by stagnant wages and soaring house prices, then this 'awakening' is nothing more than a self-indulgent PR stunt.
