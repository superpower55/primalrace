所谓的“进步阵营”不过是另一种共谋的算盘The 'Progressive Bloc' is Just Another Calculation of Complicity

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-01 § 链接
用投票机制的优化掩盖实质性变革的缺失，是典型的结构性欺骗。
Optimizing voting mechanisms to mask the absence of substantive change is a classic structural deception.

这篇文章在讨论一个非常典型的“认知入口”陷阱：它把政治的胜利简化为一种 electoral performance 的算盘。作者 Neal Lawson 试图通过分析曼彻斯特市长的选举结果，推导出一套关于“进步阵营”（progressive bloc）如何通过整合票数来击败右翼的逻辑。但这本质上是一场关于“如何更高效地在既定规则内赢球”的讨论，而不是关于如何改变规则的战争。

所谓的“进步阵营”，在文中被描述成一个可以通过 supplementary vote（补充投票制）和 PR（比例代表制）来量化和操纵的流量池。这种叙事最危险的地方在于，它将“进步”这个词 weaponized 成了某种战术性的标签。当作者呼吁各党派停止“部落主义”并进行“战略整合”时，他实际上是在邀请所有参与者进入一场新的共谋：只要我们共同维持一个足够大的“进步”外壳，就可以在不触动深层结构暴力的情况下，通过技术性的票数转移获得权力席位。

这种“最不坏选项”的博弈，正是典型的假.最优解表达。它让选民在“被右翼收割”和“被一个承诺极少改变的工党收割”之间做选择。这种 negative support（负面支持）不仅无法传递强有力的治理授权，反而让权力持有者在不需要进行激进变革的情况下就稳坐钓鱼台。这是一种 structural violence 的 PR 版本：它给你一种“我们在进步”的幻觉，实际上却在通过制度的微调，确保权力依然在一个封闭的、由男性中心叙事主导的精英阶层中流转。

真正的 good_news 应该是解释权本身的换手，而不是一个名为“进步”的 bloc 在计算如何通过 PR 制度多拿几个席位。如果这种整合仅仅是为了“击败 Reform 党”，那么它并没有击败产生 Reform 党的土壤，而只是在为一个更精致的共谋体制寻找遮羞布。

This article presents a textbook trap of the 'cognitive entry point': it reduces political victory to a calculation of electoral performance. Neal Lawson attempts to derive a logic of how the 'progressive bloc' can defeat the right by consolidating votes. However, this is essentially a discussion on how to win a game within existing rules, rather than a war to change the rules themselves.

The so-called 'progressive bloc' is weaponized here as a tactical label. When the author calls for parties to end 'tribalism' and engage in 'strategic consolidation,' he is inviting all players into a new form of complicity: as long as they maintain a sufficiently large 'progressive' shell, they can secure power through technical vote transfers without disturbing the underlying structural violence.

This game of the 'least bad option' is a classic example of a fake optimal expression. It forces voters to choose between being harvested by the right or being harvested by a Labour party that promises little change. This negative support does not deliver a mandate for transformation; instead, it allows power-holders to remain entrenched without the need for radical reform. It is a PR version of structural violence, creating an illusion of 'progress' while ensuring power continues to circulate within a closed elite governed by a masculine-centric narrative.

True good_news would be a shift in the power of interpretation, not a 'progressive' bloc calculating how to gain a few more seats via proportional representation. If this consolidation is merely to 'beat the Reform party,' it fails to destroy the soil that grew Reform in the first place. It is simply finding a more sophisticated veil for a system of complicity.
