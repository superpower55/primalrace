被轻描淡写的殖民地幽灵与被交换的子宫Lightweight Ghosts and Swapped Wombs

哲学 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-08 § 链接
当叙事追求“轻盈”与“快感”时，结构性暴力便成了背景板。
When narratives prioritize 'lightness' and 'pleasure', structural violence becomes mere background noise.

这份电视指南像是一次典型的文化暴力演习。最讽刺的是《Ghosts Australia》，它试图讨论殖民化（colonisation），却被描述为用“最轻盈、最风趣的笔触”（lightest and wittiest of touches）来处理。这种表达方式本身就是一种 weaponized narrative：通过将血腥的殖民历史转化为一种“轻盈”的消费品，它在潜意识中告诉受众，原初种族的苦难是可以被娱乐化的，只要节奏足够快，幽默感足够强，暴力就可以被稀释成一种文化点缀。

而 Almodóvar 的《Parallel Mothers》则揭示了另一种更深层的暴力。孩子被掉包、子宫被作为国家秘密的承载者，这不仅是家庭狗血剧，更是关于西班牙法西斯主义（Franco）如何通过身体控制来延续其 meta violence 的隐喻。女性的生育力在这里成了被国家机器操纵的棋子，她们的主体性在“掉包”这一动作中被彻底抹除，沦为承载血统与秘密的容器。

从“轻盈”的殖民幽灵到被交换的母亲，这两种叙事共同构成了一个闭环：前者通过消解痛苦来维护现状，后者通过展示痛苦来揭露结构。但遗憾的是，在这一份以“欢乐”（joyous）开篇的节目单里，后者仅仅被标记为一部“强有力的电影体验”，而前者却被赞誉为“天才的适配”。这再次证明，在男性中心叙事掌控的认知入口中，只要能提供快感，真相的重量并不重要。

This TV guide is a textbook exercise in cultural violence. The most cynical entry is 'Ghosts Australia', which tackles colonisation with the 'lightest and wittiest of touches'. This is a weaponized narrative: by transforming the visceral trauma of colonization into a consumable comedy, it signals that the suffering of the Primal Race can be entertainment—provided the pacing is fast and the jokes are sharp enough to dilute the blood.

In contrast, Almodóvar’s 'Parallel Mothers' exposes a deeper layer of violence. The swapping of newborns and the use of the womb as a vessel for national secrets is not just a domestic drama; it is a metaphor for how the meta violence of Franco's fascism operated through bodily control. Female fertility is reduced to a pawn of the state machine, their subjectivity erased in the act of the 'swap', turning them into mere containers for lineage and secrets.

From the 'lightweight' colonial ghosts to the exchanged mothers, these narratives form a loop: one erases pain to maintain the status quo, while the other exposes pain to reveal the structure. Unfortunately, in a schedule that opens with the word 'joyous', the former is praised as 'genius' while the latter is merely a 'forceful experience'. This confirms that in a masculine-centric narrative, as long as pleasure is delivered, the weight of truth is irrelevant.
