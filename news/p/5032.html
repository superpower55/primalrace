排泄物、金牌与被遗忘的生物墙Excrement, Gold Medals, and the Forgotten Biological Wall

性别 结构层 · 文化层 The Athletic ↗ 2026-09-15 § 链接
生理功能的崩溃是极限博弈的代价，而女性承担了双重的生物税。
Physiological collapse is the price of extreme gaming, but women pay a double biological tax.

当世界在讨论一名顶级运动员在比赛中“失禁”的尴尬时，人们习惯性地将其归类为偶然的生理事故或是个人的饮食失误。但剥开这些 a-political 的医学解释，你会发现这其实是一场关于身体极限的残酷博弈。在精英体育的叙事里，身体被简化为一台追求效率的机器，而任何不能转化为速度或力量的生理反应——包括排泄——都被视为需要被克服的“故障”。

有趣的是，这篇报道揭示了一个被长期忽视的 structural violence：女性运动员在这场博弈中面对的是一道更高的生物墙。男性运动员的失禁往往被归结为“坏桃子”或“咖啡因”，是一种随机的外部干扰；而女性的失禁则与盆底肌功能障碍、分娩、月经周期以及绝经期这些深层的生物学事实绑定。这意味着，女性在追求相同竞技高度的同时，必须额外支付一份由生物结构决定的“税收”。

这种差异在文化层面上被进一步抹平。尽管数据表明女性面临更高的风险，但体育界的“最优解表达”依然是那句冷酷的 "Go hard or go home"。当女性运动员像 Wietrzyk 一样以淡然面对失禁并夺冠时，这看起来像是一种主体性的胜利，但本质上是她们在内化一套男性中心主义的竞争逻辑：只要能赢，身体的尊严和生理的崩溃都可以被当作可忽略的 collateral damage。

真正的公正表达不应该是鼓励运动员在身体崩坏时继续冲刺，而应该是承认生物墙的存在，并将针对女性生理特性的科研与医疗资源分配，从一个“Wild West”般的边缘地带，移至竞技体育的核心议程中。

While the world discusses the 'embarrassment' of an elite athlete soiling themselves, the public instinctively categorizes it as a random accident or a dietary slip-up. But stripping away these a-political medical explanations reveals a brutal game of physical limits. In the narrative of elite sports, the body is reduced to a machine optimized for efficiency; any physiological response that doesn't translate into speed or power—including excretion—is treated as a 'malfunction' to be overcome.

Interestingly, the report exposes a long-ignored structural violence: female athletes face a higher Biological Wall in this game. For male athletes, incontinence is often attributed to a 'bad peach' or 'caffeine'—a random external interference. For women, it is bound to pelvic floor dysfunction, childbirth, menstruation, and menopause. This means women must pay an additional 'tax' determined by biological structure while pursuing the same competitive heights.

This disparity is further flattened at the cultural level. Despite the higher risk, the 'optimal expression' in sports remains the cold "Go hard or go home." When female athletes like Wietrzyk face incontinence with nonchalance and win, it looks like a victory of subjectivity, but it is actually the internalization of a masculine-centric competitive logic: as long as you win, bodily dignity and physiological collapse are mere collateral damage.

Just Expressions would not be encouraging athletes to sprint through bodily breakdown, but acknowledging the Biological Wall and moving research and medical resources tailored to female physiology from a 'Wild West' fringe to the core agenda of elite sports.
