一条爆料，喂饱所有人One Leak, Everyone Fed

文娱 文化层 Polygon.com ↗ 2026-11-02 § 链接
新闻摘要据 CartierFilms 爆料，瑞安·雷诺兹正在为漫威撰写一个以加拿大超级英雄团队「阿尔法飞行」为核心的项目，尚不确定是独立电影还是并入下一部《死侍》。同一爆料还称有新演员洽谈接演金刚狼，并提及 2028 年 5 月 5 日上映的《X 战警》选角传闻。
The newsA leak from CartierFilms claims Ryan Reynolds is writing a Marvel project centered on Alpha Flight, the Canadian superhero team, though it is unclear whether it becomes its own film or figures in the next Deadpool. The same leak mentions a new actor in talks for Wolverine and casting rumors for the X-Men film due May 5, 2028.
未证实的爆料不是新闻，是给粉丝的期货。
An unverified leak isn't news. It's a futures contract sold to fans.

这篇报道里没有一个事实是这家媒体自己拿到的。通篇的动词是「据 CartierFilms 爆料」「CartierFilms 还说」「CartierFilms 也声称」，从头到尾只有一个人在说话，而报道所做的，是把这个人说的话整理成条目、加上小标题、再补一段阿尔法飞行是什么的科普，让它看起来像新闻。

这就是认知入口的运作方式。信息是不是真的，在这个环节根本不重要；重要的是它被排版成了什么形状。一条论坛爆料，经过「据报道」的转手、经过条目的分栏、经过「尚不清楚是独立电影还是并入下一部死侍」这种把不确定性也写成信息量的句式，就获得了事实的外观。读者记住的不是「有人声称」，是「雷诺兹要拍阿尔法飞行」。

而这条爆料服务的对象很清楚。报道自己列了：金刚狼换人、2028 年 5 月 5 日的《X 战警》在谈 Morgan Spector、Cailee Spaeny 试镜 Polaris、Kitty Pryde 已定、可能还有 Jubilee。一个爆料源，一次性放出六七个名字，覆盖了从今年到 2028 年所有能炒的档期。这不是泄露，这是投放。每一条都精准落在粉丝的期待管理上，让他们在未来几年里持续有东西可以讨论、可以争论、可以站队。

代价由谁付？报道里的角色名单给了答案。阿尔法飞行的原始成员里，Northstar 是漫威第一个重要的公开同性恋角色，Shaman 是 Tsuutʼina 民族的医生，Snowbird、Aurora 都是女性。这些身份在漫画里是设定，在爆料里是筹码——它们被列出来，是因为它们能被用来做话题，而不是因为有人在认真处理这些角色意味着什么。一个原住民族的角色、一个公开出柜的角色，在这个阶段的功能是让选角传闻多几个可以吵的关键词。

我不反对看超级英雄电影。我反对的是把投放当报道，把期待当事实，把角色的身份当预热素材，然后告诉读者这就是他们需要知道的全部。这篇报道唯一确凿的信息，是一个叫 CartierFilms 的人说了话。剩下的部分，等它真的发生再说。

Not one fact in this piece was obtained by the outlet publishing it. The verbs are all the same: according to a leak from CartierFilms, CartierFilms also claimed, CartierFilms said. One person is talking, and the report's job is to arrange his sentences into bullet points, add subheads, tack on an explainer about who Alpha Flight are, and make the result look like news.

This is how a cognitive entry point works. Whether the information is true is irrelevant at this stage. What matters is the shape it's been given. A forum leak, passed through the phrase "according to reports," sorted into sections, dressed in a sentence like "it's unclear whether the group will get its own movie or feature in the next Deadpool" — a line that turns uncertainty itself into content — acquires the appearance of fact. What readers retain isn't "someone claimed." It's "Reynolds is doing Alpha Flight."

And the leak's purpose is plain. The report itself lists what's on offer: a new Wolverine, Morgan Spector eyed for the X-Men film due May 5, 2028, Cailee Spaeny auditioning for Polaris, Kitty Pryde cast, possibly Jubilee. One source, six or seven names at once, covering every slot from this year to 2028. This isn't a leak. It's a drop. Each item lands precisely on fan expectation management, giving people something to discuss, argue over, and take sides on for years.

Who pays? The character list answers. Among Alpha Flight's original members, Northstar was Marvel's first major openly gay character, Shaman is a doctor and member of the Tsuutʼina Nation, Snowbird and Aurora are women. In the comics these are characterizations. In a leak they are chips — listed because they generate话题, not because anyone is seriously engaging with what these characters mean. A Indigenous character and an openly gay one function here as keywords that make the casting rumor arguable.

I have nothing against superhero movies. I object to a marketing drop presented as reporting, expectation presented as fact, and identity presented as preheat material — then handed to readers as everything they need to know. The only solid information in this piece is that a person named CartierFilms said something. The rest can wait until it happens.
