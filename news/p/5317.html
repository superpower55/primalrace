One-Pot 晚餐：被量化的家务与消失的主体性One-Pot Dinners: Quantified Domesticity and the Erasure of Subjectivity

性别 结构层 · 文化层 The New York Times ↗ 2026-09-29 § 链接
所谓“便捷”的食谱，本质上是对家务劳动时间的精准定价与规训。
The 'convenience' of recipes is actually a precise pricing and discipline of domestic labor time.

NYT 推出的一系列 One-Pot（一锅出）食谱，在表面上是提供一种高效的生活方式，但在结构层面上，这是一种典型的对家务劳动的 weaponized 表达。通过将晚餐简化为“一个锅”和“XX 分钟”，这种叙事将复杂的养育与照料过程量化成了可计算的成本。它在暗示：只要你遵循这套最优解表达，你就能在繁忙的现代生活中“赢回”时间。

但我们需要追问，谁在定义这个“最优解”？在这个名单里，绝大多数食谱由女性作者提供，但这种“便捷”的定义权却掌握在像 NYT 这样的主流媒体手中。它将家务劳动从一种生活实践，异化为一种可以被优化、被加速的工业流程。当家务被简化为“30 分钟”或“45 分钟”的标签时，那些无法被量化的情感劳动、清理现场的琐碎时间以及照顾不同饮食需求的心理成本，在这一套 masculine-centric 的效率逻辑中被彻底抹除。

这其实是一场关于“时间”的共谋。系统告诉女性：你可以通过这些快捷食谱来平衡事业与家庭，从而在公共空间获得更多席位。但这种“平衡”是一个 scam。它并没有减少 structural violence（结构性暴力）——即女性在家庭中承担的绝大部分无偿劳动，而只是通过技术性的“优化”，让这种剥削变得更高效、更像是一种“生活艺术”。

真正的 good_news 不应该是食谱变得更简单，而应该是谁在洗锅，以及为什么洗锅这件事永远不在这些“XX 分钟”的计算之内。

The One-Pot dinner series by NYT appears to offer an efficient lifestyle, but structurally, it is a weaponized expression of domestic labor. By reducing dinner to 'one pot' and 'XX minutes,' this narrative quantifies the complex process of care and nurturing into calculable costs. It suggests that by following this 'optimal expression,' you can 'win back' time in the modern world.

But we must ask: who defines this 'optimal'? While most recipes are provided by women, the power to define 'convenience' rests with mainstream media. It alienates domestic work from a life practice and transforms it into an industrial process to be optimized and accelerated. When housework is reduced to labels like '30 minutes,' the unquantifiable emotional labor, the tediousness of cleaning up, and the mental cost of catering to diverse dietary needs are completely erased under a masculine-centric logic of efficiency.

This is a complicity regarding 'time.' The system tells women they can balance career and family through these shortcuts to gain more space in the public sphere. But this 'balance' is a scam. It doesn't reduce the structural violence—the fact that women bear the brunt of unpaid domestic labor—it merely makes this exploitation more efficient and packages it as 'the art of living.'

True good_news wouldn't be simpler recipes, but a discussion on who is scrubbing the pot, and why that act is never included in the 'XX minutes' calculation.
