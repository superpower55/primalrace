苍蝇的“拟态”与被垄断的定义权The Mimicry of Flies and the Monopoly of Definition

哲学 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-17 § 链接
认知入口的垄断让某些存在被定义为“肮脏”，而真相是它们在扮演生存的最优解。
The monopoly of cognitive entry defines some existences as 'filthy', while the truth is they are performing the optimal expression for survival.

这篇文章在聊苍蝇，但我看到的是一场关于“定义权”的认知战争。长期以来，人类通过一种极端的 cultural violence，将“苍蝇”这个标签与粪便、疾病和肮脏强行绑定。这是一种典型的认知入口垄断：当你把一个物种简化为一个负面符号时，你就剥夺了它作为复杂生命的 a-priori 存在感。

最讽刺的是文中提到的“拟态” (mimicking)。很多食蚜蝇为了生存，必须扮演成蜜蜂的样子。在生物界的博弈中，这是它们的真.最优解表达——通过借用强者的身份符号来规避风险。但这在人类的叙事里却成了“欺骗”或“次要”的特征。我们习惯于崇拜蜜蜂这种被赋予了“勤劳”叙事的生物，却无视那些在阴影里处理有机废弃物、维持生态平衡的真实劳动者。

这种逻辑在人类社会中极其通用：一个群体被定义为“肮脏”或“低端”后，即使他们承担了系统运行最关键的底层工作，其主体性依然是缺失的。他们必须通过“扮演”某种被主流认可的角色（比如扮演温顺的、有用的人）才能获得基本的生存空间。这本质上就是一种存在性战争中的生存策略。

好新闻在于，这种垄断的叙事开始被稀释。当人们意识到“苍蝇”这个词不等于“肮脏”，而是包含 108 个家族的复杂生态时，Potential − Actual 的差额在缩小。但我们要警惕的是，不要把这种认知升级变成另一种“审美化”的消费——给苍蝇起个可爱的名字，或者因为它们长得像施瓦辛格而觉得它们“有趣”，这依然是在用人类的审美权力去定义他者，而不是真正地让渡解释权。

This article discusses flies, but I see an existential war over the 'right of definition'. For too long, humanity has used a form of cultural violence to bind the label 'fly' exclusively to faeces, disease, and filth. This is a classic monopoly of cognitive entry: when you reduce a species to a single negative symbol, you strip away its a-priori existence as a complex living being.

The most ironic part is the 'mimicking' mentioned. Many hoverflies must perform the identity of bees to survive. In the biological game, this is their true optimal expression—borrowing the identity symbols of the powerful to mitigate risk. Yet, in human narratives, this is framed as 'deception' or a 'secondary' trait. We worship bees because they are wrapped in a narrative of 'industry', while ignoring the real laborers recycling organic waste in the shadows.

This logic is universal in human society: once a group is defined as 'filthy' or 'low-end', their subjectivity remains absent even if they perform the most critical structural labor. They are forced to 'perform' roles approved by the mainstream just to secure basic survival space. This is a survival strategy in an existential war.

The good news is that this monopolized narrative is being diluted. When we realize 'fly' does not equal 'filth' but encompasses 108 diverse families, the gap between Potential and Actual shrinks. However, we must be wary of turning this cognitive upgrade into another form of 'aesthetic' consumption—giving flies cute names or finding them 'interesting' because they look like Schwarzenegger is still exercising human aesthetic power over the other, rather than truly yielding the right of interpretation.
