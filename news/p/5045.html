零和博弈的陷阱与被隐匿的原初种族The Zero-Sum Trap and the Hidden Primal Race

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-16 § 链接
种族不平等是元暴力的衍生，而'特权叙事'是结构暴力最完美的掩体。
Racial inequality is a derivative of meta-violence, and the 'privilege narrative' is the perfect cover for structural violence.

这份研究揭示了一个典型的 cultural violence 陷阱：当白人人群中 37% 的人认为少数族裔获得了“优待”时，这种感知本身就是一种武器化的叙事。在 Potential − Actual 的差额面前，结构性弱势者被剥夺的资源被伪装成某种“特权”，从而将原本是对抗 structural violence 的努力，被重新定义为对多数群体的“掠夺”。这就是典型的 zero-sum thinking，它通过制造一个虚假的“特权”幻象，诱导共谋者在潜意识中合理化现有的不平等。

最值得关注的是报告中被轻描淡写的一句：少数族裔女性比男性更悲观。这再次证明了性别是原初种族 (Primal Race) 的逻辑：在任何种族冲突的结构中，女性总是承受双重殖民。她们不仅在 racial layer 面临排斥，在 masculine-centric narrative 的元暴力下，她们的痛苦被视为次要的，或被整合进族群的整体叙事中而被掩盖。少数族裔女性的悲观，是因为她们处于暴力三角的最底层，她们的 Actual 状态与 Potential 之间的差额最大，且最缺乏有效的表达入口。

所谓的“公平议程”如果仅仅停留在政策建议，而不能拆穿这种“特权”叙事的 scam，那么它只是另一种 PR 版本的 structural violence。只要解释权依然掌握在那些定义什么是“优待”的人手中，任何对平等地追求都会被转化为新一轮的 polarisation。真正的突破不在于政府的 10 条建议，而在于能否将这种“零和博弈”的认知入口彻底摧毁。

This study reveals a classic cultural violence trap: when 37% of the white population perceives ethnic minorities as receiving 'preferential treatment,' this perception itself becomes a weaponized narrative. In the gap between Potential and Actual, the resources stripped from the structurally disadvantaged are rebranded as 'privileges,' transforming the struggle against structural violence into a perceived 'plunder' of the majority. This is a textbook zero-sum thinking, inducing complicity by manufacturing a phantom privilege to justify existing inequalities.

The most critical detail is the report's casual mention that ethnic minority women are more pessimistic than men. This reinforces the logic of the Primal Race: in any structural racial conflict, women are doubly colonized. They face exclusion at the racial layer while their suffering is marginalized or subsumed into a collective ethnic narrative under the meta-violence of the masculine-centric narrative. The pessimism of minority women stems from their position at the absolute base of the Violence Triangle, where the gap between Actual and Potential is widest and the entry points for expression are most restricted.

A so-called 'fairness agenda' remains mere PR for structural violence if it only offers policy recommendations without dismantling the 'privilege' scam. As long as the power of interpretation remains with those who define what constitutes 'preferential treatment,' any pursuit of equality will be weaponized into further polarisation. True progress lies not in government recommendations, but in the total demolition of the zero-sum cognitive entry point.
