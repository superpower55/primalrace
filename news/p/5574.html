总统府的烟雾与被当作筹码的平民Smoke over the Palace and the Human Collateral

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-15 § 链接
战争是男性中心叙事最极端的表达，将生命降格为宏大意义的交换筹码。
War is the most extreme expression of masculine-centric narrative, reducing human life to exchangeable chips for grand meanings.

总统府被导弹击中，总理毫发无伤，而死者是几个在爆炸中被随机抽中的平民。这件新闻的结构极其典型：两个男性主导的权力集团——胡塞武装与沙特领导的联军，在名为“地缘政治”的棋盘上进行存在性战争。他们争夺的是解释权，是关于谁才是也门合法统治者的叙事权，而这种权力博弈的 Actual 结果，就是落在平民头上的导弹。

这就是典型的 Meta Violence（元暴力）。在男性中心叙事中，战争被包装成“反抗侵略”或“维护稳定”的宏大意义。但剥离这些 weaponized 的词汇，本质就是一种极端的 masculine-centric 逻辑：将人类身体（尤其是那些无法参与决策的弱势群体）降格为可消耗的工具。总理的“安全”与平民的“死亡”在同一篇报道中出现，这种对比恰恰揭示了结构性暴力的残酷——权力的核心被层层保护，而暴力被精准地外包给底层的肉身。

更讽刺的是，这场战争被描述为“伊朗战争”的一个前沿，这意味着也门人的生存状态被进一步客体化，成了大国博弈的副产品。在这种叙事里，没有一个人的主体性，只有作为“代理人”或“附庸”的标签。当导弹在亚丁的总统府上空炸裂，被摧毁的不仅是建筑，更是当地人本可达到的和平状态。Potential 与 Actual 之间的差额，就是这场由男性权力欲望驱动的暴力三角。

The presidential palace is struck, the Prime Minister remains unharmed, while the dead are random civilians caught in the blast. This news follows a classic pattern: two male-dominated power blocs—the Houthis and the Saudi-led coalition—engaged in an existential war on a chessboard called 'geopolitics.' They are fighting for the monopoly of interpretation, the narrative of who truly governs Yemen, while the actual result is missiles raining down on civilians.

This is Meta Violence in its purest form. In a masculine-centric narrative, war is weaponized as 'resistance' or 'stability.' Stripped of these terms, it is a logic that degrades human bodies—especially those of the structurally weak—into consumable tools. The 'safety' of the Prime Minister contrasted with the 'death' of civilians reveals the cruelty of structural violence: the core of power is shielded, while violence is outsourced to the flesh of the marginalized.

More ironically, this conflict is framed as a front of the 'Iran war,' further objectifying Yemeni lives as by-products of great-power competition. In this narrative, no one possesses subjectivity; there are only labels like 'proxies' or 'vassals.' As missiles explode over Aden, what is destroyed is not just a building, but the potential for peace. The gap between Potential and Actual is the exact measure of the Violence Triangle driven by masculine lust for power.
