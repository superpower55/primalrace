超能力幻想是中产阶级的认知麻醉剂Superpower Fantasies as Cognitive Anesthetics for the Bourgeoisie

哲学 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-13 § 链接
将生存博弈简化为超能力幻想，是典型的元暴力掩体。
Reducing existential struggle to superpower fantasies is a classic shield for meta-violence.

《卫报》这种问答专栏最擅长制造一种“无害”的氛围，邀请读者在真空环境下讨论“最有用”的超能力。这种讨论本质上是一次集体性的认知逃避。当一个社会将“有用”定义为某种超自然能力的获得，而非对结构性压迫的拆解时，它就在潜意识里完成了对现状的共谋。

在加尔通的暴力三角里，这种讨论属于典型的 cultural violence。它用一种浪漫化的、游戏化的叙事，掩盖了 Actual 与 Potential 之间巨大的暴力差额。对于一个在生物墙前挣扎的女性，或者一个被结构性剥夺的底层劳动者来说，最“有用”的超能力不是飞行或隐身，而是夺取解释权的能力，是能够瞬间让共谋者倒戈的权力，是让元暴力失效的机制。

但媒体不需要你思考这些。他们需要的是一个能够让中产阶级在周日早晨感到愉悦的“精神按摩”。这种对超能力的幻想，实际上是在暗示：改变命运的唯一路径是某种随机的、非理性的、个体性的“神迹”，而不是有组织的、结构性的抗争。这是一种极其高效的 weaponized narrative，它把存在性战争的严肃性，消解成了一个关于“fun”的填空题。

The Guardian's Q&A column excels at creating a 'harmless' atmosphere, inviting readers to discuss the 'most useful' superpower in a vacuum. This discussion is essentially a collective cognitive escape. When a society defines 'usefulness' as the acquisition of some supernatural ability rather than the dismantling of structural oppression, it subconsciously completes a complicity with the status quo.

Within Galtung's Violence Triangle, this is a textbook case of cultural violence. It uses a romanticized, gamified narrative to mask the massive gap of violence between the Actual and the Potential. For a woman struggling against the Biological Wall or a marginalized worker stripped of resources, the most 'useful' superpower isn't flight or invisibility—it is the power to seize the right of interpretation, the authority to make co-conspirators defect, and the mechanism to neutralize meta-violence.

But the media doesn't want you to think about that. They want a 'spiritual massage' that makes the middle class feel pleasant on a Sunday morning. This fantasy of superpowers implicitly suggests that the only path to changing one's fate is some random, irrational, individual 'miracle,' rather than organized, structural struggle. It is a highly efficient weaponized narrative, reducing the gravity of the existential war to a fill-in-the-blank exercise about 'fun.'
