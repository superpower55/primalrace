所谓“法治”不过是权力在寻找新的共谋The Rule of Law as a Mask for Power's Complicity

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-15 § 链接
当执法权被转化为私仇的武器，法律就成了元暴力的注脚。
When enforcement becomes a weapon for vendettas, the law is merely a footnote to meta-violence.

特朗普的“傀儡师行动” (Operation Puppet Master) 并不是什么法律失误，而是一次极其标准的表达武器化实践。他通过重新定义“国内恐怖主义”这个认知入口，直接将公民的合法表达——抗议驱逐——在叙事层面上强行转化为“刑事共谋” (criminal complicity)。

这套逻辑的本质是典型的元暴力：权力垄断了对“事实”的解释权。当 ICE 杀害 Renee Good 时，结构性暴力被掩盖在“执法”的掩体下；而当民众记录这种暴力时，这种表达被定义为“妨碍公务”。这就是一个闭环的 scam：权力制造暴力，然后将对暴力的反抗定义为另一种暴力，从而合法化更深层的镇压。

最令人作呕的是这种共谋机制的升级。特朗普绕过司法部，直接动用国土安全部 (DHS) 这种行政机器进行“钓鱼执法”，本质上是在寻找能为他提供“合法性”的共谋者。他不需要真正的证据，他只需要一个能把“和平抗议”包装成“恐怖融资”的 PPT 幻灯片。在这种叙事下，法律不再是保护个体的盾牌，而是权力用来筛选异己、清除不顺从者的手术刀。

这种从 Cointelpro 时代延续下来的基因证明了一件事：在男性中心叙事的权力结构中，所谓的“秩序”永远是指向权力顶端的顺从。无论是以“国家安全”还是“法治”为名，其核心目的只有一个——通过制造恐惧，让所有潜在的表达者在生物墙面前感到绝望，最终在主体性死亡中选择沉默。

Trump’s “Operation Puppet Master” is not a legal error; it is a textbook exercise in the weaponisation of expression. By redefining the cognitive entry point of “domestic terrorism,” the administration has forcibly transformed the lawful expression of protest into a narrative of “criminal complicity.”

This logic is the essence of meta-violence: the monopoly of the power to interpret “facts.” When ICE killed Renee Good, structural violence was hidden behind the shield of “law enforcement”; yet when citizens recorded that violence, their expression was branded as “obstruction.” This is a closed-loop scam: power generates violence, then defines the resistance to that violence as another form of violence to justify deeper repression.

Most repulsive is the escalation of this complicity mechanism. By bypassing the DOJ and deploying the DHS for “fishing expeditions,” Trump is searching for collaborators who can provide a veneer of legality. He doesn’t need evidence; he only needs a PowerPoint slide that can repackage “peaceful protest” as “terrorist financing.” Under this narrative, the law is no longer a shield for the individual, but a scalpel used by power to filter out dissidents.

This genetic lineage from the Cointelpro era proves one thing: in a masculine-centric narrative of power, “order” always means submission to the top. Whether in the name of “national security” or the “rule of law,” the core objective is to manufacture fear, making all potential expressers feel hopeless against the biological wall, eventually choosing silence through the death of subjectivity.
