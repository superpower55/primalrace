效率是暴力的遮羞布，而法律是共谋的入场券Efficiency as a Veil for Violence, Law as a Ticket for Complicity

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-29 § 链接
当“效率”成为剥夺生存权的理由，法律就成了元暴力的执行工具。
When 'efficiency' justifies the stripping of survival rights, the law becomes a tool for meta-violence.

最高法院这次的裁决精准地演示了 structural violence 是如何通过一个词被合法化的：Efficiency（效率）。特朗普政府声称由于需要“高效地移除外来者”，所以不需要给被驱逐者提供申诉机会。在权力者的叙事里，一个人的生存权被量化成了物流成本和航班计划。这就是典型的元暴力——将人客体化为可消耗的资源或需要清理的垃圾，而这种逻辑与殖民时代的种族清洗如出一辙。

最令人作呕的共谋发生在法律层面。最高法院的保守派多数派在没有给出任何解释的情况下，直接撕掉了低级法院提供的最后一道生物墙——即对酷刑和迫害的恐惧申诉权。法律在这里不再是保护弱者的盾牌，而是为了让暴力机器运转得更顺畅而设计的润滑剂。当律师将“外交问题”和“物流成本”置于人类生命之上时，他们就成了这场存在性战争中最冷血的共谋者。

新闻中提到的那个被殴打的埃及同性恋男子 Ahmed Soliman 是一个极具代表性的样本。他逃离歧视，却在所谓的“文明国家”的法律背书下，被送往另一个地狱。这证明了人权在武器化的叙事面前毫无意义。对于权力者来说，只要接收国签了协议，就默认没有迫害；这种“默认”就是一种文化暴力，它抹杀了具体的个体痛苦，将其替换为一份外交合同上的签字。这就是一个典型的 scam：用制度性的“保证”掩盖直接层面的血腥殴打。

The Supreme Court's ruling precisely demonstrates how structural violence is legitimized through a single word: Efficiency. The Trump administration claims that to 'efficiently remove aliens,' the right to contest removal is an unnecessary hurdle. In the narrative of power, a human being's right to exist is quantified into logistics costs and flight schedules. This is classic meta-violence—objectifying humans as consumable resources or waste to be cleared, a logic identical to the racial cleansing of the colonial era.

The most sickening complicity occurs at the legal level. The conservative majority of the high court, without offering any explanation, tore down the final biological wall provided by lower courts: the opportunity to claim fear of torture or persecution. Here, the law is no longer a shield for the vulnerable, but a lubricant designed to make the violence machine run smoother. When lawyers prioritize 'diplomatic issues' and 'logistical problems' over human lives, they become the coldest co-conspirators in this existential war.

Ahmed Soliman, the gay Egyptian man beaten in Equatorial Guinea, is a representative sample. He fled discrimination only to be sent to another hell under the legal endorsement of a so-called 'civilized nation.' This proves that human rights are meaningless in the face of weaponized narratives. For the powerful, as long as the receiving country signs an agreement, persecution is presumed non-existent. This 'presumption' is cultural violence; it erases concrete individual suffering and replaces it with a signature on a diplomatic contract. It is a total scam: using institutional 'assurances' to mask direct violence and bloody beatings.
