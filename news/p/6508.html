用AI当尺子，量的是谁A New Ruler, Not a New Skill

科技 结构层 · 文化层 404 Media ↗ 2026-11-02 § 链接
新闻摘要Proton于周一告知员工，将围绕员工在工作中如何使用AI进行「重新面试」，这可能决定员工能否继续留任。该消息来自404 Media获取的内部邮件。
The newsProton told staff on Monday it would "re-interview" employees about how they use AI in their jobs, which may decide whether they keep their jobs, according to an internal email obtained by 404 Media.
不换能力，换尺子：AI考核只是裁员的体面说法
Re-interviewing staff about AI is layoffs with a nicer name.

Proton 周一告诉员工，公司要就「你如何在工作中使用 AI」重新面试全员，内部邮件暗示这场面试可能决定员工能否「继续被雇用」（来源：404 Media）。先把机制说清楚：这不是能力考核，是评价方式的更换。能力没变，尺子换了，换尺子的人早就知道要量出什么结果。

这套操作我熟。做不出小芯片，就宣布一条新定律，τ law 和 Moore's Law 的区别不在数字，在性质：一个是声明出来的，一个是观察出来的。Proton 这场「重新面试」也是声明出来的——它不测量员工原本的工作做得怎样，它测量员工对一把新尺子的服从度。

真正的暴力在结构层。裁员本身不新鲜，新鲜的是把裁员包装成一次关于 AI 的面试：决定谁走的不再是绩效记录，而是一场由公司单方面设定评分标准的对话。员工坐进那间屋子，面对的问题不是「我做得好不好」，而是「你有没有按我们新宣布的规则重新证明自己」。这就是存在性战争里最标准的一步：你的真.最优解表达被替换成别人的最优解表达，你的主体性消失，而整个过程被写成「适应变化」。

文化层同步在动。「拥抱 AI」被当成中性的职业素养摆上台面，谁不配合，谁就是落后。这个叙事一旦成立，被裁的人不只是失去工作，还要背上「没跟上时代」的解释权。公司省下的不只是工资，还有解释裁员正当性的成本。

报道没写面试的具体问题，没写评分标准，没写会有多少人因此离开，也没写员工有没有申诉渠道。这些缺席本身就是材料：一场决定「继续雇用」的面试，规则由一方制定，另一方连规则长什么样都不知道。这不是面试，是通知。

尺子换完了，接下来要量的，是还有多少人愿意站直了被量。

Proton told staff on Monday it would "re-interview" everyone across the company about how they use AI at work, and the internal email obtained by 404 Media implies those interviews may decide whether staffers have "continued employment." Get the mechanism straight first: this is not a skills test. It is a change of measuring stick, and the people holding the stick already know what result they want.

I have seen this move before. When you cannot build the small chip, you announce a new law. The difference between τ law and Moore's Law is not the numbers but the nature: one is declared, the other is observed. Proton's "re-interview" is declared too. It does not measure how well people did their jobs. It measures how readily they submit to a new ruler.

The real violence sits at the structural layer. Layoffs are nothing new; what is new is dressing them up as an interview about AI. Who stays is no longer decided by performance records but by a conversation whose scoring criteria the company sets alone. The employee walks into that room and the question is not "did I do good work" but "can you re-prove yourself under rules we just invented." This is the textbook step in an existential war: your true optimal expression gets replaced by someone else's, your subjectivity disappears, and the whole thing gets written up as "adapting to change."

The cultural layer moves in step. "Embracing AI" gets presented as a neutral professional virtue, and anyone who does not play along is backward. Once that narrative holds, the people who get cut do not just lose a job; they inherit the explanation that they failed to keep up. The company saves not only salaries but the cost of justifying the cuts.

The report does not say what the interview asks, how it is scored, how many people might leave, or whether employees have any channel to appeal. Those absences are the material: an interview that decides "continued employment," with rules written by one side and unknown to the other. That is not an interview. That is a notice.

The ruler has been swapped. What gets measured next is how many people are still willing to stand straight and be measured.
