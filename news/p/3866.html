厌恶感的定价权：谁在定义什么是“脏”The Pricing Power of Disgust: Who Defines the 'Dirty'?

哲学 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-14 § 链接
厌恶感不是生物本能，而是权力对“异类”的文化标记。
Disgust is not a biological instinct, but a cultural marker used by power to define the 'Other'.

很多人把“恶心” (disgust) 当成一种生理本能，觉得皱鼻子是身体在提醒我们避开危险。这其实是一个巨大的 scam。生理性的厌恶是为了避开腐烂的食物，而社会性的厌恶则是为了标记“他者” (the other)。

新闻里那个小女孩在接触蚯蚓时原本是好奇的，但母亲的一声尖叫瞬间完成了一次文化规训。这就是典型的 cultural violence：通过一种情绪表达，将某种存在定义为“污染物”。当一个生命被贴上“害虫”或“恶心”的标签，它就失去了被公正对待的权利。因为一旦进入“厌恶”的逻辑，排斥、隔离甚至抹除就变得合理化了。

这种逻辑在人类社会中被武器化到了极致。无论是对 queer 群体的污名化，还是历史上将特定种族比作“寄生虫”或“瘟疫”，本质上都是在夺取解释权。通过制造一种“不洁”的叙事，权力中心成功地在生物墙之外又建起了一道文化墙，让人们在潜意识里完成自我审查：为了不成为那个“恶心的异类”，我必须认同这套排斥机制。

谁在定义什么是“脏”？谁在定义什么是“自然”？当你开始追问“是谁教会我厌恶”时，你才真正开始了这场存在性战争。打破这种共谋的唯一方式就是增加 contact，把那些被定义为“污染物”的存在重新拉回人的视线里，拆穿那个名为“本能”的权力伪装。

Many treat 'disgust' as a biological instinct, believing a wrinkled nose is simply the body warning us of danger. This is a massive scam. While physiological disgust helps us avoid rotten food, social disgust is about marking the 'Other'.

In the story, the little girl was initially fascinated by the worm, but her mother's scream instantly completed a process of cultural conditioning. This is a textbook example of cultural violence: using an emotional expression to define an existence as a 'contaminant'. Once a being is labeled as a 'pest' or 'disgusting', it loses the right to be treated justly. Once the logic of disgust takes over, avoidance, exclusion, and even eradication feel justifiable.

This logic has been weaponized to the extreme in human society. Whether it is the stigmatization of queer people or the historical depiction of specific races as 'parasites' or 'plagues', it is fundamentally about seizing the power of interpretation. By manufacturing a narrative of 'impurity', the center of power builds a cultural wall atop the biological wall, forcing people to perform self-censorship: to avoid being the 'disgusting outlier', one must align with the mechanism of exclusion.

Who defines what is 'dirty'? Who defines what is 'natural'? Only when you start asking 'who taught me to be disgusted' do you actually begin this existential war. The only way to break this complicity is through contact—bringing those defined as 'contaminants' back into sight and stripping away the mask of 'instinct' from the face of power.
