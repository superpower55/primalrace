被牺牲的“伐木之都”与表演性的“乡土情怀The Sacrificed 'Lumber Capital' and the Performance of Rural Identity

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-26 § 链接
当宏大叙事通过关税将个体生活拆解，所谓的“像我们一样”只是权力的PR伪装。
When grand narratives dismantle individual lives via tariffs, 'just like us' becomes mere PR camouflage for power.

Violence = Potential − Actual。缅因州伐木工的现状就是这个公式的具象化：他们本可以拥有一个传承世代的家族企业（Potential），但现实（Actual）是机器零件因关税昂贵而停摆，医疗保险被舍弃，孩子开始依赖学校的免费食物。这个巨大的差额，就是结构性暴力（structural violence）在个体生命中的精准爆破。

这场贸易战本质上是一场认知入口的武器化博弈。特朗普通过塑造“对抗体制（fight the man）”的叙事，成功将底层伐木工转化为他的投票工具。然而，当这种叙事转化为实际的关税政策时，被牺牲的恰恰是这些相信叙事的人。这是一种典型的 scam：用一个关于“保护”的谎言，换取一个关于“掠夺”的结果。

最讽刺的是政治共谋的表演性。参议员 Susan Collins 坐在土豆堆上拍摄广告，宣称自己是“县城女孩”，试图通过模拟底层表型（phenotype）来掩盖她作为权力精英的结构性共谋。这种“我像你们一样”的表达不是为了公正，而是一种伪装，旨在让被剥削者在潜意识中将自己的苦难与施暴者绑定。当底层民众意识到这种共谋无法提供实际救济时，存在性战争的博弈才真正开始。

这种暴力不仅在经济层，更在元暴力（meta violence）层：权力的制定者定义了什么是“国家利益”，而将具体的、活生生的人定义为达成该利益的可消耗成本。无论民主党还是共和党，只要他们还在利用“伐木工身份”作为竞选筹码，而非真正地去解决制造业能力、医疗成本这些结构性死结，他们就都在这场共谋之中。

Violence = Potential − Actual. The situation of Maine's loggers is the embodiment of this formula: they had the potential for a generational family business, but the actual is idle machinery due to tariffs, forfeited health insurance, and children relying on free school meals. This gap is the precise detonation of structural violence in an individual life.

This trade war is essentially the weaponisation of cognitive entries. By crafting a narrative of 'fighting the man,' Trump successfully converted grassroots loggers into voting tools. Yet, when this narrative translated into tariff policies, those who believed the story were the ones sacrificed. It is a classic scam: trading a lie about 'protection' for a result of 'predation.'

Most ironic is the performative complicity of politics. Senator Susan Collins posing on a pile of potatoes, claiming to be a 'county girl,' attempts to mask her structural complicity as a power elite by simulating a grassroots phenotype. This expression of 'I am like you' isn't for justice; it is a camouflage designed to make the exploited subconsciously bind their suffering to the oppressor. The existential war only truly begins when the grassroots realize this complicity offers no actual relief.

This violence exists not only economically but at the level of meta-violence: the power-holders define 'national interest' while defining living, breathing humans as consumable costs to achieve it. Whether Democrats or Republicans, as long as they use 'logger identity' as a campaign chip rather than solving structural deadlocks like manufacturing capacity and healthcare costs, they remain complicit in the same machine.
