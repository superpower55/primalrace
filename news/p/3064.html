从 Aperol 到 Hugo：一场关于“轻盈”的审美定价权转移From Aperol to Hugo: The Shift in Aesthetic Pricing Power

哲学 文化层 The New York Times ↗ 2026-07-31 § 链接
品味没有高下，只有谁掌握了定义“酷”的定价权。
There is no high or low taste, only who holds the pricing power to define what is 'cool'.

纽约时报在讨论一种酒的更替，但本质上是在记录一次典型的审美武器化过程。从 Aperol Spritz 到 Hugo Spritz，这不是消费者的自发觉醒，而是一场由 St-Germain 驱动的认知入口抢占战。当一家公司通过社交媒体、名人效应和节庆活动将“接骨木花”与“轻盈”、“夏季”、“无苦味”绑定时，它制造了一种新的真实：你不再是选择了一种口味，而是在通过这杯酒表达你属于那个“追求轻盈、审美升级”的阶层。

这种现象是典型的身份入口武器化。Hugo Spritz 成功的核心不在于口感，而在于它的“可见度”——那种巨大的酒杯、薄荷叶和淡绿色，在餐厅中扮演着类似“投名状”的角色。当一个人看到邻桌在喝 Hugo，潜意识里接收到的信号是：那是当前最优解的表达。于是，人们为了在存在性战争中不被标记为“过时”，迅速地将这种由资本定义的新品味内化为自己的真实喜好。

最讽刺的是，这种“轻盈”的叙事掩盖了背后的商业共谋。从酒吧老板到社交媒体经理，所有人都在通过“微调配方”来分赃，试图在既定的流行趋势中通过一点点差异化来获得自己的定价权。所谓的“超越季节性”，不过是商业机器在试图将一个短期爆款转化为长期结构性消费的尝试。人们以为自己在追求自由的口感，其实只是在另一个由钱权势设计的审美笼子里，完成了再一次的自我规训。

The New York Times is discussing the replacement of one drink with another, but in essence, it is recording a classic case of the weaponisation of aesthetics. The shift from Aperol Spritz to Hugo Spritz is not a spontaneous awakening of consumers, but a strategic seizure of cognitive entries driven by St-Germain. When a company binds 'elderflower' with 'lightness,' 'summer,' and 'lack of bitterness' through social media and celebrity campaigns, it manufactures a new reality: you are not just choosing a flavor, but expressing your belonging to a class that values 'lightness' and 'aesthetic upgrades.'

This is a textbook example of weaponising identity entries. The success of the Hugo Spritz lies not in its taste, but in its visibility—the oversized glass, the mint, and the pale green hue act as a kind of 'pledge of loyalty' in a restaurant setting. When a person sees the Hugo on a neighboring table, the subconscious signal is: this is the current optimal expression. Consequently, to avoid being labeled as 'outdated' in the existential war, people rapidly internalise this capital-defined taste as their own authentic preference.

Most ironically, this narrative of 'lightness' masks an underlying commercial complicity. From bar owners to social media managers, everyone is splitting the spoils by 'tinkering with recipes,' attempting to gain their own pricing power through slight differentiation within a preset trend. The so-called 'transcending seasonality' is merely the commercial machine attempting to convert a short-term hit into a long-term structural consumption. People believe they are pursuing a free taste, while they are actually completing another round of self-discipline within an aesthetic cage designed by money and power.
