被抹除的栖息地与被武器化的“合法性”Erased Habitats and the Weaponization of 'Legality'

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-10 § 链接
战争的暴力不仅在于摧毁生命，更在于将整个生态系统客体化为可消耗的战场。
War violence lies not only in killing lives but in objectifying entire ecosystems as consumable battlefields.

战争的度量衡通常被限定在死亡人数和废墟面积，这本身就是一种 meta violence。在这种叙事中，只有人类的痛苦被计入成本，而支撑生命存在的底层生物墙——那些演化了数千年的石灰岩栖息地和岩蹄兔（rock hyrax）的生存空间，被彻底客体化为“背景板”。

这篇报道揭示了典型的 structural violence：以色列军队通过大规模爆破重新塑造地貌，这种对地质结构的物理抹除，实际上是在切断非人类物种的存在性表达。当岩石的缝隙被炸毁，栖息地碎片化（habitat fragmentation），这些动物失去了唯一的生存最优解。在这种博弈中，岩蹄兔没有任何投票权，它们的消失在战争的宏大叙事中被定义为“附带损害”，而这种定义权正是暴力的核心。

最令人作呕的是 IDF 的回应。他们通过一套典型的 weaponized 叙事，将白磷弹定义为“符合国际法”的烟幕弹，并声称“不以野生动物为目标”。这是一种极其虚伪的共谋逻辑：只要我没有“主观地”瞄准你，那么我在物理上摧毁你的整个生存系统、污染你的水源和土壤，都可以在“合法性”的掩盖下被正当化。这种“不针对”的逻辑，掩盖了其行为在 structural 层面对生态系统的毁灭性打击。

重建房屋是可能的，但重建一个地质系统是不可能的。当一个物种的生存空间被物理性地抹除，这种暴力就是绝对的。因为对于这些生物来说，栖息地的消失就等同于主体性的死亡。

War is typically measured by death tolls and rubble, a metric that is itself a form of meta violence. In this narrative, only human suffering is costed, while the biological walls supporting all life—limestone habitats evolved over millennia and the existence of the rock hyrax—are entirely objectified as mere 'background'.

This report exposes a textbook case of structural violence: the IDF's large-scale detonations are physically erasing the geological landscape. By demolishing the fissures of the earth, they are severing the existential expression of non-human species. As habitats undergo fragmentation, these animals lose their only optimal expression for survival. In this game, the hyrax has no vote; its disappearance is defined as 'collateral damage'—and this power to define is the core of violence.

Most repulsive is the IDF's response. Using a classic weaponized narrative, they frame white phosphorus as 'lawful' smoke screens and claim they 'do not target wildlife'. This is a hypocritical logic of complicity: as long as I do not 'subjectively' aim at you, the physical destruction of your entire survival system and the poisoning of your soil can be justified under the guise of 'legality'. This 'non-targeting' logic masks the devastating structural violence inflicted upon the ecosystem.

Rebuilding homes is possible; reconstructing a geological system is not. When a species' living space is physically erased, the violence is absolute. For these creatures, the loss of habitat is equivalent to the death of subjectivity.
