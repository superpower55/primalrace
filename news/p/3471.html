在野外扮演温柔，掩盖不了共谋的底色Performing Gentleness in the Wild, Masking the Color of Complicity

哲学 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-09 § 链接
温柔的男性表达若不触碰特权，只是另一种形式的叙事武器。
Male gentleness that avoids challenging privilege is merely another weaponized narrative.

看到 Bear Grylls 在节目里赞美同伴的 gentleness，评论者竟然觉得这在 toxic masculinity 的世界里很 radical。这简直是典型的认知陷阱。在生存主义的丛林叙事里，男性通过展示某种“被允许的温柔”来完成一次低成本的形象升级，但这并不意味着他们放弃了对解释权的垄断。

这种“温柔”是建立在绝对的安全感之上的。当这些男性在犹他州的沙漠里聊信仰、谈接纳时，他们依然处于一个由男性导演（Dallas Jenkins）掌控、由男性主持人引导的 closed loop 中。这不过是一次关于“好男人”的表演性表达，旨在制造一种“我们既强大又平和”的伪最优解，从而在 cultural 层面上稀释掉男性中心叙事中的攻击性，使其更易被大众消费。

最讽刺的共谋发生在节目之外。Bear Grylls 面对参与 Russell Brand 洗礼的质疑时，祭出了那套经典的“不评判、去爱”的宗教叙事。这正是元暴力的精髓：用一种看似普世的“慈悲”作为掩体，为真实的施暴者提供精神庇护。当“爱”被武器化为一种拒绝公正审判的挡箭牌时，这种温柔就变成了 structural violence 的共谋。

不要被这种 soothing formula 骗了。一个能一边在镜头前扮演平和导师，一边在现实中为涉嫌强奸者提供仪式支持的男人，他的“温柔”不过是权力在舒适区的一次伸展。真正的 radical 不是两个男人在野外互相赞美，而是他们敢于审视并拆除那座保护彼此的共谋之墙。

Seeing Bear Grylls praise a companion's gentleness and having a reviewer call it 'radical' in a world of toxic masculinity is a classic cognitive trap. In a survivalist jungle narrative, men use a 'permitted gentleness' to upgrade their image at a low cost. This is not an abandonment of the monopoly over interpretation.

This gentleness is predicated on absolute security. While these men discuss faith and acceptance in the Utah desert, they remain within a closed loop controlled by a male director (Dallas Jenkins) and led by a male host. It is a performative expression of the 'Good Man,' designed to dilute the aggression of the masculine-centric narrative at a cultural level, making it more consumable.

The most cynical complicity occurs off-screen. When questioned about his role in Russell Brand's baptism, Grylls deploys the classic religious narrative of 'not judging' and 'loving.' This is the essence of meta-violence: using a seemingly universal 'compassion' as a shield to provide spiritual sanctuary for actual perpetrators. When 'love' is weaponized to obstruct just judgment, gentleness becomes a form of complicity in structural violence.

Do not be fooled by this soothing formula. A man who can play the peaceful mentor on camera while providing ritualistic support for an alleged rapist in reality is simply stretching his power within a comfort zone. True radicalism is not two men praising each other in the wild; it is the courage to examine and dismantle the wall of complicity that protects them both.
