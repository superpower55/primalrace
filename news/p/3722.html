宇宙的“换皮”游戏与定义的权力Cosmic Skin-Walking and the Power of Definition

哲学 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-12 § 链接
当事实不符合既有模型，科学家通过重新定义对象来维持叙事。
When facts defy models, scientists redefine the object to preserve the narrative.

一个比太阳系还大、发着红光、能量输出比恒星高出1000亿倍的东西出现了。面对这个 a typical anomaly，天文学家的反应非常有意思：他们没有质疑现有的恒星或黑洞模型，而是直接创造了一个新词——“黑洞之星” (Black Hole Star)。

这在本质上是一次典型的认知入口争夺。按照之前的逻辑，它像恒星但能量像黑洞，于是结论是：它是一个被气体包裹得像恒星的黑洞。这就像是在说，一个穿着礼服的杀手不是杀手，而是一个“礼服化杀手”。通过给对象“换皮”，科学家成功地将一个无法解释的异类纳入了既有的 astrophysical 叙事框架中，避免了基础理论的崩塌。

这种“定义即事实”的机制在科技界极其通用。当现实不配合时，最快捷的方案不是推翻底层逻辑，而是重新定义现实。所谓的“发现新物种”，很多时候其实是给“无法解释的旧物种”贴了一张新标签。这种命名权（naming power）决定了我们如何感知宇宙：它不是在揭示真相，而是在制造一种能够被现有学术共谋者（complicit community）接受的“可能性”。

我们被告知这些“红点”是超大质量黑洞的种子，决定了星系的命运。但请记住，所有的科学结论在被证伪前，都只是一套被精心包装的叙事。真正的真相往往不在于那个新词叫什么，而在于为什么我们必须用一个新词来掩盖旧模型的失效。

An object larger than the solar system, glowing red with energy 100 billion times that of any star, has appeared. The reaction of astronomers is telling: rather than questioning existing models of stars or black holes, they simply coined a new term—the "Black Hole Star."

This is a classic struggle for the cognitive entry point. Since the object looks like a star but behaves like a black hole, the conclusion is that it's a black hole swaddled in gas. It's like calling a killer in a tuxedo a "Tuxedo-ized Killer." By "skin-walking" the object, scientists successfully fold an anomaly into the existing astrophysical narrative, preventing a collapse of foundational theory.

This mechanism of "definition as fact" is universal in tech and science. When reality refuses to cooperate, the fastest solution isn't to overturn the logic, but to redefine reality. Many "new discoveries" are merely new labels for old anomalies that the current model can't handle. This naming power dictates our perception of the universe; it's not about revealing truth, but about manufacturing a "possibility" that the academic complicity can digest.

We are told these "Little Red Dots" are seeds of supermassive black holes. But remember, every scientific conclusion is just a carefully packaged narrative until it's falsified. The real truth isn't in the new name, but in why a new name is required to mask the failure of the old model.
