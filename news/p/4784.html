曼德海 B 峡的血色贸易：被客体化的死者与战略性资产The Crimson Trade of Bab al-Mandeb: Objectified Dead and Strategic Assets

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-05 § 链接
战争叙事将人类身体降格为战略筹码，死亡被简化为地缘博弈的注脚。
War narratives degrade human bodies into strategic chips, reducing death to a footnote of geopolitical gaming.

这篇报道在典型的 masculine-centric narrative 中运行：它花大量篇幅讨论 Bab al-Mandeb 的战略地位、10% 的全球贸易额、以及沙特出口原油的通道，而将 60 多个死者，包括那些在巴士上被导弹炸死的平民，处理成一种背景噪音。在元暴力的逻辑里，人的生命不是主体，而是被客体化的“损耗”。

这种叙事方式通过将战争定义为“政府军”与“胡塞武装”的权力博弈，成功地将直接暴力 (direct violence) 包装成了一种战略必然。平民的死亡被轻描淡写为“collateral damage”（附带损伤），这种 cultural violence 的目的就是让读者接受一个设定：为了维持全球贸易的流动性，局部地区的血流成河是合理的成本。所谓的“决定性时刻”是指对战略高地的掌控，而非对生命权的保障。

本质上，这是一场由男性主导的政权在进行关于“解释权”的战争。无论是沙特支持的政府军还是伊朗支持的胡塞武装，他们共谋地使用同一套暴力逻辑：将土地、港口和海峡视为资产，将士兵和平民视为消耗品。在这种结构性暴力 (structural violence) 中，人权被彻底剥离，只剩下关于“控制权”的计算。当新闻将重点放在“全球商品成本上涨”而非“平民生命消逝”时，它已经在潜意识里完成了对暴力的合法化。

This report operates within a typical masculine-centric narrative: it devotes extensive space to the strategic importance of Bab al-Mandeb, 10% of global trade, and Saudi oil exports, while treating the 60+ dead—including civilians obliterated in a bus missile strike—as mere background noise. In the logic of meta-violence, human life is not a subject, but an objectified 'attrition'.

By defining the war as a power struggle between 'government forces' and 'Houthi rebels', the narrative successfully packages direct violence as a strategic necessity. Civilian deaths are trivialized as 'collateral damage'. This cultural violence serves to convince the reader that bloodshed in a local region is a reasonable cost for maintaining the fluidity of global trade. The so-called 'decisive hours' refer to the control of strategic hills, not the protection of the right to life.

Essentially, this is a war over the 'right of interpretation' waged by male-dominated regimes. Whether it is the Saudi-backed government or the Iran-aligned Houthis, they are complicit in the same violent logic: treating land, ports, and straits as assets, and soldiers and civilians as consumables. Within this structural violence, human rights are stripped away, leaving only the calculus of 'control'. When the news prioritizes 'rising global goods costs' over 'vanishing civilian lives', it has already subconsciously legitimized the violence.
