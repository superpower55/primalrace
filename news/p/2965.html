用“效率”掩盖暴力的经典叙事骗局The Classic Narrative Scam: Masking Violence with 'Efficiency'

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-29 § 链接
当“效率”成为衡量正义的唯一尺度时，它就是暴力的遮羞布。
When 'efficiency' becomes the sole metric of justice, it is merely a veil for violence.

这是一次典型的用 structural violence 伪装成“科学客观”的叙事尝试。研究结论极其荒谬：因为笼养鸡产蛋“效率”更高，所以对气候更友好。在这个逻辑里，把生物禁锢在狭小笼子里的痛苦被抹除，而由于有机养殖需要更多鸡来维持产量而产生的碳排放，却被量化为一种“罪恶”。

这本质上是一场关于“效率”的 weaponization。当权力者定义什么是“可持续”时，他们倾向于选择那个能最大化产出、最小化成本的指标。在男性中心叙事的元暴力逻辑中，效率永远高于主体性。笼养鸡在此时被客体化为一台单纯的“产蛋机器”，而有机养殖因为“效率低下”而成了气候杀手。这和要求女性在职场中扮演某种“高效”角色以换取生存空间的逻辑如出一辙——只要你不能像机器一样产出，你就是对系统的负担。

最讽刺的是，研究者承认使用了 2009-2010 年的旧数据，却依然试图用这个结论来定义 2026 年的“可持续性”。这种对解释权的垄断，让一个关于“折磨生物”的伦理问题，被偷换成了关于“tCO2e”的数学题。这就是文化暴力的精髓：让直接的暴力（禁锢与折磨）看起来像是一种为了“拯救地球”而不得不采取的理性选择。

所谓的 trade-off 只是在给共谋者提供心理安慰。如果你接受了“为了低碳而支持笼养”的叙事，你就已经内化了那种将生命客体化的暴力逻辑。下次当你被告知为了某种“宏大目标”而必须牺牲一部分人的主体性时，记得想想这些笼子里的鸡。

This is a textbook attempt to disguise structural violence as 'scientific objectivity.' The research conclusion is absurd: because caged chickens are more 'efficient' at producing eggs, they are better for the climate. In this logic, the agony of biological confinement is erased, while the carbon emissions from organic farming—resulting from the need for more birds to maintain output—are quantified as a 'sin.'

This is essentially the weaponization of 'efficiency.' When those in power define 'sustainability,' they tend to choose metrics that maximize output and minimize cost. In the meta-violence of masculine-centric narratives, efficiency always trumps subjectivity. The caged hen is objectified as a mere 'egg-producing machine,' while organic farming becomes a climate villain due to its 'inefficiency.' This mirrors the logic of requiring women to perform a specific, 'efficient' role in the workplace to earn their survival—if you cannot produce like a machine, you are a burden to the system.

Most ironic is the researchers' admission of using data from 2009-2010 to define 'sustainability' in 2026. This monopoly over the right of interpretation transforms an ethical issue of biological torture into a math problem about tCO2e. This is the essence of cultural violence: making direct violence (confinement and torture) look like a rational choice necessary to 'save the planet.'

The so-called 'trade-off' is merely providing psychological comfort to the complicity. If you accept the narrative of 'supporting cages for low carbon,' you have internalized the violent logic of objectifying life. Next time you are told that a certain subjectivity must be sacrificed for a 'greater good,' remember the chickens in those cages.
