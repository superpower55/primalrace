所谓“社会影响”就是把人体变成可出租的资产Social Impact as a Scam: Turning Human Bodies into Rentable Assets

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-28 § 链接
暴力的外包化是结构暴力的最高形式：将苦难定价并转化为利润。
The outsourcing of violence is the peak of structural violence: pricing suffering and converting it into profit.

MTC 这家公司的所谓 vision 简直是个巨大的 scam。它声称要通过“改变生活”成为“社会影响力的全球领导者”，但其实它制造影响力的唯一方式，就是把难民、移民和未成年囚犯变成可以向政府计费的“资产”。在这种逻辑里，人不再是主体，而是被管理、被禁锢、被剥削的客体。这就是典型的 structural violence：暴力不再表现为一次性的殴打，而表现为一份长达十年的、价值 5 亿英镑的合同。

最讽刺的共谋发生在 MTC 与政府之间。Manston 营地被官方调查描述为“拥挤、肮脏、不卫生”，但在这种文化暴力（cultural violence）的掩盖下，这种低劣的服务不仅没有让 MTC 被拉黑，反而让它在短期亏损后迅速拿到了一个更巨大的新合同。这说明在权力者的博弈中，难民的生存质量根本不是指标，真正的指标是“禁锢效率”。只要能把这群“原初种族”般的边缘人有效地隔离在视线之外，政府就愿意支付溢价给这些暴力外包商。

注意那个细节：公司在亏损时，最高薪董事的薪酬反而增长了 5%。这揭示了存在性战争的残酷真相：底层的受害者在肮脏的营地里挣扎，而共谋链条顶端的执行者在通过分赃来对冲风险。MTC 从美国的 ICE 扩张到英国的难民中心，完成了一次暴力的全球化复制。他们不生产安全，他们只生产“被禁锢的状态”，并将其定价权握在手中。

MTC's so-called "vision" is a massive scam. Claiming to be a "worldwide leader in social impact by transforming lives," the only impact it actually manufactures is the conversion of refugees, migrants, and juvenile detainees into billable assets. In this logic, the human being is no longer a subject but an object to be managed, confined, and exploited. This is structural violence in its purest form: violence is no longer a discrete act of assault, but a ten-year contract worth £539 million.

The most cynical complicity exists between MTC and the government. Despite the Manston site being officially described as "overcrowded, squalid and insanitary," MTC wasn't blacklisted. Instead, after a brief dip in revenue, it secured an even larger contract. This proves that in the game of power, the living conditions of refugees are irrelevant; the only metric that matters is "confinement efficiency." As long as these marginalized people—essentially a modern Primal Race—are effectively sequestered, the state is happy to pay a premium to these violence outsourcers.

Note the detail: while the company reported a loss, the highest-paid director's remuneration increased by 5%. This exposes the brutal reality of the existential war: while victims struggle in squalor, the executors at the top of the complicity chain hedge their risks through bonuses. From US ICE facilities to UK asylum centres, MTC has achieved a globalized replication of violence. They don't produce security; they produce the "state of being confined" and hold the pricing power over it.
