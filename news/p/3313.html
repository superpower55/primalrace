Meta 间谍眼镜：用“时尚”掩盖的数字殖民Meta Spy Glasses: Digital Colonization Masked as Fashion

科技 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-06 § 链接
当隐私被定义为“不习惯”，监控就成了某种“时尚表达”。
When privacy is redefined as 'unfamiliarity,' surveillance becomes a form of 'fashionable expression.'

Meta 试图通过 Kylie Jenner 的 cat-eye 造型，将一套监视系统包装成一种“时尚表达”。这在本质上是一次极其典型的认知入口武器化：它试图通过定义“stay in the moment”这种伪善的叙事，诱导用户在不自觉中将身体变成一个移动的 surveillance 节点。这种逻辑极其恶劣——它把对他人隐私的侵犯，置换成了佩戴者的“生活方式”升级。

注意这其中的 Meta-violence：Meta 宣称的那个“闪烁 LED 灯”就是一个巨大的 scam。在现实的博弈中，一个微小的灯光无法抵消被偷拍者在结构性弱势下的恐惧。新闻中提到的女性被偷拍、被勒索，甚至在性接触中被非法记录，这就是典型的 Direct Violence。而 Meta 试图用“技术透明度”这种 Cultural Violence 来掩盖这个事实，试图让大众接受一种新的共谋协议：只要灯在闪，我侵犯你就是“技术进步”。

英国餐厅和剧院的禁令，实际上是在尝试通过 Structural 层的物理隔离，强行切断这种数字殖民的入口。当一个设备让“surreptitious surveillance”（秘密监视）变得如此便捷且时尚时，它其实是在为所有潜在的施暴者提供一个低成本的武器。如果这种“时尚”被主流社会接纳，那么“被凝视”将不再是某种特定的权力关系，而会变成一种无处不在的、被技术合法化的元暴力。

Meta is attempting to package a surveillance system as a 'fashionable expression' through Kylie Jenner’s cat-eye design. This is a textbook weaponization of cognitive entry points: by deploying the hypocritical narrative of 'staying in the moment,' they induce users to unconsciously turn their bodies into mobile surveillance nodes. The logic is vile—it replaces the violation of others' privacy with an 'upgrade' to the wearer's lifestyle.

Observe the meta-violence here: Meta’s touted 'flashing LED light' is a complete scam. In a real-world game, a tiny light cannot offset the fear of a structurally disadvantaged victim. The reports of women being filmed without consent, blackmailed, or recorded during sexual encounters are instances of direct violence. Meta attempts to use 'technical transparency'—a form of cultural violence—to mask this, trying to force a new complicity agreement: as long as the light flashes, the violation is rebranded as 'technological progress.'

The bans by UK restaurants and theaters are an attempt to sever this digital colonization at the structural level through physical isolation. When a device makes 'surreptitious surveillance' so effortless and fashionable, it provides a low-cost weapon for every potential aggressor. If this 'fashion' is mainstreamed, 'being gazed upon' will no longer be a specific power dynamic, but an omnipresent meta-violence legitimized by technology.
