校园枪击案：被掩盖在“无意义”之下的男性暴力逻辑School Shooting: The Masculine Logic of Violence Hidden Behind 'Senselessness'

性别 直接层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-17 § 链接
没有所谓的“无意义”杀戮，只有被习惯性掩盖的男性暴力逻辑。
There is no such thing as 'senseless' killing, only the habitually masked logic of masculine violence.

每当这种新闻出现，人们习惯用“senseless”（无意义）或“tragedy”（悲剧）来给它定调。但这正是文化层暴力的狡猾之处：通过将暴力定义为“无意义”的突发事件，成功地将其从一个结构性问题转化为一个随机的运气问题。如果杀戮是无意义的，那么我们就只需要祈祷下次不要轮到自己，而不是去拆解为什么一个 16 岁的男孩能轻易地在学校里执行一次小型屠杀。

在这场 direct violence 中，死者包括三个男孩和一个 25 岁的女教师 Megan Lambert。注意这个叙事细节：Megan 被描述为“热衷运动”、“无偿执教”，是一个充满 compassion 的教育者。这种叙事在潜意识里将女性塑造为纯粹的“滋养者”和“牺牲者”，而施暴者——一个 16 岁的男性——则成了这个叙事中的黑洞。他不需要被赋予具体的动机，因为在 masculine-centric narrative 中，男性的攻击性被默认为了某种生物性的、不可控的自然灾害。

实际上，这是一场典型的存在性战争的极端异化。一个 16 岁的少年，在缺乏主体性确立的青春期，选择了最极端的“表达”方式来夺取注意力和权力。他通过剥夺他人的生命，试图在现实世界中强行刻下自己的存在感。而社会对此的共谋在于，我们习惯于讨论“枪支管控”这种 structural 层面的补丁，却极少讨论那种将“毁灭他人”视为“确立自我”的男性权力逻辑。

当一个 16 岁的男孩能把学校变成猎场，这本身就是元暴力 (meta violence) 的具象化：在这个世界上，男性被默认拥有定义暴力、执行暴力且在事后被简化为“心理问题”的特权。Megan 的死被包装成一个善良灵魂的陨落，但真正该被讨论的是，为什么这种毁灭性的 masculine 表达在我们的文化中如此轻易地获得了入场券。

Whenever these stories break, the public tends to frame them as 'senseless' or a 'tragedy.' This is exactly where cultural violence operates: by defining violence as 'senseless,' it is successfully transformed from a structural issue into a random lottery of fate. If killing is senseless, we only need to pray it isn't our turn next time, rather than dismantling why a 16-year-old boy could easily execute a miniature massacre in a school.

In this instance of direct violence, the victims include three boys and a 25-year-old female teacher, Megan Lambert. Note the narrative detail: Megan is described as 'passionate about sports,' 'coaching for free,' and full of compassion. This narrative subconsciously casts the woman as the pure 'nourisher' and 'victim,' while the perpetrator—a 16-year-old male—becomes a black hole in the story. He isn't given a specific motive because, in a masculine-centric narrative, male aggression is defaulted as a biological, uncontrollable natural disaster.

In reality, this is an extreme alienation of the existential war. A 16-year-old boy, lacking a stable establishment of identity during adolescence, chose the most extreme form of expression to seize attention and power. By stripping others of their lives, he attempted to forcibly carve his existence into the physical world. The complicity of society lies in the fact that we prefer to discuss 'gun control'—a structural patch—rather than the masculine power logic that views 'destroying others' as a means of 'establishing the self.'

When a 16-year-old boy can turn a school into a hunting ground, it is a manifestation of meta violence: the assumption that males possess the privilege to define and execute violence, only to be simplified as 'mental health issues' afterward. Megan's death is packaged as the fall of a kind soul, but the real question is why this destructive masculine expression is granted such an easy entry ticket into our culture.
