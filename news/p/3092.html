被定价的健康焦虑与补剂 scamPriced Anxiety and the Supplement Scam

哲学 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-01 § 链接
补剂产业是将生物性缺失武器化，通过制造焦虑来夺取定价权。
The supplement industry weaponizes biological deficiency to seize pricing power through manufactured anxiety.

大多数补剂的本质是一场关于“优化”的 scam。当人们开始痴迷于通过胶囊来精准控制微量元素时，他们实际上是在将自己的身体客体化，把生命简化为一组可量化的数据指标。这种叙事由资本驱动，它在认知入口处植入一个逻辑：你的自然状态是不完整的，只有通过购买特定的“插件”，你才能接近某种理想的、最优化的存在。

最讽刺的是，补剂产业精准地利用了结构性弱势者的生物墙。比如针对经期女性的补铁建议，或者针对特定年龄段女性的胶原蛋白营销。它并不试图解决导致营养缺失的结构性贫困或医疗资源分配不均，而是把这种结构性暴力转化为一个消费机会。它告诉女性：你的疲惫和衰老是由于缺乏某种分子，而不是因为你正处于一个榨取你生育力且不提供足够保障的结构中。

真正的“最优解表达”不是在补剂清单里寻找安全感，而是夺回对身体的解释权。当一个营养师建议你通过多样化的真实食物来获得营养，而不是依赖昂贵的合成物时，这实际上是在削弱补剂产业的认知垄断。好新闻在于，这种基于生物学事实的回归，正在让人们意识到：那些被包装成“健康必需品”的胶囊，很多时候只是为了让消费者在心理上完成一次对“掌控感”的低效投票。

Most supplements are essentially a scam centered on the concept of "optimization." When people become obsessed with precisely controlling micronutrients via capsules, they are effectively objectifying their own bodies, reducing life to a set of quantifiable data points. This narrative is driven by capital, planting a logic at the cognitive entry point: your natural state is incomplete, and only by purchasing specific "plug-ins" can you approach some ideal, optimized existence.

Most ironically, the supplement industry precisely leverages the biological walls of structurally disadvantaged groups. Take the iron supplementation advice for menstruating women or collagen marketing for specific age groups. It doesn't attempt to solve the structural poverty or the unfair distribution of healthcare resources that cause these deficiencies; instead, it converts this structural violence into a commercial opportunity. It tells women: your fatigue and aging are due to a missing molecule, not because you exist within a structure that extracts your reproductive labor without providing adequate support.

True optimal expression is not about finding security in a supplement checklist, but about reclaiming the power of interpretation over one's own body. When a dietitian suggests diverse real foods over expensive synthetics, they are actively weakening the cognitive monopoly of the supplement industry. The good news here is that this return to biological facts is making people realize that those capsules, packaged as "health essentials," are often just inefficient votes cast by consumers seeking a psychological illusion of control.
