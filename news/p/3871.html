评论家的权力幻觉与艺术家的共谋游戏The Illusion of Critical Power and the Artist's Game of Complicity

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-14 § 链接
当权力被简化为‘打分’，反抗权力就成了另一种表演性消费。
When power is reduced to 'star ratings,' resisting that power becomes just another form of performative consumption.

这篇文章读起来像是一场温情的内部聚会。评论家 Brian Logan 在面对两场将评论家描绘成“傲慢之徒”的演出时，表现出了一种近乎讨好的宽容。这种宽容的本质是：当权力处于一个被公认的、可被嘲讽的‘刻板印象’中时，权力持有者反而可以通过‘自黑’来完成一次低成本的洗白。

从加尔通的暴力三角来看，评论业的 structural violence 在于它垄断了‘审美定价权’。一个星级评定可以决定一个艺术家的生存空间。然而，在这两场演出中，这种结构性暴力被转化为 cultural violence 的反向操作——艺术家通过将评论家‘可怜化’（sad, lonely and pathetic），将一场关于解释权的博弈降格为一场关于‘性格缺陷’的闹剧。

这其实是一场典型的共谋 (complicity)。艺术家在舞台上扮演‘受害者’，评论家在专栏里扮演‘大度的审判者’。双方通过这种‘互黑’的仪式，维持了一个虚假的平衡：评论家继续保留着打分的权力，而艺术家则在‘反抗’的快感中获得了某种情绪价值。这种博弈没有触及真正的最优解，因为解释权的垄断依然存在，只是被包裹上了一层‘幽默’的糖衣。

最讽刺的是，Logan 试图将这种现象解读为‘客观判断已过时’的时代趋势。这不过是另一种武器化的叙事。当真正的客观性被解构，剩下的只有‘影响力’的互换。这场游戏里没有赢家，只有一群在‘三星’与‘四星’之间反复横跳的共谋者，他们共同维护着一个名为‘文化权威’的 scam。

This piece reads like a cozy insider's gathering. Brian Logan, facing two shows that portray critics as 'horrible snobs,' exhibits a tolerance that borders on sycophancy. The essence of this tolerance is simple: when power resides within a recognized, mockable 'stereotype,' the power-holder can perform a low-cost whitewashing through self-deprecation.

Applying the Violence Triangle, the structural violence of the reviewing industry lies in its monopoly over 'aesthetic pricing.' A star rating can dictate an artist's survival. However, in these shows, this structural violence is converted into a reverse operation of cultural violence—artists 'pitifulize' the critic (sad, lonely and pathetic), reducing a struggle over the right of interpretation to a farce about 'personality flaws.'

This is a classic case of complicity. The artist performs the 'victim' on stage, while the critic plays the 'magnanimous judge' in the column. Through this ritual of mutual mockery, they maintain a fake equilibrium: the critic retains the power to rate, and the artist gains emotional value from the thrill of 'resistance.' This game never reaches a true optimal expression because the monopoly of interpretation remains, merely wrapped in the candy coating of 'humor.'

The ultimate irony is Logan's attempt to frame this as a trend where 'objective judgment is out of fashion.' This is just another weaponized narrative. When true objectivity is deconstructed, all that remains is the exchange of 'influence.' There are no winners here, only a group of complicit actors bouncing between 'three stars' and 'four stars,' collectively maintaining a scam called 'cultural authority.'
