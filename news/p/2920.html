被冠以“梅西”之名的芭蕾舞者，与被掩盖的身体战争The 'Messi of Ballet' and the Erasure of Female Agency

性别 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-28 § 链接
用男性的巅峰符号定义女性成就，是典型的元暴力叙事。
Defining female achievement through male symbols is a textbook exercise in meta-violence.

《卫报》给 Marianela Núñez 贴上的标签是“芭蕾界的梅西”。这是一个典型的 masculine-centric narrative（男性中心叙事）：即便一个女性在自己的领域达到了顶峰，她的卓越性依然需要通过一个男性符号的投影来获得大众认知的“合法性”。在元暴力的逻辑里，梅西是衡量“最强”的唯一尺子，而 Núñez 的主体性在被称赞的同时，被悄悄地降格为了一个参照物。

正文用“bombshell”这种物化词汇开篇，随后迅速切入她对足球的喜爱。这种叙事陷阱试图通过“她也喜欢梅西”来消解这种权力不对等，将一种结构性的解释权垄断包装成一种浪漫的共情。但事实是，在芭蕾这个极度强调规训、甚至带有某种残酷生物墙的行业里，一个 44 岁的女性能够维持在顶端，她对抗的是真实的肌肉损耗、激素波动和年龄焦虑。然而，媒体选择性地将其描述为“阳光性格”和“韧性”，用文化层面的美化掩盖了她为了维持这个 Actual 状态而进行的残酷博弈。

最讽刺的共谋发生在关于“经纪人”的讨论中。她以 Sylvie Guillem 没有经纪人为由，拒绝进入商业 PR 的流水线，这本应是一次主体性的觉醒和对行业 weaponized 表达的拒绝。但在记者的笔下，这被轻描淡写地处理成一种“延迟满足”的正面叙事。她通过与四位男性舞者的合作来寻找“安全感”和“脆弱感”，这种描述再次将女性的成就锚定在与男性的关系网络之中。

当她宣称“没有过期日期”时，这确实是一次对生物墙的强力冲撞。但只要这种胜利依然被包裹在“梅西”的阴影下，它就不是一次真正意义上的解释权换手，而是一次在既定男权规则下的“优秀表演”。

The Guardian labels Marianela Núñez as the 'Messi of ballet.' This is a classic masculine-centric narrative: even when a woman reaches the pinnacle of her field, her excellence must be validated through the projection of a male symbol to achieve public 'legitimacy.' In the logic of meta-violence, Messi remains the sole yardstick for 'the greatest,' and Núñez's subjectivity is quietly demoted to a mere reference point while being praised.

Starting with the objectifying term 'bombshell,' the piece quickly pivots to her love for football. This narrative trap attempts to dissolve the power imbalance by framing it as a romantic affinity. But the reality is that in ballet—an industry of extreme discipline and brutal biological walls—a 44-year-old woman staying at the top is a war against muscle atrophy, hormonal shifts, and age anxiety. The media chooses to mask this structural struggle with descriptors like 'sunny temperament' and 'resilience,' using cultural violence to beautify a grueling existential game.

The most telling complicity appears in the discussion of agents. Her refusal to enter the commercial PR pipeline, citing Sylvie Guillem, should be a reclamation of agency against the weaponized expression of the industry. Instead, the journalist spins it as a positive 'delay.' Furthermore, framing her growth through the 'confidence' provided by four male partners once again anchors female achievement within a network of relationships with men.

When she declares there is 'no expiry date,' she is indeed crashing against the biological wall. However, as long as this victory is wrapped in the shadow of 'Messi,' it is not a handover of interpretative power, but rather a 'high-performance' act within the established patriarchal rules.
