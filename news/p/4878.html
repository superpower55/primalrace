女巫的轮回：在浪漫爱陷阱里打转的女性共谋The Witch's Loop: Female Complicity Spinning Around the Romantic Love Trap

性别 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-09 § 链接
所谓的女性团结，如果仍以男性为轴心，就只是在囚笼里互助。
Sisterhood is a cage when it remains centered on the masculine axis.

《Practical Magic 2》这种电影的回归，本质上是一场关于 nostalgia 的商业 scam。它试图用“女性团结” (feminine solidarity) 这种时髦的包装纸，去包裹一个极其陈旧的内核：女性的生命价值依然被锚定在与男性的关系中，甚至连她们的“魔法”和“诅咒”，都必须通过男性的死亡或昏迷来触发。这种叙事逻辑极其讽刺——女巫们拥有改变现实的超自然力量，但她们在存在性战争中的最优解，竟然依然是围绕着“如何爱上一个男人且不让他死掉”这种低级博弈。

电影里提到的 matrilineal romance (母系浪漫) 是个伪命题。真正的母系叙事应该是关于女性权力的传承与自我确证，而不是把“爱是我们的毁灭” (love is our undoing) 当成一种 camp 风格的真理来传颂。当电影把“姐妹情深” (sisterhood) 简化为在面对男性带来的麻烦时互相扶持，这其实是一种典型的 cultural violence。它通过美化这种循环往复的受难，让女性在潜意识里内化了一种设定：女性的成长必须经过心碎，而这种心碎必须由男性施予。这根本不是赋权，而是对女性主体性的再次剥夺。

最令人作呕的是那种对“男性多样性”的拙劣呈现——一个纹身教授鼓励女性“感受情绪”，另一个阴郁巫师则表现出“受害者心态”的权力欲。这不过是男性中心叙事 (masculine-centric narrative) 的升级版：无论男人是温柔的还是暴戾的，他们依然是剧情的驱动者，是女性魔法的触发键。女巫们在电影里扮演着被动角色，她们的力量被用来修补男性的伤口，而非定义自己的世界。

这种电影之所以能“施展魔力”，是因为它精准地利用了女性对浪漫爱叙事的依赖。它告诉观众：即便你 60 岁了，你依然可以被定义为某个男人的爱人，且这种定义是神圣的。这是一种高级的共谋，好莱坞通过贩卖这种温情，让女性在舒适的 nostalgia 中忘记了，真正的魔法应该是彻底地切断与这种毒性叙事的绑定。

The return of Practical Magic 2 is essentially a commercial scam of nostalgia. It attempts to wrap a prehistoric core in the fashionable packaging of "feminine solidarity." The core? A woman's value is still anchored to her relationship with men. Even their "magic" and "curses" are triggered by the death or coma of a male. It is a bitter irony: these witches possess supernatural power to alter reality, yet their optimal expression in the existential war is still a low-level game of "how to love a man without him dying."

The film's notion of "matrilineal romance" is a pseudo-concept. True matrilineal narrative should be about the inheritance of female power and self-confirmation, not preaching that "love is our undoing" as a campy truth. When "sisterhood" is reduced to supporting each other while dealing with male-induced chaos, it becomes a form of cultural violence. By romanticizing this cycle of suffering, it internalizes a setting: female growth must pass through heartbreak, and that heartbreak must be administered by a man. This is not empowerment; it is the further stripping of female subjectivity.

Most repulsive is the clumsy presentation of "masculine diversity"—one tattooed professor encouraging women to "feel their feelings," and another brooding warlock exhibiting the aggrieved entitlement of a predator. This is merely an upgraded version of the masculine-centric narrative: whether the man is gentle or violent, he remains the driver of the plot and the trigger for female magic. The women play passive roles, using their power to patch up male wounds rather than defining their own world.

Such films "cast a spell" because they precisely exploit the female dependence on romantic love narratives. It tells the audience: even at 60, you can still be defined as a man's lover, and this definition is sacred. This is a high-level complicity. Hollywood sells this warmth to make women forget, amidst the comfort of nostalgia, that real magic would be to completely sever the bond with this toxic narrative.
