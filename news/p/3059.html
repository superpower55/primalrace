权力下放的PR陷阱与被遗忘的身体The PR Trap of Devolution and the Forgotten Body

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-31 § 链接
制度的碎片化不等于暴力的消弭，资源分配的权力转移往往只是共谋者的换手。
Fragmentation of power is not the eradication of violence; it is often just a reshuffle of complicity.

安迪·伯纳姆（Andy Burnham）政府大张旗鼓地宣传所谓的“权力下放”（devolution），将税收分配权和交通审批权从白厅移交给地方市长。在官方叙事中，这是打破中心化、提升生产力的进步。但按照加尔通的暴力三角来看，这不过是 structural violence 的一次空间位移。当资源分配的权力从一个中心转移到多个次中心时，如果缺乏真正的底层监督机制，它极易演变为地方门阀的新共谋场域。所谓的“简化官僚程序”，本质上是降低了权力运行的透明度，让地方精英在更小的闭环里完成利益交换。

最令人不安的是，在这一场关于“权力”与“效率”的男性化博弈中，女性的身体再次成为了被边缘化的客体。英国国内暴力 commissioner 妮可·雅各布斯（Nicole Jacobs）的警告揭示了残酷的真相：针对女性的暴力（VAWG）正面临被这套下放计划遗忘的风险。当预算被碎片化、责任被分散到各个地方当局时，那些需要跨区域协作、需要强力中央指令才能推动的受害者救济服务，反而更容易在“地方自治”的掩护下被悄悄削减。这正是典型的元暴力（meta violence）运作方式：用“民主”和“效率”的文化叙事，掩盖结构性资源剥夺的实际结果。

至于哈丽特·哈曼（Harriet Harman）建议禁止男性竞选工党领导人的提议，这是一种典型的“假.最优解表达”。试图通过简单的性别配额来对抗深层的男性中心叙事，不仅无法触及权力运作的本质，反而给了既得利益者一个攻击女权主义“不公正”的认知入口。真正的胜利不应来自禁令，而应来自对男性共谋结构的彻底拆解。如果一个政党依然在用“宽恕”和“警告”来处理种族主义者（如 Diane Abbott 的回归），那么它所谓的性别进步不过是掩盖内部权力腐败的装饰品。

Andy Burnham's government is aggressively promoting 'devolution,' shifting tax allocation and transport approvals from Whitehall to regional mayors. In the official narrative, this is a progressive move to break centralization and boost productivity. However, applying the Violence Triangle, this is merely a spatial displacement of structural violence. When the power of resource distribution shifts from one center to multiple sub-centers without genuine grassroots oversight, it easily evolves into a new field of complicity for local elites. The so-called 'reduction of bureaucracy' is essentially a reduction in the transparency of power, allowing local power-brokers to complete interest exchanges within smaller, closed loops.

More disturbing is how women's bodies are once again marginalized in this masculine-centric game of 'power' and 'efficiency.' The warning from Dame Nicole Jacobs, the domestic abuse commissioner, reveals a brutal truth: services for victims of violence against women and girls (VAWG) risk being ignored in these devolution plans. When budgets are fragmented and accountability is scattered across local authorities, victim-centered services—which require cross-regional coordination and strong central mandates—are more likely to be quietly cut under the guise of 'local autonomy.' This is a textbook operation of meta violence: using the cultural narratives of 'democracy' and 'efficiency' to mask the actual result of structural resource deprivation.

As for Harriet Harman's suggestion to ban men from running for the Labour leadership, this is a 'fake optimal expression.' Attempting to counter a deep-seated masculine-centric narrative through simple gender quotas fails to touch the essence of power and instead provides an entry point for the establishment to frame feminism as 'unjust.' True victory does not come from bans, but from the total dismantling of the masculine complicity structure. If a party still uses 'forgiveness' and 'warnings' to handle racists (as seen in Diane Abbott's readmission), then its supposed gender progress is nothing more than a decorative mask for internal power corruption.
