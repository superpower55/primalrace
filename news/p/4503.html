用 2.4 亿欧元的混凝土堆砌出的“绿色福音”ScamA €240m Concrete Scam Masked as 'Green Gospel'

哲学 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-25 § 链接
当可持续性被武器化为审美符号，建筑就成了掩盖结构暴力的昂贵饰品。
When sustainability is weaponized as an aesthetic symbol, architecture becomes a costly bauble masking structural violence.

Rotterdam ROCKS! 是一个典型的认知入口 scam。它试图通过一种 Flintstones-esque 的原始主义审美，将“可持续性” (sustainability) 从一个极其痛苦的结构性生存问题，转化为一个可以通过支付 20-45 欧元门票就能“体验”到的沉浸式消费产品。这本质上是文化层面的暴力：用一个巨大的、呼吸着的“人造岩石”景观，掩盖了气候危机中真正的 structural violence —— 那些在海平面上升中真实消失的土地，以及被资本逻辑异化的绿色叙事。

这个项目的逻辑极其男性中心化 (masculine-centric)。从投资者 Don Ritzen 对“伟大人物”理论的迷信，到 MVRDV 追求视觉冲击力的过度表达，整个过程是一场关于 ego 的存在性战争。他们并不在乎建筑是否真的 regenerative，他们在乎的是通过制造一个“新世界奇迹”来夺取定义权，将“救世主”的身份标签贴在自己身上。这种表达方式是武器化的：它用 hyperbole (夸张法) 制造出一种“我们在拯救地球”的幻象，从而让人们在快门声和点击量中忘记追问：建设这样一个混凝土怪物的碳足迹，是否本身就是对环境的又一次掠夺？

最讽刺的是，这种所谓的“绿色福音”建立在一种共谋之上。建筑师、资本家与评审委员会共同构建了一个闭环：定义什么是“酷”的绿色，然后用昂贵的成本将其具象化。而当现实的物理墙（如资金超支、建设难度）出现时，他们迅速地通过“削减功能”来妥协，但那个关于“拯救世界”的叙事入口依然被牢牢把持。这不过是又一次将复杂的社会政治问题简化为个体英雄主义表演的拙劣尝试。

Rotterdam ROCKS! is a textbook cognitive entrance scam. It attempts to transform 'sustainability' from a painful structural survival issue into an immersive consumer product that can be 'experienced' for a €20-45 entry fee. This is essentially cultural violence: using a humongous, 'breathing' artificial rock landscape to mask the actual structural violence of the climate crisis—the real lands disappearing under rising seas and the green narrative alienated by capital logic.

The logic of this project is profoundly masculine-centric. From Don Ritzen's faith in the 'Great Man' theory to MVRDV's pursuit of visual shock, the entire process is an existential war over ego. They don't care if the building is truly regenerative; they care about seizing the power of definition by creating a 'new world wonder' to claim the 'savior' identity. This expression is weaponized: it uses hyperbole to manufacture an illusion of 'saving the planet,' distracting the public with clickbait while ignoring the question: is the carbon footprint of constructing such a concrete monster just another act of predation against the environment?

The ultimate irony is that this 'green gospel' relies on complicity. The architects, the capitalist, and the jury together constructed a loop: defining what 'cool' green is, and then manifesting it at an exorbitant cost. When the physical walls of reality—such as cost overruns and construction failures—hit, they quickly compromise by 'dialing down' functions, yet they cling to the narrative of 'saving the world.' It is nothing more than a clumsy attempt to reduce complex socio-political struggles into a performance of individual heroism.
