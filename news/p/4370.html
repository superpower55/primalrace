用新的尺子定义“好日子”：英国生产力的叙事魔术Defining 'Good Times' with a New Ruler: The Narrative Magic of UK Productivity

国际 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-24 § 链接
当现实不配合时，权力通过更换度量衡来制造“增长”的幻觉。
When reality refuses to cooperate, power manufactures the illusion of growth by changing the yardstick.

这是一场典型的认知入口争夺战。Resolution Foundation 告诉我们，英国的生产力其实在增长，只是官方数据没算对。逻辑很简单：既然官方数据太难看，导致生活水平低迷、经济停滞，那么只要换一套“更准确”的计算方法，就能在不改变任何实际生存状态的情况下，宣布经济正在复苏。

这种操作在本质上是 cultural violence 的一种伪装。生产力（Productivity）在宏观叙事中被神化为经济健康的唯一指标，但它掩盖了最核心的 structural violence：那些被定义为“更高效”的工人，其生活质量是否真的提升了？还是仅仅在相同的岗位上被榨取了更多的剩余价值，然后被量化成一个漂亮的增长百分比？

最讽刺的是，摩根士丹利甚至用 “The vibes they are a-changin’” 这种极其轻佻的词汇来描述这种转变。当经济分析变成了“氛围感”的博弈，事实本身就成了可以随意揉捏的橡皮泥。这种从“官方数据”到“智库分析”再到“市场情绪”的共谋，目的只有一个：通过重新定义现实，让人们相信一个并不存在的 recovery，从而在心理上接受当前的结构性压迫。

如果 Actual（实际生活水平）没有向 Potential（应有的生活质量）靠近，那么无论你换多少把尺子，那个差额依然是暴力。

This is a classic battle for the cognitive entry point. The Resolution Foundation claims UK productivity is actually growing, but official figures simply missed it. The logic is simple: since official data is too dismal, leading to stagnant living standards, one can simply implement a 'more accurate' calculation method to announce a recovery without changing a single material condition of existence.

This operation is essentially a camouflage for cultural violence. Productivity is fetishized in macro-narratives as the sole metric of economic health, yet it masks the underlying structural violence: have the workers defined as 'more productive' actually seen an improvement in their quality of life? Or are they simply being squeezed for more surplus value in the same roles, only to be quantified as a pretty growth percentage?

Most ironic is Morgan Stanley using the phrase “The vibes they are a-changin’” to describe this shift. When economic analysis devolves into a game of 'vibes,' facts become malleable clay. This complicity—stretching from official data to think-tank analysis to market sentiment—serves one purpose: to manufacture a belief in a non-existent recovery, ensuring the public accepts current structural oppression through a redefined reality.

As long as the Actual does not move toward the Potential, no matter how many rulers you switch, the gap remains violence.
