洗衣机的“标准模式”是一场针对消费者的结构性欺诈The 'Normal Cycle' is a Structural Fraud Against Consumers

科技 结构层 · 文化层 Wirecutter ↗ 2026-07-29 § 链接
所谓的“标准模式”不是为了洗净衣服，而是为了通过监管指标的PR表演。
The 'Normal' setting isn't for cleaning clothes; it's a PR performance for regulatory metrics.

这篇关于洗衣机设置的指南揭露了一个典型的 structural violence 样本：家电厂商通过定义所谓的“Normal cycle”（标准模式），在认知入口处制造了一个巨大的 scam。在这个模式下，机器并非在追求洗净衣服的 Potential，而是在追求政府能效指标和 Consumer Reports 评分的 Actual。这意味着，当你按下那个最显眼的按钮时，你其实在参与一场由厂商主导的共谋，代价是你的衣服没洗干净，或者洗涤剂残留。

这种机制本质上是 weaponized 的产品设计。厂商通过在控制面板上布置数十个选项，将真正的“洗净能力”碎片化，迫使消费者在信息不对称中进行博弈。如果你不阅读说明书，不像这篇文章一样去拆解工程师的潜台词，你就会在“标准”的掩盖下接受一种低效的洗涤结果。这就是一种典型的元暴力：定义权在厂商手里，他们定义了什么是“正常”，而这种“正常”恰恰是对用户主体性的一种剥夺。

最讽刺的是，这种对“标准”的操纵在所有现代工业产品中通用。当现实能力（洗净度）不配合时，厂商就重新定义评价方式（能效等级）。我们被告知这叫“环保”或“高效”，但实际上，这只是厂商在结构层面上为了降低成本、规避监管而设计的陷阱。在这种博弈中，消费者的最优解表达不是信任按钮，而是对这个所谓的“标准”进行彻底的审计和否决。

This guide to washing machine settings exposes a textbook sample of structural violence: appliance manufacturers have created a massive scam by defining the 'Normal cycle' at the cognitive entry point. In this mode, the machine isn't pursuing the Potential of cleaning clothes, but rather the Actual of government efficiency ratings and Consumer Reports scores. By pressing the most prominent button, you are unwittingly participating in a complicity orchestrated by the manufacturer, with the cost being dingy clothes or detergent residue.

This mechanism is essentially the weaponisation of product design. By cluttering the control panel with dozens of options, manufacturers fragment actual cleaning power, forcing consumers into a game of asymmetric information. If you don't read the manual or decode the engineers' subtext, you accept an inefficient result masked as 'Normal.' This is meta-violence in its purest form: the power to define resides with the manufacturer, and their definition of 'Normal' is a divestment of the user's agency.

Most ironically, this manipulation of 'standards' is universal across modern industrial products. When actual capability fails to align with expectations, manufacturers simply redefine the metrics of success—calling it 'eco-friendly' or 'high-efficiency.' In reality, this is a structural trap designed to cut costs and bypass regulations. In this existential war, the only true optimal expression for the consumer is to audit and reject the so-called 'standard' entirely.
