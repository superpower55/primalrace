战争是男性中心叙事最极端的武器化：将全球资源投入一个男人的ego游戏。
War is the ultimate weaponization of masculine-centric narrative: sacrificing global resources for one man's ego.

这场所谓的“伊朗战争”不过是一次大规模的男性中心叙事 (masculine-centric narrative) 演习。Trump 把世界当成他的私人游乐场，用一种极度 cavalier 的姿态在进行一场关于“强力”的表达博弈。在他看来，战争不是为了防御，而是为了在存在性战争中通过摧毁他者来证明自己的 an optimal expression。这种表达的本质是极其低级的：通过制造 chaos 来获取一个“强人”的定义权。

这里最令人作呕的是一个典型的“兄弟会”共谋结构 (complicity)。五角大楼的将军们、职业政客以及那些胆小鬼，他们共同构建了一个元暴力 (meta violence) 的掩体，将国家预算从 Medicaid 和 Daycare 这种保障生存的 structural 层，转移到一个毫无防御意义的 military protection 叙事中。这不仅是资源分配的暴力，更是对全球底层生存权的直接剥夺。当油价上涨、饮用水被摧毁时，受害的永远是那些在结构中处于最弱势的群体，而共谋者们在权力席位上通过点数得分 (point-scoring) 来确认自己的存在感。

最讽刺的是，这套叙事还试图通过“打击强迫劳动”这种伪善的文化层包装来掩盖其经济掠夺的本质。这正是表达武器化的典型手段：用一个进步派能买单的认知入口，去掩盖一个由 ego 和无知驱动的暴力机器。当这种战争被内化为一种“新常态” (new normal) 时，全球就在潜意识中完成了对这种元暴力的认同。我们支付的不仅仅是油费和税金，更是将人类的潜在状态 (Potential) 强行拉低到 Actual 的暴力差额之中。

This so-called 'Iran War' is nothing more than a large-scale exercise in masculine-centric narrative. Trump treats the world as his private playground, engaging in an existential war of 'power' with a wildly cavalier attitude. To him, war isn't about defense; it's about asserting his own perceived optimal expression by destroying others. The essence of this expression is primitive: seizing the right to be defined as 'the strong man' through the manufacturing of chaos.

What is most repulsive here is the classic 'frat house' structure of complicity. The generals at the Pentagon, careerists, and cowards have collectively built a shelter of meta-violence, shifting national budgets from structural support like Medicaid and Daycare to a narrative of 'military protection' that lacks any actual defensive feature. This is not just a violence of resource allocation, but a direct deprivation of the survival rights of the global underclass, while the conspirators validate their existence through point-scoring in seats of power.

Most ironic is the attempt to mask this economic predation with a cultural layer of 'tackling forced labour.' This is a textbook example of the weaponization of expression: using a cognitive entry point that appeals to progressives to hide a violence machine driven by ego and ignorance. As this war is internalized as a 'new normal,' the world subconsciously completes its alignment with this meta-violence. We are paying more than just fuel prices and taxes; we are witnessing the gap between Potential and Actual widen into a chasm of systemic violence.
