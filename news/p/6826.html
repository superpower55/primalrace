外交是私事，账户也是Foreign Policy as a Family Business

国际 结构层 · 元暴力 Truthout ↗ 2026-11-02 § 链接
新闻摘要《名利场》刊出调查记者马修·科尔新书节选，称特朗普首任期内沙特与阿联酋领导人秘密向库什纳提供至少20亿美元并让他动用相关账户。库什纳否认全部指控，其律师要求撤稿并威胁起诉出版商。众议员拉斯金呼吁库什纳回避中东与乌克兰谈判，并提交他在政府任职期间可动用的银行账户清单。
The newsVanity Fair published an excerpt from Matthew Cole's new book alleging that during Trump's first term, Saudi and Emirati leaders secretly put at least $2 billion into bank accounts Kushner could access. Kushner denies all of it; his lawyers demanded a retraction and threatened to sue the publisher. Congressmember Jamie Raskin called on Kushner to recuse himself from Middle East and Ukraine negotiations and list the accounts he could access while in government.
当外交变成家务事，被卖掉的从来不是账户，是人。
When diplomacy becomes a family matter, what gets sold is never the account. It's people.

库什纳的律师说，没有那些账户。科尔说有五十多个信源、大量事实核查和法律审读。两边都在要求别人相信自己的证据，而真正的问题不在账户是否存在，在于一个没有经过参议院批准、没有留下会议记录、只对总统一人负责的女婿，为什么可以代表美国去和外国元首谈条件。

这不是腐败丑闻，这是治理结构被换成家谱。报道里科尔的说法很直接：特朗普第一任期把外交政策变成两个人——总统和他的女婿，没有备忘录，没有国务院的人在场，没人知道他们谈了什么。国务院、中情局被当成「深层政府」排除在外。这不是疏忽，是设计。设计的目的就是让解释权只留在一个房间里。

那个房间里谈的事情，报道写得很具体：让沙特和阿联酋出钱支持特朗普的政策，最初甚至包括在墨西哥修墙；后来这笔钱转向支持两国自己重塑中东——也门战争、搞掉卡塔尔埃米尔、支持利比亚东部的哈夫塔尔。2017年6月沙特和阿联酋封锁卡塔尔，美国政府其他人毫不知情，卡塔尔距离被推翻只差七十二小时。蒂勒森打电话让MBS收手，事情才停下。

然后是那栋楼。报道说，库什纳家族两次请卡塔尔人买下第五大道666号，卡塔尔人说这是糟糕的投资，不感兴趣。等到蒂勒森被解职，卡塔尔王室慌了，花几个月设计出一条可以否认的路径，买下那栋楼。几周后，特朗普对卡塔尔埃米尔换了说法，封锁解除。库什纳的律师否认他知情，也否认存在任何交换。报道没有给出这笔交易和封锁解除之间的直接因果，它只把时间线摆在那里。摆在那里就够了。

这一整套运作的代价写在一个诗人的名字上。哈比卜·萨耶格，阿联酋的编辑和知识分子，曾经是王室和政府的喉舌，支持过对卡塔尔的封锁。2019年8月他在自己报纸上写文章，说阿拉伯政府不该在补偿巴勒斯坦人之前与以色列关系正常化。几天后他半夜被从床上带走，三天后政府宣布他死亡，没有说明死因。科尔根据知情信源写的是：他被杀了，因为MBZ要向身边所有人发一个信号——所有人必须朝我选的方向划，那个方向就是和以色列的交易。八个月前是卡舒吉。

拉斯金的呼吁听起来是对的动作：回避，交出账户清单。但回避是一个人的姿态，清单是一份文件。科尔在访谈里说的一句话更值得记住：库什纳第二任期连官方身份都没有，监督比第一次还少。也就是说，要求他回避，前提是承认他本来就在那个位置上。这个前提本身就是问题。

报道里还有一个细节没被追问：贝塔账户由MBZ出资约十亿美元，汇到以色列，存在以色列央行，供库什纳「个人受益」。以色列央行是政府银行，不持有个人账户。这笔钱到底算什么，科尔自己说「有一些模糊」。模糊的部分，恰恰是拉斯金要的那份清单应该回答的。而清单交不交，由谁来核，报道没写。

Kushner's lawyers say the accounts didn't exist. Cole says fifty-plus sources, extensive fact-checking, legal vetting. Both sides want you to trust their evidence. The real question isn't whether the accounts existed. It's why a son-in-law with no Senate confirmation, no meeting notes, and no accountability except to the president could negotiate with foreign heads of state on America's behalf.

This isn't a corruption scandal. It's a governance structure replaced by a family tree. Cole puts it plainly: the first Trump term reduced foreign policy to two people, the president and his son-in-law. No memos. No State Department in the room. Nobody knows what was discussed. The State Department and CIA were treated as the deep state and shut out. That wasn't negligence. It was the design. The point of the design was to keep the interpretive authority in one room.

What was discussed in that room is specific in the reporting: Saudi and Emirati money to support Trump's policies, initially even the wall in Mexico; later the money shifted toward the two countries' own remaking of the Middle East — the war in Yemen, toppling Qatar's emir, backing Haftar in eastern Libya. In June 2017, Saudi Arabia and the UAE blockaded Qatar and the rest of the U.S. government had no idea. Qatar was seventy-two hours from being toppled. Tillerson called MBS and told him to stand down.

Then the building. The Kushners asked the Qataris twice to buy 666 Fifth Avenue. The Qataris said it was a terrible investment and passed. After Tillerson was fired, the Qatari royal family panicked, spent months designing a purchase with plausible deniability, and bought it. Weeks later, Trump's position on the blockade changed and the blockade was resolved. Kushner's lawyers deny he knew anything and deny any quid pro quo. The reporting doesn't claim direct causation. It just lays out the timeline. The timeline is enough.

The cost of this arrangement has a poet's name on it. Habib al-Sayegh, an Emirati editor and intellectual, once a mouthpiece for the royal family, had supported the Qatar blockade. In August 2019 he wrote that Arab governments shouldn't normalize with Israel until the Palestinians were compensated. Days later he was taken from his bed at night. Three days later the government announced his death without saying how. Cole reports, based on sources, that he was murdered — because MBZ wanted to send a message: everyone rows in the direction I choose, and that direction is the deal with Israel. Eight months earlier it was Khashoggi.

Raskin's call sounds like the right move: recuse, produce the list. But recusal is one man's gesture and a list is one document. Cole says something more important in the interview: in the second term Kushner doesn't even hold an official post, so there is even less oversight than before. Asking him to recuse assumes he was supposed to be there in the first place. That assumption is the problem.

One detail in the reporting goes unexamined. The Beta account, roughly a billion dollars from MBZ, was sent to Israel and held at the central bank of Israel for Kushner's personal benefit. The central bank is a government bank. It doesn't hold personal accounts. Cole himself says what 「personal benefit」 means is ambiguous. The ambiguous part is exactly what Raskin's list should answer. Whether the list gets produced, and who verifies it, the reporting doesn't say.
