用内战血腥味给橄榄球赛给观众打鸡血Using Civil War Blood to Spice Up a Football Game

哲学 文化层 · 元暴力 The Athletic ↗ 2026-09-11 § 链接
将历史创伤武器化为体育娱乐，是典型的文化暴力洗白。
Weaponizing historical trauma for sports entertainment is a textbook case of cultural violence laundering.

把内战时期的屠杀、纵火和奴隶制冲突，剪辑成一段 pregame hype video 来给橄榄球赛造势，这简直是 weaponization 的教科书级案例。Kansas 的数字部门非常聪明，他们知道单纯的校友荣誉不足以在 2026 年吸引 1600 万次点击，所以他们直接挖掘了历史的坟墓，把真实的 blood spilled 变成了体育竞技的 seasoning。

这段视频的叙事逻辑极其危险：它把极其复杂的结构性暴力（structural violence）——奴隶制与种族屠杀——简化成了某种“代代相传的仇恨” (hatred passed down through generations)。当 Gilbert Brown 在视频里宣称“这里不存在爱与仁慈”时，他实际上是在完成一次文化层面的 scrub。真实的战争创伤被转化为一种具有审美价值的“宿敌叙事”，让观众在屏幕前通过消费这种“深沉的仇恨”来获得某种存在性的快感。

最讽刺的是那个标题 “HEROES AND VILLAINS”。在真实的内战血腥冲突中，谁是英雄谁是恶棍是由权力定义并书写进正史的。而现在，这种定义权被交给了一个体育营销团队，用来为一场商业比赛增加戏剧冲突。这是一种典型的 meta violence：它告诉人们，历史上的死亡和毁灭只要能为当下的娱乐服务，就可以被重新定义为“激情”和“传统”。

当人们在 X 上为这段视频点赞时，他们共谋完成了一次对历史暴力的消解。真实的受害者消失了，只剩下两个吉祥物在绿茵场上扮演敌对角色。这种把战争叙事娱乐化的行为，本质上是在告诉我们：只要足够“酷”，血腥的历史也可以成为一个绝佳的营销入口。

Editing the massacres, arsons, and slavery conflicts of the Civil War into a pregame hype video for a football match is a textbook example of weaponization. Kansas's digital department is clever; they know school pride alone isn't enough to grab 16 million views in 2026, so they dig up the graves of history and use actual blood spilled as seasoning for a sporting event.

The narrative logic here is dangerous: it reduces structural violence—slavery and racial massacres—into a simplified "hatred passed down through generations." When Gilbert Brown claims that "love and mercy don't exist here," he is performing a cultural scrub. Real historical trauma is converted into an aestheticized "rivalry narrative," allowing the audience to experience an existential thrill by consuming this "deep hatred."

The most ironic part is the title "HEROES AND VILLAINS." In the actual bloodbath of the Civil War, who was the hero and who was the villain was defined by power and written into official history. Now, that power of definition is handed over to a sports marketing team to add drama to a commercial game. This is meta violence: it tells us that death and destruction in history can be redefined as "passion" and "tradition" as long as it serves current entertainment.

As people like this video on X, they become complicit in the erasure of historical violence. The real victims vanish, leaving only two mascots playing the role of enemies on a green field. This entertainment of war narratives essentially tells us that as long as it's "cool" enough, a bloody history can be turned into a perfect marketing entry point.
