婚姻证婚人的觉醒：从浪漫爱共谋到安全边界的撤退The Celebrant's Awakening: From Romantic Complicity to the Border of Safety

性别 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-17 § 链接
当浪漫爱的仪式感成为暴力结构的掩体，离开即是唯一的最优解。
When the ritual of romantic love becomes a shield for structural violence, exit is the only optimal expression.

一个职业证婚人的职业倦怠，本质上是一次关于“表达”与“真实”的认知脱壳。这篇文章最锋利的地方在于，它拆穿了婚姻作为一种 institution 的伪装：那些在厨房桌边谈论的“个人故事”，最终在法律文书的签署下，变成了许多女性难以逃离的 structural violence 囚笼。

婚姻在文化层被 weaponized 成一种“里程碑”或“传统”，但这种叙事在实际操作中往往是利男的。证婚人发现自己签署的不仅仅是爱情的见证，更是给 intimate partner violence 加上了一把法律锁。当“爱”被定义为一种必须通过制度固化的契约时，它就从一种情感表达变成了对 autonomy 的掠夺。在这种结构里，所谓的“浪漫爱”不过是掩盖权力不对等的一种文化暴力。

而作者自身的转型——从扮演一个符合体制要求的证婚人，到决定用 Crocs 和 overalls 迎接真实生活——是一次典型的真.最优解表达。在必须使用 birth name 工作的体制中，每一次关于“真诚”的宣誓都是一场对自我的背叛。当他意识到自己的 values 与这个以“传统”为名的共谋场域不可调和时，选择 back away 不是职业放弃，而是主体性的回归。

好新闻在于，一个掌握认知入口的执行者公开承认：制度化的爱如果不能包含 safety 和 freedom to leave，那么它就是一种 scam。

The professional burnout of a marriage celebrant is, in essence, a cognitive shedding of 'expression' and 'truth.' The sharpest point of this narrative is the exposure of marriage as an institution: those 'personal stories' shared around kitchen tables eventually transform, through legal paperwork, into structural violence cages from which many women cannot escape.

Marriage has been weaponized at the cultural layer as a 'milestone' or 'tradition,' but in practice, this narrative is predominantly masculine-centric. The celebrant realized they weren't just witnessing love, but signing a legal lock on intimate partner violence. When 'love' is defined as a contract that must be institutionalized, it shifts from an expression of emotion to a predation of autonomy. In this structure, so-called 'romantic love' is merely cultural violence used to mask power imbalances.

The author's own transition—from playing a role that fit the system's requirements to embracing a life of Crocs and overalls—is a textbook case of a true optimal expression. In a system where they were forced to use their birth name, every vow of 'truth' was a betrayal of the self. Once they realized their values were irreconcilable with this field of complicity masked as 'tradition,' backing away was not a professional failure, but a reclamation of subjectivity.

The good_news here is that an executor who held the cognitive entry point has publicly admitted: if institutionalized love does not include safety and the freedom to leave, it is a scam.
