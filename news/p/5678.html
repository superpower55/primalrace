国际 文化层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-20 § 链接

JD Vance 把非人化叙事 (dehumanizing rhetoric) 称为“不完美的措辞” (imperfect rhetoric)，这是一个典型的文化暴力 scam。在元暴力的逻辑里，通过定义某些群体为“异类”或“威胁”——比如诬陷海地移民吃宠物——来夺取认知入口，这从来不是因为 sloppy word choice，而是一次精准的武器化表达。其目的在于将特定人群从“人”的范畴中剔除，从而为接下来的结构性剥夺和直接暴力铺平道路。

当施暴者在获得权力后用“道歉”来掩盖策略时，他实际上是在进行第二次博弈：通过扮演一个“意识到错误的文明人”，来稀释之前通过制造仇恨而获得的政治红利。这种“道歉”本身就是一种假.最优解表达，它试图在不放弃权力结构的前提下，换取一种道德上的合法性。Jon Stewart 撕掉这张皮的行为是对的，因为在这种叙事里，所谓的“措辞”就是暴力本身。

而更讽刺的是，这种将他人客体化的逻辑在权力内部同样通用。从五天就下岗的新闻秘书到被特朗普认为“太腐败”而开除的大使，这些人在权力机器中仅仅是可消耗的零件。当一个人被定义为工具，他的存在性就被让渡给了权力中心。无论是被妖魔化的移民，还是被随意抛弃的共谋者，他们共同证明了：在男性中心叙事的权力游戏中，只有掌控解释权的人才是“人”，其余皆为耗材。

JD Vance labeling his dehumanizing rhetoric as "imperfect" is a classic cultural violence scam. In the logic of meta-violence, defining certain groups as "others" or "threats"—such as the lie about Haitian immigrants eating pets—is never about sloppy word choice; it is a precise weaponization of expression. The goal is to strip these people of their humanity, clearing the path for structural deprivation and direct violence.

When the aggressor uses an "apology" after securing power, they are engaging in a second game: by performing the role of a "civilized man who realized his mistake," they attempt to dilute the political dividends gained from inciting hate. This apology is a fake optimal expression—a performance designed to gain moral legitimacy without dismantling the oppressive power structure. Jon Stewart is right to tear this mask off, because in this context, the "rhetoric" is the violence itself.

Ironically, this logic of objectification is universal within the power machine. From the press secretary who lasted five days to the ambassador fired for being "too corrupt" for Trump, these individuals are merely disposable parts. Once defined as a tool, one's existence is surrendered to the center of power. Whether it is the demonized immigrant or the discarded complicity partner, they all prove the same point: in the masculine-centric game of power, only those who control the interpretation are "human"; the rest are just consumables.
