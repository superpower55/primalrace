网络空间：父权制的数字化殖民地Cyberspace: The Digital Colony of Patriarchy

性别 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-22 § 链接
当暴力成为“参与成本”，这意味着系统在共谋地消费女性。
When violence becomes a 'cost of entry,' the system is complicit in consuming women.

所谓的“网络暴力”从来不是什么随机的个体行为，而是一场精准的数字化殖民。Jane 的经历是典型的 structural violence：私密照片被窃取、散播，而她求助的两个权力节点——警察和平台——迅速地通过一种“礼貌的冷漠”完成了共谋。警察告诉她“研究照片去向可能违法”，这简直是教科书级别的 victim-blaming，将施暴者的权力延伸到了受害者的防御行为之上。

Refuge 报告中提到的“接受风险” (accepted risk) 是最令人作呕的叙事。当 misogyny 成为进入公共空间的门票，这本质上是元暴力 (meta violence) 的数字化升级：男性中心叙事定义了互联网的底层逻辑——女性在这里是作为“被凝视的客体”而存在的，因此，当客体被损坏、被亵渎时，系统认为这只是某种“正常的损耗”。

最讽刺的是 Ofcom 的“自愿指南”和平台的“快速下架”承诺。这种表演性让步 (performative concession) 掩盖了核心事实：这些平台通过算法喂养仇恨来获取流量，它们在商业逻辑上是 misogyny 的受益者。只要这种“认知入口”依然由男性掌控，任何所谓的 safety guidance 都只是给殖民地发了几张毫无意义的安全传单。

Jane 最终通过律师事务所 Leigh Day 强行撕开了一个口子，这证明了在当前的结构中，正义不是被“赋予”的，而是被“赢”回来的。但一个必须面对的刺是：如果一个女性需要雇佣昂贵的律师才能让警察重新睁眼，那么绝大多数处于 structural 弱势的女性，她们的主体性在被 Doxx 的那一刻就已经被系统集体处决了。

What we call 'online abuse' is never a series of random individual acts; it is a precise digital colonization. Jane's experience is a textbook case of structural violence: her intimate photos were stolen and weaponized, and the two power nodes she turned to—the police and the platforms—completed their complicity through a 'polite indifference.' The police warning her that researching her own photos might be illegal is a masterclass in victim-blaming, extending the perpetrator's power over the victim's own defense.

The 'accepted risk' mentioned in the Refuge report is the most nauseating part of the narrative. When misogyny becomes the ticket to enter public spaces, it is a digital upgrade of meta violence: the masculine-centric narrative defines the underlying logic of the internet—women exist here as 'objects to be gazed upon.' Therefore, when the object is damaged or desecrated, the system views it as 'normal wear and tear.'

The most ironic part is Ofcom's 'voluntary guidance' and the platforms' promises of 'fast takedown.' This performative concession masks the core fact: these platforms profit from hatred via algorithms; they are the commercial beneficiaries of misogyny. As long as these cognitive entries are controlled by men, any safety guidance is merely handing out meaningless leaflets to the colonized.

Jane eventually forced a breach through the law firm Leigh Day, proving that in the current structure, justice is not 'granted'—it is 'won.' But the thorn we must face is this: if a woman needs an expensive lawyer just to make the police open their eyes, then for the vast majority of structurally disadvantaged women, their subjectivity is collectively executed by the system the moment they are doxed.
