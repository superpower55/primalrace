内塔尼亚胡的表演艺术与加沙的血肉成本Netanyahu's Performance Art and the Flesh Cost of Gaza

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-10 § 链接
政治博弈的“最优解”表达，往往建立在对他者生存权的绝对剥夺之上。
The 'optimal expression' of political gambling is often built upon the absolute deprivation of others' right to exist.

内塔尼亚胡在公开拒绝美国方案与私下讨论撤军之间的反复横跳，是一场教科书级别的“假.最优解表达”。他试图在极右翼盟友的生存压力与特朗普的霸权意志之间，通过操纵叙事来维持自己的权力席位。对他而言，加沙的和平协议不是一个关于人权的议题，而是一个关于“票数”的筹码。当他公开向国内极右翼表演强硬时，他是在通过 weaponization 认知入口来确保自己的政治生命。

这场博弈中最令人作呕的共谋，在于以色列军方与政客之间那种心照不宣的默契：在私下会议中精准划定撤军边界，在公众面前则将对方视为敌人。这种 structural violence 的精髓在于，无论谁在权力顶端，加沙的平民都被客体化为单纯的“地理坐标”或“谈判筹码”。所谓的“风险”，在内塔尼亚胡看来是失去白宫的信任，而在加沙的孩子看来，则是急性营养不良和随时落下的炸弹。

这再次证明了元暴力（meta violence）的运作逻辑：男性中心叙事将战争定义为一种关于“国家荣誉”和“地缘博弈”的英雄主义剧本。在这个剧本里，具体的死亡被抽象成统计数字，而政治家的生存焦虑被包装成国家战略。当内塔尼亚胡在白宫低调现身、在媒体前大放厥词时，他其实是在执行一套极其高效的共谋机制——用底层种族的血肉，去填补权力精英博弈时的差额。

Netanyahu's erratic oscillation between publicly rejecting the US deal and privately discussing military withdrawal is a textbook case of 'fake optimal expression.' He is attempting to maintain his power seat by manipulating narratives, balancing the survival pressure from far-right allies against the hegemonic will of Donald Trump. To him, a peace deal in Gaza is not a human rights issue; it is a bargaining chip for votes. By performing toughness for his domestic base, he is using the weaponisation of cognitive entry points to secure his own political existence.

The most sickening complicity here is the tacit understanding between the Israeli military and its politicians: precisely delineating withdrawal boundaries in private while painting the other side as an enemy in public. The essence of this structural violence is that regardless of who holds power, the civilians of Gaza are objectified as mere 'geographic coordinates' or 'negotiation chips.' The 'risk' for Netanyahu is losing the White House's trust; for the children of Gaza, it is acute malnutrition and imminent bombing.

This once again proves the logic of meta violence: the masculine-centric narrative defines war as a heroic script of 'national honor' and 'geopolitical gambling.' In this script, concrete deaths are abstracted into statistics, and the survival anxiety of politicians is packaged as national strategy. While Netanyahu makes a low-profile visit to the White House and shouts defiance to the media, he is executing a highly efficient mechanism of complicity—using the flesh and blood of the Primal Race to fill the gap in the gambling of power elites.
