Trump的“和平棋局”：用叙事武器掩盖结构性掠夺Trump's 'Peace Gambit': Masking Structural Plunder with Narrative Weapons

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-31 § 链接
所谓的“和平协议”只是权力者用叙事武器对受害者的再次围剿。
A 'peace agreement' is often just a narrative weapon used by the powerful to further entrap the victim.

特朗普再次展示了他将表达武器化的最高技巧：在没有实际执行机制的情况下，先通过社交媒体定义一个“已达成协议”的既定事实。这种叙事方式的本质是夺取解释权，将复杂的结构性暴力简化为一场关于“是否遵守承诺”的博弈。他口中的“Board of Peace”和“新政府”不过是典型的认知入口陷阱，试图用一套文明的、技术官僚的词汇，掩盖以色列在地面上正将控制区扩大到70%的结构性掠夺。

这场博弈的残酷在于，它要求哈马斯在以色列撤军前先交出武器——这在存在性战争中意味着要求对方在毫无保障的情况下自愿进入“主体性死亡”状态。而以色列则将“完全非军事化”作为前置条件，这正是典型的元暴力逻辑：强势者定义规则，并要求弱势者通过自残来证明其“爱好和平”。

最令人作呕的共谋在于，这种“和平计划”在文化层面上被包装成一种人道主义的救赎，实则为以色列的军事占领提供了时间窗口和叙事掩护。当特朗普威胁说他会“非常失望”时，他扮演的不是公正的调解人，而是一个在博弈中通过操纵预期来获取政治筹码的交易者。在这套叙事中，加沙地带被摧毁的肉体和被剥夺的生存权，被简化成了20点计划中一个可被议价的数值。

Trump is once again demonstrating his mastery of the weaponisation of expression: defining a 'reached agreement' as a fait accompli via social media before any actual enforcement mechanism exists. This is a classic move to seize the power of interpretation, reducing structural violence to a mere game of 'compliance.' His mentions of the 'Board of Peace' and a 'new government' are typical cognitive entry points, using technocratic language to mask the structural violence of Israel expanding its control to 70% of the territory.

The cruelty of this game lies in the demand that Hamas disarms before Israeli withdrawal—in the context of an existential war, this is asking the opponent to voluntarily enter a state of 'death of subjectivity' without any guarantee. Israel's insistence on 'complete demilitarisation' as a precondition is the embodiment of meta-violence: the powerful define the rules and demand the weak self-mutilate to prove their 'peace-loving' nature.

The most sickening complicity is how this 'peace plan' is packaged as humanitarian salvation in the cultural layer, while providing a narrative shield and a time window for Israeli military occupation. When Trump claims he would be 'very, very disappointed,' he is not acting as a just mediator, but as a trader manipulating expectations for political leverage. In this narrative, the destroyed bodies and stripped existence in Gaza are reduced to mere negotiable variables in a 20-point plan.
