用“环保”掩盖的能源殖民与共谋游戏Energy Colonialism and the Game of Complicity

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-17 § 链接
所谓能源安全是元暴力的叙事陷阱，真正的博弈在出口利润与气候代价之间。
Energy security is a meta-violence trap; the real game is between export profits and climate costs.

这出戏的剧本很经典：一边是200多位艺术家用道德感和气候焦虑在公共空间呐喊，另一边是政府用“能源安全”和“就业岗位”这种标准的 masculine-centric narrative 来对冲。但在这种对立的叙事之下，隐藏着一个最阴险的 structural violence：Rosebank 油田的大部分原油将被 export，这意味着所谓的“国家资产”实际上是全球资本的提款机，而英国民众承担的是 2.5 亿吨 CO2 带来的气候崩溃。

政府口中的“能源安全”是一个典型的 weaponized concept。它把一个纯粹的商业利润问题，包装成一种保护性的、父权式的“安全感”叙事。这和“保护女性”的逻辑如出一辙——通过制造一个虚拟的威胁，从而合法化对环境的掠夺和对未来的抵押。Adura 公司承诺的 3500 个就业岗位，在 2.5 亿吨二氧化碳的潜在破坏力面前，不过是给底层劳动者的一颗廉价糖果，诱导他们成为这起生态犯罪的共谋者。

有趣的是，环保主义者的抗争虽然在 cultural 层面上撕开了口子，但如果仅仅停留在“呼吁”和“写信”，这依然是在既定规则内寻求最优解的表达。真正的博弈不在于首相是否“良心发现”，而在于谁在垄断对“现实”的定义权。当政府将化石燃料定义为“vital national asset”时，它已经在元暴力层面完成了对未来的殖民。

The script is classic: 200+ musicians shouting with moral urgency in the public square, while the government counters with a standard masculine-centric narrative of 'energy security' and 'jobs.' Beneath this clash lies a sinister structural violence: most of the oil from Rosebank will be exported. This means the so-called 'national asset' is actually an ATM for global capital, while the public inherits the climate collapse of 250 million tonnes of CO2.

'Energy security' is a textbook weaponized concept. It repackages a pure profit motive into a protective, paternalistic narrative of 'safety.' This mirrors the logic of 'protecting women'—creating a virtual threat to justify the plunder of the environment and the mortgaging of the future. The 3,500 jobs promised by Adura are merely cheap candies for the working class, coaxing them to become complicit in this ecological crime.

Interestingly, while environmentalists have torn a hole in the cultural layer, merely 'urging' and 'writing letters' remains an optimal expression within a rigged system. The real battle isn't whether the PM has a 'change of heart,' but who monopolizes the power to define 'reality.' By defining fossil fuels as a 'vital national asset,' the state has already completed the colonization of the future at the meta-violence level.
