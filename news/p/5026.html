伦敦房东的“鼠政”：关于结构性暴力的幽默掩体The 'Rat-Politics' of London Landlords: Humor as a Shield for Structural Violence

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-15 § 链接
当生存危机被写成幽默随笔，结构性暴力就完成了伪装。
When survival crises are written as humorous essays, structural violence completes its camouflage.

这篇文章读起来像个荒诞的都市喜剧，但如果你把视角从“老鼠飞向脸庞”的惊悚感中抽离，你会发现这是一个标准的 structural violence 现场。在伦敦 Hackney 和 Brixton 这种地段，租客面对的是一个极其简单的权力博弈：房东垄断了居住权，而租客在支付高额租金的同时，却在物理意义上被抛弃在鼠患的地狱里。

最令人不安的不是老鼠的“军事演习”，而是文中那句轻描淡写的“Naturally, our landlord did nothing about any of this”。这个 Naturally 就是元暴力的体现——在一个极度倾斜的租赁市场中，房东的无能与冷漠被内化成了某种“自然规律”。租客在文中用 Pixar 和 Ratatouille 这种 pop culture 的叙事来消解恐惧，这实际上是一种生存策略，但这种幽默感恰恰成了房东的掩体：当受害者开始用段子讲述苦难，结构性的盘剥就变成了个体的“倒霉经历”。

从加尔通的暴力三角来看，这里的 direct 层是老鼠的袭击，但真正的暴力在 structural 层：法律与市场的共谋让房东可以心安理得地无视卫生危机，只要租客还在支付租金。而这种现状被包装成“伦敦生活的一部分”这种 cultural 叙事，最终完成了对暴力的合法化。这种“鼠政”背后，是资本对生存底线最彻底的定价权掌控。

This piece reads like an absurd urban comedy, but if you strip away the thrill of 'rats flying at faces,' you'll find a textbook case of structural violence. In areas like Hackney and Brixton, the power game is simple: landlords monopolize housing, while tenants pay exorbitant rents only to be physically abandoned in a rodent hellscape.

The most disturbing part isn't the 'rodent military exercise,' but the casual phrase: 'Naturally, our landlord did nothing about any of this.' This 'Naturally' is the manifestation of meta-violence—in a severely skewed rental market, the landlord's incompetence and indifference are internalized as a 'law of nature.' The tenants use pop culture references like Pixar and Ratatouille to dilute their terror, a survival strategy that inadvertently provides a shield for the landlord: when victims turn their suffering into anecdotes, structural exploitation is rebranded as individual 'bad luck.'

Applying the Violence Triangle, the direct layer is the rat attacks, but the true violence resides in the structural layer: a complicity between law and market that allows landlords to ignore hygiene crises as long as rent is paid. This reality is then wrapped in a cultural narrative of 'just London life,' ultimately legitimizing the violence. This 'rat-politics' is nothing more than the absolute control of pricing power over the baseline of human existence.
