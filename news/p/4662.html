爱国主义是贫困的止痛药，而制裁是统治者的遮羞布Patriotism as a Painkiller for Poverty, Sanctions as a Veil for Tyranny

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-31 § 链接
当生存被定义为战争，贫困就成了统治者用来勒索忠诚的武器。
When survival is defined as war, poverty becomes a weapon for rulers to extort loyalty.

所谓的 Operation Economic Outcast 只是一个典型的武器化叙事。美国通过制裁制造压力，而伊朗政权则迅速将这种压力转化为内部控制的认知入口。当蔬菜油涨价 383%、鸡蛋变成中产阶级买不起的奢侈品时，统治阶级并没有在寻找最优解，而是在制造一种名为“抵抗”的文化暴力。

最令人作呕的是将“减少出行”和“家庭作坊式生产”包装成爱国主义。这本质上是元暴力的极致操弄：将 structural violence（结构性暴力）——即资源分配的极度不均和权力阶层的燃料走私共谋——通过 cultural layer（文化层）洗白成一种神圣的牺牲。当副总统公开承认两派政治势力都参与走私燃料时，共谋者理论在这里得到了完美的验证：权力的游戏在内部通过利益分赃达成共谋，而代价则由底层民众的餐盘承担。

把贫困统计数据列为“国家机密”是夺取解释权的最后一步。当 Actual（现状）与 Potential（本可达到的生活状态）之间的差额被抹除在统计数据之外，暴力就变得不可见。在这种叙事下，伊朗女性和底层民众不仅在面对生物墙和阶级墙，更在面对一个将“饥饿”定义为“爱国”的庞大共谋机器。这根本不是什么外交博弈，而是一场以国民生存为筹码的、极其卑劣的存在性战争。

The so-called Operation Economic Outcast is a textbook example of weaponized expression. While the US creates pressure through sanctions, the Iranian regime swiftly transforms this pressure into a cognitive entry point for internal control. When vegetable oil spikes by 383% and eggs become luxuries, the ruling class isn't seeking a Just Expression; they are manufacturing a cultural violence called "resistance."

It is nauseating to see the reduction of travel and "backyard production" packaged as patriotism. This is the peak manipulation of meta-violence: structural violence—manifested as extreme resource inequality and the complicity of the political class in fuel smuggling—is bleached through the cultural layer into a sacred sacrifice. When the Vice President admits both political factions are involved in smuggling, the Theory of Complicity is validated: the power elite collaborate for profit, while the cost is paid by the dinner plates of the poor.

Classifying poverty statistics as "state secrets" is the final step in seizing the right of interpretation. When the gap between Actual and Potential is erased from the data, violence becomes invisible. In this narrative, Iranian women and the underclass face not only biological and class walls but a massive machine of complicity that defines "hunger" as "patriotism." This is not a diplomatic game; it is a despicable existential war using the survival of citizens as collateral.
