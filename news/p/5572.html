色情诱饵、药物与生命：一场关于“客体化”的共谋犯罪Sexual Bait, Drugs, and Death: A Conspiracy of Objectification

性别 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-15 § 链接
当女性被武器化为诱饵，她既是施暴的共谋，也是结构性暴力的零件。
When women are weaponized as bait, they are both accomplices in violence and cogs in a structural machine.

一个典型的 weaponized expression 案例：利用“性”作为认知入口，将游客诱入一个精心设计的陷阱。Danette Colbert 和她的同伙并非简单的抢劫犯，而是在运行一套成熟的、基于性别刻板印象的 scam。他们精准地捕捉到了男性对“性服务”的渴望，将这种渴望转化为一个致命的入口，随后通过药物剥夺受害者的主体性，将其彻底客体化为可被掠夺的资源。

在这场犯罪中，Colbert 扮演的是一个极其阴险的共谋者。她利用社会对女性身体的性化认知，将自己的表达武器化，把自己伪装成一个“可消费的客体”来诱捕猎物。这种行为本质上是对性别博弈的极端扭曲——她并不在追求什么公正的表达，而是在父权结构的阴影下，利用结构性弱势者被物化的特质，反向地、恶意地操纵那些认为自己是“消费者”的男性。

好新闻的部分在于，司法系统这次没有陷入“女性犯罪者”的柔弱叙事陷阱。Judge Nancy Miller 将其定为 habitual offender 并判处终身监禁，这在 direct 层面上削减了暴力。但更深层的 structural violence 依然存在：一个运行了十余年的犯罪企业能在新奥尔良的 French Quarter 如此肆无忌惮地运作，说明当地的治安结构与监管存在巨大的空洞。

Adan Manzano 的死是一个悲剧，但这个案件揭示了一个更残酷的事实：在元暴力的世界里，无论是被诱骗的受害者，还是被雇佣/利用的女性诱饵，都被简化成了某种功能性的零件。当性被彻底武器化，它就不再是表达，而是一把锁死生还可能的钥匙。

A textbook case of weaponized expression: using 'sex' as a cognitive entry point to lure tourists into a meticulously designed trap. Danette Colbert and her accomplices weren't just robbers; they were running a sophisticated scam based on gender stereotypes. They precisely captured the male desire for 'sexual services,' transforming that desire into a lethal portal, then using drugs to strip the victims of their subjectivity, reducing them to objectified resources for plunder.

In this crime, Colbert acted as a sinister co-conspirator. She weaponized her expression by utilizing the social sexualization of the female body, disguising herself as a 'consumable object' to trap prey. This is a perverse distortion of the existential game—she wasn't seeking a Just Expression, but was instead leveraging the objectification inherent in the masculine-centric narrative to maliciously manipulate men who believed they were the 'consumers.'

The good_news here is that the judicial system didn't fall into the 'fragile female offender' narrative. Judge Nancy Miller's decision to label her a habitual offender and sentence her to life reduces violence at the direct level. However, the structural violence remains: the fact that a criminal enterprise could operate so brazenly in the French Quarter for over a decade reveals massive voids in local security and regulation.

Adan Manzano's death is a tragedy, but the case reveals a grimmer truth: in a world governed by meta-violence, both the deceived victim and the female bait are reduced to functional parts. When sex is fully weaponized, it ceases to be expression and becomes a key that locks the door to survival.
