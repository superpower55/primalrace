CBS的“专业主义”：一种完美的共谋表演CBS's 'Professionalism': A Perfect Performance of Complicity

哲学 文化层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-15 § 链接
用“专业主义”掩盖权力更迭，是典型的元暴力叙事操纵。
Using 'professionalism' to mask power shifts is a classic meta-violence narrative manipulation.

Sir Trevor Phillips 在 CBS 内部会议上的那句 “fuck off”，不是在捍卫新闻自由，而是在为权力的更迭举行一场名为“专业主义”的洗礼。当亿万富翁 David Ellison 将 Bari Weiss 这种带有强烈意识形态色彩的操盘手安插为总编时，CBS 的 editorial scales 已经完成了物理意义上的倾斜。此时，Phillips 抛出“客观性” (objectivity) 和“互相信任” (having each other's backs) 这套话术，本质上是在构建一个封闭的共谋场域。

这种叙事极其阴险：它将编辑层对员工的恐吓（不服从即开除）包装成“组织常识”，将外部对意识形态偏移的质疑定义为“试图吓唬我们”的干扰。在这种逻辑下，任何试图讨论新闻独立性的员工都被定义为“脆弱”的，因为他们没有进入这个所谓的“新游戏” (new game)。这正是典型的 cultural violence——通过重新定义“专业”和“忠诚”，让结构性的权力压迫看起来像是团队精神。

Phillips 所谓的“新游戏”，其实就是夺取解释权的战争。当一个组织开始用“别管我们怎么做，让我们干活”来回应关于公正性的质疑时，它实际上是在宣布：解释权已经易主，而内部的共谋者必须通过闭嘴来换取在这个新结构中的生存票据。这根本不是在做新闻，而是在用新闻业的皮囊，为资本的意志提供一个名为“客观”的掩体。

Sir Trevor Phillips's blunt "fuck off" at the CBS internal meeting isn't a defense of press freedom; it's a baptism of 'professionalism' for a power coup. When billionaire David Ellison installed Bari Weiss—a figure of intense ideological bias—as editor-in-chief, the editorial scales of CBS were physically tilted. Now, Phillips deploys concepts like "objectivity" and "having each other's backs" to construct a closed field of complicity.

This narrative is insidious: it repackages the leadership's threats (disobeying equals firing) as "common sense," and frames external concerns about ideological drift as mere "interference" intended to frighten them. In this logic, any staffer questioning editorial independence is labeled "vulnerable" for failing to play the "new game." This is textbook cultural violence—redefining "professionalism" and "loyalty" to make structural oppression look like team spirit.

Phillips's "new game" is actually a war for the monopoly of interpretation. When an organization responds to questions of integrity by saying "just let us do our jobs," it is announcing that the power to define truth has shifted. The internal co-conspirators must now trade their silence for a ticket to survive in this new structure. This isn't journalism; it's using the facade of the industry to provide a cover of "objectivity" for the will of capital.
