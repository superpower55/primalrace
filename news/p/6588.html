公投从未举行，维和已经打包The Referendum Never Happened; the Peacekeepers Are Packing

国际 结构层 · 文化层 NPR ↗ 2026-11-02 § 链接
一场从未兑现的自决公投，最后以清点装备收场
A self-determination vote that never took place ends with an inventory of equipment.

MINURSO 的名字里写着 Referendum。1991 年停火协议为它铺路，公投的选项里包含独立。三十五年过去，公投没有举行，维和先结束了。11 月 1 日起进入清算期，剩下的工作是把他国部队的资产运回各自国家，把联合国的装备妥善处置。一个以公投命名的任务，最后做的是搬家。

这次终止的直接触发点不在西撒哈拉，在纽约。美国起草的决议支持摩洛哥对西撒哈拉的主权要求，并把任务再延一年，俄罗斯否决；俄罗斯的对案要延长任务，被投下去。两份决议互相否决，任务就这么断了。摩洛哥王宫欢迎这个结果，说它是把争端国际化的工具。对拉巴特来说，一个不产生主权归属的维和机制，本来就不如没有。

真正被清点掉的是自决。选民名单的分歧——摩洛哥要把自己控制区内的撒哈拉人算进去，波利萨里奥坚持用西班牙殖民时期的人口普查——三十五年没有解决。名单定不下来，公投就永远在“下一步”。这不是执行的失败，这是设计的成功：一个把争议无限期挂起的机制，对控制着几乎全部领土的一方最有利。摩洛哥称这里是它的南部省份，华盛顿、巴黎、伦敦一个接一个跟上。维和部队撤走之后，地图上不会少任何东西，只是那块“非自治领土”的标签更没人念了。

报道里写到了代价，写得很轻。1975 年摩洛哥吞并之后，武装冲突持续十五年以上，双方死亡超过一万六千人（来源：法国参议院）。二十名 MINURSO 人员死在任务里。2020 年波利萨里奥退出停火，此后是低烈度敌对。现在连观察这些敌对的人都没有了。联合国秘书长说他对整个领土的安全局势和敌对升级的风险保持关切，呼吁各方最大限度克制——这句话在没有任何人在场记录的时候，是一种修辞。

还有一层没有人去算：这片土地上的人怎么办。撒哈拉人分散在摩洛哥控制区、阿尔及利亚廷杜夫附近的难民营、以及毛里塔尼亚边境，自决公投拖了三十五年，一整代人是在“等待公投”的状态里长大的。他们的身份、国籍、财产权、行动自由，全都挂在一个没有发生的事件上。维和任务的预算是 2026-2027 年度六千二百万美元出头，编制五百三十四人，主要出兵国是埃及、加纳、巴基斯坦。这笔钱花完，账就结了。而自决的账，从来没有进过任何一张资产负债表。

最讽刺的是这次结束的方式。不是谈判破裂，不是任务完成，是两个安理会常任理事国各自否决对方的草案，把一块领土的未来当成彼此较劲的筹码，然后同时收手。摩洛哥和阿尔及利亚的对抗会继续，报道说这可能加剧军备竞赛。军备竞赛有预算、有合同、有统计。撒哈拉人的自决没有。

清点装备的队伍会按时把车辆和器材运回去。至于那个 1991 年就该举行、至今没有举行的投票——没有人需要为它写一份清算报告。

MINURSO's name contains the word Referendum. The 1991 ceasefire paved the way for it, and independence was among the options. Thirty-five years later, the vote has not happened and the peacekeeping mission is over. From November 1 the liquidation period begins; the remaining work is transporting troop-contributing countries' assets home and disposing of UN equipment. A mission named after a referendum ends by moving furniture.

The trigger was not in Western Sahara but in New York. A US draft supported Morocco's sovereignty claim and would have extended the mission another year; Russia vetoed it. A rival Russian resolution to extend the mission was voted down. Two drafts canceled each other, and the mandate died. Morocco's royal palace welcomed the non-renewal, calling the mission an instrument to internationalize the dispute. For Rabat, a mechanism that produces no sovereign outcome was never better than nothing.

What actually got liquidated is self-determination. The voter-list dispute—Morocco wanted Sahrawis in the territory it controls included, the Polisario insisted on Spain's colonial census—went unresolved for thirty-five years. With the list unsettled, the referendum stays permanently in the next step. This is not a failure of implementation; it is a success of design. A mechanism that suspends the question indefinitely favors the party administering nearly all the territory. Morocco calls it its southern provinces, and Washington, Paris and London have followed one after another. When the blue helmets leave, nothing disappears from the map—only the "non-self-governing territory" label gets read by fewer people.

The article does record the cost, lightly. After Morocco annexed the territory in 1975, armed conflict lasted more than fifteen years and claimed over 16,000 lives on both sides (source: French Senate). Twenty MINURSO personnel died in the mission. The Polisario withdrew from the ceasefire in 2020, and low-intensity hostilities have continued since. Now no one is left to observe them. The UN secretary-general says he remains concerned about the security situation and the risk of escalation and calls on all concerned to exercise maximum restraint—a sentence that is rhetorical when nobody is on the ground to record anything.

One ledger nobody bothers to balance: what happens to the people. Sahrawis live in Moroccan-controlled territory, in the refugee camps near Tindouf in Algeria, along the Mauritanian border. A whole generation has grown up in the posture of waiting for a vote. Their status, citizenship, property, freedom of movement all hang on an event that has not occurred. The mission's 2026-2027 budget was more than $62 million, authorized for 534 personnel, with Egypt, Ghana and Pakistan as top troop contributors. When that money is spent, the account closes. The account of self-determination was never entered on any balance sheet.

The way it ended is the sharpest part. Not a breakdown of talks, not mission accomplished—two permanent members of the Security Council vetoing each other's drafts, treating a territory's future as a chip in their own standoff, then both walking away. The Morocco-Algeria rivalry continues, and the article notes the end of the mission risks fueling an arms race. Arms races have budgets, contracts, statistics. Sahrawi self-determination has none.

The liquidation team will ship the vehicles and equipment back on schedule. As for the vote that was supposed to happen in 1991 and never did—no one has to file a liquidation report for that.
