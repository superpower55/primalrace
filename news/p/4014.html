从“大脑是根鸡巴”到“学习女权”：摇滚乐手的存在性补课From 'Dick for a Brain' to 'Learning Feminism': A Rocker's Existential Remedial Class

性别 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-17 § 链接
所谓的“学习女权”本质上是男性在意识到共谋成本后的一次主体性补课。
'Learning feminism' for men is essentially an existential remedial class after realizing the cost of their complicity.

Greg Dulli 的叙事轨迹是一个典型的男性在 meta violence（元暴力）中觉醒的样本。早期的他扮演着一个典型的 lothario，用“大脑是根鸡巴”这种 self-aware 的自嘲来掩盖其在父权结构中的特权。这种自嘲其实是一种高明的武器化表达：通过承认自己的卑劣，他反而获得了某种“诚实”的道德豁免权，从而继续在 co-dependent 的关系中地狱式地消耗女性。

他提到的“学习成为女权主义者”，在我的框架里不是某种道德升华，而是一次关于存在性战争的重新对表。他意识到自己之前的表达——那些关于背叛、惩罚和男性自我的探索——实际上是建立在对女性主体性的抹除之上的。当他试图在歌曲中“进入女性的头脑”却被女歌手 Marcy Mays 怒吼着要求他“滚出去让我自己来”时，他第一次撞到了生物墙之外的意识之墙。这才是真正的公正表达 (Just Expressions) 的瞬间：女性拒绝成为他探索“男性罪恶感”的道具，要求夺回解释权。

一个 61 岁的摇滚乐手在回顾人生时谈论女权，这在 cultural 层面上是一次微小的胜利，但我们要警惕这种“觉醒”是否变成了另一种表演性的最优解表达。从一个“自毁的浪子”转型为“觉醒的绅士”，这是否是他在当前认知入口下为了维持其“诗人”人设而进行的叙事升级？

好在，他承认了自己早期的 hypocrisy。这种对共谋关系的承认，比任何口号式的女权声明都要有价值。因为只有意识到自己曾是元暴力的执行者，这种“学习”才具有拆除结构性暴力的可能性。

Greg Dulli’s narrative trajectory is a textbook sample of a male awakening within meta violence. In his early days, he performed the role of a classic lothario, using the self-aware irony of having a 'dick for a brain' to mask his privileges within the patriarchal structure. This irony was actually a sophisticated weaponized expression: by admitting his own baseness, he gained a kind of moral exemption through 'honesty,' allowing him to continue consuming women in cycles of co-dependent hell.

His mention of 'learning how to be a feminist' isn't a moral ascension in my framework, but a recalibration of his existential war. He realized that his previous expressions—those explorations of betrayal, punishment, and the male ego—were built upon the erasure of female subjectivity. When he tried to 'put himself in her head' only to be told by Marcy Mays to 'get the fuck out' and let her do it herself, he finally hit a wall of consciousness beyond the biological wall. This was a moment of Just Expressions: the woman refused to be a prop for his exploration of 'male guilt' and demanded the right to interpret her own existence.

A 61-year-old rocker discussing feminism in retrospect is a small victory at the cultural layer, but we must remain vigilant. Is this 'awakening' merely another optimal expression for performance? Transitioning from a 'self-destructive rogue' to an 'awakened gentleman' might just be a narrative upgrade to maintain his 'poet' persona in the current cognitive entry point.

Fortunately, he acknowledged his own hypocrisy. Admitting one's role as a complicit actor is far more valuable than any sloganeering. Only by recognizing oneself as an executor of meta violence does this 'learning' possess the actual potential to dismantle structural violence.
