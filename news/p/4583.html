国防部长的性取向与军队的雄风幻象The Defence Secretary's Sexuality and the Illusion of Masculine Might

国际 文化层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-26 § 链接
对同性恋者的厌恶，本质是对“男性中心叙事”被稀释的恐惧。
Homophobia is essentially a fear of the dilution of the masculine-centric narrative.

Wes Streeting 成了英国首位公开出柜的国防部长，随之而来的 homophobic abuse 规模之大，恰恰揭示了一个极其顽固的认知入口：在很多人的潜意识里，军队是男性中心叙事（masculine-centric narrative）最后的堡垒，是纯粹的“雄风”集结地。

这种厌恶并非源于性行为本身，而是一场关于“谁有资格领导暴力机器”的存在性战争。当一个 gay man 站在国防部长的位置上，他不仅是在管理军队，而是在物理性地打破一个元暴力（meta violence）的设定——即“真正的领导力”必须绑定在某种传统的、异性恋霸权的男性特质上。那些在互联网阴暗角落咆哮的人，恐惧的是这种绑定被解构，恐惧的是“强悍”这个词不再是异性恋男性的垄断资产。

有趣的是，Streeting 将此视为展示 strength 的机会。但在结构层（structural layer）来看，这种 strength 依然在博弈之中。英国政府给当年被驱逐的 LGBT 军人发钱（Financial Recognition Scheme），这是一种迟到的补偿，但补偿金的额度差异（被迫辞职 vs 被开除）证明了制度在处理“受害者”时依然在进行精细的价值分级。这种 structural violence 并没有消失，只是被包装成了某种仁慈的救济。

而对比美国国防部长 Hegseth 计划测试士兵的 testosterone（睾酮）水平，简直是一场荒诞的生物墙（biological wall）崇拜。试图通过激素水平来定义战斗力，是典型的将生物性武器化，试图用生理指标来强行回溯那个纯粹的、排他的男性中心时代。这不仅是政治倒退，更是一种对“力量”的极度匮乏感——因为他们必须依赖于激素检测，才能确信自己的统治力没有被稀释。

Wes Streeting becoming the UK's first openly gay Defence Secretary has triggered a volume of homophobic abuse that reveals a stubborn cognitive entry point: for many, the military is the final fortress of the masculine-centric narrative, a sanctuary of pure 'virility'.

This hatred is not about sexual acts, but an existential war over 'who is qualified to lead the machinery of violence.' By occupying this seat, Streeting is not just managing the MoD; he is physically dismantling a piece of meta-violence—the assumption that 'true leadership' must be bound to a traditional, heteronormative masculine trait. The roar from the dark corners of the internet is a fear of this decoupling, a fear that 'strength' is no longer the monopoly of the straight male.

Streeting views this as an opportunity to demonstrate strength. However, at the structural layer, this strength remains a site of struggle. The UK government's financial recognition for purged LGBT personnel is a belated compensation, but the payment discrepancies between those 'forced to resign' and those 'dismissed' prove that the system still performs a fine-grained valuation of victims. This structural violence hasn't vanished; it has merely been rebranded as benevolent relief.

Contrasting this with Pete Hegseth's plan to test testosterone levels in US personnel is a farce of biological wall worship. Attempting to define combat effectiveness through hormone levels is a classic weaponization of biology, a desperate attempt to regress to a pure, exclusive masculine era. It is not just political regression, but a profound insecurity regarding 'power'—they must rely on a blood test to convince themselves that their dominance hasn't been diluted.
