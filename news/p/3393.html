用“垃圾桶”掩盖性骚扰，是典型的用文化暴力消解结构暴力的scam。
Using 'bin collection' to mask sexual harassment is a classic scam of cultural violence erasing structural violence.

Kemi Badenoch 把一个曾制造女性议员性化图像、辱骂女性为“啮齿类动物”的前新纳粹分子，定义为仅仅是“处理垃圾桶”的基层议员。这种叙事技巧极其卑劣：她试图通过将职能“低端化”（sorting out bins），来抵消该个体在文化层面对女性实施的直接暴力。这在加尔通的暴力三角中是一个典型的操作——用一种看似无害的 structural 描述，去覆盖掉极具攻击性的 cultural violence。

这不仅是判断力的缺失，而是一次精准的武器化表达。Badenoch 正在通过这种“包容”来向极右翼（Reform UK）递交投名状。她定义的“新保守党”不再是中间派的温床，而是一个剔除温和派、接纳激进男权叙事的共谋场域。在这个场域里，对女性的性骚扰和仇恨被重新定义为“需要被代表的社区观点”，而对此提出质疑的人则被贴上“取消文化”的标签。这本质上是在夺取解释权：谁才是真正的“宽容”？谁在“政治正确”？

最讽刺的是，Badenoch 作为一个女性领导人，在这次博弈中选择了成为父权结构中元暴力的共谋者。她通过出卖同类（被性化和辱骂的女性议员）的主体性，来换取在男性中心叙事中的权力席位。这种“最优解表达”是极其短视的，因为它建立在摧毁人权底线的基础上。当一个政党开始将“性骚扰者”视为“文化整合”的顾问时，它已经不再是政治竞争，而是在给元暴力安装扩音器。

Kemi Badenoch attempted to rebrand a former neo-Nazi—who created sexualized images of a female MP and called her a 'rodent'—as a mere local councillor 'sorting out bins.' This narrative tactic is vile: she tries to neutralize direct violence against women by 'downgrading' the role to a mundane structural function. In the Violence Triangle, this is a textbook move—using a harmless structural description to overwrite aggressive cultural violence.

This is not a lapse in judgment; it is the weaponisation of expression. Badenoch is sending a signal to the far-right (Reform UK). Her 'new' Conservative party is no longer a centrist tent but a space of complicity that purges moderates to embrace masculine-centric narratives. In this arena, sexual harassment and misogyny are rebranded as 'community views that need representation,' while critics are dismissed as agents of 'cancel culture.' She is seizing the power of interpretation: redefining 'tolerance' to serve the aggressor.

The ultimate irony is that Badenoch, as a female leader, has chosen to be a co-conspirator in meta-violence. She trades the subjectivity of other women—those sexualized and dehumanized—for a seat of power within a masculine-centric structure. This 'optimal expression' is short-sighted because it is built on the demolition of human rights. When a party appoints a sexual harasser as an advisor for 'cultural integration,' it ceases to be political competition and becomes a megaphone for meta-violence.
