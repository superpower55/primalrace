骂影评人，是导演最省力的作品Calling Critics Failed Artists Is the Cheapest Review

文娱 文化层 · 元暴力 A.V. Club ↗ 2026-11-01 § 链接
新闻摘要塔伊加·维迪提执导、改编自石黑一雄小说的科幻讽刺片《克拉拉与太阳》在烂番茄仅获26%好评。维迪提周六在Instagram发文，称批评者是从未实现艺术家梦想的「可悲迷失灵魂」，并称他们「无关紧要、脱节、过时、该进垃圾桶」。
The newsTaika Waititi's sci-fi satire Klara And The Sun, adapted from Kazuo Ishiguro's novel, holds only a 26% positive rating on Rotten Tomatoes. On Saturday, Waititi posted on Instagram calling critics "sad lost souls" who failed to become artists, and declared them "irrelevant, out of touch, obsolete, in the bin."
把解释权收回去的人，通常先骂提问的人
Whoever can't win the argument redefines who's allowed to make it

Taika Waititi 说批评他新片的人是"sad lost souls who couldn't achieve their dreams of becoming artists, and thus settled, and became critics"。这句话不是在回应影评，是在给影评人重新定身份：你们不是观众，不是同行，是失败者。一旦身份被这样定义，你们说的话就不必被回答了。这是认知入口的争夺，最老的一套。

《Klara And The Sun》在 Rotten Tomatoes 上是 26% 的好评率。26% 是一个可以被讨论的数字：改编石黑一雄哪里没接住，Jenna Ortega 的表演被放在什么位置，讽刺的靶子到底有没有立起来。A.V. Club 自己的影评人 Jesse Hassenger 给出的判断是"undercooked social satire, adrift performers, and Waititi's tendency to resort to smarmy caricature"。这些是可以被反驳的具体指控。Waititi 一条都没碰。他碰的是提出指控的那群人的资格。

"Irrelevant. Out of touch. Obsolete. In the bin." 四个词，一个动作：把整个职业扔进垃圾桶。这不是情绪，这是操作。当一个人没法在内容上赢，他就去改评价的规则——不是我的片子不行，是评价这个行当本身不作数了。这套机制和宣布一条新定律来替代旧定律是同一种手艺：现实不配合，就重新定义什么算现实。

他自己说"I haven't read a review aimed at me and my peers for almost 15 years"。十五年不读评论的人，在 26% 出来之后立刻知道该发什么。报道没写他是怎么知道的，这个空白比他的帖子更说明问题：他不需要读，他需要的是一个可以被公开否定的评价体系，好让 26% 变成一群失败者的怨气。

这不是艺术家和评论家的古老战争。那是双方都在同一个场子里争谁说得对。Waititi 做的不是争，是撤掉场子。他有过好片子，报道也承认他多年来从评论那里拿到过不少暖意——正因为如此，这次翻脸才不是被逼急了，是算过的。

被扔进垃圾桶的那群人里，有一部分人写影评是因为他们真的想写，而不是因为当不成导演。把"没当成艺术家"当成他们唯一的动机，等于说这世上不存在独立的判断，只存在没抢到位置的嫉妒。这句话对评论是暴力，对艺术也是——它承认了作品只由位置决定。

26% 不会因为他骂人变成 80%。会变的是下一次有人想写差评时，先想一想自己会不会被叫作 sad lost soul。这一层没人测量，但它比票房持久。

Taika Waititi says the people who disliked his new film are "sad lost souls who couldn't achieve their dreams of becoming artists, and thus settled, and became critics." That is not a reply to criticism. It's a reclassification of the critic: not a viewer, not a peer, a failure. Once the identity is set, the argument doesn't have to be answered.

Klara And The Sun sits at 26% on Rotten Tomatoes. Twenty-six percent is a number you can argue with: where the Ishiguro adaptation misses, what Jenna Ortega is given to do, whether the satire has a target at all. The A.V. Club's own Jesse Hassenger called it "undercooked social satire, adrift performers, and Waititi's tendency to resort to smarmy caricature." Those are specific claims. Waititi engages none of them. He goes after the standing of the people making them.

"Irrelevant. Out of touch. Obsolete. In the bin." Four words and one move: the whole profession into the bin. This isn't temper, it's technique. When you can't win on content, you change the rules of evaluation—the film isn't weak, the act of evaluating is void. Same craft as declaring a new law when the old one won't cooperate: reality doesn't fit, so redefine what counts as reality.

He says he hasn't "read a review aimed at me and my peers for almost 15 years." A man who hasn't read reviews for fifteen years knew exactly what to post the moment 26% landed. The piece doesn't say how he knew. That gap says more than the post: he didn't need to read the reviews, he needed a public evaluation system he could discredit, so that 26% becomes the grudge of failed people.

This is not the old artist-versus-critic war. That war had both sides in the same arena arguing over who was right. Waititi doesn't argue, he removes the arena. He has made good films, and by the piece's own account he's had plenty of warmth from critics over the years—which is why the turn isn't desperation. It's calculated.

Some of the people he put in the bin write criticism because they want to write criticism, not because they couldn't direct. Reducing their motive to envy of a job they didn't get says there is no independent judgment, only people who lost the race for position. That's violence against criticism, and against art too: it concedes that work is decided by position alone.

26% won't become 80% because he insults the people who wrote it. What changes is the next person who hesitates before a bad review, wondering if they'll be called a sad lost soul. Nobody measures that layer. It outlasts the box office.
