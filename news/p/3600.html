灾难中的“团结”叙事与被掩盖的结构性暴力The 'Solidarity' Scam and the Hidden Structural Violence of Disaster

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-10 § 链接
自然灾害是随机的，但死亡人数是结构性暴力计算后的结果。
Natural disasters are random; death tolls are the calculated result of structural violence.

面对 7.4 级地震，全球领导人的反应像是一场精准的 PR 演出： solidarity, commitment, friendship。这种密集且统一的“团结”叙事是典型的 cultural violence，它通过制造一种“全球共情”的温情面纱，将一场巨大的悲剧简化为一次单纯的自然灾害，从而掩盖了底层的 structural violence。

65 栋建筑在佩雷拉（Pereira）坍塌，人们被困在废墟中。我们需要追问的是：为什么在 2026 年，现代城市的建筑在 7.4 级地震面前如此脆弱？是建筑标准缺失，还是监管层面的共谋（complicity）让劣质工程在权力勾兑中通过了验收？当总统宣布通过“绕过官僚障碍”来动员资金时，这本身就是一个讽刺——一个在平时就让资源分配失能的结构，在灾难发生后通过“特权”来补救，这依然是在既定权力逻辑下的运作。

最令人不安的是这种“英雄主义”的认知入口：总统在就职几天后迅速宣布国家紧急状态，国际盟友纷纷表态。这种叙事将权力者的“反应速度”置于中心，而将那些在废墟中死亡的人客体化为需要被“救助”的受害者。在这种 masculine-centric narrative 中，拯救者的姿态比受害者的生存权更具有可见度。

自然灾害本身不产生暴力，但 Potential（本可达到的安全标准）与 Actual（实际的建筑质量与救灾能力）之间的差额，就是最残酷的暴力。当全世界在 X 上交换“深切关怀”时，那些被劣质混凝土埋葬的人，成了这套文明叙事中最安静的注脚。

The global response to the 7.4-magnitude earthquake is a masterclass in PR: solidarity, commitment, friendship. This synchronized 'unity' narrative is a form of cultural violence. By weaving a veil of global empathy, it reduces a systemic tragedy to a mere 'act of God,' effectively masking the underlying structural violence.

With 65 buildings collapsed in Pereira, we must ask: Why, in 2026, is urban infrastructure still this fragile? Is it a lack of building codes, or a complicity between regulators and developers that allowed substandard construction to pass? The president's claim that he must 'bypass bureaucratic hurdles' to mobilize funds is a bitter irony—a system that fails in resource distribution during peacetime now uses 'exceptional powers' to fix its own failure. This is still operating within the same flawed power logic.

More disturbing is the weaponized narrative of heroism: a president sworn in days ago declares a state of emergency, and international allies rush to express support. This narrative centers the 'responsiveness' of the powerful, objectifying the victims as passive recipients of aid. In this masculine-centric narrative, the posture of the savior is more visible than the right to survive.

Natural disasters do not create violence; however, the gap between Potential (attainable safety standards) and Actual (real-world construction and response) is where the violence resides. While the world exchanges 'deepest condolences' on X, those buried under substandard concrete remain the silent footnotes of this civilized narrative.
