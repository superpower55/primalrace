用“风险登记册”掩盖国家失能的scamThe 'Risk Register' Scam: Masking State Failure with Child Labels

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-11 § 链接
将结构性失业转化为教育预判，是典型的用个体标签掩盖制度暴力的叙事武器化。
Converting structural unemployment into educational prediction is a weaponized narrative using individual labels to hide systemic violence.

前政府顾问 Alan Milburn 建议小学就该识别出那些 16 岁可能成为 NEET（不就业、不教育、不培训）的孩子。这听起来像是一种“早干预”的慈悲，实际上是一场极其阴险的叙事武器化。它试图将一个宏观的 structural violence —— 英国经济的停滞、劳动力市场的崩塌、社会资源的分配不均 —— 强行压缩进一个 4 到 11 岁孩子的“风险标签”里。

这套逻辑的本质是：既然国家无法提供足够的就业机会，那么我们就通过在小学建立“风险登记册”来预判谁会失败。这种做法把教育者变成了某种程度上的“人生判官”，让教师在认知入口端就为孩子贴上“潜在失业者”的标签。这不仅是行政上的冗余，更是一种文化层面的元暴力：它定义了什么样的孩子是“有风险的”，从而在潜意识里完成了对底层种族的预先筛选和规训。

最讽刺的是，正如文中提到的，英国小学的识字率在国际排名中处于高位，但青年失业率依然上升。这意味着 Potential（潜在能力）在增加，但 Actual（实际达成）在下降。这个差额就是暴力。而政府现在的做法不是去缩小这个差额，而是要求学校在孩子还没学会阅读之前，就先在数据库里给他们定好“失败”的价码。

这种“全方位协同治理” (Joined-up government) 永远只有一个方向：让学校去为财政部和内政部的失职买单。当一个国家试图通过给 8 岁孩子打标签来解决 20 年后的经济危机时，它已经放弃了治理，转而投向了某种病态的、基于预言的社会管理。这种用“预判”代替“创造可能性”的行为，本身就是对教育主体性的谋杀。

Alan Milburn's suggestion that primary schools identify future NEETs is framed as 'early intervention,' but it is a sinister weaponisation of expression. It attempts to compress structural violence — the stagnation of the UK economy and the collapse of the labour market — into a 'risk tag' for children aged 4 to 11.

The essence of this logic is simple: since the state cannot provide sufficient employment, it will instead predict who will fail. By creating a 'NEET risk register,' educators are turned into judges of destiny. This is not just bureaucratic bloat; it is meta-violence at the cognitive entry point, defining which children are 'at risk' and pre-sorting the primal race of the underprivileged.

The irony is stark: UK primary literacy is high, yet youth unemployment rises. The gap between Potential and Actual is the violence. Instead of closing this gap, the government asks schools to price the 'failure' of a child before they can even read.

This so-called 'joined-up government' always flows in one direction: schools compensating for the failures of the Treasury and Home Office. When a state attempts to solve a long-term economic crisis by labeling 8-year-olds, it has abandoned governance in favour of a pathological, predictive social management. Using 'prediction' to replace the 'creation of possibility' is nothing less than the murder of educational subjectivity.
