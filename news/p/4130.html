瑞典的“中立”是结构性暴力的绝佳掩体Sweden's 'Neutrality' is the Perfect Cover for Structural Violence

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-18 § 链接
程序正义是权力的遮羞布，当规则被用来抹除人时，它就是暴力。
Procedural justice is a mask for power; when rules are used to erase people, they are violence.

瑞典在脱欧后驱逐英国公民的效率之高，简直像是一场精心策划的行政清洗。一个 78 岁的退休护士，在瑞典生活了 21 年，孩子和孙辈都在这里，却因为申请表提交晚了而被要求离开。这种叙事在很多 naive 的观察者看来是“遗憾的个案”，但在我的框架里，这是典型的 structural violence（结构层暴力）。

最令人作呕的不是驱逐本身，而是瑞典政府所标榜的“中立规则”和“独立机构”。这正是 meta violence（元暴力）的一种伪装：将极具个体差异的人类生命简化为 data points（数据点），然后用“程序统一”来替代“实质正义”。当一个政府宣布其规则是“中立”的时候，它实际上是在宣布：我不再关心你是谁，我只关心你是否符合我设定的那个狭窄的、有利于我进行筛选的入口。

这场博弈中存在着典型的共谋 (complicity)。英国政府在 Whitehall 的官僚主义中扮演了失职的共谋者，而欧盟委员会则在维持表面和谐中选择了沉默。他们共同制造了一个“政治完美风暴”，让最脆弱的群体——老人、认知障碍者、没有电脑的人——在生物墙和数字墙的双重挤压下，直接掉进权利的悬崖。

瑞典将迁移政策转向限制主义，但它不敢直接撕毁 EU law，于是它选择了最阴险的武器化表达：通过糟糕的 outreach（外展宣传）和模糊的统计数据，在不违反法律字面的情况下，达成法律事实上地剔除“异己”的目的。这不是行政失误，这是利用程序漏洞进行的权力行使。

所谓的“公正表达”应该是让 Actual 朝 Potential 走近，而瑞典在做相反的事：它在人为地扩大 Violence = Potential − Actual 的差额。一个生活了 25 年的人被定义为“非法”，这种定义权的垄断就是暴力的本质。

The efficiency with which Sweden is deporting British citizens post-Brexit reads like a meticulously planned administrative purge. A 78-year-old retired nurse, living in Sweden for 21 years with her children and grandchildren there, is told to leave because her application was late. To a naive observer, this is a 'tragic isolated case'; in my framework, this is textbook structural violence.

What is most repulsive is not the deportation itself, but the 'neutral rules' and 'independent agencies' touted by the Swedish government. This is a manifestation of meta violence: reducing complex human lives to data points and substituting 'procedural uniformity' for 'substantive justice.' When a government claims its rules are 'neutral,' it is actually declaring that it no longer cares who you are—only whether you fit the narrow entry point designed for their own screening convenience.

There is a clear complicity at play here. The UK government, trapped in the bureaucratic inertia of Whitehall, is a negligent co-conspirator, while the European Commission chooses silence to maintain a facade of harmony. Together, they created a 'political perfect storm,' pushing the most vulnerable—the elderly, those with cognitive impairments, those without computers—off a cliff of rights, squeezed between biological and digital walls.

Sweden has shifted its migration politics toward restriction. Unable to openly tear up EU law, it employed a weaponized expression: using inadequate outreach and obscured statistics to achieve the factual removal of 'others' without violating the letter of the law. This is not an administrative error; it is the exercise of power through the exploitation of procedural loopholes.

Just Expressions should move the Actual closer to the Potential. Sweden is doing the opposite: it is artificially widening the gap of Violence = Potential − Actual. Defining a person who has lived in a country for 25 years as 'illegal' is the very essence of the monopoly over the power of definition—which is the essence of violence.
