红光疗法与美式橄榄球：用补丁修补被献祭的大脑Red-Light Therapy and American Football: Patching the Sacrificed Brain

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Athletic ↗ 2026-09-11 § 链接
用技术手段对冲结构性暴力，是典型的“补丁式”生存博弈。
Using tech to hedge structural violence is a classic 'patch-style' existential game.

这篇报道读起来像是一个充满希望的 medical miracle，但如果用加尔通的暴力三角来看，它其实揭露了一场极其残酷的 structural violence。美式橄榄球这套游戏，本质上就是一种通过合法化“头部撞击”来换取娱乐价值和商业利润的体制。球员在场上被鼓励像炮弹一样相撞，这种对生理极限的压榨在文化层被包装成“坚韧”和“荣耀”，实际上是把年轻男性的神经系统当作消耗品在献祭。

现在的 situation 是：这些球员在退役后，面对的是 CTE（慢性创伤性脑病）带来的认知崩塌、抑郁和暴力冲动。而红光疗法（itPBM）的出现，在叙事上被塑造成一种“救赎”。但请注意，这项技术的应用逻辑非常有趣——BYU 的教练直接点出，球员使用它能获得“竞争优势”（reaction time, executive control）。这意味着，这项技术不仅被用来治疗受害者，还被用来让受害者能更高效地在那个充满暴力的系统里生存，从而延长他们的“使用寿命”。

这是一种典型的“补丁式”博弈。系统并不打算改变导致大脑损伤的根源（即撞击本身），而是试图提供一个高效的补丁，让个体在被摧毁的同时能维持基本的 function。对于受害者来说，这确实是 Actual 朝 Potential 走近了一步，是 good_news；但对于系统来说，这让 structural violence 变得更加隐蔽——既然有了补丁，那么撞击带来的代价就被部分抵消，这反而给了体制继续维持原状的理由。

最讽刺的是，这种对大脑的“维护”，最终是为了让球员能更稳地留在场上，或者在退役后能勉强维持一个销售员的社会身份。当我们将目光聚焦于“红光是否能穿透颅骨”这种技术细节时，我们恰恰掉入了元暴力的陷阱：我们讨论如何修补被毁掉的人，却忘了讨论是谁、为了什么，如此理所当然地毁掉了他们。

This report reads like a medical miracle, but through the lens of the Violence Triangle, it reveals a brutal structural violence. American football is a system that legitimizes head impacts to trade neurological health for entertainment and profit. Players are encouraged to collide like missiles, a process wrapped in cultural narratives of 'toughness' and 'glory,' while their nervous systems are treated as consumables.

Now, we see itPBM framed as 'redemption' for those facing the cognitive collapse and violence of CTE. But the logic of its application is telling: the BYU coach explicitly mentions 'competitive advantage' in reaction time and executive control. This means the tech isn't just treating victims; it's being used to help them survive more efficiently within a violent system, effectively extending their 'service life.'

This is a 'patch-style' game. The system has no intention of altering the root cause—the impacts themselves—but instead provides a high-tech patch to maintain basic function while being destroyed. For the individual, this is a good_news because it reduces the gap between Actual and Potential. For the system, however, it makes structural violence more invisible; if there's a patch, the cost of the impact is offset, justifying the status quo.

The irony is that this 'maintenance' of the brain is ultimately about keeping players on the field or allowing them to barely function as salesmen after retirement. When we obsess over technical details like whether light can penetrate the skull, we fall into the trap of meta-violence: we discuss how to fix the broken, while ignoring who broke them and why it was considered acceptable.
