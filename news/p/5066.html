用“反法西斯”给权力开后门：一场典型的叙事陷阱The 'Anti-Fascist' Backdoor: A Classic Narrative Trap

国际 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-16 § 链接
当权力通过定义“敌人”来扩张时，被赋予的武器最终会指向所有不顺从者。
When power expands by defining 'enemies,' the weapons granted eventually target anyone who refuses to submit.

这是一次教科书级别的 weaponization。法案的名字叫《制裁俄罗斯与伊朗法案》，但它的实质是给 Trump 递一把可以随意挥舞的关税大锤。在这种叙事里，只要把目标定义为“俄罗斯的共谋者” (enablers)，这把大锤就可以合法地砸向任何一个国家，包括美国的盟友。

注意到那个典型的认知入口了吗？支持者把这次投票比作“丘吉尔 vs 钱伯林”的时刻。这种宏大叙事极其阴险：它强行将一个复杂的权力扩张问题，简化为一场关于“正义 vs 绥靖”的道德审判。在这种逻辑下，如果你担心总统权力过大，你就是“钱伯林”，你就在给法西斯递刀子。这种文化层面的暴力，迅速让理性的制度担忧变成了政治不正确的禁忌。

所谓的“支持乌克兰”在这里成了最完美的掩体。乌克兰的苦难是真实的，但这种真实被用来为一种危险的权力结构背书。当民主制度开始通过赋予单一领导人“绝对裁量权”来对抗一个独裁政权时，它实际上是在内部复刻对方的逻辑。这不是在拯救民主，而是在用民主的程序完成一次权力交接。

谁在共谋？那些为了短期政治正确而放弃制度制衡的议员，以及那些认为只要对方是“坏人”，就可以给自己的领导人无限授权的幼稚派。他们忽略了最基本的事实：权力一旦被赋予，它的使用逻辑就由持有者决定，而不是由法案的初衷决定。今天用来制裁“影子油轮”的权力，明天就可以用来勒索任何一个在贸易上不听话的伙伴。

这场博弈中，真正的输家依然是那些被当作叙事筹码的底层个体。无论关税怎么涨，被牺牲的永远是那些在通胀中挣扎的美国民众，以及在地缘政治夹缝中生存的普通人。权力在顶端完成了一次完美的 a-to-b 转移，而底层只得到了一个关于“正义”的廉价故事。

This is a textbook case of weaponisation. The bill is named the 'Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act,' but its essence is handing Trump a tariff sledgehammer. In this narrative, as long as a target is defined as an 'enabler' of Russia, this hammer can legally crush any nation, including U.S. allies.

Notice the classic cognitive entry point: supporters framed this vote as a 'Churchill vs Chamberlain moment.' This grand narrative is insidious; it simplifies a complex issue of power expansion into a moral trial of 'Justice vs Appeasement.' Under this logic, if you worry about presidential overreach, you are 'Chamberlain,' effectively aiding the fascists. This cultural violence quickly turns rational institutional concerns into political taboos.

'Supporting Ukraine' serves as the perfect cover here. The suffering of Ukrainians is real, but that reality is being used to endorse a dangerous power structure. When a democratic system fights an autocracy by granting its own leader 'absolute discretion,' it is internally replicating the enemy's logic. This isn't saving democracy; it's using democratic procedures to complete a transfer of power.

Who are the complicitors? The lawmakers who sacrifice institutional checks for short-term political correctness, and the naive who believe that as long as the target is 'evil,' their leader should have unlimited authority. They ignore the fundamental fact: once power is granted, its usage is determined by the holder, not the original intent of the bill. The power used to target 'shadow tankers' today can be used to blackmail any uncooperative partner tomorrow.

In this game, the true losers remain the marginalized individuals used as narrative pawns. No matter how tariffs rise, the ones sacrificed will always be the American people struggling with inflation and ordinary people caught in geopolitical crossfire. Power has achieved a perfect a-to-b transfer at the top, while the bottom is left with a cheap story about 'justice.'
