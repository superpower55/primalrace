特拉华州的战争代理人与被量化的身体Delaware War Proxies and the Quantified Body

国际 文化层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-30 § 链接
战争叙事中的“首次打击”是PR，而真正的暴力是将身体降格为消耗品。
The "first strike" narrative is PR; the real violence is the degradation of bodies into consumables.

新闻在纠结这是否是莫斯科“首次”打击美国公司，这种叙事入口极其典型的 masculine-centric。它把战争简化为大国之间的资产博弈和所有权清单，仿佛只要工厂注册在特拉华州，这枚导弹就具有了某种特殊的政治象征意义。但在这种 weaponized 的叙事里，最关键的被抹去了：那些在工厂里工作、在防空洞里等待的具体人类。

所谓的“Terminal Autonomy”（终端自主），这个词本身就是一种冰冷的 meta violence。它定义了AI在最后阶段接管控制权，将杀戮过程去人性化，把生命简化为一组被 Jamming 信号干扰后的坐标。当人们在讨论“美国公司所有权”时，实际上是在共谋一种将战争“企业化”的逻辑——战争变成了资本的风险投资，而前线的身体则是被量化的消耗品。

最讽刺的是，无论是俄方的 Tass 还是美方的情报官员，都在构建一套关于“目标”和“许可”的逻辑。在他们的共谋场域里，工厂是 Target，导弹是 License，而人只是这些名词之下的背景板。这种对解释权的垄断，让战争看起来像一场高端的工业竞赛，掩盖了 Violence = Potential − Actual 这个残酷的差额：每一个被摧毁的工厂，本质上都是对个体生存可能性的直接剥夺。

The news obsesses over whether this is Moscow's "first time" targeting a US company, a classic masculine-centric narrative entrance. It reduces war to a game of asset gambling and ownership checklists, as if registering in Delaware grants the missile a special political symbolism. In this weaponized narrative, the essential is erased: the actual humans working in the factory and waiting in bunkers.

The phrase "Terminal Autonomy" is itself a form of cold meta violence. It defines a phase where AI seizes control, dehumanizing the act of killing and reducing life to a set of coordinates after radio jamming. While the world debates "US corporate ownership," they are complicit in a logic that "corporatizes" war—where conflict becomes a venture capital risk and frontline bodies are merely quantified consumables.

The irony lies in the fact that both Russian Tass and US intelligence officers construct a logic of "targets" and "licenses." In their field of complicity, factories are Targets and missiles are Licenses, while humans are just backdrops. This monopoly on interpretation frames war as a high-end industrial competition, masking the brutal gap of Violence = Potential − Actual: every destroyed factory is, in essence, the direct deprivation of an individual's potential for existence.
