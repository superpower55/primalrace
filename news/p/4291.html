被当作“女性”攻击的政治权力Political Power Weaponized as 'Femininity'

国际 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-20 § 链接
当权力者用“女性化”作为羞辱词汇时，他正暴露其元暴力的底色。
When power uses 'feminine' as an insult, it exposes the raw mechanics of meta-violence.

白宫发言人 Davis Ingle 攻击参议员 Ossoff 是个“女性化的戏剧少年”，这种表达极其典型地揭示了 masculine-centric narrative 的运作逻辑：在他们的认知入口里，“女性”不代表一个独立的人，而是一个代表“弱小”、“轻量级”和“不值得关注”的贬义标签。这就是典型的 meta violence，将生理性别的社会建构直接武器化，用来剥夺对方的政治主体性。

更有趣的是 Natalie Harp 的处境。一个没有安全审查权限却能成为总统“守门人”的女性，在反对者眼中是“哄睡者”，在支持者眼中是“关键助手”。但无论哪种叙事，她都被物化为 Trump 权力延伸的附属品。她通过扮演一个特定的角色（flattery 供应商）获得了短期利益，这在存在性战争中看似是赢了，但代价是主体性的死亡——她成了总统权力游戏的一个 component，而非一个独立的政治主体。

而白宫团队对 Ossoff 的反应，则是典型的用 cultural violence 来掩盖 structural violence。他们试图通过人身攻击将讨论引导至“男子气概”的博弈，从而转移大众对国家债务、中东泥潭以及权力私有化这些结构性暴力的注意力。这种“制造愤怒”的艺术，本质上就是用一种低级的性别歧视，来掩护高级的政治掠夺。

White House spokesman Davis Ingle's attack on Senator Ossoff as a "feminine theatre kid" is a textbook example of how the masculine-centric narrative operates. In their cognitive entry point, 'feminine' is not a human identity but a weaponized label for 'weakness' and 'irrelevance.' This is pure meta-violence: using the social construction of gender to strip an opponent of their political agency.

Then there is Natalie Harp. A woman working without security clearance yet acting as a 'gatekeeper' to the president. Whether she is viewed as a 'tucker-in' by critics or a 'key aide' by supporters, she remains objectified as an appendage of Trump's power. By performing a specific role—the supplier of flattery—she may have found a temporary optimal expression to gain access, but the cost is the death of her subjectivity. She has become a component of a power game, not an independent political actor.

Finally, the White House's meltdown is a strategic use of cultural violence to mask structural violence. By redirecting the discourse toward a crude battle over 'masculinity,' they attempt to distract the public from the structural violence of national debt, the Middle East quagmire, and the privatization of public trust. This art of 'manufactured outrage' is simply using low-level sexism to shield high-level political predation.
