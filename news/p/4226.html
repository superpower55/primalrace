电子烟禁令：一场关于“保护”的叙事补丁Vape Bans: A Narrative Patch for Structural Addiction

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-19 § 链接
所有的“保护”如果只在包装上动刀，本质上都是在为结构性成瘾买单。
Any 'protection' that only targets packaging is merely paying the bill for structural addiction.

电子烟厂商用甜点味和彩色包装精准地在青少年中开辟了认知入口，这本身就是一场极其成功的武器化表达：将尼古丁这种成瘾性毒药包装成“成长的无害点缀”。现在，医学界和政府跳出来说这很危险，要求把包装改成“苹果味”或“无名色”，这种逻辑极其滑稽——它试图通过修改表达的表象来解决一个生物性的成瘾问题。

这在加尔通的暴力三角里是典型的 cultural 层面的修补。政府通过 Tobacco and Vapes Act 试图通过 plain packaging 这种 structural 层面的小手术来对冲损害，但它忽略了最核心的元暴力：一个允许成瘾产品在青少年市场大规模渗透的资本结构。当一个产品被“刻意设计成吸引儿童”时，这种恶意已经写进了产品的 DNA，而不是写在包装纸上。

所谓的“烟草自由一代”叙事，更像是一场 PR 演出。它把责任推给“诱导性的口味”，却不去触碰尼古丁产业的定价权和分发逻辑。这种“保护”实际上是一种共谋：政府通过扮演救世主来获得政治正确，而厂商在经历短暂的包装调整后，会迅速寻找下一个能绕过监管的表达入口。如果 Actual 依然被资本定义的“最优解”掌控，那么 Potential 的健康差额永远无法弥补。

Vape manufacturers successfully weaponised expression by using dessert flavors and colorful packaging to carve out a cognitive entry point among youth, framing nicotine—a biological toxin—as a 'harmless accessory of growing up.' Now, the medical establishment and government claim it's dangerous and demand flavors be renamed to 'apple' or packaging be made plain. The logic is absurd: attempting to solve a biological addiction by tweaking the aesthetics of expression.

Within the Violence Triangle, this is a textbook attempt to patch the cultural layer. The government uses the Tobacco and Vapes Act to perform a structural 'minor surgery' via plain packaging, but it ignores the meta-violence: a capitalistic structure that permits addictive products to penetrate youth markets on a massive scale. When a product is 'deliberately designed to appeal to children,' the malice is encoded in the product's DNA, not just on its wrapper.

The narrative of a 'smoke-free generation' feels more like a PR performance. It shifts the blame onto 'seductive flavors' while avoiding the pricing power and distribution logic of the nicotine industry. This 'protection' is a form of complicity: the government gains political correctness by playing the savior, while manufacturers, after a brief adjustment, will simply find a new expression entry point to bypass regulation. As long as the Actual is governed by the 'optimal expression' defined by capital, the gap between Potential and Actual health will never close.
