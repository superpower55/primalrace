法西斯礼赞与民主掩体：海牙暴动的元暴力逻辑Fascist Salutes and the Democratic Shield: The Meta-Violence of The Hague Riots

国际 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-19 § 链接
极右翼的暴力不是失控，而是男性中心叙事对‘异己’的公开狩猎。
Far-right violence is not a loss of control, but a public hunt for the 'Other' driven by masculine-centric narratives.

海牙街头的烟花和纳粹礼，在主流叙事里被简化为“令人厌恶的场景”或对“法治”的挑战。但如果用 Violence = Potential − Actual 的公式来看，这场暴动根本不是突发的 an anomaly，而是一次极其典型的 Meta Violence（元暴力）演习。极右翼的内核是对“纯洁性”的病态执念，而这种纯洁性的定义权，永远掌握在试图通过排他来确立自身存在感的男性手中。

这场暴动的逻辑链路非常清晰：首先通过 weaponized 的反移民叙事制造一个“敌人”；接着在公共空间通过身体的侵略性表达（投掷物、攻击警察、焚烧车辆）来确认自身的权力等级。这种暴力不是为了达成某种具体的政策改变，而是一场关于“谁才是这个空间主人”的存在性战争。当他们做出纳粹礼时，他们是在召唤那个最极端的 masculine-centric narrative——一个通过剥夺他者主体性来获得绝对掌控感的幻梦。

最讽刺的是，这种暴力往往在一种诡异的共谋中生存。极右翼在街头撕裂，而体制内的共谋者可能在潜意识里将这种“强硬”视为某种秩序的底色。当司法部长在 X 上强调“坚定响应”时，这种话语在 cultural layer 上完成了一次闭环：暴力被定义为对法治的攻击，从而掩盖了暴力本身就生长于这套男本位权力结构的客观事实。法治成为了掩体，而掩体之下，原初种族的殖民逻辑依然在高效运转。

The fireworks and Nazi salutes in The Hague are simplified by mainstream media as "disgusting scenes" or challenges to the "rule of law." However, applying the formula Violence = Potential − Actual reveals that this riot is not an anomaly, but a textbook exercise in Meta Violence. The core of the far-right is a pathological obsession with "purity," and the power to define that purity always rests with men seeking to establish their existence through exclusion.

The logic of this unrest is transparent: first, manufacture an "enemy" through the weaponization of anti-immigration narratives; then, confirm a power hierarchy in public spaces through aggressive physical expressions—projectiles, attacking police, and burning cars. This violence is not about achieving specific policy changes; it is an existential war over "who owns this space." When they perform the Nazi salute, they are summoning the most extreme masculine-centric narrative—a fantasy of absolute control achieved by stripping others of their subjectivity.

Most ironic is how this violence survives through a strange complicity. While the far-right tears through the streets, complicitors within the system may subconsciously view this "toughness" as the bedrock of order. When the Justice Minister calls for an "extremely firm" response on X, the discourse completes a loop at the cultural layer: violence is defined as an attack on the rule of law, thereby masking the fact that such violence grows directly from this masculine power structure. The rule of law becomes a shield, while beneath it, the colonial logic of the Primal Race continues to operate with lethal efficiency.
