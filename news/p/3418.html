气候否认论：一场关于生存权的认知武器化 scamClimate Denial: A Weaponised Scam of Existential Erasure

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-08 § 链接
否认气候危机不是认知偏差，而是权力阶层通过制造叙事噪音来延续掠夺的武器化表达。
Climate denial is not a delusion; it is a weaponised expression used by power elites to maintain predatory structures.

萨迪克·汗（Sadiq Khan）在文章中揭露了一个极其阴险的机制：气候否认论（Climate Denial）并不是一群单纯的“疯子”在自嗨，而是一场精心策划的认知武器化（Weaponisation of Expression）。当欧洲在烈日下燃烧，当伦敦的公园变成焦土，否认论者通过在社交媒体投放 disinformation，将一个关乎生存的 structural violence 议题，强行拖入所谓的“文化战争”（Culture War）泥潭。

这套逻辑的恶劣之处在于它精准地利用了“身份入口”进行筛选和排斥。否认论者将绿色政策包装成“精英的奢侈品”或“Woke nonsense”，试图让底层劳动者相信，保护地球是上层阶级的政治正确，而与他们的生活成本无关。这是一种典型的元暴力（Meta Violence）操作：通过垄断对“真实生活”的解释权，让受害者在潜意识中与真正的压迫者——化石燃料巨头和权力政客——达成共谋（Complicity）。

根据加尔通的暴力三角，气候危机导致的死亡和绝收是 direct violence，而导致这种现状的碳排放制度是 structural violence。而那些宣称“气候危机是骗局”的叙事，正是最底层的 cultural violence。它的目的只有一个：让 structural violence 看起来像是个“观点之争”，从而让掠夺者在 Potential（可达到的低碳未来）与 Actual（被焚毁的现状）的巨大差额中，继续榨取最后一滴油利。

好在，伦敦的 Ulez 实践证明了 Actual 可以向 Potential 移动——空气污染相关死亡率下降 40% 就是最硬的数据。但我们要警惕的是，只要解释权依然被那些能够制造“噪音”的权力者掌控，任何具体的胜利都可能被叙事武器化地消解。这场战争的本质不是关于气温，而是关于谁有权定义什么是“事实”，以及谁在为这个星球的死亡买单。

Sadiq Khan exposes a sinister mechanism: Climate Denial is not merely the rambling of the delusional, but a calculated weaponisation of expression. As Europe burns and London's parks turn to ash, deniers use disinformation to drag a matter of structural violence into the mud of a manufactured "Culture War."

The malice of this logic lies in its use of identity as an entry point. By branding green policies as "elite luxuries" or "woke nonsense," deniers trick the working class into believing that planetary survival is a bourgeois political performance. This is a textbook operation of meta-violence: by monopolising the narrative of "real life," they induce the victims to enter into a complicity with their true oppressors—fossil fuel giants and political opportunists.

Applying Galtung's Violence Triangle, the deaths and crop failures are direct violence, and the carbon-dependent system is structural violence. The narratives claiming the crisis is a "hoax" are the cultural violence that legitimises the other two. Its sole purpose is to frame structural violence as a mere "debate of opinions," allowing predators to profit from the gap between the Potential (a low-carbon future) and the Actual (a burning world).

London's Ulez success—a 40% drop in air pollution-related deaths—proves that the Actual can move toward the Potential. However, we must remain vigilant. As long as the power to define "fact" is held by those who manufacture noise, any concrete victory can be erased by weaponised narratives. This is not a war over temperature; it is a war over the right to define reality and who pays the price for a dying planet.
