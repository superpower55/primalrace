在男人们的博弈场里，世界只是一个被消耗的客体The World as an Object in the Men's Gambling Den

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-31 § 链接
战争是男性中心叙事最极致的武器化，将生命降格为宏大意义的筹码。
War is the ultimate weaponization of masculine-centric narrative, reducing life to mere chips for grand meanings.

这就是典型的 masculine-centric narrative：一群男人在 Camp David 的度假村里，把一场导致能源危机、物价飞涨、无数生命被摧毁的战争，描述成一次 "a lot of fun" 的内阁团建。这种极度的认知脱节，正是元暴力的核心——在权力顶端的男性眼中，战争不是关于死亡和苦难，而是一场关于“路径”的博弈，是中期选举的选票计算，是 ExxonMobil 利润翻倍的财务报表。

从霍尔穆兹海峡的炮火到黎巴嫩的废墟，所有的暴力都被包装在“国家安全”或“确保安全”的武器化叙事中。所谓的“非合规油轮”或“确保北部居民安全”，不过是掩盖掠夺和控制的 cultural violence。在这一层叙事下，具体的生命被客体化为可消耗的资源，而决定这些资源如何被消耗的权力，被垄断在极少数男性的手中。

最讽刺的是，这场战争的逻辑是完全闭环的：男性制造问题，然后通过“解决问题”来彰显价值。特朗普承诺的“几周内结束”变成了五个月的泥潭，但即便在支持率暴跌时，这种博弈依然在继续。因为在男本位的权力结构中，承认失败比发动战争更不可接受。而那些在战火中失去家园的女性和孩子，在这些“精英”的议程表里，甚至连一个被提及的词条都算不上。

This is a textbook example of masculine-centric narrative: a group of men at a Camp David retreat describing a war that triggered energy crises, soaring prices, and mass casualties as "a lot of fun" team-building. This extreme cognitive dissonance is the core of meta-violence—to the men at the top, war is not about death or suffering, but a game of "paths," a calculation of midterm election votes, and the doubling of ExxonMobil's profits.

From the shelling in the Strait of Hormuz to the ruins of Lebanon, all violence is wrapped in weaponized narratives of "national security." Terms like "non-compliant tankers" or "ensuring the security of residents" are merely cultural violence masks for predation and control. Under this narrative, concrete lives are objectified into consumable resources, while the power to decide how these resources are spent remains monopolized by a few men.

The irony is that the logic of this war is a perfect closed loop: men manufacture the problem, then attempt to "solve" it to validate their own existence. Trump's promise to end the war in "a few weeks" has dragged into five months of quagmire, yet the game continues. In a masculine-centric power structure, admitting defeat is more intolerable than perpetuating a war. Meanwhile, the women and children losing their homes in the crossfire aren't even a footnote on these "elites'" agendas.
