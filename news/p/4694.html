被量身定制的“客观”：媒体如何共谋抹除反穆斯林暴力Tailored Objectivity: How Media Complicity Erases Anti-Muslim Violence

国际 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-02 § 链接
所谓的“新闻标准”是元暴力的伪装，用来筛选谁需要为言论负责，谁可以心安理得地共谋。
So-called 'journalistic standards' are masks for meta-violence, filtering who is held accountable and who is permitted to be a co-conspirator.

这件看似是关于“双标”的政治新闻，本质上是一次典型的 cultural violence 演示。媒体在处理密歇根州参议员选举时，将大量 attention 投放给一个非竞选团队成员的 Twitch 主播 Hasan Piker，却对共和党候选人 Mike Rogers 阵营中真正的 senior leadership 成员——一个公开称伊斯兰教为“死亡崇拜”的宗教极端分子 Lorenzo Sewell——保持死一般的沉默。

这种不对称的报道逻辑揭示了一个残酷的事实：在主流媒体的认知入口中，反穆斯林歧视被设定为一种“低成本”的暴力。当一个民主党候选人与一个激进左翼主播同行，这被定义为“需要被追责的风险”；而当一个共和党候选人将纳粹旗帜和宗教仇恨纳入其领导团队，这被定义为“可见但无需讨论的背景”。

这不仅仅是报道的疏忽，而是一场深层的 complicity。所谓的“自由派媒体”在潜意识中认同了一套 masculine-centric narrative：他们可以容忍右翼的粗鄙与仇恨，只要这种仇恨符合某种既定的政治权力分布。他们通过定义什么是“争议性言论”（Piker 的言论）和什么是“宗教信仰”（Sewell 的仇恨），完成了对事实的重新书写。

当媒体要求 El-Sayed 为一个外部支持者的言论道歉，却不对 Rogers 阵营中的纳粹符号发问时，他们实际上是在执行一种元暴力：他们通过掌控解释权，告诉世界哪些群体的痛苦是可见的，而哪些群体的被殖民经历（如穆斯林在美境内的处境）是可以被忽略的。这是一种极其高效的武器化表达，它让施暴者在“客观”的掩护下，心安理得地继续其结构性压迫。

This political story, framed as a matter of 'double standards,' is in fact a textbook demonstration of cultural violence. In the Michigan Senate race, the media poured immense attention into Hasan Piker—a Twitch streamer who isn't even on the campaign team—while maintaining a deathly silence regarding Lorenzo Sewell, a senior leadership member of Mike Rogers' camp who openly labels Islam a 'death cult.'

This asymmetric logic reveals a brutal truth: in the cognitive entry points of mainstream media, anti-Muslim bigotry is categorized as a 'low-cost' violence. When a Democratic candidate aligns with a radical leftist, it is framed as a 'risk requiring accountability.' When a Republican candidate integrates Nazi flags and religious hatred into his leadership team, it is framed as 'visible but irrelevant background.'

This is not mere negligence; it is deep-seated complicity. Even so-called 'liberal media' subconsciously adhere to a masculine-centric narrative, tolerating right-wing bigotry as long as it fits a predetermined distribution of political power. By defining what constitutes 'controversial rhetoric' (Piker's) versus 'religious belief' (Sewell's), they rewrite reality.

When reporters demand El-Sayed answer for an external supporter while ignoring Nazi symbols in Rogers' camp, they are executing meta-violence. By monopolizing the power of interpretation, they dictate whose suffering is visible and whose colonial experience—specifically that of Muslims in the US—can be erased. This is a highly efficient weaponization of expression, allowing the oppressor to continue structural violence under the guise of 'objectivity.'
