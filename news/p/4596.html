AI 幻象与权力者的“活体陵墓”AI Phantasms and the Living Mausoleum of Power

国际 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-27 § 链接
当现实不再配合，权力者便通过 AI 重新定义事实。
When reality refuses to comply, power simply redefines facts through AI.

特朗普在 Truth Social 上发布自己与华盛顿、巴顿的 AI 合影，这根本不是什么社交媒体营销，而是一次典型的认知入口夺取战。在面对伊朗战争僵局和支持率暴跌的 Actual 状态时，他试图通过 AI 制造一个 Potential 的假象：将自己直接锚定在“建国者”和“战争英雄”的叙事之上。

这正是典型的 weaponized expression。当一个权力者无法在现实世界中赢得尊重时，他会转向制造“事实”。AI 成了他的权力延伸，让他能够跳过历史的积累和真实的功绩，直接通过像素的排列组合，给自己颁发勋章，给自己安排与国父的会面。这种行为的本质是试图通过控制解释权来覆盖真实，把一个处于危机中的总统，伪装成一个超越时代的图腾。

最讽刺的是白宫发言人的回应，称 Truth Social 是“真实的声音”(authentic voice)。在 AI 生成的幻象面前，所谓的“真实”已经变成了一个被权力定义的新词。这种叙事逻辑与华为宣布新定律如出一辙：如果现实中的能力不足以支撑野心，那就换一把尺子，重新定义什么是“成功”和“伟大”。

特朗普正在建造的不是舞厅，而是一座“活体陵墓”(living mausoleum)。他试图在死后之前，就先在数字世界里完成自己的神格化。在这种元暴力的运作下，事实不再重要，重要的是谁拥有制造“真实”的算力。

Trump's AI-generated portraits with Washington and Patton on Truth Social are not mere social media marketing; they are a calculated strike to seize the cognitive entry point. Facing the Actual state of a stalemate in Iran and plummeting approval ratings, he is attempting to manufacture a Potential illusion: anchoring himself directly to the narratives of the "Founder" and the "War Hero."

This is the textbook weaponisation of expression. When a leader fails to earn respect in the physical world, he turns to manufacturing "facts." AI becomes an extension of his power, allowing him to bypass historical accumulation and genuine achievement. Through the simple rearrangement of pixels, he awards himself medals and arranges meetings with the Founding Fathers. The essence here is the attempt to overwrite reality by controlling the power of interpretation, disguising a president in crisis as a timeless totem.

The most cynical part is the White House spokesperson's claim that Truth Social represents an "authentic voice." In the face of AI phantasms, "authenticity" has become a new term defined by power. This logic mirrors the τ-scaling narrative: if actual capability cannot support the ambition, simply change the ruler and redefine what "success" and "greatness" mean.

Trump is not building a ballroom; he is constructing a "living mausoleum." He seeks to complete his own apotheosis in the digital realm before he even dies. Under the operation of this meta-violence, facts are irrelevant; what matters is who possesses the computing power to manufacture "truth."
