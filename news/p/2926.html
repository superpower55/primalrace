AI 幻觉：用不存在的文档重启性别暴力的传送带AI Hallucinations: Restarting the Conveyor Belt of Gender Violence

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-28 § 链接
技术性谎言是结构暴力的升级版，它让剥夺主体性变得更高效且不可追溯。
Technological lies are an upgrade of structural violence, making the erasure of subjectivity more efficient and untraceable.

一个摩洛哥女性在经历强迫童婚、强奸和暴力后寻求庇护，结果被英国内政部用一份根本不存在的 AI 幻觉文档给拒了。这不仅是所谓的“程序不规范”，而是一次极其精准的 structural violence。内政部试图用一个虚构的“权威文档”来定义一个女性在原籍国的“安全性”，本质上是在用算法制造事实，强行将受害者推回那个由强权男性主导的暴力场域。

最令人作呕的是这种共谋机制：内政部先是依赖 AI 编造证据，在被高级法官拆穿后，又迅速抛出一份从未公开的内部指南来打掩护。这份指南本身就记录了摩洛哥法律不将婚内强奸入罪，且举报者可能被反向定罪。这意味着，体制在明知该女性面临极高生存风险的情况下，依然选择通过“修改尺子”的方式将其排除在保护之外。这种行为就是典型的 weaponized expression——通过垄断解释权，将“安全”定义为一种可以随意篡改的行政指标，而非具体的生存事实。

AI 的“幻觉”在这里成了完美的掩体。当暴力通过代码和文档被自动化，施暴者可以把责任推给“技术故障”，而受害者则在 Potential 和 Actual 的巨大差额中被再次肢解。这种对解释权的垄断正是元暴力的现代变体：无论技术如何迭代，其核心逻辑依然是让强者通过定义“什么是事实”，来合法化对弱势种族的殖民与抛弃。

A Moroccan woman, fleeing forced child marriage, rape, and extreme violence, had her asylum claim rejected by the UK Home Office based on a document that simply did not exist—an AI hallucination. This is not a mere "procedural irregularity"; it is a precise execution of structural violence. By using a fictional "authoritative document" to define a woman's "safety" in her home country, the state attempted to manufacture a reality that forcibly pushes a victim back into a field of violence dominated by powerful men.

The complicity here is nauseating: the Home Office first relied on AI-generated fabrications and, upon being exposed by a senior judge, pivoted to an undisclosed internal guidance note. This very document acknowledges that Morocco does not explicitly criminalize marital rape and that women reporting it may be criminalized themselves. The system, fully aware of the lethal risks, chose to modify the "ruler" of measurement to exclude her from protection. This is a classic case of weaponized expression—monopolizing the power of interpretation to define "safety" as a manipulatable administrative metric rather than a concrete survival fact.

AI "hallucinations" serve as the perfect shield. When violence is automated through code and documentation, the perpetrator can deflect responsibility toward "technical glitches," while the victim is dismantled once again in the gap between Potential and Actual. This monopoly over interpretation is the modern mutation of meta-violence: regardless of technological iteration, the core logic remains the same—allowing the powerful to define "what is fact" to legitimize the colonization and abandonment of the Primal Race.
