用虚构的死亡，为真实的投降定价Pricing Surrender with Fictional Deaths

国际 文化层 · 元暴力 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-19 § 链接
当权力试图通过篡改死亡数字来定义和平，这本身就是一种元暴力。
When power manipulates death tolls to define peace, it is an act of meta-violence.

特朗普在堪萨斯州的集会是一场典型的认知入口抢夺战。他通过重复一个被证伪的数字——“一百万乌克兰人死亡”——来制造一种紧迫的虚假真实。在他的叙事逻辑里，死亡数字越高，泽连斯基的“不合作”就越显得不可原谅，而他自己主导的“交易”就越显得像是一场人道主义救赎。这不是在讨论事实，而是在通过武器化表达来夺取解释权：他试图定义什么是“正确的和平”，即通过牺牲主权来换取他口中的“不再死亡”。

这种操作是典型的 meta violence。他不仅在结构层通过撤销制裁来削弱乌克兰，更在文化层试图通过一种“家长式”的叙事，将受害者（乌克兰）定义为不听话的孩子，而将自己塑造成唯一的救世主。当他宣称“应该由别人代表乌克兰谈判”时，他实际上是在剥夺一个民族的存在性表达。在存在性战争中，最彻底的失败不是失去土地，而是失去定义自己命运的话语权。

与此同时，共和党在法国骚乱中寻找“移民”这个认知入口，并将其与美国国内政治绑定，这是一种极其低劣的共谋。他们将复杂的社会结构暴力（生活成本、公共开支削减）简化为单一的生物墙问题（移民/穆斯林），目的是为了在即将到来的中期选举中，通过制造一个共同的“异己”来巩固内部的权力共谋。这种将“文明”作为掩体、将“反文明”贴在他人身上的做法，正是元暴力最常见的伪装方式。

Trump's rally in Kansas is a textbook attempt to seize the cognitive entry point. By repeating a debunked figure—'a million Ukrainians dead'—he manufactures a false urgency. In his narrative logic, the higher the death toll, the more inexcusable Zelenskyy's 'non-compliance' becomes, and the more his own 'deal' appears as a humanitarian rescue. This isn't a debate over facts; it is the weaponisation of expression to seize the right of interpretation. He is attempting to define 'correct peace' as the surrender of sovereignty in exchange for his version of 'stopping the dying.'

This is pure meta-violence. Beyond the structural violence of waiving sanctions to weaken Ukraine, he employs a cultural narrative that casts the victim as a disobedient child and himself as the sole savior. When he suggests 'somebody else' should negotiate for Ukraine, he is effectively erasing their existential expression. In an existential war, the most absolute defeat is not the loss of land, but the loss of the agency to define one's own destiny.

Simultaneously, the GOP's exploitation of the French riots to target 'immigrants' is a crude form of complicity. They reduce complex structural violence—soaring costs of living and crumbling infrastructure—to a simple biological wall (migrants/Muslims). The goal is to consolidate internal power for the midterms by manufacturing a common 'Other.' Using 'civilization' as a shield while labeling others 'anti-civilization' is the most common disguise for meta-violence.
