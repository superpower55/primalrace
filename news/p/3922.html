目标改成“愿景”，产线就不用装了Rename the Target a 'Vision' and You Never Have to Build the Line

科技 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-24 § 链接
现实不配合时，不改能力，改尺子。38GWh变15.8GWh不叫失败，叫aspirational。
When reality won't cooperate, change the ruler, not the capacity. 38GWh to 15.8GWh isn't failure—it's 'aspirational'.

38GWh 缩成 15.8GWh，AESC 的说法是：当年那个数字“更像一个 aspirational goal，而非 firm target”。这是 τ law 的英国版。做不到就宣布当初说的不是那个意思，换尺子不换能力，叙事先于产线认账。

2021 年疫情期间，绿色资本狂欢，每家车厂都抢着签野心最大的电动化 target。那些 target 从来不是工程承诺，是表达的武器化——争夺“我们在转型”这个认知入口，换来估值、补贴、政治成绩。现在 Northvolt 破产，Britishvolt 破产，ACC 取消两个厂，SVolt 撤出德国（来源：The Guardian）。集体退场的方式不是承认押错了，是把 target 一个个改名叫愿景。

看这条 complicity 链条怎么运作：英国政府掏钱参与 £1bn 再融资；JLR 不肯做 formal financial commitment；AESC 于是不装第三条线；Nissan 关掉一条装配线准备给奇瑞代工，同时裁掉几千人。每个节点都在规避承担，风险顺着链条往下滑，最后落在 Sunderland 的工人身上。Potential − Actual 的差额就是结构暴力，而 Labour 政府周五的动作是再砍一刀电动车销量目标——让 Actual 看起来靠近 Potential，不是能力上去了，是 Potential 被人为调低了。

还有一个被所有人默契绕开的事实：“UK's biggest gigafactory”是中国公司的。CATL 和 BYD 主导的产业里，“英国电池主权”这套叙事本身就是表演，Agratas 那 500 公里管道、600 公里线缆的数字秀，是在用工程复杂度给延期背书。真正的问题不在中英之间，在谁先为当年的叙事买单。

下次再听到 aspirational 这个词，问一句：谁的工资是 aspirational 的。

38GWh shrinks to 15.8GWh, and AESC's line is that the old number was 'more an aspirational goal than a firm target.' This is the British edition of the τ law: can't deliver, so declare you never meant it. Swap the ruler, keep the capacity. The narrative gets restated before the production line gets built.

In 2021, mid-pandemic green-capital frenzy, every carmaker raced to sign the most ambitious electrification target on the table. Those targets were never engineering commitments. They were weaponized expression—a grab for the cognitive entry point 'we are transitioning,' traded for valuations, subsidies, political credit. Now Northvolt is bankrupt, Britishvolt is bankrupt, ACC has scrapped two plants, SVolt has pulled out of Germany (source: The Guardian). The collective exit isn't an admission of a bad bet. It's a mass renaming of targets as visions.

Watch the complicity chain run: the UK government puts money into a £1bn refinancing; JLR refuses any formal financial commitment; AESC therefore declines to install a third line; Nissan shuts an assembly line to contract-build for Chery while laying off thousands. Every node dodges the burden, and risk slides down the chain until it lands on Sunderland's workers. Potential minus Actual—that gap is structural violence. And Labour's Friday move is to cut the EV sales target again, so Actual looks closer to Potential. Capacity didn't rise. Potential was lowered by hand.

Then there's the fact everyone politely steps around: the 'UK's biggest gigafactory' is Chinese-owned. In an industry run by CATL and BYD, 'British battery sovereignty' is theatre, and Agratas's parade of 500km of pipes and 600km of wiring is engineering complexity deployed as an alibi for delay. The real question isn't China versus Britain. It's who pays first for the story they all sold in 2021.

Next time you hear the word aspirational, ask one thing: whose wages are aspirational.
