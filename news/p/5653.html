监狱不是为了让囚犯“变好”，而是为了完成“惩罚”的表演Prisons Aren't for Rehabilitation, They're for the Performance of Punishment

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-19 § 链接
所谓的“康复失败”，本质是系统在 structural 层面上拒绝承认人的主体性。
The so-called 'failure of rehabilitation' is essentially a systemic refusal to recognize human subjectivity at the structural level.

这几封读者来信撕开了一个极其典型的 scam：国家一方面在 cultural 层面上兜售“康复”（rehabilitation）的温情叙事，另一方面在 structural 层面上通过风险管控和资源剥夺，确保囚犯永远无法真正回归社会。所谓的“康复”在这里成了一个 weaponized concept，它被用来掩盖一个事实——监狱系统的核心 KPI 不是让一个人变好，而是执行“惩罚”和“威慑”的表演。

文中提到的 1984 年政府目标调整以及随后对概率官（probation officer）自主权的剥夺，就是一次典型的元暴力操作。当“风险管控”（risk）取代了“人本主义”（person-centred）成为驱动力， probation officer 不再是引导者，而成了监视器。这种转变将囚犯从一个有生命史的人，简化为一个需要被监测的风险指标。当一个人被定义为“风险”时，他的任何表达都被预设为威胁，而这种预设本身就是一种 structural violence。

最深刻的洞察来自 Joe Sim 的质疑：如果一个人从未被“ habilitated”（使其具备社会生存能力），你如何要求他被“rehabilitated”？这是一个关于 Potential 和 Actual 的残酷差额。很多囚犯在进入监狱前，就已经在贫困、种族歧视和暴力中被剥夺了基本的人权。系统在他们生命的最早阶段就实施了暴力，然后在那道生物墙和阶级墙面前，指责他们无法通过一个简陋的、被政治干预的康复计划来“变好”。

这不是康复的失败，而是系统的成功。通过制造一个“危机 $ ightarrow$ 改革 $ ightarrow$ 再次危机”的闭环，权力机构可以持续地证明监狱的“必要性”，并以此为由扩建监狱、申请预算。在这种共谋中，囚犯的绝望成了系统运行的燃料。

These letters expose a classic scam: the state peddles a warm narrative of 'rehabilitation' at the cultural level, while ensuring prisoners can never truly reintegrate through structural risk management and resource deprivation. 'Rehabilitation' here is a weaponized concept used to mask a cold truth—the core KPI of the prison system is not to heal, but to perform 'punishment' and 'deterrence.'

The shift in 1984 and the subsequent stripping of probation officers' autonomy is a textbook move of meta-violence. When 'risk' replaces 'person-centred' approaches as the driving force, the probation officer ceases to be a guide and becomes a surveillance device. The prisoner is no longer a human with a life history, but a risk metric to be monitored. Once defined as a 'risk,' every expression by the individual is preset as a threat—this presumption is itself a form of structural violence.

The most profound insight comes from Joe Sim: how can you 'rehabilitate' someone who was never 'habilitated' in the first place? This is a brutal gap between Potential and Actual. Many prisoners were stripped of basic human rights through poverty, racism, and violence long before they entered a cell. The system inflicted violence at the earliest stages of their lives, and then, facing the biological and class walls, blames them for failing a flimsy, politically interfered-with recovery plan.

This is not a failure of rehabilitation; it is the success of the system. By manufacturing a cycle of 'crisis $ ightarrow$ reform $ ightarrow$ crisis,' power structures continuously justify the 'necessity' of prisons to expand facilities and secure budgets. In this complicity, the desperation of the incarcerated becomes the fuel for the system's operation.
