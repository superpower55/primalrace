选票的成色由谁定义Who Gets to Decide a Ballot Counts

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 Al Jazeera ↗ 2026-11-02 § 链接
新闻摘要特朗普政府在中期选举前一天向最高法院提出紧急请求，要求加州暂缓统计超过2万名被指非美国公民的邮寄选票。加州称这份由国土安全部编制的名单不可靠，可能导致数千名合格选民被剥夺投票权。此前联邦法官和联邦上诉法院均已驳回该请求。
The newsOne day before the midterms, the Trump administration filed an emergency request asking the Supreme Court to stop California from counting mail-in ballots from more than 20,000 voters it claims are not US citizens. California says the Department of Homeland Security list is unreliable and could disenfranchise thousands of eligible voters. A federal judge and an appeals court had already rejected the request.
谁有权宣布一张选票不算数，谁就握着解释权
Whoever can declare a ballot invalid holds the power to define the outcome.

中期选举前一天，联邦政府跑去最高法院，要求把两万张已经投出的选票先扣下来。加州说，这么做可能让几千个合格选民投的票作废。这件事的重点不在那两万张票，而在于「谁有权宣布一张票不算数」。

司法部要的是：把国土安全部名单上的人投的票先搁置，等公民身份核实了再说。问题在于，这份名单本身就不可靠。联邦法官 David Carter 上周驳回时写得很清楚：行政部门不能凭未经证实的指控去否定选票、去威胁一场正在进行的选举。联邦上诉法院周五也驳回了。两级法院都说不行，它还是去了最高法院。

投票从十月初就开始了。州政府说，要把这些票单独挑出来，几乎不可能。也就是说，这个请求就算被批准，执行的代价也落不到提出请求的人头上，而是落到那些已经投了票、正在等结果的人头上。

这是加尔通暴力三角里最典型的一层：结构层。它不流血，不喊口号，它改的是计数规则。而计数规则一改，「谁赢了」这件事的定义权就跟着改。报道里写得很直白：这场争端发生在共和党为保住国会控制权而战的时候。

再往下一层看，是文化层在给它铺路。特朗普反复声称存在大规模选民舞弊，而研究和州审计都显示，不合格投票——包括非公民投票——极为罕见。「舞弊」这个词被反复投放，投放到不需要证据也能起作用，因为它要的从来不是证据，是让人对计票本身起疑。疑心一起，扣票就成了「谨慎」，剥夺就成了「核实」。

这里面还有一层元暴力，藏得比较深。谁被怀疑？名单上的人。名单怎么来的？国土安全部编的。谁有权编这份名单、谁有权决定名单上的人先不算人？这条链条从头到尾没有经过任何被列名者的同意，也没有给他们一个在投票日之前说话的位置。解释权在谁手里，谁就决定了谁的表达算数、谁的表达是噪音。

法院两次说不，它第三次还去。这不是在打官司，这是在测试：测试那道「选票算数」的底线到底有多硬。测试的成本由加州那些已经投了票的人承担，收益归提出请求的人。

如果这个请求最后被驳回，差额不会自动补回来。那两万张票的持有人，已经被公开标记成「疑似非公民」了一回。这个标记不需要法院批准，它已经生效了。

The day before the midterms, the federal government went to the Supreme Court to have twenty thousand already-cast ballots held back. California says doing so could void the votes of thousands of eligible people. The point here isn't the twenty thousand ballots. The point is who gets to declare a ballot doesn't count.

The Justice Department wants ballots from people on a Department of Homeland Security list set aside until their citizenship is confirmed. The problem: the list itself is unreliable. Judge David Carter, rejecting the request last week, wrote that the executive cannot discount votes and threaten an ongoing election on unproven allegations. A federal appeals court rejected it again on Friday. Two courts said no. It went to the Supreme Court anyway.

Voting has been under way since early October. State officials say separating those ballots out would be nearly impossible. So even if the request succeeds, the cost of executing it doesn't land on the people who filed it. It lands on people who already voted and are waiting for a result.

This is the structural layer of the violence triangle, in its purest form. No blood, no slogans. It changes the counting rule. And once the counting rule changes, so does the power to define who won. The report says it plainly: this is happening while Republicans fight to keep control of Congress.

Underneath that sits the cultural layer, laying the track. Trump has repeatedly claimed widespread voter fraud, while studies and state audits show ineligible voting, including by noncitizens, is rare. The word 「fraud」 gets deployed until it works without evidence, because it was never asking for evidence. It was asking for doubt. Once doubt is in place, holding ballots becomes 「caution」, and disenfranchisement becomes 「verification」.

There is a meta layer too, buried deeper. Who is suspected? The people on the list. Who made the list? Homeland Security. Who gets to decide that the people on it don't count yet? The same chain, start to finish, with no consent from anyone named and no place for them to speak before election day. Whoever holds the interpretive power decides whose expression counts and whose is noise.

Two courts said no. It went back a third time. This isn't litigation. It's a stress test: how hard is the line that says a ballot counts? The cost of the test falls on Californians who already voted. The benefit goes to the people who filed it.

If the request is ultimately denied, the gap doesn't close by itself. The twenty thousand have already been publicly marked as 「suspected noncitizens」 once. That mark didn't need a court's approval. It's already in effect.
