算法喂养下的政治表演：谁在制造“真实”？Political Performance in the Age of Algorithms: Who Manufactures 'Truth'?

国际 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-15 § 链接
社交媒体不是沟通工具，而是权力的认知入口与武器化表达。
Social media is not a communication tool, but a weaponized entry point for cognitive control.

这篇文章在庆祝社交媒体从业者的“职业化”，但我看到的是一场关于认知入口的全面围猎。当政治人物像 Andy Burnham 这样开始大规模招募社交媒体专业团队，甚至为之设立高薪岗位时，这绝非简单的“与时俱进”，而是一次精准的 weaponization。他们不再关心政策本身，而是在关心 TikTok 的 hook 怎么写，自拍角度怎么选。这意味着，政治正在从“治理”转向“表演”，从“共识”转向“算法喂养”。

这种转变本质上是夺取解释权的战争。通过一个由编辑、制片和战略师组成的后台 squad，政治人物可以制造一种名为“亲民”或“真实”的虚假叙事。这种被精心计算过的“真实”，实际上是最高级的 cultural violence——它用碎片化的快感掩盖了结构性的失权，用算法的精准投喂替代了公共空间的严肃讨论。当人们在 TikTok 上看到从弹坑传来的自拍时，他们以为在接触真相，实际上他们只是在消费被加工过的“真实”。

最讽刺的是，文中提到的“教育上层” (educating up) 的挑战，其实是共谋者在寻找更高效的作弊方式。C-suite 和政治领袖并不在乎受众的真实需求，他们只在乎如何通过算法操纵来维持自己的存在性。这种机制将公共讨论彻底变成了存在性战争的博弈场：谁能掌握算法的规律，谁就定义了什么是“事实”。

所谓的“全球广场” (global town square) 只是一个浪漫的 scam。真正的广场是互不掠夺的公正表达，而现在的社交媒体是权力者通过认知入口对大众进行的大规模精神规训。当政治变成了 content creation，民主就变成了流量博弈。

This article celebrates the 'professionalization' of social media, but what I see is a systematic siege of cognitive entry points. When politicians like Andy Burnham recruit squads of social media professionals with high salaries, it is not 'modernization'—it is weaponization. They no longer care about policy; they care about the TikTok 'hook' and the perfect selfie angle. Politics is shifting from governance to performance, and from consensus to algorithmic feeding.

This shift is essentially a war over the right to interpret. Through a backstage squad of editors and strategists, politicians manufacture a curated narrative of 'authenticity.' This calculated 'truth' is a form of high-level cultural violence—it uses fragmented pleasure to mask structural disempowerment and replaces serious public discourse with algorithmic echo chambers. When audiences see a selfie from a crater on TikTok, they believe they are witnessing truth, while they are merely consuming a processed product.

Ironically, the mentioned challenge of 'educating up' is simply co-conspirators seeking more efficient ways to cheat. C-suites and political leaders do not care about the genuine needs of their audience; they only care about maintaining their existence through algorithmic manipulation. This mechanism turns public discussion into an existential war: whoever masters the algorithm defines the 'facts.'

The notion of a 'global town square' is a romantic scam. A real square requires just expressions and mutual non-predation, whereas today's social media is a tool for the powerful to implement mass mental discipline via cognitive entry points. When politics becomes content creation, democracy becomes a game of traffic.
