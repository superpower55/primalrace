死掉的嫌疑人与被抹除的受害者The Dead Suspect and the Erased Victim

性别 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-22 § 链接
当元暴力在司法博弈中闭环，受害者的痛苦被彻底客体化。
When meta-violence closes the loop in judicial gaming, the victim's suffering is fully objectified.

这是一个典型的关于“解释权”的权力游戏。在这条新闻的叙事结构里，原本的受害者——那个在2017年被推向巴士的女性——已经彻底消失了。她不再是一个活生生的人，而成了定义嫌疑人 Nicholas Brandram 身份的一个标签：“Putney pusher”案的受害者。她的痛苦被简化为一个案件编号，成了这个男人生死博弈的背景板。

现在，叙事重心发生了剧烈的偏移。焦点变成了 Brandram 的死亡、他的家庭晚餐、他姐妹的控诉，以及警方是否对他造成了“巨大的压力”。这是一种极其隐蔽的 cultural violence：当一个被指控实施性别暴力的男性死亡时，整个公共讨论迅速地将他重新定义为“被体制压迫的受害者”。他的主体性被找回来了，而那个被推入车流的女性的主体性在九年前就被剥夺了，现在则被彻底抹除了。

Brandram 的家属在媒体上强调他“不符合描述”，试图通过这种表达来夺回他的名誉。但请注意，这种博弈的本质是 masculine-centric narrative 的内部消化。警方的失误（如果确实存在）和嫌疑人的自杀成了新闻的主角，而那个被攻击的女性是否得到了救济？她是否在法律上获得了公正？这些在 current expression 中完全不重要。

Violence = Potential − Actual。对于受害者来说，她本应获得的公正（Potential）与她实际得到的结果（Actual）之间的差额，因为嫌疑人的死亡而变成了一个永恒的空洞。这个空洞被 Brandram 姐妹的眼泪和 IOPC 的调查程序填满了。这就是元暴力的最高效之处：它能让施暴者的消亡，变成一场关于“程序公正”的悲剧，从而让真正的暴力在沉默中合法化。

This is a textbook case of the power game over the 'right to interpret.' In the narrative structure of this news, the original victim—the woman pushed into the path of a bus in 2017—has completely vanished. She is no longer a living human being, but a mere label used to define the identity of Nicholas Brandram: the victim in the 'Putney pusher' case. Her agony has been reduced to a case number, a backdrop for this man's existential gamble.

Now, the narrative center of gravity has shifted violently. The focus is on Brandram's death, his missed family meal, the accusations of his sisters, and whether the police exerted 'immense pressure' on him. This is a subtle form of cultural violence: when a man accused of gender-based violence dies, the public discourse rapidly re-defines him as a 'victim of the system.' His subjectivity is restored, while the subjectivity of the woman pushed into traffic was stripped away nine years ago and is now entirely erased.

Brandram's family emphasizes in the media that he 'did not match the description,' attempting to reclaim his reputation through this expression. But the essence of this game is the internal digestion of a masculine-centric narrative. The police failures (if any) and the suspect's suicide become the protagonists of the story, while the question of whether the attacked woman received justice or relief is treated as irrelevant in the current expression.

Violence = Potential − Actual. For the victim, the gap between the justice she should have received (Potential) and the actual outcome (Actual) has become an eternal void due to the suspect's death. This void is filled with the tears of Brandram's sisters and the procedural investigations of the IOPC. This is the efficiency of meta-violence: it transforms the demise of a perpetrator into a tragedy of 'procedural fairness,' allowing the original violence to be legitimized through silence.
