希望的叙事与权力的旧壳Narratives of Hope and the Old Shell of Power

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-29 § 链接
用诚实换取希望的叙事，本质上是结构暴力在PR层面的重新包装。
Trading honesty for hope is just a PR repackaging of structural violence.

Burnham 站在利物浦的演讲台上，用一套标准的“诚实”叙事试图重启英国的希望。他把去工业化、私有化和 Brexit 统称为“错误的道路”，试图通过回顾 1945 年的集体主义辉煌来建立一种情感契约。但这正是典型的 cultural violence 运作方式：通过定义一个共同的“敌人”（既得利益者）和一个虚幻的“黄金时代”，来掩盖权力结构在本质上并未发生位移的事实。

最典型的例子就是对 Council Housing 的承诺。Burnham 将住房描述为“解锁抱负”的钥匙，这在叙事上极其动人，但在 structural 层面，这依然是在父权制和国家机器的框架内分配“恩赐”。当他谈论“每个人都有屋顶遮头”时，他是在扮演一个慈父般的保护者，而非在拆除导致住房匮乏的权力垄断。这种表达方式是典型的假.最优解：它给受害者提供了一种“被看见”的心理补偿，但没有触及资源定价权的根本转移。

至于 Angela Rayner 提出的限制房东许可费（permission fees），这更像是一场表演性的让步。把一个原本由房东随心所欲定价的 scam 变成一个由政府监管上限的 scam，这改变的是手续费的额度，而不是房东与租客之间极不对等的权力关系。这不过是在 structural violence 的边缘修剪枝叶，好让整体的盘剥结构看起来更“文明”。

当一个政客说“首先必须诚实”时，他其实是在通过出卖一部分次要的真实，来换取对核心解释权的垄断。在这种叙事里，工人阶级的 aspiration 再次被定义为“获得一个稳定的居所”，而不是“拥有定义生活方式的权力”。

Standing in Liverpool, Burnham attempts to reboot British hope using a standard narrative of 'honesty.' By labeling deindustrialisation, privatisation, and Brexit as the 'wrong path' and evoking the collective glory of 1945, he is building an emotional contract. This is a classic operation of cultural violence: defining a common 'enemy' (vested interests) and a mythical 'golden age' to mask the fact that the power structure remains fundamentally unchanged.

His focus on Council Housing is a prime example. Describing a home as the key to 'unlocking aspiration' is emotionally potent, but at the structural level, it remains a distribution of 'grace' within the framework of a masculine-centric state. When he promises a roof over every head, he performs the role of a paternal protector rather than dismantling the monopolies of power that created the crisis. This is a fake optimal expression: providing psychological compensation to the victims without shifting the actual pricing power of resources.

As for Angela Rayner's cap on permission fees, it feels like a performative concession. Turning a scam where landlords price arbitrarily into a scam with a government-mandated ceiling doesn't change the fundamentally asymmetric power relation between landlord and tenant. It is merely trimming the edges of structural violence to make the overall system of exploitation look more 'civilised.'

When a politician says 'first must come honesty,' he is trading a fraction of secondary truths to monopolize the core interpretation of reality. In this narrative, working-class aspiration is once again defined as 'securing a stable home' rather than 'possessing the power to define one's own life.'
