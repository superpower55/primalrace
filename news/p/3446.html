用“公共卫生”之名，完成一次精准的身体殖民Colonizing the Body in the Name of 'Public Health'

性别 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-08 § 链接
当公共卫生数据成为监视工具，医疗就变成了暴力的共谋。
When public health data becomes a surveillance tool, medicine becomes an accomplice to violence.

一个黑人女性成为了 CDC 的负责人，这在主流叙事里是典型的“进步”PR。但如果你把目光从表型 (Phenotype) 移开，看她与反堕胎议员 Hawley 的对话，你会发现这不过是一场精心设计的身份掩护。所谓的“第一位黑人女性主管”，在这里成了某种政治掩体，掩盖了她正准备将公共卫生系统武器化 (Weaponization) 的事实。

注意那个词：Abortion Surveillance（堕胎监视）。在公共卫生领域，Surveillance 本意是监测，但当它与“committed”（犯罪/实施）这种带有强烈审判色彩的词汇绑定时，它的性质就变了。这不再是为了改善医疗资源分配，而是在构建一个针对女性身体的精准捕获系统。所谓的“准确定义”和“剔除隐藏数据”，本质上是在为后续的刑事追责建立数据库。这是一种典型的 structural violence：通过定义“什么是真实数据”，将医疗记录转化为呈堂证供。

这正是元暴力 (Meta Violence) 的运作方式——由男性主导的权力结构制定规则，然后招募一个具有“正确身份”的共谋者来执行。Schwartz 扮演的角色就是这种共谋者：她通过承诺提供“准确数据”来换取在体制内的生存最优解。她可能认为自己在走钢丝，但实际上她已经成为了一个工具，将女性的生殖健康从“医疗权利”降级为“监管对象”。

当一个国家的最高公共卫生机构开始讨论如何“揪出”被隐藏的堕胎数据时，它已经不再是对抗疾病，而是在通过数据采集完成一次对原初种族 (Primal Race) 的再次殖民。在这种叙事下，怀孕的女性不再是病人，而是潜在的罪犯。

A Black woman heading the CDC is a classic 'progress' PR move in mainstream narratives. But if you look past the phenotype and analyze her exchange with anti-abortion senator Hawley, it's clear this is merely an identity cover. The 'first Black woman director' serves as a political shield, masking the fact that she is preparing to weaponize the public health system.

Note the term: 'Abortion Surveillance.' In public health, surveillance is about monitoring, but when paired with words like 'committed'—a term loaded with criminal intent—its nature shifts. This is no longer about improving healthcare resource allocation; it is the construction of a precision capture system for female bodies. The pursuit of 'accurate definitions' and 'uncovering hidden data' is essentially building a database for future criminal prosecution. This is a textbook example of structural violence: using the power to define 'truth' to turn medical records into evidence.

This is exactly how meta-violence operates—a masculine-centric power structure sets the rules and recruits a collaborator with the 'correct identity' to execute them. Schwartz is that accomplice; she trades the promise of 'accurate data' for her own optimal expression of survival within the system. She may think she is walking a tightrope, but she has already become a tool, downgrading reproductive health from a 'medical right' to a 'regulatory object.'

When the highest public health agency in a country discusses how to 'extract' hidden abortion data, it is no longer fighting disease. It is completing another colonization of the Primal Race through data collection. Under this narrative, pregnant people are no longer patients; they are potential criminals.
