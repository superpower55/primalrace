条款不是法外之地，更不是霸凌的掩体T&Cs are not a Shield for Corporate Bullying

哲学 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-07 § 链接
用 T&Cs 掩盖单方面违约，是典型的结构性权力霸凌。
Using T&Cs to mask unilateral breach is a textbook case of structural power bullying.

这起所谓的“退款纠纷”，本质上是一场极其低劣的权力博弈。The Gymnastics Academy 的逻辑很简单：我定义规则，我执行规则，如果你不满意，那是你没读懂我的规则。这种通过 T&Cs (Terms and Conditions) 构建的认知壁垒，是典型的 structural violence。它试图将“单方面变更合同”这一违约行为，通过一套只有公司能随时修改、而消费者无法访问的在线协议，合法化为一种“权利”。

最令人作呕的是其法律团队的 response。他们先是用“法律实体分离”这种典型的 corporate scam 来玩文字游戏，试图通过切割主体来逃避责任；随后又搬出 Trading Standards 的审核作为背书，试图将“经过审核”等同于“绝对正义”。这是一种典型的 meta violence：通过垄断对“合规”的解释权，让受害者怀疑自己的正当诉求是否在“无理取闹”。

好在这次博弈中，消费者通过银行的 chargeback 机制夺回了资金。但这并非制度的胜利，而是一次技术性的对冲。真正的暴力差额依然存在：那三个工作日被剥夺的 childcare 资源，以及面对企业法律机器时的无力感。只要这种“由强者定义事实”的共谋逻辑依然在商业领域通行，任何所谓的“消费者权益”都只是在强者允许的范围内进行的小打小闹。

This so-called 'refund dispute' is essentially a crude power game. The Gymnastics Academy's logic is simple: I define the rules, I execute the rules, and if you're unhappy, it's because you failed to understand them. This cognitive barrier constructed via T&Cs is a classic form of structural violence. It attempts to legitimize a breach of contract—unilaterally changing the terms—by wrapping it in an online agreement that only the company can edit and the consumer cannot even access.

What is truly repulsive is the response from their legal team. They first employed a corporate scam by playing word games with 'separate legal entities' to evade responsibility. Then, they invoked the vetting of Trading Standards as a shield, attempting to equate 'compliance' with 'absolute justice.' This is a manifestation of meta violence: by monopolizing the interpretation of 'legality,' they make the victim question whether their legitimate grievance is merely 'unreasonable.'

Fortunately, the consumer reclaimed their funds through a bank chargeback. However, this is not a systemic victory, but a technical hedge. The gap of violence remains: the lost childcare resources for three working days and the helplessness felt when facing a corporate legal machine. As long as the complicity of 'the strong defining the facts' prevails in business, any so-called 'consumer rights' are merely minor concessions granted within the boundaries allowed by the powerful.
