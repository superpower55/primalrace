AI 无法投出完美的 doosra，但它可以定义什么是“优秀”AI Can't Bowl the Perfect Doosra, But It Can Define 'Excellence'

科技 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-16 § 链接
技术升级的本质不是能力的增强，而是评价体系的垄断。
The essence of tech upgrades is not enhancing ability, but monopolizing the evaluation system.

这篇关于板球 AI 的报道在讨论一个典型的 scam：所谓的“辅助”与“增强”。当分析师 Wilde 说 AI 让他的工作更 autonomous 时，他其实是在描述一种权力的移交。AI 并没有创造新的板球技巧，它只是在用数据重新定义什么是“正确”的投球角度和击球风险。当“最优解”被算法量化，人类球员就不再是运动员，而成了执行算法指令的生物插件。

最危险的部分在于 EquiPlay 这种所谓“标准化”的评估平台。它试图通过 AI 将教练的判断“可视化”并消除 bias。这听起来像 good_news，但实际上是 structural violence 的升级版。当一个 15 岁的球员被标记为“进度下滑”并进入“干预计划”时，他的主体性就被简化成了一个数据点。这种“标准化”本质上是元暴力的延伸——由掌握定义权的人决定谁是“天才”，谁是“失败者”，而那些无法被量化的“文化、性格、更衣室平衡”则成了权力者保留的最后一块私产，用来在算法之外继续实施主观的筛选。

至于新闻业的 AI 化，那简直是 cultural violence 的快进版。当学生开始扮演 AI 的编辑，当报道变得“高效”且“符合预期”，语言的指纹消失了，表达被 flatten 了。这正是武器化叙事的终极目标：抹除个体表达的独特性，让所有声音都像 AI 生成的那段“深刻”结尾一样，wafty 且 acceptable。这种“可接受性”就是最深的枷锁，因为它让人们在不知不觉中接受了由算法定义的平庸作为现实。

This piece on cricket AI peddles a classic scam: the narrative of 'assistance' and 'enhancement.' When analyst Wilde claims AI makes him more autonomous, he is actually describing a transfer of power. AI isn't creating new cricket skills; it's using data to redefine what constitutes the 'correct' bowling angle or batting risk. Once the 'optimal solution' is quantified by algorithms, human players cease to be athletes and become biological plugins executing algorithmic commands.

The most dangerous element is the 'standardization' of platforms like EquiPlay. It claims to make coaching judgment 'visible' and eliminate bias. While framed as good_news, this is an upgrade of structural violence. When a 15-year-old player is flagged as 'dipping' and put into an 'intervention,' their subjectivity is reduced to a data point. This 'standardization' is an extension of meta-violence—those who hold the power of definition decide who is a 'prodigy' and who is a 'failure,' while 'culture' and 'character' remain the last private reserves for the powerful to exercise subjective screening outside the algorithm.

As for AI in journalism, it's cultural violence in fast-forward. As students shift from writers to AI editors and reports become 'efficient' and 'expected,' the fingerprints of individual expression vanish; discourse is flattened. This is the ultimate goal of weaponized narratives: erasing the uniqueness of expression so that all voices become as 'wafty and acceptable' as that AI-generated profound conclusion. This 'acceptability' is the deepest shackle, as it tricks people into accepting algorithmically defined mediocrity as reality.
