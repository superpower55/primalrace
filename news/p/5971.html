墙里长出个Hugging FaceA Hugging Face Grows Behind the Wall

中国科技 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 Rest of World ↗ 2026-10-31 § 链接
被封锁逼出来的替代品，不是竞争，是收容。
A substitute born of blockade isn't competition. It's containment.

先把这条新闻的账面数字摆清楚：Hugging Face 上托管着三百多万个开放模型，ModelScope 十七万，MoArk 两万。这不是三家在同一个赛道上赛跑，这是一个人被关进院子之后，院子里自己长出来的两棵树。报道说得很直白——2023 年监管把 Hugging Face 封了，才打开了一个国内市场。所以这两家平台的起点不是产品比 Hugging Face 好，是用户没得选。

OSChina 的 CEO 徐勇那句话值得单独拎出来看：不是所有人都能一直用 VPN。这句话是整篇报道里最诚实的一句，也是它最不诚实的一句。诚实在于它承认了墙的存在和墙的成本；不诚实在于它把墙的成本包装成了创业的机会。一个昆明开发者说得更直接：Hugging Face 和 GitHub 是开源世界的默认选项，它们提供更多样的东西，国内平台得先说服我们为什么要用。这就是真实的市场态度——不是不需要，是被挡住。

报道里最有意思的结构在这里：政府一边封 Hugging Face，一边默许 AI 实验室绕过封锁把中国模型传到墙外去。封的是入口，放的是出口。这不是矛盾，这是精确的算计——国内用户不能看外面的，但中国的模型必须让外面看见。同一堵墙，对内是筛子，对外是喇叭。

这套操作的本质是控制认知入口，而不是建设生态。ModelScope 办黑客松、在杭州开联合办公空间、MoArk 派工程师把模型适配到国产芯片上——这些都是真实的投入，我不否认。但它们的驱动力不是“我们能做出更好的东西”，而是“美国随时可能掐断我们”。报道里 Nvidia 在收购 Hugging Face 的监管文件里就明确标了风险：监管可能禁止中国模型在该平台分享。Mercator 的研究员 Arcesati 说得清楚，中国政府的理想状态是整个 AI 技术栈尽可能国产化。国产化在这里不是技术目标，是安全目标——安全的对象不是用户，是政权自己。

所以这条新闻的正确读法不是“中国有了自己的 Hugging Face”，而是“中国在给自己造一个不会被人关掉的门”。门后面的人以为自己有了选择，其实选择早在 2023 年就被替他们做完了。

真正的问题在于：一个靠封锁获得用户的平台，没有动力去赢得用户。Hugging Face 要跟全球开发者竞争，ModelScope 只要比 VPN 稳定就行。这不是说 ModelScope 做不出好东西——十七万个模型是实打实的产出——而是说它的成功标准被墙重新定义了。墙在，它就是必需品；墙没了，它就得回答那个昆明开发者的问题：凭什么用你。

最后一个细节：报道说 GitHub 2013 年被短暂封锁，程序员抗议，后来恢复了。Hugging Face 封了三年，没恢复，也没听说有大规模抗议。不是程序员变顺从了，是他们学会了用 VPN，然后把这件事当成日常。这才是最彻底的胜利——不是让人用不了，是让人习惯用不了。

Start with the numbers the story actually gives: Hugging Face hosts over 3 million open models. ModelScope has 170,000. MoArk has 20,000. This isn't three platforms racing on the same track. This is two trees growing in a yard after someone locked the gate. The story says it plainly: regulators blocked Hugging Face in 2023, and that opened a domestic market. So the starting point for these platforms isn't a better product. It's users with nowhere else to go.

Xu Yong, CEO of OSChina, gets the most honest line in the piece: not everyone can use a VPN all the time. That sentence is honest about the wall's existence and its cost. It's dishonest in how it repackages that cost as entrepreneurial opportunity. An independent developer in Kunming is more direct: Hugging Face and GitHub are the default in open source, they offer greater variety, and domestic platforms need to convince us why we should use them. That's the real market attitude. Not preference. Blockage.

The most interesting structure in the story: the government blocks Hugging Face while quietly tolerating AI labs that bypass the block to share Chinese models with the world. The entrance is sealed; the exit is open. This isn't contradiction. It's precise calculation. Domestic users can't look out, but Chinese models must be seen outside. Same wall, sieve facing in, loudspeaker facing out.

The logic here is controlling the cognitive entrance, not building an ecosystem. ModelScope runs hackathons and opens a coworking space in Hangzhou. MoArk sends engineers to adapt models to run on Chinese chips. These are real investments and I won't pretend otherwise. But the driver isn't "we can build something better." It's "the U.S. could cut us off at any moment." The story notes that Nvidia's regulatory filing about acquiring Hugging Face flagged the risk that regulators could ban Chinese models from being shared there. Arcesati from Mercator says Beijing's ideal is for the entire AI stack, including software tools and libraries, to be indigenized as much as possible. Indigenization here isn't a technical goal. It's a security goal, and the thing being secured isn't the user. It's the regime.

So the correct reading of this story isn't "China has its own Hugging Face." It's "China is building itself a door that can't be shut from outside." The people behind that door think they have options. The options were chosen for them in 2023.

The real problem: a platform that gets its users through blockade has no incentive to earn them. Hugging Face has to compete with global developers. ModelScope only has to be more stable than a VPN. That doesn't mean ModelScope can't produce good work. 170,000 models is real output. It means the wall has redefined what success looks like. With the wall, it's a necessity. Without it, it has to answer the Kunming developer's question: why use you?

One last detail: the story says GitHub was briefly blocked in 2013, programmers made an outcry, and access was restored. Hugging Face has been blocked for three years. No restoration, no reported mass protest. Not because programmers became obedient. Because they learned to use a VPN and then learned to treat it as normal. That's the most complete victory available. Not making people unable to access something. Making them used to not having it.
