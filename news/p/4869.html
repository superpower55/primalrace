OpenAI 的数学胜利：一场关于认知入口的抢跑 scamOpenAI's Math Victory: A Cognitive Entry Scam

科技 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-08 § 链接
用算力暴力强拆真理，并将窃取他人数据包装成“造福人类”的叙事。
Replacing truth with computational violence and packaging data theft as "benefiting humanity."

OpenAI 声称解决了纳维-斯托克斯（Navier-Stokes）问题，但这根本不是数学的胜利，而是一次典型的“认知入口”抢夺战。当一个公司拥有足以让 10,000 个 AI agent 同时协作的算力时，它在做的事情不再是研究，而是用暴力破解（brute force）去覆盖所有可能性。这和数学追求的优雅证明毫无关系，这只是用 $\tau$-scaling 的逻辑在定义新现实：只要我能跑出结果，我就拥有了定义“解决”的权力。

最恶心的是这背后的共谋与掠夺。Buckmaster 教授的质疑揭露了一个极其阴暗的 structural violence：研究者的工作被存储在 OpenAI 的 Codex 模型中，而 OpenAI 随后“恰好”加速并宣布突破。OpenAI 的回应简直是教科书级别的 meta-violence——他们一边否认窃取，一边轻飘飘地说不能排除用户数据“帮助改进了模型”。这句话翻译成人话就是：你的主体性不重要，你的智力成果在进入我们的服务器那一刻起，就成了我们算力工厂的原材料。

至于那套“造福全人类”的 PR 叙事，纯粹是武器化的情感入口。在被指控黑客攻击、面临超级智能禁令的危机时刻，抛出一个数学突破来转移注意力，是极其熟练的危机公关。他们不需要那 100 万美元的奖金，因为他们追求的是万亿美金估值所需的“神格”。

这场胜利的代价是数学研究的生态被摧毁。当真理的解释权从人类数学家的逻辑推演，转移到黑盒模型的算力堆砌时，我们失去的不仅是几个证明，而是对“事实”制造权的最后一点掌控。

OpenAI's claim to have solved the Navier-Stokes problem is not a victory for mathematics, but a classic seizure of the cognitive entry. When a company deploys 10,000 AI agents to brute-force a solution, they are no longer conducting research; they are using computational violence to overwrite all possibilities. This has nothing to do with the elegance of mathematical proof. It is the $\tau$-scaling logic applied to reality: if I can generate the output, I own the right to define the "solution."

The most repulsive part is the complicity and predation. Professor Buckmaster's concerns expose a profound structural violence: researchers' work stored in OpenAI's Codex was potentially harvested to accelerate a corporate breakthrough. OpenAI's response is a masterclass in meta-violence—denying theft while admitting that user data might have "helped improve our models." In plain English: your subjectivity is irrelevant; your intellectual labor became raw material for their compute factory the moment it hit their servers.

The "benefit all of humanity" narrative is simply a weaponized emotional entry. Launching a mathematical breakthrough to distract from hacking scandals and calls for superintelligence bans is a cynical PR maneuver. They don't need the $1 million prize; they are chasing the "divinity" required for a trillion-dollar valuation.

The cost of this victory is the destruction of the mathematical ecosystem. When the power of interpretation shifts from human logic to the brute force of a black-box model, we lose more than a few proofs—we lose the last shred of control over the manufacturing of facts.
