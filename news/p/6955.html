居留权按收入定价，护理工豁免是补丁Residency Priced by Income, Care Worker Exemption Is a Patch

国际 结构层 · 文化层 Al Jazeera ↗ 2026-11-03 § 链接
新闻摘要英国修改移民规则，多数外籍劳工申请永久居留的等待期从五年延长至十年，乘小船抵英的难民最长需等三十年；年收入超过五万英镑者仍可五年申请。内政大臣马哈茂德宣布护理工作者豁免，满足签证条件且年收入至少两万五千英镑者仍可五年定居。新规明年生效，并追溯适用于已在英工作者。
The newsThe UK has doubled the wait for permanent residency for most foreign workers to 10 years, with refugees arriving by small boat facing up to 30 years. Those earning over £50,000 can still apply after five years. Home Secretary Shabana Mahmood exempted care workers earning at least £25,000. The rules take effect next year and apply retroactively.
定居权被明码标价，护理工豁免只是把伤口贴上一块胶布
Settlement rights now come with a price tag; the care worker exemption is a bandage on a wound.

「定居不该是自动的，它应该被挣得。」马哈茂德在议会说这句话的时候，挣得的单位是英镑。年收入五万以上五年，十二万五以上三年，其余人十年，中位工资三万九。这不是移民政策，这是一份价目表：谁能成为「我们」，按年薪分档。

永久居留权在英国意味着工作、上学、用国民保健署、领福利。把这些权利和收入挂钩，等于宣布：一个在仓库、在厨房、在清洁公司上班的人，十年之内不算这个社会的人。而新规追溯适用于已经在英国的人——他们当初是按五年规则来的，现在规则在他们脚下被抽走。契约可以单方面改写，这就是结构暴力最干净的形态：没有施暴者，只有「规则调整」。

护理工的豁免被写成了仁慈。工会和工党内部的反对声浪太大，政府退了一步：满足签证条件、年收入至少两万五的护理工可以五年定居。注意这个门槛——护理行业本来就长期招不满人，这批人多在疫情早期抵英，填补脱欧后欧洲工人离开的空缺。他们被叫来的时候是「回答我们的召唤」，现在被留下来是因为不留下整个行业会塌。这不是承认他们的价值，是承认他们不可替代的劳动力价格。

真正被放弃的是难民。走「新的安全合法途径」的五年，其他难民二十年，工作或上学减到十年，乘小船来的最长三十年。三十年是什么概念？一个人二十岁渡海，拿到定居资格时已经五十岁。 Reform UK 把小船描绘成危机，反移民活动者在收容设施外抗议，政府用三十年来回应这份压力。马哈茂德说这会省下数十亿英镑的福利支出——省下的钱，是从那些还没被允许成为「人」的人身上扣的。

还有那串取消资格的条件：犯罪记录、英语不「达到高标准」、欠政府钱、国民保险缴不满三年。每一条单独看都合理，合在一起是一套筛选机制，筛掉的是最没有议价能力的人。英语标准由谁定、欠款怎么算，解释权全在签发方手里。

最讽刺的是那句「贡献最多的人定居最快」。护理工贡献的是命，拿的是两万五；金融城的人贡献的是数字，拿的是十二万五，三年。贡献这个词在这里被彻底武器化——它不是描述你做了什么，是描述你值多少。

护理工的五年豁免是一块补丁，缝在一件正在缩水的衣服上。真正的问题不是谁被豁免，是谁有权力决定哪些人的十年可以变成三十年，而这项权力从来没有被交到那些等待的人手里。

"Settlement should not be automatic. It should be earned," Shabana Mahmood told Parliament. The unit of earning is pounds. Over £50,000 a year: five years. Over £125,000: three. Everyone else: ten. The median salary is about £39,000. This is not an immigration policy. It is a price list, and it decides who gets to become "us."

Indefinite leave to remain carries the right to work, study, use the NHS, claim benefits. Tying it to income announces that someone stacking shelves, cooking, cleaning does not count as a member of this society for a decade. And the rules apply retroactively to people already in the country, who came under the five-year promise. The contract is rewritten under their feet. This is structural violence at its cleanest: no perpetrator, only a "rules adjustment."

The care worker exemption is written as mercy. Unions and parts of Labour kicked up a fight, so the government stepped back: care workers meeting their visa conditions and earning at least £25,000 can settle in five years. Look at that threshold. The sector has long struggled to fill vacancies; many arrived in the early 2020s to fill gaps left after Brexit. They were called in when needed, and they are kept now because the sector would collapse without them. This is not a recognition of their worth. It is a recognition of their irreplaceable labour price.

The refugees are the ones actually abandoned. Five years via "new safe and legal routes," twenty for others, ten if they work or study, up to thirty for anyone arriving by small boat. Thirty years means a person who crosses at twenty gets settlement at fifty. Reform UK has painted the boats as a crisis, activists protest outside asylum facilities, and the government answers with three decades. Mahmood says the overhaul saves billions in benefits. The savings are deducted from people not yet permitted to be persons.

Then the disqualifiers: a criminal record, English not "to a high standard," owing the government money, fewer than three years of national insurance contributions. Each looks reasonable alone. Together they are a filter, and what they filter out is anyone with the least bargaining power. Who defines the English standard, how the debt is calculated — the解释权 sits entirely with the issuer.

The cruelest line is "those who contribute most will settle fastest." Care workers contribute their bodies for £25,000. The City contributes numbers for £125,000 and three years. Contribution has been fully weaponised here: it no longer describes what you do, only what you are worth.

The five-year exemption for care workers is a patch sewn onto a shrinking garment. The real question is not who gets exempted. It is who holds the power to turn someone's ten years into thirty — and that power has never been handed to the people waiting.
