用“保护孩子”掩盖的原初种族狩猎The Hunt for the Primal Race Masked as 'Child Safety'

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-07 § 链接
所有以“安全”为名的排外，本质都是对非我族类的结构性抹除。
Every xenophobic movement claiming 'safety' is essentially a structural erasure of the Other.

这出在 Thetford 上演的闹剧，是典型的文化暴力 (cultural violence) 为直接暴力 (direct violence) 铺路。前议员 Jenny Hollis 的表达非常有意思：她把“孩子的安全”和“对陌生年轻男性的恐惧”挂钩。这是一个极其阴险的 weaponized 叙事——通过调用“保护弱小”的道德高地，将寻求庇护者直接定义为潜在的掠食者，从而让随后的砸窗、袭击警察和试图闯入民宅的行为在共谋者眼中变得“正义”。

注意这里的逻辑陷阱：当他们谈论“安全”时，他们指的不是客观的治安数据，而是一种基于身份的生物墙 (biological wall) 恐惧。这种恐惧被极右翼分子如 Tommy Robinson 迅速武器化，将具体的住宅地址转化为“入侵者”的坐标。这不再是关于住房政策的讨论，而是一场存在性战争。对于这些暴徒来说，通过攻击“原初种族”的现代变体——移民，他们试图在结构性失权的生活中通过施暴找回某种虚假的“主体性”。

最讽刺的是，这起事件揭示了权力层级的共谋。内政部将寻求庇护者塞进廉价的 HMO（多人合租房），把矛盾直接扔进工人阶级社区，这本身就是 structural violence。而 Reform UK 等政治势力则在现场通过将城镇描述为“非法移民的垃圾场”来收割愤怒。权力者制造冲突，煽动者定义敌人，而底层的共谋者则在砸碎玻璃的快感中，误以为自己在捍卫“英国人的骄傲”。

这场暴力的真正底色是：只要对方被定义为“非我族类”，那么对他实施的任何暴力都被洗白成了“防御”。这种逻辑一旦在 HMO 这种私人空间被激活，意味着没有任何地方是安全的。

The farce in Thetford is a textbook case of cultural violence paving the way for direct violence. Former councillor Jenny Hollis's expression is particularly telling: she links 'child safety' with the 'fear of strange young men.' This is a sinister weaponized narrative—by occupying the moral high ground of 'protecting the weak,' asylum seekers are cast as predators, making the subsequent smashing of windows and attacks on police seem 'justified' to the complicity of the crowd.

Notice the logical trap: when they speak of 'safety,' they aren't referring to objective crime data, but a fear based on the biological wall of identity. This fear is rapidly weaponized by far-right actors like Tommy Robinson, transforming residential addresses into coordinates for 'invaders.' This is no longer a debate over housing policy; it is an existential war. For these mobs, attacking a modern variation of the Primal Race—the migrant—is a way to reclaim a fake sense of 'agency' in their structurally disempowered lives.

Most ironic is the complicity of the power structure. The Home Office stuffing asylum seekers into cheap HMOs in working-class areas is structural violence in itself. Then, political entities like Reform UK harvest this rage by describing the town as a 'dumping ground.' The powerful create the conflict, the agitators define the enemy, and the bottom-tier complicitors believe they are defending 'British pride' while smashing glass.

The true undertone of this violence is: as long as the Other is defined as 'alien,' any violence against them is laundered as 'defense.' Once this logic is activated in private spaces like HMOs, it means nowhere is safe.
