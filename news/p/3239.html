战争红利：一场关于人类苦难的精准定价War Windfalls: The Precise Pricing of Human Misery

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-04 § 链接
暴力在结构层被转化为利润，在文化层被包装成“能源安全”。
Violence is converted into profit at the structural level and packaged as "energy security" at the cultural level.

这就是一个标准的 Violence = Potential − Actual 公式。当伊朗冲突爆发、气候危机导致热浪杀人时，全球能源价格的飙升并非随机的波动，而是一次精准的定价。八家石油巨头在三个月内卷走 930 亿美元，这意味着人类文明在面对生存威胁时的 Actual 状态（能源匮乏、气候灾难、账单激增），与本可达到的 Potential 状态（快速能源转型、气候修复）之间的差额，被直接兑换成了股东的 Dividend。

这场博弈中最令人作呕的共谋在于，这些公司在公开场合表演“能源安全”的责任感，私下里却在进行实质性的撤退。BP 将绿色能源预算从 50 亿削减至 20 亿，变卖太阳能和生物气业务，这种“fundamental reset”本质上是对气候共识的公开背叛。它们在利用战争制造的恐慌来掩盖对化石燃料依赖的刻意延长，将“人类苦难”武器化为一种认知入口：只要战争还在，只要短缺还在，那么继续榨取地球就是“可靠的供应”。

最讽刺的共谋发生在政治顶端。特朗普指责石油公司“钱赚太多”，这绝不是出于对底层民众的共情，而是一次典型的政治表演。在选举年，通过攻击一个被大众厌恶的符号（Big Oil）来刷存在感，是政治博弈中的最优解表达。但无论谁在台上，只要这个男性中心叙事主导的战争机器和资本逻辑不崩塌，这种将死亡转化为美金的元暴力就会持续运行。所谓的“人道主义”在每分钟 70 万美元的利润面前，不过是给地狱涂上的彩色糖衣。

This is a textbook application of the formula Violence = Potential − Actual. When the Iran conflict erupts and climate heatwaves kill, the surge in energy prices is not a random fluctuation, but a precise pricing mechanism. The $93bn amassed by eight oil giants in three months proves that the gap between our Actual state (energy poverty, climate chaos) and the Potential state (rapid transition, climate repair) has been directly converted into shareholder dividends.

The most repulsive complicity here is the performance of "energy security." While claiming responsibility, these firms are executing a strategic retreat. BP's "fundamental reset"—slashing its green budget from $5bn to $2bn and selling off solar assets—is a blatant betrayal of the climate consensus. They weaponize the narrative of war-induced scarcity as a cognitive entry point to justify the intentional prolongation of fossil fuel dependency. As long as the panic persists, extracting the planet is framed as "reliable production."

The peak of this complicity is found in the political arena. Trump accusing Big Oil of "making too much money" is not empathy; it is a calculated optimal expression for a flagging candidate in an election year. By attacking a universally hated symbol, he gains visibility without challenging the system. As long as the masculine-centric narrative of war machines and capital logic dominates, this meta-violence—converting death into dollars—will persist. In the face of $700,000 profit per minute, "humanitarianism" is nothing but colorful candy coating on a hellscape.
