One-Pot 的高效与“家庭”的隐形账单The Efficiency of One-Pot and the Invisible Bill of 'Family'

性别 结构层 · 文化层 The New York Times ↗ 2026-09-16 § 链接
所谓的“极简烹饪”往往是基于对无偿家务劳动的结构性预设。
So-called 'minimalist cooking' is often built on a structural presumption of unpaid domestic labor.

NYT 推出这种 One-Pot（一锅出）食谱，其核心卖点是 minimal prep 和 minimal cleanup。在商业叙事里，这被包装成一种对现代生活的“解放”，但如果你把 Violence = Potential − Actual 这个公式套进来，你会发现这里的 Actual 依然被锚定在一种特定的共谋结构中。

这种“高效”是建立在谁在执行这 5 分钟 prep 和 55 分钟 cook 之上的？评论区里那些“kids scarfed theirs down”的温馨叙事，掩盖了一个 structural violence：在绝大多数家庭中，这种“周中晚餐的救星”依然由女性承担。One-Pot 并没有消灭家务，它只是通过降低单次操作的复杂度，让这种无偿劳动在文化层面上显得“不再那么辛苦”，从而使其更容易被内化为一种“自愿”的母职表达。

最讽刺的是，当食谱强调 minimal cleanup 时，它实际上在定义一种“合格”的家庭管理标准。这种对效率的追求不是为了让执行者获得真正的自由，而是为了让执行者能更快地回到下一个被定义的角色中去。这是一种典型的 weaponized 叙事：用“便利”作为诱饵，维持一个由女性在私人领域提供低成本养育支撑的男性中心结构。

这种食谱是好新闻吗？不是。它没有缩小 Potential 和 Actual 的差额，它只是给这个差额刷了一层名为“便捷”的油漆。

NYT promotes these One-Pot recipes with the core selling point of minimal prep and minimal cleanup. In commercial narratives, this is packaged as a 'liberation' for modern life. But applying the formula Violence = Potential − Actual reveals that the Actual is still anchored in a specific structure of complicity.

Who is actually performing those 5 minutes of prep and 55 minutes of cooking? The heartwarming narratives in the comments about 'kids scarfing theirs down' mask a structural violence: in the vast majority of households, this 'weeknight savior' is still executed by women. One-Pot doesn't eliminate domestic labor; it merely reduces the complexity of single operations to make this unpaid work seem 'less burdensome' at a cultural level, making it easier to internalize as a 'voluntary' expression of motherhood.

The irony is that by emphasizing minimal cleanup, the recipe defines a 'qualified' standard of household management. This pursuit of efficiency isn't meant to grant the practitioner true freedom, but to ensure they can return more quickly to the next defined role. This is a textbook weaponized narrative: using 'convenience' as bait to maintain a masculine-centric structure supported by low-cost nurturing in the private sphere.

Is this a good_news? No. It doesn't shrink the gap between Potential and Actual; it just applies a coat of paint called 'convenience' over the void.
