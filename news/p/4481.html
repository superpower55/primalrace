林肯号的记录：被消耗的肉身与被美化的荣耀The Lincoln Record: Consumed Bodies and Glorified Exhaustion

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-25 § 链接
战争叙事将人体降格为可消耗工具，而疲惫被包装成勋章。
War narratives degrade the human body into a consumable tool, packaging exhaustion as a medal.

200天不靠岸，这在现代海军史上是个“记录”。但在加尔通的暴力三角里，这根本不是什么荣誉，而是一场典型的 structural violence。当 5000 名水兵在食物短缺、水管损坏和精神崩溃的边缘徘徊时，他们不再是具有主体性的“人”，而成了维持霸权机器运转的生物电池。这种将人体降格为可消耗工具的逻辑，正是 masculine-centric narrative 的核心：个体感受被抹除，宏大叙事（如“中东部署”、“国家安全”）被置顶。

最讽刺的是，这种暴力在文化层被精心伪装。水兵妻子的绝望在新闻中成了点缀，而军方的叙事则是“休息与娱乐”。这种 weaponized 的浪漫化处理，试图掩盖一个事实：在元暴力的运作下，无论是执行命令的士兵还是下达命令的政客，都陷入了一场共谋。男性在战争中既是执行者，也是牺牲品，他们被训练成相信“疲惫是光荣的”，从而在主体性死亡的过程中完成对权力的献祭。

林肯号终于要靠岸了，但这不过是给电池充一次电，好让它能被重新投入下一场由男性定义的“存在性战争”。真正的 victory 不是靠岸，而是意识到这种把肉身当成零件的逻辑本身就是一种 scam。

200 days without a port call is called a 'record' in naval history. In the Violence Triangle, however, this is not an honor but a textbook case of structural violence. When 5,000 sailors drift toward mental collapse amidst food shortages and plumbing failures, they cease to be subjects with agency; they become biological batteries powering the machinery of hegemony. This logic of degrading the human body into a consumable tool is the core of the masculine-centric narrative: individual suffering is erased, and grand narratives like 'Middle East deployment' are prioritized.

The irony lies in how this violence is masked at the cultural layer. The despair of a sailor's wife is treated as a footnote, while the official narrative focuses on 'rest and recreation.' This weaponized romanticization hides a grim truth: under meta-violence, both the soldiers executing orders and the politicians issuing them are complicit. Men in war are simultaneously the executors and the victims; they are trained to believe that 'exhaustion is glorious,' completing their sacrifice to power through the death of their own subjectivity.

The USS Lincoln is finally docking, but this is merely recharging the battery so it can be plugged back into another 'existential war' defined by men. The real victory would not be docking, but recognizing that the logic of treating human flesh as a spare part is a total scam.
