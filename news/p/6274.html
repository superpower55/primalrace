兽人吃人，也开会Orcs Eat People, and They Also Hold Meetings

文娱 文化层 · 元暴力 Polygon.com ↗ 2026-11-01 § 链接
最像人的怪物，是那个会管团队的。
The most human monster is the one managing a team.

一篇讲《双塔奇兵》里那句"Looks like meat's back on the menu, boys!"的文章，绕了二十四圈，最后落在"兽人到底知不知道什么叫菜单"上。它自己说了，Uglúk 听起来像个刚让全组提前下班的经理。它只把这句当笑话。

笑话底下是这一整部片子里最精确的一笔：兽人不是被写成人，是被写成一支有管理层级的队伍。Saruman 交代得很清楚——别杀那两个霍比特人，活着带回来。没人听。Uglúk 的处境是任何一个中层都认得的处境：上面下了死命令，下面的人饿了、累了、开始内讧，Snaga 摸到 Merry 和 Pippin 背后想先尝一口。他一刀砍了 Snaga，说肉又上菜单了。这不是兽性的爆发，这是一次人事处理。用一个人的尸体换整支队伍的服从，成本最低，还顺带把"别吃他们"的命令执行了。

这就是共谋的原始形态，不需要文明来包装。上面要的是"活着的战利品"，下面是"今晚吃什么"，中间那个人负责把两套利益对齐，对齐的代价由谁付，没人问。报道说兽人"没有文化，只有卑劣的算计和小打小闹"，可这一刀恰恰是算计，而且是最社会化的那种算计——它需要等级、需要服从、需要一句能让所有人同时听懂的话。"菜单"这个词之所以让人出戏，是因为我们不愿意承认：能说出"肉又上菜单了"的嘴，和会议室里说"这个季度的目标"的嘴，是同一套语法。

报道里最诚实的一句来自 Elijah Wood："It's a good bit of writing, and it gets attention."二十四年前的一句台词，今天还在被人写文章，说明它抓住的东西不是中土世界的餐饮业，而是"把别人变成资源"这件事有多么日常。

真正没被问出口的是：Uglúk 砍掉的那个 Snaga，在这套结构里有名字、有台词、有欲望，他的死被全场欢呼。这支队伍里唯一一次"人的"反应，是被吃掉。

An article about one line from The Two Towers — "Looks like meat's back on the menu, boys!" — circles for twenty-four years and lands on whether orcs know what a menu is. It notices that Uglúk sounds like a manager who just let the whole staff leave early. It files that under joke.

Under the joke is the most exact stroke in the whole film: the orcs aren't written as people, they're written as a crew with a chain of command. Saruman was clear — don't kill the halflings, bring them in alive. Nobody listens. Uglúk is in the position every middle manager knows: the order comes from above, the people below are hungry and tired and turning on each other, and Snaga creeps up behind Merry and Pippin for a taste. Uglúk takes his head off with one swing and announces the menu is back. This isn't a burst of beastliness. It's a personnel decision. One corpse buys the crew's obedience, and it enforces the "don't eat them" order at the same time.

This is collusion in its raw form, no civilization required to dress it up. Above wants live captives; below wants dinner; the man in the middle aligns both interests, and nobody asks who pays. The piece says orcs have "no culture outside of nasty schemes and petty squabbles," yet this swing is precisely a scheme, and the most social kind — it needs hierarchy, obedience, and one sentence everyone understands at once. "Menu" feels wrong because we don't want to admit that the mouth saying "meat's back on the menu" and the mouth saying "our targets for the quarter" run on the same grammar.

The most honest line in the piece is Elijah Wood's: "It's a good bit of writing, and it gets attention." A line still generating articles twenty-four years later caught something, and it isn't Middle-earth's restaurant industry. It's how routine it is to turn another person into a resource.

The question never asked: Snaga, the one Uglúk beheads, has a name, lines, and desires in this structure, and his death gets a cheer. The only human response in that crew is being eaten.
