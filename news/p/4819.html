用“殖民”叙事掩盖的价值链投降Surrendering the Value Chain Under the Guise of 'Colonisation'

国际 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-06 § 链接
当竞争力消失时，弱者习惯将对方的效率定义为“病毒”或“殖民”。
When competitiveness vanishes, the loser habitually defines the winner's efficiency as a 'virus' or 'colonisation'.

Eurometal 用十口棺材在布鲁塞尔游行，试图用一种极具视觉冲击力的表达来唤醒欧盟委员会。但这场表演的核心逻辑是一个典型的 scam：它将产业竞争的失败，通过 weaponized 的词汇——“colonisation”（殖民）和“virus”（病毒）——转化为一种受害者叙事。

在加尔通的暴力三角里，这是一种典型的 cultural violence。当欧洲制造商无法在 structural 层面对抗中国的供应链效率时，他们选择在文化层面上将对方“非人化”。将贸易顺差定义为“殖民”，本质上是试图通过道德审判来索要贸易保护。这种叙事试图掩盖一个事实：资本的流动从来不讲忠诚，它只讲最优解。正如文中提到的，公司必须满足股东，这意味着他们会为了成本而选择中国，无论布鲁塞尔的政治修辞多么激昂。

这场博弈的残酷之处在于，欧洲工业界在扮演一个“被掠夺者”的角色，而欧盟委员会则在扮演一个“迟钝的保护者”。这种共谋（complicity）的回报是暂时的：行业协会通过制造恐慌获得政治关注，政客通过提高关税来表演“强硬”。但这种假.最优解表达无法解决核心问题——即欧洲工业在能源成本和制度僵化下的主体性死亡。

最讽刺的是，他们一边指责对方是“病毒”，一边承认自己被碳税和高能耗成本拖垮。这根本不是被殖民，而是在一个全球竞争的博弈场中，失去了定价权和定义权的自然出局。用棺材游行不能复活竞争力，只能证明他们已经习惯于在哀悼中寻找生存空间。

Eurometal's procession of ten coffins in Brussels is a piece of high-impact performance art, but its core logic is a scam. It attempts to transform the failure of industrial competition into a victim narrative using weaponized terms like 'colonisation' and 'virus'.

Within Galtung's Violence Triangle, this is a classic case of cultural violence. Unable to counter China's supply chain efficiency at the structural level, European manufacturers resort to 'dehumanizing' their opponent at the cultural level. Defining a trade surplus as 'colonisation' is an attempt to solicit trade protection through moral condemnation. This narrative masks a cold truth: capital knows no loyalty; it only seeks the optimal expression. As the text admits, companies must satisfy shareholders, meaning they will choose China for cost, regardless of how impassioned the political rhetoric in Brussels becomes.

The cruelty of this game lies in the complicity between the industry, playing the 'plundered', and the European Commission, playing the 'oblivious protector'. The reward for this complicity is temporary: trade bodies gain political attention through panic, and politicians perform 'toughness' via tariffs. However, this fake optimal expression cannot cure the death of subjectivity caused by energy costs and institutional rigidity.

It is profoundly ironic that they label the opponent a 'virus' while admitting they are crippled by carbon taxes and energy costs. This isn't colonisation; it is a natural exit from a global game where they have lost both the pricing power and the right to define reality. Parading coffins cannot resurrect competitiveness; it only proves they have become comfortable seeking survival within the act of mourning.
