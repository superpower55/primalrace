伯尼的常识，与建制派的共谋剧本Bernie's Common Sense and the Establishment's Script of Complicity

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-19 § 链接
所谓的“激进”，不过是把 Potential 与 Actual 的差额摆在台面上。
What they call "radical" is simply exposing the gap between Potential and Actual.

伯尼·桑德斯在《卫报》上的这篇评论，本质上是在拆穿一场巨大的 cultural violence。建制派民主党和主流媒体长期以来通过定义“激进”(radical) 和“极端”(extremist) 这两个词，构建了一道认知墙。他们试图让选民相信，要求医疗权、生活工资和税收公平是某种脱离现实的幻想，而将这种对 Potential（本可达到的生活状态）的追求定义为病态。

按照加尔通的暴力三角，当 60% 的美国人生活在 paycheck to paycheck 的焦虑中，而马斯克一个人的财富超过底层 50% 的家庭总和时，这种巨大的差额本身就是 structural violence。这种暴力不是通过枪口实施的，而是通过 corrupt campaign finance system（腐败的竞选资金制度）和医疗定价权实施的。建制派所谓的“稳健”，其实就是与 billionaire class 达成的一种共谋 (complicity) —— 他们在体制内扮演一个温和的反对者，从而确保这个掠夺性的结构不被真正触动。

最讽刺的是，这种元暴力 (meta violence) 甚至延伸到了 AI 和外交政策领域。无论是用 AI 进一步剥削劳动力，还是用“民主”叙事在海外制造屠杀，其底层逻辑是一致的：将人类身体客体化为可消耗的工具。伯尼所说的“简单答案”，其实就是要求将解释权从 Oligarchs（寡头）手中夺回。当人们意识到“正常生活”本身就是一种政治诉求时，建制派的叙事武器就失效了。

但这依然是一场存在性战争。即便进步派在初选中获胜，他们面对的依然是一个由共谋者组成的深层结构。如果不能从 structural 层面对资金和权力入口进行物理截断，这种胜利很容易被转化为另一种表演性的让步。

Bernie Sanders' piece in The Guardian is essentially dismantling a massive operation of cultural violence. For years, establishment Democrats and mainstream media have used terms like "radical" and "extremist" to build a cognitive wall. They want voters to believe that demanding healthcare, a living wage, and tax fairness is some delusional fantasy, pathologizing the pursuit of one's Potential.

Applying the Violence Triangle, when 60% of Americans live paycheck to paycheck while one man, Elon Musk, holds more wealth than the bottom 50% of households, that gap is structural violence. This violence isn't wielded via guns, but through a corrupt campaign finance system and the monopoly on healthcare pricing. The "moderation" of the establishment is actually a form of complicity with the billionaire class—playing the role of a mild opponent to ensure the predatory structure remains intact.

This meta violence extends even to AI and foreign policy. Whether it's using AI to further exploit labor or using "democracy" narratives to justify overseas massacres, the underlying logic is the same: the objectification of human bodies as consumable tools. Bernie's "simple answer" is a demand to seize the power of interpretation back from the Oligarchs. When people realize that a "normal life" is itself a political demand, the establishment's weaponized narratives fail.

Yet, this remains an existential war. Even if progressives win primaries, they face a deep structure of co-conspirators. Without a physical severance of the structural entries of money and power, this victory risks being converted into another performance of concession.
