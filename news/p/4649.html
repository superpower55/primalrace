在宏大叙事的废墟上扮演“正常”女性Performing 'Normalcy' atop the Ruins of Grand Narratives

哲学 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-30 § 链接
当女性角色被简化为“专业且体贴”的背景板，这依然是男本位叙事的胜利。
When female leads are reduced to 'professional and nice' background noise, the masculine-centric narrative still wins.

这部剧试图用地缘政治的危急感来掩盖其剧本的贫瘠。它把背景设定在北极、核潜艇和中东，试图用一种典型的 masculine-centric narrative（男性中心叙事）来制造紧张感：特工、武器贸易、大国博弈。在这种叙事里，世界总是处于毁灭的边缘，而这种“拯救世界”的宏大感，本质上是元暴力的某种美学包装。

最讽刺的是剧中对女性角色的处理。DCI Amy Silva 和 DI Kirsten Longacre 是一对女同性恋伴侣，且其中一人刚结束产假。在表面上看，这像是某种进步的、多元的表达。但实际上，这是一种典型的“假.最优解表达”。编剧赋予她们“专业”、“理智”且“没有家庭摩擦”的属性，实际上是将她们彻底去主体化。她们不再是拥有复杂欲望和冲突的人，而成了两个完美的、被规训的职能模块。她们的浪漫关系没有戏剧冲突，她们的母职被简化为一个背景设定，这根本不是在写女性，而是在写两个扮演女性的、高效的公务员。

当一个女性角色在剧评中被描述为“一张白纸”或“仅仅是挺好的”，这意味着她的存在性被剥夺了。她不需要性格，不需要欲望，只需要在男本位构建的阴谋论世界里充当一个合格的观察者。这种“专业且体贴”的设定，恰恰是 cultural violence 的高级形式：它通过给予女性一个“正确”且“文明”的社会位置，完成了对她们主体性的抹除。

这部剧最 boring 的地方不在于反转太多，而在于它在用一种极其乏味的共谋方式，试图告诉观众：看，我们现在可以让女性处理国际危机了，只要她们足够“正常”且不给男性叙事添乱。

The show attempts to mask its skeletal script with the urgency of geopolitical crises. By setting the action in the Arctic and nuclear submarines, it employs a classic masculine-centric narrative: special forces, arms deals, and Great Power competition. In this framework, the world is always on the brink of collapse—a 'save the world' aesthetic that is, in essence, a stylized packaging of meta-violence.

Most ironic is the treatment of the female leads. DCI Amy Silva and DI Kirsten Longacre are a lesbian couple, one returning from maternity leave. On the surface, this looks like progressive, diverse expression. In reality, it is a 'fake optimal expression.' By stripping them of friction and making them 'professional' and 'sensible,' the writers have completely dehumanized them. They aren't characters with complex desires; they are efficient functional modules. Their romance lacks tension, and their motherhood is a mere plot point. This isn't writing women; it's writing civil servants performing femininity.

When a female character is described by a critic as a 'blank slate' or 'quite nice,' it means her existentiality has been erased. She requires no personality or desire, only to serve as a qualified observer in a conspiracy-driven world built by men. This 'professional and nice' trope is a sophisticated form of cultural violence: it grants women a 'correct' and 'civilized' social position while simultaneously erasing their subjectivity.

The true boredom of the show isn't the excessive plot twists, but its tedious complicity. It tries to tell the audience: Look, we now allow women to handle international crises—as long as they remain 'normal' and don't disrupt the masculine narrative.
