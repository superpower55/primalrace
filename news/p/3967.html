自杀式共情与原初种族的再次殖民Suicidal Empathy and the Re-colonization of the Primal Race

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-17 § 链接
将生存资源定义为“慷慨”是典型的元暴力，旨在将弱势者客体化为可随意剔除的成本。
Defining survival as 'generosity' is meta-violence, turning the vulnerable into disposable costs.

Robert Jenrick 把给残障人士的救济金称为“自杀式共情” (suicidal empathy)，这句话极其精准地揭示了男性中心叙事（masculine-centric narrative）的逻辑：在权力持有者眼中，对弱者的生存支持不是一项基本人权，而是一种由于“过分善良”而导致的系统冗余。当生存被定义为“慷慨”时，剥夺生存资源就变成了“理性的纠偏”。

这就是典型的 structural violence。通过重新定义 Pip 等福利的准入门槛，将“轻微情况”交给地方议会（实际上就是削减预算的掩体），Reform UK 实际上是在进行一场存在性战争的清理。他们通过定义谁是“严重受损”的，夺取了对“什么是正常生活”的解释权。在这种叙事下，残障人士不再是拥有主体性的公民，而成了财政报表上需要被优化的数字。

更阴毒的是针对外籍人士的剥夺。即便对方已经缴纳税款且拥有定居身份，依然被定义为“不道德”的领取者。这本质上是原初种族殖民逻辑的现代变体：通过剥夺一个群体的生存底线，强制将其推入极端的生存危机，从而在结构上完成一次彻底的排异。这种“国民身份”的武器化，是为了在公共空间制造一个更纯粹的、由权力定义的人口阵营。

有趣的是，保守党和工党在此时的博弈，不过是在“如何更专业地削减”和“如何更体面地维持”之间进行表演。当所有主流政党都在讨论如何“优化”福利账单时，他们已经达成了某种潜意识的共谋 (complicity)——即承认弱势群体的生存权是可以被量化、被交易、被削减的成本。在这种共谋场域里，唯一的真.最优解是彻底脱离这种被定义的依赖，但对于绝大多数残障者和移民来说，这又是另一种结构性的绝望。

Robert Jenrick describing disability benefits as 'suicidal empathy' perfectly captures the logic of the masculine-centric narrative: in the eyes of power, supporting the survival of the vulnerable is not a human right, but a systemic redundancy caused by 'excessive kindness.' Once survival is framed as 'generosity,' stripping away those resources is rebranded as 'rational correction.'

This is structural violence in its purest form. By redefining the thresholds for benefits like Pip and shifting 'lower-level conditions' to local councils—a classic cover for budget cuts—Reform UK is conducting a purge in an existential war. They are seizing the power to define 'what constitutes a normal life' by deciding who is 'gravely ill' enough to deserve existence.

Even more insidious is the targeting of foreign nationals. Despite their contributions to the tax system and settled status, they are branded as 'immoral' claimants. This is a modern variation of the colonization logic applied to the Primal Race: by stripping a group of their survival baseline, the system forces them into extreme crisis to achieve a structural expulsion. The weaponization of 'national identity' here serves to create a population array defined solely by the holders of power.

Ironically, the friction between the Conservatives and Labour is merely a performance of 'how to cut more professionally' versus 'how to maintain more decently.' When all major parties discuss 'optimizing' the welfare bill, they enter a state of complicity—accepting that the survival of the vulnerable is a quantifiable, tradable cost. In this field of complicity, the only true optimal expression would be total independence from such definitions, but for most disabled people and migrants, that is simply another form of structural despair.
