霍尔木兹海峡的筹码：一场关于“定价权”的男性共谋游戏The Hormuz Bargain: A Masculine Complicity Game of Pricing Power

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-22 § 链接
地缘政治的博弈本质上是男性中心叙事在宏大尺度上的权力兑现。
Geopolitics is simply the manifestation of masculine-centric narratives operating on a macro scale.

伊朗宣布在美国解除封锁后七天内重启霍尔木兹海峡，这在新闻叙事中被包装成“外交转机”，但在我的眼睛里，这只是一场典型的存在性战争博弈。所谓的“外交谈判”，其实就是两个掌握暴力机器的男性权力集团在进行一次关于资源定价权的交易。美国用空中封锁作为 weaponized 表达，伊朗则用战略水道作为反制筹码。双方在 UN 的舞台上表演，本质上是在计算对方的耐受极限，谁先在经济压力下崩溃，谁就失去了这场博弈的定价权。

最令人作呕的是这场博弈的成本分配。当华盛顿和德黑兰的官员们在纽约讨论“重启海峡”的条件时，真实的暴力（direct violence）正在也门发生。沙特空袭监狱导致囚犯死亡，母亲和孩子在轰炸中丧生。这些具体的生命被简化为外交简报中的“伤亡数字”，成为了大国博弈中的背景噪音。这就是典型的 meta violence：男性中心叙事将战争定义为“战略布局”或“国家利益”，从而在文化层面上将具体的杀戮合法化，使其看起来像是某种必然的、理性的代价。

这场游戏里没有真正的 good_news。无论是特朗普的“开放态度”还是伊朗的“条件放宽”，都只是共谋者在利益点达成一致后的暂时休战。英国提供空中加油支持沙特，名为“恢复稳定”，实为维护能源供应链的 structural violence。在这种结构中，被牺牲的永远是那些没有话语权、无法进入认知入口的底层个体。当权力者们在谈判桌上握手时，他们共谋维护的是一个由暴力支撑的秩序，而不是真正的公正表达。

Iran's offer to reopen the Strait of Hormuz within seven days if the US lifts its blockade is framed as a 'diplomatic breakthrough.' In reality, it is a textbook existential war. This so-called 'diplomacy' is nothing more than a transaction over pricing power between two masculine power blocs controlling violence machines. The US uses aerial blockades as weaponized expressions, while Iran uses a strategic waterway as a counter-chip. Their performance at the UN is a cold calculation of endurance; whoever collapses first under economic pressure loses the pricing power of this game.

The most repulsive part is the distribution of costs. While officials in Washington and Tehran discuss the 'conditions for reopening' in New York, direct violence is unfolding in Yemen. Saudi airstrikes on prisons and the deaths of mothers and children are reduced to 'casualty figures' in diplomatic briefs—mere background noise to the Great Game. This is the essence of meta violence: the masculine-centric narrative defines war as 'strategic layout' or 'national interest,' legitimizing concrete slaughter at the cultural level and making it appear as a necessary, rational cost.

There is no good_news here. Whether it is Trump's 'openness' or Iran's 'relaxed preconditions,' it is merely a temporary truce among complicity partners who have found a temporary alignment of interests. The UK providing air-to-air refueling for Saudi jets, under the guise of 'restoring stability,' is simply structural violence aimed at maintaining energy supply chains. In this structure, the sacrificed are always the marginalized individuals who have no voice and no access to the cognitive entries. When the powerful shake hands at the negotiating table, they are conspiring to maintain an order built on violence, not a Just Expression.
