谁有资格算乡村Who Gets to Be Country

文娱 文化层 · 元暴力 Billboard ↗ 2026-11-03 § 链接
新闻摘要Billboard 盘点了历届获得格莱美年度专辑提名的乡村专辑，从 1966 年 Eddy Arnold 的《My World》到 2025 年 Beyoncé 的《Cowboy Carter》。Beyoncé 曾说「这不是一张乡村专辑，这是一张 Beyoncé 专辑」，但格莱美仍把它列入乡村类提名。文章以是否登上 Billboard 乡村专辑榜为准，把 Lionel Richie 的《Can't Slow Down》也算了进来。
The newsBillboard lists every country album ever nominated for Grammy Album of the Year, from Eddy Arnold's My World in 1966 to Beyoncé's Cowboy Carter in 2025. Beyoncé has said «This ain't a Country album, this is a 'Beyoncé' album,» yet Grammy voters placed it in country categories. The list counts any album that made Billboard's Top Country Albums chart, which is how Lionel Richie's Can't Slow Down got in.
分类不是描述，是发证
A category is not a description. It is a license.

Billboard 这份名单最诚实的地方，是它自己承认了标准有多松：只要上过乡村专辑榜，就算乡村专辑。于是 Lionel Richie 的《Can't Slow Down》在 1984 年摸到第 55 名，也进了这份「乡村专辑获年度专辑提名」的名单。榜单是尺子，尺子是谁做的、为谁做的，决定了什么被量成乡村。

Beyoncé 说「这不是一张乡村专辑，这是一张 Beyoncé 专辑」，格莱美说不是，我们把你放进乡村类。两句话争的不是音乐，是发证权。一个黑人女性做了一张带乡村元素的专辑，产业的第一反应是给它归类，第二反应才是给它提名。归类在前，承认在后，顺序不能反。

再往下看，这份名单本身就是一部解释权的账本。Bobbie Gentry 1968 年凭《Ode to Billie Joe》在同一届拿下四大通类的提名，是格莱美史上第一个做到的人，最后只拿到最佳新人。The Chicks 拿了三次年度专辑提名，是乡村艺人里最多的，第三次靠《Taking the Long Way》获奖，那是在 Natalie Maines 说了布什那句话、被乡村电台抵制之后——格莱美用奖杯表了态，乡村产业用抵制表了另一个态。同一个行业，两套解释。

名单里女性名字不少：Gentry、Ronstadt、Parton、Twain、The Chicks、Musgraves。但「乡村」这个门类的守门人一直在决定她们是以乡村的身份被看见，还是以「跨界的」「流行的」「太政治化的」身份被看见。门开着，门槛在。

Beyoncé 这次被放进乡村类，不是墙倒了。是墙换了一块牌子，牌子上写着「我们一直欢迎你」，而名单本身告诉你，六十年里能走到年度专辑提名的乡村专辑，一只手数得过来。

The most honest thing about Billboard's list is that it admits how loose its own standard is: any album that made the Top Country Albums chart counts as a country album. That is how Lionel Richie's Can't Slow Down, which peaked at No. 55 on that chart in 1984, ends up on a list of country albums nominated for Album of the Year. The chart is a ruler. Who built the ruler, and for whom, decides what gets measured as country.

Beyoncé said «This ain't a Country album, this is a 'Beyoncé' album.» The Grammys said otherwise and filed her under country. The argument is not about music. It is about who issues the license. A Black woman makes an album with country in it, and the industry's first move is to classify it, its second to nominate it. Classification comes first, recognition second. The order does not reverse.

The list itself is a ledger of interpretive power. Bobbie Gentry in 1968 became the first person in Grammy history nominated in all four Big Four categories in one year, and won only Best New Artist. The Chicks have three Album of the Year nominations, more than any other country act; the third, Taking the Long Way, won — after Natalie Maines' comment about Bush and the country radio backlash. The Grammys voted one way with a trophy; the country industry had already voted another way with a boycott. Same business, two interpretations.

Women are all over this list: Gentry, Ronstadt, Parton, Twain, The Chicks, Musgraves. But the gatekeepers of «country» have always decided whether they are seen as country or as crossover, pop, too political. The door is open. The threshold remains.

Beyoncé being filed under country this year is not the wall coming down. It is the wall getting a new sign, and the sign says «we have always welcomed you.» The list tells you otherwise: in sixty years, the country albums that reached an Album of the Year nomination can be counted on two hands.
