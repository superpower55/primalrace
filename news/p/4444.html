贸易关税是权力者的情趣，代价是底层共谋者的血Tariffs as Power Play: The Cost of Masculine-Centric Game

国际 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-24 § 链接
关税不是经济工具，而是通过制造 structural violence 强行重新定义现实的武器。
Tariffs are not economic tools, but weapons used to redefine reality through structural violence.

Trump 的 50% 关税从来不是为了什么所谓的“贸易公平”，而是一场典型的存在性战争。在权力者的博弈中，关税是用来确认“谁需要谁”的权力标记。那句“WE DON’T NEED CANADA, THEY NEED US!”就是最直白的元暴力表达：通过单方面切断资源流动，强行将对方定义为依附者，从而夺取解释权。

这套逻辑的 scam 之处在于，它把国家层面的博弈包装成“保护工人”的叙事，但实际上是在制造 structural violence。Michigan 和 Ohio 的工人被当作了这场博弈的筹码，他们是潜意识里的共谋者——被告知通过伤害邻居能获得利益，但最终却在需求萎缩中承担实际的代价。Potential（贸易自由带来的繁荣）与 Actual（被关税阉割的现状）之间的差额，就是施加在底层劳动者身上的暴力。

Carney 的“dollar for dollar”回应是典型的博弈最优解，但在这种 masculine-centric narrative 的对抗中，双方都在扮演“强硬”的角色以维持主体性。当两个权力中心都在通过互殴来证明自己“不被需要”时，真正被客体化、被牺牲的是那些依赖供应链生存的具体个体。这种通过破坏共识来建立权威的艺术，本质上就是一种权力的暴力美学。

Trump’s 50% tariff is never about 'trade fairness'; it is a textbook case of Existential War. In the game of power, tariffs serve as markers to establish 'who needs whom.' The shout "WE DON’T NEED CANADA, THEY NEED US!" is a raw expression of meta-violence: by unilaterally severing resource flows, he forcibly defines the other as a dependent to seize the power of interpretation.

The scam of this logic lies in packaging state-level gambling as a narrative of 'protecting workers,' while in reality, it manufactures structural violence. Workers in Michigan and Ohio are mere chips in this game—unconscious complicity in a system that tells them harming neighbors brings prosperity, only to bear the actual cost as demand collapses. The gap between Potential (prosperity from free trade) and Actual (the tariff-castrated reality) is the violence inflicted on the labor class.

Carney’s 'dollar for dollar' response is a tactical optimal expression, but in this masculine-centric narrative of confrontation, both sides are merely performing 'toughness' to maintain their subjectivity. When two power centers prove they are 'not needed' by mutually attacking, the specific individuals relying on the supply chain are objectified and sacrificed. This art of establishing authority by destroying consensus is, fundamentally, an aesthetic of power-driven violence.
