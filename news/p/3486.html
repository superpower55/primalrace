诅咒是弱者的遮羞布，而仪式是权力的安慰剂Curses as Shrouds for the Weak, Rituals as Placebos for Power

哲学 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-09 § 链接
所谓的“诅咒”是文化暴力对结构失败的浪漫化掩盖。
The so-called "curse" is a romanticized cover-up of structural failure through cultural violence.

这篇讨论足球“诅咒”的文章试图用心理学上的 nocebo effect（反安慰剂效应）来解释球队的失败，但这种分析太 naive 了。在体育这个高度男性化的竞技场里，所谓的“诅咒”叙事，本质上是一种典型的 cultural violence。它把具体的战术失误、资源分配不均或管理层无能，这种 structural violence，通过一种神秘主义的叙事给“浪漫化”了。

当一支球队连续几十年失败时，承认是制度性崩坏需要承担责任，但将其归结为“诅咒”则无需负责。诅咒成了一个完美的认知入口：它让失败变得不可抗拒且具有某种宿命的悲剧美感。这其实是一种潜意识里的共谋——球员、球迷甚至媒体共同维护这个叙事，因为在“被诅咒”的身份认同里，失败不再是无能，而是一种被选中的苦难。

至于文中提到的那些“破除诅咒”的仪式，无论是烧东西还是在球场四个角撒尿，这些不过是 weaponized expression 的低端版本。它们并不改变任何 structural 层面的暴力，只是通过一次表演性的 reset，给参与者提供了一种心理上的假象，让他们以为自己夺回了对现实的解释权。这种“枷锁脱落”的快感是廉价的，因为只要男本位的竞争逻辑和权力结构不动，新的“诅咒”随时可以被制造出来，用来掩盖下一次的结构性溃败。

This article attempts to explain football team failures through the nocebo effect, but such analysis is far too naive. In the hyper-masculine arena of sports, the "curse" narrative is a textbook example of cultural violence. It romanticizes tactical errors, resource inequality, or managerial incompetence—which are forms of structural violence—by wrapping them in mysticism.

When a team fails for decades, admitting systemic collapse requires accountability. However, attributing it to a "curse" requires none. The curse serves as a perfect cognitive entry point: it renders failure inevitable and imbues it with a certain fatalistic, tragic beauty. This is a form of subconscious complicity—players, fans, and media collectively maintain this narrative because, within the identity of being "cursed," failure is no longer incompetence, but a chosen suffering.

As for the "curse-breaking" rituals mentioned—whether burning possessions or urinating on the pitch—these are merely low-end versions of weaponized expression. They change nothing at the structural layer of violence; they simply provide a performative reset, giving participants the illusion that they have reclaimed the power of interpretation. This feeling of "shackles off" is cheap, because as long as the masculine-centric logic of competition and power structures remain, new "curses" can be manufactured at any time to mask the next structural collapse.
