禁令、掩盖与刻名：特朗普的认知入口战争Bans, Cover-ups, and Engravings: Trump's War for Cognitive Entry

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-18 § 链接
权力不满足于统治肉体，它必须垄断定义现实的解释权。
Power is never satisfied with controlling bodies; it must monopolize the right to define reality.

特朗普禁止 CNN 等媒体进入白宫，这根本不是什么“打击假新闻”的正义之举，而是一次典型的认知入口（cognitive entry）争夺战。在权力者的逻辑里，事实不重要，重要的是“什么是事实”的制造权。通过物理性地切断信息流，他试图将白宫变成一个封闭的叙事工厂，在这个工厂里，只有符合他意志的表达才能被定义为“真实”。

这种对解释权的垄断在伊朗战争的伤亡人数上体现得淋漓尽致。五角大楼公开的死亡人数是 18 人，而内部消息指向 22 人。这多出的 4 个人不是简单的统计误差，而是 structural violence 的掩盖。当权力者决定哪些死亡“应当被记录”，他实际上是在对生命进行定价，将不符合政治叙事的牺牲直接抹除在 Actual 之外，从而缩小 Potential 与 Actual 之间的差额，以此维持一个“战争在掌控中”的假象。

最令人作呕的 meta-violence 则是他试图将名字刻在和平研究所和肯尼迪中心的墙上。这已经超越了简单的虚荣，而是一种极具侵略性的身份确立。他要让物理空间本身成为他的表达武器，强行将国家机构的独立使命转化为个人的品牌资产。这种“刻名”行为在潜意识里告诉所有人：这个世界的规则是由我定义的，所有的公共资源本质上都是我的私产。

从操纵邮寄选票的名单到将无证儿童像货物一样运往德克萨斯的“遣返工厂”，特朗普的整套操作逻辑高度统一：通过制造混乱、定义敌人、垄断入口，最终达成对个体主体性的彻底剥夺。在这种元暴力的覆盖下，所谓的“法律”和“制度”正沦为他个人意志的 PR 插件。

Trump banning CNN and other outlets from the White House isn't about 'fighting fake news'—it's a textbook struggle for cognitive entry. In the logic of the powerful, facts are irrelevant; what matters is the power to manufacture 'what is a fact.' By physically severing the information flow, he seeks to turn the White House into a closed narrative factory where only expressions aligned with his will are defined as 'truth.'

This monopoly over interpretation is starkly evident in the casualty counts of the Iran war. The Pentagon publicly claims 18 deaths, while internal sources point to 22. Those four extra people aren't a statistical error; they are a manifestation of structural violence. When power decides which deaths 'should be recorded,' it is effectively pricing human life, erasing sacrifices that don't fit the political narrative to maintain the illusion that the war is 'under control.'

The most visceral meta-violence is his attempt to engrave his name onto the facades of the US Institute of Peace and the Kennedy Center. This transcends mere vanity; it is an aggressive act of identity establishment. He wants physical space itself to become a weapon of expression, forcibly converting the independent missions of state institutions into personal brand assets. This 'engraving' signals a clear message: I define the rules of this world, and all public resources are essentially my private property.

From manipulating mail-in ballot lists to funneling undocumented children into 'deportation factories' in Texas, Trump's operational logic is consistent: create chaos, define the enemy, and monopolize the entry points to strip individuals of their subjectivity. Under this blanket of meta-violence, 'law' and 'institution' are being reduced to mere PR plugins for his personal will.
