喜马拉雅的洪水：一场关于“基础设施”的结构性谋杀Himalayan Floods: A Structural Murder Masked as Infrastructure

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-26 § 链接
自然灾害是表象，资源分配的差额才是真正的暴力。
Natural disasters are the symptom; the gap in resource allocation is the actual violence.

冰川崩塌和山洪爆发被报道为“突发自然灾害”，但在加尔通的暴力三角里，这典型的 structural violence。当报道提到“水力发电项目 (hydropower projects)”被摧毁时，我们需要追问：这些项目是为了当地村民的生存，还是为了向外部资本输出廉价能源？

在尼泊尔这样的边缘地带，基础设施的建设往往是 weaponized 的。所谓的“发展”通常意味着将脆弱的生态系统推向极限，而风险则被精准地分配给了当地最底层的居民。当洪水冲走村庄时，消失的不仅是生命，更是那些在权力博弈中被剥夺了安全冗余的生存权。Potential（本可达到的安全状态）与 Actual（被摧毁的现状）之间的差额，就是这场灾难的暴力本质。

最讽刺的是，失踪名单中包含大量来自美英印的 foreign tourists。这种叙事上的对比再次揭示了认知的不对称：当游客失踪时，它变成了全球关注的“新闻”；而当当地村庄被抹除时，它只是背景板上的“广泛破坏 (widespread damage)”。在元暴力的逻辑下，生命被赋予的价值取决于其背后的资本与国籍。这不再是自然灾害，而是一场由贪婪驱动、由结构性忽视完成的谋杀。

The ice avalanche and flash floods are reported as 'natural disasters,' but within the Violence Triangle, this is textbook structural violence. When the report mentions the destruction of 'hydropower projects,' we must ask: were these projects for the villagers' survival, or for exporting cheap energy to external capital?

In marginal zones like Nepal, infrastructure is often weaponized. 'Development' typically means pushing fragile ecosystems to the brink, while the risks are precisely allocated to the most marginalized residents. When floods erase villages, what is lost is not just life, but the safety redundancy stripped away through power games. The gap between Potential (the safety that could have been) and Actual (the destroyed reality) is the essence of violence in this disaster.

Cruelly, the missing list includes foreign tourists from the US, UK, and India. This contrast reveals the asymmetry of cognition: when tourists vanish, it becomes 'global news'; when local villages are wiped out, it is merely 'widespread damage' in the background. Under the logic of meta-violence, the value of a life is determined by its capital and nationality. This is no longer a natural disaster; it is a murder driven by greed and executed through structural neglect.
