傀儡大师的剧本：当国家机器在扮演抗议者The Puppet Master's Script: When the State Plays the Protester

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-13 § 链接
用“反恐”定义异见，是结构暴力将合法表达武器化的极致 scam。
Defining dissent as 'terrorism' is the ultimate scam of weaponizing legal expression via structural violence.

这场名为“傀儡大师”（Operation Puppet Master）的行动，其命名本身就是一种极其傲慢的 meta violence。它预设了抗议者并非具有主体意识的公民，而仅仅是某种秘密网络中被操纵的“傀儡”。当 DHS 的特工潜入 Signal 群组、潜入社区会议，甚至在教会的拳击训练中偷偷录音时，他们不是在调查犯罪，而是在进行一场关于“定义事实”的狩猎。

最令人作呕的共谋发生在叙事层：政府将“反 ICE”的合法抗议强行挂钩到“国内恐怖主义”这个 weaponized 概念上。通过定义“antifa”为恐怖组织，国家机器成功地将 structural violence（移民镇压）伪装成秩序维护，而将对这种暴力的反抗定义为“共谋犯罪”。这就是典型的文化暴力——通过篡改词义，让监视和政治迫害看起来像是在“保护国家安全”。

更讽刺的是，记录显示特工在潜伏期间甚至试图诱导抗议者讨论“直接行动”，这种 agent provocateur（挑衅代理人）的行为证明了政府并不在乎是否存在犯罪，而是在“制造”犯罪。他们试图通过操纵表达，来证明自己的监视是正当的。这不再是法律的执行，而是一场政治报复的 fantasy。

当一个政权需要通过潜入图书馆的“非暴力沟通培训”来寻找威胁时，它真正恐惧的不是暴力，而是人们开始意识到自己被殖民的处境并尝试结盟。这种对左翼组织和工会的财务审计，本质上是对“组织能力”的恐惧。它试图通过切断资金和制造恐惧，将个体重新推回孤立无援的生物墙之内。

The naming of "Operation Puppet Master" is an act of extreme meta violence. It presupposes that protesters are not autonomous citizens but merely "puppets" in a secret network. When DHS agents infiltrated Signal chats, community meetings, and even recorded boxing practices in churches, they weren't investigating crimes—they were hunting for a way to define reality.

The most sinister complicity occurs at the narrative level: the government forcibly linked lawful anti-ICE protests to the weaponized concept of "domestic terrorism." By designating "antifa" as a terrorist organization, the state disguised its own structural violence (immigration crackdowns) as the maintenance of order, while framing the resistance to that violence as a "criminal conspiracy." This is textbook cultural violence—altering definitions to make surveillance and political repression appear as "national security."

More absurdly, records show agents actively enticing activists to discuss "direct action." This agent provocateur behavior proves the government wasn't looking for crimes; it was manufacturing them. They attempted to manipulate expression to justify their own surveillance. This is no longer law enforcement; it is a fantasy of political retribution.

When a regime feels threatened by a "non-violent communication training" at a public library, it isn't fearing violence—it is fearing the moment people realize they are being colonized and begin to ally. The financial auditing of unions and nonprofits is a direct attack on the capacity to organize. It is an attempt to sever the ties of solidarity and push individuals back behind the biological walls of isolation.
