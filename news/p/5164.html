美国正义的“外包”与海地的血色空窗Outsourced Justice and Haiti's Bloody Vacuum

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-20 § 链接
用美国法院的判决掩盖结构性暴力的共谋，是最高级的叙事武器化。
Using US courts to mask complicity in structural violence is the ultimate weaponization of narrative.

18个人被押送到佛罗里达州受审，FBI在X上表演着一种名为“正义”的仪式感。但请看这个剧本：一个海地总统在自家卧室被哥伦比亚雇佣兵枪杀，而他的安保人员全程围观。这根本不是什么个案谋杀，而是一场典型的、由美国资本和安全承包商驱动的权力清洗。CTU这家佛罗里达安全公司在其中扮演的角色，就是典型的“武器化表达”——将政治暗杀包装成商业合同，将政权更迭量化为“利润空间”。

最荒诞的共谋在于，美国一边通过联邦法院对这些“执行者”进行法律审判，一边在结构层面上维持着让海地崩溃的元暴力。海地现在没有总统，80%的首都被帮派控制，而所谓的“联合国支持部队”在人员配置上完全是个scam。这种对比极其讽刺：美国有能力在五年后精准地把嫌疑人从海地运到迈阿密，却没能力（或者说没兴趣）在海地建立一个最基本的安全秩序。

这就是一种典型的“认知入口”操控：通过在American courts进行审判，美国试图将自己定义为“正义的维护者”，从而掩盖其在拉美地区长期通过支持私营安全公司、干预主权国家而制造的structural violence。这种正义是表演性的，它只负责处理那些被抛弃的棋子，而不负责修复被它亲手拆毁的社会结构。海地人的Potential与Actual之间的差额，在这些判决书面前毫无意义。

Eighteen people transported to Florida for trial—a performance of "justice" curated by the FBI on X. But look at the script: a Haitian president executed in his bedroom by Colombian mercenaries while his security detail watched. This wasn't a random murder; it was a textbook power purge driven by US capital and private security contractors. The role of CTU, a Florida-based firm, is a prime example of weaponized expression—packaging political assassination as a business contract and regime change as "profit margins."

The most cynical complicity lies here: while the US celebrates its federal courts' ability to punish the "executors," it maintains the meta-violence that keeps Haiti in collapse. Haiti has no president, 80% of its capital is gang-controlled, and the UN-backed force is a complete scam in terms of staffing. The irony is sharp: the US has the logistical capacity to airlift suspects to Miami five years later, but zero interest in establishing basic security in Haiti.

This is a manipulation of the cognitive entry point. By centering the trial in American courts, the US defines itself as the "guardian of justice," effectively masking the structural violence it has fostered in Latin America through private security and sovereign interference. This justice is performative; it cleans up the discarded pawns while ignoring the shattered social structures it helped create. For the people of Haiti, the gap between Potential and Actual remains a yawning abyss, regardless of how many convictions are handed down in Florida.
