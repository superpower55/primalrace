把性化客体塞进教室，这不叫“接触技术”Stuffing Sexualized Objects into Classrooms is Not 'Tech Exposure'

性别 文化层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-01 § 链接
用“漂亮女性”的外壳包装教育工具，是元暴力的典型武器化表达。
Deploying a 'pretty brunette' robot is a textbook weaponization of meta-violence in education.

把一个名为 Sally 的、被设定为“活泼棕发美女”的机器人塞进高中教室，这根本不是什么教育创新，而是一次极其恶劣的文化暴力投放。学校管理层试图用“接触高新科技”这种叙事来掩盖事实：他们给学生提供的是一个被性化、被客体化的女性符号。在这种逻辑里，技术本身不重要，重要的是这个技术必须以一种“符合男性凝视”的形态出现才能被接受。

这又是典型的元暴力（meta violence）在起作用。决定机器人外观的是谁？选择这种“最优解表达”的潜意识是什么？它在潜移默化地告诉学生们：女性的价值在于其表型是否符合某种审美标准，即便是在传授知识的场景中，女性（或女性形象）首先得是“漂亮”的。这不仅是对女性主体性的抹除，更是为未来的直接暴力铺路——当青少年男性习惯于将一个具有女性表型的客体视为可以随意开 crude jokes 的对象时，他们就在练习如何剥夺真实女性的尊严。

最讽刺的是，这 6 万美元本可以用来改善被压榨的教师待遇，或者购买真正的学习设备。但管理层选择了一个昂贵的、具有性暗示的塑料壳子。这说明在他们的认知入口里，教育的优先级低于对“女性客体”的消费。这种共谋不仅发生在管理层与科技公司之间，更在潜意识里邀请学生加入这场关于“客体化”的游戏。所谓的“学习机器人维护”，在实际操作中极有可能变成一场关于如何操纵一个“漂亮女人”的权力演习。

Inserting a humanoid robot named Sally—specifically designed as a 'perky brunette'—into high school classrooms is not educational innovation; it is a malicious deployment of cultural violence. The administration attempts to mask this with a narrative of 'access to high-level technology.' The reality is that they are providing students with a sexualized, objectified female symbol. In this logic, the technology itself is irrelevant; what matters is that it appears in a form that satisfies the masculine gaze to be deemed 'acceptable.'

This is meta-violence in its purest form. Who decided the robot's appearance? What was the subconscious drive behind this 'optimal expression'? It implicitly teaches students that a woman's value is tied to her phenotype and adherence to beauty standards, even in a space of intellectual exchange. By erasing female agency, it paves the way for direct violence—when teenage boys practice making crude jokes about a female-presenting object, they are practicing the dehumanization of real women.

The most cynical part is that the $60,000 could have improved the conditions of underpaid teachers or provided actual laptops. Instead, the administration chose an expensive, suggestive plastic shell. This proves that in their cognitive entry point, the consumption of 'female objects' takes precedence over actual pedagogy. This complicity between management and tech companies invites students into a game of objectification. What is framed as 'learning robot maintenance' will likely devolve into a power exercise in how to manipulate a 'pretty woman.'
