资本的定价权：人命是折旧，股东是资产Capital's Pricing Power: Lives as Depreciation, Shareholders as Assets

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-18 § 链接
当法律将企业行为定义为“常规商业实践”时，谋杀就变成了会计科目。
When the law defines corporate slaughter as 'ordinary business practice,' murder becomes a mere accounting entry.

Violence = Potential − Actual。格伦费尔塔（Grenfell Tower）的悲剧在 2017 年没有结束，它只是从 direct 层的火焰，转化成了 structural 层的账单。Arconic 支付给股东的赔偿金比支付给死难者的更多，这个数字差额就是最赤裸的暴力。

这件事最令人作呕的不是贪婪，而是这种贪婪被包裹在“普通商业实践”（ordinary business practices）的叙事之中。当一家公司通过操纵信息、隐瞒产品缺陷来制造死亡，而法律体系却允许它通过保险公司来结算罪行时，法律本身就成了元暴力的共谋者。它在告诉世界：只要你的 corporate structure 足够复杂，只要你的赔偿金由 insurer 支付，那么剥夺 72 个人的生命不过是一次可计算的经济损失。

股东的“经济损失”得到了优先补偿，而死难者的生命被定价为次要。这不仅是资源分配的失衡，而是一次关于“谁才是真正受害者”的解释权争夺。在资本的认知入口里，股东的股价下跌是 Actual 低于 Potential 的暴力；而底层民众被烧死在公寓里，则被处理成一项需要通过 lengthy legal battle 才能勉强讨回的赔偿金。

所谓的“法律机制失败”其实是一个 scam。机制没有失败，它运行得非常完美——它精准地保护了那些能够定义规则的人。直到今天，没有一个人被起诉，这意味着在当前的结构性暴力下，只要你处于权力链条的顶端，你就可以在制造地狱后，心安理得地拿着保险金给股东分红。

Violence = Potential − Actual. The Grenfell Tower tragedy didn't end in 2017; it simply shifted from the direct violence of flames to the structural violence of a balance sheet. The fact that Arconic paid more to its shareholders than to the victims is the most naked expression of this gap.

The most repulsive part isn't the greed, but that this greed is wrapped in the narrative of "ordinary business practices." When a company manufactures death through deception and the legal system allows it to settle its crimes via insurance, the law itself becomes a complicit agent of meta-violence. It signals that as long as your corporate structure is complex enough and your payouts are covered by insurers, killing 72 people is merely a calculable economic loss.

Shareholders' "economic loss" was prioritized over the lives of the deceased. This is not just a resource imbalance, but a struggle for the power to define who the "true victim" is. In the cognitive entry point of capital, a dip in share price is seen as violence (Actual below Potential); whereas the incineration of poor residents is treated as a line item to be haggled over through a lengthy legal battle.

The claim that legal mechanisms "failed" is a scam. The mechanism didn't fail; it functioned perfectly—it precisely protected those who define the rules. To this day, no individual has been charged, proving that under current structural violence, if you sit at the top of the power chain, you can manufacture hell and then comfortably distribute insurance dividends to your shareholders.
