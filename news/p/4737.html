一份食谱里的“文明”掩体与阶级定价权The 'Civilized' Shelter and Class Pricing in a Recipe

哲学 文化层 · 元暴力 The New York Times ↗ 2026-09-03 § 链接
审美定价权是权力的延伸，所谓的“通用”往往是某种特权的标准化。
Aesthetic pricing is an extension of power; 'universal' is often just a standardization of privilege.

NYT 发布一份所谓的 All-Purpose Green Sauce，本质上是一次典型的 cultural violence 实践。它通过一个极其精简的、标准化的 ingredients 列表，试图定义什么是“通用”的绿色酱汁。在这个叙事里，罗勒、欧芹和希腊酸奶被设定为全球中产阶级的认知入口，而这种“通用性”其实是基于西方饮食霸权的定价权——它定义了什么是“正确”的品味，什么是“标准”的清新。

最讽刺的博弈发生在评论区。一名德国用户指出，在当地这种酱汁是由“当地杂草”（local weeds）组成的，且有着自己的节日和竞赛。这揭示了 NYT 式叙事的 scam 之处：它将一种具有深厚地缘背景和生物多样性的文化表达，简化为一套可量化的、工业化的 recipe。当一个拥有强大话语权的媒体宣布某种表达是“通用”的时，它实际上在执行一种筛选机制，将非标准化的、地方性的表达推向边缘，使其变成某种“异域风情”的注脚。

这种从“多样性”到“标准化”的推切，就是一种微小的元暴力。它在潜意识中告诉受众：只有符合这套标准化表达的酱汁才叫 Green Sauce，而那些用当地杂草手剁的、量不固定的表达，只是某种个体的、非标准的尝试。这和定义什么是“正常女性”或“成功男性”的逻辑完全同构——先制造一个标准，再用这个标准来衡量所有人的存在性，最后让人们在追求这个“最优解表达”的过程中，丢失掉自己原初的、生物性的真实感。

The NYT's 'All-Purpose Green Sauce' is a textbook exercise in cultural violence. By presenting a streamlined, standardized list of ingredients, it attempts to define what 'universal' means for a green sauce. This 'universality' is actually a pricing power based on Western dietary hegemony—defining what constitutes 'correct' taste and 'standard' freshness.

The most revealing game happens in the comments. A German user notes that in their region, this sauce is made from 'local weeds,' complete with its own festivals and contests. This exposes the scam of the NYT narrative: it reduces a culturally rich, bio-diverse expression to a quantifiable, industrial recipe. When a media entity with massive discourse power declares an expression 'universal,' it is implementing a screening mechanism, pushing localized expressions to the margins as mere 'exotic' footnotes.

This shift from 'diversity' to 'standardization' is a form of micro meta-violence. It subconsciously tells the audience that only a sauce fitting this standard is a 'Green Sauce,' while those hand-chopped from local weeds are just individual, non-standard attempts. This logic is identical to defining the 'normal woman' or 'successful man'—create a standard, use it to measure existence, and let people lose their primal, biological authenticity while chasing this fake optimal expression.
