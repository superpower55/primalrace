用“生活成本”为掩护的战争共谋War Complicity Masked as 'Cost of Living'

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-21 § 链接
所谓的“防御性支持”只是为了维持一个利男的能源结构而进行的共谋。
'Defensive support' is merely a complicity to maintain a masculine-centric energy structure.

Andy Burnham 试图用一个极其拙劣的逻辑来向英国民众兜售他的军事部署：为了降低国内的 living cost，所以必须给沙特战机加油，确保石油流动。这不仅是一个经济 scam，更是一次典型的 cultural violence。他将地缘政治的血腥博弈包装成“为了民众生活”的温情叙事，试图让民众在潜意识里接受一个设定——我们的面包价格取决于我们是否愿意在远方支持一个威权政权去轰炸叛军。

这在加尔通的暴力三角里是教科书级的联动。Direct 层是 RAF 的加油机和沙特的炸弹；Structural 层是全球能源依赖和石油资本的分配机制；而 Cultural 层则是 Burnham 这种“第三条道路”的政治表演。他一边在 UN 场合谈论 AI 和气候变化这种 high-level 的词汇，一边在实际操作中通过 military deployment 来维护一个极度 masculine-centric 的权力结构。这种“既要又要”的姿态，本质上是想在不破坏精英共谋圈的前提下，通过操纵认知入口来安抚底层。

最讽刺的是，Burnham 试图在 Trump 面前扮演一个“有性格”的领导者。但在元暴力的逻辑里，无论谁在位，这种以“稳定”为名、以“能源安全”为饵的共谋永远不会停止。因为这个系统的最优解表达从来不是和平，而是确保那些掌控油井和管道的人能继续掌控定价权。所谓的“dark clouds of global instability”，其实就是这套共谋机制在失效时产生的阵痛，而他们的药方永远是投入更多的武器，而不是拆除这个暴力的结构。

Andy Burnham is attempting to sell his military deployment to the British public through a clumsy logic: to lower the domestic living cost, we must refuel Saudi jets to keep oil flowing. This is not just an economic scam, but a textbook case of cultural violence. By framing geopolitical bloodshed as a mission to 'help people's lives,' he attempts to implant a narrative where the price of bread is tied to our willingness to support an authoritarian regime bombing rebels.

In the Violence Triangle, this is a perfect linkage. The direct layer consists of RAF tankers and Saudi bombs; the structural layer is the global energy dependency and the distribution of oil capital; and the cultural layer is Burnham's 'Third Way' political performance. He speaks of AI and climate change at the UN, while in practice, he uses military deployment to sustain a heavily masculine-centric power structure. This attempt to 'have it both ways' is simply an effort to appease the masses by manipulating cognitive entries without betraying the elite complicity.

Most ironic is Burnham's desire to appear as a 'strong personality' before Trump. Within the logic of meta-violence, it doesn't matter who is in power; the complicity of using 'stability' as a mask and 'energy security' as bait will never cease. The system's optimal expression is never peace, but ensuring that those who control the wells and pipelines continue to control the pricing power. The so-called 'dark clouds of global instability' are merely the withdrawal symptoms of this complicity mechanism, and their only cure is always more weapons, never the dismantling of the structural violence.
