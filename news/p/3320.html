在科文特花园，暴力不分性别，但叙事依然在分In Covent Garden, Violence is Genderless, but Narratives are Not

性别 直接层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-06 § 链接
个案的血腥掩盖了结构性的失能，直接暴力是元暴力的末端回弹。
Bloody incidents mask structural failure; direct violence is the terminal rebound of meta-violence.

一个47岁的女性在伦敦中心地带捅伤四个男人。这条新闻在社交媒体上被迅速地撕裂成两种极端：一边是男权主义者狂欢的“反杀”快感，试图将其定义为某种“女性觉醒”的暴力样本；另一边则是传统的“女性温顺”叙事在面对一个失控中年女性时的惊恐。但这种基于性别的快感或恐惧，本质上都是在共谋同一个逻辑——将暴力简化为性别博弈的个案。

按照加尔通的暴力三角，这次捅人是 direct violence，但它绝不是孤立的。一个“无固定住所”（no fixed address）的47岁女性，在生物墙与社会结构的双重挤压下，她的 Potential（本可达到的生存状态）与 Actual（现实处境）之间存在巨大的差额。这种差额就是结构性暴力。当一个人被剥夺了住所、社会支持系统且处于精神或生存边缘时，她被推向了暴力的末端。这里的讽刺在于，无论她是出于正当防卫还是精神崩溃，大众的注意力点依然在“女性捅男人”这个身份标签上，而非“一个被社会抛弃的女性如何走向极端”。

这种对“身份”的执迷，正是元暴力的运作方式：它让你在讨论暴力时，首先思考的是性别，而不是权力分配。无论结果如何，这种个案被消费成某种“性别战争”的战果或耻辱，最终都服务于那个男性中心叙事——它让人们相信，只要打破了“温顺”的扮演，女性就变成了“疯子”或“怪物”，从而进一步合法化对女性的规训。这不过是又一次关于“谁在施暴”的认知入口争夺，而真正的结构性绝望被掩盖在了血迹之下。

A 47-year-old woman stabbed four men in central London. This news is rapidly being torn apart into two extremes on social media: one side is the sadistic pleasure of masculine-centric circles seeing a 'reversal' of power, attempting to define this as a sample of 'female awakening' violence; the other is the horror of traditional 'feminine submission' narratives facing an uncontrolled middle-aged woman. But this pleasure or fear based on gender is essentially complicity in the same logic—reducing violence to a gender-based game of cases.

According to Galtung's Violence Triangle, this stabbing is direct violence, but it is never isolated. A 47-year-old woman with 'no fixed address' exists in a massive gap between her Potential and her Actual state, squeezed by both the biological wall and structural failure. This gap is structural violence. When a person is stripped of housing, social support, and pushed to the edge of existence, she is driven toward the terminal end of violence. The irony here is that regardless of whether this was self-defense or a mental breakdown, public attention remains fixed on the identity label 'woman stabbing men,' rather than 'how a socially abandoned woman reached this extreme.'

This obsession with 'identity' is exactly how meta-violence operates: it forces you to think about gender first, rather than power distribution, when discussing violence. Whether viewed as a 'victory' or a 'shame' in a gender war, it ultimately serves the masculine-centric narrative—convincing the world that once a woman breaks the performance of 'docility,' she becomes a 'lunatic' or a 'monster,' thereby further legitimizing the discipline of women. This is just another attempt to seize the cognitive entry of 'who is the aggressor,' while the actual structural despair remains hidden beneath the blood.
