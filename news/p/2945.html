微软的“安全”叙事与计划性报废 scamMicrosoft's 'Security' Narrative and the Planned Obsolescence Scam

科技 结构层 · 文化层 Wirecutter ↗ 2026-07-28 § 链接
用安全更新作为筹码，强迫用户进入硬件消费循环。
Using security updates as leverage to force users into a hardware consumption cycle.

微软在 Windows 10 停更这件事上的操作，是典型的 structural violence。它通过定义一个所谓的“安全基准线”，把一个纯粹的软件维护问题，转化为一个让用户必须面对的硬件升级压力。这就是一种 weaponized narrative：它不告诉你软件本身可以继续运行，而是通过制造“不更新即不安全”的恐惧，强行关闭旧设备的生存入口。

所谓的 ESU 扩展安全更新计划，不过是一场表演性的让步。让用户通过付费或同步数据来“买时间”，本质上是在测试用户的耐受度。微软口中的“现代化原则”其实就是“消费主义原则”——它不关心你的电脑是否还能高效工作，它只关心你是否在它的计划内完成了硬件迭代。这种通过软件定义硬件寿命的权力，就是一种对用户存在性的掠夺。

最讽刺的是，微软在文中提到的“回收与可持续”，不过是 cultural violence 的遮羞布。真正的可持续是让软件适配硬件，而不是让硬件为了适配软件而进入垃圾填埋场。这种“为了环保请你去买新电脑”的逻辑，是这个时代最无耻的 scam。

Microsoft's handling of the Windows 10 end-of-support is a textbook case of structural violence. By defining a so-called 'security baseline,' they transform a simple software maintenance issue into a forced hardware upgrade pressure. This is a weaponized narrative: it doesn't tell you the software can still function; instead, it creates a fear that 'no update equals no security,' effectively shutting down the existential entry point for old devices.

The Extended Security Updates (ESU) program is nothing more than a performative concession. Forcing users to pay or sync data to 'buy time' is simply a test of user tolerance. What Microsoft calls a 'modernization principle' is actually a 'consumerist principle'—they don't care if your computer still works efficiently; they only care if you've completed the hardware iteration according to their schedule. This power to define hardware lifespan through software is a form of predation on the user's existence.

The most ironic part is the mention of 'recycling and sustainability' in the text, which serves as a veil for cultural violence. True sustainability means making software adapt to hardware, not pushing hardware into landfills to adapt to software. The logic of 'buy a new computer for the sake of the environment' is the most shameless scam of our era.
