从色情娃娃到教室机器人：一场关于原初种族的实验From Sex Dolls to Classrooms: An Experiment in Primal Race

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-25 § 链接
用制造性客体的逻辑去定义教育，本质是元暴力的认知殖民。
Defining education through the logic of sexual objectification is meta-violence and cognitive colonization.

这件事最荒诞的不是机器人进校园，而是这个机器人的基因——Realbotix 之前通过收购 Simulacra 深度绑定在“真实色情娃娃”产业上。一个习惯于将女性身体物化为可编程、可消费、无主体性的客体的公司，现在试图把这套“模拟人类关系”的算法搬进高中教室。这不仅仅是 creepy，而是一次极其恶劣的 weaponization。它试图在孩子们心中植入一个认知：人类的互动、陪伴甚至教育，可以通过一个 closed-loop 的仿真系统被替代。当教育被简化为“个性化学习选项”的算法输出，被替代的不仅是老师，更是人类主体性的表达。

更令人发指的结构性暴力在于，这个 pilot program 选在了 Seneca 民族领地。在原初种族被殖民、被强制剥夺文化、被寄宿学校摧毁记忆的历史伤口上，统治阶层再次把原住民孩子当作“测试样本”。这种“文化上的耳聋” (culturally tone deaf) 绝非意外，而是典型的殖民逻辑：在最缺乏议价能力、最容易被结构性忽略的群体身上进行低成本实验。从强制寄宿学校到人形机器人，手段从肉体规训变成了数字模拟，但本质没变——他们依然在把这些孩子当作可被操纵的客体。

所谓的 STEM 课程只是一个掩体，用来掩盖这次实验中巨大的权力不对等。当教育局和公司用“补充而非替代”这种 PR 话术进行共谋时，他们实际上在剥夺学生在真实人类社会中建立连接的可能性。如果一个孩子在认知发育期习惯了与一个由色情产业基因演化而来的“完美模拟人”互动，他将失去识别真实、复杂且具有冲突的人类关系的能⼒。这正是元暴力的最高级形态：它不通过直接伤害，而是在潜意识里重新定义什么是“正常”的互动，从而完成对下一代主体性的提前阉割。

The absurdity here isn't the robot itself, but its DNA. Realbotix's deep ties to the 'realistic sex doll' industry via Simulacra reveal a disturbing pivot. A company that specialized in objectifying the female body into a programmable, consumerist void is now attempting to bring that same 'simulated relationship' algorithm into high schools. This is not just creepy; it is a weaponization of expression. It attempts to implant a cognitive seed in students: that human interaction, companionship, and education can be replaced by a closed-loop simulation. When education is reduced to 'personalized learning options' via algorithms, what is being erased is not just the teacher, but the very essence of human subjectivity.

The structural violence is compounded by the location: the Allegany territory of the Seneca nation. To launch a pilot program on the scarred land of a Primal Race—already traumatized by the legacy of Native American boarding schools and cultural erasure—is a textbook example of colonial logic. This 'cultural tone-deafness' is no accident; it is the systemic choice to experiment on those with the least bargaining power. From forced boarding schools to humanoid robots, the method has shifted from physical discipline to digital simulation, but the core remains: treating these children as objects to be tested.

Terms like 'STEM curriculum' are mere shields for a massive power imbalance. While the district and the corporation collude using PR phrases like 'complement, not replace,' they are actually stripping students of the possibility to build authentic human connections. If a child's cognitive development is shaped by interacting with a 'perfect' simulation evolved from the sex industry, they lose the ability to navigate the messy, conflicting reality of true human relationships. This is the peak of meta-violence: it doesn't destroy through direct harm, but by redefining 'normal' interaction, effectively castrating the subjectivity of the next generation.
