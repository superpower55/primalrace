Drake 的狗叫与权力者的“降级”表演Drake's Barking and the Performer's Power Play

哲学 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-14 § 链接
当解释权不再由艺术垄断，权力的最优解就是通过扮演客体来收割流量。
When authority is no longer monopolized by art, the optimal strategy for power is to perform objectification to harvest attention.

Drake 在直播间像贵宾犬一样吠叫，给 OnlyFans 模特买房，这在 Vulture 等媒体看来是一场“中年危机” (midlife crisis)。但用存在性战争的视角看，这根本不是危机，而是一次极其精明的“假.最优解表达”迁移。他意识到传统的 Monoculture（单文化）已经崩塌，那个由唱片公司和电台定义“巨星”的时代结束了，现在的认知入口在 Kick, OnlyFans 和 Manosphere 这些碎片化的 Creator Economy 手里。

这场表演的本质是：一个拥有顶层资源的人，通过主动降低自己的主体性（degrading himself），扮演一个被女性支配的、卑微的客体，来换取年轻一代的注意力。这是一种极其危险的武器化叙事。他把“被物化”包装成一种幽默的、自嘲的娱乐，但实际上他依然是那个掌控 $100m 年薪合同、操纵加密赌场流量的权力主体。他不是在被支配，而是在通过“模拟被支配”来完成对新一代受众的殖民。

最讽刺的是他与 Manosphere（男性圈层）网红的结盟。从《Nice For What》的伪赋权，到现在的《Janice, STFU》，Drake 完成了一次从“ Certified Lover Boy”到“Incel-coded”的身份切换。他精准地捕捉到了当代男性在结构性焦虑中的心理投射：一个顶级成功者通过表现出“我也很委屈/我也被女性玩弄”来获得共情。这是一种典型的元暴力伪装——用弱者的姿态来巩固强者的统治。

Drake 并没有在曲线前面，他只是在向这个时代的暴力逻辑低头：只要能换来流量和钱，主体性的死亡根本不算代价，而是一次成功的商业套利。

Drake barking like a poodle in a livestream to buy houses for OnlyFans models is framed by media as a 'midlife crisis.' In the context of the existential war, however, this is not a crisis, but a calculated migration toward a 'fake optimal expression.' He recognizes that the Monoculture is dead; the gateways of cognition have shifted from radio and labels to the fragmented Creator Economy of Kick and the Manosphere.

The essence of this performance is the strategic surrender of agency. By simulating the role of a degraded object, Drake converts his top-tier status into a currency of 'relatability' for Gen Z. This is a weaponized narrative: he packages 'objectification' as self-deprecating humor, while remaining the power-player controlling $100m contracts and crypto-casino traffic. He is not being dominated; he is colonizing new audiences by mimicking the act of being dominated.

More sinister is his alliance with Manosphere influencers. Shifting from the pseudo-empowerment of 'Nice For What' to the hostility of 'Janice, STFU,' Drake has pivoted from 'Certified Lover Boy' to 'Incel-coded.' He is weaponizing the structural anxiety of modern men, using the persona of the 'misunderstood successful man' to build a new kind of complicity.

Drake isn't ahead of the curve; he is simply submitting to the current logic of violence: as long as it yields traffic and capital, the death of subjectivity is not a cost, but a successful commercial arbitrage.
