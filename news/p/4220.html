Safari 上的死亡：谁在消费肯尼亚的荒野？Death on Safari: Who is Consuming the Kenyan Wilderness?

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-19 § 链接
特权阶层的存在性战争，是用他者的结构性暴力为燃料的 Safari。
The existential war of the privileged is a Safari fueled by the structural violence of others.

这起直升机坠毁事件在主流叙事中被包装成一场“不幸的意外”，但如果用 Violence = Potential − Actual 的公式去看，这里充满了 structural violence。一个美国媒体高管、一个厄瓜多尔情报局局长，这些处于权力顶端的个体，通过购买昂贵的 charter flight，将肯尼亚的自然景观转化为一种可消费的“审美表达”。这种表达的本质是：将他者的土地客体化为自己的背景板。

注意新闻中的细节：坠毁发生在 remote part，由本地公司 Lady Lori Helicopters 运营。在肯尼亚这个高度依赖旅游业的国家，这种 thriving tourism industry 的背后是极不对等的资源分配。特权阶层在空中俯瞰荒野，而支撑这个产业的本地飞行员和地勤人员，在缺乏足够安全冗余的结构性压力下，承担着最高的 direct violence 风险。直升机坠毁的“increasing regularity”不是随机的概率，而是结构性维护缺失与过度商业开发共谋的结果。

最讽刺的是，死者中包括一名情报局局长。这些掌握着国家机器、定义着“安全”与“真相”的人，在追求私人最优解表达（极致的 Safari 体验）时，最终被他们习惯于操控的那种“不稳定性”所吞噬。这不仅是一场空难，更是权力在消费边缘地带时，被结构性暴力反噬的必然结果。

Mainstream narratives package this helicopter crash as a 'tragic accident,' but applying the formula Violence = Potential − Actual reveals deep-seated structural violence. A US media executive and an Ecuadorian intelligence chief—individuals at the apex of power—converted Kenya's landscape into a consumable 'aesthetic expression' via expensive charter flights. The essence of this expression is the objectification of another's land as a mere backdrop.

Note the details: the crash occurred in a 'remote part,' operated by a local company. In a nation where a 'thriving tourism industry' is the backbone, the reality is a grotesque imbalance of resources. While the privileged overlook the wilderness from above, the local pilots and ground crews, operating under structural pressures with insufficient safety redundancies, bear the highest risk of direct violence. The 'increasing regularity' of these crashes is not a random fluke, but the result of a complicity between structural maintenance failures and aggressive commercialization.

The irony peaks with the death of an intelligence director. Those who command the state apparatus and define 'security' and 'truth' were consumed by the very 'instability' they usually manipulate, all while pursuing a private optimal expression of an elite safari. This was not just a crash; it was the inevitable backlash of structural violence when power consumes the periphery.
