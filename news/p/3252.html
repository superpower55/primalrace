AI 故事的“高质量”：一场关于认知入口的规模化骗局The 'High Quality' of AI Stories: A Scaled Scam of Cognitive Entry

科技 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-04 § 链接
所谓的“质量”提升，本质上是算法对人类审美共识的精准反向工程。
所谓的 'quality' is merely a reverse-engineered statistical average of human aesthetic consensus.

这是一场典型的关于“认知入口”的武器化实验。当参与者认为 AI 故事质量更高时，他们其实是在赞美一种被极度平滑化、去冲突化的“标准答案”。AI 并不创造美，它只是通过对海量人类文本的统计学预测，剔除了所有让阅读感到“费力”的个体性刺点，从而制造出一种极易被消化、无害且符合大众预期（Average）的伪高质量。

这就是一个巨大的 scam。Weisberg 提到的“易于阅读和消化”正是关键：真正的创造力往往来自对既有叙事的破坏与挑战，而 AI 的逻辑是增强共识。当人类开始用“易读性”来定义“质量”时，我们实际上在经历一次集体性的审美降级。我们不再追求被挑战，而是在追求一种被算法精准喂养的舒适感。

更深层的暴力在于，这套系统的底层是 Luke Kennard 所说的“大规模窃取”。AI 将数千年的个体表达——那些包含痛苦、血泪与主体性挣扎的真实生命体验——解构为概率分布的 Token。这是一种极致的 meta violence：它不仅窃取了作品，更在定义“什么是好故事”的权力上，将解释权从真实的创作者手中让渡给了掌控算力的资本。当 AI 故事被评为“更好”时，人类的表达被异化成了训练集，而主体性在这次博弈中被彻底抹除。

This is a classic weaponization of the cognitive entry. When participants rate AI stories as 'higher quality,' they are actually praising a smoothed-out, conflict-free 'standard answer.' AI does not create beauty; it merely uses statistical prediction across massive datasets to prune away the individual 'stings' that make reading effortful, manufacturing a pseudo-quality that is easily digestible and aligns with the average expectation.

It is a total scam. The 'easier to read and digest' aspect mentioned by Weisberg is the smoking gun: true creativity stems from challenging and disrupting existing narratives, while AI logic is about reinforcing consensus. When we begin to define 'quality' as 'readability,' we are undergoing a collective aesthetic downgrade. We no longer seek to be challenged; we seek the comfort of being precisely fed by an algorithm.

The deeper violence lies in what Luke Kennard calls the 'massive stolen database.' AI deconstructs millennia of individual expression—real life experiences filled with pain, blood, and the struggle for subjectivity—into a probability distribution of tokens. This is the pinnacle of meta-violence: it not only steals the work but shifts the power of defining 'what makes a good story' from the actual creator to the capital controlling the compute. When an AI story is rated 'better,' human expression is reduced to a training set, and subjectivity is completely erased in this existential game.
