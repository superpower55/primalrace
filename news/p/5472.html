选举的幻象与被献祭的左翼叙事The Illusion of Election and the Sacrificed Leftist Narrative

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-09 § 链接
低投票率是选民对既定政治共谋的无声投票。
Low turnout is a silent vote against the established political complicity.

这场补选被包装成左翼阵营的“高风险之战”，但数据揭露了真相：38.7% 的投票率，比 2024 年暴跌 15.4%。当 Actual 如此远离 Potential，这根本不是什么“胜利”，而是一场在废墟上的权力交接。选民用脚投票，表达了对无论 Labour 还是 Green 这种结构性共谋者的集体厌倦。

Labour 的胜利是典型的 structural violence 运作。他们推出 Sagal Abdi-Wali，一个前难民背景的女性领导者，这在叙事上是极佳的 weaponization。通过将 Reform UK 的种族主义攻击标签化，Labour 成功地将一场关于政策的博弈转化为一场关于“身份政治”的道德审判。在这种叙事下，选民被引导去投票给一个“正确”的身份，而非一个能解决实际问题的方案。这是典型的用 identity politics 掩盖主体性缺失的 scam。

而绿党（Greens）的溃败则揭示了左翼内部的 meta violence。他们试图通过将 Zionism 定义为 racism 来争夺认知入口，但这在内部引发了剧烈的撕裂。Polanski 在关键时刻的缺席，证明了他缺乏在存在性战争中制定规则的能力，只能在 Labour 划定的战场上扮演一个“挑战者”的配角。当一个政党在定义“什么是种族主义”这种元叙事上陷入混乱时，它就失去了向结构性权力发起进攻的合法性。

最终，Andy Burnham 获得了所谓的“支持率回升”，但这不过是共谋者在权力更迭后的短暂蜜月期。这场选举没有缩小任何暴力的差额，它只是在旧的男本位、建制派叙事中，换了一个更具“多样性”的面孔来维持原有的权力分配。真正的胜利应该是投票率的激增和对结构性不平等的实质性挑战，而不是在低参与度的游戏中庆祝一次预设的胜出。

This byelection was packaged as a 'high-stakes battle' for the British left, but the data reveals a grimmer reality: a 38.7% turnout, a plunge of 15.4% from 2024. When Actual is this far from Potential, this isn't a 'victory'—it's a power handover in a wasteland. Voters used their absence to express a collective exhaustion with the structural complicity of both Labour and the Greens.

Labour's win is a textbook operation of structural violence. By fielding Sagal Abdi-Wali, a former child refugee, they employed a potent weaponization of narrative. By framing Reform UK's attacks as 'straightforward racism,' Labour successfully pivoted a policy debate into a moral trial of identity politics. Voters were nudged to vote for a 'correct' identity rather than a viable solution—a classic scam where identity politics masks a void of agency.

Meanwhile, the Greens' defeat exposes the meta violence within the left. Their attempt to seize the cognitive entry point by defining Zionism as racism triggered an internal rupture. Polanski's absence during the critical vote proves his inability to set the rules in this existential war; he remained a mere 'challenger' in a game designed by Labour. When a party collapses while trying to define the meta-narrative of 'what is racism,' it loses its legitimacy to attack structural power.

Ultimately, Andy Burnham's 'recovered support' is merely a brief honeymoon for co-conspirators after a leadership shuffle. This election narrowed no gap of violence; it simply replaced a face within the masculine-centric, establishment narrative with one that looks more 'diverse' to maintain the same power distribution. A true victory would be a surge in turnout and a substantive challenge to structural inequality, not celebrating a predetermined win in a low-engagement game.
