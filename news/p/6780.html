一句公道话的定价权Who Gets To Re-Judge The Oscars

文娱 元暴力 A.V. Club ↗ 2026-11-02 § 链接
新闻摘要Michael Douglas 近日在《The Graham Norton Show》与 Glenn Close 同台时表示，Close 应凭《Fatal Attraction》拿下奥斯卡影后，当年该奖颁给了凭《Moonstruck》获奖的 Cher。Close 今年将获奥斯卡荣誉奖，她此前已获八次提名、从未获奖。
The newsOn The Graham Norton Show, Michael Douglas said Glenn Close should have won the Best Actress Oscar for Fatal Attraction; the award went to Cher for Moonstruck. Close, who has eight nominations and no competitive wins, will receive an honorary Academy Award this year.
四十年前的输赢，今天还得由他盖章
Forty years on, the verdict still needs his signature.

Michael Douglas 在 Graham Norton 的沙发上替 Glenn Close 打抱不平：Cher 那座影后该是 Close 的。听起来像迟到的公道，其实是另一回事——谁有资格在四十年后重新宣判一场评选，谁就还握着那张椅子。

Close 自己说的是另一套话：荣誉奖来了，她说「他们大概不想再内疚了」。这句话比 Douglas 的赞美锋利得多，它直接点出荣誉奖的性质——不是补发奖杯，是补发一张收据，让学院不必再欠着。八次提名，零次获奖，这个数字不需要任何框架来加工，它本身就是一份记录。

问题在于，这份记录由谁来读。Douglas 说「我们都这么觉得」——这个「我们」是谁？是当年投票的那群人，还是当年没投票但同样有意见的那群人？他说的是「她演出了史上最好的表演之一」，可这个「之一」是谁定的级？一个男演员在脱口秀上把一位女演员的职业生涯重新定级，观众点头，掌声响起，事情就结束了。Close 的八次提名里有多少次是输给了「我们都这么觉得」的共识，报道没写，但那个共识的制造过程，从来不在沙发上。

荣誉奖是好事，Close 拿得理直气壮。但它和 Douglas 的这句话放在一起，就成了一个完整的闭环：先由同行男性认证她的伟大，再由学院用一座非竞赛奖杯把账结清，最后所有人都觉得公道回来了。真正没被回答的是——如果她当年就赢了，今天还需要谁来替她说这句话？

Cher 什么都没说。她只是四十年前赢了，现在被当成一个参照物。这场对话里唯一没有被问到的人，是那个被拿来当反例的人。

Michael Douglas told Graham Norton's couch that Cher's Oscar should have gone to Glenn Close. It sounds like a late correction. It is really a reminder of who still holds the gavel — and that it was never Close's to hold.

Close said the sharper thing herself: about the honorary award, she joked that they probably don't want to feel guilty anymore. That is the whole mechanism in one line. An honorary Oscar is not a trophy returned; it is a receipt issued so the Academy stops owing. Eight nominations, zero wins. The number needs no framework to do its work.

But Douglas supplied the framework anyway: one of the greatest performances of all time. Who assigned that rank? The same room that voted, or the room that didn't vote but always had an opinion? A male co-star re-grades a woman's career on a talk show, the audience nods, and the matter is closed. How many of Close's eight losses were to the same consensus is not in the report. Neither is who manufactured it.

The honorary award is real and Close has earned it. Put next to Douglas's line, though, it completes a loop: a male peer certifies her greatness, the Academy settles the account with a non-competitive prize, and everyone agrees justice has been done. What goes unanswered is the only question that matters — if she had won then, would anyone need to say this now?

Cher said nothing. She won forty years ago and is now a prop in someone else's correction. The one person in this conversation nobody thought to ask is the one used as the counterexample.
