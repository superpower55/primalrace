死亡的定价权与战争的财务报表The Pricing of Death and the Balance Sheets of War

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-30 § 链接
战争叙事是最大的文化暴力，它将人类的毁灭转化为企业的季度利润。
War narratives are the ultimate cultural violence, converting human annihilation into corporate quarterly profits.

这是一场极其典型的共谋：政府通过制造“全球威胁”的波动性叙事，为军工复合体地毯式铺设订单。在 Rolls-Royce 和 BAE Systems 的财报里，战争不是灾难，而是“增长机遇” (growth opportunities)；死亡不是悲剧，而是“利润指引” (earnings guidance) 的上调。这就是元暴力的极致体现——男性中心叙事将世界定义为一个永恒的博弈场，而在这个场域中，身体被降格为可消耗的零件，而杀戮被包装成“国防”与“安全”。

这种共谋的逻辑链条极其闭环：国家机器制造恐惧 $\rightarrow$ 恐惧转化为预算 $\rightarrow$ 预算转化为军工企业的自由现金流 $\rightarrow$ 资本通过股市上涨回馈共谋者。注意，这种结构性暴力 (structural violence) 具有极强的欺骗性，它让人们相信增加军备能带来安全，但实际上，它增加的是杀戮的效率和资本的贪婪。当一个 CEO 用“转型” (transformation) 来描述公司在自主推进系统上的突破时，他实际上是在优化如何更高效地在远程操控中抹除他人的存在。

最讽刺的是，这种暴力在文化层面上被完全合法化。无论是在北约峰会的承诺中，还是在向土耳其出口台风战斗机的合同里，这些交易都被冠以“盟友”和“支持”之名。这正是文化暴力 (cultural violence) 的功能：让直接暴力看起来像是一种理性的、文明的战略部署。在这种叙事下，人们关注的是 FTSE 100 的涨幅，而非被导弹击中的躯体。

This is a textbook case of complicity: governments manufacture a narrative of 'global volatility' to lay a carpet of orders for the military-industrial complex. In the financial reports of Rolls-Royce and BAE Systems, war is not a catastrophe but a 'growth opportunity'; death is not a tragedy but an upward revision of 'earnings guidance.' This is the pinnacle of meta-violence—the masculine-centric narrative defines the world as a perpetual battlefield where bodies are downgraded to consumable parts and slaughter is rebranded as 'defense' and 'security.'

The logic of this complicity is perfectly closed: State machines manufacture fear $\rightarrow$ fear transforms into budgets $\rightarrow$ budgets transform into free cash flow for defense firms $\rightarrow$ capital rewards the co-conspirators via stock surges. This structural violence is profoundly deceptive, tricking the public into believing that arming up brings safety, while it actually only increases the efficiency of killing and the greed of capital. When a CEO describes the 'transformation' of his company's autonomous propulsion, he is effectively optimizing how to erase human existence more efficiently via remote control.

Most cynically, this violence is entirely legitimized at the cultural level. Whether in NATO summit commitments or contracts exporting Typhoon jets to Turkey, these transactions are cloaked in terms like 'allies' and 'support.' This is precisely the function of cultural violence: making direct violence appear as a rational, civilized strategic deployment. Under this narrative, the world watches the rise of the FTSE 100, while ignoring the bodies torn apart by missiles.
