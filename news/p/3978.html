用“恐惧”重新定义事实的认知 scamThe Cognitive Scam of Redefining Reality Through Fear

国际 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-17 § 链接
认知偏差不是因为无知，而是武器化叙事对真实世界的成功覆盖。
Cognitive bias is not ignorance, but the successful overlay of weaponized narratives over actual facts.

这篇报道揭露了一个典型的认知入口被劫持的现场：公众对杀人犯族裔的认知与事实之间存在巨大的 gap。人们认为 55% 的杀人犯来自少数族裔，而真实数字是 35%；认为白人杀人犯仅占 45%，而真实数字是 64%。这种规模的偏差绝不是简单的“误解”，而是一场精心策划的 cultural violence。

极右翼政党（如 Reform）通过将犯罪与族裔、国籍强行绑定，成功地将恐惧 weaponized。他们不需要事实，只需要制造一种“感觉”。当这种叙事通过社交媒体和政治沟通密集投放时，它就成了受众的认知底色。人们在潜意识中通过这种偏差表达自己的“担忧”，而这种担忧反过来又成了他们筛选事实的滤镜——只看符合预期的，无视违背事实的。这就是典型的认知入口被夺取后的结果：事实不再重要，重要的是叙事能为谁赢得选票。

最阴险的共谋在于“两级司法” (two-tier justice) 这种伪命题的流行。通过制造“少数族裔更容易逃脱惩罚”的假象，共谋者们成功地让人们质疑现有的法律系统，从而为推动更激进、更具排他性的“激进变革”铺路。这是一种元暴力的升级：先用虚构的受害者身份（被不公正对待的多数族裔）来掩盖真实的施暴结构。

这场博弈的赢家是那些通过制造恐惧来获取权力的人。而真正的受害者，是那些被贴上“原初种族”式标签的少数族裔，以及在恐惧中丧失理性判断力的普通公众。当一个社会开始用“情绪”替代“事实”来定义真实，那么任何群体都可能在下一个叙事周期里成为被献祭的客体。

This report exposes a textbook case of cognitive entry points being hijacked: the massive gap between public perception and the reality of killers' ethnicities. People believe 55% of killers are from ethnic minorities, while the real figure is 35%; they think white killers account for only 45%, whereas the reality is 64%. A deviation of this scale is never a simple 'misunderstanding'—it is a calculated act of cultural violence.

Far-right parties, such as Reform, have successfully weaponized fear by forcibly linking crime to ethnicity and nationality. They don't need facts; they only need to manufacture a 'feeling.' When this narrative is intensively deployed through social media and political communications, it becomes the cognitive baseline for the audience. People express their 'concerns' through this bias, and these concerns then act as a filter—they only notice what fits their expectations and ignore what contradicts them. This is the result of the cognitive entry point being seized: facts are irrelevant; only the narrative that wins votes matters.

The most sinister complicity lies in the proliferation of the 'two-tier justice' myth. By fabricating the illusion that ethnic minorities face more lenient sentencing, conspirators drive the public to question the legal system, paving the way for more radical, exclusionary 'reforms.' This is an escalation of meta-violence: using a fictional victim identity (the 'mistreated' majority) to mask the actual structure of violence.

The winners of this game are those who acquire power by manufacturing fear. The real victims are the ethnic minorities—treated as the Primal Race—and the general public who lose their capacity for rational judgment. When a society begins to define reality through 'emotion' rather than 'fact,' any group can become the sacrificed object in the next narrative cycle.
