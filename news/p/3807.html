利用“羞耻感”的狩猎与共谋的代价Hunting via Shame and the Price of Complicity

性别 文化层 · 结构层 · 直接层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-13 § 链接
犯罪不在于性交易，而在于对“男性羞耻”这一认知入口的精准武器化。
The crime lies not in sex work, but in the weaponization of 'masculine shame' as a cognitive entry.

这起案件最阴险的地方不在于投毒，而在于犯罪者对认知入口（cognitive entry）的精准计算。Adina Mihai 和她的共谋者 Dumitru 并非在提供性服务，而是在利用一个极其稳固的文化共谋：男性在寻求非法性交易时产生的“羞耻感” (embarrassment)。

在父权结构的叙事中，男性被定义为狩猎者和掌控者，但当他们处于“购买性服务”这一灰色地带时，他们突然变成了潜在的受害者，且由于社会对这种行为的隐形污名化，他们失去了寻求法律保护的勇气。犯罪者正是捕捉到了这个心理漏洞，将“羞耻”变成了保护伞，把受害者的沉默预设为犯罪计划的一部分。这是一种典型的表达武器化——利用对方在身份政治中的弱点，制造一个无需担心被举报的“安全”犯罪空间。

至于这对伴侣的合作模式，则是最平庸的共谋 (complicity)。女性扮演诱饵，男性在外部待命，两人共同分担风险并分享赃物。在这种博弈中，女性的身体被工具化为开启受害者大门的钥匙，而男性则通过掌控整体计划来确保利益分配。这并非什么“女性反杀”或“弱者崛起”，而是一场纯粹的、基于贪婪的低端犯罪，其本质依然是在既定秩序的缝隙中寻找漏洞进行掠夺。

法官提到的“缺乏研究”和“随意投药”揭示了这种暴力在 direct 层面的粗糙。但更深层的 structural violence 在于，只要社会依然将某些欲望定义为“不可言说”的羞耻，这种利用羞耻感进行狩猎的逻辑就永远有生存土壤。死亡是直接暴力的结果，但沉默才是这起案件真正的共谋基础。

The most sinister aspect of this case is not the poisoning, but the precise calculation of the cognitive entry. Adina Mihai and her accomplice Dumitru were not providing sexual services; they were exploiting a deeply entrenched cultural complicity: the 'embarrassment' felt by men seeking illegal sex work.

In the masculine-centric narrative, men are defined as hunters and controllers. However, when positioned in the grey area of purchasing sex, they suddenly become vulnerable victims who, due to the implicit social stigma of such acts, lose the courage to seek legal protection. The criminals captured this psychological loophole, turning shame into a shield and presupposing the victims' silence as a core part of their operational plan. This is a textbook example of the weaponisation of expression—using the opponent's vulnerability in identity politics to create a 'safe' space for crime.

As for the partnership, it is a banal form of complicity. The woman acted as the bait while the man waited nearby, sharing both the risk and the loot. In this game, the female body was instrumentalized as the key to the victims' homes, while the man ensured the distribution of profit by controlling the overall scheme. This is neither 'female empowerment' nor 'the weak striking back,' but a crude, greed-driven crime seeking loopholes in the existing order.

The 'lack of research' and 'haphazard administration' mentioned by the judge reveal the crudeness of the direct violence. Yet, the deeper structural violence is that as long as society continues to define certain desires as unspeakable shame, the logic of hunting via shame will always have a breeding ground. Death is the result of direct violence, but silence was the true foundation of complicity in this case.
