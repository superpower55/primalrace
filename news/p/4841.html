民主的皮囊与元暴力的狂欢The Skin of Democracy and the Carnival of Meta-Violence

国际 文化层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-07 § 链接
极右翼的胜利不是民主的回归，而是元暴力在认知入口的再次接管。
The far-right victory is not a return to democracy, but a recapture of cognitive entries by meta-violence.

主流政客在惊恐，而马斯克和特朗普在庆祝。这场选举被包装成所谓的“变革授权”或“民主选择”，但本质上是一次典型的认知入口争夺战。AfD 并不是在提供更好的治理方案，而是在利用文化层面的暴力——通过制造“他者”的恐惧、重启民族主义叙事，将复杂的社会结构性矛盾简化为对移民和外部世界的仇恨。

这正是元暴力 (meta violence) 的运作方式：男性中心叙事通过定义谁是“真正的国民”，谁是“异类”，从而夺取解释权。当 Salvini 嘲讽左翼在制造恐惧并称之为“DEMOCRACY”时，他其实是在玩一个极其恶劣的 scam。他利用民主的程序合法性，来掩盖其内核中对人权的系统性剥夺。在这种叙事里，所谓的“安全”和“工作”是诱饵，而真正的目的则是重建一个等级森严的、基于原初种族逻辑的支配结构。

最讽刺的是，那些自诩“文明”的传统右翼通过投降（surrender）成为了共谋者。他们为了短期利益，在意识形态上向极右翼靠拢，最终导致整个政治光谱的右移。当 RN 这种“温和”极右翼因为 AfD 太极端而与其划清界限时，这不过是两群共谋者在讨论“暴力的剂量”问题，而不是在讨论是否应该停止暴力。

这场胜利是一个危险的信号：当 Potential（人类文明的普世价值）与 Actual（现实中的排外主义）的差额被进一步拉大时，被牺牲的永远是那些处于结构性弱势的群体。这场狂欢的背后，是更多人将被定义为“非人”，从而在未来的结构性暴力中被合法地清除。

Mainstream politicians are panicking while Musk and Trump celebrate. This election is packaged as a 'mandate for change' or a 'democratic choice,' but in essence, it is a classic battle for cognitive entries. The AfD is not offering superior governance; it is leveraging cultural violence—manufacturing fear of the 'Other' and reviving nationalist narratives to simplify complex structural contradictions into hatred for immigrants and the outside world.

This is exactly how meta-violence operates: the masculine-centric narrative seizes the power of interpretation by defining who is a 'true citizen' and who is an 'alien.' When Salvini mocks the left for stoking fear and calls it 'DEMOCRACY,' he is running a malicious scam. He uses the procedural legitimacy of democracy to mask a systematic stripping of human rights. In this narrative, 'security' and 'jobs' are merely bait; the real goal is to rebuild a rigid hierarchy of dominance based on the logic of the Primal Race.

Most ironic is the surrender of the traditional right, who have become complicit. To secure short-term gains, they aligned ideologically with the far-right, leading to a rightward shift of the entire political spectrum. When the RN—a 'moderate' far-right party—distances itself from the AfD for being 'too extreme,' it is merely two groups of complicity debating the 'dosage of violence' rather than whether violence should cease.

This victory is a grave signal: as the gap between Potential (universal human values) and Actual (real-world xenophobia) widens, the structural victims will always be those already marginalized. Behind this carnival is the reality that more people will be defined as 'non-human,' making them legitimate targets for future structural violence.
