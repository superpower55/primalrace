用宪法为 Gerrymandering 刷漆的共谋游戏The Complicity Game: Painting Gerrymandering with Constitutional Paint

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-22 § 链接
法律被武器化为一种掩体，用程序正义掩盖结构性掠夺。
Law is weaponized as a shield to mask structural predation through procedural justice.

这是一场典型的关于“认知入口”的博弈。共和党通过重新绘制选区地图（Gerrymandering），试图在物理空间上通过切分选民来预设选举结果。这本质上是一次对民主投票权的 structural violence：它不是通过说服选民来赢，而是通过修改“怎么算赢”的规则来赢。在这种逻辑下，选民不再是决策的主体，而成了被摆放的零件。

最令人作呕的是这次上诉法院的叙事——他们宣称回归旧地图会“违反宪法”。这是一种极其典型的 meta violence：将权力者的掠夺行为包装成对法律的忠诚。当法律不再是为了保障人权，而是被用来为特定的政治阵营提供“合法性”掩护时，法律就成了武器化的表达工具。法院在这里扮演的是共谋者（complicit），它通过提供一个法律解释的入口，试图将一场赤裸裸的权力操弄转化为一次“宪法维护”行为。

好在最高法院目前还挡在前面，但这不代表正义地赢了，而仅仅是两个权力中心在博弈。在这种结构性暴力中，普通选民的主体性被彻底抹杀。他们被告知这在讨论“法律程序”，但实际上，这在讨论谁能垄断解释权，以及谁能决定哪些人的票在统计学上“毫无意义”。

This is a textbook battle over the 'cognitive entry point.' By redrawing congressional maps—Gerrymandering—the Republicans are attempting to predetermine election outcomes by physically carving up the electorate. This is structural violence against the right to vote: they aren't winning by persuading voters, but by modifying the rules of 'how to win.' In this logic, the voter is no longer the subject of decision-making, but a component to be rearranged.

The most repulsive part is the appellate court's narrative—claiming that reverting to old maps would 'violate the Constitution.' This is a classic manifestation of meta violence: packaging the predator's grab as loyalty to the law. When the law ceases to protect human rights and instead provides 'legitimacy' for a specific political camp, it becomes a weaponized expression. The court here acts as a complicit agent, providing a legal entry point to transform naked power manipulation into an act of 'constitutional maintenance.'

Fortunately, the Supreme Court currently stands in the way, but this isn't a victory for justice; it is merely a game between two centers of power. Within this structural violence, the subjectivity of the average voter is completely erased. They are told this is a debate over 'legal procedure,' but in reality, it is a fight over who monopolizes the power of interpretation and who decides whose vote is statistically 'meaningless.'
