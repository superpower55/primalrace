用“不可抗力”给系统性崩溃买单Paying for Systemic Collapse with 'Extraordinary Circumstances'

其他 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-12 § 链接
当结构性漏洞被个体错误触发，定义权决定谁是受害者，谁是共谋。
When structural flaws are triggered by individual errors, the power of definition decides who is the victim and who is the complicit.

这就是典型的 structural violence：一个军用飞机的“异常数据”就能让整个英国空中交通管制系统 meltdown，这根本不是什么“意外”，而是系统在极度脆弱状态下的必然崩溃。Nats 所谓的“系统全面升级”其实是一个巨大的 scam，在没有备份方案的情况下强行推进，把数以万计的乘客变成了这场技术博弈的肉盾。

最令人作呕的是关于“赔偿”的叙事。航空公司和 Nats 迅速将此定义为“ extraordinary circumstances”（异常情况），这意味着乘客被剥夺了索赔的权利。在认知入口上，他们试图将一次严重的管理失职包装成不可抗力的“天灾”，从而在经济上完成一次完美的掠夺。乘客的损失被定义为零，而系统的低能被定义为“罕见”。

而国防部（MoD）的反应则揭示了权力结构的共谋：只要军方宣布“没有过错”，那么无论造成多少混乱，军事优先权永远高于民用秩序。Martin Rolfe 试图用“50年来未见”这种叙事来掩盖其在 backup 系统上的吝啬。这不仅是技术故障，而是一场关于谁该为崩溃支付代价的权力游戏。最终，成本由最底层的乘客承担，而决策者们在调查报告的文字游戏里继续共谋。

This is a textbook case of structural violence: the fact that a single military aircraft's "spurious data" could trigger a total meltdown of the UK's air traffic control proves this wasn't an accident, but an inevitable collapse of a fragile system. Nats' so-called "complete overhaul" is a scam—pushing through upgrades without a dedicated backup, effectively using hundreds of thousands of passengers as human shields for their technical gamble.

The most repulsive part is the narrative around compensation. By quickly labeling the outage as "extraordinary circumstances," airlines and Nats have stripped passengers of their right to claim damages. At the cognitive entry point, they are weaponizing language to rebrand gross managerial negligence as an unavoidable act of God, completing a seamless economic plunder. Passenger loss is defined as zero, while systemic incompetence is defined as "rare."

The Ministry of Defence's reaction further reveals the complicity of the power structure: as long as the military declares "no wrongdoing," military priority remains absolute over civilian order. Martin Rolfe attempts to hide his stinginess regarding backup systems behind the narrative of an event "unseen in 50 years." This isn't just a technical glitch; it's a power game over who pays for the collapse. In the end, the cost is borne by the passengers at the bottom, while the decision-makers continue their complicity within the wordplay of investigation reports.
