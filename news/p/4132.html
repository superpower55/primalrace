主权是强权者的遮羞布，而正义是他们的scamSovereignty as a Shield for Power, Justice as a Scam

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-18 § 链接
用“主权”定义法律豁免权，是典型的元暴力武器化。
Using 'sovereignty' to define legal immunity is a classic weaponization of meta-violence.

美国这次对ICC总统和检察官的制裁，把“ weaponized expression”玩到了极致。马可·鲁比奥口中的“腐败”和“政治化”，其实是他自己正在执行的剧本：通过定义什么是“合法授权”，来夺取对“事实”的最终解释权。当一个国家不认同某种法律裁决时，它不讨论证据，而是直接宣布对方“越权”，然后用经济制裁这种 structural violence 来物理性地封杀正义的表达空间。

这不仅是地缘政治，更是典型的 masculine-centric narrative。在这种叙事里，所谓的“主权” (Sovereignty) 不是为了保护公民，而是作为一种特权，允许强权者在实施 direct violence（如加沙的战争罪行）后，能够免疫任何形式的外部审判。这种逻辑极其简单：我定义规则，我定义谁是合法执行者，而任何试图用普世人权来约束我的尝试，都被定义为对“主权”的威胁。

最讽刺的是，美国在全球范围内推销“基于规则的国际秩序”，但当规则指向自己的盟友或士兵时，这套秩序瞬间变成了 scam。这是一种深层的 meta violence：它通过垄断对“文明”和“法律”的解释权，将施暴者包装成秩序的维护者，而将追求正义的法律官员标记为“恶意滥权”的罪犯。在这种共谋下，受害者的血在 structural 层面上被抹除，因为他们失去了在法律上被“看见”的可能性。

The US sanctions against the ICC president and prosecutor are a masterclass in the weaponisation of expression. Marco Rubio’s rhetoric about the court being “corrupt” and “politicized” is simply a projection of his own script: by redefining “legal mandate,” the US is attempting to seize the final interpretation of facts. When a superpower disagrees with a legal ruling, it doesn't debate evidence; it declares the process “illegitimate” and employs structural violence—via sanctions—to physically erase the space for just expressions.

This is more than geopolitics; it is a textbook masculine-centric narrative. In this framework, “Sovereignty” is not a tool for protecting citizens, but a privilege allowing the powerful to commit direct violence—such as war crimes in Gaza—while remaining immune to external judgment. The logic is crude: I define the rules, I define the legitimate actors, and any attempt to constrain me through universal human rights is branded as a threat to “sovereignty.”

The irony is palpable. The US promotes a “rules-based international order” globally, yet the moment those rules target its allies or soldiers, the order is revealed as a scam. This is a form of meta-violence: by monopolizing the interpretation of “civilization” and “law,” the aggressor is rebranded as the guardian of order, while legal officials seeking justice are labeled as “malicious” actors. Through this complicity, the blood of victims is erased at a structural level, because they are stripped of the possibility of being “seen” by the law.
