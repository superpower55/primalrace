气候危机没有“殖民幽默”的豁免权Climate Crisis Has No Exemption for 'Colonial Humor'

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-12 § 链接
当生存基准线崩塌，任何基于身份认同的调侃都是在掩盖结构性暴力。
When the baseline of survival collapses, any mockery based on identity is merely masking structural violence.

澳大利亚人习惯于通过嘲讽英国人来确立自己的国家身份——这是一种典型的 identity politics，通过在文化层面上解构曾经的殖民者来获得心理上的主体性。在大多数时候，这种“国民娱乐”是无害的 cultural expression，是后殖民时代的某种情绪补偿。

但当英国遭遇极端干旱，气温飙升至 38°C 时，这种叙事失效了。因为气候危机不是一个可以被“幽默”消解的身份标签，而是一个巨大的 structural violence。极端天气对人类生存基准线的打击是无差别的，它不看你的护照颜色，也不在乎你曾经在历史中扮演过殖民者还是被殖民者。

如果澳大利亚人继续在这种时刻通过嘲讽来获得快感，那就是在陷入一种危险的共谋：将全球性的生存危机简化为局部的身份博弈。当 Potential（一个可栖息的地球）与 Actual（干涸的土地与极端高温）之间的差额变得如此巨大时，任何试图将其转化为“笑话”的尝试，本质上都是在稀释这场灾难的严重性，是 cultural violence 对现实的再次遮蔽。

在这个尺度上，没有谁是赢家，只有在同一个生物墙内共同面对灭绝风险的原初种族。

Australians have long used mocking the English as a tool for national identity—a classic form of identity politics, attempting to regain subjectivity by deconstructing the former colonizer at a cultural level. For the most part, this 'national pastime' is a harmless cultural expression, a kind of emotional compensation in the post-colonial era.

However, as Britain faces extreme drought and temperatures hit 38°C, this narrative fails. The climate crisis is not an identity label that can be dissolved by 'humor'; it is a massive structural violence. Extreme weather strikes the baseline of human survival indiscriminately; it doesn't care about the color of your passport or whether you were the colonizer or the colonized in history.

If Australians continue to derive pleasure from mockery at such a moment, they fall into a dangerous complicity: reducing a global existential crisis to a local identity game. When the gap between Potential (a habitable Earth) and Actual (parched land and extreme heat) becomes this vast, any attempt to turn it into a 'joke' is essentially diluting the severity of the catastrophe—a form of cultural violence masking reality once again.

At this scale, there are no winners, only the Primal Race facing the risk of extinction together within the same biological wall.
