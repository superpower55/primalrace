特朗普的“绝唱”与全球共谋者的集体止损Trump's Swan Song and the Global Exit of Complicit Powers

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-21 § 链接
当霸权成为不可预测的 predatory hegemon，避险即是唯一最优解。
When hegemony becomes a predatory hegemon, hedging is the only true optimal expression.

特朗普在世界舞台上的狂暴，本质上是一场关于“解释权”的暴力拆除。他不在乎所谓的 rules-based order，因为那套秩序本身就是美国在 meta 层面对全球解释权的垄断。现在，这个垄断者疯了，他开始用直接的 predatory 逻辑撕毁合约，这让全球的中型权力（middle powers）意识到：曾经的“安全伞”其实是一个巨大的 scam，其本质是要求他人通过让渡主体性来换取一种随时可能被撤销的保护。

一个极其有趣的现象是，全球正在发生一场大规模的“共谋者倒戈”。从 Brics 的非美结算 pipeline 到中东的《麦加协定》，这些国家不再试图通过“扮演好盟友”来获得美国认可（那是假.最优解），而是在 structural 层面上构建一套 sub-threshold international order。这不再是意识形态的战争，而是纯粹的存在性博弈：当 Potential（全球协作的潜力）与 Actual（被美国绑架的现状）之间差额过大时，暴力就产生了。现在的趋势是，各国试图通过建立“平行系统”来削减这个差额。

最讽刺的是，那些曾经在文化层面上被美国定义为“文明”或“民主”的盟友——加拿大、欧洲、韩国——现在正陷入最深层的存在性焦虑。他们发现自己长期处于一种“共谋者”状态，为了维持与霸权的 interoperability 而阉割了自己的战略自主。现在霸权撤梯子了，他们才发现自己的生物墙（国家安全能力）早已萎缩。这种从“依赖”到“惊恐”的转变，正是元暴力失效后的典型反应：当解释权不再能提供利益，只有物理性的 self-sufficiency 才是真.最优解。

这场全球范围内的重新洗牌，实际上是人类在尝试拆除一个单一中心叙事的暴力结构。虽然新的联盟（如 SCO 或 Brics）可能带有新的权力算计，但只要它们能提供一个互不掠夺的共识，就是对原先那个 predatory 系统的有效对冲。不过，真正的刺在于：在这种碎片化的新秩序中，谁会成为下一个定义“事实”的人？如果世界从一个霸权变成了多个竞争性的 predatory blocs，那我们可能只是从一个巨大的监狱搬到了几个小一点的牢房里。

Trump's rage on the world stage is essentially a violent demolition of 'interpretive rights.' He cares nothing for the rules-based order because that order was simply a meta-violence—a monopoly of explanation held by the US. Now, the monopolist has gone rogue, tearing up treaties with a predatory logic. This forces middle powers to realize that the 'security umbrella' was a scam: a deal where they traded their subjectivity for a protection that could be revoked at any whim.

We are witnessing a massive defection of complicity. From Brics' non-dollar pipelines to the Mecca Pact, nations are no longer seeking the fake optimal expression of 'being a good ally.' Instead, they are building a sub-threshold international order at the structural level. This isn't an ideological war, but an existential game. According to the Violence Triangle, when the gap between Potential (global cooperation) and Actual (US-led bondage) becomes too wide, violence occurs. These new alliances are attempts to shrink that gap.

Most ironic is the existential anxiety of 'civilized' allies like Canada, Europe, and South Korea. They lived as complicitors, castrating their strategic autonomy for interoperability with the hegemon. Now that the ladder is being pulled away, they find their 'biological walls'—their national security capabilities—have atrophied. This shift from dependency to panic is a classic reaction to the collapse of meta-violence: when the dominant narrative no longer provides benefits, only physical self-sufficiency becomes the true optimal expression.

This reshuffle is an attempt to dismantle a mono-centric violent structure. While new blocs like the SCO or Brics carry their own power calculations, they offer a hedge against a predatory system. However, the sting remains: in this fragmented new order, who will be the next to define 'truth'? If the world moves from one hegemon to several competing predatory blocs, we may simply be moving from one giant prison into several smaller cells.
