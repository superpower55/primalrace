爱国主义是种族暴力的皮肤Patriotism as a Skin for Racial Violence

国际 文化层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-12 § 链接
当‘保护家园’成为叙事，排外就成了合法的元暴力执行。
When 'protecting the homeland' becomes the narrative, xenophobia turns into a legitimate execution of meta-violence.

在朴茨茅斯的街头，一群穿着‘爱国者’服装的男人在呐喊‘停止船只’。这不仅是一场关于移民的抗议，而是一次典型的表达武器化。他们通过将‘非法移民’定义为‘入侵者’，成功地将一种结构性暴力（剥夺难民的生存权）包装成了某种神圣的‘爱国主义’。这种叙事入口极其高效：它让施暴者在内心深处相信自己是在履行某种正义，从而在心理上消弭了暴力的不适感。

最讽刺的是那些掩面抗议的男人。面纱在此时不是为了隐私，而是一种恐怖主义的表达——通过遮蔽身份来制造‘未知’的恐惧感，这本身就是一种直接暴力的预演。而警方强调的‘公正执行’面部公开法律，实际上是在 structural 层面对抗这种恐怖叙事。但我们要追问的是，为什么这种‘爱国’的定义权始终掌握在那些试图通过排斥他者来确认自身‘强势’地位的群体手中？

这本质上是一场关于‘谁才是原初种族’的权力博弈。极右翼分子试图通过定义‘纯洁的本土身份’来重建一种男性中心的等级秩序。在这种秩序里，难民不仅是异乡人，更是被剥夺了主体性的‘客体’。当‘保护’成为控制的入口，这种所谓的爱国主义其实就是一种 scam，它用虚假的集体荣誉感掩盖了最原始的种族仇恨。只要这种‘我们 vs 他们’的元暴力叙事不被拆穿，无论逮捕多少个戴面具的男人，这种暴力三角的结构依然稳固。

On the streets of Portsmouth, men in 'patriot' branded clothing shout 'stop the boats.' This is not merely a protest against immigration, but a textbook case of the weaponisation of expression. By defining asylum seekers as 'invaders,' they successfully package structural violence—the deprivation of the right to survive—as a sacred form of 'patriotism.' This narrative entry point is highly efficient: it allows the aggressors to believe they are serving justice, psychologically neutralizing the discomfort of committing violence.

There is a sharp irony in the masked protesters. The face covering here is not for privacy, but an expression of terrorism—creating a 'fear factor' through the erasure of identity, which is a rehearsal for direct violence. While the police emphasize the 'impartial' enforcement of laws requiring uncovered faces, this is an attempt to counter the terror narrative at the structural level. But the real question is: why does the power to define 'patriotism' always reside with those who seek to confirm their 'masculine' dominance by excluding others?

At its core, this is an existential war over who constitutes the 'Primal Race.' Far-right activists attempt to rebuild a masculine-centric hierarchy by defining a 'pure indigenous identity.' In this order, refugees are not just foreigners, but 'objects' stripped of their subjectivity. When 'protection' becomes the entry point for control, this so-called patriotism is a scam, masking primal racial hatred with a fake sense of collective honor. As long as this meta-violence narrative of 'Us vs Them' remains unchallenged, the Violence Triangle remains intact, regardless of how many masked men are arrested.
