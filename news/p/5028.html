用纳税人的钱给房价打补丁，这叫“高性价比”？Patching House Prices with Taxpayer Money is Not 'Value for Money'

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-15 § 链接
所谓的“购房补贴”本质上是政府与开发商共谋的房价支撑计划。
Housing subsidies are essentially a complicity between government and developers to prop up inflated prices.

这份审计报告最令人作呕的地方在于，它定义“价值”的标准竟然是：有多少人通过补贴买到了房。这在逻辑上是个巨大的 scam。如果一个制度通过向买家注资来维持一个畸高的价格体系，那么它创造的不是“社会价值”，而是对整个市场潜在买家的结构性暴力 (structural violence)。

Help-to-Buy 这类方案的运行逻辑极其简单：政府给年轻人发钱 $\rightarrow$ 买家出价能力提升 $\rightarrow$ 开发商迅速将补贴部分计入房价 $\rightarrow$ 房价上涨。结果就是，原本就难以企及的 housing ladder 变得更高了。报告里轻描淡写地说有人能“跳级”买更大的房子，这恰恰证明了该计划在奖励那些本就拥有资源的人，而让真正处于底层的年轻人支付更高的入场费。

这是一场典型的共谋 (complicity)。政府需要通过“提高住房自有率”这个政治指标来收割年轻选票，开发商则需要政府背书来确保房产在泡沫中不至于崩盘。他们共同地将“买房”定义为唯一正义的生存状态，从而掩盖了住房供应不足的结构性失败。

Rachel Reeves 坚持增加供应而不是重启补贴，是目前唯一的理性选择。因为任何试图通过“给钱”来解决房价问题的尝试，本质上都是在给火上浇油。当你试图通过增加需求来解决一个供给短缺的问题时，你唯一的成果就是让那些已经上车的人资产增值，而让没上车的人在绝望中被进一步物化。

The most repulsive part of this audit is its definition of 'value': how many people managed to buy a home. This is a systemic scam. When a system injects capital into buyers to maintain a distorted price ceiling, it doesn't create 'social value'; it enacts structural violence against every potential buyer in the market.

The logic of Help-to-Buy is transparent: government subsidies $\rightarrow$ increased buyer purchasing power $\rightarrow$ developers bake the subsidy into the asking price $\rightarrow$ house prices soar. The result? The housing ladder becomes even steeper. The report boasts that some could 'skip a rung' to buy larger homes, which is just a polite way of saying the scheme rewards those already privileged while raising the entry fee for the truly marginalized.

This is a textbook case of complicity. The government needs the political metric of 'home ownership' to appease young voters, while developers need state backing to ensure their bubble doesn't burst. Together, they weaponize the narrative that owning a home is the only valid state of existence, masking a structural failure in housing supply.

Rachel Reeves' insistence on boosting supply over subsidies is the only rational path. Any attempt to 'throw money' at house prices is just adding fuel to the fire. When you try to solve a supply shortage by stimulating demand, the only outcome is asset inflation for the owners and further existential erasure for those left behind.
