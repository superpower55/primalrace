中东战场的“男人们”与被抹除的背景板The 'Men' of the Middle East and the Erased Backgrounds

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-25 § 链接
战争是男性中心叙事的最高形式：将人体降格为消耗品以交换宏大意义。
War is the ultimate masculine-centric narrative: degrading human bodies into consumables to exchange for grand meanings.

看这篇新闻，你看到的不是一场战争，而是一场巨大的 masculine-centric narrative 的闭环演出。胡塞武装在 Telegram 上发誓要“报复”，沙特在 X 上宣称“决定性回应”，特朗普在椭圆形办公室里玩弄“大规模打击”的心理游戏。这就是典型的元暴力（meta violence）：一群掌握着暴力机器的男人，在定义什么是“升级”，什么是“恐怖分子”，什么是“严肃的谈判”。

在这个叙事闭环里，所有的表达都被武器化了。他们争夺的是解释权，是关于“谁在威胁商业航运”或“谁在袭击民用设施”的定义权。但请注意，在这种宏大叙事的缝隙中，真正的受害者——那些被炸毁的民用基础设施里的女性、孩子，以及被波及的平民——在新闻里甚至不配拥有一个具体的形容词。他们只是“civilian infrastructure”这个结构性词汇里的背景板。这就是文化暴力（cultural violence）的最高效率：让直接暴力看起来像是一场关于“战略”和“尊严”的博弈，而非一场血腥的屠杀。

最讽刺的是特朗普关于“信任”普京和习的表述。这种基于强人之间“不要让我失望”的私人信任，本质上是某种兄弟会（Fraternity）式的结盟。他们把全球地缘政治简化成了几个 Alpha Male 之间的博弈，而这个博弈的筹码，是无数不参与决策的肉体。在这种元暴力的逻辑下，人体被彻底客体化为可消耗的资源，用以换取所谓的“国家利益”或“地区稳定”。

这就是一场典型的存在性战争，只不过赢家定义了规则，而输家在统计数据中被抹除。这场战争没有所谓的“公正表达”，只有不同层级的共谋：从执行打击的士兵到书写叙事的新闻业，大家都在维持这个男本位世界的运转。

Reading this news, you don't see a war; you see a massive closed-loop performance of a masculine-centric narrative. Houthi rebels vow 'retaliation' on Telegram, Saudi Arabia claims a 'decisive response' on X, and Trump plays psychological games with 'massive attacks' in the Oval Office. This is classic meta-violence: a group of men controlling the machinery of violence defining what constitutes an 'escalation,' who are 'terrorists,' and what makes a negotiation 'serious.'

In this narrative loop, all expression is weaponized. They are fighting for the power of interpretation—the right to define who is 'threatening commercial shipping' or 'targeting civilian facilities.' But notice how the actual victims—women and children in those bombed civilian infrastructures—don't even merit a specific adjective. They are merely background noise within the structural term 'civilian infrastructure.' This is the peak efficiency of cultural violence: making direct violence appear as a game of 'strategy' and 'dignity' rather than a bloody slaughter.

Most ironic is Trump's rhetoric about 'trusting' Putin and Xi. This private trust based on 'don't let me down' is essentially a Fraternity-style alliance. They have simplified global geopolitics into a game between a few Alpha Males, where the chips are the bodies of countless people who have no say in the decision. Under this logic of meta-violence, the human body is completely objectified as a consumable resource to be exchanged for so-called 'national interest' or 'regional stability.'

This is a textbook existential war, where the winners define the rules and the losers are erased from the statistics. There is no 'Just Expression' here, only complicity at different levels: from the soldiers executing strikes to the journalists writing the narratives, all maintaining the machinery of this masculine-centric world.
