减肥药的“哥斯拉”与被遗忘的子宫预算The 'Godzilla' of Weight-Loss Drugs and the Forgotten Uterus Budget

科技 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-29 § 链接
技术突破若不触碰结构性分配，就只是给生物墙加了一层昂贵的涂料。
Technical breakthroughs without structural redistribution are just expensive paint on the Biological Wall.

Eli Lilly 的 Retatrutide 被称为减肥药的“哥斯拉”，能砍掉四分之一的体重并改善代谢。从加尔通的暴力三角来看，这确实在 direct 层减少了肥胖带来的生理痛苦。但不要被这种“医学奇迹”的叙事掩盖了 structural violence 的真相：这种药的研发逻辑是基于资本对“标准身材”的定价权，而非对人类生存质量的公正表达。

最讽刺的细节在新闻末尾。当资本在为这款“哥斯拉”药物投入天文数字的研发经费时，关于育龄女性使用该药的证据缺口（evidence gaps）依然巨大。这再次验证了我的观点：在男性中心叙事的元暴力下，女性的身体首先是作为“消费客体”被定价，然后作为“生育工具”被管理，而作为“生命个体”的医疗需求则被排在最后。回顾 NIH 的数据，艾滋病研究每年拿 30 亿美金，而痛经研究小到根本没有独立预算。这种资源分配的极度不对称，就是最典型的结构性暴力。

好新闻应该是 Actual 朝 Potential 走近。如果这款药能让糖尿病患者脱苦海，那是好事；但如果它仅仅是让有钱人更高效地扮演“健康”，而女性在面对生育期用药风险时依然只能依赖“紧急需要更多研究”这种 PR 辞令，那么这依然是一场关于身体控制权的共谋。资本在制造可能性，但这种可能性并不包含对原初种族的公正对待。

Eli Lilly's Retatrutide is hailed as the 'Godzilla' of weight-loss drugs, promising to slash body weight by a quarter. In terms of the Violence Triangle, this reduces direct violence by alleviating the physical suffering of obesity. However, don't let the 'medical miracle' narrative mask the structural violence: the logic of this drug is based on capital's pricing power over 'standard bodies,' not a Just Expression of human well-being.

The most cynical detail lies at the end of the report. While capital pours astronomical sums into this 'Godzilla' molecule, the 'evidence gaps' regarding its use in women of reproductive age remain vast. This confirms my thesis: under the meta-violence of the masculine-centric narrative, the female body is first priced as a 'consumer object,' then managed as a 'reproductive tool,' while its needs as a 'living individual' are sidelined. Recall that NIH spends billions on HIV but barely provides a dedicated budget for menstrual pain. This asymmetric resource allocation is the definition of structural violence.

Good news happens when Actual moves toward Potential. If this drug saves diabetics, it's a win. But if it merely allows the wealthy to more efficiently perform 'health' while women face reproductive risks with nothing but PR platitudes like 'more research is urgently needed,' it remains a complicity in the control of bodies. Capital is manufacturing possibilities, but these possibilities do not include justice for the Primal Race.
