AI 泡沫的崩塌：一场关于“定义权”的金融 ScamThe AI Crash: A Financial Scam of 'Definition Power'

中国科技 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-28 § 链接
当能力无法突破生物墙，资本便通过重新定义现实来制造虚假的 Potential。
When actual capability hits the biological wall, capital creates a fake Potential by redefining reality.

这场 AI 芯片股的暴跌，本质上是一场关于“认知入口”的崩塌。过去几年，AI 产业通过一种典型的武器化叙事，将“算力”等同于“生产力”，制造了一个巨大的 Potential 幻象。但当投资者发现这个 Potential 并非来自技术的指数级突破，而是一场由 Nvidia 等巨头主导的 circular funding（循环融资）骗局时，泡沫自然地破裂了。

所谓的“AI 革命”，在很大程度上是资本在无法突破物理极限（如芯片能效比、电力供应）时的无奈之举——既然无法在实际能力上实现跨越，那就通过大规模借贷扩张数据中心，用规模效应掩盖效率停滞，重新定义什么是“先进”。这和某些科技公司宣布新定律来替代摩尔定律的逻辑如出一辙：现实不配合，就换掉尺子。

而中国 DUV 设备的量产，则成了捅破这个泡沫的最后一根针。它揭示了一个残酷的事实：当所谓的“技术壁垒”可以通过另一个权力中心通过结构性投入而快速抹平时，原先由垄断者定义的“稀缺性”就变成了一个巨大的 scam。资本在恐惧，因为他们发现自己之前买的不是未来的入场券，而是一堆被高估的硅片。

这次 sell-off 是一次迟到的校准。它提醒我们，任何脱离了真实物理能力而仅靠叙事支撑的 Potential，最终都会在 Actual 的匮乏中被暴力地抹除。

The AI chip sell-off is essentially the collapse of a weaponized narrative. For years, the industry conflated 'compute' with 'productivity,' manufacturing a massive illusion of Potential. But as investors realize this Potential wasn't driven by exponential technical breakthroughs, but by a circular funding scam led by giants like Nvidia, the bubble burst.

This so-called 'AI Revolution' was largely a desperate move by capital when faced with physical limits—such as power efficiency and electricity supply. Unable to achieve a real leap in capability, they expanded data centers via massive borrowing, using scale to mask stagnation and redefining 'progress' on their own terms. It is the same mechanism as announcing a new 'law' when the old one fails: if reality doesn't cooperate, change the ruler.

The news of China's homegrown DUV production served as the final pin. It revealed that when 'technical barriers' can be rapidly bypassed by another power center through structural investment, the 'scarcity' defined by the monopolists becomes a blatant scam. Investors are jittery because they realize they weren't buying tickets to the future, but overpriced silicon.

This sell-off is a long-overdue calibration. It reminds us that any Potential supported only by narrative, detached from actual physical capability, will eventually be violently erased by the void of Actual.
