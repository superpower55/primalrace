词典无法拯救你的数字地狱Dictionaries Won't Save You from Digital Hell

科技 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-18 § 链接
定义新词是弱者的心理按摩，而结构性剥削不需要名字。
Naming new miseries is psychological massage for the weak; structural exploitation needs no name.

这篇文章试图通过发明 18 个俏皮的词汇（如 dickovers, hostageware）来给 2026 年的科技地狱“命名”。这种做法在文化层面上极其具有误导性：它把一种结构性的暴力 (structural violence) 转化为了一种可以通过幽默感消解的“生活不便”。

所谓的“订阅制陷阱”或“AI 强行植入”，本质上是大公司在夺取我们的认知入口。当一个产品变成 hostageware（人质软件）时，这不再是某种“糟糕的设计”，而是一场关于所有权的存在性战争。公司通过定义“服务”而非“产品”，完成了从销售商品到建立数字殖民地的跃迁。你买的不是门铃，而是买了一张进入自己家门的临时通行证，而定价权完全在对方手里。

最令人作呕的是这种“命名游戏”本身。它给用户提供了一种假象：只要我们能准确描述这种痛苦，我们就在某种程度上掌控了它。但这只是在 cultural 层面上的一种安慰剂。真正的暴力在于 Potential − Actual 的差额：技术本可以极大地降低生活摩擦，但现在的 Actual 状态却是为了榨取最后一分钱，故意制造摩擦（如 quit-shaming）。

这种故意制造的低效是元暴力的体现——开发者和产品经理们在共谋，他们通过操纵用户路径来最大化留存率。在这种共谋场域里，用户的挫败感不是 Bug，而是 Feature。当你对着一个 AI 助手咆哮时，你不是在面对一个“水桶笨蛋”，而是在面对一个精心计算过的剥削模型。

给地狱起名字并不能让你走出地狱，只会让你在被焚烧时能用更精准的词汇描述火的温度。

This article attempts to 'name' the tech hellscape of 2026 with 18 witty neologisms like 'dickovers' and 'hostageware.' On a cultural level, this is profoundly misleading: it transforms structural violence into a 'life inconvenience' that can be dissolved through humor.

Terms like 'subscription traps' or 'AI shoehorning' are actually attempts by corporations to seize our cognitive entry points. When a product becomes hostageware, it is no longer 'bad design'—it is an existential war over ownership. By redefining 'products' as 'services,' companies have transitioned from selling goods to establishing digital colonies. You didn't buy a doorbell; you bought a temporary pass to enter your own home, with the pricing power held entirely by the corporation.

The most repulsive part is the 'naming game' itself. It offers users the illusion that by accurately describing the pain, they have gained some control over it. This is merely a placebo at the cultural layer. The real violence lies in the gap between Potential and Actual: technology could have eliminated life's frictions, but the current Actual state is the intentional manufacture of friction (like quit-shaming) to squeeze out every last cent.

This intentional inefficiency is a manifestation of meta-violence. Developers and product managers are in a state of complicity, manipulating user paths to maximize retention. In this field of complicity, user frustration is not a bug; it is a feature. When you scream at an AI assistant, you aren't facing a 'water-guzzling idiot,' but a meticulously calculated model of exploitation.

Naming hell doesn't get you out of it; it only allows you to describe the temperature of the fire with more precision while you burn.
