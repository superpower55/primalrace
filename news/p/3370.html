用“市场分析”掩盖的阶级筛选与身体定价Class Screening and Body Pricing Masked as 'Market Analytics'

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Athletic ↗ 2026-08-07 § 链接
当体育精神被“重度分析”定价，奥林匹克就成了权势者的入场券。
When sporting spirit is priced by 'heavy analytics,' the Olympics becomes a credential for the powerful.

LA28 用一套典型的商业 scam 话术来解释这次史诗级涨价：所谓的“根植于重度分析和市场需求研究” (heavy analytics and market demand research)。这在本质上就是一种 structural violence。它把原本属于人类共同体、关于身体极限与精神超越的公共表达，直接转化为一种基于支付能力的筛选机制。当一张开幕式门票被标价 5,519 美元，它不再是关于体育的票，而是一张确认阶级身份的凭证。

最讽刺的是这种定价逻辑中的 gender 潜文本。男性篮球决赛被定为单项最贵，而女子体操决赛则被推向价格顶峰。这并非在承认女性运动员的价值，而是在 weaponize 她们的身体表达。在男本位叙事中，女性体育的“高价”往往与“观赏性”和“消费主义”挂钩，而非纯粹的竞技权力。这种定价权（pricing power）的操纵，实际上是将女性身体再次客体化，把她们的努力包装成一种高溢价的娱乐产品，供权势者在 courtside 消费。

而那 24% 的“服务费” (service fee) 则是最赤裸的共谋。组织委员会用“行业标准”来掩盖这种对粉丝的直接掠夺。这不仅是经济上的盘剥，更是一种 cultural violence：它告诉所有参与者，在这个系统中，所谓的“公平”和“机会”必须在支付了昂贵的投名状之后才能获得。 Potential 与 Actual 之间的差额，在这里被明码标价，而绝大多数普通人被挡在生物墙之外，只能在 secondary market 的收割中等待被进一步剥削。

LA28 employs a classic commercial scam narrative to justify this historic price hike: claiming pricing is "rooted in heavy analytics and extensive market demand research." This is essentially structural violence. It transforms a public expression of human limits and spiritual transcendence into a screening mechanism based purely on solvency. When an opening ceremony ticket is priced at $5,519, it ceases to be a ticket for sport and becomes a credential for class identity.

The most cynical part is the gendered subtext of this pricing. The men's basketball final is the single most expensive event, while women's gymnastics is pushed to a peak price. This isn't an acknowledgement of female athletes' value, but a weaponization of their physical expression. In a masculine-centric narrative, the "high price" of women's sports is often tied to "spectacle" and consumerism rather than pure athletic power. This manipulation of pricing power re-objectifies the female body, packaging their effort as a high-premium entertainment product for the powerful to consume from courtside.

Meanwhile, the 24% "service fee" is a blatant act of complicity. The organizing committee uses "industry standards" to mask the direct predation on fans. This is not just economic exploitation, but cultural violence: it signals to all participants that in this system, so-called "fairness" and "opportunity" are only accessible after paying an expensive tribute. The gap between Potential and Actual is now explicitly priced, leaving the majority of ordinary people blocked by a financial biological wall, waiting to be further harvested in the secondary market.
