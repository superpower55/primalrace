邮局洗钱：用“服务弱势群体”给黑产打掩护The Post Office Laundry: Masking Crime as 'Financial Inclusion'

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-25 § 链接
当系统性漏洞被包装成“普惠服务”，它就成了最完美的犯罪温床。
When systemic loopholes are rebranded as 'inclusive services,' they become the perfect sanctuary for crime.

这起英国邮局洗钱案揭示了一个典型的 structural violence 闭环：国家通过关闭银行网点制造了金融资源匮乏的现状，随后由国有的 Post Office Ltd 提供一个“简化”的替代方案，美其名曰是为那些被银行抛弃的弱势群体提供服务。但这套所谓的“普惠”逻辑，本质上是剥离了所有风控机制的 naked system。它用“便捷”掩盖了对反洗钱检查的刻意缺失，让邮局变成了黑产洗钱的 reverse funnel（反向漏斗）。

最讽刺的共谋在于，Post Office Ltd 将这种漏洞武器化为一种商业模型。银行支付的服务费成了其收入的支柱，而基层邮政经营者则通过每笔交易的抽成获得利益。这意味着，系统不仅在默许洗钱，而且在经济激励上鼓励经营者对可疑资金“睁一只眼闭一只眼”。这种共谋被包裹在“支持小微企业”的文化叙事中，使得任何试图加强监管的尝试（如 FCA 的限额令）都被贴上“伤害弱势群体”的标签而被政治化地扼杀。

而此前 Horizon 丑闻制造的 meta violence 进一步加固了这堵墙。因为系统曾经大规模冤枉过基层员工，现在的司法系统在面对邮局数据时产生了某种“创伤性回避”——警察担心证据被律师以“系统不可信”为由驳回，从而导致大量洗钱案件被搁置。这种由于之前的结构性暴力而产生的“政治正确”禁忌，反而成了当下犯罪分子的最佳掩体。在这个闭环里，受害者是公众，受益者是黑产和依赖这些交易生存的官僚系统。

The UK Post Office money laundering scandal reveals a classic loop of structural violence: the state creates financial deserts by closing bank branches, then introduces a 'simplified' alternative via the state-owned Post Office Ltd, framing it as a lifeline for the marginalized. This 'inclusion' narrative is a scam; it is a naked system stripped of all risk controls. By prioritizing 'convenience' over compliance, the system has effectively created a reverse funnel for organized crime.

The most cynical complicity lies in how Post Office Ltd weaponized this vulnerability into a business model. Service fees from banks have become a revenue pillar, while local postmasters profit from transaction fees. The system doesn't just tolerate money laundering; it provides economic incentives for operators to ignore suspicious activity. This complicity is shielded by a cultural narrative of 'supporting small businesses,' allowing any attempt at regulation—such as the FCA's deposit limits—to be politically neutralized as 'harming the vulnerable.'

Furthermore, the meta violence stemming from the Horizon scandal has reinforced this wall. Because the system previously victimized hundreds of innocent operators, the judiciary now suffers from a 'traumatic avoidance' of Post Office data. Police fear that evidence will be discredited in court due to the system's history of failure, leading to a paralysis in investigations. This political correctness, born from previous structural violence, has ironically become the ultimate cover for current criminals. In this loop, the public pays the price while organized crime and the bureaucrats dependent on these flows reap the rewards.
