重制旧游戏，谁在被定价A Remake Is Not a Resurrection

文娱 文化层 Polygon.com ↗ 2026-11-01 § 链接
重制不是复活，是把旧表达重新定价卖一遍。
A remake doesn't revive expression; it reprices it.

一条两分钟的内部演示视频在万圣节漏出来，据 Polygon 报道，是《女神异闻录》初代的重制版。报道说这视频像是做给内部汇报用的，粉丝还发现它复用了《Persona 4 Revival》的部分动画。这不算丑闻，只是把这家公司做生意的逻辑露了一角：旧的东西不用重做，换个皮，接着卖。

第三章讲表达武器化，审美和品味是入口之一。重制就是这条入口最干净的形态——不需要新故事，只需要把二十多年前的表达重新包装成"现代 Persona 的美术风格"，然后重新定价。谁定这个价？有话语权的人。玩家以为自己在为怀旧买单，其实是在为一次审美标准的更新付费：旧版被判定为"过时"，新版被判定为"正统"，中间那点差额，就是定价权在动。

报道里有一句很实在：Atlus 对《Persona 3 Reload》的反响满意，所以想把更老的游戏也按现代 Persona 的样子重做一遍。这句话翻译过来就是——尺子换了，能力没换。做不出新东西的时候，就把旧东西按新尺子量一遍，宣布它"焕新"。τ law 那套机制在这里通用：现实不配合，就重新定义现实。

更有意思的是泄露本身。报道列了一串：Persona 3 Reload 公布前泄露过实机，Persona 4 Revival 是被没被邀请参与的配音演员提前捅出来的，Persona 6 的成就今年早些时候也漏了。报道说这些对 Atlus 的士气"肯定不好"。可泄露的"好消息"是让玩家提前兴奋——这句话是报道自己说的，也是这个行业的标准话术：把失控说成福利，把内部管理的漏洞说成给粉丝的惊喜。谁在为这份"惊喜"付代价？报道没写。配音演员没被邀请，这件事本身就是答案：重制里最先被省掉的，是人的声音。

所以这条新闻里真正在动的，不是 direct 层的暴力，是 cultural 层：一个被垄断的审美标准又完成了一次自我确认。玩家拿到的是"终于能玩到初代重制"，Atlus 拿到的是旧资产的新一轮折旧摊销，被换掉的那批人拿到的是沉默。

报道最后一句是作者自己的兴奋：他从没玩过初代，终于有机会体验新版本。这份兴奋是真的，代价也是真的——他体验到的"初代"，已经不是初代了。

Two minutes of internal presentation footage leaked on Halloween. According to Polygon, it appears to be a remake of the first Persona game. The footage looks like it was made for an internal pitch, and fans noticed it reuses animations from Persona 4 Revival. This isn't a scandal. It's the business logic showing its seams: don't rebuild the old thing, just reskin it and sell it again.

Chapter 3 puts aesthetics and taste among the weaponized entry points of expression. A remake is the cleanest form of that entry. No new story required. Just take an expression from twenty-plus years ago, repackage it as "modern Persona aesthetics," and reprice it. Who sets the price? Whoever holds the discourse. Players think they're paying for nostalgia. They're actually paying for an update to the standard of taste: the old version gets ruled obsolete, the new one gets ruled canonical, and the gap between them is pricing power in motion.

The report says something honest: Atlus was happy with the response to Persona 3 Reload and wants to remake the older games in an aesthetic closer to modern Persona titles. Translated: change the ruler, not the capability. When you can't make something new, measure the old thing against a new ruler and declare it renewed. The τ law mechanism generalizes. When reality won't cooperate, redefine reality.

The leak itself is the more interesting part. The report lists them: Persona 3 Reload gameplay leaked before its announcement, Persona 4 Revival was outed by voice actors disappointed they weren't involved, and Persona 6 achievements leaked earlier this year. The report says this can't be great for morale at Atlus. Then it offers the industry's standard consolation: the bright side of leaks is that they get us excited. That's how a management failure gets reframed as a fan perk. Who pays for the perk? The report doesn't say. The voice actors who weren't invited already answered: the first thing a remake cuts is the human voice.

So the layer moving here isn't direct violence. It's cultural: a monopolized standard of taste confirming itself one more time. Players get "finally, the first game remade." Atlus gets a fresh depreciation cycle on old assets. The people replaced get silence.

The last line of the report is the author's own excitement: they've never played the original and are glad to finally experience a new version. The excitement is real. So is the cost. What they'll experience isn't the original. It's the original, repriced.
