被画在成本曲线里的“救世主”与被抹除的身体The 'Savior' on the Cost Curve and the Erased Bodies

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-02 § 链接
政治表演的成本由底层支付，而解释权永远在画笔手中。
The cost of political performance is paid by the bottom, while the interpretation remains with the brush.

Nicola Jennings 的这幅漫画精准地揭示了一场关于“成本”的共谋。Andy Burnham 这种政治人物最擅长的就是 weaponized 叙事：将生活成本危机 (cost of living crisis) 包装成一个可以被管理、被讨论的政策议题，而在这个过程中，真正承受生物墙压力的底层人群——尤其是那些在私人领域承担绝大部分养育和照料责任的女性——被简化成了背景板上的一个数据点。

这就是典型的元暴力 (meta violence)。在男性中心叙事的政治博弈中，所谓的“经济方案”永远是关于税收、支出和宏大叙事的权力游戏。在这种叙事里，女性的无偿劳动被结构性地榨取，却在公共空间的讨论中被彻底 invisibility。无论 Burnham 扮演的是救世主还是管理者的角色，他所操作的尺子永远不包含那些在厨房和育儿室里被消磨的生命个体。

这种漫画的讽刺之处在于，它揭露了政治精英的 Optimal Expression：通过在公共空间表演对危机的“关注”，来掩盖结构性暴力 (structural violence) 的持续运行。他们并不打算改变资源分配的底层逻辑，而只是在争夺谁能定义“危机”的解释权。对于底层女性来说，无论谁在画图，她们依然是那个被殖民的原初种族，在每一个所谓的“经济增长”或“成本核算”中，被作为最廉价的成本项给抹掉了。

Nicola Jennings' cartoon precisely exposes a complicity regarding 'cost.' Politicians like Andy Burnham excel at the weaponisation of narrative: packaging the cost of living crisis as a manageable policy issue. In this process, the bottom-tier population—especially women who bear the brunt of the biological wall through care work in the private sphere—are reduced to mere data points in the background.

This is a classic manifestation of meta violence. In the masculine-centric narrative of political gambling, so-called 'economic solutions' are always power games of taxes, spending, and grand narratives. Within this framework, women's unpaid labor is structurally extracted, yet they remain completely invisible in public discourse. Whether Burnham performs as a savior or a manager, his ruler never measures the individual lives being eroded in kitchens and nurseries.

The irony of this cartoon lies in its revelation of the Optimal Expression of political elites: performing 'concern' for the crisis in public spaces to mask the continued operation of structural violence. They have no intention of altering the underlying logic of resource distribution; they are merely fighting for the interpretation of what constitutes a 'crisis.' For women at the bottom, regardless of who holds the brush, they remain the Primal Race, erased as the cheapest cost item in every so-called 'economic growth' or 'cost calculation.'
