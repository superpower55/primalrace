气候紧急状态：一场由男性主导的文明自毁共谋Climate Emergency: A Masculine Conspiracy of Civilizational Self-Destruction

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-27 § 链接
所谓的“气候危机”是元暴力在生态层面的最终兑现。
The so-called 'climate crisis' is the ecological manifestation of meta-violence.

当马克龙和桑切斯在危机会议上讨论如何“对抗”火灾时，他们扮演的是典型的救世主角色。这种叙事极其诡异：一群在权力顶端地通过工业文明、资本扩张和资源掠夺获利的男性，现在对着被他们亲手点燃的森林，宣布进入“气候紧急状态”。

按照加尔通的暴力三角，这不仅是 direct 层的火灾，更是 structural 和 cultural 层的暴力。工业文明的底层逻辑是 masculine-centric 的——征服自然、榨取资源、将地球客体化为可消耗的工具。这种元暴力在潜意识中将“生长”定义为“待开发的资源”，将“自然”定义为“需要被管理的对象”。现在的山火，不过是这个系统在运行到极限后的必然回弹。

最讽刺的是，在面对这种毁灭性暴力时，权力的反应依然是“动用国家全部力量”这种典型的男性化表达。他们试图用更强的暴力（消防、军队、行政指令）去压制自然的暴力，而绝口不提如何拆除那个制造危机的结构。桑切斯提到的“能源转型”不过是另一种武器化的叙事，试图在不触动权力结构的前提下，通过更换一种“绿色”的剥削方式来维持现状。

这场火灾中，最先被提醒需要“采取预防措施”的是老年人、孕妇和呼吸道疾病患者。这再次揭示了生物墙在结构暴力中的作用：最脆弱的身体永远是最后被考虑的，而决定谁该戴上 FFP2 口罩的，依然是那些坐在空调房里讨论“赛跑”的权力者。

As Macron and Sánchez chair their crisis meetings to 'battle' the wildfires, they are performing the classic savior role. It is a grotesque narrative: a group of men at the peak of power, who profited from the very industrial civilization and capital expansion that ignited these fires, now declaring a 'climate emergency' while staring at the ashes.

Applying Galtung's Violence Triangle, this is not just direct violence in the form of fire, but structural and cultural violence. The underlying logic of industrial civilization is masculine-centric—conquering nature, extracting resources, and objectifying the Earth as a consumable tool. This meta-violence defines 'growth' as 'resource to be exploited' and 'nature' as 'something to be managed.' These wildfires are simply the inevitable rebound of a system pushed to its limit.

The most ironic part is that the response to this devastation remains the 'full power of the state'—a typical masculine expression. They attempt to suppress the violence of nature with a stronger, institutionalized violence (firefighters, military, administrative decrees), while avoiding any discussion on dismantling the structure that created the crisis. Sánchez's 'energy transition' is another weaponized narrative, attempting to maintain the status quo by switching to a 'green' version of exploitation without disturbing the power structure.

In these fires, the first to be warned to 'take precautions' are the elderly, pregnant women, and those with chronic illnesses. This again reveals how the biological wall functions within structural violence: the most vulnerable bodies are always the last priority, while the decision of who gets an FFP2 mask is still made by those in air-conditioned rooms discussing a 'race against time' and managing the catastrophe.
