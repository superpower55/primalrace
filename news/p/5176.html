将战争机器直接转化为立法机器，是元暴力最赤裸的闭环。
Converting the war machine directly into a legislative machine is the rawest loop of meta-violence.

普京这次在杜马（Duma）中安插 49 名战争退伍军人，绝不是什么“社会贡献”，而是一次极其精准的表达武器化操作。在男性中心叙事（masculine-centric narrative）的元暴力逻辑下，战争被包装成某种神圣的、具有最高优先级的“男性勋章”。当这些执行直接暴力（direct violence）的人直接进入立法机构，意味着俄罗斯的结构层（structural layer）正在被暴力逻辑全面接管。

这不仅是权力的巩固，更是一场深层的共谋（complicity）。统治党 United Russia 制造了一个虚假的“民主外壳”，通过禁掉 Yabloko 等反对党，强行清空所有非暴力叙事的认知入口。剩下的共谋者——无论是共产党还是极右翼——都在这套“特别军事行动”的叙事中分到了利益。他们共同完成了一次对现实的重新定义：将对乌克兰的侵略定义为“社会的最高凝聚力”。

最令人作呕的是这种叙事的闭环：用战争制造死亡 $\rightarrow$ 用死亡塑造“英雄” $\rightarrow$ 用“英雄”立法 $\rightarrow$ 用法律为更大规模的动员（mobilisation）背书。这不再是政治博弈，而是一场纯粹的生存性战争，只不过被牺牲的永远是那些无法进入立法议事厅的底层士兵和乌克兰平民。当“文明”和“法律”变成了暴力机器的润滑剂，这种所谓的“凝聚力”其实就是一种集体性的精神病态。

Putin's placement of 49 war veterans in the Duma is far from a 'social contribution'; it is a precise operation of weaponized expression. Under the meta-violence of the masculine-centric narrative, war is packaged as a sacred 'masculine medal' of the highest priority. When those who execute direct violence enter the legislature, it means the structural layer of Russia is being fully hijacked by the logic of violence.

This is more than power consolidation; it is a deep complicity. The ruling party, United Russia, has manufactured a fake 'democratic veneer,' forcibly clearing all non-violent cognitive entries by banning opposition parties like Yabloko. The remaining co-conspirators—whether Communists or ultra-nationalists—have all secured their slice of the pie within the 'special military operation' narrative. Together, they have redefined reality: framing the invasion of Ukraine as the 'highest level of social consolidation.'

The most repulsive part is the closure of this loop: using war to create death $\rightarrow$ using death to shape 'heroes' $\rightarrow$ using 'heroes' to legislate $\rightarrow$ using laws to endorse larger-scale mobilisation. This is no longer political gaming, but a pure existential war, where the sacrifices are always the bottom-tier soldiers and Ukrainian civilians who will never enter the legislative halls. When 'civilization' and 'law' become lubricants for the violence machine, this so-called 'consolidation' is nothing but a collective psychosis.
