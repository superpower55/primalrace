百年伙伴关系的浪漫叙事与战争消耗品The Romantic Narrative of a Century Partnership and the Consumables of War

国际 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-10 § 链接
用血缘纽带包装的军援，本质是将他国身体转化为地缘博弈的消耗品。
Military aid wrapped in kinship is essentially turning foreign bodies into consumables for geopolitical gaming.

加拿大这次给乌克兰的所谓“百年伙伴关系” (hundred-year partnership)，是典型的武器化表达。它精准地利用了阿尔伯塔省的大量乌克兰裔社区作为认知入口，将冰冷的军工贸易和地缘政治包装成一种基于血缘和历史的“浪漫爱”叙事。当 Carney 说“乌克兰人帮助建设了加拿大，加拿大人将帮助重建乌克兰”时，他实际上是在用一种温情脉脉的 cultural violence 掩盖一个残酷的 structural violence：乌克兰的身体正在被转化为西方防空系统和无人机库存的实战测试场。

这种叙事最阴险的地方在于，它把战争的延续定义为“守护家庭和孩子”。Zelenskyy 在镜头前强调无人机是为了“拯救孩子”，这让战争在文化层面上被正当化为一种人道主义救援。但从 Violence = Potential − Actual 的公式来看，这种援助并没有缩小暴力差额，而是在维持一个极高水平的 Actual Violence。通过提供贷款担保和无人机，加拿大实际上是在为这场消耗战提供续航，确保战场上的博弈能够继续进行，直到某个地缘政治的最优解出现。

最值得玩味的是，在这种宏大的“伙伴关系”背后，所有的决策者依然是典型的 masculine-centric narrative。从防空系统到关键矿产，从能源供应到无人机生产，这场博弈的筹码是土地、资源和士兵的生命。而那些被提及的“家庭”和“孩子”，仅仅是作为情感入口被武器化，用来在西方公众心中制造一种“正义感”，从而让纳税人心甘情愿地为这场远方的存在性战争买单。

所谓的“百年协议”不过是一张长期信用票据。在 Trump 这种潜在的博弈变量出现之前，加拿大在用一种表演性的慷慨，试图在未来的地缘定价权中占据一个席位。至于乌克兰能否真的被“重建”，取决于它在西方博弈模型中是否依然具有被利用的价值。

Canada's so-called "hundred-year partnership" with Ukraine is a textbook case of weaponised expression. It precisely uses the large Ukrainian-descendant communities in Alberta as a cognitive entry point, packaging cold military trade and geopolitics into a "romantic" narrative based on kinship and history. When Carney claims that "Ukrainians helped build Canada and Canadians will help rebuild Ukraine," he is using a sentimental layer of cultural violence to mask a brutal structural violence: Ukrainian bodies are being converted into live-fire testing grounds for Western air defense systems and drone inventories.

The most insidious part of this narrative is defining the prolongation of war as "protecting families and children." Zelenskyy's emphasis on drones "saving children" justifies the war as a humanitarian rescue at the cultural level. However, applying the formula Violence = Potential − Actual, this aid does not shrink the violence gap; it sustains a high level of Actual Violence. By providing loan guarantees and drones, Canada is providing the endurance for this war of attrition, ensuring the existential war continues until some geopolitical optimal expression is reached.

Crucially, behind this grand "partnership," the decision-makers remain locked in a masculine-centric narrative. From air defense to critical minerals, from energy supply to drone production, the stakes of this game are land, resources, and the lives of soldiers. The mentioned "families" and "children" are merely weaponised as emotional entry points to manufacture a sense of "justice" among Western taxpayers, making them willing to pay for this distant existential war.

This "century agreement" is nothing more than a long-term credit note. Before a variable like Trump enters the game, Canada is using a performative generosity to secure a seat in the future pricing power of geopolitics. Whether Ukraine can truly be "rebuilt" depends entirely on whether it remains a useful asset in the Western gaming model.
