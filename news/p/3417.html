认知外包：一场关于主体性的集体投降Cognitive Outsourcing: A Collective Surrender of Subjectivity

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-08 § 链接
当思考被‘效率’替代，人类正在经历一场认知层面的自愿殖民。
When thinking is replaced by 'efficiency', humanity undergoes a voluntary colonization of the mind.

Katherine Rundell 的焦虑精准地捕捉到了一个事实：AI 在教育领域的渗透，本质上是一场关于‘表达’的权力交接。在我的框架里，表达不是沟通工具，而是存在的确证。当你写一篇关于《麦克白》的论文时，那个痛苦的、反复推敲的写作过程，就是你在进行存在性战争，你在通过与文本的搏斗确立自己的主体性。而 LLM 提供的‘流畅声音’（greasy little fluent voice）是一个巨大的 scam，它用一个伪造的最优解，诱导年轻人放弃掉构建自我的过程。

这是一种典型的 structural violence。你看，EdTech 厂商承诺的‘民主化教育’和‘一对一学习’，实际上是在为底层学生量身定制一套‘认知假肢’。富有的孩子在瑞典和挪威回归纸笔，重新夺回对思维的掌控权；而贫穷的孩子则被喂食 AI 产出的 slop，在‘高效’的假象中完成认知投降。这种差异不是能力的差异，而是‘定义权’的差异：谁在思考，谁在被喂养？

最可怕的是这种 meta violence 的内化。当学生习惯于‘认知外包’，他们不仅失去了思考的能力，更失去了‘意识到自己失去能力’的自觉。这就像是把大脑的运动皮层交给算法，最终导致的是 cognitive surrender。当一个孩子认为 AI 生成的文字就是自己的‘掌控’（mastery）时，他的主体性已经死亡了，他成了 AI 叙事的一个共谋者，在一种伪造的成就感中被悄悄地阉割。

阅读不再仅仅是获取知识，而是一种抵抗。在一个由少数几个持有‘受损价值观’的男性掌控的 AI 帝国面前，阅读人类的作品是为了确认‘我们是人’。这不是什么温情的文学情怀，而是一场关于生存空间的博弈。如果我们将思考的权力让渡给机器，那么我们面对未来的武器将只有‘伪造的知识’，而这正是统治者最希望看到的——一群高效、顺从且大脑空洞的数字化原住民。

Katherine Rundell's anxiety precisely captures a grim reality: the infiltration of AI into education is essentially a handover of the power of Expression. In my framework, Expression is not a tool for communication, but the confirmation of existence. When you struggle through an essay on Macbeth, that painful process of drafting and revising is your existential war; you are establishing your subjectivity through a collision with the text. The 'greasy little fluent voice' of LLMs is a massive scam, tempting the youth to abandon the process of self-construction for a forged optimal expression.

This is a textbook case of structural violence. The 'democratization of education' promised by EdTech is, in fact, a customized 'cognitive prosthesis' for the underprivileged. While wealthy children in Sweden and Norway return to paper and pen to reclaim mastery over their minds, poorer children are fed AI-generated slop, completing their cognitive surrender under the guise of 'efficiency'. This is not a gap in ability, but a gap in the power to define: who is thinking, and who is being fed?

More terrifying is the internalization of this meta-violence. As students habituate to 'cognitive outsourcing', they lose not only the capacity to think but the awareness that they have lost it. It is a surrender of the brain's executive function to an algorithm, leading to total cognitive surrender. When a child mistakes AI's output for their own mastery, their subjectivity has died; they have become complicitors in the AI narrative, castrated by a simulated sense of achievement.

Reading is no longer just about knowledge; it is an act of resistance. Facing an AI empire controlled by a tiny number of men with 'dangerously damaged views', reading human work is a way to insist that 'we are human'. This isn't sentimental literary passion; it is a struggle for existential space. If we cede the power of thought to machines, our only weapon against the future will be 'counterfeit knowledge'—exactly what the rulers desire: a generation of efficient, compliant, and hollow digital natives.
