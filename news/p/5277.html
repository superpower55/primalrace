在深渊边缘扮演的“文明”与被量化的暴力The Performance of 'Civility' at the Edge of the Abyss

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-26 § 链接
所谓的“妥协”与“冷静”，往往是权力在维持结构性暴力时的PR话术。
所谓的 'compromise' is often just PR for the maintenance of structural violence.

这场关于北爱尔兰 Garvaghy Road 游行的司法拉锯，是典型的 cultural violence 掩盖 structural violence 的样本。橙色勋章（Orange Order）的游行本质上不是什么“文化传统”，而是一次精心设计的 weaponized expression。在这种叙事里，走过某条特定的街道，不是为了行走，而是为了通过物理空间的侵占来宣告某种身份的绝对统治权。这是一种典型的“存在性战争”：我必须出现在你的生活空间里，以此证明你在这个空间中的主体性是缺失的。

有趣的是法官 Smyth 试图用“为了大家”这种普世价值来劝说双方“远离深渊”。这种话术在 meta-violence 的逻辑下非常滑稽——当一方将“侵占”视为权利，而另一方将“被侵占”视为暴力时，所谓的“公正表达”根本不存在。法官试图通过延期和限制人数来制造一种“公正”的假象，但这种 structural 层面的微调，无法抵消那道深不见底的 mistrust 墙。在这种高压博弈中，人数从 5000 降到 300 的修订，不过是双方在认知入口上的博弈结果，而非真正的共识。

最讽刺的是那些呼吁“冷静”的政治精英们。对于权力上位者来说，只要没有发生大规模的 direct violence（流血冲突），现状就是“稳定”的。但根据加尔通的公式，Violence = Potential − Actual。即便游行被暂时禁令拦截，那种长期存在的、被制度化了的歧视与敌意依然在运行。这种“冷静”实际上是共谋者们为了维持一个名为“秩序”的 scam 而共同维持的表演。真正的胜利不是法官的一次临时禁令，而是当这种通过侵占空间来确认身份的权力逻辑彻底失效的那一天。

The judicial tug-of-war over the Garvaghy Road parade is a textbook case of cultural violence masking structural violence. The Orange Order's parade is not a 'cultural tradition'; it is a weaponized expression. In this narrative, marching down a specific street is not about walking, but about asserting absolute dominance through the physical occupation of space. This is a classic existential war: I must occupy your living space to prove that your subjectivity within that space is void.

It is almost comical to see Justice Smyth urging both sides to 'step back from the abyss' for the 'sake of everyone.' In the logic of meta-violence, such rhetoric is futile. When one party views 'occupation' as a right and the other views 'being occupied' as violence, a 'Just Expression' cannot be manufactured. The judge's attempt to create a facade of fairness through delays and number restrictions is a structural tweak that fails to erode the wall of mistrust. The revision of counter-march numbers from 5,000 to 300 is merely a result of gaming the cognitive entry points, not a genuine consensus.

Most ironic are the political elites calling for 'cool heads.' For those in power, as long as there is no large-scale direct violence, the status quo is 'stable.' But according to Galtung's formula, Violence = Potential − Actual. Even if the parade is temporarily halted by an injunction, the long-standing, institutionalized hatred remains operational. This 'calm' is a performance by co-conspirators to maintain a scam called 'order.' True victory is not a temporary court order, but the day the logic of confirming identity through spatial occupation becomes completely obsolete.
