所谓的“最优耳机”，不过是为标准耳道定制的共谋The 'Best' Earbuds: A Conspiracy Tailored for Standard Ear Canals

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 Wirecutter: Reviews for the Real World ↗ 2026-08-10 § 链接
当测评以“适配大多数”为标准，它就在结构层地暴力抹除少数派的生理存在。
When reviews prioritize 'most people,' they structurally erase the physiological existence of the minority.

Wirecutter 这种典型的“权威测评”看似客观，实则是一场关于“标准”的共谋。它测试了 600 款耳机，设立了所谓-Great sound-和-Secure fit-的指标，但请注意那个关键的叙事陷阱：它在寻找能适配“大多数”耳形的产品。在消费主义的逻辑里，只要 80% 的人觉得舒服，这就是一个-Top pick-。

但从加尔通的暴力三角来看，这正是典型的 structural violence。耳道的生理差异是生物墙，而工业设计和测评标准则在构建一道无形的墙。那些被定义为“小耳道”或“特殊耳形”的人，在这样的-Top pick-名单中被系统性地忽视了。测评者提到的-fit might be challenging for people with small ears-被轻描淡写地处理成一个-Flaw-，而不是一个关于“谁被排除在舒适之外”的政治问题。

更讽刺的是，Apple 等巨头通过推出 XXS 尺寸的耳塞来表演-Inclusive-。但这不过是 weaponized expression 的一种，通过微小的让步来掩盖其产品逻辑依然基于-Masculine-centric-（或标准成年男性）生理模型的本质。当你被告知某款耳机“不适合小耳朵”时，系统在潜意识里告诉你：你的生物特征是偏离标准的-Error-，而你应该去寻找能让你“像正常人一样”佩戴的替代品。

这种测评逻辑将生理多样性简化为-Fit issues-。它不关心谁在忍受疼痛，它只关心哪个产品能最快地在市场中达成共识。在这种共谋下，消费者的-最优解表达-被异化成了：在被定义的“标准”中，寻找一个勉强能忍受的次优解。

Wirecutter's 'authoritative' review is a classic conspiracy of 'standards.' Testing 600 models and setting benchmarks for 'Great sound' and 'Secure fit' creates a narrative trap: searching for products that fit 'most' ear shapes. In consumerist logic, if 80% find it comfortable, it's a 'Top pick.'

Through the lens of the Violence Triangle, this is structural violence. Physiological differences in ear canals are a biological wall, and industrial design—reinforced by review standards—builds a secondary wall. Those with 'small' or 'unusual' ear shapes are systematically marginalized. The mention that 'fit might be challenging for people with small ears' is treated as a minor 'Flaw' rather than a political question of who is excluded from comfort.

Even more ironic is how giants like Apple perform inclusivity by adding XXS tips. This is simply weaponized expression; a minor concession to mask a product logic still rooted in a masculine-centric physiological model. When you're told a product 'isn't ideal for small ears,' the system subtly signals that your biology is an 'Error' relative to the norm.

This logic reduces physiological diversity to 'fit issues.' It doesn't care who is in pain; it only cares which product reaches a market consensus fastest. In this complicity, the consumer's optimal expression is alienated: searching for a tolerable sub-optimal solution within a pre-defined 'standard.'
