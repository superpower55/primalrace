埃博拉没疫苗，但有人先撤了No Vaccine, But Someone Got a Plane

国际 结构层 Al Jazeera ↗ 2026-10-31 § 链接
疫情里最先被保护的，从来不是最先被感染的人。
In an outbreak, the first protected are never the first infected.

布尼布吉奥（Bundibugyo）株在刚果（金）自五月起杀了五千多人，至今没有获批的治疗或疫苗。而这条新闻里最先被写清楚去向的两个人，是从乌干达过去的瑞士女性和她做外交官的丈夫——周一已撤回瑞士（来源：Al Jazeera）。病毒没有国籍，撤离的飞机有。

刚果（金）26个省里有8个省被波及，确诊超过一万零三百例。卫生部门自己说，只有约12%的确诊病例是在已知接触者中被发现的，大量死亡发生在社区里。这句话的意思不是追踪做得不好，而是追踪根本没铺到人身上。早期识别和接触追踪的持续薄弱，是结构层的事：没有人力、没有实验室、没有路。结构暴力不需要施暴者，它只需要预算表上的一行空白。

同一周，两名年轻的刚果（金）医生在北基伍和伊图里死于埃博拉，非洲疾控中心周五呼吁加强对一线工作人员的保护。他们不是被病毒随机选中的，他们是在一个没有疫苗、防护不足的系统里被安排到最前面的位置。保护他们的话说出来了，防护物资有没有跟上，报道没写。

尼日利亚这两例还只是疑似，官方特意强调疑似不等于确诊。这个谨慎是对的，但它同时说明另一件事：在结果出来之前，能做的只有隔离和等。等，是这套系统给普通人提供的全部方案。

真正值得盯的不是这两例疑似的化验结果，而是疫苗试验的进度表和一线人员的防护清单——谁先拿到，谁还在等。

The Bundibugyo strain has killed more than 5,000 people in the DRC since May, and there is still no approved treatment or vaccine. Yet the two people whose movements this report traces most precisely are a Swiss woman who had travelled from Uganda and her husband, a Swiss diplomat — evacuated to Switzerland on Monday (source: Al Jazeera). Viruses don't carry passports. The evacuation plane does.

Eight of the DRC's 26 provinces are affected, with over 10,300 confirmed cases. Health authorities say only about 12 percent of confirmed cases were identified among previously known contacts, and that many deaths are occurring in communities. That is not a tracking failure so much as tracking that never reached the people. Weak early detection and contact tracing is structural: no staff, no labs, no roads. Structural violence needs no perpetrator — only a blank line in a budget.

In the same week, two young Congolese doctors died of Ebola in North Kivu and Ituri, and Africa CDC called on Friday for stronger measures to protect front-line workers. They were not randomly chosen by the virus. They were placed at the front of a system with no vaccine and insufficient protection. The call was made; whether the protective equipment followed, the report does not say.

Nigeria's two cases remain suspected, and officials are right to stress that suspected is not confirmed. The caution is correct, and it also reveals the rest: until the lab results come back, isolation and waiting are the whole of what the system offers ordinary people.

The number to watch is not these two lab results. It is the vaccine trial timeline and the front-line protection list — who gets it first, and who is still waiting.
