用“括号”掩盖的结构性共谋Structural Complicity Masked by Brackets

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-13 § 链接
当制度用“协商”掩盖掠夺，共识就成了权力的武器化叙事。
When systems use 'consultation' to mask plunder, consensus becomes a weaponized narrative of power.

这份所谓的塑料条约草案，本质上是一次完美的 structural violence 表演。在外交辞令中，最阴险的武器不是否决，而是“括号” (brackets)。把生产限额、健康影响和化学毒性全部打在括号里，意味着这些核心事实在法律上处于“待定”状态。这哪里是协商？这是在用程序正义为化石燃料巨头争取时间，让 Potential（一个可呼吸的地球）与 Actual（塑料泛滥的现状）之间的差额在法律的掩护下无限扩大。

这场博弈的本质是典型的共谋 (complicity)。少数石油和塑料生产国利用 UN 的“共识机制” (consensus system) 实施元暴力——他们垄断了关于“可行性”的解释权。只要有一个生产国拒绝，整个系统就陷入 inertia。这种机制被 weaponized 了：它将“达成一致”包装成文明的理性，实际上却是让少数掠夺者拥有了对全球公共健康的定价权。所谓的“废物管理条约” (waste-management treaty) 只是一个 scam，它试图将问题从“生产端”转移到“回收端”，让受害者在分拣垃圾中寻找救赎，而让生产端继续心安理得地榨取化石燃料。

最讽刺的是，在这种 masculine-centric 的权力逻辑中，人类健康在文本中仅出现 9 次，且大部分被括号化。这再一次证明了：在资本和权力的叙事里，生物性的生存底线永远是次要的，而生产能力的扩张才是唯一的主体。当科学家在谈论 heatwave 和 microplastics 时，外交官在谈论“参考文档”和“参与度”。这种认知入口的错位，正是元暴力最令人作呕的地方。

This so-called plastic treaty draft is a masterclass in structural violence. In the realm of diplomacy, the most sinister weapon is not the veto, but the 'brackets'. By placing production caps, health impacts, and chemical toxicity within brackets, these core facts are rendered legally 'pending'. This isn't negotiation; it is a performance of procedural justice designed to buy time for fossil fuel giants, ensuring the gap between Potential and Actual remains wide under legal cover.

At its core, this is a case of systemic complicity. A small minority of oil and plastic-producing nations are leveraging the UN's consensus system to exert meta-violence—monopolizing the interpretation of 'feasibility'. As long as one producer refuses, the entire system suffers inertia. This mechanism has been weaponized: it packages 'reaching agreement' as civilized rationality, while in reality, it grants a few plunderers the pricing power over global public health. The promise of a 'waste-management treaty' is a total scam, attempting to shift the burden from production to recycling, forcing victims to seek salvation in sorting trash while the producers continue to extract fossil fuels unchecked.

Most cynical of all is the masculine-centric logic where human health appears only nine times in the text, most of them bracketed. It proves once again that in the narrative of capital and power, biological survival is always secondary to the expansion of production capacity. While scientists discuss heatwaves and microplastics, diplomats discuss 'reference documents' and 'engagement'. This misalignment of cognitive entries is exactly where meta-violence is most repulsive.
