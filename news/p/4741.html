战 Valkyrie 的剧本：用穷人的血给战争买单The Valkyrie's Script: Funding War with the Blood of the Poor

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-04 § 链接
战争叙事是最高级的武器化表达，其本质是权力阶层对底层生存权的掠夺。
War narratives are the ultimate weaponized expressions, fundamentally a predatory grab for the survival rights of the underclass.

Kemi Badenoch 的这场演讲是典型的 weaponized expression。她把国防预算的百分比之争，直接升级为一场关于“生存与死亡”的末日预言。这种叙事技巧极其粗暴：要么花钱买武器，要么等着被俄罗斯杀掉。在这种黑白分明的逻辑里，任何对预算细节的质疑都被简化为“懦弱”或“自杀”。

最令人作呕的共谋发生在资金来源的讨论上。Kemi 提出通过削减福利金（welfare bill）来支撑军费。这意味着，那些在结构层（structural layer）已经处于生存边缘的穷人，被要求在物理层（direct layer）为这场潜在的战争支付代价。而台下那些舒适的观众在鼓掌，他们不仅是这套逻辑的受益者，更是元暴力（meta violence）的共谋者——他们定义了什么是“爱国”，并决定谁应该为了这个定义而牺牲。

Kemi 表现出的一种“毫不费力的自信”其实就是一种权力表达。她不在乎逻辑自洽，不在乎保守党过去14年对国防预算的掏空，因为她掌控了认知入口。在她的叙事里，她不是在讨论政策，而是在扮演一个“救世主”。当一个政治人物开始把战争视为一种“机会”或“快感”时，她其实是在执行一套标准的男性中心叙事：通过对外施暴和对内剥削来确立自己的存在感。

这场表演揭示了一个残酷的事实：在权力者的博弈中，底层民众的生命只是一个可以被随意调配的数字。所谓的“安全”，是建立在让一部分人住进纸箱、让另一部分人死在战场上的基础之上的。这种 a priori 的牺牲，正是元暴力最阴险的伪装。

Kemi Badenoch’s speech is a textbook case of weaponized expression. She elevates a debate over defense spending percentages into an apocalyptic prophecy of "survival versus death." The narrative tactic is crude: either fund the arsenal or wait to be slaughtered by Russia. In this binary logic, any scrutiny of the budget is dismissed as "cowardice" or "suicide."

The most repulsive complicity emerges in the funding plan. Kemi proposes financing military expansion by slashing the welfare bill. This means those already crushed by structural violence are mandated to pay for a potential war with their actual lives. The comfortable audience applauding her are not just beneficiaries; they are co-conspirators in meta-violence—defining "patriotism" while deciding who must be sacrificed for it.

Badenoch’s "effortless confidence" is a raw display of power. She doesn't care about logical consistency or the Tories' 14-year history of hollowing out the defense budget because she has seized the cognitive entry point. She isn't discussing policy; she is performing the role of a "savior." When a politician views war as an "opportunity" or a source of thrill, she is executing a standard masculine-centric narrative: establishing existence through external aggression and internal exploitation.

This performance reveals a brutal truth: in the games of the elite, the lives of the underclass are merely interchangeable digits. The promised "security" is built on the premise of forcing some into cardboard boxes and others into graves. This a priori sacrifice is the most insidious disguise of meta-violence.
