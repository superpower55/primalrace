素食食谱里的阶级审美与认知入口Class Aesthetics and Cognitive Entry in Vegan Recipes

哲学 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-10 § 链接
品味是区分阶级的武器，而“纯净”叙事是最高级的筛选屏障。
Taste is a weapon for class distinction; the 'purity' narrative is the ultimate filter.

一篇关于番茄素食的食谱，在《卫报》这种认知入口里，本质上不是在教你做饭，而是在定义一种“正确”的生存美学。注意那些细节：vegan puff pastry, extra-virgin olive oil, almond ‘ricotta’。这套叙事将“素食”从一种生存选择或伦理抗争，转化为一种高门槛的审美表达。当你需要浸泡四个小时的杏仁来模拟里科塔奶酪，或者在伦敦 EC1 区的餐厅老板指导下追求番茄的 umami kick 时，这已经变成了一场关于 Taste 的阶级筛选。

这种表达的武器化在于，它通过定义什么是“高级”的纯净（Clean Eating），潜移默化地建立起一套新的鄙视链。它告诉受众：真正的健康和文明，不在于简单的不吃肉，而在于你是否有能力、有时间、有认知去完成这种复杂的、去动物化的模拟。这是一种典型的文化暴力（cultural violence），它用“健康”和“环保”的糖衣，掩盖了其背后对资源占用能力和阶级品味的定价权。

最讽刺的是，这种叙事让人们在追求“主体性”的饮食选择时，再次掉入由商业团队和精英阶层预设的认知陷阱。你以为你在实践一种进步的、人道的生活方式，但实际上你只是在扮演一个符合中产阶级审美期待的角色。在这种共谋中，素食被剥离了政治性，变成了一件昂贵的装饰品。

A vegan tomato recipe in a cognitive entry like The Guardian is not about cooking; it is about defining a 'correct' aesthetic of existence. Notice the markers: vegan puff pastry, extra-virgin olive oil, almond ‘ricotta’. This narrative transforms veganism from a survival choice or ethical struggle into a high-threshold expression of Taste. When you are required to soak almonds for four hours to simulate ricotta or chase an 'umami kick' under the guidance of a London EC1 chef, it becomes a class filter.

The weaponisation of this expression lies in how it defines 'sophisticated' purity (Clean Eating) to establish a new hierarchy of contempt. It suggests that true health and civilisation are not found in simply avoiding meat, but in possessing the resources, time, and cognition to execute this complex, de-animalised simulation. This is a form of cultural violence, using the sugar-coating of 'health' and 'eco-friendliness' to mask the pricing power over resource access and class taste.

Ironically, while people seek agency in their dietary choices, they fall back into cognitive traps preset by commercial teams and the elite. You believe you are practicing a progressive, humane lifestyle, but you are merely performing a role that fits the middle-class aesthetic expectation. In this complicity, veganism is stripped of its politicality and turned into an expensive ornament.
