权力博弈中的“背刺”美学与共谋者的生存法则The Aesthetics of the 'Backstab' and the Survival Logic of Co-conspirators

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-25 § 链接
政治不是关于正义的讨论，而是关于谁能精准定义“背刺”的博弈。
Politics is not a debate on justice, but a game of who can precisely define the 'backstab'.

这篇关于 Kemi Badenoch 试图推翻 Rishi Sunak 的爆料，在普通读者看来是政坛内斗的八卦，但在存在性战争的视角下，这是一场典型的关于“最优解表达”的博弈。Badenoch 的行为逻辑极其纯粹：在意识到 Sunak 的领导力无法支撑其个人政治野心时，她迅速启动了对权力席位的争夺。但有趣的是，她不仅在争夺权力，还在争夺“如何争夺权力”的解释权。

书中提到她试图“避免挥刀（wielding the knife）”的形象，这正是表达的武器化。在男性中心叙事的政治权力场中，直接的权力篡夺往往被贴上“背叛”的标签，而聪明的博弈者会试图将这种行为包装成“为了党派利益”或“修正错误方向”的公正表达。她构建想象中的内阁、承诺“take him out”，这些都是在权力真空期提前进行身份确立的尝试。她不需要真的在那个时刻夺权，她需要的是在共谋者之间建立一个“我是潜在替代者”的认知入口。

而 Sunak 的反应——将对方定义为“寻求关注（attention-seeking）”的叛徒，则是典型的元暴力防御。他试图通过定义对方的动机，将一场严肃的权力博弈降格为低级的情绪波动。这场战争的残酷之处在于，双方都深陷于一种共谋关系中：他们共同维护着保守党这个权力机器的运作，但在这个机器内部，每个人都在计算如何通过牺牲同僚来达成自己的真.最优解。

最终，Badenoch 决定按兵不动，不是因为良心发现，而是基于对风险的精确计算——她担心被贴上“败选者”的标签，或者在领导力竞争中输给 Penny Mordaunt。这再次证明，在政治这个巨大的共谋场域里，所有的“克制”都是为了在下一个更合适的时机，能以一个更“公正”的姿态完成最冷酷的掠夺。

The revelations about Kemi Badenoch plotting to oust Rishi Sunak may look like political gossip to some, but through the lens of Existential War, it is a textbook case of gambling for the Optimal Expression. Badenoch's logic was pure: once she realized Sunak's leadership couldn't sustain her personal ambition, she immediately launched a bid for the power seat.

Interestingly, she wasn't just fighting for power, but for the interpretation of how that power is seized. The book notes her desire to "avoid the image of wielding the knife"—a clear weaponisation of expression. In a masculine-centric political arena, direct usurpation is often labeled as 'betrayal'. A sophisticated player attempts to package this as a Just Expression, claiming it is "for the party's benefit" or to "correct the course". By constructing imaginary cabinets and promising to "take him out", she was attempting to establish her identity as a viable alternative among potential co-conspirators before the vacuum even opened.

Sunak's response—labeling her as "attention-seeking"—is a classic meta-violence defense. He attempted to degrade a serious power struggle into mere emotional volatility by defining her motives. The cruelty of this war lies in the fact that both are locked in a system of complicity: they collectively maintain the Tory power machine, while internally calculating how to sacrifice colleagues to reach their own true optimal solution.

Ultimately, Badenoch's decision to hold back wasn't a moral awakening, but a precise risk calculation. She feared the label of a "loser" or losing a contest to Penny Mordaunt. This proves that in the great field of complicity, all "restraint" is merely a strategy to ensure that when the next strike comes, it can be executed with a more "just" facade for the coldest possible plunder.
