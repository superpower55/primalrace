Bill Simmons 的“无知”与权力的代持Bill Simmons' 'Ignorance' and the Proxy of Power

其他 结构层 · 文化层 The Athletic ↗ 2026-09-02 § 链接
所谓的“不了解规则”，是权力者在触碰禁区后最廉价的掩体。
Claiming 'ignorance of the rules' is the cheapest shield for the powerful after they cross the line.

Bill Simmons 在播客里轻描淡写地承认自己用女儿男朋友当 proxy betting 的代理人，并用一句“不知道规则”试图把这件事消解为一次无心之过。这太典型的 power move 了：一个掌控着顶尖体育媒体资源、身价数亿的 CEO，在面对一个极其简单的法律红线时，竟然表现得像个刚入职的实习生一样 naive。

这里的核心不是 proxy betting 本身，而是 Simmons 对“规则”的认知方式。对于处于权力顶端的人来说，规则（rules）通常被视为一种可以协商的建议，或者是在被抓到之前可以忽略的背景噪音。他习惯于定义叙事，习惯于在自己的播客里掌控话语权，因此他潜意识里认为，只要自己拥有足够的资源和影响力，物理上的地理限制（加州禁赌）可以通过一个简单的“代码共享”被抹除。

最讽刺的是，他选择的代持人是“女儿的男朋友”。这不仅是一次法律上的违规，更是一次微型的权力博弈。在 Simmons 的私人领域里，这个年轻人是被雇佣或被请求的工具，是他进入马萨诸塞州博彩市场的“生物接口”。这种将他人工具化的行为，正是典型的 masculine-centric narrative：强者通过掌控弱者的接入权来获取利益，而当风险降临，强者只需要通过扮演“无知”来寻求宽恕。

FanDuel 撤销了赌注，监管机构在审查，但这绝不会成为一个真正的教训。因为在权力的共谋场域中，这种“轻微违规”往往被视为一种特权阶层的调情。真正的代价应该由那些没有媒体帝国保护、因为 proxy betting 被封号或被起诉的底层赌徒承担，而 Simmons 只需要在下一集播客里自嘲一下，然后继续定义什么是“体育精神”。

Bill Simmons casually admitted on his podcast to using his daughter's boyfriend as a proxy for betting, attempting to dissolve the scandal with a simple 'I didn't know the rules.' This is a classic power move: a CEO controlling elite sports media resources, worth hundreds of millions, suddenly acting as naive as an intern when facing a blatant legal red line.

The core issue here isn't proxy betting, but Simmons' perception of rules. For those at the top, rules are often viewed as negotiable suggestions or background noise to be ignored until caught. He is used to defining the narrative and controlling the discourse; thus, he subconsciously believed that physical geographical restrictions (California's gambling ban) could be erased via a simple 'shared code.'

Most ironic is his choice of proxy: his daughter's boyfriend. This is more than a legal violation; it is a micro-game of power. In Simmons' private domain, this young man is a tool, a 'biological interface' for his entry into the Massachusetts gambling market. This instrumentalization of others is a textbook masculine-centric narrative: the strong leverage the access of the weak for gain, and when risk arrives, the strong simply perform 'ignorance' to seek leniency.

FanDuel voided the bets and regulators are reviewing, but this will never be a real lesson. In the field of complicity, such 'minor infractions' are often treated as the flirtations of the privileged. The real cost is paid by bottom-tier gamblers who lack a media empire for protection and face permanent bans or prosecution. Simmons only needs to self-deprecate in his next episode and continue defining what 'sportsmanship' means.
