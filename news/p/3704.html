不要在“希望”的叙事里掩盖结构性的饥饿Don't Let the Narrative of 'Hope' Mask Structural Hunger

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-12 § 链接
技术突破不能抵消资源分配的结构性暴力
Technical breakthroughs cannot offset the structural violence of resource distribution.

这是一篇典型的、充满温情的“好新闻”：一名南非女性科学家发现了某种天然真菌，能够有效杀死摧毁农作物的非洲草地贪夜蛾。叙事逻辑很完整：发现 $ ightarrow$ 实验 $ ightarrow$ 论文 $ ightarrow$ 给农民带来“希望”。在这种叙事里，我们看到的是一个 Individual 战胜了 Biological 威胁的英雄故事。

但如果用加尔通的暴力三角来看，这件好事只在 direct 层面上提供了一种潜在的救济。它试图缩小 Potential（没有虫害的作物）与 Actual（被虫蛀空的田地）之间的差额。然而，真正的暴力隐藏在 structural 层：为什么赞比亚的玉米生产能被一次虫害摧毁 40%？为什么津巴布韦和马拉维的农民在面对生物灾难时，唯一的应对方式是“恐慌”和等待一个偶然的科学发现？

这种“希望”的表达其实是一种危险的武器化叙事。它将结构性的农业脆弱、匮乏的农业基础设施以及极端的通货膨胀（津巴布韦 736%），简化为了一个“寻找杀虫剂”的技术问题。当媒体在赞美这种“突破”时，实际上是在共谋掩盖一个事实：即使真菌产品落地，如果底层的土地所有权、种子公司垄断以及国际贸易不公的结构不改变，农民依然是那个在生物墙面前瑟瑟发抖的、被殖民的原初种族。

好新闻的机制应该是 Actual 朝 Potential 走近。但如果这次走近只是给一个破旧的笼子换了一把更好的锁，那么这种“希望”不过是 cultural violence 的高级版本——它让我们在庆祝科学胜利的同时，忘记追问谁在制造饥饿。

This is a classic, heartwarming 'good news' story: a South African female scientist discovers a natural fungus that kills the devastating African armyworm. The narrative is seamless: discovery $ ightarrow$ experiment $ ightarrow$ paper $ ightarrow$ 'hope' for farmers. It presents a heroic tale of an Individual overcoming a Biological threat.

However, applying the Violence Triangle, this 'good news' only offers potential relief at the direct level. It attempts to shrink the gap between Potential (pest-free crops) and Actual (ravaged fields). But the real violence is structural: why can a single outbreak destroy 40% of Zambia's maize? Why is 'panic' and waiting for a serendipitous scientific discovery the only response for farmers in Zimbabwe and Malawi?

This expression of 'hope' is actually a weaponized narrative. It reduces structural agricultural fragility, lack of infrastructure, and hyperinflation (736% in Zimbabwe) to a simple technical problem of 'finding a pesticide.' While the media celebrates this 'breakthrough,' they are in complicity in masking a fact: even if the fungus product is deployed, as long as the structures of land ownership, seed monopolies, and unfair international trade remain, the farmers remain a Primal Race trembling before a biological wall.

True good_news happens when Actual moves toward Potential. But if this movement is merely replacing an old lock with a better one on a broken cage, then this 'hope' is just a sophisticated version of cultural violence—it makes us celebrate scientific victory while forgetting to ask who is manufacturing the hunger.
