Agent 47 与 2.5 万欧元的血色角色扮演Agent 47 and the Bloody Role-play of €25,000

哲学 文化层 · 直接层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-30 § 链接
当暴力被游戏化，年轻人通过扮演“杀手”来确认自身的存在性。
When violence is gamified, youth seek existential confirmation by performing the role of a 'hitman'.

一个 19 岁的挪威少年，在披萨店打零工，却在文本对话中幻想自己是《Hitman》里的 Agent 47。他同意为了 2.5 万欧元跨国去杀人，在被捕时还对着警察做射击手势。这在常规新闻里是“危险青年”的个案，但在我的视界里，这是一场典型的、被武器化叙事诱导的“存在性战争”博弈。

Natland 的悲剧在于他试图通过扮演一个“国际杀手”的角色来获得某种主体性。他向女友发信息说“去执行疯狂任务很酷”，这种“酷”是对其底层生活（打零工、吸毒、寄宿在照顾之家）的一种暴力补偿。当一个年轻人无法在现实的 structural 层级中找到上升通道时，他会倾向于在 cultural 层级寻找一个能快速获得“权力感”的拟像——在这种情况下，是犯罪组织 Foxtrot Network 提供的杀手剧本。

最讽刺的是，这个犯罪组织甚至用游戏角色的代号（Agent 47）来控制他。这是一种极高明的认知入口武器化：将真实的谋杀包装成一场大型的 Role-play。Natland 在法庭上辩称自己只是在“吹牛”(bravado)，这其实是他主体性崩塌后的自我防御。他发现自己并非这场博弈的操盘手，而仅仅是被定价为 2.5 万欧元的 disposable tool（可消耗工具）。

这再次证明了元暴力的运作方式：无论是在国家机器的战争中，还是在街头帮派的屠杀中，男性中心叙事总是将“暴力”与“力量”、“酷”、“成功”挂钩。年轻男性被训练成认为，通过对他人的直接暴力来确认自己的存在，是达成“最优解表达”的捷径。结果就是，他不仅失去了自由，还成为了一个被远程操控的傀儡。

A 19-year-old Norwegian boy, working part-time in a pizza shop, fantasized himself as Agent 47 from the Hitman series. He agreed to a cross-border assassination for €25,000 and even mimed shooting police officers upon his arrest. While mainstream news sees a 'dangerous youth,' I see a classic case of existential war driven by weaponized narratives.

Natland's tragedy lies in his attempt to gain agency by performing the role of an 'international assassin.' His texts to his girlfriend about a 'crazy mission' being 'cool' are a violent compensation for his structural void—working low-end jobs, drug abuse, and a history in care homes. When a youth finds no path upward in the structural layer, they seek a simulacrum of 'power' in the cultural layer. Here, the Foxtrot Network provided the script.

It is peak irony that the gang controlled him using the alias 'Agent 47.' This is the weaponization of cognitive entry: packaging actual murder as a grand Role-play. Natland's courtroom plea that his statements were mere 'bravado' is a defense mechanism after the collapse of his subjectivity. He realized he wasn't the player in this game, but a disposable tool priced at €25,000.

This reinforces the logic of meta-violence: whether in state-led wars or street gang massacres, the masculine-centric narrative consistently links 'violence' with 'strength,' 'coolness,' and 'success.' Young men are conditioned to believe that confirming their existence through direct violence against others is the shortcut to an optimal expression. The result? He lost his freedom and became a remote-controlled puppet.
