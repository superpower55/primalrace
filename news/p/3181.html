边境的“控制感”与被抹除的生命成本The 'Sense of Control' and the Erasure of Human Cost

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-02 § 链接
所谓的“控制边境”本质上是权力的表演，而难民的死亡是这出戏的背景噪音。
Border 'control' is a performance of power; the deaths of refugees are merely background noise to the play.

Andy Burnham 所谓的“relentless”（不懈）和“bring back control”（找回控制感），是典型的男性中心叙事下的权力博弈。在政治人物的口径里，边境线不是地理标志，而是一道关于“尊严”和“主权”的心理防线。当他说公众不喜欢边境看起来“不受控”时，他关注的不是人的生存，而是这种“失控感”对他政治票价的损耗。这是一种极其傲慢的表达：把数万名绝望者的生存冲动，定义为对某种“秩序”的侵犯。

西班牙在 Ceuta 的操作更是冷酷的 structural violence。98% 的人被直接扔回摩洛哥，不经过任何难民申请流程。这种高效的“清除”被描述为处理问题，但被抹除的是个体在法律意义上的主体性。72 具尸体被简化为“领土完整被侵犯”的附带损害。在权力者的逻辑里，只要能维持 Schengen 区域的统一和选民的心理舒适度，让难民在海边溺毙或在铁丝网下被踩死，就只是一个需要被“管理”的统计数字。

最讽刺的是 Shabana Mahmood 试图通过将永久居留权门槛从 5 年延长到 10 年来“改变计算方式”（change the calculus）。这根本不是什么政策优化，而是一种 weaponized 的绝望策略。通过增加生存成本来恐吓潜在的迁徙者，本质上是在利用生物墙之外的制度墙，将绝望的人群推向更危险的走私渠道。这种所谓的“改革”，其实是在为权力者制造一个可以合法施暴的真空地带。

这场关于边境的战争，本质上是权力的共谋。无论左翼还是右翼，当他们谈论“安全路径”时，其实是在讨论如何更体面地维持排外机制。真正的 Potential（生存权）与 Actual（被驱逐/死亡）之间的差额，就是这场政治表演中被刻意忽略的暴力。

Andy Burnham’s pledge to be “relentless” and “bring back control” is a textbook example of power gaming within a masculine-centric narrative. In the lexicon of politicians, the border is not a geographical marker, but a psychological frontline of “dignity” and “sovereignty.” When he claims the public dislikes the idea of a border looking “uncontrolled,” he is not concerned with human survival, but with how this “loss of control” affects his political polling. It is an arrogant expression: defining the survival instinct of tens of thousands as an infringement on a curated “order.”

Spain’s operation in Ceuta is an exercise in cold, structural violence. Returning 98% of people to Morocco without processing asylum claims is a highly efficient “cleansing” described as “dealing with the issue.” What is being erased here is the legal subjectivity of the individual. 72 corpses are reduced to collateral damage in a narrative about the “violation of territorial integrity.” In the logic of power, as long as the Schengen unity and voter comfort are maintained, letting refugees drown or be crushed against fences is merely a statistical anomaly to be “managed.”

Most cynical is Shabana Mahmood’s attempt to “change the calculus” by doubling the time for indefinite leave to remain from 5 to 10 years. This is not policy optimization; it is the weaponisation of despair. By increasing the cost of survival to deter migrants, she is utilizing a systemic wall to push desperate people into even more dangerous smuggling routes. This “reform” is simply creating a vacuum where power can exercise violence with legal impunity.

This war over borders is a grand act of complicity. Whether left or right, when they speak of “safe routes,” they are actually discussing how to maintain exclusionary mechanisms more tastefully. The gap between the Potential (the right to survive) and the Actual (deportation and death) is the violence deliberately ignored in this political performance.
