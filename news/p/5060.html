不要把进步派当成可以随意踢的小狗Stop Treating Progressives Like Puppies to Be Kicked

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-16 § 链接
所谓的“进步”共谋，在权力兑现面前永远是脆弱的
The complicity of 'progressivism' is always fragile when faced with the realization of power.

工党（Labour）现在陷入了一种极其尴尬的认知危机：他们发现自己曾经用来收割城市选民的“进步”叙事，正在被绿党（Greens）反向武器化。ThinkLabour 的报告里有个很有意思的比喻，说工党攻击绿党就像在“踢一只小狗”（kicking a puppy）。这个比喻精准地揭示了某种文化共谋——在城市中产和所谓 liberal 的认知里，绿党被定义为一个“纯真、无害、虽然天真但善良”的客体。在这种叙事里，绿党不是一个竞争对手，而是一个被宠溺的宠物。

但这次 polling 揭露了一个残酷的 structural 事实：选民的流动不再仅仅基于某种审美上的“纯真”，而是基于 Actual 的生存压力。那些财务状况糟糕的城市选民正在大规模转向绿党。这意味着，当工党在 Downing Street 玩弄权术、削减冬季燃料补贴这种 structural violence 时，他们原本以为可以通过“恐吓选民（如果不投我，法拉奇就会上台）”这种认知入口来维持垄断，但这个 scam 失效了。选民意识到，一个扮演“强权”的工党，比一个扮演“小狗”的绿党更让他们感到不安。

最讽刺的是，工党现在的策略是试图在移民问题上通过“限制合法移民”来向右转，同时又想在叙事上维持“进步”的皮囊。这是一种典型的假.最优解表达：试图在两个互斥的阵营中同时占便宜。而绿党领袖 Polanski 那些关于“催眠增大胸部”的荒唐过去，在目前的博弈中竟然变得不重要了。因为在生存压力面前，一个有缺陷的个体表达，远比一个毫无价值观、随时准备出卖底层利益的权力机器更具可信度。

工党以为自己在踢小狗，但实际上，他们是在亲手拆除自己与城市进步派之间最后的共谋纽带。当“小狗”开始在议会拿走 40 个席位时，工党才会发现，被他们轻视的叙事，其实是另一种形式的存在性战争。

Labour is currently trapped in a profound cognitive crisis: they've discovered that the 'progressive' narrative they once used to harvest urban voters is being weaponized against them by the Greens. The ThinkLabour report offers a telling metaphor, suggesting that Labour's attacks on the Greens look like "kicking a puppy." This reveals a specific cultural complicity—in the eyes of the urban liberal, the Green Party is defined as an innocent, harmless, and perhaps naive object. In this narrative, the Greens aren't competitors; they are pets.

However, this polling exposes a brutal structural fact: the shift in voters is no longer based on an aesthetic of 'purity,' but on actual survival pressure. Financially struggling urban voters are moving toward the Greens in large numbers. This means that while Labour performs structural violence from Downing Street—such as cutting winter fuel payments—their attempt to maintain a monopoly via a cognitive scam ("vote for me or Farage wins") has failed. Voters have realized that a Labour Party playing the 'strongman' is far more unsettling than a Green Party playing the 'puppy.'

The irony is that Labour's current strategy is to pivot right on immigration—targeting legal migrants—while trying to maintain a progressive facade. This is a classic fake optimal expression: attempting to profit from two contradictory camps simultaneously. Meanwhile, Zack Polanski's absurd past regarding 'hypnotherapy for breast enlargement' has become irrelevant in the current game. In the face of existential struggle, a flawed individual expression is far more credible than a power machine that has no values and is ready to betray the bottom line.

Labour thinks they are kicking a puppy, but in reality, they are dismantling the final bond of complicity with urban progressives. When the 'puppy' begins to take 40 seats in Parliament, Labour will realize that the narrative they dismissed was actually another front in an existential war.
