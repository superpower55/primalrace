用“航运自由”掩盖的男性中心战争游戏A Masculine War Game Masked by 'Freedom of Navigation'

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-01 § 链接
所有宏大叙事的战争，本质上都是男性用他者的身体交换权力。
All grand narratives of war are simply men trading others' bodies for power.

美国中央司令部在 X 上轻描淡写地宣布袭击伊朗 IRGC 目标，理由是“保护商业航运”和“回应袭击”。这种典型的武器化叙事，通过定义一个“正义”的认知入口（航运自由、保护士兵），将一次纯粹的权力博弈包装成必要的防御。但剥开这层皮，你会发现这依然是一场极其标准的 masculine-centric narrative：一群身处权力顶端的男性，在地图上划线，决定用多少枚导弹去交换多少地缘影响力。

这场战争的 direct 层是爆炸和死亡，但其 structural 层是全球资源分配的霸权逻辑。而最深层的 cultural violence 在于，这种“为了秩序而开战”的逻辑被默认为理所当然。在这种叙事中，无论是被袭击的伊朗平民，还是被当作消耗品的士兵，都被降格为可计算的工具。他们不是作为完整的人存在，而是作为“目标”或“损失”被处理。这正是元暴力的体现：将人客体化，以实现某种宏大意义的交换。

历史上，这类战争经常夹带“解放中东女性”或“传播文明”的 side project 来骗取进步派的良心背书，但本质上，这只是男性权力场中的一次肌肉展示。所谓的“危机”和“敌对”，不过是权力定价权的重新调整。在这种博弈中，真正受损的永远是那些没有定义权、没有解释权的原初种族，而制定规则的人，在空调房里通过屏幕完成了一次关于“存在性”的暴力确认。

US Central Command casually announces strikes on IRGC targets on X, citing the 'protection of commercial shipping' and 'responding to attacks.' This is a textbook example of weaponized expression: creating a 'just' cognitive entry point—freedom of navigation—to package a raw power struggle as necessary defense. But strip away the veneer, and you find a standard masculine-centric narrative: men at the top of the power hierarchy drawing lines on a map, deciding how many missiles are worth a slice of geopolitical influence.

The direct layer of this conflict is explosions and death, but the structural layer is the hegemonic logic of global resource distribution. The deepest cultural violence lies in the assumption that 'warring for order' is a natural necessity. In this narrative, whether it's Iranian civilians or deployed soldiers, humans are downgraded into consumable tools. They don't exist as subjects, but as 'targets' or 'losses.' This is meta-violence in its purest form: the objectification of humans to facilitate an exchange of 'grand meaning.'

Historically, these wars often carry side projects like 'liberating Middle Eastern women' to scam the conscience of progressives, but in reality, it's just a flex of masculine power. The so-called 'crisis' is merely a recalibration of the pricing power of existence. In this game, the ones who truly suffer are the Primal Race—those without the power to define or explain—while the rule-makers confirm their own existence through a screen-mediated act of violence.
