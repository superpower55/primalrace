主权是掩体，而经济封锁是最高级的元暴力Sovereignty as a Shield, Sanctions as Meta-Violence

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-24 § 链接
sanctions 并非经济手段，而是通过定义“合法性”来实施的全球解释权战争。
Sanctions are not economic tools, but a war over the global power to define legitimacy.

美国财政部所谓的“史上最大金融攻势”，本质上是一次典型的 weaponized expression。它不再是简单的贸易禁运，而是在试图通过 secondary sanctions 夺取全球贸易的“解释权”和“定价权”。当 Scott Bessent 宣称“要么与我们在一起，要么与我们相对立”时，他其实是在定义一个全球性的共谋场域：顺从美国的叙事即是“自由”和“接入资本”，而拒绝则被标记为“敌对”。

这种逻辑极其阴险，它将 structural violence 伪装成一种“选择”。它告诉盟友，切断与伊朗的联系能“增强其自身的连接性”——这其实是一种典型的 scam。它用一种虚假的“最优解”诱导其他国家成为美国维护霸权的共谋者，代价是牺牲伊朗这个原初种族般的被殖民者，将其生存空间压缩至 Potential 与 Actual 的极端差额之中。

有趣的是，伊朗和中国在此时的博弈姿态。伊朗试图用“主权”和“联合国宪章”来反击，但这依然是在男性中心叙事（masculine-centric narrative）的框架内讨论。真正的战场在于：当美国试图通过 naval blockade 这种 direct violence 将伊朗物理性地从全球贸易中抹除时，它已经不再掩饰其元暴力的本质。这种暴力不仅针对政权，更通过 hyperinflation 直接作用于普通公民的肉身，将经济指标转化为一种精准的规训武器。

这场博弈的最终结果不取决于谁的口号更响，而取决于谁能定义什么是“事实”：是美国定义的“金融孤立”，还是中俄等国试图建立的、脱离美元霸权的另一种“真实”。

The US Treasury's so-called “greatest financial offensive” is a textbook case of weaponized expression. It is no longer about simple trade embargoes, but an attempt to seize the “right of interpretation” and “pricing power” of global trade through secondary sanctions. When Scott Bessent declares “either you are with us or against us,” he is constructing a global field of complicity: compliance with the US narrative is branded as “freedom” and “access to capital,” while refusal is marked as “hostility.”

This logic is insidious, disguising structural violence as a “choice.” It lures allies with a fake optimal expression—that severing ties with Iran will “reinvigorate their own connectivity.” This is a pure scam. It induces other nations to become co-conspirators in maintaining US hegemony, at the cost of sacrificing Iran, treated here like a Primal Race, pushing the gap between its Potential and Actual existence to a breaking point.

Interestingly, the博弈 positions of Iran and China reveal the limits of current narratives. Iran attempts to fight back using “sovereignty” and the “UN Charter,” but this remains within the framework of a masculine-centric narrative. The real battlefield is this: when the US employs a naval blockade—a form of direct violence—to physically erase Iran from global trade, it ceases to hide its meta-violence. This violence targets not just a regime, but the physical bodies of ordinary citizens through hyperinflation, turning economic indicators into precise weapons of discipline.

The outcome of this game depends not on who shouts louder, but on who can manufacture the “truth”: whether it is the “financial isolation” defined by the US, or another “reality” that China and Russia are attempting to build, independent of the dollar hegemony.
