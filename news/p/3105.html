马斯克的“真实”：一场关于解释权的暴力掠夺Musk's 'Truth': A Violent Seizure of Interpretive Power

国际 文化层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-01 § 链接
真相在权力面前不是事实问题，而是解释权的武器化博弈。
Truth is not a matter of fact before power, but a weaponized game of interpretation.

马斯克与《经济学人》编辑的这场对话，本质上是一次典型的“表达武器化”演示。马斯克并不在乎统计数据或地面报道，因为他追求的不是真.最优解表达，而是在一个由他掌控的认知入口（X平台）中，通过重新定义“正常人”来夺取解释权。当他把极右翼定义为“正常人”时，他是在制造一套新的事实，从而将所有不认同此定义的人直接剔除出“正常”的范畴。

这场博弈最阴暗的部分在于其后期的共谋与反噬。编辑在采访结束后的礼貌互动，在马斯克的叙事里被迅速转化为一种“被攻略”的战利品。他先通过 repost 制造一种“双方达成共识”的假象，随即迅速反手将其定义为“西方的叛徒”。这种从“zany”到“traitor”的极速切换，揭示了元暴力（meta violence）的运作逻辑：在男性中心叙事中，女性即便占据了专业的高地，其存在性依然被允许在男性的意志下被随意定义和抹除。

值得关注的是，报道中提到的记者自我审查，尤其是女性记者承受的最高强度攻击。这证明了直接暴力（direct violence）是如何通过文化暴力（cultural violence）的铺垫而实现的。马斯克通过在平台上投放 vitriolic comments，将女性记者的专业表达降格为可被攻击的客体，从而在结构层面上封锁女性获取真相的路径。这不再是简单的观点之争，而是一场旨在让女性在公共空间中消失的存在性战争。

The clash between Elon Musk and The Economist's editor is a textbook demonstration of the weaponization of expression. Musk doesn't care about statistics or ground reports because he isn't seeking a Just Expression; he is seizing the power of interpretation through a cognitive entry point he owns—the X platform. By redefining the far-right as "normal people," he is manufacturing a new reality, effectively casting anyone who disagrees as "abnormal."

The darkest part of this game is the transition from complicity to backlash. The editor's polite post-interview interaction was instantly transformed into a trophy of conquest in Musk's narrative. He first created a facade of consensus with a "zany" repost, only to swiftly pivot and label her a "traitor to the west." This rapid shift from "zany" to "traitor" reveals the logic of meta violence: in a masculine-centric narrative, a woman's existence can be arbitrarily defined and erased by male will, regardless of her professional standing.

Crucially, the self-censorship of journalists, particularly the brunt of aggression borne by women, proves how direct violence is enabled by cultural violence. By deploying vitriolic comments on his platform, Musk degrades the professional expression of female journalists into objects of attack, structurally blocking their path to truth. This is no longer a mere disagreement over views; it is an existential war designed to erase women from the public square.
