“98%的人选择留下”是句什么话What '98% Choose to Stay' Actually Says

科技 结构层 · 文化层 CNBC ↗ 2026-11-02 § 链接
被偷走的钱由平台兜底，兜底的钱由谁定价
The platform covers the loss; the platform also sets the price of trust.

币安被黑客卷走 63 亿美元，重开提现的九个半小时里，用户净提走 36.4 亿。赵长鹏在 X 上写：“98% 的人选择留下！”

这句话值得停一下。一个平台刚被偷了 63 亿，用户的第一反应是跑，跑掉的钱占它总资产的 2.1%。创始人不说“我们怎么补上这个洞”，先说“留下来的人占多数”。这是把提现当成一次投票，把跑掉的人当成叛徒，把留下来的人当成忠诚的证明。信任在这里变成了一种表态，而不是一种可以被检验的东西。

币安说它会自己吸收损失，不转嫁给用户。它说已经覆盖了约 95%，剩下 5% 是流动性差的代币。这句话的意思是：那 63 亿里，有一部分不是钱，是账面数字。黑客拿走了能拿走的，剩下的窟窿用“我们会补”来填。谁在定价这个“补”？平台自己。谁的资产被锁在 15 条网络里等它“分阶段恢复”？用户。

FBI 说这是朝鲜黑客干的，代号 TraderTraitor。国家级的行为体在偷交易所，交易所在用“98% 的人留下”安抚剩下的人。这两件事放在一起，说明加密世界最核心的承诺——你的资产是你的——在结构上从来没有成立过。你把它交给一个中心化的平台保管，平台被偷了，你等它开门，它告诉你大部分人都没走。这不是去中心化，这是把银行的风险和赌场的语气缝在一起。

赵长鹏还说：“信任不是靠顺境时说的话赢得的，是靠逆境时做的事赢得的。”对。逆境时它做的事是：先恢复提现，再公布净流出，再宣布自己兜底，再让创始人出来说大多数人没走。顺序很清楚——先让你能跑，再让你觉得跑的人不多，最后让你觉得自己没跑是对的。

36.4 亿净流出之后，币安剩下的资产里还有多少是那 5% 的“流动性差的代币”，报道没写。谁在承担这部分的不确定性，报道也没写。写了的只有一句“98% 选择留下”——这句话不是给用户听的，是给还没跑的那 98% 听的。

Binance lost $6.3 billion to hackers. In the nine and a half hours after withdrawals reopened, users pulled out $3.64 billion net. Changpeng Zhao wrote on X: "98% choose to stay!"

Stop there. A platform has just been robbed of $6.3 billion, and its founder's first public line is not how the hole gets filled but that most people didn't run. That turns a withdrawal into a vote, the ones who left into defectors, the ones who stayed into proof of loyalty. Trust becomes a statement of allegiance rather than something that can be tested.

Binance says it will absorb the loss rather than pass it on. It says it has covered about 95%, and the remaining 5% is less liquid tokens. Read that again: part of the $6.3 billion was never cash. The hackers took what could be taken; the rest of the hole is filled with "we will cover it." Who prices that coverage? The platform. Whose assets sit locked across 15 networks waiting for phased restoration? Users'.

The FBI attributes the attack to North Korean hackers, calling it TraderTraitor. A state-level actor is robbing an exchange while the exchange reassures the remainder with a statistic about who stayed. Put the two together and the core promise of crypto—your assets are yours—was never structurally true. You hand them to a centralized platform, the platform gets robbed, you wait for the door to open, and it tells you most people didn't leave. That is not decentralization. It is banking risk with casino diction.

Zhao also wrote: "Trust isn't earned by what you say in good times. It's earned by what you do in hard times." Correct. What it did in hard times, in order: reopen withdrawals, publish the net outflow, announce it would absorb the loss, then have the founder say most people stayed. The sequence is clear—let you run, then make running look rare, then make staying look right.

After $3.64 billion net out, how much of what remains is that 5% of illiquid tokens? The report doesn't say. Who carries that uncertainty? The report doesn't say. The only number offered for comfort is "98% choose to stay"—a line not aimed at the users who left, but at the 98% who haven't yet.
