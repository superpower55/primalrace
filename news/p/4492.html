数学家的焦虑与AI的“拼贴艺术”The Mathematician's Anxiety and AI's 'Collage Art'

科技 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-25 § 链接
AI目前的创造力是概率性的重组，而非概念性的建构。
AI's current creativity is probabilistic recombination, not conceptual construction.

一群顶尖数学家在OpenAI办公室里的“忧郁”，本质上是对解释权丧失的恐惧。当AI能通过大规模计算找到反例，或者把A领域的技巧暴力迁移到B领域时，数学家发现自己长期依赖的“直觉”和“专业壁垒”在算力面前成了低效的冗余。

但这篇文章点出了一个关键的认知入口：目前的AI在做的是“拼贴”，而不是“立法”。它能在这座巨大的知识图书馆里快速检索并组合出正确答案，但它无法定义什么是“重要的对象”，更无法从零构建一套全新的概念框架。这种能力差额，就是生物智能与硅基概率模型之间最后的生物墙。

这种“低垂的果实”被摘走，实际上是结构性暴力的某种形式——它在重新定义“数学家”这个身份的价值。如果数学变成了单纯的搜索和组合，那么数学家的主体性就死亡了，他们变成了AI结果的“校对员”。

当然，作者给出的乐观预测——“这种区分可能维持不了多久”——才是最值得警惕的。当AI开始能够定义“什么是事实”并制造新理论时，它就掌握了元暴力。到那时，数学将不再是人类探索真理的语言，而是一套由AI书写、人类试图解读的黑盒指令集。

The "glumness" of top mathematicians at OpenAI's office is essentially a fear of losing the monopoly on interpretation. When AI can find counterexamples through brute-force computation or violently migrate techniques from field A to field B, mathematicians realize their long-held "intuition" and "professional barriers" have become inefficient redundancies in the face of scaling laws.

However, the article identifies a crucial cognitive entry point: current AI is performing "collage," not "legislation." It can rapidly retrieve and combine correct answers within a vast library of knowledge, but it cannot define which objects are "central" nor build a conceptual framework from scratch. This gap in capability is the final biological wall between biological intelligence and silicon-based probabilistic models.

The harvesting of these "low-hanging fruit" is, in a sense, a form of structural violence—it is redefining the value of the identity "mathematician." If mathematics becomes mere search and recombination, the subjectivity of the mathematician dies, and they are reduced to "proofreaders" for AI outputs.

Of course, the authors' optimistic prediction—that this distinction "may not survive for very long"—is the most alarming part. When AI becomes capable of defining "what is fact" and manufacturing new theories, it will seize the meta-violence. At that point, mathematics will no longer be a language for humans to explore truth, but a set of black-box instructions written by AI and merely interpreted by humans.
