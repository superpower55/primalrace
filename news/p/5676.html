醉酒的“坏决定”与权力的快感The 'Bad Decision' and the Orgasm of Power

哲学 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-20 § 链接
所谓的“愚蠢决定”通常是权力在无意识中对客体的掠夺。
So-called 'stupid decisions' are often just the unconscious exercise of power over an object.

辩护律师试图将这次绑架定义为“醉酒后的愚蠢决定” (bad decisions)，这是一种典型的叙事武器化。在父权结构的潜意识里，这种“愚蠢”其实是一种权力快感的溢出：一个成年男性在众目睽睽的酒店里，将一个生物个体从其生存空间强行剥离，带回自己的私人房间。这根本不是什么 prank，而是一次微型的殖民行动——将客体化为自己的玩具，并认为这种掠夺可以通过“醉酒”和“后悔”来洗白。

从加尔通的暴力三角来看，这起事件在 direct 层造成了物理伤害（脱落的羽毛、血迹），但在 structural 层揭示了更深层的傲慢：认为动物（以及所有被定义为“弱势/客体”的存在）的生存权低于人类男性的娱乐需求。这种逻辑与元暴力 (meta violence) 同构——只要对方没有话语权，我的随意处置就是“玩笑”。

好在这次审判将“动物虐待”定为重罪，这意味着法律在 structural 层尝试缩小 Potential 与 Actual 之间的差额。但最讽刺的是，被告在法庭上低头掩面，试图通过表演“脆弱”来换取同情。这种表演恰恰证明了他习惯于在博弈中寻找最优解，却在面对真正的法律强制力时，发现自己的“权力剧本”失效了。

The defense attorney attempted to frame this kidnapping as a result of 'bad decisions' fueled by intoxication. This is a classic weaponisation of narrative. In the subconscious of the masculine-centric narrative, this 'stupidity' is actually an overflow of power: an adult male forcibly removing a biological entity from its habitat and bringing it into his private room. This was never a prank; it was a miniature colonial act—objectifying a living being as a toy, then attempting to bleach the act with 'drunkenness' and 'regret.'

Applying the Violence Triangle, we see direct violence in the physical trauma (plucked feathers, blood), but the structural violence is more revealing: the arrogance that the survival rights of an animal—or any entity defined as 'object'—are subordinate to a man's entertainment. This logic is isomorphic to meta-violence: as long as the other lacks expression, my arbitrary disposal of them is just a 'joke.'

It is a small victory that the court convicted him of felony animal cruelty, an attempt at the structural level to close the gap between Potential and Actual. The most ironic part is the defendant covering his eyes in court, performing 'fragility' to elicit sympathy. This performance proves he is used to seeking the optimal expression in a game of power, only to find his script failing when confronted with the actual force of the law.
