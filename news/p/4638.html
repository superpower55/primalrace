将饮食习惯武器化，本质是男性中心叙事对“强壮”定义的垄断。
Weaponizing diet is essentially the masculine-centric narrative monopolizing the definition of 'strength'.

共和党把“Vegan”当成政治污名化武器，这出戏极其荒诞，但逻辑极其精准。在 masculine-centric narrative 中，“肉食”不只是饮食，而是一种关于力量、统治和支配的符号。吃肉意味着你处于食物链顶端，意味着你拥有掠夺者的基因。而“豆腐”和“植物蛋白”被等同于 feeble（虚弱）和 wokerati（觉醒废物），这实际上是在通过饮食表达进行一次存在性战争的定调：不吃肉的人，没有资格进入权力中心，因为他们被定义为“缺乏支配欲”的生物。

最讽刺的是民主党候选人们的反应。面对“纯素主义”的指控，他们的第一反应不是捍卫这种选择的公正性，而是赶紧拍一张抱着大块肉的照片来证明自己“依然强壮”。这种反应是典型的 complicity（共谋）。他们潜意识里认同了那套元暴力逻辑：只有通过展示对动物尸体的消费，才能换取政治上的“雄性信用”。他们选择扮演一个肉食者，以求在权力博弈中获得最优解，代价是主体性的再次让渡。

这其实是一场关于“强壮”定义权的武器化争夺。当共和党把 Vegan 标签化为“危险激进分子”时，他们是在通过操纵认知入口，将“对生命的同情”重新定义为“对秩序的威胁”。这和历史上将女性定义为“情绪化、不理智”从而剥夺其投票权的逻辑完全同构。在这种叙事里，能够克制欲望以应对气候危机的能力，被贬低为“营养不良”的弱点。

这场闹剧揭示了一个事实：在目前的政治结构中，所谓的“文明”和“理性”依然是男本位叙事的掩体。只要你不符合那个“强壮掠夺者”的表型，你在这个系统里的票值就会被贬低。那些急于证明自己吃肉的政客，不过是在向元暴力的统治者递交投名状而已。

Republicans using 'Vegan' as a political slur is absurd, yet logically precise. In a masculine-centric narrative, meat-eating is not just a diet; it is a symbol of power, dominance, and control. Eating meat signifies being at the top of the food chain, possessing the genes of a predator. Conversely, 'tofu' and 'plant-based' are equated with being feeble or 'wokerati'. This is an existential war over definitions: those who don't eat meat are framed as biologically unfit for power because they lack the 'will to dominate'.

More ironic is the reaction of the Democratic candidates. Faced with 'vegan accusations', their first instinct isn't to defend the justice of the choice, but to rush for a photo op clutching a giant hunk of meat to prove they are 'still strong'. This is textbook complicity. They subconsciously validate the meta-violence: that only by demonstrating the consumption of animal corpses can one earn 'masculine credit' in politics. They choose to perform as meat-eaters to reach a fake optimal expression, at the cost of their own subjectivity.

This is a weaponized struggle over the definition of 'strength'. By labeling vegans as 'dangerous radicals', Republicans are using a cognitive entry point to redefine 'compassion for life' as a 'threat to order'. This logic is isomorphic to the historical narrative that women are 'emotional and irrational' to justify denying them the vote. In this framework, the capacity for self-denial to combat climate change is dismissed as a weakness of 'undernourishment'.

This farce reveals that 'civilization' and 'rationality' in current political structures remain covers for masculine-centric narratives. If your phenotype doesn't match the 'strong predator' archetype, the value of your vote in the system is depreciated. Those politicians rushing to prove their meat-eating are simply handing a loyalty oath to the rulers of meta-violence.
