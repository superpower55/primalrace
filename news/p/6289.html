谁在给非洲音乐排名Who Gets to Rank African Music

文娱 文化层 · 元暴力 Billboard ↗ 2026-11-01 § 链接
榜单不是记录，是定价权的一次行使
A list is not a record. It is an exercise of the power to price.

Billboard 这份「史上最伟大的 50 首 Afrobeats 歌曲」里，最值得读的不是歌名，是它开头那一段：2022 年 Billboard 自己开了美国 Afrobeats 榜，英国官方榜早两年开了，格莱美去年才设了最佳非洲音乐表演奖，MTV 和 AMA 更早一步。奖项和榜单先落地，然后才有「史上最佳」的资格。顺序不能反。

这就是第三章说的认知入口。一个流派先被邀请上「世界上最大的舞台」，和 Beyoncé、Drake、Ed Sheeran 合作，再被美国机构发榜、发奖、发排名——到这一步，谁有资格定义「这个流派的历史」就已经定了。榜单的评选标准写得清清楚楚：风格代表性、本地流行度、区域扩张、文化影响、商业成功。前四条都还可以商量，第五条是硬的。

再看名单本身。50 首里女性艺人的歌，我数得出来：Weird MC 的「Ijoya」、Libianca 的「People」、Ayra Starr 的「Rush」、Amaarae 和 MOLIY 的「Sad Girlz Luv Money」、Tiwa Savage 的「Eminado」、Yemi Alade 的「Johnny」。六首，其中两首是合作。而 Wizkid、Davido、Burna Boy 各自占满三首上限，Billboard 还专门解释了为什么设这个上限——为了「容纳更多艺人」，为了「捕捉音乐的广度」。广度是给男人的广度。

更有意思的是 Weird MC 那条。榜单写她「穿着宽松裤子、剃光头、带着嘻哈式的嚣张登场——对于在尼日利亚音乐里本来就是少数的女性来说，这新鲜得难以置信」。这是在夸她，也是在承认：一个女性要进入这份历史，先得看起来不像女人。表达即身份，第一章的老话。她的「激进」被记下来了，被记下来的方式是她的外表先于她的技艺。

第一名是 2Baba 的「African Queen」，2004 年的甜腻情歌，榜单说它是「写给这片大陆一代女性的情书」。一首男人赞美女人的歌，被放在「非洲节奏走向世界」的叙事顶端，作为这个流派的历史起点。这个位置给得很有讲究：非洲女性在这里是被赞美的客体，不是写歌的人。

榜单里唯一真正碰到代价的条目是第 48 名 Timaya 的「Dem Mama」，写 1999 年 Odi 的屠杀，军队的攻击夺走 30 条命。它排在第 48。而第 45 名 Olu Maintain 的「Yahooze」，榜单自己都承认歌词指向的是「yahoo boys」——尼日利亚人对网络诈骗者的称呼——「早期 Afrobeats 缺乏机构支持，骗子是急需现金的关键来源」。这句写得很坦白，坦白到像是无意间说漏了：这个流派早期的钱从哪来。

所以这份榜单真正排的不是歌，是一个流派被西方机构收编之后的自我叙述。它把「Afrobeats to the world」当成口号写在评选标准里，可这句话的主语从来不是世界，是那些终于被世界看见的人。至于谁一直在被看见、谁刚刚才被允许进入这份名单的第六个位置——榜单不负责回答。

The most revealing part of Billboard's 50 Best Afrobeats Songs of All Time is not the songs. It is the opening: Billboard launched its U.S. Afrobeats chart in 2022, the UK had its own two years earlier, the Recording Academy added a best African music performance category last year, MTV and the AMAs got there before that. The charts and the awards landed first. Only then did anyone have the standing to declare a canon.

That is the cognitive entrance from Chapter 3, in plain sight. A genre gets invited onto the world's biggest stages, collaborates with Beyoncé, Drake, Ed Sheeran, then gets ranked and awarded by American institutions — and by that point the question of who gets to define its history is already settled. The criteria are printed right there: stylistic representation, local popularity, regional expansion, cultural impact, commercial success. The first four are arguable. The fifth is hard.

Now count. Of the 50 songs, the women are Weird MC's "Ijoya," Libianca's "People," Ayra Starr's "Rush," Amaarae and MOLIY's "Sad Girlz Luv Money," Tiwa Savage's "Eminado," Yemi Alade's "Johnny." Six, two of them collaborations. Meanwhile Wizkid, Davido and Burna Boy each hit the three-song cap, which Billboard explains was set to "capture the breadth of the music" and "make room for more artists." The breadth is a male breadth.

The Weird MC entry is the tell. The list praises her for striding onstage in baggy pants and a shaved head with hip-hop bravado — "incredibly novel for women who were already the minority in Nigerian music." That is a compliment and a confession in the same sentence: to enter this history, a woman first had to stop looking like one. Expression is identity, Chapter 1. Her radicalism gets recorded, and the way it gets recorded puts her appearance ahead of her craft.

Number one is 2Baba's "African Queen," a 2004 ballad the list calls "a love letter to a generation of women from the continent." A man praising women sits at the top of the story of how Afrobeats went global, as its origin point. The placement is precise: African women appear here as the object of admiration, not as the ones writing the songs.

The only entry that touches an actual cost is No. 48, Timaya's "Dem Mama," about the 1999 Odi massacre, a military attack that cost 30 lives. It ranks 48th. No. 45, Olu Maintain's "Yahooze," is described by the list itself as pointing at the yahoo boys — and then admits that with little institutional support in early Afrobeats, "fraudsters were a critical source of much-needed cash." That line is candid enough to sound like a slip: where the genre's early money came from.

So the list is not ranking songs. It is ranking a genre's self-narration after Western institutions absorbed it. "Afrobeats to the world" is printed in the criteria as a rallying cry, but the subject of that sentence was never the world. It was the people the world finally agreed to see. As for who has been seen all along, and who just got admitted into the sixth slot — the list does not answer.
