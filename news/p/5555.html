用低端服务业的“阶梯”掩盖福利削减的暴力Low-End Service 'Ladders' as a Mask for Welfare Violence

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-14 § 链接
所谓的“就业阶梯”本质上是结构性暴力下的廉价劳动力收割。
The so-called 'employment ladder' is essentially a harvest of cheap labor under structural violence.

把 10 万个服务业岗位包装成给 NEET 青年的“第一步阶梯”，这套叙事简直是标准的 weaponized expression。在 Wagamama 和 Burger King 这种快餐连锁店里做四周的“实习”，能给一个失业青年带来什么？不是 skills，而是让他们习惯于在低端服务业中被客体化，习惯于在极低议价能力下出卖时间。这根本不是阶梯，而是一个巨大的漏斗，把结构性弱势者过滤成最廉价的耗材。

真正值得警惕的是这背后的 complicity。政府、快餐巨头和福利部门达成了一场共谋：用一个听起来很 a-nice 的“就业计划”作为掩体，掩盖其核心目的——削减福利金 (benefits bill)。新闻里明确提到，政府正在考虑削减 25 岁以下人群的残疾津贴。这意味着，如果你不能进入这个所谓的“阶梯”，你将失去生存底线。这是一种典型的 structural violence：通过制造一个极低门槛的“机会”，来证明那些依然无法就业的人是“不努力”，从而正当化剥夺他们的社会救济。

这种逻辑在元暴力 (meta violence) 的驱动下运行得极其顺畅。无论是工党还是保守党，他们争论的焦点不是如何解决青年失业的根源，而是如何通过“削减福利”来给国防预算腾空间。在他们的叙事里，年轻人的生存权被量化成了财政报表上的一个数字。所谓的“ brighter future”，其实就是让年轻人尽快地、低成本地进入劳动力市场，成为支撑服务业运转的生物电池。

Packaging 100,000 hospitality jobs as a 'first step' for NEET youth is a textbook example of weaponized expression. What does a four-week placement at Wagamama or Burger King actually offer? Not skills, but an initiation into being objectified in low-end services and accepting a total lack of bargaining power. This isn't a ladder; it's a funnel designed to filter the structurally disadvantaged into the cheapest possible consumables.

What's truly alarming is the complicity at play. The government, fast-food giants, and welfare departments have formed a pact: use a 'nice' employment scheme as a shield to mask the real objective—slashing the benefits bill. The report explicitly mentions considering the scrapping of critical disability benefits for under-25s. The message is clear: if you can't climb this fake ladder, you lose your safety net. This is structural violence in its purest form—creating a low-bar 'opportunity' to label those who still can't work as 'unmotivated,' thereby justifying the withdrawal of social support.

This logic operates seamlessly under meta violence. Whether it's Labour or the Conservatives, the debate isn't about the root causes of youth unemployment, but about how to 'cut welfare' to fund defense spending. In their narrative, the survival rights of young people are reduced to figures on a fiscal balance sheet. The promised 'brighter future' is simply the low-cost integration of youth into the labor market to serve as biological batteries for the service industry.
