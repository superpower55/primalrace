Epstein 的‘模特册’：被物化的原初种族存档Epstein’s ‘Modeling Book’: Archiving the Primal Race

性别 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-17 § 链接
CSAM 不是色情，是权力对生物墙的暴力标记与存档。
CSAM is not pornography; it is the violent marking and archiving of the biological wall by power.

Epstein 的‘模特册’ (modeling book) 是一个极其精准的 meta violence 样本。在男本位叙事中，‘模特’意味着被凝视的客体，而当这个词被用来描述被盗窃、被非法拍摄的儿童图像时，它揭露了权力如何通过将生物学上的弱势（儿童/女性）定义为‘产品’，从而完成从直接暴力到结构暴力的闭环。

这不仅仅是几个个案的受害，而是一场关于‘解释权’的战争。Epstein 的律师和会计师在诉讼中辩称‘不知情’，这正是典型的共谋者 (complicity) 逻辑：在权力金字塔中，只要不直接参与施暴，通过维持这个暴力机器的运转（财务管理、法律掩护）来获取利益，就被视为‘中立’。但事实上，这种中立就是对元暴力的最大支撑。

最令人作呕的是，即便在有举报的情况下，执法部门依然选择了不作为。这意味着 structural violence 已经将这类针对原初种族的掠夺合法化为一种‘特权阶级的游戏’。当受害者的身体被数字化为 CSAM 并跨州运输时，她们的身份被剥夺，只剩下被定价的肉体碎片。

这次诉讼追求的‘通知’和‘赔偿’是 Actual 向 Potential 靠近的一小步，但真正的刺在于：在这个被男性中心叙事垄断的系统中，有多少类似的‘存档’依然在权力的保险柜里被当作社交筹码流通？如果只有 Epstein 一个人被钉在耻辱柱上，而他的共谋网络依然在定义什么是‘权力’，那么这场胜利仅仅是表演性的让步。

Epstein’s ‘modeling book’ is a precise specimen of meta-violence. In a masculine-centric narrative, a ‘model’ is a gazed-upon object. When this term is applied to stolen, illegally captured images of children, it reveals how power transforms biological vulnerability into a ‘product,’ completing the loop from direct to structural violence.

This is more than a few isolated cases; it is a war over the right of interpretation. Epstein’s former lawyer and accountant claim ‘no knowledge’—a classic logic of complicity. In the power pyramid, as long as one doesn't directly commit the act, maintaining the machine's operation (financials, legal cover) for profit is framed as ‘neutrality.’ In reality, this neutrality is the ultimate support for meta-violence.

Most sickening is that law enforcement remained idle despite contemporaneous reports. This proves that structural violence has legitimized the predation of the Primal Race as a ‘privilege of the elite.’ When a victim's body is digitized into CSAM and transported across state lines, their identity is erased, leaving only priced fragments of flesh.

Seeking ‘notification’ and ‘damages’ is a small step of Actual moving toward Potential. But the real thorn remains: in a system monopolized by masculine-centric narratives, how many similar ‘archives’ are still circulating as social chips in the safes of power? If only Epstein is nailed to the cross while his network of complicity continues to define ‘power,’ this victory is merely a performative concession.
