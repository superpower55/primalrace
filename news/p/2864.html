进口的末日想象与叙事武器的同步率Imported Doomsday Visions and the Synchronization of Narrative Weapons

国际 文化层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-25 § 链接
极右翼不生产恐惧，他们只是跨国搬运恐惧的共谋者。
The far-right doesn't produce fear; they are merely complicit transporters of transnational dread.

这场所谓的“文明更新”本质上是一次大规模的叙事武器化实验。保林·汉森和汤米·罗宾逊的会面，不是两个政治人物的交流，而是两套关于“末日”的认知入口在进行同步。他们通过制造一个被异化的、恐怖的“他者”形象（无论是以往的亚裔还是现在的穆斯林），来夺取对“事实”的解释权。在这种叙事中，复杂的社会经济崩溃被简化为单一的生物学入侵，而这种简化正是为了掩盖那些真正通过 unregulated markets 榨干底层的结构性暴力。

最令人作呕的是这种“共谋”的逻辑：极右翼政客通过扮演受害者和殉道者，将自己的欺诈或犯罪记录洗白为“为了真理而承受的代价”。这是一种典型的假.最优解表达——通过扮演一个被体制迫害的英雄，来换取底层焦虑者的信任。他们把英国的焦虑像插件一样安装到澳大利亚的政治语境中，试图用一套标准化的“文明崩溃”模版，来抹除澳大利亚本就具有的、基于日常体面（decency）的多元共存可能性。

这种跨国搬运的恐惧，其实是元暴力的一种延伸。它通过定义谁是“原初的”核心文化，将所有不符合该定义的人口标记为“稀释剂”或“病毒”。当汉森说出“他们在繁殖”这种极具生物学攻击性的词汇时，她实际上是在激活一种最原始的种族排斥机制。这不再是政治讨论，而是一场关于谁拥有生存权的、被武器化的存在性战争。而那些在早餐电视节目中把“大规模驱逐”描述为“珠宝”一样方案的共谋者们，正在将这种文化暴力转化为具体的结构性威胁。

This so-called 'renewal of civilization' is essentially a large-scale experiment in the weaponisation of expression. The meeting between Pauline Hanson and Tommy Robinson is not a dialogue between politicians, but a synchronization of two cognitive portals of 'doomsday'. By manufacturing a distorted, terrifying 'Other'—whether Asians in the past or Muslims now—they attempt to seize the power of interpretation over 'facts'. In this narrative, complex socio-economic collapses are reduced to a simple biological invasion, a reduction specifically designed to mask the structural violence of unregulated markets that bleed the working class dry.

Most repulsive is the logic of this complicity: far-right politicians play the role of martyrs and victims to whitewash their records of fraud or crime as 'prices paid for truth'. This is a classic fake optimal expression—playing the persecuted hero to gain the trust of an anxious underclass. They install British anxieties into the Australian political context like a software plugin, attempting to erase Australia's inherent capacity for multicultural coexistence built on ordinary acts of decency.

This transnational transport of fear is an extension of meta-violence. By defining who constitutes the 'original' core culture, they mark all non-conforming populations as 'dilutants' or 'viruses'. When Hanson remarks that 'they breed,' she is activating a primal racial exclusion mechanism. This is no longer political discourse; it is a weaponized existential war over who is permitted to exist. And the complicitors who describe 'mass deportations' as a 'jewel' on breakfast television are actively converting cultural violence into a concrete structural threat.
