导弹的“无人机化”与死神的量产逻辑The 'Drone-ification' of Missiles and the Mass Production of Death

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-07 § 链接
战争的“效率”提升，本质是直接暴力在结构层面的量产化。
The 'efficiency' of war is the mass-production of direct violence at a structural level.

泽连斯基提出要用“制造无人机的方式”来制造导弹。这句话翻译成大白话就是：我们要把这种昂贵的、需要数十年研发的战略武器，变成一种快速迭代、低成本、大规模量产的消耗品。在军事上这叫“非对称作战”，但在暴力三角（Violence Triangle）的视角下，这是一种极其危险的 structural violence 升级——它在尝试将“大规模杀伤”这一行为，从精英化的工业产品转化为快消品。

最令人作呕的讽刺在于，当基辅在讨论如何加速导弹量产以追求“具体结果”时，联合国（UN）在扮演一个完美的共谋者。法尔汉·哈克用一种典型的、看似中立的 masculine-centric narrative 宣布：秘书长谴责俄罗斯攻击平民，同时也谴责乌克兰攻击俄罗斯。这种所谓的“平衡报道”是典型的 meta violence。它通过将“侵略者的屠杀”与“被侵略者的反击”在道德量级上强行等同，抹杀了结构性的权力差，从而在文化层（cultural layer）上为持续的暴力提供了“客观”的掩护。

一个细节极具代表性：俄罗斯将杀害平民的行为定义为“攻击军事设施”，而将乌克兰攻击其经济目标定义为“攻击平民”。这就是第三章提到的“表达的武器化”：谁掌握了定义权，谁就掌握了事实的制造权。当死亡数字在 UN 的报告中变成 37% 或 114% 的增长率时，这些数字已经成了某种战争博弈的 KPI。

战争从来不是关于“解放”或“正义”的，它是最高等级的存在性战争。当导弹被“无人机化”，意味着杀戮的门槛被进一步降低，而 Potential − Actual 的差额被暴力机器填满。在这种逻辑下，唯一真实的只有那些被炸毁的油库和死在港口的船员。至于所谓的“人道主义”，不过是量产导弹间隙用来润滑舆论的 PR 词汇。

Zelenskyy wants to build missiles 'the way we built drones.' In plain language: turn strategic weapons, which usually take decades to develop, into fast-iterating, low-cost, mass-produced consumables. Militarily, this is 'asymmetric warfare.' From the perspective of the Violence Triangle, it is a dangerous escalation of structural violence—an attempt to transform 'mass destruction' from an elite industrial product into a fast-moving consumer good.

The most sickening irony is the UN's role as a perfect complicit actor. Farhan Haq employs a typical masculine-centric narrative, claiming the Secretary-General condemns both Russian strikes on civilians and Ukrainian strikes on Russia. This so-called 'balanced reporting' is pure meta violence. By equating the slaughter of an aggressor with the counter-strikes of the invaded, it erases structural power imbalances and provides a 'neutral' cultural cover for ongoing violence.

A telling detail: Russia defines the killing of civilians as 'targeting military facilities,' while portraying Ukrainian strikes on economic targets as 'aimed at civilians.' This is the weaponisation of expression: whoever controls the definition controls the manufacture of facts. When death tolls in UN reports become growth rates of 37% or 114%, these numbers become mere KPIs in a geopolitical game.

War is never about 'liberation' or 'justice'; it is the ultimate existential war. When missiles are 'drone-ified,' the threshold for killing is lowered, and the gap in the Violence Triangle is filled by the war machine. In this logic, the only truths are the destroyed refineries and the dead crewmen. 'Humanitarianism' is just a PR lubricant used between the production cycles of mass-produced missiles.
