国家安全是所有认知入口的万能遮羞布National Security: The Universal Cloak for Cognitive Entry

中国科技 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-12 § 链接
当能力无法竞争时，就通过重新定义“风险”来制造禁区。
When capacity fails to compete, redefine 'risk' to manufacture a forbidden zone.

美国 FCC 禁掉 Unitree G1 这类人形机器人，给出的理由依然是那套陈词滥调：National Security。在国际政治的 weaponized 叙事里，“国家安全”是一个完美的黑盒，它可以把任何竞争劣势包装成一种正义的防御。本质上，这不过是一场关于“定义权”的博弈。

当中国机器人在成本、迭代速度和实际表现上形成某种 Potential 压制时，美国政府面对的不是技术问题，而是存在性战争的危机。如果无法在 Actual 层面通过创新赢回来，最快捷的路径就是通过 Structural violence——利用行政权力直接切断认知入口和市场通路，将对方定义为“风险”。

这种逻辑在科技领域极其通用：无法在 $\tau$-scaling 层面超越，就宣布对方的定律是不可信的；无法在商业上竞争，就宣布对方是间谍工具。这是一种典型的“现实不配合就重新定义现实”的机制。它不关心机器人是否真的在窃听，它关心的是如何通过制造恐惧，让美国市场在潜意识里完成一次对中国科技的“异类化”筛选。

这场禁令实际上是共谋者的集体表演。政府提供禁令，媒体提供“风险”解释，企业获得保护屏障。他们共同构建了一套叙事，试图让美国消费者相信，购买一个会跳舞的机器人等于在给潜在的敌人递钥匙。但这套 scam 的代价是，它在结构层面上扼杀了真正的技术碰撞，用行政手段强行维持一个虚假的、滞后的“安全”现状。

The FCC's ban on humanoid robots like the Unitree G1 is just another iteration of the 'National Security' trope. In the weaponized narrative of international politics, 'national security' is a perfect black box used to disguise competitive inferiority as a righteous defense. At its core, this is a game for the power of definition.

When Chinese robotics achieve a certain Potential dominance in cost and iteration speed, the US government faces an existential war. If they cannot win in the Actual plane through innovation, the fastest route is Structural violence—using administrative power to sever cognitive entries and market access by labeling the opponent as a 'risk.'

This logic is ubiquitous in tech: if you can't beat them at $\tau$-scaling, declare their laws unreliable; if you can't compete commercially, label them as spy tools. It is the mechanism of 'redefining reality when reality doesn't cooperate.' It doesn't matter if the robots are actually eavesdropping; what matters is manufacturing fear to ensure the US market completes a subconscious 'othering' of Chinese tech.

This ban is a performance by complicity. The government provides the decree, the media provides the 'risk' narrative, and domestic firms gain a protective barrier. Together, they build a scam convincing consumers that a dancing robot is a key handed to an enemy. The price of this scam is the structural strangulation of genuine technical collision, using administrative force to maintain a fake, lagging 'security' status quo.
