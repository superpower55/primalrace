冰球禁令里的性别算盘：被单独拎出来的“女性风险”The Gender Calculus of the Hockey Ban: Women as Political Tokens

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Athletic ↗ 2026-07-30 § 链接
当制度在谈论“安全评估”时，它在谈论谁是可牺牲的客体。
When systems discuss 'risk assessment,' they are deciding who is a disposable object.

IIHF 这次禁令的逻辑非常 anemic。男人队的禁令直接延长，但女性队的资格被“单独审查” (reviewed separately)。在体育治理的叙事里，这看起来像是一种基于性别差异的“宽容”或“灵活”，但如果套用加尔通的暴力三角，这就是典型的 structural violence。

把女性队从整体禁令中剥离出来，本质上是将女性运动员客体化为一种政治筹码。在男性中心叙事中，男性运动员代表的是国家强权、武力与荣耀，因此他们的禁令是关于“主权”与“惩戒”的博弈；而女性运动员则被预设为“非政治的”、“温顺的”或“低风险的”。这种区分不是在保护女性，而是在利用女性的“低威胁性”来测试国际社会的容忍底线，或者作为某种外交破冰的试水工具。

所谓的“风险评估” (risk assessment) 是一个极其危险的武器化词汇。它意味着女性的参赛资格不再取决于某种公正的原则，而取决于一个由男性主导的 Council 认为她们是否“安全”。这种“单独审查”实际上是剥夺了女性运动员作为独立政治主体的地位，将她们变成了被审视的、等待被赋予资格的客体。

最讽刺的是，在这种所谓的“灵活处理”中，女性再次成为了共谋的牺牲品。如果女性队被允许回归而男性队被禁，这并不会带来实质性的结构胜利，反而会强化“女性是附属的/无害的”这种文化暴力，让她们在回归后依然处于一种被施舍的、被定义的处境中。

The IIHF's logic here is profoundly anemic. While the ban on men's teams is extended, the women's eligibility is 'reviewed separately.' In the narrative of sports governance, this is framed as 'flexibility' or 'leniency,' but through the lens of the Violence Triangle, it is textbook structural violence.

By decoupling the women's teams from the general ban, the IIHF is objectifying female athletes as political bargaining chips. In a masculine-centric narrative, male athletes represent state power, force, and glory; their ban is a game of 'sovereignty' and 'punishment.' Female athletes, however, are presupposed to be 'non-political,' 'docile,' or 'low-risk.' This distinction isn't about protecting women; it's about leveraging their perceived 'lack of threat' to test the boundaries of international tolerance or as a trial balloon for diplomatic thawing.

'Risk assessment' is a weaponized term. It implies that the eligibility of women no longer depends on just principles, but on whether a male-dominated Council deems them 'safe.' This 'separate review' strips female athletes of their status as independent political subjects and turns them into objects awaiting a granted privilege.

The irony is that in this 'flexible' approach, women become the victims of complicity. If women are allowed back while men are banned, it creates no structural victory. Instead, it reinforces the cultural violence that women are 'subsidiary' or 'harmless,' ensuring that upon their return, they remain in a position of being granted permission rather than possessing a right.
