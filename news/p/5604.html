用“法律”恐吓选举官员：一场低劣的权力敲诈Threatening Election Officials: A Crude Power Shakedown

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-17 § 链接
当事实不足以支撑权力时，威胁就成了唯一的表达工具。
When facts fail to sustain power, threats become the only tool of expression.

这出戏的逻辑极其粗糙：联邦政府拿出一份所谓的“非公民名单”，在没有提供任何实质证据的情况下，直接跳到最高级别的威胁——刑事起诉。这根本不是在维护法律，而是一次典型的 weaponized 表达。通过将选举官员定义为“共谋者” (aiders and abettors)，特朗普政府试图在选举前制造一种恐怖氛围，强迫基层官员在恐惧中自我审查，从而在 structural 层面上剔除那些可能不利于自己的票数。

最荒诞的是数据上的极度不对称。在 2.27 亿注册选民中，被指控的非公民投票仅 50 人，这个概率低到可以忽略不计。但联邦政府却用这 0.00002% 的极低概率，去威胁 2300 万加州选民的表达权。这种“抓典型”的叙事目的只有一个：通过制造一个虚假的“安全危机”，夺取对选举合法性的解释权。这就是典型的元暴力 (meta violence) 运作——先定义一个“敌人”（非公民投票者），再将所有不配合的体制内人员标记为“敌人的共谋”。

这种恐吓行为本质上是一场存在性战争。特朗普政府在意识到无法通过合法程序影响结果时，选择直接攻击选举的执行端。他们试图把选举官员变成他们权力意志的延伸。但好在，法律在此时成了防守方的生物墙。当这种 bullying 失去了事实支撑，它就变成了一场拙劣的 scam。这场博弈的胜负不在于谁的声音更大，而在于谁能守住事实的底线。

The logic here is embarrassingly crude: the federal government produces a so-called "noncitizen list" and, without providing a shred of evidence, jumps straight to the highest level of threat—criminal prosecution. This isn't about upholding the law; it is a textbook case of weaponized expression. By labeling election officials as "aiders and abettors," the Trump administration is attempting to manufacture a climate of terror, forcing local officials into self-censorship and effectively purging unfavorable votes at the structural level.

The data asymmetry is the most absurd part. Out of 227 million registered voters, only about 50 were alleged noncitizen voters—a probability so low it's statistically irrelevant. Yet, the federal government uses this 0.00002% anomaly to threaten the expression of 23 million Californians. This "cherry-picking" narrative serves one purpose: to manufacture a fake "security crisis" and seize the power to define electoral legitimacy. This is meta violence in action—first define an "enemy" (the noncitizen voter), then mark all non-compliant officials as complicity in that enemy's crime.

This intimidation is an existential war. Realizing they cannot influence the outcome through legal channels, the administration chooses to attack the execution end of the process. They want to turn election officials into extensions of their own will. Fortunately, the law serves as a biological wall for the defense. When bullying is stripped of its factual basis, it reveals itself as a pathetic scam. The winner of this game isn't whoever screams loudest, but whoever holds the line of fact.
