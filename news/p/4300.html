用“社会建构”掩盖生物墙：一场极其卑劣的叙事武器化The 'Social Construct' Scam: Weaponizing Narratives Against Biological Walls

哲学 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-20 § 链接
将生物事实定义为“社会建构”是典型的认知掠夺，旨在剥夺弱势者的救济权。
Defining biological facts as 'social constructs' is cognitive predation designed to strip the vulnerable of their right to relief.

Channel 4 这次玩了一把非常典型的 weaponization。他们把 ADHD 定义为“social construct”，听起来像是进步派在挑战医疗霸权，试图让学校适应孩子而非让孩子适应规范，但本质上这是在通过夺取解释权来抹除生物事实。对于神经多样性群体来说，ADHD 不是某种文化标签，而是实实在在的生物墙——大脑前额叶皮层的功能差异直接决定了他们与世界的博弈成本。当你把这种生理差异定义为“神话”或“建构”时，你实际上是在实施一种 cultural violence：你告诉那些在痛苦中挣扎的人，你们的痛苦是假的，你们需要的药是枷锁。

最令人作呕的是节目组对“实验”的操弄。他们让一个 10 岁孩子停药，同时移除了屏幕、垃圾食品，增加了自然接触和家庭互动。这在科学上叫 confounding factors（混杂变量）。他们用一套“生活方式的改良”掩盖了“药物治疗”的缺失，然后把结果归功于“ADHD 是神话”。这种 cherry-picking 的叙事逻辑极其阴险，它在诱导家长为了某种虚幻的“自然主义”而剥夺孩子通过药物获得情绪控制能力的权利。这种权利的丧失，直接导致了 Actual 状态与 Potential 状态之间差额的扩大，而这个差额，就是暴力。

Channel 4 的回应更是典型的共谋者姿态。他们用“激发公共讨论”和“挑战现状”作为掩体，将医学上的少数派观点伪装成对等的学术争论。但医学事实不是投票决定的，而是由数据决定的。当 94% 的受访者感到 stigma 增加时，这个节目已经完成了它的目标：它不是在挑战现状，而是在制造一种新的、更高级的元暴力——通过定义“什么是真实”，让那些依赖药物生存的人在社会认知中被进一步客体化和边缘化。

Channel 4 has executed a textbook case of weaponisation. By framing ADHD as a 'social construct,' they masquerade as progressives challenging medical hegemony, but in reality, they are attempting to erase biological facts by seizing the power of interpretation. For the neurodivergent, ADHD is not a cultural label; it is a biological wall—functional differences in the prefrontal cortex that dictate the existential cost of interacting with the world. When you define this physiological reality as a 'myth' or a 'construct,' you are deploying cultural violence: telling those in agony that their suffering is illusory and their medication is a shackle.

The most repulsive part is the manipulation of the 'experiment.' They took a 10-year-old off medication while simultaneously removing screens and junk food and adding nature and family interaction. In science, these are confounding factors. By using 'lifestyle improvements' to mask the absence of 'medical treatment,' they framed the result as proof that 'ADHD is a myth.' This cherry-picking narrative is insidious; it lures parents into stripping their children of the ability to regulate emotions for the sake of a romanticized 'naturalism.' This loss of agency widens the gap between Actual and Potential states—and that gap is the definition of violence.

Channel 4's response is a classic posture of complicity. They use 'stimulating public debate' and 'challenging the status quo' as bunkers to disguise a minority medical fringe as a balanced academic debate. But medical facts are not decided by votes; they are decided by data. When 94% of respondents report increased stigma, the programme has achieved its goal: it is not challenging the status quo, but manufacturing a new, higher form of meta-violence—objectifying and marginalizing those dependent on medication by redefining 'what is real.'
