废墟下的死亡并非自然灾害，而是结构性谋杀Death Under Rubble is Structural Murder, Not a Natural Disaster

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-11 § 链接
自然灾害是触发点，但死亡人数由结构性暴力决定。
Natural disasters are triggers; death tolls are determined by structural violence.

地震是地质运动，但建筑物坍塌是政治决定。111人死亡，61栋建筑像积木一样垮掉，这在 2026 年的今天绝不是什么“不可抗力”。当总统在电视上表演“心碎” (heart aches) 时，他掩盖的是一个简单的事实：那些坍塌的旧建筑，是长期以来监管缺位、资源分配不均以及对底层生存空间漠视的物理结果。

这就是典型的 structural violence。在 Cali 和 Pereira 的街头，坍塌的不仅是水泥，更是对公共安全承诺的背弃。为什么在地震发生时，某些阶层住在抗震标准达标的堡垒里，而另一些人则在随时可能成为棺材的旧楼中醒来？这种生存概率的差异，就是 Violence = Potential − Actual 的具体量化。人们本可以活下来，但制度将他们推向了死亡。

最讽刺的是，新任右翼总统在宣布紧急状态的同时，迅速部署警察和军队应对“抢劫威胁”。在救援尚未覆盖所有废墟时，国家机器的第一反应不是如何高效挖掘幸存者，而是如何防止底层在绝望中抢夺生存资源。这种对“秩序”的执念远高于对“生命”的尊重，揭示了权力结构中根深蒂固的逻辑：保护资产的优先级永远高于救赎肉体。

至于美国政府那 1550 万美元的紧急援助，不过是一场政治投喂。在支持右翼总统的政治交易中，这笔钱是用来购买地缘忠诚的筹码，而非为了填补那些被结构性暴力挖开的坟墓。这种援助是表演性的，它不能修复一个已经腐烂的建筑监管体系，只能在镜头前完成一次人道主义的 PR 闭环。

An earthquake is a geological event, but a building collapse is a political decision. 111 dead and 61 buildings crumbling like toy blocks in 2026 is never an "act of God." While the president performs his "aching heart" on national TV, he obscures a simple truth: those collapsed ruins are the physical manifestation of long-term regulatory failure, systemic resource maldistribution, and a profound indifference toward the living spaces of the marginalized.

This is textbook structural violence. In the streets of Cali and Pereira, what collapsed was not just concrete, but the very promise of public safety. Why do some classes wake up in earthquake-proof fortresses while others awaken in old tenements that function as pre-fabricated coffins? The disparity in survival probability is the precise quantification of Violence = Potential − Actual. People could have lived, but the system ensured they died.

Most cynical of all is the new right-wing president's immediate deployment of police and military to counter "looting threats" alongside the emergency declaration. Before rescue efforts could even reach all the ruins, the state's primary instinct was not the efficient extraction of survivors, but the prevention of the desperate seizing survival resources. This obsession with "order" over "life" reveals a deep-seated logic of power: protecting assets always takes precedence over redeeming flesh.

As for the $15.5 million in emergency aid from the US government, it is nothing more than political feeding. In a transaction to secure a right-wing ally, this money is a chip bought for geopolitical loyalty, not a means to fill graves dug by structural violence. This aid is performative; it cannot repair a rotten building regulatory system, it only completes a humanitarian PR loop for the cameras.
