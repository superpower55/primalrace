沙丁鱼的‘贵族化’：一场关于审美定价权的认知诈骗The 'Aristocratization' of Sardines: A Cognitive Scam of Aesthetic Pricing Power

哲学 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-25 § 链接
当底层生存物资被包装成‘核心审美’，其本质是权力对定义的掠夺。
When survival staples are repackaged as 'core aesthetics,' it is the seizure of definition by power.

从 cheap staple 到 luxury item，沙丁鱼的阶级跃升不需要产品升级，只需要一个 hashtag。#SardineGirlSummer 和 sardinecore 的出现，是典型的表达武器化：通过将一种廉价的生物性生存物资重新定义为‘健康’、‘时尚’和‘核心审美’，权势阶层成功夺取了这件商品的定价权。

这是一场教科书式的认知入口操纵。Influencers 并不在乎沙丁鱼本身，他们在乎的是通过定义‘什么是酷的’来筛选阵营。当‘sardinemaxxing’成为一种身份标签，底层民众原本廉价的蛋白质来源被抽离出原有的生存语境，被贴上‘高级’的标签重新卖回给大众。这不是消费升级，而是一次精准的文化殖民。

最讽刺的共谋发生在生态崩溃的背景下。摩洛哥的鱼类资源在 collapse，而社交媒体上的‘审美狂欢’却在加速需求。资本与网红共谋，用一种名为‘可持续’的伪装（sustainability PR）来掩盖对资源的掠夺。在这种叙事里，当地渔民的生存危机被简化为视频中的‘背景板’，而真正的获利者是那些掌控了认知入口、能够随手定义‘流行’的权力中枢。

这种‘审美化’的暴力在于，它让人们在追求‘松弛感’和‘核心审美’的同时，心甘情愿地为资本的定义权买单，而完全忽视了 Actual 状态下资源的枯竭与分配的不公。

From a cheap staple to a luxury item, the class ascent of the sardine requires no product upgrade—only a hashtag. #SardineGirlSummer and sardinecore are textbook examples of the weaponisation of expression: by redefining a cheap biological necessity as 'healthy,' 'fashionable,' and a 'core aesthetic,' the power class has successfully seized the pricing power of the commodity.

This is a textbook manipulation of cognitive entry points. Influencers don't care about the fish; they care about filtering their camp by defining 'what is cool.' When 'sardinemaxxing' becomes an identity tag, the cheap protein source of the lower class is stripped of its survival context and sold back to the masses with a 'premium' label. This isn't a consumption upgrade; it's a precise cultural colonisation.

The most cynical complicity occurs against a backdrop of ecological collapse. While Morocco's sardine stocks are collapsing, the 'aesthetic carnival' on social media accelerates demand. Capital and influencers conspire, using a facade of 'sustainability' (sustainability PR) to mask the plunder of resources. In this narrative, the survival crisis of local fishermen is reduced to a 'background' in a video, while the true profiteers are the power centers who control the cognitive entry points and can define 'trends' at will.

The violence of this 'aestheticization' lies in the fact that it makes people pay for the definition power of capital while pursuing 'effortless chic,' all while remaining blissfully ignorant of the collapse of resources and the injustice of distribution in Actual terms.
