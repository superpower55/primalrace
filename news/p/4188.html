慈善是结构性暴力的止痛药Charity as a Painkiller for Structural Violence

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-19 § 链接
用“社区精神”填补预算缺口，是典型的用文化共谋掩盖结构失能。
Filling budget gaps with 'community spirit' is a classic move of using cultural complicity to mask structural failure.

首相 Andy Burnham 的这套叙事非常经典：先抛出一个宏大的、具有道德光环的目标（圣诞节前清空街头流浪者），然后迅速在预算不足时，把责任从 government 转移到 community 和 faith sector。这在逻辑上叫“号召”，在本质上叫“甩锅”。

根据加尔通的暴力三角，流浪者在街头生存的状态是典型的 structural violence。在这个系统里，住房资源分配的失衡、社会福利的削减是导致 Potential 与 Actual 之间产生巨大差额的暴力源。而 Burnham 试图通过调用“社区精神”和“志愿组织”来弥补这个差额，实际上是在用 cultural layer 的温情叙事，去修补 structural layer 的漏洞。这是一种危险的 scam：它让公众误以为流浪问题可以通过“大家心往一处想”来解决，从而掩盖了政府在资源配置上的失能。

最讽刺的是，他把疫情期间的成功作为背书。疫情期间能把人弄进室内，是因为那时“流浪”被定义为公共卫生威胁，系统启动了最高级别的强制力。而现在，他希望在没有同等强制力的情况下，靠志愿者的善意来达成同样的效果。这种对“共谋”的依赖，实际上是在利用社会底层和慈善组织的无偿劳动，来为政府的预算不足做 PR。这种“好新闻”的表象下，是结构性暴力在继续运作，而慈善成了最好的止痛药。

PM Andy Burnham's narrative is a textbook case: set a grand, morally charged goal (clearing rough sleepers by Christmas), then swiftly shift the burden from government to the community and faith sector when the budget falls short. In political terms, it's a 'rallying call'; in reality, it's a strategic dump.

Applying Galtung's Violence Triangle, rough sleeping is a manifestation of structural violence. The gap between Potential and Actual is carved by systemic failures in housing allocation and austerity. By invoking 'community spirit' to fill this gap, Burnham uses a narrative in the cultural layer to patch a hole in the structural layer. This is a scam: it tricks the public into believing homelessness can be solved by 'pulling in the same direction,' thereby obscuring the government's failure in resource distribution.

The irony lies in his reference to the pandemic. During COVID-19, rough sleepers were moved indoors because homelessness was redefined as a public health threat, triggering maximum state power. Now, he hopes to achieve the same result through the goodwill of volunteers without that power. This reliance on complicity is essentially exploiting the unpaid labor of the marginalized and charities to perform PR for a bankrupt budget. Under the guise of 'good news,' structural violence continues, and charity serves as the perfect painkiller.
