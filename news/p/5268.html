进步主义的拼贴画与权力的叙事修补The Progressive Collage and the Patchwork of Power

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-26 § 链接
所谓的“重建联盟”不过是权力在失去解释权后的叙事修补。
The so-called 'reuniting the coalition' is merely a narrative repair after losing the power of definition.

Lucy Powell 谈论的“重新统一进步派选民联盟” (reunite the progressive voter coalition)，在政治修辞里叫战略，在存在性战争里叫“补票”。工党在 Starmer 时代陷入了典型的“技术主义陷阱”——试图用中立、客观、理性的管理逻辑来掩盖主体性的缺失。当一个政党变得“太技术化且悲观”时，它实际上是放弃了对现实的定义权，沦为了一个单纯的行政执行机构，而这恰恰给 Reform UK 这种擅长制造简单、粗暴、具有攻击性叙事的右翼留下了巨大的认知入口。

有趣的是，Powell 试图通过在学校推广“反厌女” (anti-misogyny) 和打击 deepfakes 来重新夺回进步主义的旗帜。但这恰恰暴露了结构性暴力的逻辑：将“厌女”简化为一套教学大纲或一项政策指标，试图通过“嵌入健康关系”来解决问题。正如她自己承认的，不能仅靠教学走出厌女。因为厌女不是知识匮乏，而是元暴力 (meta violence) 的具体表现——它是男性中心叙事在文化层面的根基。如果工党的权力结构本身依然是男性中心化的，那么这种在学校里的“反厌女”就成了一场表演性的让步，是用 cultural layer 的微调来掩盖 structural layer 的僵化。

所谓的“进步联盟”，本质上是一场关于“谁在定义进步”的博弈。当工党在养老金、福利等生存底线问题上犹豫不决时，它实际上是在与既有的权力结构共谋 (complicity)。现在发现选票流失，于是赶紧地把“价值观”重新包装成武器。这种从“技术管理”到“故事讲述”的切换，不是价值观的回归，而是发现“技术逻辑”在存在性战争中输给了“情绪逻辑”后的战术调整。他们不是想成为进步的领导者，他们只是想赢回那张入场券。

Lucy Powell’s talk of 'reuniting the progressive voter coalition' is called strategy in political rhetoric, but in the existential war, it is simply 'collecting missing votes.' Labour, in the Starmer era, fell into a classic 'technocratic trap'—attempting to mask a loss of subjectivity with a logic of neutrality, objectivity, and rationality. When a party becomes 'too technical and pessimistic,' it effectively abdicates its power to define reality, becoming a mere administrative executor. This created a massive cognitive entry point for the right, like Reform UK, who excel at manufacturing simple, crude, and aggressive narratives.

Interestingly, Powell attempts to reclaim the progressive banner by promoting 'anti-misogyny' work and tackling deepfakes in schools. This precisely exposes the logic of structural violence: simplifying misogyny into a curriculum or a policy metric, attempting to solve it through 'embedding healthy relationships.' As she admits, you cannot simply teach your way out of misogyny. Misogyny is not a lack of knowledge; it is a manifestation of meta violence—the very foundation of the masculine-centric narrative at the cultural layer. If Labour's own power structure remains masculine-centric, then this 'anti-misogyny' in schools is a performative concession, using a cultural layer tweak to hide structural layer rigidity.

The so-called 'progressive alliance' is essentially a game of 'who defines progress.' When Labour hesitated on survival baselines like pensions and welfare, it was in complicity with the existing power structure. Now that they see the polls dropping, they are hurriedly repackaging 'values' as weapons. This shift from 'technocratic management' to 'storytelling' is not a return to values, but a tactical adjustment after realizing that 'technical logic' lost to 'emotional logic' in the existential war. They don't want to lead progress; they just want their ticket back into the game.
