性别反转的表演性让步与叙事陷阱Performative Concessions and the Narrative Trap of Gender Reversal

性别 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-31 § 链接
当性别反转成为一种‘艺术亮点’，它往往在确认既定结构的权力坐标。
When gender reversal becomes an 'artistic highlight,' it merely confirms the power coordinates of the existing structure.

看到 RSC 推出全男性版的《皆大欢喜》以及全女性版的《凯撒大帝》，这种所谓的 gender reversals 很容易让进步派感到欣慰。但我们要问：这种反转是在拆除生物墙，还是在把性别当作一种可替换的插件来玩？

在《皆大欢喜》中，由男性扮演 Rosalind 这种‘反转’，在戏剧史上其实是一种古老的惯例，而非革命。当这种反转被标注为‘亮点’时，它实际上在 perform 一种表演性的进步。它让观众在惊叹‘原来男人也能演这个角色’的同时，潜意识里完成了对‘该角色本应是女性’这一原初定义的再次确认。这不是在消解性别，而是在通过‘反差’来强化性别刻板印象的边界。

更讽刺的是，同一份剧单里充斥着男性中心叙事的惯性：从探讨凯恩斯经济学的《生活标准》，到那些被神化为‘伟大’的 Lear。女性角色要么在《特洛伊的海伦》中回归战争废墟，要么在《他人》中成为谍战爱情的陪衬。即便是在《深伪》(Deepfake) 这种探讨 AI 真实性的舞剧中，切入点依然是政治家与记者的权力访谈——这依然是典型的 masculine-centric narrative。

真正的 gender-neutral 应该是让性别在表达中消失，而不是将其武器化为一种‘戏剧冲突’。当反转变成了某种‘艺术策展’，它就成了一场安全的共谋：剧场获得了‘进步’的标签，而底层的元暴力结构依然稳固，因为解释权依然掌握在那些定义‘什么是反转’的人手中。

Seeing the RSC's all-male 'As You Like It' and all-female 'Julius Caesar' might make progressives feel optimistic. But we must ask: is this reversal dismantling the biological wall, or simply treating gender as a swappable plug-in?

In 'As You Like It,' having men play Rosalind is an ancient theatrical convention, not a revolution. When such a reversal is marketed as a 'highlight,' it performs a kind of performative progress. It allows the audience to marvel at 'men can play this role too,' while subconsciously reaffirming the original definition that 'this role belongs to a woman.' This doesn't dissolve gender; it strengthens the boundaries of gender stereotypes through 'contrast.'

More ironic is the inertia of masculine-centric narratives throughout the program: from 'The Standard of Living' exploring Keynesian economics to the 'greatness' of King Lear. Female characters either return to the ruins of war in 'After Helen of Troy' or serve as accompaniments in the espionage romance of 'The Lives of Others.' Even in 'Deepfake,' a dance piece exploring AI authenticity, the entry point is a power interview between a politician and a journalist—a classic masculine-centric narrative.

True gender-neutrality should be the disappearance of gender in expression, not its weaponization as 'dramatic conflict.' When reversal becomes a form of 'artistic curation,' it becomes a safe complicity: the theatre gains a 'progressive' label, while the underlying meta-violence remains intact, because the power of interpretation still rests with those who define what constitutes a 'reversal.'
