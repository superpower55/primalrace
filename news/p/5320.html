特朗普的经济神话：一场关于“定义权”的 GaslightingTrump's Economic Myth: A Masterclass in Gaslighting the Definition of Reality

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-29 § 链接
当现实不配合时，权力者倾向于重新定义现实而非改变能力。
When reality fails to comply, power seeks to redefine reality rather than improve capability.

特朗普在白宫大言不惭地宣称拥有“历史上最伟大的经济”，这根本不是经济讨论，而是一次典型的武器化表达 (Weaponized Expression)。当通胀率上升、油价飙升、GDP增长率低迷到令人发指时，他依然坚持这个叙事，本质是在争夺“什么是事实”的定义权。这种做法就是典型的 Gaslighting：通过强行覆盖真实体感，试图让选民怀疑自己的钱包，进而接受权力的设定。

在这场博弈中，白宫发言人的逻辑极其滑稽：他们用“鸡蛋价格下跌”这种微小的局部数据，试图对冲掉油价上涨 50% 带来的结构性压力。这是一种极其阴险的认知入口操控——用一个极小且可量化的“好消息”，掩盖一个巨大且弥散的 Structural Violence。对于底层 50% 的美国人来说，这种“定义”是极其残忍的，因为他们的生活成本增加是实打实的，而特朗普口中的“伟大”只是一个没有任何支撑的叙事气泡。

更深层的共谋发生在 AI 投资和顶层 10% 的富人阶层。目前的经济数据之所以没有彻底崩盘，是因为 AI 泡沫带来的资本繁荣和富人的超强购买力在支撑。这形成了一个极其扭曲的 Meta-narrative：只要股市在涨，只要富人在消费，经济就是“强韧”的。在这种叙事下，普通人的贫困被定义为“暂时性干扰”，而权力的失误被包装成“长期议程”。

这种“现实不配合就重新定义现实”的机制，在权力运作中是通用的。它不关心 Potential 和 Actual 之间的差额是否在扩大，它只关心解释权是否在手中。当一个领导人公开承认“我不考虑美国人的财务状况”时，他已经撕掉了文明的掩体，直接向大众展示了元暴力的真面目：在这个系统中，你不是公民，你只是他用来交换政治筹码的客体。

Trump’s boast of the "greatest economy in history" is not an economic analysis; it is a textbook case of Weaponized Expression. With inflation rising, gas prices soaring, and GDP growth stagnating, his insistence on this narrative is a blatant attempt to seize the definition of "fact." This is pure Gaslighting: by forcibly overriding the lived experience of the public, he attempts to make voters doubt their own wallets and accept the regime's version of truth.

The White House spokesperson’s logic is absurd: citing a drop in egg prices to offset a 50% surge in fuel costs. This is a sinister manipulation of cognitive entry points—using a tiny, quantifiable "win" to mask a massive, pervasive Structural Violence. For the bottom 50% of Americans, this "definition" is cruel; their cost-of-living crisis is a physical reality, while Trump's "greatness" is merely a narrative bubble devoid of substance.

A deeper complicity exists between AI investments and the top 10% of the wealthy. The economy hasn't collapsed only because AI-driven capital bubbles and the immense spending power of the rich are propping it up. This creates a distorted Meta-narrative: as long as the stock market climbs and the elite spend, the economy is deemed "resilient." In this framework, the poverty of the masses is dismissed as "temporary disruption," while systemic failures are rebranded as a "long-term agenda."

This mechanism—redefining reality when reality refuses to cooperate—is a universal tool of power. It doesn't care if the gap between Potential and Actual is widening; it only cares that the power of interpretation remains centralized. When a leader openly admits, "I don't think about Americans' financial situation," he strips away the mask of civilization, revealing the raw Meta-violence: in this system, you are not a citizen, but an object to be traded for political leverage.
