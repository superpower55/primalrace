JavaScript 墙与被截断的真相The JavaScript Wall and the Severed Truth

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 Support the Guardian ↗ 2026-08-13 § 链接
技术门槛是现代文化暴力最隐蔽的认知入口。
Technical barriers are the most covert cognitive entries for modern cultural violence.

这条新闻本身不是新闻，而是一次典型的认知入口截断。当你试图通过链接进入《卫报》的支持页面，迎接你的不是关于新闻价值的讨论，而是一道 JavaScript 墙。在技术语境下，这叫“用户体验”；在权力语境下，这叫“准入筛选”。

根据加尔通的暴力三角，这属于典型的 structural violence。当信息的获取被绑定在特定的技术配置（如必须开启 JS、必须使用特定浏览器、必须拥有某种数字素养）之上时，一个隐形的生物墙被数字化地重建了。那些无法通过技术门槛的人，在物理层面被剥夺了参与公共讨论的 potential，而 Actual 状态则是被排除在认知之外。这种差额，就是暴力。

更讽刺的是，这种“为了提供最佳体验”的叙事，是典型的 weaponized expression。它将一种排他性的技术要求包装成一种“服务”和“关怀”。这和那些声称“为了保护女性而限制其出行”的父权叙事如出一辙：用一个看似合理的伪装，掩盖其本质上的控制与剔除。

当一个号称代表进步主义和公共利益的媒体，将自己的入口构建在这样一道数字门槛之后时，它实际上在潜意识里完成了与技术精英阶层的共谋。它在筛选它的支持者，而这种筛选机制本身，就是一种元暴力的延伸——只有符合某种“现代性”标准的人，才配拥有被告知真相的权利。

This news item isn't news; it is a typical truncation of the cognitive entry. When you attempt to access The Guardian's support page, you are met not with a discourse on journalistic value, but with a JavaScript wall. In technical terms, this is 'user experience'; in the language of power, it is 'access screening'.

According to Galtung's Violence Triangle, this is a classic case of structural violence. When access to information is bound to specific technical configurations—enabling JS, using specific browsers, or possessing certain digital literacy—an invisible biological wall is digitally reconstructed. Those unable to clear this technical hurdle are physically deprived of the potential to participate in public discourse, while their actual state is exclusion from cognition. This delta is violence.

More ironically, the narrative of 'providing the best experience' is a weaponized expression. It packages an exclusive technical requirement as 'service' and 'care'. This mirrors patriarchal narratives that restrict women's movement 'for their own protection': using a plausible guise to mask the essence of control and elimination.

When a medium claiming to represent progressivism and the public interest builds its entry behind such a digital barrier, it effectively enters into a complicity with the technical elite. It is filtering its supporters, and this filtering mechanism itself is an extension of meta-violence—only those meeting a certain standard of 'modernity' are granted the right to be informed.
