法律豁免权的本质是元暴力的物理隔离，将女性身体变为法外之地。
Legal immunity is the physical isolation of meta-violence, turning female bodies into lawless zones.

这不仅仅是法律管辖权的冲突，而是一场典型的结构性暴力 (structural violence) 实验。美国空军基地在英国领土上制造了一个“准主权”的平行时空，用美元、美式糖果和 911 电话构建起一道生物墙之外的制度墙。但这道墙最核心的功能，是为男性施暴者提供一个由“同类”组成的共谋者 (complicity) 审判庭。

一个由八名全男性美国空军军官组成的陪审团，审判一名被指控强奸的飞行员，这简直是对“公正表达”的公开嘲弄。在元暴力 (meta violence) 的逻辑中，男性中心叙事通过垄断解释权，将“强奸”这种直接暴力通过术语转换，降级为所谓的“加重性接触”。这种 weaponized 的法律语言，目的就是为了让施暴者在同类共谋的庇护下，将女性的痛苦转化为一种可以被轻量化处理的“行政瑕疵”。

Sarah Steele 的经历揭示了原初种族 (Primal Race) 被殖民的现代版本：女性的身体在这些基地周围被定义为可随意进入且无需承担法律后果的资源。当英国政府在新闻稿中赞美受害者的“勇敢”时，他们掩盖了一个事实——这种勇敢是对抗一个由国家机器背书的性别暴力系统的绝望博弈。所谓的“回顾与审查”如果不能彻底摧毁这种由男性共谋构建的法律特权，那么它仅仅是 structural violence 的 PR 版本。

这场胜利目前仅停留在 cultural 层面的叙事反击，即通过《卫报》的报道将这种隐秘的暴力曝光。但真正的 good_news 应该发生在 structural 层：除非这些“准主权”领地被强行拉回当地司法管辖，否则只要那道围栏还在，女性在其中的存在性就是被剥夺的。

This is not merely a conflict of legal jurisdiction, but a textbook experiment in structural violence. The core function of this wall is to provide a tribunal of complicity for male perpetrators, where they are judged by their own 'kind'.

An all-male panel of eight US Air Force officers judging a pilot accused of rape is a public mockery of Just Expressions. Under the logic of meta-violence, the masculine-centric narrative monopolizes the power of interpretation, downgrading direct violence like rape into a weaponized term: 'aggravated sexual contact'. This linguistic scam ensures that the perpetrator, sheltered by a network of complicity, can transform female suffering into a minor 'administrative flaw'.

Sarah Steele’s ordeal reveals a modern version of the Primal Race being colonized: female bodies around these bases are defined as resources to be accessed without legal consequence. When the UK government praises the victim's 'bravery', they mask a grim truth—this bravery is a desperate gamble against a system of gender violence backed by the state machine. Any 'review' that fails to dismantle the legal privilege built on male complicity is nothing more than a PR version of structural violence.

This victory currently exists only at the cultural layer, through the Guardian’s exposure of hidden violence. True good_news would require a structural shift: unless these 'quasi-sovereign' territories are forcibly returned to local jurisdiction, as long as those fences stand, the existence of women within them remains erased.
