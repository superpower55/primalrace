用“个人责任”为新纳粹洗白：一场典型的权力共谋Whitewashing a Neo-Nazi with 'Personal Responsibility': A Classic Case of Complicity

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-10 § 链接
所谓的“康复”叙事，不过是权力者为共谋者提供的合法化掩体。
The narrative of 'rehabilitation' is merely a legitimate cover provided by power for its co-conspirators.

Kemi Badenoch 把一个前新纳粹、反犹主义者、且曾对 Luciana Berger 实施种族骚扰的罪犯 Joshua Bonehill-Paine 塞进国家顾问角色，然后用一套关于“个人责任”和“法律与秩序”的词汇来包装。这简直是教科书级别的 cultural violence：通过重新定义“康复” (rehabilitation)，将曾经的施暴者洗白成一个“通过法律程序获得救赎”的模范，从而抹除受害者在结构中依然存在的痛苦。

在这场博弈中，Badenoch 扮演的是定义权的掌控者。她将针对该任命的质疑定义为“pile-on”（围攻），这是一种典型的武器化表达——通过将合理的道德质疑标签化为“政治正确”的集体霸凌，从而在认知入口上封死讨论空间。她口中的“高标准”和“一致性”，实际上是元暴力 (meta violence) 的伪装：只要施暴者在法律层面上“服刑完毕”，他就可以被迅速地重新整合进权力中心，而受害者的心理创伤在她的计算模型里，仅仅是一个被随口提及的“passing reference”。

这不仅是政党内部的人事决定，而是一场权力共谋 (complicity)。保守党通过接纳一个曾经的极端右翼领袖，在潜意识中向其背后的原教旨主义群体发送信号：只要你认同我们的权力逻辑，你的暴力历史可以被快速折现为政治资本。而 Luciana Berger 的愤怒，本质上是对这种“解释权垄断”的绝望反抗。当一个曾经称你为“啮齿动物”的人被赋予定义“文化与融合”的权力时，这种 structural violence 已经完成了闭环：施暴者不仅赢得了自由，还赢得了对受害者的二次审判权。

Kemi Badenoch has slotted Joshua Bonehill-Paine—a former neo-Nazi, antisemite, and convicted harasser of Luciana Berger—into a national advisory role, wrapping it in a lexicon of 'personal responsibility' and 'law and order.' This is textbook cultural violence: by redefining 'rehabilitation,' the aggressor is rebranded as a model of legal redemption, effectively erasing the structural suffering of the victims.

In this game, Badenoch acts as the gatekeeper of definition. She labels the backlash as a 'pile-on,' a weaponized expression designed to frame legitimate moral objections as a form of 'politically correct' mobbing, thereby closing the cognitive entry point for discussion. Her claims of 'higher standards' and 'consistency' are mere disguises for meta violence: as long as the perpetrator has 'served their time' legally, they can be swiftly reintegrated into the center of power, while the victim's trauma is reduced to a mere 'passing reference' in her calculation.

This is more than a party appointment; it is a systemic complicity. By embracing a former far-right leader, the Conservative party signals to the originalist fringes that as long as you align with their power logic, your history of violence can be cashed in for political capital. Luciana Berger's outrage is a desperate resistance against this monopoly of interpretation. When someone who once called you a 'rodent' is given the power to define 'culture and integration,' the structural violence is complete: the aggressor has not only won their freedom but has won the right to judge the victim a second time.
