共谋者的Mafia与被禁的书The Mafia of Complicity and the Banned Book

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-15 § 链接
当体制变成Mafia，反对者的唯一最优解是制造不被定义的真实。
When the system becomes a Mafia, the only optimal expression is creating an undefined truth.

Art Spiegelman 的生活轨迹是一个典型的存在性战争样本：从地下漫画的 oddball 到艺术建制派的 power couple。但这种跃迁没有让他成为体制的共谋者，反而让他成了那个在体制内部精准识别 meta-violence 的观察者。

他把现在的共和党比作 Mafia 的 made men，这个比喻极其辛辣且精确。在共谋者理论中，一旦个体通过参与某种结构性暴力（如对他者的 othering）获得了利益，他们就进入了一个不可逆的共谋闭环。这种闭环不再基于理念，而基于共同的罪行和利益绑定。当整个政党变成一个 complicit unit，内部的 whistle-blowing 就成了不可能的任务，因为背叛共谋意味着失去在结构中生存的票值。

最讽刺的 structural violence 在于《Maus》被禁。权力者试图通过删除认知入口来抹除事实，但他们低估了表达的武器化潜能：禁令反而成了最好的营销，让更多人意识到那些被定义的“老鼠”其实是权力的镜像。当权力试图定义谁是猫、谁是老鼠时，它其实在向世界宣告自己的恐惧。

Spiegelman 提到的 counter-revolution 依然遥远，因为民主党的 timid 是另一种形式的共谋——一种通过维持“体面”而默认现状的消极共谋。在这种环境下，艺术唯一的真.最优解表达，就是像他那样，在 78 岁依然把自己定义为一个“idiot”，拒绝被任何既定的成功叙事所捕捉，在权力的缝隙里持续制造那些让权力感到不安的真实。

Art Spiegelman's trajectory is a textbook case of an existential war: from an oddball of underground comics to a power couple of the art establishment. Yet, this ascent didn't turn him into a complicit actor; instead, it made him an observer capable of pinpointing meta-violence from within the system.

His comparison of the current GOP to Mafia 'made men' is sharp and precise. In the theory of complicity, once an individual gains advantage by participating in structural violence—such as the 'othering' of others—they enter an irreversible loop of complicity. This loop is no longer based on ideology, but on shared crimes and mutual interests. When an entire party becomes a complicit unit, whistle-blowing becomes impossible because betraying the conspiracy means forfeiting one's value within the structure.

The most ironic structural violence is the banning of 'Maus'. The powers attempt to erase facts by closing the cognitive entry points, but they underestimate the potential of weaponized expression: the bans became the best marketing, forcing more people to realize that the defined 'mice' are actually mirrors of power. When power tries to define who is the cat and who is the mouse, it is announcing its own fear to the world.

The 'counter-revolution' Spiegelman longs for remains distant because the timidity of the Democrats is another form of complicity—a passive one that accepts the status quo in the name of 'decency'. In such an environment, the only true optimal expression for art is to remain, as he does at 78, a self-proclaimed 'idiot,' refusing to be captured by any established narrative of success, and continuing to manufacture truths that make power uncomfortable.
