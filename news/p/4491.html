AI 的碳足迹：一场被 NSIP 掩盖的结构性 scamAI's Carbon Footprint: A Structural Scam Masked by NSIP

科技 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-25 § 链接
用‘数字增长’定义的文明，本质上是把物理世界的毁灭外包给算法。
A civilization defined by 'digital growth' is simply outsourcing physical destruction to algorithms.

两个数据中心就比埃克森美孚（ExxonMobil）排碳还多。这个数字本身就是一种极具讽刺意义的 direct violence：我们被告知 AI 是通往未来的门票，但这张门票的代价是直接在英国的土地上重建小型燃气电站。所谓的“数字转型”，在物理层面上不过是把碳排放从烟囱移到了服务器机房的冷却系统里。

最恶心的是这套 NSIP（国家重大基础设施项目）机制。它通过“快速通道”绕过地方规划审查，直接由中央政府拍板。这在本质上是一次典型的 structural violence：通过制度设计，将环境成本从决策者的办公桌转移到当地居民的呼吸道中，并用“国家重要性”这个巨大的叙事盾牌，屏蔽掉所有可能的 local scrutiny。当政府说这些数据“误导”时，他们其实在玩一种典型的认知游戏——用“利用率不一定是 100%”这种技术细节，来掩盖“只要项目建成，它必然追求 100% 利用率”的商业逻辑。

这是一场完美的共谋。AI 公司需要算力来喂养贪婪的模型，政府需要“科技增长”的政绩来掩盖经济的疲软，而 NSIP 机制则为这场交易提供了法律掩体。他们把数据中心包装成“经济增长的引擎”，但这个引擎的燃料是法律目标的背叛。在这种叙事里，气候目标变成了可以随时被“路径优化”的 PR 词汇，而真实的物理世界则在 1.3GW 的电力渴求中被缓慢地肢解。

Two datacentres emitting more carbon than ExxonMobil. This figure is a piece of direct violence wrapped in irony: we are told AI is the ticket to the future, but the price of that ticket is the reconstruction of small-scale gas power stations on British soil. The so-called 'digital transformation' is, at its physical core, merely moving carbon emissions from industrial chimneys to server cooling systems.

The most repulsive part is the NSIP regime. By creating a 'fast-track' that bypasses local planning authorities in favor of central government decisions, it executes a classic structural violence. It shifts the environmental cost from the decision-makers' desks to the lungs of local residents, using the narrative of 'national significance' as a shield to insulate the projects from local scrutiny. When the government calls the figures 'misleading' based on utilisation rates, they are playing a cognitive game—using technical minutiae to mask the commercial reality that any operational datacentre will inevitably strive for 100% capacity.

This is a perfect complicity. AI firms need compute to feed greedy models, the government needs 'tech growth' as a trophy to mask economic stagnation, and the NSIP provides the legal cover. They package datacentres as 'engines of growth,' but the fuel for this engine is the betrayal of legal climate targets. In this masculine-centric narrative of 'progress,' climate goals become PR buzzwords subject to 'pathway optimization,' while the actual physical world is slowly dismantled by a 1.3GW thirst for power.
