森林里的枪支与国家机器的性欲Guns in the Forest and the Libido of State Machines

国际 直接层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-21 § 链接
战争叙事本质上是男性中心叙事对物理暴力的最高级审美化。
War narratives are the ultimate aestheticization of physical violence within a masculine-centric narrative.

在柏林森林里挖出俄军特工的枪支库，这在新闻里被处理成一个关于“国家安全”和“间谍战”的悬疑剧。但如果我们剥离这些 weaponized 的词汇，这件事的本质极其简单：一群男性通过掌控暴力机器，在另一个男性的领土上，计划用物理消灭来解决政治分歧。

这正是典型的 masculine-centric narrative。在这种叙事里，刺杀、破坏、潜伏被赋予了一种“专业”且“冷峻”的色彩，仿佛这是一种高级的博弈。但请记住，所有的 meta-violence 都始于这里：将人类身体降格为可消耗的工具，将杀戮视为一种实现“宏大意义”的手段。无论是俄罗斯的情报员还是德国的检察官，他们共谋地维持着一套关于“权力”的语言，而这套语言的底色就是对生命权的绝对漠视。

潜在目标包括国防工业高管和反对派，这意味着这场博弈的参与者全部是这个权力结构的既得利益者或竞争者。在这种纯粹的男性权力游戏里，女性要么是被忽略的背景板，要么是被当作筹码的客体。这种对物理暴力的崇拜，正是所有 structural violence 的原点——因为一旦社会认可了“为了某种目标可以合法地抹除他人存在”，那么在更微观的领域，对弱势群体的规训和压迫就变得极其自然。

这种新闻最危险的地方在于，它让读者在潜意识里接受了这样一种设定：暴力是解决问题的唯一有效路径，而唯一区别在于谁的暴力更隐蔽、更专业。这种对暴力的审美化，就是最典型的 cultural violence。

Finding a Russian gun cache in a Berlin forest is framed as a suspense thriller about 'national security' and 'espionage.' But if we strip away these weaponized terms, the essence is brutally simple: a group of men, controlling the machinery of violence, planning to use physical erasure to resolve political disagreements on another man's territory.

This is a textbook example of a masculine-centric narrative. In this framing, assassinations and sabotage are imbued with a 'professional' and 'cold' allure, as if it were a high-stakes game. But remember, all meta-violence starts here: degrading the human body into a consumable tool and treating slaughter as a means to achieve some 'grand meaning.' Whether it is Russian intelligence or German prosecutors, they are complicit in maintaining a language of 'power'—a language whose foundation is the absolute disregard for the right to exist.

The potential targets—defense executives and opposition figures—confirm that the players in this game are all either beneficiaries or competitors within the same power structure. In this pure masculine power play, women are either invisible background noise or objectified pawns. This worship of physical violence is the origin of all structural violence; once society accepts that 'erasing others is legitimate for a certain goal,' the discipline and oppression of the marginalized in micro-spheres become naturalized.

The danger of such news lies in its subconscious suggestion: that violence is the only effective solution, and the only difference is who is more discreet or professional. This aestheticization of violence is the purest form of cultural violence.
