用 1800 人的肌肉表演，掩盖 73 米的机械真相1,800 Muscles as Props: The Romantic Scam of the Castle Pull

哲学 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-25 § 链接
仪式感是结构性低效的遮羞布，是把体力劳动浪漫化为共谋的 scam。
Ritual is a veil for structural inefficiency, romanticizing labor into a collective scam of complicity.

1800 个人，分成 80 人的轮值小组，在口号声中把一座 360 吨的城堡拉动了 2 米。这在新闻叙事里被包装成“社区建设”和“传统技艺”，但剥掉这层 cultural layer，这就是一场典型的、由权力主导的体力表演。

最讽刺的数据在这里：这 1800 人费尽心力拉动的 5 米，在整个 73 米的搬运工程中只占不到 7%。剩下的 93% 将由 machinery 完成。这意味着，这场所谓的“公民驱动”其实是一次精心设计的 PR 活动。政府并不需要这 1800 人的肌肉，它需要的是 8000 个申请者在社交媒体上制造的“参与感”和“文化自豪感”。

这是一种极其阴险的武器化叙事。通过将现代工程问题转化为一场“全民参与”的仪式，管理层成功地将一个枯燥的修缮项目变成了某种精神图腾。参与者在其中获得了一种假想的“主体性”——觉得自己参与了历史，实际上他们只是被租赁的、免费的装饰品，用来填充一个名为“传统”的认知入口。

当 61 岁的参与者感叹“没有其他机会能拉城堡”时，他已经完成了内化。他把被利用的体力劳动认作了人生的高光时刻。这种共谋完成了最完美的闭环：权力提供一个象征性的座位，弱势者通过竞争（4 倍超额申请）来抢夺这个被定义的“特权”，最后心满意足地为这个低效的系统背书。

1,800 people, rotating in teams of 80, pulling a 360-tonne castle keep for a mere two metres. The narrative frames this as 'community building' and 'traditional craft,' but strip away the cultural layer, and it is a textbook performance of physical labor orchestrated by power.

The most cynical data point is this: the 5 metres moved by these 1,800 people account for less than 7% of the total 73-metre journey. The remaining 93% will be handled by machinery. This means the so-called 'citizen-driven' effort is actually a meticulously designed PR stunt. The government doesn't need the muscles; it needs the 'sense of participation' and 'cultural pride' generated by 8,000 applicants on social media.

This is a weaponized narrative. By transforming an engineering task into a ritual, the administration turns a boring renovation into a spiritual totem. Participants experience a fake sense of agency—believing they are part of history—while they are actually just unpaid props filling a cognitive entry point labeled 'Tradition.'

When a 61-year-old participant laments that there is 'no other opportunity to pull a castle,' the internalisation is complete. He perceives his exploited labor as a life highlight. This complicity closes the loop: power provides a symbolic seat, the subordinates compete (4x oversubscribed) for this defined 'privilege,' and finally, they gratefully endorse an inefficient system.
