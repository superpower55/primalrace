潘多拉的盒子：被AI叙事掩盖的原初禁锢Pandora's Box: The Primal Imprisonment Masked by AI Narratives

哲学 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-24 § 链接
用AI做警示叙事，是最高明的文化共谋：它让我们关注技术，而遗忘了被定义为'毒药'的女性主体。
Using AI as a cautionary tale is the ultimate cultural complicity: it makes us fear technology while forgetting the female subject defined as 'poison'.

这出芭蕾舞剧《凡人》(Mere Mortals) 玩了一个很典型的叙事置换：把潘多拉的盒子比作AI。这种比喻在文化层面上非常 a-typical，因为它试图用一种‘现代的恐惧’（AI）来覆盖一个‘古老的暴力’（对女性的定义）。

编舞 Aszure Barton 试图通过作品为潘多拉正名，称她为‘改变的代理人’而非‘有毒的生物’。但这恰恰揭示了元暴力的运作方式：即便在 2026 年的艺术创作中，女性主体依然需要通过‘被重新解读’来获得某种迟到的 agency。潘多拉在神话中被设定为人类苦难的入口，这本身就是一种典型的 masculine-centric narrative——将灾难的解释权交给女性的‘好奇心’或‘天性’，从而掩盖了制定规则的宙斯才是真正的施暴者。

最讽刺的共谋在于，这部剧在旧金山这个 AI 中心上演。当人们在 LED 巨幕和电子音乐的包裹下，感叹 AI 像潘多拉之盒一样不可控时，他们实际上在潜意识里完成了第二次内化：将‘女性’与‘失控的危险’再次绑定。AI 成了掩体，让观众在享受‘警示寓言’的快感时，忽略了那个被殖民的原初种族——女性，在数千年的叙事中是如何被强制定义为‘毒药’的。

Barton 赞美舞者 Jennifer Stahl 的强大与美丽，这很好，但这种赞美是个体层面的。在结构层面上，当一部作品把‘女性神话人物’作为‘技术危机’的隐喻时，它依然在利用原有的文化暴力来制造戏剧冲突。真正的 Just Expression 不应该是‘给潘多拉正名’，而应该是拆穿那个把女性定义为‘盒子’的权力机制本身。

The ballet 'Mere Mortals' performs a typical narrative displacement: equating Pandora's box with AI. This metaphor is a classic piece of cultural violence, attempting to overwrite an 'ancient violence' (the definition of women) with a 'modern fear' (AI).

Choreographer Aszure Barton tries to reclaim Pandora, calling her an 'agent of change' rather than a 'toxic being'. This precisely reveals how meta-violence operates: even in 2026, female agency must be 're-interpreted' to be recognized. In myth, Pandora was cast as the gateway to human suffering—a masculine-centric narrative that attributes catastrophe to female 'curiosity' to mask the fact that Zeus, the rule-maker, was the true perpetrator.

The most cynical complicity is that this show premieres in San Francisco, the AI capital. As the audience marvels at the AI-Pandora analogy amidst LED screens and electronic beats, they subconsciously complete a second internalization: re-binding 'femininity' with 'uncontrollable danger'. AI becomes a shield, allowing the audience to enjoy the thrill of a 'cautionary tale' while ignoring how the Primal Race—women—has been systematically defined as 'poison' for millennia.

Barton praises dancer Jennifer Stahl's power and beauty, which is a positive individual expression. However, at the structural level, using a female mythological figure as a metaphor for 'technological crisis' still relies on existing cultural violence to create dramatic tension. A Just Expression wouldn't be about 'redeeming Pandora,' but about dismantling the power mechanism that defined women as the 'box' in the first place.
