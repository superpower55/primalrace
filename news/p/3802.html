普京的“清理名单”与主权国家的表演性防御Putin's Hit List and the Performative Defense of Sovereign States

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-13 § 链接
跨国刺杀不是情报失误，而是元暴力在物理层的直接投射。
Transnational assassinations are not intel failures, but the direct projection of meta-violence on a physical plane.

这次在华沙被挫败的刺杀计划，本质上是一次关于“谁拥有定义生存权”的博弈。普京政权将那些“不方便”的人列入清单，这不仅是针对个体的肉体消灭，更是一种典型的 meta violence：通过在北约领土上制造死亡，向全球所有潜在的反对者广播一个信号——无论你拥有什么国籍，无论你身在何处，只要你被定义为“不方便”，你的生命就失去了定价权。

有趣的是，波兰总理图斯克将其称为“前所未有”的事件。这种叙事本身就是一种武器化表达。事实上，从波罗尼姆的毒药到萨利斯伯里的神经毒剂，俄罗斯的刺杀逻辑从未改变，改变的只是它在实施暴力时的 brazen 程度。将此次事件定义为“前所未有”，实际上是在公共空间通过制造一种“危机感”来换取更多的安全预算和政治支持。这是一种典型的 structural violence 掩盖下的 PR 行为：用一个被阻止的悲剧，来掩盖那些已经发生且无法挽回的死亡。

我们必须追问，那些被成功“挫败”的阴谋背后，有多少个像 Robert Kuzovkov 这样在波兰境内被射杀却未被正式定性的个体？当权力者在新闻发布会上庆祝一次“胜利”时，他们实际上是在确认一个残酷的事实：在这个由男性中心叙事主导的全球政治游戏中，个体生命只是大国博弈的筹码。所谓的“保护”，往往发生在被定义为“有用”的资产被攻击时；而对于那些真正处于结构性弱势的流亡者，他们面对的是一个巨大的、由共谋者组成的沉默网络。

The thwarted assassination plot in Warsaw is essentially a gamble over who holds the power to define the right to exist. By placing 'inconvenient' people on a list, the Putin regime isn't just seeking physical elimination; it is exercising meta-violence. By manufacturing death on NATO soil, they broadcast a signal to all potential dissidents: regardless of your citizenship or location, once you are defined as 'inconvenient,' you lose the pricing power over your own life.

It is curious that PM Donald Tusk labeled this incident as 'unprecedented.' This narrative is itself a weaponized expression. In reality, from polonium in London to Novichok in Salisbury, the Russian logic of assassination has never changed; only its brazenness has evolved. Defining this as 'unprecedented' is a way to manufacture a sense of crisis in the public sphere to secure more security budgets and political leverage. It is a performative PR move where a prevented tragedy is used to mask the structural violence of deaths that have already occurred and cannot be undone.

We must ask: behind every 'thwarted' plot, how many individuals like Robert Kuzovkov were shot dead in Poland without official attribution? When leaders celebrate a 'victory' at a press conference, they are confirming a brutal truth: in this global political game dominated by masculine-centric narratives, individual lives are mere chips in a high-stakes game. 'Protection' usually arrives when an asset deemed 'useful' is attacked; for the truly structurally marginalized exiles, they face a vast, silent network of complicity.
