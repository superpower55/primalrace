在“平等”的笑话里，共谋Cloakroom的掠夺Egalitarian Jokes, Predatory Cloakrooms

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-20 § 链接
表演性的平等叙事，掩盖不了结构性的掠夺逻辑。
Performative egalitarian narratives cannot mask structural predatory logic.

Kevin Bridges 的表演被评价为“平等主义” (egalitarian)，因为他能在段子里解构 mansplaining，或者用一种“开明”的姿态对待性别认同。这种表达在文化层 (cultural layer) 制造了一种进步的假象：一个传统男性脱口秀演员通过在舞台上扮演一个“觉醒者”，获得了当代观众的认同票。这是一种典型的假.最优解表达——通过在不触动权力结构的前提下，在叙事层面进行微调，来维持其作为“主流成功者”的地位。

但真正的暴力隐藏在剧场那 10 英镑的 Cloakroom charge 里。一个装着笔记本和眼镜的小包被定义为“危险”，从而被强制剥离，并被课以高额费用。这就是典型的结构性暴力 (structural violence)：场馆通过定义“安全”来制造一个垄断的收费入口。这种“安全叙事”是武器化的，它将观众置于被动地位，让掠夺变得合法且“必要”。

讽刺的是，评论者希望 Bridges 能用讽刺的目光审视这场 racket。但 Bridges 选择了在舞台上扮演一个温和的平等主义者，而对他所在的这个通过剥削观众、通过共谋场馆权力来获利的商业系统保持沉默。当一个演员在台上解构男权，而他的商业链路在台下执行着最粗暴的权力掠夺时，这种“平等”就成了一场表演性的 PR。他在文化层削减了暴力的感知，却在结构层共谋了暴力的执行。

Kevin Bridges is praised as an 'egalitarian' because he deconstructs mansplaining or adopts an 'open-minded' stance on gender identity. In the cultural layer, this expression manufactures an illusion of progress: a traditionally masculine comic earns votes from a contemporary audience by performing the role of the 'awakened.' This is a fake optimal expression—tweaking the narrative without disturbing the actual power structure to maintain his status as a mainstream success.

However, the real violence is hidden in the £10 cloakroom charge. A small bag with a notebook and glasses is defined as 'incendiary,' forced away from the owner, and taxed heavily. This is structural violence: the venue weaponizes the narrative of 'security' to create a monopoly entry for extortion. By defining what is 'safe,' they render the audience passive and make predation appear legitimate and 'necessary.'

It is ironic that the reviewer hopes Bridges would turn his satirical eye toward this racket. Instead, Bridges chooses to play the gentle egalitarian on stage while remaining silent about the commercial system—a system of complicity with the venue's power—that profits from exploiting the audience. When a performer deconstructs patriarchy on stage while his commercial pipeline executes raw power predation off stage, this 'equality' becomes a performative PR. He reduces the perception of violence in the cultural layer, only to facilitate its execution in the structural layer.
