Noel Clarke 的指控：权力场中的 voyeurism 并非偶然Noel Clarke's Charges: Voyeurism as a Tool of Power Monopoly

性别 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-09 § 链接
性犯罪不是个案，而是权力垄断者对客体的必然掠夺。
Sexual crimes are not isolated incidents, but the inevitable predation of power monopolists over objects.

Noel Clarke 被指控六项性犯罪，包括 sexual assault 和 voyeurism。时间跨度从 2007 年到 2016 年，这意味着在近十年的时间里，他在这场存在性战争中处于绝对的优势位。对于一个演员兼电影制作人来说，他不仅掌握着资源，更掌握着定义“谁能进入这个行业”的认知入口。

Voyeurism（偷窥）在这里是一个极具象征意义的词。它本质上是一种极端的权力不对等表达：一方在完全不知情的情况下被客体化，而另一方则在暗处通过凝视来确认自己的掌控感。这种行为不是因为性冲动，而是因为 meta violence 的内化——在男性中心叙事中，女性的身体被定义为可被随意观测、消费和占有的资源。当一个男人在行业内拥有足够多的共谋者，或者能通过其地位让受害者认为“反抗会导致职业自杀”时，这种掠夺就成了他的最优解表达。

值得注意的细节是，指控在 2025 年才开始调查，而罪行发生在十年前。这揭示了 structural violence 的惯性：在很长一段时间里，这种行为被掩盖在“才华”或“行业潜规则”的文化掩体之下。只有当结构性的共谋链条出现裂缝，受害者能够通过身份政治结盟并获得足够的话语权时，这些 Actual 的暴力才会被推向法庭。

我们不需要关心他是否有 fair trial，因为在这种权力结构中，所谓的“公正”往往是为既得利益者准备的。真正的公正应该是：让这种基于权力不对等的掠夺在机制上变得不可能。

Noel Clarke faces six charges, including sexual assault and voyeurism, spanning from 2007 to 2016. For nearly a decade, Clarke occupied the dominant position in the existential war. As both an actor and filmmaker, he didn't just hold resources; he controlled the cognitive entry point of who gets to enter the industry.

Voyeurism is a symbolic act here. It is an extreme expression of power asymmetry: one party is objectified without consent, while the other confirms their dominance through the gaze. This is not about sexual impulse, but the internalization of meta violence—the masculine-centric narrative that defines female bodies as resources to be observed and consumed. When a man has enough complicity within his circle, or can make victims believe that resistance equals professional suicide, this predation becomes his optimal expression.

The fact that the investigation only began in 2025 for crimes committed a decade ago exposes the inertia of structural violence. For years, such behavior was hidden under the cultural cover of "talent" or "industry norms." Only when the chain of complicity cracks and victims align through identity politics to gain discourse power does this actual violence reach the court.

We needn't fret over his "fair trial," as "justice" in such structures is often designed for the privileged. True justice would be making this kind of power-based predation structurally impossible.
