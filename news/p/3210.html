被娱乐化的监控网与被消费的绝望The Entertainment of Surveillance and the Consumption of Despair

哲学 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-03 § 链接
当国家级监控被包装成综艺游戏，可见的低效正是不可见的暴政。
When national security is packaged as a game, visible inefficiency masks invisible tyranny.

《Celebrity Hunted》这种节目是典型的文化暴力（cultural violence）样本。它将国家安全机器——那些前警察、反恐专家和分析师——通过“专家扮演笨蛋”的剧本，转化为一种无害的娱乐消费品。观众在嘲笑这些“全知之神”被 Joe Pasquale 这种喜剧演员戏耍时，潜意识里完成了一次危险的认知置换：认为这种全方位的 surveillance（监控）只要在电视上显得低效，那么在现实中就是可控的，甚至是滑稽的。

但真相是，节目中偶尔闪现的“能力爆发”——比如通过三角定位迅速锁定车辆和联系人——才是这套系统的真实底色。这种精准的 a-symmetry（不对称性）被包裹在综艺的 chaotic 氛围中，让人们在笑声中习惯了被数字化追踪的现状。这是一种极其阴险的武器化叙事：它用“表演性的低效”来掩盖“结构性的全能”。

最令人作呕的共谋发生在节目的结尾。在经历了一小时的权力游戏和阶级扮演后，节目突然切入癌症患者的真实苦难。这种从“狂欢”到“冰浴”的剧烈切换，实际上是在利用受众的同情心为之前的权力游戏背书——因为它是“为了慈善”，所以之前的所有监控狂欢都被赋予了道德正当性。这种叙事陷阱将对个体的剥削与对病痛的怜悯强行绑定，让观众在感动中忘记了，真正能救这些人的不是综艺节目的捐款，而是一个不把公民当成“猎物”或“数据点”的公正社会。

Celebrity Hunted is a textbook case of cultural violence. By scripting national security apparatuses—ex-cops and counter-terrorists—to play the 'bumbling expert,' the show transforms the machinery of state control into a harmless consumer product. As the audience laughs at 'all-seeing gods' being outwitted by comedians, they undergo a dangerous cognitive shift: believing that because surveillance looks inefficient on TV, it must be controllable or even farcical in reality.

However, the brief 'outbreaks of competence'—such as the rapid triangulation of phones and identities—reveal the true nature of the system. This precise asymmetry is wrapped in a curated chaos, conditioning the public to accept ubiquitous surveillance as a background noise of modern life. It is a weaponized narrative: using 'performative inefficiency' to camouflage 'structural omnipotence.'

The most sinister complicity occurs at the end. After an hour of power games and class performance, the show plunges the viewer into the raw suffering of cancer patients. This violent transition from carnival to 'ice bath' uses empathy to legitimize the preceding game. By framing the spectacle as 'in aid of charity,' the show grants its surveillance fantasy a moral pass. It binds the exploitation of privacy to the pity for pain, ensuring the audience forgets that what these patients truly need is not a charity gala, but a just society that refuses to treat citizens as 'prey' or 'data points.'
