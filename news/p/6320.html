选票被删掉一亿三千万张One Hundred Thirty Million Votes Erased

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 直接层 Al Jazeera ↗ 2026-11-01 § 链接
清点选民名单的人，决定谁还算人
Whoever counts the voter rolls decides who counts as a person.

一亿三千万个名字从选民册上被划掉，然后选举委员会说：被误删的可以重新登记。这句话本身就是暴力的形状——它把一次系统性的清除，包装成一个需要受害者自己跑腿更正的技术小故障。

这不是舞弊，这是重新划定谁算“人民”。反对党说被删的名字集中在穆斯林和其他少数群体身上，也就是那些不太可能投给 Modi 的印度教民族主义政党的人。选举委员会否认偏袒任何一方。否认是免费的，一亿三千万人的登记资格不是。

加尔通的三角在这里三层同时转动。结构层：名单的修订程序、数字系统、登记规则，被改动的地方正好是入口。文化层：卫生部长 Nadda 把这场游行叫做“精心编排的戏剧”“以民主之名把国家推入无政府状态的阴谋”——把要求清点选票的人说成是民主的敌人，这是最标准的武器化叙事，先夺走“民主”这个词的解释权，再宣布只有掌权者配使用它。直接层：数百名警察带着警棍和盾牌、防暴车部署在集会现场。目前还没有打起来，但警棍摆在那里，就是在告诉所有人：你们可以喊，喊的边界由我们定。

Rahul Gandhi 站在敞篷吉普上，反对党、学生组织、还有那个名字起得很妙的 Cockroach Janta Party——蟑螂人民党——从九月末开始几乎天天上街。分析人士说这是 Modi 2014 年上台以来最严重的政治危机。

“最严重的政治危机”这个说法值得停一下。危机之所以严重，不是因为街上人多，而是因为这次被碰的是选举本身——那个让“多数”得以被制造出来的机器。名字被删掉，多数就可以被生产；多数可以被生产，选举就只是仪式。反对党要求 Kumar 辞职，但辞职换不掉那套已经跑过一遍的名单修订程序。

还有一层没说出口的：一亿三千万人里，女性占多少？报道没写。选民册被删的人没有性别统计，就像被删的人没有名字——他们只是“名字”。当一个系统可以一次性抹掉一亿三千万个存在而不需要给出名单，它抹掉的从来不只是选票，是这些人被承认为政治主体的资格。

2029 年才大选。现在被删掉的人，有三年时间可以重新登记，也有三年时间可以放弃。

One hundred thirty million names were struck from India's voter lists, and the Election Commission's answer is that anyone wrongly removed may register again. That sentence is the shape of the violence: a systematic erasure repackaged as a clerical glitch the victims are expected to fix themselves.

This is not fraud, it is the redrawing of who counts as "the people." Opposition parties say the removed names fall disproportionately on Muslims and other minorities — precisely those unlikely to vote for Modi's Hindu nationalist BJP. The Commission denies favouring any party. Denials are free. The registration of 130 million people is not.

Galtung's triangle turns on all three layers at once. Structural: the revision procedures, the registration rules, the digital systems — the entrances were exactly where the changes landed. Cultural: Health Minister Nadda calls the march a "carefully orchestrated drama," a "calculated conspiracy to plunge the nation into anarchy, all in the name of democracy" — calling people who want the rolls audited enemies of democracy is the most standard weaponized narrative there is: seize the word "democracy" first, then declare only the incumbents may use it. Direct: hundreds of police with batons and shields, riot-control vehicles parked around the rally. Nothing has broken out yet, but the batons are there to say: you may shout, and we draw the line where the shouting stops.

Rahul Gandhi rode an open-top jeep; opposition parties, student organisations and the gloriously named Cockroach Janta Party have been demonstrating almost daily since late September. Analysts call it Modi's most serious political crisis since 2014.

Pause on "most serious political crisis." It is serious not because the streets are full but because what got touched is the election itself — the machine that manufactures "majority." Delete names and you can produce a majority; produce a majority and elections become ritual. The opposition wants Kumar to resign, but a resignation does not undo a roll revision that has already run its course.

One layer goes unmentioned: of those 130 million, how many are women? The report doesn't say. There is no gender breakdown for the erased, just as the erased have no names — they are only "names." When a system can wipe out 130 million existences at once without producing a list, what it erases was never just votes. It is the standing of these people as political subjects.

The next general election is in 2029. The erased have three years to re-register, and three years to give up.
