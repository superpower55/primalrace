骗子是白人，广告里是黑人The Fraudsters Were White. The Ads Show Black Faces.

国际 文化层 · 元暴力 The New Republic ↗ 2026-11-02 § 链接
福利诈骗的账，最后记在了黑人脸上
A welfare scam gets billed to Black faces.

明尼苏达的选情被一个词压着：the fraud。报道写得很清楚，Feeding Our Future 案是拜登的司法部在 2022 年 9 月揭出来的，47 名被告，虚报给几万个不存在的孩子供餐，从纳税人手里拿走 2.5 亿到 3 亿美元。共和党现在拿它当棍子，广告里放的是索马里社区被捕者的模糊照片，诈骗网络里很多头目是白人，广告里一个白人都没有。

这是元暴力最标准的操作：事实不必被否认，只需要被重新分配面孔。谁的脸被贴上去，谁就承担解释。Ellison 是黑人，是公开的进步派，背上的靶子又大又亮；共和党广告用黑人被告的大头照，把"诈骗"这个词从财政问题翻译成种族问题。这套配方他们用了快一百年，报道自己都写了。

报道里最值得记下的一句是：选民不是愤怒，是觉得被冒犯。愤怒可以指向骗子，被冒犯要找一个人来负责。共和党赌的就是这个转换——把对政府失职的不满，导流到对黑人和索马里邻居的怀疑上。但文章也写了，Tabke 说人们不喜欢你把事情搞成种族问题；Rosemont 的一位女性说她反感把被捕的人和"我们的索马里邻居"混为一谈。

真正让民主党难受的不是诈骗本身，是它撞上了一个更早的叙事：自由派政府对钱松、对少数族裔软。一位资深民主党幕僚的原话是，这事"正在证明共和党是对的"。共谋不需要恶意，只需要有人顺着既有的刻板印象走一步。诈骗是真的，失职是真的，但把这两件事打包成"黑人抢了你们的钱"，是另一层暴力，而它正在被两党共同消费。

报道里还有一条线几乎被 fraud 盖过去了：Walz 1 月 5 日宣布不选连任，1 月 7 日 ICE 探员杀了 Renee Good，随后是 Alex Pretti。六名联邦助理检察官辞职抗议，其中包括福利诈骗案的主诉检察官；一个月后又走了八名。特朗普的司法部要求他们去查 Good 的遗孀和国内恐怖主义的关联，并停止调查 Good 之死是否正当。也就是说，共和党拿来攻击民主党的那套诈骗叙事，正是被他们自己拆掉的检察机器在办。

结尾处那位朋友说得对：aftermath？它从来没结束。差额在这里——政府本该帮人，有人拿它谋私，该付代价的人却按肤色被挑选。骗子里的白人不在广告里，这个缺席本身就是一条新闻。

One word is pressing down on Minnesota's races: the fraud. The article is precise about it. Biden's Justice Department uncovered the Feeding Our Future network in September 2022, charging 47 defendants for billing the government for millions of nonexistent meals served to tens of thousands of imaginary children, taking between $250 and $300 million from taxpayers. Republicans now use it as a club. Their ads show grainy images of arrestees from the Somali community. Many ringleaders in the embezzlement web were white. The ads feature none of them.

This is meta-violence in its standard form: the fact doesn't need to be denied, only reassigned a face. Whoever's face gets attached carries the explanation. Ellison is Black and an outspoken progressive, so the target on his back is large and inviting. The ads use mugshots of the Black defendants and none of the white ones. The formula is almost a century old, as the piece itself notes.

The sharpest line in the reporting: voters are not angry, they are offended. Anger can point at fraudsters. Offense needs someone to hold responsible. That is the switch Republicans are betting on, routing disappointment in government toward suspicion of Black people and Somali neighbors. But the article also records the resistance: Tabke says people don't like it when you make a racial thing, and a woman from Rosemont is disgusted by the lumping together of arrestees with "our Somali neighbors."

What actually hurts Democrats is not the fraud itself but the older narrative it collides with: liberals are loose with money and soft on minorities. A senior Democratic staffer says it plainly, the story is proving the Republicans right. Conspiracy needs no malice, only someone willing to take one step along an existing stereotype. The fraud is real. The failure is real. Packaging both into Black people raiding your coffers is another layer of violence, and both parties are consuming it.

Almost buried under the fraud is another thread: Walz announced on January 5 that he would not seek re-election. On January 7, an ICE agent murdered Renee Good, then Alex Pretti. Six federal assistant U.S. attorneys resigned in protest, including the lead prosecutor in the welfare fraud investigations; eight more left a month later. Trump's DOJ asked them to investigate Good's widow for ties to domestic terrorism and to stop investigating whether the shooting was warranted. The prosecutorial machine Republicans now attack Democrats with is the one they themselves dismantled.

The friend at the end is right: aftermath? It never ended. Here is the gap. Government is supposed to help people. Someone used it for private gain. The people who should pay are being selected by skin color. The white fraudsters are absent from the ads, and that absence is itself the story.
