1% 的施舍与 270 亿的黑洞：战争叙事的精准喂养1% Alms and a $27B Hole: The Precision Feeding of War Narratives

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-26 § 链接
战争中的资源分配不是能力问题，而是权力对存在性的定价。
Resource allocation in war is not about capacity, but the pricing of existence by power.

泽连斯基在求援，但他求的是 1% 到 5% 的份额差。在国际政治的博弈场里，这种“小批量”的交付不是为了赢得战争，而是一种精准的 weaponized 表达：它在 cultural 层面上维持了“西方在支持乌克兰”的叙事，但在 structural 层面上却确保了乌克兰永远处于一种“刚好能撑住，但绝不能反杀”的受控状态。这种 1% 的施舍，本质上是在用最低成本维持一个认知入口，让全球观众相信这场战争还在进行，而实际上，决定权在那些掌控 Patriot 生产线的人手里。

更讽刺的是内部的互撕。前国防部长 Fedorov 说乌克兰在慢慢输，而总统办公室反击说他离职后才变负面，并甩出一个 270 亿美金的预算黑洞。这典型的就是一场关于“谁该为失败买单”的存在性战争。当资源匮乏到极致，内部的共谋者们开始通过定义对方的“无能”来确立自己的“合法性”。

至于那个为俄罗斯而战的英国渔夫，他的死亡是这出剧目中最底层的注脚。一个 33 岁的底层男性，在缺乏认知能力的情况下，被卷入一个由元暴力驱动的战争机器，最终被无人机这个现代化的直接暴力（direct violence）抹除。他以为自己在参与某种宏大叙事，实际上他只是被俄方作为廉价消耗品的一次定价。在权力的棋盘上，无论是 1% 的导弹还是一个误入歧途的渔夫，都只是被计算好的损耗。

Zelenskyy is pleading for a shift from 1% to 5% of production. In the game of international politics, such "small batches" are not intended to win the war, but are a form of weaponized expression: they maintain the cultural narrative that "the West supports Ukraine," while structurally ensuring Kyiv remains in a controlled state of "just barely surviving, but never overcoming." This 1% alms is simply the lowest cost to maintain a cognitive entry point, convincing the global audience that the fight continues, while the actual kill-switch remains with those controlling the Patriot production lines.

Even more cynical is the internal cannibalism. Former Defense Minister Fedorov claims Ukraine is slowly losing, while the President's office counters by citing a $27bn budget hole. This is a textbook existential war over "who pays for the failure." When resources are depleted, complicitors within the system attempt to establish their own legitimacy by defining the other's "incompetence."

Then there is the British fisherman who died fighting for Russia—the lowest footnote of this drama. A 33-year-old working-class male, devoid of critical cognitive tools, was sucked into a war machine driven by meta-violence, only to be erased by a drone—the ultimate tool of direct violence. He believed he was part of a grand narrative; in reality, he was just a cheap consumable priced by the Russian state. On the board of power, whether it is 1% of missiles or a misguided fisherman, everything is just a calculated loss.
