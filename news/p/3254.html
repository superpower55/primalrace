MI6的“顶级”排名与特工世界的共谋游戏MI6's 'Top' Ranking and the Complicity Game of the Spy World

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-04 § 链接
情报机构的排名不是能力赛，而是共谋者之间通过互换定义权完成的权力背书。
Intelligence rankings are not skill tests, but a mutual validation of power by co-conspirators swapping the right to define.

所谓的“同行评审” (peer review) 简直是最大的 scam。由 60 个来自 25 个国家的专业人士给彼此打分，这不叫评估，这叫共谋 (complicity)。在情报这种极度依赖秘密和信任的领域，所谓的“得分”其实是这些机构在元暴力 (meta violence) 逻辑下，通过互换定义权来确认彼此的合法性。MI6 拿了最高分，本质上是西方情报共同体在通过一种仪式感，确认谁在当前的全球博弈中拥有最强的“解释权”。

正文中提到的“渗透统治精英”和“擅长肮脏伎俩” (dirty tricks)，被包装成了某种专业能力的勋章。事实上，这正是典型的武器化表达 (weaponisation of expression) —— 将对他国主权和个体生命的结构性暴力 (structural violence)，通过“专业主义”的叙事入口，转化为一种值得称赞的“能力”。当一个前 CIA 官员称赞 MI6 “extraordinary” 时，他不是在评价效率，而是在确认一种基于男性中心叙事的、关于掌控与操纵的权力快感。

唯一值得关注的细节是 Blaise Metreweli 成为首位女性领导者。但在这种极度男性化的权力结构中，一个女性进入顶层，往往意味着她成为了这套共谋系统的最高级执行者。如果她的存在不能改变情报机构将人体客体化、将暴力正当化的底层逻辑，那么这种“突破”仅仅是结构性暴力在文化层面上的一次表演性让步。她拿到了票，但她依然在为那个名为“国家利益”的男性中心机器投票。

This so-called 'peer review' is a total scam. Having 60 professionals from 25 countries rate each other is not an assessment; it is complicity. In a field as reliant on secrecy and trust as intelligence, these 'scores' are simply a way for these agencies to validate their legitimacy through the exchange of definition rights under the logic of meta-violence. MI6 taking the top spot is essentially the Western intelligence community performing a ritual to confirm who holds the strongest 'right to interpret' in the current global game.

The mentions of 'penetrating ruling elites' and a 'taste for dirty tricks' are packaged as badges of professional expertise. In reality, this is a textbook weaponisation of expression—transforming structural violence against foreign sovereignty and individual lives into a commendable 'capability' via the narrative entry of 'professionalism.' When a former CIA official calls MI6 'extraordinary,' he isn't praising efficiency; he is confirming a power trip of control and manipulation rooted in a masculine-centric narrative.

The only detail worth noting is Blaise Metreweli becoming the first woman to lead MI6. However, in such an intensely masculine power structure, a woman reaching the top often means she has become the highest-level executor of this complicit system. Unless her presence changes the underlying logic that objectifies human bodies and justifies violence, this 'breakthrough' is merely a performative concession of structural violence at the cultural layer. She got the vote, but she is still voting for a masculine-centric machine called 'national interest.'
