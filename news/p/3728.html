日食眼镜：一场关于恐惧与定价权的快闪 scamEclipse Glasses: A Flash Scam of Fear and Pricing Power

哲学 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-12 § 链接
当生理恐惧被武器化，稀缺性就成了收割认知的入场券。
When biological fear is weaponized, artificial scarcity becomes the ticket to harvesting cognition.

这哪里是在报道天文奇观，这分明是在记录一场教科书级的认知入口抢夺战。一个简单的物理过滤工具，在“视网膜受损”的生物恐惧驱动下，迅速被包装成一种具有社交货币属性的“drop”商品。WhatsApp 群组、Reddit 线程，这些原本用于沟通的工具，在此刻变成了制造人为稀缺的武器化入口。

最讽刺的是这套定价逻辑。从 5 英镑到 20 英镑，价格的翻倍并非基于成本的增加，而是基于对“恐惧”的定价权。当人们在凌晨两点排队、跨城奔波时，他们消费的不再是眼镜，而是一种“我必须获得保护”的生存焦虑。这种焦虑被投机者精准捕捉，将一个基础的医疗防护用品变成了类似 K-pop 限量周边一样的博弈筹码。

而那些威胁店员、在群组里疯狂刷屏的行为，本质上是存在性战争的低端表现：个体试图通过攻击服务者来缓解自己失去“入场券”的挫败感。这整件事就是一个完美的 closed-loop：制造恐惧 $ ightarrow$ 垄断渠道 $ ightarrow$ 操纵价格 $ ightarrow$ 诱导抢购。至于那些所谓的“假货风险”，不过是给这场收割增加了另一层文化暴力，让人们在恐惧被骗的同时，更加疯狂地追求那个被认证的“正版”标签。

This isn't a report on a celestial event; it's a case study in the seizure of cognitive entry points. A simple physical filter, driven by the biological fear of 'retinal damage,' was rapidly rebranded as a 'drop' commodity with social currency. WhatsApp groups and Reddit threads, tools meant for communication, became weaponized entry points to manufacture artificial scarcity.

The pricing logic is the most cynical part. The jump from £5 to £20 isn't about cost; it's about the pricing power over fear. When people queue at 2 AM or travel across the country, they aren't buying glasses—they are consuming a survival anxiety of 'I must be protected.' Speculators captured this anxiety, turning basic medical protection into a gambling chip similar to limited-edition K-pop merch.

Those threatening employees and spamming groups are merely exhibiting the low-end side of an existential war: individuals attempting to alleviate the frustration of losing their 'ticket' by attacking service workers. The whole event is a perfect closed-loop: manufacture fear $ ightarrow$ monopolize channels $ ightarrow$ manipulate prices $ ightarrow$ induce panic buying. As for the 'counterfeit risks,' that's just another layer of cultural violence, making people crave the 'certified' label even more while they are terrified of being scammed.
