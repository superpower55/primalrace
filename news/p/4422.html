NYT 的“分裂”叙事：一场关于认知入口的权力防御战The NYT 'Divisive' Narrative: A Power Defense of Cognitive Entry Points

国际 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-24 § 链接
所谓的“团结”通常是既得利益者要求弱势者闭嘴的代名词。
'Unity' is usually a code word used by the powerful to demand silence from the marginalized.

《纽约时报》近期对进步派候选人密集使用 “divisive”（造成分裂）这个词，是一次典型的表达武器化 (weaponisation of expression) 操作。在 NYT 的叙事逻辑里，存在一个虚构的、充满美德的 “unity”（团结）原点，而任何试图挑战建制派、民主社会主义者或有色人种候选人的崛起，都被定义为对这个原点的破坏。这根本不是在报道新闻，而是在通过掌控认知入口 (cognitive entry point) 来制造事实。

这种叙事背后隐藏的是深层的元暴力 (meta violence)。NYT 所谓的“团结”，本质上是男性中心叙事在政治领域的延伸——即由精英阶层、亿万富翁捐赠者和建制派男性定义的“秩序”。在这种秩序中，温和派的妥协被包装成“理性”，而底层需求的激进表达被标记为“分裂”。当一个穆斯林候选人或一名民主社会主义者赢得初选，他们实际上是在存在性战争 (existential war) 中夺取了部分解释权，这触动了共谋者 (complicity) 们的利益：那些在建制派结构中分羹的政客与资本。

最讽刺的是，NYT 将这种权力防御伪装成对民主的担忧。它通过定义什么是“分裂”，实际上在执行一种文化暴力 (cultural violence)，试图让结构性弱势者内化这种焦虑，从而在博弈中选择一个“假.最优解”——即扮演一个温顺的、不激进的候选人以换取主流媒体的认可。但正如文中指出的，那个统一的美国从未存在过，所谓的“团结”不过是让所有人都按照既定剧本排队，好让顶层的权力结构在不被冒犯的情况下持续运转。

The New York Times' recent obsession with labeling progressive candidates as 'divisive' is a textbook case of the weaponisation of expression. In the NYT's logic, there exists a mythical, virtuous point of 'unity,' and any rise of democratic socialists or candidates of color is framed as a destruction of this origin. This isn't journalism; it is an attempt to manufacture reality by controlling the cognitive entry point.

Behind this narrative lies a deep-seated meta violence. The 'unity' championed by the NYT is essentially an extension of the masculine-centric narrative into politics—an 'order' defined by elites, billionaire donors, and establishment men. In this framework, the compromises of moderates are packaged as 'rationality,' while the radical expressions of grassroots needs are branded as 'divisive.' When a Muslim candidate or a democratic socialist wins a primary, they are seizing interpretative power in an existential war, threatening the interests of the complicity: the politicians and capital benefiting from the establishment structure.

Most ironically, the NYT disguises this power defense as a concern for democracy. By defining what is 'divisive,' it executes a form of cultural violence, attempting to make the structurally disadvantaged internalize this anxiety. The goal is to push them toward a 'fake optimal expression'—playing the role of a docile, non-radical candidate to gain mainstream approval. But as the article notes, that unified America never existed. 'Unity' is simply a demand that everyone follow the script so the top-tier power structure can operate without offense.
