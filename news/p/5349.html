知识产权是男人们用来垄断历史的围栏Intellectual Property as a Fence for Male Monopoly of History

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-01 § 链接
所谓的“版权保护”，本质是权力对历史解释权的再次圈地。
So-called 'Copyright Protection' is essentially a re-enclosure of the right to interpret history by power.

Oasis 赶紧起诉前录音师之子，试图阻止那些未公开录音被拍卖。这在法律上叫“知识产权保护”，但在存在性战争中，这叫“清理认知入口”。

这批录音里不仅有音乐，还有 Noel 和 Liam 之间那些 candid conversations。对于 Gallagher 兄弟这种以“男性气概”和“兄弟阋墙”为核心叙事的乐队来说，真实的对话是危险的。真实的对话会打破他们精心维护的、被武器化了的乐队人设。他们不能允许历史被一个前员工的儿子以 100 万英镑的价格卖给第三方，因为一旦所有权转移，解释权就失控了。

这又是典型的 masculine-centric narrative：我创造了作品，所以我拥有定义这段历史的绝对权力。录音师 Huw Richards 提供了技术支持，他的存在被简化为“工具”，而他的产出被定义为“财产”。这种逻辑与原初种族的殖民逻辑同构——通过定义谁是所有者，将贡献者的主体性彻底抹除。

法律成了这起共谋的执行工具。拍卖行失望，录音师之子妥协，而 Gallagher 兄弟继续在 Live ’27 的世界巡演中扮演他们想要扮演的角色。他们不是在保护艺术，而是在保护一个没有瑕疵的、可以被无限售卖的商业 scam。

Oasis rushed to sue the son of their former sound engineer to block the auction of unheard recordings. Legally, it's 'IP protection'; in the existential war, it's 'clearing the cognitive entry points'.

These tapes contain not just music, but candid conversations between Noel and Liam. For a band whose entire brand is weaponized around 'masculinity' and 'brotherly feud', raw dialogue is a liability. Real conversations threaten the curated personas they've built. They cannot allow history to be sold to a third party for £1 million, because once ownership shifts, the power to interpret the narrative is lost.

This is a textbook masculine-centric narrative: 'I created the work, therefore I own the absolute right to define this history.' Huw Richards provided the technical labor, but his role is reduced to a 'tool' and his output to 'property'. This logic is isomorphic to the colonization of the Primal Race—erasing the subject's agency by defining who the 'owner' is.

The law serves as the execution tool for this complicity. The auction house is disappointed, the son concedes, and the Gallagher brothers continue to perform their desired roles in the Live ’27 tour. They aren't protecting art; they are protecting a flawless, infinitely marketable commercial scam.
