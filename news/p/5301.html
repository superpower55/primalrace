用“反恐”掩盖的战争外包与叙事骗局The Terrorism Scam: Outsourcing War and Manufacturing Narratives

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-27 § 链接
战争叙事是最高级的武器化表达，它将地缘博弈包装成文明防卫。
War narratives are the ultimate weaponised expression, framing geopolitical gambles as defenses of civilization.

这起所谓的“恐怖袭击未遂”事件，是典型的武器化表达 (Weaponised Expression) 样本。从认知入口看，叙事路径极其标准：发现炸药 $ ightarrow$ 逮捕男性 $ ightarrow$ 关联伊朗 $ ightarrow$ 升级为“恐怖主义”。通过将一个地缘政治冲突中的报复行动定义为“恐怖主义”，权力持有者成功地将一场关于军事基地使用权的博弈，转化为一场关于“文明与野蛮”的道德战争。

最讽刺的共谋 (Complicity) 发生在特朗普和英国情报部门之间。情报部门在民众举报前毫无察觉，而特朗普却迅速跳出来声明已经“监视很久”，试图将一次纯粹的运气（ chance）包装成一次精准的 intelligence-led 胜利。这种对事实的重新定义，就是为了在存在性战争中夺取“掌控力”的解释权。对于他们来说，真相不重要，重要的是制造出一种“我们掌控全局”的真实感。

而真正的暴力发生在结构层 (Structural Violence)。英国政府允许美国使用 RAF Fairford 基地攻击伊朗，实际上是将英国领土变成了美国战争机器的外包节点。这种结构性的共谋，让英国平民在不知情的情况下成为了地缘战争的肉盾。当 85 户居民被紧急撤离时，他们面对的不是来自伊朗的威胁，而是英国政府将战争引入家园的决策结果。

所谓的“反恐”在这里成了最好的掩体。它掩盖了这样一个事实：当一个国家允许另一个国家在自己领土上部署轰炸机去攻击第三国时，它就已经主动参与了这场暴力的循环。在这种男性中心叙事 (Masculine-centric narrative) 的战争逻辑中，领土、基地和人体都被降格为可消耗的工具，而所有的代价都被冠以“国家安全”之名而被正当化。

This alleged "foiled terror plot" is a textbook case of Weaponised Expression. The cognitive entry point follows a predictable script: explosives found $ ightarrow$ men arrested $ ightarrow$ linked to Iran $ ightarrow$ labeled as "terrorism." By defining a retaliatory act in a geopolitical conflict as "terrorism," those in power transform a gamble over military base usage into a moral crusade of "civilization versus barbarism.

The most cynical Complicity occurs between Trump and UK intelligence. While the authorities were blind until a citizen called 999, Trump immediately claimed to have had them "under view for a long time." This is a blatant attempt to rebrand pure luck as an intelligence-led victory, seizing the interpretation of "control" in an existential war. For them, the fact is irrelevant; the goal is to manufacture a reality where they are the masters of the game.

Meanwhile, the real violence is Structural. By allowing the US to use RAF Fairford for strikes on Iran, the UK government has effectively outsourced its territory to the US war machine. This structural complicity turns British civilians into unwitting human shields for geopolitical ambitions. The evacuation of 85 households wasn't a response to an Iranian threat, but a consequence of a government decision to invite war into its own backyard.

"Counter-terrorism" serves as the perfect cover. It masks the reality that when a state permits another to launch bombers from its soil, it has already entered the cycle of violence. In this Masculine-centric narrative of war, territories, bases, and bodies are reduced to expendable tools, while all costs are legitimized under the banner of "national security."
