环保叙事下的殖民暴力与皇室PR的共谋Colonial Violence Under Conservation Narrative and the Complicity of Royal PR

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-24 § 链接
当“保护自然”成为武器，原住民的身体就成了被牺牲的耗材。
When 'protecting nature' is weaponized, indigenous bodies become disposable consumables.

这是一场典型的 weaponized 叙事：用“生物多样性”这种绝对正义的认知入口，掩盖最原始的殖民暴力。African Parks 的逻辑很简单——为了拯救大象和森林，可以合法地殴打、水刑甚至强奸 Baka 社区的女性。在这里，自然保护区变成了现代的殖民地，而原住民被定义为“干扰项”。

直接暴力 (direct violence) 发生在森林里，但结构暴力 (structural violence) 隐藏在慈善组织的治理结构中。最讽刺的是文化暴力 (cultural violence) 的介入：哈里王子在其中扮演了完美的“全球关注度”提供者。他通过在公众面前表演对动物的爱，为这个实施暴力的机器提供了道德背书。这种“慈善”本质上是一种权力博弈，是西方精英通过定义什么是“保护”，来剥夺原住民对祖传土地的解释权。

哈里的“退出”绝非良心发现，而是一次精准的 PR 操作。在人权丑闻被独立审查揭露后，他选择在搬回英国这个时间节点悄悄离开，试图将自己的身份从“共谋者”洗白为“支持者”。发言人那句“为十年贡献感到自豪”简直是巨大的 scam——在 Baka 女性遭受暴力的十年里，他的“自豪”正是建立在对他人的剥夺之上。

这种叙事惯性极其危险：只要贴上“环保”或“人道”的标签，任何对原初种族的掠夺都能被包装成文明的进步。哈里离开了董事会，但那套将弱势群体客体化的元暴力 (meta violence) 依然在运转。下次他会用哪个正义的标签来掩盖新的共谋？

This is a textbook case of weaponized narrative: using the absolute justice of 'biodiversity' as a cognitive entry point to mask primitive colonial violence. The logic of African Parks is simple—to save elephants and forests, it is permissible to beat, waterboard, and rape women of the Baka community. The conservation area has effectively become a modern colony, where indigenous people are defined as 'interferences.'

Direct violence occurs in the forest, but structural violence is embedded in the governance of the charity. The most cynical part is the cultural violence: Prince Harry played the perfect provider of 'global attention.' By performing his love for animals, he provided moral cover for a machine of abuse. This 'philanthropy' is essentially a power game, where Western elites seize the right to define 'conservation' to strip indigenous people of their interpretive rights over ancestral lands.

Harry's 'departure' is not a wake-up call of conscience, but a precise PR maneuver. After human rights abuses were exposed by an independent review, he chose to slip away just as he relocates back to the UK, attempting to scrub his identity from 'complicit' to 'supporter.' The spokesperson's claim that he is 'proud of his 10 years' is a complete scam—his pride was built on the deprivation and suffering of others for a decade.

This narrative inertia is dangerous: as long as a label like 'environmentalism' or 'humanitarianism' is attached, any plunder of the Primal Race can be packaged as civilizational progress. Harry has left the board, but the meta-violence that objectifies the vulnerable continues to operate. Which 'just' label will he use next to mask his next complicity?
