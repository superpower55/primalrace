用“精神”立法，给资本留门Legislating by 'Spirit' is Just Leaving the Door Open for Capital

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-10 § 链接
所有不限制总额的局部禁令，都是给共谋者准备的PR剧本。
Any partial ban that ignores the total cap is merely a PR script for complicity.

这出戏太熟悉了。工党政府在2024年承诺禁掉那些污染河流的自来水公司高管的bonus，在文化层面上制造了一场极其愉悦的“正义叙事”，让选民觉得污染者终于被惩罚了。但结果是，CEO和CFO的总薪酬反而上升了1.5%。

这就是典型的 structural violence：立法者在设计制度时，故意留下一个巨大的认知漏洞。他们禁掉了“绩效奖金”这个标签，却没敢触碰“总薪酬”这个底线。于是，资本迅速完成了表达的切换——奖金变成了“岗位津贴”，变成了“留任补偿”，变成了像Thames Water那样直接拍出一笔100万英镑的签约费。这种操作在金融圈早已是 standard procedure，只不过这次被搬到了水务行业。

最令人作呕的是政府事后的“震惊”。这种震惊是表演性的，是为了维持其在文化层面的道德高地。部长们难道真的以为只要改个名字，钱就不会流进高管的口袋？不，他们正是通过这种“漏洞百出”的立法，完成了一次与资本的深层共谋：政府拿到了“禁奖金”的政治正确，而高管们拿到了更稳固、更难以被监管的固定薪水。

在这种博弈中，唯一的受害者是那些忍受污染水源的公众。政府在用一种名为“改革”的武器化叙事，掩盖一个事实：他们根本没有意愿剥夺这些垄断资本的定价权。只要总额不减，所有的禁令都只是在给钱换个马甲。

This is a rerun of a tired play. In 2024, the Labour government promised to ban bonuses for water bosses who polluted rivers, crafting a highly satisfying 'justice narrative' at the cultural level to appease voters. The actual result? Total pay for CEOs and CFOs rose by 1.5%.

This is a textbook example of structural violence. The legislators designed a system with a deliberate cognitive loophole. They banned the label 'performance-related bonus' but refused to touch the bottom line of 'total remuneration.' Consequently, capital swiftly pivoted its expression—bonuses were rebranded as 'role-based allowances,' 'retention payments,' or, in the case of Thames Water, a blunt £1m signing fee. This was already standard procedure in the banking sector; it has simply been imported into the water industry.

Most nauseating is the government's subsequent 'shock.' This shock is performative, designed to maintain a moral high ground. Did the ministers truly believe that changing a name would stop the money from flowing into executives' pockets? No. Through this 'leaky' legislation, they completed a deep complicity with capital: the government secured the political correctness of 'banning bonuses,' while executives secured fixed pay that is more stable and harder to regulate.

In this game, the only victims are the public enduring polluted water. The government is using a weaponized narrative of 'reform' to mask a simple truth: they have zero intention of stripping the pricing power from these monopolies. As long as the total amount remains untouched, every ban is just helping the money change its costume.
