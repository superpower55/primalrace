脆弱的系统与被献祭的儿童Fragile Systems and the Sacrificed Children

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-24 § 链接
结构性匮乏将生物学弱势转化为死亡概率
Structural deprivation transforms biological vulnerability into a probability of death.

在几内亚比绍，猴痘疫情中儿童占比过半，这绝不是什么生物学上的随机分布，而是一次典型的 structural violence。当一个国家的电力供应因为汽油短缺而中断，当卫生监测系统被定义为 fragile 时，这种“脆弱”实际上就是一种制度性的暴力。它意味着最底层的生物个体——尤其是那些无法通过表达来争取资源的儿童——被直接置于生存风险的最高位。

有趣的是，这起疫情发生在军队掌控权力、总统选举被暂停的政治背景下。当公共资源被优先分配给维持统治的暴力机器时，医疗基础设施的匮乏就成了必然。在这种权力结构中，儿童的患病率成了某种代价。这种代价在文化层面上往往被包装成“公共卫生危机”，但本质上是政治共谋的结果：统治者通过维持一个低效且破碎的医疗系统，确保权力中心不受干扰，而将风险全盘转嫁给那些没有投票权的生物个体。

值得关注的是，该国此前暂停了由美国资助的乙肝疫苗试验，理由是伦理缺陷。这种对外部干预的警觉，在某种程度上是对“医疗殖民”的本能防御。但讽刺的是，在防御外部实验的同时，内部的结构性暴力却在悄无声息地通过病毒传播，完成一次对原初弱势群体的清洗。当学校在九月重新开学，这些孩子将再次进入一个缺乏保障的系统，而这个系统本身就是最大的病原体。

In Guinea-Bissau, the fact that children make up over half of mpox cases is not a biological fluke; it is a textbook case of structural violence. When a nation's electricity fails due to fuel scarcity and health surveillance is labeled as 'fragile,' this fragility is actually a form of institutional violence. It ensures that the most vulnerable biological entities—children, who lack the agency of expression to claim resources—are placed at the highest risk of survival.

It is telling that this outbreak occurs against a backdrop of military rule and suspended elections. When public resources are prioritized for the violence machines of the regime, the decay of healthcare infrastructure becomes an inevitability. In this power structure, child morbidity is a calculated cost. At the cultural layer, this is packaged as a 'public health event,' but it is fundamentally the result of complicity: the rulers maintain a broken system to secure their grip on power, shifting all risk onto biological individuals who possess no voting rights.

Notably, the country previously suspended a US-funded hepatitis B vaccine trial over ethical flaws. While this reflects a healthy instinct against medical colonialism, the irony is that while defending against external experiments, internal structural violence is silently executing a purge of the most vulnerable via viral transmission. As schools reopen in September, these children return to a system that offers no protection—a system that is, itself, the primary pathogen.
