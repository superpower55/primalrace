Double Cleansing: A New Scam for the BodyDouble Cleansing: A New Scam for the Body

性别 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-15 § 链接
The pursuit of 'squeaky clean' is a weaponized aesthetic for consumption.
The pursuit of 'squeaky clean' is a weaponized aesthetic for consumption.

所谓的“双重清洁”（Double Cleansing）从脸部蔓延到全身，本质上是一场精心设计的商业 scam。当 #ShowerTok 的博主们告诉你“这会改变你的生活”时，她们在推销的不是卫生，而是一种被武器化的审美（Weaponized Aesthetics）。通过定义什么是“真正的干净”，资本成功地将一个简单的生理行为转化为了一个复杂的、分步骤的消费仪式。

这套逻辑极其阴险：首先制造焦虑，暗示你之前的洗澡方式是“错误的”，甚至用“像用乳液洗澡”这种极端的类比来制造认知失调；接着提供解决方案——不仅要买两款产品，还要买一个“套装”（Bundle）。这正是典型的认知入口争夺战：通过重新定义“干净”的标准，让消费者在潜意识中将“步骤的繁琐”等同于“结果的有效”。

最讽刺的是，这种趋势在 Gen Z 中最盛行，这群最焦虑气候危机的人，却在浴室里花费四小时进行所谓的“Everything Shower”。这种矛盾揭示了文化层面的暴力：当主体性被消费主义剥夺，人们只能通过扮演一个“极致自律且精致”的角色来获得短暂的存在感。正如皮肤科医生所言，这种过度清洁是在摧毁皮肤屏障。但这不重要，因为在商业叙事中，破坏屏障导致的干燥和瘙痒，将成为下一步推销保湿霜和药膏的新入口。

这是一场完美的闭环共谋：社交媒体制造趋势 $ ightarrow$ 消费者内化焦虑 $ ightarrow$ 品牌提供产品 $ ightarrow$ 身体受损 $ ightarrow$ 消费者购买更多修复产品。在这个循环里，你的皮肤不是生命器官，而是一个被不断定价的消费界面。

The migration of 'Double Cleansing' from facial care to the entire body is nothing more than a meticulously designed commercial scam. When #ShowerTok influencers claim this 'will change your life,' they aren't selling hygiene; they are selling a Weaponized Aesthetic. By redefining what 'true cleanliness' means, capital has successfully transformed a simple biological act into a complex, multi-step consumption ritual.

The logic is insidious: first, it manufactures anxiety, suggesting your previous showering habits were 'wrong'—using extreme analogies like 'washing with lotion' to trigger cognitive dissonance. Then, it offers the solution: not just two products, but a 'bundle.' This is a classic battle for the cognitive entry point: by redefining the standard of 'clean,' consumers are conditioned to equate the complexity of the process with the effectiveness of the result.

It is profoundly ironic that this trend peaks among Gen Z, the generation most anxious about the climate crisis, yet spending four hours in an 'Everything Shower.' This contradiction reveals a layer of cultural violence: when subjectivity is stripped by consumerism, individuals attempt to reclaim a sense of existence by performing the role of the 'ultimately disciplined and refined' subject. As dermatologists warn, this over-cleansing destroys the skin barrier. But that is irrelevant to the commercial narrative, because the resulting dryness and irritation simply create a new entry point for selling moisturizers and ointments.

This is a perfect loop of complicity: social media creates the trend $ ightarrow$ consumers internalize the anxiety $ ightarrow$ brands provide the products $ ightarrow$ the body is damaged $ ightarrow$ consumers buy more repair products. In this cycle, your skin is no longer a biological organ; it is a priced consumption interface.
