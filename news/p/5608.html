战争叙事中的“自卫”是最高级的武器化表达，掩盖的是元暴力的共谋。
The 'self-defense' narrative is the ultimate weaponized expression, masking the complicity of meta-violence.

阿联酋与沙特在也门问题上的“破冰”，本质上是一场关于生存空间的紧急共谋 (complicity)。当胡塞武装的导弹开始威胁到利雅得的机场和能源设施时，这些海湾君主才意识到，他们之前在也门玩弄的“代理人战争”游戏，其代价最终由谁承担并不重要，重要的是谁在掌控解释权。

这篇新闻中充斥着典型的武器化叙事 (weaponized narrative)。沙特声称是在“自卫”，联合国安理会甚至通过声明背书其“自卫权”。但请记住，在加尔通的暴力三角中，这种所谓的“自卫”是 cultural layer 的伪装，目的是让 structural violence（资源剥夺、地缘操弄）和 direct violence（空袭、轰炸）看起来合法且自然。所谓的“自卫”，其实是统治阶级在保护他们的权力席位，而将战争的实际损耗——那些被流离失所的 20 万平民——定义为某种不可避免的“背景噪音”。

最讽刺的是，英国在法律风险与军事援助之间地反复横跳。2019 年的法院判定其武器出口违法，但现在面对沙特的请求，讨论的依然是如何提供更多援助。这证明了在国际政治的博弈中，所谓的人道主义法律只是一个可以被随时调整的认知入口。只要叙事能够被定义为“反恐”或“维持秩序”，任何规模的杀戮都能在共谋者的默契下被正当化。

也门成了这群男性统治者进行存在性战争的棋盘。无论是以阿联酋资助的“巨人旅”还是沙特的空军，他们争夺的是对这片土地的定义权，而也门人的生命则被彻底客体化为一种可消耗的资源。这场战争没有赢家，只有在元暴力 (meta violence) 驱动下，不断升级的杀戮循环。

The 'ice-breaking' between the UAE and Saudi Arabia over Yemen is essentially an emergency complicity based on shared fear. When Houthi missiles began threatening Riyadh's airports and energy infrastructure, these Gulf monarchs realized that the 'proxy war' game they played in Yemen had real costs—though they only care about who controls the narrative, not who pays the price.

This news is saturated with weaponized narratives. Saudi Arabia claims 'self-defense,' and the UN Security Council even endorses this 'right.' But according to the Violence Triangle, this 'self-defense' is a cultural layer disguise, designed to make structural and direct violence appear legitimate and natural. Their 'self-defense' is actually the protection of their own power seats, while the actual cost—the 200,000 displaced civilians—is dismissed as inevitable 'background noise.'

It is profoundly cynical to watch the UK oscillate between legal risks and military aid. The 2019 court ruling on unlawful arms exports is now just a technical hurdle to be bypassed. This proves that in international politics, humanitarian law is merely a cognitive entry point that can be adjusted. As long as the narrative is framed as 'counter-terrorism' or 'maintaining order,' any scale of slaughter can be justified through the complicity of powerful states.

Yemen has become a chessboard for the existential war of male rulers. Whether through the UAE-funded 'Giant Brigades' or Saudi air strikes, they are fighting for the right to define this land. The Yemeni people are completely objectified as consumable resources. There are no winners here, only a cycle of killing driven by meta-violence.
