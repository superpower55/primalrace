用“自愿”包装的种族清洗：一种典型的武器化叙事Ethnic Cleansing Wrapped in 'Voluntarism': A Classic Weaponization of Narrative

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-03 § 链接
将强迫驱逐定义为“自愿移民”，是文化暴力为结构暴力提供合法性的标准动作。
Defining forced expulsion as 'voluntary migration' is a standard move of cultural violence to legitimize structural violence.

这份 20 页的计划书最恶心的地方不在于它要驱逐 200 万人，而在于它使用了“自愿移民” (Voluntary Emigration) 这种 euphemism。这是典型的表达武器化：通过重新定义事实，将一个战争罪行伪装成一种“行政方案”。当一个群体在饥饿、轰炸和种族灭绝的绝境中被要求“自愿”离开时，这种“自愿”本身就是一种暴力。文化层面的叙事在这里成了直接暴力的掩体，试图让国际社会在认知入口上产生一种“这或许是个解决方案”的错觉。

Ben-Gvir 和 Netanyahu 的博弈逻辑非常简单：他们并不在乎所谓的“和平”，而是在进行一场关于“残酷度”的竞争。在以色列的选举逻辑里，谁能提供更激进的驱逐方案，谁就能在极右翼的 base 中获得更多票数。这不仅是政治博弈，更是一场关于“谁更有权定义这片土地”的存在性战争。他们通过将巴勒斯坦人彻底客体化——将其视为可以被“清除”的冗余数据，而非拥有主体性的人——来完成对该族群的终极剥夺。

而最令人作呕的共谋在于，这种计划竟然在试图寻求外部大国的“批准”。当“由任何手段促成移民”成为一种外交议题时，所谓的国际秩序就成了一场巨大的共谋场域。在这种叙事下，人权被简化为一种可以被交易的筹码。这再次证明了：只要不涉及对自身阵营的肉体杀戮，所有的“文明讨论”本质上都是在为元暴力寻找新的解释权。

The most repulsive part of this 20-page document is not the intent to expel 2 million people, but the use of the euphemism 'Voluntary Emigration.' This is a textbook weaponization of expression: by redefining facts, a war crime is disguised as an 'administrative plan.' When a population is asked to 'voluntarily' leave while facing famine, bombardment, and genocide, that 'voluntarism' is itself a form of violence. The cultural layer of narrative serves as a shield for direct violence, attempting to create a cognitive illusion for the international community that this might be a 'solution.'

The game logic of Ben-Gvir and Netanyahu is simple: they aren't interested in 'peace,' but are competing over the degree of cruelty. In the logic of Israeli elections, whoever offers the most radical expulsion plan gains more votes from the far-right base. This is not just political maneuvering; it is an existential war over who has the power to define the land. By completely objectifying Palestinians—treating them as redundant data to be 'cleared' rather than subjects with agency—they achieve the ultimate deprivation of a race.

The most sickening complicity lies in the attempt to seek 'approval' from global powers. When 'facilitating emigration by any means' becomes a diplomatic topic, the so-called international order turns into a massive field of complicity. In this narrative, human rights are reduced to tradable chips. This proves once again: as long as it doesn't involve the physical slaughter of one's own camp, all 'civilized discussions' are merely attempts to find new interpretative rights for meta-violence.
