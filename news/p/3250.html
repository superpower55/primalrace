Iceflation：被浪漫化叙事掩盖的定价权掠夺Iceflation: The Predatory Pricing Masked by Romantic Narratives

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-04 § 链接
所谓的“生活成本危机”，本质上是定价权者的单方面掠夺。
The so-called 'cost-of-living crisis' is essentially a unilateral plunder by those holding pricing power.

把冰淇淋涨价起名为“Iceflation”，这种轻盈的、带有幽默感的词汇本身就是一种 cultural violence。它通过将结构性的掠夺包装成一种生活方式的“趣闻”，诱导受众在轻笑中接受一个事实：你被宰了，但这是一个关于夏天、孩子和气候变化的“可爱”故事。

从加尔通的暴力三角来看，这里的 structural violence 隐藏在极高的定价权不对等中。冰淇淋车主将成本上升（牛奶、巧克力、柴油）直接且不透明地转嫁给消费者，而消费者在“度假”这种特定的 cultural 叙事中，被要求接受这种非理性的溢价。在伦敦博物馆外 7 英镑一个球的定价，根本不是什么“气候冲击”，而是一次精准的认知入口操纵：利用游客的短暂停留和对“度假体验”的心理预期，实施一次毫无掩饰的掠夺。

最荒诞的共谋在于，媒体在报道这种掠夺时，竟然试图用“吃冰淇淋能降低糖尿病风险”这种伪科学叙事来给这种剥削打补丁。这种逻辑极其恶劣：既然它对你有好处，那么被贵地宰一次也是值得的。这是一种典型的 weaponized narrative，试图用一个虚假的 biological benefit 来抵消经济上的 structural loss。

不要被那些俏皮的 jingles 欺骗。当一个产品的定价不再基于价值而是基于“你能忍受多少被宰”时，这已经不是经济问题，而是一场关于存在性价值的博弈。你支付的不是冰淇淋的钱，而是你在这种不对等权力结构中作为“被收割者”的入场券。

Naming the surge in ice-cream prices 'Iceflation' is a form of cultural violence. By packaging structural predation as a lighthearted 'lifestyle anecdote,' it lures the audience into accepting a brutal fact through a chuckle: you are being ripped off, but it's just a 'cute' story about summer, children, and climate change.

Applying Galtung's Violence Triangle, the structural violence here is hidden in the extreme asymmetry of pricing power. Vendors transfer rising costs (milk, chocolate, diesel) directly and opaquely to consumers, who are conditioned by the cultural narrative of 'vacationing' to accept irrational premiums. Charging £7 for a single scoop outside the British Museum isn't a 'climate shock'; it's a precise manipulation of the cognitive entry point, exploiting the transience of tourists and their psychological expectations of the 'holiday experience.'

The most cynical complicity lies in the media's attempt to patch this exploitation with pseudo-scientific narratives, suggesting that ice-cream lowers diabetes risk. This logic is vile: since it's 'good for you,' being ripped off is a price worth paying. This is a classic weaponized narrative, attempting to use a fake biological benefit to offset a structural economic loss.

Do not be deceived by catchy jingles. When the price of a product is no longer based on value but on 'how much exploitation you can tolerate,' it ceases to be an economic issue and becomes a game of existential value. You aren't paying for ice-cream; you are paying an admission fee to be the 'harvested' in an asymmetrical power structure.
