用“惊叹”掩盖的生态殖民与认知入口Ecological Colonialism Masked by 'Wonder'

哲学 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-13 § 链接
将生物客体化为“奇观”的叙事，是典型的元暴力延伸。
Narratives that objectify biological life as 'spectacle' are extensions of meta-violence.

《卫报》这种年度评选本质上是一场关于“认知入口”的权力游戏。它把无脊椎动物——这些在生物墙之外、完全不具备人类表型认同的生命——简化为一种“mindblowing”的奇观。当科学编辑和记者们在讨论谁该“登顶”时，他们其实是在行使一种解释权：定义什么是“不可思议”的，从而决定哪些生命值得被公众关注，哪些可以继续在结构性暴力中被抹除。

这种叙事极其危险，因为它把生态保护变成了某种“审美竞赛”。在元暴力的逻辑里，如果一个物种不够“惊艳”，它在文化层面的存在感就趋近于零，进而导致其在结构层面的资源分配（如保护资金、立法优先级）被进一步剥夺。这和把女性身体性化为“资源”或“产品”的逻辑同构：只有符合观察者（通常是男性中心叙事下的权力持有者）审美或好奇心的客体，才能获得短暂的“可见性”。

不要被这种看似温情的科普外壳欺骗。如果保护一个物种的前提是它必须足够“mindblowing”，那么这依然是一场关于“定义权”的殖民。真正的公正表达应该是承认这些生命本身的存在价值，而不是把它们变成人类社交货币中的一个投票选项。这种所谓的“年度评选”，不过是文化暴力在自然界的一次温和演习。

The Guardian's annual competition is essentially a power game over the 'cognitive entry point.' It reduces invertebrates—beings existing entirely outside the human biological wall—into a 'mindblowing' spectacle. When editors and reporters debate who should 'come top,' they are exercising the power of interpretation: defining what is 'incredible,' and thus deciding which lives deserve public attention and which can remain erased by structural violence.

This narrative is dangerous because it transforms ecological conservation into a form of 'aesthetic competition.' In the logic of meta-violence, if a species isn't 'stunning' enough, its cultural presence shrinks to zero, leading to further deprivation of structural resources, such as funding and legislative priority. This is isomorphic to the logic of sexualizing the female body as a 'resource' or 'product': only objects that satisfy the aesthetic or curiosity of the observer (typically the power-holder in a masculine-centric narrative) gain temporary visibility.

Do not be deceived by this gentle, popular-science veneer. If the prerequisite for protecting a species is that it must be 'mindblowing,' it remains a colonization of the 'right to define.' A Just Expression would be to acknowledge the inherent value of these lives, rather than turning them into a voting option for human social currency. This so-called 'annual election' is merely a mild rehearsal of cultural violence played out in the natural world.
