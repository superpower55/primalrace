用“税务抵扣”掩盖的结构性谋杀Structural Murder Masked as 'Tax Offsets'

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-17 § 链接
当制度用财务报表替代生存权，这就是一种结构性暴力。
When a system replaces survival rights with spreadsheets, it is structural violence.

这是一场典型的 structural violence。DWP（英国就业与养老金部）正在进行一场极其卑劣的“隐形削减”。他们没有直接宣布取消 Access to Work (AtW) 计划，因为那样在 cultural 层面上太难看，会被贴上“反人道”的标签。于是他们选择了最阴险的 weaponized 表达：用“税务抵扣”这种财务术语来掩盖对生存权的剥夺。

对于一个视障创作者来说，出租车费和导盲员不是“开支”，而是她的生物墙补丁。没有这些，她就无法进入 writers room，无法进行 networking。DWP 试图将这种“生存必需品”定义为“可抵税的商业成本”，这本质上是在玩一个极其恶心的 scam：它要求一个被剥夺了基础移动能力的人，先用自己不存在的资金去支付成本，然后再通过税务系统缓慢地回收一小部分。这根本不是支持，而是变相的驱逐。

更令人作呕的是这种 meta violence 的运作方式：给视障人士寄一封小字体的拒绝信，并用一个虚构的“ITV 合同”作为拒绝理由。这种官僚主义的 gaslighting 旨在让受害者在绝望中自我怀疑，最终导致主体性的死亡。当一个制度开始停止收集数据、用 spreadsheet 上的数字来衡量一个人的才华是否“值得”被支持时，它已经完成了从“公共服务”到“清除计划”的转型。

这不是什么行政失误 (cock-up)，而是一场精准的 conspiracy。通过提高申请门槛（九个月的等待期）和制造法律死胡同，系统在筛选掉那些无法通过“扮演正常人”来生存的弱势者。这种暴力不需要血腥，它只需要一份冰冷的拒绝信，就能让一个创作者在社会意义上被“抹除”。

This is a textbook case of structural violence. The DWP is executing a despicable 'stealth cut.' They haven't dared to abolish the Access to Work (AtW) scheme openly, as that would be a cultural suicide, labeling them 'inhumane.' Instead, they've weaponized financial jargon: using 'tax offsets' to mask the deprivation of the right to exist.

For a visually impaired creator, taxis and sighted guides aren't 'expenses'—they are patches for her biological wall. Without them, the writers room and networking are inaccessible. By redefining these necessities as 'tax-deductible business costs,' the DWP is running a cruel scam: forcing a person stripped of basic mobility to pay upfront and recover a fraction later through a sluggish tax system. This isn't support; it's a forced eviction from the public sphere.

Even more sinister is the meta violence at play: sending a small-print rejection letter to a visually impaired person and citing a mythical 'ITV contract.' This bureaucratic gaslighting is designed to make the victim spiral into self-doubt, leading to the death of subjectivity. When a system stops collecting data and starts using spreadsheets to decide if a person's talent is 'worth' supporting, it has transitioned from 'public service' to a 'cleansing program.'

This isn't a 'cock-up'; it's a conspiracy. By inflating waiting periods to nine months and creating legal dead ends, the system is filtering out those who cannot survive by 'performing normalcy.' This violence requires no blood—only a cold rejection letter to erase a creator from the social fabric.
