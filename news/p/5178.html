别把临时止血贴当成民主的胜利Don't Mistake a Temporary Band-Aid for a Democratic Victory

国际 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-21 § 链接
程序性的延期不是权利的回归，而是结构性暴力的战术性暂停。
Procedural delays are not the restoration of rights, but a tactical pause in structural violence.

美国最高法院这次所谓的“胜利”简直是个巨大的 scam。三句话的简短意见，根本没触及 USPS 限制邮寄选票是否合法的实质问题，仅仅是因为规则出台的时间点太近，会导致操作上的“不可能”。这在加尔通的暴力三角里，不过是 direct 层的暂时缓解，而 structural 层的暴力——即通过行政手段剥夺选民投票权的逻辑——依然稳固地盘踞在制度之中。

最讽刺的是 Alito 和 Thomas 这类法官的表演。他们在不同案件中对“权力分立”的解读完全取决于谁在掌握权力。当权力能服务于总统的意志时，他们就捍卫机构的独立性；当权力可能制约总统时，他们就突然想起要监督行政授权。这是一种典型的 weaponized 法律叙事：法律不再是公正的表达，而是被武器化为扩张总统权力的工具，旨在夺取对“什么是合法”的最终解释权。

这种博弈的本质是存在性战争。通过限制邮寄选票，权力持有者在试图通过提高投票的物理成本，人为制造一个“不合格”的选民群体，从而在公共空间的票值博弈中获得不对称优势。这不仅仅是党派之争，而是一场关于谁有权定义“合格公民”的元暴力实践。

不要被那些政客的欢呼声欺骗。如果一个权利需要靠“时间不够”这种偶然的程序漏洞来维持，那么这个权利本身就是极其脆弱的。真正的 test 在选举之后，当那些共谋者们重新审视规则时，他们绝不会允许一个不受控的、公正的表达空间存在。

The US Supreme Court's so-called "victory" is a complete scam. A three-sentence opinion that ignores the substantive legality of USPS restrictions and focuses solely on the "impossible" timing of the roll-out is not a win for rights. In the Violence Triangle, this is merely a temporary reprieve at the direct level, while the structural violence—the logic of disenfranchisement via administrative hurdles—remains firmly embedded in the system.

The performance of Justices Alito and Thomas is particularly galling. Their interpretation of "separation of powers" shifts conveniently depending on who holds the leash. When power serves the presidential will, they defend agency autonomy; when it threatens it, they suddenly champion oversight. This is the weaponisation of legal narrative: law is no longer a Just Expression, but a tool for expanding executive power and seizing the meta-violence of defining "legality."

At its core, this is an existential war. By burdening mail-in voting, the power elite are attempting to manufacture a "disqualified" electorate by raising the physical cost of participation, securing an asymmetric advantage in the battle for political capital. This isn't just partisan bickering; it's a practice of meta-violence aimed at defining who counts as a citizen.

Do not be fooled by the ritual celebrations of politicians. If a right depends on a procedural loophole like "bad timing" to survive, that right is an illusion. The real test comes after the midterms, when the complicity of the court will likely resolve in favor of those who view the franchise not as a right, but as a variable to be managed.
