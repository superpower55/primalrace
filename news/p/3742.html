被定价的嘴唇与“母亲”的退场Priced Lips and the Exit of the 'Mother'

性别 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-12 § 链接
女性在权力结构中的最优解表达，往往是扮演一个完美的工具人。
The optimal expression for women in power structures is often playing the perfect tool.

Karoline Leavitt 的离职声明是一场标准的、充满温情的 cultural violence。在“为了孩子”这个永恒的浪漫叙事掩盖下，一个 28 岁的女性在权力巅峰完成了她的工具人周期。她被 Trump 定义为“明星”，但这种定义的入口极其恶心：不是她的策略，而是她的 face, brain 和 lips。当一个女性的表达能力被比作“机关枪”且与她的嘴唇形状挂钩时，她的主体性已经死亡，她被物化为一个高效的、具有装饰性的发声机器。

Leavitt 的职业路径是典型的“假.最优解表达”：通过极端的忠诚、对异己的攻击（如辱骂记者为 leftwing hack）以及对男性权力中心的绝对顺从，换取了历史上最年轻白宫新闻秘书的席位。她扮演了父权结构最认可的角色——一个强悍但绝对忠诚的执行者。然而，这种“强悍”是武器化的，是替男性权力者挥舞的鞭子。在这种博弈中，她获得的权力并非来自她自身，而是来自她作为 Trump 意志延伸的效能。

最讽刺的是，当她因为生育而陷入 Motherhood Penalty，在“最佳母亲”与“最佳秘书”之间做选择时，她选择了退场。在男本位叙事中，女性的生育能力永远是结构性弱势的生物墙，而男性（如 Trump）则在对此进行点评的同时，继续占据决策席位。所谓的“顶级外部顾问”不过是权力结构在给予工具人一份体面的安置费，以维持其“爱护女性”的 PR 形象。

她离开后，接替她的人大概率依然会是一个能被 Trump 评价“嘴唇好看”且能高效执行指令的女性。因为在元暴力的共谋下，这种“成功女性”的模版才是这个系统最想要的。Leavitt 赢了短期博弈，但她输掉了存在性战争：她从未被当作一个独立的政治主体，而是一个被精准定价的零件。

Karoline Leavitt's resignation is a textbook case of cultural violence wrapped in warmth. Under the eternal romantic narrative of "spending time with family," a 28-year-old woman completed her cycle as a tool in the corridors of power. Trump defined her as a "star," but the entry point of this definition is repulsive: not her strategy, but her face, brain, and lips. When a woman's expression is compared to a "machine gun" and linked to the shape of her lips, her subjectivity is dead; she has been objectified into an efficient, decorative speaking machine.

Leavitt's career path represents a "fake optimal expression": exchanging extreme loyalty, attacks on dissidents (calling journalists "leftwing hacks"), and absolute submission to the masculine power center for the title of the youngest Press Secretary. She played the role most approved by the patriarchal structure—a fierce but absolutely loyal executor. However, this "fierceness" was weaponized; she was merely wielding the whip for the man in power. In this game, her power derived not from herself, but from her efficiency as an extension of Trump's will.

The irony peaks as she faces the Motherhood Penalty, forced to choose between being the "best mom" and the "best secretary." She chose to exit. In the masculine-centric narrative, female fertility remains a structural biological wall, while the man continues to hold the decision-making seat while commenting on her departure. The title of "top outside advisor" is nothing more than a severance package to maintain the PR image of "caring for women."

Whoever succeeds her will likely be another woman whose lips are "beautiful" and who can execute orders efficiently. Under the complicity of meta-violence, this template of the "successful woman" is exactly what the system desires. Leavitt won the short-term game, but lost the existential war: she was never treated as an independent political subject, but as a precisely priced part.
