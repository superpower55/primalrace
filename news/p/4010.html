炸弹、白旗与被物化的中东棋盘Bombs, White Flags, and the Objectified Middle East Chessboard

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-17 § 链接
战争叙事是元暴力的最高形式：将主权国家简化为可丢弃的筹码。
War narratives are the ultimate meta-violence: reducing sovereign nations to disposable bargaining chips.

特朗普威胁要轰炸阿曼，如果它“挡路”的话。这句话的恐怖之处不在于炸弹本身，而在于它极其自然地揭示了男性中心叙事（masculine-centric narrative）如何运作：世界被简化为一个由强权定义、通过掠夺和屈服来达成共识的博弈场。在特朗普的认知入口里，没有所谓的“外交”或“主权”，只有“赢”和“投降” (surrender)。

这是一种典型的元暴力。在这种叙事中，阿曼、伊朗甚至加沙的平民，都被客体化为棋盘上的筹码。所谓的“和平路线图”或“15点计划”，本质上不是为了消除暴力，而是为了重新分配解释权——谁能定义什么是“真正的解除武装”，谁能决定撤军的节奏。这种“制造可能性的艺术”其实是武器化的表达：用所谓的“和平”作为掩体，掩盖其核心是强迫对方接受一个不对等的、被定义好的“最优解”。

更讽刺的是，在这种宏大叙事的背后，是极度的共谋。无论是通过秘密渠道接触的伊朗革命卫队，还是在开罗会面的库什纳，所有参与者都在这场存在性战争中寻找自己的生存位。而真正的受害者——那些在加沙被推土机铲平房屋的平民，或者在也门面临全面战争威胁的人们——在这些“大人物”的博弈中完全失去了表达权。他们的生命被量化为谈判桌上的筹码，这种 structural violence 在“国家安全”和“地缘政治”的文化掩盖下，变得如此理所当然，以至于被大众接受为某种“必要的代价”。

当一个人可以随口宣布要“把对方炸得体无完肤”时，他实际上是在宣布：我的意志就是事实，而你的存在仅取决于我对你的利用价值。

Trump threatens to bomb Oman if it "gets in the way." The horror here isn't just the bombs, but how naturally it reveals the inner workings of a masculine-centric narrative: the world is simplified into a game of power, where consensus is reached only through plunder and submission. In Trump's cognitive entry point, there is no "diplomacy" or "sovereignty," only "winning" and "surrender."

This is textbook meta-violence. In this narrative, Oman, Iran, and the civilians of Gaza are objectified as mere chips on a chessboard. So-called "peace roadmaps" or "15-point plans" are not designed to eliminate violence, but to redistribute the power of interpretation—who defines "genuine disarmament" and who controls the tempo of withdrawal. This "art of manufacturing possibilities" is simply weaponized expression: using "peace" as a cover to force others into a predefined, asymmetrical "optimal expression."

More ironic is the deep complicity involved. Whether it's the IRGC via back channels or Kushner in Cairo, every player is seeking their own survival slot in this existential war. Meanwhile, the true victims—Palestinians watching their homes be bulldozed in Gaza or Yemenis facing full-scale war—are completely stripped of their expression. Their lives are quantified as leverage, a form of structural violence that is made to seem natural and "necessary" under the cultural cloak of "national security" and "geopolitics."

When a man can casually announce he will "bomb the shit out of" a nation, he is declaring: my will is the fact, and your existence depends entirely on your utility to me.
