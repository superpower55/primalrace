马斯克的“言论自由”只是一个定价后的 ScamMusk's 'Free Speech' is Nothing But a Priced Scam

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-04 § 链接
所谓的言论自由在权力面前，不过是一场关于解释权的商业定价博弈。
Free speech is merely a commercial pricing game over the right of interpretation when facing power.

马斯克在收购 X 之后，把“言论自由” (Free Speech) 包装成了一种具有神圣感的认知入口，试图在公共空间建立一个不被审查的乌托邦。但这次对沙特异议人士的 geoblocking 撕掉了这张皮：所谓的自由，在面对主权国家的行政指令和资本利益时，迅速退化为一种可以被定价的商品。

这在我的框架里是典型的文化暴力 (cultural violence) 掩盖结构暴力 (structural violence)。X 之前声称“捍卫用户声音”，这是一种 weaponized 叙事，用来吸引那些厌恶传统审查的流量；而当它执行沙特政府的指令时，则通过“遵守当地法律”这种中立的法律术语来完成共谋 (complicity)。这种从“自由斗士”到“政权帮凶”的无缝切换，证明了马斯克并不在乎任何公正的表达 (Just Expressions)，他只在乎他的最优解表达——即在不影响平台估值的前提下，最大限度地降低与权力中心的摩擦成本。

最讽刺的是，沙特政府用来封杀异议者的理由是“侵犯公共秩序、宗教价值和公共道德”。这些词汇是典型的元暴力 (meta violence) 伪装，将权力对主体性的剥夺定义为对“道德”的守护。而 X 及其背后的资本，通过技术手段将这种元暴力结构化，让流亡海外的活动家在数字空间里再次经历一次被殖民的快感。

不要被“自由”这个词给骗了。当一个平台的定义权掌握在一个人手里，且这个人习惯于通过博弈来获取最大利益时，这个平台本身就是一个巨大的 scam。这次封杀不是个案，而是一个信号：在这个全球化的共谋场域中，资本对权力的顺从速度，永远快于它对自由的承诺。

Since acquiring X, Elon Musk has weaponized 'Free Speech' as a cognitive entry point, attempting to build a sanctuary free from censorship. However, the geoblocking of Saudi dissidents strips away this facade: so-called freedom rapidly degenerates into a priced commodity when confronted with sovereign administrative orders and capital interests.

This is a textbook case of cultural violence masking structural violence. X's previous claims of 'defending the voice of users' were a weaponized narrative designed to attract traffic from those averse to traditional censorship. When executing Saudi government orders, it completes its complicity by using neutral legal jargon like 'complying with local laws.' This seamless transition from 'freedom fighter' to 'regime lackey' proves that Musk cares nothing for Just Expressions; he only cares about his own optimal expression—minimizing friction with power centers while maintaining platform valuation.

Most ironic is the Saudi government's justification for silencing dissent: 'infringing on public order, religious values, and public morals.' These terms are classic disguises for meta-violence, defining the deprivation of subjectivity as a defense of 'morality.' X and its underlying capital structuralize this meta-violence through technology, forcing exiled activists to experience a digital re-colonization.

Do not be fooled by the word 'freedom.' When the power of definition is held by a single individual who views everything as a game of stakes, the platform itself becomes a massive scam. This blackout is not an isolated incident, but a signal: in this global field of complicity, capital's speed of submission to power always outpaces its promise of liberty.
