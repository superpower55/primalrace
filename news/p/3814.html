用三周 AI 训练营掩盖的结构性抛弃The Structural Abandonment Masked by AI Boot Camps

中国科技 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-13 § 链接
用工具的更新掩盖能力的贬值，是典型的 PR 式结构暴力。
Replacing systemic collapse with tool updates is a classic form of PR-driven structural violence.

这出戏的剧本极其标准：面对失业率攀升的 NEET 群体，政府抛出一个充满科技感的“AI Boot Camp”作为救命稻草。逻辑很简单——年轻人失业是因为没掌握 AI，只要经过三周的训练，就能从“被 AI 取代”变成“掌控 AI”。

但这纯粹是一个 scam。AI 真正威胁的是 entry-level 的 grunt work，也就是那些原本由新人承担的、用于积累经验的基础工作。当这些工作被 AI 吞噬，新人的“入场券”消失了。政府试图通过三周的培训给年轻人发一张假票，让他们以为能进入职场，但实际上，决定一个人是否被雇佣的定价权在企业手中，而企业现在需要的不是能用 ChatGPT 的初级员工，而是能直接产出结果的 AI 自动化流程。

最讽刺的是，这个计划依赖于 Microsoft 和 OpenAI 等美国巨头的工具。这不仅是认知入口的让渡，更是将底层失业者的生存希望寄托在那些正在加速消灭他们岗位的算法之上。所谓的“职场技能培训”——比如考勤和办公软件，不过是把年轻人重新规训成合格的、低成本的数字零件。

这不是救赎，而是一次表演性的让步。它把结构性的就业危机（Structural Violence）转化为个体的“技能不足”问题，从而将失败的责任重新推给年轻人：如果你还是没工作，那一定是因为你没学好 AI。

The script is textbook: facing a surge in NEETs, the government tosses out a high-tech 'AI Boot Camp' as a lifeline. The logic is simple—youth unemployment is a skill gap; master AI, and you move from being 'replaced' to 'controlling.'

This is a complete scam. AI is primarily cannibalizing the 'grunt work'—the entry-level tasks that historically served as the training ground for newcomers. When these roles vanish, the 'entry ticket' to the professional world disappears. The government is issuing fake tickets via a three-week course, pretending it grants access, while the actual pricing power remains with corporations who no longer need junior staff to prompt a chatbot, but rather automated workflows that bypass juniors entirely.

It is peak irony that this scheme relies on tools from US giants like Microsoft and OpenAI. This is not just a surrender of cognitive entry points; it is anchoring the survival of the unemployed to the very algorithms accelerating their obsolescence. The inclusion of 'workplace skills' like timekeeping is merely a re-disciplining of youth into low-cost digital components.

This is not a rescue; it is a performative concession. It transforms a systemic crisis of structural violence into a narrative of individual deficiency. The message is clear: if you remain unemployed, it is not the system's failure, but your failure to 'learn AI' fast enough.
