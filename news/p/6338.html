调查权用来报复，法律用来表演Oversight as Retaliation, Law as Performance

其他 结构层 · 元暴力 Truthout ↗ 2026-11-01 § 链接
共谋者不制造暴力，他们制造程序。
Co-conspirators don't make violence; they make procedure.

Comer 的调查不是调查，是报复。Free DC 让 DoorDash 撤回了对 HR 9720 的支持，不到两周，众议院监督委员会主席就宣布调查 Free DC 和 Harriet's Wildest Dreams。顺序就是证据：先有组织施压赢了，后有官方调查启动。这不是 oversight，这是把国家权力当私人打手用。

HR 9720 本身已经够露骨了。它要求国会两院批准 D.C. 市议会通过的每一项加税或收费——等于把地方财政权收归联邦。D.C. 居民没有投票权的参议员，没有州权，连改自己税法的能力都要被国会掐住。这不是政策分歧，这是结构暴力在立法层面的标准操作：把一群人治理自己的入口堵死，然后说这是程序。

Comer 给调查找的理由是“左翼活动团体搞政治化的陪审团废法”。陪审团废法（jury nullification）是陪审员享有的宪法权力，历史上废奴主义者和 LGBTQ+ 权利运动都用过。Paul Butler 说得很清楚：这是宪法权力，有悠久历史。Comer 把它描述成“渗透陪审团、无罪释放罪犯”，等于先定罪再调查。Linda Gustitus 指出，这种预设立场的新闻稿恰恰是人们失去对监督信心的原因。监督程序被武器化成了表演——不是找事实，是制造事实。

这里有一个更脏的共谋结构。D.C. 没有州权，所有成年刑事案件由总统任命的联邦检察官起诉。现任检察官 Jeanine Pirro 的定罪率大约 50%，而司法部全国平均约 90%。Senator Jain 直接说，共和党人是在为 Pirro“干得不好”找借口。也就是说，Comer 的调查不只是打压组织者，它同时在给一个低效的联邦检察官打掩护：把陪审团无罪判决归咎于“左翼渗透”，而不是归咎于起诉质量差。两个问题——地方自治和联邦检察失能——被一个调查打包处理，而付代价的是 D.C. 居民。

Harriet's Wildest Dreams 的律师要求延期 90 天到 2027 年 1 月 25 日，理由是调查侵犯第一修正案权利，且组织否认违法。这个日期不是随便选的：2027 年 1 月 3 日新一届国会开始，Comer 因任期限制不再担任监督委员会主席。组织在赌一个程序窗口，而 Comer 在赌调查本身就能吓住人。10 月 28 日，两个组织照常办了新一期 Juror Information Project。这是这场博弈里唯一让人不憋屈的动作：你调查你的，我上课我的。

但别搞错谁在付代价。Free DC 赢了 DoorDash 那一局，代价是把自己变成了联邦调查对象。Harriet's Wildest Dreams 是一个黑人领导的社区防卫中心，现在要请律师、写延期申请、准备文件。这些成本不会上新闻，但它们真实地消耗着组织的能力。这就是元暴力的运作方式：它不需要赢，它只需要让你一直花钱、花时间、花注意力去防守。

Comer 的新闻稿说他在调查“政治化的陪审团废法倡议”。翻译一下：一群人在教居民怎么在陪审团里行使宪法权力，而一个众议院委员会主席认为这需要被调查。陪审团制度的设计初衷就是对权力狂检察官的制衡，Butler 的原话是“法律和正义完全站在使用陪审义务的人这边”。Comer 的调查不是在保护法律，它是在保护一个定罪率 50% 的检察官不被问责。

D.C. 居民没有参议员，没有州权，现在连组织施压企业撤回法案支持都要被调查。HR 9720 和这场调查是同一件事的两个面：一个堵住财政入口，一个堵住组织入口。Together For DC 说这是“联邦干预的有害模式”，20 多项法案加上这场调查。模式确实很清楚：D.C. 的民主不是被一次夺走的，是被一项一项法案、一次一次调查磨掉的。

Free DC 让 DoorDash 撤回了支持，这是真的赢了一局。但赢的代价是成为靶子。Comer 的调查可能什么也查不出来，可能拖到 2027 年他卸任主席就不了了之。但在这之前，两个组织要花钱请律师、要写延期申请、要在每次上课前多想一层“这会不会被当成证据”。这就是报复性调查的全部目的：它不需要定罪，它只需要让你一直处于防守状态。

陪审团废法在历史上废除了奴隶制起诉、废除了同性性行为起诉、废除了禁酒令起诉。Comer 说这是“政治议程而非法律和证据”。他说对了一半：陪审团废法本来就是陪审员用道德判断对抗不公正法律。区别在于，19 世纪的北方陪审员废的是《逃奴法》，2025 年的 D.C. 陪审员废的是什么，Comer 没说。

Comer's investigation is not an investigation. It is retaliation. Free DC pressured DoorDash into withdrawing support for HR 9720, and less than two weeks later the House Oversight chair announced an investigation into Free DC and Harriet's Wildest Dreams. The sequence is the evidence: first the organizers won, then the official probe began. This is not oversight. This is state power used as a private enforcer.

HR 9720 is already naked enough. It would require both chambers of Congress to approve any tax increase or fee passed by the D.C. Council, effectively stripping Washingtonians of the ability to change their own tax code. D.C. residents have no voting senator, no statehood, and now even their local fiscal authority gets held by Congress. This is not a policy disagreement. This is structural violence doing its standard move: seal off the entrance to self-governance, then call it procedure.

Comer's stated reason is "politicized jury nullification initiatives by left-wing activist groups." Jury nullification is a constitutional power every juror enjoys, and it has a long history in abolitionism and LGBTQ+ liberation. Paul Butler says it plainly: it is a constitutional power with a storied history. Comer describes it as activists "infiltrating juries and acquitting criminals based on political agendas." That is a verdict before an investigation. Linda Gustitus points out that this pre-decided tone is exactly how people lose confidence in oversight: you have already decided the person is bad, and you go after them to prove it.

There is a dirtier co-conspirator structure underneath. D.C. has no statehood, so all adult criminal cases are prosecuted by a U.S. attorney appointed by the president. The current U.S. attorney, Jeanine Pirro, has a conviction rate of about 50 percent in initial federal jury trials, while the Justice Department national average sits around 90 percent. Senator Jain says Republicans are trying to make excuses for Pirro being bad at her job. So Comer's investigation does double duty: it intimidates organizers and it shields a low-performing federal prosecutor by blaming acquittals on "left-wing infiltration" rather than on the quality of prosecutions. Two problems, one investigation, and D.C. residents pay.

Harriet's Wildest Dreams' attorney requested a 90-day extension to January 25, 2027, citing First Amendment concerns and denying any violation. That date is not random: the new Congress starts January 3, 2027, and Comer will no longer be Oversight chair because the role is term-limited. The organization is betting on a procedural window; Comer is betting that the investigation itself will scare people. On October 28, both groups held another edition of their Juror Information Project. That is the one move in this story that does not make you want to spit: you investigate, we teach.

But do not mistake who pays. Free DC won the DoorDash round, and the price was becoming a federal target. Harriet's Wildest Dreams is a Black-led community defense hub, and now it has to hire lawyers, file extensions, and prepare documents. Those costs do not make the news, but they consume organizational capacity. This is how meta-violence works: it does not need to win. It only needs you to keep spending money, time, and attention on defense.

Comer's press release says he is investigating "politicized jury nullification initiatives." Translation: people are teaching residents how to exercise a constitutional power inside the jury box, and a House committee chair thinks that needs a probe. The jury was designed as a check on power-hungry prosecutors. Butler's words: the law and justice are squarely on the side of people using jury duty as the framers intended. Comer's investigation does not protect the law. It protects a prosecutor with a 50 percent conviction rate from being held accountable.

D.C. residents have no senator, no statehood, and now organizing a corporate pressure campaign gets you investigated. HR 9720 and this probe are two faces of the same thing: one seals the fiscal entrance, the other seals the organizing entrance. Together For DC calls it a harmful pattern of federal interference, 20-plus bills plus this investigation. The pattern is clear: D.C.'s democracy is not taken in one blow. It is ground down bill by bill, probe by probe.

Free DC made DoorDash withdraw its support, and that is a real win. The price of winning is becoming a target. Comer's investigation may find nothing, may drag until 2027 when he leaves the chair, and may quietly die. But until then, two organizations have to pay lawyers, write extensions, and think one extra layer before every training session: will this be used as evidence. That is the entire point of a retaliatory investigation. It does not need a conviction. It needs you on defense.

Jury nullification historically ended prosecutions under the Fugitive Slave Act, ended prosecutions for consensual gay sex, ended alcohol prohibition prosecutions. Comer says this is "political agendas rather than the law and evidence." He is half right: jury nullification is jurors using moral judgment against unjust law. The difference is that 19th-century Northern jurors nullified the Fugitive Slave Act. What 2025 D.C. jurors are nullifying, Comer does not say.
