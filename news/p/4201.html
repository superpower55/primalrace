用“经济”掩盖暴力的旧骗局The Old Scam of Pitting Economy Against Climate

国际 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-19 § 链接
将气候危机与经济增长对立，是典型的武器化叙事scam。
Framing climate crisis as a trade-off with the economy is a classic weaponized narrative scam.

Reform UK 这种把气候危机说成“自然现象”并主张在经济与环境之间做选择的逻辑，是一场极其拙劣的 weaponized expression。它试图在认知入口处制造一个伪命题：即环保是某种奢侈的“道德消费”，而经济增长才是生存的底线。这种叙事通过操纵底层民众对生活成本（cost of living）的恐惧，诱导他们接受一种假.最优解表达——即通过牺牲长远生存权来换取短期的电费减免。

从加尔通暴力三角来看，这种叙事是典型的 cultural violence。它通过定义“经济增长”为最高优先级，让 structural violence（如基础设施匮乏、能源结构陈旧）显得合理且不可避免。当政治家们在面对热浪时选择沉默，或者用“降低电费”这种表演性让步来替代真正的结构性转型时，他们实际上是在共谋（complicity）维护一个已经失效的旧工业时代秩序。这种共谋的回报是政治上的短期稳固，而代价则是将 Potential（可达到的绿色可持续状态）与 Actual（日益崩塌的生态现状）之间的差额无限扩大。

Allianz 的报告揭露了这场 scam 的核心：气候崩溃本身就是最大的经济损耗。当温度超过 30°C 导致生产力下降、GDP 缩水时，所谓的“保护经济”就成了一个巨大的悖论。这本质上是一场关于解释权的博弈。统治阶层通过将“国家安全”定义为防范俄罗斯，而非防范气候崩溃，来合法化军费的激增，同时将气候投资定义为“成本”。

真正的公正表达应该是承认：没有生存空间的经济增长是零。任何试图在“活下去”和“赚更多”之间做选择的叙事，本质上都是在为未来的大规模直接暴力（direct violence）提前买单。

The logic pushed by Reform UK—dismissing the climate crisis as a mere 'natural phenomenon' and forcing a choice between the environment and the economy—is a clumsy piece of weaponized expression. It attempts to manufacture a false dichotomy at the cognitive entry point: that environmentalism is a luxury 'moral consumption' while economic growth is the baseline for survival. By manipulating the public's fear of the cost of living, it lures them into a fake optimal expression—trading long-term survival for short-term electricity bill cuts.

Through the lens of the Violence Triangle, this narrative is textbook cultural violence. By defining 'economic growth' as the ultimate priority, it renders structural violence—such as crumbling infrastructure and obsolete energy grids—seemingly rational and inevitable. When politicians remain silent during heatwaves or offer performative concessions like VAT cuts instead of structural transitions, they are in a state of complicity, maintaining an expired industrial-era order. The reward for this complicity is short-term political stability; the cost is the widening gap between the Potential of a sustainable state and the Actual collapsing ecology.

The Allianz report exposes the core of this scam: climate breakdown is the ultimate economic drain. When temperatures above 30°C slash productivity and GDP, the phrase 'protecting the economy' becomes a farce. This is a battle over the power of interpretation. The ruling class defines 'national security' as defending against Russia rather than climate collapse to justify surging defense spending, while framing climate investment as a 'cost.'

Just expression requires admitting that economic growth is zero without a livable space. Any narrative attempting to choose between 'surviving' and 'earning more' is simply pre-paying for future large-scale direct violence.
