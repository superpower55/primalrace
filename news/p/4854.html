黑衫、十字架与丢失的钥匙：一场关于“秩序”的拙劣表演Black Shirts, Crosses, and Lost Keys: A Clumsy Performance of 'Order'

国际 文化层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-08 § 链接
当暴力试图通过模仿“纪律”来合法化时，它就成了最易被拆穿的 scam。
When violence attempts to legitimize itself by mimicking 'discipline', it becomes the most transparent scam.

这场所谓的“爱国者平台” (Patriot Platform) 闹剧，是典型的用 cultural violence 包装 direct violence 的尝试。Daniel Thomas 试图通过统一的黑衫、禁烟禁酒的“纪律”以及对法律术语的拙劣引用，将一群极右翼暴徒伪装成一个有组织、有文明、有信仰的“抵抗力量”。这种表达的本质是夺取“秩序”的解释权：他们不再满足于单纯的街头抢劫或纵火，而是试图通过模仿 Extinction Rebellion 等进步派的组织形式，来制造一种“我们在执行某种更高正义”的假象。

但这场博弈在 Portsmouth 彻底崩盘。当一个自诩为领袖的人连监管机构的名字 (IOPC) 都记错，并在直播中因为丢了钥匙而陷入窘迫时，他精心构建的“专业主义”叙事瞬间瓦解。这证明了一个事实：元暴力 (meta violence) 驱动的男性中心叙事，在试图通过“表演纪律”来掩盖其排外本质时，其主体性是极其脆弱的。他们口中的“基督是王” (Christ is king)，不过是将宗教武器化，用来为针对难民的结构性暴力提供道德遮羞布。

最令人作呕的共谋发生在政治层面。Reform UK 的发言人将这种戴着面罩、封锁道路的恐吓行为描述为“礼貌的交谈”。这种共谋逻辑极其阴险：通过将暴徒“正常化”，他们实际上在公共空间为未来的大规模暴力预留入口。当“保护女性”被用作袭击难民的借口时，这又是典型的 weaponized narrative——将女性身体作为开战的旗帜，而真正的执行者依然是那群享受着特权的、渴望通过排挤异己来确认自身存在感的男性。

The so-called 'Patriot Platform' farce is a textbook attempt to wrap direct violence in cultural violence. Daniel Thomas tried to disguise a mob of far-right thugs as an organized, civilized 'resistance' through uniform black shirts, a ban on drugs and alcohol, and a clumsy invocation of legal jargon. The essence of this expression is the seizure of the interpretative right over 'order': they are no longer content with simple street looting, but seek to manufacture a facade of 'higher justice' by mimicking the organizational forms of progressive groups like Extinction Rebellion.

However, this game collapsed entirely in Portsmouth. When a self-proclaimed leader forgets the name of the regulatory body (IOPC) and descends into panic over lost keys during a livestream, the narrative of 'professionalism' evaporates instantly. This proves that the masculine-centric narrative driving meta violence is fragile when it tries to hide its xenophobic core behind a performance of discipline. Their chant of 'Christ is king' is nothing more than the weaponisation of religion to provide a moral shroud for structural violence against refugees.

The most repulsive complicity occurs at the political level. The Reform UK spokesperson describing this masked, road-blocking intimidation as a 'respectful conversation' is a sinister logic. By 'normalizing' thugs, they are effectively opening a gateway for future large-scale violence in the public sphere. Using the 'protection of women' as a pretext to attack refugees is a classic weaponized narrative—using the female body as a banner for war, while the actual executors remain men seeking to confirm their own existence by marginalizing the other.
