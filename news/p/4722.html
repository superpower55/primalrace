一分钟的审判与权力者的免死金牌One-Minute Justice and the Power Elite's Get-Out-of-Jail-Free Card

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-03 § 链接
所谓的‘传送带正义’是结构性暴力，而‘撤销’则是权力的特权。
‘Conveyor-belt justice’ is structural violence; its reversal is a privilege of power.

这件所谓的‘诈骗案’揭露了一个极其阴森的真相：在英国的基层司法系统中，存在着一种被定义为‘conveyor-belt justice’（传送带正义）的结构性暴力。法官每天处理数百件案件，每件案子分配的时间不足一分钟。在这种机制下，法律不再是关于事实的裁决，而是一场高效的行政清理。对于绝大多数没有资源抗辩的底层民众来说，这种‘一分钟审判’就是最终的定论，是不可逆的结构性压迫。

但有趣的是，当这个‘传送带’意外地卷入了伦敦市长 Sadiq Khan 时，这台机器突然学会了‘刹车’。在原审判中，市长在缺席的情况下被判定有罪，这证明了该系统对普通公民的杀伤力是无差别的。然而，一旦这件事被‘widely publicised’（广泛报道），DVLA 迅速请求撤销，法院在另一个‘一分钟听证会’中轻而易举地将指控抹除。同样是一分钟，前者是剥夺权利的暴力，后者是恢复特权的仁慈。

这背后是一场典型的权力共谋。司法系统通过对底层的‘高效’处理来维持运转，而通过对权力者的‘灵活’修正来维持体面。市长被 scam 了，但这掩盖了更深层的 scam：整个司法程序在面对弱势群体时是冰冷的传送带，在面对权力精英时则是可以随时回滚的软件。所谓的‘撤销’，不过是系统在意识到自己误伤了‘同类’后的一次紧急补丁。

This so-called ‘scam’ case exposes a sinister reality: the existence of ‘conveyor-belt justice’ within the UK's lower courts—a form of structural violence. When magistrates decide hundreds of cases a day, spending less than a minute on each, law ceases to be about truth and becomes a mechanism of administrative clearance. For the marginalized without resources to fight back, this one-minute verdict is final and irreversible.

Ironically, when this conveyor belt accidentally snagged London Mayor Sadiq Khan, the machine suddenly learned how to brake. The Mayor was initially convicted in absentia, proving the system's indiscriminate lethality toward ordinary citizens. Yet, once the matter became ‘widely publicised’, the DVLA swiftly requested a withdrawal, and the court erased the conviction in another one-minute hearing. One minute to strip rights, one minute to restore them—the difference is purely political.

This is a classic display of complicity. The judicial system maintains its operation through the ‘efficient’ processing of the powerless, and its dignity through ‘flexible’ corrections for the powerful. The Mayor was the victim of a scam, but that obscures the larger scam: a legal process that acts as a cold conveyor belt for the weak and a reversible software for the elite. The ‘withdrawal’ is nothing more than an emergency patch applied when the system realizes it accidentally injured one of its own.
