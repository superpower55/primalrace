拍一天戏，换一张税收抵免One Day of Shooting, One Tax Credit

其他 Deadline ↗ 2026-10-31 § 链接
为保住补贴而开机的一天，是制度在演戏。
A one-day shoot staged to keep a subsidy is the system performing itself.

《大小谎言》第三季开拍了，也不算出。 Merrin Dungey 在 Burbank 的片场拍了一天，Nicole Kidman、Reese Witherspoon、Laura Dern、Zoe Kravitz、Shailene Woodley 一个都没来。这一天的唯一产出，是让这个项目保住加州的税收抵免（来源：Deadline）。

加州给的条件很直接：拿到批准后 180 天内必须开机，成本超过一亿美元的可以延到 240 天。于是剧组拍了一天。这不是创作，是对着条款做的合规动作，把“开机”这个词的所指从“开始拍戏”挪到“满足审计”。换尺子不换能力，芯片领域这么干，影视补贴领域也这么干。

真正在动的不是文化层，是结构层。税收抵免本来是为了把就业和制作留在加州，现在它的执行方式是奖励一场表演性的开机：一天、一个配角、一座片场，就能撬动一笔公共财政的让渡。规则被写成了可以被一天糊弄过去的样子，这本身就是结构暴力的日常形态——它不流血，只是把公共资源按“谁最会读条款”重新分配了一遍。

而这部剧的卖点，恰恰是几个中年女人的生活被摊开来看。第一季 2017 年，第二季 2019 年，第三季确认在去年，原著作者 Liane Moriarty 今年出了《Big Little Truths》，故事设在十年前的剧情之后，孩子们上了高中，面对毒品、性、酒精。Woodley 说大家“都老了十岁”，孩子们“大多已经成年”。这些女人在剧里被观看、被定价、被安排进一场关于母职与婚姻的博弈；在剧外，她们连档期都凑不齐，HBO 的 Casey Bloys 说“好消息是我们有一堆很红的电影明星，坏消息是他们的档期都很满”。

所以第三季现在有两个时间：一个是合同上的，已经开动；一个是身体上的，还没开始。税收抵免保住了，戏没拍。差额在这里——公共补贴买到的不是作品，是一张收据。

Big Little Lies season three has started production, sort of. Merrin Dungey shot scenes at Warner Bros. Studios Burbank's The Ranch Lot this week. Nicole Kidman, Reese Witherspoon, Laura Dern, Zoe Kravitz and Shailene Woodley were not involved. The day's only output was keeping the show's California tax credit alive (source: Deadline).

California's terms are blunt: principal photography must start within 180 days of approval, or 240 days for projects with qualified expenditures over $100M. So the show shot for a day. This isn't creation, it's compliance aimed at a clause—moving the referent of "start production" from "begin filming" to "satisfy an audit." Change the ruler, keep the capability. Chipmakers do this. Film subsidies do this.

What's moving here isn't the cultural layer, it's the structural one. The tax credit exists to keep jobs and production in California; its enforcement mechanism now rewards a performative start—one day, one supporting actress, one lot, enough to lever a transfer of public money. The rule was written so a single day could slip through it. That is structural violence in its everyday form: no blood, just public resources redistributed by whoever reads the fine print best.

And the show's selling point is precisely that these middle-aged women's lives get laid open. Season one in 2017, season two in 2019, season three confirmed last year. Liane Moriarty published Big Little Truths this year, set ten years on, the children now in high school, dealing with drugs, sex and alcohol. Woodley says they're all ten years older, the kids mostly adults now. In the show these women are watched, priced, slotted into a game about motherhood and marriage. Off screen, their schedules can't even be aligned. HBO's Casey Bloys: the good news is a lot of very popular movie stars, the bad news is very busy schedules.

So season three now has two timelines: the contractual one, already rolling; the bodily one, not yet begun. The tax credit is secured. The show is not shot. The gap is right there—what public subsidy bought wasn't a work, it was a receipt.
