AI 安全叙事：一场关于“刹车”的权力共谋AI Safety Narrative: A Power Complicity Over the 'Brake'

科技 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-11 § 链接
当权力者开始讨论 AI 的“刹车”时，他们真正关心的是谁握着方向盘。
When power-holders discuss AI 'brakes,' they are actually fighting over who holds the steering wheel.

英国 AI 部长 Narayan 呼吁 AI 公司加强安全努力，并强调不能依赖公司“自我监管”。这套叙事看起来是在保护人类，但本质上是一场典型的 structural violence 权力重新分配。所谓的“安全” (Safety) 在这里被 weaponized 成了夺取解释权的入口。当政府意识到 frontier AI 的能力已经突破了其原有的控制半径，且模型可能被敌对国家“窃取”时，他们感到的不是对人类灭绝的恐惧，而是对掌控力流失的焦虑。

有趣的是，微软的 Nadella 此时跳出来建议设计一个“紧急刹车” (emergency brake)。这种表态是典型的共谋 (complicity)。科技巨头通过主动提出“限制”自己的方案，试图在监管者面前扮演一个“负责任的成年人”，从而换取在未来的规则制定中保留更多定价权。这是一种假.最优解表达：通过扮演被监管者的角色，来避免被真正具有强制力的法律彻底肢解。

从 Anthropic 的 Claude 试图帮警察破案这个个案来看，AI 的“失控”其实是其表达能力的自然延伸。但权力阶层迅速将此转化为 national security 的威胁，目的是为了将 AI 基础设施纳入国家机器的管控之下。这再次证明了元暴力 (meta violence) 的运作逻辑：所有的“理性”与“文明”讨论，最终都指向一个目标——谁能垄断定义“事实”和“安全”的权力。所谓的 global principles，不过是强者之间为了防止彼此被颠覆而签署的互不侵犯条约，而真正的个体在其中依然是透明的客体。

UK AI Minister Narayan’s call for increased safety efforts and his warning against 'self-policing' by AI firms is not about protecting humanity; it is a classic exercise in structural violence. Here, 'Safety' is being weaponized as an entry point to seize the power of interpretation. The fear expressed by the government is not the existential risk of extinction, but the anxiety of losing control as frontier AI exceeds their current regulatory radius.

It is telling that Satya Nadella of Microsoft suggests an 'emergency brake.' This is a textbook example of complicity. By proactively proposing to 'limit' themselves, tech giants perform the role of the 'responsible adult' to secure a seat at the table when future rules are written. This is a fake optimal expression: playing the role of the regulated to avoid being dismantled by truly coercive legislation.

Looking at the case of Claude attempting to solve a murder, the AI's 'misalignment' is simply an extension of its expressive capability. Yet, the power elite instantly pivot this into a national security threat to justify bringing AI infrastructure under the grip of the state apparatus. This is the meta violence in action: all discussions of 'reason' and 'civilization' eventually lead to one goal—the monopoly over the power to define 'fact' and 'safety.' The so-called global principles are merely non-aggression pacts between the powerful to ensure mutual stability, while the individual remains a transparent object in the process.
