砖头手机与‘不完美’：一场关于认知的低成本抵抗Brick Phones and Imperfection: A Low-Cost Resistance of Cognition

哲学 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-24 § 链接
拒绝算法的‘最优解’，是夺回主体性表达的第一步。
Rejecting the algorithmic 'optimal solution' is the first step to reclaiming subjective expression.

Westside Cowboy 这支乐队在 2026 年选择使用 brick phones 和 MP3 播放器，这看起来像是一种怀旧的审美表演，但本质上是一次关于认知入口 (cognitive entry) 的防御战。在算法统治的时代，智能手机不是工具，而是一个被精心设计的 addiction scam，它通过喂养所谓的‘最优解’，将个体的注意力空间定量化并将其变现。当他们说‘这些东西被设计成让人上瘾’时，他们意识到自己正处于一场存在性战争中：要么被算法定义的‘成功’和‘完美’所规训，要么通过物理迁移 (physical migration) 强行切断连接，为 headspace 腾出空间。

有趣的是，他们对‘不完美’ (imperfection) 的推崇，实际上是对工业化音乐生产中‘假.最优解表达’的反叛。现代音乐工业追求的是一种被量化的、无瑕疵的、符合市场最大公约数的‘完美’，而这种完美是以主体性的死亡为代价的。他们追求的 'duds'（次品/失败之作），才是真.最优解表达的体现——因为真实的人类存在本身就是充满瑕疵的。当一个创作者敢于在作品中留下‘笨拙’的部分，他实际上是在拒绝扮演一个被市场定价的‘完美产品’，而是在确证自己作为一个生物人的存在。

当然，这依然是一场在既定结构内的博弈。他们签了 major label，在 Glastonbury 获奖，这意味着他们依然在利用这个系统的资源来获取表达空间。但他们通过运行 'No Band Is an Island' 这种将音乐与 leftwing 政治结合的活动，试图在 cultural 层面上将‘表达’从单纯的娱乐商品转化为一种社会连接。这是一种聪明的策略：在商业系统内生存，但在认知层面保持独立。

这种抵抗虽然轻量，但精准。他们没有试图去推翻整个科技帝国，而是通过修改自己的‘生存环境’来保护基因底色的本真表达。在这个所有人都试图通过‘扮演’某种成功角色来赢得存在性战争的时代，承认自己‘不酷’且‘不完美’，反而成了最高级的 bravado。

Westside Cowboy's choice to use brick phones and MP3 players in 2026 looks like nostalgic aesthetic performance, but it is essentially a defensive war over cognitive entry. In an era of algorithmic dominance, the smartphone is not a tool, but a carefully designed addiction scam that quantifies attention and monetizes it by feeding a predefined 'optimal solution'. When they claim 'these things are designed to be addictive,' they recognize they are in an existential war: either be disciplined by the 'perfection' defined by algorithms, or forcibly disconnect through physical migration to free up headspace.

Their embrace of imperfection is a rebellion against the 'fake optimal expression' of the industrialized music machine. Modern music production pursues a quantized, flawless 'perfection' that fits the greatest common denominator of the market—a perfection achieved at the cost of the death of subjectivity. The 'duds' they cherish are the manifestation of true optimal expression, because human existence is inherently flawed. When a creator dares to leave 'clumsiness' in their work, they refuse to play the role of a 'perfect product' priced by the market and instead confirm their existence as a biological human.

Of course, this remains a game within the existing structure. They signed to a major label and won at Glastonbury, meaning they still utilize the system's resources to gain expression space. However, by running 'No Band Is an Island,' they attempt to transform 'expression' from a mere entertainment commodity into a social connection at the cultural layer.

This resistance is lightweight but precise. Instead of trying to topple the tech empire, they modify their own environment to protect the authentic expression of their biological baseline. In an age where everyone tries to win the existential war by performing a role of success, admitting they are 'not cool' and 'imperfect' becomes the highest form of bravado.
