背叛者游戏：一场关于“信任”的资本共谋The Traitors: A Capitalist Complicity of 'Betrayal'

哲学 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-02 § 链接
大众对“背叛”的狂热，本质是对结构性互害社会的镜像投射。
The mass obsession with 'betrayal' is a mirror projection of a structurally mutually-harming society.

900万观众，54.9%的份额，尤其是16-34岁年轻人对《Celebrity Traitors》的狂热，这根本不是什么“优质内容”的胜利，而是一场精准的认知收割。这个节目的核心逻辑是：在一个封闭空间里，通过扮演“背叛者”来获取利益，并观察他人如何被欺骗。这不就是现代社会最真实的生存模型吗？

从存在性战争的视角看，这个节目提供了一种极低成本的“拟像博弈”。观众在沙发上观看名人如何通过假装信任、精准背叛来赢得比赛，实际上是在潜意识里确认一种残酷的共识：信任是弱点，背叛是最优解。当这种叙事被包装成“精彩的博弈”并成为全民讨论的 water cooler moment 时，它就在 cultural 层面上完成了一次元暴力的加固——它告诉年轻人，社交的本质就是识破对方的伪装，并抢在对方之前出卖对方。

最讽刺的是，那些参与其中的名人将此视为“职业助推器”。在这种共谋场域中，名人的主体性被简化为博弈中的一个 token。他们通过扮演一个“会背叛”或“被背叛”的角色，换取流量的溢价。这是一种典型的假.最优解表达：通过出卖真实的人格信任，换取在资本结构中的可见度。BBC 将其视为“皇冠上的明珠”，不过是因为它成功地将社会的互害焦虑转化为了一场可量化的商业盛宴。

9 million viewers and a 54.9% share, especially among Gen Z, is not a victory for 'quality content,' but a precise harvest of cognition. The core logic of the show is simple: gain advantage by playing the 'traitor' in a closed space and watch others be deceived. Is this not the most honest model of modern survival?

From the perspective of existential war, this show provides a low-cost simulation of game theory. As audiences watch celebrities use fake trust and precise betrayal to win, they subconsciously reinforce a brutal consensus: trust is a vulnerability, and betrayal is the optimal expression. When this narrative is packaged as 'compelling gameplay' and becomes a national water cooler moment, it completes a reinforcement of meta-violence at the cultural level—telling the youth that the essence of socializing is to unmask the other and betray them first.

The irony lies in the celebrities viewing this as a 'career boost.' In this field of complicity, the celebrity's subjectivity is reduced to a token in a game. They trade their authentic interpersonal trust for a premium in visibility. This is a textbook fake optimal expression: sacrificing the capacity for trust to gain visibility within a capital structure. For the BBC, calling this a 'crown jewel' simply means they have successfully converted social anxiety over mutual harm into a quantifiable commercial feast.
