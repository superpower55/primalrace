伦敦股市的“廉价大甩卖”与政治共谋The London Market Fire Sale and Political Complicity

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-01 § 链接
当国家叙事在喊“再工业化”，而制度在默许“贱卖家产”，这就是典型的结构性暴力。
When national narrative screams 'reindustrialisation' while institutions permit 'fire sales', it is structural violence.

英国首相在台上大谈“再工业化” (reindustrialising)，而现实是伦敦股市正沦为全球私募股权基金 (Private Equity) 的廉价自助餐厅。Bodycote 这类在冶金技术领域拥有全球领先地位的企业，被美国 Veritas 这种 buyout firm 以一个极其平庸的溢价（仅 25%）收割。这根本不是什么商业竞争，而是一场精准的资产掠夺。

最讽刺的是 Bodycote 董事会的表达：他们一边在公告里吹嘘中长期战略执行得“极其出色”，一边在最后一句用“宏观不确定性”这种万金油措辞给自己挖坑，然后迅速地把公司卖掉。这种“假.最优解表达”的本质是：在资本市场缺乏关注的结构性压力下，管理层选择扮演“顺从的出售者”来换取个人确定性，而代价是企业主体性的死亡和国家工业能力的流失。

Westminster（英国政界）的沉默则是这场博弈中最深层的共谋 (complicity)。当 150 多家优质公司被像“家族银器”一样贱卖时，政治权力层竟然“几乎没有一声异议”。这种沉默不是因为他们没看到，而是因为在当前的元暴力 (meta-violence) 叙事中，短期金融资本的流动被定义为“市场效率”，而长期的工业主权被定义为“过时的执念”。

这正是结构层暴力的运作方式：通过定义一套“理性”的金融游戏规则，让掠夺变得合法且自然。当一个国家失去了对核心工业解释权的掌控，所谓的“再工业化”就成了一个巨大的 scam，不过是给外资在本地建工厂、抽干利润提供一个好听的 PR 包装而已。

The UK Prime Minister preaches 'reindustrialisation' while the London Stock Exchange is effectively becoming a budget buffet for global Private Equity. The takeover of Bodycote—a global leader in metallurgical tech—by US-based Veritas at a mediocre 25% premium isn't market competition; it is a precise asset strip.

There is a sharp irony in the board's expression: they spend paragraphs praising their 'splendid' execution, only to cave in the final sentence to 'macroeconomic uncertainty'. This is a textbook 'fake optimal expression'. Under the structural pressure of a neglected mid-cap market, management chooses to play the role of the 'compliant seller' to secure personal certainty, at the cost of the company's subjectivity and the nation's industrial capacity.

The silence from Westminster is the deepest layer of complicity. When over 150 prime companies are sold off like 'family silver', the political class barely squeaks. This silence isn't blindness; it's a choice. Within the current meta-violence narrative, short-term financial liquidity is rebranded as 'market efficiency', while long-term industrial sovereignty is dismissed as 'obsolete nostalgia'.

This is how structural violence operates: by establishing a 'rational' financial rulebook that makes predation look natural. Once a state loses the power to define its industrial reality, 'reindustrialisation' becomes a total scam—nothing more than a PR wrapper for foreign capital to extract value from local soil.
