赞比亚选举：两个男人的权力死结与被抹除的生存者Zambia Elections: The Power Deadlock of Two Men and the Erased Survivors

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-13 § 链接
政治被简化为两个男人的私人恩怨，而民众的生存只是这场权力博弈的背景板。
Politics reduced to a private feud between two men, while survival becomes a mere backdrop to their power game.

赞比亚的这场选举像极了一场关于“谁才是正统强者”的男性权力游戏。Hichilema 和已故的 Lungu 之间，不仅是政见之争，更是典型的 Masculine-centric narrative：从生前的权力厮杀，到死后的葬礼争夺，权力博弈的逻辑甚至延伸到了坟墓。政府试图控制死者的安葬，这种对“定义权”的病态执着，本质上是元暴力的延续——即便对方已经死亡，我也要通过掌控你的最后一次表达来确认我的绝对统治。

在这种极致的男性权力叙事中，我们看到了典型的共谋机制。反对派将死者的遗体作为竞选筹码，承诺将其接回国内以换取选票。这是一种极其阴险的武器化表达：将死者的尊严转化为政治资本，将民众对死亡的敬畏转化为对现任者的不满。在这种博弈中，权力的更迭只是两个男性阵营之间票数的转移，而真正决定人们生死的是 Structural Violence。

最讽刺的是，在这种“强人博弈”的喧嚣之下，普通人的生存被彻底客体化了。政府在吹嘘通胀率的下降和铜矿投资的增加，但在底层，人们在面对 22 小时的停电和无法保证三餐的现实。这种经济数据的“好新闻”实际上是一场 Scam。当权力者在争夺谁能主持葬礼时，那些在停电中挣扎、在物价翻倍中挨饿的真实生命，在他们的认知入口里根本不存在。这就是典型的元暴力：定义什么是“重要问题”，而将生存危机定义为“次要的噪音”。

The Zambian election resembles a typical masculine power game. The conflict between Hichilema and the late Lungu is not merely a political disagreement, but a textbook Masculine-centric narrative. From their lifelong rivalry to the battle over a funeral, the logic of power extends beyond the grave. The government's attempt to control the burial is a pathological obsession with the 'right to define'—a continuation of meta-violence where the victor seeks to confirm absolute dominance by controlling the opponent's final expression.

Within this narrative, we see a clear mechanism of complicity. The opposition uses the deceased's body as a campaign tool, promising a dignified return to trade on the ghost of the past. This is a weaponized expression: converting the dignity of the dead into political capital and leveraging cultural reverence for death to fuel resentment against the incumbent. In this game, the transition of power is simply a shift of votes between two masculine camps, while the actual survival of the people is governed by structural violence.

Most ironic is how the lived experience of ordinary people is objectified. The government boasts about stabilized inflation and copper investments, yet on the ground, people face 22-hour blackouts and the inability to afford three meals a day. This 'good news' of economic data is a scam. While the power players fight over who presides over a funeral, the real lives struggling in the dark are non-existent in their cognitive entry points. This is meta-violence in its purest form: defining what constitutes a 'major issue' while dismissing survival crises as 'minor noise'.
