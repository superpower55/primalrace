用“非新闻”掩盖的认知入口：英国的制度性共谋The 'Non-News' Loophole: Institutional Complicity in the UK

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-16 § 链接
定义权就是权力，将政治操弄定义为“时政节目”是典型的文化暴力。
The power to define is the power to rule; labeling political manipulation as 'current affairs' is cultural violence.

Ofcom 的逻辑简直是一个完美的 scam：只要把节目定义为“时政”（current affairs）而非“新闻”（news），政治人物就可以合法地在屏幕前扮演客观的观察者。这不仅是法律定义的漏洞，而是一次极其精准的认知入口抢夺。当 Nigel Farage 这样的政治人物掌控节目主持权时，他掌控的不再是观点，而是“什么是事实”的解释权。

在加尔通的暴力三角中，这属于典型的 structural violence。英国的监管机制通过一个语义上的微小差别，为政治操弄提供了制度性的掩体。这种“非新闻”的定义，实际上是在文化层（cultural layer）通过重新定义现实，来合法化政治人物对公共舆论的垄断。当政治人物可以一边接受数千万英镑的加密货币巨头捐款，一边在自己的节目里为这些捐款辩护时，所谓的“公正表达”已经彻底死亡。

这其实是一场关于共谋的表演。GB News、Ofcom 以及部分政治精英在潜意识或意识层面达成了一致：只要在形式上符合所谓的“广播准则”，实质上的客观性缺失是可以被容忍的。这种共谋的回报是极高的注意力红利和议程设置权。他们把公共空间的讨论变成了政治人物的私人扩音器，而大众在不知不觉中被植入了被精心修剪过的现实。

最讽刺的是，这种机制在欧洲绝大多数国家被视为禁忌，唯独在英国被玩成了“特例”。这证明了元暴力（meta violence）是如何运作的：它通过建立一套看似“理性”、“文明”且“符合程序”的规则，将原本应该是公共资源的认知入口，变成了特定权力集团的武器化工具。

Ofcom's logic is a textbook scam: by defining a show as 'current affairs' rather than 'news,' politicians can legally masquerade as objective observers. This isn't just a legal loophole; it's a precise seizure of the cognitive entry point. When figures like Nigel Farage control the host's chair, they aren't just presenting an opinion—they are seizing the power to define 'what is a fact.'

In the Violence Triangle, this is structural violence. The UK's regulatory mechanism provides an institutional shield for political manipulation through a mere semantic distinction. This 'non-news' label is a tool of cultural violence, redefining reality to legitimize the monopoly of political actors over public discourse. When a politician can accept tens of millions in crypto-billionaire donations and then defend those donations on his own show, Just Expressions are officially dead.

This is a performance of complicity. GB News, Ofcom, and certain political elites have aligned—consciously or subconsciously—to agree that as long as the 'broadcasting code' is formally satisfied, the actual loss of objectivity is acceptable. The reward for this complicity is immense attention dividends and the power of agenda-setting. They've turned the public square into a private megaphone for politicians, while the public is fed a carefully curated reality.

It is farcical that this practice is a taboo across most of Europe yet a 'specialty' in the UK. This is exactly how meta violence operates: it builds a set of 'rational,' 'civilized,' and 'procedural' rules to transform a public cognitive resource into a weaponized tool for specific power groups.
