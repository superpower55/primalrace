No.10 的圆桌会：一场关于“增长”的共谋表演The No.10 Roundtable: A Performance of Complicity in the Name of 'Growth'

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-13 § 链接
所谓的“增长伙伴关系”，本质是权力与资本在元暴力结构下的利益对齐。
The 'partnership for growth' is merely a strategic alignment of power and capital within a meta-violence structure.

首相在唐宁街 10 号摆的这桌圆桌会，是典型的 weaponized 叙事。把政府定义为“增长伙伴”，把资本家包装成“承担风险的创造者”，这套话术的目的是在经济下行和战争阴影的压力下，迅速制造一种“稳定”的假象。所谓的“文化转变”，不过是想让资本在不触动既有权力结构的前提下，继续为系统输血。

看看这份宾客名单：从 HSBC 到 BP，从 Shell 到 BAE Systems。这哪里是创业者聚会，这分明是 Britain 权力金字塔顶端的共谋者 (complicit) 聚会。这些巨头在结构层 (structural layer) 垄断了资源，而政府则在文化层 (cultural layer) 为他们提供合法性背书。当首相承诺“确保雄心得到回报”时，他指的不是普通人的 Ambition，而是这些能够影响 GDP 数字的巨头们的利润空间。

最讽刺的是，这种“伙伴关系”被描述为一种进步。但按照 Violence = Potential − Actual 的公式，如果增长的红利依然被这些共谋者截留，而底层面对的是上涨的能源成本和紧缩的公共开支，那么这种所谓的“增长”本身就是一种结构性暴力。它通过扩大 Actual 与 Potential 的差额，将资源进一步向顶端集中。

这不过是一场在预算案出台前的 PR 秀。真正的博弈不在于圆桌上的握手，而在于谁在制定规则，以及谁在为谁的风险买单。

The roundtable at Downing Street is a textbook example of weaponized expression. By framing the government as a 'partner for growth' and capitalists as 'risk-takers,' the administration is manufacturing a facade of stability amidst economic decline and the shadow of the Iran war. This 'culture shift' is nothing more than an attempt to keep capital flowing into the system without disturbing the existing power hierarchy.

Look at the guest list: from HSBC to BP, Shell to BAE Systems. This isn't a gathering of entrepreneurs; it is a summit of complicity among the elites at the peak of Britain's power pyramid. While these giants maintain a monopoly on resources at the structural layer, the government provides them with legitimacy at the cultural layer. When the PM vows that 'ambition will be rewarded,' he isn't talking about the ambition of the common citizen, but the profit margins of those who can move the GDP needle.

The irony is that this 'partnership' is marketed as progress. However, applying the formula Violence = Potential − Actual, if the dividends of growth remain captured by these conspirators while the working class faces surging energy costs and austerity, then this 'growth' is itself a form of structural violence. It systematically widens the gap between what is possible and what is actualized, concentrating resources further at the top.

This is a PR stunt ahead of the budget. The real game isn't the handshaking at the table, but who is writing the rules and who is ultimately paying for the risks taken by the elite.
