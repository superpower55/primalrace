注意力被掠夺后的“愚蠢”：一场认知入口的围猎The 'Stupidity' of Reading Less: A Siege of Cognitive Entry Points

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-11 § 链接
阅读量的下降不是习惯问题，而是认知入口被武器化后的结构性掠夺。
The decline in reading is not a habit issue, but a structural plunder of cognitive entry points.

《卫报》这篇报道把阅读量下降归结为“让我们变蠢”，这种叙事太 naive 了。它把一个典型的 structural violence 包装成了某种个人习惯的退化或认知能力的衰减。事实上，这不是我们在“选择”少读书，而是我们的 attention span 在一场不对等的博弈中被系统性地拆解了。

现在的认知入口被高度武器化。从算法驱动的短视频到碎片化的信息流，这些产品的底层逻辑就是通过高频的多巴胺轰炸，强行改写人类的神经回路。这本质上是一场关于“解释权”的围猎：当你失去了深度阅读的能力，你就失去了构建复杂逻辑、审视元暴力的能力，只能在被喂养的碎片化叙事中寻找认同。一个无法阅读长文的人，是不可能在存在性战争中找到真.最优解的，因为他连对方是如何操纵他的都看不出来。

最讽刺的是，报道提到“书籍从未如此流行”，这正是典型的文化层伪装。书籍变成了某种审美符号（aesthetic symbol）或阶级标签，而非认知工具。人们购买书籍是为了完成某种“知识分子”的身份表达，而非为了进行认知的升级。这种“表演性阅读”掩盖了真实的认知坍塌，让人们在以为自己依然掌握话语权的幻觉中，心甘情愿地成为算法的共谋者。

真正的危机不是 IQ 的下降，而是主体性的死亡。当我们的思维被切割成 15 秒一段的短片，我们就不再是思考的主体，而成了认知流水线上的一个数据点。

The Guardian's claim that reading less is 'making us stupid' is painfully naive. It frames a clear case of structural violence as a mere decline in personal habit or cognitive faculty. We aren't 'choosing' to read less; our attention spans are being systematically dismantled in an asymmetrical game of power.

Cognitive entry points have been weaponized. From algorithm-driven short videos to fragmented feeds, the underlying logic is to rewrite human neural circuits through high-frequency dopamine bombing. This is a siege for the right of interpretation: once you lose the capacity for deep reading, you lose the ability to construct complex logic and scrutinize meta-violence. You are left to seek identity within fragmented narratives fed to you by the system. A person unable to process long-form text cannot find a true optimal expression in the existential war, because they cannot even perceive how they are being manipulated.

The irony is that 'books have never been trendier.' This is a classic cultural violence masquerade. Books have become aesthetic symbols or class markers rather than cognitive tools. People buy books to perform an identity of 'intellectualism' rather than to upgrade their cognition. This performative reading masks a genuine cognitive collapse, leading people to become complicit in the algorithm while hallucinating that they still hold discourse power.

The real crisis isn't a drop in IQ, but the death of subjectivity. When our thoughts are sliced into 15-second clips, we cease to be thinking subjects and become mere data points on a cognitive assembly line.
