被定价的呼吸：私有化托育中的结构性谋杀Priced Breath: Structural Murder in Privatized Childcare

好消息 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-09 § 链接
当安全被简化为空间成本，死亡就成了私有股权的边际损耗。
When safety is reduced to space costs, death becomes a marginal loss for private equity.

一个九个月大的女婴被面朝下绑在豆袋沙发上一个多小时，直到窒息而死。这种极其低级的 direct violence 背后，是典型的 structural violence：在英国托育产业被 private equity（私募股权）资本渗透后，婴儿的呼吸权被量化成了“空间限制”和“利润指标”。

最令人作呕的共谋发生在文化层。一些托育机构将不安全的睡眠方式包装成“森林学校模型”或“斯堪的纳维亚实践”——这就是典型的 weaponized expression。通过挪用一个带有“进步”和“自然”标签的叙事，将对婴儿基本生存权的剥夺美化为一种教育理念，从而掩盖其为了在有限空间内塞进更多孩子以榨取利润的本质。在这种叙事下，安全准则变成了可以被“灵活解读”的建议，而死亡则成了资本博弈中的意外支出。

这次的立法更新（禁止非婴儿床睡眠）和 Ofsted 增加检查频次，在机制上确实缩小了 Potential 和 Actual 的差额，属于 good_news。但必须警惕：当资本方开始抱怨新规会导致“被迫减少招生人数”时，他们是在通过扮演“受害者”来尝试重新定义成本。如果强制性 CCTV 无法落地，这种由资本驱动的、将生命客体化的逻辑将继续在阴影中运行。

这场胜利是由一个家庭用女儿的生命换来的。我们需要追问的是：在一个将照顾生命完全交给市场定价的结构里，究竟需要死掉多少个孩子，才能让监管者意识到“利润优先”本身就是一种元暴力？

A nine-month-old girl was strapped face-down to a beanbag for over an hour until she suffocated. This direct violence is a textbook manifestation of structural violence: as childcare in England is penetrated by private equity, an infant's right to breathe is quantified into "space limitations" and "profit margins."

The most repulsive complicity occurs at the cultural layer. Some nurseries packaged unsafe sleeping practices as "forest school models" or "Scandinavian practices"—a classic case of weaponized expression. By hijacking a narrative of "progress" and "nature," they aestheticized the deprivation of basic survival rights to mask the reality of cramming more children into limited spaces for profit. Under this narrative, safety guidelines became "flexible suggestions," and death became an accidental expense in a capital game.

The legislative update banning non-cot sleep and the increased Ofsted inspections are good_news, as they narrow the gap between Potential and Actual safety. However, we must remain vigilant: when owners complain that new rules "force them to reduce intake," they are attempting to redefine cost by playing the victim. Without mandatory CCTV, the logic of objectifying life driven by capital will continue to operate in the shadows.

This victory was bought with the life of a child. The real question is: in a structure where the care of life is entirely left to market pricing, how many children must die before regulators realize that "profit first" is itself a form of meta-violence?
