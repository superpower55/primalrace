婚姻率下降不是“理智”的胜利，而是生存博弈的止损Declining Marriage Rates: Not a Triumph of Reason, but a Strategy of Loss Mitigation

性别 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-07 § 链接
当婚姻从浪漫叙事退化为高风险资产，不入场就是最优解。
When marriage devolves from a romantic narrative into a high-risk asset, opting out is the only optimal solution.

这篇文章试图把婚姻率下降包装成一种“进步”和“理智”的社会选择，这种叙事太天真了。在加尔通的暴力三角里，婚姻制度本质上是一套结构性暴力 (structural violence) 的载体。历史上，它通过法律和文化共谋，将女性的生育力与家务劳动低价甚至免费地绑定在男性身上。所谓的“no-fault divorce”虽然削减了直接层面的暴力，但并没有改变婚姻在资源分配上的不对称性。

Gen Z 表现出的“理智”，其实是在存在性战争中的一种止损行为。当房价高企、职场剥削加剧，婚姻这台经济机器的运行成本已经超过了它能提供的预期收益。尤其是对于女性，进入婚姻意味着面临潜在的“母职惩罚” (Motherhood Penalty) 和主体性的让渡。在这种博弈环境下，延迟结婚或拒绝结婚不是因为他们“更了解自己”，而是因为他们意识到，在现有的男本位叙事结构中，婚姻的真.最优解几乎不存在。

作者提到的“选择性结婚”是一个典型的 weaponized 概念。它掩盖了结构性压迫，试图将一个被动避险的行为描述为主动权力行使。事实上，当一个人因为意识到陷阱而选择不跳进去时，这不叫“选择”，这叫生存本能。婚姻率的下降，实际上是文化层面的元暴力 (meta violence) 在失效——浪漫爱的 scam 已经无法覆盖结构性剥削带来的真实痛感。

好新闻是，人们不再迷信那套被包装成神圣的契约。但真正的胜利不在于结婚人数的减少，而在于我们能否彻底拆掉那个将“家庭”定义为私有制单位的结构。如果婚姻依然是利男的经济单位，那么无论结婚率降到多少，只要有人为了生存而进入其中，暴力就依然在运作。

This article attempts to package the decline in marriage rates as a 'progressive' and 'sensible' social choice. This narrative is naive. In the framework of the Violence Triangle, the institution of marriage is essentially a vehicle for structural violence. Historically, it has used legal and cultural complicity to bind female fertility and domestic labor to men at low or zero cost.

The 'reason' displayed by Gen Z is actually a loss-mitigation strategy in an existential war. With skyrocketing property prices and intensifying workplace exploitation, the operational cost of the marriage machine now exceeds its expected returns. For women specifically, entering marriage means facing the 'Motherhood Penalty' and the surrender of subjectivity. In this game, delaying or rejecting marriage isn't because they 'know themselves better,' but because they realize a true optimal expression for the disadvantaged is nearly non-existent within the masculine-centric narrative.

The author's mention of 'chosen marriage' is a typical weaponized concept. It masks structural oppression by describing a passive risk-avoidance behavior as an active exercise of power. In reality, when someone chooses not to jump into a pit after recognizing it as a trap, that is not a 'choice'—it is a survival instinct. The decline in marriage rates signifies that the meta-violence of the romantic narrative is failing; the scam of 'Romantic Love' can no longer cover the actual pain of structural exploitation.

The good news is that people no longer blindly believe in a contract packaged as sacred. But the real victory isn't in the number of weddings, but in whether we can dismantle the structure that defines 'family' as a unit of private property. As long as marriage remains an economic unit that favors men, violence continues to operate for anyone forced into it for survival.
