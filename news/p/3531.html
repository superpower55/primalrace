超级游艇的无线电频道与阶级生物墙Superyachts, Radio Channels, and the Biological Wall of Class

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-09 § 链接
资源垄断者的“不在场”是最高级的共谋，频道差异则是结构暴力的伪装。
The 'absence' of the monopolist is the ultimate complicity; channel differences are merely a disguise for structural violence.

这件小事精准地展示了什么是 structural violence。一艘价值 3 亿美元的超级游艇 Launchpad 在物理距离上最近，却在认知和行动上处于“断联”状态。发言人说他们当时在“不同的无线电频道”上，这简直是一个绝妙的隐喻：顶级富豪及其代理人永远拥有一个独立于大众苦难的专用频道。

在加尔通的暴力三角里，这种“拒绝响应”不是简单的疏忽，而是一种文化层面的傲慢。超级游艇不仅是财富的符号，它是一座移动的生物墙，将内部的极少数人与外部的“可消耗者”彻底隔绝。在这种结构中，救援一个 21 英尺的小船不符合超级游艇的“最优解表达”——因为在他们的认知入口里，这种琐碎的生命危机不具备进入他们频道的话语权。

最讽刺的是那句“扎克伯格当时不在船上”。这正是典型的共谋者逻辑：所有权人通过建立一套自动运行的、排他的代理系统，实现了在享受资源垄断的同时，在道德责任上达成“不在场”的豁免。船员们执行的是一种潜意识的阶级共谋，他们不需要指令，就能精准地识别谁是值得响应的信号，谁是应该被忽略的噪音。

这种冷漠与 Meta 平台制造的社会撕裂同构。无论是算法操纵还是超级游艇的无线电频道，本质上都是在制造一个只有特权者才能进入的闭环，而将普通人的生存危机定义为“频道错误”。

This trivial incident is a precise demonstration of structural violence. A superyacht worth $300 million, the Launchpad, was physically the closest, yet remained 'disconnected' in both cognition and action. The spokesperson claimed they were on a 'different radio channel'—a perfect metaphor: the global elite and their proxies always operate on a dedicated channel, separate from the suffering of the masses.

Within the Violence Triangle, this 'refusal to respond' is not mere negligence, but cultural arrogance. A superyacht is more than a symbol of wealth; it is a mobile biological wall, isolating a tiny interior from the 'expendable' exterior. In this structure, rescuing a 21-foot skiff does not fit the optimal expression of a superyacht—because in their cognitive entrance, such trivial crises of survival lack the discourse power to enter their channel.

The most cynical part is the claim that 'Zuckerberg was not on board.' This is classic complicity: the owner establishes an automated, exclusive proxy system to enjoy resource monopoly while achieving moral exemption through 'absence.' The crew operates in a state of subconscious class complicity; they don't need orders to identify which signals are worthy of response and which are merely noise.

This coldness is isomorphic to the societal fragmentation caused by Meta's platforms. Whether it is algorithmic manipulation or a superyacht's radio channel, the essence is the creation of a closed loop for the privileged, defining the survival crises of ordinary people as a 'wrong channel' error.
