FIFA的权力游戏：不过是共谋者的利益分赃失败The FIFA Power Game: Nothing But a Failed Distribution Deal Among Co-conspirators

国际 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-04 § 链接
权力更迭不是因为道德觉醒，而是因为分赃协议失效。
Power shifts occur not due to moral awakening, but because the loot-sharing agreement failed.

FIFA这个组织本身就是元暴力的绝佳样本：一个由男性精英构建的、以“足球”为掩体的全球权力共谋场。Infantino 的危机根本不是什么“治理失败”，而是他试图通过将世界杯部分权益私有化，在既有的分赃协议之外，强行地为少数顶级资本制造新的认知入口。当这套“私有化”方案无法让足够多的 Concacaf 和欧洲成员国分到足够的蛋糕时，原本的共谋者们自然会迅速转变为“正义的反对者”。

这种权力更迭的叙事极其滑稽。在 FIFA 的世界里，所谓的“支持”或“撤回支持”从来不是基于足球的纯粹性，而是一次次关于利益分配的博弈。Infantino 试图用资本的逻辑去重塑权力的定价权，结果却低估了既得利益集团对既有结构性暴力（structural violence）的依赖。他们不需要一个更高效的商业模型，他们需要的是一个能维持现状且听话的管家。

人们习惯于把这种人事变动看作是某种“正义的审判”，但事实上，这只是共谋者们在进行一次内部的风险对冲。无论谁接手，只要男性中心叙事（masculine-centric narrative）依然垄断着足球的解释权，只要女性在这个权力结构中依然被定义为“点缀”或“客体”，这场游戏就没有任何实质性的改变。唯一的不同是，下一个管家可能会在分钱时表现得更像个绅士。

FIFA is a textbook example of meta-violence: a global co-conspiracy of masculine elites using "football" as a cover. Infantino's crisis is not about "governance failure," but about his attempt to create new cognitive entries for a few top capitalists by privatizing parts of the World Cup. When this "privatization" scheme failed to deliver enough cake to the Concacaf and European members, the original co-conspirators naturally pivoted to become "righteous opponents."

This narrative of power transition is farcical. In FIFA's world, "support" or "withdrawing support" is never about the purity of the sport, but a series of games over interest distribution. Infantino tried to use the logic of capital to reshape the pricing power of authority, but he underestimated the established interests' reliance on existing structural violence. They don't need a more efficient business model; they need a manageable butler who maintains the status quo.

People are accustomed to viewing such personnel changes as a "judgment of justice," but in reality, it is merely an internal risk hedge among co-conspirators. Regardless of who takes over, as long as the masculine-centric narrative continues to monopolize the interpretation of football, and as long as women remain defined as "ornaments" or "objects" in this power structure, nothing fundamentally changes. The only difference is that the next butler might pretend to be a gentleman while dividing the spoils.
