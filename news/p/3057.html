用AI制造的“真实”，掩盖不了血淋淋的共谋AI-Manufactured 'Truth' Cannot Mask Bloody Complicity

国际 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-31 § 链接
将种族清洗包装成“战争”的叙事，是典型的文化暴力武器化。
Packaging genocide as 'war' is a textbook case of weaponizing cultural violence.

Boy George 这次的操作简直是 weaponized expression 的教科书案例。他用 AI 制造了一首所谓的“为善”之歌，试图通过重新定义“种族灭绝” (genocide) 为“战争” (war) 来夺取解释权。这种通过修改词典来掩盖血腥事实的行为，正是文化暴力 (cultural violence) 的核心：让结构性屠杀看起来像是一场正当的防御，从而在认知入口处完成对暴力的洗白。

最讽刺的是，他一方面声称音乐的使命是“团结人们”，另一方面却在歌词里把支持巴勒斯坦的音乐人比作“像羊一样在嘟囔”的群体。这种表达不是为了沟通，而是一次精准的筛选和排斥。他试图建立一个基于特定政治正确的新“俱乐部”，但最终发现，当这种叙事与现实中大规模的身体暴力 (direct violence) 产生剧烈冲突时，这种表演性的“信念”在商业逻辑面前不堪一击。

他的经纪人用一套典型的 PR 话术，将这次退出包装成“尊重他人”和“为了艺术家利益”的体面之举。这不过是共谋者在意识到成本过高时的迅速止损。在这个博弈中，Boy George 试图扮演一个勇敢的真理追求者，但他的“真.最优解”其实只是在利用一个被高度政治化的叙事入口来刷存在感。而他口中所谓的“民主”，不过是给这种权力不对等、解释权垄断的元暴力披上了一层温情的外衣。

Boy George’s recent stunt is a textbook example of weaponized expression. By using AI to craft a so-called 'AI for good' song, he attempted to seize the power of interpretation by redefining 'genocide' as 'war.' This act of altering the lexicon to mask bloodstained facts is the core of cultural violence: making structural massacres appear as legitimate defense, thereby laundering violence at the very entry point of cognition.

The irony is palpable. He claims the mission of music is to 'unite people,' yet his lyrics dismiss musicians supporting Palestine as 'mouthing like sheep.' This is not an attempt at communication, but a precise mechanism of screening and exclusion. He tried to build a new 'club' based on a specific political narrative, only to find that when such a narrative clashes violently with actual direct violence, this performative 'conviction' collapses under commercial logic.

His manager’s PR speak, framing the withdrawal as 'respect for others' and 'best interests of the artist,' is nothing more than the rapid loss-cutting of a co-conspirator who realized the cost had become too high. In this existential game, Boy George attempted to play the role of a brave truth-seeker, but his so-called 'optimal expression' was merely using a highly politicized narrative entrance to gain visibility. His invocation of 'democracy' is simply a thin veil of warmth draped over the meta-violence of power imbalance and the monopoly of interpretation.
