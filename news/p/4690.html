可持续性是殖民者的最新掩体Sustainability as the New Colonial Bunker

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-02 § 链接
当资本定义“正向价值”时，原住民的生存权就成了可被抵消的成本。
When capital defines 'positive value,' the survival rights of indigenous people become offsettable costs.

典型的殖民逻辑：先通过直接暴力（direct violence）抢夺资源，再用文化暴力（cultural violence）通过重新定义词汇来掩盖罪行。加拿大公司 Aura Minerals 此时抛出的“可持续性”（sustainability）和“正向价值”（positive value），不过是第三章中提到的 weaponized 叙事——它试图夺取解释权，将一场资源掠夺包装成一项社区福利工程。

这场博弈的残酷在于 structural violence 的深层共谋。2007 年那份被质疑的 EIA（环境影响评估）就是一张通行证，它证明了政府机构如何通过更换负责人来让不合格的方案“合法化”。这种共谋机制确保了无论公司怎么换，从 Goldcorp 到 Bluestone 再到 Aura，掠夺的逻辑从未改变。所谓的“闭环系统”和“水净化厂”是典型的假.最优解表达，它们在 PR 层面看起来公正，但在实际运作中，原住民的井水干涸、牲畜患病才是真实的 Actual 状态。

最令人作呕的是这种叙事对“母亲”意象的侵占。当地渔民将湖泊视为母亲，而资本将这片土地视为一个巨大的、待开采的矿床。在元暴力（meta violence）的视角下，这种冲突本质上是强势主体对弱势客体的物化。当 CEO 谈论“创造价值”时，他定义的价值是每年的 111,000 盎司黄金，而他完全屏蔽了 Elda Dinora Ramírez 望向干涸水井时的绝望。

这不是发展，这是对原初种族生存空间的再一次殖民。当一个系统的“最优解”建立在让另一个群体走向“自我毁灭”的基础上时，这个系统本身就是一场巨大的 scam。

Classic colonial logic: first, seize resources through direct violence, then use cultural violence to mask the crime by redefining vocabulary. The 'sustainability' and 'positive value' now touted by Canada's Aura Minerals are textbook examples of weaponized narratives—an attempt to seize the power of interpretation and repackage resource plunder as a community benefit project.

The cruelty of this game lies in the deep complicity of structural violence. That widely criticized 2007 EIA is a permit of complicity, proving how government agencies 'legalize' failing plans simply by swapping personnel. This mechanism ensures that while companies change—from Goldcorp to Bluestone to Aura—the logic of extraction remains constant. The promised 'closed-loop systems' and 'purification plants' are fake optimal expressions; they appear just in PR, while the actual state is dried-up wells and sick livestock.

Most nauseating is the appropriation of the 'mother' imagery. Local fishers see the lake as a mother; capital sees the land as a massive, exploitable ore body. Through the lens of meta violence, this conflict is the objectification of a vulnerable subject by a dominant one. While the CEO speaks of 'creating value,' the value he defines is 111,000 ounces of gold per year, completely ignoring the despair in Elda Dinora Ramírez's eyes as she looks at her dry well.

This is not development; it is a re-colonization of the Primal Race's living space. When a system's 'optimal solution' is built upon the 'self-destruction' of another group, the system itself is a massive scam.
