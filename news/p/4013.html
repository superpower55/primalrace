99.98% 的精准度与 100% 的权力傲慢The 99.98% Accuracy and 100% Arrogance of Power

科技 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-17 § 链接
AI 不是在识别罪犯，而是在为人类的盲从提供合法性掩体。
AI isn't identifying criminals; it's providing a legitimacy shield for human blind obedience.

Sainsbury’s 的公关辞令非常典型：一面抛出 99.98% 这个近乎完美的数字，一面将错误归咎于 "human error"。这正是典型的武器化叙事——用一个无法被普通用户验证的统计学指标，去掩盖结构性的暴力。在权力博弈中，0.02% 的误差不是数字问题，而是关于谁有权定义你“是谁”的问题。

这件事最令人作呕的不是 AI 的误报，而是店员的共谋。当红圈出现在屏幕上，人类员工瞬间完成了主体性的让渡。他们不再是具有判断力的个体，而成了 AI 意志的执行终端。这种 "blindly following the machine’s orders" 的行为，本质上是权力结构的简化：机器给出指令 $ ightarrow$ 管理者执行 $ ightarrow$ 受害者被剥夺尊严。在这个链路里，人类的理性被刻意关闭，因为“听机器的”是成本最低的生存最优解，也是最安全的共谋方式。

Sainsbury’s 暂停单店扫描纯属表演性让步。一个中心化的系统，如果其逻辑底座存在将个体“客体化”的倾向，那么在任何一家门店，只要你符合某种被算法标记的“嫌疑表型”，你就是那个被献祭的 0.02%。这种 surveillance culture 的本质，是将所有顾客预设为潜在的罪犯，然后通过一个所谓的“精准度”来施舍你作为正常人的权利。

真正的暴力不在于那个红圈，而在于当受害者试图通过行为（如让朋友回来取货）证明自己的清白时，系统依然拒绝接收任何非机器生成的信号。这是一种元暴力的升级：事实不再由行为定义，而由数据定义。如果你被定义为罪犯，那么你的所有清白行为都将被解读为罪犯的伪装。

Sainsbury’s PR playbook is textbook: toss out a near-perfect figure of 99.98% while blaming the incident on "human error." This is a weaponized narrative—using a statistical metric that the average customer cannot verify to mask structural violence. In the game of power, a 0.02% error rate isn't a math problem; it's a question of who holds the right to define who you are.

The most repulsive part of this isn't the AI's false positive, but the complicity of the staff. The moment that red circle appeared on the screen, the human employees surrendered their subjectivity. They ceased to be thinking individuals and became mere execution terminals for the AI's will. This "blindly following the machine’s orders" is essentially a simplification of the power structure: Machine issues command $ ightarrow$ Manager executes $ ightarrow$ Victim is stripped of dignity. In this chain, human reason is deliberately switched off because obeying the machine is the lowest-cost optimal expression for survival and the safest form of complicity.

Sainsbury’s pausing the scan in a single store is purely a performative concession. In a centralized system, if the logical foundation tends to objectify individuals, then in any store, as long as you fit a certain "suspicious phenotype" marked by the algorithm, you are the sacrificed 0.02%. The essence of this surveillance culture is to preset all customers as potential criminals, then grant you the "right" to be normal based on a so-called accuracy rate.

The real violence isn't the red circle, but the fact that when the victim tried to prove their innocence through behavior (like asking a friend to pick up the goods), the system refused to accept any signal not generated by the machine. This is an escalation of meta-violence: facts are no longer defined by actions, but by data. If you are defined as a criminal, then all your innocent behaviors are re-interpreted as the disguises of a criminal.
