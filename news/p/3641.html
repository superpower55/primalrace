学历通胀是场关于“入场券”的共谋骗局The Degree Scam: A Complicit Fantasy of Credential Inflation

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-11 § 链接
当教育被量化为投资回报率，学位就成了结构性剥削的金融产品。
When education is quantified as ROI, degrees become financial products for structural exploitation.

这篇报道揭露了一个极其残酷的真相：现代教育系统已经从“知识传递”异化成了某种形式的金融衍生品。那个辍学的年轻人用 business sense 拆穿了 scam——当学费成本与知识获取的边际效益不成正比时，大学学位本质上是一张昂贵的、由债务支撑的“入场券”。

这里的暴力不在于某个教授的糟糕教学，而在于 structural violence。系统通过制造“学历门槛”来垄断资源分配，迫使年轻人通过背负巨额债务来购买一个所谓的“身份”。这是一种典型的 existence war：你必须支付昂贵的代价来获得一个能够参与博弈的身份，但当你进入赛场时，发现这个身份的定价权掌握在那些制定规则的人手里。

最令人作呕的是文中提到的那种“社会幻想” (social fantasy)。无论是家长、教师还是评论者，大家在潜意识里都成了共谋者 (complicity)。我们一边质疑学位的实际价值，一边继续向下一代推销这套叙事。这种共谋的回报是维持现状的稳定——只要大家都相信债务是“必然的投资”，这个由学历等级构建的阶级筛选机制就能高效运转。

当阅读和思考被简化为“可定义的就业状态”时，主体性就死亡了。我们正在把年轻人变成某种标准化的零件，让他们在追求“最优解表达”的幻觉中，心甘情愿地被系统定价。

This piece exposes a brutal reality: the modern education system has mutated from knowledge transfer into a kind of financial derivative. The dropout's business sense stripped away the scam—when the cost of tuition outweighs the marginal utility of knowledge, a degree is nothing more than an expensive, debt-fueled 'entry ticket.'

The violence here isn't found in a bad professor, but in structural violence. The system monopolizes resource distribution by manufacturing 'credential thresholds,' forcing youth to incur massive debt to purchase a social identity. This is a classic existential war: you pay a premium to acquire the identity needed to play the game, only to find that the pricing power of that identity is held by the rule-makers.

Most repulsive is the 'social fantasy' mentioned. Parents, teachers, and commentators are all complicit in the subconscious. We question the actual value of a degree while simultaneously selling the narrative to the next generation. The reward for this complicity is the stability of the status quo—as long as everyone believes debt is an 'inevitable investment,' this class-screening mechanism based on academic hierarchy continues to function efficiently.

When reading and thinking are reduced to a 'definable employment status,' subjectivity dies. We are turning young people into standardized parts, letting them be priced by the system while they chase the illusion of an optimal expression.
