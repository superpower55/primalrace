Ariana Grande 的身体：一场关于“可见性”的共谋博弈Ariana Grande's Body: A Game of Complicity over Visibility

性别 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-08 § 链接
女性身体的“健康”讨论，往往是元暴力掩盖下的权力审视。
Discussions of female 'health' are often just meta-violence masking a power struggle for definition.

当大众在 X 上用 AI 把 Ariana Grande “修成健康肥胖”时，这根本不是什么人文关怀，而是一次典型的武器化表达。在这种叙事里，所谓的“健康”被当成了认知入口，用来合法化对女性身体的再次殖民。本质上，这依然是男性中心叙事（masculine-centric narrative）的变体：无论你是要求她“瘦”还是要求她“胖”，你都在行使一种定义权，试图将她的身体修剪成你心中那个“正确”的样本。

这篇文章触及了一个极其阴险的共谋机制：Body Positivity（身体正向）运动在某种程度上成为了结构暴力的掩体。当“不要评论女性身体”成为一种政治正确时，它在屏蔽厌女攻击的同时，也顺便屏蔽了对结构性病理的讨论。于是，女性在公共空间被推入一个死胡同——要么在沉默中被物化为 thinspo（瘦身灵感）的符号，要么在被冠以“健康”之名的审视中失去主体性。这种“不可说”的禁忌，反而成了 eating disorders（进食障碍）最完美的温床，因为秘密本身就是这种病理的一部分。

Ariana 的退缩被其团队定义为“赋权的选择”，但在存在性战争的逻辑里，这更像是一种防御性的撤退。当一个女性的身体成为文化焦虑的 proxy（代理人），她的真实存在就被抹除了，剩下的只有一个被无数人投射欲望和恐惧的壳。最讽刺的是，医疗系统对此的共谋更为直接：只有当 BMI 低到极点，保险公司才愿意支付治疗费用。这意味着，系统在潜意识里要求女性必须“足够病态”才能获得救济。

这场关于“怎么谈论身体”的争论本身就是一个 scam。真正的暴力不在于我们是否谈论她的锁骨，而在于这个系统如何定义什么是“正常”的女性身体，以及它如何通过制造这种定义，让女性在追求“最优解表达”的过程中，不自觉地走向自我毁灭。

When users on X use AI to make Ariana Grande 'healthy and fat,' it is not humanitarian concern, but a weaponised expression. In this narrative, 'health' is used as a cognitive entry point to justify the re-colonisation of the female body. Essentially, this is a variant of the masculine-centric narrative: whether demanding she be 'thin' or 'fat,' the actor is exercising a right of definition, attempting to prune her body into a 'correct' specimen.

The article touches upon a sinister mechanism of complicity: the Body Positivity movement has, in some ways, become a shield for structural violence. When 'do not comment on women's bodies' becomes a political correctness, it blocks misogynistic attacks but simultaneously silences discussions of structural pathology. Consequently, women are pushed into a dead end in public space—either objectified as symbols of thinspo in silence, or stripped of agency under the guise of 'health' scrutiny. This taboo of the 'unspeakable' provides the perfect breeding ground for eating disorders, as secrecy is intrinsic to the pathology.

Ariana's retreat is framed by her team as an 'empowered choice,' but in the logic of the existential war, it looks more like a defensive withdrawal. When a woman's body becomes a proxy for cultural anxiety, her actual existence is erased, leaving only a shell onto which others project their desires and fears. The most ironic part is the direct complicity of the medical system: insurance often only covers care when BMI is extremely low. This implies the system subconsciously requires women to be 'sick enough' to be worthy of rescue.

This debate over 'how to talk about the body' is a scam. The real violence is not whether we mention her clavicles, but how the system defines the 'normal' female body and how, by manufacturing this definition, it leads women toward self-destruction while they search for their optimal expression.
