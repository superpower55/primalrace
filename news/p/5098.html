用枪口定义的“安全”是一场政治诈骗Security Defined by Gunbarrels is a Political Scam

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-18 § 链接
暴力不产生安全，它只产生对暴力的服从与共谋。
Violence does not produce security; it only produces compliance and complicity with violence.

这是一场典型的用“结果”掩盖“机制”的 scam。特朗普政府和田纳西州州长 Bill Lee 试图通过部署国民警卫队来制造一种“强力治理”的叙事，将孟菲斯谋杀率的下降归功于军队入城。但数据揭露了真相：在军队到来之前，犯罪率已经在下降。这种将自然趋势抢夺为政治功劳的行为，本质上是夺取解释权，将一种 structural violence（制度性暴力）包装成“拯救者”的姿态。

真正的暴力不在于统计数字的波动，而在于 Potential − Actual 的差额。当 Tyrin Johnson 被从背后开枪击毙，当 120 名移民在夜店突袭中被逮捕并驱逐，这种 direct violence 成了维持所谓“秩序”的代价。国民警卫队的入城并非为了减少暴力，而是为了建立一套由联邦权力主导的监控网络。在这种叙事里，被保护的不是市民，而是那种“只要有枪在街上，就是安全”的 masculine-centric narrative（男性中心叙事）。

有趣的是，孟菲斯的反抗始于一个披萨店的“拒绝服务”。这种从私人消费领域延伸到公共政治的行动，正是“个人即政治”的实践。当当地商家通过“buy-cott”形成新的共谋阵营，他们实际上是在挑战那种由国家机器垄断的认知入口。然而，这种胜利目前仍停留在 cultural 层面的觉醒，只要结构性的权力（如联邦行政命令）依然有效，这种反抗就只是在暴力的缝隙中寻找呼吸空间。

这场“政治表演”最阴险的地方在于，它让人们相信安全必须通过增加暴力来实现。如果一个城市的安全需要靠在公民背后开枪来维持，那么这种“安全”本身就是最大的暴力。

This is a classic scam of using 'outcomes' to mask 'mechanisms.' The Trump administration and Governor Bill Lee attempted to manufacture a narrative of 'strong governance' by deploying the National Guard into Memphis, claiming credit for the drop in homicide rates. However, the data exposes the truth: crime was already falling before the troops arrived. This act of hijacking a natural trend as a political victory is a blatant attempt to seize the power of interpretation, packaging structural violence as a 'savior' complex.

The real violence is not found in the fluctuations of statistics, but in the gap between Potential and Actual. When Tyrin Johnson was shot in the back, and 120 immigrants were swept up in a nightclub raid, this direct violence became the hidden cost of maintaining a so-called 'order.' The deployment was never about stopping crime; it was about establishing a surveillance network dominated by federal power. In this narrative, the protected entity is not the citizen, but the masculine-centric narrative that equates the presence of guns on the street with security.

Interestingly, the resistance in Memphis began with a pizza shop's refusal of service. This extension of action from private consumption to public politics is a textbook practice of 'The Personal is Political.' As local businesses form a new alliance through 'buy-cotts,' they are challenging the cognitive entry points monopolized by the state machine. Yet, this victory remains at the cultural level. As long as the structural power—such as federal executive orders—remains intact, this resistance is merely searching for breathing room within the cracks of violence.

The most insidious part of this 'political charade' is that it tricks people into believing security must be achieved by increasing violence. If a city's safety requires shooting citizens in the back, then that 'security' is itself the ultimate form of violence.
