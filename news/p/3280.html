迪士尼的“策展”：一次关于认知入口的权力收编Disney's "Curation": A Power Grab of Cognitive Entry Points

科技 结构层 · 文化层 The New York Times ↗ 2026-08-05 § 链接
所谓的“开放”不过是把野生表达转化为可控的资产
What they call "opening the door" is actually the enclosure of wild expression into controllable assets.

迪士尼宣布在 Disney+ 中引入 TikTok 创作者视频，并使用了两个极其傲慢的词："thoughtfully curated"（深思熟虑地策展）和 "tightly vetted"（严格审核）。这根本不是在给创作者开门，而是在构建一座电子围栏。它在向世界宣告：你可以使用我的 IP，但你必须在我的尺度内跳舞，且只有被我选中的“最优解表达”才能获得进入核心流量池的入场券。

这是一场典型的认知入口争夺战。迪士尼意识到，传统的长视频叙事在短视频时代的注意力战争中正在失效，于是它试图通过“社交媒体化”来对冲流失。但它绝不会允许真正的、野生的、可能解构其权力结构的表达进入。它需要的不是创作者的灵魂，而是创作者为它的 IP 免费提供的情感劳作和流量补完。

TikTok 在这里扮演了共谋者的角色。它通过与这个“家庭品牌”绑定，试图洗刷其在监管层面前的污名，用迪士尼的“纯洁性”为自己的增长背书。双方达成了一个完美的共谋：迪士尼获得了低成本的注意力抓手，TikTok 获得了品牌信用背书，而真正的创作者则在不知不觉中成为了这个庞大商业机器的免费零件。

这种“开放”是武器化的叙事。它用“支持创作者”的进步外壳，掩盖了对解释权的进一步垄断。当一个巨头决定定义什么是“优质”的粉丝创作时，它实际上是在抹除所有不符合其商业利益的表达可能性。所谓的“可能性艺术”，在迪士尼这里，变成了如何精准地把野生生命力修剪成盆景的艺术。

Disney's decision to bring TikTok creator videos into Disney+ is framed as an opening, but the terms "thoughtfully curated" and "tightly vetted" reveal the truth: this is an electronic fence. Disney isn't empowering creators; it is signaling that you may use its IP, but only within its margins, and only the "optimal expressions" that serve its interests will be granted entry to the core traffic pool.

This is a calculated war for cognitive entry points. Recognizing that traditional long-form narratives are losing the attention war to short-form content, Disney is "social-media-fying" its platform to hedge against churn. However, it will never permit wild, subversive expressions that might deconstruct its power. It doesn't want the creators' souls; it wants their free emotional labor and the traffic they generate to supplement its corporate IP.

TikTok acts as the perfect complicit party here. By tethering itself to a "trusted family brand," TikTok seeks to scrub its regulatory stigma and use Disney's perceived purity to legitimize its growth. It is a symbiotic complicity: Disney gets a low-cost attention hook, TikTok gets brand credibility, and the creators are reduced to unpaid cogs in a massive commercial engine.

This "openness" is a weaponized narrative. Using the progressive veneer of "supporting creators," Disney is actually tightening its monopoly over the right of interpretation. When a behemoth defines what constitutes "quality" fan content, it is effectively erasing all expressive possibilities that do not align with its profit motives. The "art of manufacturing possibilities" has here become the art of pruning wild vitality into a corporate bonsai.
