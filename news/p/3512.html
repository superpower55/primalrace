记录的崩塌与生物墙的傲慢The Collapse of Records and the Arrogance of the Biological Wall

哲学 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-09 § 链接
所谓的“人类极限”不过是男性中心叙事对生物墙的单向定义。
The so-called "human limit" is nothing more than a unilateral definition of the biological wall by a masculine-centric narrative.

这篇读者问答在讨论一个极其 naive 的问题：为什么人类能不断打破体育记录？它把答案指向了运动服科技，或者某种模糊的“人类进化”。这种发问方式本身就是一种 cultural violence，因为它默认了“人类”这个主体是同质的，默认了记录的刷新是某种普世的生物学奇迹。

事实上，体育记录的刷新从来不是一个纯粹的生物学问题，而是一个关于“谁被允许进入赛场”的权力问题。回顾体育史，记录的跳跃往往伴随着对生物墙的重新定义。当女性被允许参赛，当训练科学从男性身体的样本中剥离并精准化，记录在变。但最核心的 meta violence 在于：我们习惯于将 Armand Duplantis 这种男性的身体表现定义为“人类极限”的标杆，而将女性的生理极限视为某种“次等”的副本。

这种叙事掩盖了一个事实：体育记录的持续刷新，本质上是资本和科技在男性身体这个特定样本上的极致压榨。当我们将这种压榨包装成“人类的进步”时，我们实际上是在强化一种 masculine-centric narrative。所谓的“物理可能性”，其实是资源分配的极度倾斜——最好的营养、最顶尖的生物力学分析、最昂贵的装备，全部投喂给了那个被定义为“标准人类”的性别。

打破记录并不神奇，神奇的是我们至今仍在使用一套男本位的尺子，来衡量一个名为“人类”的物种。如果有一天，我们不再用男性的身体作为 Potential 的唯一参照系，那么现在的这些记录可能根本不叫“世界纪录”，而只能叫“男性样本记录”。

This reader Q&A asks a profoundly naive question: How is it physically possible to keep breaking sporting records? It points to sportswear technology or some vague "human evolution." This framing is a form of cultural violence; it assumes the subject "human" is homogeneous and that the shattering of records is a universal biological miracle.

In reality, the breaking of records has never been a purely biological issue, but a question of power regarding "who is allowed on the field." Looking back at sports history, jumps in records often coincide with the redefinition of the biological wall. Records shift when women are allowed to compete, or when training science is decoupled from male-only samples. But the core meta-violence lies in our habit of defining the physical performance of men, like Armand Duplantis, as the benchmark for the "human limit," while treating female physiological limits as a "secondary" copy.

This narrative masks a fact: the continuous breaking of records is essentially the extreme extraction of the male body by capital and technology. When we package this extraction as "human progress," we are reinforcing a masculine-centric narrative. The "physical possibility" is actually a result of extreme skewing in resource allocation—the best nutrition, the top biomechanical analysis, and the most expensive gear are all fed to the gender defined as the "standard human."

Breaking records isn't magical; what's magical is that we still use a male-centric ruler to measure a species called "human." If we stop using the male body as the sole reference for Potential, these records wouldn't be "world records" at all—they would simply be "male sample records."
