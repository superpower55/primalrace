特朗普及他的“强力行动”：一场关于雄风的表演性诈骗Trump and His 'Strong Action': A Performative Scam of Masculine Power

国际 文化层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-27 § 链接
战争叙事是元暴力的最高级武器化，将肉体损耗包装为雄性权力。
War narratives are the ultimate weaponization of meta-violence, packaging physical attrition as masculine potency.

特朗普再次祭出了他的招牌词汇——“strong military action”。这种表达不是为了沟通，而是一次典型的存在性博弈。在他构建的叙事里，外交是由于“强力打击”才产生的附庸，而战争则是证明其“雄风”的唯一度量衡。这种将暴力逻辑等同于有效性的表达，正是元暴力（meta violence）的教科书式样本：它通过定义什么是“强”，强行将所有不符合其男性中心叙事的理性协商，贬低为弱势的妥协。

事实上，这不过是一场掩盖结构性窘迫的 scam。新闻细节揭露了真实的 structural violence：美军弹药库告急，前线指挥官警告轰炸已达效能极限。此时的“强力警告”并非来自力量的盈余，而是来自力量衰减后的恐慌。他试图通过武器化表达来制造一种“我依然掌控局面”的假象，通过威胁来夺取解释权，试图让世界相信他依然是那个能通过“beat the hell out of them”来解决问题的救世主。

最讽刺的是，这场关于“强力”的博弈中，真正的受害者被彻底客体化了。无论是在霍尔木兹海峡被拦截的商船，还是未来可能被送上战场的士兵，在特朗普的叙事中都只是用来交换“政治成绩”的耗材。这种将人降格为工具的逻辑，与原初种族被殖民的逻辑完全同构：强者通过定义真实，将他者的生存权转化为自己的筹码。所谓的“强力行动”，本质上就是一种通过制造直接暴力来维持其男性中心权力结构的元暴力循环。

Trump has once again deployed his signature phrase—“strong military action.” This expression is not for communication, but a classic existential game. In his narrative, diplomacy is merely a byproduct of “hard hitting,” and war is the only metric to prove his potency. This logic, equating violence with effectiveness, is a textbook sample of meta-violence: it defines what is “strong” and dismisses all rational negotiations that defy the masculine-centric narrative as weak compromise.

In reality, this is a scam designed to mask structural predicament. The details reveal the actual structural violence: dwindling US munitions stockpiles and warnings from military commanders that bombing has reached its limit of effectiveness. The “strong warning” now stems not from a surplus of power, but from a panic over its decay. He attempts to weaponize expression to manufacture a facade of control, seizing the right to interpret reality to convince the world he is still the savior who can “beat the hell out of them.”

Most ironically, the actual victims in this game of “strength” are completely objectified. Whether it is the commercial ships blocked in the Strait of Hormuz or the soldiers potentially sent to war, they are merely consumables used to exchange for “political achievements” in Trump’s narrative. This logic of degrading humans into tools is isomorphic to the colonization of the Primal Race: the powerful define reality to transform the survival rights of others into their own bargaining chips. So-called “strong military action” is essentially a cycle of meta-violence that maintains a masculine-centric power structure through the manufacture of direct violence.
