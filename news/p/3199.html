DEET-Free：一场关于“天然”的认知收割 scamDEET-Free: A Cognitive Harvest Scam of "The Natural"

哲学 文化层 · 结构层 Wirecutter: Reviews for the Real World ↗ 2026-08-03 § 链接
恐惧是商业定价权最廉价的燃料，而“天然”是最高级的武器化叙事。
Fear is the cheapest fuel for commercial pricing power, and "Natural" is the most sophisticated weaponized narrative.

DEET-Free 的标签不是在提供选择，而是在制造一种认知陷阱。当一个产品在标签上大字标出“不含 XX”时，它实际上在完成一次成功的武器化表达：它先定义 XX 是危险的，然后将自己塑造为救世主。这是一种典型的通过制造恐惧来夺取认知入口的商业 scam。

从加尔通的暴力三角来看，这里发生了典型的 cultural violence。商业公司利用大众对“化学合成”的本能恐惧，将一个经过 70 年验证、安全性极高的 gold standard 污名化。这种叙事让消费者在选择“天然”产品时，潜意识里认为自己在追求健康，而实际上他们是在用真实的生物安全（面对莱姆病、西尼罗河病毒的风险）去交换一种虚假的心理安慰。这就是 Potential（高效防护）与 Actual（低效天然产品）之间的差额，这个差额就是由资本制造的结构性暴力。

最讽刺的共谋者竟然是部分研究者。为了获得经费去研发所谓的“植物替代品”，他们通过发表选择性偏差的论文来制造恐慌，从而为自己的科研项目正名。这种学术共谋将“恐惧”商品化，让消费者在不知不觉中成为了这场利益博弈的燃料。

当人们在药店货架前因为一个“DEET-Free”标签而放弃最高效的防护时，他们失去的不仅仅是防虫能力，而是主体性的死亡——他们的决策权被一个精心设计的营销叙事给接管了。

The "DEET-Free" label is not offering a choice; it is constructing a cognitive trap. When a product bolds "Free of XX" on its label, it performs a successful weaponization of expression: it first defines XX as dangerous, then positions itself as the savior. This is a classic commercial scam that seizes the cognitive entry point by manufacturing fear.

In terms of Galtung's Violence Triangle, this is a textbook case of cultural violence. Corporations leverage the instinctive fear of "synthetic chemicals" to stigmatize a gold standard with 70 years of proven safety. This narrative tricks consumers into believing they are pursuing health by choosing "natural" options, while in reality, they are trading actual biological safety (exposure to Lyme disease and West Nile virus) for a fraudulent psychological comfort. The gap between the Potential (effective protection) and the Actual (ineffective natural products) is a structural violence engineered by capital.

The most ironic complicity comes from certain researchers. To secure funding for "botanical alternatives," they manufacture panic through selectively biased papers to justify their own academic projects. This academic complicity commodifies fear, turning consumers into fuel for an interest-driven game.

When people abandon the most effective protection because of a "DEET-Free" label, they lose more than just insect repellency; they experience a death of subjectivity—their decision-making power has been hijacked by a meticulously designed marketing narrative.
