用谎言作为投名状，在元暴力的共谋中寻找存在感A Pledge of Allegiance in Lies: Seeking Existence within Meta-Violence

国际 文化层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-18 § 链接
所有的政治造谣本质上都是在向权力中心递交一份‘我足够邪恶’的投名状。
Political hoaxes are essentially pledges of allegiance to power centers, proving one is 'evil enough' to be useful.

Ryan Amacher 这场拙劣的造谣表演，最精彩的部分不在于他如何试图欺骗医院，而在于他如何通过‘制造事实’来完成一次存在性博弈。在右翼网红经济的生态位里，真相是最低廉的资产，而‘能够有效地攻击政敌’才是硬通货。Amacher 这种 lower-tier 玩家通过编造 Talarico 住进精神病院的谎言，本质上是在向共和党权力中心递交投名状：看，我不仅能制造认知入口，还能通过 weaponized 表达让对手社会性死亡。

这种行为是典型的元暴力 (meta violence) 运作。他不仅在直接层面对 Talarico 实施名誉攻击，更在文化层面上复刻纳粹德国的‘清洗’逻辑。从他崇拜 Freikorps 到将穆斯林和黑人定义为 sub-human，他的整个认知系统是建立在‘剥夺他者主体性’之上的。对他而言，Talarico 不是一个政治对手，而是一个需要被‘清除’的 filth。这种将人客体化的逻辑，正是所有结构性暴力的原点。

最令人作呕的是这场博弈中的共谋 (complicity) 链条。从匿名账号 TickleTexts 起风，到共和党竞选团队人员的推波助澜，再到 Elon Musk 一个简单的“!!”确认，一个谎言被迅速工业化。共谋者们并不在乎事实，他们在乎的是这个谎言是否符合他们的 masculine-centric narrative。在这种叙事中，‘强力地击垮对手’比‘诚实地竞争’具有更高的价值。Amacher 以为自己在通过这种方式获得权力，但实际上，他只是在扮演一个被权力结构所利用的、可替换的工具人。

当他面对 Guardian 的质疑时，试图用‘电话记录’这种伪事实来掩盖其逻辑崩溃，这不过是弱势表达者在博弈失败后的垂死挣扎。他所谓的‘独立核实’，在面对 Lindner Center 的官方否认时显得如此滑稽。这场闹剧证明了：当一个人将‘仇恨’作为其唯一的表达武器时，他最终会被这套武器反噬，因为他已经彻底失去了定义‘真实’的能力。

Ryan Amacher's clumsy performance in fabricating rumors is less about the attempt to deceive a hospital and more about an existential game. In the economy of right-wing influencers, truth is the cheapest asset; the only hard currency is the ability to 'effectively destroy an opponent.' By inventing the lie about Talarico's mental health admission, Amacher was delivering a pledge of allegiance to the Republican power center: proving he could seize cognitive entries and weaponize expression to cause social death.

This is a textbook operation of meta-violence. He isn't just attacking Talarico's reputation at the direct level; he is replicating the 'purging' logic of Nazi Germany at the cultural level. From his admiration for the Freikorps to defining Muslims and Black people as sub-human, his entire cognitive system is built on the stripping of others' subjectivity. To him, Talarico is not a political opponent, but 'filth' to be purged. This logic of objectification is the origin of all structural violence.

Most repulsive is the chain of complicity. From the anonymous TickleTexts account to the boosting by campaign staffers, and finally to Elon Musk's characteristic '!!', a lie was rapidly industrialized. The conspirators didn't care about facts; they cared whether the lie fit their masculine-centric narrative. In this framework, 'powerfully crushing an opponent' holds higher value than 'honest competition.' Amacher believes he is gaining power, but he is merely playing a replaceable tool within the power structure.

His attempt to hide behind 'phone records' when questioned by the Guardian is nothing more than the desperate struggle of a losing player in an existential war. His claim of 'independent verification' becomes farcical in the face of the Lindner Center's official denial. This fiasco proves that when one uses 'hate' as their only weapon of expression, they are eventually consumed by it, for they have utterly lost the ability to define reality.
