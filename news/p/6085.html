她站上台，被夸的是屁股She Took the Stage, They Praised Her Ass

文娱 文化层 · 元暴力 Billboard ↗ 2026-10-31 § 链接
她被邀请站到人群前，被介绍的是屁股，不是政治。
She was brought onstage and introduced by a body part, not a politics.

特朗普在播客里被问到 Nicki Minaj 当白宫新闻秘书，他说这是个好主意，他得想想。这条新闻的笑点不在这个提议本身，在另一件事：十月二十五日 Port Huron 的集会上，特朗普把 Minaj 请到人群前，向支持者隆重介绍的，是她的"beautiful a—"。

这就是表达的全部内容。她坐空军一号去密歇根，发帖写 "#AirForceOneBARBIE"，为共和党候选人站台，当小企业比赛的评委——一路攒下来的政治资本，到了台上被兑换成的，是一个身体部位。

这不是失言，是定价。一个女性在男性中心的政治场里获得的最高曝光时刻，她本人被降格成了她被展示的那一部分。她拿到的回报是真的：空军一号、集会、一个可能的内阁职位传闻。代价也是真的：她在这个场域里被承认的方式，和任何一个被男性结构收编的女性没有区别——你的位置越高，你被看见的方式越接近一件展品。

共谋不分性别。Minaj 不是被骗进去的，她是自己走进去的，而且走得很高兴。这套交换在男性中心叙事里运行得非常顺畅：女性贡献名气和忠诚，结构回赠她一个被展示的位置，展示的方式由结构决定。她不是第一个，也不会是最后一个。

新闻秘书这个职位本身也在这场表演里被贬值了。Katie Zacharia 上任五天就走人，白宫通讯主任 Steven Cheung 发一段假采访视频说"我们可能找到新新闻秘书了"——一个负责向公众解释政府的人选，就这样在社交媒体上被当成选秀节目处理。解释权在这里不是被谁夺走的，是被当成段子发出去的。

特朗普说他会想想。他不用想。这件事从头到尾都不是关于她能不能干这个工作。

Trump was asked on a podcast whether Nicki Minaj could be the next White House press secretary. He said it was a good idea, that he'd have to think about it. The joke in this story isn't the suggestion. It's the other thing: at the October 25 rally in Port Huron, Trump brought Minaj before the crowd and, by way of introduction, drew attention to her "beautiful a—."

That is the whole expression. She flew to Michigan on Air Force One, posted "#AirForceOneBARBIE," stumped for Republican candidates, judged a small-business contest — all of it accumulated political capital, and at the moment of display it was cashed in for a body part.

This isn't a slip. It's a price tag. The highest-visibility moment a woman gets in a masculine-centric political arena is the moment she is reduced to the part of her that's being shown. The payoff is real: Air Force One, a rally, a rumor about a cabinet-level job. So is the cost: the way she gets recognized in this arena is indistinguishable from any woman absorbed into a male structure — the higher your position, the closer you're seen as an exhibit.

Complicity isn't gendered. Minaj didn't stumble into this; she walked in, cheerfully. The exchange runs smoothly inside the masculine-centric narrative: women contribute fame and loyalty, the structure hands back a position on display, and the structure decides how the display works. She is not the first and won't be the last.

The press secretary job itself gets devalued in the same performance. Katie Zacharia left after five days. Communications director Steven Cheung posted a mock interview with the caption that they may have found the new press secretary — a role whose entire function is explaining the government to the public, handled like a talent show on social media. The explanatory authority here wasn't seized by anyone. It was posted as a bit.

Trump says he'll think about it. He doesn't need to. None of this was ever about whether she can do the job.
