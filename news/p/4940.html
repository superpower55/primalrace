瑞典的“人权面具”与行政暴力的精准收割Sweden's 'Human Rights Mask' and the Precision Harvest of Administrative Violence

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-11 § 链接
当“自由裁量权”成为武器，文明的叙事就是最完美的掩体。
When 'discretion' becomes a weapon, the narrative of civilization is the perfect cover.

瑞典一直以来在 global 叙事中扮演着“人权标杆”和“进步政治”的领路人。但这次对英国国民的驱逐数据撕开了这个 scam：在欧盟所有驱逐英国人的国家中，瑞典竟然贡献了 33% 的份额。对比法国的 6% 和德国的 2%，这个数字不仅是行政效率的问题，而是一次极其典型的 structural violence。

最讽刺的是，这种暴力被包裹在“法律适用”和“自由裁量权” (discretion) 的文明外壳之下。欧盟委员会的表态极其阴险：瑞典没有违反协议，它只是在行使裁量权。这意味着，只要制度给权力留了一道名为“合理理由”的缝隙，权力就可以在这个缝隙里随意定义谁是“合理的”，谁又是“必须被驱逐的”。

这种逻辑在瑞典的右转趋势中被武器化了。一个在瑞典生活了 25 年、患有认知障碍的老人，或者一个 95 岁的伊拉克女性，在行政机器眼中不再是具体的“人”，而是需要被清理的“数据点”。法官那句“国家对移民监管的利益高于个人居住利益”是典型的 masculine-centric narrative：宏大的国家利益永远优先于微观的个体生存，而个体被客体化为一种可以被随意处置的资源或垃圾。

所谓的“人权国家”，其 meta violence 在于它让你相信它拥有最高的道德标准，从而在它实施暴力时，受害者甚至会产生一种“这不应该发生在这里”的认知失调。这种认知入口的掌控，让瑞典在实施硬核驱逐的同时，依然能维持其文明的- image。这种共谋发生在欧盟、瑞典政府以及那些用“程序正义”掩盖实质暴力的法庭之间。

Sweden has long played the role of a global leader in human rights and progressive politics. But the deportation data of British nationals tears through this scam: Sweden accounts for 33% of all British deportees from the EU. Compared to France's 6% and Germany's 2%, this isn't just about administrative efficiency; it is a textbook case of structural violence.

The irony is that this violence is wrapped in the civilized shell of 'legal application' and 'discretion.' The European Commission's response is particularly sinister: Sweden hasn't breached the agreement; it is simply exercising its discretion. This means as long as the system leaves a gap called 'reasonable grounds,' power can arbitrarily define who is 'reasonable' and who is 'expendable.'

This logic has been weaponized by Sweden's broader lurch to the right. A 74-year-old with dementia who lived in Sweden for 25 years, or a 95-year-old Iraqi woman, are no longer seen as 'humans' by the administrative machine, but as 'data points' to be cleared. The judge's ruling that the 'state's interest in regulated immigration outweighs the individual's interest' is a classic masculine-centric narrative: grand state interests always supersede micro-individual survival, and the individual is objectified into a resource or waste to be disposed of.

The meta violence of a 'human rights state' lies in making you believe it holds the highest moral standard, so that when it exerts violence, the victim suffers from a cognitive dissonance—a feeling that 'this shouldn't happen here.' By controlling this cognitive entry point, Sweden maintains its civilized image while executing hardline deportations. The complicity exists between the EU, the Swedish government, and the courts that use 'procedural justice' to mask substantive violence.
