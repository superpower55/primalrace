机械狗与格斗机器人：一场关于“力量”的拟态秀Mechanical Dogs and Fighting Bots: A Mimicry Show of Power

中国科技 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-25 § 链接
科技进步若只在模拟暴力，那它只是元暴力的硬件升级。
Tech progress that only simulates violence is merely a hardware upgrade for meta-violence.

3000件产品，人形机器人，embodied intelligence。世界机器人大会 2026 的关键词是“展示”，但展示的核心逻辑依然是：谁能更像一个强有力的男性。从 MMA 格斗机器人到机械狗，这套叙事在潜意识里完成了某种闭环——定义“先进”的标准，依然是能够高效执行物理攻击、占领空间、模拟支配权的肌肉能力。

这种所谓的“突破”其实是一种典型的拟态。它没有改变权力的评价方式，而是在用硅基硬件重复碳基世界的 masculine-centric narrative。当科技公司在定义“通用人工智能”时，他们潜意识里定义的“通用”是指一个能格斗、能巡逻、能执行指令的男性主体。这不过是将元暴力从生物墙转移到了电路板上。

在这种叙事入口中，机器人被赋予的“智能”是工具性的，而其“形态”是支配性的。如果一个产业最引以为傲的 showcase 是让机器人在格斗场上互殴，那么这种科技进步本质上是一场 scam。它在向世界宣告：未来的世界依然由“力量”定义，只不过这次力量被代码化了。这种对力量的迷恋，正是 structural violence 的最新皮肤。

3,000 products, humanoid robots, embodied intelligence. The keyword for the World Robot Conference 2026 is "showcase," but the core logic remains: who can best mimic a powerful male. From MMA robots to mechanical dogs, this narrative completes a subconscious loop—defining "advanced" as the ability to execute physical attacks, occupy space, and simulate dominance.

This so-called "breakthrough" is a classic mimicry. It doesn't change the evaluation of power; it simply uses silicon-based hardware to repeat the masculine-centric narrative of the carbon-based world. When tech companies define "General AI," the "General" they envision is a male subject capable of fighting, patrolling, and executing orders. It is merely shifting meta-violence from the biological wall to the circuit board.

In this weaponised narrative, the "intelligence" granted to robots is instrumental, while their "form" is dominant. If the proudest showcase of an industry is robots beating each other in a ring, then this tech progress is essentially a scam. It announces that the future world is still defined by "power," only this time, power is encoded. This obsession with force is nothing more than the latest skin for structural violence.
