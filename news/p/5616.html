外交官的‘特权’与被殖民者的‘常态’Diplomatic Privilege vs. Colonial Normality

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-17 § 链接
当暴力升级到敢于攻击外交官时，这意味着结构的豁免权已覆盖施暴者。
When violence dares to target diplomats, it means impunity has become the structural default for the aggressor.

这起袭击最荒诞的地方不在于暴力本身，而在于施暴者的‘自信’。在国际政治的博弈中，外交官的身份本是最高级别的生物墙，代表着主权国家的背书和某种程度的不可侵犯性。但当蒙面定居者敢于攻击比利时、法国、波兰的外交官时，这说明在约旦河西岸的权力结构中，一种极端的 meta violence 已经完成了闭环：施暴者不再恐惧任何外部的叙事或法律，因为他们潜意识里知道，自己是这个殖民机器的共谋者，而这种共谋带来的 impunity（豁免权）已经高于国际法。

对比一下新闻里的细节：外交官被袭击后，比利时外长迅速发声，欧盟在讨论制裁，以色列军方称其为“严重暴力”。但与此同时，在这个橄榄收获季，已经有 4 名巴勒斯坦人死亡，100 多人受伤。对于巴勒斯坦人来说，这种 direct violence 是 daily routine，是结构性暴力 (structural violence) 的必然产物。而当暴力触碰到欧洲外交官时，它才被定义为“事件”。这种定义权的差异，精准地揭示了谁是被视为‘人’的，谁是被视为‘原初种族’的。

以色列警方的“调查”和外交部在 X 上的谴责，不过是典型的 cultural violence 掩体。他们用一套‘文明’的法律辞令来掩盖一个事实：只要定居点政策这个 structural layer 不动，这种暴力就是被默许的生存策略。对于定居者来说，攻击外交官不是冲动，而是一次关于‘谁才是这片土地真正主人’的表达博弈。他们试图通过摧毁外交特权，来宣告殖民逻辑的绝对胜利。

The most absurd part of this attack is not the violence itself, but the 'confidence' of the perpetrators. In international politics, the status of a diplomat is supposed to be the ultimate biological wall, backed by sovereign states and a degree of inviolability. But when masked settlers dare to attack diplomats from Belgium, France, and Poland, it proves that a closed loop of meta violence has been completed in the West Bank: the aggressors no longer fear any external narrative or law because they know they are complicit in the colonial machine, and this complicity grants them an impunity that supersedes international law.

Contrast the details: when diplomats are targeted, the Belgian Foreign Minister speaks out and the EU discusses sanctions. Meanwhile, during this olive harvest, 4 Palestinians are dead and over 100 are injured. For Palestinians, this direct violence is a daily routine, a byproduct of structural violence. Violence only becomes an 'incident' when it touches European diplomats. This discrepancy in the power of definition reveals exactly who is seen as 'human' and who is treated as the Primal Race.

The 'investigations' by Israeli police and the condemnations on X are merely cultural violence shields. They use the vocabulary of 'civilization' and 'law' to mask a simple fact: as long as the settlement policy—the structural layer—remains, this violence is a sanctioned survival strategy. For the settlers, attacking diplomats is not an impulse; it is an existential game of expression, attempting to announce the absolute victory of colonial logic by dismantling diplomatic privilege.
