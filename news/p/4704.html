泥泞中的‘自愿’与被遗忘的结构性暴力The 'Voluntary' Mud and the Erasure of Structural Violence

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-02 § 链接
自然灾害是触发点，但死亡的分布是由结构性暴力预设的。
Natural disasters are triggers; the distribution of death is preset by structural violence.

报道中那个试图救鸡救羊而死在屋里的 Jamuna，以及在河岸非正式定居点失踪的数百名孩子，被包装成了‘悲剧’。但如果用加尔通的暴力三角来看，这根本不是纯粹的意外。Violence = Potential − Actual。这些人的 Actual 状态——住在随时会被冲走的河岸泥地里，缺乏有效的预警系统，依赖于随时可能崩塌的临时道路——就是一种 structural violence。他们的生命被定价在最低端，被安置在最危险的边缘，这种空间分配本身就是暴力。

最令人作呕的是那种‘miracle’（奇迹）的叙事。当一个当兽医的儿子幸存而被描述为奇迹时，那些在走学路时被冲走的、没有‘职业身份’的孩子们就变成了背景板。这种叙事武器化了情感，用个体的幸存掩盖了集体的被抛弃。这些人经历了 2015 年的大地震，却依然在河边重建简陋的房屋，这说明什么？说明在元暴力的结构中，他们从未获得真正的救济，只是在一次次被摧毁后被要求‘坚强地’在同一个陷阱里重建。

救援直升机在对岸盘旋，而老人用手在泥里扒妻子的尸体。这种 direct 层的绝望，本质上是 structural 层长期剥夺的结果。当一个社会习惯于将贫困人口安置在‘可牺牲’的地带，并将其死亡归结于‘不可抗力的自然灾害’时，这种文化共谋完成了对暴力的洗白。这不是天灾，这是一场关于谁有权住在安全地带、谁必须在泥泞中消失的存在性战争。

The story of Jamuna, who died trying to save chickens, and the hundreds of children missing from informal riverbank settlements, are framed as 'tragedies'. But through the lens of the Violence Triangle, this is no accident. Violence = Potential − Actual. Their actual state—living in precarious mudflats, lacking early warning systems, and relying on crumbling roads—is structural violence. The spatial distribution of these lives, priced at the lowest end and placed on the most dangerous edges, is violence itself.

What is truly nauseating is the 'miracle' narrative. When the survival of a son who is a vet is called a miracle, the children washed away on their way to school, devoid of any 'professional identity', become mere background noise. This is the weaponisation of emotion, using individual survival to mask collective abandonment. These people survived the 2015 earthquake only to rebuild in the same traps; this proves they never received actual relief, only the demand to be 'resilient' within a system of meta-violence.

Rescue helicopters hover on the opposite bank while an old man digs for his wife's body with his bare hands. This direct violence is the inevitable result of long-term structural deprivation. When a society accepts placing the poor in 'expendable' zones and labels their death as an 'act of God', a cultural complicity completes the whitewashing of violence. This is not a natural disaster; it is an existential war over who is entitled to safe ground and who is destined to vanish in the mud.
