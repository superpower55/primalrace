特鲁普、因凡蒂诺与那场关于‘成功’的共谋Trump, Infantino, and the Complicity of 'Success'

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Athletic ↗ 2026-08-11 § 链接
当‘成功’被定义为利润而非公正，权力就成了互换筹码的闭环。
When 'success' is defined as profit over justice, power becomes a closed loop of trading chips.

这是一场教科书级别的共谋 (complicity)。特鲁普在 Truth Social 上的发声，不是在评价一个体育管理者的能力，而是在给自己的利益链条打补丁。所谓的“最成功”的世界杯，在特鲁普和因凡蒂诺的语境里，从来不是指足球的纯粹或公平，而是指 Profit——利润。在这种 masculine-centric narrative 中，成功被量化为金钱和权力，而任何试图通过程序正义（如 FIFA Council 的审批）来约束权力的人，都被视为阻碍“成功”的噪音。

最讽刺的细节在于那个 FFE 提案。FIFA 试图将赛事运营的 21% 股份以 42 亿美元卖给 Thrive Eternal，而这家风投公司的创始人正是特鲁普女婿的兄弟。这就是典型的 meta violence：通过建立一个只有内部人能进入的认知入口，将公共体育资源私有化，然后用“商业成功”这个武器化的叙事来掩盖结构性的掠夺。在这种逻辑下，规则（Approval process）是累赘，而关系（Relationship）才是真理。

至于那个所谓的“FIFA 和平奖”，不过是两个在存在性战争中渴望被定义为“伟大”的男人之间的一次互换。因凡蒂诺用一个自创的奖项填补了特鲁普在诺贝尔奖上的挫败感，特鲁普则用总统的政治背书为因凡蒂诺的权力续命。他们共同构建了一个封闭的叙事空间：只要能赢，过程中的 deception (欺骗) 只是成功者的特权。

这场博弈没有公正的表达 (Just Expressions)，只有假的最优解。他们通过扮演彼此的“救世主”，在权力顶端完成了一次完美的闭环。而真正的暴力，就隐藏在那些被他们定义为“不重要”的规则和被出卖的公共利益之中。

This is a textbook case of complicity. Trump's outburst on Truth Social isn't an evaluation of a sports administrator's competence; it's a patch for his own interest chain. In the vocabulary of Trump and Infantino, the "most successful" World Cup has nothing to do with the purity or fairness of football—it's about Profit. Within this masculine-centric narrative, success is quantified by money and power, while anyone attempting to constrain power through procedural justice (such as FIFA Council approval) is dismissed as noise.

The most cynical detail is the FFE proposal. FIFA attempted to sell a 21% stake in its tournament operations for $4.2 billion to Thrive Eternal, founded by the brother of Trump's son-in-law. This is pure meta violence: creating a cognitive entry point accessible only to insiders to privatize public sporting resources, then using the weaponized narrative of "commercial success" to mask structural plunder. In this logic, rules are burdens, and relationships are the only truth.

As for the "FIFA Peace Prize," it is merely a trade between two men desperate to be defined as "great" in their existential war. Infantino used a self-invented award to fill the void left by Trump's failure to win a Nobel, while Trump provided the political cover to extend Infantino's tenure. Together, they built a closed narrative: as long as you win, deception is simply the privilege of the successful.

There are no Just Expressions in this game, only false optimal expressions. By playing the role of each other's "savior," they completed a perfect loop at the peak of power. The real violence remains hidden in the rules they deemed "unimportant" and the public interests they betrayed.
