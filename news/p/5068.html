用受害者身份兑现的“认同感”：一场关于权力话语的拙劣套现Cashing in on Victimhood: A Clumsy Liquidation of Power Discourse

性别 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-17 § 链接
当受害者身份成为一种可定价的货币，欺诈是对元暴力的某种畸形模拟。
When victim status becomes a priced currency, fraud is a distorted simulation of meta-violence.

这件新闻最荒诞的地方不在于 75 万美元的金额，而在于 Jennifer Percival 提供的作案动机：她称这笔钱给了她一种“认同感” (sense of validation)。

在 Epstein 这种典型的 meta-violence 结构中，受害者的身份本应是极其沉重的生物墙与创伤记忆。但在这里，Percival 将这种身份 weaponized（武器化）了。她通过伪造文件，试图在一个由银行和遗产基金会主导的赔偿系统中，通过“扮演”受害者来获取经济利益。这是一种极其扭曲的博弈：她不追求真实的救济，而追求一种被系统“承认”为受害者的状态，并将其直接兑现为美金。

这种行为揭示了一个残酷的 structural violence：在当下的权力叙事中，受害者的身份在某种程度上被“定价”了。当一个人认为通过伪造受害者经历能获得 validation 时，说明在她的认知入口里，这种身份已经从一种痛苦的处境变成了一种可以被交易的 symbol。她试图通过窃取真正受害者的资源，来填补自己主体性的缺失。

Percival 作为一个学者，深谙如何操控 records 和 emails 来制造“事实”。这种对解释权的贪婪，恰恰是 Epstein 及其共谋者（如 JPMorgan Chase）当年操控女性身体和生活的逻辑缩影——只要能掌控叙事，就能定义现实。她不是在反抗元暴力，而是在用元暴力的逻辑，试图在受害者的阵营里分一杯羹。

The most absurd part of this news is not the $750,000, but the motive Jennifer Percival provided: she claimed the money gave her a "sense of validation."

In the typical meta-violence structure of the Epstein case, the identity of a victim should be a heavy biological wall of trauma and memory. Here, however, Percival weaponized this identity. By falsifying records, she attempted to obtain financial gain by "performing" the role of a victim within a compensation system governed by banks and estate funds. This is a distorted game: she didn't seek actual relief, but rather the state of being "validated" as a victim by the system, directly liquidating that validation into USD.

This behavior exposes a cruel structural violence: in current power narratives, victimhood has been "priced." When an individual believes that forging a victim's experience can bring validation, it means that in their cognitive entry point, this identity has shifted from a painful situation to a tradable symbol. She attempted to fill the void of her own subjectivity by stealing resources from genuine victims.

As a scholar, Percival knew exactly how to manipulate records and emails to manufacture "facts." This greed for the power of interpretation is a miniature of the same logic used by Epstein and his complicity (such as JPMorgan Chase) to control women's bodies and lives—as long as you control the narrative, you define reality. She wasn't fighting meta-violence; she was using its logic to try and grab a share of the spoils from the victims' camp.
