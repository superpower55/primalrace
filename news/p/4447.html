用经济绞杀掩盖军事溃败的叙事scamThe Narrative Scam of Economic Strangulation as a Mask for Military Failure

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-24 § 链接
当武力无法实现客体化，权力便通过定义“经济生命线”来实施结构暴力。
When force fails to objectify, power employs structural violence by defining 'economic lifelines'.

把一场经济制裁命名为“经济弃民行动”(Operation Economic Outcast)，并将其比作诺曼底登陆，这是典型的武器化叙事。美国财政部长 Bessent 试图通过这种宏大叙事，将一种单方面的经济霸凌包装成某种正义的“转折点”。但事实是：在杀了伊朗最高领袖六个月后，华盛顿发现军事打击不仅没能让政权崩溃，反而让对方的硬核派在元暴力的驱动下更加团结。当 direct violence 无法迅速达成目的，权力便迅速切换到 structural violence 层，试图通过切断“经济生命线”来实现对一个国家的客体化。

这种“经济绞杀”的本质是夺取解释权。美国通过垄断美元系统，将“贸易”定义为“资助压迫”，从而赋予自己随时剔除任何不顺从者的权力。这不再是外交博弈，而是一场存在性战争。美国在告诉全球：如果你不成为我的共谋者，你就是我的敌人。在这种逻辑下，所谓的“政治和外交手段”成了弱势方的求饶词，而“最后通牒”才是唯一的沟通语言。

有趣的是，这场博弈现在进入了中美之间关于“定价权”的碰撞。中国作为伊朗最大的贸易伙伴，其抵抗并非单纯的政治立场，而是对美国垄断全球认知入口和经济规则的防御。当美国试图用“美元系统”这个武器去定义谁是“合法贸易者”时，它实际上在加速一个旧共谋体系的瓦解。这场 zero-sum game 的结果大概率不是德黑兰的孤立，而是美国发现自己的叙事武器在面对一个同样拥有结构性力量的对手时，已经失效了。

Naming an economic sanction 'Operation Economic Outcast' and comparing it to the D-Day landings is a textbook example of weaponized expression. Treasury Secretary Bessent is attempting to package unilateral economic bullying as a righteous 'turning point' through grand narrative. The reality is simpler: six months after assassinating Iran's supreme leader, Washington found that direct violence failed to trigger a collapse, instead driving hardliners to consolidate their grip under the influence of meta-violence. When direct violence fails, power pivots to structural violence, seeking to objectify a nation by severing its 'economic lifelines'.

This 'economic strangulation' is essentially a struggle for the power of interpretation. By monopolizing the US dollar system, the US defines 'trade' as 'funding repression,' granting itself the authority to purge any non-compliant entity. This is no longer diplomacy; it is an existential war. The US is signaling to the world: either be my complicitor or be my enemy. In this logic, 'political and diplomatic means' are merely pleas for mercy from the weak, while the 'deadline' is the only recognized language of communication.

Interestingly, this game has evolved into a clash over 'pricing power' between the US and China. China's resistance as Iran's largest trading partner is not merely a political stance, but a defense against the US monopoly of global cognitive entries and economic rules. As the US attempts to use the dollar system as a weapon to define who is a 'legitimate trader,' it is effectively accelerating the collapse of an old system of complicity. This zero-sum game will likely result not in Tehran's isolation, but in Washington discovering that its narrative weapons are obsolete when facing an opponent with equivalent structural power.
