私下妥协的‘保证’与权力共谋的足球剧本Private Assurances and the Art of Power Complicity in Football

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-26 § 链接
私下达成的 binding assurance 只是权力共谋的润滑剂，而非结构的胜利。
Private binding assurances are merely lubricants for power complicity, not a structural victory.

FIFA 试图将世界杯股权私有化（FFE proposal），本质上是一次典型的权力武器化尝试：通过改变游戏的底层所有权，将足球从一项体育运动彻底转化为一种金融资产。这不仅是对竞技精神的 scam，更是对全球足球生态的一次结构性掠夺。Infantino 试图通过这种方式，将 FIFA 的解释权和定价权进一步垄断在极少数资本手中。

有趣的是，UEFA 的‘抵制’威胁在私下沟通（private discussions）后迅速瓦解。这种‘公开互怼、私下握手’的模式是典型的权力共谋（complicity）。UEFA 并不在意足球是否被‘毁容’（disfigure），他们在意的是在私有化这个巨大的利益蛋糕中，欧洲足球的席位是否被足够保障。当 FIFA 给出了私下的‘绑定保证’（binding assurances），共谋的条件就达成了。

最讽刺的是，这场关于‘足球未来’的权力博弈，最终由 U-20 女足选手的参赛权来作为筹码。女性球员的职业机会被当作男性权力斗争中的交易零件，这再次证明了在男性中心叙事（masculine-centric narrative）的元暴力下，女性的存在性永远是次要的、可被牺牲的。所谓的‘宽慰’（relief），不过是权力者在分赃完毕后，允许被剥削者继续扮演他们的角色。

FIFA's attempt to privatize World Cup stakes through the FFE proposal was a textbook weaponisation of expression: an attempt to transform football from a sport into a financial asset by altering the underlying ownership. This is not just a scam of the sporting spirit, but a structural plunder of the global football ecosystem. Infantino sought to further monopolise the power of interpretation and pricing within a tiny circle of capital.

Crucially, UEFA's 'boycott' threat collapsed the moment private discussions began. This pattern of 'public barbs, private handshakes' is a classic case of complicity. UEFA does not genuinely care if the game is 'disfigured'; they care whether the European seat at the table is sufficiently guaranteed in the event of such a massive profit-sharing scheme. Once FIFA provided private binding assurances, the terms of the conspiracy were met.

The most cynical part is that the participation of U-20 women's players was used as a bargaining chip in this existential war for football's future. The professional opportunities of female athletes are treated as disposable components in a masculine power struggle, proving once again that under the meta-violence of a masculine-centric narrative, the female existence is always secondary. The so-called 'relief' is merely the powerful allowing the exploited to continue playing their roles after the spoils have been divided.
