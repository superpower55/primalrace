所谓“第二次机会”是权力的特权洗白The 'Second Chance' is a Privilege Wash

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-04 § 链接
用“宽容”包装的共谋，本质上是对受害者主体性的二次抹除。
Tolerance used as a cover for complicity is essentially a second erasure of the victim's subjectivity.

Kemi Badenoch 谈论“second chances”时，她其实在谈论一种典型的男性中心叙事下的特权交换。在她的逻辑里，一个前新纳粹、有多次种族歧视和厌女暴力前科的男性，只要通过了 Home Office 的某种“认证”，就可以被洗白为“反极端主义专家”并重新进入权力席位。这种叙事最阴险的地方在于，它将一个施暴者的“悔改”设定为故事的主角，而将受害者 Luciana Berger 的创伤——包括在怀孕六个月时经历的“两天地狱”——降格为需要被宽容的背景板。

这不仅是 structural violence，更是典型的 cultural violence。当政党用“ rehabilitation”（康复/改造）这个词来覆盖一个曾发布大量 misogynistic 和 pornographic 材质的暴徒时，他们实际上是在共谋定义什么是“可以原谅的罪行”。在元暴力的逻辑中，男性的权力欲望和对他人的摧毁力被视为某种“能量”，只要能被体制收编，这种能量就可以被转化为“资历”。

而 Luciana Berger 的愤怒揭示了这场博弈的真相：对于施暴者来说，重新获得候选人资格是他的“最优解表达”；但对于制度而言，这种所谓的“宽容”其实是对受害者的一次集体性 gaslighting。如果一个体制允许一个曾将女性身体客体化、将种族仇恨武器化的男性通过 PR 话术重返权力中心，那么这个体制本身就是元暴力的共谋者。它在告诉所有受害者：你的痛苦在“权力重组”的叙事面前，不具备任何定价权。

When Kemi Badenoch speaks of "second chances," she is operating within a classic masculine-centric narrative of privilege exchange. In her logic, a former neo-Nazi with a track record of antisemitic and misogynistic violence can be rebranded as an "anti-extremism expert" and regain a seat of power simply by being "certified" by the Home Office. The cruelty of this narrative lies in centering the perpetrator's "contrition" as the protagonist, while relegating Luciana Berger's trauma—including her "two days of hell" while six months pregnant—to a mere footnote that must be tolerated.

This is not just structural violence; it is blatant cultural violence. When a party uses the term "rehabilitation" to cover for a man who published reams of misogynistic and pornographic material, they are complicit in redefining which crimes are "forgivable." Under the logic of meta-violence, a man's capacity for destruction is viewed as a form of "energy" that, once co-opted by the system, can be laundered into "expertise."

Luciana Berger's outrage exposes the reality of this game: for the perpetrator, regaining candidacy is his optimal expression; for the system, this "tolerance" is a collective gaslighting of the victim. If a system allows a man who weaponized pornography and racial hatred to return to power through PR spin, the system itself is a co-conspirator of meta-violence. It signals to every victim that their pain has zero pricing power in the face of power restructuring.
