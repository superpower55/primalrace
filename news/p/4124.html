英国天然气危机：一场关于“市场万能”的共谋骗局UK Gas Crisis: The Collapse of the 'Market-Will-Provide' Complicity

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-18 § 链接
用“分析”掩盖决策，用“市场”掩盖失职，这就是典型的结构性暴力。
Using 'analysis' to mask indecision and 'the market' to mask negligence is structural violence.

英国政府现在的姿态非常有意思：一边在报告里用“需要更多分析”这种典型的官僚语言来拖延，一边在意识到冬天快到了、气管快断了的时候突然“醒悟”。这根本不是什么认知升级，而是一场关于“市场万能”论的共谋 (complicity) 破产现场。

多年来，连续几届政府的默认立场是“市场会提供供应”。这是一个巨大的 scam。当政府将基础能源安全这种生存底线交给追求短期利润的 market 时，它实际上是在进行一种 structural violence。结果就是：北海产量在掉，储气能力在缩，进口终端在老化，而政府在扮演一个纯真的观察者。在这种叙事里，只要不出事，这就是“高效的自由市场”；一旦出事，那就是“不可抗力的全球供应链危机”。

现在，他们开始讨论干预，讨论给 Centrica 这种公司打钱来修复储气场。这在博弈论里叫作“事后补救的最优解”——在 Potential（能源安全）与 Actual（随时可能断气）的差额大到无法忽视时，才勉强把 Actual 往上拉一点。但最讽刺的是，他们依然在描述“菜单”，而不是下订单。对于数百万依赖天然气取暖的家庭来说，这种“描述菜单”的行为本身就是一种文化层面的暴力，因为它用一种理性的、专业的外壳，掩盖了决策层在存在性战争中对底层民众生存权的弃权。

至于通过批准 Jackdaw 气田来缓解危机，这不过是另一种叙事权力的操弄。在“绿色转型”的 optics 和“冬天没气”的现实之间，政府终于发现，比起在社交媒体上扮演环保先锋，不让民众在家里冻死才是维持其统治合法性的最低要求。这场危机的本质不是技术问题，而是权力在共谋中丢失了对基础生存资源的掌控权。

The British government's current posture is a textbook study in bureaucratic evasion: cloaking a desperate situation in the language of 'further analysis' while suddenly 'waking up' to a crisis they spent a decade ignoring. This isn't a cognitive shift; it's the bankruptcy of a long-term complicity in the 'market-will-provide' myth.

For years, successive governments defaulted to the belief that the market would ensure energy security. This was a systemic scam. By outsourcing the baseline of survival—heating and energy—to a market driven by short-term profit, the state engaged in structural violence. The result: declining North Sea volumes, skeletal storage, and decaying import terminals, all while ministers played the role of innocent observers. In this narrative, as long as the lights stayed on, it was 'market efficiency'; the moment they flicker, it's a 'global supply chain crisis.'

Now, they discuss intervention and subsidies for companies like Centrica. In game theory, this is a desperate attempt at an optimal expression after the gap between Potential (energy security) and Actual (imminent shortage) has become an abyss. The cruelty lies in the fact that they are still 'describing the menu' rather than placing an order. For millions of households, this professionalized hesitation is a form of cultural violence—using the veneer of 'rational analysis' to mask a total abdication of responsibility for the people's survival.

Approving the Jackdaw gasfield is simply a pivot in the war of narratives. Caught between the 'green transition' optics and the reality of freezing citizens, the government has realized that not letting the populace freeze to death is a more urgent requirement for legitimacy than playing the environmental vanguard. The essence of this crisis is not technical; it is the loss of control over basic survival resources through a decade of ideological complicity.
