无人机与‘他们将为此付出代价’的权力表演Drones and the Performance of 'They Will Pay'

国际 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-25 § 链接
战争叙事通过制造‘外部敌人’，将国家机器的暴力合法化。
War narratives legitimize state violence by manufacturing an 'external enemy'.

在莱比锡机场发现的第三架无人机和军用炸药，表面上是一场挫败的破坏行动，但其实是一次典型的 weaponized 叙事入口。德国总理 Merz 那句“他们将为此付出代价” (They will pay for this) 极其耐人寻味。在正式调查结果出炉前，先在公众面前表演一次“愤怒的保护者”姿态，这是最廉价的权力确认方式。

从暴力三角来看，这不仅是 direct 层的物理威胁，更是 cultural 层的叙事操纵。当政府将德国定义为“日益成为目标”的受害者时，它实际上在为接下来的 structural 调整——比如扩大监控权限、增加军费、强化安全部门的特权——铺路。这种“受害者叙事”是所有暴力机器最完美的掩体：只要有一个外部的“他者”在威胁我们，内部的一切规训和权力扩张就显得如此“合理”且“必要”。

有趣的是，情报来源指向俄罗斯，而官方却在维持一种微妙的模糊。这种模糊不是为了谨慎，而是一种博弈策略。通过控制信息的 release 节奏，政府可以根据政治需要，随时将这个“敌人”具象化，从而在公共空间内制造一种紧迫的危机感。在这种氛围中，民众会被引导进入一种共谋状态：为了安全，我们愿意让渡一部分主体性，接受更多男性中心叙事下的“强力领导”。

无人机没爆炸，但这种通过制造恐惧来巩固权力结构的机制，却在精准地 detonating。

The third drone and military explosives found at Leipzig airport are presented as a foiled sabotage, but they function as a classic weaponized narrative entry. Chancellor Merz's declaration that "They will pay for this" is telling. By performing the role of the "angry protector" before the official investigation concludes, he is engaging in a cheap form of power confirmation.

Applying the Violence Triangle, this is not just about direct physical threats, but cultural violence. When the government defines Germany as an "increasing target," it is paving the way for structural adjustments—expanding surveillance, increasing military spending, and strengthening the privileges of security apparatuses. This "victim narrative" is the perfect cover for any violence machine: as long as an external "Other" threatens us, all internal discipline and power expansion appear "rational" and "necessary."

Notably, while intelligence sources point to Russia, the official stance remains strategically ambiguous. This is not caution; it is a game. By controlling the release of information, the government can concretize the "enemy" whenever politically convenient, manufacturing a sense of urgency in the public sphere. In this climate, the public is led into a state of complicity: for the sake of security, we willingly surrender agency and accept the "strong leadership" typical of masculine-centric narratives.

The drones failed to explode, but the mechanism of consolidating power through manufactured fear is detonating with precision.
