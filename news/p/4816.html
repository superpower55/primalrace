罗杰·古德尔的续约：一场关于“成功”的共谋Roger Goodell's Extension: A Masterclass in Complicity

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Athletic ↗ 2026-09-06 § 链接
所谓的“绩效指标”不过是权力阶层为彼此分赃而设计的遮羞布。
所谓的 'performance metrics' are merely fig leaves for the ruling class to divide the spoils.

NFL 宣布给罗杰·古德尔续约到 2030 年，这根本不是什么职业成就，而是一场典型的共谋 (complicity)。

看看这套叙事：补偿委员会雇佣“独立第三方顾问”和“外部法律顾问”来确保薪酬与“长期利益”挂钩，并且 95% 的薪酬是“基于绩效”的。这简直是个巨大的 scam。在这样一个由老板们组成的封闭权力圈里，谁来定义绩效？是那些通过合法化体育博彩、通过压榨球员体力（计划增加到 18 场常规赛）而让球队估值翻倍的球队老板们。当“成功”被定义为营收从 80 亿增长到 250 亿美金时，古德尔就成了这个系统的完美执行者。

这正是元暴力 (meta violence) 的运作方式：用一套看似“理性”、“专业”且“客观”的商业指标，掩盖其本质上的掠夺性。古德尔的薪水可能超过联盟中最高薪的球员，而他所定义的“绩效”，正是通过进一步剥削这些球员的生物墙（肌肉、关节、神经系统）来换取的。球员的身体是消耗品，而古德尔的合同是资产。

这场续约最讽刺的地方在于，它恰好与集体谈判协议 (CBA) 的到期时间重合。这意味着在接下来的博弈中，球员面对的不是一个客观的管理者，而是一个已经拿到了老板们“投名状”的共谋者。在这种结构性暴力 (structural violence) 面前，球员所谓的谈判权不过是权力阶层允许他们参与的一场表演。

The NFL's extension of Roger Goodell through 2030 isn't a professional achievement; it's a textbook case of complicity.

Look at the narrative: a compensation committee hires 'independent third-party consultants' and 'outside counsel' to align pay with 'long-term interests,' claiming 95% of compensation is 'performance-based.' This is a total scam. In a closed circle of power composed of owners, who defines performance? The same owners who saw team valuations double by legalizing sports betting and squeezing more labor out of players (the push for an 18-game season). When 'success' is defined as revenue jumping from $8 billion to $25 billion, Goodell becomes the perfect executor of the system.

This is how meta violence operates: using a veneer of 'rationality,' 'professionalism,' and 'objectivity' to mask inherent predation. Goodell's salary may exceed that of the highest-paid players in the league he presides over, yet his 'performance' is achieved by further exploiting the biological walls—the muscles, joints, and nervous systems—of those very players. The players' bodies are consumables; Goodell's contract is an asset.

The most cynical part is that this extension coincides with the expiration of the CBA. This means in the upcoming existential war, players aren't facing an objective administrator, but a co-conspirator who has already delivered the 'pledge of loyalty' to the owners. In the face of such structural violence, the players' 'bargaining power' is nothing more than a performance permitted by the power elite.
