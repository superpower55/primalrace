Rockstar 的‘专业主义’：用保密协议掩盖的结构性清洗Rockstar's 'Professionalism': A Structural Purge Masked by NDAs

科技 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-11 § 链接
所谓的‘严重违纪’，通常是权力在清洗异见者的叙事伪装。
所谓的 'Gross Misconduct' is usually just a narrative disguise for purging dissenters.

Rockstar Games 这套逻辑非常典型：当员工试图通过 petition 争取 remote working 这种基本权益时，公司启动了 watch list；而当员工在 Discord 这种私密空间表达不满时，公司将其定义为 sharing confidential information。这在博弈论里叫‘定义权抢占’。公司并不在乎你是否真的泄密，它在乎的是你拥有了组织能力，并且试图挑战它定义的‘专业主义’ (professionalism)。

所谓的‘专业’和‘建设性’ (constructive)，在元暴力的叙事里，本质就是要求弱势方在被剥削时保持优雅。当你称呼管理层为 ‘corporate fucks’ 时，公司迅速将焦点从‘非法解雇’转移到‘语言不文明’上。这是一种极其阴险的 cultural violence：它通过攻击受害者的表达方式，来证明施暴者的行为正当性。它想告诉法庭：这些人不专业，所以他们被开除是合理的。

这不仅是一场关于劳动法的纠纷，而是一场存在性战争。在 GTA VI 这种预计营收数十亿美金的巨型项目面前，个体员工的主体性被简化为生产线上的零件。公司通过 watch list 建立起一套监视机制，将‘组织起来的工人’标记为‘潜在的违纪者’。这种 structural violence 的目的很明确：在游戏发布前的最高压时刻，通过清洗关键节点，彻底摧毁工会的共谋可能性，确保资本对生产资料的绝对控制。

Rockstar 辩称 154 个签名者中只有 13 个被开除，试图用概率论来掩盖定向清洗。但事实上，只要这 13 个组织者消失，剩下的 141 个人就会在恐惧中内化‘顺从’。这就是权力的最优解表达：不需要杀掉所有人，只需要杀掉那个敢于定义‘不公正’的人。

Rockstar Games is playing a classic game of narrative capture. When employees signed a petition for remote working, the company initiated a watch list; when those employees expressed frustration in a private Discord, the company labeled it 'sharing confidential information.' This is a textbook move in the existential war for definition: the company doesn't care about the actual leak, but about the fact that workers have developed the capacity to organize and challenge the corporate definition of 'professionalism.'

In the masculine-centric narrative of corporate power, 'professional' and 'constructive' are simply codes for requiring the oppressed to remain elegant while being exploited. By pivoting the conversation from 'unlawful firing' to 'uncivil language' (like calling managers 'corporate fucks'), Rockstar employs cultural violence. They attempt to justify structural violence by attacking the expression of the victims, suggesting that their lack of 'professionalism' makes their dismissal legitimate.

This is more than a labor dispute; it is a battle over existence. Facing a project like GTA VI, which is projected to generate billions, individual employees are reduced to mere components. The watch list is a mechanism of structural violence designed to mark 'organized workers' as 'potential offenders.' The goal is clear: destroy the possibility of union complicity at the peak of production pressure to ensure absolute control over the means of production.

Rockstar's defense—that only 13 of 154 signatories were fired—is a cynical use of statistics to mask a targeted purge. In reality, once the 13 organizers are removed, the remaining 141 will internalize obedience through fear. This is the optimal expression of power: you don't need to kill everyone; you only need to eliminate those who dare to define the 'injustice.'
