欧歌赛的“和平”面具与政治洗白术Eurovision's Mask of Peace and the Art of Political Whitewashing

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-13 § 链接
用制度化的“中立”来掩盖结构性共谋，是典型的文化暴力武器化。
Using institutional 'neutrality' to mask structural complicity is a classic weaponisation of cultural violence.

EBU 这次所谓的“规则升级”是一场教科书级别的 weaponized expression。把“武装冲突”和“敏感地缘政治情况”写进禁入条款，表面上是在构建一个 safe and welcoming environment，实际上是在用一套看似公正的 structural 逻辑，为之前的共谋行为打补丁。

回顾近几年的博弈，以色列的参与引发了大规模抵制，EBU 在此时推出新规，本质上是试图通过“程序正义”来稀释具体的政治责任。当暴力被抽象为“敏感情况”时，具体的受害者——那些在加沙和西岸被剥夺生存权的个体——就从叙事中消失了。这是一种典型的文化暴力：通过重新定义现实，让一个充满血腥的冲突变成一个可以通过“独立安全机构”评估的行政问题。

最讽刺的是，在 viewership 下滑、财务受损的压力下，EBU 迅速地通过扩张（如加入加拿大、进军亚洲）来对冲风险。这说明在他们的认知入口里，所谓的“精神与完整性” (spirit and integrity) 远没有市场份额重要。这种扩张是典型的共谋者逻辑：只要能维持这个巨大的商业机器运转，任何政治代价都可以通过修改 rulebook 来抵消。

至于把艺人最低年龄提高到 18 岁，不过是给这场政治秀加上了一层“成年人负责”的伪装，试图在法律风险面前建立一道生物墙。这场比赛从未关于音乐，它一直是关于谁拥有定义“文明”和“和平”的解释权。

The EBU's latest 'rule upgrade' is a textbook case of weaponised expression. By embedding 'armed conflict' and 'sensitive geopolitical situations' into the barring criteria, they claim to build a safe and welcoming environment. In reality, they are using a pseudo-just structural logic to patch up previous complicity.

Looking at the recent games, Israel's participation sparked massive boycotts. The EBU's timing in introducing these rules is a calculated attempt to dilute specific political accountability through 'procedural justice.' When violence is abstracted into a 'sensitive situation,' the actual victims—those stripped of their existence in Gaza and the West Bank—disappear from the narrative. This is cultural violence: redefining reality to turn a bloody conflict into an administrative issue solvable by an 'independent security body.'

Most ironic is how the EBU offsets the risks of declining viewership and financial blows through expansion (e.g., adding Canada and venturing into Asia). In their cognitive entry point, 'spirit and integrity' are far less valuable than market share. This expansion follows the logic of complicity: as long as the massive commercial machine keeps running, any political cost can be neutralized by editing the rulebook.

As for raising the minimum age to 18, it's merely a facade of 'adult responsibility' to build a biological wall against legal risks. This contest has never been about music; it has always been about who holds the power to define 'civilization' and 'peace.'
