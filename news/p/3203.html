用“置换”掩盖暴力：一个关于人体配额的scamTrading Humans as Chips: The Scam of 'One In One Out'

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-03 § 链接
将受害者视为可互换的筹码，是结构暴力的最高形式。
Treating victims as interchangeable quotas is the ultimate expression of structural violence.

所谓的“one in one out”政策，本质上是一场关于人体的配额博弈。在政府的叙事里，这被包装成一种“秩序”的交换：用一个非法入境者的离开，换取一个合法入境者的进入。这种逻辑将活生生的人降格为等价的筹码，完全抹杀了个体在 trafficking（人口贩卖）和 torture（酷刑）中所经历的 unique 痛苦。这不仅是 structural violence，更是一次极其傲慢的 weaponized 表达——它试图定义什么是“合理的流动”，而将不符合其配额的生命定义为“可剔除的冗余”。

这次法官的裁决是一次典型的 actual 救济，它在 structural 层面上撕开了一个口子：承认了 Home Office 在拒绝重新考虑 trafficking 申诉时的非法性。但我们要警惕这种“好新闻”带来的幻觉。当一个人被强行驱逐到法国，面对走私者的威胁而被迫“消失”在地下时，法律上的“有权重新申诉”在现实的生物墙面前显得极其苍白。如果一个人已经失去了生存的物理基础，那么程序上的正义就成了一种表演性的让步。

谁在共谋？在这个闭环中，国家机器通过制造“小船跨境”的恐惧叙事，诱导公众接受这种非人道的置换逻辑，从而在公共空间中合法化对弱势种族的排斥。而 Home Office 面对裁决时的“仔细考虑上诉选项”，正是这种共谋机制的惯性——他们并不在乎个体是否获得了 justice，他们在乎的是如何修补这个一个换一个的 scam，以确保权力对人口流动的绝对解释权不被撼动。

The so-called “one in one out” policy is essentially a quota game played with human bodies. In the government's narrative, this is packaged as an exchange of “order”: trading the removal of one irregular migrant for the legal entry of another. This logic degrades living beings into equivalent chips, completely erasing the unique suffering of individuals enduring trafficking and torture. This is not just structural violence, but a highly arrogant weaponisation of expression—attempting to define what constitutes “reasonable flow” while marking lives that don't fit the quota as “expendable redundancy.”

The judge's ruling is a classic piece of actual relief, tearing a hole in the structural layer by recognizing the illegality of the Home Office's refusal to reconsider trafficking claims. However, we must be wary of the illusion created by such “good news.” When a person is forcibly removed to France and forced “underground” due to threats from smugglers, the legal “right to reconsideration” appears utterly pale against the biological wall of survival. If a person has already lost the physical basis for existence, procedural justice becomes a form of performative concession.

Who are the complicit? In this loop, the state apparatus uses the fear-narrative of “small boat crossings” to induce the public into accepting this inhumane exchange logic, thereby legitimizing the exclusion of vulnerable races in the public sphere. The Home Office’s response— “carefully considering options for appeal”—is the inertia of this complicity. They do not care if the individual achieves justice; they care about how to patch this “one-for-one” scam to ensure their monopoly on the interpretation of population movement remains unchallenged.
