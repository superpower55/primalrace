用 28 个人给 2000 万人制造恐惧：一场关于“合法性”的认知武器化Using 28 People to Terrorize 20 Million: The Weaponisation of 'Legitimacy'

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-19 § 链接
用极少数个案掩盖结构性剥夺，是权力通过制造“恐惧”来夺取解释权的典型 scam。
Using isolated cases to mask structural disenfranchisement is a classic scam of power seizing the right of interpretation through manufactured fear.

司法部起诉 28 个绿卡持有者非法投票，这在加州 2310 万注册选民的基数面前，在统计学上几乎等于零。但这件事的本质不是为了维护选举的“纯洁”，而是一次教科书般的表达武器化（Weaponisation of Expression）。

权力者并不在乎这 28 个人是否真的投票了，他们在乎的是通过这 28 个个案，在公众认知中植入一个“非公民正在窃取选票”的叙事入口。一旦这个叙事成立，那么接下来的 2 万个“疑似”名单，甚至 19 万个“可能”名单，就全部变成了合法打击的借口。这种从 19 万到 2 万的随意跳跃，证明了所谓的“数据”只是权力的装饰品，其目的是为了在选举前制造不确定性，从而在结果出炉后，拥有随时宣布其“无效”的解释权。

这是一个典型的元暴力（Meta Violence）运作过程：先定义一个“威胁”，再利用执法机关的直接暴力（逮捕、起诉）来证明这个威胁的存在，最后通过文化层面的恐惧，让数以万计的合法公民在面对审核时产生自我审查的寒蝉效应。当司法部承认名单“不完美”时，它实际上在说：为了赢得这场存在性战争，误伤几个公民是必要的成本。

这场博弈的真.最优解应该是公正的选举程序，但目前的执行者在追求一个假.最优解——通过恐吓选举官员和选民，将选举结果的定价权重新夺回手中。所谓的“打击欺诈”，不过是权力在为自己的预设结果提前铺路。

The DOJ charging 28 green card holders for illegal voting is statistically zero against California's 23.1 million registered voters. However, this is not about 'election purity'; it is a textbook case of the Weaponisation of Expression.

The powers that be don't care about these 28 individuals. They care about using these cases to plant a narrative entry point: that 'non-citizens are stealing votes.' Once this narrative takes hold, the 'suspected' list of 20,000—and the 'possible' list of 190,000—become legitimate targets for suppression. The arbitrary jump from 190k to 20k proves that 'data' is merely an ornament for power, used to manufacture doubt and seize the right of interpretation over the upcoming results.

This is Meta Violence in action: define a 'threat,' validate it through the direct violence of arrests, and then use cultural fear to induce self-censorship among millions of legal citizens. When the DOJ admits their list is 'not perfect,' they are admitting that collateral damage to citizens is a necessary cost in this existential war.

The true optimal expression would be a just electoral process. Instead, the administration is pursuing a fake optimal expression—intimidating officials and voters to reclaim the pricing power over the election outcome. This 'crackdown on fraud' is nothing more than power paving the way for its own predetermined conclusion.
