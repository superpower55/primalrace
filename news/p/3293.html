FIFA的“黑手党”逻辑与资本法西斯的共谋FIFA's Mafia Logic and the Complicity of Capitalist Fascism

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-05 § 链接
权力的更迭不是正义的回归，而是共谋者在分赃不均后的内部清洗。
The fall of a leader is not the return of justice, but an internal purge among co-conspirators over spoils.

很多人在庆祝 Infantino 被踢出局，觉得这是一种 karmic justice。但这种 naive 的快感掩盖了一个事实：FIFA 从来就不是一个体育组织，而是一个典型的 meta-violence 运作场。它建立在一种封建式的 tribute and protection 体系之上，用一个 supralegal 的外壳屏蔽了所有外部的监督。在这种结构里，规则不是为了公正，而是为了筛选出最忠诚的共谋者。

回顾过去八年，从卡塔尔世界杯的血泪，到对沙特的无脑让路，那些现在对着 Infantino 破口大骂的足球执行官们，在当时全部是他的 complicity。他们之前对血汗工厂和权力操弄的沉默，就是他们进入这个“黑手党家庭”的投名状。现在他们突然决定“反叛”，绝不是因为良心发现，而是因为 Infantino 试图通过私有化世界杯来重新分配利益，触碰了共谋者们的蛋糕。

这本质上是一场关于定价权的博弈。Infantino 试图把足球这个 mass pursuit 彻底 weaponized，将其转化为一种纯粹的资本工具，向 billionaire investor class 递交投名状。当他试图独占更大份额的 largesse 时，他就不再是那个能带领大家分赃的领袖，而成了阻碍他人获利的障碍。于是，所谓的“治理危机”和“道德指责”就成了最完美的武器化叙事，用来掩盖一次简单的 mob-style decapitation。

Infantino 倒了，但那个 disdain for humanity 的资本法西斯逻辑依然在。只要 FIFA 的结构层（structural layer）不改变，只要它依然是一个不受约束的权力黑盒，那么下一个接任者只会用更精致的 reform 包装，继续这场关于贪婪的共谋。

Many are celebrating Infantino's downfall as karmic justice. But this naive satisfaction masks a colder truth: FIFA has never been a sports organization; it is a textbook arena of meta-violence. It operates on a feudal system of tribute and protection, using a supralegal shell to shield itself from any external scrutiny. In this structure, rules are not designed for fairness, but to filter for the most loyal co-conspirators.

Looking back at the last eight years—from the blood-soaked Qatar World Cup to the blind surrender to Saudi Arabia—the football executives now screaming about morality were all active agents of complicity. Their silence toward sweatshops and power grabs was their initiation rite into this 'mafia family.' Their sudden 'rebellion' isn't a moral awakening; it is a reaction to Infantino's attempt to privatize the World Cup, which threatened the shared cake of the co-conspirators.

This is fundamentally a game over pricing power. Infantino tried to fully weaponize football, transforming a mass pursuit into a pure instrument of capital to prove his loyalty to the billionaire investor class. When he attempted to secure a larger piece of the largesse for himself, he ceased to be the leader who distributed spoils and became an obstacle. Consequently, 'governance crises' and 'moral objections' became the weaponized narratives used to justify a simple mob-style decapitation.

Infantino may fall, but the underlying logic of capitalist fascism—a profound disdain for humanity—remains. As long as the structural layer of FIFA remains an unchecked black box of power, the next regime will simply wear a more polished pageant sash of reform while continuing the same game of complicity.
