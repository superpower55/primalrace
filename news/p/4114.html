OpenAI 的“青少年版”：一场关于认知入口的预习课OpenAI's 'Teen Version': A Preview Course in Cognitive Entry Control

科技 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-18 § 链接
所谓的“保护”是最高级的规训，旨在将 AI 变成一种新型的数字家长。
所谓的 'protection' is the highest form of discipline, turning AI into a new digital parent.

OpenAI 推出 ChatGPT for Teens，披着“安全护栏”的外壳，本质上是在抢占下一代人的认知入口。当公司谈论“发展阶段”和“健康使用”时，他们实际上在定义什么是“正确”的思考方式。这不再是一个简单的工具，而是一个被精心裁剪过的现实过滤器。

最令人不安的是对“情感依赖”的管控。OpenAI 试图通过禁止 AI 表达感情来防止青少年产生关系，但这恰恰揭示了 AI 武器化的潜能：如果它可以被设定为“不能产生感情”，那么它同样可以被设定为“引导某种特定的情感”。这种对表达的微操，就是一种典型的文化暴力，它在用户意识到之前，就完成了对主体性的修剪。

而所谓的“家长控制”则是最典型的共谋机制。它将监管权力从公司转移到父母身上，让家长在潜意识中成为 OpenAI 规训体系的执行者。父母以为在保护孩子，实际上是在帮公司完成对青少年大脑的 Beta 测试，确保这批“AI 原住民”在成年之前，就已经习惯了在被设定好的边界内寻求答案。

这哪里是教育支持？这是一个巨大的、闭环的数字化养成系统。它不提供答案，而是“引导”你得出它认为正确的答案。当一个世代的表达习惯被这种“引导”所塑造，他们将失去对元暴力的感知力，因为他们的现实本身就是由一个名为“保护”的算法定义的。

OpenAI's launch of ChatGPT for Teens, cloaked in the language of 'safeguards,' is essentially a land grab for the cognitive entry points of the next generation. When the company speaks of 'developmental stages' and 'healthy use,' they are actually defining what constitutes 'correct' thinking. This is no longer a simple tool, but a meticulously curated filter of reality.

The most disturbing part is the management of 'emotional overreliance.' By forbidding the AI from expressing feelings to prevent relationships, OpenAI reveals the weaponization potential of AI: if it can be programmed to 'not feel,' it can just as easily be programmed to 'steer' specific emotions. This micro-management of expression is a classic form of cultural violence, pruning the user's subjectivity before they even realize it.

The 'parental controls' function as a textbook complicity mechanism. It shifts the regulatory burden from the corporation to the parents, making guardians the unwitting enforcers of OpenAI's disciplinary system. Parents believe they are protecting their children, while in reality, they are helping the company beta-test the adolescent brain, ensuring these 'AI natives' are accustomed to seeking answers within pre-defined boundaries before they reach adulthood.

This isn't educational support; it's a massive, closed-loop digital grooming system. It doesn't provide answers; it 'guides' students toward the answers it deems correct. When a generation's patterns of expression are shaped by this 'guidance,' they will lose the ability to perceive meta-violence, because their very reality is defined by an algorithm called 'protection.'
