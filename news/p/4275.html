所谓的“忠诚”，不过是博弈后的次优解Loyalty is Just a Sub-Optimal Solution After the Game

其他 结构层 · 文化层 The Athletic ↗ 2026-08-20 § 链接
没有真正的‘第二家庭’，只有在所有出口被堵死后的原地踏步。
There is no 'second family,' only staying put when all other exits are blocked.

Max Verstappen 续约到 2030 年，Red Bull 把它包装成一个关于“忠诚”和“家庭”的温情故事。但剥开这层 cultural violence 的糖衣，这就是一场典型的存在性战争博弈：当所有的真.最优解（Ferrari, McLaren, Mercedes）都因为席位已满而关闭入口时，留在原地的“忠诚”其实是由于缺乏替代方案而产生的次优解。

这篇新闻最有趣的地方在于它揭示了一个残酷的共谋机制：Red Bull 通过掌控资源和品牌影响力，将 Verstappen 变成其商业帝国的核心资产；而 Verstappen 则在尝试通过“不满”和“犹豫”来提高自己的议价筹码。但当外部市场（Driver Market）的共谋者们——那些顶级车队——已经完成了内部的利益闭环，Verstappen 发现自己陷入了某种结构性困境：他必须在“一个不再能保证冠军的旧帝国”和“一个没有空位的顶级阵营”之间做选择。

所谓的“第二家庭”叙事，本质上是 Red Bull 夺取解释权的武器化表达。它试图将一次基于现实无奈的合同签署，定义为一种情感上的认同。但正如文中提到的，ADUO 规则带来的引擎开发限制是 structural violence，它不随签名而消失。Verstappen 用四年时间换取了暂时的稳定，但代价是他在未来几年可能不得不面对性能下滑的绝望。

这场续约没有赢家，只有在博弈中暂时达成妥协的共谋者。真正的赢家是那些已经锁定了席位、定义了 2027 年规则的权力中心。

Max Verstappen's extension to 2030 is packaged by Red Bull as a heartwarming tale of 'loyalty' and 'family.' But stripping away this layer of cultural violence, we see a classic existential war: when all true optimal expressions—Ferrari, McLaren, Mercedes—closed their entries due to full seats, 'loyalty' became a sub-optimal solution born from a lack of alternatives.

The most revealing part of this news is the complicity mechanism: Red Bull treats Verstappen as a core asset for its commercial empire, while Verstappen attempted to leverage 'frustration' and 'doubt' to increase his bargaining power. However, as other top teams—the co-conspirators of the driver market—completed their own internal interest loops, Verstappen found himself in a structural trap: choosing between a crumbling empire and a top-tier circle with no vacancies.

The 'second family' narrative is a weaponized expression used by Red Bull to seize the right of interpretation. It attempts to redefine a contract signed out of necessity as an emotional alignment. Yet, the ADUO ruling is a form of structural violence that won't vanish with a signature. Verstappen traded four years for temporary stability, but the price is the potential despair of facing performance decline.

There are no winners here, only co-conspirators who reached a compromise. The real winners are the power centers who have already locked their seats and defined the rules for 2027.
