AI边检员与贸易战的PR表演AI Border Detectives and the PR Theater of Trade War

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-20 § 链接
用技术手段掩盖战略胆怯，是典型的用‘换尺子’代替‘换能力’。
Using technical tools to mask strategic cowardice is a classic case of changing the ruler instead of the capability.

白宫发布的《大转运骗局》（The Great Transhipment Scam）报告，本质上是一场极其拙劣的 PR 行为。当特朗普政府发现直接对华贸易战在宏观数据上失效时，他们选择了一个最符合其审美且成本最低的路径：制造一个名为“AI边检员”的技术图腾，通过在越南、墨西哥等地的转运环节玩一场“打地鼠”游戏，来向底层的制造业选民表演一种“我在战斗”的姿态。

这在我的框架里是典型的武器化叙事。通过将问题定义为“转运骗局”，白宫成功地将矛盾从战略层面的博弈（如汇率操纵、预算赤字）转移到了执行层面的“抓小偷”游戏。AI 侦探被赋予了“永不疲倦”的神圣属性，试图用一种技术决定论来掩盖政治上的 chicken-shit。这种逻辑和某些公司宣布新定律来掩盖芯片突破失败如出一辙：既然现实不配合，那就重新定义现实。把“进口额下降”定义为胜利，把“价值链依然被掌控”定义为他国转运的骗局，这不过是在用一种假的最优解表达来维持权力者的体面。

真正的 structural violence 在于全球贸易失衡的深层机制——低估的汇率和被压制的国内消费。但解决这个问题的代价是巨大的：它要求美国面对自己的预算赤字，要求中国面对其社会契约的失效。对于共谋者而言，维持现状是最优解。美国精英阶层需要中国的廉价商品和资本来支撑其财政赤字，而北京需要出口来维持增长。在这种共谋下，AI 边检员成了最好的掩体：它让双方在不触动核心利益的前提下，在文化层面上完成了关于“贸易战”的叙事闭环。

这场游戏最讽刺的地方在于，那个 AI 侦探永远无法找回托莱多或凤凰城的工厂岗位。因为岗位消失不是因为几个电机绕道越南，而是因为全球价值链的定价权早已易手。只要解释权依然在那些试图用 AI 掩盖胆怯的人手中，这种表演性让步就会一直持续下去。

The White House's 'The Great Transhipment Scam' report is essentially a clumsy piece of PR. Having realized that the direct trade war is failing at the macro level, the Trump administration chose a path that fits their aesthetic and minimizes cost: creating a technical totem called the 'AI Border Detective.' By playing a game of 'Whac-A-Mole' with transshipments in Vietnam and Mexico, they are performing a theater of 'fighting' for their rust-belt voters.

In my framework, this is a textbook weaponization of narrative. By defining the problem as a 'transhipment scam,' the White House successfully shifts the conflict from strategic gaming (such as currency manipulation and budget deficits) to an operational game of 'catching thieves.' The AI detective is endowed with the sacred attribute of being 'tireless,' attempting to use technical determinism to cover up political chickenshit. This logic mirrors companies announcing new laws to hide failures in chip breakthroughs: when reality doesn't cooperate, simply redefine reality. Defining 'falling imports' as victory and 'continued value-chain control' as a scam by third parties is merely using a fake optimal expression to maintain the dignity of the powerful.

The real structural violence lies in the deep mechanisms of global trade imbalance—undervalued currencies and suppressed domestic consumption. But solving this requires a massive price: it asks the US to face its budget deficit and China to face the failure of its social contract. For the complicity involved, maintaining the status quo is the optimal solution. US elites need cheap Chinese goods and capital to support their fiscal deficits, while Beijing needs exports to sustain growth. Under this complicity, the AI detective becomes the perfect cover: it allows both sides to maintain a narrative closure of 'trade war' at the cultural level without touching core interests.

The most ironic part is that the AI detective will never restore factory jobs to Toledo or Phoenix. Jobs didn't vanish because a few motors routed through Vietnam; they vanished because the pricing power of the global value chain has already shifted. As long as the power of interpretation remains with those attempting to hide cowardice behind AI, this performative concession will continue.
