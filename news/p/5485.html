直播处决：美式元暴力的审美回归Livestreaming Execution: The Aesthetic Regression of US Meta-Violence

国际 文化层 · 元暴力 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-10 § 链接
当权力不再通过制度而通过“景观”来确证，文明就成了暴力的掩体。
When power is validated through spectacle rather than institution, civilization becomes a mere shield for violence.

Pete Hegseth 想要直播处决 Nidal Hasan，这根本不是什么“正义的伸张”，而是一次极其典型的表达武器化 (weaponisation of expression)。他选择在原案发地、用原案发武器、通过直播这种现代认知入口来执行，本质上是在制造一场精心编排的 Spectacle。这种对暴力的“审美化”处理，目的只有一个：夺取解释权，将国家机器的暴力直接转化为一种可见的、具有威慑力的政治符号。

这种操作让美国直接进入了塔利班和伊朗的共谋俱乐部。虽然在 cultural 层面上，美国习惯于用“人权”和“文明”作为掩体，但在 meta 层面上，这种直播处决与 IS 的斩首视频没有任何本质区别——它们都在利用恐惧来 cowering a population，通过将人体客体化为可消费的恐怖景观，来确证统治者的权力。当“法律程序”变成了“直播剧本”，司法公正就死在了流量之中。

最讽刺的是，这种“tough on crime”的叙事通常被包装成对受害者的补偿，但实际上，它服务的是一种极端的 masculine-centric narrative：通过一个强有力的、施暴的男性形象（国防部长）去惩罚另一个被标记为“异端”的男性，从而在男性社群中完成一次权力认同的闭环。这依然是元暴力 (meta violence) 的逻辑：通过定义谁是“非人”，来合法化对肉体的摧毁。

如果这次直播成行，它标志着美国在 structural 层的法治退步，以及在 cultural 层面的彻底溃败。它告诉世界，所谓的“文明”只是一个随时可以撕掉的标签，而底层的逻辑永远是：谁掌握了定义“真实”的权力，谁就能在直播间里合法地杀人。

Pete Hegseth’s plan to livestream the execution of Nidal Hasan is not about 'justice'; it is a textbook case of the weaponisation of expression. By choosing the original site, the original weapon, and the modern cognitive entry point of livestreaming, he is manufacturing a Spectacle. This 'aestheticization' of violence serves one purpose: to seize the power of interpretation and transform state violence into a visible, deterrent political symbol.

This move places the US in a complicity club with the Taliban and Iran. While the US habitually uses 'human rights' and 'civilization' as cultural shields, at the meta level, this livestreamed execution is no different from IS beheading videos. Both utilize terror to cow a population, objectifying the human body into a consumable landscape of horror to validate the ruler's power. When 'due process' becomes a 'livestream script,' judicial justice dies in the pursuit of traffic.

Crucially, this 'tough on crime' narrative is often packaged as compensation for victims, but it actually serves a hyper-masculine-centric narrative: a powerful, violent male figure (the Secretary of Defense) punishing another male marked as a 'heretic' to complete a loop of power identification within the masculine hierarchy. This is the logic of meta violence: legitimizing the destruction of flesh by defining the target as 'non-human.'

If this livestream proceeds, it marks a structural regression of the rule of law and a total cultural collapse. It signals that 'civilization' is merely a detachable label, while the underlying logic remains: whoever controls the power to define 'reality' can legally kill in a livestream.
