被精准猎杀的救护车：当“人道”成为战争的掩体Hunting the Healers: When 'Humanitarianism' Becomes a War Mask

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-29 § 链接
Direct violence 只是结果，真正的暴力是赋予杀戮医者的“合理性”叙事。
Direct violence is merely the result; the true violence lies in the narrative that justifies the slaughter of healers.

在加尔通的暴力三角中，直接暴力 (direct violence) 往往是最容易被看见的血腥，但最令人心惊的其实是文化层 (cultural layer) 的共谋。以色列军队通过一种典型的 weaponized 叙事——声称救护车被用作武器运输——将医疗工作者从“受保护者”重新定义为“合法目标”。

这是一次极其阴险的认知入口夺取：当一个医疗设施被定义为“掩体”或“军事目标”时，杀掉一名医生就不再是战争罪行，而成了“清除威胁”。这种叙事将医疗人员的主体性彻底抹除，把他们降格为某种政治博弈中的可消耗零件。在这种 meta-violence 的笼罩下，即便穿着救护员制服的 16 岁少年 Joud 也会被无人机精准猎杀，因为在对方的解释权里，制服已不再是免死金牌，而是伪装的掩护。

最残酷的 structural violence 在于，这种杀戮是通过“双击打击” (double-tap strikes) 实现的——在第一波袭击后，针对前来救援的医护人员发起第二波攻击。这不仅是对肉体的毁灭，更是对人类文明底线中“救助”这一行为的系统性摧毁。当救助者本身成为猎物，整个社会的 Potential 状态被强行拉低，Actual 的生存空间被极度压缩。

所谓的 ceasefire 在这种解释权垄断面前只是一个 PR 词汇。只要杀戮的“合理性”依然被掌握在权力者手中，救护车就永远无法在战场上获得公正的表达 (Just Expression)，它们只是在死亡的概率分布中，等待被下一枚导弹定义的客体。

In Galtung's Violence Triangle, direct violence is the most visible bloodbath, but the most chilling part is the complicity at the cultural layer. The Israeli military employs a classic weaponized narrative—claiming ambulances are used for weapon transport—to redefine healthcare workers from 'protected persons' to 'legitimate targets.'

This is a sinister seizure of the cognitive entry point: once a medical facility is defined as a 'shield' or 'military objective,' killing a doctor is no longer a war crime, but 'neutralizing a threat.' This narrative erases the subjectivity of medical personnel, reducing them to disposable parts in a political game. Under this meta-violence, even a 16-year-old paramedic like Joud, wearing a uniform, is hunted by drones because, in the occupier's interpretation, the uniform is no longer a shield but a camouflage.

The most brutal structural violence manifests in 'double-tap strikes'—launching a second attack specifically targeting the rescuers arriving after the first blast. This is not just physical destruction; it is a systematic demolition of 'rescue' as a fundamental human act. When the rescuer becomes the prey, the society's Potential state is forcibly lowered, and the Actual space for survival is crushed.

Any so-called ceasefire is merely a PR term in the face of this monopoly over interpretation. As long as the 'justification' for slaughter remains in the hands of the powerful, ambulances will never achieve a Just Expression on the battlefield; they remain mere objects awaiting their definition by the next missile.
