所谓的“证据”不过是国家机器的剧本The 'Evidence' is Just a Script Written by the State Machine

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-13 § 链接
法律在元暴力面前只是一个用来合法化陷阱的掩体。
Law is merely a cover to legitimize traps under the shadow of meta-violence.

所谓的“伯明翰四人组”案，本质上是一场由国家机器导演的 existence war。当警方建立一家名为“Hero Couriers”的假公司，并派遣名为“Vincent”的卧底进入时，这已经不是在侦查犯罪，而是在“制造”犯罪。在元暴力的逻辑下，只要你被标记为“异己”或“潜在威胁”，你就不再是一个拥有人权的主体，而是一个可以被随意编辑的剧本角色。

这起案件最令人作呕的 structural violence 在于证据的“巧合”。武器刚好出现在车里，卧底刚好“偶然”发现。这种叙事在法庭上被包装成正义的胜利，而律师关于“栽赃”的指控被嘲笑。为什么？因为法庭、陪审团和法官共同构成了一个 complicity 场域。他们共谋维护一个简单的叙事：国家机器永远正确，而“恐怖分子”永远有罪。在这种共谋中，真相的价值低于系统稳定性的价值。

现在，undercover policing inquiry 揭露了警方伪造证据的惯例。这证明了这种暴力不是个案，而是一种标准操作流程（SOP）。当一个系统习惯于通过 fabricate evidence 来达成政治目的时，它所宣称的“法治”就是一个巨大的 scam。CCRC 的拖延和警方的傲慢，不过是 structural violence 的惯性，他们在等待这个剧本被时间冲淡，或者等待下一个更强大的叙事将其覆盖。

真正的正义不是通过几个律师的申请来“争取”的，而是要拆穿那个名为“文明秩序”的掩体，让人们意识到：只要解释权被垄断在权力中心，任何一个不顺从的人，都可能在某一天被定义为“恐怖分子”。

The 'Birmingham Four' case is essentially an existence war directed by the state machine. When the police set up a fake company called 'Hero Couriers' and embedded an undercover agent named 'Vincent', they weren't investigating crime—they were manufacturing it. Under the logic of meta-violence, once you are tagged as 'the other' or a 'potential threat', you cease to be a subject with human rights and become a character in a script that can be edited at will.

The most sickening structural violence in this case is the 'coincidence' of the evidence. Weapons just happen to be in the car; the agent just happens to 'stumble upon' them. This narrative was packaged as a victory for justice in court, while the lawyers' claims of planting evidence were ridiculed. Why? Because the court, the jury, and the judge together formed a field of complicity. They conspired to maintain a simple narrative: the state machine is always right, and 'terrorists' are always guilty. In this complicity, the value of truth is lower than the value of systemic stability.

Now, the undercover policing inquiry reveals a pattern of fabricating evidence. This proves that such violence is not an isolated incident but a Standard Operating Procedure (SOP). When a system habitually uses fabricated evidence to achieve political ends, its proclaimed 'rule of law' is a massive scam. The delays of the CCRC and the arrogance of the police are merely the inertia of structural violence; they are waiting for the script to fade with time or be overwritten by a newer, more powerful narrative.

True justice is not something to be 'won' through a few lawyers' applications; it requires tearing down the cover called 'civilized order' to realize that as long as the power of interpretation is monopolized by the center of power, anyone who refuses to conform can be defined as a 'terrorist' one day.
