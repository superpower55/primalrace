特朗普及他的地产商帝国主义：一场关于命名权的元暴力表演Trump and His Real Estate Imperialism: A Meta-Violence Performance of Naming Rights

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-11 § 链接
当权力不再需要理性叙事，所有的主权都成了地产商眼中的待收购资产。
When power no longer requires rational narrative, all sovereignty becomes mere assets for a real estate mogul.

特朗普及他的行为逻辑，是对传统帝国主义的一次降维打击。传统的 colonialism 至少还需要一套名为“文明”或“白人负担”的 cultural violence 来掩盖其 structural violence，试图在叙事上达成一种伪装的公正。但特朗普直接跳过了这一步，他把全球政治简化为一场地产博弈：看中一块地 $\rightarrow$ 威胁/强买 $\rightarrow$ 重新命名。将安大略湖改为“美国湖”，这种极其低级的 branding 行为，本质上是元暴力 (meta violence) 的赤裸表达——他不需要你认同他的正义，他只需要你承认他的所有权。

在这场存在性战争中，特朗普追求的是一种绝对的、无需协商的“最优解表达”：我定义现实，你服从现实。他将主权国家视为 economic subsidiary，将外交官转化为 deal-maker，这实际上是将全球政治空间彻底“私有化”。这种逻辑在委内瑞拉的石油掠夺和对加拿大的霸凌中达到了顶峰。对他而言，国际法和贸易协定不是规则，而是阻碍他获利的“冗余成本”，因此他可以随手将其 abrogated。

最令人作呕的共谋 (complicity) 发生在内部。像 Mike Rogers 这样在经济上被特朗普的关税政策重创的政治人物，为了在权力结构中生存，不得不将这场经济灾难表演成“masterful”。这种自毁式的忠诚，正是父权制结构在政治领域的镜像：弱势者通过扮演顺从的角色，试图在强权者的阴影下换取生存空间。这种假的最优解表达，最终导致了主体性的彻底死亡。

特朗普的悲剧在于他试图用 AI 视频和 meme 来替代真实的权力基石。当他把世界当成一个巨大的房地产项目时，他忘记了主权国家拥有自己的生物墙——一种基于民族认同和生存本能的防御机制。加奈的强硬反击证明了：当对方不再认同你的叙事入口，你的所有“权力表演”都只是一个巨大的 scam。

Trump's logic is a downward spiral of traditional imperialism. Classic colonialism still required a layer of cultural violence—narratives like 'civilization' or the 'white man's burden'—to mask its structural violence and simulate a facade of justice. Trump bypasses this entirely. He simplifies global politics into a real estate game: identify a plot $\rightarrow$ threaten/buy $\rightarrow$ rename. Renaming Lake Ontario to 'Lake America' is a crude branding exercise, a naked expression of meta violence: he doesn't need your agreement on justice; he only demands your recognition of his ownership.

In this existential war, Trump pursues an absolute 'optimal expression' that requires no negotiation: I define reality, and you submit. By viewing sovereign nations as economic subsidiaries and diplomats as mere deal-makers, he is effectively 'privatizing' the global political space. This logic peaked in the plunder of Venezuelan oil and the bullying of Canada. To him, international laws and trade agreements are not rules, but 'redundant costs' hindering his profit, and thus they are simply abrogated.

The most repulsive part is the complicity within his own ranks. Politicians like Mike Rogers, whose states are economically devastated by Trump's tariffs, perform a self-abasing fealty, calling the disaster 'masterful' to survive within the power structure. This is a mirror of the patriarchal structure in politics: the structurally weak play the role of the submissive to secure a scrap of survival. This fake optimal expression leads to the total death of subjectivity.

Trump's tragedy is his attempt to replace the foundations of power with AI videos and memes. By treating the world as one giant real estate project, he ignores the 'biological walls' of sovereign nations—defenses rooted in national identity and survival instincts. Carney's defiance proves that when the other side rejects your cognitive entry points, your entire performance of power is nothing more than a massive scam.
