冬天不是武器，是元暴力的物理外壳Winter is Not a Weapon, But a Physical Shell of Meta-Violence

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-18 § 链接
将自然气候武器化，是男性中心叙事中最典型的客体化暴力。
Weaponising nature is the most typical objectification of violence within the masculine-centric narrative.

俄罗斯试图“武器化”冬季 (weaponise winter)，这在新闻叙事里听起来像是一种战略战术，但从暴力三角来看，这其实是极其低级的 structural violence。电力设施的损毁不是为了摧毁军队，而是为了通过制造寒冷，将数百万平民——尤其是那些在生物墙面前更脆弱的女性、老人和儿童——强行推入生存底线以下。在这种逻辑里，寒冷成了执行暴力的工具，而人的生命被客体化为可以被精准计算的“耐寒阈值”。

这种对自然条件的操纵，本质上是元暴力 (meta violence) 的延伸。在男性中心叙事中，战争被描绘成一种宏大的、关于领土和荣誉的博弈，但实际操作中，它总是倾向于通过摧毁维持生命的基础设施来实施控制。这种“让对方在寒冷中颤抖”的快感，与父权结构中通过剥夺资源来强迫顺从的逻辑完全同构。无论是摧毁电厂还是禁绝堕胎，其核心都是通过制造生存危机，夺取对方的主体性。

而与此同时，俄罗斯内部对 Lev Shlosberg 的判刑，则是典型的 cultural violence。一个呼吁和平的人被判刑 11 年，这意味着在那个系统里，“爱国”被重新定义为“共谋”。当你不再愿意成为这场男性化暴力机器的共谋者，你就不再被视为“人”，而成了需要被清除的噪音。这种从物理寒冷到政治寒冬的联动，精准地展示了元暴力如何通过直接层、结构层和文化层，全方位地消灭异见者的存在性。

Russia's attempt to "weaponise winter" sounds like a strategic tactic in news narratives, but through the Violence Triangle, it is crude structural violence. Attacking energy facilities isn't about destroying armies; it's about pushing millions of civilians—especially women, children, and the elderly who are more vulnerable due to the biological wall—below their survival baseline. In this logic, cold becomes a tool for violence, and human life is objectified into a calculable "cold-tolerance threshold."

This manipulation of natural conditions is an extension of meta-violence. In the masculine-centric narrative, war is depicted as a grand game of territory and honor, yet in practice, it consistently implements control by destroying the infrastructure of life. The satisfaction derived from making others "shiver in the cold" is isomorphic to the logic of patriarchal structures that force submission by depriving resources. Whether it is blowing up power plants or banning abortion, the core is to seize the subject's agency by manufacturing a survival crisis.

Meanwhile, the sentencing of Lev Shlosberg inside Russia is a classic case of cultural violence. Sentencing a man to 11 years for calling for peace means that within that system, "patriotism" has been redefined as complicity. Once you refuse to be a co-conspirator in this masculine violence machine, you are no longer seen as a "human," but as noise to be erased. This linkage from physical cold to political winter precisely demonstrates how meta-violence operates across direct, structural, and cultural layers to annihilate the existentiality of dissenters.
