信息高速公路上的仇恨工厂：俄式武器化的认知入口Hate Factories on the Information Superhighway: The Russian Weaponisation of Cognition

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-07 § 链接
认知入口的垄断比单一谎言更可怕，它在制造一个自动扩音的仇恨结构。
Monopoly over cognitive entry points is deadlier than a single lie; it builds an automated structure for hate.

这不是一次简单的“假新闻”传播，而是一场典型的表达武器化 (weaponisation of expression) 实验。俄罗斯通过 Rybar 等认知入口，将塞塔 (Ceuta) 的移民危机迅速定义为一场“袭击” (assault)。请注意这个词的选择：它将绝望的生存博弈瞬间转化为一种军事化的进攻叙事，从而为随后的极右翼暴力合法化提供了文化层面的背书。

最令人不安的是 411 分析中提到的那个“信息高速公路” (information superhighway)。这本质上是一个预设的结构性暴力 (structural violence) 机制。当一个叙事被选入这个“特权通道”，它不再需要逻辑，只需要通过“重复器”账户进行指数级放大。这种 machinery 的目的不是为了说服谁，而是为了制造一种“共识”的假象，让原本处于边缘的极右翼话语在短时间内通过量级压制，夺取对“事实”的定义权。

这套机制的底层逻辑是元暴力 (meta violence) 的全球延伸：通过制造敌我对立，将移民——这个最缺乏表达权的群体——彻底客体化为“威胁”。而那些在广场上集结的“巡逻者”，不过是这套武器化叙事下的共谋者 (complicit)，他们在执行一种被精心设计的“保护”表演，而真正的受益者是那些试图通过撕裂欧洲社会来获取地缘政治筹码的权力中心。

当这种 machinery 成为常态，下一次危机到来时，人们看到的将不再是具体的人，而是一组被预设好的标签。这种对认知入口的精准操控，正在将公共讨论变成一个高效的仇恨加工厂。

This is not a simple case of 'fake news,' but a textbook example of the weaponisation of expression. By utilizing cognitive entry points like Rybar, Russia swiftly reframed the Ceuta migration crisis as an 'assault.' Note the choice of words: it transforms a desperate existential struggle for survival into a militarized offensive narrative, providing the cultural violence necessary to legitimize subsequent far-right aggression.

The most disturbing part is the 'information superhighway' identified by 411. This is essentially a mechanism of structural violence. Once a narrative is selected for this 'privileged onramp,' it no longer requires logic; it only needs exponential amplification via 'repeater' accounts. The goal of this machinery is not to persuade, but to manufacture a facade of consensus, allowing fringe far-right discourse to seize the power to define 'fact' through sheer volume.

The underlying logic here is the global extension of meta violence: by creating a binary of enemy and friend, migrants—the group with the least expression power—are completely objectified as 'threats.' The 'patrollers' gathering in the squares are merely complicit actors in this weaponized narrative, performing a choreographed act of 'protection' while the real beneficiaries are the power centers seeking geopolitical leverage by fracturing European society.

As this machinery becomes normalized, the next crisis will not present us with human beings, but with a set of pre-installed labels. This precision control of cognitive entry points is turning public discourse into an efficient factory for hatred.
