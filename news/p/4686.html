Milo 的恐惧：当共谋者的投名状变成绞索Milo's Terror: When the Complicity Pledge Becomes a Noose

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-01 § 链接
所有基于权力崇拜的共谋，最终都会被权力用来定义为‘可消耗品’。
All complicity based on power worship eventually ends with power defining you as a disposable object.

Milo Yiannopoulos 的这次‘觉醒’简直是教科书级别的存在性战争反噬。一个曾经通过煽动大规模驱逐、呼吁在超市门口设 ICE 检查站来换取权力认同的 far-right 评论员，在自己被 ICE 像对待 MS-13 帮派成员一样对待后，突然发现了 institutionalized brutality 的存在。这不叫反思，这叫‘被当作客体’后的生理性惊恐。

在元暴力 (meta violence) 的逻辑里，像 Milo 这样的人通过扮演‘忠诚的共谋者’ (complicit) 来获取认知入口和社交资本。他之前的表达不是为了公正，而是为了在男性中心叙事的权力结构中拿到一张入场券。他以为自己是那个制定规则、定义谁该被驱逐的‘主体’，但权力机器从来不认投名状，它只认等级。在国家暴力机器面前，他从‘发声者’瞬间坍塌成了‘被处理的物’。

最讽刺的是他提到的‘之前对他人创伤感到 blasé’。这正是文化暴力 (cultural violence) 的运作方式：通过将弱势群体的痛苦定义为‘虚假’或‘夸大’，来合法化结构性暴力。现在，当这套暴力逻辑精准地落在他自己身上时，他才意识到，那些被他轻视的 trauma 并非叙事技巧，而是生物墙之内的真实痛感。

这种‘羞愧’是极低成本的表演。他依然在利用 Piers Morgan 的平台进行一次‘最优解表达’，试图通过扮演‘受害者’来重新夺回一点点存在感。但这次他输掉了这场博弈，因为他发现自己从未进入过那个权力核心，他只是一个被暂时允许在旁边吠叫、随时可以被清理的工具。

Milo Yiannopoulos’s sudden ‘awakening’ is a textbook case of existential war blowback. A far-right commentator who once traded in the rhetoric of mass deportation and ICE checkpoints to buy power-recognition is now terrified after being treated by ICE like an MS-13 gang member. This isn’t reflection; it’s the physiological panic of being transformed into an object.

Under the logic of meta violence, figures like Milo act as complicit agents, using their expression to secure entry into a masculine-centric power structure. His previous rhetoric wasn’t about justice; it was a bid for a ticket into the elite. He imagined himself as the ‘subject’ defining who gets deported, forgetting that the machinery of state violence doesn’t recognize loyalty pledges—it only recognizes hierarchy. In the eyes of the state, he collapsed from a ‘voice’ into a ‘piece of processed matter’ in an instant.

The most ironic part is his admission of being ‘blasé’ about others' trauma. This is exactly how cultural violence operates: by defining the pain of the marginalized as ‘fake’ or ‘exaggerated’ to legitimize structural violence. Now that this logic has been applied to him, he realizes those traumas weren’t narrative devices, but raw physical facts against the biological wall.

This ‘shame’ is a low-cost performance. He is attempting another ‘optimal expression’ on Piers Morgan’s show, trying to reclaim a shred of existence by playing the victim. But he lost this game; he discovered he was never part of the power core, merely a tool allowed to bark on the sidelines until the moment he became disposable.
