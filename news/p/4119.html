气候危机的叙事战争：当权力在扮演“理性”The Narrative War of Climate Crisis: Power Performing 'Rationality'

国际 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-18 § 链接
气候否认主义不是科学争议，而是权力对生存权的结构性掠夺。
Climate denialism is not a scientific debate, but a structural plunder of the right to exist.

这张漫画精准地捕捉到了当代政治中一种极其恶劣的 weaponized expression。Richard Tice 及其代表的 Reform UK 试图将气候危机定义为一种“骗局”或“精英的操纵”，这在表面上看起来是在挑战权威，但本质上是典型的 masculine-centric narrative：通过否定客观的生物事实（Biological Wall）来维持既有的权力结构。

在加尔通的暴力三角中，这种叙事是极具杀伤力的 cultural violence。当权力者通过定义“什么是事实”来抹除气候危机时，他们实际上在为 structural violence 铺路——让资源继续向资本和权力中心倾斜，而将由于气候崩溃导致的 direct violence（如饥荒、洪水、极端天气）转嫁给那些在存在性战争中毫无票权的底层群体，尤其是全球南方的女性。

这是一种极其卑劣的共谋。政客通过扮演“敢于说真话的理性者”来收割选票，而资本通过这种叙事掩盖其对地球资源的掠夺。他们制造了一个假的“最优解表达”：告诉民众，拒绝气候行动才是真正的自由和理性。但这只是一个 scam，旨在让人们在主体性死亡的过程中，心甘情愿地成为这场生态自杀的共谋者。

真正的公正表达应该是：承认生物限制，承认潜在的暴力差额（Potential − Actual），并迅速将 Actual 向生存的 Potential 靠拢。而 Tice 们的把戏，就是试图在船快沉没时，说“水淹没甲板”是一种新型的审美风格。

This cartoon precisely captures a particularly vile form of weaponized expression in contemporary politics. Richard Tice and the Reform UK movement attempt to define the climate crisis as a "scam" or "elite manipulation." While appearing to challenge authority, this is essentially a masculine-centric narrative: denying biological facts to maintain existing power structures.

Within Galtung's Violence Triangle, this narrative functions as potent cultural violence. When those in power redefine "what is fact" to erase the climate crisis, they are paving the way for structural violence—ensuring resources continue to flow toward centers of capital and power, while shifting the direct violence of climate collapse (famine, floods, extreme weather) onto the marginalized who hold no votes in the existential war, particularly women in the Global South.

This is a wretched complicity. Politicians harvest votes by performing the role of the "rational truth-teller," while capital uses this narrative to mask its plunder of planetary resources. They manufacture a fake optimal expression, convincing the public that rejecting climate action is the only path to true freedom and rationality. It is a scam designed to make people accomplices in their own ecological suicide while their subjectivity dies.

Just Expressions would require acknowledging biological limits and the gap between Potential and Actual, rapidly moving the Actual toward the potential for survival. Tice's trick is simply to claim that the water flooding the deck is a new aesthetic style while the ship is sinking.
