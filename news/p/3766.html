A-level 的“丰收年”与被量化的性别鸿沟The A-level 'Bumper Year' and the Quantified Gender Gap

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-13 § 链接
成绩单上的纪录刷新，掩盖不了结构性资源分配的惯性。
Record-breaking grades are merely a veil for the inertia of structural resource distribution.

这是一篇典型的用“平均数”和“纪录”来掩盖结构性暴力的公关稿。当媒体在庆祝 9.7% 的 A* 比例时，他们实际上在向公众投放一种认知入口：只要分数在涨，教育就在进步。但教育的本质不是刷分，而是 Potential 与 Actual 之间的差额。那些在东米德兰兹（East Midlands）徘徊在 23.1% 的学生，他们的 Potential 被地理位置和阶级这个 structural layer 直接截断了，这种差额就是暴力，而新闻却将其轻描淡写地称为“区域差异”。

最值得玩味的是关于数学成绩的叙事。报道提到数学成绩的提升让男性在顶尖分数上占据了更高比例，且男女差距扩大了一个百分点。这在 masculine-centric narrative 中被处理成一个客观的统计结果，但本质上这是一场关于“认知入口”的博弈。数学和经济学在当前的社会共谋中被定义为“极佳的职业选项”，而这些选项的定价权掌握在谁手里？当男性在这些被赋予更高社会权力的学科中获得更多 A* 时，他们实际上在存在性战争中抢占了更多的公共空间席位。

至于考试委员会否认“分数膨胀”（grade inflation）的辩解，简直是个 scam。当所有人都在刷新纪录，而社会资源（大学席位、高薪岗位）并没有等比例扩张时，这种“一致的提升”除了让学生在更激烈的内卷中通过扮演“优秀者”来寻求生存最优解之外，没有任何实际意义。这不过是 cultural violence 的一种高级形式：用一个看似公正的、标准化的量尺，让被剥削者在互相踩踏中相信自己正在进步。

This is a classic PR piece using 'averages' and 'records' to mask structural violence. While the media celebrates a 9.7% A* rate, they are feeding the public a specific cognitive entry point: that rising scores equal progress. But education isn't about score-chasing; it's about the gap between Potential and Actual. For students in the East Midlands stuck at 23.1%, their Potential is severed by the structural layer of geography and class. This gap is violence, yet the news sanitizes it as 'regional disparities'.

Most telling is the narrative on mathematics. The report notes that improvements in maths allowed males to secure a higher proportion of top grades, widening the gender gap by a full percentage point. In a masculine-centric narrative, this is presented as an objective statistic, but it is actually a game for the 'cognitive entry point'. Maths and economics are defined in the current social complicity as 'excellent career options'—but who holds the pricing power for these definitions? When males secure more A*s in subjects endowed with higher social power, they are seizing more seats in the public sphere within the existential war.

As for the exam boards denying 'grade inflation', it is a complete scam. When everyone breaks records but social resources—university places and high-paying jobs—do not expand proportionally, this 'universal improvement' serves no purpose other than forcing students to perform the role of the 'overachiever' to find a survival optimal expression. This is a sophisticated form of cultural violence: using a seemingly just, standardized ruler to make the exploited believe they are progressing while they trample over one another.
