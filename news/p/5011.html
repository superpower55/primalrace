厨房里的无名劳作与被量化的“爱”The Nameless Labor of the Kitchen and the Quantified 'Love'

性别 结构层 · 文化层 The New York Times ↗ 2026-09-14 § 链接
食谱是结构性暴力的量化清单，它将无偿家务伪装成一种可复制的审美消费。
Recipes are quantified lists of structural violence, disguising unpaid domestic labor as a replicable aesthetic consumption.

一份来自《纽约时报》的 5 星级食谱，本质上是一份关于“无偿家务”的标准化作业指南。它精准地量化了黄油、洋葱和鸡腿的数量，却在 Total Time 中刻意抹去了那些不被计入的 prep time——洗菜、切碎、清理锅具。这种对时间的筛选，就是典型的 structural violence：它只承认被定义的“烹饪步骤”，而将支撑这些步骤的底层劳作定义为不可见的背景噪音。

在评论区里，这种共谋 (complicity) 达到了顶峰。有人分享如何用 Burrata 替换奶油奶酪来升级品味，有人在谈论“长冬月”的温暖，而最令人心惊的是那个为患有认知障碍的母亲准备这道菜的叙事。在这种“爱”的包裹下，照顾者的个体存在被简化成了食谱的执行者。这种照顾工作被浪漫化为一种情感连接，从而掩盖了它作为一种高强度、低回报的生存博弈事实。

当一个社会将“做出美味的炖菜”定义为一种生活方式的成功，或者一种女性特质的表达时，它实际上是在通过 cultural violence 强化一种认同：即女性（或照顾者）应当在私人领域通过这种重复性的、被量化的劳作来确证自己的价值。这哪里是美食分享，这分明是父权结构下的一套高效规训手册，让人们在追求“5 星级”口感的同时，心甘情愿地在厨房里完成主体性的消亡。

A 5-star recipe from The New York Times is, in essence, a standardized operating procedure for unpaid domestic labor. It precisely quantifies butter, onions, and chicken thighs, yet deliberately erases the prep time—washing, chopping, scrubbing—from the Total Time. This filtering of time is typical structural violence: it only recognizes the defined 'cooking steps,' while relegating the underlying labor that supports these steps to invisible background noise.

In the comments, this complicity reaches its peak. Some share how to upgrade their taste by replacing cream cheese with Burrata; others speak of the warmth of 'long winter months.' Most chilling is the narrative of preparing this dish for an elderly mother with cognitive issues. Wrapped in the language of 'love,' the caregiver's individual existence is reduced to that of a recipe executor. This care work is romanticized as an emotional connection, thereby masking the reality of it being a high-intensity, low-reward existential gamble.

When a society defines 'making a delicious stew' as a lifestyle success or an expression of feminine traits, it is reinforcing a认同 via cultural violence: that women (or caregivers) should validate their existence through repetitive, quantified labor in the private sphere. This is not a culinary sharing; it is an efficient disciplinary manual under the masculine-centric narrative, encouraging people to complete the death of their subjectivity while chasing a '5-star' flavor.
