空调战争：一场关于生存权与绿色叙事的共谋骗局The AC War: A Collusive Scam of Survival vs. Green Narratives

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-24 § 链接
当生存本能被包装成意识形态战争，真正的结构性暴力就隐身了。
When survival instincts are weaponized as ideological warfare, structural violence becomes invisible.

这篇 YouGov 的调查揭露了一个极其讽刺的博弈：欧洲民众在“死于热浪”和“拯救地球”之间，毫不犹豫地选择了前者。但这根本不是一个简单的环保 vs. 舒适的选择题，而是一场典型的文化层暴力（cultural violence）对结构层缺失的掩盖。

所谓的“空调文化战争”是一个巨大的 scam。右翼将空调推向“生存权”的高地，试图以此解构气候危机；而主流绿党则在意识形态的纯洁性中，将空调视为某种“罪恶”的符号。双方在舆论场上打得不可开交，但他们共同共谋（complicity）掩盖了一个事实：欧洲的住房结构已经成了某种结构性暴力（structural violence）。

调查显示，大多数人认为外部遮阳、隔热和反光屋顶比空调更有效且应被优先考虑。但这在现实中意味着什么？意味着需要大规模的建筑改造，需要政府对底层住房进行结构性升级。但这太慢了，成本太高，且不具备“政治表演性”。

于是，空调成了最便捷的“最优解表达”。对于个体，安装空调是短期生存的真.最优解；但对于系统，鼓励空调普及则是最阴险的假.最优解——它通过增加能源消耗来缓解高温，进而加剧全球变暖，最终制造更多热浪。这是一个完美的闭环，让人们在依赖机械冷却的同时，继续忍受糟糕的住房结构，并为之支付昂贵的电费。

这场博弈中没有赢家，只有被收割的消费者和被牺牲的未来。真正的胜利应该是将“居住正义”从空调的叙事中剥离出来，而不是在“装不装空调”的伪命题里打转。

This YouGov survey reveals a cynical game: Europeans, faced with the choice between 'dying in heatwaves' and 'saving the planet,' have decisively chosen the former. But this isn't a simple trade-off between ecology and comfort; it's a classic case of cultural violence masking a structural void.

The so-called 'AC culture war' is a total scam. The right-wing pushes air conditioning as a 'right to survival' to dismantle the climate crisis narrative, while mainstream Greens treat AC as a symbol of 'sin' to maintain ideological purity. Both sides are in a state of complicity, ignoring the core issue: European housing has become a site of structural violence.

The survey shows most people believe external shutters, insulation, and reflective roofs are more effective and should be prioritized. In reality, this would require massive architectural overhauls and systemic upgrades for low-income housing. That is too slow, too expensive, and lacks 'political performativity.'

Consequently, AC becomes the 'optimal expression' for the individual—a short-term survival hack. But for the system, encouraging AC is a fake optimal expression: it mitigates heat by increasing energy consumption, which fuels global warming, leading to more heatwaves. It's a perfect loop where people pay expensive electricity bills to survive a crisis caused by the very tools they use, all while living in decaying housing structures.

There are no winners here, only harvested consumers and a sacrificed future. Real victory would mean decoupling 'housing justice' from the AC narrative, rather than spinning in circles over a false dichotomy of whether to install a machine or not.
