NYT 的葱油鱼：一次典型的文化殖民式简化NYT's Scallion-Oil Fish: A Case of Cultural Colonial Simplification

哲学 文化层 The New York Times ↗ 2026-09-05 § 链接
剥离语境的“美食化”是最高级的文化暴力。
The 'gastronomic' simplification of culture is the most insidious form of cultural violence.

NYT 这篇葱油鱼食谱是一个极其典型的 weaponized expression 案例。它把一种深植于东亚饮食文化、讲究火候与食材原味的料理，通过“标准化”和“简化”变成了某种中产阶级的快餐实验。把葱油鱼定义为“25分钟快餐”，本身就是一种对原真性的 Erasure。

最荒谬的是食材建议：Cod, Halibut, Tilapia。这些鱼在东亚葱油鱼的语境里几乎不存在。葱油鱼的核心在于鱼肉的质地与葱油香气的博弈，而 NYT 把它变成了一个关于“如何用橄榄油煎鱼”的说明书。这种做法将复杂的文化实践降格为简单的生物化学反应（加热+盐），完成了从“文化表达”到“消费产品”的转换。

评论区里那些关于“鱼没味道”或“中间是生的”抱怨，恰恰揭示了这种 structural violence 的结果：当一个文化符号被强行剥离其赖以生存的认知入口（Context），它就变成了一个失效的 scam。它不再是美食，而是一次关于“尝试异域风情”的表演性消费。这种“简化”让西方读者产生了一种掌控全球口味的错觉，而真正的文化主体性在 20 分钟的 Cook Time 中被彻底杀死了。

This NYT recipe is a textbook case of weaponized expression. By 'standardizing' and 'simplifying' a dish deeply rooted in East Asian culinary philosophy—where heat control and the essence of the ingredient are paramount—they have reduced it to a middle-class fast-food experiment. Defining Scallion-Oil Fish as a '25-minute meal' is an act of erasure of its authenticity.

The absurdity peaks in the ingredient suggestions: Cod, Halibut, Tilapia. These are virtually non-existent in the actual context of this dish. The core of the dish is the game between the texture of the fish and the aroma of the oil, yet NYT turned it into a manual on 'how to pan-fry fish with olive oil.' This transforms a complex cultural practice into a mere biochemical reaction (heat + salt), shifting it from a cultural expression to a consumer product.

The complaints in the comments about 'boring taste' or 'raw centers' reveal the result of this structural violence: when a cultural symbol is forcibly stripped of its cognitive entry point (Context), it becomes a failed scam. It is no longer cuisine, but a performative consumption of 'exoticism.' This 'simplification' grants Western readers an illusion of mastering global tastes, while the true subjectivity of the culture is slaughtered within a 20-minute cook time.
