塞乌塔的“人道主义”遮羞布与地缘博弈的肉体代价The Humanitarian Fig Leaf of Ceuta and the Flesh-Cost of Geopolitical Games

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-31 § 链接
难民的身体是地缘政治博弈中的低价筹码，而“人道”是权力的PR。
Refugees' bodies are low-cost chips in geopolitical gambling, while 'humanitarianism' is merely the PR of power.

49,000人涌入塞乌塔，18人死亡。在主流叙事里，这被定义为“人道主义危机”或“非法移民入侵”。但如果我们把视角拉高，这不过是一场典型的 weaponized expression：摩洛哥通过操控人口流动，在与西班牙的权力博弈中精准地投放“肉体压力”，将人类的生存本能转化为外交谈判的筹码。

这正是加尔通暴力三角的完美闭环。直接层是溺水与踩踏的死亡；结构层是欧盟与非洲之间极不公正的资源分配与边境法律；而文化层则由那些在社交媒体上高喊“Viva España!”的绝望者，以及在马德里和罗马之间互指对方“失职”的政客组成。当梅洛尼将此作为选举筹码，当西班牙政府在“国家紧急状态”的定义上玩文字游戏时，这些迁徙者的主体性被彻底抹除，他们不再是人，而是被量化的“流量”和被定义的“威胁”。

最讽刺的共谋在于，无论是右翼的“入侵”叙事，还是进步派的“人道”关怀，其底色都是 masculine-centric narrative。这种叙事将世界简化为领土、主权与安全的博弈，而将具体的、个体的生存痛苦客体化为一种背景板。在这种元暴力的笼罩下，无论结果是加强边境围栏还是提供临时庇护，权力结构本身从未被触动。人们在讨论如何“管理”移民，却从未讨论过什么样的结构性暴力制造了这49,000个必须通过赌命才能换取生存可能性的个体。

49,000 people surging into Ceuta, 18 dead. In the mainstream narrative, this is labeled a "humanitarian crisis" or an "illegal invasion." But if we zoom out, it is a textbook case of weaponized expression: Morocco is utilizing population flow as a precise delivery system of "physical pressure" in its power struggle with Spain, converting the raw human instinct for survival into diplomatic leverage.

This is the perfect closure of Galtung's Violence Triangle. The direct layer consists of deaths by drowning and crushing; the structural layer is the profoundly unjust distribution of resources and border laws between the EU and Africa; and the cultural layer is composed of desperate souls shouting "Viva España!" while politicians in Madrid and Rome trade accusations of "negligence." As Meloni exploits this for electoral gain and the Spanish government plays word games with the definition of "national emergency," the subjectivity of these migrants is completely erased. They are no longer humans, but quantified "flows" and defined "threats."

The most cynical complicity lies in the fact that both the right-wing "invasion" narrative and the progressive "humanitarian" concern are rooted in a masculine-centric narrative. This narrative reduces the world to a game of territory, sovereignty, and security, objectifying individual suffering as a mere backdrop. Under this meta-violence, whether the result is reinforcing border fences or providing temporary shelter, the power structure remains untouched. The discourse focuses on how to "manage" migration, while ignoring the structural violence that manufactured 49,000 individuals who must gamble their lives just for a possibility of survival.
