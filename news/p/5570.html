用“商业机会”兜售主权：一场关于代理人的共谋博弈Peddling Sovereignty as 'Business Opportunity': A Game of Proxy Complicity

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-15 § 链接
当主权被定义为“业务机会”，战争就成了大国共谋的资产重组。
When sovereignty is defined as a 'business opportunity,' war becomes an asset restructuring for great powers.

Wes Streeting 在华盛顿的这场表演，精准地展示了西方政治叙事如何将惨烈的生存战争 weaponize 为一种商业谈判。他试图用“defence-industrial relationship”这种典型的结构性语言，将乌克兰的生死存亡翻译成美国的“business opportunity”。这根本不是在讨论正义或自由，而是在讨论一个代理人的“定价权”：如果支持乌克兰能让美国军工复合体赚更多钱，那么主权就值得被捍卫；如果不能，那么 Starlink 的开关随时可以被关掉。

Starlink 事件揭露了最残酷的 structural violence：乌克兰的战场生存权被高度垄断在一家私人公司和一个政客的意志之中。当 Trump 为了大选期间的油价，通过 throttle 卫星信号来强迫乌克兰停止攻击俄方炼油厂时，所谓的“自由人民决定自身命运”就成了一个彻头彻尾的 scam。在这种逻辑下，乌克兰不是一个主体，而是一个被租赁的战争实验场，其潜在的 Potential（生存与主权）与 Actual（被掐断的信号和被威胁的情报）之间的差额，就是纯粹的暴力。

Streeting 所谓的“呼吁”，本质上是在维护一种 masculine-centric narrative 的共谋。在这种叙事里，英美两国作为“成年人”在讨论如何分配“资产”，而乌克兰只是一个需要被管理、被激励、被威胁的执行端。这种 meta violence 掩盖了一个事实：无论是通过支持还是通过背叛，大国在博弈中追求的永远是自身的最优解表达，而代理人的主体性在这一过程中被彻底抹除。

Wes Streeting's performance in Washington precisely demonstrates how Western political narratives weaponize a brutal war of survival into a commercial negotiation. By employing the structural language of 'defence-industrial relationship,' he translates Ukraine's existence into a 'business opportunity' for the US. This is not a discussion about justice or freedom; it is a negotiation over the pricing power of a proxy: if supporting Ukraine profits the US military-industrial complex, sovereignty is worth defending; if not, the Starlink switch can be flipped at any moment.

The Starlink row exposes the rawest form of structural violence: Ukraine's right to survive on the battlefield is monopolized by a single private company and the whims of one politician. When Trump throttles satellite signals to force Ukraine to stop bombing Russian refineries just to lower oil prices before an election, the narrative of 'free people determining their own destiny' becomes a complete scam. In this logic, Ukraine is not a subject, but a leased experimental ground for war, where the gap between its Potential (sovereignty) and Actual (cut signals and threatened intelligence) is pure violence.

Streeting's 'appeal' is essentially an attempt to maintain a complicity rooted in a masculine-centric narrative. In this framework, the UK and US act as the 'adults' discussing how to allocate 'assets,' while Ukraine is merely an executive end to be managed, incentivized, or threatened. This meta violence masks a grim reality: whether through support or betrayal, great powers only pursue their own optimal expression in the game, while the subjectivity of the proxy is completely erased.
