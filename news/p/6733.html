谁有资格定义事实Who Gets to Define the Facts

国际 元暴力 · 文化层 · 结构层 · 直接层 Al Jazeera ↗ 2026-11-02 § 链接
新闻摘要以色列法院上周批准将半岛电视台在以色列的运营禁令再延长90天，包括停播、关闭办公室和封锁网站。半岛电视台11月2日发表声明谴责此举是对新闻自由的「攻击」，称延期依据的是该台及其律师都无法查看的机密材料。以方2024年5月通过法律，允许关闭被视为「威胁国家安全」的外国广播机构。
The newsAn Israeli court last week approved extending the ban on Al Jazeera's operations in Israel for another 90 days, covering broadcasts, offices and websites. On November 2 the network condemned the move as an assault on press freedom, saying the extension rested on secret material neither it nor its lawyers could see. Israel passed a law in May 2024 allowing it to shut foreign broadcasters deemed a threat to national security.
禁掉一个台，是抢走解释权的最后一步。
Banning a broadcaster is the last step in seizing the power to explain.

以色列把半岛电视台的禁令又续了90天。停播、关办公室、封网站。这不是新闻自由受损，是解释权被收走了——收走的人同时还在打仗，还在被国际刑事法院通缉战争罪。

法院批准，政府提案，法律背书。三层结构叠在一起，看上去完全合法。2024年5月议会通过的那条法律，允许关闭被认定为「威胁国家安全」的外国广播机构——「威胁国家安全」这五个字就是全部的开关，谁按下去、按在谁身上，不需要向被按的人解释。半岛电视台的声明说得很清楚：延期的依据是机密材料，该台和它的律师都不被允许查看或回应。等于是在一场自己不能到场、不能看证据、不能答辩的审判里被判了刑。

这是元暴力最标准的操作。男性中心叙事垄断解释权的办法，从来不是宣布「你不许说话」，而是先定义什么是「安全」、什么是「事实」、什么是「可以公开的材料」，然后让被定义的一方连申辩的入口都找不到。加尔通的三角在这里是联动的：文化层先把一家媒体写成安全威胁，结构层用法律和法院把它关掉，直接层早就在加沙发生过了——两百多名巴勒斯坦记者死于这场战争，半岛电视台的记者在其中；2022年5月，希琳·阿布·阿克莱赫在约旦河西岸被以军射杀，以方先否认，后承认「极有可能」是自己士兵所为。杀记者、关办公室、封网站，是同一件事的三个进度条。

半岛电视台周日刚过完三十周年。它说 silencing 不会让它停下，正在与律师评估法律途径。这些话都对，但报道没写的是：谁在为这条禁令付代价。加沙的观众看不到这些报道，约旦河西岸的办公室被抄走设备后就空着，被杀的记者的家属没有等到任何人被追责。禁令的账单从来不由下禁令的人付。

还有一层报道点了但没展开：内塔尼亚胡本人被国际刑事法院以加沙战争罪通缉。一个被通缉的人，用「国家安全」的名义，关掉正在报道这场战争的媒体，再让法院盖章。这不是程序瑕疵，这是程序本身被拿来当武器用。

30年前半岛电视台开播的时候，阿拉伯世界的叙事第一次有了一个不被邻国政府垄断的出口。现在这个出口在以色列境内被封，在约旦河西岸被抄，在加沙的办公室里连人带楼一起被炸过。禁令会到期，也可能再续90天。真正的问题不是这90天，是「谁有资格定义事实」这件事，已经被写进了法律，而且不需要给被定义的人看证据。

Israel has extended the ban on Al Jazeera for another 90 days. Broadcasts suspended, offices closed, websites blocked. This is not damage to press freedom. It is the power to explain being taken away — by people who are fighting a war and who are wanted by the International Criminal Court on war crimes charges.

A court approved it, the government proposed it, a law backs it. Three layers stacked so it all looks legal. The law passed in May 2024 lets Israel shut foreign broadcasters deemed a threat to national security — and those five words are the whole switch. Whoever flips it never has to explain to the one it lands on. Al Jazeera's statement is precise: the extension rested on secret material that neither the network nor its lawyers were allowed to see or answer. That is a verdict delivered in a trial you cannot attend, cannot read the evidence for, cannot plead in.

This is meta-violence at its most standard. Masculine-centric narrative does not monopolize explanation by announcing you may not speak. It first defines what counts as security, as fact, as material fit to be shown — then leaves the defined party without an entrance to argue from. Galtung's triangle moves together here: the cultural layer writes a broadcaster into a security threat, the structural layer closes it with a law and a court, and the direct layer already happened in Gaza — more than 200 Palestinian journalists killed in this war, Al Jazeera's among them; in May 2022 Shireen Abu Akleh was shot dead by Israeli soldiers in the occupied West Bank, and Israel first denied it, then admitted a high possibility one of its soldiers did it. Killing journalists, closing offices, blocking websites are three progress bars of one thing.

Al Jazeera marked its 30th anniversary on Sunday. It says silencing will not stop it, and that it is reviewing legal options with counsel. All true. What the report does not say is who pays for the ban. Viewers in Gaza do not see these reports. The Ramallah office has sat empty since soldiers carried off its equipment. The families of the killed journalists are still waiting for anyone to be held to account. The bill for a ban is never paid by the people who issue it.

One more layer the report names but does not unfold: Netanyahu himself is wanted by the ICC on war crimes charges in Gaza. A wanted man shuts down, in the name of national security, the outlet reporting that war — and a court stamps it. This is not a procedural flaw. The procedure is the weapon.

Thirty years ago Al Jazeera gave the Arab world its first outlet for a narrative not monopolized by neighboring governments. Now that outlet is banned inside Israel, raided in the West Bank, and its Gaza office was bombed with the building. The ban will expire, or be renewed for another 90 days. The real question is not those 90 days. It is that who gets to define the facts has been written into law — and the defined are not shown the evidence.
