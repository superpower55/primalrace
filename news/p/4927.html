用1万亿美金购买的‘存在性投票’A $1 Trillion Buyout of Existential Voting

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-11 § 链接
用金钱直接购买选票，是将民主表达彻底降格为一场商业交易。
Buying votes with cash degrades democratic expression into a mere commercial transaction.

特朗普这次的 $5,000 承诺不是政策，而是一次赤裸裸的 weaponized expression。他试图通过直接的经济激励，将公民的政治表达（Expression）——也就是在民主博弈中的那张票——直接定价。当投票不再是对社会方向的认同，而变成了领取现金的凭证时，政治表达被彻底商品化了。

从加尔通的暴力三角来看，这是一种典型的 structural violence。通过许诺一笔天文数字的资金（1万亿美元），他实际上在制造一种认知入口：即政治权力可以通过金钱购买，而公民的身份可以被简化为受资助的客体。这种操作的核心不在于钱，而在于他试图定义一种新的‘真实’——即只要能提供即时利益，任何法律和伦理的边界都可以被抹除。

这本质上是一场关于‘存在性战争’的粗暴简化。正常的民主博弈应该是不同阵营的最优解表达（Optimal Expression）在公共空间碰撞，而特朗普在用金钱直接买断博弈过程。他不需要说服你，他只需要让你在经济上产生依赖。这种‘利诱’是另一种形式的规训：它告诉选民，你们的意志不值钱，但你们的票值 5000 美金。

最讽刺的是，这种承诺本身就是一个巨大的 scam。一个支持率跌至 38% 的领导人，试图用一个在法律和财政上几乎不可能实现的承诺来挽回颓势。这不过是再一次地利用底层民众对经济焦虑的共谋，将国家财政作为他个人政治生存的筹码。

Trump’s $5,000 pledge is not a policy; it is a nakedly weaponized expression. He is attempting to put a direct price tag on political expression—the vote in a democratic game—by turning it into a cash voucher. When voting shifts from an endorsement of social direction to a receipt for payment, political expression is completely commodified.

Through the lens of the Violence Triangle, this is classic structural violence. By promising an astronomical sum of $1 trillion, he is constructing a cognitive entry point: that political power is purchasable and citizenship can be reduced to a subsidized object. The core of this operation isn't the money, but the attempt to manufacture a new 'reality' where legal and ethical boundaries vanish in the face of immediate gain.

This is a crude simplification of the existential war. In a healthy democratic game, the Optimal Expressions of different camps collide in the public square. Trump is simply buying out the process. He doesn't need to persuade you; he only needs to create economic dependency. This 'incentive' is another form of discipline: it tells the voter that their will is worthless, but their vote is worth $5,000.

The ultimate irony is that this pledge is a massive scam. A leader with a record-low approval rating of 38% is using a legally and fiscally impossible promise to survive. It is once again a gamble on the complicity of those driven by economic anxiety, treating the national treasury as a personal chip in his own existential war for power.
