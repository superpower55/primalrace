Meta 的“鼓点叙事”：用 PR 掩盖结构性成瘾Meta's Drumbeat Narrative: Masking Structural Addiction with PR

科技 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-26 § 链接
所谓的“安全功能”只是武器化的叙事入口，旨在通过表演性进步维持剥削合法性。
Safety features are merely weaponised narrative entries used to maintain the legitimacy of exploitation through performative progress.

Adam Mosseri 在法庭上的表演非常典型：当被问到只有 1.8% 的青少年使用“休息一下” (Take a Break) 功能时，他的回答是“我们不会公布每一项统计数据”。这不仅是傲慢，更是 Meta 这种认知入口掌控者的标准操作。在他们的逻辑里，事实不重要，重要的是“叙事”的占有率。

这就是典型的武器化表达 (Weaponised Expression)。Meta 制造了一个名为“保护青少年”的认知入口，通过密集投放所谓的安全工具，在文化层面上构建一个“负责任的企业”形象。而那个 1.8% 的真实数据，就是 Potential（本可达到的安全状态）与 Actual（实际产品状态）之间巨大的暴力差额。Meta 并不在乎功能是否有效，他们在乎的是这个功能被“宣布”出来之后，能为他们换取多少监管豁免权和品牌好感度。

最讽刺的是 Mosseri 承认的 “drumbeat messaging”（鼓点叙事）。这种通过重复单一叙事来洗脑大众的手段，本质上是在制造一种伪事实：只要我足够大声且频繁地重复“我们在保护孩子”，那么即便产品在结构上依然是成瘾性的，即便内部工程师在警告，公众也会在潜意识中接受这种设定。这是一种高级的文化暴力，它让直接的心理伤害和结构性的数据剥削看起来像是“技术迭代中的小瑕疵”。

这场审判如果最终只能换来一笔赔偿金，那依然是 Meta 的最优解表达——用钱买断罪名，继续维持其成瘾机制。真正的胜利应该是强迫其交出算法解释权，拆除那个旨在收割注意力的认知陷阱。否则，所谓的“安全功能”永远只是他们用来掩盖元暴力的一块遮羞布。

Adam Mosseri's performance in court is a textbook example: when confronted with the fact that only 1.8% of teens used the 'Take a Break' tool, his response was, 'We don’t publish every single stat.' This isn't just arrogance; it's the standard operating procedure for those who control cognitive entries. In their logic, facts are irrelevant; what matters is the market share of the narrative.

This is a clear case of Weaponised Expression. Meta created a cognitive entry titled 'Protecting Teens,' using the deployment of so-called safety tools to build a cultural image of a 'responsible corporation.' The real data—that 1.8%—represents the massive gap of violence between the Potential (the safety state that should be) and the Actual (the reality of the product). Meta doesn't care if the tool works; they care about how the 'announcement' of the tool buys them regulatory leniency and brand equity.

The most cynical part is Mosseri's admission of 'drumbeat messaging.' This tactic of repeating a single narrative to brainwash the public is essentially the manufacturing of a pseudo-fact: as long as I repeat 'we are protecting children' loudly and frequently enough, the public will subconsciously accept it, even while the product remains structurally addictive and internal engineers are screaming warnings. This is a form of cultural violence that makes direct psychological harm and structural data exploitation look like 'minor glitches in technical iteration.'

If this trial ends only with a financial settlement, it will remain Meta's optimal expression—buying off their guilt with money while maintaining their addictive mechanisms. True victory would be forcing them to surrender algorithmic transparency and dismantle the cognitive traps designed to harvest attention. Otherwise, 'safety features' will always be nothing more than a veil used to mask meta-violence.
