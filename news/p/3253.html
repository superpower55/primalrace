用“慈善”洗白殖民：一场关于解释权的共谋Whitewashing Colonization via 'Charity': A Game of Complicity

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-04 § 链接
慈善外壳是结构暴力的润滑剂，而调查则是权力在表演性让步。
Charity is the lubricant for structural violence; investigations are merely performative concessions of power.

把钱交给非法定居点，却在账本上写成“慈善捐款”，这是典型的表达武器化。通过将“殖民扩张”重新定义为“教育支持”或“宗教援助”，这些机构成功地在文化层面上将一次资源掠夺包装成了道德高地。这不仅是资金的转移，更是对“什么是慈善”这一认知入口的强行占领，让非法占领在税务减免和公众信任的掩护下变得“合法”。

这里存在一个巨大的共谋网络：从提供资金的英国慈善机构，到利用这些资金在巴勒斯坦土地上扩建宿舍的右翼政权，再到此前对此心知肚明却维持沉默的监管部门。这种 complicity 确保了结构性暴力（structural violence）能够高效运行——只要不直接在新闻头条写“资助非法占领”，就可以在制度的灰色地带里心安理得地通过“慈善”来完成对原住民生存空间的挤压。

至于现在的“官方调查”，不要被这种 ritual 化的程序欺骗。从 Melanie Ward 的质疑可以看出，监管部门在已知事实一年后才启动调查，这本身就是一种典型的 meta violence：通过掌控调查的节奏和定义权，将一个迫切需要“禁令”的政治问题，降级为一个需要“核实事实”的行政流程。当调查结果出炉时，新的定居点可能已经建成了，而这场表演性的让步，恰恰给了结构性暴力更多缓冲和伪装的时间。

Funneling funds into illegal settlements while labeling them as 'charitable donations' is a textbook case of weaponized expression. By redefining 'colonial expansion' as 'educational support' or 'religious aid,' these organizations successfully repackage resource plunder as a moral high ground at the cultural layer. This is not just a transfer of capital, but a hostile takeover of the cognitive entry point for 'what constitutes charity,' making illegal occupation appear 'legitimate' under the cover of tax relief and public trust.

There is a massive network of complicity here: from the UK charities providing the funds, to the far-right regime utilizing them to expand dormitories on Palestinian land, and the regulators who remained silent despite knowing the facts. This complicity ensures that structural violence operates efficiently—as long as the headlines don't explicitly say 'funding illegal occupation,' they can comfortably facilitate the erasure of indigenous living spaces through the 'charity' loophole.

As for the current 'official investigation,' do not be deceived by this ritualized procedure. As Melanie Ward pointed out, the regulator waited a year after the facts were known to act. This is a manifestation of meta violence: by controlling the pace and definition of the inquiry, they downgrade an urgent political demand for a 'ban' into a tedious administrative process of 'fact-finding.' By the time the findings are published, new settlements will likely be completed, and this performative concession will have given structural violence more time to buffer and camouflage itself.
