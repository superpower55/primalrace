Mayfair 的盘中餐与被焚毁的游乐场Mayfair's Platter and the Incinerated Playground

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-12 § 链接
所谓‘传统’的猎杀，本质是权势者将自然改造为私人游乐场的共谋。
The so-called 'tradition' of shooting is a complicity of the powerful turning nature into a private playground.

在 Mayfair 的高级餐厅里，食客们等待着 8 月 12 日的‘光荣十二日’ (Glorious Twelfth)，把红原 grouse 搭配面包酱吃下去。而这场关于‘传统’的狂欢，其背后是一场极其残酷的 structural violence：为了让这些鸟足够多、足够好地被猎杀，地主们排干泥炭地，焚烧植被，将自然生态强行改造为高效的‘猎鸟工厂’。

这种管理方式把泥炭地变成了巨大的tinderbox。当气候危机带来的极端高温袭来，这些被人为破坏的土地迅速起火，焚毁了栖息地，也讽刺地导致了今年高级餐厅的 grouse 短缺。这不是简单的自然灾害，而是人类试图通过操控自然来服务于少数人娱乐需求的必然反噬。

有趣的是，文中提到的名厨 Richard Corrigan 试图通过‘只买小庄园的鸟’或‘拒绝买杀害猛禽庄园的鸟’来表达某种道德优越感。但这不过是一种表演性的最优解表达 (fake optimal expression)。只要他还在参与这个基于‘猎杀野生动物作为娱乐’的商业链条，他就是这个共谋场域的一部分。他所谓的‘尊重’，是在确认对方也是‘文明’猎杀者的前提下，给这套血腥的 masculine-centric 叙事贴上了一层人文主义的糖衣。

苏格兰绿党说得对，这些原野不该是富人的私人游乐场。当‘传统’被定义为‘为了娱乐而管理自然’时，这种定义权本身就是一种元暴力。它抹杀了土地作为公共资产、生态屏障的 Potential，将其 Actual 状态压缩成一个巨大的、可被定价的猎杀场。所谓的‘光荣’，不过是权力对生命和土地的彻底物化。

In the plush restaurants of Mayfair, diners await the 'Glorious Twelfth' to feast on grouse. But this celebration of 'tradition' is underpinned by a brutal structural violence: to ensure birds are plentiful for the shoot, landowners drain peatlands and burn vegetation, forcibly transforming wild ecosystems into efficient 'grouse factories'.

This management has turned the moors into a tinderbox. As the climate crisis brings record heat, these anthropogenically damaged lands ignite, incinerating habitats and ironically causing the shortage of grouse on fine-dining menus. This isn't a mere natural disaster; it is the inevitable backlash of attempting to manipulate nature for the entertainment of a few.

It is amusing to see chef Richard Corrigan attempt a form of moral superiority by sourcing from 'small estates' or boycotting those who persecute raptors. This is a classic fake optimal expression. As long as he participates in a commercial chain based on 'shooting wild animals for sport,' he remains a part of this complicity. His 'respect' is merely adding a layer of humanist sugar-coating to a blood-soaked masculine-centric narrative, provided the other party is also a 'civilized' hunter.

The Scottish Greens are correct: the countryside should not be a private playground for the wealthy. When 'tradition' is defined as 'managing nature for the sake of killing,' that definition itself is meta-violence. It erases the Potential of the land as a public asset and ecological barrier, compressing its Actual state into a priced hunting ground. The 'Glory' here is nothing more than the total objectification of life and land by power.
