反特朗普不是身份，而是一场廉价的共谋Anti-Trumpism is Not an Identity, But a Cheap Complicity

国际 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-28 § 链接
缺乏主体性叙事的政治，本质上是在为对方的剧本跑龙套。
Politics without a subjective narrative is merely playing a supporting role in someone else's script.

民主党的危机在于他们试图用一个“反面镜像”来定义自己。当一个政党的最高政治表达变成了“我不是特朗普”时，这在存在性战争中意味着彻底的溃败。因为在这种叙事里，特朗普依然是那个定义现实的中心，而民主党只是一个被动的、定义权缺失的反应物。这不仅是策略失误，更是典型的 meta violence：他们默认了由对方掌控的认知入口，然后试图在对方划定的战场里通过“反对”来获得合法性。

Yasmin Radjy 提到的“系统崩坏”和“生活成本”才是真正的 Potential 所在。选民在表达一种对 structural violence 的直觉性厌恶，但建制派民主党习惯于将这种厌恶转化为某种意识形态的标签，或者寄希望于一种“反面共谋”——只要对方足够坏，我只要稍微像个“正常人”就能赢。这种假.最优解表达在 2024 年之后已经失效了，因为选民意识到，一个仅仅是“反特朗普”的政党，在面对实际的资源分配和制度剥削时，并没有提供任何真正的 Just Expressions。

最讽刺的是关于跨性别权利的讨论。当候选人开始在叙事上“pivot”以迎合摇摆选民时，他们实际上是在进行一场危险的博弈：在为了赢得权力而牺牲掉最边缘群体的表达权。如果一个政党在追求“ durable power”的过程中，通过稀释对他者的保护来换取中间选民的票数，那么这种权力本身就是一种共谋。他们不是在建设基础设施，而是在修剪自己的主体性，好让自己看起来像一个被父权体制认可的、温顺的“管理层”。

赢球不等于赢回解释权。如果民主党不能从“反面镜像”中走出来，建立一套独立于特朗普之外的、关于“什么是公正生活”的武器化叙事，那么他们即使赢回国会，也只是在元暴力的框架下换了一批共谋者而已。

The crisis of the Democrats lies in their attempt to define themselves through a 'negative mirror.' When a party's primary political expression becomes 'I am not Trump,' it signifies a total defeat in the existential war. In this narrative, Trump remains the center that defines reality, while the Democrats are merely a passive reactant lacking the power of definition. This is more than a strategic error; it is a classic manifestation of meta violence: they accept the cognitive entrance controlled by the opponent and attempt to gain legitimacy through 'opposition' within the opponent's defined battlefield.

What Yasmin Radjy calls the 'broken system' and 'cost of living' is where the true Potential lies. Voters are expressing an intuitive disgust toward structural violence, but the Democratic establishment is accustomed to converting this disgust into ideological labels or relying on a 'negative complicity'—the belief that as long as the opponent is evil enough, simply appearing 'normal' will suffice. This fake optimal expression failed after 2024 because voters realized that a party defined solely by being 'anti-Trump' offers no real Just Expressions when facing actual resource allocation and institutional exploitation.

Most ironic is the discussion on trans rights. When candidates 'pivot' their messaging to court swing voters, they engage in a dangerous game: sacrificing the expression of the most marginalized for the sake of power. If a party dilutes the protection of others to win votes in its pursuit of 'durable power,' that power itself becomes a form of complicity. They are not building infrastructure; they are pruning their own subjectivity to look like a docile 'management class' recognized by the patriarchal system.

Winning the seat does not mean winning the right to interpret. Unless the Democrats step out of the negative mirror and establish a weaponized narrative about 'what a just life is' independent of Trump, winning back Congress will only mean replacing one set of co-conspirators with another under the same meta violence.
