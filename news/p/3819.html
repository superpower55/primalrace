生物墙的崩塌与被殖民的生态The Collapse of Biological Walls and Colonized Ecology

哲学 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-14 § 链接
当生物墙无法抵御外来入侵，生存就成了一场被动博弈。
When biological walls fail against invasion, survival becomes a passive game of attrition.

这则关于蝙蝠白鼻综合征（WNS）的新闻，本质上是一场关于“生物墙”崩溃的悲剧。对于北方长耳蝠这种体重仅 6-9g 的生物来说，它们的生物墙——也就是其生理代谢基线和脂肪储备——在面对这种来自欧洲的真菌时，完全没有防御能力。在这种不对称的博弈中，生理上的“弱势”直接被转化为死亡率，这正是最直接的 direct violence：一种物种在生理结构上被另一种生物彻底地、系统性地掠夺生存空间。

更有趣的是这里面的 structural violence。真菌的传播被认为是由于人类洞穴探险者的鞋底带来的，而随后的森林大火（野火）则进一步摧毁了蝙蝠的栖息地，迫使它们在极端的生存压力下进行更高风险的迁移。人类在文化层面上将此视为一种“生态危机”，但在结构层面上，这是人类活动（全球流动性+气候改变）对非人类物种实施的一场无意识殖民。蝙蝠被推入了一个它们无法通过进化速度跟上的存在性战争中。

至于那个所谓的“益生菌鸡尾酒”疗法，虽然被描述为 hope，但我们要警惕这种技术干预的逻辑。它实际上是在尝试用一种人工制造的“生物墙”去修补被人类破坏的生态结构。这种干预能否成为真.最优解？如果它只能延缓死亡而不能恢复栖息地，那么它不过是一次表演性的救赎。真正的 victory 不应该是用试管里的细菌去对抗真菌，而应该是停止对这些生物生存底线——也就是它们的栖息环境——的持续性掠夺。

This news about White-Nose Syndrome (WNS) is essentially a tragedy of the collapse of the Biological Wall. For the northern long-eared bat, weighing a mere 6-9g, its biological wall—its metabolic baseline and fat reserves—offers zero defense against this European fungus. In this asymmetric game, physiological weakness is directly converted into mortality. This is direct violence: one species systematically plundering the existential space of another based on structural vulnerability.

More telling is the structural violence at play. The fungus was likely introduced by the boots of human cavers, and subsequent wildfires—driven by human-induced climate shifts—destroyed habitats, forcing bats into high-risk migrations. While humans frame this as an "ecological crisis" in the cultural layer, it is structurally a form of unconscious colonization. Bats are thrust into an existential war where the pace of biological adaptation cannot keep up with the pace of human-driven disruption.

As for the "probiotic cocktail" treatment, we must be wary of this logic. It is an attempt to patch a shattered ecological structure with an artificial biological wall. Can this be a true optimal expression? If it only delays death without restoring habitat, it is merely a performative rescue. True victory is not about using lab-grown bacteria to fight fungi, but about ending the continuous plunder of the survival baseline—the habitat—of these species.
