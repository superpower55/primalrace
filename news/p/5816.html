空椅子不是反腐成绩单Empty Chairs Are Not an Anti-Corruption Scorecard

中国科技 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-30 § 链接
换尺子不换能力，反腐也一样。
Change the ruler, keep the ability. Anti-corruption works the same way.

33人被开除，279人出席，2022年原本点名376人——超过四分之一的位子空着。报道说这是现代中国历史上空椅子最多的一次全会。先把这条新闻的计量单位看清楚：它数的是椅子，不是腐败。

一个系统如果真在清除腐败，应该能说出清除之后什么变好了：钱去了哪里，谁被追回了损失，哪个盘剥的入口被堵上了。这些报道里一个都没有。有的只是人数、席位、谁上谁下。这不是反腐的账本，这是人事的账本。用开除人数当反腐的指标，等于把“抓了多少人”当成“病好了多少”——抓得越多显得越有成绩，那这个指标只会越抓越多，永远不会收工。

再看缺席的是谁。张又侠，军队系统里的二号人物；马兴瑞，新疆的前党委书记。中央军委七个人被清到只剩两个：习近平，和张升民。张升民这周进了政治局，成了最高领导层里唯一的军人。报道自己都写了，外界在问这场运动是不是也在清除政治对手、惩罚不忠。这个问题不需要答案，看结构就够了：一支军队的最高决策机构只剩一个人加一个管抓人的，这不叫纪律严明，这叫把所有人都变成可替换的零件，只留下一个不需要被替换的。

这里真正在动的不是哪一层暴力，是解释权本身。2012年以来“反腐”一直是一个不需要被质疑的词——谁会反对反腐？于是它可以装下任何东西：清对手、换班子、收权、立威。一个词被武器化到这种程度，它的功能就不再是描述事实，而是制造“事实已经变好了”的观感。报道里那位智库研究员把空椅子读成“聚焦于习近平严格内部纪律的愿景”，这就是标准的接收端动作：把缺席翻译成决心，把清洗翻译成治理。

最讽刺的一句在结尾。中央委员会和政治局明年要换血，但分析人士认为近年的清洗范围太广，合适的人选已经不多。这就是这套机制的自我消耗：它清掉的不只是“问题官员”，还有任何可能积累起独立分量的人。一个组织把“可靠”定义为“没有自己的根基”，最后能提拔的只剩下一批谁都不认识、谁也不欠、也谁都撑不起的人。2027年的党代会要给他第四个任期，让他成为毛泽东之后在位最久的领导人——而支撑这个任期的班子，正在被这场运动自己掏空。

空椅子会补上。补上的人不会比被清掉的人更能干，只会更安全。

Thirty-three expelled. 279 attending, against 376 originally named in 2022. More than a quarter of the seats empty — the most empty chairs at a plenum in modern Chinese history, according to the report. First, look at what this news is actually counting: chairs, not corruption.

If a system were genuinely cleaning out corruption, it could tell you what got better afterward: where the money went, whose losses were recovered, which extraction channel was closed. None of that is in this report. There are only headcounts, seats, who rises and who falls. This is not an anti-corruption ledger; it is a personnel ledger. Using expulsion numbers as the metric of anti-corruption is like using arrests as the metric of a cure — the more you arrest, the better you look, so you arrest forever and never declare the job done.

Now look at who is missing. Zhang Youxia, the former second-in-command in the military hierarchy. Ma Xingrui, former party secretary of Xinjiang. The seven-man Central Military Commission has been gutted down to two: Xi himself, and Zhang Shengmin. Zhang was promoted to the politburo this week, becoming the only military member of the top leadership. The report itself notes the question of whether this campaign is also a tool to remove rivals and punish disloyalty. That question doesn't need an answer; the structure answers it. A military's top decision body reduced to one man plus the man who arrests people is not strict discipline. It is a machine that turns everyone into a replaceable part, leaving only one part that never needs replacing.

What is really moving here is not any one layer of violence. It is the monopoly on explanation itself. Since 2012, "anti-corruption" has been a word nobody can oppose — who is against anti-corruption? So it can hold anything: rivals, reshuffles, centralization, deterrence. A word weaponized to this degree stops describing facts and starts manufacturing the impression that facts have already improved. The thinktank fellow quoted in the report reads the empty chairs as a plenum "focused on Xi's vision of strict internal discipline" — the standard receiving-end move: translate absence into resolve, translate purge into governance.

The sharpest line is at the end. The Central Committee and politburo will be refreshed at next year's congress, but analysts believe the purges have been so wide-ranging that few suitable candidates remain. That is the mechanism eating itself: it removes not only "problem officials" but anyone who could accumulate independent weight. When an organization defines "reliable" as "has no base of their own," what's left to promote is a group nobody knows, nobody owes, and nobody can carry anything with. The 2027 congress is expected to hand Xi a fourth term, making him the longest-serving leader since Mao — and the bench propping up that term is being hollowed out by the very campaign that built it.

The empty chairs will be filled. The replacements won't be more capable than the ones removed. Only safer.
