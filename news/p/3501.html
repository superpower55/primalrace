线粒体不生产智能，它只生产被剥削的能量Mitochondria Don't Produce Intelligence, They Produce the Energy to Be Exploited

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-09 § 链接
生物学上的能量底座，决定了社会学上的存在性成本。
The biological energy base determines the sociological cost of existence.

这篇文章试图用一个“优雅”的生物学叙事——线粒体（Mitochondria）——把睡眠、运动和饮食这三件琐事统一起来。结论很温情：我们不过是 20 亿年前一次细菌吞噬演化而来的“能量过程”。但从存在性战争的视角看，这恰恰揭示了生物墙最残酷的底层逻辑：认知是有代谢成本的（metabolic cost）。

当神经科学告诉我们“思考是极度耗能的”时，它实际上在定义一种生物学上的资源分配。如果认知、记忆和创造力都依赖于线粒体的高效运转，那么谁能掌控自己的睡眠、饮食和运动，谁就掌握了进入公共空间博弈的“入场券”。

这里存在一个隐蔽的结构性暴力（structural violence）。在现代资本主义的共谋下，睡眠被定义为“懒惰”，运动被定义为“中产阶级的奢侈品”，而优质饮食则被定价权垄断。当底层群体被剥夺了充足的睡眠和营养，他们的线粒体功能受损，导致认知处理速度和压力韧性下降。这种生物学上的“低能”，随后在文化层被解读为“能力不足”或“缺乏自律”，从而合法化了他们被排除在权力席位之外的现状。

所谓的“健康建议”在没有资源支撑的情况下，不过是另一种形式的规训。它告诉受害者：你之所以在存在性战争中输掉，是因为你没睡好、没吃对。它将结构性的资源剥夺，伪装成了个体生物学维护的失败。线粒体是公平的，但能让线粒体高效运转的环境绝对不公平。

This article attempts an "elegant" biological narrative—the mitochondria—to unify sleep, exercise, and diet. The conclusion is heartwarming: we are merely "energetic processes" evolved from a bacterial engulfment 2 billion years ago. But from the perspective of Existential War, this reveals the most brutal logic of the Biological Wall: cognition has a metabolic cost.

When neuroscience tells us that thinking is "energy-intensive," it is actually defining a biological resource allocation. If cognition, memory, and creativity depend on mitochondrial efficiency, then those who control their sleep, diet, and exercise control the "entry ticket" to the public arena of gaming.

There is a hidden structural violence here. Under the complicity of modern capitalism, sleep is defined as "laziness," exercise as a "middle-class luxury," and quality diet is captured by those with pricing power. When the underclass is deprived of sleep and nutrition, their mitochondrial function suffers, leading to lower cognitive processing speed and stress resilience. This biological "low energy" is then interpreted at the cultural layer as a "lack of ability" or "lack of discipline," thereby legitimizing their exclusion from power seats.

So-called "health advice," in the absence of resources, is merely another form of discipline. It tells the victim: you lost the Existential War because you didn't sleep enough or eat right. It disguises structural resource deprivation as a failure of individual biological maintenance. Mitochondria are fair, but the environment that allows them to flourish is absolutely not.
