用炸弹封口，是法兰西式“教育”的最优解Blowing Out Mouths: The French 'Optimal Solution' for Education

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-06 § 链接
当结构性匮乏被定义为现状，肉体暴力就成了唯一的表达
When structural deprivation is normalized, physical violence becomes the only viable expression.

Violence = Potential − Actual。法国学生的愤怒不是因为他们突然发现了什么政治理想，而是因为 Actual（破败的教室、缺失的教师、漫长的课时）与 Potential（作为公民应得的教育权）之间的差额已经大到了无法忍受的地步。当 structural violence（制度性的资源剥夺）在校园里常态化，学生们只能通过 direct violence（纵火、封锁）来强行夺回认知入口。

最令人作呕的是这套权力机器的反馈机制。一个14岁孩子被近距离发射榴弹导致牙齿脱落，只能用吸管进食；一个15岁孩子被炸掉一只手。这种极端的 direct violence 不是为了维持秩序，而是一种元暴力的具象化：在统治者眼中，这些未成年人不是需要被教育的个体，而是可以被随时损毁的客体。用 blast ball 毁掉一个少年的眼睛，本质上是在告诉所有抗争者：你们的表达不具备价值，只有服从才安全。

而政府的反应是典型的 weaponized narrative。一方面指责左翼政党在“操纵”学生，试图将一场关于生存权的抗争转化为党派斗争的认知博弈；另一方面在选举压力下才勉强承诺“处理核心诉求”。这不过是表演性的让步。在预算赤字和债务压力面前，教育投入永远是最低优先级的。这不仅是经济问题，更是共谋问题：政治精英们共谋维持一个看似文明的共和国外壳，内部却用警棍和催泪瓦斯来填补结构性崩塌的窟窿。

这场战争的残酷在于，学生们试图用身体去填补那个差额，而国家则试图用致残来抹除那个差额。当一个14岁的孩子失去说话的能力，这不再是简单的“冲突”，而是一次彻底的、带有性别与年龄歧视的肉体殖民。

Violence = Potential − Actual. The rage of French students isn't a sudden political awakening; it's the result of a gap between the Actual (crumbling classrooms, missing teachers) and the Potential (the right to education) that has become unbearable. When structural violence becomes the norm in schools, students are forced to use direct violence—arson and blockades—to forcibly reclaim their cognitive entry points.

The feedback mechanism of this power machine is nauseating. A 14-year-old loses his teeth to a close-range grenade; a 15-year-old loses a hand. This extreme direct violence isn't about maintaining order; it's the manifestation of meta-violence. In the eyes of the state, these minors are not subjects to be educated, but objects to be damaged. Blinding a teenager with a blast ball is a message: your expression has no value; only complicity ensures safety.

The government's response is a classic weaponized narrative. By accusing left-wing parties of 'orchestrating' the movement, they attempt to shift a struggle for survival into a partisan game. The promise to 'tackle core demands' under election pressure is mere performative concession. Amid budget deficits, education is the lowest priority. This is a pact of complicity among political elites to maintain the facade of a civilized Republic while using batons and tear gas to patch the structural collapse.

The cruelty of this war is that students are using their bodies to close the gap, while the state is using maiming to erase the gap. When a 14-year-old is robbed of his ability to speak, it is no longer a 'clash'—it is a total physical colonization based on age and power.
