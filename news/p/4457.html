被掩盖在“心血管疾病”下的气候屠杀Climate Massacre Masked as 'Cardiovascular Failure'

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-25 § 链接
死亡证明上的病因是 structural violence 掩盖 direct violence 的最高级伪装。
Causes of death on certificates are the ultimate camouflage of structural violence masking direct violence.

35,000 人的超额死亡，在官方统计中却被巧妙地拆解为心肌梗死、中风或肾衰竭。这就是典型的 structural violence：当高温成为环境底色，它不再被记录为直接的杀手，而成了某种“诱因”。这种统计学上的 trick 让气候危机在法律和责任认定上变得模糊——你不是死于热浪，你是死于你原本就有的疾病。

这种叙事逻辑完成了对暴力的脱罪。如果死亡被归类为“自然疾病的恶化”，那么政府在城市规划中对绿地的剥夺、对低收入群体居住环境的漠视，以及对气候治理的迟缓，就全部变成了无关痛痒的背景板。Violence = Potential − Actual。人们本可以生活在有空调、有绿荫、有预警机制的城市里，而现实是他们被困在水泥热岛中等待心血管系统崩溃。这个差额，就是被制度化了的暴力。

更残酷的是 population ageing 这一叙事入口。WHO 把死亡率上升归结于人口老龄化，这在生物学上是事实，但在政治上是 scam。将弱势群体（老人、贫困者）的死亡自然化，是为了让系统共谋者们在面对 40C+ 的高温时，依然能心安理得地维持那个低效且傲慢的公共卫生体系。他们不打算改变结构，只打算在死者名单增加后，在报告里写上“由于高龄”。

35,000 excess deaths are neatly disassembled into myocardial infarctions, strokes, or kidney failures. This is structural violence in its purest form: when extreme heat becomes the environmental baseline, it is no longer recorded as the direct killer, but as a 'trigger'. This statistical trick blurs the lines of legal and political accountability—you didn't die from the heatwave; you died from a pre-existing condition.

This narrative logic completes the decriminalization of violence. If death is categorized as the 'exacerbation of natural illness,' then the systemic deprivation of urban greenery, the neglect of housing for low-income groups, and the lethargy of climate governance all become irrelevant background noise. Violence = Potential − Actual. People could have lived in cities with cooling centers, canopy cover, and functional warning systems; instead, they were trapped in concrete heat islands waiting for their cardiovascular systems to collapse. This gap is institutionalized violence.

Even more sinister is the weaponization of the 'population ageing' narrative. By attributing the rise in mortality to an aging population, the WHO turns a political failure into a biological inevitability. Naturalizing the deaths of the most vulnerable—the elderly and the poor—allows the complicity of the system to persist. They have no intention of changing the structure; they only intend to write 'due to old age' in the report after the death toll rises.
