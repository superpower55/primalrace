俄罗斯的选票：一种被阉割的表达Russia's Ballot: A Castrated Form of Expression

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-11 § 链接
当投票不再是选择，而成了对服从的强制确认，选举就成了元暴力的表演。
When voting is no longer a choice but a forced confirmation of obedience, elections become a performance of meta-violence.

俄罗斯最高法院剥夺 Yabloko 党参选资格，本质上是完成了一次对表达权的彻底清理。在加尔通的暴力三角里，这属于典型的 structural violence。当一个系统通过法律手段，把所有不符合其叙事的选项全部剔除，它其实是在告诉国民：你的 Potential（本可有的政治选择）被强制归零，Actual（实际状态）只能是唯一的服从。这个差额，就是国家对公民实施的暴力。

这种操作极其典型的 masculine-centric narrative。战争被定义为某种宏大的、不可质疑的“国家意志”，而任何反对声音都被标记为背叛或弱点。在这种元暴力的笼罩下，选举不再是博弈，而是一场 ritual。所谓的“其他政党”如果被允许存在，其唯一的 functional role 就是扮演 spoiler（搅局者）或共谋者，用来制造一种“我们依然有民主”的文化伪装（cultural violence），从而让结构性压迫看起来像是一种正常的政治秩序。

最讽刺的是，当俄罗斯在战场上快速复制 Starlink 替代方案、超额生产超音速导弹时，它在政治表达上的逻辑却是极端的“反创新”——它不允许任何一种非官方的真实存在。这种对认知入口的绝对垄断，是为了确保每一个俄罗斯公民在精神上都被禁锢在同一个武器化的叙事里。当一个人在选票上找不到反对战争的选项时，他被剥夺的不止是一次投票权，而是作为独立主体存在的确证。

The Russian Supreme Court's disqualification of the Yabloko party is a total clearance of the right to expression. In Galtung's Violence Triangle, this is classic structural violence. When a system uses legal mechanisms to erase all options that clash with its narrative, it tells its citizens that their Potential (political choice) is forcibly zeroed out, and their Actual (reality) can only be obedience. This gap is the violence the state exerts over the individual.

This operation is a textbook example of masculine-centric narrative. War is defined as a grand, unquestionable "national will," and any dissent is labeled as betrayal or weakness. Under this meta-violence, elections are no longer a game of stakes but a ritual. Any "other parties" tolerated only serve as spoilers or complicit actors, providing a cultural violence mask of "democracy" to make structural oppression appear as a normal political order.

The irony is that while Russia rapidly copies Starlink alternatives and over-produces supersonic missiles on the battlefield, its logic of political expression is radically "anti-innovation"—it permits no non-official truth. This absolute monopoly over the cognitive entrance ensures every citizen is imprisoned within a single weaponized narrative. When a person finds no option to vote against the war, they lose more than a vote; they lose the confirmation of their existence as an independent subject.
