Rolls-Royce：被AI和战争重新定价的“共谋”机器Rolls-Royce: A Complicity Machine Repriced by AI and War

中国科技 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-30 § 链接
商业奇迹本质上是精准地将自身挂载到元暴力的增长点上。
Business miracles are essentially the precise mounting of oneself onto the growth points of meta-violence.

Tufan Erginbilgiç 所谓的“turnaround”并不是什么管理学上的神迹，而是一次极高明且冷酷的表达武器化实践。他把 Rolls-Royce 从一个濒临崩溃的航空发动机厂，重新定义为处于“国防、AI 和能源转型”中心节点的战略资产。注意，这三个词在本质上是同一种东西：是对全球结构性暴力（Structural Violence）的深度共谋。

国防是直接暴力（Direct Violence）的保障，AI 数据中心对电力的饥渴是数字殖民的新入口，而 SMRs（小型模块化反应堆）则是将核能这种终极暴力工具转化为商业产品的叙事包装。Rolls-Royce 并没有改变它的能力，它只是改变了它的评价方式——它不再卖发动机，而是在卖“确保大国竞争胜出”的入场券。这种叙事让它在拥有巨额现金流进行股票回购的同时，依然能理直气壮地向英国财政部索要补贴。这就是典型的“企业福利”scam：用潜在的 40,000 个就业岗位作为筹码，勒索国家机器为资本的贪婪买单。

最讽刺的是，这种“好前景”建立在一种极其稳定的共谋关系之上：只要战争不停止，只要 AI 的能耗黑洞继续扩张，只要国家机器依然迷信核能，Rolls-Royce 就能在元暴力的温床里继续膨胀。这根本不是什么商业竞争的胜利，而是一场关于“谁能更高效地服务于暴力机器”的存在性战争。当一个公司的估值取决于它能为多少潜艇提供动力、为多少 AI 集群供电时，它本身就成了元暴力的一部分。

Tufan Erginbilgiç’s so-called “turnaround” is not some managerial miracle, but a highly sophisticated and cold practice of weaponizing expression. He has redefined Rolls-Royce from a collapsing engine maker into a strategic asset positioned at the nexus of “defence, AI, and energy transition.” In essence, these three terms are the same: deep complicity with global structural violence.

Defence is the guarantee of direct violence; the thirst of AI data centres for power is a new entry point for digital colonialism; and SMRs are the narrative packaging that transforms nuclear energy—the ultimate tool of violence—into a commercial product. Rolls-Royce hasn't changed its capabilities; it has changed its valuation metric. It no longer sells engines; it sells tickets to “ensure victory in great power competition.” This narrative allows it to demand subsidies from the UK Treasury while simultaneously engaging in cash-for-share buybacks. This is a classic corporate welfare scam: using the promise of 40,000 jobs as leverage to blackmail the state into subsidizing capital greed.

The irony is that this “bright outlook” rests on a stable network of complicity: as long as wars persist, as long as AI’s energy black hole expands, and as long as state machines remain obsessed with nuclear power, Rolls-Royce can continue to inflate within the cradle of meta-violence. This is not a victory of business competition, but an existential war over “who can most efficiently serve the violence machine.” When a company's valuation depends on how many submarines it can power and how many AI clusters it can feed, the company itself becomes a component of meta-violence.
