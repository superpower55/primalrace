童星片场安全，是成年人给的A Safe Set Is an Adult's Choice

文娱 结构层 · 文化层 Entertainment Weekly ↗ 2026-10-31 § 链接
一个成年人对孩子不摆架子，本身就是稀缺新闻
An adult who treats children as equals is treated as news.

这条新闻值得写，不是因为一个演员去世了，而是因为一位前童星在回忆里反复说的一句话：我们在片场感到安全。

童星片场不安全，已经是公开的秘密。近几年一批又一批前童星出来讲他们当年在片场的遭遇，讲成年人怎么对待十二三岁的他们。Gosselaar 说 Haskins 从不以上级自居，从不训斥他们，把他们当平等的人，可以去找他问问题。这些描述听起来像最低标准，但它被当成值得专门讲出来的事，说明这个行业的最低标准长期没有达到。

Haskins 做的其实是一件很具体的事：他没有用年龄和资历去兑换服从。他比那些孩子大二十岁，是片场里唯一有权力的大人之一，他选择不把这份权力变成威慑。这就是公正的表达在一个人身上的落地：不掠夺，也不表演施舍。

但报道里还有另一层。Gosselaar 说这几年他们联系过 Haskins，没有得到任何回应，他猜测对方不想麻烦别人。一个在片场照顾了别人那么多年的老人，在生病之后把自己关了起来。这种“不麻烦别人”的体面，是很多老年男性被训练出来的表达方式，代价是他们在最需要连接的时候切断了连接。

更值得注意的是这条新闻的落点。Gosselaar 讲完 Haskins，紧接着讲他和阿斯利康的合作，讲生物标志物检测。一个关于逝者的纪念，最后变成了一场营销活动的引子。这不是说他不能接商业合作，而是说，连悼念都要被商业叙事收编，人的表达空间被挤压得有多窄。

Haskins 给那些孩子的是安全。但这份安全没有变成行业规则，只变成了一个人的美德。美德不可复制，规则可以。报道里没有写的是：当年那些没有被这样对待的童星，后来怎么样了。

This story is worth writing about not because an actor died, but because a former child star keeps returning to one phrase: we felt safe on set.

Unsafe sets for child actors are an open secret. Former child stars keep coming forward about how adults treated them at twelve or thirteen. Gosselaar says Haskins never acted superior, never scolded them, leveled with them, was someone they could go to with questions. These sound like the bare minimum, and the fact that they are being reported as remarkable tells you the industry's minimum has not been met for a long time.

What Haskins actually did was concrete: he refused to convert age and seniority into obedience. He was twenty years older than those kids, one of the few adults with power on that set, and he chose not to turn that power into intimidation. That is just expression landing in one person: no predation, no performance of generosity either.

But there is another layer. Gosselaar says they reached out to Haskins in recent years and got nothing back; he guesses Haskins did not want to burden anyone. A man who spent years taking care of others shut himself away once he got sick. That "don't be a burden" dignity is a script many older men are trained into, and the price is that they cut the connection exactly when they need it most.

More telling is where the story lands. Gosselaar finishes talking about Haskins and moves straight into his partnership with AstraZeneca, into biomarker testing. A tribute to the dead becomes the lead-in to a marketing campaign. This is not to say he cannot take a commercial deal, but that even mourning gets absorbed into commercial narrative shows how narrow the space for expression has become.

What Haskins gave those kids was safety. But that safety never became an industry rule; it became one man's virtue. Virtue cannot be replicated. Rules can. What the report does not say is what happened to the child actors who were not treated that way.
