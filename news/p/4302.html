用“垫脚石”掩盖的教育仓鼠轮The Education Hamster Wheel and its 'Stepping Stone' Scam

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-20 § 链接
用低阶资格证替代失败，是结构性暴力的 PR 换皮。
Replacing failure with low-tier certifications is just a PR skin for structural violence.

英国政府试图用一个所谓的“垫脚石” (stepping stone) 资格证来解决 GCSE 数学和英语不及格的危机。在教育部长 Lucy Powell 的叙事里，这叫作给学生提供“信心”和“护照”，是为了打破那个像“仓鼠轮”一样的重复补考机制。但事实上，这不过是把 structural violence 进行了更隐蔽的包装。

当一个系统无法提供多样化的能力评价方式时，它最擅长的就是通过重新定义“成功”来掩盖失败。所谓的 Level-1 资格证，本质上是为那些无法通过标准化考试的人制造的一个“低阶隔离区”。这不是在提供路径，而是在通过行政手段人为地制造一个 two-tier system。那些来自资源匮乏背景的学生，会被更高效地引导至这个“低阶路径”，从而在潜意识中内化自己的“次等”身份，完成一次关于社会阶级的自我规训。

最讽刺的是，这种“垫脚石”依然被定位为以后补考 GCSE 的准备阶段。这意味着学生依然被困在那个男本位、标准化、追求单一结果的评价体系中，只是在被彻底判定为“失败”之前，被喂了一颗名为“信心”的安慰剂。这种对认知入口的操纵，让学生在进入 NEET (不就业、不教育、不培训) 状态之前，先在心理上接受了这种分层。

至于文中提到的数学成绩中男性的强势增长，恰恰证明了这套标准化评估体系在某种程度上依然是为某种特定的认知模式设计的。当系统拒绝改革评估机制，而只在资格证的边缘进行 tinker 时，它在维护的不是学生的未来，而是那个垄断了“什么是能力”解释权的权力结构。

The UK government is attempting to resolve the GCSE maths and English failure crisis with a so-called "stepping stone" qualification. In Education Secretary Lucy Powell's narrative, this is about providing "confidence" and a "passport" to break the "hamster wheel" of repeated resits. In reality, this is merely a more covert packaging of structural violence.

When a system fails to provide diverse ways of evaluating capability, its go-to move is to redefine "success" to mask failure. This Level-1 qualification is essentially the creation of a "low-tier ghetto" for those who cannot pass standardized tests. It is not providing a pathway; it is using administrative means to manufacture a two-tier system. Students from under-resourced backgrounds will be disproportionately funneled into this lower route, internalizing a "subordinate" identity and completing a process of social class self-discipline.

The irony is that this "stepping stone" is still positioned as preparation for future GCSE resits. Students remain trapped in a masculine-centric, standardized evaluation system that prizes a single outcome, only receiving a placebo called "confidence" before being branded as failures. This manipulation of the cognitive entry point prepares students psychologically for their eventual slide into NEET status.

Moreover, the reported surge in maths performance among boys proves that this standardized assessment remains designed for a specific cognitive mode. As long as the system refuses to reform the assessment mechanism and only tinkers at the edges of certifications, it is not protecting students' futures—it is protecting the power structure that monopolizes the interpretation of "what constitutes ability."
