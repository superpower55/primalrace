用“真实”的定义权，在废墟上玩一场零和博弈Playing a Zero-Sum Game with the Definition of 'Reality'

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-09 § 链接
所谓的和平计划只是权力博弈的注脚，而“真实”是最高级的武器。
Peace plans are mere footnotes to power struggles; the definition of 'Reality' is the ultimate weapon.

内塔尼亚胡拒绝特朗普的加沙计划，理由是哈马斯没有“真正地” (genuinely) 解除武装。请注意这个词。在存在性战争中，定义权就是定价权。当一个人垄断了对“真实” (real) 和“虚构” (fictitious) 的解释权时，他实际上是在制造一个永远无法被满足的门槛，从而让“不撤军”这个结果变得正当化。这在第三章里就是典型的 weaponized expression：通过定义一个不可达到的标准，来合法化结构性的暴力。

而特朗普的 15 点计划，本质上是一场关于“认知入口”的表演。他试图通过一个看似公正的 roadmap 来扮演秩序的制造者，但这种“和平”是建立在要求弱势方彻底交出主体性（武器）的基础之上的。这种不对称的博弈根本不是为了消弭暴力，而是为了在新的权力分配中重新确立谁是主导。在这种叙事里，加沙的平民只是背景板，他们的生存权被量化成了谈判桌上的筹码。

更讽刺的是，这场博弈的共谋者不仅在加沙，而在整个中东。美国一边在谈停火，一边通过国防部备忘录疯狂催促军工复合体加速生产武器。这就是最典型的 structural violence：表面的 cultural layer 在谈和平，底层的 structural layer 在为下一场更大的战争铺路。战争叙事被武器化为一种经济驱动力，油价的波动、军火的订单，才是这场“危机”中真正的最优解表达。

最终，所有关于“人道”和“安全”的讨论都成了掩体。当元暴力（男性中心、强权中心的叙事）掌控了整个中东的解释权，和平就成了一种可以被随时撤回的恩赐，而不是一项基本的人权。

Netanyahu rejects Trump’s Gaza plan because Hamas isn't 'genuinely' disarmed. Note the word 'genuinely.' In an existential war, the power to define is the power to price. By monopolizing the interpretation of what is 'real' versus 'fictitious,' he creates an unreachable threshold, making the refusal to withdraw legitimate. This is the weaponisation of expression: using an unattainable standard to justify structural violence.

Trump’s 15-point plan is a performance of 'cognitive entry.' He attempts to play the architect of order through a seemingly just roadmap, but this 'peace' requires the weaker party to surrender their entire subjectivity (weapons). This asymmetric game isn't about eliminating violence; it's about re-establishing dominance in a new power distribution. In this narrative, Gazan civilians are mere background noise, their right to exist quantified as bargaining chips.

More cynically, the complicity extends beyond Gaza. While the US discusses ceasefires, the Department of Defense is aggressively pushing the military-industrial complex to accelerate weapon production. This is structural violence in its purest form: the cultural layer performs peace while the structural layer paves the way for a larger war. The war narrative is weaponised into an economic driver; oil prices and munitions contracts are the true optimal expressions here.

Ultimately, all discourse on 'humanity' and 'security' serves as cover. When meta-violence—the masculine-centric, power-centric narrative—controls the interpretation of the Middle East, peace becomes a revocable favor rather than a fundamental human right.
