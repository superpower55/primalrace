用“神谕”装修教室：一场关于解释权的权力霸凌Decorating Classrooms with 'Divine Decrees': A Power Bullying of Interpretation

国际 文化层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-23 § 链接
法律强制挂海报不是为了信仰，而是为了垄断对“事实”的定义权。
Mandating posters isn't about faith; it's about monopolizing the power to define 'fact'.

在路易斯安那州和德克萨斯州，强制在教室挂起《十诫》的海报被包装成是对“历史遗产”的继承。这纯粹是一个 scam。所谓的 heritage 实际上是 weaponized 叙事的入口：当一个政权试图通过法律强制某种宗教文本出现在公共教育空间时，它争夺的不是信仰，而是认知入口 (cognitive entry)。它在告诉孩子们：在这个空间里，只有这一套解释权是合法的，其余的都是噪音。

这典型的 cultural violence。它通过将特定宗教文本“自然化”为法律和道德的基石，试图让随之而来的 structural violence 看起来合理。比如，当一个非裔女性教师 Taria Usher 指出美国遗产是奴隶制和压迫时，这种真相就被这种“神圣叙事”给覆盖了。元暴力 (meta violence) 的逻辑就在这里：通过垄断解释权，让施暴者定义什么是“文明”和“秩序”，从而将所有反抗定义为“不敬”或“激进”。

好在这次出现了有趣的博弈。Usher 老师通过挂起伊斯兰教五功、佛教五戒来对抗，这是一种极其聪明的“最优解表达”。她没有陷入“宗教 vs 无神论”的二元陷阱，而是通过引入其他叙事，强行将垄断的单向通道变成了多元的博弈场，从而拆穿了这种霸凌的排他性。当《十诫》不再是唯一的“神谕”，它就从一个权力工具变回了一张普通的纸。

但不要 naive 地庆祝。这种反抗目前仍处于个体博弈阶段。真正的战场在最高法院，在那个由男性中心叙事主导的权力顶端。如果最高法院再次通过某种“历史语境”的诡辩给这种强制行为背书，那么这就不再是几张海报的问题，而是国家机器在系统性地拆除政教分离的生物墙，将公共教育彻底转化为 Christian nationalism 的驯化场。

In Louisiana and Texas, the mandate to display the Ten Commandments in classrooms is being packaged as an inheritance of 'historical heritage.' This is a pure scam. This so-called heritage is actually a weaponized narrative entry: when a regime uses law to force religious texts into public education, it isn't fighting for faith, but for the cognitive entry. It signals to students that only one set of interpretation is legitimate in this space, while all others are noise.

This is textbook cultural violence. By 'naturalizing' specific religious texts as the foundation of law and morality, it attempts to make the subsequent structural violence appear reasonable. For instance, when Taria Usher, an African American teacher, points out that American heritage is one of slavery and oppression, that truth is smothered by this 'sacred narrative.' The logic of meta violence is precisely this: by monopolizing the right to interpret, the oppressor defines 'civilization' and 'order,' labeling all resistance as 'disrespectful' or 'radical.'

Fortunately, we see an interesting game of existence here. Teacher Taria Usher's response—hanging the Five Pillars of Islam and Five Precepts of Buddhism alongside the Commandments—is a brilliant 'optimal expression.' She avoided the binary trap of 'religion vs atheism' and instead forced the monopolized one-way channel into a pluralistic battlefield, exposing the exclusivity of this bullying. When the Ten Commandments are no longer the sole 'oracle,' they revert from a tool of power to a mere piece of paper.

But don't be naive. This resistance is currently limited to individual博弈. The real battlefield is the Supreme Court, the pinnacle of power dominated by masculine-centric narratives. If the Court again uses the sophistry of 'historical context' to endorse this mandate, it won't just be about posters; it will be the state machine systematically demolishing the biological wall between church and state, turning public education into a grooming ground for Christian nationalism.
