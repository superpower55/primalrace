神坛上的女性，还是货架上的女性Goddesses on a Pedestal, or Women on a Shelf

文娱 文化层 · 元暴力 Polygon.com ↗ 2026-11-03 § 链接
新闻摘要迪士尼发布《波西·杰克逊与奥林匹斯众神》第三季预告，第三季改编自2007年小说《泰坦的诅咒》，将于11月20日在Disney+和Hulu上线。新一季加盟的演员包括达芙妮·基恩、温明娜、凯特·麦克金农等，但迪士尼尚未正式续订第四季。
The newsDisney has released the trailer for season 3 of Percy Jackson and the Olympians, adapting the 2007 novel The Titan's Curse, premiering Nov. 20 on Disney+ and Hulu. New cast includes Dafne Keen, Ming-Na Wen and Kate McKinnon, but Disney has not yet greenlit a fourth season.
把女神一个个请下神坛，再一个个摆上货架。
They take the goddesses off the altar one by one, then put them on the shelf one by one.

这条预告最值得看的不是特效，是选角表：阿尔忒弥斯、赫拉、阿佛洛狄忒，三个希腊神话里权力最大的女性神，被一次性打包成三位知名女演员的客串。迪士尼没说谎，这确实是「全明星阵容」。问题在于，这些角色在剧里的功能是什么。

希腊神话里的女神从来不是装饰品。阿尔忒弥斯掌管狩猎与分娩，赫拉掌管婚姻与权力，阿佛洛狄忒掌管欲望本身——她们是解释权的持有者，是凡人命运的定义者。而到了这部剧里，她们成了「第三季的看点之一」，和「胡佛水坝」并列出现在预告片的清单上。一个神被降格成一处景点，这就是武器化最温和也最彻底的形式：不删掉你，只把你变成布景。

更精确的机制在安娜贝丝身上。预告说这一季的主线是「波西去救安娜贝丝」。原著读者知道这个结构，剧集也照搬了：女性角色被掳走，男性角色获得旅程、成长和英雄叙事。她被拿走的不是安全，是主体性——她在那几集里的全部表达就是「等待被救」。这不是编剧的恶意，这是叙事模板的惰性。救赎叙事需要一个被救的人，而那个位置，几乎总是留给女性。

然后是这个行业最诚实的一句话：迪士尼还没续订第四季。第三季在第二季播出前几个月就官宣了，第四季至今没有绿灯。同时迪士尼已经下单了同一作者的《凯恩编年史》改编。这说明什么？说明在迪士尼眼里，这些故事是可以替换的原料，一部没续订，还有下一部顶上。演员、角色、观众的情感投入，都是可消耗品。

粉丝的恐惧也是真的：他们已经经历过一次失败的改编。但那种恐惧被引导向了「别砍剧」，而不是「为什么一部剧的生死由一份没有公开的续订决定」。前者是情绪，后者才是结构。

所以这条预告真正在卖的东西，不是泰坦的诅咒，是「我们请来了凯特·麦克金农演阿佛洛狄忒」。一个女神的价值，被换算成一个演员的知名度；一个女主角的整季戏份，被压缩成男主角的动机。十一月二十日上线，观众会看到很多神，但很难看到任何一个神拥有自己的解释权。

至于瑞秋·伊丽莎白·戴尔——原著里那个能看穿未来的凡人女孩——预告里连影子都没有。报道说粉丝可能得等到上线才知道谁来演。一个不靠神谕、自己说出真相的女性角色，恰恰是这部剧最需要、也最容易被剪掉的那一个。

The most telling thing in this trailer isn't the effects. It's the cast list: Artemis, Hera, Aphrodite — the three most powerful female gods in Greek myth — packaged in one go as guest turns by three famous actresses. Disney isn't lying. It really is a star-studded lineup. The question is what these roles do in the show.

In Greek myth the goddesses were never decoration. Artemis held hunting and childbirth, Hera held marriage and power, Aphrodite held desire itself — they were holders of the interpretive power, the ones who defined mortal fate. Here they become "one of the highlights of season 3," listed alongside the Hoover Dam. A god demoted to a tourist site: that is weaponization in its mildest and most complete form. You aren't deleted. You're turned into scenery.

The sharper mechanism sits with Annabeth. The trailer says the season's spine is Percy setting out to save her. Book readers know the structure, and the show copies it: the female character is taken, the male character gets the journey, the growth, the hero narrative. What's taken from her isn't safety, it's subjectivity — her entire expression for those episodes is waiting to be rescued. This isn't the writers' malice. It's the inertia of a template. Redemption stories need someone to save, and that slot is almost always reserved for a woman.

Then comes the most honest sentence in the trade: Disney hasn't renewed season 4. Season 3 was confirmed months before season 2 aired; season 4 still has no green light. Meanwhile Disney has already ordered an adaptation of the same author's Kane Chronicles. Which tells you these stories are interchangeable feedstock — one doesn't get renewed, the next one steps up. Actors, characters, viewers' emotional investment: all consumable.

The fans' fear is real too: they've already lived through one failed adaptation. But that fear gets channeled into "don't cancel the show," not into "why does a show's life hinge on a renewal nobody can see." The first is a feeling. The second is a structure.

So what this trailer actually sells isn't the Titan's curse. It's "we got Kate McKinnon to play Aphrodite." A goddess's worth is converted into an actress's fame; a heroine's whole season is compressed into the hero's motivation. It premieres Nov. 20. Audiences will see plenty of gods. They won't see one who holds her own interpretive power.

And Rachel Elizabeth Dare — the mortal girl in the books who sees the future — isn't in the trailer at all. The report says fans may have to wait until release to learn who plays her. A female character who needs no oracle and speaks the truth herself is exactly what this show needs most, and exactly what gets cut first.
