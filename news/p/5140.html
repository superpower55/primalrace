用底层人的生存权为战争叙事买单Paying for War Narratives with the Survival Rights of the Poor

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-19 § 链接
所谓的国防危机，往往是权力阶层通过制造恐惧来合法化剥夺的 weaponized narrative。
Defense crises are often weaponized narratives used by elites to legitimize systemic deprivation.

Badenoch 的逻辑极其粗暴：为了应对一个被她定义的“燃烧的欧洲”，必须削减福利预算。这在加尔通的暴力三角里是典型的 structural violence 升级。她试图将社会保障（Social Security）定义为一种奢侈的“讨好”，而将战争准备定义为唯一的“严肃”。这种叙事通过制造外部敌人的恐惧，将内部的资源掠夺包装成一种必然的牺牲。

最令人作呕的是那种典型的 masculine-centric narrative：把对底层生存权的剥夺简化为“艰难的选择”。当保守党提出通过削减住房补贴来筹集 40 亿英镑时，他们轻描淡写地说有些人只需要“搬到更便宜的地方”。这种表达抹杀了具体的生存痛苦，将活生生的人简化为国防预算表上的一个数字。这就是元暴力的运作方式——定义谁是可牺牲的，并赋予这种牺牲以“国家安全”的神圣性。

这场所谓的“福利 vs 战争”辩论本质上是一场关于认知入口的争夺。Badenoch 试图通过攻击 Burnham 的“讨好”形象，将对他人的共情定义为软弱，将对战争机器的投喂定义为强悍。这种 weaponization of expression 旨在建立一种共识：在一个所谓的“非历史终结”时代，生存权必须让位于杀戮权。而那些被要求搬迁、失去保障的底层人群，就是这场存在性战争中被预先出卖的共谋代价。

Badenoch's logic is brutally simplistic: to counter a 'burning Europe' of her own definition, the welfare budget must be slashed. In Galtung's Violence Triangle, this is a textbook escalation of structural violence. She attempts to frame social security as a mere 'people-pleasing' luxury, while casting war preparedness as the only 'serious' priority. By manufacturing fear of an external enemy, the predation of internal resources is rebranded as an inevitable sacrifice.

What is most repulsive is the typical masculine-centric narrative: reducing the deprivation of basic survival rights to 'difficult choices.' When the Tories propose raising £4bn by capping housing benefits, they casually suggest some people simply 'move somewhere cheaper.' This expression erases concrete human suffering, reducing living beings to mere digits on a defense ledger. This is how meta-violence operates—defining who is disposable and granting this disposal a sense of 'national security' sanctity.

This 'welfare v warfare' debate is essentially a struggle over cognitive entry points. Badenoch seeks to frame empathy as weakness and the feeding of the war machine as strength. This weaponization of expression aims to establish a consensus: in a supposed 'non-end of history' era, the right to exist must yield to the right to kill. The marginalized who will be displaced and stripped of support are the pre-sold costs of this existential war.
