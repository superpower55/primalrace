好莱坞的“回声室”与权力结构的禁令The Hollywood 'Echo Chamber' and the Ban of Power Structures

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-07 § 链接
舆论的转向不代表权力的让渡，禁令才是结构暴力的真面目。
A shift in public opinion is not a transfer of power; the ban is the true face of structural violence.

Susan Sarandon 的遭遇揭露了一个极其残酷的真相：大众认知的 shift 并不等同于权力结构的变动。即使民调显示公众对巴勒斯坦的同情在上升，但在好莱坞这个高度集权的 industry 里，决定谁能出演、谁被 shun 的不是“公众意见”，而是那几个掌握认知入口的 major studios 和 agencies。

这就是典型的 structural violence。在这种结构中，表达被武器化为一种筛选机制。当你挑战了权力的核心叙事（在本案中是美国政治与锡安主义的共谋关系），你就不再是一个“演员”，而变成了一个“风险因子”。Agency 告诉人们不要雇佣她，这不是在讨论艺术，而是在执行一次政治清洗。这种禁令的本质是夺取解释权：通过剥夺一个人的表达空间，来警告所有潜在的共谋者——谁敢脱离既定叙事，谁就会被从系统中剔除。

Sarandon 提到的“power structure”正是这种元暴力的具象化。好莱坞自诩为多元与进步的殿堂，但其底层逻辑依然是极其保守的男性中心叙事：权力由顶层定义，底层通过扮演“正确”的角色来换取生存空间。所谓的“进步”在触碰到核心利益（如地缘政治共谋）时，会瞬间切换回最原始的压制模式。

她现在能在这个名为《回声室》（The Echo Chamber）的电影里找到位置，本身就是一个巨大的反讽。在这个行业里，大多数人依然生活在权力构建的巨大回声室中，重复着被允许的台词，直到他们发现，当他们试图走出回声室时，门早已被锁死了。

Susan Sarandon's experience reveals a brutal truth: a shift in public perception does not equate to a shift in power structures. Even if polls show rising sympathy for Palestinians, in the highly centralized industry of Hollywood, the decision of who is cast or shunned is not driven by 'public opinion,' but by a few major studios and agencies that control the cognitive entry points.

This is a textbook case of structural violence. In this system, expression is weaponized as a screening mechanism. When you challenge the core narrative of power—in this case, the complicity between US politics and Zionism—you cease to be an 'actor' and become a 'risk factor.' When agencies tell people not to hire her, they aren't discussing art; they are executing a political purge. The essence of this ban is the seizure of the right to interpret: by stripping an individual of their space for expression, the system warns all potential co-conspirators that anyone who deviates from the prescribed narrative will be excised from the system.

The 'power structure' Sarandon mentions is the manifestation of meta-violence. Hollywood poses as a sanctuary of diversity and progress, but its underlying logic remains a rigid masculine-centric narrative: power is defined at the top, and those at the bottom trade their subjectivity for survival by playing 'correct' roles. The so-called 'progress' instantly reverts to primitive suppression the moment it touches core interests, such as geopolitical complicity.

It is a grand irony that she found a place in a film titled *The Echo Chamber*. In this industry, most people still reside within a massive echo chamber constructed by power, repeating permitted lines, only to realize that when they attempt to step outside, the door has already been locked.
