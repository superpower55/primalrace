迷恋的真相是权力的单向剥夺The Truth of Obsession is the Unilateral Seizure of Power

性别 直接层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-25 § 链接
Stalking 不是爱，而是一场试图强行夺取他人主体性的存在性战争。
Stalking is not love; it is an existential war aimed at forcibly seizing another's subjectivity.

很多新闻会把这种行为描述为“痴迷”或“妄想”，但剥开这些温情的词汇，这就是一场典型的存在性战争。Philip Jerome 所谓的“性兴趣”和“希望建立关系”，本质上是对 Billie Piper 作为一个独立主体的彻底否定。在他构建的叙事里，Piper 不是一个有意志的人，而是一个被他定义、被他凝视、被他通过 GQ 奖项照片“定价”的客体。这种表达的武器化极其卑劣：他试图通过打破物理边界（闯入家中）和数字边界（骚扰家人），强行将对方拉入他设定的博弈场。

Many news reports describe this as 'obsession' or 'delusion,' but stripping away these soft terms reveals a textbook Existential War. Philip Jerome's so-called 'sexual interest' and 'hope for a relationship' are essentially a total negation of Billie Piper's agency as a human subject. In his manufactured narrative, Piper is not a person with will, but an object to be defined, gazed upon, and 'priced' via a GQ awards photo. This is the weaponisation of expression at its most vile: he attempted to force her into his game by breaching both physical boundaries (home intrusion) and digital boundaries (harassing her family).
