算法定价：一场关于时间与尊严的精准掠夺Algorithmic Pricing: A Precise Plunder of Time and Dignity

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-28 § 链接
当定价权变成AI驱动的心理战，消费者在博弈中已失去主体性。
When pricing becomes an AI-driven psychological war, consumers lose their agency in the game.

大多数人把物价上涨看作经济波动，但Lindsay Owens揭露的是一场精心策划的 weaponized expression。这不再是简单的供需关系，而是一场利用大数据和AI构建的认知不对称战争。企业通过 EverSight 等数据经纪人，将消费者的年龄、健康甚至党派倾向转化为定价参数。这本质上是在探测每个个体的“痛点” (pain point)，然后精准地在那个临界点上开价。这哪里是商业竞争，这分明是对个体生存状态的实时监控与剥削。

最阴险的在于，这种 structural violence 正在通过“个体化”来瓦解集体的反抗。当你和你的伴侣在床上用两台电脑对比机票价格时，你们以为在进行一种聪明的博弈，但实际上，你们在用最宝贵的资源——时间，去对抗一个由超级计算机驱动的掠夺系统。这种“比价”行为本身就是一种被异化的最优解表达：你通过牺牲主体性的时间，试图在被操纵的规则里抢回三块钱。而企业正是赌在大多数人无法承受这种时间成本，从而心安理得地完成收割。

这种机制是典型的元暴力 (meta violence) 延伸：定义什么是“市场规律”、什么是“合理涨价”的权力被极少数资本共谋者垄断。他们利用战争的迷雾 (fog of war) 掩盖利润的贪婪，将结构性的掠夺包装成宏观经济的必然。当一个社会的生存基础变成了需要通过“研究算法”才能勉强维持的生存游戏，这已经不是经济问题，而是一场存在性战争。在这种战争中，消费者的主体性被降格为一组可被计算的标签，而所谓的“购物权利”，在算法面前不过是一个可被随意修改的变量。

Most people view rising prices as economic fluctuation, but Lindsay Owens reveals a weaponized expression of corporate greed. This is no longer about supply and demand; it is a cognitive asymmetry war powered by Big Data and AI. By using data brokers like EverSight, corporations translate age, health, and even political affiliation into pricing parameters. They are not pricing products; they are probing the individual's 'pain point' to extract the maximum possible value. This is real-time surveillance and exploitation of human existence.

The most sinister part is how this structural violence dissolves collective resistance through 'individualization.' When you and your partner compare flight prices on two laptops in bed, you think you are playing a clever game. In reality, you are spending your most precious resource—time—to fight a predatory system driven by supercomputers. This 'comparison shopping' is a distorted optimal expression: you sacrifice your agency and time to claw back a few dollars within a manipulated rule-set. Corporations bet on the fact that most people cannot afford this time cost, making the harvest seamless.

This mechanism is an extension of meta violence: the power to define 'market laws' and 'reasonable hikes' is monopolized by a small circle of corporate complicity. They use the 'fog of war' to mask greed, packaging structural plunder as macroeconomic inevitability. When basic survival requires 'studying algorithms' to avoid being ripped off, it ceases to be an economic issue and becomes an existential war. In this war, the consumer's agency is reduced to a set of calculable tags, and the so-called 'shoppers' bill of rights' is merely a variable to be tweaked by the algorithm.
