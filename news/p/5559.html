绿党的迷失：当气候危机沦为权力博弈的装饰品The Green Party's Drift: When Climate Crisis Becomes a Prop for Power Games

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-15 § 链接
政治人物在追求‘存在感’的博弈中，最先抛弃的是定义其存在的基石。
In the game for 'visibility', politicians are always the first to betray the foundations of their own existence.

这几封读者来信揭露了一个典型的政治 scam：一个政党在进入权力博弈场后，迅速将自己的‘认知入口’从具体的生存危机（气候与生态）切换到了廉价的 populist posturing（民粹姿态）。Zack Polanski 的自我辩护之所以被指责为 self-serving，是因为他试图用一种‘泛政治化’的叙事来掩盖主体性的丧失。

按照加尔通的暴力三角，气候危机是最高等级的 structural violence，它决定了人类 Potential 与 Actual 之间巨大的、致命的差额。绿党的原初使命应该是缩小这个差额。然而，当 Polanski 开始玩弄外交姿态和‘攻击富人’这种低成本的政治表演时，他实际上是在进行一次错误的‘最优解表达’——他以为在扮演一个‘全面政党’能获得更多选票，但代价是让绿党失去了其作为政治实体的唯一解释权。

更危险的是这种叙事真空带来的副作用。当一个政党不再聚焦于其核心的、公正的表达，它就成了一个空壳，极易被 entryism（渗透主义）和仇恨叙事填充。正如信中所言，反犹主义的潜入并非偶然，而是因为领导层在通过模糊核心议题来换取短期政治红利时，失去了对党内叙事边界的掌控力。

这本质上是一场失败的存在性战争。绿党在试图‘像主流政党一样思考’的过程中，把自己变成了男性中心政治叙事的一个子集。他们不再是那个挑战规则的异类，而成了在既定规则里抢地盘的共谋者。一个忘记了自己为什么存在的政党，其表达已经死亡，剩下的只是政治表演的尸骸。

These letters expose a classic political scam: a party that, upon entering the arena of power, swiftly shifts its cognitive entry point from a concrete existential crisis (climate and ecology) to cheap populist posturing. Zack Polanski’s apologia is dismissed as self-serving because he attempts to mask the loss of his party's subjectivity with a narrative of 'generalized politics'.

According to the Violence Triangle, the climate crisis is the highest form of structural violence, creating a lethal gap between the Potential and Actual state of human survival. The Green Party's primal mission was to close this gap. However, by pivoting toward foreign-policy posturing and 'bashing the rich', Polanski is pursuing a false optimal expression—believing that playing the role of a 'comprehensive party' wins more votes, while the actual cost is the surrender of the party's only unique claim to interpretive power.

More dangerous is the side effect of this narrative vacuum. When a party ceases to focus on its core, just expression, it becomes a hollow shell, easily filled by entryism and narratives of hate. The infiltration of anti-Jewish sentiment is not an accident; it is the result of a leadership that lost control over the narrative boundaries while chasing short-term political dividends through the blurring of core issues.

This is essentially a failed existential war. In trying to 'think like a mainstream party', the Greens have transformed themselves into a subset of the masculine-centric narrative. They are no longer the outsiders challenging the rules, but complicit players fighting for scraps within the existing framework. A party that forgets why it exists has seen its expression die; what remains is merely the corpse of political performance.
