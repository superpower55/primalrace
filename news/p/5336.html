用反恐特警对付高中生：一种制度化的暴力美学Riot Police vs. High Schoolers: The Aesthetics of Institutionalized Violence

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-30 § 链接
当 Actual 资源匮乏到极限，暴力便成了系统唯一的沟通语言。
When Actual resources hit rock bottom, violence becomes the system's only language of communication.

这件新闻的荒诞之处在于，它把一个典型的 structural violence（结构性暴力）问题，通过 direct violence（直接暴力）的方式试图将其抹除。学生们抗议的是什么？是 35 人的大班额，是教师短缺，是早 8 点到晚 6 点这种剥夺睡眠的 timetable。在加尔通的公式里，Potential（本应有的教育资源）与 Actual（现实中的匮乏）之间的差额，就是施加在这些青少年身上的结构性暴力。

但政府的 response 极其典型：它不打算缩小这个差额，而是打算通过“维持秩序”来掩盖差额。内政部长和总理的叙事非常 anemic（贫血），他们迅速将讨论从“资源匮乏”转移到“暴力行为”上。当学生扔出瓶子，政府就有了理由派出 CRS（反暴警）和 BAC（反犯罪大队）。这是一种典型的武器化叙事：通过定义学生为“不负责任的暴徒”，将国家机器的暴力合法化。在他们的认知入口里，只要学生戴上口罩、扔出石头，他们就失去了作为“受教育者”的身份，变成了需要被“镇压”的敌对目标。

最令人作呕的共谋在于，政府一边在预算中砍掉 5400 万欧元的公共支出，一边指责学生们的愤怒是“被政客利用”。这是一种元暴力的伪装——用“共和国的职责”和“理性”作为掩体，掩盖其本质上的资源掠夺。当 15-18 岁的孩子面对的是盾牌和警棍，而政府在 X 上谈论“责任”时，这种断裂本身就是一种极端的 cultural violence。

好在，这次博弈中出现了一些不愿共谋的人。那些站在学生面前挡住警察的教师，是在用身体尝试建立一个公正的表达空间。但这场战争的胜负不在于谁扔了多少瓶子，而在于那个 5400 万欧元的预算缺口是否会被填回。如果结构不改变，那么今天被拘留的 440 个孩子，明天只会变成 4400 个。

The absurdity of this news lies in the attempt to erase structural violence through the application of direct violence. What are the students protesting? Overcrowded classrooms of 35, teacher shortages, and a grueling timetable from 8 am to 6 pm that robs them of sleep. In Galtung's formula, the gap between Potential educational resources and the Actual scarcity is the structural violence imposed on these teenagers.

Yet, the government's response is textbook: instead of closing this gap, they seek to mask it through "maintaining order." The Interior Minister and Prime Minister employ an anemic narrative, swiftly shifting the discourse from "resource scarcity" to "violent behavior." The moment students throw bottles, the government finds justification to deploy the CRS and BAC. This is a classic weaponisation of expression: by defining students as "irresponsible rioters," the state legitimizes its own violence. In their cognitive entry point, once a student wears a mask or throws a stone, they cease to be a "pupil" and become a hostile target to be "suppressed."

The most sickening complicity is found in the government cutting €54m from public spending while claiming students' anger is merely "exploited by politicians." This is a mask for meta-violence—using "republican duty" and "rationality" as cover for systemic resource plunder. When 15-to-18-year-olds face shields and batons while the government tweets about "responsibility," this rupture is itself a form of extreme cultural violence.

Fortunately, some refuse to be co-conspirators. The teachers who stood between the students and the police are attempting to create a space for Just Expressions with their own bodies. But the outcome of this existential war doesn't depend on how many bottles were thrown; it depends on whether that €54m budget gap is filled. If the structure remains, the 440 children detained today will simply become 4,400 tomorrow.
