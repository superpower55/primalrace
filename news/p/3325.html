用会计技巧掩盖的增长骗局The Growth Scam Masked by Accounting Tricks

中国科技 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-07 § 链接
定义权的变更不等于能力的提升，换尺子不等于长高了。
Changing the definition of debt is not an increase in capacity; changing the ruler doesn't make you taller.

英国财政部的这场博弈，本质上是一场关于“定义权”的文字游戏。前任 Reeves 搞出的 PSNFL（所谓的 persnuffle）定义，就是典型的武器化表达：通过重新定义什么是“债务”，让政府在不触碰财政规则的前提下增加借贷。这就像华为宣布 $\tau$-scaling 定律一样，当现实的物理上限（财政规则）不配合时，就通过重新定义现实来制造“增长”的幻象。

这种操作在结构层（structural layer）上毫无进展。无论是借钱买金融资产，还是让公共公司直接在市场融资，都只是在既有的资本逻辑里打转。经济学家们在争论该如何“大胆借贷”，却没人问这些钱最终会流向谁。在男本位的经济叙事中，所谓的“基础设施”和“增长”，往往意味着更多由男性主导的工程项目和资本增值，而真正能缩小 Potential − Actual 差额的公共服务——如护理、育儿、基础医疗——在这些“大胆”的借贷计划中永远是次要的。

最讽刺的是，这些政客在讨论如何“利用灵活性”来绕过规则。当一个系统需要通过不断修改会计准则来证明自己能增长时，这个系统就已经在进行一种元暴力（meta violence）的自我欺骗。他们试图用一种“专业且理性”的金融术语，掩盖其无法在结构上产生实质性突破的无能。这不过是一场共谋：财政部、智库和金融专家共同维持一个“我们正在尝试增长”的叙事，而代价则是未来被进一步抵押的公共债务。

The current game at the UK Treasury is essentially a battle over the 'right of definition.' The PSNFL (or 'persnuffle') definition introduced by Reeves is a classic weaponisation of expression: by redefining what constitutes 'debt,' the government can increase borrowing without technically breaching fiscal rules. It is the same logic as claiming a new law of physics when the hardware fails—redefining reality because the actual capacity is lacking.

On a structural layer, there is zero progress. Whether it's borrowing to acquire financial assets or allowing public corporations to tap into markets, they are merely orbiting the same capital logic. Economists argue over how to be 'bold' with borrowing, yet no one asks who actually benefits. In a masculine-centric economic narrative, 'infrastructure' and 'growth' typically translate into male-dominated engineering projects and capital gains, while the services that would actually reduce the gap between Potential and Actual—such as care and childcare—remain peripheral.

The irony is that these politicians are debating how to 'exploit flexibility' to bypass their own rules. When a system must constantly tweak its accounting standards to prove it can grow, it is engaging in a form of meta-violence—a collective self-deception. They use 'professional and rational' financial jargon to mask a structural impotence. This is a clear case of complicity: the Treasury, think tanks, and financial experts collaborate to maintain a narrative of 'attempted growth,' while the actual cost is a further mortgaging of the public's future.
