175个账户，和四个月牌照175 Accounts and a Four-Month License

其他 结构层 · 文化层 ProPublica ↗ 2026-11-01 § 链接
新闻摘要在 ProPublica 调查报道发布后，路易斯安那州监管机构于10月15日暂缓续发 DraftKings 的五年牌照，改为发放四个月临时许可。俄亥俄州随后提议，禁止公司在用户启用负责任博彩工具后48小时内向其推送营销信息。三名民主党联邦议员也致信多家博彩公司，要求说明 VIP 项目的运作情况。
The newsAfter a ProPublica investigation, Louisiana regulators on Oct. 15 tabled DraftKings' five-year license renewal, granting a four-month temporary permit instead. Ohio then proposed barring companies from sending marketing to users for 48 hours after they enable responsible-gaming tools. Three Democratic lawmakers also demanded information from major sportsbooks about their VIP programs.
监管不是暴力退场，是暴力换了个更体面的入口
Regulation is not violence leaving; it is violence entering through a nicer door.

路易斯安那州博彩管理委员会把 DraftKings 的五年牌照按下不表，只给四个月临时许可。看起来是执法，其实是把结构暴力重新包装了一遍：牌照照发，生意照做，调查照拖。

ProPublica 的记者用赌徒的方式下注，三周输掉近六千美元，DraftKings 给他发了 VIP 身份。VIP 是什么？是认知入口的会员卡。这个系统奖励的不是赢家，是输得最多、追得最凶的人。输得越多，待遇越好。这不是漏洞，是设计。

更直接的是那个时间线：记者在 24 小时内输掉近 4500 美元，设了限额工具，30 分钟后收到四条推送。工具是给人看的，推送是给钱看的。直到他用更严格的 cool-off，推送才停。所谓 responsible gaming，是公司自己写的说明书，不是监管。

DraftKings 总裁 Paul Liberman 说，我们不用 AI 针对输钱的赌客。可《纽约时报》报道说，他们用机器学习和 AI 把促销推给输钱的赌客。两句话不能同时为真。

俄亥俄州提议：用户启用负责任博彩工具后 48 小时内禁止直接营销。这条规则直接对着 ProPublica 的发现写，但 Keith Whyte 担心它会让用户不敢用工具，因为用工具等于承认有问题。这个担心有道理，但它证明的是另一件事：工具已经被设计成羞耻的标志，而不是保护。

路易斯安那的会上，DraftKings 的首席负责任博彩官 Lori Kalani 说，今年在路易斯安那关了 175 个账户，但不知道这个州总共有多少用户。她补了一句：希望这个数字是零。175 是分子，分母未知。这就是共谋的算术：给你一个数字，不给你比例。

联邦层面，三位民主党议员发信要 VIP 项目的数据。信是压力，不是判决。纽约州总检察长办公室联系了前员工，缅因、马萨诸塞、密歇根说在审查。都在动，都没有落地。

真正被削掉的差额很小：一个牌照从五年变成四个月，一条规则还在听证。直接暴力没有减少，结构暴力没有松动，文化暴力还在用“负责任”三个字给赌瘾贴金。

175 个账户被关掉，可这个州有多少人正在被 VIP 系统奖励着越输越多，报道没写。这就是缺口。

The Louisiana Gaming Control Board tabled DraftKings' five-year license renewal and handed it a four-month temporary permit. That looks like enforcement. It is structural violence repackaged: the license still issues, the business still runs, the investigation still drags.

A ProPublica reporter bet like a problem gambler, lost nearly $6,000 in three weeks, and DraftKings gave him VIP status. VIP is a membership card to the cognitive entrance. The system does not reward winners. It rewards the people who lose the most and chase the hardest. The more you lose, the better you are treated. That is not a bug. That is the design.

The timeline is even more direct: the reporter blew nearly $4,500 on tennis in 24 hours, set limits on time and money, and 30 minutes later got the first of four push notifications promoting new bets. The tools are for show. The push notifications are for revenue. Only a stricter cool-off stopped them. Responsible gaming is a manual written by the company, not by a regulator.

DraftKings president of operations Paul Liberman told regulators: "We do not use artificial intelligence to target losing gamblers by any means." The New York Times reported that DraftKings used machine learning and AI to target promotions to losing bettors. Both statements cannot be true.

Ohio proposed an amendment barring direct marketing for 48 hours after a user opts into responsible gaming tools. The rule speaks directly to what ProPublica found. But Keith Whyte, a former director of the National Council on Problem Gambling, warned it could discourage users from using tools like deposit limits because using them implies you have a problem. He is right about the incentive, and that is the point: the tools have been designed as a badge of shame, not a shield.

At the Louisiana meeting, DraftKings' chief responsible gaming officer, Lori Kalani, said 175 accounts in the state had been shut down this year. She said she did not know how many total users DraftKings had in Louisiana. She wished the number were zero. 175 is a numerator with no denominator. That is the arithmetic of complicity: give a number, withhold the rate.

At the federal level, three Democratic lawmakers sent letters demanding data on VIP programs. Letters are pressure, not verdicts. New York's attorney general's investigators contacted former DraftKings employees. Maine, Massachusetts and Michigan say they are reviewing. Everyone is moving. Nothing has landed.

The gap actually closed is small: a license cut from five years to four months, a rule still in hearings. Direct violence has not decreased. Structural violence has not budged. Cultural violence is still gilding addiction with the word "responsible."

175 accounts were closed. How many people in that state are being rewarded by the VIP system for losing more, the report does not say. That is the hole.
