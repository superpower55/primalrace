被遗忘的战争，从未停止的暴力Forgotten War, Persistent Violence

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-06 § 链接
战争的终结只是叙事的切换，而物理层面的暴力具有漫长的半衰期。
The end of war is merely a shift in narrative; physical violence has a long half-life.

这篇报道在讲述一个关于“排爆艺术”的专业故事，但其底色是关于 structural violence 的残酷注脚。人们习惯于将二战视为历史书上的一个章节，但在英国的泥土和海床下，数以万计的未爆弹成了某种物理性的“幽灵”。这种状态完美诠释了加尔通的暴力三角：即便直接暴力（战争）在 81 年前停止了，但其留下的结构性威胁依然在 2026 年地雷般地潜伏在人们的家门口。

最讽刺的是，这些暴力在现代社会被转化为一种“专业博弈”。排爆队员在泥泞中通过 X 光机和听诊器与 80 年前的死亡进行对话，而保险公司则在法庭上通过“战争行为” (act of war) 的定义权来决定谁该为损失买单。当一个 250kg 的炸弹被吊起时，人们争论的是财务责任和法律条款，而非这枚炸弹本身代表的、那个试图抹除整个街道的元暴力。这种将死亡威胁“行政化”和“专业化”的过程，实际上是一种 cultural violence，它让我们在某种程度上习惯了这种潜伏的危险，将其视为一种需要被管理的技术问题，而非一个尚未被偿还的血债。

那些被排爆队员戏称为“宝可梦卡片”的炸弹，本质上是原初种族被殖民和掠夺历史的微缩版——强权者在天空投下毁灭，而代价则由几代之后、甚至从未参与过战争的平民在日常生活中分担。当炸弹在海中发出轻微的“plop”一声时，差额被缩小了，但这仅仅是 direct 层的局部清除。只要这些战争遗产继续定义着城市扩张的风险，只要法律依然在用“战争”来逃避赔偿，这种结构性的暴力就从未真正结束。

This report presents a professional tale of 'defusing art,' but its undertone is a brutal footnote to structural violence. We are accustomed to treating WWII as a closed chapter in history books, yet thousands of unexploded bombs beneath British soil and seabeds act as physical 'ghosts.' This perfectly illustrates Galtung's Violence Triangle: while direct violence ended 81 years ago, the structural threat remains, lurking like landmines in people's backyards in 2026.

The irony lies in how this violence is converted into a 'professional game' in modern society. EOD technicians engage in a dialogue with 80-year-old death via X-rays and stethoscopes, while insurance companies fight in court over the definition of an 'act of war' to evade payouts. As a 250kg bomb is hoisted, the debate shifts to financial liability and legal clauses, ignoring the meta-violence that once sought to erase entire streets. This process of 'administratizing' and 'professionalizing' death is a form of cultural violence; it conditions us to treat latent danger as a technical issue to be managed rather than an unpaid debt of blood.

These bombs, jokingly referred to by operators as 'Pokémon cards,' are microcosms of the history of the Primal Race—where the powerful drop destruction from the sky, and the cost is borne by civilians generations later. When the bomb finally goes off with a faint 'plop' in the sea, the gap between Potential and Actual is narrowed, but only at the direct level. As long as these war legacies define the risks of urban expansion and laws use 'war' as a shield for liability, the structural violence has never truly ceased.
