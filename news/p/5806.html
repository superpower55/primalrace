两亿五的冻结，谁的损失$2.5bn Frozen, Whose Loss

科技 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-30 § 链接
一笔钱被冻住了，但谁在付代价没人说。
The money is frozen. Who pays is not in the story.

币安说用户资金"完全安全"，同时稳定币发行方冻掉了二十五亿美元。这两句话放在一起，等于承认了一件事：钱在链上流动的时候，安全从来不是用户的安全，是发行方和交易所的安全。

冻结这个动作值得多看两眼。Tether 冻了十五亿多，Circle 冻了八亿多，两家加起来就是二十五亿四千万。它们能在几小时之内锁住这么大体量的资产，说明这套稳定币体系从来不是去中心化的——它有一个中心，中心知道每一笔钱在哪里，随时可以按下去。去中心化是卖给散户的叙事，是第三章那种认知入口：让你以为你掌握了自己的资产，实际上你掌握的是一个可以被别人一键冻结的地址。

赵长鹏的发言最诚实。他说"用户资金安全"，又说"作为股东，我希望所有用户资金都会被完全覆盖"。前一句是公告，后一句是博弈。股东希望覆盖，是因为不覆盖股东要赔；用户安不安全，取决于股东愿不愿意赔、赔得起多少。报道没写这二十五亿里有多少是散户的钱，没写冻结之后谁拿得回来、多久拿得回来。这些缺席的名字，就是这条新闻真正的结构。

再看赵长鹏这个人。2023 年他承认了一项未能阻止平台洗钱的联邦重罪，辞去 CEO；2025 年被特朗普赦免。一个因为反洗钱失职被定罪的创始人，现在以股东身份出来安抚用户，说资金会覆盖。这套安排里，被定罪的是个人，被赦免的是个人，被冻住的是用户的钱，被追责的没有一个是用户能碰到的东西。这就是共谋的样子：规则对制定者是可协商的，对存钱进去的人是硬的。

比特币跌了 3.2%，跌破七万九。价格波动是这条新闻里唯一被公开定价的代价，而它恰恰是最不重要的那部分——跌了会涨回来，被冻住的钱不一定。

Binance says user funds are "completely safe" while stablecoin issuers froze $2.5bn. Put those two sentences together and you get the real admission: when money moves on-chain, safety was never the user's safety. It's the issuer's and the exchange's.

The freeze deserves a second look. Tether locked up over $1.5bn, Circle over $800m, and together that's $2.54bn within hours. Two companies able to freeze that much that fast tells you the stablecoin system was never decentralized. There is a center. The center knows where every dollar sits and can press a button. Decentralization is the story sold to retail — a cognitive entry point of the kind that makes you believe you hold your own assets when what you hold is an address someone else can switch off.

Zhao is the most honest voice here. He says user funds are safe, then says that as a shareholder he expects all user funds will be fully covered. The first is a press release. The second is a game. Shareholders want coverage because uncovered losses land on them. Whether users are safe depends on whether shareholders choose to pay and how much they can afford. The report never says how much of that $2.5bn belongs to ordinary depositors, who gets their money back, or when. Those missing names are the actual structure of this story.

Then there's Zhao himself. In 2023 he pleaded guilty to a felony for failing to prevent money laundering, stepped down as CEO, and in 2025 was pardoned by Trump. A founder convicted of anti-money-laundering failure now appears as a shareholder to reassure users the funds will be covered. In this arrangement the individual gets convicted, the individual gets pardoned, the users' money gets frozen, and nothing users can touch is ever held accountable. That is what co-conspiracy looks like: rules are negotiable for the people who write them and hard for the people who deposit.

Bitcoin fell 3.2% to under $79,000. The price move is the only cost in this story that gets publicly priced, and it's the least important part. Prices recover. Frozen funds may not.
