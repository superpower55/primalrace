用分红掩盖的结构性掠夺Structural Plunder Masked by Dividends

国际 结构层 · 文化层 Reuters ↗ 2026-10-21 § 链接
用金钱买断反对权，是典型的将结构暴力转化为商业交易的scam。
Buying off dissent with cash is a classic scam that converts structural violence into a commercial transaction.

特朗普提出的所谓“分红”（dividend）逻辑极其简单：如果社区不喜欢数据中心，那就给他们钱。这根本不是在解决问题，而是在给结构性暴力贴上一个“交易”的标签。数据中心带来的能耗、水资源抢占以及对当地生态的破坏，是典型的 structural violence，而特朗普试图用短期财务激励将其转化为一种“共谋” (complicity)。

这种叙事极其危险，它在定义一种新的“最优解表达”：只要价格合适，任何被剥夺的权利都可以被定价。当一个社区接受了分红，他们就从受害者变成了既得利益者，从而在潜意识中完成了自我规训——他们不再讨论环境正义，而是在讨论分红额度。这就是典型的武器化表达，通过制造一个“财务方案”来堵住认知入口，让人们忘记数据中心本质上是对公共资源的掠夺。

谁在共谋？科技巨头通过支付少量分红换取低廉的资源准入和政治通行证；政客通过扮演“交易撮合者”获取选票；而部分社区成员在短期利益面前出卖了长期的生存环境。这种“金融交易”让直接暴力（环境破坏）在文化层面上变得“合理”且“受欢迎”。

最讽刺的是，这种方案将公共议题私有化。当“受欢迎”程度取决于银行账户的数字，所谓的民主协商就变成了纯粹的定价博弈。这不仅没有缩小 Potential 和 Actual 之间的差额，反而通过金钱完成了对反对声音的精准清除。

Trump's proposed "dividend" logic is brutally simple: if a community dislikes data centers, pay them. This isn't problem-solving; it's slapping a "deal" label on structural violence. The energy consumption, water depletion, and ecological disruption caused by data centers are textbook examples of structural violence, which Trump attempts to transform into a form of complicity through short-term financial incentives.

This narrative is dangerous because it defines a fake optimal expression: the idea that any stripped right can be priced if the offer is high enough. Once a community accepts the dividend, they shift from victims to beneficiaries, completing a process of internalised discipline. They stop discussing environmental justice and start discussing payout ratios. This is the weaponisation of expression—using a "financial deal" to hijack the cognitive entry point and erase the fact that data centers are essentially plundering public resources.

Who are the co-conspirators? Tech giants buy cheap resource access and political passes with minor payouts; politicians gain votes by playing the "dealmaker"; and some community members trade long-term survival for immediate cash. This "financial transaction" makes direct violence (environmental ruin) appear "reasonable" and even "popular" at the cultural layer.

The irony is the privatisation of public discourse. When "popularity" is determined by bank balances, democratic deliberation becomes a mere pricing game. This doesn't close the gap between Potential and Actual; it uses money to precisely eliminate the capacity for dissent.
