BP的“去情感化”：一场关于资本定价权的权力游戏BP's 'Unsentimental' Pivot: A Power Game of Capital Pricing

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-04 § 链接
所谓的“去情感化”不过是资本在剥离低效共谋时的冷酷定价权。
So-called 'unsentimentality' is merely the cold exercise of pricing power when capital sheds inefficient complicity.

BP新任CEO Meg O’Neill 抛出的“unsentimental”（去情感化）叙事，是一次典型的认知入口操纵。她将资产剥离描述为一种理性的、剔除“情感”与“历史”的商业决策，试图让外界相信这是一种纯粹的效率博弈。但事实上，无论是抛售北海油气业务，还是将前任在“绿色转型”时代高价买入的Archaea再次推向市场，这本质上是资本在进行一次残酷的定价权重置。

有趣的是，这种“冷酷”在面对政治博弈时迅速切换成了“实用主义”的武器化表达。O’Neill在建议英国政府维持北海油气开发时，迅速地将叙事锚定在“就业”和“税收”这些典型的结构性共谋筹码上。她一边用“不具竞争力”为由抛弃资产，一边用“能源安全”诱导政府降低风盈税（windfall tax）。这哪里是去情感化？这分明是最高级别的共谋艺术：用市场的冷酷来向投资者表忠心，用国家的焦虑来向立法者讨价还价。

而这场资本游戏的背景，是伊朗战争带来的油价暴涨。这种由直接暴力（Direct Violence）驱动的利润 bonanza，被BP通过结构层（Structural Layer）的资产优化，转化为股东的潜在红利。所谓的“战略混乱”被迅速清理，其实就是将战争带来的血腥红利，通过一套“理性管理”的文化叙事进行洗白。资本从不关心历史，它只关心在当前的元暴力结构中，如何通过重新定义“竞争力”来最大化自己的存在性收益。

BP's new CEO Meg O’Neill is deploying 'unsentimental' as a weaponized narrative to control the cognitive entry point. By framing the pruning of portfolios as a decision stripped of 'sentiment' and 'history,' she attempts to mask a brutal reset of pricing power. Whether it is the disposal of North Sea assets or the dumping of Archaea—a prized acquisition from the brief 'green-tinged' era—this is not about rationality, but about the cold calculation of capital.

Crucially, this 'coldness' pivots instantly into pragmatic weaponization when dealing with political power. O’Neill’s advice to the UK government regarding North Sea oil is a masterclass in structural complicity, anchoring her argument in 'jobs' and 'tax revenue.' She uses 'lack of competitiveness' to justify asset sales to investors, while simultaneously using 'energy security' to lobby for the reduction of the windfall tax. This is not the absence of sentiment; it is the highest form of the art of complicity: performing coldness for the City while exploiting national anxiety for the state.

Underpinning this entire game is the profit bonanza triggered by the Iran war. The direct violence of conflict is processed through the structural layer of 'portfolio optimization' to be converted into shareholder dividends. The so-called 'strategic muddle' is being cleared not for the sake of the planet, but to scrub the blood off the profits using a cultural narrative of 'rational management.' Capital never cares about history; it only cares about how to redefine 'competitiveness' within the existing meta-violence to maximize its existential gain.
