被定义的“恶心”与被剥夺的解释权Defined as 'Disgusting': The Erasure of Interpretive Power

哲学 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-05 § 链接
审美偏见是结构性暴力的前哨，定义权决定了谁能生存。
Aesthetic prejudice is the outpost of structural violence; the power to define determines survival.

大多数人看到蟑螂的第一反应是厌恶，这种厌恶被内化为一种生物本能，但实际上它是典型的 cultural violence。当一个物种被贴上“vile”或“unsightly”的标签，它就失去了在公共讨论中作为“生命”的合法性。这种通过定义“恶心”来剥夺解释权的机制，与父权制定义女性为“客体”或“附属品”的逻辑完全同构：先通过审美和叙事将其非人化，然后理直气壮地实施直接暴力（杀戮）或结构性忽视（不给保护资金）。

Cristian Lucañas 的工作本质上是在进行一场关于认知入口的夺回战。他通过将新物种命名为 Tolkien 或 Pokémon 角色，实际上是在利用大众认知的“文化符号”来对冲原有的“厌恶符号”。这是一种聪明的博弈，用一种被认可的、具有趣味性的表达方式，强行在人们的认知中为蟑螂开辟一个非“害虫”的席位。这证明了表达如何能成为制造可能性的武器——当你改变了称呼，你就在改变这个物种在人类社会中的存在性价值。

最讽刺的是，人们认为蟑螂“不可摧毁”，却在潜意识里共谋着通过破坏栖息地和全球变暖将它们推向灭绝。这种“强大”的叙事掩盖了它们在结构层面的脆弱。当一个群体（无论是昆虫还是原初种族）被定义为“耐操”或“天生如此”时，这种定义往往成为施暴者减轻罪恶感、正当化剥削的完美掩体。所谓的“害虫”定义，不过是人类为了维持自身统治地位而制造的认知 scam。

Most people react to cockroaches with instinctive loathing, but this is a classic form of cultural violence. When a species is labeled as 'vile' or 'unsightly,' it is stripped of its legitimacy as a 'living being' in public discourse. This mechanism—using the definition of 'disgust' to seize interpretive power—is perfectly isomorphic to how the patriarchy defines women as 'objects' or 'appendages': first dehumanize them through aesthetics and narratives, then justify direct violence or structural neglect.

Cristian Lucañas’s work is essentially a war of recovery over the cognitive entrance. By naming new species after Tolkien or Pokémon characters, he leverages recognized 'cultural symbols' to offset the existing 'loathing symbols.' This is a clever game of expression, using a sanctioned, playful narrative to force a non-pest seat for cockroaches in the human mind. It proves how expression can be weaponized to manufacture possibilities—when you change the name, you change the existential value of the species.

The ultimate irony is that while humans perceive cockroaches as 'indestructible,' they collectively engage in a complicity of habitat destruction and global warming that pushes them toward extinction. The narrative of 'toughness' masks their structural vulnerability. When a group—be it insects or the Primal Race—is defined as 'resilient' or 'naturally so,' that definition often serves as a perfect shield for the oppressor to alleviate guilt and justify exploitation. The label 'pest' is nothing more than a cognitive scam designed to maintain the dominance of the definer.
