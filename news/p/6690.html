先宣布选举无效Declare the Election Rigged First

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 WIRED ↗ 2026-11-02 § 链接
新闻摘要中期选举前，美国司法部向全部50州选举官员发函，警告若不保存选举记录22天将面临刑事追责；此前该部已在加州、阿拉斯加、内华达、华盛顿等地提起诉讼、索取选民名单、要求封存选票，多数被法院驳回。报道称这些行动缺乏证据支撑，目的是为质疑选举结果预设伏笔。
The newsBefore the midterms, the Justice Department sent letters to election officials in all 50 states warning of criminal action if records are not preserved for 22 days. It has sued or demanded voter data in California, Alaska, Nevada and Washington; courts have rejected most of these moves, which the report says are meant to pre-load doubts about the results.
他们不是在赢选举，是在预留一个「选举被偷了」的剧本。
They are not trying to win the election. They are pre-booking the script for when it is stolen.

先看清这件事在做什么：不是在赢选举，是在预留一个「选举被偷了」的剧本。

司法部向全部50州发函，威胁选举官员不保存记录就刑事追责；在加州要求封存约两万选民的邮寄选票，被联邦法官以措辞严厉的命令驳回，随即上诉，又被驳回；在阿拉斯加起诉，想阻止民主党候选人 Mary Peltola 胜选结果被认证，被法官以可能侵犯秘密投票权为由驳回，几个小时后再次上诉。这些诉讼自己也知道赢不了。UCLA 法学院的 Shelby Wayment 说得直白：现在起诉，是知道几乎没机会赢，输了之后就可以宣称选举舞弊。

这就是第三章的机制：夺取解释权，夺取「什么是事实」的制造权。武器不是选票，是叙事——先制造一个「非公民大规模投票」的事实，再让这个事实在败选那天派上用场。内华达那组数字最能说明这套东西怎么运作：国土安全部先说该州有一万五千多名非公民选民，被追问后承认没有证据，数字降到185，州副检察长回信说这些人的记录显示是美国公民，「你们没有提供任何实质性的东西」。数字从一万五掉到185，靠的不是调查，是撤回。可撤回不登上头条，一万五登上头条。

第四层也在动。司法部发言人回应时搬出上一届政府在大选前起诉 Alabama 和 Virginia 的旧例，前司法部律师 David Becker 的对照更狠：司法部有明文规则禁止在选举临近时进行这类调查，而 Todd Blanche 2024年9月替他的当事人 Donald Trump 辩护时，引用的正是这条规则。同一套规则，替客户用时是盾，自己用时就不是了。所谓「遵循事实和法律，不遵循任何人的政治时间表」，这句话本身就是元暴力最标准的伪装：把一方的操作说成中立程序。

真正被削掉的东西也很清楚。Brennan Center 的统计是，自2025年上任以来已有34个联邦地区法院作出不利于 Trump 的裁决，挡住了选民名单的获取和行政令的执行。David Becker 说「我们的系统撑住了，我们的制度撑住了」。这是 structural 层的一次真实防守，不是气氛上的安慰——法院一次一次地驳回，选票才没被扣下。但撑住不等于没代价：加州那两万个名字已经被交出去、被分析、被反诉 contempt，Alaska 州长下令调查 Peltola，还说调查没结束就不承诺认证她的胜利；选举官员在投票前几周被一封封刑事威胁的信钉在办公桌前。防守是要人付工时的。

Levitt 说这些动作大部分是「营销」，是展示力量，因为联邦政府根本不管选举，管的是州和地方。这话对，但营销不是无害的。营销的目的不是这一次赢，是让足够多的人在下一次不再相信计票。Potter County 共和党那起要求全州手工计票的诉讼，理由是 AI 能黑进任何投票机——没有证据，Levitt 说这官司「nuts」，还希望它是用 Comic Sans 打在汉堡王餐垫背面递上去的。好笑，但笑完要问：这种官司为什么有人递，为什么有人接。

34次败诉是制度在动。可制度的另一头，是一整套已经铺好的说法在等结果。差额没有被完全堵上——被驳回的诉讼可以再上诉，被撤回的数字已经进了某些人的记忆。

Look at what this actually does: it is not an attempt to win an election, it is an attempt to pre-book the script for when the election is stolen.

The Justice Department sent letters to election officials in all 50 states threatening criminal action if records are not preserved. In California it sought to sequester mail-in ballots tied to roughly 20,000 voters; a federal judge rejected the request in a strongly worded order, the DOJ appealed, and the appeal was quickly denied. In Alaska it sued to block certification if Democrat Mary Peltola wins; a judge threw the suit out, citing Alaskans' right to a secret ballot, and hours later the DOJ asked an appeals court to overturn that ruling. These suits know they lose. Shelby Wayment of UCLA Law puts it plainly: they file now knowing they have almost no chance of winning, so that afterward they can claim the election was fraudulent.

This is the machinery from Chapter 3: seize the interpretative gate, seize the power to manufacture what counts as fact. The weapon is not the ballot, it is the narrative — build a "mass noncitizen voting" fact first, then deploy it on the night the results come in. Nevada shows the mechanism best. DHS claimed 15,903 noncitizens on the voter rolls, then conceded to state officials it had no evidence; the number fell to 185, and the state's deputy attorney general wrote back that records show those people are citizens and "you have provided nothing of substance to suggest otherwise." Fifteen thousand became 185 not through investigation but through retraction. Retractions do not make headlines. Fifteen thousand does.

The fourth layer is moving too. The DOJ spokesperson answers by citing the previous administration suing Alabama and Virginia before the 2024 election. David Becker's comparison cuts deeper: the department has written rules barring investigative activity close to an election — the same rules Todd Blanche cited in September 2024 when representing his client, Donald Trump. The same rule is a shield for a client and no obstacle for the department. "Following the facts and the law, not anyone's political timetable" is meta-violence in its standard costume: one side's operation dressed as neutral procedure.

What has genuinely been blocked is also clear. The Brennan Center counts 34 federal district courts ruling against Trump since 2025, blocking access to voter rolls and stopping executive orders. Becker says the systems held, the institutions held. That is a real structural defense, not a mood — the courts kept the ballots from being seized. But holding is not free. California's 20,000 names were handed over, analyzed, and the DOJ is now urging a judge to consider contempt against the group that analyzed them. Alaska's governor opened an investigation into Peltola and said he cannot commit to certifying her victory while it runs. Election officials spend the weeks before the vote answering letters that threaten them with prosecution. Defense is paid in someone's hours.

Levitt calls most of this marketing, a show of force, because the federal government does not run elections — states and localities do. True, and marketing is not harmless. Its purpose is not to win this time; it is to leave enough people doubting the count next time. The Potter County GOP suit demanding a hand count of all Texas ballots cites AI hacking any voting machine undetected, with no evidence; Levitt calls it "nuts" and wishes it had been filed in Comic Sans on the back of a Burger King placemat. Funny, but ask why such a suit gets filed and why anyone takes it up.

Thirty-four losses are the system working. On the other side of the system, a fully built story is waiting for the results. The gap is not closed — rejected suits get appealed, retracted numbers stay in memory.
