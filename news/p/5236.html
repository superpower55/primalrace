巨轮的航向与两个强人的共谋The Giant Ship and the Complicity of Strongmen

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-24 § 链接
外交辞令是最高级的武器化表达，旨在用叙事掩盖结构性掠夺。
Diplomatic rhetoric is the ultimate weaponized expression, designed to mask structural predation with narratives.

这场会晤是典型的 Meta-violence 演出。习用“修昔底德陷阱”这种学术词汇给权力博弈贴金，将地缘政治的零和游戏包装成一种可以被“沟通”化解的误会。而特朗普则用“伟大的友谊”这种极具个人主义色彩的叙事，试图将国家间的结构性冲突简化为两个强人之间的私人交情。这本质上是一场关于“认知入口”的争夺：他们试图向世界定义，中美关系的定调权不在于贸易数据或AI芯片的禁令，而在于两个男人的握手。

注意这种极其典型的 Masculine-centric narrative：两个男性领导人在白宫的草坪上，在国歌和仪仗队的陪衬下，讨论着如何操纵“巨轮”的航向。在这种叙事中，具体的个体——无论是被关税影响的农民，还是在AI军备竞赛中被异化的程序员——全部被客体化为背景板。所谓的“互利共赢”其实是共谋者的术语，意味着两个顶级掠夺者在达成某种暂时性的利益分配协议，以确保他们各自在内部统治的稳定性。

最讽刺的是，当他们大谈“和平共处”时，结构层 (structural layer) 的暴力正在加速。芯片禁令、贸易壁垒、AI竞争，这些 Actual 状态与 Potential 发展之间的差额，正是由这两位“好友”亲手制造的。他们用文化层 (cultural layer) 的和谐表演来对冲结构层的残酷撕裂。这种“现实不配合就重新定义现实”的机制，让外交辞令变成了最高级的 scam。

这场戏演完了，剩下的依然是那个残酷的博弈：谁在占便宜，谁在被牺牲？在这种强人政治的共谋中，真正的牺牲品永远是那些没有定义权的人。

This summit is a textbook performance of Meta-violence. Xi uses the "Thucydides Trap"—an academic label—to gild a raw power struggle, packaging a zero-sum geopolitical game as a mere misunderstanding that can be solved through "communication." Meanwhile, Trump employs a narrative of "great friendship," attempting to reduce structural international conflicts to a private rapport between two strongmen. This is essentially a battle for the cognitive entry point: they want to define the China-US relationship not by trade deficits or AI chip bans, but by the handshake of two men.

Observe the blatant Masculine-centric narrative: two male leaders on the White House lawn, flanked by anthems and guards, discussing how to steer the "giant ship." In this narrative, actual individuals—from farmers crushed by tariffs to programmers alienated by the AI arms race—are reduced to mere background scenery. The so-called "mutual gain" is simply the jargon of complicity, meaning two top predators have reached a temporary agreement on the distribution of spoils to ensure their own internal stability.

The irony is that while they preach "peaceful coexistence," structural violence is accelerating. The gap between the Actual state and the Potential development—manifested in chip bans and trade barriers—is precisely what these two "friends" have engineered. They use a cultural layer of harmonious performance to hedge against the structural layer of brutal fragmentation. This mechanism of "redefining reality when reality doesn't cooperate" turns diplomacy into the ultimate scam.

Once the performance ends, the brutal game remains: who is profiting, and who is being sacrificed? In this complicity of strongman politics, the victims are always those without the power to define the narrative.
