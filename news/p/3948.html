等待的酷刑与被定义的“悲剧”The Torture of Waiting and the Definition of Tragedy

哲学 直接层 · 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-15 § 链接
死亡是直接暴力，但将个体简化为“失踪者”叙事是文化暴力。
Death is direct violence; reducing an individual to a 'missing person' narrative is cultural violence.

一个18岁的地质学学生在罗马尼亚的山区失踪，最终变成了一具被发现的尸体。新闻的重点在于搜救队的“不懈努力”、警方的“超越职责”以及家属对这种“等待酷刑”终于结束的宽慰。在这种标准的新闻叙事里，这被定义为一场自然灾害导致的意外悲剧，一个关于勇气、遗憾与救赎的 closed loop。

但如果用加尔通的暴力三角来看，这里的 Violence = Potential − Actual。一个18岁青年本可拥有的未来与他最终成为山间一具尸体的差额，就是最残酷的直接暴力。而文化层面的暴力则在于，媒体迅速将这个生命物化为一个“案例”。他被简化为“失踪的英国徒步者”，他的死亡成为了验证搜救机制、警政合作和家庭韧性的背景板。这种叙事抹去了他作为个体的具体存在，将其转化为一种关于“风险”与“救援”的公共认知素材。

最讽刺的是家属提到的“等待的酷刑终于结束”。当一个生命在结构性的孤独和极端的自然环境中被缓慢吞噬时，系统提供的是一种事后的、程序性的安慰。这种安慰是 structural violence 的一种温柔伪装：它告诉我们，只要程序运行了，只要搜救队出动了，只要警察在支持，那么这个结果就是“不可避免”的。在这种逻辑下，个体的消失被合理化为自然力的胜利，而制度的失效则被掩盖在“全力以赴”的措辞之中。

An 18-year-old geology student vanishes in the Romanian mountains, eventually becoming a recovered corpse. The news focuses on the 'tireless' rescue efforts, the police 'going above and beyond,' and the family's relief that the 'torture of waiting' is over. In this standard narrative, it is defined as an accidental tragedy caused by nature—a closed loop of courage, regret, and closure.

But through the lens of the Violence Triangle, Violence = Potential − Actual. The gap between the future this 18-year-old could have had and his final state as a body in the wild is the most brutal form of direct violence. Simultaneously, cultural violence occurs when the media objectifies this life into a 'case.' He is reduced to a 'missing British hiker,' and his death becomes a backdrop to validate rescue mechanisms, police cooperation, and family resilience. This narrative erases his specific existence, converting him into public cognitive material about 'risk' and 'rescue.'

The most ironic part is the family's mention that the 'torture of waiting is finally over.' While a life is slowly consumed by structural loneliness and extreme environments, the system provides a post-hoc, procedural consolation. This consolation is a gentle mask for structural violence: it suggests that as long as the protocol was followed, as long as the rescuers deployed, and as long as the police supported, the outcome was 'inevitable.' Under this logic, the disappearance of an individual is rationalized as a victory of nature, while systemic failure is hidden behind the phrasing of 'doing everything possible.'
