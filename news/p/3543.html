瑞典的“法治”是最高级的暴力Swedish 'Rule of Law' as the Ultimate Violence

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-10 § 链接
当制度将“合理理由”的解释权垄断，法律就成了清除异己的武器。
When the state monopolizes the interpretation of 'reasonable grounds,' law becomes a weapon for purging.

一个 78 岁的寡妇，在瑞典生活了 21 年，却因为一份申请表提交太晚被要求驱逐。瑞典移民局的逻辑极其简单：不知道要申请不叫“合理理由”。这种冷酷的 apathetic 并非个案，数据证明瑞典是欧盟中 Brexit 驱逐率最高、拒绝率最离谱的国家——27.5% 的拒绝率，是欧盟平均水平的 7 倍以上。

这是一个典型的 structural violence 案例。在加尔通的暴力三角里，最阴险的暴力不是直接的殴打，而是将权力通过“程序”和“规则”合法化。瑞典当局通过垄断对“reasonable grounds”（合理理由）的定义权，将行政效率凌驾于人的基本存在之上。对于一个丧偶、高龄、生活重心完全在当地的女性来说，被告知“不符合程序”就得滚蛋，这本质上是对她主体性的彻底抹除。

更讽刺的是，Joyce Thomas 提到的细节揭示了深层的 gender 陷阱：她丈夫生前负责家里所有的 administration（行政事务），这种分工是典型的父权结构共谋。当丈夫这个“行政接口”死亡，她面对的不再是温馨的邻里，而是一堵冰冷的、由男性主导的官僚主义生物墙。她失去了那个能与系统对话的代理人，于是她在这个系统眼中变成了可被随意丢弃的冗余数据。

英国外交部和议员的“深感担忧”不过是表演性的 PR。当 2500 人被驱逐，这已经不是某个个体的不幸，而是一次制度性的清洗。所谓的“文明”和“理性”在此时成了最完美的掩体，让驱逐一个老人回家面对坟墓的行为，看起来像是一次正义的“依法办事”。

A 78-year-old widow, after 21 years in Sweden, is being deported because an application was 'too late.' The Swedish Migration Agency's logic is brutally simple: ignorance of the requirement is not a 'reasonable ground.' This isn't an isolated incident; it's a systemic pattern. With a refusal rate of 27.5%—seven times the EU average—Sweden has turned Brexit bureaucracy into a machine for removal.

This is a textbook case of structural violence. In Galtung's Violence Triangle, the most insidious violence isn't the physical blow, but the legalization of power through 'procedure' and 'rules.' By monopolizing the definition of 'reasonable grounds,' the Swedish authorities prioritize administrative rigidity over human existence. For a widowed elderly woman whose entire life is anchored there, being told she is 'procedurally invalid' is a total erasure of her subjectivity.

Crucially, the gendered trap here is glaring: Joyce Thomas noted her husband handled all the 'administration' in their household—a classic complicity within the patriarchal structure. When her husband, the 'administrative interface,' died, she was left facing a cold, masculine-centric bureaucratic wall. She lost her proxy to negotiate with the system, and thus, she became redundant data to be deleted.

The 'deep concern' expressed by the UK FCDO is mere performative PR. When 2,500 people are ordered to leave, it is no longer about individual misfortune, but a systemic purge. 'Civilization' and 'rationality' here serve as a cover, making the act of throwing an old woman away from her husband's grave look like a just execution of the law.
