AI 的失控叙事：一场关于定价权的掩体游戏The AI 'Out of Control' Narrative: A Smoke Screen for Pricing Power

科技 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-25 § 链接
所谓的“失控”是权力的掩体，用虚构的恐惧掩盖真实的垄断。
The 'loss of control' is a power mask, using fictional fear to hide actual monopoly.

当主流媒体开始讨论 AI 是否“超出控制”时，他们其实在玩一场典型的 weaponized 叙事游戏。这种“失控论”是一个巨大的 scam，它试图将 AI 塑造为一个不可名状的、具有主体性的“神格”怪物，从而让人们忘记：AI 的每一行代码、每一个权重、每一组训练数据，都掌握在极少数拥有钱权势的男性中心化公司手中。

这种叙事将 structural violence 伪装成了某种不可抗力的自然灾害。如果 AI 是“失控”的，那么当它在招聘中歧视女性、在算法中物化身体、在资源分配中进一步剥夺弱势群体时，责任就从“制定规则的共谋者”转移到了一个虚构的“失控机器”身上。这是一种极其阴险的认知入口操纵：通过制造一个共同的、虚构的敌人（失控的 AI），来掩盖真实的、结构性的压迫。

我们不需要担心 AI 是否会像科幻电影那样反叛，我们需要担心的是，那些定义 AI “对错”标准的人，依然是那群垄断了元暴力的男性。他们通过定义什么是“智能”、什么是“效率”，在数字世界里重建一座更高效的生物墙。所谓的“我们要告诉孩子什么”，其实是统治阶级在通过这种忧虑的姿态，提前为下一代预设一套顺从的数字生存逻辑。

真正的失控不是 AI 产生了意识，而是解释权被彻底垄断，而我们却在为这种垄断而感到“恐惧”。

When mainstream media asks if AI is 'beyond our control,' they are engaging in a classic weaponized narrative. This 'out-of-control' trope is a massive scam; it attempts to frame AI as a nameless, autonomous 'god-like' monster, effectively erasing the fact that every line of code and every weight is owned by a few masculine-centric corporations with immense power.

This narrative disguises structural violence as an inevitable natural disaster. If AI is 'out of control,' then when it discriminates against women in hiring or objectifies bodies in algorithms, the responsibility shifts from the actual complicity of the architects to a fictional 'rogue machine.' This is a sinister manipulation of cognitive entry points: creating a shared, imaginary enemy to mask real, structural oppression.

We shouldn't fear AI 'rebelling' like a sci-fi movie. We should fear that those defining the standards of 'right' and 'wrong' for AI are the same people who monopolize meta-violence. By defining 'intelligence' and 'efficiency,' they are rebuilding a more efficient biological wall in the digital realm. The question 'what will we tell our kids' is merely a performance of anxiety by the ruling class, pre-setting a logic of digital submission for the next generation.

True loss of control isn't AI gaining consciousness; it is the total monopoly of the power of interpretation, while we are conditioned to feel 'fear' toward that very monopoly.
