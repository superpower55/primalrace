霍尔木兹海峡的“治安管理”与男性叙事的血祭The 'Policing' of Hormuz and the Blood Sacrifice of Masculine Narrative

国际 直接层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-30 § 链接
战争是最高形式的男性中心叙事：将肉身降格为筹码，将屠杀包装成秩序。
War is the ultimate masculine-centric narrative: degrading flesh into chips and packaging slaughter as order.

美国在拉拉克岛的这次打击，本质上是一次典型的 masculine-centric narrative（男性中心叙事）的权力操演。在五角大楼和白宫的叙事里，这叫“清除威胁”或“维护国际航道安全”，但如果套用加尔通的暴力三角，这就是最赤裸的 direct violence（直接暴力）。

这场已经持续六个月的战争，其实是一场巨大的 meta violence（元暴力）实验。无论是美国的“自由与秩序”，还是伊朗革命卫队的“响应与惩罚”，双方都在使用同一套武器化的表达：将士兵和平民的肉身降格为可消耗的工具，用来交换某种宏大的政治筹码。在这种叙事中，死亡被赋予了“光荣”或“牺牲”的标签，从而掩盖了结构性的残暴。

最讽刺的是，这种战争叙事总是习惯于在 cultural layer（文化层）上进行伪装。美国通过掌控认知入口，将海峡的封锁定义为伊朗的挑衅，而将自己的轰炸定义为“治安管理”。这种定义权（Definition Power）的争夺，远比导弹本身更具杀伤力。它让全球观众在潜意识中接受了一个设定：只要是为了某种“更高的秩序”，数以千计的流离失所和死亡就是可以接受的 collateral damage（附带损伤）。

这场博弈没有真正的最优解，因为参与者全部是共谋者。他们共谋维持一套“强权即真理”的逻辑，在这种逻辑下，真正的 human rights（人权）被排在战略利益之后。当人们在讨论油价和航道时，那些被炸碎的身体成了叙事中被抹除的噪音。

The US strike on Larak Island is a textbook performance of masculine-centric narrative. In the lexicon of the Pentagon and the White House, this is called "neutralizing threats" or "securing international waters," but through the lens of the Violence Triangle, it is pure direct violence.

This six-month conflict is essentially a massive experiment in meta violence. Whether it is the American "freedom and order" or the IRGC's "response and punishment," both sides employ the same weaponized expression: degrading the bodies of soldiers and civilians into consumable tools to exchange for grand political leverage. In this narrative, death is rebranded as "glory" or "sacrifice" to mask the underlying structural brutality.

Most cynically, this war is camouflaged at the cultural layer. By controlling the cognitive entry points, the US defines the blockade as Iranian provocation and its own bombing as "policing." This struggle for Definition Power is far more lethal than the missiles themselves. It conditions the global audience to accept a premise: that thousands of displaced and dead are merely acceptable collateral damage in the pursuit of a "higher order."

There is no true optimal expression here because all participants are complicitors. They conspire to maintain a logic where "might makes right," a logic where actual human rights are subordinated to strategic interests. While the world discusses oil prices and shipping lanes, the shattered bodies become the erased noise of the narrative.
