扮演‘坏女人’的红利与主体性的献祭The Dividend of Playing the 'Bad Woman' and the Sacrifice of Subjectivity

性别 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-03 § 链接
当女性通过扮演‘施暴者’获得权力时，她依然在共谋元暴力的剧本。
When a woman gains power by playing the 'oppressor', she is still complicit in the meta-violence script.

Claudia Winkleman 在《The Traitors》里的成功，被媒体解读为一种‘坏主持人’ (bad host) 的艺术。从表面看，这像是一次权力的反转：一个女性不再扮演支持性的、安慰性的角色，而是成了冷酷的、带有轻微虐待倾向的掌控者。在这种叙事里，观众看到的是一个女性在公共空间中夺取了‘审判权’。

但如果用存在性战争的视角来看，这不过是一次精准的‘假.最优解表达’。Winkleman 并没有打破任何结构，她只是在父权制认可的‘坏女人’ (the bad woman) 剧本里找到了一个高薪的席位。这种‘坏’是表演性的，是被制作组 (producers) 授权的，更是被观众消费的。她通过扮演一个‘略带哥特风的施暴者’，在男性中心叙事的框架内获得了一种‘特许的权力’。

最讽刺的是，这种扮演导致了她主体性的进一步萎缩。正如文中提到的，当她尝试回归‘友好对话’的脱口秀主持人角色时，她失败了。因为系统已经将她定价为‘那个能把人赶进棺材的坏女人’。当一个女性的表达被成功地 weaponized 为某种特定的人设，她就从一个完整的人变成了这个角色的囚徒。她赢得了流量和名声，但代价是她的‘公正表达’被抹杀了——她不再是 Claudia，而是一个高效的、符合大众口味的‘权力拟像’。

这依然是一场共谋。观众在观看她‘虐待’参赛者时获得快感，而她通过出卖自己的多面性来换取顶流地位。这种权力是租赁来的，而不是赢回来的。只要她还在扮演‘坏主持人’，她就依然在维护那个‘权力必须通过压制他人来体现’的元暴力逻辑。

Claudia Winkleman's success in 'The Traitors' is framed by the media as the art of the 'bad host'. On the surface, it looks like a reversal of power: a woman no longer playing the supportive, consoling role, but instead becoming a cold, slightly sadistic controller. In this narrative, the audience sees a woman seizing the 'right to judge' in a public space.

However, through the lens of existential war, this is merely a precise 'fake optimal expression'. Winkleman hasn't broken any structure; she has simply found a high-paying seat within the patriarchal script of 'the bad woman'. This 'badness' is performative, authorized by producers, and consumed by the audience. By playing a 'goth-tinged oppressor', she acquires a 'licensed power' within the masculine-centric narrative.

The irony is that this performance leads to the further atrophy of her subjectivity. As the article notes, when she attempted to return to the role of a 'friendly chat-facilitator' in her talk show, she failed. The system has already priced her as 'the bad woman who forces people into coffins'. Once a woman's expression is successfully weaponized into a specific persona, she ceases to be a whole person and becomes a prisoner of that role. She won fame and traffic, but at the cost of her 'Just Expression'—she is no longer Claudia, but an efficient 'simulacrum of power' tailored to public taste.

This is still a form of complicity. The audience derives pleasure from watching her 'torment' contestants, while she trades her multifaceted self for top-tier celebrity status. This power is leased, not won. As long as she continues to play the 'bad host', she remains a maintainer of the meta-violence logic that suggests power must be manifested through the suppression of others.
