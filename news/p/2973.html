FIFA的“皇冠珠宝”拍卖会与欧洲的共谋快感FIFA's Crown Jewel Auction and the Euphoria of European Complicity

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-29 § 链接
用“红线”叙事掩盖利益分赃，是典型的文化暴力伪装。
Using 'red line' narratives to mask profit-sharing is a textbook case of cultural violence.

Infantino 试图将世界杯碎片化地卖给私人投资者，这本质上是一次极其粗暴的结构性掠夺。他把足球最核心的认知入口——世界之巅的解释权，直接标价出售。而这场闹剧中最讽刺的，是 UEFA（欧足联）在其中扮演的“正义之师”角色。

UEFA 习惯于在公共空间通过密集投放“红线” (red line) 这种 weaponized 词汇来 perform 自己的骨气。但翻开账本就会发现，这种所谓的“骨气”是分等级的。当面对欧洲超级联赛 (ESL) 威胁到自己的垄断地位时，UEFA 反应迅速；而面对 FIFA 的资本游戏，它在愤怒的同时，依然在与 EFC 探讨如何通过 joint venture 分一杯羹。这就是典型的共谋者逻辑：在不影响自己作为“大股东”的定价权之前，所有的愤怒都只是为了在接下来的分赃谈判中获得更高筹码的表演。

至于那些为了不成为“眼中钉”而选择顺从的足协，则是这场存在性战争中的失败者。他们将主体性让渡给了 Infantino 的金元政治，通过扮演“忠诚的下属”来换取短期生存最优解。这种 a fait accompli 的局面，正是元暴力在体育治理中的具体呈现——定义规则的人，决定谁能在这个游戏中生存，而其他人只能在“理解方案”的拖延叙事中被逐渐吞噬。

好笑的是，文中提到英足协为了 2035 年女足世界杯的主办权而迅速表态。这又是另一场经典的武器化叙事：用“女足赋权”作为掩护，去交换一个在父权结构中依然被视为“次要”的荣誉席位。在资本和权力的博弈场上，所谓的“体育精神”不过是用来掩盖 structural violence 的彩色糖衣。

Infantino’s plan to sell off chunks of the World Cup to private investors is a brutal structural plunder. He is literally putting a price tag on the ultimate cognitive entry point of football—the authority to define the world's peak.

The irony lies in UEFA's role as the 'moral guardian.' UEFA is addicted to performing 'backbone' by deploying weaponized terms like 'red line' in public spaces. However, their courage is tiered. They moved with lightning speed to block the European Super League when their own monopoly was threatened, yet they remain in a state of complicity with FIFA, exploring joint ventures through the EFC to secure a slice of the pie. This is the core of complicity: all this indignation is merely a performance to gain higher leverage in the upcoming loot-sharing negotiations.

As for the federations choosing not to be an 'eyesore,' they are the losers in this existential war. They have surrendered their subjectivity to Infantino’s plutocracy, opting for a fake optimal expression—playing the 'loyal subordinate'—to survive. This fait accompli is the manifestation of meta-violence in sports governance: those who define the rules decide who survives, while the rest are consumed by a narrative of 'seeking complete understanding.'

Most laughable is the English FA’s rush to endorse the regime to secure the 2035 Women's World Cup. This is another classic weaponization of expression: using 'women's empowerment' as a shield to trade for a seat that remains 'secondary' within the masculine-centric structure. In the game of capital and power, 'sportsmanship' is nothing more than a colorful candy coating used to hide structural violence.
