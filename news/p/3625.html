纪录片里的“真相”与被抹除的搬运工The 'Truth' in Documentaries and the Erased Porter

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-11 § 链接
所谓的“澄清事实”，往往是权力者对受害者的第二次抹除。
Setting the record straight is often just a second erasure of the victim by the powerful.

Sky Documentaries 准备播出的这部《Savage Mountain》是个典型的叙事陷阱。挪威登山家 Kristin Harila 拿到了 14 座高峰的纪录，但这个 record 的代价是一个叫 Mohammad Hassan 的搬运工（porter）被遗弃在生死边缘。现在，这部纪录片宣称要 "set the record straight"——翻译成人话就是：我们要用掌握解释权的镜头，把那场争议洗白成某种“不可抗力”或“合理选择”。

这在加尔通的暴力三角里是标准的文化暴力（cultural violence）。直接暴力是 Hassan 在高海拔的绝望，结构暴力是登山家与搬运工之间极不对等的阶级与种族关系——白人登山家购买原住民的体力来完成自己的存在性证明。而这部纪录片，则是试图通过掌控认知入口（cognitive entrance），将这种结构性的剥削包装成一个关于“勇气”和“纪录”的英雄叙事。

最讽刺的是，纪录片成了武器化的表达。当登山家通过镜头定义什么是“事实”时，那个可能已经死掉或被噤声的搬运工，在叙事中彻底失去了主体性。他不再是一个人，而成了纪录片里一个需要被“澄清”的干扰项。这种共谋极其恶心：资本、媒体与特权阶级共同地将一个底层生命的消亡，简化为一次成功的 PR 危机公关。

The upcoming 'Savage Mountain' on Sky Documentaries is a textbook narrative trap. Kristin Harila broke a world record, but the cost was a porter, Mohammad Hassan, allegedly left to die. The film claims it will "set the record straight"—which in reality means using the power of the lens to sanitize a tragedy into a tale of "unavoidable circumstances."

This is classic cultural violence within Galtung's Violence Triangle. The direct violence was Hassan's desperation at high altitude; the structural violence is the grotesque inequality between the white mountaineer and the indigenous porter, where the former purchases the latter's physical existence to validate her own existential win. Now, the documentary weaponises the cognitive entrance to rebrand structural exploitation as a heroic saga of "courage."

It is profoundly cynical. When the mountaineer defines the "facts," the silenced porter loses all subjectivity. He is no longer a human being, but a "controversy" to be managed. This is a dark complicity: capital, media, and privilege conspiring to reduce a marginalized life to a mere PR hurdle in a success story.
