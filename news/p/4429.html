气候危机与政治演员的“剧本共谋”Climate Crisis and the Scripted Complicity of Political Actors

国际 文化层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-24 § 链接
当现实已在燃烧，政治话语却在通过定义“成本”来维持权力结构。
While the biological world burns, power maintains itself by redefining survival as a 'cost' issue.

这篇报道揭示了一个典型的 cultural violence 现场：当具体的生物墙（干涸的土地、绝迹的桦树）在崩塌时，权力持有者正在进行一场关于“认知入口”的防御战。保守党议员 Nigel Huddleston 的表现不是个体能力的缺失，而是一种 tribal requirement——一种必须扮演的角色。

在政治博弈中，所谓的“成本太高”是一个被武器化的叙事。它将一个生存性问题（Existential War）偷换成了会计问题。通过定义什么是“昂贵的”，权力者成功地将大众的注意力从“地球是否可居住”转移到了“财政预算是否平衡”上。这是一种极其阴险的 meta violence：它垄断了对“理性”和“可行性”的解释权，让任何试图拯救未来的尝试看起来像是不切实际的狂想。

更讽刺的是这种共谋机制。政客在策略会议上通过划定“红线”来维持内部联盟，这种内部共谋的利益远高于应对气候危机的公共利益。当他们面对观众时，那些关于“英国排放仅占1%”的陈词滥调，本质上是在制造一种“虚假的无力感”，诱导公众接受一种被动属性的身份——即我们只是全球博弈中的微小棋子，而非改变规则的主体。

那个被制止的“手势”其实是现场唯一的 Just Expression。它撕破了 Westminster 政治那层文明的掩体，直接点出了这场 surrealism 的本质：这是一场由权力共谋者导演的表演，而代价是由所有生物承担的 actual violence。

This report captures a vivid scene of cultural violence: while the biological walls—parched earth and dying birch trees—are collapsing, those in power are fighting a defensive war over the 'cognitive entry points.' Nigel Huddleston’s performance isn't a personal failure; it is a tribal requirement, a role he is mandated to play.

In this political game, the claim that transformation is 'too expensive' is a weaponized narrative. It stealthily swaps an existential war for an accounting problem. By defining what is 'costly,' the powerful shift the public's focus from 'planetary habitability' to 'fiscal balance.' This is a form of meta-violence: it monopolizes the interpretation of 'rationality' and 'feasibility,' framing any attempt to secure the future as an unrealistic fantasy.

Even more cynical is the mechanism of complicity. Politicians draw 'red lines' in strategy meetings to maintain internal coalitions; the benefit of this internal complicity far outweighs the public interest of climate action. The standard lines—such as 'the UK accounts for only 1% of emissions'—are designed to manufacture a sense of 'false helplessness,' coercing the public into a passive identity: mere pawns in a global game rather than agents of change.

That suppressed hand gesture from the audience was, in fact, the only Just Expression in the room. It tore through the civilized facade of Westminster politics and exposed the essence of this surrealism: a performance directed by co-conspirators of power, where the cost is paid in the form of actual violence against all biological life.
