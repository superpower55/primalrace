用一个基金会给化石燃料洗白Whitewashing Fossil Fuels with a Fund

国际 结构层 · 文化层 Reuters ↗ 2026-10-21 § 链接
用未来的‘转型基金’为当下的‘碳排放’买单，是典型的叙事 scam。
Using a 'transition fund' to pay for current emissions is a classic narrative scam.

英国政府批准 Jackdaw 气田，这场戏演得很标准：左手拿着气候承诺的剧本，右手签发开采许可。这种所谓的“务实主义” (pragmatism) 实际上是典型的 weaponized 叙事。它通过制造一个“能源安全”与“气候目标”的伪矛盾，然后抛出一个 20 亿英镑的“能源财富基金”作为缓冲垫，试图让公众相信：我们可以一边通过破坏环境赚钱，一边用这笔钱去拯救环境。

这在加尔通的暴力三角里是典型的 structural violence。真正的 Potential 是一个彻底脱离化石燃料的能源结构，而 Actual 却是继续在北海钻孔。这两者之间的差额，就是被掩盖的生态暴力。而那个所谓的基金会，不过是 cultural layer 上的一个修辞补丁，目的是让这种结构性剥削看起来像是一种“负责任的过渡”。

更讽刺的是，这种决策逻辑完全是 masculine-centric narrative 的延伸：通过对资源的绝对占有和短期利益的最大化来定义“安全”和“保护”。所谓的“保护数百个就业岗位”，本质上是权力阶层与能源巨头（Shell 和 Equinor）之间的一次利益共谋 (complicity)。他们通过定义什么是“必要的能源需求”，夺取了对未来的解释权，将气候危机转化为一场关于 GDP 和就业的算术题。

当政府说“在人们挣扎的时刻，最大化现有储备是正确的”时，他们实际上是在利用底层的生存焦虑来为资本的利润背书。这种叙事陷阱让受害者在潜意识里共谋了对自己的长期伤害。

The UK government's approval of the Jackdaw gas field is a textbook performance: holding a climate pledge script in one hand while signing drilling permits with the other. This so-called "pragmatism" is a weaponized expression. By manufacturing a fake contradiction between "energy security" and "climate goals," and tossing in a £2 billion "Energy Wealth Fund" as a buffer, they want the public to believe we can profit from environmental destruction and then use that money to fix the planet.

In the Violence Triangle, this is pure structural violence. The Potential is a total transition away from fossil fuels; the Actual is more drilling in the North Sea. The gap between them is the hidden ecological violence. The fund is merely a rhetorical patch at the cultural layer, designed to make structural exploitation look like a "responsible transition."

More ironically, this logic is an extension of the masculine-centric narrative: defining "security" and "protection" through absolute resource appropriation and short-term maximization. The claim of "protecting hundreds of jobs" is simply a moment of complicity between the power elite and energy giants like Shell and Equinor. By defining what constitutes "necessary energy needs," they seize the power of interpretation over the future, reducing the climate crisis to a math problem of GDP and employment.

When the government claims it is "right to maximize reserves" while people are struggling, they are weaponizing the survival anxiety of the underclass to endorse corporate profit. This narrative trap tricks the victims into becoming complicitors in their own long-term destruction.
