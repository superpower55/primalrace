用死者作为武器：一场关于认知入口的卑劣博弈Weaponizing the Dead: A Despicable Game for Cognitive Entry

国际 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-15 § 链接
当悲剧被武器化，受害者的记忆就成了排挤异己的入场券。
When tragedy is weaponized, the memory of victims becomes a ticket for exclusionary politics.

这篇报道描述的不是一场关于“尊重”的争论，而是一次典型的表达武器化 (weaponisation of expression) 尝试。右翼政客和部分受害者家属试图通过一个请愿书，将 9/11 的集体创伤转化为一个认知入口，目的只有一个：定义谁才是“合格的”纽约公民，而穆斯林 Mayor Mamdani 则被预设为这个圈层之外的异类。

这种逻辑极其卑劣。它利用了 cultural violence 的惯用手段，将极其小规模的极端行为强行等同于整个族群的生物特征。在这些抗议者的叙事里，Mamdani 的身份（Muslim）本身就是一种“原罪”。他们并不在乎他做了什么，他们在乎的是他“是谁”。这本质上是一场关于解释权的争夺战：谁有权代表死者？谁有权定义什么是“ dishonor the dead”？

最讽刺的是，这种排外行为被包装在“纪念”和“正义”的皮囊之下。这正是元暴力 (meta violence) 的运作方式——利用一个被公认的“文明”或“神圣”的场景，来合法化对特定族群的结构性排挤。当抗议者在街头唾弃记者、攻击市长时，他们其实是在执行一套潜意识的共谋：通过制造一个共同的敌人，来掩盖自身在经济崩溃和药物危机中的挫败感。

Mamdani 的出席是一次真.最优解表达。他没有用对立的叙事去反击，而是通过“人性、优雅与宽容”这种公正的表达 (Just Expressions) 来消解对方的攻击性。但这不足以摧毁已经根深蒂固的认知墙。只要“受害者身份”还能被当作政治筹码来兑换权力，这种以爱之名行暴力之实的 scam 就永远不会停止。

This report is not about a debate over 'respect,' but a textbook attempt at the weaponisation of expression. Right-wing politicians and some victims' families tried to turn the collective trauma of 9/11 into a cognitive entry point with one goal: to define who is a 'qualified' New Yorker, while pre-setting Mayor Mamdani as the outsider.

This logic is despicable. It employs the standard tools of cultural violence, forcibly equating the actions of a tiny extremist faction with the biological traits of an entire race. In the narrative of these protesters, Mamdani's identity as a Muslim is a 'primal sin.' They don't care what he has done; they care who he is. This is essentially a war over the power of interpretation: who has the right to represent the dead, and who defines what 'dishonoring the dead' means?

Most ironic is that this exclusionary behavior is wrapped in the cloak of 'commemoration' and 'justice.' This is exactly how meta violence operates—using a recognized 'civilized' or 'sacred' setting to legitimize the structural exclusion of a specific group. When protesters spit on reporters and attack the mayor, they are executing a subconscious complicity: creating a common enemy to mask their own frustrations amidst economic collapse and the opioid crisis.

Mamdani's attendance was a true optimal expression. He didn't fight back with opposing narratives but dissolved the aggression through Just Expressions of 'decency, grace, and tolerance.' However, this is not enough to tear down the established cognitive wall. As long as 'victim status' can be traded as a political chip for power, this scam—acting in the name of love to commit violence—will never end.
