用核弹头喂养的“工业复兴”scamThe 'Industrial Renewal' Scam Fed by Nuclear Warheads

国际 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-29 § 链接
将大规模杀伤性武器包装成社区就业计划，是典型的文化暴力掩盖结构暴力。
Packaging weapons of mass destruction as community job plans is typical cultural violence masking structural violence.

Andy Burnham 的叙事逻辑极其卑劣：他试图把 Trident 核潜艇计划这个巨大的杀戮机器，包装成一个“振兴社区”和“创造青年就业”的社会福利项目。这在本质上是一种 weaponized expression，通过将“国家安全”与“学徒岗位”强行绑定，夺取关于“工业复兴”的解释权。他把 310 亿英镑的军费开支描述为一种对纳税人的“投资”，这种叙事将一个毁灭性的武器系统转化为一个温情的就业数字，试图让人们在面对核战争的可能性时，先看到 22,000 个学徒名额的诱惑。

这正是加尔通暴力三角的经典运作：在 Cultural 层面上，通过“振兴社区”的浪漫叙事，使 Structural 层面的军备竞赛合法化，最终为 Direct 层面的核威慑（即潜在的全球大屠杀）提供正当性。所谓的“安全”在这里被异化为一种共谋：政府、国防承包商 BAE Systems 以及依赖这些岗位的工人，共同构成了一个巨大的利益共谋体。他们通过维持一个“必须被更新”的核武库，来确保资金流向特定区域，从而在存在性战争中通过牺牲全球潜在的生存权，来换取局部地区的经济指标。

最讽刺的是，这种“工业复兴”建立在一种极其不稳定的博弈之上。100 亿英镑的预备金（contingency）足以说明这个项目的低效与贪婪。Burnham 避谈 2030 年的短期目标，只画 2035 年的饼，这不过是在用一个遥远的 NATO 承诺来掩盖当下的预算混乱。在这种男性中心叙事主导的国防逻辑里，人被降格为“国防工业的零件”，而所谓的“路径”不过是进入一个以毁灭为目标的工业体系。这不是复兴，这是一场用核弹头作为抵押的经济骗局。

Andy Burnham’s narrative logic is despicable: he attempts to package the Trident nuclear program—a massive killing machine—as a social welfare project for 'community revitalization' and 'youth employment.' This is a textbook case of weaponized expression, hijacking the interpretation of 'industrial renewal' by forcibly binding national security to apprenticeship quotas. By describing a £31bn military expenditure as an 'investment' for taxpayers, he transforms a destructive weapon system into a heartwarming employment statistic, tempting the public with 22,000 apprenticeships while ignoring the reality of nuclear annihilation.

This is the classic operation of the Violence Triangle: at the Cultural layer, the romantic narrative of 'revitalizing communities' legitimizes the Structural violence of an arms race, which in turn provides justification for the Direct violence of nuclear deterrence. 'Security' here is mutated into a form of complicity: the government, defense contractors like BAE Systems, and the dependent workforce form a massive co-conspiratorial bloc. They ensure the flow of capital by maintaining a 'necessary' nuclear arsenal, trading the potential survival of the global population for local economic metrics in their own existential war.

Most ironic is that this 'industrial renewal' rests on a highly unstable gamble. A £10bn contingency fund reveals the sheer inefficiency and greed of the program. Burnham’s refusal to commit to 2030 targets while waving a 2035 NATO carrot is merely using a distant pledge to mask current budgetary chaos. In this masculine-centric defense narrative, human beings are downgraded to 'components of the defense industry,' and the promised 'path' is simply an entry into a system designed for destruction. This isn't renewal; it's an economic scam collateralized by nuclear warheads.
