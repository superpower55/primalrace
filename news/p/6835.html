邦德换人，换的从来不是人Bond Recasts the Man, Never the Machine

文娱 文化层 · 元暴力 IndieWire ↗ 2026-11-02 § 链接
新闻摘要长期制片人芭芭拉·布洛柯里和迈克尔·威尔逊去年退出007系列，丹尼尔·克雷格也确认不再出演，新制片人艾米·帕斯卡尔、大卫·海曼与导演丹尼斯·维伦纽瓦近日将新人选缩小到杰克·劳登、卡勒姆·特纳、乔什·奥康纳几人。文章回顾1969年康纳利辞演后，制片方花一年多面试数百人，最终选中只拍过巧克力广告的乔治·拉赞比出演《女王密使》，该片以800万美元成本收获8200万美元票房。
The newsAfter longtime producers Barbara Broccoli and Michael Wilson stepped away from the James Bond franchise last year and Daniel Craig confirmed he was done, new producers Amy Pascal and David Heyman and director Denis Villeneuve have narrowed the next 007 to a few contenders including Jack Lowden, Callum Turner and Josh O'Connor. The piece recalls how, after Sean Connery quit in 1969, the producers spent over a year auditioning hundreds of actors before casting George Lazenby, whose only credits were chocolate commercials, in On Her Majesty's Secret Service, which grossed $82 million on an $8 million budget.
选谁演007是幌子，谁有权定义「最佳」才是真问题
The question was never who plays 007. It is who gets to define "best."

选谁演007从来不是选演员，是选一套男性气质的标准答案。这条新闻里，新制片人把范围缩到杰克·劳登、卡勒姆·特纳、乔什·奥康纳，报道说无论选谁都会引发争议——争议的对象从来不是演技，是这张脸、这副身板、这个口音够不够格代表「男人该有的样子」。

拉赞比的故事正好把这套机制摊开。他面试时撒谎说自己演过德国和香港的电影，赌制片人查不到，第二天又坦白；亨特和制片人担心他的演技，但喜欢他的外形，于是赌了一把，花几个小时做语音训练去掉澳洲口音，再做特技训练。外形过关，口音可以改，演技可以练——顺序很清楚：先要是那个「样子」，其余都是后期加工。表达在这里被当成可采购的零件，而生物墙那一层，也就是这张脸和这副身体，才是真正的入场券。

更值得看的是「最佳邦德」这个头衔是怎么被生产出来的。《女王密使》当年评价温吞，拉赞比收到的是不冷不热的评论，票房也不如上一部康纳利，于是它被盖上了失败的气味。五十七年后，索德伯格说它是「唯一值得反复看的那一部」，诺兰说 ski chase 影响了《盗梦空间》，报道的结论是：当年影评人的判断错了，索德伯格和诺兰的判断对了。

注意这里发生了什么。一部电影的好坏没有变，变的是谁在说话。当年握着解释权的是报纸影评人，现在握着解释权的是两位作者导演，而这两位都是男性，都是这个类型片体系里被供上神坛的人。他们的品味成了新的定价权，一部片子的价值就跟着重新上架。这不是影评的胜利，是解释权换了一次手，而且换给的是同一类人。

拉赞比本人在这套叙事里始终是个道具。报道写他拍戏不愉快，亨特对他冷淡，布洛柯里说他又娇气又自以为是、疏远了很多同事；他让一场戏演到眼里有泪，亨特要求重拍，理由是邦德不该哭。这一句比整篇文章都诚实：这个角色不允许有情绪，因为情绪属于「女性」那一侧，而007是「男性」那一侧的招牌。拉赞比被夸「更有人性」，恰恰是因为他演的这个位置本来就不该有人性。

他自己也没赢。七年合约、多条限制他职业和生活方式的条款，片子拍完他还没签；他算了一笔账，觉得在意大利拍一部片比在邦德合约里干五年赚得多，还判断这个系列在《逍遥骑士》的时代要死了。结果《女王密使》以八百万成本收了八千两百万，他后来承认退出是个大错。这是一场存在性战争，他以为自己在跟合约博弈，其实是在跟一个他看不见的机器博弈，而那台机器连他退出的消息都能改写成「他被解雇了」。

报道最后给新人的建议是：忽略社交媒体上必然到来的风暴，历史会给出公正评价。这句话听着温柔，其实是把定价权又交回给了时间——而时间从不说话，说话的是活着的、有平台的人。五十七年前替拉赞比翻案的是索德伯格和诺兰，不是拉赞比自己；今天被选中的那个人，等到被追认的那一天，也还是别人替他说话。

Casting James Bond was never about casting an actor. It is about casting a standard for masculinity. The new producers have narrowed the field to Jack Lowden, Callum Turner and Josh O'Connor, and the piece says whoever is chosen will be controversial. The controversy is never about craft. It is about whether a face, a frame and an accent qualify as the right kind of man.

George Lazenby's story lays the mechanism bare. He lied at his first meeting, claiming film work in Germany and Hong Kong, betting the producers couldn't check, then confessed the next day. Hunt and the producers worried about his chops but loved his look, so they took the chance, put him through hours of voice coaching to lose the Australian accent and stunt training. Look first, accent second, acting last. Expression gets treated as a purchasable part, and the biological wall, the face and the body, is the actual ticket in.

More revealing is how the title of "best Bond" gets manufactured. On Her Majesty's Secret Service opened to lukewarm reviews and weaker business than the last Connery film, so it carried the smell of failure. Fifty-seven years later Soderbergh calls it the only Bond film worth watching repeatedly, Nolan cites its ski chases as an influence on Inception, and the piece concludes that the critics were wrong and these two directors were right.

Look at what changed. The film didn't. The people holding the interpretive power did. Once it was newspaper critics; now it is two auteurs, both men, both enthroned within the same genre system. Their taste becomes the new pricing power and the film gets re-listed. This is not criticism winning. It is a change of hands, to the same kind of hands.

Lazenby himself stays a prop throughout. The shoot was unhappy; Hunt went cold on him, Broccoli called him spoiled and entitled and said he alienated colleagues. When Lazenby played a devastating scene with tears in his eyes, Hunt ordered a second take because Bond shouldn't cry. That line is more honest than the whole article: the role is not allowed to feel, because feeling belongs to the feminine side, and 007 is the signboard of the other side. He is praised for being "more human" precisely because the slot was never meant to hold a human.

He didn't win either. Seven years, multiple clauses he felt would restrict his career and lifestyle, still unsigned when the film wrapped. He figured one film in Italy paid more than five years on the Bond contract, and assumed the franchise was dying in the Easy Rider era. The film grossed $82 million on an $8 million budget. He later called quitting a major mistake. He thought he was bargaining with a contract; he was bargaining with a machine he couldn't see, one that could rewrite his exit as a firing.

The closing advice to the next actor: ignore the social media firestorm, history will render a fair assessment. That sounds kind, but it hands the pricing power back to time, and time never speaks. The people with platforms do. Lazenby was rehabilitated by Soderbergh and Nolan, not by himself. Whoever gets cast next will also wait to be spoken for.
