10万个“短期实习”：一场关于廉价劳动力与PR的共谋100k Placements: A Complicity of Cheap Labour and PR

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-08 § 链接
用“信心”包装的短期实习，本质上是结构性暴力下的廉价劳动力收割。
Short-term placements branded as 'confidence building' are merely the harvesting of cheap labour under structural violence.

10万个短期 placement，两到四周的时长，听起来像是一次慷慨的社会救赎，实际上是一场典型的 structural violence 升级版。把 100 万个 NEET 青年的生存危机，简化为缺乏“信心”和“技能”的个人问题，这套叙事极其阴险——它将制度性的就业崩溃，成功地转化成了个体的“能力缺失”。

看看这场共谋的阵容：政府（DWP）、零售巨头（M&S, Tesco）、以及前 CEO 组成的 taskforce。这是一个完美的 closed-loop。企业获得了极低成本（甚至免费）的短期试用劳动力，政府通过一个漂亮的数字（100,000）在 PR 层面宣布“干预”了危机，而年轻人则在“保证面试”的虚假希望中，完成了对低端服务业的初步驯化。

最荒诞的细节在于，参与者仍领取 universal credit，这意味着政府在用纳税人的钱补贴企业，让企业免费雇佣年轻人来做短期体力活。这根本不是什么“职业阶梯” (ladder to paid work)，而是一个精心设计的缓冲垫，用来掩盖结构性失业的真相。所谓的“buddy”制度，不过是在文化层面上给这种短期剥削加上一层“关怀”的糖衣。

当一个社会开始用“两周实习”来应对“丢失的一代”时，它其实在传递一个明确的信号：你们的价值仅限于此。这场胜利属于谁？属于那些通过 weaponized narrative 成功将社会危机转化为商业机会的精英共谋者。而真正的 Potential − Actual 差额，在这次所谓的“机会”面前，不仅没有缩小，反而被进一步合法化了。

100,000 short-term placements, lasting two to four weeks—this is not a generous social rescue, but a textbook escalation of structural violence. By reducing the survival crisis of 1 million NEETs to a lack of 'confidence' and 'skills,' the narrative cleverly shifts the blame from systemic employment collapse to individual deficiency.

Observe the complicity: the government (DWP), retail giants (M&S, Tesco), and a taskforce of former CEOs. It is a perfect closed-loop. Corporations secure ultra-low-cost (or free) trial labour, the government claims 'intervention' via a flashy PR number, and young people are domesticated into low-end service work under the illusory hope of a 'guaranteed interview.'

The most absurd detail is that participants continue to receive universal credit. In essence, the state is subsidising corporations to employ youth for free. This is not a 'ladder to paid work,' but a carefully engineered buffer to mask the reality of structural unemployment. The 'buddy' system is simply the cultural violence layer, adding a sugary coating of 'care' to short-term exploitation.

When a society attempts to solve a 'lost generation' with two-week internships, it signals that this is the ceiling of your value. Who wins here? The elite co-conspirators who used weaponized narratives to turn a social crisis into a business opportunity. The gap between Potential and Actual hasn't shrunk; it has been legitimised.
