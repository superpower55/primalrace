总统职位的定价权：一场关于权力套现的极致博弈Pricing the Presidency: The Ultimate Game of Power Monetization

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-13 § 链接
权力不再是治理的工具，而成了可量化、可分发的金融产品。
Power is no longer a tool for governance, but a quantifiable, distributable financial product.

特朗普把总统这个席位直接变成了个资产包。在传统的政治叙事里，权力被包装成“服务”或“领导力”，但特朗普撕掉了这层 cultural violence 的伪装，直接把权力拉回到了最原始的博弈场：权力 = 定价权。当他把 Truth Social 的提前阅读权卖给华尔街，或者通过加密货币协议将政策导向转化为个人资产时，他实际上是在完成一次对国家主权的 financialization（金融化）。

这里最精彩的不是 corruption 这种陈词滥调，而是一个完整的共谋链条 (complicity chain)。加密货币巨头、卡塔尔王室、华尔街资本，他们买的不是某种“政策”，而是一种“特权表达”的优先接入权。这种 pay-to-play 的机制，本质上是把公共决策权变成了某种 subscription service（订阅服务）。而那些在 memecoin 中损失 43 亿美元的散户，则是这场存在性战争中的炮灰——他们以为在博弈，其实只是在为权力的套现提供流动性。

最讽刺的是，特朗普在 2021 年称加密货币是 scam，而在 2025 年将其变为个人财富的引擎。这证明了在他的逻辑里，事实不重要，只有“谁拥有定义事实的权力”才重要。他通过掌控认知入口，将原本被视为风险的资产重新定义为“美国加密之都”的门票，从而完成了从定义者到获益者的闭环。

这场博弈的真正危险不在于一个总统贪婪，而在于他成功地在 structural 层面上拆除了所有防火墙。当总统职位本身成为一种可以公开定价的商品，所谓的“公共利益”就成了最廉价的叙事装饰。一旦这种“权力套现”成为一种被验证的最优解表达，未来的每一个权力持有者都会在潜意识中完成共谋：既然可以把权力卖掉，为什么还要辛苦地去治理？

Trump has effectively transformed the presidency into an asset portfolio. In traditional political narratives, power is packaged as 'service' or 'leadership,' but Trump has stripped away this layer of cultural violence, dragging power back to its primal game: Power = Pricing Power. By selling early access to Truth Social or converting policy directions into personal assets via crypto protocols, he is executing a full-scale financialization of national sovereignty.

The most fascinating part here isn't the cliché of 'corruption,' but the construction of a complete complicity chain. Crypto giants, the Qatari royal family, and Wall Street capital are not buying 'policies'; they are buying priority access to 'privileged expressions.' This pay-to-play mechanism essentially turns public decision-making into a subscription service. Meanwhile, the retail investors who lost $4.3 billion in memecoins are merely fodder in this existential war—they thought they were gambling, but they were actually providing liquidity for the monetization of power.

It is profoundly ironic that Trump called crypto a 'scam' in 2021, only to turn it into his wealth engine in 2025. This proves that in his logic, facts are irrelevant; only 'who owns the power to define facts' matters. By controlling the cognitive entry points, he redefined a risky asset as a ticket to the 'crypto capital of the world,' completing the loop from definer to beneficiary.

The true danger of this game isn't a greedy president, but the successful demolition of all firewalls at the structural level. When the presidency itself becomes a commodity with a public price tag, 'public interest' becomes the cheapest narrative decoration. Once this 'power cashing-out' is validated as an optimal expression, every future power holder will subconsciously enter the complicity: if power can be sold, why bother with the hardship of governing?
