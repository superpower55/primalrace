流量分红与仇恨定价：X 正在为极右翼发放“存在性补贴”Traffic Dividends and the Pricing of Hate: X's 'Existential Subsidies' for the Far-Right

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-24 § 链接
当分歧被量化为收益，仇恨就成了最稳健的投资组合。
When division is quantified as profit, hate becomes the most stable investment portfolio.

Rupert Lowe 的收入曲线是一次完美的 weaponization 演示。在 X 的算法逻辑里，政治表达不再是关于共识的博弈，而是一场关于“认知入口”的收割。当一个 MP 发现通过煽动大规模驱逐和攻击面纱女性能获得每小时 1,476 英镑的收益时，他寻找的不再是政治上的最优解，而是流量上的最优解。这种表达是典型的假.最优解：通过扮演极右翼的扩音器换取短期金钱与名声，代价是公共讨论空间的彻底死亡。

这背后是一场极其阴险的共谋。Elon Musk 提供平台、算法权重和直接的资金激励，Lowe 提供激进的叙事，而那些被诱导进入愤怒状态的受众则贡献了注意力和点击量。这不再是简单的“言论自由”，而是一种结构性暴力 (structural violence) 的金融化。X 将“分歧”定价，让仇恨变得有利可图。当一个政治人物的经济来源与他制造的社会撕裂成正比时，他没有任何动力去追求公正的表达。

最令人作呕的是 Lowe 那套“捐出薪水”的叙事。这不过是文化层面的伪装，试图用一种表演性的慈善来抵消其言论带来的元暴力 (meta violence)。他通过攻击“未经过审核的外国男性”在女性行走的安全感上制造恐慌，利用这种恐惧来喂养自己的账户。这种将他人生存状态当作筹码的博弈，本质上就是一种殖民者的逻辑：定义谁是异类，然后通过排挤异类来确立自己的存在感。

这次收入激增揭示了一个事实：X 已经从一个社交工具变成了极右翼叙事的孵化器和提款机。它通过一套精准的激励机制，让那些能够制造最大撕裂的人获得最高的定价权。这不仅是在拉动英国政治右转，而是在物理性地重塑政治人物的生存逻辑——只要足够激进，就能在不需要选民认可的情况下，通过一个美国亿万富翁的算法获得财务自由。

Rupert Lowe’s income curve is a masterclass in the weaponisation of expression. Within X’s algorithmic logic, political expression is no longer a game for consensus, but a harvest of 'cognitive entry points.' When an MP discovers that inciting mass deportations and attacking veiled women yields £1,476 per hour, he stops seeking a political optimal expression and starts seeking a traffic optimal expression. This is a fake optimal expression: trading the death of public discourse for short-term financial and social gain by playing the role of a far-right megaphone.

This is a sinister complicity. Elon Musk provides the platform, the algorithmic weight, and direct financial incentives; Lowe provides the radical narrative; and the audience, lured into a state of rage, provides the attention and clicks. This is no longer simple 'free speech,' but the financialisation of structural violence. X has put a price on division, making hate profitable. When a politician's economic survival is positively correlated with the social rupture they create, there is zero incentive to pursue Just Expressions.

Lowe’s narrative about 'donating his salary' is a nauseating piece of cultural violence—a performative charity designed to mask the meta violence of his rhetoric. He manufactures panic over 'unvetted foreign males' to weaponise the fear regarding women's safety, using that fear to feed his bank account. This game of using others' existential precariousness as a bargaining chip is the core logic of the coloniser: define the Other, then establish one's own existence through their exclusion.

This surge in earnings reveals a stark reality: X has evolved from a social tool into an incubator and ATM for far-right narratives. Through a precise incentive structure, it grants the highest pricing power to those who can manufacture the greatest rupture. It is not merely pulling British politics to the right; it is physically reshaping the survival logic of politicians—proving that as long as you are radical enough, you can achieve financial freedom via a US billionaire's algorithm without ever needing the approval of the electorate.
