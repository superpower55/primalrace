格陵兰的潜油井与特朗普及共谋者的“殖民幻梦”Greenland's Oil Wells and the Colonial Dream of Trump's Co-conspirators

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-12 § 链接
资源掠夺总是披着“能源危机”或“文明开发”的叙事外衣，本质是权力的强行扩张。
Resource plunder always wears the mask of "energy crisis" or "civilized development"; it is fundamentally the forced expansion of power.

这不仅是一次环保监管的胜利，而是一次典型的“武器化叙事”在现实物理墙面前的撞墙。特朗普及其特使 Landry 试图将格陵兰定义为美国能源危机的“救世主”，这种叙事将一个主权领土简化为可购买、可强占的资源矿区。在他们的逻辑里，格陵兰不是一个拥有生态系统和原住民社群的生存空间，而是一个待定价的资产包。

最令人作呕的是这场博弈背后的 Complicity（共谋）网络：从华尔街亿万富翁 Kenneth Griffin 的资本注入，到 Dr Phil 试图用纪录片将掠夺包装成“现代拓荒者”的浪漫叙事，再到军方背景的人士坐镇董事会。这套组合拳精准地覆盖了资本、文化和武力三个入口。他们试图通过制造一个“即将开采”的假象来诱导股价上涨（Trump pump），这本质上是一场利用政治影响力进行的金融 Scam。

Greenland Energy 的 CEO 在社区会议上撒谎称已获得许可，这种行为揭示了元暴力的傲慢：在强权者眼中，弱势方的监管程序只是可以被跳过或伪造的冗余代码。他们认为只要背靠白宫，格陵兰的法律和生态红线在“美国利益”面前就是透明的。

这次延迟虽然是 structural 层的暂时胜利，但真正的战争才刚刚开始。当资本和军队已经把集装箱运到岸边，这种“延迟”究竟是制度的坚守，还是在为更大规模的强权交易争取议价时间？只要“资源=权力”的男性中心叙事不崩塌，格陵兰的冻土就永远是殖民者的猎场。

This is more than a victory for environmental regulation; it is a classic instance of weaponized narrative crashing into a physical wall. Trump and his envoy Landry attempted to define Greenland as the "savior" of the US energy crisis, a narrative that reduces a sovereign territory to a resource zone to be bought or seized. In their logic, Greenland is not a living space with ecosystems and indigenous communities, but a priced asset package.

Most repulsive is the network of Complicity behind this game: from the capital injection of Wall Street billionaire Kenneth Griffin to Dr Phil's attempt to package plunder as a romantic saga of "modern-day wildcatters" via a documentary, and the presence of military-linked figures on the board. This combination precisely covers the three entry points: capital, culture, and force. They sought to induce a stock surge (Trump pump) by manufacturing a facade of "imminent extraction," which is essentially a financial scam leveraging political influence.

The fact that Greenland Energy's CEO lied to the community about having permission reveals the arrogance of meta-violence: in the eyes of the powerful, the regulatory processes of the weak are merely redundant code to be bypassed or forged. They believed that with the White House behind them, Greenland's laws and ecological red lines would be transparent before "American interests."

While this delay is a temporary victory at the structural level, the real war has just begun. When capital and military assets have already landed containers on the shore, is this "delay" a steadfastness of the system, or merely buying time for a larger power deal? As long as the masculine-centric narrative of "resources = power" persists, the permafrost of Greenland will remain a hunting ground for colonizers.
