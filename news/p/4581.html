Semi-routine：一个典型的权力掩体Semi-routine: A Classic Power Bunker

国际 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-26 § 链接
用“常规”定义非正常，是权力夺取解释权的第一步。
Defining the abnormal as 'routine' is the first step in seizing the power of interpretation.

当CIA局长不请自来地出现在莫斯科，而白宫、五角大楼和空军全部集体噤声时，这件事本身就是一次剧烈的存在性博弈。在这种极高密度的信息真空里，特朗普抛出“semi-routine”（半常规）这个词，不是在陈述事实，而是在制造一个认知入口。

在权力博弈中，最有效的武器化表达就是将“异常”定义为“常规”。一旦这件事被贴上 routine 的标签，所有关于北约、伊朗制裁或乌克兰停战的深度质疑都会被自动归类为“crazy”的猜测。这是一种典型的元暴力操作：通过垄断对“什么是正常”的解释权，直接抹除掉公众感知结构性危机的能力。

这场秘密访问的背景是美俄在伊朗问题上的深度对立，以及乌克兰战场的持续血腥。在这种 Actual 状态下，没有任何一次 CIA 局长的莫斯科之行是“常规”的。所谓的“半常规”，不过是权力者在面对无法在公共空间交代、又必须在私下达成共谋的交易时，为自己搭建的一个语言掩体。

我们要问的不是 Ratcliffe 见了谁，而是为什么一个如此关键的权力动作，需要通过一个如此模糊的词汇来掩盖。当权力开始定义“常规”时，真相就成了被排挤的边缘叙事。

When a CIA director arrives unannounced in Moscow while the White House, Pentagon, and Air Force maintain a collective silence, the event itself becomes a fierce existential game. In this high-density information vacuum, Trump's use of the term "semi-routine" is not a statement of fact, but the creation of a cognitive entry point.

In power games, the most effective weaponized expression is defining the 'anomaly' as 'routine.' Once an event is labeled as routine, all deep inquiries regarding NATO, Iranian sanctions, or the Ukraine ceasefire are automatically categorized as 'crazy' speculation. This is a classic operation of meta-violence: by monopolizing the interpretation of what is 'normal,' power erases the public's ability to perceive structural crises.

This secret visit unfolds against a backdrop of deep US-Russia antagonism over Iran and continued bloodshed in Ukraine. Given this Actual state, no visit by a CIA director to Moscow is 'routine.' The so-called "semi-routine" is merely a linguistic bunker built by those in power when they must reach a complicity in private that cannot be justified in the public square.

The question is not who Ratcliffe met, but why such a critical power move requires such a vague term for cover. When power begins to define 'routine,' the truth is relegated to a marginalized narrative.
