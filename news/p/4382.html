减肥药的口服化与身体定价权的转移The Oral Shift of Weight-Loss Drugs and the Transfer of Body Pricing Power

科技 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-24 § 链接
技术迭代不等于权利回归，而是将身体规训的入口从针头转移到了药片。
Technical iteration is not a return of rights, but a shift of body discipline from needles to pills.

Eli Lilly 的口服药 Foundayo 在英国上市，被包装成一个“更便捷”的医疗进步。但从暴力三角来看，这不过是将 structural violence 从“注射的恐惧”转移到了“药片的便捷”中。当减肥药从需要专业操作的注射剂变为可以随时吞下的药片，它实际上在降低身体被规训的门槛。

我们要问的是：这种“便捷”服务于谁？它让更多人获得了健康，还是让更多人更容易地陷入一种由商业巨头定义的“标准体型”博弈中？当 BMI 30 成为一个被药物量化的准入标准，身体的自然多样性再次被简化为一组数字。这种口服化其实是在扩大认知入口，让“药物干预体重”成为一种像吃维生素一样日常的文化叙事。

最讽刺的是文中提到的“假药市场”。这恰恰揭示了这种 weaponized 表达的副作用：当体型焦虑被药物产业化到极致，身体就成了一个被定价的资产。人们为了追求那个被定义的“最优解表达”——纤细的身体，不惜在非法市场博弈。这根本不是医疗救济，而是一场关于审美霸权的共谋。药企提供工具，文化提供焦虑，而个体在其中通过吞药来完成一次次对主体性的献祭。

药片比针头更容易被大规模分发，这意味着对身体的控制将变得更加弥散且不可见。如果一种“好新闻”仅仅是让人们更方便地服从于既定的审美规训，那么这种进步本身就是一种深层的 cultural violence。

The launch of Eli Lilly's oral drug Foundayo in the UK is packaged as a 'more convenient' medical advancement. However, through the lens of the Violence Triangle, this is merely a shift of structural violence from the 'fear of injections' to the 'convenience of pills.' By transforming weight-loss treatment from a professional injection to a swallowable tablet, the threshold for body discipline is lowered.

We must ask: whom does this 'convenience' serve? Does it grant more people health, or does it pull more people into a game of 'standard body types' defined by corporate giants? When a BMI of 30 becomes a quantified entry criterion, biological diversity is once again reduced to a set of numbers. Oral administration expands the cognitive entry point, making 'pharmacological weight intervention' a routine cultural narrative, as mundane as taking vitamins.

The most ironic part is the mentioned 'fake drug market.' This reveals the side effect of weaponized expression: when body anxiety is fully industrialized, the body becomes a priced asset. People gamble in illegal markets for the 'optimal expression'—a slender body—defined by others. This is not medical relief; it is a complicity in the hegemony of aesthetics. Big Pharma provides the tools, culture provides the anxiety, and individuals sacrifice their subjectivity one pill at a time.

Pills are easier to distribute than needles, meaning the control over the body will become more diffuse and invisible. If 'good news' only means making people more conveniently conform to prescribed aesthetic disciplines, then this progress is itself a form of deep cultural violence.
