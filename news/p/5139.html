亮片下的共谋：舞池是最好的权力掩体Complicity Under Sequins: The Ballroom as a Power Shield

哲学 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-19 § 链接
所谓的‘欢乐’是文化暴力的润滑剂，让结构性剥削看起来像场派对。
Forced jollity is the lubricant of cultural violence, making structural exploitation look like a party.

Strictly Come Dancing 的这场 launch show 是一次标准的 cultural violence 表演。在这种‘闪耀’的氛围中，所有的博弈都被包装成了 ‘forced jollity’（强迫性的欢乐）。当观众在讨论主持人的化学反应、舞者的发型以及谁更‘迷人’时，他们实际上在参与一场巨大的共谋：将权力关系转化为审美消费。

注意那些评论者的措辞：‘女性看起来比男性更强’，或者‘期待 64 岁的女性依然灵活’。在这种叙事里，女性的‘强’被定义在舞池的灵活性和耐力上，这是一种典型的 weaponized 审美——将女性的身体能力限制在‘表演’和‘被凝视’的框架内。无论女性在节目中表现得多么 dominant，她们最终的身份依然是‘被评分者’和‘被挑选者’。这种结构性弱势被亮片和音乐掩盖，变成了某种‘赋权’的假象。

最讽刺的是，一个职业舞者转行做主持人被赞美为‘自然’且‘极具价值’，而另一部分舞者则被安排在‘替补席’上等待机会。这种内部的等级制度被包装成‘节目效果’。在男性中心叙事的元暴力下，这种竞争被简化为‘谁更努力’或‘谁更有魅力’，而忽略了背后由制作团队（权力中心）掌控的定价权。

这场秀本质上是一次认知入口的占领：它告诉观众，只要音乐响起，所有的不平等都可以被‘舞蹈’化解。这是一种极其高效的 scam，它让人们在享受‘浪漫爱’和‘艺术美’的同时，习惯性地忽略了身体被物化、主体性被剥夺的 structural violence。

The launch show of Strictly Come Dancing is a textbook performance of cultural violence. In this 'glittering' atmosphere, all power struggles are packaged as 'forced jollity.' While the audience debates the chemistry of presenters or a dancer's hairstyle, they are participating in a massive complicity: transforming power dynamics into aesthetic consumption.

Notice the language of the commenters: 'women seem stronger than the men' or hoping to be 'as flexible as a 64-year-old.' In this narrative, female 'strength' is defined by agility and endurance on the dance floor. This is a weaponized aesthetic—confining female physical capability within the framework of 'performance' and 'being gazed upon.' No matter how dominant a woman appears, her ultimate identity remains that of the 'rated' and the 'chosen.' This structural weakness is masked by sequins and music, masquerading as a form of empowerment.

Most ironically, a pro-dancer turning presenter is praised as 'natural,' while others are relegated to the 'subs' bench. This internal hierarchy is branded as 'entertainment.' Under the meta-violence of a masculine-centric narrative, this competition is simplified into 'who is more hardworking' or 'more charming,' ignoring the pricing power held by the production team at the center of power.

The show is essentially a seizure of the cognitive entry point: it tells the audience that as long as the music plays, all inequalities can be dissolved through 'dance.' It is a highly efficient scam, allowing people to enjoy 'romantic love' and 'artistic beauty' while habitually ignoring the structural violence of objectification and the erasure of subjectivity.
