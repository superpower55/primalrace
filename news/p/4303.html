用“学术自由”掩护的种族清洗与性别共谋Academic Freedom as a Cover for Racial Purge and Gender Complicity

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-20 § 链接
学术自由不是歧视的遮羞布，更不是元暴力的合法入口。
Academic freedom is not a veil for discrimination, nor a legal entry point for meta-violence.

这是一场典型的关于“认知入口”的武器化博弈。Nathan Cofnas 试图通过抛出“抄袭”这种学术界最致命的标签，来完成一次对 Jason Arday 的存在性抹杀。在这种叙事里，Arday 的身份——剑桥最年轻的黑人教授——被 Cofnas 刻意地从“成就”转化为“原罪”。在 Cofnas 的逻辑中，任何少数族裔的成功都被预设为对“精英主义 (meritocracy)”的亵渎。这根本不是学术讨论，而是试图通过定义“事实”来夺取解释权，将一名学者的职业生涯直接推向死亡。

更恶心的是 Cofnas 展现出的那种典型的共谋逻辑。他在攻击黑人学者的同时，顺便给女性来了一刀，声称女性通过“指认和哭泣”就能让男人丢工作，并将女性的职场存在定义为“配额”下的劣质替代品。这种论调揭示了元暴力 (meta violence) 的底层逻辑：在男性中心叙事中，白人男性是唯一的标准度量衡，而黑人和女性都被简化为被施舍的、不合格的客体。他试图构建一个只有“直白男”才能定义真理的纯净世界，而任何打破这个结构的表达都被他定义为“woke”的骗局。

根特大学的停职决定是此次 Violence Triangle 中 structural 层的正向修正。当一个学术机构意识到其成员正在利用制度提供的“学术自由”来实施文化暴力 (cultural violence) 时，撤销其特权是唯一的 Just Expression。学术自由的边界在于它不能成为实施种族清洗和性别歧视的武器。如果一个所谓的“研究者”认为黑人应该在除体育和娱乐外的所有高位消失，那么他本身就是这个系统中最不合格的冗余。

这场悲剧最深层的刺在于：即便 Cofnas 被开除，他所代表的那套“反 woke”叙事依然在社交媒体上拥有大量共谋者。他们通过扮演“真理的捍卫者”来掩盖自己对权力丧失的恐惧。Arday 的死亡将成为这群人叙事中的一个注脚，而真正的暴力，依然潜伏在那些自称“理性”和“客观”的学术话语之中。

This is a textbook case of the weaponisation of 'cognitive entry points.' Nathan Cofnas attempted to execute an existential erasure of Jason Arday by deploying the 'plagiarism' label—the most lethal weapon in academia. In this narrative, Arday's identity as Cambridge's youngest Black professor was deliberately pivoted from an achievement to a sin. For Cofnas, any success by a minority is pre-set as a desecration of 'meritocracy.' This wasn't an academic debate; it was an attempt to seize the power of interpretation by manufacturing 'facts' to destroy a scholar's existence.

Even more repulsive is the complicity logic Cofnas employs. While attacking a Black scholar, he simultaneously struck at women, claiming they get men fired by 'pointing and crying' and defining female professional presence as a low-quality substitution for 'quotas.' This reveals the core of meta-violence: in a masculine-centric narrative, the straight white male is the only standard of measurement, while Black people and women are reduced to unqualified objects of charity. He sought to construct a world where only 'straight white men' define truth, and any expression breaking this structure is labeled a 'woke' scam.

The suspension by Ghent University is a positive structural correction within the Violence Triangle. When an institution realizes its member is using 'academic freedom' as a cover for cultural violence, stripping that privilege is the only Just Expression. The boundary of academic freedom is that it cannot be a weapon for racial purging or gender discrimination. If a so-called 'researcher' believes Black people should vanish from all high-profile positions except sports and entertainment, he is the most unqualified redundancy in the system.

The deepest sting of this tragedy is that even if Cofnas is fired, the 'anti-woke' narrative he represents still has a massive army of complicity on social media. They perform as 'defenders of truth' to mask their fear of losing power. Arday's death will become a mere footnote in their narrative, while the real violence continues to lurk behind the mask of 'rational' and 'objective' academic discourse.
