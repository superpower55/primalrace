系统性失能是给捕食者的特赦令Systemic Failure as a Pardon for Predators

性别 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-07 § 链接
所谓“系统失误”是结构性暴力的遮羞布，本质是共谋者的集体渎职。
So-called 'systemic errors' are a veil for structural violence and collective complicity.

把 Simon Levy 称为“捕食性怪物” (predatory monster) 是一种极其廉价的叙事逃避。当警方和检方用这个词时，他们试图将暴力个体化，把这场悲剧定义为“运气不好遇到了疯子”，而非“系统为疯子开了门”。

从加尔通的暴力三角来看，这不仅是 direct 层的谋杀，更是 structural 和 cultural 层的双重崩塌。一个有前科的性犯罪者，在多次袭击、多次逮捕、甚至在第一起谋杀案的保释期间，依然能自由地在伦敦街头地毯式狩猎，这绝不是简单的“文书错误”或“程序疏忽”。这是典型的 structural violence：资源分配的缺失、对女性受害者报告的轻视、以及对男性施暴者“人权”的过度保护（如 BTP 提到的所谓“搜查房屋可能侵犯人权”）。

最令人作呕的是其中的共谋机制 (complicity)。检方在三次听证会中“忘记”提及他涉嫌谋杀，警方在逮捕他后竟然把偷来的交通卡还给他，让他能毫无阻碍地前往下一个杀戮现场。这种规模的失能已经超越了能力问题，而是一种潜意识的元暴力 (meta violence)——在男性中心叙事中，一个男性的“程序正义”权重高于多个女性的“生存权利”。

这再次证明，所谓的“保护女性”在目前的司法结构中只是 PR 版本的表演。当制度本身成为施暴者的掩体，每一个所谓的“错误”都是在给捕食者发放特赦令。

Calling Simon Levy a 'predatory monster' is a cheap narrative escape. By using this term, the police and prosecutors attempt to individualize the violence, framing the tragedy as 'bad luck in meeting a lunatic' rather than 'the system opening the door for one.'

Through the lens of the Violence Triangle, this is not just direct murder, but a dual collapse of structural and cultural layers. A proven sex offender, free to hunt across London despite multiple attacks, arrests, and even being on bail for his first murder, is not the result of 'clerical errors.' This is textbook structural violence: the systemic devaluation of reports from female victims and the perverse prioritization of a male perpetrator's 'human rights' (exemplified by BTP's hesitation to search his home to avoid 'breaching rights').

Most repulsive is the mechanism of complicity. Prosecutors 'forgot' to mention a murder suspect status in three hearings; police returned a stolen Oyster card to him, effectively providing the transport for his next kill. This scale of dysfunction transcends incompetence; it is a manifestation of meta violence—where the 'procedural justice' of one man outweighs the basic survival rights of multiple women.

This proves once again that 'protecting women' within the current judicial structure is merely a PR performance. When the institution itself serves as a shield for the abuser, every so-called 'error' is nothing more than a pardon issued to a predator.
