国家安全是掩体，而漏洞是共谋的红利National Security as a Shield, Vulnerabilities as Complicity Dividends

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-27 § 链接
用 2030 年的 deadline 应对当下的 hack，是典型的 structural violence。
Using a 2030 deadline to counter current hacks is a textbook case of structural violence.

伊朗背景的黑客已经把英国的小型电厂给 shut down 了，但英国政府的反应是：没关系，我们的安全标准升级计划在 2030 年底前完成就行。这种时间线简直是个 scam。在 cyber war 的维度里，2030 年相当于下个世纪，用一个遥远的 deadline 来掩盖当下的脆弱，本质上是在用国民的生存安全为某些利益集团的成本开脱。

这不仅是技术漏洞，更是 structural violence。大型电厂有高标准，小型电厂被允许处于低标准，这种资源分配的差异化就是一种暴力。政府和监管机构 Ofgem 在这里形成了典型的 complicity：政府通过发布一个“看起来在行动”的计划来维持其“掌控感”的叙事，而小型电厂运营商则在低标准下节省安全成本，享受着规避监管的红利。双方共谋，把国家安全这个宏大叙事当成掩体，在掩体后面心安理得地 gambling。

最讽刺的是，内阁办公室一边让公民囤积罐头和水以应对“极端天气和敌对国家攻击”，一边在基础设施的 baseline 标准上玩慢动作。这种认知入口的撕裂极其危险：它试图通过制造个体层面的恐惧（让你囤水）来转移对结构性失职（不升级电厂安全）的注意力。当“灯光熄灭”成为一种可能性，受苦的永远是那些在结构底层、没有冗余资源去应对断电的普通人，而不是那些制定 2030 时间表的官员。

Iran-linked hackers have already shut down a UK small power plant, yet the government's response is a shrug and a promise that security standards will be updated by the end of 2030. This timeline is a total scam. In the dimension of cyber war, 2030 is practically the next century. Using a distant deadline to mask current fragility is essentially subsidizing the costs of certain interest groups with the survival security of the populace.

This is more than a technical glitch; it is structural violence. Large plants have high standards, while small ones are permitted to remain vulnerable—this disparity in resource allocation is violence. The government and the regulator, Ofgem, are in a state of complicity: the government maintains a narrative of "control" by publishing a plan that looks active, while small plant operators save on security costs by exploiting low standards. Together, they use the grand narrative of "National Security" as a shield to gamble in the shadows.

The most cynical part is the Cabinet Office urging citizens to stockpile tinned food and water for "hostile state attacks" while simultaneously dragging its feet on baseline infrastructure standards. This split in the cognitive entry point is dangerous: it attempts to divert attention from structural negligence by manufacturing individual-level fear. When the lights eventually go out, the ones who suffer will be the ordinary people at the bottom of the structure with no redundancies, not the officials drafting 2030 timelines.
