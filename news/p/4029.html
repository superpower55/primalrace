刚果金的埃博拉：一场被矿产与政治定价的生物学屠杀DRC's Ebola: A Biological Massacre Priced by Minerals and Politics

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-17 § 链接
当生命被结构性地定价为矿产的附属品，病毒只是执行了既定的暴力程序。
When lives are structurally priced as appendages to minerals, viruses merely execute a pre-set program of violence.

Violence = Potential − Actual。刚果金这次埃博拉疫情的差额，不是因为 Bundibugyo 病毒太罕见，而是因为当地人的生命在 global system 里的定价太低。从 2 月份发病到 5 月份才被察觉，这三个月的 a-priori 沉默，是典型的 structural violence。在一个矿产丰富但被武装组织和外部势力撕裂的地区，医疗监测网络的崩溃不是意外，而是结果。

这是一个极其讽刺的共谋场域：一方面是资源被全球供应链掠夺，另一方面是维持基本生存的 health system 被 Trump 政府这种政治操弄直接 freeze 掉。当人道主义援助被当作政治筹码，所谓的 "global health emergency" 实际上成了一场关于谁在牺牲的博弈。医疗工作者欠薪三个月而罢工，这证明了在这个结构里，即便执行救赎的人也是被剥削的零件。

最令人作呕的 cultural violence 是那些关于埋葬习俗的 misinformation。在长期的 unrest 和 outside interference 导致的创伤性社会中，民众对医疗机构的 mistrust 是元暴力的延伸——当权力机构长期以来只在掠夺矿产时出现，他们为什么会相信权力机构在分发疫苗时是出于善意？

这不再是一场单纯的 zoonotic spillover（动物源性溢出），而是一次结构性的溢出。病毒只是在一个被刻意维持在低能级、高创伤状态的社会中找到了最优的传播路径。所谓的 "Ebola is winning" 是个 scam，赢的不是病毒，而是那个通过削减援助、维持动荡来低价获取资源的全球权力结构。

Violence = Potential − Actual. The gap in the DRC Ebola outbreak isn't due to the rarity of the Bundibugyo strain, but because the lives there are priced too low in the global system. The a-priori silence from February to May is a textbook case of structural violence. In a mineral-rich region torn apart by armed groups and external interference, the collapse of health surveillance is not an accident—it is the intended result.

This is a cynical field of complicity: while resources are plundered by global supply chains, the health system essential for survival is frozen by political maneuvering, such as the Trump administration's aid cuts. When humanitarian aid becomes a political chip, the "global health emergency" becomes a game of who is sacrificed. Healthcare workers striking over three months of unpaid wages proves that even the rescuers are merely exploited parts in this machinery.

The most insidious cultural violence is the misinformation regarding burial practices. In a traumatized society shaped by decades of unrest and outside interference, the mistrust of health workers is an extension of meta-violence. When the state only appears to plunder minerals, why would the people believe it appears with vaccines out of benevolence?

This is no longer a simple zoonotic spillover, but a structural spillover. The virus simply found the optimal transmission path in a society deliberately maintained in a low-energy, high-trauma state. Claiming that "Ebola is winning" is a scam; it is not the virus that wins, but the global power structure that profits from cutting aid and maintaining instability to secure cheap resources.
