特朗普及李在明的“交易之舞”：谁在扮演谁的 pacemarker？The Transactional Dance: Who is Whose Pacemaker?

国际 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-19 § 链接
安全感从来不是被赐予的，而是博弈后的定价权。
Security is never a gift; it is the pricing power won through existential gaming.

李在明试图用一套充满文学色彩的叙事——“你是 peacemaker，我是 pacemaker”——来试图在美韩关系中夺回主动权。这本质上是一次关于“表达”的博弈，他试图通过扮演一个协助者，将韩国的战略意图（减少军事对抗、重启对话）通过特朗普的口说出来。但在国际政治的存在性战争中，这种试图通过“扮演”来达成目的的假.最优解表达，在绝对的权力不对等面前极其脆弱。

特朗普用社交媒体一个 blunt 的通知就实现了李在明想要的结果，但这恰恰揭示了 structural violence 的残酷：当你的安全需求变成了对方随手可以处置的筹码时，结果的达成不仅不是胜利，而是一次深刻的羞辱。这正是典型的“Korea passing”——在元暴力的逻辑下，韩国的生存状态被简化为美国交易清单上的一个 line item。李在明得到了他想要的“结果”，但失去了定义这个结果的“解释权”。

更有趣的是韩国内部的共谋与撕裂。左翼在实质上认同结果但对形式感到“遗憾”，保守派则将其定义为“安全灾难”。这种分歧掩盖了一个底层事实：无论左还是右，他们都在一个由美国定义的 security umbrella 结构中寻找生存的最优解。当这个 umbrella 变成一个 transactional lens（交易透镜）时，韩国内部对独立核武的渴望（70% 以上的支持率）其实是对这种结构性不安全感的最真实表达。

这场戏的讽刺之处在于，特朗普试图通过削减负担来减轻美国压力，但这种不稳定的表达反而推高了盟友追求独立核威慑的动力。当一个系统通过随意剥夺他人的安全感来维持自身效率时，它实际上在加速这个系统的崩溃。李在明以为自己在带节奏，结果发现自己只是在为对方的剧本跑龙套。

President Lee Jae Myung attempted a romanticized narrative—"You be the peacemaker, I'll be the pacemaker"—to regain agency in the US-ROK alliance. This was a gamble on expression, an attempt to use a specific role to channel South Korea's strategic intent. However, in the existential war of geopolitics, this kind of fake optimal expression—achieving goals by playing a role—is fragile when faced with absolute power asymmetry.

Trump delivered the result Lee wanted via a blunt social media post, but the manner of delivery exposes the structural violence of the relationship: when your security needs become a chip that the other side can flip at will, the outcome is not a victory, but a humiliation. This is the essence of "Korea passing"—under the logic of meta-violence, South Korea's existence is reduced to a line item on a US transactional ledger. Lee got the result, but lost the interpretation rights over that result.

The internal complicity and friction within Seoul are equally telling. The left accepts the substance but regrets the form; the right screams "security disaster." This divide masks a fundamental truth: both sides are merely seeking an optimal expression within a security umbrella defined by Washington. Once that umbrella is viewed through a transactional lens, the surge in support for an independent nuclear deterrent (over 70%) becomes the most honest expression of structural insecurity.

The irony is that Trump's attempt to reduce the American burden is actually accelerating the drive for nuclear independence among allies. When a system maintains its efficiency by arbitrarily stripping others of their security, it accelerates its own dissolution. Lee thought he was setting the pace, only to find he was merely an extra in someone else's script.
