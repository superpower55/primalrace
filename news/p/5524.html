离职不是正义，而是共谋者的止损Resignation is not Justice, but Loss-Cutting for Co-conspirators

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-12 § 链接
结构性暴力通过“专业能力”洗白，最终在 PR 危机中通过离职完成闭环。
Structural violence is laundered via 'professionalism' and finalized through PR-driven resignations.

这就是典型的 structural violence 运作逻辑：一个通过削减预算将 72 个人送进火海的 Housing Director，在几年后能以 15 万英镑年薪再次入职管理 1.3 万套公屋。在 Barnet Group 的招聘叙事里，她不是一个导致灾难的责任人，而是一个在竞争中胜出的“strongest candidate”。这就是元暴力的狡猾之处——它将“削减成本”这种杀人行为定义为“专业经验”和“管理能力”。

Laura Johnson 的入职本身就是一场巨大的 complicity。招聘方、背书人以及整个住房管理体系共同构建了一个认知入口：只要你拥有正确的 qualifications 和 references，你之前造成的死亡就可以被视为“管理失误”而非“道德破产”。在这种逻辑下，受害者的痛苦被排在“专业能力”之后，成为了一个需要被处理的 PR 问题，而非一个必须面对的正义问题。

现在她离职了，但这绝不是什么 good_news。这不过是当 survivors 的愤怒在公共空间制造了足够大的噪音，使得她的存在不再是 Barnet Group 的“最优解”时，系统进行的一次快速止损。从入职到离职，整个过程没有涉及任何刑事定罪，没有涉及任何对受害者的实质补偿。她只是从一个权力席位跳到了另一个，最后因为“不合适”而离开。

真正的暴力差额（Potential − Actual）依然存在。如果一个能通过削减防火预算来“优化成本”的人，依然能在同一个行业的精英圈层里自由流动，那么所有公屋居民的生命在系统眼中依然只是一个可以被 swap 掉的 budget 选项。离职是表演，而免责才是这套共谋系统的真.最优解。

This is the textbook operation of structural violence: a Housing Director who sent 72 people to their deaths by cutting budgets can reappear years later in a senior role managing 13,000 council homes with a £150k salary. In Barnet Group's narrative, she wasn't a catalyst for tragedy, but the 'strongest candidate' in a competitive field. This is the cunning of meta-violence—it redefines the act of killing via cost-cutting as 'professional experience' and 'management capability.'

Laura Johnson's appointment was a massive act of complicity. The recruiters, the referees, and the entire housing management system collaborated to create a cognitive entry point where causing a disaster is merely a 'management failure' rather than 'moral bankruptcy,' provided you have the right qualifications. In this framework, the agony of survivors is treated as a PR crisis to be managed, not a demand for justice to be met.

Her departure is absolutely not good_news. It is simply a rapid loss-cutting exercise by the system once the backlash from survivors created too much noise, making her presence no longer the 'optimal expression' for Barnet Group. From hiring to firing, no criminal convictions were made, and no actual restitution was provided. She simply shifted from one power seat to another, only to leave because she became 'inappropriate.'

The gap of violence (Potential − Actual) remains. As long as someone who 'optimizes costs' by stripping fire safety can still circulate freely within the industry's elite circles, the lives of council tenants remain nothing more than a budget item to be swapped. The resignation is a performance; immunity is the true optimal expression of this system of complicity.
