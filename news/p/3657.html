用‘学术背景’给权力博弈打补丁Patching Power Games with Academic Credentials

性别 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Athletic ↗ 2026-08-11 § 链接
当表达被指责为武器时，试图通过证明‘专业性’来脱罪，本身就是一种共谋。
Attempting to excuse the weaponization of a term by proving 'professional expertise' is itself a form of complicity.

DiJonai Carrington 的这场公关表演非常典型：先抛出一个极具进攻性的标签 "WHITE PRIVILEGE"，在引发舆论海啸后，迅速将其定义为对联赛‘一致性’（consistency）的结构性诉求。最精彩的部分在于她搬出了斯坦福和贝勒大学的学历，试图证明自己拥有定义该词的‘学术资格’，从而将一次情绪化的博弈包装成一次理性的社会学探讨。

这在存在性战争中是一个典型的‘假.最优解表达’。Carrington 试图通过证明自己‘懂’这个词的重量，来消解她将这个词‘武器化’的事实。但事实是，在 WNBA 这种高度商业化且充满身份政治的场域里，没有任何一个词是在真空里被讨论的。在造成身体接触、被驱逐出场后立即发布该标签，这就是一次精准的认知入口抢占。后续的‘我不是在针对个人’，不过是在感受到结构性反弹后的战术撤退。

而 Cunningham 扮演的角色则更具讽刺意味。她此前关于‘保护年轻女孩’免受跨性别运动员侵害的言论，是典型的利用‘生物墙’（biological wall）构建的保护主义叙事。这种叙事本质上是在维护一种传统的、排他的女性身份定义。当两个持有不同身份政治逻辑的球员在球场上发生物理碰撞时，这场 flagrant foul 就不再仅仅是篮球动作，而是两种权力叙事在 structural 层面的直接对撞。

联赛设立的‘任务小组’（task force）则是最标准的 structural violence 伪装。通过一个没有实质执行权的委员会来应对球员的愤怒，是用‘程序正义’来掩盖‘分配不公’的 PR 手段。在这种环境下，无论是通过学历自证的 Carrington，还是通过‘保护女孩’构建壁垒的 Cunningham，都在这套男性中心叙事构建的竞技体制中，通过扮演特定的角色来争夺仅有的解释权。

DiJonai Carrington's PR performance is a textbook case: first, deploy a highly aggressive label like "WHITE PRIVILEGE," and once the backlash hits, pivot to define it as a structural plea for 'consistency' in officiating. The most calculated move is invoking her Stanford and Baylor credentials to prove she has the 'academic qualification' to use the term, attempting to rebrand an emotional gamble as a rational sociological discourse.

In the existential war, this is a 'fake optimal expression.' Carrington tries to erase the fact that she weaponized the term by proving she 'understands' its weight. But in a highly commercialized arena like the WNBA, saturated with identity politics, no word exists in a vacuum. Posting that tag immediately after a physical altercation and ejection is a precise seizure of the cognitive entry point. The subsequent 'I wasn't targeting an individual' is merely a tactical retreat after feeling the structural blowback.

Cunningham's role is equally ironic. Her previous rhetoric about 'protecting young girls' from transgender athletes is a classic use of the 'biological wall' to construct a protectionist narrative. This is an attempt to maintain a traditional, exclusive definition of feminine identity. When two players with opposing identity politics collide physically on the court, this flagrant foul ceases to be a mere basketball play; it becomes a direct clash of two power narratives at the structural level.

The league's 'task force' is the most standard disguise for structural violence. Using a committee with no real enforcement power to appease players is a PR stunt that uses 'procedural justice' to mask 'distributive injustice.' In this environment, whether it's Carrington proving her intellect or Cunningham building walls via 'protection,' both are merely playing roles within a masculine-centric athletic system to fight for a sliver of interpretive power.
