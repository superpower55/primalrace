亲吻衰退：当浪漫爱成为一种过时的生物性 ScamThe Kissing Recession: When Romantic Love Becomes an Obsolete Biological Scam

性别 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-13 § 链接
亲吻的消失不是社交萎缩，而是女性在博弈中选择性撤回的红利。
The decline of kissing is not social atrophy, but a strategic withdrawal of investment by women.

这篇报道把亲吻的减少描述成一场“危机”或“社交萎缩”，这种叙事典型的男性中心。它在哀悼一种能力的丧失——男性失去了通过简单的物理接触就获得情感确认的低成本路径。在加尔通的暴力三角里，这种“丧失”其实是结构性暴力的反向修正：当女性意识到所谓的“浪漫爱”叙事大多是利男的 scam 时，她们开始在最基础的表达层面上撤回投资。

文中提到的 Gen Z 的“风险厌恶”和“缺乏自信”，其实是主体性觉醒后的防御机制。在 #MeToo 之后的 cultural layer 中，亲吻不再是无害的“试探”，而是一次关于 consent 的严肃博弈。对于女性来说，与其在不确定的博弈中扮演一个“被动等待被亲吻”的客体，不如直接 opt-out。这种撤回不是因为“不敢”，而是因为在当前的成本收益比下，亲吻已经不再是她们的最优解表达。

最讽刺的是那些感到“被抛弃”的男性。他们将亲吻视为一种“被需要”的验证，试图通过找回这个动作来缓解存在性焦虑。但他们忽略了，亲吻的意义是由解释权定义 the meaning 的。当女性不再愿意将亲吻作为进入亲密关系的低门槛入场券时，这个动作就失去了它在父权结构中的武器化功能。这根本不是什么“社交萎缩”，而是一次安静的、大规模的个体自治运动。

这种“衰退”其实是 good_news。它标志着一种基于生物本能的、未经审视的共谋正在瓦解。人们开始意识到，真正的 connection 无法通过一个机械的物理动作来达成。当人们不再为了“亲吻而亲吻”，我们才真正开始讨论什么是公正的表达。

The article frames the decline of kissing as a 'crisis' or 'social atrophy,' a classic masculine-centric narrative. It mourns the loss of a low-cost path for men to gain emotional validation. In the Violence Triangle, this 'loss' is actually a correction of structural violence: as women realize the romantic love narrative is largely a scam favoring men, they withdraw their investment at the most basic level of expression.

The 'risk aversion' and 'lack of confidence' attributed to Gen Z are, in fact, defense mechanisms of an awakening subjectivity. In the cultural layer post-#MeToo, a kiss is no longer a harmless 'probe' but a serious game of consent. For women, rather than playing the object 'waiting to be kissed,' opting out becomes the true optimal expression given the current cost-benefit ratio.

It is ironic to see men feeling 'abandoned,' treating the kiss as a validation of being wanted to soothe their existential anxiety. They forget that the meaning of a kiss is defined by whoever holds the power of interpretation. When women stop using the kiss as a low-barrier entry ticket to intimacy, the act loses its weaponized function within the patriarchal structure. This is not 'social atrophy'; it is a quiet, mass movement of individual autonomy.

This 'recession' is actually good_news. It signals the collapse of an unexamined complicity based on biological instinct. We are realizing that true connection cannot be achieved through a mechanical physical act. When we stop kissing just for the sake of kissing, we finally begin to discuss what Just Expressions really are.
