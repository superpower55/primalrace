监狱的“旋转门”：一场关于控制权的结构性骗局The Prison Revolving Door: A Structural Scam of Control

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-30 § 链接
当回收率超过100%，监狱已不再是惩戒机构，而是一个巨大的、通过剥夺主体性来维持的共谋场域。
When the recall rate exceeds 100%, prison is no longer about punishment, but a complicity field designed to permanently strip away agency.

Recall rate 超过 100%。这个数字在数学上很荒诞，在政治上则极其精准：它标志着一个系统的彻底失效，或者说，标志着这个系统终于完成了它的真实目的——将特定人群永久地锁定在“被管理”的状态中。

绝大多数被拉回监狱的人并没有犯新罪，仅仅是因为违反了所谓的 licence conditions（许可条件）。什么是 licence conditions？它是一套精密的 structural violence。要求一个刚出狱、可能面临 homeless、药物成瘾或精神崩溃的人必须在特定时间出现在特定地点，这本身就是一种 weaponized 表达。它不是在提供回归社会的路径，而是在制造一个必然失败的陷阱。只要你因为贫穷而无法维持一个“approved address”，你就再次成为了系统可以合法回收的客体。

这背后是一场典型的共谋。政府在公共空间表演“缓解监狱压力”，通过 early release 释放囚犯；但在结构层，他们通过提高 recall 的频率来对冲这种表演。这种机制确保了监狱人口的基数在一个可控的动态平衡中，同时让那些被释放者在一种极度的不安全感中生活——你的自由不是被赋予的，而是被临时“租用”的，只要你表现出任何不符合系统预期的瑕疵，你就会被立刻回收。

这就是元暴力的具体运作方式：用“法律”和“秩序”的文明掩体，掩盖对人类主体性的持续剥夺。当一个系统通过制造失败来证明自己存在的必要性时，它就已经成了一个巨大的 scam。所谓的“正义”在这里被异化成了对“可预测性”的病态追求，而代价就是数以万计的人在旋转门中被磨灭了寻找真.最优解表达的所有可能性。

A recall rate exceeding 100%. Mathematically absurd, but politically precise: it marks the total failure of a system, or rather, the achievement of its true purpose—locking specific populations into a permanent state of 'being managed.'

Most of those recalled haven't committed new crimes; they've simply breached 'licence conditions.' What are these conditions? They are a form of structural violence. Requiring a recently released individual—likely facing homelessness, addiction, or mental collapse—to adhere to rigid bureaucratic schedules is a weaponized expression. It doesn't provide a path back to society; it manufactures a trap where failure is inevitable. The moment you cannot afford an 'approved address' due to poverty, you become a subject to be legally reclaimed.

This is a classic case of complicity. In the public sphere, the government performs 'prison decongestion' via early release. Yet, at the structural level, they offset this performance by increasing the frequency of recalls. This mechanism ensures the prison population remains in a controlled dynamic equilibrium, while keeping releasees in a state of extreme precariousness—your freedom is not granted, but 'leased,' and any flaw in your performance of compliance triggers immediate reclamation.

This is how meta-violence operates: using the civil cover of 'law' and 'order' to mask the continuous stripping of human agency. When a system manufactures failure to justify its own existence, it becomes a massive scam. 'Justice' here is distorted into a pathological pursuit of predictability, and the cost is the erasure of any possibility for thousands to find their true optimal expression.
