马斯克的“心理战”叙事：一种典型的权力防御机制Musk's 'Psyop' Narrative: A Textbook Power Defense Mechanism

科技 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-10 § 链接
当事实无法被反驳时，权力者通过定义“叙事本身是伪造的”来夺取解释权。
When facts are irrefutable, power seizes control by defining the narrative itself as a fabrication.

这出戏极其经典。一边是 Anthropic 的研究员在用生物学和数学的逻辑警告 extinction（灭绝），另一边是马斯克在用 X 的流量逻辑将其定义为 psyop（心理战）。

请注意这里的博弈点：研究员们在谈论 structural violence——即一个缺乏安全计划的 ASI（超级人工智能）如何对全人类构成潜在的结构性暴力。而马斯克在做什么？他在进行典型的 weaponization（武器化）。他并不试图在科学层面反驳“AI 是否危险”，因为那需要证据和逻辑；他直接跳到了 meta 层，通过将对方定义为“民主党的 PR 操作”，把一场关于生存的科学讨论，强行转化为一场关于党派斗争的身份政治博弈。

这是权力者最常用的认知入口：当你无法在事实层面赢过对方，就宣布对方的“事实”是某种阴谋的产物。通过把警告者标记为“被资助的演员”，马斯克成功地让公众的注意力从“AI 是否会杀掉我们”转移到了“谁在操纵这场舆论战”上。这是一种极其高效的掩盖手段，因为它让所有严肃的警告在被听到之前，就先被贴上了“政治操弄”的标签。

最讽刺的是，Anthropic 刚刚发布了他们拦截生物武器尝试的报告。这意味着 Potential − Actual 的差额正在剧增，而马斯克等共谋者却在忙着给这个差额贴上“心理战”的封条。当这种“定义现实”的能力被滥用，真正的危险不再仅仅是代码，而是我们已经失去了讨论危险的语言。

This is a classic performance. On one side, Anthropic researchers are warning of extinction using the logic of biology and mathematics; on the other, Elon Musk is using X's traffic logic to label it a psyop.

Observe the game here: the researchers are discussing structural violence—how an ASI without a viable safety plan poses a systemic threat to all humanity. What is Musk doing? He is engaging in blatant weaponization. He doesn't attempt to refute the 'danger of AI' on a scientific level, as that would require evidence and logic. Instead, he leaps to the meta level, transforming a scientific debate about survival into a partisan identity politics battle by framing it as a 'Democratic PR operation.'

This is the most common cognitive entry point for those in power: when you cannot win on the level of facts, you declare the 'facts' to be products of a conspiracy. By labeling the warners as 'funded actors,' Musk successfully shifts public attention from 'Will AI kill us?' to 'Who is manipulating this discourse?' It is a highly efficient concealment tactic, ensuring that all serious warnings are tagged as 'political manipulation' before they are even heard.

The irony is peak: Anthropic just released a report on dismantling a bio-weapon attempt. The gap between Potential and Actual is widening, while Musk and his complicity network are busy sealing that gap with a 'psyop' label. When the power to 'define reality' is abused this way, the real danger is no longer just the code, but the fact that we have lost the language to discuss the danger.
