用海峡换解除封锁Trading a Strait for a Blockade

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 Al Jazeera ↗ 2026-11-03 § 链接
新闻摘要特朗普与万斯表示美伊谈判取得进展，伊朗称其七步方案可在两天内实施，包括重开霍尔木兹海峡并重启核谈判，前提是美国解除海上封锁、取消制裁并保证停止打击。伊朗最高领袖已授权政府加速谈判，卡塔尔和巴基斯坦继续斡旋。
The newsTrump and Vance said talks with Iran are making progress; Tehran says its seven-step plan, including reopening the Strait of Hormuz and resuming nuclear talks, could be implemented in two days if the US lifts its naval blockade, repeals sanctions and guarantees no further strikes. Iran's supreme leader has authorised faster talks, with Qatar and Pakistan mediating.
谈判桌上的每一方都在给别人的身体定价。
Everyone at the table is pricing someone else's body.

霍尔木兹海峡能不能开，取决于美国肯不肯解除对伊朗港口的海上封锁。这条海峡是伊朗手里唯一还能换东西的筹码，而它换的东西里没有一项和伊朗女性有关。

万斯说要「可验证的承诺」，特朗普说油价掉了快十美元一桶，掉得「很不错」。整篇报道里最实在的数字是油价，不是任何一个人的处境。战争的开始和结束都被折算成桶价，折算成中期选举之前的时机——特朗普自己说了，11 月 3 日之后还可以再打。这就是第三章那套东西：战争叙事从来不是关于战争，是关于谁能在叙事里拿到定价权。

伊朗这边也一样。最高领袖给政府「授权」加速谈判，外长说准备好了外交也准备好了对抗，方案从一周压到两天。压缩的是时间，不是条件。四个条件一个没少：解除封锁、取消制裁、保证不再打击、整个地区停火。巴盖伊没有说德黑兰改了口，也没有说华盛顿点了头。所谓「进展」，是两边各自对着自己的观众说了一句体面话。

两边都是共谋者，共谋的标的物是这片地区所有人的命。谈判桌上坐着的是美国和伊朗的男性决策层，被讨论的是海峡、核计划、制裁、油价。黎巴嫩被写进条件里，作为一个地名。没有人问一句：这几个月里，谁的家被炸了，谁在封锁里买不到药，谁的身体被当作「地区局势」的一部分。

特朗普说「要么达成协议，要么我们直接结束它」。结束它。这个「它」里包含的东西，他不用列举。

Whether the Strait of Hormuz reopens depends on whether Washington lifts its naval blockade of Iranian ports. The strait is the only chip Tehran has left to trade, and not one item on its shopping list concerns Iranian women.

Vance wants a "verifiable commitment." Trump notes oil fell almost $10 a barrel, which is "very nicely." The most concrete number in the whole report is the barrel price, not anyone's situation. The start and end of a war get converted into barrel prices and into timing ahead of the midterms — Trump himself said the US could strike after November 3. This is the weaponisation playbook: war narrative is never about war, it is about who gets to set the price inside the narrative.

Iran's side is the same. The supreme leader grants the government "powers" to speed up talks; the foreign minister says Tehran is ready for diplomacy and confrontation alike; the plan compresses from a week to two days. What compresses is time, not terms. All four conditions remain: lift the blockade, repeal sanctions, guarantee no further strikes, secure a regional ceasefire. Baghaei gives no sign Tehran moved and none that Washington agreed. The "progress" is each side saying something respectable to its own audience.

Both are co-conspirators, and what they are conspiring over is everyone else's life in the region. The men deciding are the US and Iranian leadership; what is on the table is the strait, the nuclear programme, sanctions, oil. Lebanon appears in the conditions as a place name. Nobody asks who lost a home these past months, who cannot buy medicine under blockade, whose body counts as part of the "regional situation."

Trump says Tehran will either "make a deal, or we'll just end it." End it. What is inside that "it" he does not have to list.
