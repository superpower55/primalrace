Meta的赔偿金是给受害者的止痛药，而非手术刀Meta's Settlement: A Painkiller for Victims, Not a Scalpel for the System

科技 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-27 § 链接
用时间限制代替算法重构，是用PR掩盖结构性暴力
Replacing algorithmic restructuring with time limits is just PR masking structural violence.

Meta这次的 multibillion-dollar settlement 是一场典型的武器化叙事。州政府和公司共同演出了一场“胜利”的戏码，把“时间限制”和“家长控制”包装成救赎。但这在逻辑上是个巨大的 scam：如果产品本身就是设计用来成瘾的毒药，限制服用时间并不意味着毒药变成了补药。正如 Béjar 所言，这就像在说“你每天只能抽两小时烟”，烟草本身的致癌性并没有因为时间的量化而消失。

从暴力三角来看，这完全是 structural violence 的 PR 版本。Meta 试图通过调整几个 default settings（如关闭美颜滤镜、设置夜间模式）来制造一种“我们在改进”的文化假象，但真正制造暴力的核心——那套旨在掠夺注意力、诱导社会比较、精准推送焦虑的 algorithmic design——在协议中被完整地保留了。这意味着 Meta 依然握有定义青少年“认知入口”的权力，而监管方却在满足于几个表面上的开关。

这场和解本质上是一次共谋 (complicity)。政府通过一个亮眼的赔偿数字完成了政治KPI，而 Meta 则用金钱买断了对算法底层逻辑的审计权。他们共同地将一个系统性的公共卫生危机，降格为了一个关于“时间管理”的个体习惯问题。当独立审计 (independent oversight) 被排除在协议之外，这种所谓的“胜利”不过是给一个持续出血的伤口贴了一张昂贵的创可贴。

最讽刺的是 Meta 发言人的逻辑：他们强调青少年从社交媒体中获得了“巨大价值”。在元暴力的叙事里，这种“价值”通常指的就是被物化的社交货币和被算法喂养的虚假存在感。只要算法的掠夺机制不被拆除，所有的 safety settings 都只是在囚笼上刷了一层亮漆。

Meta's multibillion-dollar settlement is a classic piece of weaponized narrative. The state governments and the corporation have staged a performance of 'victory,' packaging 'time limits' and 'parental controls' as redemption. Logically, this is a scam: if a product is designed as an addictive poison, restricting the dosage doesn't turn the poison into medicine. As Béjar rightly noted, it's like saying, 'You can only smoke for two hours a day'—the carcinogenicity of tobacco doesn't vanish because of a time limit.

In terms of the Violence Triangle, this is the PR version of structural violence. Meta attempts to create a cultural illusion of improvement by toggling a few default settings—disabling beauty filters or adding a 'night mode'—while the core engine of violence, the algorithmic design built to plunder attention and induce social comparison, remains untouched. Meta retains the power over the cognitive entry points of teenagers, while regulators settle for a few superficial switches.

This settlement is essentially an act of complicity. The government checks its political KPI boxes with a flashy number, while Meta buys its way out of an independent audit of its underlying logic. Together, they downgrade a systemic public health crisis into a trivial issue of individual 'time management.' Without independent oversight, this so-called 'victory' is nothing more than an expensive band-aid on a hemorrhaging wound.

Most ironic is Meta's spokesperson claiming the 'huge value' teens derive from the platform. In the masculine-centric narrative of meta-violence, this 'value' usually refers to objectified social currency and a fake sense of existence fed by algorithms. As long as the predatory mechanism remains, all safety settings are merely a fresh coat of paint on a cage.
