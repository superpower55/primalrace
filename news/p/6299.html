万圣节红毯上的假血Fake Blood on the Red Carpet

文娱 文化层 Deadline ↗ 2026-11-01 § 链接
一场装扮游戏，把身体当成了可拆装的零件。
A costume party where the body becomes detachable parts.

一条断手，blinged-out，被当作红毯造型的顶点。这不是恐怖，这是把身体拆成配件来展示的产业逻辑——断手可以镶钻，身体就可以被定价。

报道里只写了谁穿了什么、谁到场。没有写的是：这个派对的入场券是什么。一个超模主办、明星云集的夜晚，表达在这里不是身份的确证，是身份的商品化——你穿什么，等于你值多少。

万圣节的装扮本来是最自由的表达：你可以成为任何东西。但这里的装扮恰恰相反，它被红毯、被镜头、被"谁穿了什么"的清单收编成一次筛选。谁被拍到，谁没被拍到，名单本身就是阶级。

Klum 连续 25 年办这个派对，报道说她是在"捍卫自己的皇冠"。皇冠这个词用得准确：这不是玩，这是守擂。

真正缺席的是那些没被邀请的人——他们今晚也在装扮，只是没有镜头。

A severed hand, blinged-out, presented as the peak of a red-carpet look. This isn't horror. It's the industrial logic of taking a body apart into accessories—a hand can be diamond-studded, so a body can be priced.

The report lists who wore what and who showed up. What it doesn't say is what the ticket costs. A supermodel-hosted, star-studded night: expression here isn't a confirmation of identity, it's the commodification of identity—what you wear equals what you're worth.

Halloween costume is supposed to be the freest kind of expression: you can become anything. But this costume does the opposite. It's absorbed by the red carpet, the cameras, the "who wore what" list, turned into a filter. Who gets photographed and who doesn't—the list itself is class.

Klum has hosted this party for 25 years straight, and the report says she's defending her crown. Crown is the right word: this isn't play, it's holding the title.

What's truly absent are the people who weren't invited—they're dressing up tonight too, just without a camera.
