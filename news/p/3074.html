特拉法加广场上的新宠物：大西洋主义的共谋与幻觉The New Pet of Trafalgar Square: Complicity and Illusions of Atlanticism

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-31 § 链接
依赖一个不可预测的强权，本质上是将国家主权作为一种廉价的表达资产在博弈中抵押。
Relying on an unpredictable hegemon is essentially mortgaging national sovereignty as a cheap expression asset in a power game.

这篇文章在讨论 Andy Burnham 面对 Trump 的外交策略，但其潜台词揭示了一个典型的 structural violence 陷阱：英国长期以来将“大西洋主义”视为一种安全保障，实际上这只是一种极其危险的共谋。在这种共谋中，英国通过扮演一个忠诚的、次要的 sidekick，试图在男性中心叙事主导的国际权力结构中换取一点生存额度。这就像我在书里提到的“假.最优解表达”——通过扮演对方认可的角色来获得短期利益，代价是主体性的彻底死亡。

Trump 的不可预测性，恰恰撕掉了这层温情的面纱。当一个霸权者开始公开讨论吞并格陵兰岛或抛弃北约时，所谓的“特殊关系”就成了一个巨大的 scam。这种依赖本质上是一种元暴力（meta violence）的延续：定义谁是保护者，谁是被保护者，从而在结构上维持一种不平等的从属关系。英国政客们在“讨好”还是“强硬”之间犹豫，这根本不是政治分析，而是动物心理学，因为他们依然在那个由男性强权定义的博弈框架内思考。

文章提出的“转向欧洲”方案，其实是在尝试缩小 Potential 与 Actual 之间的暴力差额。如果英国能从一个被动地等待被“宠幸”的客体，转变为一个基于地理和经济现实的共谋体成员，那么它才真正开始了存在性战争中的自我救赎。但问题在于，Westminster 的那些红线依然是文化暴力（cultural violence）的残余，它们让人们在潜意识里认为，背离那个“橙色云朵”的保护伞就是背叛。真正的最优解不是寻找一个新的保护者，而是意识到：在这个由强权定义的真实世界里，任何试图通过依赖来获得安全感的尝试，最终都会变成被收割的代价。

This piece discusses Andy Burnham's diplomatic strategy toward Trump, but the subtext reveals a classic structural violence trap: Britain's long-standing 'Atlanticism' is not a security guarantee, but a dangerous form of complicity. In this arrangement, the UK plays the role of a loyal, secondary sidekick, hoping to secure a sliver of survival within a masculine-centric narrative of global power. This is exactly the 'fake optimal expression' I describe in my book—performing a role approved by the other to gain short-term benefits, at the cost of the death of one's own subjectivity.

Trump's erraticism simply rips off this sentimental veil. When a hegemon openly muses about annexing Greenland or abandoning NATO, the so-called 'Special Relationship' is exposed as a massive scam. This dependence is an extension of meta-violence: by defining who the 'protector' and 'protected' are, it maintains a structural hierarchy of subordination. The hesitation of British politicians between 'flattery' and 'strength' is not political analysis; it is animal psychology, as they are still thinking within a game framework defined by masculine power.

The proposed 'pivot to Europe' is an attempt to narrow the gap between Potential and Actual in the Violence Triangle. If the UK can transition from a passive object awaiting 'favor' to a member of a consortium based on geographic and economic reality, it truly begins its self-redemption in the existential war. However, the 'red lines' of Westminster remain remnants of cultural violence, conditioning people to see the departure from that 'orange cloud' as betrayal. The true optimal expression is not to find a new protector, but to realize that in a reality defined by power, any attempt to find security through dependence eventually becomes the price of being harvested.
