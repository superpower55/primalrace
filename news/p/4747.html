教育的Monastic Scam：用考试掩盖存在性的阉割The Monastic Scam: Existential Castration via Standardized Testing

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-04 § 链接
教育系统的失效不是技术故障，而是通过剥夺表达能力实现的结构性暴力。
The failure of education is not a technical glitch, but structural violence achieved by stripping away the power of expression.

英国出现100万Neets（不就业、不教育、不培训青年）被描述为一场“危机”，但本质上这是一次大规模的 structural violence。学校变成了 Simon Jenkins 所说的“修道院”（monastic institutions），它们并不在乎学生是否能生存，而是在乎能否被“测量”。

当教育系统将所有价值锚定在 GCSE 这种可量化的指标上时，它实际上在执行一种元暴力：它定义了什么是“有价值的知识”，而将一个生命在社会中生存最核心的能力——表达（Expression）、自信与社交博弈——定义为不可测量的、无需教授的冗余。这是一种极其阴险的 scam。系统通过让学生在考试中获得某种虚假的“成就感”，掩盖了他们在真实世界中主体性的死亡。一个能拿到好成绩却无法在面试中清晰表达自我的人，实际上是被教育系统阉割了存在性战争的武器。

更讽刺的是，这些青年在校门外通过手机进入电子内向（electronic introversion）状态。社交媒体成为了另一种 weaponized expression，它用算法制造的伪社交替代了真实的博弈。当年轻人习惯于在屏幕后扮演角色而非在现实中确立身份时，他们就失去了寻找“真.最优解表达”的能力，只能在焦虑和福利补贴中成为结构的共谋者。

不要被那些呼吁增加“职业培训”的药方欺骗。如果教育的底层逻辑仍然是“由权威定义事实并测量结果”，那么无论增加多少职业课程，都只是在为资本市场制造更标准化的零件。真正的 good_news 应该是教育权的让渡：让学生重新夺回定义自己身份的解释权，而不是在一个19世纪的僵化结构里，等待被某个 Milburn 报告定义为“失败者”。

The emergence of one million Neets in Britain is framed as a 'crisis,' but it is fundamentally a manifestation of structural violence. Schools have become 'monastic institutions,' as Simon Jenkins notes, indifferent to whether students can survive, obsessed only with what can be 'measured.'

By anchoring all value to quantifiable metrics like GCSEs, the education system enacts a form of meta-violence: it defines what constitutes 'valuable knowledge' while dismissing the most core competencies for survival—Expression, confidence, and social gaming—as immeasurable redundancies. This is a sophisticated scam. The system grants students a false sense of achievement through exams while masking the death of their subjectivity in the real world. A student who aces a test but cannot articulate themselves in an interview has had their weapons for the existential war castrated by the system.

Even more ironic is the 'electronic introversion' occurring outside the school gates. Social media has become another form of weaponized expression, replacing authentic social struggle with algorithmically manufactured pseudo-socialization. When youth learn to perform roles behind screens rather than establish identities in reality, they lose the capacity to find their true optimal expression, remaining mere complicitors in a structure of anxiety and state benefits.

Do not be fooled by prescriptions for more 'vocational training.' As long as the underlying logic of education remains 'authority defines facts and measures results,' adding vocational courses is merely producing more standardized parts for the capital market. True good_news would be the surrender of educational power: allowing students to reclaim the interpretative right to define their own identities, rather than being labeled as 'failures' by some Milburn report within a rigid 19th-century structure.
