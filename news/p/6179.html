三十八个人漂在海上，荷兰说很少见38 People Adrift, and the Netherlands Calls It Rare

国际 结构层 · 直接层 Al Jazeera ↗ 2026-10-31 § 链接
新闻摘要荷兰当局在泽兰省海岸救起一艘漂流橡皮艇上的38人，均为男性，其中36人来自阿富汗，2名苏丹籍男子涉嫌偷渡被捕。当局尚不清楚船只来自何处、驶向何方，周日将展开调查。
The newsDutch authorities rescued 38 men adrift in a rubber boat off Zeeland province; 36 were from Afghanistan and two Sudanese men were arrested on suspicion of people smuggling. It is unclear where the boat came from or was heading, and an investigation opens Sunday.
船没有马达，人没有救生衣，解释权在岸上
No motor, no life vests, and the explanation stays on shore.

三十八个人，一条没有马达的橡皮艇，漂在荷兰泽兰省外海。没有一件救生衣。军方警察发言人 Wallin 说，不知道他们从哪来，法国、荷兰还是比利时，也不知道船往哪去。

不知道从哪来、不知道往哪去，这句话本身就是这条新闻的核心。三十八个人里有三十六个阿富汗人，两个苏丹人被当作蛇头抓了。前者是货物，后者是嫌犯。中间那层把人装进橡皮艇、拆掉马达、收钱的人，不在船上，也就不在叙述里。

这是结构暴力最标准的形态：没有具体的施暴者站在现场，暴力藏在把人变成可运输单位的整条链路上。战争把阿富汗人推出国境，边境政策把合法入境的通道堵死，需求还在，于是市场接手，用橡皮艇和没有救生衣的夜晚来定价。荷兰警察救的是结果，不是原因。原因在喀布尔，在布鲁塞尔，在那些把“管控边境”当作政绩、把通道越收越窄的会议室里。

Wallin 说，这种事通常发生在法国和比利时，在荷兰很少见。很少见，是相对于荷兰自己的经验说的。对那三十八个人来说，这不是“罕见事件”，这是他们唯一的路径。一句“罕见”，把结构性的常态包装成了偶发的意外，让读者以为这是一次异常，而不是一个系统在正常运转。

两个苏丹人被逮捕，罪名是人口走私。这个罪名成立的话，他们要为这条船负责。但一条没有马达的船，漂在离岸不知多远的地方，三十八个人一个救生衣都没有——这不是两个蛇头能独立组织的规模。抓两个苏丹人，是给公众一个可指认的对象，让整件事看起来有施害者、有受害者、有执法者，闭环完整。真正的施害者不在船上，也不在泽兰省的海岸线上。

报道写得很干净：人数、国籍、省份、有没有救生衣、谁被逮捕、周几开调查。没有写这三十八个人现在在哪，有没有人受伤，有没有人申请庇护，调查之后他们会怎样。这些人被救上来了，然后从报道里消失了。救人是把直接暴力拦下来了，但拦下来之后的那一段，报道没写，荷兰当局也没说。

三十八个人全是男性，这倒是值得停一下。阿富汗的出境人群里男性占多数，通常被解释成为了先出去、再把家人接出来。但这个解释本身也说明了一件事：能上这条船的，是家里被选中去冒险的那一个。剩下的人留在原地，等一个不知道会不会来的消息。

一条没有马达的船漂到荷兰，被当作“罕见”记录下来。罕见的是荷兰的海岸线，不是这条船。

Thirty-eight people, a rubber boat with no motor, drifting off Zeeland. Not one life vest. Military police spokesperson Wallin says they don't know where the boat came from — France, the Netherlands or Belgium — and they don't know where it was heading.

Not knowing where they came from, not knowing where they were going: that sentence is the news. Thirty-six of the thirty-eight are Afghan. Two Sudanese men are held as suspected smugglers. The first group is cargo, the second is suspects. The layer in between — the people who put thirty-eight men in a dinghy, stripped the motor, took the money — is not on the boat, and so is not in the story.

This is structural violence in its standard form: no single visible perpetrator on the scene, the violence embedded in a whole chain that turns people into transportable units. War pushes Afghans out; border policy closes the legal routes; the demand remains, so the market steps in and prices the crossing in rubber and a night without life vests. The Dutch police rescued the outcome, not the cause. The cause is in Kabul, in Brussels, in the meeting rooms where tightening the border counts as an achievement.

Wallin says this usually happens in France and Belgium, and is rare in the Netherlands. Rare relative to Dutch experience. For the thirty-eight, this was not a rare event; it was the only route. Calling it rare packages a structural constant as an accident, and lets the reader file it under anomaly rather than under a system working as designed.

Two Sudanese men are arrested on suspicion of people smuggling. If the charge holds, they answer for the boat. But a boat with no motor, drifting an unknown distance offshore, thirty-eight men and zero life vests — that is not the scale two smugglers organize alone. Arresting two Sudanese men gives the public an identifiable figure: a perpetrator, victims, enforcers, a closed loop. The real organizers are not on the boat and not on the Zeeland coast.

The report is clean: numbers, nationalities, province, life vests, arrests, the day the investigation opens. It does not say where the thirty-eight are now, whether anyone was hurt, whether anyone will apply for asylum, what happens to them after the investigation. They were pulled out of the water and then they leave the story. The rescue stopped direct violence. What comes after the rescue is unwritten — by the reporter and by the Dutch authorities alike.

All thirty-eight are men, which is worth a pause. Afghan departures skew male, usually explained as sending someone ahead to bring the family later. That explanation also says something else: the one chosen to take the risk is the one who gets on the boat. The rest stay behind, waiting for a message that may never come.

A boat with no motor drifts to the Netherlands and gets recorded as rare. What is rare is the Dutch coastline, not the boat.
