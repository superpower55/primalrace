所谓的“务实”，不过是投降者的自我洗脑Pragmatism as a Mask for Surrender

国际 文化层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-20 § 链接
当进步派开始使用压迫者的词汇，他们就成了共谋者。
When progressives adopt the oppressor's lexicon, they become complicit in their own erasure.

AOC 把 2020 年的社会正义运动戏称为 "Woke 1" 并称其为 "crazyyyyy"，这根本不是什么政治演进，而是一次典型的主体性死亡。在存在性战争中，最危险的时刻不是被敌人攻击，而是当你开始用敌人的尺度来衡量自己。"Woke" 这个词早已被右翼 weaponized，从一个关于觉醒的形容词变成了用来嘲讽黑人解放运动的标签。当 AOC 和其他自由派开始接纳这个 branding 时，他们实际上是在向元暴力（meta violence）递交投名状。

这种行为在本质上是寻求一个“假.最优解表达”。他们试图通过扮演一个“理性的、成熟的、不再激进”的政治人物，来换取在民主党建制派结构中的生存空间和未来的权力席位。这种所谓的 pragmatism 是一个巨大的 scam：它要求你首先稀释自己的核心诉求，然后用一个被敌方定义的词汇将过去的自己客体化。当一个政治人物开始嘲笑曾经的激进，她其实是在告诉选民：我终于学会了如何在这个男性中心且保守的权力结构中地道地生存。

这正是共谋者理论的教科书案例。AOC 此时的身份不再是那个反企业、反建制的挑战者，而是一个试图在既定秩序中获利的共谋者。她通过将 2020 年的诉求定义为“疯狂”，完成了与建制派的心理结盟。这种从 structural 层面的背叛，最终会转化为 cultural 层的暴力——它告诉所有真正的底层抗争者：你们的愤怒只是一个阶段性的“潮流”，而我的权力才是永恒的现实。

最讽刺的是，这种“进化”是以牺牲那些依然在前线面对警察暴力的原初种族为代价的。当解释权被让渡给建制派，那些关于“废除警察”的真.最优解表达就被打上了“疯狂”的标签。这种叙事的反转，让 Potential 和 Actual 之间的差额再次扩大。如果一个进步派领袖认为承认制度性暴力是“疯狂”的，那么这个系统永远不会产生真正的公正表达。

AOC dismissing the 2020 social justice movement as "Woke 1" and calling it "crazyyyyy" is not political evolution; it is a textbook case of the death of subjectivity. In an existential war, the most dangerous moment is not when you are attacked by the enemy, but when you begin to measure yourself by the enemy's yardstick. The term "woke" has long been weaponized by the right, shifting from an adjective of awareness to a mocking label used to ridicule Black liberation. By adopting this branding, AOC and other liberals are effectively submitting a letter of allegiance to meta violence.

This is a pursuit of a "false optimal expression." They attempt to play the role of the "rational, mature, and no longer radical" politician to secure survival and power within the Democratic establishment. This so-called pragmatism is a scam: it requires you to first dilute your core demands and then objectify your past self using a term defined by the opposition. When a politician mocks her former radicalism, she is signaling that she has finally learned how to survive authentically within a masculine-centric, conservative power structure.

This is a classic example of the co-conspirators theory. AOC's identity has shifted from an anti-corporate challenger to a co-conspirator seeking profit within the existing order. By labeling 2020's demands as "crazy," she completes her psychological alignment with the establishment. This betrayal at the structural level manifests as cultural violence—telling grassroots activists that their rage was merely a temporary "trend," while her power is the only enduring reality.

Most ironically, this "evolution" comes at the expense of the Primal Race still facing police brutality on the front lines. When the power of interpretation is surrendered to the establishment, the true optimal expression of "abolition" is branded as "insanity." This narrative reversal widens the gap between Potential and Actual. If a progressive leader finds the acknowledgment of systemic violence "crazy," the system will never achieve a Just Expression.
