Gianni Infantino 的逻辑很简单：他认为自己就是 FIFA。这种将个人意志与机构意志完全等同的叙事，是典型的 masculine-centric narrative。在元暴力的逻辑里，总统不是在服务于足球，而是在利用足球这个认知入口，通过将世界杯部分权益 sell-off 给私人投资者，来完成一次权力与资本的深度共谋。

Kevin Lamour 的被开除，本质上是一次关于“表达”的存在性战争。Lamour 试图通过一次公正的表达 (Just Expression) 来夺回机构的定义权——他指出总统应该是 FIFA 的服务者，而非其化身。这种表达直接挑战了 Infantino 的权力基座。在 FIFA 这种高度垄断的权力结构中，任何试图将“公共服务”置于“个人意志”之上的尝试，都会被视为对系统最优解的背叛。

有趣的是，Lamour 曾与 Infantino 在 Uefa 长期共事。这意味着他曾经是这套权力运作机制的深度参与者，甚至可能是共谋者。但当他选择公开反对 FFE 计划并接受被开除的代价时，他实际上是在进行一次主体性的回收。他说“今晚能睡个好觉”，这是一种典型的通过否决现状来达成自我确证的表达。

FIFA 的官方声明充满了典型的 cultural violence：用“工作关系结束”这种中立且文明的措辞，掩盖掉内部的 contempt 和 intimidation。这种文明的掩体，让一次政治清洗看起来像是一次正常的人事变动。但事实是，在这个共谋场域里，不愿扮演角色的人，唯一的出路就是消失。

Under the logic of meta-violence, the president is not serving football; he is using football as a cognitive entry point to execute a deep complicity between power and private capital via the World Cup sell-off plan.

Kevin Lamour's sacking is essentially an existential war over expression. Lamour attempted a Just Expression to reclaim the definition of the institution—stating that the president should be at FIFA's service, not its embodiment. This expression directly challenged Infantino's power base. In a highly monopolized power structure like FIFA, any attempt to prioritize 'public service' over 'personal will' is viewed as a betrayal of the system's fake optimal expression.

It is telling that Lamour and Infantino had a longstanding working relationship at Uefa. He was once a deep participant, perhaps even a co-conspirator, in this machinery. His statement about 'sleeping well tonight' is a classic act of establishing existence by negating an unjust status quo.

FIFA's official statement is saturated with cultural violence: using neutral, 'civilized' phrasing like 'working relationship has ended' to mask internal contempt and intimidation. This veneer of civilization makes a political purge look like a routine HR move. The reality remains: in this field of complicity, those who refuse to play the assigned role have only one destination—disappearance.
