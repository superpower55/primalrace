用经济窒息替代军事疲劳：一场关于“生存额度”的武器化博弈Replacing Military Fatigue with Economic Asphyxiation: A Game of 'Survival Quotas'

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-20 § 链接
当直接暴力触碰物理极限，权力便通过重新定义“经济生存权”来实施结构性屠杀。
When direct violence hits physical limits, power implements structural slaughter by redefining the 'right to economic survival'.

特朗普的这套操作是典型的“武器化表达”转向。当五角大楼承认弹药库见底、目标清单耗尽，direct violence（直接暴力）在物理层面达到了效能上限。于是，权力迅速切换赛道，将战场从轰炸机转移到 treasury（财政部）的制裁名单上。这不是战略升级，而是因为在物理世界里打不动了，所以试图在 structural layer（结构层）制造一场窒息。

The 'Operation Economic Fury' is a scam of narrative. By threatening secondary sanctions, the US is not seeking 'negotiation', but is attempting to seize the definition of who is allowed to exist in the global financial ecosystem. This is the essence of meta-violence: the hegemon claims the sole right to decide which country's 'lifeline' is legitimate and which is 'smuggling'.

Moreover, the complicity of the UAE—suspending trade based on 'detected missiles'—shows how structural violence is maintained through a network of allies who trade their autonomy for the protection of the hegemon. The real game here isn't about Iran's 'resolve', but about the collision of two different structural powers: the US dollar's monopoly vs. China's trading volume. The 'tremendous economic consequences' are just a weaponized expression used to force China into a complicity agreement before the next summit. The cost of this 'economic operation' will not be paid by the leaders in Tehran, but by the Iranian civilians whose actual potential for survival is being subtracted by the gap between political ego and structural reality.
