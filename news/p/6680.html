先判选举无效，再去投票Write the Loss First, Vote Later

国际 文化层 · 结构层 WIRED ↗ 2026-11-02 § 链接
新闻摘要中期选举前，美国司法部向全美50州选举官员发信，警告若不在22天内保存选举记录将面临刑事追诉；此前该部已在加州、阿拉斯加、内华达、华盛顿等地就选民名册和候选人资格提起诉讼，多数被法院驳回。34个联邦地区法院自2025年以来已作出不利于特朗普政府的裁决。
The newsBefore the midterms, the Justice Department sent letters to election officials in all 50 states warning of criminal action if they failed to preserve election records for 22 days. It has also sued California, Alaska, Nevada and Washington over voter rolls and candidate eligibility; most of those efforts have been rejected in court. Since 2025, 34 federal district courts have ruled against the Trump administration.
他们不是在赢选举，是在给输准备说法。
They aren't trying to win the election. They're preparing the alibi for losing it.

司法部这一年干的不是执法，是提前给败选写好判决书。

起诉加州、阿拉斯加、内华达、华盛顿，全部在选举前几周抛出，全部被法院驳回，然后全部上诉。加州那个两万人的名单，被民权组织一查，四成以上的名字根本不在该州现行选民册里；内华达从15903人缩到185人，最后州副检察长写信说这185人的记录显示都是公民。数字一路缩水，诉讼一路加码，这不叫查案，这叫把一个已经破产的指控反复抵押。

Wayment 那句话说得最直白：他们明知赢不了也要立案，为了事后可以说选举有诈。这是武器化的标准动作——夺取的不是判决，是「什么是事实」的制造权。判决输了没关系，只要足够多的人记住「有人在查选民册」，怀疑就已经种下了。

阿拉斯加那场更露骨。联邦法官周日在裁决里说，司法部要求阻止认证的做法很可能侵犯了阿拉斯加人的秘密投票权，几小时后司法部就上诉。州长 Dunleavy 公开说自己不能承诺在调查结束前认证 Peltola 的胜选。选票还没点完，胜负已经先被行政权力预定了。

但这里要看清谁在共谋、回报是什么。Becker 说「他们什么都失败了，我们的制度扛住了」——这句话是真的，34个联邦地区法院的裁决摆在那里。可「扛住了」的代价是各州选举办公室一年里被传票、被要名单、被威胁刑事追诉，是 Shelby Wayment 这类律师和法律援助组织替公众挡下来的每一封信。制度不是自己站住的，是有人一直用手扶着。这些人的名字报道里只出现了几个，剩下的在五十个州的选举办公室里加班。

Levitt 说这是「大量的营销」，说联邦政府根本不管选举，管的是州和地方。我同意一半。营销确实没成功，但营销的目标从来不是说服法院，是说服那四成会把「选举被偷了」当常识的人。真正的暴力在这里不在直接层——没有人被打，选举办公室被「突袭」也没有人被逮捕——它发生在文化层：把「非公民大规模投票」这个已被两党调查反复证伪的说法，重新变成值得讨论的议题。议题一旦成立，结果就可以被拒绝。

德州 Potter County 共和党那份要求手工计票的诉讼，理由是 AI 能黑进任何投票机、投票机零件产自中国，Levitt 说这诉讼「nuts」，还说希望它是用 Comic Sans 写在汉堡王餐垫背面提交的。这个笑话好笑，但它和司法部那五十封信是同一件事的两端：一端是西装，一端是餐垫，说的是同一句话——这次选举不算数。

所以别把这场胜利算得太满。法院挡住了诉讼，没挡住叙事。34比0的裁决记录，抵不过一条 Truth Social 帖子在选举夜之后的传播速度。下一次他们不会起诉，会直接宣布。

What the Justice Department has been doing this year isn't law enforcement. It's drafting the verdict on a loss that hasn't happened yet.

Suits against California, Alaska, Nevada, Washington—all filed weeks before an election, all rejected, all appealed. California's list of 20,000 supposedly noncitizen voters: a civil rights group analyzed it and found over 40 percent of the names weren't even in the state's current voter files. Nevada's 15,903 shrank to 185, and the state's deputy attorney general wrote back that records show those 185 are citizens. The numbers collapse; the litigation escalates. That's not an investigation. That's a bankrupt claim being re-mortgaged.

Wayment put it plainly: they file knowing they can't win, so that afterward they can claim the election was fraudulent. This is the standard weaponization move—not seizing the ruling, but seizing the power to manufacture what counts as fact. Losing in court is fine. As long as enough people remember that someone was looking into the voter rolls, the doubt is planted.

Alaska is the naked version. A federal judge ruled Sunday that the DOJ's bid to block certification likely violated Alaskans' right to a secret ballot; hours later the DOJ appealed. Governor Dunleavy said publicly he couldn't commit to certifying a Peltola win while an investigation was ongoing. The votes aren't counted and the outcome is already reserved by executive power.

But look at who is conspiring, and what they get. Becker says they failed at everything and the systems held. True—34 district court rulings say so. But the price of holding was a year of subpoenas,名单 demands and criminal-threat letters landing on fifty state election offices, and the lawyers like Wayment who stood in front of each one. Institutions don't hold themselves up. Someone's arm is under them. Most of those arms never get named in the story.

Levitt calls it marketing, and notes the federal government doesn't run elections—states and localities do. Half right. The marketing isn't aimed at courts. It's aimed at the people who will treat "the election was stolen" as common sense. The violence here isn't direct—nobody was beaten, the "raids" on election offices produced no arrests. It's cultural: reviving a claim that bipartisan investigations have repeatedly debunked until it becomes a topic worth discussing. Once it's a topic, the result can be refused.

The Potter County GOP suit in Texas demands hand-counting every ballot because AI can supposedly hack any voting machine undetected and because voting machines contain Chinese parts—Levitt calls it "nuts" and wishes it had been filed in Comic Sans on the back of a Burger King placemat. Funny, but it's the same sentence as the fifty DOJ letters, one end in a suit, the other on a placemat: this election doesn't count.

So don't score this as a clean win. The courts blocked the lawsuits. They didn't block the narrative. A 34–0 record loses to one Truth Social post on election night. Next time they won't sue. They'll just declare.
