普京的“保护”：一场以安全为名的国有化 scamPutin's 'Protection': A Nationalization Scam Masked as Security

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-25 § 链接
当国家定义什么是“保护不足”时，所有私有财产都成了待收割的祭品。
When the state defines 'insufficient protection,' all private property becomes a harvestable sacrifice.

这场战争的叙事正在发生一个极其阴险的偏移。普京签署的总统令表面上是在应对乌克兰的无人机袭击，通过所谓的“特殊权力”接管那些“保护不足”的物流和能源企业。这在逻辑上是一个完美的闭环：先允许（或默认）基础设施被摧毁，然后定义这种摧毁为企业的“失职”，最后以“国家安全”和“技术升级”为由，将 Ozon 和 Wildberries 这种具有强大认知入口的电商巨头直接国有化。

这本质上是一次典型的武器化叙事。普京不是在提供救济，而是在利用 structural violence 制造一个合法的掠夺入口。对于 Ozon 这种背负巨额债务、缺乏国家银行背书的公司来说，这根本不是什么“恢复计划”，而是一场生存博弈中的强制清盘。国家通过定义“什么是事实”（即：你没保护好仓库 $ ightarrow$ 你失去了所有权），直接剥夺了企业的主体性。

更深层的元暴力在于，这种“国家接管”的逻辑在俄罗斯的政治生态中是通用的。它将所有经济活动都置于一个男性中心化的、强权式的保护者叙事之下：你弱，所以你需要我的保护；因为你弱，所以你的财产属于我。在这种共谋结构中，企业主在面对国家机器时，其最优解表达被简化为绝对的顺从，否则就等着被定义为“不合格”而被吞噬。

至于西方盟友在基辅讨论的“挖掘库存”提供导弹，这不过是另一种层面的武器化博弈。双方都在利用对方的损耗来重新定义战争的 Potential 和 Actual。但最讽刺的是，无论导弹落在哪个仓库，最终获益的往往是那个掌握了“解释权”的人——他决定了这次爆炸是企业的失败，还是国家接管的契机。

The narrative of this war is shifting in an insidious direction. Putin's presidential decree, ostensibly reacting to Ukrainian drone strikes, allows the state to seize logistics and energy firms deemed to have 'insufficient protection.' It is a perfect logical loop: allow the infrastructure to be crippled, define this destruction as corporate 'negligence,' and then nationalize e-commerce giants like Ozon and Wildberries under the guise of 'national security' and 'technological upgrades.'

This is a classic weaponization of narrative. Putin is not providing relief; he is using structural violence to create a legitimate entry point for plunder. For a company like Ozon, burdened by debt and lacking the state-bank backing of its rivals, this isn't a 'recovery program'—it is a forced liquidation in an existential war. By defining 'what the facts are' (i.e., you failed to protect the warehouse $ ightarrow$ you lose ownership), the state strips the entity of its subjectivity.

The deeper meta-violence lies in the universality of this logic within the Russian political ecosystem. All economic activity is placed under a masculine-centric, authoritarian 'protector' narrative: you are weak, therefore you need my protection; because you are weak, your assets belong to me. In this system of complicity, the only optimal expression for business owners is absolute submission, or risk being defined as 'unfit' and consumed.

Meanwhile, the 'coalition of the willing' in Kyiv discussing 'digging into stocks' for missiles is simply another layer of weaponized gaming. Both sides are redefining the Potential and Actual of the war through attrition. The irony remains: regardless of where the missile lands, the ultimate winner is always the one who controls the interpretation—the one who decides whether an explosion is a corporate failure or an opportunity for state seizure.
