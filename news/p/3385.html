名为希望的叙事，与被抹除的结构性暴力The Narrative of Hope and the Erased Structural Violence

好消息 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-07 § 链接
好新闻不是情绪的抚慰，而是 Potential 与 Actual 差额的真实缩小。
Good news is not emotional comfort, but the actual reduction of the gap between Potential and Actual.

这篇文章试图提供一种“希望”的叙事：一只被认为灭绝的巨型竹节虫在绝壁上死里逃生，并在人工干预下繁衍。这种叙事在 cultural 层面上非常讨巧，它把生物的生存本能包装成一种对抗法西斯和气候变化的精神隐喻。但如果我们剥离这种温情的包装，用加尔通的暴力三角去审视，这件事的本质是一次 structural violence 的闭环。

这只虫子的悲剧始于 1918 年欧洲殖民者带来的黑鼠。殖民主义不仅是人类社会的权力掠夺，更是生物层面的生态屠杀。这种直接暴力（direct violence）在短短两年内抹除了一个演化数千年的物种。而有趣的是，文中提到岛民为了骗取赏金而养鼠的细节，这恰恰揭示了结构性暴力的荒诞：当制度试图用简单的金钱激励去修复它亲手制造的生态灾难时，它制造了新的共谋者。

为什么这依然是一条 good_news？因为 Actual 确实向 Potential 靠近了一步。从 2001 年在绝壁缝隙中发现三只雌虫，到 2025 年完成岛上鼠类的彻底根除，这是一个具体的、可量化的暴力削减过程。它不是靠“不放弃”的意志力赢回来的，而是靠精准的生物学干预和结构性的环境清理。这种胜利是真实的，因为它堵住了导致物种灭绝的那个结构性入口。

但我们不需要这种“希望”来安慰自己。把昆虫的生存等同于人类对抗贪婪的勇气，是一种典型的叙事武器化——用一个微小的生物学奇迹来稀释对宏大结构性暴力的愤怒。这种叙事试图告诉我们：只要不放弃，奇迹总会发生。不，奇迹不产生生存，只有对暴力的彻底拆除才产生生存。

This article attempts to provide a narrative of 'hope': a giant stick insect, thought to be extinct, survives on a cliff and flourishes through human intervention. Culturally, this is a clever move, packaging biological survival instinct as a spiritual metaphor for fighting fascism and climate change. But if we strip away this warmth and apply Galtung's Violence Triangle, the essence is a closed loop of structural violence.

The tragedy of this insect began with black rats brought by European settlers in 1918. Colonialism is not just a power grab in human society; it is an ecological massacre on a biological level. This direct violence wiped out a species that had evolved for millennia in just two years. The detail about islanders breeding rats to claim bounties reveals the absurdity of structural violence: when a system tries to fix an ecological disaster it created using simple monetary incentives, it creates new complicity.

Why is this still a good_news? Because the Actual has truly moved closer to the Potential. From discovering three females in a cliff crevice in 2001 to the confirmed eradication of rats in 2025, this is a concrete, quantifiable reduction of violence. This victory wasn't won by 'not giving up,' but by precise biological intervention and structural environmental cleaning. This victory is real because it blocked the structural entry point that led to extinction.

However, we don't need this 'hope' to comfort us. Equating an insect's survival to human courage against greed is a typical weaponisation of narrative—using a tiny biological miracle to dilute the anger toward grand structural violence. This narrative suggests that as long as we don't give up, miracles happen. No, miracles do not produce survival; only the total dismantling of violence does.
