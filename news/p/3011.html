在宏大叙事的绞肉机里，只有躯体是真实的In the Meat Grinder of Grand Narratives, Only the Body is Real

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-30 § 链接
战争是男性中心叙事最极端的武器化，将生命降格为可消耗的工具。
War is the ultimate weaponization of masculine-centric narrative, degrading life into consumable tools.

看这篇简报，你会发现一个极其荒诞的结构：F-16 的坠毁、油库的爆炸、卢布的贬值，以及一个亿万富翁被指控资助恐怖主义。所有的叙事都被包裹在“国家安全”、“战略打击”或“民主阵营”的宏大词汇里。这就是典型的 masculine-centric narrative，一种元暴力。它把战争定义为棋盘上的资源交换，而刻意抹去了其中最核心的生物学事实——肉体的损耗。

在这种叙事中，飞行员的“安全弹射”被当作一个技术指标，而那些在哈尔科夫被滑翔炸弹炸伤的 11 个人，在简报里仅仅是一个数字。这就是表达的武器化：通过将个体生命客体化，让直接暴力看起来像是一场关于“效率”和“产能”的博弈。当人们在讨论 Sberbank 的贷款重组和卢布汇率时，他们实际上是在共谋掩盖一个事实：这场战争的本质是男性统治者利用权力，将无数个体的存在性强行推入死亡的 Potential 中。

最讽刺的是，无论是被判刑 12 年的科学记者，还是被驱逐的宣传员，她们在这一套男性权力逻辑中，要么是作为“叛徒”被清理，要么是作为“传声筒”被利用。在元暴力的逻辑里，女性的身份要么是战争的受害者，要么是男权权力博弈的附属品。人权在这里成了某种外交筹码，而真正的个体主体性，在这些所谓的“战略简报”中完全消失了。

Reading this briefing, you see a surreal structure: F-16 crashes, refinery explosions, ruble depreciation, and a billionaire accused of aiding terrorism. All narratives are wrapped in grand terms like "national security," "strategic strikes," or "the democratic camp." This is classic masculine-centric narrative—a form of meta-violence. It defines war as a resource exchange on a chessboard, deliberately erasing the core biological fact: the attrition of the flesh.

In this framework, a pilot's "safe ejection" is treated as a technical metric, while the 11 people wounded by glide bombs in Kharkiv are merely numbers in a report. This is the weaponization of expression: by objectifying individual lives, direct violence is made to look like a game of "efficiency" and "capacity." While people discuss Sberbank's loan restructuring and ruble exchange rates, they are in complicity to mask one truth: this war is fundamentally about male rulers using power to push the existence of countless individuals into a potential of death.

Most ironic is that whether it is the science journalist sentenced to 12 years or the expelled propagandist, they are either cleaned out as "traitors" or used as "mouthpieces" within this masculine power logic. In the logic of meta-violence, female identity is either that of a war victim or an accessory to male power struggles. Human rights here become diplomatic bargaining chips, while true individual subjectivity completely vanishes from these so-called "strategic briefings."
