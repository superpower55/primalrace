体育纪录的崩塌与被抹除的生物墙The Collapse of Sporting Records and the Erased Biological Wall

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-17 § 链接
所谓的“人类极限”不过是特定权力结构下的样本筛选结果。
The so-called "human limit" is merely a sampling result of specific power structures.

这篇读者来信试图用营养、训练、人口基数和装备来解释纪录的不断被刷新。这是一种典型的、试图将体育视为“纯粹生物学”的naive叙事。他们讨论的是 marginal gains（边际收益），却完全忽略了最核心的 structural violence：谁被允许进入赛场，谁被定义为“运动员”。

回顾历史，体育纪录的跳跃往往不是因为人类进化了，而是因为原本被禁锢在生物墙之外的群体被允许参与博弈。当肯尼亚的跑者不再被视为“异类”而成为职业运动员，当女性不再被告知“运动会损害子宫”而进入赛场，纪录的刷新本质上是样本池的扩张。这不是 Potential 的提升，而是 Actual 终于在权力让步后向 Potential 靠近了一步。

最讽刺的是文中提到 Enhanced Games 的失败。人们在讨论“干净”与“药物”的对立，却不敢讨论体育本身就是一种 masculine-centric narrative（男性中心叙事）的武器化。竞技体育追求的极致速度与力量，本质上是对男性生理特权的崇拜与量化。在这种叙事下，女性的身体被视为“次要”的，她们的纪录被认为是对男性纪录的某种“模拟”。

纪录会继续被打破，直到所有的生物墙被拆除，直到我们不再用一套基于男性生理基准的尺度去衡量所有人类的存在。否则，所谓的“世界纪录”永远只是一个被权力定义了入口的精英俱乐部游戏。

This collection of reader replies attempts to explain the continuous breaking of world records through nutrition, training, population size, and equipment. It is a typical, naive narrative that treats sports as "pure biology." They discuss marginal gains while completely ignoring the core structural violence: who is permitted to enter the arena and who is defined as an "athlete."

Historically, jumps in sporting records have rarely been about human evolution; they are about groups previously trapped behind biological walls being allowed to participate in the game. When Kenyan runners are no longer viewed as "others" but as professional athletes, or when women are no longer told that "exercise harms the uterus," the breaking of records is essentially an expansion of the sample pool. This is not an increase in Potential, but Actual finally moving toward Potential after a concession of power.

The most ironic part is the mention of the Enhanced Games' failure. People debate the opposition between "clean" and "doped," yet they dare not discuss how sports themselves are a weaponized masculine-centric narrative. The pursuit of extreme speed and strength is essentially the worship and quantification of male physiological privilege. Under this narrative, the female body is viewed as "secondary," and their records are treated as a kind of "simulation" of male records.

Records will continue to fall until all biological walls are dismantled, and until we stop using a scale based on male physiology to measure all human existence. Otherwise, the so-called "world record" remains nothing more than an elite club game with an entrance defined by power.
