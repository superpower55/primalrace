面罩、Facebook群组与右翼的共谋表演Balaclavas, Facebook Groups, and the Performance of Right-Wing Complicity

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-16 § 链接
公开的谴责是PR，私下的结盟是生存最优解。
Public condemnation is PR; private alliance is the existential optimal expression.

这出戏的剧本极其经典：党魁在聚光灯下用“disgusting”和“horrible”给极右翼暴力定调，而基层议员则在封闭的Facebook群组里通过tag和邀请完成身份认同。这种分裂不是内部混乱，而是一种极其精明的 weaponized expression。公开的谴责是为了在 structural 层维持合法性，确保政党不被定义为非法组织；而私下的共谋则是为了在 cultural 层锚定核心基本盘，通过与“面罩帮”这类 vigilante group 结盟，获取最纯粹的权力燃料。

那位议员 James Wright 的辩解简直是教科书级别的“扮演”：声称加入群组是为了“监测”和“了解本地讨论”。这在存在性战争中是一种典型的假.最优解表达——试图通过扮演一个“客观的观察者”来掩盖其作为共谋者的事实。在封闭群组里被tag，意味着你已经通过了对方的筛选机制，进入了那个特定的认知入口。你不是在监测暴力，你是在为暴力提供政治背书的静默额度。

最讽刺的是 Reform UK 的内政发言人 Zia Yusuf。他一边在警察与暴徒冲突后的第二天会见犯罪分子，一边在社交媒体上挑战 Kemi Badenoch 对“暴徒”的定义。这已经不是简单的政治分歧，而是在尝试夺取“什么是事实”的解释权。当“尊重地表达观点”被用来为掩面暴徒洗地时，这种叙事本身就是一种 meta violence。它试图通过重新定义“文明”与“尊重”，将直接暴力 (direct violence) 转化为一种被允许的、甚至是被美化的“爱国表达”。

这种共谋的逻辑很简单：只要不被抓到在现场戴面罩，在数字空间里通过 membership 维持与极右翼的血缘联系，就是目前这类政客在存在性战争中的最优解。他们不需要真正认同每一场暴乱，他们只需要确保在暴力机器真正启动时，自己处于那个能够分配利益的阵营之中。

This is a classic script: the party leader uses terms like “disgusting” and “horrible” to set the tone for far-right violence under the spotlight, while grassroots councillors solidify their identity in closed Facebook groups. This split isn't internal chaos; it's a calculated weaponisation of expression. Public condemnation maintains legitimacy at the structural level, while private complicity anchors the core base at the cultural level, drawing power from vigilante groups like the 'balaclava gang'.

Councillor James Wright’s defense is a textbook 'performance' of the fake optimal expression: claiming he joined the group to 'monitor' local discussions. In the existential war, this is an attempt to play the 'objective observer' to mask his role as a co-conspirator. Being tagged in a closed group means you've passed a screening mechanism and entered a specific cognitive entry point. You aren't monitoring violence; you are providing a silent quota of political endorsement for it.

The peak of this irony is Zia Yusuf. Meeting a convicted criminal the day after clashes with police, then challenging the term 'thugs' on X, is more than political disagreement—it's an attempt to seize the power of interpretation. When 'respectful expression' is used to whitewash masked thugs, the narrative itself becomes meta violence. It attempts to redefine 'civility' and 'respect' to transform direct violence into a permissible, even glorified, 'patriotic expression'.

The logic of this complicity is simple: as long as you aren't caught wearing a balaclava on the street, maintaining a digital bloodline with the far-right is the current optimal expression for these politicians. They don't need to endorse every single riot; they just need to ensure they are in the profit-sharing camp when the violence machine truly activates.
