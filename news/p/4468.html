肌酸热潮：被阉割的生物墙与男性肌肉的认知入口The Creatine Hype: Castrated Biological Walls and the Masculine Muscle Entry

哲学 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-25 § 链接
补剂的流行不是关于健康，而是关于对“强势表型”的焦虑性投喂。
Supplement trends aren't about health; they are an anxious feeding of the 'dominant phenotype'.

Guardian 这篇关于肌酸（Creatine）的播客讨论，表面在审视 science，实际上在触碰一个极其敏感的认知入口：肌肉。在男本位叙事中，肌肉不仅是生理组织，它是权力的物理外化，是直接暴力的潜在额度。当健身博主将肌酸从 gym 推向认知增强（cognitive benefits）时，他们实际上在试图通过化学手段，强行拓宽那个被定义为“强势”的生物墙。

我们需要追问的是：谁在定义这种“boost”？当一种补剂被包装成“每个人都应该服用”的通用解时，它在潜意识里完成了一次对身体的定价——即，一个未经增强的、自然的身体是“不足”的。这种焦虑是由元暴力驱动的：在男性中心叙事里，只有不断增长的肌肉量和认知效能才具有更高的存在性价值。肌酸在这里成了一个 weaponized 的符号，它承诺的是一种通过消费即可获得的“强势表型”。

这种 hype 的本质是一场关于存在性战争的低端博弈。人们试图通过补剂来弥补 Potential 与 Actual 之间的差额，但他们忽略了，这种对“强壮”的病态追求，本身就是结构性暴力的产物。当你试图通过化学手段让自己看起来更像一个“强者”时，你已经在潜意识里共谋了那个“强者吃弱者”的丛林法则。真正的最优解表达不是通过补剂去扮演一个更强壮的客体，而是拆穿这套将身体量化为竞争工具的 scam。

The Guardian's podcast on Creatine pretends to scrutinize science, but it is actually touching a sensitive cognitive entry: muscle. In a masculine-centric narrative, muscle is not just biological tissue; it is the physical externalization of power and the potential quota for direct violence. When influencers push creatine from the gym toward 'cognitive benefits,' they are attempting to forcibly widen the biological wall defined as 'dominant' via chemical means.

We must ask: who defines this 'boost'? When a supplement is packaged as a universal solution that 'everyone should take,' it completes a subconscious pricing of the body—suggesting that a natural, unenhanced body is 'insufficient.' This anxiety is driven by meta-violence: in the masculine-centric narrative, only increasing muscle mass and cognitive efficiency hold higher existential value. Creatine here becomes a weaponized symbol, promising a 'dominant phenotype' attainable through consumption.

The essence of this hype is a low-end game in an existential war. People attempt to bridge the gap between Potential and Actual through supplements, ignoring that this pathological pursuit of 'strength' is a product of structural violence. When you try to look like a 'stronger' person through chemicals, you are subconsciously entering into complicity with the jungle law where the strong devour the weak. The true optimal expression is not playing the role of a stronger object via supplements, but exposing the scam that quantifies the human body as a competitive tool.
