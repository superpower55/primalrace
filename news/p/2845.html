名为禁令的PR，实为共谋的漏洞A Bonus Ban as PR, a Loophole as Complicity

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-24 § 链接
制度性禁令若不触及定义权，只是给掠夺穿上合规的外衣。
Institutional bans are mere theater if they do not challenge the power of definition.

这是一场典型的关于“定义权”的博弈。政府宣布 bonus ban（奖金禁令），在文化层制造了一种“正义在执行”的叙事，但实际上 structural layer（结构层）的漏洞大到可以开进一辆装满现金的卡车。当水务公司把钱从“奖金”改为“留任金 (retention payment)”或“年度津贴 (annual allowance)”时，他们完成了一次极其低成本的语义置换。只要不叫 bonus，它就不是 bonus。

这种操作揭示了权力运行的潜规则：规则是由制定者（通常是共谋者）定义的。水务公司、监管机构 Ofwat 以及政府部门在同一个元暴力系统下运作。他们共同维持着一种“私有化公共资源”的共谋关系——让公众承担污染和高额账单的 Actual 成本，而让管理层通过语义操纵攫取 Potential 收益。所谓的“禁令”不过是一次表演性让步，目的是通过微小的、可绕过的限制来平息公众愤怒，从而保护核心的利益分配结构不被触动。

最讽刺的是，这种“留任金”的逻辑是将管理层神化为不可替代的稀缺资源。在公司濒临破产、河流被污水填满的现实面前，他们依然在定义什么是“领导力连续性”。这本质上是一种认知武器化：通过重新定义报酬的性质，将赤裸裸的贪婪包装成必要的企业治理。在这种共谋场域里，公众的愤怒被精准地引导至“个别贪婪的 CEO”身上，而真正的问题——将基本生存资源商品化的结构性暴力——被成功地掩盖在账单和术语之中。

This is a textbook game of 'definition power.' The government announces a bonus ban, creating a narrative of justice at the cultural layer, while the structural layer remains riddled with loopholes large enough to drive a cash-laden truck through. By rebranding 'bonuses' as 'retention payments' or 'annual allowances,' water companies perform a low-cost semantic shift. As long as it isn't called a bonus, it isn't a bonus.

This reveals the hidden mechanism of power: rules are defined by the definers, who are often the complicitors. Water companies, the regulator Ofwat, and government departments operate within the same system of meta-violence. They maintain a complicity of privatizing public resources—forcing the public to bear the actual costs of pollution and soaring bills, while executives extract potential gains through semantic manipulation. The 'ban' is nothing more than a performative concession, designed to pacify public fury with bypassable restrictions to protect the core distribution of wealth.

Most cynical is the logic of the 'retention payment,' which mystifies executives as irreplaceable assets. While companies teeter on insolvency and rivers choke on sewage, they continue to define 'leadership continuity.' This is the weaponization of cognition: rebranding naked greed as necessary corporate governance. In this field of complicity, public anger is precisely steered toward 'greedy individuals,' while the structural violence of commodifying basic survival resources remains hidden behind bills and terminology.
