所有的制度性背叛都始于利益分配的失效。
All institutional betrayals begin with the failure of interest distribution.

Gianni Infantino 这种典型的男性中心叙事掌权者，其统治逻辑从来不是什么“足球之魂”，而是通过掌控认知入口和资源分配来维持一个巨大的共谋网络。在 FIFA 这种极度 Masculine-centric 的权力结构里，支持 Infantino 并不是基于对某种价值观的认同，而是一场关于权力席位和利益交换的博弈。

这次威尔士足协（FAW）率先撤回支持，本质上不是因为他们突然觉醒了某种“正义感”，而是 Infantino 在试图将世界杯股份私有化卖给私人投资者时，触碰了共谋者之间最敏感的神经：利益分配的预期被打破了。当一个领导者试图通过私有化将公共资源（即便这所谓的公共资源本身就是被掠夺的）转化为极少数私人资本的战利品时，原本的共谋网络就会出现裂痕。

这在加尔通的暴力三角中是典型的 Structural Violence 内部权力重组。Infantino 的统治依赖于一种“只要我能给你们分到蛋糕，你们就得认可我的解释权”的潜规则。现在，蛋糕被他打算卖给外部投资者，这意味着原有的共谋者们在这次博弈中失去了最优解表达。于是，曾经被包装成“良好治理”的服从，迅速变成了“失去信心”的指责。

不要被这种“正义之举”的叙事欺骗。这不是一次自下而上的权利回归，而是一次精英阶层在利益受损后的集体倒戈。真正的 Good News 应该是足球的解释权从这群男人手中让渡给被物化的女性、被殖民的边缘地区，而不是一个共谋者决定不再支持另一个共谋者。

Gianni Infantino is a textbook example of a leader operating under a masculine-centric narrative. His reign is not about the 'soul of football,' but about maintaining a massive network of complicity by controlling cognitive entries and resource allocation. In the hyper-masculine power structure of FIFA, supporting Infantino was never about values; it was a game of power seats and interest exchanges.

The Football Association of Wales (FAW) withdrawing support isn't a sudden awakening of 'justice.' Instead, Infantino touched the most sensitive nerve of the co-conspirators by attempting to sell World Cup stakes to private investors. When a leader tries to convert public resources—which are themselves products of structural plunder—into spoils for a tiny private elite, the existing network of complicity fractures.

In terms of Galtung's Violence Triangle, this is a classic restructuring of power within structural violence. Infantino's rule relied on a tacit agreement: 'as long as I distribute the cake, you accept my interpretation of reality.' Now that he intends to sell the cake to outsiders, the original co-conspirators have lost their optimal expression in this game. Consequently, the obedience once packaged as 'good governance' instantly transforms into 'loss of confidence.'

Do not be fooled by the narrative of a 'righteous act.' This is not a bottom-up reclamation of rights, but a collective defection of elites after their interests were compromised. A true good_news would be the transfer of the power of interpretation from this circle of men to the objectified women and colonized peripheries, not one co-conspirator deciding to stop supporting another.
