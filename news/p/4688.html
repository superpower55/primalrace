“非荷兰式”的暴力，与全球共谋的毒品版图The 'Un-Dutch' Violence and the Global Complicity of Narcotrafficking

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-02 § 链接
暴力从未消失，它只是在“文明”的掩体下完成了全球分工。
Violence never disappears; it simply completes its global division of labor under the cover of 'civilization'.

荷兰村庄的枪战被当地市长定义为“un-Dutch”（非荷兰式），这种表达极其典型：通过将暴力定义为“异类”或“外来”，试图维持一个文明、理性的国家叙事。但 Violence = Potential − Actual，当数百公斤可卡因在欧洲边境流动时，这种“文明”的 Actual 状态本身就是建立在巨大的结构性暴力之上的。暴力不是突然降临的意外，而是这个贸易链条的必要组成部分。

这场枪战揭示了一个极其高效的共谋网络（complicity network）：从塞拉利昂的总统之女，到在荷兰村庄开枪的阿尔及利亚、法国和比利时雇佣兵。这是一个跨国界的元暴力结构——权力上位者在安全地带定义规则并攫取利润，而执行层则由被剥削的边缘群体承担。所谓的“国际性质”犯罪，本质上是资本在利用不同地域的生物墙和阶级差异进行风险对冲。

最讽刺的共谋在于，毒枭 Leijdekkers 能在塞拉利昂通过与总统之女的关系获得庇护，这证明了权力如何将犯罪武器化为一种特权。在这种结构中，法律不再是公正的表达，而成了筛选谁该被逮捕、谁该被保护的工具。当市长感叹暴力“跨越了所有边界”时，他忽略了毒品贸易本身就是通过抹除边界、将人体客体化为运输工具而获利的。

这场血腥冲突不是对文明的侵犯，而是文明在阴影中运行的真实逻辑：在舒适的欧洲村庄背后，永远有一群被定价的生命在为上位者的欲望进行存在性战争。

The shootout in a Dutch village was labeled 'un-Dutch' by the local mayor—a classic expression of cultural violence. By defining violence as 'alien' or 'foreign', the state attempts to maintain a narrative of a civilized, rational society. But according to the Violence Triangle, this 'civilized' Actual state is built upon massive structural violence. Violence is not a sudden accident; it is a functional requirement of the trade chain.

This event exposes a highly efficient complicity network: from the daughter of the President of Sierra Leone to the Algerian, French, and Belgian mercenaries firing guns in the Netherlands. This is a globalized meta-violence structure where the powerful define rules and harvest profits in safe zones, while the execution is outsourced to marginalized groups. The 'international nature' of the crime is simply capital leveraging biological walls and class disparities across borders to hedge risk.

The most cynical complicity lies in how Leijdekkers secured sanctuary in Sierra Leone through a relationship with the president's daughter, proving how power weaponizes crime into privilege. In this structure, the law is not a Just Expression, but a tool to filter who is arrested and who is protected. When the mayor laments that violence 'crosses all boundaries', he ignores that the drug trade itself profits by erasing boundaries and objectifying human bodies as mere logistics.

This bloody conflict is not an assault on civilization, but the actual logic of civilization operating in the shadows: behind the comfort of a European village, there are always priced lives fighting an existential war for the desires of the elite.
