红制服下的殖民幽灵与共谋预算Colonial Ghosts and Complicit Budgets Under the Red Tunic

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-09 § 链接
用预算掩盖结构性暴力，是典型的元暴力维持手段。
Using budgets to mask structural violence is a classic tactic of meta-violence.

RCMP（皇家骑警）那套标志性的红制服，本质上是一件精心设计的 cultural violence 武器。它通过好莱坞和流行文化的 weaponization，将一个带有浓厚殖民色彩的暴力机器包装成加拿大的“国家符号”。当大众在讨论这套制服是否代表“信任”时，其实已经掉入了叙事陷阱：信任是建立在主体性被尊重的基础上，而 RCMP 的基因就是抹除原住民的主体性。

从强迫原住民儿童进入寄宿学校，到系统性地性骚扰女性成员，RCMP 的暴力三角完整地覆盖了三个层面：直接层是致命的暴力执法，结构层是殖民时代的权力分配，而文化层则是那套“维护秩序”的伪装。尤其是那笔 12.5 亿加元的性骚扰赔偿金，它不是正义的实现，而是一次昂贵的 structural violence 结算——用金钱买断受害者的噤声，维持一个 masculine-centric narrative 的内部稳定。

最讽刺的是 Carney 政府的操作。在 Trudeau 提出改革、试图让其脱离基层执法的时刻，新政府选择通过增加预算和提高薪资来“投资”RCMP。这根本不是什么公共安全升级，而是一场典型的共谋 (complicity)。在 Trump 政府的压力下，加拿大政府选择用金钱喂养这头殖民怪兽，试图用更多的人力去填补信任的黑洞。这在逻辑上极其荒谬：一个因为 paramilitary 和 colonial approach 而失效的系统，解决办法竟然是投入更多钱去强化这种 paramilitary 属性？

这再一次证明了元暴力 (meta violence) 的运行逻辑：只要不触动权力解释权的让渡，所有的“现代化”和“转型”都只是 PR 版本的修补。只要红制服依然定义了什么是“秩序”，那么被它碾压的人永远只是这个秩序下的代价。

The iconic red tunic of the RCMP is essentially a weapon of cultural violence. Through the weaponisation of Hollywood and pop culture, a violent machine with deep colonial roots has been packaged as a 'national symbol' of Canada. When the public debates whether this uniform represents 'trust,' they have already fallen into a narrative trap: trust is built on the respect of subjectivity, whereas the very DNA of the RCMP is the erasure of Indigenous subjectivity.

From forcing Indigenous children into residential schools to systemic sexual harassment of female members, the RCMP's violence triangle covers all three layers: direct violence in lethal enforcement, structural violence in colonial power distribution, and cultural violence in the facade of 'maintaining order.' The $125 million settlement for sexual harassment is not a triumph of justice, but a costly settlement of structural violence—using money to buy the silence of victims and maintain the internal stability of a masculine-centric narrative.

The most cynical part is the Carney government's maneuver. At the moment Trudeau suggested reform and a retreat from boots-on-the-ground policing, the new government chose to 'invest' in the RCMP through increased budgets and higher pay. This is not a public safety upgrade; it is a textbook case of complicity. Under pressure from the Trump administration, the Canadian government is feeding this colonial beast, attempting to fill a black hole of trust with cash. The logic is absurd: for a system that is failing specifically because of its paramilitary and colonial approach, the solution is to spend more to reinforce those very paramilitary traits?

This proves once again how meta-violence operates: as long as the monopoly over the power of interpretation remains untouched, all 'modernization' and 'transformation' are merely PR patches. As long as the red tunic defines what 'order' is, those crushed by it will remain mere collateral of that order.
