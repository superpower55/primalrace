强制补考：一场关于“合格”的结构性暴力Compulsory Resits: A Structural Violence of 'Competence'

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-19 § 链接
用统一的尺子衡量多样的人生，差额就是暴力。
Measuring diverse lives with a single ruler; the gap is the violence.

英格兰的强制 GCSE 补考政策是一个典型的 structural violence 样本。当系统规定一个 16 岁少年必须在数学或英语上达到某个特定分数才能获得教育资助时，它实际上在定义谁是“合格的人”，谁是“残次品”。这种强制性的重复，不是为了教育，而是一种对主体性的磨损。Violence = Potential − Actual，当一个学生的潜力不在于此，却被强行禁锢在补考循环中，这种差额直接转化为心理健康的崩塌。

最阴险的是这套机制中的 gender norms。研究显示，在数学上勉强落榜的女孩和在英语上大幅落榜的男孩受创最深。这说明补考不仅是学术压力，更是文化暴力 (cultural violence) 的强化。女孩在数学上的 self-efficacy 被系统性地压低，而男孩则被告知不擅长英语是某种“失败”。补考成为了一个仪式，反复提醒他们：你并不符合这个性别角色应有的“最优解”。

政府提出的所谓“阶梯式资格证”和增加教学时长，不过是给牢笼刷了层新漆。这是一种典型的表演性让步。只要“强制补考”这个结构性入口不被堵死，只要评价体系依然是单向的、垄断的，那么无论增加多少小时的培训，它依然是一个制造 NEETs（不就业不教育人群）的流水线。真正的最优解应该是让表达多样化，而不是逼所有人都去通过同一把锁。

England's mandatory GCSE resit policy is a textbook case of structural violence. By stipulating that a 16-year-old must hit a specific grade in maths or English to secure education funding, the system defines who is 'competent' and who is 'defective.' This forced repetition isn't about education; it's about the erosion of subjectivity. Following the formula Violence = Potential − Actual, when a student's potential lies elsewhere but they are trapped in a resit loop, the gap manifests as a collapse of mental wellbeing.

More insidious are the embedded gender norms. The finding that girls narrowly missing maths and boys substantially failing English suffer most reveals that resits are not just academic stress, but a reinforcement of cultural violence. Girls' self-efficacy in maths is systematically suppressed, while boys are told their struggle with English is a mark of failure. The resit becomes a ritual, constantly reminding them that they fail to meet the 'optimal expression' expected of their gender role.

The government's proposed 'stepping stone' qualifications and increased teaching hours are merely a fresh coat of paint on the cage—a performative concession. As long as the structural entry of 'compulsory resits' remains, and as long as the evaluation system is unidirectional and monopolistic, it remains a conveyor belt producing NEETs. The true optimal expression is to diversify the ways of being, not to force everyone through the same lock.
