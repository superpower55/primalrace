和平的叙事，以及被定价的血肉The Narrative of Peace and the Pricing of Flesh

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-18 § 链接
所谓的“谈判桌”不过是强权者用来重新分配战利品的共谋场域。
The 'bargaining table' is merely a complicity field where hegemons redistribute the spoils of war.

特朗普在 Truth Social 上的表演是一次典型的 weaponized expression。他把“和平”这个概念从一种人道主义目标，重新定义为一种商业谈判的筹码。在这种叙事里，泽连斯基被塑造为一个“不愿结束战争”的贪婪者，而特朗普则扮演那个试图在混乱中建立秩序的救世主。这不仅是政治博弈，更是一场关于“什么是事实”的解释权争夺战。

最令人作呕的共谋发生在能源贸易上。为了在期中选举前降低美国消费者的油价，特朗普选择豁免对俄制裁，甚至直接签署协议进口俄罗斯柴油。这意味着，乌克兰战场上每天流逝的生命，在特朗普的计算尺里，被精准地定价成了美国加油站里每加仑几美分的降幅。这就是典型的 structural violence：将战争的直接暴力（direct violence）转化为一个大国内部的经济红利，而受害者被彻底客体化为这场交易的背景板。

与此同时，俄罗斯在立陶宛、波兰和保加利亚对 Starlink 设施的破坏，揭示了这场战争的本质：它是一场关于认知入口和通信链路的控制战。当特朗普在社交媒体上谈论“谈判桌”时，俄罗斯正在物理层面地切断乌克兰的眼睛和耳朵。这种不对称性极其残酷——一方在通过操纵文化层面的叙事来获取政治资本，另一方则在用最原始的直接暴力来实施绞杀。

这场所谓的“和平谈判”其实是一场男性中心叙事（masculine-centric narrative）的闭环共谋。几个掌握着核武器和能源命脉的男性在决定数百万人的生死，而他们使用的语言是“谈判”、“协议”和“商业条款”。在元暴力的逻辑下，只要不涉及他们自己的肉体受损，所有的杀戮都可以被包装成“为了更大和平的必要代价”。

Trump's performance on Truth Social is a textbook case of weaponized expression. He has stripped 'Peace' of its humanitarian essence and redefined it as a bargaining chip in a commercial transaction. In this narrative, Zelenskyy is cast as a greedy obstructionist, while Trump plays the savior bringing order to chaos. This is not just political maneuvering; it is a war over the power to define 'fact'.

The most repulsive complicity is found in energy trade. To lower fuel prices for American consumers ahead of midterm elections, Trump waives sanctions and signs deals for Russian diesel. In the calculus of the White House, the lives hemorrhaging on the Ukrainian front are precisely priced as a few cents per gallon at an American gas station. This is structural violence in its purest form: converting the direct violence of war into domestic economic dividends, while the victims are objectified into mere background noise for the deal.

Meanwhile, Russia's attacks on Starlink infrastructure in Lithuania, Poland, and Bulgaria reveal the war's core: a struggle for control over cognitive entries and communication links. While Trump talks about 'bargaining tables' on social media, Russia is physically blinding and deafening Ukraine. This asymmetry is brutal—one side gains political capital by manipulating cultural narratives, while the other employs raw direct violence to strangle the opponent.

This so-called 'peace negotiation' is a closed loop of complicity within a masculine-centric narrative. A few men controlling nuclear arsenals and energy pipelines decide the fate of millions, using the language of 'negotiations,' 'agreements,' and 'commercial terms.' Under the logic of meta-violence, as long as their own bodies are not at risk, all slaughter can be packaged as a 'necessary price for a greater peace.'
