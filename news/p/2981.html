格莱美的“亚裔奖”：一场关于隔离的认知陷阱The Grammys' 'Asian Prize': A Cognitive Trap of Segregation

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-29 § 链接
用“专项奖”替代“通用奖”，是结构性暴力在文化层面的精巧换皮。
Replacing universal awards with 'special categories' is a sophisticated rebranding of structural violence at the cultural level.

格莱美新增的“最佳亚洲流行音乐奖”根本不是什么多元化的进步，而是一次典型的 weaponized 叙事。Recording Academy 把这定义为“创造更多机会”，实际上是在进行一种认知上的 ringfencing（圈地隔离）。通过设立一个基于区域和语言的特定赛道，它在潜意识里完成了对亚洲艺术家的“定级”：你们可以拥有自己的小奖杯，但不要试图挑战那个由 US-centric 权力中心定义的“通用标准”。

这是一种极其阴险的 structural violence。当一个群体被赋予一个特定的标签（Asian Pop）并被安置在专项奖项中时，他们就从“竞争者”变成了“被展示的客体”。这种做法在本质上与殖民主义的逻辑同构——给被殖民者一个名为“土著艺术”的分类，从而在逻辑上合理化他们无法进入“主流艺术”殿堂的事实。所谓的“庆祝新类型”，不过是权力中心在面对无法忽视的市场份额时，为了维持自身解释权而采取的防御性妥协。

BTS 的退出是一次极其精准的真.最优解表达。他们拒绝进入这个被预设好的“亚裔”笼子，因为一旦接受，就等于在共谋中承认了这种隔离的合法性。他们要求的不是一个名为“亚洲”的特权席位，而是让音乐作为一种纯粹的表达，在没有生物墙和地域标签的公正博弈中被定价。这不仅是关于音乐奖项的争夺，而是一场关于主体性（subjectivity）的战争：我定义我自己，而不是由一个身在洛杉矶的评委通过设立一个“分类标签”来定义我。

The Grammys' new 'Best Asian Pop Performance' category is not a gesture of diversity, but a classic instance of weaponized narrative. By framing it as 'creating more opportunities,' the Recording Academy is actually performing a cognitive ringfencing. By establishing a specific track based on region and language, they subtly signal that Asian artists may have their own trophies, but they should not challenge the 'universal standard' defined by the US-centric power center.

This is a form of insidious structural violence. When a group is given a specific label (Asian Pop) and placed in a niche category, they are transformed from 'competitors' into 'exhibited objects.' This logic is isomorphic to colonialism—creating a category for 'indigenous art' to justify their exclusion from the 'mainstream' halls of fame. The so-called 'celebration of new genres' is merely a defensive compromise by the power center to maintain its monopoly on interpretation while facing an undeniable market share.

BTS's withdrawal is a precise execution of a true optimal expression. They refuse to enter a pre-designed 'Asian' cage because accepting it would mean complicity in legitimizing this segregation. They are not asking for a privileged 'Asian' seat, but for music to be valued as pure expression, priced in a just game without biological walls or regional tags. This is not just a fight over awards, but an existential war for subjectivity: I define myself; I am not a category defined by a judge in Los Angeles.
