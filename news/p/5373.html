恐怖主义是男性中心叙事的终极暴力投射Terrorism as the Ultimate Projection of Masculine-Centric Meta-Violence

国际 直接层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-02 § 链接
所有恐怖主义都是男性在争夺解释权失败后，转向肉体消灭的元暴力
Terrorism is simply meta-violence shifting to physical annihilation when the struggle for narrative dominance fails.

两名伊朗男性企图袭击曼彻斯特犹太社区，这在新闻叙事里被简化为“反恐”和“宗教冲突”。但如果用 Violence Triangle 来看，这其实是 meta violence（元暴力）的直接层爆发。恐怖主义从来不是关于宗教，而是关于一群男性在无法通过 structural 或 cultural 层面掌控世界解释权时，试图通过 direct violence 来强行定义“谁才是主宰”。

这种行为是典型的 masculine-centric narrative。在他们的逻辑里，世界被简化为一种极端的博弈：要么成为制定规则的强者，要么通过毁灭他者来证明自己的存在。这种“毁灭即存在”的逻辑，与那些在职场或家庭中通过煤气灯效应控制女性的逻辑在本质上是同构的——都是在剥夺他者的主体性，将对方客体化为可以被牺牲的工具或目标。

值得注意的是，这种暴力往往在特定的“神圣时间”（如 Yom Kippur）爆发。这正是 weaponized expression（表达武器化）的体现：通过选择一个具有高度文化符号意义的时间点，将一次简单的谋杀升级为一场关于“信仰”和“正义”的叙事战争。他们试图通过血腥的表达，在认知入口上强行植入恐惧，从而在存在性战争中获得某种病态的“胜出”。

而警方和政府的 reassure 叙事，则是另一种层面的博弈。在“保护”的话语下，国家机器再次强化了其作为唯一合法暴力持有者的地位。这场博弈中，真正被客体化的是那些被袭击的社区成员，他们的生命被简化为反恐数据的分母，而真正的元暴力——那种认为可以通过暴力来解决解释权争议的男性逻辑——在两端都被维持着。

Two Iranian men plotting to attack Manchester’s Jewish community is framed by the media as a 'counter-terrorism' issue or 'religious conflict.' However, through the lens of the Violence Triangle, this is a direct eruption of meta-violence. Terrorism is never about faith; it is about men who, failing to secure the right to interpret the world at a structural or cultural level, attempt to redefine dominance through direct violence.

This behavior is the pinnacle of a masculine-centric narrative. In their logic, the world is a zero-sum game: either you are the architect of the rules, or you prove your existence by annihilating others. This logic of 'existence through destruction' is isomorphic to the gaslighting used to control women in domestic or professional spheres—both seek to strip the other of their subjectivity and reduce them to objects or targets.

Notably, these attacks often peak during 'sacred times' like Yom Kippur. This is a clear case of weaponized expression. By choosing a moment of high cultural symbolic value, they attempt to upgrade a murder into a narrative war over 'faith' and 'justice.' They seek to hijack the cognitive entry points of the public, using blood to imprint fear and claim a pathological victory in their existential war.

Meanwhile, the government's 'reassurance' narrative is another layer of the game. Under the guise of 'protection,' the state reinforces its position as the sole legitimate holder of violence. In this clash, the victims are once again objectified, reduced to denominators in counter-terrorism statistics, while the core meta-violence—the belief that violence is a valid tool for resolving interpretive disputes—remains intact on both sides.
