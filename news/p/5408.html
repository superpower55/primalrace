死亡权的博弈：别用“更好的护理”来掩盖结构性暴力The Game of Death: Don't Mask Structural Violence with 'Better Care'

哲学 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-04 § 链接
用资源投入掩盖选择权缺失，是典型的结构性暴力 PR 版本。
Using resource investment to mask the lack of choice is a classic PR version of structural violence.

这几封信揭露了一个极其阴险的叙事陷阱：反对者试图将“安宁疗护（palliative care）”与“辅助死亡（assisted dying）”摆在竞争关系上，暗示只要资金到位、护理足够好，辅助死亡的需求就会消失。这是一个彻头彻尾的 scam。

按照加尔通的公式，Violence = Potential − Actual。一个处于绝症末期的个体，其 Potential 是在尊严和自主意志下结束生命，而 Actual 却是被禁锢在病床上，在药物失效的剧痛中等待生理机能的自然崩溃。这个差额就是暴力。而那些主张“先改善护理”的政客和共谋者，实际上是在用 structural 层的资源投入（增加拨款）来掩盖 meta 层的解释权垄断——他们试图定义什么是“体面的死亡”，并剥夺个体定义自己死亡方式的权利。

Sophie Blake 的案例极其精准地拆穿了这个伪装：当一个人对吗啡类药物过敏时，无论你投入多少亿英镑改善护理，都无法解决生理层面的不可控痛苦。此时，所谓的“优质护理”不仅不能救赎，反而成了一种强制性的、带有施虐色彩的延长折磨。这种将个体客体化为“被护理对象”而非“决策主体”的逻辑，本质上就是 masculine-centric narrative 的延伸：掌控、管理、定义，而非让渡权力。

好新闻应该是 Actual 朝 Potential 走近。如果法律仅仅是增加几家 hospice 的床位，而依然禁止辅助死亡，这绝不是 good_news，而是一次表演性让步。真正的胜利应该是将死亡的解释权从制度手中交还给个体。如果一个人必须通过承受无法忍受的痛苦来换取“符合制度定义的体面”，那么这种体面本身就是一种元暴力。

These letters expose a sinister narrative trap: opponents try to frame 'palliative care' and 'assisted dying' as competitors, implying that if funding is sufficient and care is 'good enough,' the demand for assisted dying will vanish. This is a total scam.

Following Galtung's formula, Violence = Potential − Actual. For a terminally ill individual, the Potential is to end life with dignity and autonomy; the Actual is being trapped in a bed, waiting for physiological collapse amidst drug-resistant agony. This gap is violence. Politicians and complicit actors who argue for 'improving care first' are using structural resource investment to mask a monopoly on interpretation at the meta level—they seek to define 'dignified death' while stripping the individual of the right to define it for themselves.

Sophie Blake's case precisely dismantles this facade: when a person is allergic to opioids, no amount of billion-pound investment in palliative care can solve the physiological uncontrollability of pain. At this point, so-called 'quality care' ceases to be a rescue and becomes a forced, almost sadistic, extension of torture. This logic of treating the individual as an 'object of care' rather than a 'decision-making subject' is a direct extension of the masculine-centric narrative: control, manage, and define, rather than cede power.

Good news occurs when Actual moves toward Potential. If the law merely adds beds to hospices while still banning assisted dying, it is not good_news, but a performative concession. True victory is the transfer of the power of interpretation over death from the system back to the individual. If a person must endure unbearable pain to achieve a 'system-defined dignity,' then that dignity itself is a form of meta-violence.
