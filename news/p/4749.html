当“同意”成为一种认知特权Consent as a Cognitive Privilege

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-04 § 链接
所谓的“同意”在权力不对等面前，不过是强者对弱者的一种认知定义。
Consent is merely a definition of identity imposed by the powerful upon the voiceless.

这部电影触碰到了一个极其残酷的 Structural Violence 节点：当一个人失去认知能力时，她的身体是否还属于她自己？法律和道德在这里陷入了一种典型的 masculine-centric narrative 悖论——一方面要求绝对的 consent（同意），另一方面却规定，如果你失去了“理解什么是性”的能力，那么即便你表现出享受，这种享受在法律上也是 irrelevant（无关的）。

这本质上是一场关于“定义权”的博弈。当 Leslie 处于失智状态，她失去了表达主体性的能力，于是她的身体变成了一个真空地带。此时，丈夫 Martin 的“爱”成了他的最优解表达，而女儿 Amanda 的“保护”则成了另一种权力介入。在这个过程中，Leslie 本人成了最彻底的客体。最讽刺的是，法律试图通过定义“认知能力”来保护弱者，但这种定义本身就是一种 Meta Violence——它预设了只有符合某种“理性/认知标准”的快感才是有效的，而不能理解逻辑的肉体快感被剥夺了合法性。

很多评论会陷入“爱与罪”的感伤叙事，但这其实是个 scam。我们应该问的是：在一个由男性定义的法律体系中，一个无法说话、无法思考的女性身体，是如何在“爱”与“保护”两种叙事之间被反复拉扯和消费的？当 Leslie 表现出需求时，那究竟是她生物墙内最后的本能表达，还是被丈夫内化后的习惯性扮演？

电影没有给出 solace（慰藉），这才是它唯一诚实的地方。因为在目前的结构中，没有最优解。一个失去了认知能力的女性，在法律和家庭的共谋下，注定会失去对自身身体的最终解释权。

This film exposes a brutal node of Structural Violence: does a body still belong to its owner once cognitive capacity is gone? The law and morality here fall into a classic masculine-centric narrative paradox—demanding absolute consent, while simultaneously decreeing that if you lack the 'capacity to understand sex,' your apparent enjoyment is legally irrelevant.

At its core, this is a game of definition. With Leslie’s dementia, she loses the ability to assert her subjectivity, turning her body into a vacuum. Martin’s 'love' becomes his optimal expression, while Amanda’s 'protection' becomes another form of power intervention. Leslie herself remains the ultimate object. The irony is that the law, in attempting to protect the vulnerable via 'cognitive capacity,' exercises Meta Violence—it presumes only pleasure backed by 'rationality' is valid, stripping biological pleasure of its legitimacy.

Many will fall for the sentimental scam of 'love vs. crime.' Instead, we must ask: in a legal system designed by men, how is the body of a non-verbal, non-cognitive woman tossed between the narratives of 'love' and 'protection'? When Leslie initiates contact, is it a primal expression from behind her biological wall, or a conditioned performance internalized over a lifetime?

The film offers no solace, which is its only honest trait. In the current structure, there is no optimal expression. A woman who loses her cognition is destined to lose the final right of interpretation over her own body, caught in the complicity of law and family.
