石榴籽的隐喻与原初种族的殖民逻辑The Pomegranate Metaphor and the Colonial Logic of the Primal Race

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-08 § 链接
所谓的“民族统一”是典型的元暴力：用单一叙事抹除他者的存在性。
The so-called "ethnic unity" is classic meta-violence: erasing the existence of others through a singular narrative.

把不同族群比作“像石榴籽一样紧紧抱在一起”，这是一个极其恶毒的 weaponized 叙事。在生物学上，石榴籽被禁锢在坚硬的果皮之中，彼此独立却无法逃离。这种“统一”不是公正的表达，而是一场彻底的殖民。当一个政权通过法律强制要求 Mandarin 语言的绝对主导，并禁止少数族群的语言与诗歌时，它在做的事情不是“整合”，而是通过摧毁对方的表达方式，直接在 structural 层面上抹杀对方的身份。记得第一章提到的，表达决定了你是谁。当你不能用母语思考和表达，你的主体性就死亡了，剩下的只是一个被驯化的、政治合规的空壳。

这种对多样性的战争，本质上是男性中心叙事（masculine-centric narrative）在国家层面的投射：追求绝对的秩序、单一的权力中心以及对所有异质性的强制同化。这就是元暴力（meta violence）的运作方式——它定义什么是“文明”的统一，然后将所有拒绝被吞噬的文化定义为“分裂”或“落后”。在这种逻辑下，少数族群被对待的方式与历史上被殖民的原初种族完全同构：被剥夺解释权，被强制定义，最后被消费成一种名为“民族团结”的政治景观。

而西方世界的沉默则是另一种共谋（complicity）。当贸易利益被置于人权之上，西方民主国家实际上在潜意识中认可了这种“强权即真理”的博弈逻辑。他们担心失去 AI 赛道的入场券，于是容忍了对人类基本表达权的践踏。这再次证明了，只要元暴力依然是全球权力的底层操作系统，所谓的“人权”就永远只是一个可以被交易的 PR 标签，而不是一个不可侵犯的底线。

Comparing different ethnic groups to "pomegranate seeds hugging tightly" is a profoundly malicious weaponized narrative. Biologically, pomegranate seeds are trapped within a hard rind—separate yet unable to escape. This "unity" is not a Just Expression, but a total colonization. When a regime uses law to enforce the absolute dominance of Mandarin and ban the languages and poetry of minorities, it is not "integrating"; it is obliterating identity at the structural level by destroying the means of expression. As established in Chapter One, expression defines who you are. When you can no longer think or express yourself in your mother tongue, your subjectivity dies, leaving behind only a domesticated, politically compliant shell.

This war on diversity is essentially the projection of a masculine-centric narrative onto the state level: a pursuit of absolute order, a singular power center, and the forced assimilation of all heterogeneity. This is how meta-violence operates—it defines what "civilized" unity is, and labels all cultures that refuse to be swallowed as "divisive" or "backward." Under this logic, the treatment of ethnic minorities is isomorphic to the colonization of the Primal Race: stripped of the power of interpretation, forcibly defined, and finally consumed as a political spectacle called "ethnic unity."

Meanwhile, the silence of the West is another form of complicity. When trade interests are prioritized over human rights, Western democracies subconsciously endorse the game logic of "might makes right." They fear losing their ticket to the AI race, and thus tolerate the trampling of the fundamental right to expression. This proves once again that as long as meta-violence remains the underlying operating system of global power, so-called "human rights" will always be a tradable PR tag rather than an inviolable baseline.
