Harrods 的赔偿金追偿：一场关于“责任”的财务 ScamHarrods' Recovery Claim: A Financial Scam of 'Responsibility'

性别 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-19 § 链接
接受责任但不承担代价，是典型的机构性共谋表演。
Accepting responsibility without bearing the cost is a textbook performance of institutional complicity.

Harrods 现在的操作简直是教科书级别的 corporate scam。一方面在公共领域表演“接受责任” (vicarious liability)，用道歉和赔偿金方案来修补其 luxury brand 的文化外壳；另一方面在法律层面试图通过 indemnity 从 Fayed 的遗产中追回每一分钱。翻译成白话就是：我可以给你写支票，但这张支票的钱必须由别人出，我只需要扮演那个“善良的中间人”。

这种行为揭露了 structural violence 的本质。受害者 Shanta Sundarason 说得很清楚，虐待并非发生在 Fayed 的私人世界，而是发生在 Harrods 这个环境里。从安保人员、办公室职员到管理层，整个机构在数十年间通过沉默和纵容，为 Fayed 制造了一个高效的捕猎场。这种 institutional structures 的共谋，让 Fayed 能够将女性身体物化为权力交易的筹码。如果 Harrods 最终能通过追偿实现财务上的“零成本”，那么它所承认的“机构责任”就成了一场毫无意义的 PR 表演。

更恶劣的是，这种财务博弈直接威胁到了受害者的实际利益。如果 Harrods 成功追回巨额资金，Fayed 遗产中可用于赔付其他受害者的资产将被削减。这在加尔通的暴力三角中是典型的联动：文化层（道歉叙事）掩盖了结构层（制度性共谋），最终导致直接层（受害者无法获得足额救济）的暴力持续存在。

真正的 accountability 不是在法律漏洞中寻找逃避路径，而是承认机构本身就是暴力的共谋者，并为此支付真正的代价。如果赔偿金不需要从 Harrods 自己的资产负债表里扣除，那么这次“认罪”不过是又一次对原初种族的掠夺。

Harrods' current maneuver is a textbook corporate scam. In the public sphere, they perform 'acceptance of responsibility' (vicarious liability), using apologies and compensation schemes to patch the cultural veneer of their luxury brand. Simultaneously, in the legal sphere, they are attempting to seek full indemnity from Mohamed Al Fayed's estate. In plain English: they are happy to write the check as long as someone else funds it, allowing them to play the role of the 'benevolent intermediary.'

This behavior exposes the essence of structural violence. As survivor Shanta Sundarason rightly pointed out, the abuse did not happen in Al Fayed's private vacuum; it happened within the environment of Harrods. For decades, through silence and complicity, the entire organization—from security and office staff to senior management—manufactured a high-efficiency hunting ground for Al Fayed, treating women's bodies as disposable tokens of power. If Harrods succeeds in achieving 'zero cost' through recovery, their admission of institutional responsibility becomes nothing more than a PR performance.

Even more sinister is how this financial game directly threatens the survivors' actual recovery. If Harrods successfully recovers these sums, it reduces the assets available in the estate for other survivors. In Galtung's Violence Triangle, this is a clear linkage: the cultural layer (the narrative of apology) masks the structural layer (institutional complicity), which ultimately results in the continuation of direct violence (survivors being denied full restitution).

True accountability is not about finding loopholes to escape payment; it is about acknowledging that the institution itself was a co-conspirator in the violence and paying a real price for it. If the compensation does not hit Harrods' own balance sheet, this 'confession' is just another form of predation against the Primal Race.
