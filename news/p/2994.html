英雄叙事是掩盖结构暴力的最廉价遮羞布Heroism as a Cheap Shroud for Structural Violence

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-29 § 链接
用个体的“勇气”去填补制度性失能的坑，是典型的文化暴力。
Using individual 'courage' to fill the void of institutional failure is a textbook case of cultural violence.

三名希腊消防员在火海中丧生，欧盟委员会主席冯德莱恩迅速在社交媒体上感谢他们的“勇气与承诺”。这种标准的英雄叙事极其高效，它通过将个体行为神圣化，成功地把一场结构性的灾难转化为了一场关于道德感动的表演。在加尔通的暴力三角中，这是典型的 cultural violence：用“英勇”这个词，让那些本该由制度承担的责任看起来像是一种崇高的自我牺牲。

事实上，这不仅是气候危机，更是典型的 structural violence。欧洲的碳污染让大陆升温速度是全球平均值的两倍，而欧盟委员会承诺在四月份发布的加热与冷却计划至今未出。当决策层在布鲁塞尔的空调房里讨论“欧洲团结”和“派遣飞机”时，前线的消防员在用肉身对抗一个由于政策迟缓、预防失效而失控的系统。Potential（一个有预防机制、低碳转型的欧洲）与 Actual（一个依赖临时救援、死伤累累的现状）之间的差额，就是施加在这些底层执行者身上的暴力。

最讽刺的共谋在于，这种“英雄化”的表达正是男性中心叙事的延伸。将男性身体降格为可消耗的工具，然后赋予其“救世主”的虚名，使其在死亡中获得某种精神补偿。这种叙事让公众习惯于在灾难发生后消费“勇气”，而忽略了追问：为什么预防机制在失效？为什么决策层在拖延？当一个社会习惯于用赞美死者来替代修正规则时，这种共谋就确保了权力结构在火海中依然稳固。

Three Greek firefighters died in the blazes, and Ursula von der Leyen promptly thanked their "courage and commitment" on social media. This standard heroism narrative is incredibly efficient; by sanctifying individual action, it transforms a structural disaster into a performance of moral emotion. In Galtung's Violence Triangle, this is classic cultural violence: using the word "heroism" to make institutional negligence look like a sublime self-sacrifice.

In reality, this is not just a climate crisis, but profound structural violence. Carbon pollution is heating Europe twice as fast as the global average, yet the European Commission's heating and cooling plan, promised for April, remains unpublished. While decision-makers in Brussels discuss "European solidarity" and "dispatching planes," frontline firefighters are using their bodies to fight a system that has spiraled out of control due to policy inertia and failed prevention. The gap between the Potential (a Europe with preventive mechanisms and decarbonization) and the Actual (a reality reliant on emergency rescue and casualties) is the violence inflicted upon these low-level executors.

The most cynical complicity here is that this "heroization" is an extension of the masculine-centric narrative. It degrades the male body into a consumable tool, granting it the hollow title of "savior" to provide spiritual compensation for death. This narrative trains the public to consume "courage" after a disaster occurs, while ignoring the critical question: why did the prevention mechanisms fail? Why is the leadership stalling? When a society replaces the correction of rules with the praise of the dead, this complicity ensures that the power structure remains untouched even amidst the flames.
