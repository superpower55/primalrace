身份的盗版与主体性的空心化Identity Piracy and the Hollowed-out Subjectivity

科技 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-18 § 链接
当表达被AI剥离主体性，身份变成了可以随意套用的皮肤。
When expression is stripped of subjectivity by AI, identity becomes a skin to be worn.

这起所谓的“阿加莎式谜案”，本质上是一场关于表达权的低端掠夺。在第一章的逻辑里，表达是存在的确证，是“我是谁”的声明。但当 Generative AI 介入，表达被解构成了一套可复制的 pattern。Fake Linda 并不需要成为 Linda，她只需要通过 AI 模拟出 Linda 的“表达风格”和“专业背景”，就能在认知入口处骗过编辑。这是一种极其恶劣的 identity scam：盗贼偷走的不是钱包，而是一个专业人士用数十年构建的信用资产。

最讽刺的环节在于 New York Post 的反应——在被告知发生了身份窃取后，他们竟然要求真正的 Linda 提供信息来“验证身份”。在这个结构里，一个人的真实性不再取决于她的生物墙（Biological Wall）或长期的社会关系，而取决于她是否持有某个特定域名的邮箱。当验证机制变成了对“数字凭证”的依赖，人的主体性就被空心化了。你不再是你，你只是一个符合验证协议的账号。

这种现象揭示了一种新型的 structural violence：AI 降低了伪装成本，导致信任成本被转嫁给了真正的劳动者。正如 Nature 编辑所言，这种诈骗会让新人自由撰稿人的生存空间进一步被挤压，因为“信任”变成了某种特权，只有被既定权力结构认可的“老面孔”才能获得入场券。这是一种典型的共谋——平台为了效率牺牲验证，诈骗者利用漏洞套利，而最终买单的是那些在存在性战争中努力寻找最优解的真实个体。

This so-called "Agatha Christie-style mystery" is essentially a low-end plunder of the right to expression. In the logic of the first chapter, expression is the confirmation of existence; it is the declaration of "who I am." But with the intervention of Generative AI, expression has been decomposed into a replicable pattern. Fake Linda doesn't need to become Linda; she only needs to use AI to simulate Linda's "expression style" and "professional background" to deceive editors at the cognitive entry point. This is a malignant identity scam: the thief isn't stealing a wallet, but the credit assets built over decades by a professional.

The most ironic part is the reaction of the New York Post—after being informed of the identity theft, they actually asked the real Linda to provide information to "verify her identity." In this structure, a person's authenticity no longer depends on their Biological Wall or long-term social relations, but on whether they hold an email address from a specific domain. When verification mechanisms become a reliance on "digital credentials," human subjectivity is hollowed out. You are no longer you; you are merely an account that satisfies a verification protocol.

This phenomenon reveals a new form of structural violence: AI lowers the cost of disguise, shifting the cost of trust onto the real laborers. As the Nature editor noted, such fraud further squeezes the survival space for early-career freelancers, because "trust" becomes a privilege reserved for "old faces" recognized by the established power structure. This is a form of complicity—platforms sacrifice verification for efficiency, fraudsters profit from the loophole, and the final bill is paid by real individuals struggling for an optimal expression in the existential war.
