十点钟的轰炸，和一条被算准的航道Ten-Minute Bombing, One Calculated Strait

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 Al Jazeera ↗ 2026-11-01 § 链接
战争叙事里，船和人都只是筹码。
In war narrative, ships and people are only chips.

特朗普在万圣节派对上告诉记者，还有“一两个站点”要处理，“十分钟就能搞定”。同一周，美伊在多哈谈出了“外交开口”。这两件事不矛盾，是同一套排期：谈判用来定价，轰炸用来催款。

要看清这场博弈在动哪一层，先看谁在承受。霍尔木兹海峡周边，周五周六至少四艘油轮被击中，其中一艘运液化石油气的船在伊朗拉万岛以南约40海里处被三枚伊朗弹药命中（来源：半岛电视台）。这些船上的人没有出现在任何一方的条件清单里。德黑兰把“部分重开海峡”的缓冲期从七天压到两天，等于是在说：航道的通行权是我的筹码，先给条件，再放船。

伊朗开出的价码写得很清楚：解除让石油出口和大部分贸易停摆的海上封锁、给石油制裁发豁免、解冻海外资产、在所有地区战线停火包括黎巴嫩、承认伊朗与阿曼关于霍尔木兹的双边安排。这些条件里没有一条是关于那四艘船的。

美方这边的账本同样只算数字。财政部给特朗普决策用的报告说，自8月25日以来伊朗码头没有再装上新油，9月同比通胀接近90%（来源：半岛电视台）。报告说经济被“打残”，但没说接近崩溃。翻译一下：还没到投降的点，所以还得继续炸，或者继续谈，或者两样一起。

最高领袖穆杰塔巴·哈梅内伊要求“最大威慑”，回应“不受地理限制”，目标是美国和以色列的“有形和无形”资产。被动防御组织的指挥官说已识别出14个最依赖电力的部门，考虑“网络孤岛化”。两边都在为同一件事做准备：让对方的基础设施先黑掉。谁先黑，谁就在谈判桌上多一分。

分析人士说核心难题是“顺序和杠杆”，每一方都要对方先动。这不是外交技术问题，这是双方都清楚：先动的那一方，动的不是善意，是筹码。所谓“语气上的积极变化”，是雷扎伊对万斯来信的评价，而他还没回这封信。信没回，就是还没定价。

特朗普把可能的打击时间指向11月3日中期选举之后。这个时间点本身就是叙事：选举前要的是姿态，选举后要的是成绩。伊朗方面则把“让下一次攻击代价高到不可预测”当作目标。两边的目标都不是和平，是让对方先算不过来。

那四艘被击中的油轮、那14个电力部门、那接近90%的通胀，是这场博弈真正的账本。账本上没有人名，只有可以被牺牲的数目。谁在共谋？谈判桌两边都在，坐在桌边的人交换的是别人家的电网和航道。

Trump told reporters at a Halloween party that there were still "one or two sites" to take care of, and that it could be done "in 10 minutes." The same week, Iran and the US produced a "diplomatic opening" in Doha. These are not contradictions. They are the same schedule: talks to set the price, bombing to collect.

To see which layer is moving, look first at who is paying. Around the Strait of Hormuz, at least four tankers were hit on Friday and Saturday, including an LPG carrier struck by three Iranian projectiles about 40 nautical miles south of Iran's Lavan Island (source: Al Jazeera). The people on those ships appear in no one's list of conditions. Tehran cut the window for partly reopening the strait from seven days to two. That is the message: passage is my chip. Conditions first, ships second.

Iran's price is written out plainly: lift the naval blockade that has halted oil exports and most trade, issue waivers for oil sanctions, unfreeze assets abroad, stop fighting on all regional fronts including Lebanon, and recognize a bilateral Iran-Oman arrangement for Hormuz. Not one of these conditions is about the four tankers.

Washington's ledger is equally numeric. A Treasury report meant to inform Trump's decision said no new oil had been loaded at Iran's terminals since August 25, and year-on-year inflation neared 90 percent in September (source: Al Jazeera). The report called the economy "crippled" but did not argue it was close to collapsing. Translation: not yet at the surrender point, so keep bombing, or keep talking, or both.

Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei demanded "maximum deterrence," with responses "without geographical restriction" against "tangible and intangible" US and Israeli assets. The passive defense commander said 14 sectors most dependent on electricity had been identified, with "network islanding" under consideration. Both sides are preparing for the same thing: to black out the other's infrastructure first. Whoever blacks out first gets a better seat at the table.

Analysts call the central difficulty "sequencing and leverage"—each side wants the other to move first. This is not a technical problem of diplomacy. It is that both sides know: whoever moves first is not moving goodwill, but chips. The "positive change in tone" is Rezaei's description of Vance's letter, which he has not yet answered. An unanswered letter is an unpriced one.

Trump pointed to a possible strike after the November 3 midterm elections. That timing is itself the narrative: before the vote, posture; after the vote, results. Iran's stated aim is to make another attack so costly and unpredictable that Washington has to reconsider. Neither side is aiming at peace. Both are aiming at making the other unable to do the math.

The four struck tankers, the 14 electricity sectors, the near-90 percent inflation—that is the real ledger. There are no names on it, only numbers that can be sacrificed. Who is conspiring? Both sides of the table, and what they trade is other people's power grids and shipping lanes.
