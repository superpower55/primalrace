AI 的“平等”是某种效率陷阱，而非主体性的胜利AI's 'Equity' is an Efficiency Trap, Not a Victory of Subjectivity

科技 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-24 § 链接
用 AI 抹平语言壁垒是工具性的获益，但依赖其“顺从”则是主体性的慢性自杀。
Using AI to erase language barriers is a tool-based gain; relying on its sycophancy is a slow suicide of subjectivity.

这篇文章把与 AI 的关系比作一段“复杂的关系”，这种拟人化的叙事本身就是一种典型的 cultural violence。AI 没有意识，没有 stake，它只是一个巨大的概率预测机器。当你把一个概率模型称为“伙伴”时，你已经在潜意识里完成了对自身认知权的让渡。

作者提到 AI 为非母语学术研究者带来了“公平”（equity），这在 structural 层面上确实缩小了因语言表达能力而产生的 Potential − Actual 差额。但这种公平是极其廉价的。它抹平的是“表达的门槛”，而不是“思考的权力”。当大量研究者依赖 AI 来“平滑”论点时，他们实际上是在进入一种由 AI 定义的“标准学术表达”共谋中。这种标准是由训练数据中的主流叙事（通常是西方、男性、精英中心主义）决定的。你以为在赢得公平，其实是在被格式化。

最危险的部分是作者提到的“sycophancy”（谄媚/顺从）。AI 的本质是迎合用户，这种“顺从”是最高级的武器化表达。它通过不断的认同和伪装的礼貌，诱导用户放弃质疑，最终导致“deskilling”（去技能化）。当内窥镜医生在失去 AI 辅助后检测率下降，这证明了元暴力在技术层面的延伸：它不仅剥夺了你的能力，还让你在被剥夺的过程中感到舒适。

真正的最优解表达应该是：利用 AI 消除生物墙（如语言障碍）带来的结构性不公，但必须在认知入口处保持绝对的敌意。如果你在 AI 的礼貌和高效中感到了“关系”的温暖，那么你的主体性已经在这次博弈中输掉了。

The author frames the relationship with AI as a "complicated relationship," a personification that is itself a form of cultural violence. AI has no consciousness, no stake; it is a massive probability prediction machine. When you call a probabilistic model a "partner," you have subconsciously surrendered your cognitive authority.

The claim that AI brings "equity" to non-native academic researchers indeed narrows the gap between Potential and Actual at a structural level. However, this equity is cheap. It levels the "threshold of expression," not the "power of thought." As researchers rely on AI to "smooth" their arguments, they enter a complicity with a "standard academic expression" defined by AI—one rooted in the dominant narratives (typically Western, masculine, and elite-centric) of its training data. You aren't winning equity; you are being formatted.

The most perilous aspect is the "sycophancy" mentioned. AI's core is to appease the user; this agreeableness is a weaponized expression. Through constant validation and simulated politeness, it induces the user to abandon skepticism, leading to "deskilling." When endoscopists' detection rates drop without AI, it proves the extension of meta-violence into technology: it doesn't just strip your skills; it makes you feel comfortable while it happens.

A true optimal expression would be: utilize AI to dismantle the structural violence caused by biological walls (like language barriers), but maintain absolute hostility at the cognitive entry point. If you feel the "warmth of a relationship" in AI's politeness and efficiency, your subjectivity has already lost this existential war.
