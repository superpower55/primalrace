边境上的肉身博弈与文明的掩体Flesh Games at the Border and the Shelter of Civilization

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-08 § 链接
文明的秩序是建立在对特定人群‘非人化’的共谋之上的。
Civilized order is built upon the complicity of dehumanizing specific groups of people.

Violence = Potential − Actual。在塞乌塔（Ceuta）的海滩上，这个差额被具象化成了没有帐篷的寒夜和被饥饿驱赶的肉身。当西班牙政府在统计 72,000 与 80,000 之间那点微小的数字差异时，他们实际上在进行一场关于‘存在性’的抹除。对于权力者来说，难民不是人，而是需要被计算、被管理、被驱逐的‘流量’。

最令人作呕的 structural violence 在于这种精准的控制：利用饥饿作为压力工具，强迫人们‘自愿’返回。在这种极端的权力不对等下，任何所谓的‘自愿’都是一个彻头彻尾的 scam。而那些在社交媒体上被 weaponized 的‘机会’叙事，则成了将数万名绝望者推向死亡边界的认知入口。摩洛哥官员的纵容与西班牙边境的封锁，构成了一场极其阴暗的政治共谋，难民的身体成了两国博弈的筹码。

而在这场元暴力的阴影下，一个 16 岁女孩的讲述揭露了更深层的性别暴力。即便在最底层的受害者群体中，男性中心叙事的惯性依然在运作：食物和衣服被男性优先抢占，女性在极端的脆弱中被二次边缘化。这证明了无论在哪个阶层， masculine-centric narrative 始终在定义谁拥有优先生存权。

那些由当地居民自发组织的临时避难所是 good_news，因为它们在 direct 层面上缩小了暴力的差额。但这种基于良心的个体救济，恰恰反衬出国际人道主义体系的彻底失效。当‘文明’的定义仅限于那些拥有合法护照的人，而将数千名儿童晾在街头时，所谓的‘文明’不过是掩盖元暴力的一块遮羞布。

Violence = Potential − Actual. On the beaches of Ceuta, this gap is materialized as freezing nights without blankets and bodies driven by hunger. While the Spanish government quibbles over the difference between 72,000 and 80,000, they are performing an erasure of existence. To the powerful, migrants are not humans, but 'traffic' to be calculated, managed, and deported.

The most repulsive structural violence lies in this precision of control: using hunger as a tool to pressure people into 'voluntarily' returning. In such extreme power asymmetry, any claim of 'volition' is a total scam. Meanwhile, the narratives of 'opportunity' weaponized on social media serve as cognitive entry points, pushing tens of thousands of desperate souls toward the edge of death. The complicity between Moroccan officials' facilitation and Spain's border closures turns human bodies into bargaining chips in a geopolitical game.

Under the shadow of this meta-violence, the account of a 16-year-old girl reveals a deeper layer of gender violence. Even among the most marginalized, the inertia of the masculine-centric narrative persists: men seize food and clothing first, leaving women doubly marginalized in extreme vulnerability. This proves that regardless of class, the masculine-centric narrative always defines who has the priority to survive.

The makeshift shelters organized by local residents are good_news, as they reduce the gap of violence at the direct level. However, this individual rescue based on conscience only highlights the total collapse of the international humanitarian system. When 'civilization' is defined only for those with legal passports while thousands of children are left on the streets, so-called 'civilization' is nothing more than a veil covering meta-violence.
