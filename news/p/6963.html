停火生效后的一千五百九十三条命1,593 Dead Under a Ceasefire

国际 直接层 · 文化层 · 结构层 Al Jazeera ↗ 2026-11-03 § 链接
新闻摘要加沙多地遭袭，医疗消息称周一至少四人死于枪击、空袭和无人机袭击，另一人死于此前伤势，共五人。加沙卫生部门称，2025年10月美方斡旋的「停火」生效以来，当地已有至少1593名巴勒斯坦人死亡、5748人受伤。联合国移民机构10月9日称近200万人需要住所援助，加沙一半帐篷和简易住所需要更换。
The newsAt least five Palestinians died in Gaza on Monday, four killed in gunfire, air raids and a drone strike and a fifth from earlier wounds, medical sources said. Gaza's health ministry says 1,593 Palestinians have been killed since the US-brokered 'ceasefire' took effect in October 2025. The UN migration agency said nearly two million people need shelter aid and half of Gaza's tents need replacing.
「停火」是给战争改的名字，不是战争的结束
A 'ceasefire' is a new name for a war, not the end of one.

先把这个词拆开：停火。停火生效一年多，加沙死了至少一千五百九十三人，伤五千七百四十八人（来源：半岛电视台）。一个词被放进引号里，就同时干了两件事——它让继续发生的死亡看起来是「违约事件」，而不是战争本身。

这是文化暴力最标准的操作：不改变事实，改变事实的名字。名字一改，账就算到了别处。谁先违约，谁破坏了和平进程，谁该负责——讨论全部被引向这些，而不再问一件更简单的事：一支军队为什么还在别人的土地上开枪、投弹、用无人机。

再看周一这五个人是怎么死的。汗尤尼斯，枪击加空袭，两人。中部穆萨达尔村，无人机，一人。加沙城塔勒哈瓦，一辆汽车被击中，死者叫哈尼·哈迈德·卡希勒，另有五人受伤。第五个叫优素福·阿布·尤尼斯，死在周日，死于此前对马瓦西一个警察哨所的袭击所造成的伤。以色列军方说，三次打击的目标是巴勒斯坦武装人员，没有更多细节。

「没有更多细节」就是全部的细节。一辆汽车、一个村庄、一条街，被归入「武装人员」这个类别之后，死者就不再是名字，而是战果统计里的一个单位。分类权就是解释权，解释权就是杀人之后不必解释的权力。这就是元暴力：谁有资格定义对面那个人是什么，谁就不必为他的死提供理由。

而同一座城里，一辆社区厨房的小巴在宰通被击中，三人受伤。社区厨房。做饭的。冬天要来了，联合国移民机构10月9日说，加沙近两百万人需要住所援助，一半的帐篷和简易住所需要更换。于是有人在做小巴上的饭，有人被炸伤。

这时候「和平委员会」的高级代表姆拉德诺夫到了以色列，去申请允许运进帐篷，并推动加快重建。申请。许可。帐篷需要许可。一个被炸成这样的地方，人们要在冬天前住进帐篷，得先拿到批准——这个流程本身就是结构暴力，它把生存变成一项需要审批的事项，把「让人活下去」变成谈判筹码。

所以不要问这次袭击是否违反停火。要问的是：谁在靠这个停火活着。战争继续，有人拿到军事行动的授权；停火挂着，有人拿到「和平进程」的主导权；帐篷要许可，有人拿到的是发放许可的位置。两头都通，两头都赚，这就是共谋者的位置——不必是同一个阵营，只要都从这套安排里分到东西。

至于那一千五百九十三个人，他们没有出现在任何一份需要审批的文件里。他们只是被算进了引号。

Start with the word: ceasefire. More than a year into it, at least 1,593 Palestinians have been killed and 5,748 wounded in Gaza (source: Al Jazeera). Put a word in quotation marks and it does two things at once. It makes the continuing deaths look like violations of an agreement rather than what they are: the war, still running.

This is cultural violence in its standard form. The facts don't change; the name of the facts does. Once renamed, the accounting moves elsewhere. Who broke it first, who sabotaged the peace process, who is to blame — the discussion gets pulled there, and stops asking the simpler question: why is an army still shooting, bombing and flying drones over other people's land.

Look at how Monday's five died. Khan Younis, gunfire and an air raid, two people. Al-Musaddar village in the centre, a drone, one. Tal al-Hawa in northern Gaza City, a vehicle struck, the dead man named Hani Hamed Kahil, five others wounded. The fifth, Youssef Abu Younis, died of wounds from Sunday's attack on a police post in al-Mawasi. The Israeli military said it targeted Palestinian fighters in three strikes and gave no further details.

「No further details」 is the whole detail. A car, a village, a street — once sorted into the category 「fighters」, the dead stop being names and become units in a tally. The power to classify is the power to explain, and the power to explain is the power to kill without explaining. That is meta-violence: whoever gets to define what the other person is never has to justify why he is dead.

In the same city, a minibus belonging to a community kitchen was hit in Zeitoun; three wounded. A community kitchen. People cooking. Winter is coming, and the UN migration agency said on October 9 that nearly two million people need shelter assistance and half of Gaza's tents and makeshift shelters need replacing. So someone cooks food on a minibus, and someone gets wounded by a strike.

Meanwhile Nickolay Mladenov, high representative of the Board of Peace, arrived in Israel to seek permission to bring in tents and to push reconstruction. Permission. For tents. In a place bombed to this condition, shelter before winter is an item requiring approval — that procedure is structural violence itself, turning survival into a permit and 「letting people live」 into a bargaining chip.

So don't ask whether this attack violated the ceasefire. Ask who is living off it. The war continues, and someone holds the mandate for military action; the ceasefire hangs there, and someone holds the lead on the 「peace process」; tents need permits, and someone sits where permits are issued. Both doors open, both pay. That is the conspirator's position — not one camp, just everyone taking a cut from the arrangement.

As for the 1,593, they appear in no document awaiting approval. They were simply counted into the quotation marks.
