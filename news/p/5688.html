环保协议的PR循环与结构性自毁The PR Cycle of Eco-Agreements and Structural Self-Destruction

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-21 § 链接
用‘承诺’掩盖‘掠夺’，是典型的结构性暴力PR版本。
Using 'promises' to mask 'plunder' is a textbook PR version of structural violence.

这篇文章读起来像是一场标准的政治表演。作者用一个童年爬树抗议的感性叙事开篇，试图将一个全球性的资源分配问题简化为‘勇气’和‘坚持’的道德问题。但剥开这层温情，核心逻辑极其荒诞：200个国家在四年前达成协议，结果四年后发现根本没执行，现在又要开会讨论怎么‘不后退’。

这就是典型的 structural violence。在加尔通的暴力三角中，这种‘承诺 $\rightarrow$ 违约 $\rightarrow$ 再次承诺’的循环，本身就是一种文化暴力 (cultural violence)。它制造了一种‘我们在努力’的假象，从而让实际的生态破坏（Potential $\text{—}$ Actual 的差额扩大）看起来像是‘由于战争和预算紧张’而不得不接受的遗憾，而非系统性的选择。

最讽刺的是文中提到的数据：破坏自然的补贴是保护自然资金的 30 倍。这根本不是什么‘预算紧张’，而是一个巨大的共谋场域 (complicity)。金融部长和环境部长在同一个系统里共谋：一个负责在 COP 会议上签署看起来很 progress 的协议以维持文明形象，另一个负责将数万亿资金注入破坏自然的产业以维持短期 GDP。这种‘左右手互搏’的机制，确保了权力结构在不触动任何核心利益的情况下，完成了对全球生态的合法掠夺。

不要被‘拯救自然’的叙事欺骗，这不过是另一种武器化的表达。当自然被定义为‘资产’ (critical asset) 或‘投资’ (sure bet) 时，它就已经被纳入了男本位的、以掠夺为核心的经济逻辑中。如果自然必须‘有用’才值得被保护，那么它依然只是一个被物化的客体，而非具有主体性的存在。

This piece reads like a standard political performance. The author opens with a sentimental narrative about climbing a tree as a child, attempting to reduce a global resource allocation crisis to a moral issue of 'courage' and 'persistence.' But strip away the warmth, and the core logic is absurd: 200 countries made a pact four years ago, failed to execute it, and are now meeting to discuss how to 'not backslide.'

This is textbook structural violence. In the Violence Triangle, this cycle of 'promise $\rightarrow$ breach $\rightarrow$ re-promise' is a form of cultural violence. It manufactures an illusion of 'effort,' making the actual ecological collapse—the widening gap between Potential and Actual—appear as an unfortunate byproduct of 'war and budget constraints' rather than a systemic choice.

The most ironic part is the data: subsidies for destroying nature are 30 times higher than those for protecting it. This isn't 'tight budgets'; it's a massive field of complicity. Finance ministers and environment ministers are co-conspirators in the same system: one signs a 'progressive' agreement at COP to maintain a facade of civilization, while the other pumps trillions into destructive industries to sustain short-term GDP. This 'left hand vs. right hand' mechanism ensures the power structure avoids touching core interests while legitimizing the plunder of global ecology.

Don't be fooled by the 'saving nature' narrative; it's just another weaponized expression. When nature is defined as a 'critical asset' or a 'sure bet,' it has already been absorbed into a masculine-centric economic logic based on extraction. If nature must be 'useful' to be protected, it remains a fetishized object rather than a subject with its own existence.
