比尔·盖茨的“人类保留区”：一场关于定义权的顶级scamBill Gates' 'Human Reserved' Zones: A Top-Tier Scam of Definition

科技 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-26 § 链接
所谓的“保留区”不是为了保护人类，而是为了在AI时代重新定义谁拥有“不可替代”的特权。
The 'reserve' is not about protecting humans, but about redefining who holds the privilege of being 'irreplaceable'.

比尔·盖茨把AI时代的就业危机比作“自然保护区”，提出设立“人类保留区” (human reserved)。这个比喻极其阴险：它在潜意识里将绝大多数普通劳动者定义为“可以被道路和建筑覆盖的荒地”，而将少数被选中的岗位定义为“需要被保护的稀有物种”。

这根本不是在讨论就业保障，而是在进行一次关于“不可替代性”的定价权博弈。盖茨提到的医疗护理、情感陪伴，这些长期被结构性低估、由女性主导的 care work，在AI无法触达的时刻被他赋予了“神圣的人性”光环。但这是一种典型的 weaponized 叙事——当这些工作被定义为“保留区”时，它们就从“需要提高薪资和地位的劳动”变成了“因为人性而应当被保留的特权”。结果就是：这些岗位将继续在低薪和情感剥削中运行，因为它们被贴上了“不可替代”的道德标签，从而堵死了通过技术升级来提升其结构性地位的可能性。

更讽刺的是，一个曾与 Epstein 这种顶级掠夺者共谋、在权力顶端操纵资源数十年的 billionaire，在谈论“政府准备不足”和“国际框架”。他所谓的“架构” (architecture)，本质上是希望在AI彻底颠覆生产力之前，由他这样的认知入口掌控者来制定谁能进入“保留区”的门票。这不是在救赎人类，而是在为未来的数字殖民地划分等级。

最后，他担心AI导致年轻人缺乏 critical thinking。一个掌控着全球最大慈善基金会、试图与最高权力者达成共识的男人，最担心的当然是底层失去了定义现实的能力。因为一旦人们意识到所谓的“人性保留”只是另一种形式的 structural violence，这场关于“保留区”的 scam 就演不成了。

Bill Gates likens the AI employment crisis to 'nature reserves,' proposing 'human reserved' jobs. This metaphor is insidious: it subconsciously defines the vast majority of workers as 'wasteland' to be paved over by digital roads, while designating a few selected roles as 'endangered species' to be preserved.

This isn't a discussion on job security; it's a game of pricing power over 'irreplaceability.' He invokes the 'irreplaceably human' quality of care work—sectors historically dominated by women and structurally undervalued. This is a weaponized narrative. By labeling care work as a 'reserve,' these roles are shifted from 'labor requiring better pay and status' to 'sacred human duties.' The result: these jobs will continue to operate under low pay and emotional exploitation, trapped by a moral label that prevents any structural improvement through technical leverage.

It is peak irony that a billionaire who shared complicity with a predator like Epstein, manipulating global resources for decades, now warns that governments are 'unprepared.' The 'architecture' he envisions is simply a desire for the gatekeepers of cognitive entry to decide who gets a ticket into the 'reserve' before AI disrupts everything. This isn't salvation; it's the zoning of a future digital colony.

Finally, his concern that AI erodes critical thinking in youth is telling. A man who controls a massive global foundation and seeks consensus with the highest powers naturally fears a populace that can dismantle his narratives. If people realize that 'human reserves' are just another form of structural violence, the scam fails.
