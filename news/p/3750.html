用新词汇给地狱命名，是最高级的共谋Naming Hell with New Words: The Ultimate Complicity

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-13 § 链接
当现实不再配合，权力就通过重新定义词汇来掩盖结构性暴力。
When reality no longer complies, power redefines vocabulary to mask structural violence.

日本气象厅给 40 度以上的高温定义了一个新词：‘酷暑日’ (kokushobi)。这简直是典型的 weaponized expression。当全球变暖让极端高温变成 new normal，权力阶层的第一反应不是通过 structural 调整去保护户外劳动者，而是发明一个听起来很新鲜的词汇来给地狱命名。把‘绝望’重新定义为‘一个新类别’，其本质是夺取解释权，将气候灾难转化为一种可以被记录、被观察的‘气象现象’，从而消解了其中蕴含的元暴力。

这种‘定义权’的游戏在香港的预警系统里演变成了赤裸裸的 structural violence。记录破纪录的高温，但预警等级依然维持在最低档。正如前天文台负责人所言，这个系统就是个‘橱窗装饰品’。在这种逻辑下，户外劳动者的身体被客体化为气候数据的采样点，他们的生存风险在官僚系统的阈值设定中被直接抹除。这种‘阈值’不是科学，而是权力对谁该被牺牲的定价权。

最残酷的共谋发生在保险机制中。韩国的作物保险只覆盖‘指定作物’和‘指定区域’，这意味着当气候剧变让原本安全的区域变成烤箱时，农民的损失被定义为‘不在覆盖范围内’。这是一种极其阴险的 structural violence：它先用一个‘保险’的叙事给农民一种安全感，但在真正发生灾难时，通过精细的边界定义，将风险全部推回给个体。农民的绝望被转化为‘农户债务’，而制度在‘审核标准’的掩护下完成了对底层生产者的二次掠夺。

不要被‘基础性改革’这种政治词汇欺骗。如果一个系统在面对破纪录的高温时，首先想到的是给温度起个新名字，而不是让户外工人立即停止工作并获得补偿，那么这个系统并没有在解决问题，它只是在升级它的掩体。

The Japan Meteorological Agency introduced a new term, 'kokushobi' (cruelly hot day), for temperatures above 40C. This is a textbook example of weaponized expression. As global warming turns extreme heat into a new normal, the powers that be respond not with structural adjustments to protect outdoor workers, but by inventing a fresh word to name hell. Redefining 'despair' as a 'new category' is an attempt to seize the right of interpretation, transforming a climate catastrophe into a mere 'meteorological phenomenon' and neutralizing the meta-violence within.

This game of 'definition rights' manifests as naked structural violence in Hong Kong's warning system. Despite record-breaking heat, the warning level remained at the lowest tier. As the former head of the Observatory noted, the system is a mere 'shop window decoration.' In this logic, the bodies of outdoor workers are objectified as sampling points for climate data, while their survival risks are erased by the bureaucratic setting of 'thresholds.' These thresholds are not science; they are the pricing power of authority deciding who is expendable.

The most sinister complicity occurs in insurance mechanisms. South Korea's crop insurance covers only 'designated crops' and 'regions,' meaning when climate shifts turn safe zones into ovens, losses are defined as 'out of scope.' This is a sophisticated form of structural violence: it offers a narrative of security through 'insurance,' only to push all risk back onto the individual through precise boundary definitions during a crisis. Farmers' despair is converted into 'household debt,' while the system completes a second raid on primary producers under the guise of 'audit standards.'

Do not be deceived by political phrases like 'fundamental overhaul.' If a system's first instinct when facing record heat is to give the temperature a new name rather than ensuring outdoor workers stop immediately and receive compensation, that system is not solving the problem—it is simply upgrading its bunker.
