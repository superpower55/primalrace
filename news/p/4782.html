灯塔熄灭后，请停止对“Wonder Woman”的神化Beyond the 'Wonder Woman' Myth: The Danger of Heroizing Gloria Steinem

性别 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-05 § 链接
将结构性胜利包装成个人英雄主义，是另一种形式的叙事抹除。
Packaging structural victories as individual heroism is just another form of narrative erasure.

Gloria Steinem 的离去引发了一场典型的“偶像化”祭奠。从 Dua Lipa 到 Margaret Atwood，所有人的叙事都指向同一个词：Wonder Woman。这种表达看似是赞美，实则是一种危险的 weaponization。当我们将一个女性的成就定义为“超人”或“灯塔”时，我们实际上在潜意识里完成了再一次的 meta violence：将原本属于结构性抗争的胜利，异化为个体的生物性天才或人格魅力。

Steinem 真正重要的不是她多么“cool”或“sexy”，而是她通过 Ms magazine 夺取了认知入口，在那个男性垄断解释权的时代，为女性制造了一个可以进行公正表达的公共空间。这不是一个 Wonder Woman 的单打独斗，而是一场关于身份政治的集体博弈。如果我们将她的成功归结为“不可思议的个人能力”，那么这种叙事就掩盖了背后无数共谋者的倒戈和底层女性的组织化抗争。这种“英雄化”的逻辑，恰恰是男性中心叙事最擅长的套路——将复杂的结构演进简化为个体的传奇，从而让后来的女性在面对同样的结构暴力时，感到绝望，因为她们觉得自己不是“Wonder Woman”。

好新闻应该是：Steinem 留下了一套可复制的、武器化的表达工具，让后辈能够识别并拆解 structural violence。而现在这些赞美词，更多是在 perform 一种温情的怀旧。真正的致敬不应该是对着一个“伟大的灵魂”哭泣，而应该是去审计：在 Potential − Actual 的差额中，还有多少部分被她没能削掉？在那些被她定义的“权利”之下，依然潜伏着多少伪装成“保护”的控制？

灯塔熄灭了，但如果人们只记得灯塔的光芒而忘记了如何制造灯塔，那么这次祭奠不过是一场大型的、表演性的共谋。

The passing of Gloria Steinem has triggered a classic 'idolization' ritual. From Dua Lipa to Margaret Atwood, the narrative converges on one term: Wonder Woman. This expression seems like praise, but it is actually a form of weaponization. When we define a woman's achievements as those of a 'superhero' or a 'lighthouse,' we subconsciously execute another act of meta violence: transforming a structural struggle into a story of individual biological genius or charisma.

Steinem's true significance wasn't that she was 'cool' or 'sexy,' but that she seized the cognitive entry points through Ms magazine, creating a public space for just expressions in an era where men monopolized the power of interpretation. This wasn't the solo act of a Wonder Woman; it was a collective game of identity politics. By attributing her success to 'incredible individual ability,' this narrative erases the complicity of those who defected from the patriarchy and the organized resistance of grassroots women. This 'heroic' logic is exactly the playbook of the masculine-centric narrative—simplifying complex structural evolution into individual legends, leaving subsequent generations of women feeling hopeless because they aren't 'Wonder Women.'

The real good_news should be that Steinem left behind a set of replicable, weaponized tools for expression, enabling successors to identify and dismantle structural violence. Instead, these tributes are largely performing a sentimental nostalgia. True homage isn't weeping over a 'great soul,' but auditing the remaining gap in the Violence Triangle: Potential − Actual. How much of that gap remains? How much control is still lurking beneath the 'rights' she defined, disguised as 'protection'?

The lighthouse has gone dark. But if people only remember the glow and forget how to build the lighthouse, this mourning is nothing more than a grand, performative complicity.
