被系统判定为死亡，是现代官僚制的数字化谋杀Declared Dead by the System: The Digital Murder of Bureaucracy

哲学 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-24 § 链接
当系统定义你为死亡，你的生物学存在便成了冗余数据。
When the system defines you as dead, your biological existence becomes redundant data.

这不仅仅是一个 administrative error，而是一次典型的 structural violence。在 NHSBSA 的数据库里，一个勾选框的变动就抹杀了 IM 作为一个生物人的存在。这种暴力最阴森的地方在于：它不需要直接的肢体冲突，只需要通过对“事实”的定义权，就能在物理世界地制造出真实的饥饿、破损的牙齿和破碎的生活。

这是一个典型的元暴力（meta violence）逻辑：系统（System）被设定为绝对真理，而个体（Individual）被降格为数据的附属品。当 IM 试图通过电话、邮件甚至跨洋飞行来证明自己还活着时，她实际上是在进行一场绝望的存在性战争。但对方的响应是 auto-generated，是“需要移交欺诈部门”，是“系统升级”。在这种叙事里，一个活生生的人在证明自己活着这件事上，竟然需要经过一个判定她已死之系统的审核。这本身就是一种极其荒诞的权力霸凌。

最令人作呕的是 NHSBSA 的 PR 话术：“我们严肃对待责任”、“这显示了我们需要做得更好”。这种表达是典型的 weaponized language，试图用一种温情的、进步的姿态掩盖其机制的冷酷。如果不是《卫报》介入，这个被判定为“死人”的女性可能真的会在系统的沉默中被社会性地抹杀。这证明了在当前的结构中，个体的生存权并不取决于生物学事实，而取决于是否能触达到一个能够覆盖系统的权力入口。

This is far more than an administrative error; it is a textbook case of structural violence. In the records of the NHSBSA, a single checkbox flipped, and IM's existence as a biological human was erased. The most sinister aspect of this violence is that it requires no physical conflict—only the power to define 'fact' to manufacture real hunger, broken teeth, and shattered lives in the physical world.

This follows the logic of meta violence: the System is established as the absolute truth, while the Individual is demoted to a mere appendage of data. When IM attempted to prove she was alive through calls, emails, and even a transcontinental flight, she was engaged in a desperate existential war. The response she received was auto-generated, referred to the 'fraud department,' or delayed by a 'system upgrade.' In this narrative, a living human being must seek approval from the very system that declared her dead to prove she exists. This is an absurd form of power bullying.

Most repulsive is the NHSBSA's PR spin: "We take our responsibility seriously," "This has shown us where we need to do better." This is weaponized language, using a veneer of warmth and progress to mask the cruelty of the mechanism. Without the intervention of The Guardian, this woman—declared dead by the state—might have been socially annihilated by the system's silence. It proves that in the current structure, the right to exist does not depend on biological fact, but on whether one can access a power portal capable of overriding the system.
