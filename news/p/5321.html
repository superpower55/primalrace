面试不是在筛选能力，而是在进行一次身份认同的共谋Interviews Aren't Screening Skills, They're Rituals of Complicity

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-29 § 链接
面试的本质是通过“氛围”完成一次阶级与性别的共谋确认。
Interviews are not about competence, but about confirming racial and class alignment through 'vibes'.

这篇报道把面试描述成一个“怪异的业务”，但它漏掉了最核心的真相：面试从来就不是为了筛选工作能力，而是一场关于 Expression 的博弈。所谓的“氛围 (vibes)”，其实就是面试官在潜意识中检索对方的表型、背景和文化表达，以此判定对方是否属于同一个“种族”。

当面试官在五分钟内决定录用谁时，他们不是在评估专业度，而是在确认共谋的可能性。如果你穿对了裤子，或者说话的语气符合某种特定的阶级品味，你就在这次存在性战争中赢得了门票。这种筛选机制极其残暴，因为它将主体性抹杀在“感觉”之中——你不需要优秀，你只需要像一个“正确的人”。

文中提到 AI 筛选同样充满偏见，这很正常。AI 是由人类数据喂养的，它继承了元暴力的所有基因。如果人类面试官倾向于雇佣那些看起来“稳重”的男性，AI 只会把这种偏见算法化，让结构性暴力变得更高效、更不可见。

最讽刺的是作者最后的自白：她意识到候选人比自己强得多，但她依然确信自己的快速决定是正确的。这就是典型的共谋者心态——在权力上位者看来，这种基于直觉的判定就是“专业”，而这种所谓的专业，正是维持既定权力结构最稳固的文化暴力。

The article describes job interviews as a 'weird business,' but misses the fundamental truth: interviews are never about screening professional ability; they are a gamble of Expression. What is called 'vibes' is actually the interviewer subconsciously scanning the candidate's phenotype, background, and cultural markers to determine if they belong to the same 'tribe.'

When an interviewer decides within five minutes, they aren't assessing competence, but the potential for complicity. If you wear the right trousers or speak with the correct class-based taste, you win the ticket in this existential war. This mechanism is brutal because it erases subjectivity in favor of 'feeling'—you don't need to be excellent; you just need to look like the 'right kind of person.'

The mention of AI screening being equally biased is predictable. AI is fed by human data; it inherits all the genes of meta-violence. If human interviewers prefer 'stable-looking' men, AI simply algorithmizes this bias, making structural violence more efficient and invisible.

The most ironic part is the author's admission: she knows the candidate is far more qualified, yet she remains confident in her snap judgment. This is the classic mindset of a co-conspirator—to the one in power, this intuition-based decision is 'professionalism,' and this 'professionalism' is precisely the cultural violence that maintains the existing power structure.
