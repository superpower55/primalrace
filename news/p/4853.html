鲨鱼的“公民科学”与共谋者的反转Shark 'Citizen Science' and the Inversion of Complicity

好消息 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-08 § 链接
当掠食者的记录者变成保护者，结构性暴力在认知入口处发生了位移。
When the predator's chroniclers become its protectors, structural violence shifts at the cognitive entry point.

这条新闻乍看是个温暖的环保故事，但如果套用加尔通的暴力三角，你会发现它其实是一次有趣的 structural violence 削减实验。长期以来，英国海域的鲨鱼处于一种 Potential（本应存在的生态位）与 Actual（被过度捕捞的现状）的巨大差额中。而这个差额的制造者，正是那些持有鱼竿的 anglers。

最耐人寻味的是 project 的运作机制：研究者没有选择与钓鱼者对抗，而是将这群曾经的“施暴者”转化为“公民科学家”。这在本质上是一场关于认知入口的博弈。通过 Pat Smith Database 这种历史记录，研究者把钓鱼者的“占有欲”转化为“数据贡献”，让钓鱼者在一种“保护者”的新身份表达中获得认同感。这种身份的切换，是典型的通过修改表达来改变博弈最优解：钓鱼者不再通过杀死鲨鱼来证明自己的能力，而是通过记录并释放来证明自己的“ conservation-conscious”。

但我们要警惕这种“好新闻”里的共谋。Garzon 提到钓鱼者有时会被 ostracised（排斥），但他们依然热情。这种热情是否是一种补偿性的叙事？将“捕捉并释放”定义为保护，实际上是给一种带有侵入性的行为贴上了文明的标签。如果 tags 证明 catch-and-release 依然造成高死亡率，那么这种“公民科学”就成了一场大型的 PR scam，用研究的名义为业余捕猎提供合法性外壳。

真正的 good_news 在于，如果这次研究能通过 DNA 映射和路径追踪，将数据直接转化为禁渔区的立法（structural 层面的改变），那么这才是 Actual 朝 Potential 走近了一步。否则，它仅仅是 cultural 层面的叙事修补——让人们觉得我们和鲨鱼共存得很好，而鲨鱼依然在沉默地忍受着被标记、被勾住、被释放的循环。

At first glance, this is a heartwarming environmental story. But applying Galtung's Violence Triangle reveals a fascinating experiment in reducing structural violence. For years, sharks in British waters have existed in a massive gap between their Potential (ecological niche) and Actual (overfished reality). The architects of this gap were the anglers themselves.

The most intriguing part is the project's mechanism: researchers aren't fighting the anglers; they are converting these former 'perpetrators' into 'citizen scientists.' This is essentially a game played at the cognitive entry point. By leveraging the Pat Smith Database, researchers transform the angler's desire for possession into a contribution of data, allowing them to find a new identity as 'protectors.' This shift in expression changes the optimal solution of the game: anglers no longer prove their prowess by killing sharks, but by recording and releasing them to prove they are 'conservation-conscious.'

However, we must be wary of the complicity within this 'good news.' Garzon mentions that anglers are sometimes ostracised yet remain passionate. Is this passion a compensatory narrative? Defining 'catch-and-release' as protection essentially puts a civilized label on an invasive act. If the tags reveal that catch-and-release still results in high mortality, this 'citizen science' becomes a massive PR scam, providing a legitimate shell for amateur hunting under the guise of research.

The genuine good_news would be if this research translates DNA mapping and path tracking directly into legislation for no-take zones—a change at the structural layer. Otherwise, it is merely a repair at the cultural layer—making us feel we coexist peacefully with sharks, while the sharks silently endure a cycle of being tagged, hooked, and released.
