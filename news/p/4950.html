消费主义的“省钱指南”：一场关于定价权的共谋The 'Savings Guide' Scam: A Complicity in Pricing Power

科技 结构层 · 文化层 Wirecutter: Reviews for the Real World ↗ 2026-09-12 § 链接
所谓省钱技巧，不过是品牌在掌控定价权后施舍的认知入口。
So-called saving tips are merely cognitive entries granted by brands that hold absolute pricing power.

Wirecutter 这篇指南精准地揭示了 Apple 如何通过操纵“认知入口”来维持其结构性优势。它试图教你如何通过购买旧款、等待黑五或寻找教育折扣来“省钱”，但这种叙事掩盖了一个事实：在 Apple 的生态闭环里，不存在真正的“便宜”，只存在被精心计算过的“价格阶梯”。

这是一种典型的 cultural violence。它将一个被垄断的定价体系包装成一种可以通过“技巧”去博弈的消费者游戏。当你计算 iPhone 15 和 16 之间 14.2% 的差价是否值得时，你已经进入了对方设定的博弈场。你以为你在寻找最优解，实际上你是在扮演一个“精明消费者”的角色，而这个角色本身就是为了维持 Apple 高端定价策略而存在的共谋者——因为只有当部分人群通过“省钱”进入生态后，整个闭环的粘性才更强。

最讽刺的是文中对关税（tariffs）的讨论。当企业通过库存囤积或申请豁免来对冲成本，而消费者还在讨论如何通过信用卡返现来省钱时，这种 asymmetric information（信息不对称）本身就是一种 structural violence。定价权不在用户手中，甚至不在零售商手中，而是在那个定义了“什么是美”、“什么是无缝连接”的元叙事手中。

别被这种“省钱指南”给 scam 了。真正的最优解不是在对方给的选项里挑一个最便宜的，而是意识到这种让你在“买新还是买旧”之间纠结的机制，本身就是一种对主体性的剥夺。

This Wirecutter guide perfectly illustrates how Apple maintains its structural advantage by manipulating cognitive entries. By teaching users to 'save' via older models or Black Friday deals, the narrative masks a cold truth: in Apple's closed ecosystem, there is no such thing as 'cheap,' only a meticulously calculated price ladder.

This is a form of cultural violence. It reframes a monopolized pricing system as a consumer game that can be 'won' with tips. When you calculate whether a 14.2% price difference between an iPhone 15 and 16 is worth it, you have already entered their game. You think you are seeking an optimal expression, but you are merely performing the role of the 'savvy consumer'—a role that serves as a complicity to sustain Apple's premium pricing strategy. The ecosystem's stickiness grows precisely because some users feel they've 'beaten the system' by saving.

The discussion on tariffs is the most cynical part. While corporations hedge costs through stockpiling or exemptions, consumers are encouraged to hunt for credit card cash-back. This asymmetric information is structural violence. The pricing power doesn't reside with the user or the retailer, but within the meta-narrative that defines 'beauty' and 'seamless connectivity.'

Don't be scammed by these guides. The true optimal expression isn't picking the cheapest option provided by the oppressor; it is realizing that the very mechanism forcing you to choose between 'new' and 'old' is a theft of your subjectivity.
