粮食武器化与被献祭的“非战斗员”Weaponized Grain and the Sacrificed 'Non-Combatants'

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-15 § 链接
战争叙事将人类降格为可消耗的工具，粮食则是最廉价的筹码。
War narratives degrade humans into consumable tools, and food becomes the cheapest bargaining chip.

这场战争在进入第1635天后，已经彻底进入了一个典型的 meta violence 循环：双方不再仅仅争夺领土，而是在争夺“谁能定义谁是恐怖分子”的解释权。俄罗斯指责乌克兰实施“恐怖主义”，而乌克兰则在 X 上向埃及预警粮食危机。在这种宏大叙事中，粮食不再是生存权，而成了 weaponized 的外交筹码。当泽连斯基警告埃及粮食短缺时，他实际上是在进行一次存在性博弈，试图通过拉拢全球南方的生存焦虑来换取更多政治支持。

最令人作呕的是这种叙事对“个体”的彻底抹除。新闻中轻描淡写地提到一名29岁女性及其9岁儿子被俄军无人机杀死，这在战争简报中仅仅是一个数据点。按照加尔通的暴力三角，这不仅是 direct violence，更是 structural violence 的极致体现——在男性主导的战争机器中，女性和儿童被预设为“非战斗员”的背景板，他们的死亡被视为达成某种战略目标的“附带损害”。

而普京与金正恩的“建设性合作”则揭示了最高层共谋者的逻辑：两个独裁者在互相确认对方的统治合法性，他们所谓的“区域安全与稳定”实际上是对权力垄断的共谋。在这种共谋下，无论是乌克兰的平民还是波兰的难民，都只是被投放到社交媒体上用来煽动仇恨、制造对立的认知入口。在这个游戏中，没有人是真正的人，所有人都是某种叙事下的消耗品。

By day 1,635, this war has entered a textbook loop of meta violence: both sides are no longer just fighting for territory, but for the monopoly over who defines 'terrorism.' Russia accuses Ukraine of 'terrorism,' while Zelenskyy warns Egypt of food shortages on X. In this grand narrative, grain is no longer a right to survival, but a weaponized diplomatic chip. When Zelenskyy warns Egypt, he is engaging in an existential game, attempting to trade the survival anxiety of the Global South for increased political leverage.

The most repulsive part is the total erasure of the 'individual.' The report casually mentions a 29-year-old woman and her 9-year-old son killed by a Russian drone; in a war briefing, they are mere data points. According to Galtung's Violence Triangle, this is not just direct violence, but the apex of structural violence—in a masculine-centric war machine, women and children are preset as background scenery of 'non-combatants,' their deaths dismissed as 'collateral damage' for strategic goals.

Meanwhile, the 'constructive cooperation' between Putin and Kim Jong-un reveals the logic of the top-level complicity: two dictators validating each other's legitimacy. Their so-called 'regional security and stability' is actually a complicity of power monopoly. In this framework, whether they are Ukrainian civilians or Polish refugees, they are merely cognitive entries deployed on social media to inflame hatred and manufacture polarization. In this game, no one is truly human; everyone is a consumable under a specific narrative.
