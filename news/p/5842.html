权力往下放，放到谁手里Power Moves Down. To Whose Hands?

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-30 § 链接
分权的话术在换尺子，不在换能力
The rhetoric of devolution changes the ruler, not the capacity.

伯纳姆在格拉斯哥谈"分权下一阶段"，说的是把权力从爱丁堡放到格拉斯哥。斯温尼要的是把权力从伦敦放到爱丁堡。两个人用的是同一个词，说的不是同一件事。

这就是"分权"这个词的用法：它不指一个方向，它指一种姿态。谁在台上，谁就说自己在分权。真正被分配的不是权力，是"谁有权决定权力去哪"这件事的解释权。伯纳姆的版本很清楚——"把政治放一边"，跟伦敦合作，开发"区域赋权"的模式。把政治放一边这句话本身是政治性最强的一句：它预设了现有的框架不用动，只用在框架内部多开几个窗口。

苏格兰、威尔士、北爱尔兰现在第一次同时由主张脱离英国的民族主义政党主政。这个局面下，伦敦推"区域赋权"而不是"议会赋权"，等于绕开三个政府直接跟地方谈。唐宁街的说法是分权"不应是一个议会向另一个议会转移权力"，而应"在各民族内部走得更深"。翻译一下：把谈判桌从政府那一层挪到社区那一层，对手就换了人。

斯温尼的清单是税收、移民、就业法、能源。这些是能改变资源分配的东西。伯纳姆给的是"模式"和"伙伴关系"。一个是能力，一个是评价方式。能力要真给出去，评价方式可以一直给。

北爱尔兰的奥尼尔没到场，因为统一党就德拉姆克里问题否决了她的出席，两位首席部长都得回避，只能派官员。这一条最说明问题：分权设计的初衷是让各方都在桌上，实际运作里，一个否决权就能让一位首席部长缺席一场关于分权的峰会。结构暴力不在谁说了什么，在谁被允许说话。

伯纳姆还承认他的第一份预算不够激进，原因是经济不确定性。这句话可以读成坦白，也可以读成预告：接下来给出去的东西，也会是这个尺寸。

Burnham is in Glasgow to talk about "the next stage of devolution." What he means is moving power from Edinburgh to Glasgow. What Swinney means is moving power from London to Edinburgh. Same word, not the same thing.

That is how "devolution" works as a word: it doesn't name a direction, it names a posture. Whoever is on stage gets to say they are devolving. What is actually being distributed is not power but the interpretive authority over where power goes. Burnham's version is explicit — "put politics aside," work in partnership with the UK government, develop models of "regional empowerment." Putting politics aside is the most political sentence in the statement: it presumes the frame stays fixed and only needs more windows cut into it.

Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland now, for the first time, all have first ministers from nationalist parties that want out. In that configuration, London pushing "regional empowerment" rather than "parliamentary empowerment" means going around the three governments to talk to localities directly. Downing Street's line is that devolution "shouldn't be about the transfer of powers from one parliament to another" but should go deeper within nations. Translation: move the negotiating table down a level, and the counterparty changes.

Swinney's list is taxation, immigration, employment law, energy. Those change who gets what. Burnham is offering "models" and "partnership." One is capacity. The other is a way of scoring. Capacity, once given, is gone. A way of scoring can be given indefinitely.

Michelle O'Neill is absent because the DUP vetoed her attendance over Drumcree, and both joint first ministers have to stay away; officials attend instead. That detail carries the whole story: devolution was designed so everyone sits at the table, and in practice one veto keeps a first minister out of a summit about devolution. Structural violence isn't in what anyone said. It's in who was permitted to speak.

Burnham also conceded his first budget wasn't as radical as he'd have liked, citing economic uncertainty. Read that as a confession, or as a preview: whatever gets handed out next will be that size.
