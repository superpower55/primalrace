把创伤当成噪音，是弱势者唯一的解释权Noise as the Only Agency: Turning Trauma into a Weapon

性别 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-04 § 链接
当语言被元暴力垄断，只有通过身体的自毁式表达才能确证存在。
When language is monopolized by meta-violence, only self-destructive expression can validate existence.

一个叫 PISS 的乐队，把性暴力、Andrea Dworkin 的理论和噪音朋克揉在一起。很多 naive 的乐评人会把它定义为“艺术”或“表演”，但从存在性战争的视角看，这其实是一次极其惨烈的表达博弈。对于性暴力幸存者来说，语言早已被 masculine-centric narrative 武器化了——要么被定义为“受害者”，要么被定义为“自愿”，无论哪个标签，主体性都是死亡的。

Taylor Zantingh 意识到，试图用 literal 的方式“忏悔”或“陈述”创伤是无效的，因为认知入口被掌控在施暴者和共谋者手中。于是她选择了最极端的表达：将身体作为生物墙，通过抽搐、尖叫和自毁式的表演，把 structural violence 转化为可见的 direct violence。她不是在表演痛苦，而是在通过物理性的疼痛和噪音，强行在公共空间中撕开一个属于自己的解释权入口。

最讽刺的是那些在台下嗤之以鼻、高喊“Woke”的男性。他们通过嘲笑来维持自己的共谋者身份，试图用一个政治标签掩盖掉一个生物事实。正如 Zantingh 所说，这些人其实最需要这场演出，因为他们正是那个元暴力的执行者或受益者。当他们感到“不舒服”时，正是因为 PISS 剥离了所有浪漫爱或文明的伪装，直接把元暴力的血淋淋原貌怼到了他们脸上。

但这依然是一场代价巨大的博弈。当一个人的存在性必须建立在对 suffering 的持续挖掘上，这种“最优解表达”本身就带有某种悲剧性的不可持续。她赢回了话语权，但代价是必须在每个夜晚重新经历一次精神上的凌迟。

A band called PISS blends sexual violence, Andrea Dworkin’s theories, and noise-punk. While naive critics might call this 'art,' from the perspective of existential war, it is a brutal gambit for expression. For survivors of sexual violence, language has been weaponized by the masculine-centric narrative—they are either labeled as 'victims' or 'consenting,' both of which result in the death of subjectivity.

Taylor Zantingh realized that attempting to 'confess' or 'describe' trauma literally is futile because the cognitive entry points are controlled by perpetrators and their complicity. Thus, she chose the most extreme expression: using her body as a biological wall, transforming structural violence into visible direct violence through twitching, screaming, and self-destructive performance. She isn't performing pain; she is forcibly carving out an entry point for her own interpretation in the public sphere.

The irony lies with the men sniggering in the crowd, shouting 'Woke!' They use this political label to maintain their status as co-conspirators, attempting to mask biological facts with ideology. As Zantingh noted, these people need the show most of all—they are the executors or beneficiaries of that meta-violence. Their 'discomfort' stems from the fact that PISS strips away all romanticized or 'civilized' veneers, thrusting the raw reality of meta-violence directly into their faces.

Yet, this remains a costly game. When one's existence must be built upon the continuous excavation of suffering, this 'optimal expression' becomes a tragic, unsustainable loop. She has won back the right to define her reality, but the price is a nightly psychic crucifixion.
