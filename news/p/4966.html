用女性作为“诱饵”的最高级共谋The Ultimate Complicity: Using Women as 'Bait'

性别 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-12 § 链接
将女性定义为“诱饵”不仅是厌女，更是元暴力的认知入口。
Defining women as 'enticements' is not just misogyny; it is a cognitive entry point for meta-violence.

Jesse Watters 的这段发言是一个极其标准的 meta violence 样本。在 Fox News 的叙事里，特朗普用 5000 美元贿赂选民这件事，竟然被解释为“像个女人一样在诱惑”。请注意这个逻辑：在 Watters 的认知地图中，女性的本质属性就是“诱饵” (enticement)——一个用来吸引注意力、制造好奇、但不需要负责到底的客体。他通过将“女性”这个身份标签化为一种低廉的操纵手段，成功地将一场赤裸裸的政治贿赂洗白成了某种“俏皮的性别特质”。

这正是表达武器化的典型路径：夺取解释权 $\rightarrow$ 制造事实 $\rightarrow$ 巩固权力。当他把“像女人一样”等同于“诱惑且不必兑现承诺”时，他不仅在为特朗普脱罪，更是在向受众投放一种文化暴力 (cultural violence) 的子弹：女性的表达是不真诚的，女性的身份是工具性的。这种叙事让男性中心主义的解释权再次完成一次闭环——男性定义女性是诱饵，然后男性利用这种定义来掩盖自己的欺诈。

而最讽刺的共谋 (complicity) 在于，这种叙事在右翼生态中被视为一种“幽默”或“洞察”。当这种元暴力被制度化地通过媒体广播时，它在潜意识里完成了对女性主体性的再次抹杀。在这种博弈中，女性被剥夺了作为政治主体的资格，而被降格为一种形容词，一个用来掩盖财政鲁莽和政治骗局的遮羞布。这种“诱惑”的代价，最终是由那些在结构性暴力中失去医疗权、生育权的真实女性来支付的。

Jesse Watters' remarks are a textbook sample of meta-violence. In the Fox News narrative, Trump's attempt to bribe voters with $5,000 is rebranded as 'behaving like a woman.' Notice the logic: in Watters' cognitive map, the essential attribute of a woman is to be an 'enticement'—an object used to attract attention and create intrigue without any obligation for follow-through. By tagging the female identity as a cheap manipulative tool, he successfully launders a blatant political bribe into a 'playful gender trait.'

This is the classic path of the weaponisation of expression: seizing the right to interpret $\rightarrow$ manufacturing facts $\rightarrow$ consolidating power. When he equates 'being like a woman' with 'enticing without keeping promises,' he isn't just exonerating Trump; he is deploying a bullet of cultural violence. He signals to the audience that female expression is insincere and female identity is instrumental. This masculine-centric narrative completes a closed loop: men define women as bait, and then use that definition to mask their own fraud.

The most cynical part is the complicity inherent in this ecosystem, where such rhetoric is dismissed as 'humor.' When this meta-violence is institutionalized through mass media, it subconsciously erases female subjectivity once again. In this existential war, women are stripped of their status as political subjects and downgraded to an adjective—a fig leaf used to cover fiscal recklessness and political scams. The actual cost of this 'enticement' is paid by real women who are losing their healthcare and reproductive rights under structural violence.
