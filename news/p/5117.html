特朗普及他的“法律即我”：一场关于解释权的暴力博弈Trump and the 'I am the Law' Fantasy: A Game of Interpretive Violence

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-19 § 链接
当权力试图通过重新定义“合法”来抹除异己的投票权，这就是典型的结构性暴力。
When power attempts to erase the voting rights of others by redefining 'legality,' it is structural violence in its purest form.

特朗普及他的行政命令试图限制邮寄选票，这在表面上是一场关于“选民资格”的法律争端，但剥开来看，这是一次极其粗暴的 weaponization。他试图夺取对“谁有资格投票”的解释权，进而通过操控认知入口，将一个极低概率的 fraud 叙事放大为政治武器，目的只有一个：在存在性战争中通过削弱对方的“票”值来确保自己的赢面。

这件事最令人作呕的在于其共谋机制。特朗普及他的司法部长 Todd Blanche 正在将 DOJ 变成一个私人律师事务所。当法律不再是公正的表达，而变成了权力者的 whim，这种 meta violence 就达到了顶峰——他不仅要赢，还要定义什么是“赢”的规则。他攻击那些不听话的保守派法官，称他们是“原貌的躯壳”，这实际上是在警告所有共谋者：忠诚度高于法律，服从度高于宪法。

最高法院这次的驳回确实缩小了 Potential 和 Actual 之间的暴力差额，但这仅仅是 structural 层面的暂时修补。只要特朗普及他的叙事机器还在运转，他就会继续通过制造“北卡罗来纳州是犯罪地狱”这种虚假事实来恐吓选民。这种 cultural violence 的目的就是让人们在恐惧中交出主体性，将安全感寄托于一个自封的“救世主”身上。

不要被这次的“法律胜利”冲昏头脑。当一个权力者公开宣称“我就是法律”时，他已经在尝试拆除所有制衡的生物墙。这场博弈的终点不是某个法案的通过或否决，而是我们是否还能在男本位的强权叙事之外，维持一个公正的表达空间。

Trump’s executive order to restrict mail-in ballots is framed as a legal dispute over 'voter eligibility,' but beneath the surface, it is a blatant weaponisation of expression. By attempting to seize the interpretive power over who is 'eligible' to vote, he is using a narrative of fraud—despite a statistical insignificance of 0.000043%—as a weapon to diminish the value of his opponents' votes in this existential war.

The most repulsive part is the complicity at play. Trump and his Attorney General, Todd Blanche, are effectively transforming the DOJ into a private law firm. When the law ceases to be a Just Expression and becomes a mere extension of a leader's whims, meta-violence reaches its peak: he doesn't just want to win; he wants to own the definition of 'winning.' His attacks on conservative justices who dared to rule against him are a clear signal to all co-conspirators—loyalty overrides the law, and obedience overrides the Constitution.

While the Supreme Court's rebuke temporarily narrows the gap in the Violence Triangle at the structural level, it is merely a patch. As long as Trump’s narrative machine continues to manufacture fake realities—such as painting North Carolina as a 'crime-ridden hellhole'—he is deploying cultural violence to instill fear and push voters to surrender their agency to a self-proclaimed 'savior.'

Do not be naive enough to celebrate this 'legal victory.' When a president openly claims that he is the law, he is attempting to dismantle every biological and institutional wall of restraint. The endgame of this game is not the passing or failing of a bill, but whether we can maintain a space for Just Expressions outside of a masculine-centric narrative of absolute power.
