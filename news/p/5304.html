球迷的酒精权与邻居的呕吐物：一场关于“文明”的共谋博弈Booze, Puke, and the Farce of 'Civilization' in Football Stands

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-27 § 链接
所谓的“秩序”往往是掩盖结构性失败的PR，而非真正的安全。
Order is often just a PR mask for structural failure, not actual safety.

这篇关于足球场禁酒令的争论，本质上是一场关于“谁的舒适度更优先”的存在性战争。法律专家 Pearson 揭露了一个典型的 structural violence 样本：1985年的禁酒法是一部典型的“恐慌立法” (panic law)，它在没有任何证据证明能减少骚乱的情况下被匆忙通过。这种立法逻辑在权力运作中极其常见——通过制造一个简单的禁令来表演“我在治理”，而实际上它只是将暴力和混乱从看台转移到了市中心和火车站，制造了更多的 binge drinking 和拥挤风险。这是一种典型的用“禁令”代替“管理”的懒政 scam。

而那位抱怨墙根下呕吐物的居民，则代表了另一种文化层面的共谋。他将禁酒令带来的“文明”视为理所当然，因为他的个体舒适度在结构性暴力中获益了。在这种叙事里，“文明”被定义为“不让醉汉出现在我的视线里”，而不是“如何建立一个可持续的公共秩序”。这种对“文明”的定义权被垄断在那些能够通过立法将麻烦“外包”给他人的人手中。

当首相开始讨论取消禁酒令时，他并不是在关心球迷的权利，而是在进行一次政治博弈，试图通过给出一个“不歧视”的姿态来换取选票。这依然不是公正的表达 (Just Expressions)，因为无论是维持禁令还是取消禁令，其核心都没触及那个真正的 Potential − Actual 差额：体育场周边的基础设施匮乏、公共安全管理的低效，以及球迷群体与社区之间长期缺失的共识。只要这种结构性失效存在，无论酒杯在不在看台上，暴力和呕吐物永远有地方安置。

The debate over the football alcohol ban is a textbook existential war over whose comfort takes precedence. Prof Pearson exposes a clear sample of structural violence: the 1985 Act was a 'panic law,' rushed through without evidence that it reduced disorder. This logic is pervasive in power dynamics—performing 'governance' via a simple ban while merely displacing violence and chaos to city centers and stations, fueling binge drinking and crushes. It is a lazy governance scam that substitutes prohibition for actual management.

Meanwhile, the resident complaining about vomit on their wall represents a different kind of complicity at the cultural layer. They view the 'civilization' brought by the ban as a given, because their individual comfort benefits from this structural violence. In this narrative, 'civilization' is defined as 'keeping drunks out of my sight,' rather than 'establishing a sustainable public order.' The power to define 'civilization' is monopolized by those who can outsource their inconveniences via legislation.

When the PM considers lifting the ban, he isn't championing fans' rights; he is playing a political game, trading a gesture of 'non-discrimination' for votes. This is still not a Just Expression. Whether the ban stays or goes, the core issue remains the structural failure: inadequate infrastructure, inefficient public safety management, and a void of consensus between fans and the community. As long as this structural failure persists, violence and vomit will always find a place to land, regardless of where the beer is consumed.
