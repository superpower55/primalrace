版权战争的遮羞布与俄式盗播的共谋The Shroud of Copyright Wars and Russian Piracy Complicity

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-24 § 链接
法律诉讼是版权方的PR表演，真正的暴力是资源定价权的垄断。
Legal action is a PR performance; the real violence is the monopoly of resource pricing.

英格兰足球联盟（EFL）和苏格兰联赛（SPFL）现在跳出来指责俄罗斯的 Football TV 盗播，这在叙事层面上被包装成了一场关于“法律”和“正义”的维权。但剥开这层皮，这本质上是一场关于定价权（Pricing Power）的博弈。2022年因为战争停止授权，意味着版权方在政治正确和商业风险之间做了一次对冲，而现在发现有人在偷偷地、非法地把他们的产品卖钱，这触动的是资本最敏感的神经：利润被截流了。

这场闹剧最讽刺的地方在于，所谓的“非法”只是因为没有经过官方授权的付费流程。在 Football TV 的 Telegram 频道里，这种盗播被包装成一种对体育资源的“民主化”获取，而联盟方则试图通过法律手段重新夺回认知入口。这种博弈中没有任何一方在关心体育精神，他们关心的是谁在共谋（complicity）分食这块蛋糕。盗播者通过非法链路绕过结构性壁垒，而联盟方则通过法律威胁来维护其结构性垄断。

这种“版权战争”是典型的 structural violence 的微观体现：资源被少数机构垄断，然后通过定价权来筛选谁有资格观看。当俄罗斯的平台通过盗播打破这个闭环时，它并没有在解放观众，而是在构建一套新的、非法的共谋机制。联盟方现在启动的调查和法律行动，不过是给自己的商业垄断披上一件“维护法治”的外衣，试图在 meta 层面上重新定义什么是“正确的”消费路径。

The EFL and SPFL are now calling out Football TV in Russia for illegal broadcasts, wrapping the narrative in 'law' and 'justice.' But strip away the facade, and this is fundamentally a game of Pricing Power. Stopping licenses in 2022 was a hedge between political correctness and commercial risk; discovering someone else is monetizing their product without a cut hits the most sensitive nerve of capital: intercepted profit.

The irony is that 'illegality' here simply means bypassing the official payment pipeline. While Football TV frames this as a 'democratization' of sports resources on Telegram, the leagues are trying to reclaim their cognitive entry points through legal threats. No one in this game cares about the spirit of sport; they care about who is in complicity to slice the cake. The pirates bypass structural barriers, while the leagues use legal intimidation to maintain their structural monopoly.

This 'copyright war' is a textbook micro-manifestation of structural violence: resources are monopolized by a few entities, then filtered via pricing. When Russian platforms break this loop, they aren't liberating the audience; they are building a new, illicit mechanism of complicity. The investigations and legal threats are merely a shroud of 'rule of law' draped over commercial monopoly, an attempt at the meta level to redefine the 'correct' path of consumption.
