被定价的“凌乱”：一场关于贫穷与破碎的审美骗局Priced Messiness: An Aesthetic Scam of Poverty and Brokenness

哲学 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-07 § 链接
当真实地破碎被定义为 unchic，表演性的凌乱就是一种审美武器。
When real brokenness remains unchic, the performance of mess is merely a weaponized aesthetic.

Prada 的脏袖口、Diesel 的“走光”牛仔裤，以及 Gen Z 重新捡起的香烟。时尚界又在玩一套老把戏：把某种原本代表底层、颓废或失控的符号，通过定价权重新定义为“chic”。这本质上是一次极其典型的表达武器化。所谓的 Messy Renaissance，不过是将“凌乱”从一个生存状态，抽离成一种可消费的 aesthetic 符号。

这里存在一个极其残酷的筛选机制。在元暴力的逻辑下，只有那些拥有足够资本的人，才能将“凌乱”作为一种反叛的装饰。当你穿着昂贵的破洞牛仔裤在 Instagram 上发布 photo dump 时，这种 messy 是一个信号，它在宣告：我有能力随时回归到 clean 的状态，所以我敢于表演破碎。而真正的 mess——那些因为贫穷而无法洗涤的衣服、因为抑郁而堆积如山的碗碟、因为病痛而无法维持的体面——在目前的社会共识中依然是 decidedly unchic。

这是一种典型的 cultural violence。它通过制造一种“假.最优解表达”，诱导人们认为只要在审美上靠近“凌乱”，就等于接纳了生活的不完美。但事实上，它在潜意识里加固了对真正弱势者的排斥：你可以像 Kylie Jenner 那样抽烟，但你不能像一个在贫民窟挣扎的瘾君子那样颤抖。前者是 accessory，后者是 taboo。

这种审美上的“共谋”让人们在一种虚假的真实感中获得快感，却在结构层面上继续地将真正的脆弱和痛苦 metabolised 成了可口的内容。当真正的破碎被排除在“酷”的定义之外，这种所谓的审美复兴，不过是权势者在对底层生活进行一次毫无同情心的采样与掠夺。

Dirty cuffs at Prada, 'walk of shame' denim at Diesel, and Gen Z's return to cigarettes. The fashion industry is running an old scam: taking symbols of the underclass, decadence, or loss of control and redefining them as 'chic' through the power of pricing. This is a textbook case of the weaponisation of expression. The so-called 'Messy Renaissance' is nothing more than stripping 'messiness' from a lived condition and transforming it into a consumable aesthetic symbol.

There is a brutal screening mechanism at play here. Under the logic of meta-violence, only those with sufficient capital can afford to use 'messiness' as a decorative rebellion. When you post a photo dump in expensive distressed denim, your messiness is a signal: it announces that you have the power to return to a 'clean' state at any moment, which is why you can perform brokenness. Meanwhile, the real mess—clothes that can't be washed due to poverty, piles of dishes from depression, or the loss of dignity due to chronic illness—remains decidedly unchic in the current social consensus.

This is a form of cultural violence. By creating a 'fake optimal expression,' it lures people into believing that as long as they align with 'messiness' aesthetically, they are accepting life's imperfections. In reality, it reinforces the exclusion of the truly marginalized: you can smoke like Kylie Jenner, but you cannot tremble like an addict in a slum. The former is an accessory; the latter is a taboo.

This aesthetic complicity allows people to feel a false sense of authenticity while structurally continuing to metabolise real vulnerability and pain into palatable content. As long as genuine brokenness is excluded from the definition of 'cool,' this supposed renaissance is nothing more than a heartless sampling and plunder of marginalized lives by those in power.
