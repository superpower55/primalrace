子宫内膜异位症的“圣杯”与医疗共谋的骗局The 'Holy Grail' of Endometriosis and the Scam of Medical Complicity

性别 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The New York Times ↗ 2026-09-30 § 链接
诊断延迟是结构性暴力，而缺乏监管的商业测试是文化暴力的收割。
Diagnostic delay is structural violence; unregulated commercial tests are the harvest of cultural violence.

一个女性在承受剧痛时被告知“这是正常的经痛”或“在你的脑子里”，这不是医疗误诊，而是典型的 structural violence。在男性中心叙事的医疗体系中，女性的疼痛被定义为“情绪化”或“生物性必然”，从而在 Potential（应得的救济）与 Actual（实际获得的医疗资源）之间制造了巨大的暴力差额。这种对女性身体感受的系统性漠视，本质上是元暴力在临床端的具体执行：解释权被掌握在那些不理解、也不在乎女性痛觉的权力结构手中。

现在，几家 startup 试图提供非侵入性检测，被患者视为“圣杯”。但请注意，这些产品大多通过监管漏洞（regulatory loophole）在“仅供研究”的名义下销售。这又是另一场博弈。当正规医疗体系在 structural 层面对女性痛觉失职时，商业公司迅速占领这个认知入口，利用女性的绝望进行套利。这不是救赎，而是一场精准的 scam。她们在售卖一种“可能性”，而这种可能性在缺乏 FDA 批准和大规模证据支持的情况下，不过是另一种形式的 weaponized expression。

好新闻应该是诊断标准被制度性地提前，而不是让绝望的女性在监管真空地带为未经证实的测试买单。如果一个疾病需要 7 到 9 年才能被诊断，那么真正需要被修复的是那个将女性身体客体化的医疗共谋场域，而不是几个试图通过唾液或血液样本快速变现的创业公司。

Telling a woman in agony that her pain is 'normal' or 'in her head' is not a medical error; it is textbook structural violence. In a masculine-centric medical narrative, female pain is dismissed as 'emotional' or 'biologically inevitable,' creating a massive gap between the Potential for relief and the Actual care received. This systemic disregard for the female body is the clinical execution of meta-violence: the power to interpret pain is monopolized by a structure that neither understands nor cares about female suffering.

Now, several startups offer non-invasive tests, framed as a 'holy grail.' But look closer: these products operate through regulatory loopholes under the guise of 'research purposes only.' This is another game. When the formal medical system fails women at the structural level, commercial entities seize the cognitive entry point to profit from desperation. This isn't salvation; it's a precise scam. They are selling 'possibility' as a weaponized expression, devoid of FDA approval or representative evidence.

Good news would be the institutional acceleration of diagnostic standards, not desperate women paying for unverified tests in a regulatory vacuum. When a disease takes 7 to 9 years to diagnose, the problem isn't a lack of tests—it's the complicity of a medical field that objectifies the female body. We don't need more startups; we need to dismantle the masculine-centric narrative that makes this delay acceptable.
