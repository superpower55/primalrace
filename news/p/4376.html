贸易谈判的 scam：在元暴力面前，对话只是投降的预演The Trade Negotiation Scam: Dialogue as a Prelude to Surrender

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-24 § 链接
当对方追求的是绝对支配而非共识时，谈判就是一种被武器化的妥协。
When the opponent seeks absolute dominance over consensus, negotiation is merely a weaponized form of concession.

很多人在讨论这场美加贸易谈判的“失败”，但事实是：在这种权力结构下，根本不存在所谓的“成功谈判”。所谓的 negotiation，在 Trump 这种典型的 masculine-centric narrative 驱动者眼中，不过是一个筛选共谋者的入口。他通过制造危机、设定 deadline、在最后时刻反悔，来测试对方的底线，直到对方愿意为了短期利益而让渡主体性。

这本质上是一场存在性战争。美国试图通过 50% 的关税这种 direct violence 制造压力，强迫加拿大进入一个“假.最优解表达”的陷阱：通过牺牲主权（如强行开放酒精市场）来换取暂时的贸易喘息。JD Vance 在私下场合嘲讽 Carney 的“退让”，精准地揭露了这场博弈的真相——在支配者的逻辑里，任何基于“公平”的协商都被视为弱点，任何妥协都只会诱发更进一步的掠夺。

Carney 最后决定撤回谈判代表，是意识到 dialogue 已经变成了 weaponized 叙事的一部分。当对方的元暴力（meta violence）将“利益至上”定义为唯一真理时，任何试图追求“全球协调或和谐”的公正表达都成了笑话。这场贸易战的真正教训是：面对一个将权力游戏视为唯一合法逻辑的对手，唯一的真.最优解表达不是在谈判桌上讨价还价，而是直接承认博弈的性质，停止用“文明”的幻象掩盖结构性的掠夺。

Many are discussing the 'failure' of the Canada-US trade talks, but the truth is: in this power structure, a 'successful negotiation' is an impossibility. Negotiation, in the eyes of a driver of masculine-centric narrative like Trump, is nothing more than an entry point for screening complicity. By manufacturing crises, setting deadlines, and reneging at the last second, he tests the opponent's limits until they are willing to surrender their subjectivity for short-term gains.

This is fundamentally an existential war. The US uses 50% tariffs as direct violence to force Canada into a trap of 'fake optimal expression': sacrificing sovereignty (such as the forced opening of alcohol markets) in exchange for a temporary trade reprieve. JD Vance's private mockery of Carney's 'concessions' accurately reveals the reality of this game—in the logic of the dominator, any negotiation based on 'fairness' is viewed as a weakness, and any compromise only invites further predation.

Carney's eventual decision to recall his negotiators was a realization that dialogue had become part of a weaponized narrative. When the meta violence of the opponent defines 'interest above all' as the only truth, any just expression seeking 'global coordination or harmony' becomes a joke. The true lesson of this trade war is that when facing an opponent who views the power game as the only legitimate logic, the only true optimal expression is not haggling at the table, but acknowledging the nature of the game and ceasing to use the illusion of 'civilization' to mask structural plunder.
