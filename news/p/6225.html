九场不胜，教练先谈赛程Nine Without a Win, and the Fixture List Gets the Blame

文娱 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-11-01 § 链接
输球被讲成赛程问题，解释权才是最后的战术。
Losing reframed as scheduling: the last tactic is who narrates the game.

曼联九场不胜，1988年以来第一次，主帅在赛后发布会上说，他期待的是周中带队打罗马。输球被处理成一个日程问题，而不是一个能力问题。这就是赛后采访的功能：把评价标准从比分挪到态度，从结果挪到过程。

卡拉格的说法很典型：上半场踢得不错，只是错过了一些明显的机会。机会被错过，是一个结果；机会被制造出来，是一个能力。把前者讲成运气，把后者讲成计划，一场2比0就变成了“比赛当时还在平衡中”。马丁内斯被罚下导致点球，主帅说他不会批评这名最有经验的球员之一，然后补一句这是比赛的重要部分。责任被分给了红牌、分给了时刻、分给了比赛的走势，就是没有落到排兵布阵上。

同一套话术在保级问题上又出现一次。球队赛前就在降级区上方一点，被问到是否准备好打保级战，回答是可以换个角度看，赢了球排名也能很快往上走。降级区是结构性的位置，赢球是可能性。用可能性回答位置，等于把“现在在哪”改写成“理论上还能去哪”。

这不是足球独有的东西。做不出小芯片就宣布一条新定律，评价方式一换，能力没变，叙事变了。九场不胜是观察到的结果，赛程密集、机会把握、红牌时机是声明出来的解释。两者性质不同，混在一起讲，前者就成了后者的注脚。

阿隆索那边倒是干脆：上半场很激烈，中场休息说了话，下半场反应更好，2比0加红牌之后比赛的性质变了。他没说这场赢得轻松。赢的人承认过程不轻松，输的人强调过程不算差，两边都在争夺同一件东西——这场比赛该怎么被讲述。

切尔西升到第四，埃斯特旺第一次联赛首发就被单独表扬，说他对热刺那场之后给出了最好的回应。一个人的位置是靠回应挣来的，一支球队的位置也是。曼联现在缺的不是一句“往前看”，是一个愿意把九场不胜当成九场不胜来讲的人。

Nine games without a win, the first such run at the club since 1988, and the manager's takeaway is that he is looking forward to getting the boys ready for midweek. A losing run gets processed as a calendar problem, not a capability problem. That is what the post-match interview is for: moving the yardstick from the scoreline to attitude, from result to process.

Carrick's account is textbook. United played well in the first half and missed a series of clear chances. Missing chances is an outcome; creating them is a capacity. Call the first luck and the second a plan, and a 2-0 defeat becomes a game that was right in the balance at that point. Martínez's red card and the penalty are a major part of the game, and he steers clear of criticising one of his most experienced players. Responsibility gets distributed to the red card, to the moment, to the flow of the match. It never lands on the setup.

The same move reappears on relegation. Asked whether his players are ready for a relegation battle, he offers another angle: win matches and you can move up the league quite quickly. The relegation zone is a structural position. Winning is a possibility. Answering a position with a possibility is how "where we are" gets rewritten as "where we could theoretically be."

This is not football's private trick. When you cannot build the small chip, you announce a new law; change the ruler and the capability stays the same while the story changes. Nine winless games is an observed result. Fixture congestion, chance conversion, the timing of a red card are declared explanations. They are different kinds of statement, and blending them turns the first into a footnote to the second.

Alonso, on the other side, is blunt: an intense first half, words at half-time, a better reaction after the break, and the context changed at 2-0 with the red card. He does not claim it was easy. The winner admits the process was hard; the loser insists the process was not bad. Both are fighting over the same thing: how this match gets told.

Chelsea move up to fourth, and Estêvão gets singled out after his first league start for having the best response. A player's place is earned by response. So is a club's. What United lack right now is not the phrase "move forward." It is someone willing to call nine winless games nine winless games.
