Meta 的罚单：用小额买路钱赎买算法暴力的解释权Meta's Fine: Buying the Right to Interpret Algorithmic Violence

科技 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-07 § 链接
罚金是 Meta 的运营成本，而对儿童的剥削是其算法的底层逻辑。
Fines are just operating costs; child exploitation is the core logic of the algorithm.

用 9.42 亿美元换取一个继续经营的许可，这在 Meta 2025 年 600 亿的年利润面前，不过是一次极其廉价的 risk management。法律在试图通过 structural 层面的罚款来修补漏洞，但它忽略了一个事实：Meta 的产品设计本身就是一种 weaponized expression。通过成瘾性机制和精准推送，Meta 制造了一个一个认知入口，将儿童的注意力商品化，而 child sexual exploitation（儿童性剥削）在其中不是 bug，而是这种无节制追求 engagement 的必然副产品。

最讽刺的是法院的裁决。法官认为要求 Meta 强制年龄验证会“不公平”且“过度伤害”公司，于是妥协为使用 AI 预测年龄。这简直是个巨大的 scam。让一个已经通过算法损害儿童精神健康的巨头，用同一套算法来“保护”儿童？这本质上是让狐狸在设计鸡舍的安保系统。Meta 依然掌控着解释权，它只需要在 bianner 和 informational screens 上做一些表演性的让步，就能在文化层面上维持一个“我们在努力保护孩子”的虚假叙事。

这场胜利被州检察长描述为“父母的胜利”，但从加尔通暴力三角来看，Potential − Actual 的差额依然巨大。Meta 依然在利用算法对原初种族（儿童，尤其是处于弱势地位的未成年女性）进行结构性掠夺。只要这种“利润 > 罚金”的博弈模型不被彻底摧毁，所谓的赔偿金不过是 Meta 支付给社会的 a small fraction of profit，用以维持其元暴力的合法性。

Paying $942 million to secure a license to continue operating is nothing more than cheap risk management for Meta, given its $60 billion annual profit in 2025. The court attempts to fix the leak through structural fines, but ignores a fundamental truth: Meta's product design is a form of weaponized expression. By utilizing addictive mechanisms and precision targeting, Meta creates cognitive entries that commodify children's attention, making child sexual exploitation not a bug, but an inevitable byproduct of the relentless pursuit of engagement.

The court's ruling is particularly farcical. The judge deemed mandatory age verification "inequitable" and "unduly injurious" to the company, compromising instead on AI-based age prediction. This is a complete scam. Asking a giant that has already harmed children's mental health via algorithms to use those same algorithms to "protect" them is like letting the fox design the henhouse security. Meta retains the power of interpretation; it only needs to perform some symbolic concessions on banners and informational screens to maintain a fake narrative of "working to protect children" at the cultural level.

While the Attorney General hails this as a "victory for parents," the gap between Potential and Actual in the Violence Triangle remains vast. Meta continues its structural plunder of the Primal Race—children, especially marginalized young females. As long as the game theory of "profit > fine" persists, these reparations are merely a small fraction of profit paid to maintain the legitimacy of its meta-violence.
