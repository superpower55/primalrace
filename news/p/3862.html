用警察替换军队：一场关于吞并的叙事换皮Replacing Army with Police: A Narrative Skin-Swap for Annexation

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-14 § 链接
将执法权从军队移交给警察，不是为了法治，而是为了完成吞并的法律闭环。
Transferring law enforcement from military to police is not about rule of law, but about closing the legal loop of annexation.

以色列国防部长 Katz 宣布将约旦河西岸的执法权从军队移交给警察，这在 naive 的观察者眼中可能是“从军事管制转向民事管理”的进步，但本质上这是一次极其阴险的 structural violence 升级。在占领区，军队的存在本身就是一种临时性的、不稳定的军事状态；而警察的介入则意味着将这片土地纳入以色列的国内行政体系。这不是在减少暴力，而是在通过改变执法主体的“身份”，将非法占领转化为事实上的 Annexation（吞并）。

这场博弈的潜台词极其残酷：当军队面对定居者暴徒围攻巴勒斯坦家庭时，他们表现出的“无能”或“不愿干预”其实是一种默契的共谋。现在，通过将执法权移交给警察，以色列试图构建一个 normative legal framework（规范性法律框架），从而在法律层面洗掉军队的责任，让定居者的暴力在“国内警察管理”的掩护下变得合法化或日常化。这正是典型的 weaponized expression——用“治安管理”这个认知入口，掩盖“种族清洗”的事实。

最讽刺的是，美国大使 Huckabee 将定居者称为“极少数”，这种叙事是典型的元暴力（meta violence）操作：通过定义对方为“少数”，从而赋予整体运动以合法性。当军队在现场看着定居者抢占房屋却毫无动作，甚至反过来将巴勒斯坦家庭赶出家门作为“军事基地”时，这种 direct violence 已经不需要任何解释。所谓的“移交权力”，不过是给这台绞肉机换了一套更像“文明国家”的制服而已。

Defense Minister Katz's plan to transfer law enforcement in the West Bank from the military to the police might look like a transition from military rule to civil administration to a naive observer. In reality, it is a sinister escalation of structural violence. The presence of the army signifies a temporary, unstable state of occupation; the arrival of the police signifies the integration of this land into Israel's domestic administrative system. This is not a reduction of violence, but a conversion of illegal occupation into factual Annexation through a change in the identity of the enforcer.

The subtext of this game is brutal: the army's "inability" or "unwillingness" to stop settlers besieging Palestinian families is a tacit complicity. By shifting power to the police, Israel seeks to construct a normative legal framework that absolves the military of responsibility, making settler violence appear legitimate or routine under the guise of "domestic policing." This is a textbook example of weaponized expression—using the cognitive entry of "public order" to mask the reality of ethnic cleansing.

Most ironic is Ambassador Huckabee's description of settlers as a "very small minority." This narrative is a classic operation of meta violence: by defining the aggressors as a minority, the broader movement is granted legitimacy. When soldiers stand by as settlers seize homes, or even evict Palestinian families to create "military bases," the direct violence requires no further explanation. The so-called "transfer of power" is merely putting a "civilized state" uniform on a meat grinder.
