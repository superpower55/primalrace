所谓的“系统故障”是结构性暴力的标准掩体The 'Glitch' as a Standard Bunker for Structural Violence

国际 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-16 § 链接
Glitch 不是技术事故，而是通过制造“行政冗余”实施的结构性暴力。
A glitch is not a technical accident, but structural violence implemented through administrative redundancy.

在政治博弈中，最廉价且高效的武器就是“系统故障”（glitch）。德克萨斯州这 60 万件被吞掉的选民登记申请，在官方叙事里是软件错误，但在 Violence Triangle 的框架下，这是典型的 structural violence。它不需要直接地禁止谁投票，只需要在 Potential（公民的投票权）与 Actual（实际能投票的状态）之间制造一个由“技术故障”组成的黑洞，差额就是暴力。

这种暴力的阴险之处在于它提供了完美的 Meta-violence 掩体：它把政治压迫伪装成技术失误。当 60 万人的意愿在 DPS 的数据库里被悄悄抹除时，执行者不需要面对“剥夺人权”的道德指责，只需要面对“系统不稳定”的行政压力。这是一种典型的共谋机制——技术官僚提供掩护，政治权力收割结果，而受害者在收到确认回执的假象中，完成了对自己主体性的自我误判。

尤其在像德州这样高度极化的政治场域，这种“故障”精准地发生在关键选举前夕。它不是在修复系统，而是在通过制造行政混乱来筛选选民。谁有资源去追踪自己的登记状态？谁有能力在最后时刻通过法律手段强行进入名单？那些被系统抛弃的，通常正是社会结构中最弱势的群体。这本质上是一场关于“谁被允许存在于政治名单中”的存在性战争。

这场官司能否赢回这 60 万张票并不重要，重要的是它揭露了权力如何将“客观的技术中立”武器化。当一个州政府能让 60 万条记录在一年时间里“消失”而毫无察觉，这已经不是 glitch，而是一种精准的政治工程。

In political gaming, the cheapest and most efficient weapon is the 'glitch.' The nearly 600,000 swallowed voter registrations in Texas are framed as a software error in official narratives, but within the Violence Triangle, this is textbook structural violence. It doesn't need to explicitly forbid anyone from voting; it simply creates a black hole of 'technical failure' between Potential (the right to vote) and Actual (the ability to vote). The gap is the violence.

The sinister nature of this violence lies in its use of Meta-violence as a bunker: it disguises political oppression as technical incompetence. While 600,000 intentions are quietly erased from the DPS database, the perpetrators avoid the moral stigma of 'human rights violations' and instead face mere 'administrative pressure' over system instability. This is a classic complicity mechanism—technocrats provide the cover, political power reaps the reward, and the victims are led into a self-misjudgment of their own agency by fake confirmation receipts.

Especially in a highly polarized arena like Texas, such 'glitches' are timed with surgical precision before key elections. This isn't about system maintenance; it's about filtering the electorate through manufactured administrative chaos. Who has the resources to track their registration? Who has the power to force their way onto the list via legal action at the last minute? Those discarded by the system are invariably the most structurally vulnerable.

Whether this lawsuit recovers the 600,000 votes is secondary. What matters is that it exposes how power weaponizes 'objective technical neutrality.' When a state government can let 600,000 records 'vanish' for a year without notice, it is no longer a glitch—it is a precise piece of political engineering.
