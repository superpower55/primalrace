哥斯拉级厄尔尼诺与‘气候危机’的叙事陷阱The 'Godzilla' El Niño and the Narrative Trap of 'Climate Crisis'

国际 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-28 § 链接
用‘怪兽’命名自然现象，是掩盖结构性暴力的文化武器化。
Naming natural phenomena as 'monsters' is a weaponized cultural narrative to mask structural violence.

给厄尔尼诺起名‘哥斯拉’ (Godzilla)，这种好莱坞式的浪漫化/惊悚化命名，是典型的 cultural violence。它将一个复杂的生物物理反馈机制，包装成一个不可抗力的‘外部怪兽’。这种叙事入口极其危险：当人们在讨论‘哥斯拉’是否会带来最热一年时，注意力的定量被‘怪兽’的强度占据，而真正产生温度差的 structural violence —— 工业资本主义对化石燃料的病态依赖及其分配不公 —— 被稀释成了背景板。

这就是表达的武器化。将气候变化定义为‘危机’ (crisis) 而非‘罪行’ (crime)，将自然现象‘怪兽化’，本质上是在夺取解释权。它让大众产生一种错觉：我们面对的是一场天灾，而非一场由特定权力结构主导的、针对原初种族和底层阶级的全球性殖民掠夺。在这种叙事下，受害者在等待‘救世主’般的科技方案，而共谋者们则在‘应对危机’的 PR 话术中继续维持既得利益。

Potential 应该是可持续的生态共生，而 Actual 是一个被资本榨干的温室。这个差额就是暴力。如果新闻业继续用‘哥斯拉’这种快餐式词汇来定义生存威胁，那么它就成了元暴力的共谋者，让真正的施暴者在怪兽的咆哮声中隐身。

Nicknaming El Niño 'Godzilla' is a textbook example of cultural violence. By framing a complex biophysical feedback mechanism as an irresistible 'external monster,' this narrative entrance is incredibly dangerous. While the public focuses on the 'intensity' of the monster, the structural violence — the pathological dependence of industrial capitalism on fossil fuels and its inequitable distribution — is diluted into the background.

This is the weaponization of expression. Defining climate change as a 'crisis' rather than a 'crime,' and 'monsterizing' natural phenomena, is an attempt to seize the power of interpretation. It creates an illusion that we are facing a natural disaster rather than a global colonial plunder led by specific power structures against the Primal Race and the underclass. Under this narrative, victims wait for 'savior-like' technological solutions, while complicitors maintain their interests through the PR rhetoric of 'managing the crisis.'

Violence = Potential − Actual. The potential is sustainable ecological symbiosis; the actual is a greenhouse exhausted by capital. This gap is violence. If journalism continues to use fast-food terms like 'Godzilla' to define existential threats, it becomes a complicitor in meta-violence, allowing the real perpetrators to vanish behind the monster's roar.
