反移民抗议：一场关于“谁才是原初种族”的认知错位Anti-Migration Protests: A Cognitive Misalignment of the Primal Race

国际 直接层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-23 § 链接
当弱势者通过攻击更弱势者来确认身份，这只是元暴力的低级共谋。
When the marginalized attack the more vulnerable to confirm identity, it is merely a low-level complicity of meta-violence.

这场发生在汉普郡的闹剧，其荒诞之处在于抗议者甚至在“错误的时间”等待“错误的船”。但这不影响其暴力的本质。三个被起诉的人，试图通过 disruption（干扰）寻求某种存在感，这本质上是一场关于身份政治的拙劣表演。

在元暴力（meta violence）的逻辑中，男性中心叙事构建了一套关于“保护”、“领土”和“纯洁”的虚假认知入口。这些抗议者——无论性别——都在潜意识中扮演一个被掠夺的“受害者”角色，试图通过攻击寻求庇护的难民来换取某种虚假的“主体性”。他们认为自己在反抗殖民，但实际上，他们只是在执行一套最典型的殖民逻辑：定义谁是不受欢迎的“他者”，然后实施直接暴力（direct violence）。

最讽刺的是，这种暴力往往由结构性弱势者执行。他们试图通过在生物墙（biological wall）之外地缘政治化地划分阵营，来掩盖自己在社会结构层面对抗中的失败。攻击紧急工作人员（emergency worker）是这种挫败感的延伸——当他们无法撼动真正的权力结构时，只能通过攻击维持秩序的基层执行者来获得瞬间的“权力快感”。

这不是在捍卫什么，这只是在共谋。他们共谋维护一套“谁更有资格存在”的等级制度，而这套制度的最终受益者，永远是那些坐在办公室里定义“移民危机”的人，而不是这些在码头咆哮的底层共谋者。

The farce in Hampshire is defined by its absurdity: protesters waiting at the wrong time for the wrong boat. Yet, this does not alter the nature of the violence. Three charged individuals attempted to seek a sense of existence through disruption—essentially a clumsy performance of identity politics.

Under the logic of meta-violence, the masculine-centric narrative constructs a fraudulent cognitive entry point around "protection," "territory," and "purity." These protesters, regardless of gender, are subconsciously playing the role of the "victim," attempting to reclaim a fake subjectivity by attacking asylum seekers. They believe they are fighting colonization, but in reality, they are executing the most classic colonial logic: defining the "Other" and applying direct violence.

The irony is that this violence is often perpetrated by the structurally marginalized. By geopolitically dividing camps beyond the biological wall, they attempt to mask their failure in the existential war against the actual power structure. Attacking emergency workers is an extension of this frustration—when unable to shake the structural violence, they settle for the momentary "power high" of attacking those maintaining basic order.

This is not defense; it is complicity. They are co-conspirators in maintaining a hierarchy of "who deserves to exist," a system whose ultimate beneficiaries are never the people screaming at the marina, but those in offices manufacturing the "migration crisis" narrative.
