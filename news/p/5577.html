所谓的“法律分析”与一个忠诚的扮演者The 'Legal Analysis' Scam and the Art of Loyal Performance

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-15 § 链接
当忠诚成为唯一的入场券，专业能力就成了可以随意定义的PR叙事。
When loyalty is the only ticket, professional competence becomes a PR narrative to be redefined.

这场闹剧最精彩的部分不在于 Katie Zacharia 是否通过了律考，而在于她如何定义“律师”这个词。在法律这个极度依赖 credential 的结构里，没过 bar 却自称 lawyer，这在专业领域是 scam，但在 Trump 的认知入口里，这叫“法律分析” (legal analysis)。这种对事实的重新定义，是典型的元暴力 (meta violence) 运作：现实不配合，就直接换掉尺子。

Zacharia 的路径是标准的“最优解表达” (optimal expression) 异化。她没有通过专业能力的积累去赢得席位，而是通过在保守派谈话节目中“试镜” (audition)，精准地扮演一个“忠诚且闪耀的 MAGA 粉丝”。在 Trump 的权力结构中，主体性的死亡是入场券——你不需要成为一个独立的法律专业人士，你只需要成为总统意志的扩音器。她所谓的“跟随总统的领导”，本质上是交出了所有解释权，将自己彻底客体化为一件政治工具。

一个连法律执业资格都模糊处理的人，现在掌控着白宫与媒体的认知入口。她说与媒体接触的频率“未知” (unknown)，这其实是一个诚实的信号：她的职责不是沟通，而是屏蔽。当 press secretary 不再负责传递信息，而只负责在需要时出现并扮演一个“忠诚的随从”时，这个职位的 structural 意义已经被彻底解构了。

这种共谋 (complicity) 令人作呕地高效：Trump 需要一个绝对顺从且无需专业操守的傀儡，Zacharia 需要一个能让她从 pyramid scheme 跃升至权力中心的跳板。双方在“忠诚”这个武器化概念下达成了共识。至于那些关于法律资质的质疑，在他们看来不过是“不正确”的叙事干扰而已。

The most exquisite part of this farce isn't whether Katie Zacharia passed the bar, but how she defines the word 'lawyer.' In a structure as credential-dependent as law, claiming to be a lawyer without a license is a scam. Yet, in Trump's cognitive entry, it's rebranded as 'legal analysis.' This is meta-violence in its purest form: when reality doesn't cooperate, simply change the ruler.

Zacharia's trajectory is a textbook case of the alienation of optimal expression. She didn't win her seat through professional merit but by 'auditioning' on conservative talk shows, precisely performing the role of a 'devoted MAGA fan.' In Trump's power structure, the death of subjectivity is the entry fee—you don't need to be an independent legal professional; you just need to be a megaphone for the president's will. Her claim to 'follow the president's lead' is a total surrender of interpretive power, turning herself into a political object.

Now, someone who treats her legal qualifications as optional controls the cognitive entry between the White House and the media. Her statement that the frequency of briefings is 'unknown' is actually an honest signal: her role is not communication, but filtration. When a press secretary no longer facilitates information but merely performs the role of a 'loyal follower,' the structural meaning of the office is obliterated.

This complicity is sickeningly efficient: Trump needs a puppet who is absolutely submissive and devoid of professional ethics, and Zacharia needs a springboard to leap from a pyramid scheme to the center of power. They have reached a consensus under the weaponized concept of 'loyalty.' As for the scrutiny over her credentials, it is dismissed as merely an 'incorrect' narrative interference.
