用“作弊”掩盖的结构性自杀Structural Suicide Masked as 'Cheating'

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-14 § 链接
当创作者开始用道德审判同行时，他们是在为资本的替代计划递刀子。
When creators use moral judgment against peers, they are handing the knife to capital's replacement plan.

Jack Thorne 把使用 AI 写剧本定义为“作弊” (cheat)，这种叙事极其危险。他试图在创作者内部建立一套道德纯洁性 (squeaky clean) 的审查机制，通过呼吁法律禁令将同行定义为“诈骗犯”，从而试图挽救一个正在崩塌的行业。但这恰恰是典型的认知误区：他把一场 structural violence 降格为了个体的道德瑕疵。

AI 模型的本质是 mass-scraping，是基于对人类创作者集体劳动成果的掠夺。在这个层面上，AI 本身就是一场巨大的 theft。而当 Thorne 呼吁制裁那些使用 AI 的同行时，他实际上是在帮制片厂和流媒体平台完成一次完美的叙事转移。如果“作家是问题所在”，那么资本就可以理直气壮地宣布：既然人类作家不再可靠且容易“作弊”，那么直接用 AI 替代人类，不仅更高效，而且更“诚实”。

这是一种典型的共谋 (complicity) 逻辑。Thorne 以为自己在保护弱势创作者，但他的方案是建立在加强监管和行业禁令之上。在权力不对等的博弈中，任何增加监管成本的方案，最终受益的永远是掌握定义权的人。当他要求 BBC 或 Channel 4 实施制裁时，他实际上是在请求权力机构获得更多监控创作者的权力。

真正的存在性战争不在于作家是否在用 prompts 替代文字，而在于谁拥有对“创作”的定义权。如果创作被定义为一种可以被 AI 模拟的“产出”，那么人类作家的价值就被定价为零。在这种元暴力 (meta violence) 面前，指责同行作弊就像是在泰坦尼克号沉没时，抱怨有人偷偷用了救生圈一样滑稽。这种内部互撕，正是资本最希望看到的 anemic 抵抗。

Jack Thorne defines using AI in scriptwriting as 'cheating'—a narrative that is profoundly dangerous. By attempting to establish a regime of moral purity (squeaky clean) and calling for laws to brand peers as 'frauds,' he hopes to save a collapsing industry. However, this is a classic cognitive error: he has reduced a structural violence to an individual moral flaw.

The essence of AI models is mass-scraping, a systemic theft of the collective labor of human creators. On this level, AI itself is a heist. When Thorne calls for sanctions against peers, he is inadvertently aiding studios and streamers in a narrative pivot. If 'the writer is the problem,' capital can rightfully argue that since human writers are unreliable and prone to 'cheating,' replacing them with AI is not only more efficient but more 'honest.'

This is the logic of complicity. Thorne believes he is protecting vulnerable creators, but his solution relies on increased regulation and industry bans. In a game of asymmetric power, any plan that increases surveillance costs ultimately benefits those who hold the power to define the rules. By asking the BBC or Channel 4 to impose sanctions, he is effectively requesting that power structures be granted more authority to monitor creators.

The real existential war is not about whether writers use prompts instead of words, but about who owns the definition of 'creation.' If creation is defined as a producible 'output' that AI can simulate, the value of the human writer is priced at zero. In the face of this meta violence, accusing peers of cheating is as absurd as complaining that someone secretly used a life jacket while the Titanic was sinking. This internal friction is exactly the kind of anemic resistance capital desires.
