伊朗总统的“不辞职”表演与权力黑盒的元暴力The Performance of 'Not Resigning' and Meta-Violence in Iran's Power Black-Box

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-05 § 链接
在权力黑盒中，否认辞职本身就是一种被动地确认自己的客体地位。
In a power black-box, denying resignation is merely a passive confirmation of one's status as an object.

这是一场典型的关于“存在性”的博弈，但博弈的筹码并不在总统 Pezeshkian 手中。当一个国家的最高行政首长不得不通过电视采访来否认自己“威胁辞职”这种极其低端且琐碎的指控时，这本身就是一种 structural violence 的具象化：他的主体性已经被剥夺，他现在唯一的表达功能是作为最高领袖意志的一个“接口”而存在。

整场闹剧的核心在于那个名为“最高领袖”的权力黑盒。Khamenei 在被炸伤后隐匿行踪，这种物理上的消失制造了一个巨大的解释权真空。在这种真空里，谁能声称自己“见过”或“听过”领袖的意志，谁就掌握了定义事实的武器。Kharazi 抛出辞职传闻，本质上是在利用这种信息不对称进行 weaponized expression，通过制造“总统受挫”的叙事，将 Pezeshkian 标记为弱势的、可被替换的零件。

而 Pezeshkian 的回应——“我将坚持下去”——是一种典型的假.最优解表达。他试图通过强调“共识”和“和谐”来掩盖自己被 sidelined 的事实。在男性中心叙事的权力结构中，这种所谓的“共识”其实是元暴力的伪装：它要求被统治者在被剥夺决策权的同时，还要在表达上维持一种“我们是一体”的共谋假象。所谓的“民主结果”在面对一个不可见的、神格化的最高权力时，不过是一层薄薄的 PR 纸。

最讽刺的是，这场权力内斗的背景是关于霍尔木兹海峡的国际谈判。当硬派势力将寻求和平称为“不可原谅的背叛”时，他们实际上是在通过定义“背叛”来垄断对国家利益的解释权。在这种叙事下，任何试图通过 Just Expressions 达成共识的努力，都会被武器化为“投降”的标签。这再次证明，在元暴力的统治下，事实不重要，重要的是谁在定义什么是事实。

This is a classic existential war, but the stakes are not held by President Pezeshkian. When a head of state is forced to use a TV interview to deny trivial claims that he 'threatened to resign,' it is a vivid manifestation of structural violence: his subjectivity has been stripped, and his only remaining function is to serve as an 'interface' for the Supreme Leader's will.

The core of this farce is the power black-box of the 'Supreme Leader.' Khamenei's disappearance after being injured created a massive vacuum of interpretative power. In this void, whoever claims to have 'seen' or 'heard' the Leader's will seizes the weapon of defining reality. Kharazi's resignation rumors are a form of weaponized expression, utilizing information asymmetry to label Pezeshkian as a frustrated, replaceable component.

Pezeshkian's response—'I will stand'—is a fake optimal expression. He attempts to mask his sidelined status by emphasizing 'consensus' and 'harmony.' In a masculine-centric power structure, this 'consensus' is merely a camouflage for meta-violence: it demands that the ruled remain a complicit part of the narrative, pretending 'we are one' while their decision-making power is erased. The so-called 'democratic outcome' is nothing but a thin layer of PR when facing an invisible, apotheosized power.

Most ironically, this internal struggle unfolds against the backdrop of negotiations over the Strait of Hormuz. By labeling the pursuit of peace as an 'unforgivable betrayal,' hardliners are monopolizing the interpretation of national interest. In this narrative, any attempt to reach a consensus through Just Expressions is weaponized as 'capitulation.' This proves once again that under meta-violence, facts are irrelevant; what matters is who defines what the facts are.
