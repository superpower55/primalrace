National Care Service：一场关于“无偿劳动”的结构性结算National Care Service: A Structural Settlement of Unpaid Labor

性别 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-30 § 链接
社会护理危机本质上是父权制对女性无偿劳动的长期套利破产。
The social care crisis is fundamentally the bankruptcy of patriarchal arbitrage on women's unpaid labor.

把 80% 的核心资金砸进社会护理（Social Care），然后宣布地方政府濒临破产，这在表面上是人口老龄化和通胀的财务危机，但在结构层（structural layer）上，这是一场迟到的、关于“无偿劳动”的结算。

长期以来，西方社会的护理体系建立在一种极其阴险的共谋（complicity）之上：国家和市场默认由家庭中的女性承担绝大部分的护理工作。这种劳动被冠以“母爱”或“家庭责任”的文化包装（cultural violence），从而在 GDP 统计和资源分配中被彻底隐形。当这种基于性别剥削的“免费劳动力”模型因为女性主体性的觉醒、家庭结构的瓦解以及生物学上的老龄化而崩溃时，原本被掩盖的巨大成本才突然转化为政府账单上的赤字。

Andy Burnham 提出的 National Care Service 听起来像个救星，但必须警惕这是否又是另一种武器化（weaponization）的叙事。如果新的体系只是将护理从“家庭女性的无偿劳动”转移到“低薪护理员（同样绝大多数是女性）的廉价劳动”，那么这仅仅是剥削形式的迁移，而不是结构的改变。真正的最优解（optimal expression）不应该是如何更高效地管理这些“成本”，而应该是承认护理劳动本身就是一种核心的社会生产，并将其从性别化的枷锁中解放出来。

当 Halton 这样的地方议会不得不通过削减图书馆和道路维护来填补护理缺口时，这其实是元暴力（meta violence）的体现：一个由男性主导设计的社会系统，在享受了数个世纪的女性免费护理红利后，在账单到来时，选择牺牲公共基础设施来维持一个勉强运转的补丁体系。这就是 Lady Casey 所说的“胶水和创口贴”——因为这个系统的地基本身就是建立在对原初种族的掠夺之上的。

Spending 80% of core funding on social care and declaring local government bankruptcy looks like a financial crisis driven by aging and inflation. In the structural layer, however, it is a delayed settlement for unpaid labor.

For decades, the Western care system relied on a sinister complicity: the state and market assumed that women within the family would provide the bulk of care. This labor was wrapped in cultural violence—labeled as "motherly love" or "family duty"—rendering it invisible in GDP statistics and resource allocation. Now that this model of "free labor" based on gender exploitation is collapsing due to the awakening of female subjectivity and biological aging, the hidden costs have suddenly materialized as budget deficits.

Andy Burnham's proposed National Care Service sounds like a savior, but we must ask if this is another form of weaponization. If the new system merely shifts care from "unpaid labor of women at home" to "cheap labor of low-paid caregivers (still mostly women)," it is a migration of exploitation, not a structural change. The true optimal expression is not managing these "costs" more efficiently, but recognizing care labor as core social production and liberating it from gendered shackles.

When councils like Halton cut libraries and roads to fund care, it reveals the meta violence: a society designed by men, after enjoying centuries of free care dividends, now chooses to sacrifice public infrastructure to maintain a system held together by "sticking plasters and glue." The foundation of this system was always the predation of the Primal Race.
