内塔尼亚胡在联大的这场演讲，是典型的“表达武器化” (Weaponisation of Expression)。当直接暴力 (direct violence) 已经通过卫星图像和死亡数字在全世界面前裸奔时，施暴者唯一的生存路径就是夺取解释权，通过重新定义事实来制造一个平行的现实。

他说加沙战争是“种族灭绝的反面” (opposite of genocide)。这是一个极其阴险的认知入口 (cognitive entry point) 操纵：他不是在否认死亡，而是在试图通过反转词义，将“屠杀”包装成“拯救”或“正义”。在这种叙事里，所有的结构性暴力 (structural violence) —— 饥饿、封锁、医院被毁 —— 都被抹除，取而代之的是一个关于“生存威胁”和“全球阴谋”的宏大叙事。这种叙事不是写给联大代表听的，而是写给他的右翼基本盘的，是一场纯粹的、为了政治生存而进行的stump speech。

而这场戏最讽刺的共谋 (complicity) 发生在幕后：美国政府通过拒绝签证，物理性地抹除了巴勒斯坦代表在现场的表达空间。当一个群体被剥夺了在联合国这个最高认知入口发声的权利，他们就成了被定义、被消费的“客体”。这种 structural violence 确保了内塔尼亚胡可以在一个被过滤的场域里，心安理得地指责那些离开会场的人为“道德懦夫”。

这种博弈本质上是一场存在性战争 (existential war)。内塔尼亚胡在用“生存”这个词作为盾牌，掩盖他将他人定义为“非人”的元暴力 (meta-violence)。当一个权力者可以随意宣布“如果不杀掉对方，我们都会死”时，他实际上是在向世界广播：我的存在高于你的存在，我的生存逻辑高于普世人权。

Netanyahu's address to the UN is a textbook case of the Weaponisation of Expression. When direct violence is already nakedly exposed to the world via satellite imagery and death tolls, the perpetrator's only path to survival is to seize the power of interpretation and manufacture a parallel reality by redefining the facts.

He claimed the war in Gaza is the "opposite of genocide." This is a sinister manipulation of the cognitive entry point: he isn't denying the deaths, but attempting to invert the meaning of the word, packaging "massacre" as "salvation" or "justice." In this narrative, all structural violence — starvation, blockade, the destruction of hospitals — is erased, replaced by a grand narrative of "existential threats" and "global conspiracies." This speech wasn't for the UN delegates; it was a stump speech for his right-wing base, a desperate play for political survival.

The most cynical complicity, however, happened backstage: the US government's denial of visas physically erased the Palestinian representatives' expression space. When a group is stripped of their right to speak at the highest cognitive entry point, they become mere "objects" to be defined and consumed. This structural violence ensured Netanyahu could comfortably label those walking out as "moral cowards" within a filtered environment.

This game is essentially an existential war. Netanyahu uses the word "survival" as a shield to mask the meta-violence of defining others as "non-human." When a power-holder can arbitrarily declare, "If we don't kill them, we will all die," he is broadcasting to the world: my existence is superior to yours, and my logic of survival overrides universal human rights.
