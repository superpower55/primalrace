用“超级智能”掩盖的旧时代屠杀逻辑Super Intelligence as a Mask for Old-World Slaughter Logic

国际 文化层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-22 § 链接
所有宏大叙事的终点都是将肉体客体化为可消耗的筹码
All grand narratives eventually reduce human flesh to consumable chips in a game.

特朗普在联大的表演，本质上是一次极其典型的元暴力 (meta violence) 演习。他把一个旨在防止冲突的国际论坛，直接变成了他个人存在性战争的扩音器。最令人作呕的不是他威胁要“消灭”伊朗，而是在这种威胁中，他把一个国家的生存权简化成了一个关于他个人“大决定”的博弈筹码。在这种叙事里，数千万人的生命被客体化为一种可以被随时抹除的数值，这种将肉体降格为手段的逻辑，正是男性中心叙事最底层的暴力基准。

他随口篡改的历史年份和虚构的死亡数字，揭示了武器化表达 (weaponisation of expression) 的核心：事实不重要，重要的是通过定义“事实”来夺取解释权。当他宣布委内瑞拉的石油协议是“胜利者的战利品”时，他实际上是在向世界广播一种丛林法则——即强权即正义，而所谓的国际法或人权，不过是弱者用来寻求庇护的 scam。

最讽刺的细节在于他试图通过改名将 AI 变为“超级智能” (super intelligence)。这是一种极其拙劣的认知入口操纵：通过更换尺子来掩盖能力的缺失，或者说，通过重新定义现实来逃避对风险的责任。从威胁消灭一个文明到重新定义一个技术词汇，逻辑是一脉相承的：只要我拥有定义权，我就拥有了制造真实的能力。

这场表演的共谋者不仅是那些在会场里战战兢兢的代表，更是那些习惯于将地缘政治视为“强者博弈”的观察者。当世界习惯于这种 bellicosity 时，我们实际上是在共谋一种文化暴力，让直接暴力（如战争和屠杀）看起来像是某种必然的、甚至具有某种“领导力”的艺术。

Trump’s performance at the UN is a textbook exercise in meta violence. He transformed a forum designed to prevent conflict into a megaphone for his own existential war. The most repulsive part isn't the threat to "annihilate" Iran, but how he reduces the survival of an entire nation to a mere chip in his personal game of "big decisions." In this narrative, tens of millions of lives are objectified into erasable values—a logic of degrading flesh into a means to an end, which is the foundational baseline of masculine-centric narrative.

His casual distortion of historical dates and fabricated death tolls reveal the core of the weaponisation of expression: facts are irrelevant; what matters is the seizure of the power to define them. When he hails the Venezuelan oil deal as "the spoils of the victor," he is broadcasting a jungle law to the world—that might is right, and that international law or human rights are merely scams used by the weak for shelter.

The most ironic detail is his attempt to rename AI as "super intelligence." This is a clumsy manipulation of the cognitive entry point: changing the ruler to hide a lack of capacity, or rather, redefining reality to evade responsibility for risk. From threatening to erase a civilization to renaming a technical term, the logic is identical: as long as I hold the power of definition, I hold the power to manufacture truth.

The complicity in this performance extends beyond the trembling delegates in the hall to those who view geopolitics as a "game of strongmen." As the world becomes inured to this bellicosity, we are collectively complicit in a form of cultural violence that makes direct violence—war and slaughter—appear inevitable, or even a form of "leadership" art.
