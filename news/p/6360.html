公投这个词消失的那天The Day the Word Referendum Was Voted Out

国际 结构层 · 文化层 Al Jazeera ↗ 2026-11-01 § 链接
决议输了，公投这个词先被投出去了。
The resolution lost. The word referendum went first.

美国那份草案要做的事，是把「公投」从任务团的名字里删掉，再把摩洛哥的自治方案写成今后谈判的唯一基础。名字一改，解释权就换手了——1991年停火协议里那个「六个月内举行公投」的承诺，从此不再是谈判的起点，而是一个过时的字母。俄罗斯投了否决票，于是全世界的头条都在说摩洛哥输了、俄罗斯阻挠了自决。摩洛哥在庆祝。

它庆祝得有道理。草案里那点「支持自决原则」是包装纸，里面装的是把公投从议程上搬走。俄罗斯的否决保住了那个字母R，却保不住任何一次投票。真正被削掉的不是摩洛哥的野心，是撒哈拉人手里那张从未发出去的选票——1991年说好六个月，三十多年过去，一次都没投。

这就是结构暴力的标准形态：不是谁开了枪，而是一个承诺被无限期地挂在半空，挂到所有人习惯它不落地。MINURSO有155名军事人员，本财年预算6200万美元，任务是监督停火和准备公投。现在任务团到期了，停火还在，公投早就不在。摩洛哥王室说人员和设备「这几天内」应该撤走，还说西撒哈拉问题应当从安理会议程上撤下来——监督的人走了，被监督的对象也就顺理成章地不再需要被谈论。

援助机构警告的是安全与稳定，这没错，但那是短期账。长期账是：当唯一的国际在场者撤出，摩洛哥在西撒哈拉的领土、经济、行政控制会继续巩固，而磷矿和渔业资源本来就在它手里。报道里写得很清楚，那里驻扎着10万到14万摩洛哥军人。自治方案已经被安理会采纳为谈判基础，独立则被越推越远。

街上挥旗的人穿着melfas和darra，举着国王的画像。这一幕被拍成「节日气氛」，用来证明这块土地的人心归向。可报道同时写着，至少17万原住民在1975年之后被赶进阿尔及利亚廷杜夫难民营，那里的人没有出现在任何一方的庆祝画面里。阿尔及利亚代表在安理会说，撒哈拉人的悲剧被稀释成了「难民人口」这个概念——这句话是这条新闻里最准确的一句。难民是一个行政类别，自决是一个政治权利，把前者顶替后者，权利就变成了人道主义援助的配额。

美国大使说，用否决权去保住名字里那个R，实在不幸。他说得对，那个R确实只剩一个字母了。但让他不幸的不是字母，是三十多年里没有人真的去兑现它。俄罗斯的否决、美国的草案、摩洛哥的庆祝、阿尔及利亚的遗憾，四方都在谈自决，没有一方在安排一次投票。

任务团撤走之后，西撒哈拉会成为非洲最后一块被联合国列为「非自治领土」的地方，而「非自治」这个词从此不再需要每年续期。没有观察员，就没有记录；没有记录，低烈度的交火就只是背景噪音；没有公投的日期，独立就只是一句可以无限重复的口号。差额还在那里：本可以举行的一次投票，减去零次。这次没人再提那个差额，因为负责测量它的人已经被请走了。

What the US draft set out to do was delete "referendum" from the mission's name and make Morocco's autonomy plan the only basis for future talks. Rename it, and the解释权 changes hands: the promise written into the 1991 ceasefire — a vote within six months — stops being the starting point of negotiations and becomes an outdated letter. Russia vetoed, so the headlines say Morocco lost and Russia blocked self-determination. Morocco is celebrating.

It has reason to. The draft's nod to "the principle of self-determination" was wrapping paper; inside was the removal of the vote from the agenda. Russia's veto preserved the letter R and not a single ballot. What got cut down wasn't Morocco's ambition, it was the ballot Sahrawis never got to cast — six months in 1991, and more than three decades later, zero votes.

This is structural violence in its standard form: not someone firing a gun, but a promise suspended indefinitely until everyone is used to it never landing. MINURSO has 155 military personnel and a $62m budget this fiscal year, tasked with monitoring the ceasefire and preparing a referendum. Now the mandate has expired. The ceasefire remains; the referendum left long ago. Morocco's royal palace says personnel and equipment should leave "in the coming days," and that the Western Sahara question should be withdrawn from the Security Council's consideration altogether — the monitors go, and the thing being monitored no longer needs to be discussed.

Aid organisations warn about security and stability, which is true, but that's the short ledger. The long one: with the only international presence gone, Morocco's territorial, economic and administrative control consolidates further, and the phosphate and fishing resources were already in its hands. The report is explicit — between 100,000 and 140,000 Moroccan military personnel are based there. The autonomy plan has already been adopted as the basis for negotiations; independence is pushed further and further out of reach.

The people waving flags in the street wear melfas and darra and hold portraits of the king. The scene is filmed as "festive atmosphere," proof of where the land's heart lies. The same report says at least 170,000 Indigenous people were driven into refugee camps in Tindouf, Algeria, after 1975 — and those people appear in no one's celebration footage. Algeria's delegate told the council that the Sahrawi tragedy had been diluted into the concept of "refugee populations." That is the most accurate sentence in this story. Refugee is an administrative category; self-determination is a political right. Substitute the first for the second and a right becomes a humanitarian quota.

The US ambassador said it was truly unfortunate that in 2026 the veto was used to preserve the letter R. He's right that the R is down to a single letter. But what's unfortunate isn't the letter — it's that in thirty-odd years nobody actually cashed it in. Russia's veto, the US draft, Morocco's celebration, Algeria's regret: all four are talking about self-determination, and none is scheduling a vote.

Once the mission leaves, Western Sahara becomes the last UN-designated "non-self-governing territory" in Africa — and "non-self-governing" will no longer need annual renewal. No observers, no record. No record, and low-level fighting is just background noise. No date for a referendum, and independence is a slogan you can repeat forever. The gap is still there: one vote that could have been held, minus zero. Nobody will mention it now, because the people who measured it have been asked to leave.
