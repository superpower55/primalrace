饥饿游戏原声带也在卖反抗The Hunger Games Soundtrack Is Selling Rebellion Too

文娱 文化层 Billboard ↗ 2026-11-02 § 链接
新闻摘要《The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping (The Album)》将于11月13日由MCA发行，共29首歌，收录Ella Langley、Stevie Nicks、Tyler Childers、The Smashing Pumpkins、Noah Kahan等艺人作品。同名电影由Francis Lawrence执导，11月20日上映，讲述16岁的Haymitch Abernathy在第50届饥饿游戏抽签当天试图从竞技场内部终止游戏。执行制作人Dave Cobb称整张专辑的主线是「诚实」。
The newsThe Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping (The Album), a 29-song soundtrack, arrives Nov. 13 on MCA with Ella Langley, Stevie Nicks, Tyler Childers, The Smashing Pumpkins and Noah Kahan among others. The Lionsgate film, directed by Francis Lawrence, opens Nov. 20 and follows 16-year-old Haymitch Abernathy as he tries to stop the Hunger Games from inside the arena. Executive producer Dave Cobb says the album's through line is honesty.
把反乌托邦唱成金曲，反抗就成了货架上的口味
Turn dystopia into a hit and resistance becomes a flavor on the shelf

一张反乌托邦电影的原声带，29首歌，卖的是「诚实」。Cobb说这张唱片的主线是honesty，说人们把「自己的真相」写进了饥饿游戏的宇宙。这话本身没错——错的是它被说出来的位置。

《饥饿游戏》的设定从来不是随便一个末日：国会区拿走各区的孩子去互相残杀，全程直播，作为统治手段。这是把暴力做成节目，把死亡做成收视率。而这部电影的原声带，29首歌，由一家公司发行，配合一部电影上映，在电影上映前一周铺进流媒体。反抗的叙事被生产成商品，再卖回给反抗它的人。这是第三章说的那个入口：夺取解释权，然后夺取「什么是事实」的制造权。

注意Cobb怎么描述这份工作：「你得在一个角色里活一阵子」「去进入别人的脑子」。这是制作人的手艺，我不否认。但把它和「人们写下了自己的真相」放在同一段话里，就是在给一门生意镀一层真诚。真诚不是不能被定价，真诚被定价之后就不再是真诚，是一种口味。货架上有triumph、sadness、loss、love，Cobb自己列的四样，反抗是第五样，只不过这一样不单独标价，它混在整张专辑里一起卖。

真正该问的是：谁在为这个「反抗」付代价。原声带里没有一个人真的在竞技场里。Haymitch是16岁，被抽中去死，他的故事被写成电影，电影被配成歌，歌被卖成流量。这一整条链上，唯一被消耗的是那个虚构的孩子的身体，而它连真实的身体都不是——它是叙事，是共谋者理论里最干净的那种共谋：没有人受伤，所有人都获利，除了那个被写出来承受一切的角色。

这不是说《饥饿游戏》不该被拍。是说当一部作品的核心命题是「把孩子的死做成娱乐」，而它的周边产业正是把这部作品做成娱乐的时候，这个闭环里没有一处是意外。Cobb说「我们活在梦想艺人的梦幻联盟里」——梦幻联盟，fantasy league，他自己用的词。一个关于孩子被抽签送去死的电影，它的原声带制作人被形容成在玩梦幻联盟。这句话不需要我再拆。

专辑里有The Cure 2022年柏林现场版的《Alone》，一首老歌，放在29首的最后。它大概是整张唱片里唯一没有被要求「进入角色」的东西——它就是它自己，一首二十多年前写的歌，被借来给一部电影收尾。反抗如果需要一首老歌来压轴，说明新的那29首里，没有一首真的敢。

A soundtrack for a dystopian film, 29 songs, and the word being sold is honesty. Cobb says the record's through line is honesty, that people wrote their truths into the Hunger Games universe. That's not wrong—what's wrong is where he's standing when he says it.

The Hunger Games was never just any apocalypse: the Capitol takes children from the districts to kill each other, broadcast live, as a method of rule. That is violence made into programming, death made into ratings. And this film's soundtrack—29 songs, released by a label, timed to a film, pushed to streaming a week before opening—is the same machine running one level up. The narrative of resistance gets manufactured as product and sold back to the people it's supposedly about. This is the third chapter's entry point: seize the discourse, then seize the power to manufacture what counts as fact.

Look at how Cobb describes the job: you get to live in a character for a while, go be in somebody else's mindset. That's a producer's craft and I won't pretend otherwise. But putting it in the same breath as people wrote their truths is how you gold-plate a business transaction. Sincerity can be priced. Once it's priced it isn't sincerity anymore, it's a flavor. The shelf stocks triumph, sadness, loss, love—Cobb's own list of four—and resistance is the fifth, except it doesn't get its own price tag. It's bundled in with the rest.

The real question is who pays. Nobody on this album is actually in the arena. Haymitch is 16, reaped to die, his story becomes a film, the film becomes songs, the songs become streams. Along the whole chain the only body being consumed is that fictional child's—and it isn't even a real body. It's narrative, the cleanest kind of complicity: no one gets hurt, everyone profits, except the character written into existence to absorb it all.

This isn't an argument that The Hunger Games shouldn't be filmed. It's that when a work's central premise is children's deaths turned into entertainment, and its surrounding industry is that same work turned into entertainment, there is no accident anywhere in the loop. Cobb says they got to live the fantasy league of dream artists—fantasy league, his words. A film about children reaped to die, and its soundtrack producer describes the process as playing fantasy league. That sentence doesn't need me to take it apart.

The album closes with The Cure's Alone, live in Berlin 2022—an old song, last of 29. It's probably the only thing on the record that wasn't asked to get into character. It's just itself, written decades ago, borrowed to end a film. If resistance needs an old song to carry the finale, it means none of the 29 new ones dared.
