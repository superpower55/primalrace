未来是门票，不是能力The Future Is a Ticket, Not a Capacity

文娱 文化层 · 元暴力 WIRED ↗ 2026-11-02 § 链接
新闻摘要《连线》杂志将于2026年11月4日在迈阿密温伍德举办「WIRED World Fair」一日活动，主题为「未来的节庆」，门票不可退款，仅限18岁以上人士参加。活动阵容包括《洋葱报》CEO Ben Collins、喜剧演员 Patti Harrison、Hugging Face 的 Clément Delangue、导演 Alex Gibney、Colossal Biosciences 的 Ben Lamm、律师 Mark Lanier、Reddit 联合创始人 Alexis Ohanian、保守派评论员 CJ Pearson、记者 Kat Abughazaleh 等。现场体验项目包括 Unitree G1 人形机器人、NASA 的 XR 月球模拟、Marina Abramović 的数字分身表演，以及由 Chase Sapphire Reserve for Business 赞助的抹茶、休息区与保健品空间。
The newsWIRED will hold its one-day "World Fair: A Festival of the Future" on November 4, 2026, at The Sacred Space in Miami's Wynwood. Speakers include Onion CEO Ben Collins, Hugging Face's Clément Delangue, filmmaker Alex Gibney, lawyer Mark Lanier and conservative commentator CJ Pearson. Attractions include a Unitree G1 humanoid robot, a NASA XR moon simulation, a Marina Abramović avatar performance, and Chase-sponsored matcha and supplement lounges. Tickets are nonrefundable and guests must be 18 or older.
换掉尺子的发布会，从来不缺舞台
A launch that swaps the ruler never lacks a stage

一场关于「未来」的展会，卖点全部是体验：抹茶、冥想、保健品、人形机器人打招呼。未来的定义被重新写成了「你可以在一天之内摸到多少东西」。这是第三章的老把戏——夺取认知入口不需要说服你，只需要让你在场。

看嘉宾名单就看得出尺子怎么换的。Alex Gibney 正在拍关于 Luigi Mangione 的纪录片，而这本书里已经写过，Mangione 被舆论英雄化，靠的是长相和人们对医疗保险的怨恨，不是任何劫富济贫的壮举。一个以「无滤镜审视」为招牌的导演，把一个被指控的杀人犯做成作品，再拿到「未来」的舞台上当谈资——这不是记录，是给叙事再上一道釉。

Ben Collins 的履历更直接。他2024年参与组建收购《洋葱报》的团队，2026年《洋葱报》收购了 Infowars。同一份简介里，Patti Harrison 的身份是 Infowars 节目 Nerve Center 的联合主持人。一个以拆穿 QAnon 起家的记者，把阴谋论平台收进自己手里，再让喜剧演员在上面主持节目。解释权的换手看起来完成了，但平台还在，形式还在，只是换了主人。这是共谋者理论最干净的一例：批判者和被批判者合流，回报是一个现成的受众池。

Mark Lanier 赢下了全国第一起社交媒体成瘾审判，对手是 Meta 和 Google 的 YouTube。这算 direct 层的一次具体进展——有人真的被起诉、被判了。但同一份简介里紧接着写他带领一个750人的圣经班。一个在法庭上对抗注意力机器的人，在周末用另一套叙事组织人群。这不是伪善，这是共谋的常态：一个人可以在一个场域里缩小差额，在另一个场域里维护秩序。

整场活动最诚实的一行字是赞助商那一段：Chase Sapphire Reserve for Business 提供抹茶、休息区和保健品，帮你「补充、重整、充电」。未来的入场券由信用卡公司发行，NASA 的月球模拟和 Unitree 的机器人是展品，不是能力。你摸得到它们，但摸不到制造它们的权力。

至于那个由 Marina Abramović 的数字分身表演的「慢走」——一位以用身体测试极限著称的艺术家，现在由一个有生命力的数字孪生替她走。技术被说成是探索「在场」的新机会。可在场的定义如果可以被复制，那它原本就不是在场。

门票不可退款，仅限18岁以上。未来的门槛写得很清楚：你得先买票，还得够年龄。差额没有被缩小，只是被标了价。

An event about "the future" whose selling points are all experiences: matcha, meditation, supplements, a humanoid robot saying hello. The future gets redefined as how many things you can touch in one day. This is the old move from Chapter 3 — seizing the cognitive entrance doesn't require persuading you, only putting you in the room.

The speaker list shows how the ruler got swapped. Alex Gibney is producing and directing a Hulu documentary about Luigi Mangione, and the book has already covered how Mangione was heroized by looks and public rage at health insurers, not by any Robin Hood act. A director famous for unvarnished examination turns an accused killer into a subject, then brings it to a "future" stage as material. That isn't documentation; it's another coat of glaze on a narrative.

Ben Collins's bio is more direct. He helped assemble the group that acquired The Onion in 2024, and in 2026 The Onion acquired Infowars. In the same lineup, Patti Harrison is co-host of Nerve Center on Infowars. A journalist who built a career covering QAnon takes over the conspiracy platform, then puts a comedian on air there. The handoff of interpretive power looks complete, but the platform remains, the format remains — only the owner changed. This is the co-conspirator theory in its cleanest form: the critic and the criticized merge, and the payoff is a ready-made audience pool.

Mark Lanier won the nation's first social-media addiction trial, against Meta and Google's YouTube. That counts as a concrete gain at the direct layer — someone was actually sued and actually judged. But the same bio notes he leads a 750-member Bible class in Houston. A man fighting the attention machine in court organizes a crowd with another narrative on weekends. This isn't hypocrisy; it's the normal shape of complicity. One can shrink the gap in one arena and maintain the order in another.

The most honest line on the page is the sponsor section: Chase Sapphire Reserve for Business offers matcha, a lounge, and supplements to help you refuel, regroup, recharge. The ticket to the future is issued by a credit card company. NASA's moon simulation and Unitree's robot are exhibits, not capacities. You can touch them; you can't touch the power that made them.

And then the "Slow Walking" performed by Marina Abramović's digital twin — an artist known for testing limits with her own body, now walked by a lifelike digital double. Technology is framed as a new chance to explore presence. But if presence can be copied, it was never presence.

Tickets are nonrefundable, guests must be 18 or older. The threshold to the future is stated plainly: buy a ticket, be old enough. The gap hasn't been closed. It's been priced.
