纽约的“哈比比”与美国例外论的崩塌Habibi City and the Collapse of American Exceptionalism

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-14 § 链接
当被殖民者的后代夺取解释权，美国例外论就成了过时的scam。
When the descendants of the colonized seize the power of interpretation, American Exceptionalism becomes an obsolete scam.

这篇文章揭示了一个极其有趣的博弈：一个由 SWANA 地区移民构成的新一代，正在纽约通过夺取“认知入口”来完成存在性战争的逆袭。他们不再满足于在 halal carts 这种底层服务业中扮演“勤恳的异邦人”，而是直接进入 SNL、高端餐饮和政治权力中心。这在我的框架里，是 Actual 正在向 Potential 靠近，结构层（structural layer）的暴力差额在缩小。

最精彩的部分在于这种身份的“武器化”反击。美国右翼试图用 9/11 的叙事将穆斯林永久地锁定在“敌人”这个标签里，这是一种典型的文化暴力（cultural violence），旨在通过制造“我们 vs 他们”的二元对立来维持美国例外论的合法性。但讽刺的是，这种长期的 Islamophobia 反而成了这群年轻人的生物墙和结盟基础。正如 Ramy Youssef 所说，被告知“这是你的错”反而强行激活了他们的宗教和政治身份。这种被动属性被他们主动转化成了身份政治的利器。

当 Zohran Mamdani 这种政治人物出现时，他不仅仅是一个穆斯林代表，他成了一个“解释权”的持有者。他敢于在公共空间解构“美国荣耀”的叙事，直接指出“反恐战争”的灾难性后果。这实际上是在削弱美国中心的元暴力（meta violence）。右翼之所以如此愤怒，是因为他们发现传统的“浪漫爱”式爱国主义叙事失效了，而这群经历过边缘化的人，正在用一种更具 cosmopolitan 气质的视角重新定义什么是“美国故事”。

但不要 naive 地庆祝。Mamdani 与 Giuliani 的握手虽然在文化层面上像是一场胜利，但本质上仍是在对方定义的规则（ceremony）中寻求认可。真正的胜利不是被允许参加纪念仪式，而是让那个定义谁是“威胁”的权力机制彻底失效。这场战争才刚刚进入认知入口的争夺阶段。

This article reveals a fascinating game: a new generation of SWANA immigrants in New York is reclaiming their existence by seizing the cognitive entrances of power. They are no longer content playing the role of the 'diligent alien' in halal carts; they are infiltrating SNL, fine dining, and political seats. In my framework, this is Actual moving toward Potential—the gap of structural violence is shrinking.

The most compelling part is the weaponisation of identity. The US right attempts to lock Muslims into the 'enemy' label using the 9/11 narrative—a classic form of cultural violence designed to maintain the legitimacy of American Exceptionalism via an 'us vs them' dichotomy. Ironically, this systemic Islamophobia became a biological wall and a basis for alliance for these youths. As Ramy Youssef noted, being told 'it was your fault' forcibly activated their religious and political identities. They transformed a passive attribute into a sharp tool of identity politics.

When figures like Zohran Mamdani emerge, they aren't just representatives; they are holders of the power of interpretation. By daring to deconstruct the narrative of 'American glory' and pointing out the calamities of the 'war on terror,' they are actively eroding the meta-violence of US-centric narratives. The right is furious because the 'romantic' narrative of patriotism is failing, replaced by a cosmopolitan perspective that redefines the 'American story' from the margins.

But let's not be naive. Mamdani's handshake with Giuliani may look like a cultural victory, but it is still a performance of seeking recognition within a ceremony defined by the oppressor. True victory isn't being 'allowed' to attend a memorial; it is the total obsolescence of the mechanism that defines who is a 'threat.' This war has only just entered the stage of fighting for the cognitive entrance.
