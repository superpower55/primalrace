Drip Pricing: A Low-Level Scam of the Power-HoldersDrip Pricing: A Low-Level Scam of the Power-Holders

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-19 § 链接
Hidden fees are not 'pricing strategies', but structural violence against the consumer's agency.
Hidden fees are not 'pricing strategies', but structural violence against the consumer's agency.

所谓的“滴定定价”（drip pricing）本质上是一场关于认知入口的低级 scam。通过在初始界面展示一个被阉割的低价，企业在潜意识中诱导消费者建立一个错误的“事实”锚点，随后在支付路径的末端才缓慢释放强制性费用。这不是商业技巧，而是一种典型的 weaponized expression：它利用了人类在决策时的认知惯性，强行夺取了消费者在博弈中本应拥有的透明定价权。

从加尔通的暴力三角来看，这属于典型的 structural violence。这种制度性的欺诈将 Potential（消费者基于真实价格做出的最优选择）与 Actual（被误导后的被动支付）之间制造了差额，这个差额就是企业非法攫取的利润。在这种结构中，企业通过操纵信息流，让消费者在不知情的情况下成为了这场利益共谋的牺牲品。

值得关注的是 CMA 这次动用了去年刚获得的新权力——无需经过法院即可判定违规。这意味着 structural 层面的暴力在被削减：监管者不再扮演一个缓慢的观察者，而是在尝试直接切断这种欺诈的反馈回路。如果这种权力能从“表演性监管”转化为实际的、高额的全球营业额罚款，那么 Actual 才会真正向 Potential 靠近。

但不要 naive 地庆祝。只要“低价”依然是这个时代的最高认知入口，只要企业能够通过重新定义“服务费”或“管理费”来换皮，这种滴定式的掠夺就永远不会消失。这次调查削掉的是几个具体的巨头，但那个鼓励欺诈的元逻辑依然在运作。

So-called "drip pricing" is essentially a low-level scam targeting the cognitive entry point. By displaying a castrated low price initially, companies induce customers to establish a false factual anchor, only to leak mandatory fees at the end of the payment path. This isn't a business tactic; it is a weaponized expression that exploits cognitive inertia to seize the pricing power the consumer should have held in the game.

Within the Violence Triangle, this is textbook structural violence. By manipulating the information flow, these firms create a gap between the Potential (the optimal choice based on true pricing) and the Actual (passive payment after being misled). This gap is the profit illegally extracted by the corporation, turning the consumer into a victim of a systemic complicity.

It is notable that the CMA is utilizing new powers granted last year—the ability to determine law-breaking without going through the courts. This represents a reduction of structural violence: the regulator is attempting to cut the feedback loop of fraud directly rather than remaining a slow observer. Only if this power evolves from performative regulation into actual, heavy fines based on global turnover will the Actual truly move toward the Potential.

But don't be naive. As long as "low price" remains the dominant cognitive entry point, and as long as companies can rebrand "service fees" or "admin charges" to disguise the scam, this drip-style predation will persist. This investigation trims a few giants, but the meta-logic encouraging fraud remains fully operational.
