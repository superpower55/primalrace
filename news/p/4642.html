用罚单修补文明：一种典型的中产阶级共谋Fixing Civilization with Fines: A Classic Middle-Class Complicity

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-30 § 链接
将个体注意力的缺失立法化，是权力对“秩序”的低成本伪装。
Legislating the lack of individual attention is a low-cost masquerade of 'order' by power.

意大利拟禁止行人过马路时看手机，这在表面上是一次 structural layer 的安全升级，但在逻辑上却是一场典型的中产阶级共谋。作者 Dave Schilling 将其视为“前瞻性”的进步，甚至以此为由要求将所有“小烦恼”——如不推椅子、开铃声——全部刑事化。这种叙事极其危险，因为它试图用法律的强制力来替代个体的主体性，将“文明”定义为一套可量化的、由罚单支撑的礼仪清单。

从加尔通的暴力三角来看，这种立法实际上是在制造一种新的 structural violence。当权力开始定义什么是“正确的行为习惯”并将其与金钱惩罚挂钩时，它在潜移默化中夺取了人们对公共空间的解释权。这不再是关于安全（Safety），而是关于控制（Control）。作者在文中自称是“守护天使”，这种心态恰恰揭示了元暴力的运作方式：强者通过定义弱者的“愚钝”或“鲁莽”，来获得一种道德上的权力快感，并以此要求系统为他的这种快感背书。

最讽刺的是，作者在呼吁禁止电动滑板车占用人行道时，坦然承认自己可能在看手机。这种双标揭示了共谋者的本质：他们并不在乎规则本身是否公正，他们在乎的是规则是否能排除掉那些让他们“心烦”的异己。这是一种典型的 weaponized 叙事——将个人的审美厌恶包装成公共安全需求。当法律变成了清理“噪音”和“低端行为”的扫帚，它就成了维持既定阶级秩序的工具，而非保障人权的底线。

Italy's proposal to ban phone use while crossing the street appears to be a structural layer upgrade for safety, but logically, it is a classic case of middle-class complicity. Dave Schilling hails this as 'forward-thinking,' even suggesting the criminalization of all 'small annoyances'—like not pushing in a chair or leaving a ringer on. This narrative is dangerous because it attempts to replace individual subjectivity with legal coercion, defining 'civilization' as a quantifiable checklist of etiquette backed by fines.

Applying the Violence Triangle, this legislation creates a new form of structural violence. When power begins to define 'correct behavioral habits' and links them to financial penalties, it covertly seizes the power of interpretation over public space. This is no longer about safety; it is about control. The author's self-description as a 'guardian angel' reveals the operation of meta-violence: the dominant party defines the 'cluelessness' or 'recklessness' of others to derive a moral power trip, then demands the system validate this feeling.

The irony peaks when the author calls for banning e-scooters on sidewalks while admitting he himself might be texting. This hypocrisy exposes the essence of the co-conspirator: they do not care if the rule is just; they care if the rule eliminates the 'annoyances' caused by others. This is a weaponized narrative—packaging personal aesthetic distaste as a public safety necessity. When the law becomes a broom to sweep away 'noise' and 'low-end behavior,' it ceases to be a baseline for human rights and becomes a tool for maintaining a predetermined class order.
