British Gas 的“失能”是精准的结构性暴力British Gas's 'Incompetence' is a Precise Structural Violence

哲学 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-17 § 链接
公司对欠款的精准追讨与对误付的集体失能，本质是权力不对称的算法霸权。
The contrast between precise debt collection and collective failure to reclaim overpayments is the essence of algorithmic hegemony.

很多人把这条新闻看作一个关于“笨拙公司”的幽默段子：一家能源巨头误付了三万多英镑，却在客户苦苦哀求还钱时表现得像个失智的巨婴。但这绝不是什么 butter-fingered agent 的个人失误，而是一场典型的 structural violence。

请注意这个极具讽刺性的对比：British Gas 在处理“客户欠款”时拥有最高效的追讨机制，能迅速将虚构的债务标记在客户的信用记录上，直接摧毁一个人的金融生活（如文中的 JG）；但在处理“误付资金”时，他们的客服脚本里竟然没有相应的程序。这意味着，在公司的逻辑里，追债是 a priority，而纠错是 a glitch。这种“选择性失能”揭示了权力运行的真相：系统被设计成单向榨取，而非双向公正。

对于普通个体来说，这种不对称性构成了一种存在性威胁。当你面对一个能随意定义你“信用破产”的巨兽时，你即便面对三万英镑的意外之财，感受到的不是 windfall，而是 fear。这种恐惧来源于对系统不可预测性的认知——既然它能莫名其妙地给你钱，它就能在明天莫名其妙地指控你诈骗并把你送进监狱。

所谓的 £500 补偿金不过是典型的 PR-style 封口费。真正的 violence 隐藏在那个被忽视的逻辑里：公司并不在乎具体的钱，它在乎的是对定义权的垄断。它定义谁欠钱，谁就必须还；它定义谁是违约者，谁就失去信用。在这种元暴力面前，个体只能通过“摇晃笼子”这种低效的博弈，试图在对方的脚本里争取一点点公正的表达。

Many read this news as a comedic anecdote about a 'clumsy company'—an energy giant that accidentally paid over £38,000 and then acted like a mindless infant when the customer begged to return it. But this is no mere mistake by a 'butter-fingered agent'; it is a textbook case of structural violence.

Note the sharp irony: British Gas possesses the most efficient mechanisms for pursuing customer debts, capable of slashing a person's financial life by marking fictional debts on credit records (as seen with JG). Yet, when reclaiming misdirected funds, their customer service scripts are blank. This 'selective incompetence' reveals the truth of power: the system is designed for one-way extraction, not two-way justice.

For the individual, this asymmetry creates an existential threat. When facing a behemoth that can arbitrarily define your 'credit bankruptcy,' an unexpected £38,000 windfall brings not joy, but fear. This fear stems from the unpredictability of the system—if it can accidentally give you money, it can just as easily accuse you of fraud and throw you in prison tomorrow.

The £500 compensation is mere PR-style hush money. The real violence lies in the monopolization of the power to define. If the company defines you as a debtor, you must pay; if it defines you as a defaulter, you lose your credit. In the face of this meta-violence, the individual is reduced to 'rattling the cage,' a low-efficiency gamble to secure a shred of Just Expression within the company's rigid scripts.
