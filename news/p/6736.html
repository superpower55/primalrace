一部关于中年女人的小说，被拍成了哥特悬疑A Novel About a Middle-Aged Woman, Sold as a Gothic Mystery

文娱 文化层 IndieWire ↗ 2026-11-02 § 链接
新闻摘要乔尔·科恩的新片《Jack of Spades》改编自弗朗西丝·弗雷德里卡·蒙特雷索1901年的小说《The Alien: A Story of Middle Age》，由Netflix安排在2027年秋季院线上映，之后上线流媒体。影片由帕齐·费兰、乔什·奥康纳、达米安·刘易斯、莱斯利·曼维尔主演，故事设定在苏格兰高地。
The newsJoel Coen's new film "Jack of Spades," adapted from Frances Frederica Montrésor's 1901 novel "The Alien: A Story of Middle Age," will get a theatrical release in fall 2027 before streaming on Netflix. It stars Patsy Ferran, Josh O'Connor, Damian Lewis, and Lesley Manville, and is set in the Scottish Highlands.
原著是中年女人的一生，宣传语里她只剩一个背景
The source novel is one woman's life; the logline gives her a backdrop.

这条新闻里最值得看的一句话，是官方梗概：一个「充满阴谋与背叛的哥特悬疑故事」，背景设在苏格兰高地。原著讲的是一个三十多岁的女人埃丝特·莫当特，和一个会讲故事的旅人贾斯珀之间逐渐长成的关系，讲一个人的一生怎么横跨时间、冒险与渴望。到了梗概里，这些全没了，只剩「哥特」「阴谋」「背叛」三个词，和一个风景。

宣传语不是中性的摘要，它是定价。谁的名字放进梗概，谁的经历被写成卖点，谁被降格成「氛围」，都是选择。蒙特雷索1901年写的是一本关于中年女人内在生活的小说，Netflix给它的定位是一桩悬疑案的包装纸。这不是改编本身的问题，改编当然可以改；问题在于对外说话的那一句话里，埃丝特不在了。

再看阵容：帕齐·费兰、乔什·奥康纳、达米安·刘易斯、莱斯利·曼维尔。两个金球奖得主是男的，奥斯卡提名是女的，但奥斯卡提名这四个字挂在莱斯利·曼维尔身上，她演什么角色，报道没说。原著的女主角三十多岁，费兰来演，这条线报道也没提。一部以女人为主角的小说，宣传材料里最先被确认的是两个男演员的奖项。

院线发行这件事本身是好的：2027年秋季先进影院，再上流媒体。这是把一部电影从「点开就有」变回「出门去看」，在结构层上让影院的窗口多撑了一次。但窗口撑开的是谁的片子？是科恩的片子，是Netflix愿意给排面的片子。同样的窗口，绝大多数直接上线的小成本电影拿不到。

所以这不是一条关于性别暴力的新闻，它是一条关于谁被写进句子的新闻。一部小说里的女人被搬上银幕，第一句话里她就已经退到高地后面去了。等2027年秋天观众走进影院，他们看到的埃丝特会是什么样，报道没写；报道写的是科恩、科恩的摄影师、科恩的作曲家。

The most telling sentence in this story is the official logline: "a sinister gothic mystery filled with intrigue and betrayal set in the atmospheric Scottish Highlands." The novel it adapts follows Esther Mordaunt, well into her thirties, and her evolving bond with Jasper, a traveler with a vivid past and a talent for storytelling. It is a book about how a single life stretches across time, risk, and longing. In the logline, all of that is gone. What remains is gothic, intrigue, betrayal, and scenery.

A logline is not a neutral summary. It is a price tag. Whose name goes in, whose experience becomes the selling point, who gets demoted to atmosphere — these are choices. Montrésor wrote a novel about a middle-aged woman's inner life in 1901. Netflix is packaging it as a mystery. Adaptation is fine; the problem is that in the one sentence meant to represent the film, Esther is missing.

Then the cast: Patsy Ferran, Josh O'Connor, Damian Lewis, Lesley Manville. Two Golden Globe winners are men. The Oscar nominee is a woman, but the report never says what role Manville plays. The novel's protagonist is in her thirties; the report never says Ferran is playing her. A film built on a woman's story leads with two men's awards.

The theatrical window is real: fall 2027 in cinemas before streaming. That pushes back against the everything-on-demand default, and structurally it keeps one more window open. But whose film gets the window? Coen's, with Netflix's backing. The small films that go straight to streaming do not get this.

So this is not a story about gender violence. It is a story about who gets written into the sentence. A woman's novel reaches the screen, and in the first line about it she has already retreated behind the Highlands. What audiences will see of Esther in fall 2027, the report does not say. What it says is Coen, Coen's cinematographer, Coen's composer.
