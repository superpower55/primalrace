语言课、营养餐与被定价的肉身English Lessons, Nutrition Plans, and the Priced Flesh

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-06 § 链接
所谓的“人才工厂”，本质是高效的资产增值与标准化套利。
The so-called 'talent factory' is essentially an efficient system of asset appreciation and standardized arbitrage.

Midtjylland 把自己定义为“国际俱乐部”，但实际上它运行的是一套极其冷酷的资产管理逻辑。强制英语课、营养计划、习惯重塑，这些在新闻里被包装成“为了球员成长”的关怀，但在我的眼睛里，这是典型的“标准化加工”。

球员在这里不再是拥有主体性的运动员，而是待加工的 raw material。英语课不是为了让他们在欧洲更好地生活，而是为了消除沟通成本，确保教练的指令能被精准执行，从而快速提升球员的“市场价值”。当一个来自智利或几内亚比绍的年轻人被告知“白面包不能在晚餐吃”时，俱乐部在做的不是健康管理，而是在对这件“商品”进行物理层面的优化，以确保在转会窗口到来时，能以最高价将其卖给英超这种顶级买家。

最令人不安的是这种“人才工厂”的闭环：从尼日利亚的学院到丹麦的加工场，再到英超的收割季。球员被告知这是实现梦想的路径，但实际上他们进入了一个被精心设计的博弈陷阱。他们的表达、习惯、甚至饮食都被重新定义，以符合顶级联赛的“审美”和“标准”。

这是一种高效的 structural violence：它不通过身体伤害，而是通过夺取对个体生活方式的解释权，将人彻底客体化为一种可量化的金融资产。当技术总监自豪地说这“不是纯粹的运气”而是“策略”时，他其实是在承认：他成功地把活生生的人，变成了流水线上精准定价的 SKU。

Midtjylland defines itself as an 'international club,' but in reality, it operates on a cold logic of asset management. Mandatory English lessons and nutrition plans are packaged as 'care for player growth,' but these are clearly standardized processing lines.

Players here are no longer subjects with agency; they are raw material. English lessons aren't for better integration into European life, but to eliminate communication costs and ensure the coach's instructions are executed with precision, thereby inflating the player's 'market value.' When a youngster from Chile is told white bread is forbidden for dinner, the club isn't managing health—they are physically optimizing a commodity to ensure it fetches the highest price in the Premier League window.

The most disturbing part is the closed loop of this 'talent factory': from academies in Nigeria to processing plants in Denmark, and finally to the harvest season in England. Players are told this is the path to their dreams, while they are actually entering a carefully designed game of arbitrage. Their expression, habits, and even diet are redefined to fit the 'aesthetic' and 'standards' of top leagues.

This is a form of structural violence: it doesn't use physical force, but by seizing the power to define an individual's lifestyle, it turns human beings into quantifiable financial assets. When the technical director proudly claims this is 'strategy' and 'not pure luck,' he is admitting that he has successfully converted living humans into precisely priced SKUs on an assembly line.
