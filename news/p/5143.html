Ed Sheeran 的“非政治”表演与共谋者的生存艺术Ed Sheeran's 'Apolitical' Performance and the Art of Complicity

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-20 § 链接
试图在结构性暴力中保持“中立”，本身就是对既定秩序的最高共谋。
Seeking 'neutrality' amidst structural violence is the ultimate act of complicity with the established order.

Ed Sheeran 在费城演唱会上的表态是一次典型的“假.最优解表达”。他用“心碎”、“灾难性”这种纯粹的情感词汇来消解政治性，试图在谴责暴力的同时，迅速把自己拉回到“我不想成为活动家”的避风港里。这种叙事逻辑极其阴险：它承认受害者的痛苦，但拒绝承认这种痛苦背后的权力结构，从而在维持“好人”人设的同时，确保自己的商业帝国不受损。

这场风波的本质是关于“解释权”的博弈。当 Macklemore 因为喊出 “Free Palestine” 被场馆所有者——亿万富翁 Robert Kraft 等资本方剔除时，Sheeran 的沉默就是一种 Complicity（共谋）。在资本控制的认知入口（体育场、巡演合同）面前，他选择将责任推给 promoter，扮演一个被动卷入的受害者。这种“我很难办”的姿态，实际上是他在存在性战争中为了保住最高商业利益而选择的掩体。

最讽刺的是那些认为“音乐与政治无关”的粉丝。这种所谓的“纯粹”其实是 Meta-violence（元暴力）的延伸——只有那些不需要为生存而战的人，才有资格定义什么是“非政治”的。当一个艺术家的沉默被资本用来噤声他人时，这种沉默就不再是私人选择，而是一种武器化的表达。Sheeran 所谓的“个人观点”，在没有转化为公共抗争之前，其价值等同于零。

这场表演最终证明了：在结构性暴力面前，没有所谓的“中立地带”。你要么在挑战那个定义规则的权力，要么在为那个权力提供温情的背景音乐。

Ed Sheeran's remarks at his Philadelphia show are a textbook example of a 'Fake Optimal Expression.' By using purely emotional descriptors like 'heartbroken' and 'catastrophic,' he attempts to dissolve the political nature of the crisis, retreating into the safe harbor of 'not wanting to be an activist.' This narrative logic is insidious: it acknowledges the suffering of victims while refusing to acknowledge the power structures behind that suffering, allowing him to maintain a 'good guy' persona without jeopardizing his commercial empire.

The essence of this turmoil is a battle over the right of interpretation. When Macklemore was purged by stadium owners like billionaire Robert Kraft for shouting 'Free Palestine,' Sheeran's silence became an act of Complicity. Faced with the cognitive gateways controlled by capital—stadiums and tour contracts—he chose to deflect responsibility onto the promoter, playing the role of a passive victim. This 'it's complicated' posture is simply a shield he uses to secure maximum commercial interests in his existential war.

The most ironic part is the fans who claim 'music has nothing to do with politics.' This perceived 'purity' is an extension of Meta-violence; only those who do not have to fight for their survival have the luxury of defining what is 'apolitical.' When an artist's silence is weaponized by capital to silence others, that silence ceases to be a private choice and becomes a weaponized expression. Sheeran's 'personal views,' as long as they aren't converted into public resistance, have a value of zero.

This performance proves that there is no 'neutral ground' in the face of structural violence. You are either challenging the power that defines the rules, or you are providing the soothing background music for that power.
