以“例外”掩盖的系统性屠杀The Exception That Validates the Slaughter

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-07 § 链接
表演性执法是结构暴力的最高级伪装。
Performative law enforcement is the most sophisticated camouflage for structural violence.

一个十年内杀掉 1500 人的系统，终于决定起诉其中一个。这不叫正义的回归，这叫 PR 危机公关。当 Awdeh Hathaleen 这种具有国际能见度的 activist 被杀，且有视频证据在手时，以色列政府必须在 cultural 层面上投放一个“我们依然在执法”的叙事，以对冲国际社会的压力。这是一种极其精准的武器化表达：用一个极小概率的 indictment 来证明规则的存在，从而让剩下的 1499 次屠杀在“规则”的掩护下显得更加自然。

注意这里的细节：控罪是“过失致死”（manslaughter）而非“一级谋杀”。这种在 structural 层的降级处理，本质上是在为施暴者预留逃生通道。从 2019 年杀人仅判社区服务三个月的先例来看，这种起诉不过是一场漫长的表演。共谋者们——从起草法律的官员到最终判决的法官——通过这种“有起诉、无定罪”或“有定罪、无实刑”的闭环，完成了对殖民暴力的制度性背书。

这就是典型的元暴力逻辑：定义谁是“文明”的，谁是“野蛮”的。在西岸，以色列定居者的暴力被包装成“安全需求”，而巴勒斯坦人的反抗则被定义为“恐怖”。当这种男性中心且殖民主义的叙事垄断了解释权，法律就不再是保护人的工具，而成了管理暴力的阀门。这次起诉只是阀门微调了一下，为了让整个杀戮机器能更安静地运转下去。

A system that has killed 1,500 people in a decade finally decides to indict one. This isn't a return to justice; it's a PR crisis management strategy. When a high-profile activist like Awdeh Hathaleen is murdered and the evidence is captured on film, the state must deploy a narrative of "active law enforcement" at the cultural layer to offset international pressure. This is a precise weaponization of expression: using a low-probability indictment to simulate the existence of rules, thereby making the other 1,499 killings appear more "natural" under the guise of a functioning system.

Observe the structural downgrade: the charge is reckless manslaughter, not first-degree murder. This is a calculated move to ensure a lenient exit for the perpetrator. Given the precedent of a 2019 killing resulting in mere community service, this indictment is a slow-motion performance. The complicitors—from the legislators to the judges—maintain a closed loop of "indictment without conviction" or "conviction without penalty," providing institutional endorsement for colonial violence.

This is the core of meta-violence: the monopoly over who is defined as "civilized" and who is "barbaric." In the West Bank, settler violence is framed as "security needs," while Palestinian resistance is branded as "terrorism." When this masculine-centric and colonial narrative controls the interpretation, the law ceases to be a tool for human rights and becomes a valve for managing violence. This indictment is merely a slight adjustment of that valve, designed to keep the killing machine running more quietly.
