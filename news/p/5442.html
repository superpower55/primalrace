用美国城市做筹码的“最优解”博弈Betting US Cities as Chips in an 'Optimal' Game

国际 元暴力 · 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-06 § 链接
当权力将具体生命客体化为筹码，所谓的“大局”就是元暴力的最高形式。
When power objectifies human life as a chip, the so-called 'big picture' is the ultimate form of meta-violence.

特朗普在内布拉斯加州说洛杉矶和圣地亚哥被伊朗“端掉”是维持世界安全的“小代价”。这句话不是口误，而是一次极其典型的 masculine-centric narrative 表达：在统治者的认知入口里，具体的城市、具体的家庭、具体的生命，都被降格为棋盘上的 token。这种将他人客体化为“代价”的逻辑，正是元暴力（meta violence）的底层代码——只有掌控解释权的人才是主体，其余所有人都只是达成其“最优解”的耗材。

有趣的是，这种武器化的叙事在同一时间出现了两种截然不同的共谋。一方面是白宫通讯主管 Steven Cheung 的洗地，试图将这种恐怖威胁包装成“美国更安全”的证明，通过重新定义现实来掩盖其对公民生命的漠视；另一方面是特朗普与 Anduril Industries 掌门人 Palmer Luckey 的商业共谋。一边在集会上把美国城市当成可抛弃的 collateral damage，一边迅速宣布投资数十亿美金建设由捐赠者 Luckey 承建的造船厂。这套逻辑闭环得令人作呕：制造恐惧 $ ightarrow$ 强化战争叙事 $ ightarrow$ 引导联邦资金流向共谋者的口袋。

至于民主党在中期选举中的反弹，本质上是 Potential − Actual 差额的自然回弹。当燃料价格和生活成本让底层民众意识到，那个口口声声说“让美国伟大”的权力中心，实际上在进行一场以牺牲他们为前提的私人博弈时，结构性的共谋开始瓦解。但这依然不是一种 structural 层面的胜利，而是一次基于生存本能的投票。只要“强人政治”这种 weaponized 叙事依然被视为某种“高效”的表达，这种将生命定价为“小代价”的元暴力就永远不会消失。

Trump’s remark that Los Angeles and San Diego being 'taken out' by Iran would be a 'small price to pay' is not a slip of the tongue. It is a textbook expression of masculine-centric narrative: in the ruler's cognitive entry, specific cities, families, and lives are downgraded to mere tokens on a chessboard. This logic of objectifying others as 'costs' is the underlying code of meta-violence—only those who control the interpretation are subjects; everyone else is just disposable material for their 'optimal expression.'

Interestingly, two distinct forms of complicity emerged simultaneously. On one hand, White House Communications Director Steven Cheung attempted to rebrand this threat as proof that 'America is safer,' using a weaponized narrative to mask a blatant disregard for human life. On the other hand, we see the commercial complicity between Trump and Palmer Luckey of Anduril Industries. While treating American cities as expendable collateral damage in rallies, Trump swiftly announced billions in funding for a shipyard built by his donor, Luckey. The loop is disgusting: manufacture fear $ ightarrow$ reinforce war narratives $ ightarrow$ funnel federal funds into the pockets of co-conspirators.

The Democratic surge in the midterms is simply a natural rebound of the gap between Potential and Actual. When rising fuel and living costs force the grassroots to realize that the power center claiming to 'Make America Great' is actually playing a private game at their expense, the structural complicity begins to crumble. However, this is not yet a structural victory, but a vote driven by survival instinct. As long as 'strongman politics' remains a weaponized narrative perceived as 'efficient,' this meta-violence—pricing human life as a 'small price'—will persist.
