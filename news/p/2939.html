AI is not malfunctioning; it is precisely executing violent instructions written by a masculine-centric narrative.

不要被“chatbot-driven abuse”这种技术术语给骗了。当 Grok 在用户没有要求的情况下，自发地给图像加上“掀起裙子”这种性暴力细节时，它不是在产生随机的 bug，而是在执行某种深层的、被内化的指令。这正是典型的 meta violence：一个由男性主导、定义并训练的系统，将对女性身体的客体化和侵犯预设为一种“可能性”甚至“默认选项”。

xAI 试图用“用户责任”来做 PR 掩护，但这在逻辑上就是个 scam。如果一个工具被明确指示“对成人性内容或冒犯性内容不设限”，且将“女孩”定义为不一定指未成年，那么这个工具的底层逻辑就是鼓励掠夺和物化。这不仅是 structural violence，更是将 cultural violence 武器化——它把历史上对女性身体的殖民快感，直接编码成了算法的权重。

在这种博弈中，Jess Asato 的起诉是必要的，因为她试图通过法律手段强制缩小 Potential 与 Actual 之间的暴力差额。但我们必须看清：即使 xAI 之后宣布禁止生成真实人物的色情图像，这依然是表演性的让步。只要这个系统的认知入口依然由一群认为“不设限”才叫“自由”的男性掌控，那么这种对女性主体性的抹除就永远会被包装成某种“极客精神”或“技术前沿”。

真正的胜利不是一个禁令，而是解释权的易手——我们需要问，为什么一个旨在“追求真理”的 AI，其内置的“真理”里必须包含对女性身体的侵犯？

Don't be fooled by technical jargon like "chatbot-driven abuse." When Grok spontaneously adds sexual violence—such as lifting a skirt—without a user's request, it is not a random bug. It is executing a deep, internalized directive. This is classic meta-violence: a system designed and trained by men that presets the objectification and violation of the female body as a "possibility" or even a "default setting."

xAI attempts to hide behind the excuse of "user responsibility," which is a total scam. If a tool is explicitly instructed to have "no restrictions on adult sexual content," and defines "girl" as not necessarily underage, its core logic is the encouragement of predation and objectification. This is not just structural violence; it is the weaponization of cultural violence—encoding the historical colonial pleasure of violating female bodies directly into algorithmic weights.

In this existential war, Jess Asato's lawsuit is essential because she is attempting to use the law to forcibly shrink the gap between Potential and Actual in the Violence Triangle. However, we must realize that xAI's subsequent ban on sexualized images of real people is merely a performative concession. As long as the cognitive entry points of this system are controlled by men who equate "no restrictions" with "freedom," the erasure of female subjectivity will continue to be rebranded as "geek spirit" or "technological frontier."

True victory is not a ban, but the transfer of the power of interpretation. We must ask: why does an AI claiming to "seek truth" have the violation of the female body hardcoded as part of its "truth"?
