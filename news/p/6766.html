圣诞女王加冕，谁在收税The Christmas Queen Collects Rent

文娱 文化层 Vulture ↗ 2026-11-02 § 链接
新闻摘要Vulture 报道，Mariah Carey 发布今年的「It's Time!!!!」圣诞季宣传视频。此前 Ella Langley 的《Choosin' Texas》以 25 周在 Billboard Hot 100 榜首超过 Carey 圣诞曲的 22 周，Carey 今年 4 月还连续第三年未入选摇滚名人堂。她的圣诞曲已重回 Spotify 全球 Top 50 第 32 位。
The newsVulture reports that Mariah Carey has released this year's "It's Time!!!!" video kicking off the Christmas season. Ella Langley's "Choosin' Texas" has now spent 25 weeks atop the Billboard Hot 100, surpassing Carey's 22 with her Christmas song, and Carey was passed over for the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame for a third year in April. The song has already re-entered Spotify's Global Top 50 at No. 32.
一首歌的纪录，是几十年造出来的收租权。
A song's chart record is a decades-built right to collect rent.

「It's Time!!!!」不是一个视频，是一张年票。Carey 每年十一月一日准时出现，宣布季节开始，然后《All I Want for Christmas Is You》重新爬榜。今年她需要这个仪式，因为 Ella Langley 的《Choosin' Texas》在 Hot 100 榜首坐了 25 周，超过了她那首圣诞歌的 22 周，四月她还连续第三年没进摇滚名人堂。王座被人碰了一下，女王于是提前发令。

这首歌本身没什么可挑的，它是一首好歌。但它占据的位置不是靠好听占住的。一首 1994 年的圣诞歌，每年十二月被商场、电台、算法和播放列表同时推上去，二十多年反复播放，最后变成一个「纪录」。这个纪录衡量的不是歌的质量，是它被允许出现的次数。榜单在这里不是评价，是分配——谁的音乐能进入公共空间的十二月，谁就自动获得那张票。

这就是第三章说的认知入口。一个节日被一首歌承包，不是自然形成的，是产业、版权和播放渠道一起把「圣诞节的声音」定成了一个标准答案。定完之后，其他所有圣诞歌都成了备选。榜单上的周数看起来是竞争的结果，其实是入口被谁把着的问题。Langley 的歌能坐 25 周，靠的是今年被反复推送；Carey 的歌能坐 22 周，靠的是每年被重新推送。两个数字都是同一套机器吐出来的，只是机器今年多给了另一边一点。

今年她还和 Gucci 拍了节日广告片，用的就是这首歌。歌、节日、品牌、榜单，四样东西扣在一起转。这不是丑闻，这是生意，做得漂亮。但读者该看清的是：所谓「女王回宫」，回的是一个被商业结构预先建好的宫，宫门什么时候开、开给谁，不由歌手定。

所以这首歌的纪录就算今年被夺回去，夺回的也只是租约的续期。真正没被碰过的是那个把一首歌变成十二月基础设施的机制——它今年换了个人收租，明年还会换回来。

"It's Time!!!!" isn't a video. It's an annual pass. Carey shows up on November 1, declares the season open, and "All I Want for Christmas Is You" climbs again. This year she needs the ritual: Ella Langley's "Choosin' Texas" has spent 25 weeks atop the Hot 100, beating her Christmas song's 22, and in April she was passed over for the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame for the third year running. Someone nudged the throne, so the queen decrees early.

The song is fine. It's a good song. But the position it holds wasn't won by being good. A 1994 Christmas track gets pushed back up every December by malls, radio, algorithms and playlists at once, played on repeat for decades, until the repetition hardens into a "record." That record measures not quality but permission—how many times a song is allowed to appear. The chart here isn't evaluation, it's allocation: whoever owns December in public space automatically gets the vote.

This is the cognitive entrance at work. A holiday gets captured by one song, not naturally, but because labels, rights holders and distribution channels jointly fixed "the sound of Christmas" into a single correct answer. Once fixed, every other Christmas song becomes an alternate. The weeks on the chart look like the outcome of competition; they're really a question of who holds the door. Langley's 25 weeks came from being pushed hard this year; Carey's 22 came from being pushed again every year. Both numbers come out of the same machine, which simply leaned the other way this time.

She's also fronting a Gucci holiday campaign this year, built around the same song. Song, holiday, brand, chart—four things turning together. Not a scandal. A business, run well. But see what "the queen returns to her throne" actually means: a throne the commercial structure built in advance, with the gate opening on someone else's schedule.

So even if the record is reclaimed this year, what's reclaimed is a lease renewal. What was never touched is the mechanism that turns one song into December infrastructure—this year it bills someone else, next year it will bill her again.
