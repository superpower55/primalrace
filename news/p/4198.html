扎克伯格的“保护”是另一种形式的捕食Zuckerberg's 'Protection' is Just Another Form of Predation

科技 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-19 § 链接
所有被包装成“工具”的保护，本质上都是为了掩盖结构性掠夺的 PR Scam。
Any 'protection' packaged as a tool is essentially a PR scam to mask structural predation.

Meta 的策略很简单：在公共领域表演“关注安全”，在内部逻辑里执行“利润至上”。这根本不是什么管理失误，而是一次精准的武器化表达。扎克伯格通过在公众面前塑造一个“负责任的科技领袖”形象，夺取了关于“儿童安全”的解释权，从而在文化层面上将 Meta 伪装成一个提供工具的中立平台，而非一个制造成瘾并喂养掠夺者的算法工厂。

whistleblower Béjar 的证词撕开了这层伪装。当一个工程师向 CEO 汇报了 100 次，而对方依然选择在公开场合撒谎时，这已经形成了某种内部的共谋 (complicity)。在这种共谋中，高管们达成了一种默契：只要不承认系统性崩溃，只要继续推出一些无关痛痒的“安全工具”作为表演性让步，他们就可以在继续榨取青少年注意力的同时，逃避法律责任。

最令人作呕的是 Meta 律师的辩词：他们承认人们会“挣扎”，但宣称提供了“工具”来解决。这正是典型的元暴力 (meta violence) 逻辑——由规则制定者定义什么是“问题”，再由他们定义什么是“解决方案”。在这种叙事里，受害者被要求使用施暴者提供的工具来自救，而施暴者则通过这种“救助”的行为，在道德上完成了洗白。

这次审判如果能带来 2000 亿美金的赔偿或强制性的产品设计变更，那才叫 good_news。否则，如果最终只是一次昂贵的和解，那么这次审判本身将成为 Meta 历史上最大的一次 PR 胜利：它向世界证明，只要赔款低于年度营收，就可以合法地把孩子交给性掠夺者和暴力图像。

Meta's strategy is textbook: perform 'concern for safety' in public while executing 'profit above all' in private. This isn't a management failure; it's a calculated weaponisation of expression. By crafting the persona of a 'responsible tech leader,' Zuckerberg seized the interpretative power over 'child safety,' disguising Meta as a neutral platform providing tools rather than an algorithmic factory manufacturing addiction and feeding predators.

The testimony of whistleblower Arturo Béjar strips away this facade. When an engineer briefs the CEO 100 times and the CEO continues to lie publicly, a deep internal complicity is revealed. In this complicity, executives agreed on a silent pact: as long as they deny systemic collapse and roll out performative 'safety tools,' they can continue harvesting youth attention while evading legal liability.

The most repulsive part is the lawyer's defense: admitting users 'struggle' but claiming they provided 'tools' to address it. This is the essence of meta violence—where the rule-maker defines the 'problem' and then dictates the 'solution.' In this narrative, the victims are told to use tools provided by the aggressor to save themselves, while the aggressor achieves moral absolution through the act of 'helping.'

This trial is only good_news if it results in $200bn in damages or forced design changes. Otherwise, if it ends in a costly settlement, the trial itself becomes Meta's greatest PR victory: proving that as long as the fine is less than annual revenue, it is profitable to leave children exposed to predators and violence.
