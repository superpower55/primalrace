VAR的“解释权”：一场关于定义事实的共谋The 'Interpretation' of VAR: A Complicity of Defining Facts

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-19 § 链接
规则的模糊性是权力运作的入口，VAR在定义“干扰”而非记录事实。
Ambiguity in rules is an entry point for power; VAR defines 'interference' rather than recording facts.

这场比赛最精彩的不是Barry的进球，而是VAR对“干扰” (interference) 的定义权博弈。Brennan Johnson在越位位置上，但门将根本没打算救那个球。在这种情况下，裁判依然判定他“影响了门将的救球能力”并取消进球。这在逻辑上是个 scam：它要求我们相信一个不存在的“潜在可能”——即如果那个越位的人不在，门将就能救球。

这就是典型的 weaponized expression。VAR不再是一个客观的记录工具，而变成了一个制造“事实”的机器。它通过一个模糊的、不可证伪的解释入口（“影响能力”），将一个主观判定包装成技术客观。当大卫·莫耶斯在赛后表现得“放松”时，他其实是在潜意识中完成了一次共谋 (complicity) —— 只要结果对他有利，他愿意接受这套逻辑漏洞百出的解释权，哪怕他自己也对一致性表示怀疑。

足球场上的这种权力结构是元暴力 (meta violence) 的微缩版：制定规则的人通过定义什么是“正确”的解读，让弱势方（本场是伊普斯维奇）在结构层面上被剥夺。当一个判定被宣布为“技术事实”时，任何对公正表达的追求都被简化成了“不尊重裁判”。在这种叙事下，事实不再是发生了什么，而是权力决定了什么发生了。

The most intriguing part of this match wasn't Barry's goal, but the power struggle over the interpretation of 'interference' by VAR. Brennan Johnson was offside, yet the keeper made no attempt to save the ball. Despite this, the referee ruled that he 'impacted the keeper's ability to save,' nullifying the goal. This is a logical scam: it asks us to believe in a non-existent 'potential'—that the keeper would have saved it if the offside player weren't there.

This is a textbook example of weaponized expression. VAR is no longer an objective recording tool; it has become a machine for manufacturing 'facts.' By using a vague, unfalsifiable entry point ('impacting ability'), it packages a subjective judgment as technical objectivity. When David Moyes appeared 'relaxed' post-match, he was unconsciously engaging in complicity—as long as the result favored him, he was willing to accept a flawed logic, even while doubting its consistency.

This power structure on the pitch is a microcosm of meta violence: those who set the rules use the power of interpretation to structurally deprive the disadvantaged party (Ipswich in this case). Once a decision is declared a 'technical fact,' any pursuit of just expressions is reduced to 'disrespecting the official.' In this narrative, truth is no longer about what happened, but about what power decides happened.
