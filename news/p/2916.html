换把尺子不叫突破，这只是资本的膝跳反应Changing the Ruler is Not a Breakthrough: Just a Capital Knee-Jerk

中国科技 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-28 § 链接
用定义权替代能力突破的叙事，是典型的科技元暴力 scam。
Replacing actual capability with narrative definition is a classic tech meta-violence scam.

看到中国开始量产所谓的“国产 DUV”就引发全球芯片股大跌，这场面极其荒诞。资本市场所谓的“惊恐”，本质上是对一个惯于“重新定义现实”的系统的条件反射。在华为 τ-scaling 逻辑里，如果做不到 Moore's Law 的物理突破，那就宣布一个新定律。这种“现实不配合就换尺子”的机制，在芯片领域被包装成了突破，在金融市场则被误读成了威胁。

我们需要区分两种定律：一种是观察出来的物理定律，一种是声明出来的叙事定律。真正的 DUV 突破应该是 Actual 状态向 Potential 状态的逼近，而目前的叙事更像是一种武器化的 PR，旨在夺取认知入口，制造一种“已经追平”的假象。CXMT 在 A 股首日暴涨 400%，这种典型的共谋场域证明了：在缺乏真实物理指标校验的闭环里，只要叙事足够强，估值就可以脱离地心引力。

分析师说这是“膝跳反应”，其实说对了。这不过是一场关于定价权的博弈。全球芯片巨头担心的是能力被超越吗？不，他们担心的是一套不按物理规则出牌的叙事逻辑开始接管市场。当“国产替代”从技术问题变成一种政治性表达，它就成了一种武器。在这种武器化叙事中，真正的技术进展被掩盖在巨大的噪声之下，而共谋者们则在涨跌幅中完成了一次次财富的转移。

The global chip stock slump following reports of China's mass-produced "homegrown DUV" is absurd. The market's "panic" is actually a conditioned reflex to a system that habitually "redefines reality." In the logic of Huawei's τ-scaling, if physical breakthroughs of Moore's Law are unattainable, a new law is simply declared. This mechanism of "changing the ruler when reality doesn't cooperate" is packaged as a breakthrough in chips and misread as a threat by financial markets.

We must distinguish between observed physical laws and declared narrative laws. A true DUV breakthrough should be the Actual moving toward the Potential. Instead, the current narrative functions as weaponized PR, seizing the cognitive entry point to manufacture an illusion of parity. CXMT's 400% surge on the Shanghai exchange proves that in a complicity field lacking real physical verification, valuations can defy gravity as long as the narrative is strong enough.

Analysts call this a "knee-jerk reaction," and they are right. This is a game of pricing power. Are chip giants afraid of being surpassed in capability? No, they fear a narrative logic that ignores physical rules. When "domestic substitution" shifts from a technical issue to a political expression, it becomes a weapon. In this weaponized narrative, real progress is drowned out by noise, while the complicit players complete their wealth transfers through market volatility.
