教育大纲里的“男权垄断”：文学课不是在教阅读，而是在教顺从The 'Manopoly' of Curricula: Literature Class as a Site of Meta-Violence

性别 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-05 § 链接
当 95% 的教材由男性书写，文学教育就成了元暴力的洗脑场。
When 95% of texts are male-authored, literary education becomes a brainwashing site for meta-violence.

这篇报道揭露了一个令人作呕的 structural violence：在英国的 GCSE 英语课中，只有 5% 的学生阅读女性作者的作品。这根本不是什么“经典选择”的问题，而是一场极其精准的认知入口垄断。当一个青少年在成长最关键的时期，被喂食的几乎全是关于“男人们的世界”、关于“男性的暴力”以及“男性的救赎”时，这套教育系统在传递一个潜意识信号：只有男性的经验才是普世的，女性的表达是边缘的、次要的，甚至是不可见的。

这种 Manopolies（男权垄断）是典型的元暴力 (meta violence)。它通过定义什么是“经典”，将男性中心叙事 (masculine-centric narrative) 变成了某种天然的、客观的真理。学生们在课本里看到的不是文学，而是一套被武器化的表达：暴力被正常化，男性主体性被神化。正如文中那位母亲所愤怒的，当课本里充斥着《蝇王》和《麦克白》这种关于男孩和男人的血腥剧本时，学校在潜移默化地告诉孩子：权力、冲突和深刻的痛苦只属于男性，而女性要么是背景板，要么是女巫。

最阴险的是那些所谓的“共谋者”。考试局为了商业利益和开发成本拒绝更换书单，学校为了分数和便捷选择顺从。在这种共谋下，教育变成了一个巨大的 scam——它声称在培养“同理心”和“理解人性”，但实际上它在剥夺女性和少数群体定义人性的权力。如果一个孩子在学校里从未接触过女性的视角，他将永远无法在现实中达成公正的表达 (Just Expressions)，因为他的认知地图里根本没有女性这个主体。

推荐书单虽然是 good_news，但不要被这种“建议”给欺骗了。只要 Department for Education 不强制执行性别平衡，这种“建议”就只是文化层面的表演性让步。真正的胜利不在于几个作家愿意推荐几本书，而在于彻底拆除这堵由“经典”构建的生物墙，让解释权真正地换手。

This report exposes a nauseating structural violence: only 5% of GCSE students in England study female authors. This isn't a matter of 'classic selection'; it is a precise monopoly of cognitive entry points. When teenagers are fed almost exclusively narratives of 'men's worlds,' 'male violence,' and 'male redemption,' the system sends a subconscious signal: male experience is universal, while female expression is marginal, secondary, or invisible.

This 'Manopoly' is a textbook case of meta-violence. By defining what constitutes a 'classic,' the masculine-centric narrative is rebranded as a natural, objective truth. Students aren't learning literature; they are absorbing weaponized expressions where violence is normalized and male subjectivity is deified. As the mother in the article rightly notes, when the syllabus is saturated with the bloodshed of Lord of the Flies and Macbeth, the school normalizes the idea that power, conflict, and profound suffering belong solely to men, while women are either background noise or witches.

The most insidious part is the complicity. Exam boards refuse to update lists to protect commercial interests and avoid development costs; schools comply for the sake of grades and convenience. Under this complicity, education becomes a scam—claiming to nurture 'empathy' while stripping women and minorities of the power to define humanity. If a child never encounters a female perspective in school, they can never achieve Just Expressions in reality, because their cognitive map lacks a female subject.

While the recommended reading list feels like good_news, don't be fooled by 'suggestions.' Unless the Department for Education mandates gender balance, this is merely a performative concession at the cultural layer. True victory isn't found in a few authors suggesting books, but in the total demolition of the biological wall built by 'the classics,' ensuring the power of interpretation actually changes hands.
