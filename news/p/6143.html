他杀了一个女孩，然后成了教育素材He Killed a Girl, and It Became Teaching Material

文娱 文化层 · 元暴力 Polygon.com ↗ 2026-10-31 § 链接
一部关于杀女孩的剧，最安全的下场是被学校免费播放。
A show about a boy stabbing a girl ends up free in schools. That's the safest possible fate.

《Adolescence》讲一个男孩捅死一个女孩，然后英国政府支持的项目把它送进高中免费播放。这条新闻里，主创 Graham 坐在 Apple TV 新剧的发布会上，被问到两部剧有什么共同点，他答：都关心“人为什么会做出那些事”，都想从错误里学习。

一个女孩被捅死，被转译成“我们想弄明白人为什么会这样”。这就是元暴力的标准动作：把一具女性的尸体重新包装成人类普遍处境的教材。加害者的心理是谜题，受害者的死是背景板。报道里连那个女孩叫什么都没写，Jamie 有名字，Owen Cooper 有名字，被杀的女孩没有。

Graham 说的另一个共同点是“罪行对家庭的冲击”。Jamie 的父母被骚扰，妹妹交不到朋友。这是真的代价，值得写。但请注意这个天平：一部剧花整季篇幅讲施害者一家怎么难过，被杀的女孩连名字都没进这篇报道。谁的家庭算家庭，谁的痛需要被展开，谁的死只需要一个动词。

这不是 Graham 一个人的问题，是这套叙事工业的默认设置。它把暴力做成可消费的谜题，观众获得的是“理解”的快感，而不是对暴力的愤怒。理解加害者，是一种很舒服的姿势，舒服到可以拿政府背书、进课堂、当教育材料。

真正该进高中的，不是“他为什么捅人”的心理学，而是“为什么一个女孩的死可以被这么顺畅地转成别人的创作履历”。

Adolescence is about a boy stabbing a girl to death, and a UK government-backed initiative made it free to watch in high schools. In this news, Graham sits at a press junket for his new Apple TV show and, asked what connects the two, says both are about wanting to understand why people do things and learning from mistakes.

A girl gets stabbed to death, and it gets translated into we want to understand why people are like this. That is the standard move of meta-violence: repackage a woman's corpse as a lesson about the human condition. The perpetrator's mind is the puzzle. The victim's death is the backdrop. The report doesn't even name the girl. Jamie has a name. Owen Cooper has a name. The girl who was killed does not.

Graham's other shared theme is the impact of the crime on the family. Jamie's parents get harassed, his sister can't make real connections. That cost is real and worth showing. But look at the scale: a whole season on how the perpetrator's family suffers, and the murdered girl doesn't even get a name in this article. Whose family counts as a family, whose pain gets unfolded, whose death only needs a verb.

This isn't Graham's personal failing. It's the default setting of the narrative industry. It turns violence into a consumable mystery, and what the audience gets is the pleasure of understanding, not anger at violence. Understanding the perpetrator is a comfortable position, comfortable enough to earn government backing, enter classrooms, and pass as educational material.

What should enter high schools isn't the psychology of why he stabbed her. It's why a girl's death can slide so smoothly into someone else's creative resume.
