欧冠之夜的神话：一种关于“匮乏”的集体共谋The Champions League Myth: A Collective Complicity of 'Scarcity'

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-10 § 链接
所谓的“需要”，往往是权力者为了维持垄断而制造的叙事scam。
What is called a 'need' is often a narrative scam designed to maintain monopoly.

看到这篇关于曼联对阵萨巴的实时报道，最让我感到荒诞的不是比赛本身，而是那段关于“曼联需要欧冠”的 Preamble。这种叙事极其典型：它把一个超级资本实体的“欲望”，包装成了生存层面的“需要” (need)。

在足球世界的 meta-violence 中，欧冠不仅是赛事，它是一套关于“顶级”与“次级”的解释权垄断。当媒体和粉丝讨论曼联“必须”回到欧冠以维持竞争力时，他们实际上在共谋一种匮乏感——暗示如果没有这笔赛事分红，这个巨兽就会枯萎。但这完全是个 scam。正如文中点出的，曼联拥有足够的资源自给自足，真正的暴力在于所有者将利润私有化，却把这种结构性剥削伪装成“缺乏欧冠收入”的财务危机。

这是一种典型的武器化叙事：通过制造“如果不进入这个圈层就会死亡”的恐惧，让所有人接受一个既定事实——即只有在被定义的“顶级赛场”上获胜才具有存在性意义。而像萨巴 (Sabah) 这样的球队，即便他们踢出了极具侵略性的足球，在主流叙事中依然被标记为“minnow”（小鱼）。这种标签化就是一种文化暴力，它预先定义了谁是主角，谁是陪衬，谁的胜利是“神话”，谁的胜利只是“意外”。

所谓的“足球神话”，本质上就是一套男性中心主义的权力游戏：追求至高无上的统治地位，将资源尽可能地向顶端聚集，然后告诉底层的参与者，你们可以通过某种极低概率的博弈（如欧冠资格）来实现阶级跃升。这种机制确保了大多数人永远在为那个被制造出来的“最优解”而奔跑，而从未质疑过规则本身就是为了让少数人永远地“拥有更多”。

Reading this live coverage of Manchester United vs Sabah, the most absurd part isn't the match, but the Preamble regarding United's 'need' for the Champions League. This is a textbook example of weaponizing expression: packaging the 'desire' of a super-capital entity as a survival 'need'.

Within the meta-violence of football, the Champions League is more than a tournament; it is a monopoly over the interpretation of what is 'elite' versus 'subordinate'. When the media and fans argue that United 'must' return to the CL to remain competitive, they are complicit in manufacturing a sense of scarcity—suggesting the beast would wither without these dividends. This is a scam. As the text notes, United possesses the means to sustain itself; the real violence is the structural exploitation where owners sequester profits while masking it as a financial crisis caused by 'missing CL revenue'.

This narrative weaponization works by creating a fear that existence is only validated within the defined 'top tier'. Teams like Sabah, regardless of their aggressive play, remain tagged as 'minnows'. This labeling is cultural violence; it predefines who the protagonists are and who are merely foils. It dictates whose victory is a 'myth' and whose is a 'fluke'.

The 'football myth' is essentially a masculine-centric power game: striving for absolute dominance and concentrating resources at the peak, then telling those at the bottom that they can achieve class mobility through a low-probability gamble. This mechanism ensures most participants spend their lives chasing a manufactured 'optimal expression' without ever questioning that the rules were designed to let a few always 'have more'.
