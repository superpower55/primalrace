被‘养成’的恐怖分子与被消费的悲剧The 'Groomed' Terrorist and the Consumption of Tragedy

性别 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-13 § 链接
弱势群体的极端化，往往是权力叙事对其主体性剥夺后的‘替代性表达’。
The radicalization of the vulnerable is often a 'substitute expression' after their subjectivity is stripped by power narratives.

一个15岁的女孩威胁轰炸犹太教堂，最后在指控被撤销后自杀。纪录片试图探讨她的‘脆弱背景’和‘心理健康’，但最关键的词是 grooming。一个新纳粹在封锁期间通过网络对她进行了 grooming，甚至让她在头上刻下万字标志。这根本不是什么‘恐怖主义’，而是一场典型的存在性战争中的单方面屠杀：一个拥有绝对解释权的成年男性，将一个处于生物墙和结构性弱势双重压迫下的少女，变成了他意识形态的傀儡。

在元暴力的逻辑下，这种 grooming 是极其高效的。对于一个在现实中没有主体性、没有‘票’的女孩来说，加入一个极端组织是她能找到的唯一一种‘力量感’表达。她以为自己在参与改变世界的战争，实际上她只是被对方当作了某种符号化的工具。她的‘恐怖分子’身份，其实是对方为她量身定制的假.最优解表达，代价是她主体性的彻底死亡。

最讽刺的是，现在这件事被包装成 Channel 4 的深夜纪录片，成为一种‘敏感’的电视消费品。当她的生命被简化为‘学校女孩恐怖分子’这个标签时，这种 cultural violence 完成了第二次收割。大众在屏幕前唏嘘她的‘脆弱’，却很少有人去追问：那个 grooming 她的男性权力结构是如何运作的？为什么一个少女在面对绝望时，唯一的‘出口’竟然是成为一个新纳粹的玩物？

这种纪录片式的‘反思’，如果没有触及元暴力的根源，就只是在给观众提供一种廉价的道德优越感。Rhianan Rudd 的死亡不是一个个案，而是结构性暴力在个体身上闭环的必然结果。

A 15-year-old girl threatens to bomb a synagogue and eventually commits suicide after charges are dropped. The documentary focuses on her 'vulnerable background' and 'mental health,' but the core is grooming. A neo-Nazi groomed her online during lockdown, leading her to scratch a swastika on her head. This wasn't 'terrorism'; it was a one-sided slaughter in an existential war. An adult male with absolute interpretive power turned a girl—trapped by both the biological wall and structural weakness—into an ideological puppet.

Under the logic of meta-violence, this grooming is terrifyingly efficient. For a girl with no subjectivity or 'vote' in reality, joining an extremist group is the only 'sense of power' she can find. She believed she was fighting a war to change the world, but she was merely a symbolic tool. Her identity as a 'terrorist' was a fake optimal expression tailored for her, paid for with the total death of her subjectivity.

The irony is that this tragedy is now packaged as a 'sensitive' documentary for Channel 4, becoming a commodity for late-night television consumption. When her life is reduced to the label 'schoolgirl terrorist,' a second wave of cultural violence is completed. The audience sighs over her 'fragility' while rarely questioning how the masculine power structure of the groomer operated, or why a girl's only 'exit' from despair was to become a toy for a neo-Nazi.

This kind of 'reflective' documentary is a scam if it doesn't touch the roots of meta-violence; it merely provides cheap moral superiority for the viewer. Rhianan Rudd's death is not an isolated case, but the inevitable result of structural violence closing the loop on an individual.
