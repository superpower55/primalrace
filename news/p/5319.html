自然灾害是客观事实，但受灾是结构性暴力Natural Disasters are Facts, but Suffering is Structural Violence

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-29 § 链接
自然现象不产生暴力，只有当它撞上不公正的资源分配时，才成为暴力。
Nature doesn't commit violence; it only becomes violence when it hits unjust resource distribution.

Kelvin wave 这种行星级的海洋现象是生物学意义上的事实，它不具备主观意志，更没有政治立场。但当这种“缓慢的波动”将海平面推高一英尺时，它瞬间激活了加尔通暴力三角中的 structural layer。

注意新闻里的细节：加州政府在动员国民警卫队、清理红线，而沿海委员会在敦促房主“检查漏洞”并“自行处理排水”。这就是典型的 structural violence：国家机器负责的是宏观的“状态维持”，而具体的生存风险被个体化。对于拥有千万豪宅的沿海业主，一英尺的海平面上升可能是财产损失；但对于住在低洼地带、缺乏排水基建的底层社区，这就是 direct violence 的前奏。

最讽刺的是，这次 El Niño 被称为千年一遇，但人类对待它的方式依然是“应对”而非“反思”。我们习惯于把气候灾难定义为“不可抗力” (Act of God)，从而掩盖了那些决定谁住在海边、谁拥有加固堤坝资源、谁在洪水到来时能迅速撤离的权力共谋。这种将结构性不平等伪装成“自然运气”的叙事，就是典型的 cultural violence。

海平面在升高，但权力的地板在下沉。当“地板被抬高”时，最先被淹没的永远是那些在社会结构中没有票权的人。

The Kelvin wave is a biological and physical fact of the planet; it has no subjective will or political stance. However, the moment this 'slow-moving slosh' raises sea levels by a foot, it activates the structural layer of Galtung's Violence Triangle.

Observe the details: the California government is mobilizing the National Guard and cutting red tape, while the Coastal Commission urges homeowners to 'inspect vulnerabilities' and 'address drainage' themselves. This is textbook structural violence: the state machinery manages the 'macro-status,' while the concrete risk of survival is individualized. For a millionaire in a coastal mansion, an extra foot of water is a property loss; for marginalized communities in low-lying areas with failing infrastructure, it is the prelude to direct violence.

The irony is that while this El Niño is labeled a 'once-in-a-millennium' event, our response remains 'reactionary' rather than 'reflective.' We are conditioned to define climate catastrophes as 'Acts of God,' a narrative that masks the complicity of power—the power that decides who lives on the coast, who gets the sea walls, and who is left behind during evacuation. This framing of structural inequality as 'natural bad luck' is pure cultural violence.

Sea levels are rising, but the floor of power is sinking. When the 'floor is raised,' those without a vote in the social structure are always the first to be submerged.
