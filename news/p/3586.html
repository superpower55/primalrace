印度考公风暴：被制度性掠夺的生存票据India's Exam Storm: Survival Tickets Plundered by Structure

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 直接层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-10 § 链接
当竞争被操纵，考试不再是阶级跃迁的门票，而是结构暴力的筛选器。
When competition is rigged, exams cease to be ladders for mobility and become filters for structural violence.

在印度最贫穷的邦之一，数以十万计的年轻人为了几千个政府岗位在街头与警棍搏斗。这在外界看来是单纯的“就业压力”或“考场舞弊”，但用加尔通的暴力三角来看，这是一场典型的 structural violence。政府职位的稀缺性与极高的福利保障，使得这些考试变成了年轻人唯一能抓住的、具有实际价值的生存票据。

当试题泄露、权力寻租成为潜规则，这种暴力就从制度层（structural）升级到了文化层（cultural）。系统通过制造一种“公平竞争”的假象，诱导数以百万计的年轻人投入数年生命进行内卷，而最终的定价权却掌握在能够买通考官的权力共谋者手中。这种 scam 的本质是：系统在剥夺年轻人的 Potential（本可达到的社会状态），而 Actual（现实状态）被强行锁定在失业与绝望中。差额，就是警察挥下的那根警棍。

有趣的是，这次抗争起源于一个名为“蟑螂”的 online joke。这种从解构主义开始的表达，最终演变成了对存在性战争的正面反击。年轻人意识到，在被操纵的博弈中，扮演一个“勤奋的考生”是假.最优解，因为无论怎么努力，结果早已被共谋者写好。唯一能提高自己这枚“票”价值的方式，就是通过物理性的聚集，强行撕开制度的黑盒，要求 CBI 这种独立力量介入。

不要被那些“政府已接受大部分诉求”的 PR 叙事欺骗。只要这种由权力垄断的资源分配机制不被根本性重构，每一次的让步都只是为了安抚情绪的表演性让步。真正的胜利不是一个教育部长的辞职，而是让生存机会不再成为权力的赏赐。

In one of India's poorest states, hundreds of thousands of youth are clashing with batons over a few thousand government posts. To an outsider, this looks like simple 'employment pressure' or 'cheating scandals,' but through the lens of the Violence Triangle, it is a textbook case of structural violence. The scarcity of government jobs combined with high benefits makes these exams the only survival tickets with actual value for the youth.

When paper leaks and rent-seeking become the norm, violence escalates from the structural layer to the cultural layer. The system manufactures an illusion of 'fair competition,' inducing millions to spend years in a grind, while the actual pricing power is held by the complicity of power-brokers who can buy the examiners. The essence of this scam is the systematic theft of the youth's Potential, while their Actual state is locked into unemployment and despair. The difference is the baton swung by the police.

Interestingly, this unrest began as an online joke called the 'Cockroach' movement. This expression, starting from deconstruction, evolved into a direct counter-attack in an existential war. The youth realized that playing the role of the 'diligent student' was a fake optimal expression because the outcome was already scripted by the conspirators. The only way to increase the value of their 'vote' was through physical assembly to forcibly tear open the systemic black box.

Do not be deceived by PR narratives claiming the government has 'accepted most demands.' As long as the resource allocation mechanism remains monopolized by power, every concession is merely a performative gesture to soothe the crowd. Real victory is not the resignation of a minister, but the end of survival opportunities being treated as gifts from the powerful.
