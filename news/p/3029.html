足球的私有化 scam 与共谋者的集体倒戈The Privatization Scam of Football and the Betrayal of Complicit Players

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-30 § 链接
当顶级掠夺者试图将公共资产私有化时，共谋者的背叛是唯一的制衡。
When top predators attempt to privatize public assets, the only check is the betrayal of their own complicitors.

Gianni Infantino 试图将世界杯的部分所有权出售给美国私募资本，这本质上是一次极其典型的武器化操作：通过重新定义“治理”来掩盖“掠夺”。在 FIFA 的叙事里，这可能被包装成“资本注入”或“现代化升级”，但实际上它是将足球这一全球共有的文化资产，转化为少数资本持有者的 private ownership。这种操作逻辑与那些通过定义“新定律”来掩盖能力缺失的科技公司如出一辙——现实不配合，就重新定义现实。

这场博弈中最有趣的是共谋者的态度。UEFA 的 55 个成员国此前在 Infantino 的连任支持信上签了名，这说明他们原本处于一种典型的共谋状态：在男权中心化的官僚结构中，通过适度顺从来换取资源分配的入场券。但当 Infantino 试图将“入场券”直接卖给美国资本时，UEFA 意识到自己不再是共谋的受益者，而成了被收割的客体。于是，这种“团结”迅速转化为一种存在性战争的防御机制。

值得关注的是，这次抵制行动的首波冲击点被设定在女子世界杯和 U-20 女子世界杯。这极其讽刺：在 FIFA 的权力版图中，女性足球长期被视为“次要的”、“补充的”叙事，是结构性暴力中最容易被牺牲的环节。然而，这次 UEFA 竟然选择用女性赛事作为博弈的先锋。这并非因为 FIFA 突然觉醒了人权意识，而是因为在这次关于“所有权”的残酷博弈中，女性赛事成了成本最低且最能迅速制造舆论压力、撕开 FIFA 伪善面具的突破口。

这是一次 structural 层的权力碰撞。UEFA 用“集体抵制”这一强力表达，试图夺回被 Infantino 垄断的解释权。但我们必须警惕：当这群足球官僚在谈论“足球属于足球”时，他们是在捍卫纯粹的体育精神，还是在争夺谁才是那个有资格给足球“定价”的人？这场胜利如果是结构性的，那么它应该导向治理的透明化；如果只是为了换一个欧洲主导的定价权，那么这不过是共谋者在换班。

Gianni Infantino's attempt to sell chunks of the World Cup to US private equity is a textbook example of weaponized expression: using the guise of 'governance' to mask outright plunder. In FIFA's narrative, this is likely packaged as 'capital injection' or 'modernization,' but in reality, it is the conversion of a global cultural asset into the private ownership of a few capital holders. This logic mirrors tech companies that announce 'new laws' to hide a lack of actual capability—when reality doesn't cooperate, they simply redefine reality.

The most intriguing part of this game is the behavior of the complicitors. The 55 UEFA members had previously signed letters supporting Infantino's re-election, indicating a classic state of complicity: securing a seat at the table of resource distribution through moderate submission within a masculine-centric bureaucratic structure. However, when Infantino tried to sell those very 'seats' to US capital, UEFA realized they were no longer the beneficiaries of the complicity, but the objects being harvested. Consequently, their 'unity' rapidly shifted into a defensive mechanism of existential war.

It is particularly ironic that the first wave of the boycott targets the Women's World Cup and the U-20 Women's World Cup. In FIFA's power map, women's football has long been treated as a 'secondary' or 'supplementary' narrative—the most expendable link in the chain of structural violence. Yet, UEFA has chosen women's tournaments as the vanguard of this struggle. This isn't because FIFA suddenly embraced human rights, but because in this brutal game of 'ownership,' women's events are the lowest-cost entry point to create public pressure and strip away FIFA's hypocrisy.

This is a collision of power at the structural layer. UEFA is using the 'collective boycott' as a powerful expression to seize back the right of interpretation monopolized by Infantino. But we must remain skeptical: when these football bureaucrats claim 'football belongs to football,' are they defending the spirit of sport, or are they simply fighting for the right to be the ones who set the price? If this victory is structural, it should lead to transparent governance; if it is merely about swapping a US-led pricing power for a European one, it is nothing more than a change of shift among complicitors.
