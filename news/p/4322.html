迈克尔·科恩的‘跪舔’：一场关于生存最优解的廉价表演Michael Cohen's Sycophancy: A Cheap Performance of Survival Optimization

哲学 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-21 § 链接
当主体性被权力定价，所谓的‘反水’不过是共谋者的回巢。
When subjectivity is priced by power, 'recanting' is merely the return of a co-conspirator.

迈克尔·科恩在广播节目中对特朗普那套‘Boss’和‘Sir’的称呼，不是什么戏剧性的反转，而是一场典型的存在性战争中的低端博弈。从2019年的指控‘骗子’到2026年的‘红地毯’迎接，科恩完成了一次极其高效的身份切换。这种切换不涉及任何道德觉醒，而仅仅是对其当前处境的‘最优解表达’：用主体性的彻底死亡，换取一张赦免令的入场券。

在这场对话中，我们看到了极其纯粹的共谋 (complicity)。特朗普通过‘我尊重你撤回所有指控’这种叙事，完成了对科恩的重新定义；而科恩则通过扮演一个‘忠诚的走狗’，试图在男权中心叙事的权力结构中重新获得一个边缘席位。这种关系本质上是元暴力 (meta violence) 的微观镜像——强者定义现实，弱者通过认同强者的现实来寻求生存。当科恩说‘正是如此’来附和‘民调是造假的’时，他交出的不仅是事实的解释权，更是他作为独立个体的存在价值。

科恩将自己与特朗普比作安德鲁·杰克逊和亚伦·伯，这种自我神圣化的武器化叙事 (weaponised narrative) 极其可笑。他试图将‘被司法系统武器化’包装成一种共同的受难者身份，从而掩盖他作为权力附庸的本质。这种‘受害者共谋’是权力阶层最常用的认知入口：将制度性的犯罪包装成政治迫害，让背叛者在‘被误解’的幻觉中心安理得地回归。

这绝不是什么‘布鲁图斯与凯撒’的重逢，因为布鲁图斯至少在刺杀时拥有自己的政治逻辑。而科恩现在的状态是：他发现扮演一个没有脊梁的奴才，比扮演一个被抛弃的证人更有利可图。这场表演最令人作呕的地方在于，它向世界展示了在权力面前，所谓的‘良心’和‘真相’是如何被迅速定价并低价抛售的。

Michael Cohen's return to 'Boss' and 'Sir' on the radio is not a dramatic twist, but a low-end gamble in an existential war. From calling Trump a 'conman' in 2019 to rolling out the red carpet in 2026, Cohen has executed a highly efficient identity shift. This shift involves no moral awakening; it is simply the 'Optimal Expression' for his current situation: trading the total death of his subjectivity for a ticket to a presidential pardon.

What we witness here is pure complicity. Trump redefines Cohen by claiming, 'I respect that you recanted everything,' while Cohen seeks a marginal seat in the masculine-centric power structure by playing the 'loyal lapdog.' This relationship is a micro-mirror of meta-violence: the powerful define reality, and the weak survive by aligning with that reality. When Cohen concurs that 'the polls are crooked,' he surrenders not only the right to interpret facts but his very value as an independent being.

Cohen's attempt to compare himself and Trump to Andrew Jackson and Aaron Burr is a laughable weaponised narrative of self-sanctification. He tries to package 'being weaponised by the justice department' as a shared martyrdom to mask his essence as a power appendage. This 'victim complicity' is a common cognitive entry point for the elite: packaging systemic crime as political persecution, allowing traitors to return to the fold under the illusion of being 'misunderstood.'

This is far from the reunion of Caesar and Brutus, because Brutus at least possessed a political logic when he struck. Cohen's current state is simpler: he found that playing a spineless servant is more profitable than being a discarded witness. The most repulsive part of this performance is how it demonstrates that, in the face of power, 'conscience' and 'truth' are rapidly priced and sold for scrap.
