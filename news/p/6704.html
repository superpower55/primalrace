他造谣，他收钱，他管平台He Lies, He Pays, He Owns the Platform

国际 文化层 · 元暴力 Mother Jones ↗ 2026-11-02 § 链接
新闻摘要美国中期选举前数周，X 上流传一则谣言，称得州民主党参议员候选人 James Talarico 因故缺席竞选活动，实为被私人飞机送往俄亥俄州一家精神治疗中心。X 的所有者马斯克转发了这条消息，并称其竞选团队「隐瞒了大事」。该中心随后声明 Talarico 从未在此接受治疗，Talarico 本人回应称从未去过俄亥俄州。据《纽约时报》报道，马斯克已向共和党投入 2.76 亿美元，成为本轮中期选举最大金主。
The newsWeeks before the US midterms, a rumor spread on X that Texas Democratic Senate candidate James Talarico had secretly been flown to an Ohio mental health center. X owner Elon Musk amplified it, saying the campaign was hiding something big. The center said Talarico never received treatment there, and Talarico said he has never been to Ohio. The New York Times reports Musk has put $276 million into Republican races, making him the midterms' biggest donor.
当一个人同时拥有平台、谣言和候选人支票，事实只是他随手可以下架的东西。
When one man owns the platform, the rumor, and the candidates' checks, facts are just something he can quietly take down.

马斯克转发了那条「Talarico 被送进俄亥俄精神病院」的假消息，配文说竞选团队「隐瞒了大事」。几小时后，Lindner Center of Hope 发声明说 Talarico 从未在该机构接受治疗，Talarico 本人说他从没去过俄亥俄。没有人道歉。马斯克转去发 Talarico 批评资本主义的片段，说这「令人不安」。

这就是武器化的标准操作：认知入口不需要赢，只需要在事实抵达之前先把怀疑种下去。等医院辟谣、当事人否认，注意力已经花掉了，辟谣只覆盖到原本就在看的人。第三章讲的就是这个——夺取「什么是事实」的制造权，比事实本身更值钱。

但这条新闻真正该被拆开的不是谣言，是谣言和钱的关系。《纽约时报》报道马斯克向共和党投入 2.76 亿美元，是这轮中期选举最大的金主。SpaceX 握着国防部的联邦合同，民主党若拿下国会，这些合同可能被查；众议院共和党人已多次挡下针对他在 DOGE 任职期间行为的传票。一个民主党多数，意味着传票能发出去。

所以他造谣不是立场表达，是投资保护。他花钱买选举结果，同时拥有一个没有护栏的平台来影响那个结果——2024 年的论文记录了 X 上仇恨言论上升、虚假账号活动「可能增加」，而信任与安全团队早被拆掉。连冒充这家媒体（Mother Jones）的假视频，也是律师找上门才删的。

这里没有共谋者理论的复杂版本：马斯克是共谋链条上最显眼的节点，但给他让路的是一整套已经放弃执行的规则，以及那些明知是假、仍然转发、因为转发对自己有利的政客和网红。Sortor 给 Talarico 设的那个「下午 1 点前自己交代」的期限，就是一场表演——他们要的从来不是回应，是那个倒计时本身制造的悬念。

Talarico 的辟谣之所以有效，是因为医院愿意开口，而且说得足够干脆。下一个被造谣的人未必有这种运气。差额就在这里：一个人可以花 2.76 亿买选举，再用自己拥有的平台免费为对手制造丑闻，而代价由所有还在试图分辨真假的人承担。

Musk amplified the claim that Talarico had been secretly flown to an Ohio mental hospital, saying the campaign was hiding something big. Hours later the Lindner Center of Hope said Talarico never received treatment there, and Talarico said he has never been to Ohio. Nobody apologized. Musk moved on to clips of Talarico criticizing capitalism, which he called troubling.

This is weaponization in its standard form: the cognitive entry point doesn't need to win, it only needs to plant doubt before the facts arrive. By the time the hospital denies it, the attention has already been spent, and the correction reaches only the people who were already watching. Chapter three is exactly this — seizing the power to manufacture what counts as fact is worth more than the fact itself.

But the real story isn't the rumor. It's the rumor's relationship to money. The New York Times reports Musk has put $276 million into Republican races, making him the midterms' biggest donor. SpaceX holds federal contracts with the Defense Department that could be probed if Democrats take Congress; House Republicans have repeatedly blocked subpoenas into his conduct at DOGE. A Democratic majority means those subpoenas can move.

So the lying isn't expression of opinion. It's investment protection. He buys election outcomes while owning a platform with no guardrails left to shape those outcomes — a 2024 paper found rising hate speech and a potential increase in inauthentic activity on X, whose trust and safety teams were gutted. Even fake videos impersonating this outlet were only removed after our lawyer called.

There's no complicated version of the co-conspirator theory here: Musk is the most visible node in the chain, but what clears his path is an entire set of rules that have stopped being enforced, plus the politicians and influencers who retweet knowing it's fake because retweeting pays. Sortor's 1 PM deadline for Talarico to come clean was pure theater — they never wanted a response, they wanted the suspense the countdown created.

Talarico's correction worked because the hospital was willing to speak, and spoke plainly. The next person targeted may not be so lucky. That's the gap: one man can spend $276 million on elections and then manufacture scandals against his opponents for free on a platform he owns, while the cost lands on everyone still trying to tell what's true.
