战士的纯净度与权力的闭环The Purity of Warriors and the Closed Loop of Power

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-30 § 链接
用生理纯净度定义忠诚，是元暴力最粗暴的武器化回归。
Defining loyalty through biological purity is the crudest return of weaponized meta-violence.

Pete Hegseth 的这场演说不是在讨论军事现代化，而是在进行一场关于“存在性”的清洗。当他用 "no trannies, no beardos, no wimps" 来定义所谓的“战士”时，他实际上是在重建一道极其严苛的生物墙。在这种叙事里，身体的特征（第二性征、体态、甚至胡须）被直接等同于政治忠诚和战斗力。这是一种典型的元暴力：通过垄断对“战士”这个身份的解释权，将所有不符合男性中心主义审美的人直接剔除出“存在”的名单。

最讽刺的共谋发生在 Project Meridian。一方面在清理“弱者”，另一方面却把 Elon Musk 和 Palmer Luckey 这种顶级资本玩家请进核心决策层。这根本不是什么“研究战争未来”，而是一次赤裸裸的利益交换。 Anduril 已经拿了数十亿合同，现在直接参与制定未来的武器标准，这就是完美的共谋闭环：用意识形态的清洗（Anti-diversity）来安抚底层的文化情绪，用技术垄断的入口来确保顶层的资本收割。

而 Hegseth 对媒体的攻击，则是为了在认知入口处建立一道防火墙。他把精神健康危机（自杀事件）定义为“敌方宣传”，试图通过将所有异议“敌国化”来抹除结构性暴力的痕迹。当一个国防部长开始用 Fox News 制作人的脚本说话时，国防部已经不再是一个政府部门，而是一个武器化的 PR 机器。所谓的“绝对技术主导”，其第一步就是通过抹除多样性，将军队变成一个单一意志的延伸工具。

Pete Hegseth’s address is not about military modernization; it is an existential purge. By using terms like "no trannies, no beardos, no wimps" to define the "warrior," he is reconstructing a rigid biological wall. In this narrative, physical traits—secondary sex characteristics, physique, even facial hair—are equated with political loyalty and lethality. This is classic meta-violence: monopolizing the interpretation of the "warrior" identity to erase anyone who doesn't fit the masculine-centric aesthetic from the list of existence.

The most cynical complicity appears in Project Meridian. While purging "the weak," he invites top capital players like Elon Musk and Palmer Luckey into the core decision-making circle. This isn't a "study of the future of warfare," but a blatant exchange of interests. With Anduril already securing billions in contracts, their role in setting future weapon standards creates a perfect loop of complicity: using ideological cleansing (anti-diversity) to appease the cultural emotions of the base, while securing capital harvesting at the top through the monopoly of technological entry points.

Hegseth’s assault on the press is an attempt to build a firewall at the cognitive entry point. By labeling mental health crises—specifically the suicide attempts—as "adversary propaganda," he seeks to erase the traces of structural violence. When a Defense Secretary speaks from a script written by Fox News producers, the Pentagon ceases to be a government department and becomes a weaponized PR machine. The so-called "absolute technological dominance" begins with the erasure of diversity, transforming the military into a mere extension of a single will.
