在男本位废墟上重建的“性觉醒”scamThe 'Sexual Awakening' Scam Built on Masculine-Centric Ruins

性别 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-02 § 链接
当女性将主体性建立在男性的欲望确认上，这不叫觉醒，叫共谋。
When a woman builds her identity on the validation of male desire, it is not awakening; it is complicity.

这篇文章试图呈现一种所谓的“成熟女性性觉醒”，但剥开浪漫爱叙事的糖衣，里面全是典型的元暴力残余。Vivian 所谓的觉醒，是从学会使用震动棒开始，到在一个已婚男性的欲望中寻找自我确认。请注意她的逻辑：当 Thomas 发起性邀请时，她感到自己“不再太老”、“依然有吸引力”。

这根本不是什么主体性的回归，而是一场典型的存在性战争中的溃败。Vivian 将自己的价值定价权，再次完整地交给了男性。她的快感建立在“被渴望”的客体地位上，而这种渴望依然被包裹在男性中心叙事的框架里——一个能够提供“照顾”和“关注”的男性，成了她定义自我价值的唯一尺度。

而 Thomas 的叙事则更像是一场关于“男性特权”的自我安慰。他将对前妻的冷暴力（背对而睡、穿着睡衣）美化为“尴尬”，将对 Vivian 的性幻想描述为一种对“照顾他人”特质的吸引。事实上，他只是在寻找一个能让他重新感觉到自己作为“主体”的容器。他所谓的“非性亲密”的扩张，本质上是在生理功能衰退后，通过情感操纵来维持其在关系中的统治地位。

两人在文中表现出的“和谐”，其实是两个共谋者在父权结构的废墟上达成的一种低水平平衡。Vivian 认为性是“连接和确认”，而 Thomas 认为 Vivian 的依附让他“感到舒适”。这种舒适的代价是，Vivian 依然在扮演一个需要通过男性目光来确认自身存在的角色。这种所谓的“好故事”，不过是文化暴力在老年阶段的一次温情伪装。

This piece attempts to portray a so-called 'sexual awakening' of a mature woman, but once you peel away the sugar-coating of romantic narratives, it reveals the stubborn residue of meta-violence. Vivian's 'awakening' begins with a vibrator and culminates in seeking self-confirmation through the desire of a married man. Note her logic: when Thomas initiates sex, she feels 'not too old' and 'still attractive.'

This is not a return to subjectivity; it is a total defeat in the existential war. Vivian has once again handed the pricing power of her own value entirely to a man. Her pleasure is predicated on her status as a desired object, and this desire is still framed within a masculine-centric narrative—where a man who provides 'care' and 'attention' becomes the sole metric for her self-worth.

Thomas's narrative is even more of a self-consolation for male privilege. He beautifies the emotional violence toward his ex-wife (turning his back in bed) as 'embarrassment' and frames his fantasies about Vivian as an attraction to her 'caregiving' nature. In reality, he is simply searching for a vessel that makes him feel like a 'subject' again. His so-called expansion of 'non-sexual intimacy' is essentially an emotional manipulation to maintain dominance as his physiological functions decline.

The 'harmony' presented here is actually a low-level equilibrium reached by two co-conspirators on the ruins of the patriarchal structure. Vivian views sex as 'connection and reassurance,' while Thomas finds comfort in her dependence. The price of this comfort is that Vivian continues to play the role of someone who needs the male gaze to confirm her existence. This 'heartwarming' story is nothing more than a tender disguise for cultural violence in old age.
