披萨上的洋葱薯片：一种精致的阶级筛选Onion Crisps on Pizza: A Refined Class Filter

哲学 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-17 § 链接
审美趣味的定义权，本质上是权力对“正统”的垄断与重新定价。
The power to define taste is essentially the monopoly and repricing of 'orthodoxy' by power.

把洋葱薯片铺在披萨上，这在大多数人看来是某种“黑暗料理”或低级趣味。但当这件事被《卫报》的餐厅评论家、获奖美食作家 Jimi Famurewa 以“探索英式披萨”的名义进行记录时，它瞬间从一种廉价的 DIY 行为变成了某种“创意”和“先锋”。

这就是典型的审美武器化。在食物这个认知入口里，什么样的组合是“美味”，什么样的组合是“离谱”，决定权不在于味蕾，而在于谁拥有定义权。当权力上位者决定将原本被视为底层的、廉价的快餐元素（薯片）引入所谓的“美食探索”时，他们实际上是在进行一次审美的套利：通过将低端元素“艺术化”，来彰显自己能够定义“酷”的能力。

这种叙事逻辑在时尚界同样通用——从“老钱风”到“Clean Girl”，本质上都是在用一种特定的品味来区分异己。普通人吃薯片披萨是穷酸，而评论家记录薯片披萨则是“打破常规”。这种定价权的移交，让底层的生活方式在被剥离了生存压力后，成为了上位者消费的某种“异域情调”。

所谓的“DIY 精神”在这里成了掩体。它掩盖了一个事实：真正拥有定义权的人，可以随时把任何东西定义为“美味”，而那些没有话语权的人，只能在既定的审美鄙视链中，通过模仿这些被定义好的“先锋”来寻求社交圈的晋升。

Putting cheese and onion crisps on a pizza would be seen as a 'culinary disaster' or low-brow taste by most. But when Jimi Famurewa, a Guardian critic and award-winning writer, frames it as 'exploring British-style pizza,' it instantly transforms from a cheap DIY act into 'innovation' and 'avant-garde.'

This is a classic weaponisation of aesthetics. In the cognitive entry point of food, whether a combination is 'delicious' or 'absurd' is decided not by taste buds, but by who holds the power of definition. When the power elite decide to incorporate low-end, cheap fast-food elements (crisps) into 'gastronomic exploration,' they are performing an aesthetic arbitrage: by 'artisticizing' lower-class elements, they demonstrate their ability to define what is 'cool.'

This narrative logic is identical to the fashion world—from 'Old Money' to 'Clean Girl,' it's always about using a specific taste to distinguish the 'other.' For the average person, crisp-pizza is poverty; for the critic, it's 'breaking boundaries.' This shift in pricing power turns the survival-based habits of the lower class into a kind of 'exoticism' for the upper class to consume once the actual struggle is stripped away.

The 'DIY spirit' here serves as a cover. It hides the fact that those with the power of definition can label anything as 'delicious' at will, while those without it can only seek social climbing by mimicking these pre-defined 'avant-gardes' within a fixed aesthetic hierarchy.
