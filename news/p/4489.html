经济围剿与金融核威慑：一场关于“解释权”的零和博弈Economic Siege and Financial Deterrence: A Zero-Sum Game of Interpretation

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-25 § 链接
制裁不是法律，而是用金融霸权强行定义“合法性”的武器化表达。
Sanctions are not law, but a weaponized expression using financial hegemony to forcibly define 'legitimacy'.

美国财政部长 Scott Bessent 将这场经济制裁比作“诺曼底登陆”，这种叙事本身就是一种典型的武器化表达 (Weaponisation of Expression)。他试图通过定义“经济生命线”的切断，来制造一个伊朗必须屈服的“现实”。但在这种宏大叙事背后，真正运行的是一种极其粗暴的元暴力 (Meta Violence)：美国通过垄断美元结算系统的解释权，将自己的单边意志伪装成全球金融秩序的“法律”。

有趣的是，Bessent 在威胁制裁的同时，却在名单中小心地剔除了中国金融机构。这种“谨慎”撕开了其叙事的伪装——所谓“切断所有生命线”只是一个 PR 版本的口号，真实的博弈逻辑是：美国不敢在此时“炸掉全球金融系统”。这证明了在结构层 (Structural layer) 上，中美之间已经形成了一种相互保证毁灭的金融核威慑。中国通过承接伊朗 80% 的石油出口，实际上是在用实际行动对抗美国的认知入口，重新定义什么是“国际法框架”。

这场博弈的本质是两种“最优解表达”的碰撞。美国试图通过极致的经济绞杀 (Economic strangulation) 迫使对方投降，而伊朗和中国则在尝试建立一套脱离美元霸权的平行系统。这种零和博弈最残酷的地方在于，被当作筹码的永远是底层。无论是在霍尔木兹海峡被袭击的油轮，还是被经济封锁摧毁的伊朗民众，他们只是这场关于“谁拥有定义权”的战争中的耗材。当权力者在讨论如何“切断生命线”时，他们根本不在乎那条线连接的是多少个具体的人类生命。

US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent compares this economic sanction to the 'Normandy landings,' a classic example of the Weaponisation of Expression. By attempting to define the severance of 'economic lifelines,' he aims to manufacture a reality where Iran's capitulation is inevitable. However, beneath this grand narrative lies a brutal Meta Violence: the US uses its monopoly over the interpretation of the dollar settlement system to disguise its unilateral will as the 'law' of the global financial order.

Interestingly, Bessent's caution in omitting Chinese financial institutions from the initial list strips away the facade of his narrative. The claim of 'severing every lifeline' is merely a PR version of the truth; the actual logic of the game is that the US dares not 'blow up the global financial system' at this moment. This proves that at the Structural layer, a form of mutual assured financial destruction has emerged between China and the US. By absorbing 80% of Iran's oil exports, China is actively contesting the US's cognitive entry point and redefining what constitutes the 'framework of international law.'

This conflict is a collision of two 'Optimal Expressions.' The US seeks to force surrender through intensified economic strangulation, while Iran and China attempt to build a parallel system independent of dollar hegemony. The most cruel aspect of this zero-sum game is that the underclass is always the pawn. Whether it is the oil tanker hit in the Strait of Hormuz or the Iranian people wrecked by the blockade, they are merely consumables in a war over 'who holds the power of definition.' While the powerful discuss how to 'sever lifelines,' they are utterly indifferent to how many actual human lives those lines connect.
