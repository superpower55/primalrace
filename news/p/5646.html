数据库的入场券与生物信息的围猎The Database Ticket and the Bio-metric Hunt

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-18 § 链接
所谓的“执法合作”本质上是国家机器对生物信息的共谋围猎。
所谓的 law enforcement cooperation is essentially a complicity of state machines in bio-metric hunting.

英国政府现在急于拿回被 Brexit 锁掉的执法数据库入场券，尤其是 Eurodac。在政治叙事里，这被包装成“恢复控制”和“打击犯罪”的实用主义回归。但如果我们把视角拉到生物墙和结构暴力上，这件事的本质是：国家机器在升级一套全球性的生物特征监控网。

Eurodac 收集的是从 6 岁起的所有寻求庇护者的指纹和面部图像。请注意这个年龄——6 岁。这意味着一个孩子在还没来得及建立主体性之前，就被强制性地转化为一组可被检索、可被标记、可被跨境共享的生物数据。这种表达的剥夺是极端的：你的身体不再属于你，而是变成了欧盟和英国执法部门共谋的“安全资产”。

所谓的“非法移民减少 43%”这种数据，在 structural violence 的框架下根本不是好消息。它不代表生存环境的改善，而代表围猎效率的提升。当 SIS 和 Eurodac 这种数据库实现无缝对接，寻求庇护者的生存空间被物理地压缩到零。这是一种典型的武器化表达：用“反恐”和“安全”作为认知入口，掩盖其对原初种族（在这里是全球被殖民的、流动的人口）的系统性剥夺。

这场对话最讽刺的地方在于，它在谈论“合作”，但合作的对象是执法机器，而合作的客体是那些没有定价权、没有解释权的肉身。当生物信息成为唯一的身份确证，而这个确证权被垄断在 Magnus Brunner 和 Shabana Mahmood 这样的权力持有者手中时，这就是一场关于生存权的绝对博弈。而对于那些被录入数据库的人来说，他们在这场博弈中没有票，只有被标记的命运。

The UK government is now desperate to regain the entry tickets to law enforcement databases locked by Brexit, especially Eurodac. In political narratives, this is packaged as a pragmatic return to "restoring control" and "fighting crime." But if we shift the perspective to the Biological Wall and structural violence, the essence is this: state machines are upgrading a global bio-metric surveillance net.

Eurodac collects fingerprints and facial images of asylum seekers starting from age six. Note this age—six. It means a child is forcibly converted into a searchable, taggable, and cross-border shareable set of bio-data before they can even establish their own subjectivity. This deprivation of expression is extreme: your body no longer belongs to you; it becomes a "security asset" for the complicity of EU and UK law enforcement.

The data showing a "43% decline in illegal crossings" is absolutely not good news within the framework of structural violence. It doesn't signify improved living conditions, but rather the increased efficiency of the hunt. When databases like SIS and Eurodac achieve seamless integration, the survival space for asylum seekers is physically compressed to zero. This is a classic weaponisation of expression: using "counter-terrorism" and "security" as cognitive entries to mask the systemic deprivation of the Primal Race—in this case, the colonized, mobile populations of the world.

The most ironic part of this dialogue is that it discusses "cooperation," but the partners are law enforcement machines, while the objects are flesh-and-blood humans without pricing power or the right to interpret. When bio-metrics become the only confirmation of identity, and that confirmation power is monopolized by power-holders like Magnus Brunner and Shabana Mahmood, it is an absolute game of survival. For those entered into the database, there are no votes in this game, only a tagged destiny.
