ICE 执法者的“暴力本能”与制度性共谋The 'Violent Instinct' of ICE and Institutional Complicity

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-10 § 链接
当暴力成为职业筛选标准，制服就成了施暴者的遮羞布。
When violence becomes a hiring criterion, the uniform becomes a shroud for abusers.

一个醉醺醺的 ICE 官员在凌晨三点袭击一名 72 岁老人及其女儿，最终被对方开枪击中。这在 naive 的观察者看来是一个离奇的个案，但在我的框架里，这是典型的 structural violence 具象化为 direct violence 的必然结果。

ICE 这个机构在特朗普第二任期的扩招中，已经把“暴力倾向”变成了某种隐形的最优解表达。当一个组织急于执行大规模驱逐计划时，它需要的不是懂得法律的公务员，而是能够高效执行暴力、对弱势群体缺乏共情能力的“猎犬”。所谓的招聘流程简化和规避审核，本质上就是一种 institutional complicity（制度性共谋）：系统在筛选那些本身就具有攻击性、甚至有暴力前科的人，因为这些人最能快速适配这个机构的 meta violence（元暴力）叙事。

这种暴力在体制内被合法化为“维护秩序”，但在体制外，它迅速退化为最原始的雄性攻击。从缅因州射杀移民到辛辛那提的家暴记录，再到这次在私人草坪上的袭击，逻辑完全一致：这些个体在权力结构中习得了“我可以随意处置客体”的定价权。当他们脱下制服，这种潜意识里的权力傲慢依然在驱动其生物本能。

好在这件事里，那个 72 岁的老人和他的女儿通过物理手段强行缩短了 Potential 与 Actual 之间的暴力差额。这场冲突最讽刺的细节是，最后给施暴者戴上手铐的是一名休假警察。这说明在暴力层级中，即便同为执法体系，当一个“猎犬”失去了体制赋予的临时豁免权，他依然只是个被法律定义为 battery 的罪犯。

An intoxicated ICE official assaults a 72-year-old man and his daughter at 3 AM, only to be shot by the victim. To a naive observer, this is a freak accident; in my framework, it is the inevitable manifestation of structural violence collapsing into direct violence.

Under the mass deportation agenda of Trump's second term, ICE has effectively turned 'violent tendencies' into a hidden optimal expression for recruitment. When an organization prioritizes the rapid execution of force over legal due process, it doesn't seek civil servants—it seeks 'hounds' who lack empathy for the marginalized. The reported skirting of vetting processes is a form of institutional complicity: the system is actively filtering for individuals with aggressive predispositions, as they best fit the agency's meta-violence narrative.

This violence is legitimized within the system as 'maintaining order,' but outside the system, it regresses into primal masculine aggression. From the killing of a Colombian immigrant in Maine to domestic violence in Cincinnati, and now this lawn assault, the logic is identical: these individuals have internalized a pricing power that allows them to treat others as objects. Even without the uniform, this subconscious arrogance continues to drive their biological instincts.

Fortunately, in this instance, the 72-year-old father and his daughter used physical means to forcibly close the gap in the Violence Triangle. The most ironic detail is that the one who finally handcuffed the abuser was an off-duty police officer. It proves that even within the enforcement hierarchy, once a 'hound' loses the temporary immunity granted by the state, he is nothing more than a criminal booked for battery.
