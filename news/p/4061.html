数字乌托邦是最大的一场商业 scamThe Digital Utopia: The Greatest Commercial Scam

科技 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-18 § 链接
技术中立是权力者的掩体，所谓的‘革命’不过是权力中心的扩容。
Technological neutrality is a shield for the powerful; the so-called 'revolution' is merely the expansion of power centers.

这篇长文像是一份关于‘认知入口’被夺取的复盘报告。从 1958 年的 Simulmatics 到今天的 AI 巨头，叙事逻辑从未改变：先用‘赋权’ (empowerment) 和‘民主化’ (democratization) 这种带有理想主义色彩的 weaponized concepts 诱骗大众打开大门，然后迅速将公共空间转化为 data-driven commerce 的私产。

所谓的‘数字革命’根本不是革命，因为革命需要目标和对自由的定义，而这场变革在开始时就对目标保持沉默。它唯一的真实目标就是权力中心的扩容。从 Rand 的极端个人主义到 Silicon Valley 的 messianic 愿景，这套叙事成功地将‘剥夺公共监管’包装成‘追求个体自由’。当人们在讨论‘连接全人类’的宏大叙事时，实际上是在共谋一次历史上最大规模的解释权移交——将定义‘事实’和‘真实’的权力，从民主协商移交给算法和资本。

最讽刺的是，那些早期的警告（如 Mumford 或 Weizenbaum）被当作‘过时的恐惧’而被忽略。事实上，这不是因为警告不准确，而是因为‘技术决定论’本身就是一种元暴力。它告诉我们：机器的演进是不可避免的，你只能适应，不能质疑。在这种叙事下，任何试图建立伦理边界的尝试都被标记为‘阻碍进步’。

现在的结果是：我们进入了一个由‘人工国家’ (artificial state) 统治的时代。在这个时代，表达不再是为了确证身份，而是变成了被算法喂养的反馈回路。我们以为自己在选择，其实我们只是在扮演一个被计算出来的‘最优解’。这场战争在 1996 年那个几乎完全不受监管的法案签署时就已经结束了，我们现在经历的，只是这场胜利后的漫长收割。

This long read is essentially a post-mortem on the seizure of cognitive entry points. From Simulmatics in 1958 to today's AI giants, the logic remains unchanged: lure the public in with weaponized concepts like 'empowerment' and 'democratization,' then swiftly convert the public sphere into the private property of data-driven commerce.

The so-called 'digital revolution' was never a revolution, because revolutions require goals and a definition of freedom. This transformation remained conspicuously silent about its ends from the start. Its only true objective was the expansion of power centers. From Ayn Rand's radical individualism to the messianic visions of Silicon Valley, this narrative successfully rebranded the 'stripping of public oversight' as the 'pursuit of individual liberty.' While we were distracted by the grand narrative of 'connecting all humanity,' we were complicit in the largest transfer of interpretive power in history—shifting the right to define 'fact' and 'truth' from democratic deliberation to algorithms and capital.

The irony is that early warnings from the likes of Mumford or Weizenbaum were dismissed as 'obsolete fears.' This happened not because the warnings were inaccurate, but because 'technological determinism' itself functions as meta-violence. It tells us that the evolution of machines is inevitable; you can only adapt, not question. Under this narrative, any attempt to establish ethical boundaries is labeled as 'obstructing progress.'

We have now entered the era of the artificial state. In this world, expression is no longer about the establishment of identity, but has become a feedback loop fed by algorithms. We believe we are choosing, but we are merely performing an 'optimal expression' calculated for us. This war was already won in 1996 with the signing of an almost entirely unregulated act; what we are experiencing now is simply the long harvest following that victory.
