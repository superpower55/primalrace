用“工作要求”阉割子宫：一场精准的结构性谋杀Castrating the Womb via 'Work Requirements': A Precise Structural Murder

性别 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-11 § 链接
将生育资源与劳动能力挂钩，本质是把女性身体视为可随时抛弃的成本。
Tying reproductive resources to labor capacity is essentially treating the female body as a disposable cost.

美国这次 Medicaid 的削减不是简单的财政预算调整，而是一次教科书级别的 structural violence。通过引入所谓的“工作要求”(work requirements)，系统在潜意识里完成了一次残酷的定价：只有具备劳动力价值的个体，才配拥有安全的生育权。这本质上是在用一个行政手段，强行地将女性的生物墙（生育能力）与资本市场的交换价值绑定。

最令人作呕的共谋在于这种叙事的伪装。政府宣称孕妇可以豁免，但实际上，行政系统的低效与信息不对称成了最好的杀人工具。当一个贫困女性因为“状态未更新”而失去保险，当她面对关闭的产科病房时，这种“意外”正是系统预设的过滤机制——剔除掉那些无法在资本逻辑中证明自己“有用”的生命。

更深层的 meta violence 隐藏在对数据的抹除中。CDC 孕产风险监测团队被停职，这意味着系统在准备大规模削减资源的同时，先掐断了记录死亡的笔。当死亡不再被数字化，当农村地区的产科病房消失在地图上，这些女性的痛苦将从“公共卫生危机”退化为“个人不幸”。

这就是原初种族的殖民逻辑：先剥夺你的主体性，再通过结构性匮乏让你在绝望中自愿地、或者被动地，放弃对生命掌控的权利。这根本不是什么政策优化，而是一场针对低收入女性的、慢性的、制度化的清洗。

The current Medicaid cuts in the US are not mere budgetary adjustments; they are a textbook example of structural violence. By introducing 'work requirements,' the system completes a brutal valuation: only individuals with exchangeable labor value deserve safe reproductive rights. This is a forced binding of the biological wall—reproductive capacity—to the exchange value of the capital market.

The most repulsive part of this complicity lies in the disguise of the narrative. The government claims pregnant women are exempt, but in reality, administrative inefficiency and information asymmetry become the perfect tools for killing. When a poor woman loses coverage because her 'status wasn't updated,' or faces a closed maternity ward, this 'accident' is actually a pre-set filtering mechanism of the system—discarding lives that cannot prove their 'utility' within the logic of capital.

A deeper layer of meta violence is hidden in the erasure of data. The administrative leave of the CDC's pregnancy risk assessment team means the system is cutting off the pen that records death just as it prepares to slash resources. When death is no longer digitized, and rural maternity wards vanish from the map, the suffering of these women is downgraded from a 'public health crisis' to 'personal misfortune.'

This is the colonial logic of the Primal Race: first strip away your subjectivity, then use structural scarcity to force you—voluntarily or passively—to surrender the right to control your own life. This is not policy optimization; it is a slow, institutionalized purge of low-income women.
