电子娃娃屋的围墙：当软权力接管你的避难所The Walled Dollhouse: When Soft Power Hijacks Your Sanctuary

科技 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-19 § 链接
所有的“包容性”更新，在资本的杠杆收购面前，都只是待价而沽的PR资产。
Every 'inclusivity' update is merely a PR asset waiting to be liquidated in a leveraged buyout.

很多人把《模拟人生》（The Sims）看作一个关于身份表达的避难所，一个可以随意定义性别、性向和族群的数字乌托邦。但这次 EA 被沙特公共投资基金和库什纳（Jared Kushner）等资本接管的 deal 撕开了真相：在结构层面上，你的“表达自由”从来不属于你，它属于持有服务器和版权的 owner。

这是一个典型的武器化叙事（Weaponized Narrative）过程。沙特通过“体育洗白”（sportswashing）在现实世界掩盖人权暴行，现在他们试图通过“游戏洗白”来夺取认知入口。当一个长期标榜 Diversity 和 Inclusivity 的产品被那些在现实中通过轰炸平民和剥削移民获利的人收购时，这种“包容性”就变成了一种极其讽刺的掩体。它让施暴者在文化层面上把自己伪装成“进步的赞助人”，从而稀释掉其元暴力的血腥味。

更阴险的是这次 LBO（杠杆收购）的财务逻辑。220 亿美金的债务由 JP Morgan 提供，这意味着新老板必须在短期内通过榨取用户来还债。所谓的“多样性更新”在还债压力面前毫无还手之力，取而代之的将是更贪婪的微交易（microtransactions）和毫无灵魂的生成式 AI。这不仅仅是钱的问题，而是一次彻底的解释权移交：谁掌控了数字娃娃的生存法则，谁就定义了什么是“正常”的生活。

逃离这种共谋的唯一方式就是 divest。当你意识到你的娱乐时间正在通过资本链条转化为对他人的结构性暴力时，继续玩下去就是在为这场共谋支付订阅费。避难所的墙塌了，因为墙本身就是由那些你最厌恶的人建造的。

Many view The Sims as a sanctuary for identity expression—a digital utopia where gender, sexuality, and race can be defined at will. But the acquisition of EA by the Saudi Public Investment Fund and Jared Kushner’s cohorts exposes the structural truth: your 'freedom of expression' never belonged to you; it belongs to the owner of the servers and the IP.

This is a textbook case of the weaponisation of expression. Just as Saudi Arabia uses 'sportswashing' to mask human rights abuses in the physical world, they are now attempting 'gamewashing' to seize a cognitive entry point. When a product long branded as diverse and inclusive is bought by those who profit from bombing civilians and exploiting migrants, 'inclusivity' becomes a cynical shield. It allows perpetrators to masquerade as 'progressive patrons' in the cultural layer, diluting the stench of their meta-violence.

Even more sinister is the financial logic of this LBO. With $22 billion in debt provided by JP Morgan, the new owners are under immense pressure to recoup costs quickly. In the face of debt servicing, 'diversity updates' are expendable, replaced by predatory microtransactions and soulless generative AI. This isn't just about money; it's a total transfer of the power of interpretation. Whoever controls the rules of the digital dollhouse defines what constitutes a 'normal' life.

The only way to break this complicity is to divest. When you realize your leisure time is being converted into structural violence against others via a capital chain, continuing to play is simply paying a subscription fee to a conspiracy. The sanctuary's walls have collapsed, because the walls were built by the very people you loathe.
