用“美化街道”掩盖的结构性掠夺Structural Plunder Masked as 'Street Beautification'

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-17 § 链接
将生存权与强制劳动挂钩，是典型的用文化叙事合法化结构暴力的元暴力。
Tying survival rights to forced labor is a classic move of using cultural narratives to legitimize structural violence.

Robert Jenrick 抛出的这套所谓“从福利到工作”的政策，本质上是一场精心包装的 scam。他用“美化街道”、“社区服务”这些充满温情的 cultural 词汇，试图将一种极其粗暴的 structural violence 伪装成“公平”。

在加尔通的暴力三角中，这正是文化暴力在为结构暴力背书。通过定义领取福利者为“浪费潜能”或“负担”，他成功地将生存权的保障降格为一种可以被随时撤销的“恩赐”。当他宣称“能工作而拒绝工作的人将一无所有”时，他实际上是在制造一种极其危险的认知入口：生存不再是基本人权，而是一种通过服从特定权力意志（强制劳动）换取的奖赏。

更阴险的是，这套叙事精准地在弱势群体内部制造分层。通过将残障补贴 (PIP) 缩减 40%，将外国国民排除在福利体系之外，他不仅在进行经济上的掠夺，更是在进行身份政治上的剔除。这种“suicidal empathy”（自杀式共情）的说法，实际上是元暴力的典型表达——它将对他者的基本人道关怀定义为一种“病态”，从而为接下来的残酷剥削提供道德合法性。

谁在共谋？那些在社交媒体上鼓吹“不劳而获是不公平”的中产阶级，以及那些试图通过这种低端劳动力替代市政公共开支的地方议会，都是这场掠夺的共谋者。他们共同维护着一个男性中心化的、基于“强弱”和“有用性”来定价人的价值体系。在这种体系下，无法提供商业价值的身体被视为“废料”，而强制他们去扫大街，不过是将这种废料在物理层面上进行一次廉价的利用。

Robert Jenrick's so-called "welfare to work" policy is essentially a well-packaged scam. By employing warm cultural terms like "beautifying high streets" and "community service," he attempts to disguise a brutal form of structural violence as "fairness."

Within Galtung's Violence Triangle, this is cultural violence serving as a shield for structural violence. By defining benefit claimants as "wasting potential" or a "burden," he successfully degrades the guarantee of survival from a basic human right to a "favor" that can be revoked at will. When he declares that those capable of work who refuse will "get nothing," he is creating a dangerous cognitive entry point: survival is no longer a right, but a reward earned through submission to a specific will of power (forced labor).

More sinister is how this narrative creates stratification among the marginalized. By cutting disability claims (PIP) by 40% and excluding foreign nationals, he is conducting not just economic plunder, but an erasure of identity. The phrase "suicidal empathy" is a textbook example of meta-violence—it defines basic humanitarian concern for others as a "pathology," thereby providing moral legitimacy for the subsequent cruel exploitation.

Who are the complicit parties? The middle class cheering "no free rides" on social media, and the local councils seeking to replace municipal spending with this low-end forced labor, are all complicit in this plunder. Together, they maintain a masculine-centric system that prices human value based on "strength/weakness" and "utility." In this system, bodies that cannot provide commercial value are treated as "waste," and forcing them to sweep streets is merely a cheap physical utilization of that waste.
