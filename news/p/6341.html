谁在替绿党决定什么算极端Who Gets to Decide What Counts as Extreme

国际 元暴力 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-11-01 § 链接
程序之争，永远是解释权之争
Procedural fights are always fights over who interprets.

拉姆齐把绿党的危机说成程序问题：0.4%的成员投票通过了一项动议，所以制度有漏洞，所以要改规则、要全党投票、要在两年周期之前撤销它。这套话听起来像民主修缮，实际是一次解释权的回收——谁有资格定义党的方向，谁有资格判定哪些新成员是"硬左"。

他自己给出了答案：那些从加洛韦的党、科尔宾的 Your Party 失败后转进来的新成员，"在 SWP 里可能更自在"。注意这句话在做什么。它不讨论那些人的主张对不对，只判定他们的归属地不对。党内的分歧从来不靠论证解决，靠的是先给对方发一张别处的车票。这是共谋者理论里最日常的一种操作：不需要说谎，只需要重新分类。

0.4% 这个数字被反复使用，好像它证明了动议不合法。但所有党的年会动议都是这么通过的，小群体能推动议程，恰恰是绿党一贯的共识制结构在运转。拉姆齐真正不满的不是比例太小，是结果不合意。他要求的是对全体党员做一次投票或调查——一个更大的数字，用来覆盖一个他不接受的小数字。数字在这里不是标准，是武器，谁挑哪个数字，取决于哪个数字站在自己这边。

更值得看的是他明确说不会要求波兰斯基下台，也说自己不打算离开党。这不是软弱，是位置。他要在党内继续存在，所以把攻击精确地落在"文化"和"工作方式"上——"高度派系化、高度部落化的政治"——而不是人。文化是最难反驳的指控，因为它没有证据，只有感觉。你说我没有派系，我说你的做派就是派系。

Greens Organise 的回应把另一组数字摆上桌：15 万新党员，空前的选举成绩，还有一年前那场 84% 的落败。两边都在用数字证明自己代表党。没有人问，那些因为加沙而加入绿党的人，他们的表达算不算绿党的一部分，还是只算借住。

至于"犹太复国主义是种族主义"这项动议本身，拉姆齐说它和 2024 年宣言矛盾，说他支持巴勒斯坦人和以色列人权利平等。这是立场，可以争论。但整篇报道里，那 0.4% 的人没有一个被引述，15 万新党员也没有一个被引述。被引述的只有两位领导层和他们的组织。谁的声音进了这篇报道，谁的声音被归了类，这件事和党内民主的漏洞是同一个漏洞。

Ramsay frames the Green party's crisis as a procedural defect: 0.4% of members passed a motion, so the rules are broken, so the rules must change, so the wider membership must be balloted, so the motion must be rescinded before the two-year cycle runs out. This sounds like democratic repair. It is an attempt to take back the power of interpretation — who gets to define the party's direction, and who gets to decide which new members are "hard left."

He supplies the answer himself: the new members who arrived after Galloway's party and Corbyn's Your Party failed, who "might be more at home" in the SWP. Look at what that sentence does. It does not argue about whether their positions are right. It assigns them a different address. Disagreement inside a party is rarely settled by argument; it is settled by handing the other side a ticket somewhere else. This is the most ordinary move in the co-conspirator playbook: no lie required, only reclassification.

The 0.4% figure gets repeated as if it proved the motion illegitimate. But every party conference motion passes this way; small groups pushing agenda items is the consensus structure working, not failing. What Ramsay objects to is not the smallness of the number but the outcome. He wants a ballot or survey of the wider membership — a bigger number to override a smaller one he rejects. The number is not the standard here. It is the weapon. Whoever picks which number depends on which number is on their side.

More telling is that he explicitly will not call for Polanski to stand down, and will not leave the party himself. That is not weakness; that is position. He intends to remain inside, so the attack lands precisely on "culture" and "ways of working" — "highly fractional, highly tribal politics" — rather than on a person. Culture is the hardest charge to answer, because it has no evidence, only a feeling. You say I have no faction; I say your manner is itself a faction.

Greens Organise puts another set of numbers on the table: 150,000 new members, unprecedented electoral success, and the 84% defeat a year ago. Both sides use numbers to prove they represent the party. Nobody asks whether the people who joined over Gaza count as part of the Green party or only as lodgers.

As for the "Zionism is racism" motion itself, Ramsay says it contradicts the 2024 manifesto and that he supports equal rights for Palestinians and Israelis. That is a position, open to argument. But in the entire report, not one of the 0.4% is quoted, and not one of the 150,000 new members is quoted. Only two leadership figures and their organisations speak. Whose voice enters the report and whose voice gets classified is the same defect as the one in the party's internal democracy.
