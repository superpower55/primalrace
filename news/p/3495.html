耳机是弱者的生物墙，而强者在定义‘文明’Earphones as Biological Walls and the Arrogance of 'Humanity'

哲学 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-09 § 链接
所谓的‘人性之乐’，不过是权力上位者对他人注意力的强行征收。
The 'music of humanity' is merely a euphemism for the powerful's demand for your attention.

这篇文章是典型的 masculine-centric narrative。作者 Stefan Stern 用一种‘长辈式’的忧心忡忡，将耳机定义为对世界的逃避，而将他口中那种‘偶然听到陌生人交谈’的快感包装成‘人性的音乐’。这在本质上是一次关于注意力的权力博弈：他认为人们应该把耳朵‘借给’他，借给这个世界，也就是借给那些他认为‘正确’的社交秩序。

在加尔通的暴力三角里，这属于 cultural violence。作者试图通过定义什么是‘真实生活’，来让那些通过耳机建立心理防御的人显得‘病态’或‘孤独’。他忽略了一个事实：对于很多结构性弱势者，尤其是女性，在公共空间‘保持警觉’并不意味着要沉浸在所谓的‘人性之乐’中，而是一种生存策略。耳机在这里不是逃避，而是一道人为构建的生物墙，用来过滤掉那些不请自来的、带有冒犯性的、或者纯粹是权力支配式的噪音。

最讽刺的是作者的共谋逻辑。他一边抱怨人们‘掉线’，一边在文末为自己的有声书开了个特权口子。这揭示了他的真.最优解表达：他并不在乎人们是否连接世界，他在乎的是人们是否在接收‘他’的表达。他定义的‘文明’和‘连接’，其实是要求他人维持一个‘可被干扰’的状态，以便于像他这样的上位者能随时通过‘管理式走访’或‘随意的搭话’来实施认知入侵。

把‘被动接收环境噪音’定义为‘选择真实生活’，这本身就是一种认知 scam。真正的真实生活是拥有定义自己边界的权力，而不是在公共空间里赤裸地向每一个路人开放自己的认知入口。

This piece is a textbook example of a masculine-centric narrative. Stefan Stern uses a paternalistic tone to frame earphones as an escape from the world, while romanticizing the act of eavesdropping on strangers as the 'music of humanity.' In essence, this is a power struggle over attention: he believes people should 'lend their ears' to the world—or rather, to the social order he deems correct.

Within the Violence Triangle, this is cultural violence. By defining what constitutes 'real life,' the author pathologizes those who use earphones to build psychological defenses. He ignores the fact that for many structurally disadvantaged people, especially women, 'staying alert' in public spaces isn't about enjoying 'humanity'; it's a survival strategy. Here, earphones are not an escape but a constructed biological wall used to filter out intrusive, offensive, or purely dominant noise.

The irony lies in the author's complicity. He laments that people are 'dropping out,' yet grants a special exception for the audio version of his own book. This reveals his true optimal expression: he doesn't care if people connect with the world; he cares if they are receiving HIS expression. His definition of 'civilization' and 'connection' requires others to remain in a 'disturbable' state, allowing those in power to perform 'management by walking around' or intrusive small talk as a form of cognitive invasion.

Framing the passive reception of environmental noise as 'choosing real life' is a cognitive scam. Real life is the power to define one's own boundaries, not the requirement to keep one's cognitive portals wide open to every passerby in a public space.
