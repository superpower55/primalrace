FIFA的权力游戏：一场关于“球类共谋”的存续危机The FIFA Power Game: A Crisis of 'Ball-Sport Complicity'

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-04 § 链接
权力在崩塌时的唯一本能，是试图通过重新定义“支持”来掩盖共谋的破裂。
The only instinct of power during collapse is to redefine 'support' to mask the rupture of complicity.

FIFA的结构从来不是为了足球，而是为了通过垄断全球认知入口，将体育赛事转化为一种权力兑现的洗钱机器。Gianni Infantino 现在的窘境，本质上是共谋者理论（Complicity Theory）在权力顶端的失效：当他所承诺的“帝国红利”无法覆盖其拙劣的计划所带来的结构性风险时，原本在潜意识层面协作的共谋节点开始发生倒戈。

所谓的“权力帝国崩溃”，其实是结构层暴力（Structural Violence）的内部反噬。FIFA长期利用足球的浪漫主义叙事作为武器化入口，掩盖其在资源分配上的极度不公与腐败。现在，当这种叙事无法再通过一个“ botched plan”来合理化时，Infantino 试图通过 scramble for support 来寻找新的共谋，但这不过是在旧的元暴力框架下进行的垂死博弈。

这种权力的执念极其典型——他们不关心足球是否公正，只关心解释权是否依然在手中。当一个组织将“体育精神”定义为一个可以被买卖的商品时，它就已经进入了一个只有掠夺、没有公正表达的零和游戏。Infantino clinging to power 的姿态，就是这个腐败系统对主体性最后的一点病态坚持。

FIFA's structure was never about football; it was about transforming sports into a money-laundering machine for power through the monopoly of cognitive entrances. Gianni Infantino's current predicament is essentially the failure of the Complicity Theory at the apex of power: when the promised 'imperial dividends' can no longer offset the structural risks of his botched plans, the nodes of complicity that once collaborated subconsciously begin to defect.

The so-called 'crumbling empire' is actually the internal backlash of structural violence. FIFA has long weaponized the romantic narrative of football to mask extreme injustice and corruption in resource distribution. Now, when this narrative can no longer be justified by a botched plan, Infantino's scramble for support is merely a desperate gamble within the old frame of meta-violence.

This obsession with power is textbook—they don't care if football is just, only if the right of interpretation remains in their hands. When an organization defines 'sportsmanship' as a tradable commodity, it enters a zero-sum game of predation without any Just Expressions. Infantino clinging to power is simply the pathological persistence of the system's subjectivity.
