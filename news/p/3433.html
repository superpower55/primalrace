AI 育儿：一场关于“优化”的精密 scamAI Parenting: A Sophisticated Scam of 'Optimization'

科技 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-08 § 链接
用数据优化生命，是典型的用技术掩盖结构性剥削的元暴力。
Optimizing life via data is a meta-violence that uses tech to mask structural exploitation.

把育儿定义为一项需要被“优化” (optimize) 的工作，本身就是一种极其阴险的 cultural violence。当 OpenAI 建议用播客代替亲子对话，当 Nanit 和 Owlet 把婴儿变成 24 小时监控下的生物样本时，它们在推销的不是效率，而是一套将“生命体验”量化为“数据资产”的逻辑。这本质上是 masculine-centric narrative 的延伸：将世界视为可计算、可操纵的工程项目，而忽略了表达 (Expression) 作为存在确证的生物性需求。

这套 AI 育儿叙事最恶心的地方在于它精准地捕捉了结构性暴力 (structural violence) 制造的焦虑。现代社会拆散了 extended family，剥夺了女性在社区中的互助网络，将养育压力完全孤立在核心家庭内部。在这种 structural 绝望中，资本通过 weaponized 的“科学育儿”叙事，给焦虑的父母提供一个昂贵的认知入口：只要你买了 474 美元的摄像头和订阅服务，你就能获得“掌控感”。这根本不是在解决 parenting is hard 的问题，而是在给一个被制度性榨干的群体兜售缓解剂。

最值得追问的是，这种“优化”最终服务于谁？Nanit 宣称让父母能更快恢复“正常的性生活”，这种逻辑极其荒谬——它把孩子视为一种需要被高效管理、快速排除的“干扰项”，以便让成年人回归到某种被预设的、标准化的欲望模式中。这不仅是 parental 责任的让渡，更是主体性的死亡。当父母通过屏幕观察“睡眠效率”而非通过触碰感受孩子时，他们已经成为了这套数据共谋 (complicity) 体系中的一个节点。

AI 无法教孩子认识除了自己以外的人，因为 AI 本身就是一种剔除了所有“非效率”冗余的纯粹计算。在这种逻辑下，那些无法被量化的爱、耐心和琐碎的陪伴，都被定义为需要被优化的“损耗”。这场关于育儿的存在性战争，如果最终的胜出者是算法，那么我们养育出的将不再是人，而是被精准喂养的、符合系统预期的数据模型。

Defining parenting as a job to be 'optimized' is a sinister form of cultural violence. When OpenAI suggests podcasts instead of conversations, and Nanit turns infants into 24/7 biological samples, they aren't selling efficiency—they are selling a logic that quantifies 'lived experience' into 'data assets.' This is a direct extension of the masculine-centric narrative: treating the world as an engineering project to be calculated and manipulated, ignoring the biological necessity of Expression as the confirmation of existence.

This AI parenting narrative is particularly predatory because it feeds on the anxiety produced by structural violence. Modern society has dismantled extended family networks and stripped women of communal support, isolating the pressure of rearing children within the nuclear family. In this structural despair, capital uses weaponized 'scientific parenting' as a cognitive entry point: buy a $474 camera and a subscription, and you'll regain 'control.' This isn't solving the problem of parenting; it's selling a sedative to a group already exhausted by the system.

We must ask: who does this 'optimization' actually serve? Nanit claims it helps parents return to 'regular intercourse'—a ridiculous logic that treats the child as a 'disruption' to be efficiently managed and removed to fit a standardized desire model. This is more than a surrender of parental duty; it is the death of subjectivity. When parents monitor 'sleep efficiency' via a screen instead of feeling their child through touch, they become mere nodes in a system of data complicity.

AI cannot teach a child about others because AI is pure calculation, stripped of all 'inefficient' redundancies. Under this logic, unquantifiable love, patience, and the clutter of companionship are redefined as 'waste' to be optimized away. If the final winner of this existential war over parenting is the algorithm, we will no longer be raising humans, but data models precisely fed to meet system expectations.
