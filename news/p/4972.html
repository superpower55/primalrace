扮演“好男人”是最高级的共谋The 'Nice Guy' as the Ultimate Co-conspirator

性别 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-12 § 链接
Nice Guy 不是一种人格，而是一种精准计算后的最优解表达。
Being a 'Nice Guy' is not a personality; it is a calculated optimal expression for predation.

这部电影精准地捕捉到了当代父权制中最阴险的一种 mutation：那个读着 Edna O’Brien、背着 Mubi 帆布袋、时刻意识到自己 cis white male privilege 的“好男人”。在第一章的逻辑里，这根本不是什么人格改良，而是一次极其精明的表达博弈。当传统的粗鲁掠夺在公共空间失效后，男性迅速迭代了他们的认知入口，将“觉醒”和“共情”武器化，把它们变成了进入女性私密空间的通行证。

Adam 这种人是典型的共谋者。他并不打算挑战男性中心叙事，他只是在利用这套叙事的漏洞，通过扮演一个“非典型男性”来降低女性的防备心。这种“浪漫恐怖主义” (romantic terrorism) 的本质是：他用一套进步的 cultural 表达，掩盖其 structural 上的掠夺本能。他所谓的“礼貌且符合伦理地让你上床”，其实是将女性的主体性再次客体化——你成了他证明自己“不仅是个好人，而且是个懂行的好男人”的勋章。

电影最狠的地方在于，它没有让 Adam 变成一个脸谱化的反派，而是揭露了这种“好男人”表演的疲惫感。当他抱怨当个好人很“exhausting”时，他其实是在抱怨这套最优解表达的维护成本太高。这种表演是一场存在性战争，目的只有一个：在不牺牲特权的前提下，最大化地获取性资源。当叙事外壳被撕开，剩下的依然是那个原初的、将女性视为消费品的殖民者逻辑。

最讽刺的是，这种“好男人”叙事在现实中依然极具杀伤力，因为它给了很多女性一种错觉：只要我遇到了一个“懂我”的男人，我就能逃离结构性暴力。但事实上，最稳固的控制往往不需要鞭子，而是一次温柔的、带有 Mubi 审美倾向的欺骗。

This film accurately captures the most insidious mutation of contemporary patriarchy: the 'Nice Guy' who reads Edna O’Brien, carries a Mubi tote, and is 'constantly aware' of his cis white male privilege. In the logic of my first chapter, this isn't moral growth; it's a strategic pivot in existential war. As crude predation fails in public spaces, men have weaponized 'awareness' and 'empathy' as new cognitive entries to bypass women's defenses.

Adam is the quintessential co-conspirator. He has no intention of dismantling the masculine-centric narrative; he simply exploits its gaps by performing a 'non-typical male' identity to gain access to private spaces. This 'romantic terrorism' is the act of using progressive cultural expressions to mask structural predatory instincts. His 'polite and ethical' approach to getting women into bed is just another form of objectification—the woman becomes a trophy proving he is not just a good man, but an 'enlightened' one.

The film's strength lies in not turning Adam into a cartoon villain, but in exposing the exhaustion behind the performance. When he complains that being nice is 'exhausting,' he is lamenting the high maintenance cost of this optimal expression. It is a game of existence where the goal is to maximize sexual resources without sacrificing privilege.

The tragedy is that this 'Nice Guy' narrative remains lethal in reality. It feeds the illusion that one can escape structural violence by finding a 'different' kind of man. In truth, the most stable control doesn't require a whip—it only requires a gentle, Mubi-curated lie.
