用死亡数据做筹码：当公共卫生沦为叙事博弈的祭品Death as a Bargaining Chip: Public Health as a Sacrifice to Narrative Games

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-17 § 链接
权力通过重新定义“事实”来消除暴力，而真相在博弈中被定价。
Power erases violence by redefining 'facts,' and truth becomes a priced commodity in the game.

这不再是一场关于病毒的公共卫生危机，而是一场典型的关于“解释权”的认知战争。RFK Jr. 试图通过指令 CDC 剔除宾夕法尼亚州的死亡病例，将“死亡”这一绝对事实转化为一个可质疑的“条件”。这正是第三章所讨论的表达武器化：通过掌控认知入口，将真实的死亡通过统计学手段抹除，从而制造一个“麻疹并不致命”的虚假真实。

在这场博弈中，宾州卫生厅长的反应极具讽刺意味——她将“请求援助”作为筹码，试图换回 CDC 对死亡事实的承认。这是一种绝望的、基于结构性弱势的博弈。当联邦政府掌握了定义“什么是死亡”的权力时，地方政府只能在“承认死亡以换取资源”和“为了资源而接受谎言”之间做选择。这本质上是结构性暴力 (structural violence) 的升级：资源分配不再基于实际需求，而是基于你是否愿意配合权力的叙事。

最令人作呕的是这种“统计学洗白”的共谋机制。通过将统计口径从直接死因转向 NCHS 的趋势追踪，行政权力成功地在不否认个体死亡的同时，在宏观叙事中消解了暴力的存在。当 Actual（实际死亡）被定义为“不相关”时，Potential（本可避免的死亡）与 Actual 之间的差额就被权力抹平了。在这种元暴力 (meta violence) 的操控下，疫苗犹豫不再是认知偏差，而成了权力为了削弱公共卫生权威而故意喂养的武器。

这场博弈的赢家是那些掌控定义权的政客，而输家是那四个被剔除出统计名单的死者。他们不仅失去了生命，还失去了在官方记录中作为“受害者”的存在性。

This is no longer a public health crisis; it is a textbook cognitive war over the 'right of interpretation.' By instructing the CDC to scrub Pennsylvania's death tolls, RFK Jr. is attempting to transform the absolute fact of death into a 'questionable condition.' This is the weaponisation of expression: seizing the cognitive entry point to erase real deaths through statistical manipulation, thereby manufacturing a fake reality where measles is 'not that deadly.'

The reaction from Pennsylvania’s health secretary is profoundly ironic—she is using the 'request for aid' as a bargaining chip to trade for the federal recognition of death. This is a desperate game played from a position of structural weakness. When the federal government holds the power to define 'what constitutes a death,' local governments are forced to choose between 'acknowledging death to secure resources' or 'accepting a lie for the sake of survival.' This is an escalation of structural violence: resource allocation is no longer based on actual need, but on your willingness to comply with the narrative of power.

Most repulsive is the mechanism of complicity in this 'statistical laundering.' By shifting the counting method to NCHS trend tracking, the administration succeeds in dissolving the existence of violence in the macro-narrative without explicitly denying individual deaths. When the Actual (the death) is defined as 'irrelevant,' the gap between Potential and Actual—the very definition of violence—is erased by power. Under this meta violence, vaccine hesitancy is no longer a cognitive bias, but a weapon intentionally fed by power to undermine the authority of public health.

The winners of this game are the politicians who control the definitions; the losers are the four deceased individuals scrubbed from the records. They have lost not only their lives, but their existential status as 'victims' in the official history.
