欧洲的恐慌是场精心设计的认知 scamEurope's Panic: A Carefully Designed Cognitive Scam

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-12 § 链接
移民被武器化为政治筹码，而欧洲政客在共谋这场表演。
Migrants are weaponised as political chips, while European politicians conspire in the performance.

这次 Ceuta 危机根本不是什么移民危机，而是一次教科书级别的表达武器化 (Weaponisation of Expression)。摩洛哥国王在特定的 Throne Day 恰好出现在 Tétouan，边防守卫恰好“集体失明”，而社交媒体上的“西班牙开放”叙事精准投放。这根本不是自发的迁徙，而是一次由主权国家发起的、利用人类生存本能作为弹药的地缘政治攻击。

最精彩的是欧洲内部的反应。从 Trump 到 Meloni，这些政客迅速接管了认知入口，将一个地缘博弈事件简化为“入侵”叙事。这种 hysteria 并非因为他们真的关心边境，而是因为反移民情绪是目前性价比最高的一种身份政治工具。他们通过制造恐惧来确立自己的“守护者”身份，在存在性战争中通过贬低他者来收割选票。这就是典型的共谋 (complicity)：外部的施暴者制造混乱，内部的投机者利用混乱进行权力套利。

欧盟的反应揭示了其结构性暴力的双标。当移民被白俄罗斯武器化时，它是“受害者”；当移民被摩洛哥武器化时，它迅速转向内部指责。这种选择性失明证明了所谓的“欧洲团结”只是一个 PR 版本的 facade。在元暴力 (meta violence) 的逻辑下，这些政客并不在乎移民的生死，他们在乎的是谁能定义“什么是事实”。

最终，这成了一场双赢的 scam：摩洛哥测试了欧洲的裂痕，而欧洲的极右翼政客完成了新一轮的政治动员。至于那些在海里游泳的年轻人，他们只是这场权力博弈中被客体化的耗材，是 Potential 与 Actual 之间被无限拉大的暴力差额。

The Ceuta crisis was never a migration crisis; it was a textbook case of the Weaponisation of Expression. The Moroccan King's presence in Tétouan on Throne Day, the sudden 'blindness' of border guards, and the precision-targeted social media narrative that 'Spain is open'—this wasn't spontaneous migration, but a geopolitical attack using human survival instincts as ammunition.

More fascinating is the internal European reaction. From Trump to Meloni, politicians swiftly seized the cognitive entry points, reducing a geopolitical gambit to an 'invasion' narrative. This hysteria isn't about border security; anti-migrant sentiment is simply the most cost-effective tool of identity politics available. By manufacturing fear, they establish themselves as 'protectors,' harvesting votes by dehumanising others in an existential war.

This is pure complicity: external aggressors create chaos, and internal opportunists exploit it for power. The EU's response exposes the double standard of its structural violence. When migrants are weaponised by Belarus, the EU is the 'victim'; when it's Morocco, it pivots to internal recrimination. This selective blindness proves that 'European solidarity' is merely a PR facade. Under the logic of meta violence, these leaders don't care about the migrants; they care about who controls the definition of 'fact.'

In the end, it's a win-win scam: Morocco tested Europe's fractures, and the far-right completed another round of political mobilisation. As for the young men swimming in the sea, they are merely objectified consumables in a power game—the living embodiment of the violent gap between Potential and Actual.
