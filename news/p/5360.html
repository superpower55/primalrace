Grenfell的正义之期：一场关于“时间暴力”的漫长共谋The Grenfell Date: A Long Complicity of Temporal Violence

好消息 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-01 § 链接
延迟正义不是行政低效，而是结构性暴力对受害者的二次剥削。
Delayed justice is not administrative inefficiency; it is a structural violence that re-victimizes the survivors.

Grenfell Tower火灾已经过去九年，直到现在，受害者才拿到一个关于“起诉”的日期。在加尔通的暴力三角中，这不仅是 direct 层的身体毁灭，更是典型的 structural violence：通过极端的调查周期和法律程序的冗长，将正义的 Actual 状态无限期推迟，从而在 Potential 与 Actual 的巨大差额中，对幸存者实施慢速的心理凌迟。

这场迟到九年的决定，揭露了一个巨大的共谋场域 (complicity)。警察选择在公共调查结束后才推进刑事调查，政府和地方议会虽然现在宣布在“名单”上，但之前的沉默与拖延正是元暴力 (meta violence) 的体现——即系统内部默认了一种叙事：只要程序在运行，即便它慢到令人绝望，也算作在“寻求真相”。这种用“程序正义”掩盖“结果缺失”的逻辑，是权势阶层用来消解愤怒的武器化表达。

好在，这次的进展是真正的 good_news，因为它在 structural 层削减了暴力。corporate manslaughter（企业过失杀人）的指控被摆上桌面，意味着系统开始尝试将“企业责任”从单纯的罚款（商业成本）转化为刑事责任（主体性惩罚）。然而，真正的刺在于，即便现在起诉，审判可能要等到2028年。这意味着在接下来的四年里，那些被判定为“不诚实”的供应商依然可以拿着政府的公共合同继续赚钱。

如果一个系统允许杀人凶手在被定罪前继续作为合作伙伴获利，那么所谓的“正义之期”不过是另一种形式的 PR 演出。真正的正义不应该是给受害者一个日期，而应该是让施暴者在进入法庭前就失去所有获利的能力。

Nine years after the Grenfell Tower fire, survivors are finally given a date for potential prosecutions. In Galtung's Violence Triangle, this is not just the direct violence of the blaze, but a textbook case of structural violence: by stretching the investigation to an extraordinary length, the system creates a massive gap between the potential and actual state of justice, inflicting a slow-motion psychological torture on the bereaved.

This delayed decision exposes a vast network of complicity. The police's choice to let the public inquiry run before concluding their criminal investigation, and the government's long silence, are manifestations of meta-violence. They deployed a weaponized narrative: as long as the 'process' is moving—no matter how agonizingly slow—it is framed as 'seeking the truth.' This logic uses 'procedural justice' as a shield to mask a total lack of results.

That we now have a date is a form of good_news because it attacks structural violence. The consideration of corporate manslaughter charges shifts the conversation from mere fines (a cost of doing business) to criminal liability. However, the sting remains: trials are unlikely before 2028. This means for the next four years, suppliers judged as 'dishonest' can still profit from public contracts.

If a system allows perpetrators to remain business partners before they are convicted, the 'date' is nothing more than a PR performance. True justice is not about giving survivors a calendar entry; it is about stripping the perpetrators of their ability to profit long before they ever step into a courtroom.
