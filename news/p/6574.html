人死在牢里，罪名是间谍He Died in Custody, Charged as a Spy

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 Al Jazeera ↗ 2026-11-02 § 链接
谁控制监狱和病历，谁就控制死因。
Whoever controls the prison and the medical file controls the cause of death.

一个联合国人权办公室的雇员死在胡塞武装的羁押里，家属一个多月后才被通知。报道里没写死因，因为没人给他们一个解释。穆罕默德·el-Shami 自 2024 年 6 月起被任意拘押，死讯迟到一个多月——这段时间里，监狱、医疗记录、证人全在拘押方手里。

这不是一桩孤立的羁押事故。胡塞武装 2024 年把六十多名联合国和非政府组织工作人员一并指控为“美以间谍网络”成员，抓人不需要证据，只需要一个标签。标签一旦贴上，人就从“工作人员”变成“敌方资产”，从有名字的人变成可处置的对象。这是表达武器化最省力的入口：先定义你是谁，再让你为这个定义付账。

SAM 组织的 Alhamidi 把机制说得很清楚：这个团体控制着监狱、病历和证人，羁押场所没有任何司法或人权监督。于是死因可以是疾病、猝死、自杀——报道说，许多也门人在胡塞羁押中的死亡正是这样被归档的。死因不是被查出来的，是被写出来的。谁垄断了记录，谁就垄断了“事实”。

连问责的语言都被抽空了。联合国人权高专 Turk 要求“公开所有相关医疗和羁押记录、保全证据、独立审查”，古特雷斯要求“立即无条件释放”。这些话都对，但它们的前提是对方愿意交出手里那支笔。Alhamidi 把问题问到了底：就算证明是病死，还得问一句——他得到医疗了吗，还是疏忽，还是干脆被剥夺了？这一问，才是把“病死”从结论打回原形的关键。

也门国际承认政府的人权部长把责任归于民兵的绑架、强迫失踪和死亡。但报道里没有出现的，是那些同样被关着、没有联合国头衔、死了也不会有人发声明的人。73 名联合国工作人员在押，数十名其他人道工作者在押——数字之外，是更多连数字都进不去的也门人。

el-Shami 是第二个死在胡塞羁押中的联合国雇员，第一个是世界粮食计划署的员工，2025 年 2 月。两次死亡之间隔了一年多，中间是同一套流程：抓人、贴标签、不给家属探视、不给解释。报道没有写这次会不会有第三次，也没有写谁将为第一次负责。

A UN Human Rights Office employee died in Houthi custody. His family was notified more than a month later. The report gives no cause of death, because no one has given them one. Mohammad el-Shami had been arbitrarily detained since June 2024; the news of his death arrived over a month late, and in that month the prison, the medical records and the witnesses all belonged to the detaining party.

This is not a single custodial accident. In 2024 the Houthis accused more than 60 UN and NGO workers of belonging to an "American-Israeli spy network." Arrest needs no evidence, only a label. Once the label sticks, a person stops being staff and becomes an enemy asset, stops being a name and becomes something to be disposed of. This is the cheapest entry point for weaponized expression: define who someone is first, then make them pay for the definition.

Alhamidi of SAM Organization spells out the mechanism: the group controls the prisons, the medical records and the witnesses, and there is no judicial or human rights oversight over places of detention. So a death can be filed as illness, sudden death or suicide — and the report says many Yemeni deaths in Houthi custody have been recorded exactly that way. The cause of death is not investigated. It is written. Whoever monopolizes the record monopolizes the fact.

Even the language of accountability has been hollowed out. Turk demands disclosure of all medical and detention records, preservation of evidence, independent scrutiny. Guterres demands immediate and unconditional release. All correct — but all of it presumes the other side will hand over the pen. Alhamidi pushes the question to the floor: even if illness is proven, did the detainee receive medical care, or was there negligence, or deprivation? That question is what turns "illness" from a conclusion back into an open file.

Yemen's internationally recognized government holds the militia responsible for abduction, forced disappearance and death. What the report does not show is everyone else held in the same prisons who has no UN title, whose death produces no statement. Seventy-three UN staff in detention, dozens of other humanitarian workers — and beyond the numbers, Yemenis who don't even make it into the count.

El-Shami is the second UN staff member to die in Houthi custody. The first was a World Food Programme employee in February 2025. Between the two deaths lies more than a year of the same procedure: arrest, label, no family visits, no explanation. The report does not say whether there will be a third. It does not say who will answer for the first.
