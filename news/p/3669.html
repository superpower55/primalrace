用“灵活性”包装的生物实验：特朗普的认知入侵Biological Experiments Wrapped in 'Flexibility': Trump's Cognitive Invasion

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-11 § 链接
当政治权力通过重新定义科学事实来夺取认知入口，身体就成了博弈的筹码。
When political power seizes the cognitive entry point by redefining scientific facts, the body becomes a bargaining chip.

特朗普签署的这项行政命令，本质上是一次典型的表达武器化。他没有直接废除疫苗，而是使用了“研究”和“灵活性” (flexibility) 这种带有伪科学色彩的词汇。这在认知心理学上极其阴险：它不直接对抗科学，而是通过制造“科学尚有争议”的假象，在父母的认知入口里植入怀疑的种子。当一个国家的最高权力者宣布 MMR 疫苗需要“灵活性”时，他实际上是在通过元暴力 (meta violence) 篡改事实的定义权。

将组合疫苗拆分为单次接种，在医学逻辑上是毫无意义的 scam，但在政治博弈中却是极佳的表演。这种操作通过增加就医次数和时间成本，人为制造了疫苗接种的“阻力”，从而在 structural 层面上降低接种率。这根本不是为了孩子的健康，而是一场以儿童身体为样本的政治实验。在这种叙事中，孩子的免疫系统被降格为政治筹码，用来兑现特朗普与 RFK Jr. 这种反科学共谋者的政治承诺。

最危险的是这种认知污染的跨境传播。即便其他国家不跟风，但“美国总统认为疫苗不安全”这个叙事一旦进入全球信息流，就会成为一种文化暴力 (cultural violence)。它通过破坏公众对科学机构的信任，削弱全球公共卫生系统的防御墙。这种从认知入口发起的进攻，比病毒本身的传播速度更快，且更难被清除。当权力开始定义什么是“事实”，真相就成了权力意志的附属品。

Trump's executive order is a textbook case of the weaponisation of expression. He doesn't explicitly abolish vaccines; instead, he employs terms like "research" and "flexibility"—vocabulary laced with pseudo-scientific pretenses. This is psychologically insidious: it doesn't confront science head-on but manufactures an illusion of "scientific controversy," planting seeds of doubt in the cognitive entry points of parents. When the head of state calls for "flexibility" regarding the MMR vaccine, he is using meta violence to hijack the power of definition.

Breaking combined vaccines into separate shots is a medical scam with zero logical basis, yet it serves as a perfect political performance. By increasing the time and logistical costs of medical visits, it creates artificial friction, lowering vaccination rates at a structural level. This is not about pediatric health; it is a political experiment using children's bodies as samples to fulfill the political promises of a complicity between Trump and anti-science actors like RFK Jr.

The most acute danger is the transnational spread of this cognitive pollution. Even if other nations don't copy the policy, the narrative that "the US President believes vaccines are unsafe" becomes a form of cultural violence once it enters the global information stream. By eroding trust in scientific institutions, it weakens the structural walls of global public health. This attack on the cognitive entry point spreads faster than any virus and is far harder to eradicate. When power begins to define what constitutes "fact," truth becomes a mere appendage of the will to power.
