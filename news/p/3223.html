战争红利：被合法化的结构性掠夺War Bonus: The Legitimized Structural Plunder

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-04 § 链接
所谓的“战争红利”是结构暴力的最高形式：将他人的死亡定价为股东的利润。
The so-called 'war bonus' is the peak of structural violence: pricing others' deaths as shareholder profits.

BP、ExxonMobil、Chevron 的财报不是商业成功，而是一份关于如何高效将地缘冲突转化为现金的掠夺清单。当一个公司的利润在战争期间翻倍，这在本质上就是一次精准的结构性掠夺：用中东的血腥作为杠杆，通过操纵 energy prices 这一认知入口，将全球普通人的生存成本转化为资本的 windfall profits。这就是典型的 Violence = Potential − Actual，普通人本可拥有的生活质量，被这道差额强行抽干。

最讽刺的是 Trump 的表演。他指责能源巨头“钱太多了”，这不过是另一种 weaponized narrative。这种指责并非基于公正的表达，而是在进行一场关于“谁在代表公众”的权力博弈。他要求公司“把钱还给公众”，但这种还法通常是表演性的让步，旨在通过暂时的 retail price 下调来收买选票，而不会触及资本对能源解释权的垄断。这是一种典型的共谋：政治权力通过扮演“正义的监督者”，掩盖了其本身就是这套战争机器一部分的事实。

与此同时，欧洲的干旱和野火正在将气候危机转化为具体的 direct violence。而 BP 的 CEO 还在用“全球商品定价”这种中立的词汇来掩饰其 parasitic relationship。将企业利润与公众利益完全 divorced，并把这种剥离伪装成“市场规律”，这就是元暴力的运作方式：它定义了什么是“商业逻辑”，从而让这种反人类的获利方式看起来不仅合理，而且不可避免。

The financial reports from BP, ExxonMobil, and Chevron are not markers of business success, but a precise ledger of how to convert geopolitical conflict into cash. When a company's profits double during a war, it is essentially a structural plunder: using Middle Eastern bloodshed as leverage and manipulating the cognitive entry of energy prices to drain the living standards of ordinary people into windfall profits. This is the quintessential Violence = Potential − Actual; the quality of life people could have had is forcibly extracted by this gap.

Trump's performance is the most ironic part. His claim that oil giants make 'too much money' is merely another weaponized narrative. This criticism is not a just expression, but a power game over 'who represents the public.' His demand to 'give it back' is usually a performative concession, aimed at buying votes with temporary retail price cuts without ever challenging the monopoly of capital over the interpretation of energy. It is a classic complicity: political power masquerades as the 'just supervisor' while remaining a core component of the war machine itself.

Meanwhile, the droughts and wildfires in Europe are translating the climate crisis into direct violence. Yet, BP's CEO continues to use neutral terms like 'global commodity pricing' to mask a parasitic relationship. To divorce corporate gain from public good and disguise this as 'market logic' is exactly how meta-violence operates: it defines what 'business logic' is, making this anti-human profit-seeking seem not only reasonable, but inevitable.
