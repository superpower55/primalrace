伦敦 Robotaxi 延期：用“缺乏指南”掩盖的权力共谋London Robotaxi Delay: A Conspiracy of Power Masked by 'Lack of Guidance'

科技 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-26 § 链接
所谓的“监管滞后”通常不是能力问题，而是权力在重新定价。
Regulatory lag is rarely about capacity; it is about the recalibration of power and pricing.

伦敦 Robotaxi 的推迟被包装成一个典型的“技术与监管脱节”的故事：政府加速时间表，TfL 没出指南，公司在等待。这种叙事最 naive 的读法是认为政府效率低下，但从权力博弈来看，这其实是一场关于“认知入口”和“准入定价”的共谋游戏。

TfL 战略主管那句“我不认为我们需要匆忙发布指南”，直接撕开了伪装。在一个商业竞争白热化的场域里，指南（Guidance）不是简单的操作手册，而是筛选共谋者的门票。谁定义了安全标准，谁就掌握了对 Uber, Waymo, 百度 Apollo Go 这些巨头的定价权。所谓的“幕后大量工作”，本质上是在调整 structural layer 的资源分配，确保最终落地的规则能够最大化地服务于权力持有者，而非单纯的公共交通效率。

更有趣的是，所有参与者都在表演一种“谨慎”。Wayve 的 CEO 强调不给时间表，Waymo 强调安全审批。这种表演性让步（performative concession）是为了在公众面前建立“负责任”的形象，但私下里，他们是在进行一场存在性战争：抢夺伦敦这个复杂城市的解释权。谁先通过 DVSA 的许可，谁就定义了什么是“自动驾驶的真实”。

这场延期不是因为伦敦比凤凰城复杂，而是因为在伦敦，权力需要时间把“指南”这把武器打磨得更精准，好让那些排队等待的 10 万名用户在不知不觉中，成为新权力结构下的被消费客体。

The delay of robotaxis in London is being packaged as a classic tale of 'technical and regulatory misalignment': the government accelerated the timeline, TfL failed to provide guidance, and companies are left waiting. The most naive reading is to blame government inefficiency, but in terms of power dynamics, this is a game of complicity regarding 'cognitive entry points' and 'access pricing.'

TfL's strategy director's comment—'I do not think we are in a rush to get [the guidelines] out'—strips away the facade. In a field of hyper-competition, 'guidance' is not a simple manual; it is a ticket for selecting co-conspirators. Whoever defines the safety standards holds the pricing power over giants like Uber, Waymo, and Baidu's Apollo Go. The so-called 'huge amount of work behind the scenes' is essentially an adjustment of the structural layer of resource allocation, ensuring the final rules maximize the benefits for power holders rather than public transit efficiency.

Even more amusing is how all players are performing 'caution.' Wayve's CEO avoids timelines, and Waymo emphasizes safety approvals. This performative concession is designed to build a 'responsible' image for the public, while privately, they are engaged in an existential war: fighting for the right to interpret the complexity of London. Whoever first secures the DVSA permit defines what 'autonomous reality' actually is.

This delay isn't because London is more complex than Phoenix; it's because in London, power needs time to sharpen the weapon called 'guidance,' ensuring that the 100,000 users on the waiting list unwittingly become consumed objects within a new power structure.
