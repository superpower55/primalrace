ICE 的子弹与被献祭的“非人”叙事ICE Bullets and the Sacrificial Narrative of the 'Non-Human'

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-08 § 链接
暴力不仅是扣动扳机，更是将特定人群定义为“可被射击者”的解释权垄断。
Violence is not just pulling a trigger, but the monopoly of interpretation that defines certain people as 'shootable'.

这起在纽约布朗克斯区发生的枪击事件，是典型的 Violence = Potential − Actual。一个被 ICE 围困的人，在有五岁孩子在场的极端环境下被开枪，这个 Actual 状态与他本应享有的基本人权（Potential）之间的巨大差额，就是最赤裸的直接暴力 (direct violence)。

但我们不能只盯着那几颗子弹。真正恐怖的是支撑这次扣扳机的结构层 (structural) 和文化层 (cultural) 暴力。ICE 的存在本身就是一种结构性暴力，它通过将特定身份的人标记为“非法”，在法律和制度上剥夺了他们作为“人”的完整性。当一个人被定义为“非法移民”时，他在元暴力 (meta violence) 的叙事中就被客体化了，变成了某种需要被清理的“威胁”或“物件”。

在这种男性中心且国家主义的叙事中，执法者的“安全”永远优先于被执法者的“生存”。即便身边有一个五岁的孩子，这种生物墙带来的本能恐惧在武器化的权力面前毫无价值。因为在 ICE 的认知入口里，对方不是一个父亲，而是一个待处理的 Case。这种对解释权的垄断，让施暴者在事后可以用“执行公务”或“应对威胁”来完成自我规训，将暴力合法化。

市长和州长的愤怒目前看来仍停留在表演性阶段。要求“透明交代”是典型的体制内共谋表达——它在维持一个假象：只要程序公正，暴力就是合理的。但事实是，只要这种将人分等级、定义谁是“原初种族”并将其殖民化/边缘化的逻辑不被拆除，无论换多少个市长，这种恐怖主义执法永远会找到下一个目标。

The shooting in the Bronx is a textbook case of Violence = Potential − Actual. The gap between the actual state of a man being shot with a five-year-old present and the potential of his basic human rights is the most naked form of direct violence.

But we must look beyond the bullets. The real horror lies in the structural and cultural violence supporting that trigger. ICE is a manifestation of structural violence; by labeling specific people as 'illegal', it strips them of their full humanity within the legal and institutional framework. Once a person is defined as an 'illegal immigrant', they are objectified under meta violence, transformed into a 'threat' or an 'object' to be cleared.

In this masculine-centric and nationalist narrative, the 'safety' of the enforcer always overrides the 'survival' of the subject. Even the presence of a five-year-old—a biological wall of innate vulnerability—is worthless against weaponized power. In the cognitive entry of ICE, the target is not a father, but a Case to be processed. This monopoly over interpretation allows the aggressor to rationalize the violence through 'duty' or 'threat response', making the violence appear just.

The outrage from the mayor and governor currently feels performative. Demanding a 'transparent accounting' is a typical expression of complicity within the system—it maintains the illusion that violence is acceptable as long as the procedure is followed. The truth is, as long as the logic of grading humans and colonizing the 'Primal Race' persists, this brand of state terrorism will always find its next target, regardless of who holds office.
