老鼠的红利与人类的药片：一场关于“控制”的认知骗局Rat Dividends and Human Pills: A Cognitive Scam of 'Control'

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-25 § 链接
用药片代替毒药并非人道，而是将控制权从物理消灭转向生物操纵。
Replacing poison with contraceptives is not humanity, but a shift from physical eradication to biological manipulation.

这篇报道试图用一种“人道主义”的叙事来包装一个极其典型的控制论议题。当全球变暖让老鼠在暖冬中获得繁殖红利时，人类的反应不是反思导致气候崩溃的结构性暴力，而是纠结于用“毒药”还是“避孕药”来维持对城市的绝对掌控。这本质上是一场关于认知入口的博弈：将老鼠定义为“聪明的哺乳动物”以换取某种文明的道德优越感，但最终目的依然是将其排除在人类的生存空间之外。

最讽刺的共谋发生在对“避孕药”的讨论中。支持者将其称为 compassionately（慈悲地）管理，但这种“慈悲”实际上是最高级的武器化表达。毒药是直接暴力 (direct violence)，它血腥且可见；而避孕药则是结构暴力 (structural violence) 的生物学版本——它不杀死个体，但通过操纵生殖力来抹除整个族群的可能性。这种从“屠杀”到“绝育”的叙事升级，让控制者在心理上完成了从“刽子手”到“管理者”的身份洗白。

而阿尔伯塔省的成功案例则揭示了另一种元暴力：通过极端的社会动员和立法强制，将每一个公民变成国家监控机器的末梢。这种“集体努力”并非基于对生态的尊重，而是基于一种近乎原教旨主义的纯洁性强迫——不允许任何“入侵者”存在。在这种叙事里，老鼠被塑造成带有红色瞳孔的恶魔，从而合法化了整个省份的战争状态。

人类面对气候危机时的姿态如此滑稽：我们制造了导致老鼠激增的温室环境，然后试图通过研发更先进的化学药剂来修正这个结果。这种“打补丁”式的思维就是典型的 scam。真正的最优解不是在毒药和避孕药之间选择，而是意识到人类试图通过绝对控制自然来获得安全感的这种 masculine-centric narrative 本身，就是所有危机的根源。

This report attempts to wrap a classic control-theory issue in a 'humanitarian' narrative. As global heating grants rats a reproductive dividend via warm winters, the human response is not to reflect on the structural violence causing climatic collapse, but to fret over whether to use 'poison' or 'contraceptives' to maintain absolute urban dominion. This is essentially a game of cognitive entry points: defining rats as 'smart mammals' to secure a sense of civilized moral superiority, while the ultimate goal remains their total exclusion from human space.

The most cynical complicity appears in the debate over fertility control. Proponents call it 'compassionate' management, but this 'compassion' is actually a highly weaponized expression. Poison is direct violence—bloody and visible; contraceptives are the biological version of structural violence. They do not kill the individual but erase the possibility of an entire population by manipulating reproductive capacity. This narrative shift from 'slaughter' to 'sterilization' allows the controller to bleach their identity from 'executioner' to 'manager.'

Alberta's success story reveals another layer of meta-violence: the transformation of every citizen into a peripheral sensor for the state's surveillance machine through extreme social mobilization and legislative coercion. This 'collective effort' is not based on ecological respect, but on a near-fundamentalist obsession with purity—the refusal to tolerate any 'invader.' In this narrative, the rat is cast as a demon with glowing red pupils to legitimize a province-wide state of war.

Humanity's posture toward the climate crisis is farcical: we create the greenhouse environment that fuels the rat surge, then attempt to fix the result with more advanced chemicals. This 'patchwork' thinking is a total scam. The true optimal expression is not choosing between poison and pills, but realizing that the masculine-centric narrative—the belief that we can achieve security through the absolute control of nature—is the root of all these crises.
