用“痛苦”定价的全球贸易博弈Global Trade Priced by "Pain"

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-24 § 链接
战争叙事是最高级的武器化，将地缘利益包装成“惩罚”的艺术。
War narratives are the ultimate weaponization, framing geopolitical interests as the art of "punishment."

这场冲突的本质不是什么“正义”或“和平”，而是一场关于认知入口和贸易定价权的血腥博弈。特朗普在 Truth Social 上大谈“major military punishment”和“haven’t received enough pain”，这种典型的男性中心叙事将复杂的政治对抗简化为一种简单的、基于体能和痛感的施暴逻辑。在元暴力的框架下，这种叙事把国家机器降格为某种“肌肉展示”，试图通过制造直接暴力（direct violence）来强行定义一个他认可的“现实”。

真正的 structural violence 隐藏在那些被提及的 shipping chokepoints 背后。霍桑人、伊朗、美国，争夺的不是什么意识形态，而是对霍尔木兹海峡和曼德海峡的解释权与控制权。当油价上涨 7%、可口可乐在印度涨价时，普通人成了这场博弈中被忽略的 collateral damage。这种将全球供应链作为人质的行径，正是权力者将表达武器化的极致体现：他们不关心事实，只关心如何通过制造“可能性”来勒索对方。

最讽刺的是那些试图扮演“调停者”的角色。无论是通过第三国递交的停火协议，还是所谓的“人道主义”关切，在权力不对等的博弈中，这些往往只是为了在下次进攻前争取时间的 PR 包装。当一方宣称对方在“乞求停火”而另一方否认时，真相已经不重要了，重要的是谁能掌控这个叙事，谁能在这场存在性战争中定义谁是“弱者”。

This conflict is not about "justice" or "peace," but a bloody game of cognitive entry points and pricing power. Trump's rhetoric on Truth Social regarding "major military punishment" and "haven’t received enough pain" is a textbook masculine-centric narrative, reducing complex political confrontation to a primitive logic of physical dominance and pain. Under the framework of meta-violence, this narrative degrades the state apparatus into a mere display of muscle, attempting to define a preferred "reality" through the imposition of direct violence.

The true structural violence is hidden behind those shipping chokepoints. The Houthis, Iran, and the US are not fighting over ideology, but over the interpretation and control of the Strait of Hormuz and Bab el-Mandeb. When oil prices spike 7% and Diet Coke prices rise in India, ordinary people become the ignored collateral damage. This act of holding global supply chains hostage is the peak of weaponized expression: the powerful do not care about facts, only about manufacturing "possibilities" to extort the other side.

The most ironic part is the role of the "mediators." Whether it is ceasefire proposals delivered via third parties or so-called "humanitarian" concerns, these are often just PR packaging to buy time before the next strike in an asymmetrical game. When one side claims the other is "begging for a ceasefire" while the other denies it, the truth is irrelevant. What matters is who controls the narrative and who defines the "weak" in this existential war.
