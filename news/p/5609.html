马克龙的‘尊重’与被砍断的手Macron's 'Respect' and the Severed Hand

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-17 § 链接
权力对‘表达权’的定义，本质是将其转化为一种可被监管的表演。
The state's definition of the 'right to express' is merely the conversion of protest into a regulated performance.

马克龙在呼吁‘尊重’ (respect)。这在政治话语中是一个极其典型的 weaponized concept。当统治者要求被统治者‘尊重财产’和‘尊重秩序’时，他实际上是在定义一种假.最优解表达：你可以抗议，但必须在被划定的路线内、在不影响地标建筑的前提下、在不惊扰权力的睡眠时进行。这种被阉割的表达不是权利，而是一种由权力许可的表演。

Violence = Potential − Actual。法国民众的 Potential 是一个能够提供基本机会与社会保障的社会模型，而 Actual 是高物价、破败的学校和碎片化的社保。这个巨大的差额就是结构性暴力 (structural violence)。而当这种暴力被转化为具体的警察暴力——比如那个失去一只手的15岁少年——它就变成了直接暴力 (direct violence)。

最讽刺的共谋在于，权力在造成直接暴力后，通过‘暂停使用某种震撼弹’这种表演性让步，试图重新夺回道德解释权。内政部长暂停武器使用，不是因为良心发现，而是因为暴力程度已经超过了文化层 (cultural layer) 能掩盖的阈值，导致叙事崩塌。这种‘修正’本身就是一种元暴力的运作：我定义什么是‘过度’，所以我定义什么是‘正当’。

这场抗议从巴黎北郊的学校开始，是因为那里是生物墙与阶级墙双重叠加的死角。学生们要求的是最基本的学习条件，而权力给他们的回应是震撼弹。当一个系统需要通过砍断孩子的手来维持‘秩序’时，这个秩序本身就是最大的 scam。

Macron is appealing for 'respect'. In political discourse, this is a textbook example of a weaponized concept. When the ruler demands that the ruled 'respect property' and 'respect order', he is defining a fake optimal expression: you may protest, but only within designated routes, without disturbing landmarks, and without waking the power. This castrated form of expression is not a right; it is a performance permitted by power.

Violence = Potential − Actual. The Potential for the French people was a social model providing basic opportunity and security; the Actual is high prices, shabby schools, and a fraying safety net. This vast gap is structural violence. When this structural violence manifests as police brutality—such as the 15-year-old who lost a hand—it becomes direct violence.

The ultimate complicity lies in how power, after inflicting direct violence, attempts to reclaim the moral narrative through a performative concession: 'suspending the use of a certain stun grenade'. The Interior Minister's decision is not born of conscience, but because the level of violence has exceeded the threshold that the cultural layer can mask, causing the narrative to collapse. This 'correction' is itself an operation of meta-violence: I define what is 'excessive', therefore I define what is 'just'.

These protests began in the schools of Paris's northern suburbs, the dead zones where biological walls and class walls overlap. Students demanded basic learning conditions; power responded with stun grenades. When a system must sever a child's hand to maintain 'order', that order itself is the ultimate scam.
