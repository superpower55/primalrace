停火一年，屠杀没停A Ceasefire That Never Stopped Killing

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 Al Jazeera ↗ 2026-11-01 § 链接
停火是叙事，无人机才是事实。
Ceasefire is the narrative. The drone is the fact.

停火生效一年多，加沙死了超过一千五百人。这个数字本身就是对"停火"这个词的判决：它不是和平，是给暴力换了一个可以继续运转的许可。

周日死掉的人里，五个是警察。以色列说打的是"一名恐怖分子"，巴勒斯坦内政部说他们在"执行日常勤务"。这两种说法不是同一件事的两种描述，是两套事实的争夺——谁被允许定义"恐怖分子"，谁就拿到了杀人的许可证。在al-Mawasi巡逻、保护流离失所平民的警察，被一句"targeting a terrorist"抹掉，这是第三章讲的制造事实：先夺取解释权，再让解释权去杀人。

更值得看的是警察这个身份。从去年十月到现在，至少七十五名警察和安全人员被杀。哈马斯的声明说，这是要"阻碍恢复、破坏稳定、制造混乱"。翻译一下：一个社会要重新运转，需要有人维持秩序、分发物资、让流离失所的人知道自己明天还在哪里。杀掉这些人，不需要再杀所有人，社会自己就会停摆。这是结构层的暴力——它不满足于炸楼，它要拆掉让楼重新立起来的那套东西。

然后是一个四岁的男孩。半岛电视台的记者在加沙城革命街上说，死者里有父亲和他三岁的儿子——报道前文写四岁，后文写三岁，这个不一致本身就是战争的精度：连死亡年龄都来不及核对。一个父亲带着孩子走在街上，被无人机选中。以色列军方对此没有给出任何"恐怖分子"的说法，也不需要给。

"停火"这个词在这里的功能，和第三章里的"解放""人道主义""文明"是同一类。它让继续发生的死亡看起来像例外、像摩擦、像双方都有责任的失控，而不是一个持续的、有目标的进程。国际社会需要一个"停火已经达成"的叙事来证明自己做了事，于是这个叙事被维持着，代价由加沙人付。

七万四千五百九十四。这个数字从2023年十月累计到现在，而它每天都在被更新。报道里那句"很难想起有哪一天我们没有报道过致命袭击"，是整篇里最诚实的一句话——它说的不是新闻，是常态。

常态之所以能成为常态，是因为有人需要它看起来不像屠杀。

More than 1,500 people have been killed in Gaza since the ceasefire took effect over a year ago. That number is the verdict on the word itself: not peace, but a license for the violence to keep running under a new name.

Five of Sunday's dead were police officers. Israel says it targeted "a terrorist"; the Palestinian interior ministry says they were performing routine duties. These are not two descriptions of one event. They are two competing claims to what the event was — and whoever gets to define "terrorist" gets to issue the license to kill. Police patrolling al-Mawasi to protect displaced civilians, erased by a phrase. This is how facts get manufactured: first you seize the interpretation, then the interpretation does the killing.

Look closer at the target. At least 75 police officers and security personnel have been killed since last October. Hamas calls it an attempt to obstruct recovery, undermine stability, sow chaos. Translated: a society that wants to function again needs people to keep order, distribute aid, tell the displaced where they will sleep tomorrow. Kill those people and you don't need to kill everyone — the society stalls on its own. This is structural violence that isn't satisfied with flattening buildings; it goes after whatever would let them be rebuilt.

Then a four-year-old boy. Al Jazeera's correspondent reports a father and his three-year-old son killed on Revolution Street — the report says four earlier, three later, and that inconsistency is the precision of war: not even the age of the dead gets checked. No "terrorist" claim is offered for this one, and none is needed.

The word "ceasefire" functions here exactly like "liberation," "humanitarian," "civilization." It makes the ongoing deaths look like exceptions, friction, a shared failure of control — anything but a continuous, purposeful process. The international community needs the story that a ceasefire was reached to prove it did something, so the story is maintained, and Gaza pays for it.

74,594. The count has been accumulating since October 2023 and is updated every day. The correspondent's line — hard to remember a day when we haven't reported deadly attacks — is the most honest sentence in the piece. It isn't describing news. It's describing the normal.

The normal holds because someone needs it to not look like a massacre.
