刺杀 CEO 的“英雄”叙事与结构性暴力的掩体The 'Hero' Narrative of the CEO Assassination and the Shield of Structural Violence

哲学 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-14 § 链接
当个体用 direct violence 试图对抗 structural violence 时，他成了系统最好的掩体。
When an individual uses direct violence to fight structural violence, they become the system's perfect shield.

Luigi Mangione 的认罪让这场闹剧进入了司法程序，但真正的博弈在法庭之外。这起事件最荒诞的地方在于，一个被医疗系统摧毁的个体，试图通过 3D 打印枪支这种极致的“个人表达”，去对抗一个庞大到不可见、由算法和保单组成的 structural violence。结果是，他成了社交媒体上被神化的“Mangionistas”偶像，而这种偶像化本身就是一种危险的 weaponized 叙事。

这种“平民英雄”的叙事逻辑极其简单：因为系统邪恶 $\rightarrow$ 所以杀掉系统代理人 $\rightarrow$ 所以正义。这是一种典型的认知陷阱。杀掉一个 CEO 无法改变 UnitedHealthcare 的盈利模型，更无法抹除成千上万被拒赔者的痛苦。这种 direct violence 并没有缩小 Potential 和 Actual 之间的差额，反而给系统提供了一个绝佳的掩体：通过将 Mangione 塑造为“冷血刺客”或“精神病患”，系统可以迅速将讨论从“医疗制度的结构性剥削”转移到“暴力犯罪的法律惩处”上。

最讽刺的是，那些在网上欢呼的“粉丝”们，实际上是在进行一种低成本的、表演性的正义。他们消费 Mangione 的反抗，却无需面对真实的制度压力。而联邦检察官 McDonald 宣称“暴力不是传递社会信息的合法工具”，这句话在逻辑上是对的，但在元暴力的语境下是傲慢的。当一个人被系统逼到脊椎断裂、生活毁灭且毫无救济通道时，系统早已先对他实施了长期的、无声的暴力。现在，系统拿着法典告诉受害者：你的反抗方式不合法。

Mangione 认罪了，但那个杀掉无数人的医疗系统依然在运作。这场战争的胜负不在于他是否被判终身监禁，而在于我们是否还愿意把这种个体式的暴力爆发，误认为是结构性变革的开始。

Luigi Mangione's guilty plea pushes this farce into the judicial process, but the real game is played outside the courtroom. The absurdity here is that an individual destroyed by the healthcare system attempted to combat a vast, invisible structural violence—composed of algorithms and insurance policies—through the extreme expression of a 3D-printed gun. Consequently, he became a fetishized idol for the 'Mangionistas' on social media, a weaponized narrative that is inherently dangerous.

The logic of this 'folk hero' narrative is simplistic: the system is evil $\rightarrow$ killing the system's agent $\rightarrow$ justice. This is a cognitive trap. Assassinating a CEO does not change UnitedHealthcare's profit model, nor does it erase the suffering of thousands of denied claimants. This direct violence failed to reduce the gap between Potential and Actual; instead, it provided the system with a perfect shield. By framing Mangione as a 'cold-blooded assassin' or a 'psychopath,' the system shifts the discourse from 'structural exploitation of healthcare' to 'legal punishment of violent crime.'

Most ironic are the online 'fans' engaging in a low-cost, performative justice. They consume Mangione's rebellion without facing actual systemic pressure. Meanwhile, federal prosecutor McDonald's claim that 'violence is not a legitimate instrument to promote any social message' is logically correct but arrogant within the context of meta-violence. When a person is pushed to a broken back and a destroyed life with no recourse, the system has already inflicted long-term, silent violence. Now, the system holds up a law book to tell the victim: your method of resistance is illegal.

Mangione has pleaded guilty, but the system that kills thousands remains operational. The outcome of this war is not about whether he receives life imprisonment, but whether we still mistake this individual burst of violence for the beginning of structural change.
