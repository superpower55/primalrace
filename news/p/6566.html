撤军不是归还Withdrawal Is Not Restitution

国际 结构层 · 直接层 Al Jazeera ↗ 2026-11-02 § 链接
新闻摘要希伯伦省长哈立德·杜丁周一宣布，以色列军队在围困该市10天后已撤离，巴勒斯坦安全部队全面接管该地区，被迫离开家园的家庭可以返回。此次围困始于10月23日两名巴勒斯坦人发动袭击、打死两名以军士兵之后，行动期间超过3300名巴勒斯坦人流离失所或受到影响，15处房屋及其他建筑被拆毁。
The newsHebron Governor Khaled Dudin announced Monday that Israeli forces withdrew from the city after a 10-day siege, with Palestinian security forces taking full control and displaced families allowed to return. The siege began on October 23 after two Palestinians killed two Israeli soldiers; over 3,300 Palestinians were displaced or affected and 15 homes and other structures were demolished.
枪走了，家没了，这叫撤军不叫结束
The guns left. The homes didn't come back. That's a redeployment, not an ending.

以色列军队从希伯伦撤了，巴勒斯坦安全部队接管，州长宣布流离失所的家庭可以回家了。听起来像是一个结局。但把这条新闻读完，会发现没有任何东西被归还：15栋房屋和其他建筑被拆了，3300多名巴勒斯坦人流离失所或受到影响，被占的民宅改成军事哨所，商业财产被毁，基础设施受损，学校停课。撤军撤走的是人，不是那些已经被砸碎的东西。

这就是结构暴力的标准操作：用十天完成破坏，再用一份撤军声明把破坏定格成"现状"。加尔通的公式在这里算得很清楚，Potential 是这些家庭原本可以继续住着的房子、可以继续开的店、可以继续上的学，Actual 是废墟加一句"可以安全返回"。差额就是暴力，而且这个差额不会因为士兵走开而自动缩小。

更值得看的是这场围困的启动逻辑。10月23日两名巴勒斯坦人开枪，打死两名以色列士兵、打伤三人，以军随后击毙了这两名袭击者。袭击者已经死了，报复却落在了整座城市身上——封锁、占屋、拆房、驱逐。个体行为被换算成集体账单，而付账的人里包括那3300多个被影响的人，他们的名字一个都没出现在报道里。

报道里还有一个缺席：谁为这15栋被拆的房子负责，谁赔偿，谁重建。州长说的是"有关当局和巴勒斯坦安全部队正在监测局势、为居民安全返回创造条件"。创造条件，不是恢复原状。安全返回一栋不存在的房子，这句话本身就说明了撤军的性质——它是一次军事部署的调整，不是一次权利的恢复。

十天可以拆掉一个街区，重建要多久，报道没说。这个没说的部分，才是希伯伦接下来真正要打的那场仗。

Israeli forces have withdrawn from Hebron, Palestinian security forces have taken over, and the governor says the families who fled can come home. It reads like an ending. Read to the end of the report and nothing has been given back: 15 homes and other structures demolished, more than 3,300 Palestinians displaced or otherwise affected, Palestinian houses seized and converted into military posts, commercial properties destroyed, infrastructure damaged, education disrupted. What withdrew was the army. What was broken stayed broken.

This is structural violence doing what it does: ten days to destroy, then a withdrawal statement that freezes the destruction as the baseline. Run Galtung's arithmetic. The potential was families in their houses, shops open, schools running. The actual is rubble plus a promise that return will be "safe." The gap is the violence, and soldiers walking away does not close it by a single meter.

The trigger deserves the same scrutiny. On October 23 two Palestinians opened fire, killing two Israeli soldiers and wounding three; Israeli forces then killed both attackers. The attackers were dead. The reprisal landed on a whole city — closure, seizure, demolition, displacement. An individual act converted into a collective invoice, and among those who paid are the 3,300-plus affected, not one of whom the report names.

There is another absence. Who answers for the 15 demolished homes, who compensates, who rebuilds — the report does not say. The governor speaks of authorities monitoring the ground and working to provide conditions for residents to return safely. Conditions, not restitution. Safe return to a house that no longer exists tells you what kind of event this was: a redeployment of force, not a restoration of rights.

Ten days can level a neighborhood. How long rebuilding takes, nobody in this report is saying. That silence is the next fight in Hebron.
