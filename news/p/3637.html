在天文奇观的叙事里，谁在定义“安全”？Who Defines 'Safety' in the Narrative of Celestial Spectacles?

哲学 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-11 § 链接
所有的“安全指南”本质上都是一种对个体身体管理权的温情接管。
Every 'safety guide' is essentially a gentle takeover of the individual's bodily autonomy.

一条典型的、毫无波澜的科普新闻。它在告诉你如何“安全”地观看日食，像是在提醒你过马路要看红绿灯一样自然。但如果我们把视角从天文现象拉回到身体政治，你会发现这种“安全”叙事背后隐藏着一个极其稳固的认知入口：专家定义风险，大众执行规训。

这种表达方式是典型的 cultural violence 的温和版本。它并不直接禁止你观看，而是通过建立一套“正确”的观看程序——佩戴特定的眼镜、遵循 NASA 的指令——将一个自然的生物体验转化为一次由机构背书的、被管理的行为。当你开始依赖这些“指南”来决定你的眼睛是否安全时，你已经默认了身体的解释权不在自己手中，而是在那些掌握了科学话语权的机构手中。

这其实是一种微小的、日常的 meta violence。它在潜移默化中训练我们：面对自然，个体是无能的，只有通过接入某种“权威”的认知接口，才能获得生存的入场券。这种从“我观察”到“我被告知如何观察”的转变，正是所有结构性控制的起点。从日食眼镜到疫苗指南，从健康标准到审美定义，逻辑完全一致——用“保护”的名义，完成对主体性的悄悄剥夺。

当然，不戴眼镜看日食确实会瞎。但这正是这套 scam 的高明之处：它用一个真实的生物事实（生物墙），为一套庞大的管理叙事提供了最完美的掩体。它让你在感激被保护的同时，忘记了自己已经习惯于在每一个生活细节里，向某种不可见的权力中心递交投名状。

A typical, ripple-less piece of science news. It tells you how to watch a solar eclipse 'safely,' as naturally as reminding you to look at traffic lights before crossing the street. But if we shift the gaze from astronomy to body politics, you'll find a deeply entrenched cognitive entry point: experts define risk, and the masses execute the discipline.

This mode of expression is a mild version of cultural violence. It doesn't explicitly forbid you from watching; instead, it transforms a natural biological experience into a managed behavior endorsed by institutions—wearing specific glasses, following NASA's instructions. The moment you rely on these 'guides' to determine if your eyes are safe, you've accepted that the interpretative power over your body resides not within yourself, but within the institutions that monopolize scientific discourse.

This is a subtle, everyday form of meta violence. It subconsciously trains us to believe that the individual is powerless against nature, and that the only way to secure a 'ticket to survival' is by plugging into an authoritative cognitive interface. This transition from 'I observe' to 'I am told how to observe' is the starting point for all structural control. From eclipse glasses to vaccine guidelines, from health standards to aesthetic definitions, the logic is identical: use the narrative of 'protection' to quietly strip away subjectivity.

Of course, looking at the sun without protection actually blinds you. That is precisely why this scam is so brilliant: it uses a genuine biological fact—the biological wall—as the perfect cover for a vast narrative of management. It makes you forget that while you are grateful for being protected, you have grown accustomed to submitting a pledge of allegiance to some invisible center of power in every detail of your life.
