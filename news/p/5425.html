特朗普的“经济学”：一场关于权力的元暴力表演Trump's 'Economics': A Performance of Meta-Violence

国际 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-05 § 链接
经济数据是掩体，定义权的垄断才是目的。
Economic data is the cover; the monopoly of definition is the goal.

特朗普在 Truth Social 上大谈 AI 只需要一个“高 IQ 总统”作为护栏，并试图用一种极其幼稚的算术重新定义 trade deficit。在大多数经济学家眼中，这简直是 Econ 101 的灾难，但如果用经济学去反驳他，你就是掉进了他的博弈陷阱。因为他根本不在乎 GDP 是 2% 还是 20%，他在做的是一次典型的表达武器化：通过宣布一个荒诞的“事实”，强行夺取对现实的解释权。

这是一种纯粹的 meta violence。当他把“赤字”等同于“损失”时，他不是在讨论贸易，而是在通过重构语言来筛选共谋者。在这种叙事里，复杂的全球供应链被简化为一种“赢或输”的男性中心化竞争模型。他不需要懂经济，他只需要制造一种“只有我能拯救美国”的认知入口，让支持者在情绪的共振中完成身份认同。对于他的共谋者——那些在国会保持沉默或欢呼的共和党人来说，逻辑的正确性远没有站队带来的生存优势重要。

最讽刺的是，他试图用一种“强人”的表达来掩盖其主体性的空洞。这种将国家经济视为私人公司账本的思维，本质上是将整个国家客体化为他的战利品。当他威胁要切断贸易来逼迫美联储降息时，他其实是在进行一场存在性战争：他要证明，在这个系统里，他的意志高于所有制度性的 guardrails。这种对规则的践踏，正是元暴力的最高形式——我不改变能力，我直接重新定义现实。

Trump's claims on Truth Social—that AI only needs a 'High IQ President' as a guardrail and his childish arithmetic on trade deficits—are viewed by economists as a disaster of Econ 101. But countering him with economic logic is a trap. He doesn't care if GDP is 2% or 20%; he is engaging in the weaponisation of expression: announcing absurd 'facts' to forcibly seize the power of interpretation.

This is pure meta-violence. By equating 'deficit' with 'loss,' he isn't discussing trade, but filtering for complicity through the reconstruction of language. In this narrative, complex global supply chains are reduced to a masculine-centric model of 'winning or losing.' He doesn't need to understand economics; he only needs to create a cognitive entry point of 'only I can save America,' allowing supporters to achieve identity confirmation through emotional resonance. For his co-conspirators in Congress, the alignment of interest outweighs the correctness of logic.

Ironically, he uses 'strongman' expression to mask a void in his own subjectivity. Treating the national economy as a private ledger is essentially objectifying the entire nation as his trophy. When he threatens to halt trade to bully the Fed, he is fighting an existential war: proving that his will supersedes all structural guardrails. This trampling of rules is the ultimate form of meta-violence—not improving capability, but simply redefining reality.
