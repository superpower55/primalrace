Triple Lock：一场关于养老金的PR骗局与结构性掠夺The Triple Lock: A PR Scam and Structural Predation

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-16 § 链接
用公共部门的虚高薪资给养老金注水，本质是用未来的结构性贫困换取当下的政治安抚。
Inflating pensions via public sector wage spikes is merely trading future structural poverty for immediate political appeasement.

所谓的 Triple Lock（三重锁定）机制，在新闻叙事里被包装成保护退休者的“胜利”，但如果把视角拉高到 structural violence 层级，这不过是一个精心设计的 scam。政府通过在平均工资、通胀率和 2.5% 中取最高值来决定涨幅，看似是给养老金加了保险，实则是在玩一场数字游戏。

最讽刺的共谋发生在数据的构成上：此次 3.9% 的涨幅很大程度上是由公共部门 6.3% 的薪资增长推高的，而私营部门仅为 2.9%。这意味着，政府一边通过公共部门的薪资注水来维持养老金的“体面”涨幅，一边让私营部门的劳动者在生活成本危机中被进一步榨干。这种机制将公共部门的特权转化为养老金的政治筹码，而代价则是整体经济的失衡与潜在的财政崩溃。

更深层的暴力隐藏在 tax-free personal allowance（个人免税额）的冻结中。政府承诺不给仅靠国家养老金生存的人收税，但这其实是一个典型的“表演性让步”。它制造了一个极其诡异的 divide：那些在年轻时努力进行 private pension（私人养老金）储蓄的人，反而因为总收入超过免税额而被征税。这本质上是对“主体性”和“预见性”的惩罚——你越努力为未来做最优解表达，系统就越通过 fiscal drag（财政拖累）来回笼你的资产。

当一个系统通过冻结税收门槛来悄悄抵消名义上的涨幅时，所谓的“大赢家”其实只是被喂了糖衣的囚徒。Actual（实际到手金额）并没有向 Potential（应得权益）靠近，而只是在 cultural 层面上完成了一次关于“政府关怀”的叙事闭环。

The so-called 'Triple Lock' mechanism is framed in the news as a 'victory' for retirees, but viewed through the lens of structural violence, it is a sophisticated scam. By selecting the highest of average earnings, inflation, or 2.5%, the government isn't providing a safety net; it is playing a numbers game.

The most cynical complicity lies in the data composition: the 3.9% rise is heavily skewed by a 6.3% jump in public sector pay, while the private sector stagnates at 2.9%. The government is essentially using public sector inflation to pump up pension figures as political capital, while private sector workers are further squeezed by the cost-of-living crisis. This mechanism converts public sector privilege into a political tool, leaving the broader economy to bear the structural cost.

A deeper layer of violence is found in the frozen personal allowance. The pledge not to tax those relying solely on the state pension is a textbook example of performative concession. It creates a perverse divide: those who exercised agency by building private pensions are penalized by being pushed into a higher tax bracket. It is a direct punishment for those who sought an optimal expression for their future—the system uses fiscal drag to claw back their assets.

When a system quietly offsets nominal gains by freezing tax thresholds, the 'big winners' are merely prisoners fed with candy. The Actual has not moved toward the Potential; instead, the government has simply closed a narrative loop of 'care' at the cultural level.
