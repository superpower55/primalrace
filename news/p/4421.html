用所有人的钱给能源巨头买单的共谋游戏A Game of Complicity: Paying Energy Giants with Everyone's Money

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-24 § 链接
结构性暴力通过将欠款成本分摊，让所有受害者共同供养掠夺者。
Structural violence works by spreading debt costs, forcing all victims to sustain the predators.

这篇新闻表面在讨论能源价格 cap 的上涨和 70 亿英镑的欠款，但其底层的 structural violence 极其阴险：Ofgem 官方统计 90 天逾期才算欠款，而行业协会 Energy UK 用 30 天的标准算出更高的债务额，然后把这笔钱通过某种隐秘的机制，分摊到每个消费者的账单里。结果就是，每个家庭每年多付 50 英镑，用自己的钱去填补能源供应商的亏空。

这是一场完美的共谋 (complicity)。能源公司通过操纵认知入口，将价格上涨归咎于“中东冲突”这种不可抗力的外部叙事，从而掩盖了其在能源网络升级成本转移上的贪婪。政府则在 VAT 减税这种表演性让步 (performative concession) 中扮演好人，试图给选民一点“呼吸空间”，但这种小打小闹在 4% 的涨幅面前根本就是个 scam。

最讽刺的是，由于高昂的电费，许多家庭被迫减少用能，这种被剥夺的生存状态竟然被计入到价格上限的计算方法中，从而使账单看起来没那么离谱。这正是 Violence = Potential − Actual 的具体体现：人们本可以拥有温暖的冬天，但实际状态被压低，而这个差额被能源巨头转化为利润。

谁在赢？当然是那些掌握定价权的能源巨头。他们不仅在掠夺贫困者的生存底线，还成功地让所有消费者在潜意识中成为了他们维持利润的共谋者。

On the surface, this news is about energy price caps and £7bn in debt, but the underlying structural violence is sinister: while Ofgem counts debt at 90 days, Energy UK uses a 30-day metric to inflate the figure, then spreads this cost across all consumers. The result? Every household pays an extra £50 a year to subsidize the losses of energy suppliers.

This is a textbook case of complicity. Energy companies weaponize narratives, blaming "Middle East conflicts"—an external, uncontrollable force—to mask their greed in transferring network upgrade costs to the public. Meanwhile, the government performs a small act of kindness by cutting VAT, a performative concession that is a complete scam when faced with a 4% price hike.

The most cynical part is that high costs have forced households to reduce energy consumption, and this deprivation is actually baked into the price cap methodology to make the bills look less alarming. This is the Violence Triangle in action: Violence = Potential − Actual. People's potential for a warm winter is stripped away, and that gap is converted into corporate profit.

Who wins? The energy giants who control the pricing power. They are not only plundering the survival baseline of the poor but have successfully turned every consumer into an unconscious co-conspirator in sustaining their margins.
