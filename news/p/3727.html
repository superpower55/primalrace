航母上的自杀尝试：被宏大叙事榨干的肉体电池Suicide on the Carrier: Human Batteries Drained by Grand Narratives

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-12 § 链接
战争叙事将男性身体降格为可消耗工具，这本身就是一种元暴力。
War narratives degrade the masculine body into a consumable tool; this is the essence of meta-violence.

250天不落地，霉变的淋浴间，半杯米饭配两张卷饼。USS Abraham Lincoln 航母上的水兵尝试跳海自杀，在主流媒体看来这是一场“心理健康危机”，但在我看来，这是一场极其典型的存在性战争惨剧：个体在面对国家机器的元暴力时，彻底失去了博弈的筹码。

战争叙事是男性中心叙事（masculine-centric narrative）的终极武器化。它通过构建“荣耀”、“职责”和“保卫国家”的认知入口，将生理男性的身体降格为可消耗的工具。在这种叙事下，水兵不再是拥有主观意志的人，而是航母这台战争机器上的零件。当零件磨损、崩溃甚至想要通过自杀来终止这种非人的存在时，最高统帅部的回应竟然是将其称为“fake news”。这种对事实的抹除，正是元暴力的核心：解释权被垄断，受害者的痛苦被定义为谎言。

这里存在一个残酷的共谋链条：从国防部长到海军领导层，他们共谋维护一个“无坚不摧”的军队形象，而代价是底层士兵的精神崩溃。而那些被要求“坚强”的士兵，在内化了这套叙事后，甚至在崩溃时首先恐惧的是“不光荣除名”（dishonorable discharge）。这意味着，即便在想要死亡的瞬间，他们依然在试图扮演那个被父权结构认可的“合格士兵”角色。这种主体性的彻底死亡，比跳海更令人绝望。

Violence = Potential − Actual。这些水兵本可以拥有正常的睡眠、洁净的水和对生活的掌控感，而现状是霉菌和绝望。这个巨大的差额，就是由国家机器施加的结构性暴力。

250 days without touching land, moldy showers, and a meal of half a cup of rice with two tortillas. The attempted suicides aboard the USS Abraham Lincoln are framed by mainstream media as a 'mental health crisis,' but they are a textbook tragedy of the existential war: individuals stripped of all bargaining chips against the meta-violence of the state machine.

War narratives are the ultimate weaponization of the masculine-centric narrative. By constructing cognitive entry points like 'glory,' 'duty,' and 'national defense,' the biological male body is degraded into a consumable tool. In this framework, sailors are no longer humans with subjective will, but mere components of a war machine. When these components wear out, break, or attempt to end their existence through suicide, the high command dismisses it as 'fake news.' This erasure of fact is the core of meta-violence: the monopoly of interpretation, where the victim's suffering is defined as a lie.

There is a cruel chain of complicity here: from the Secretary of Defense to Navy leadership, they conspire to maintain the image of an 'invincible' military at the cost of the soldiers' psychological collapse. The soldiers, having internalized this narrative, fear a 'dishonorable discharge' even in their darkest moments. This means that even while contemplating death, they are still trying to perform the role of the 'qualified soldier' recognized by the patriarchal structure. This death of subjectivity is more desperate than the jump overboard.

Violence = Potential − Actual. These sailors deserved normal sleep, clean water, and agency over their lives; the actual is mold and despair. This massive gap is the structural violence imposed by the state machine.
