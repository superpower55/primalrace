被“钦定”的黑豹与迪士尼的多元化scamThe 'Handpicked' Panther and Disney's Diversity Scam

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-27 § 链接
用个体的阶级跃升叙事，掩盖结构性解释权的垄断。
Using individual class ascent to mask the monopoly of structural interpretive power.

迪士尼和漫威最擅长的事情就是将“代表性” (representation) 武器化。David Jonsson 从伦敦东区被开除的少年到成为新一代黑豹，这是一个标准的、令人感动的“美国梦”变体。但请注意那个词：handpicked（钦定）。

在存在性战争中，真正的胜利应该是解释权的让渡，而不是被施舍的席位。当 Kevin Feige 说“我找到了他”时，这依然是一场典型的 masculine-centric narrative：一个拥有绝对权力、掌握认知入口的男性白人，在审视了无数个黑人身体后，决定谁才是那个“正确”的黑人表达。这种“钦定”本质上是迪士尼在进行一次 PR 维护，用一个具体的成功个体去抵消其在结构层面对多样性空间的挤压。

Jonsson 提到的“定义什么是黑人” (defining what Black is)，在个体层面是觉醒，但在系统层面却成了共谋。当黑人演员在一个由白人制定的审美和权力框架内竞争“定义权”时，他们其实是在一个被限定的容器里进行博弈。这种博弈的真.最优解应该是推翻这个容器，而不是争取成为容器里最闪亮的那颗珠子。

好莱坞的多元化叙事就是一场巨大的 scam：它给底层族群提供极少数的“阶级跃升”样本，以此诱导大众相信结构性暴力已经消失。但事实上，T'Challa 的王冠依然在漫威的账本里，解释权从未换手，只有演员在更换。

Disney and Marvel are masters at weaponizing 'representation.' David Jonsson's journey from an expelled youth in East London to the new Black Panther is a classic, touching variant of the 'American Dream.' But notice the word: handpicked.

In an existential war, true victory is the transfer of interpretive power, not the acceptance of a bestowed seat. When Kevin Feige says, 'I found him,' it remains a textbook masculine-centric narrative: a white male with absolute power over cognitive entry points surveys countless Black bodies and decides who is the 'correct' Black expression. This 'handpicking' is essentially a PR exercise, using a single success story to offset the structural violence of crowding out actual diversity.

Jonsson's ambition of 'defining what Black is' may be an awakening at the individual level, but at the system level, it becomes complicity. When actors compete for 'definition' within an aesthetic and power framework designed by white men, they are merely gaming within a predefined container. The true optimal expression would be to dismantle the container, not to strive to be the shiniest bead inside it.

Hollywood's diversity narrative is a massive scam: it provides a few samples of class ascent to trick the public into believing structural violence has vanished. In reality, T'Challa's crown remains on Marvel's balance sheet. The interpretive power has never changed hands; only the actors have.
