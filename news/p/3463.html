巡逻时间的增长是国家机器的自嗨式博弈Increased Patrol Time is Just a Performance of the State Machine

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-08 § 链接
增加 25% 的监控时间不代表安全增加，只代表暴力机器的共谋成本在上升。
A 25% increase in monitoring time doesn't mean more safety; it means rising complicity costs for violence machines.

英国皇家海军宣布监控俄罗斯舰船的时间增加了 25%，这种叙事在传统新闻里被包装成“警惕”与“保护”，但在我的眼睛里，这只是两台巨大的暴力机器在进行一场毫无意义的 a-symmetric game。所谓的“监控”和“影子舰队”的博弈，本质上是 masculine-centric narrative 的典型表演：通过增加巡逻频次、部署直升机和潜艇猎杀机，向公众展示一种“我在工作”的勤勉感。

这种表达是典型的武器化。国防部通过具体的数据（116次激活、25%的增长）来制造一种紧张感，从而在 cultural layer 上合法化巨大的军费开支。当 Cmdr Matt Millyard 说这证明了海军“存在的意义”时，他实际上是在确认一种身份：一个由男性主导的、通过对抗和威慑来确立主体性的权力结构。在这种叙事里，安全被定义为“我能盯着你”，而不是“如何消除冲突”。

最讽刺的是，俄罗斯用老旧船只组成“影子舰队”规避制裁，而英国则用昂贵的现代化战舰去“盯梢”。这是一种极其低效的共谋：一方通过钻漏洞获利，另一方通过扮演“守护者”来维持其在体制内的资源配额。双方都在这场存在性战争中获得了各自的最优解——普京得到了战争资金，而英国国防部得到了预算证明。至于真正的和平或安全，在这一套 meta violence 的逻辑里根本不在议程上。

The Royal Navy's announcement of a 25% increase in monitoring Russian vessels is packaged as 'vigilance' and 'protection.' In reality, it is a meaningless a-symmetric game between two massive violence machines. The obsession with 'shadow fleets' and patrol hours is a textbook example of masculine-centric narrative: establishing a sense of 'industry' and 'duty' through the sheer volume of deployment.

This expression is weaponised. By citing specific data—116 activations, 25% growth—the MoD manufactures a state of tension to legitimize massive military spending at the cultural layer. When Cmdr Matt Millyard claims this demonstrates the 'reason the Royal Navy exists,' he is confirming an identity: a power structure dominated by men that defines its subjectivity through confrontation and deterrence. In this framework, security is defined as 'I can watch you,' not 'How do we resolve the conflict.'

The irony is that while Russia uses a 'shadow fleet' of aging vessels to evade sanctions, the UK deploys high-cost warships to 'shadow' them. This is a form of systemic complicity: one side profits from loopholes, while the other maintains its resource quota by playing the 'guardian.' Both achieve their optimal expression in this existential war—Putin gets his war funds, and the MoD gets its budget justification. Actual peace or security is never on the agenda in this logic of meta violence.
