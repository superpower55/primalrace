特朗普及他的存在性焦虑：当权力沦为表演Trump and His Existential Anxiety: Power as Performance

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-26 § 链接
权力衰落时的暴力升级，本质是主体性崩塌后的垂死挣扎。
Violence spikes during power decline as a desperate attempt to stave off ontological death.

特朗普在联合国威胁要“消灭”伊朗，这种典型的 masculine-centric narrative 并不代表他强，反而暴露了他的极度虚弱。按照加尔通的公式，Violence = Potential − Actual。当一个人的实际权力（Actual）在法院、国会和民调中迅速缩水，而他认知中的潜在权力（Potential）依然维持在“无敌”的幻觉时，这个巨大的差额就会转化为直接的暴力冲动。威胁战争、拆除艺术中心、禁封媒体，这些不是权力的行使，而是权力的代偿。

这场戏最精彩的部分在于共和党内部的共谋者 (complicit) 们。过去十年，这群人通过 submission（顺从）来换取政治红利，他们与特朗普达成了一场关于“强人叙事”的共谋。但现在，当这个叙事不再能提供最优解，当支持率跌至 29% 变成一种负资产时，共谋者们迅速切换模式。那些曾经在他面前低头的“瞪羚”们开始闻到风向的变化。这种忠诚的切换极其高效且冷酷，因为它从不关乎价值观，只关乎在存在性战争中的生存概率。

对于特朗普来说，最致命的不是失去权力，而是失去“注意力的入口”。正如 Galston 所言，聚光灯是他的氧气。在元暴力的逻辑里，被定义为“中心”才是唯一的存在方式。一旦他被边缘化，就意味着他在精神层面的主体性死亡。一个习惯了垄断解释权的人，无法接受自己变成一个被讨论的“客体”。

所以，那个“越弱越危险”的悖论是成立的。当结构层 (structural) 的约束（法院、法律）开始生效，而文化层 (cultural) 的光环消失时，他唯一的出路就是通过 direct 层的暴力来制造存在感。一个没有退路且掌握核按钮的 lame duck，会把整个世界变成他最后一场关于“我依然存在”的表演剧场。

Trump threatening to "annihilate" Iran at the UN is a textbook masculine-centric narrative. It doesn't signal strength; it signals profound fragility. Following Galtung's formula, Violence = Potential − Actual. When actual power wanes in courts and polls while the perceived potential remains an illusion of invincibility, the gap manifests as violent impulses. Threatening war, demolition, and censorship are not exercises of power, but compensations for its loss.

The most revealing part is the behavior of the complicit within the GOP. For a decade, they traded submission for political dividends, entering a pact based on the "strongman" narrative. Now that this narrative no longer yields the optimal expression—with approval ratings at 29% becoming a liability—the co-conspirators are pivoting. The "gazelles" have smelled the wind. This shift in loyalty is efficient and cold; it's never about values, only about survival probabilities in the existential war.

For Trump, the true horror isn't the loss of office, but the loss of the cognitive entry point: attention. In the logic of meta-violence, being the "center" is the only way to exist. To be marginalized is to suffer ontological death. A man who has monopolized the right to interpret reality cannot endure becoming a mere "object" of discussion.

Thus, the paradox that "the weaker he gets, the more dangerous he gets" holds true. As structural constraints (courts, laws) tighten and the cultural aura fades, his only remaining tool is direct violence to manufacture a sense of presence. A lame duck with nothing to lose and the nuclear codes will turn the world into his final stage to scream: "I still exist."
