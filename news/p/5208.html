用“庞氏骗局”给资产阶级洗澡的金融ScamA Financial Scam Washing the Bourgeoisie with 'Ponzi' Narratives

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-23 § 链接
用底层生存权的丧失，为顶层资产的免税提供合法性叙事。
Sacrificing the survival rights of the bottom to provide a legitimacy narrative for top-tier tax evasion.

把国家养老金定义为“庞氏骗局” (Ponzi scheme) 是一个极其阴险的 weaponized 叙事。它通过将一个结构性的社会契约（Structural Contract）重新定义为一种“欺诈”，从而在文化层面上消解掉国家对公民生存权的保障义务。当底层民众开始怀疑自己的养老金是某种“骗局”时，他们就进入了被收割的认知入口。

看看这份报告的真.最优解表达是谁的：取消资本利得税、遗产税、印花税，将公司税从 25% 砍到 15%。这是一场典型的资产阶级共谋。所谓的“提高生产力”和“生活标准”只是包裹在外的彩色糖衣，其本质是把公共财政的缺口直接转嫁给那些依赖 state pension 生存的弱势群体。这不是改革，这是在用法律手段抢劫未来的老人。

最讽刺的是那个“特朗普式”的投资账户。给新生儿 1000 英镑，诱导他们将 15% 的收入投入个人账户，本质上是将生存风险从国家转移到个体。在元暴力 (meta violence) 的逻辑下，这种方案将“自我负责”包装成“财务自由”，实际上是剥夺了人们在面对生物墙（衰老、疾病）时的最后一道结构性防线。

谁在共谋？City 的高管们、Reform UK 的智库、以及那些试图通过削减公共开支来给资本市场“解渴”的政客。他们通过重新定义“什么是经济增长”，成功地将一个掠夺性的方案包装成了“激进的改革”。

Defining the state pension as a "Ponzi scheme" is an insidious weaponisation of narrative. By redefining a structural social contract as a "fraud," it erodes the state's obligation to guarantee survival rights at a cultural level. Once the working class begins to perceive their pensions as a scam, they have entered the cognitive entry point for exploitation.

Look at whose true optimal expression this report serves: abolishing capital gains tax, inheritance tax, and stamp duty, and slashing corporation tax from 25% to 15%. This is a classic complicity of the asset-owning class. The promises of "boosting productivity" and "living standards" are merely sugar-coated distractions; the essence is shifting the public finance deficit directly onto the vulnerable who rely on state pensions. This isn't reform; it's legalized robbery of future elderly.

The "Trump-style" investment account is the ultimate irony. Providing £1,000 to newborns and inducing them to put 15% of earnings into personal pots is simply transferring survival risk from the state to the individual. Under the logic of meta-violence, this frames "personal responsibility" as "financial freedom," while effectively stripping away the final structural defense against the biological wall of aging and illness.

Who are the co-conspirators? City executives, Reform UK-linked think tanks, and politicians seeking to "quench" the capital markets by cutting public spending. By redefining "what economic growth is," they have packaged a predatory scheme as "radical reform."
