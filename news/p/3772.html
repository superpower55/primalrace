被定义的“风险区”与合法的驱逐Defined 'Risk Areas' and Legalized Expulsions

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-13 § 链接
当气候危机被武器化，灾难就成了清理贫民的行政工具。
When climate crisis is weaponized, disaster becomes an administrative tool for cleansing the poor.

Porto Alegre 的洪水不是自然灾害，而是一场精准的 structural violence。按照加尔通的公式，Violence = Potential − Actual，这些生活在 quilombo（逃奴后裔社区）的人们本可以拥有基本的防洪设施和生存保障，但实际得到的却是 5000 雷亚尔的施舍和随时可能被淹没的恐惧。这个差额，就是由政府的 apathetic 构成的暴力。

最令人作呕的是对“风险区” (risk area) 的定义权。这是一种典型的 weaponized expression。政府通过定义谁处于“风险”中，掌握了合法的驱逐权。所谓的“为了安全而搬迁”，本质上是利用气候危机进行 gentrification。把穷人通过“My Debt”计划赶到城市边缘，然后将原有的土地通过风险缓解手段，重新定价并出售给富人。这哪是救灾？这是在用洪水洗地，把贫民窟洗成高档社区。

在这种叙事中，穷人成了共谋者理论里的牺牲品。他们被告知搬迁是“最优解”，但这个最优解是建立在主体性死亡基础上的：他们失去了社区，失去了历史，换来的是一份终身债务。而政府在 PR 叙事里大谈“结构性改善”和“监测系统”，却在预算上只花不到 0.5% 用于减灾。这种认知入口的操纵，让行政失职被伪装成了自然不可抗力。

当 siren 响起却不知道往哪里跑时，人们才意识到，所谓的“保护”其实是另一种形式的规训。这场存在性战争中，穷人连自己的绝望都被定价了。

The floods in Porto Alegre are not a natural disaster, but a precise act of structural violence. Following Galtung's formula, Violence = Potential − Actual; the residents of the quilombos could have had basic flood defenses and security, but instead received 5,000 reais in crumbs and a lingering dread of drowning. This gap is the violence created by government apathy.

The most repulsive part is the monopoly over the definition of 'risk area.' This is a textbook example of weaponized expression. By defining who is 'at risk,' the state seizes the legal right to expel. The narrative of 'relocation for safety' is essentially using the climate crisis as a vehicle for gentrification—pushing the poor to the urban fringes via 'My Debt' schemes, then scrubbing the land and reselling it to the wealthy. This isn't disaster relief; it's using floods to clear the slate for high-end real estate.

In this game, the poor are the victims of a complicity loop. They are told that relocation is the 'optimal expression' for survival, but it is an optimal solution that demands the death of their subjectivity: they lose their community and history in exchange for lifelong debt. Meanwhile, the state manipulates the cognitive entrance, masking administrative negligence as 'natural inevitability' while spending less than 0.5% of its budget on actual mitigation.

When the sirens wail and people realize they have nowhere to run, it becomes clear that 'protection' is just another form of discipline. In this existential war, the poor find that even their despair has been priced by the state.
