圣诞修路，谁的假期不算假期Christmas Engineering, Whose Holiday Doesn't Count

其他 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-31 § 链接
工程选在圣诞，因为默认那几天没人需要出门
Work is scheduled for Christmas because the system assumes nobody needs to travel then.

Network Rail 说圣诞假期是施工的最佳时机，因为那几天铁路本来就基本停运。这句话的潜台词很清楚：谁的出行算出行，谁的出行可以牺牲。

报道里列出的受影响者是这样的：去英格兰西部和威尔士的城际旅客、伦敦到东北英格兰和苏格兰的旅客、埃塞克斯和诺福克的通勤者、利兹和曼彻斯特之间的跨奔宁旅客。这些人有一个共同点——他们的出行被当成了可以挪动的余量。Network Rail 的集团总监 Anit Chandarana 说"圣诞是很多人想和家人朋友团聚的时候"，然后紧接着说"大部分铁路会开放"。前半句是人话，后半句是公关。

一亿五千两百万英镑的工程，换来的可靠性提升和运力提升，最终会落到谁头上，报道没写。但结构暴力的运作方式就在这里：成本被定向分配给了假期出行的普通旅客，收益被延迟、被稀释、被平均化。这不是阴谋，这是排期表上的默认值——默认那些在圣诞出门的人是弹性最大的那群人。

真正值得追问的是，为什么"最安静的时刻"总是落在同一批人身上。铁路停运两天，于是施工窗口被定义在圣诞；节日期间探亲的人被定义为"可以改期"的人；而工程验收的时间表、预算的结算周期、承包商的人力调度，这些没有一样被定义为可以改期。谁的时间被当作刚性，谁的时间被当作弹性，这张排期表本身就是一份权力清单。

查一下再出发，这句话本身没错。但当"查一下"成为唯一的救济方式时，系统已经把责任从自己身上挪到了旅客身上。

报道里没有出现任何一位旅客的名字。一亿五千两百万英镑的工程有总监出来解释，被改道的几百万人没有一个人被问过一句。

Network Rail says the Christmas period is the best time for essential works because train services generally stop on the 25th and 26th. The subtext is clear: whose travel counts as travel, and whose travel can be sacrificed.

The affected parties listed in the report share one thing in common: intercity passengers to the west of England and Wales, travellers between London and north-east England and Scotland, commuters to Essex and Norfolk, TransPennine passengers between Leeds and Manchester. Their journeys have been treated as movable slack. Network Rail's group director Anit Chandarana says Christmas is when many people want to be with family and friends, then immediately adds that most of the railway will stay open. The first half is a human sentence. The second half is PR.

What the £152m of works buys in reliability and capacity, and who ultimately receives it, the report doesn't say. But this is how structural violence operates: costs are allocated to ordinary holiday travellers, benefits are delayed, diluted, averaged out. Not a conspiracy — a default value on a scheduling spreadsheet. The default is that people travelling at Christmas are the most elastic group.

The real question is why the quietest moment always lands on the same people. Trains stop for two days, so the construction window gets defined as Christmas; holiday visitors get defined as reschedulable; but the engineering acceptance timeline, the budget settlement cycle, the contractor's labour scheduling — none of these are defined as reschedulable. Whose time is rigid and whose time is elastic: that schedule is itself a power inventory.

Check before you travel — the advice is correct. But when checking is the only remedy, the system has already shifted responsibility from itself onto the passenger.

No passenger is named in the report. A £152m project has a director to explain it. The millions being rerouted were never asked.
