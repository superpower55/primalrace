一座饿着肚子的城，还在守着旧礼仪A Hungry City Still Keeping Its Manners

文娱 结构层 · 文化层 Polygon.com ↗ 2026-11-03 § 链接
新闻摘要《Avatar: Seven Havens》第一季结尾，神通 Pavi 前往 Floating Isles 解锁第二脉轮；主创 Michael Dante DiMartino 向 IGN 确认，全剧规划四季，要在此间走完七个 Haven。第一季里已登场的 New Ba Sing Se 正遭遇「有史以来最严重的饥荒」，其使节 Kuzon 在峰会上提出用御术师换 Ellora 的粮食。
The newsAvatar: Seven Havens ends season 1 with Avatar Pavi heading to the Floating Isles to unlock her second chakra. Co-creator Michael Dante DiMartino told IGN the story is mapped across four seasons and will visit all Seven Havens. New Ba Sing Se, already introduced, is suffering "the worst famine in our long history," and its envoy Kuzon offers benders in exchange for Ellora's harvest.
传统不是被遗忘的，是被拿来当饭吃的。
Tradition isn't what they forgot; it's what they're eating.

Polygon 这篇是在猜第二季的路线图，但真正值得看的是它顺手拆出来的那个设定：New Ba Sing Se 快饿死了，穿的还是 Aang 时代那身衣服。

饥荒是结构暴力最标准的形态——没有施暴者，没有血，只有一批人系统性拿不到本可以拿到的粮食。报道写得很清楚，Kuzon 在峰会上开口就是「New Ba Sing Se has experienced the worst famine in our long history」，然后把御术师摆上桌，换 Ellora 的收成。一个 Haven 拿人换粮，这不是天灾的语法，这是配给和议价的语法。

更有意思的是它为什么饿。报道给出的线索是：这个城「clings to the oldest traditions」，Nisha 远远看着说他们活得像还在上古时代，而 Akasha 的访客直接嘲笑他们是走出「crumbling haven」的「historians」。御术师出生率全球下滑，别处已经在用更先进的技术，他们还守着旧的那一套。

这就是文化暴力替结构暴力打掩护的经典场面：一套叙事告诉所有人，是丢掉旧方式招来了 Cataclysm，守住旧方式就是解药。于是饿肚子被解释成虔诚，被解释成尊严，Kuzon 那种护着自己 Haven 体面的姿态，恰恰是这套叙事的执行。加尔通那句话在这里是活的——文化暴力的功能，就是让结构暴力看起来不像暴力，甚至看起来是对的。

报道自己都把答案递出来了：第三个脉轮是火脉轮，管意志，堵它的是羞耻。一个守着昔日荣光、却喂不饱人的社会，pride 是拿来盖 shame 的。这段话是编剧写的，但说的是现实里每一个「我们祖上阔过」的共同体。

还有一层，报道没写，但它的沉默本身是材料：整篇里没有一个 New Ba Sing Se 的普通人在说话。Kuzon 是 envoy，是 politician，是那个有权拿别人的身体去换粮食的人。饥荒里谁先饿，谁被拿去换，谁在峰会上代表这座城——这三件事从来不是同一批人决定的。

所以第二季真回到 Ba Sing Se，我希望看到的不是又一场怀旧巡礼。原始那座城靠假装战争不存在撑过了一百年，继任者靠假装饥荒是传统的代价撑着。两座城共用同一个幻觉，只是换了个剧本。

This Polygon piece is guessing at a season 2 itinerary, but what it actually cracks open is a setting: New Ba Sing Se is starving, and still dressed in Aang's era.

Famine is structural violence in its purest form — no perpetrator, no blood, just a population systematically denied food it could have had. The report is explicit: at the summit, Kuzon says "New Ba Sing Se has experienced the worst famine in our long history," then puts benders on the table for a share of Ellora's harvest. A Haven trading people for grain is not the grammar of natural disaster. It's the grammar of rationing and bargaining.

More interesting is why they're hungry. The clue the report gives: the city "clings to the oldest traditions." Nisha watches from a distance and says they live as though it's still ancient times; an Akashan visitor mocks them as "historians" who wandered out of a "crumbling haven." Bender birth rates are falling worldwide, other Havens have moved on to more advanced technology, and this one hasn't.

This is cultural violence doing its standard job of covering for structural violence: a narrative tells everyone that abandoning the old ways caused the Cataclysm, and keeping them is the antidote. So hunger gets rewritten as piety, as dignity. Kuzon's defensiveness about his Haven's standing is that narrative being executed. Galtung's line is live here — the function of cultural violence is to make structural violence look like it isn't violence, or even like it's right.

The report hands over the answer itself: the third chakra is fire, it governs willpower, and shame blocks it. A society clinging to former greatness while failing to feed its people — pride is how you hide shame. A writer typed that about a cartoon. It describes every community that used to be somebody.

One more layer the report doesn't write, and its silence is the material: no ordinary resident of New Ba Sing Se speaks anywhere in it. Kuzon is an envoy, a politician, the man with the power to trade other people's bodies for grain. Who starves first, who gets traded, and who represents the city at the summit are never the same people deciding.

So if season 2 does go back, I don't want a nostalgia tour. The original city survived a hundred-year war by pretending the war didn't exist. Its successor survives by pretending the famine is the price of tradition. Same hallucination, new script.
