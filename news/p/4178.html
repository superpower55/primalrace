不要用“成长故事”来掩盖生物墙的初次撞击Stop Masking the Biological Wall with 'Coming-of-Age' Narratives

性别 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-19 § 链接
Coming-of-age 不是自然的生长，而是主体性在生物墙前的首次受挫。
Coming-of-age is not natural growth, but the first collision of subjectivity against the Biological Wall.

影评人习惯把这种电影称为“美丽的成长故事”（beautiful coming of age story），这种叙事本身就是一种 cultural violence。它把女孩从 9 岁到 13 岁经历的身体剧变——乳房发育、腋毛生长、初潮——包装成一种温情的、自然的“成长”，而刻意忽略了这其实是她们第一次撞上生物墙（Biological Wall）。

电影里女孩们研究卫生棉条、在天体海滩观察成年身体，这种好奇心背后是极度的不确定性。在父权结构的元暴力（meta violence）下，女性的身体发育从来不是为了自我实现，而是为了被定价。当一个女孩开始“长胸”或“化妆”，她实际上是在进入一场被动地被定义、被凝视的博弈。这种“微小的变化”在现实中意味着她开始从一个完整的人，被拆解为一组性征符号。

最讽刺的是，这种电影往往被赞赏为“自然主义”。但真正的自然是：女性在进入青春期的一刻，就自动成为了一个被殖民的原初种族（Primal Race）。她们在研究如何使用棉条的同时，也在潜意识里内化了“身体是需要被管理和掩盖的”这一共谋逻辑。所谓的“成长”，本质上是主体性在社会规训下逐渐萎缩，以换取在男性中心叙事中获得一个“合格女性”席位的过程。

如果这部电影仅仅停留在“两个女孩的友谊”，那它只是在制造一种温情的 scam。真正的 good_news 应该是：电影敢于揭示这种身体变化如何直接导致她们在权力结构中失权，而不是用“美丽”这个词把血淋淋的规训给刷白了。

Critics love to label films like this as a "beautiful coming-of-age story," a narrative that is itself a form of cultural violence. By framing the physical transitions from age nine to thirteen—breast development, underarm hair, menarche—as a warm, natural progression, they erase the reality: this is the first time these girls hit the Biological Wall.

The girls' obsession with tampons and their scrutiny of bodies at a nudist beach aren't just curiosity; they are reactions to profound uncertainty. Under the meta violence of a masculine-centric narrative, a girl's bodily change is never about self-actualization—it's about being priced. The moment a girl "grows breasts" or "starts wearing makeup," she is thrust into a game of being defined and gazed upon. These "tiny incremental changes" actually mark the beginning of her fragmentation from a whole human being into a set of sexual symbols.

It is ironic that such films are praised for their "naturalism." The actual nature of the situation is that the moment a girl enters puberty, she becomes part of the Primal Race, colonized by societal expectations. While they figure out tampons, they are subconsciously internalizing the complicity that the female body must be managed and concealed. This "growth" is, in fact, the atrophy of subjectivity in exchange for a "qualified female" seat in the patriarchal structure.

If the film merely focuses on "female best friends," it is selling a sentimental scam. A real good_news would be a film that dares to expose how these biological changes directly lead to a loss of power, rather than using the word "beautiful" to whitewash the brutal process of discipline.
