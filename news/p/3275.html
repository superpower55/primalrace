无人机、炸弹与被当成耗材的“文明”叙事Drones, Bombs, and the Logistics of Civilized Violence

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-05 § 链接
战争叙事将身体降格为手段，而“关键基础设施”是元暴力的物理掩体。
War narratives degrade bodies into means, while 'critical infrastructure' serves as a physical shield for meta-violence.

在莱比锡机场发现携带爆炸物的无人机，这在新闻里被定义为“恐怖主义”或“国家恐怖主义”。但剥开这些 weaponized 的词汇，这不过是一场关于“谁能定义事实”的暴力博弈。机场被定义为 critical infrastructure，这个词本身就是一种结构性暴力：它将物理空间划分为“核心”与“边缘”，而在这个核心空间里，流动的不是人，而是军事物资和战争机器。

有趣的是，无人机被发现的位置恰好在乌克兰 Antonov 货机附近。那些写着“像布恰一样勇敢”的口号，在浪漫主义的叙事里是抵抗，但在元暴力的逻辑里，它们是精准的坐标。当一个国家将另一个国家的身体（无论是士兵还是平民）降格为可消耗的工具时，这种“勇敢”就成了战争叙事中被收割的燃料。无论是克里姆林宫的暗杀计划，还是北约的防卫逻辑，本质上都是男性中心叙事下的权力游戏——男人决定在哪里开战，男人决定谁被炸掉，然后用“文明”和“秩序”来掩盖这种最原始的、关于支配的暴力。

DHL 的 CEO 在财报电话会议里谈论“尽可能降低对客户的影响”，这种冷漠的专业主义正是典型的共谋者姿态。在他们眼中，爆炸物、暗杀和战争，不过是供应链上的一个 a-typical 波动。这种将暴力脱色、将死亡数据化的能力，正是 structural violence 的最高形式：它让人们相信，只要物流依然顺畅，世界依然在“正常”运转。

The discovery of an explosive drone at Leipzig airport is framed as 'terrorism' or 'state terrorism.' But strip away these weaponized terms, and it is merely a violent game of 'who defines reality.' The airport is classified as 'critical infrastructure'—a term that is itself a form of structural violence, partitioning physical space into 'core' and 'periphery,' where the flow consists not of humans, but of military hardware and war machinery.

Notably, the drone was found near a Ukrainian Antonov aircraft. Slogans like 'Be Brave Like Bucha' are framed as resistance in romantic narratives, but in the logic of meta-violence, they are precise coordinates. When a state degrades the bodies of another's people—soldiers or civilians—into consumable tools, such 'bravery' becomes the fuel for a war narrative. Whether it is the Kremlin's assassination plots or NATO's defense logic, both are power games under a masculine-centric narrative: men decide where to fight, men decide who gets blown up, and then use 'civilization' and 'order' to mask this primal violence of domination.

The CEO of DHL discussing 'minimizing influence on customers' during a quarterly call is a textbook example of the complicity posture. To them, explosives, assassinations, and war are merely a-typical fluctuations in the supply chain. This ability to bleach violence and quantify death is the ultimate form of structural violence: it convinces us that as long as the logistics remain smooth, the world is still operating 'normally.'
