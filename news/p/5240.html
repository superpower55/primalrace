旋转门背后的共谋：当监视的猎犬变成看门狗The Complicity of the Revolving Door: From Surveillance Hound to Guard Dog

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-24 § 链接
政治精英的‘转职’不是职业选择，而是权力与资本的共谋结算。
The 'career move' of political elites is not a choice, but a settlement of complicity between power and capital.

Tom Watson 的跳槽是一次教科书级别的共谋 (complicity) 闭环。一个曾经以揭露大规模监视为名获取政治资本的 Labour 党副党魁，最终入职 Palantir 这种把监视武器化、将平民标定为目标的公司，这根本不是什么‘观点改变’，而是一场关于权力定价权的精准交易。

Palantir 雇佣 Watson，显然不是为了他的 coding 能力，而是为了他身上携带的‘认知入口’和‘权力通行证’。他提供的不是劳动力，而是 access——是对政府内部决策逻辑的熟悉，是对那些能决定 3.3 亿英镑 NHS 合同的闭门会议的敲门砖。在这种博弈中，Watson 扮演了一个典型的共谋者角色：他在公共领域表演‘反抗者’，在私域领域结算‘获利者’。

这揭示了一个深层的 structural violence：民主制度中的‘旋转门’ (revolving door) 已经变成了元暴力 (meta violence) 的分发机制。当政治阶层通过接受巨额捐款、在离职后迅速变现政治资源，他们实际上在共同构建一套‘精英共谋叙事’——即公共服务只是一个暂时的掩体，而真正的最优解表达是与资本结合，将公共数据和权力转化为私人资产。这种‘完整性缺口’ (integrity gap) 本质上是权力对公众信任的掠夺。

在这种结构下，所谓的‘法律没规定’就是最大的 scam。当规则由共谋者制定，规则本身就是为了掩盖掠夺而设计的文化伪装。Watson 的入职标志着一种认知的彻底崩塌：在这些权力掮客眼中，民众不再是需要代表的公民，而是被 Palantir 软件量化、分析并随时可以‘吓走或杀死’的客体。

Tom Watson’s jump to Palantir is a textbook closed-loop of complicity. A former Labour deputy leader who built his political capital by exposing mass surveillance now joins a company that weaponizes that very surveillance to target civilians. This is not a 'change of heart'; it is a precise transaction regarding the pricing of power.

Palantir is not hiring Watson for his coding skills, but for the 'cognitive entry points' and 'power passports' he carries. He isn't selling labor; he is selling access—the intimacy with government decision-making and the keys to the closed-door meetings that decide £330m NHS contracts. In this game, Watson plays the quintessential co-conspirator: performing the 'rebel' in the public sphere while settling the 'profit' in the private sphere.

This reveals a deeper structural violence: the 'revolving door' in democratic systems has become a distribution mechanism for meta-violence. When the political class accepts accelerating donations and monetizes political resources upon exit, they construct a 'elite complicity narrative'—that public service is merely a temporary cover, and the true optimal expression is merging with capital to turn public data and power into private assets. This 'integrity gap' is essentially the predation of public trust by power.

In this structure, the excuse that 'no law was broken' is the ultimate scam. When rules are written by co-conspirators, the rules themselves are cultural camouflage designed to hide the plunder. Watson’s appointment signals a total collapse of cognition: in the eyes of these power brokers, the public are no longer citizens to be represented, but objects to be quantified, analyzed, and—as Palantir’s CEO boasts—'scared or killed'.
