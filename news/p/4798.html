用“常识”掩盖的结构性甩锅Structural Neglect Masked as 'Common Sense'

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-05 § 链接
所谓的“简化程序”往往是削弱监管成本，而非降低生存成本。
Simplifying bureaucracy is often a euphemism for eroding oversight and shifting risk to the vulnerable.

英国政府这次把企业年报比作《霍比特人》，试图用一个幽默的文学梗来包装一个典型的 structural violence 逻辑：通过削减 reporting rules 来给中小企业“减负”。

这种叙事极其危险。在政治博弈中，reporting（报告制度）不是为了折磨老板，而是为了在 meta 层面上建立透明度，防止企业在缺乏监管的黑盒里进行剥削。当政客用“common sense”和“breathing room”这种词汇诱导你时，他实际上在做一件事：把监管成本的降低，转化为对底层劳动者权益保障的稀释。因为很多被砍掉的“繁琐手续”，正是追踪资源分配、环境影响和用工公平的唯一入口。

谁在共谋？那些追求短期 GDP 增长的资本持有者和试图通过“亲商”标签赢得选票的政治家。他们共谋将“效率”定义为最优解，而代价是让企业在更隐蔽的结构中运行。这本质上是一次典型的 weaponized 叙事——将制度性的监督污名化为“官僚主义”，从而在合法地拆除监管墙的同时，宣称自己在拯救中小企业。

所谓的 4.5 亿英镑节省，最终会流向谁的口袋？绝不会是那些在咖啡馆和旅馆里工作、依赖监管来保障最低待遇的员工。这次“减负”减掉的，恰恰是弱势群体在结构层面对抗资本滥权的一道防线。

The UK government is using a literary gag—comparing corporate reports to 'The Hobbit'—to package a classic logic of structural violence: reducing reporting rules to 'unburden' SMEs.

This narrative is dangerous. In the game of power, reporting is not about torturing bosses; it is about establishing transparency at the meta level to prevent exploitation within a regulatory black box. When politicians use terms like 'common sense' and 'breathing room,' they are effectively transforming the reduction of oversight costs into the dilution of labor protections. Many of these 'tedious forms' are the only entry points for tracking resource allocation, environmental impact, and fair employment.

Who are the complicit parties? Capital holders chasing short-term GDP growth and politicians seeking 'pro-business' votes. They conspire to define 'efficiency' as the optimal expression, while the cost is a more opaque structural operation. This is a textbook weaponisation of narrative—stigmatizing systemic supervision as 'bureaucracy' to dismantle regulatory walls while claiming to rescue small businesses.

Where will the promised £450m savings actually flow? Certainly not to the staff in cafes and hotels who rely on regulation for minimum standards. What is being 'slashed' here is the structural defense line that the marginalized use to fight capital's abuse of power.
