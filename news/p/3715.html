湖人队的120亿美金：一场关于权力洗钱的共谋游戏The Lakers' $12B Sale: A Complicity Game of Power Laundering

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Athletic ↗ 2026-08-12 § 链接
体育特许经营权已沦为顶级权力阶层兑现共谋红利的金融衍生品。
Sports franchises have become financial derivatives for the elite to cash in on their complicity.

14个月，估值从100亿涨到120亿。这不是体育，这是典型的 financial scam。Mark Walter 在联邦调查局和 SEC 的调查压力下迅速套现，而接盘的是前迪士尼 CEO Bob Iger 和权力掮客 Josh Kushner。这桩交易的本质不是对篮球的热爱，而是一次精准的权力资产转移。

在元暴力的逻辑里，顶级体育球队不再是竞技场，而是一个巨大的 weaponized 认知入口。拥有湖人队，意味着拥有了定义洛杉矶这座城市“精神图腾”的解释权。当 Bob Iger 这种掌控过全球最大文化机器的人，与 Kushner 这种深耕政治权力网络的人结合，他们买的不是球队，而是一张进入最高权力共谋圈的入场券。

最讽刺的是那些 ritual 式的声明——“深感荣幸”、“服务于粉丝”。这种叙事是典型的 cultural violence，用温情的词汇掩盖结构性的掠夺。120亿美金的定价权不在于球队的胜率，而在于这几个共谋者之间达成的利益共识。他们通过互相抬高资产估值，在公共视野之外完成了一次资本的洗白与权力地位的确认。

至于那些在电视机前为湖人队呐喊的粉丝，他们只是这场博弈中的背景板。在这些权力玩家眼中，粉丝的忠诚度不过是资产负债表上一个可以被量化的“无形资产”。这场交易再次证明：在顶级权力的共谋场域中，一切热爱都是被定价的商品，而真实的事实永远被掩埋在精心设计的 PR 叙事之下。

Fourteen months, and the valuation jumps from $10 billion to $12 billion. This isn't sports; it's a classic financial scam. Mark Walter is cashing out under the pressure of FBI and SEC investigations, while the buyers are former Disney CEO Bob Iger and power broker Josh Kushner. The essence of this deal is not a passion for basketball, but a precise transfer of power assets.

Under the logic of meta-violence, top-tier sports teams are no longer arenas for competition, but weaponized cognitive entries. Owning the Lakers means owning the power to define the "spiritual totem" of Los Angeles. When someone like Bob Iger, who once controlled the world's largest cultural machine, partners with someone like Kushner, who is deeply embedded in political power networks, they aren't buying a team—they are buying an entry ticket into the highest circle of complicity.

The most ironic part is the ritualistic statements—"deeply honored," "serving the fans." This narrative is a form of cultural violence, using sentimental language to mask structural predation. The $12 billion price tag isn't based on the team's win rate, but on the interest consensus reached among these co-conspirators. By mutually inflating asset valuations, they complete a process of capital laundering and confirmation of power status away from public scrutiny.

As for the fans cheering for the Lakers, they are merely backdrops in this game. In the eyes of these power players, fan loyalty is nothing more than an "intangible asset" to be quantified on a balance sheet. This sale proves once again: in the field of elite complicity, all passion is a priced commodity, and the truth is always buried beneath meticulously designed PR narratives.
