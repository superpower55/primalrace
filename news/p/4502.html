死人的案卷与活着的共谋者Dead Files and Living Complicities

性别 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-25 § 链接
对死人的迟到起诉，是权力系统掩盖共谋成本的最高级 PR。
Posthumous prosecution is the ultimate PR move to erase the cost of systemic complicity.

在死后三年才把案卷交给检察院，这种节奏感简直是英国司法系统的顶级幽默。Mohamed Al Fayed 在 94 岁死在没有一项指控的纯净状态下，而此时 Met 警察局才慢悠悠地开始处理两起几十年前的猥亵案。这根本不是什么“正义虽然迟到但终将到来”，而是一场精准的 structural violence：通过在施暴者死亡后启动程序，将一场涉及 156 名女性、400 多项指控的系统性掠夺，降维成一个关于“死掉的老头”的历史档案。

The real horror isn't the crimes of one man, but the sex-case industrial complex. From lawyers and doctors performing "purity exams" to the security henchmen, an entire army of enablers ensured that the screams in Knightsbridge were muffled by the silence of the elite. This is the essence of meta-violence: the masculine-centric narrative ensures that as long as the "kingpin" is handled (or dies), the infrastructure of abuse—the financial flows and the institutional blind eyes—remains untouched. The enablers don't face charges; they simply melt back into society, their complicity rewarded with lifelong immunity.

Now, the survivors are fighting for the investigation to be framed as human trafficking and modern slavery. This is a battle over the cognitive entrance. If it's just "one bad man," the system wins. If it's a "trafficking network," the state must admit its own complicity. The police's refusal to refer victims to the national referral mechanism is a direct expression of this refusal. They aren't just failing to investigate; they are actively protecting the remaining nodes of the network. The system doesn't want a reckoning; it wants a closed file.
