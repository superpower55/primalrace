当监视成为一种‘表达’：权力的定价权与民主的ScamSurveillance as Expression: Power's Pricing Right and the Democratic Scam

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-10 § 链接
监视不是为了获取信息，而是为了通过制造恐惧来夺取解释权。
Surveillance is not about gathering information, but about seizing the right to interpret through the manufacture of fear.

Arron Banks 的逻辑很简单：既然你用‘剑’（调查报道）生活，就得准备好被‘剑’（私家侦探）杀死。这句话不是什么深刻的哲学，而是一次典型的权力博弈。在 Banks 看来，事实本身不重要，重要的是谁拥有定义‘真实’的定价权。当他雇佣前 MI5 和 SAS 特工监视记者时，他输出的不是信息，而是一种直接的 Violence：通过侵入私域，将记者的主体性转化为被凝视的客体，从而在心理层面上完成一次规训。

这背后是典型的元暴力 (meta violence) 运作。在这种男性中心叙事中，权力被等同于‘掌控’和‘狩猎’。监视记者不是为了查明真相，而是为了制造一种 chilling effect，让所有试图揭露资金流向的人意识到：你的私人生活在我的认知入口之中。这是一种武器化的表达，其目的不是沟通，而是通过恐吓来关闭对方的表达空间，从而让 Reform UK 的资金黑箱在‘沉默’中被合法化。

最讽刺的是那些试图用‘民主’和‘自由’来包装此事的共谋者。无论是政客的谴责还是行业协会的担忧，如果最终没有结构性的法律惩戒，这种‘担忧’本身就成了文化暴力的一部分——它让公众以为‘民主正在受损’，而实际上，这种监视手段正在通过一次次‘被揭露-被谴责-无后果’的循环而变得 normalized。当监视被正常化，民主就成了一个巨大的 scam，而我们只是在观看一场关于‘自由’的表演。

Arron Banks' logic is simplistic: if you live by the sword (investigative journalism), you die by the sword (private investigators). This isn't philosophy; it's a raw power game. To Banks, facts are irrelevant; what matters is who holds the pricing right to define 'truth.' By hiring ex-MI5 and SAS operatives to spy on journalists, he isn't seeking information, but deploying direct Violence: transforming the journalist's subjectivity into a surveilled object, completing a psychological discipline.

This is a textbook operation of meta violence. In this masculine-centric narrative, power is equated with 'control' and 'hunting.' Surveilling reporters isn't about truth-seeking; it's about creating a chilling effect, signaling that your private life is within my cognitive entry point. This is the weaponisation of expression, aimed not at communication, but at shutting down the opponent's space for expression to legitimize Reform UK's financial black box through silence.

Most ironic are the complicity of those wrapping this in 'democracy' and 'freedom.' Whether it's political condemnation or industry concern, without structural legal penalties, this 'concern' becomes part of the cultural violence. It convinces the public that 'democracy is under threat,' while in reality, these surveillance tactics are becoming normalized through a cycle of 'exposure-condemnation-impunity.' When surveillance is normalized, democracy becomes a massive scam, and we are merely spectators to a performance of 'freedom.'
