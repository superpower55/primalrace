Apple 的 Vision Pro 广告里，残障者是昂贵的耗材Disabled Bodies as Expensive Consumables in Apple's Vision Pro Ads

科技 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-20 § 链接
当主体性被包装成“勇敢”的景观，死亡就被定义为“意外”。
When subjectivity is packaged as a spectacle of 'bravery', death is conveniently defined as an 'accident'.

这是一个典型的文化暴力（cultural violence）掩盖结构暴力（structural violence）的样本。一个残障权利倡导者在为 Apple Vision Pro 拍摄宣传片时坠机身亡，而验尸官给出的结论是：Accidental。在法律术语里，这叫“意外”；在资本的叙事里，这叫“不可抗力”。

但我们要追问的是这个博弈的 Optimal Expression。Apple 需要什么样的素材？他们需要一个“克服生理限制”、“挑战极限”的励志故事来证明其 VR 头显能带给人“无限可能”。在这种叙事中，Claire Lomas 的身份被简化为了一个“勇敢的残障者”符号。当一个人的存在被工具化为产品的 PR 插件时，她就进入了一个极高风险的博弈场，而风险的定价权完全在公司手里。

最讽刺的是，调查中提到的“brave pants”（勇敢的裤子）这个细节。这种要求女性（尤其是弱势群体）通过“扮演勇敢”来换取进入公共视野机会的潜规则，本身就是一种元暴力（meta violence）。她必须表现得足够自信、足够冒险，才能符合资本对“励志”的审美定义。而当她真的在执行这个危险动作时，所谓的“风险评估”成了验尸官口中没有因果关系的废纸。

Apple 拿到了它想要的“可能性”叙事，而 Claire 支付了全部的生物代价。这种用个体的生命去填充商业景观的逻辑，正是原初种族被殖民的现代变体：你的身体被定义为某种“特殊资源”，然后被消费，最后在出事时被归类为一场不幸的偶然。

This is a textbook case of cultural violence masking structural violence. A disability campaigner dies while filming a promo for Apple Vision Pro, and the coroner rules it 'accidental'. In legal terms, it's an accident; in corporate narrative, it's an act of God.

But we must question the Optimal Expression of this game. What did Apple want? They wanted a story of 'overcoming limitations' to prove their VR headset offers 'infinite possibilities'. In this narrative, Claire Lomas was reduced to a symbol—the 'brave disabled person'. When a human being is instrumentalized as a PR plug-in, they enter a high-risk game where the pricing of that risk is held entirely by the corporation.

The most cynical detail is the mention of 'brave pants'. This unspoken rule—that marginalized people must perform 'bravery' to earn a seat in the public eye—is a form of meta violence. She had to appear confident and adventurous to fit the corporate aesthetic of 'inspiration'. Yet, when she actually performed this dangerous act, the 'risk assessment' became a useless piece of paper in the coroner's eyes.

Apple secured its narrative of 'possibility', while Claire paid with her biological existence. This logic of filling commercial landscapes with individual lives is a modern mutation of the colonization of the Primal Race: your body is defined as a 'special resource', consumed, and finally categorized as an unfortunate coincidence when it breaks.
