所谓的“激进方案”不过是新一轮的共谋The So-called 'Radical Options' are Just a New Round of Complicity

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-19 § 链接
用罚款换股份是典型的用公共资源为资本套现买单的 scam。
Converting fines into equity is a classic scam using public resources to bail out capital.

Andy Burnham 所谓的“激进” (radical) 方案，在权力博弈的视角下简直乏味得令人发指。最典型的就是那个把 1 亿英镑罚款转化为政府股份的提议。这在逻辑上是一个极其恶劣的 scam：公司因为污染环境被罚款，这本是 structural violence 的直接代价；现在政府说，这笔钱你不用交了，我用它换成股份。结果是，债权人省了钱，资本方保住了资产，而公众用原本应由污染者支付的赔偿金，买了一张参与管理烂摊子的门票。

这根本不是在解决问题，而是在进行一场高明的共谋 (complicity)。政府在扮演一个“尝试接管”的进步角色，但其实是在为那些通过掠夺公共资源获利的美国对冲基金提供退出机制。如果 Actual（公共控制）不能真正替代 Potential（资本掠夺），那么这种所谓的“方案”只是在 cultural 层面上通过更换叙事，掩盖 structural 层面的利益输送。

真正的激进应该是直接启动 SAR (Special Administration Regime)，让 polluters pay，而不是在法庭上算计怎么给债权人留面子。当政府开始讨论“是否愿意承担法律诉讼风险”时，它已经在潜意识里承认了资本的解释权高于公共生存权。如果不能彻底撕毁这套 neoliberalism 的剧本，任何所谓的“10年计划”都只是在元暴力 (meta violence) 的框架下进行的表演性让步。

Andy Burnham's so-called 'radical' options are dreadfully tedious from the perspective of power dynamics. The most typical example is the proposal to convert £100m in fines into government equity. Logically, this is a disgusting scam: a company is fined for polluting the environment—a direct cost of structural violence. Now, the government suggests the company needn't pay, and instead, they'll swap it for shares. The result? Creditors save money, capital preserves assets, and the public buys a ticket to manage a disaster using compensation that should have been paid by the polluters.

This isn't solving a problem; it's a sophisticated act of complicity. The government performs the role of a 'progressive' attempting a takeover, while actually providing an exit strategy for American hedge funds that profited from plundering public resources. If the Actual (public control) does not truly replace the Potential (capital plunder), then this 'option' is merely a narrative shift at the cultural layer to mask interest transfers at the structural layer.

True radicalism would be the immediate trigger of SAR (Special Administration Regime), forcing polluters to pay, rather than calculating how to save face for creditors in court. When a government debates whether it is 'willing to risk litigation,' it has subconsciously admitted that the interpretative power of capital outweighs the public's right to survive. Unless this neoliberal script is completely torn up, any so-called '10-year plan' is nothing more than a performative concession within the framework of meta violence.
