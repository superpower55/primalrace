把小学变成“失业预警站”：一场关于失败的提前定价Turning Primary Schools into Unemployment Warning Stations: Pre-pricing Failure

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-07 § 链接
用未来的失业率给儿童贴标签，是结构性暴力对生命主体的提前殖民。
Labeling children with future unemployment rates is the colonization of human subjectivity by structural violence.

这套逻辑简直是典型的 weaponized 叙事。政府把“失业”定义为一种可以从学前教育阶段就预测的“病症”，然后要求小学教师扮演诊断医生的角色。这根本不是在提供 support，而是在进行一次残酷的提前定价：在孩子还没学会独立思考之前，系统就根据贫困、心理健康或家庭背景，给他们打上了“未来失败者”的标签。

这在加尔通的暴力三角中是标准的 structural violence。系统不去解决导致失业的结构性原因——比如糟糕的就业市场、匮乏的社会资源分配——反而要求学校在 cultural 层面上完成一次“风险识别”。这种“识别”本质上是一种预判性的规训，它把个体的生命轨迹简化为一组与 NEET（不就业、不教育、不培训）相关的相关性数据。当一个孩子被标记为“at risk”时，他面对的不再是教育，而是一套旨在将其强行修正为“有用劳动力”的干预机制。

最讽刺的是，这种做法在共谋。政府通过这种方式将“失业危机”的责任从政策失败转移到了个体的“早期迹象”上。如果一个人 16 岁失业了，系统可以说：“看，我们在他 5 岁时就预见到了，我们已经尽力干预了。”这是一种极其阴险的 meta violence：它垄断了对“成功”和“失败”的解释权，将社会契约的崩溃包装成个体的生物性或背景性缺陷。

所谓的“turbocharge education”不过是一个 scam。真正的最优解表达应该是保障每个孩子无论背景如何都能拥有同等的潜在状态（Potential），而不是在他们还处于生物墙最柔软的时期，就用一套名为“预警”的枷锁，把他们的 Actual 提前锁定在一个被系统定义好的低端轨道上。

This logic is a textbook example of weaponized narrative. The government defines 'unemployment' as a 'symptom' predictable from preschool, requiring primary teachers to act as diagnostic physicians. This isn't about providing support; it's a brutal pre-pricing of human life: labeling children as 'future failures' based on poverty, mental health, or family background before they even learn to think independently.

In the Violence Triangle, this is pure structural violence. Instead of addressing the root causes of unemployment—such as a broken job market or systemic resource maldistribution—the system demands a cultural layer of 'risk identification.' This 'identification' is a preemptive discipline, reducing a life trajectory to a set of correlations with NEET status. Once a child is flagged as 'at risk,' they no longer receive education; they enter an intervention mechanism designed to force them into the mold of a 'useful laborer.'

More cynically, this is a game of complicity. The government shifts the responsibility for the unemployment crisis from policy failure to the 'early signs' of the individual. If someone is unemployed at 16, the system can claim: 'We saw this coming at age 5, and we tried to intervene.' This is a form of meta violence: it monopolizes the interpretation of 'success' and 'failure,' packaging the collapse of the social contract as a biological or background deficiency.

The promise to 'turbocharge education' is a scam. The true Optimal Expression would be ensuring every child has equal Potential regardless of background, not using a shackle called 'warning' to lock their Actual into a low-end track defined by the system while they are still at their most vulnerable.
