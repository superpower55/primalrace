骂影评人救不了票房Insulting Critics Won't Save the Box Office

文娱 文化层 Deadline ↗ 2026-11-01 § 链接
解释权抢不回来，票房也抢不回来。
You can't win back the box office by seizing the narrative.

一部电影扑了，导演的第一反应是宣布影评人不存在。Taika Waititi 说他已经快十五年没读过任何针对他和他同行的评论，但他听人民的；然后他把给了 26% 新鲜度的那些人称为“可悲的迷失灵魂”，说他们当年也没做成艺术家，所以当了影评人。Irrelevant，out of touch，obsolete，in the bin。

这套话术的结构很清楚：把评价和事实切开。观众评分 78%（现在 73%）是真的，影评人 26% 是假的；人民是真的，专业是假的。可票房不认这套。据报道 2000 万美元预算，首周末国内 701K，索尼把公映规模砍到 880 家影院。你可以把批评者扔进垃圾桶，但垃圾桶不买票。

第三章讲的就是这个：谁掌握认知入口，谁就能制造“什么是事实”。Waititi 现在做的是反向操作——他手里没有入口了，于是试图把入口本身宣布为无效。这不是武器化表达，这是武器化表达打输了之后的嘴硬。真正掌握解释权的人不需要说“irrelevant”，他说了，就等于承认那些评论扎到了他。

更值得看的是他用的那个身份。“没能当成艺术家所以去当影评人”——这句话把批评重新定义成失败者的嫉妒，于是批评的内容就不用回应了。这是最省力的一种防御：不反驳论点，只贬低说话的人。他自己也承认，他听的是“人民”。人民是谁？是那个 73%。用一个人数当论据，和用一个评分当论据，是同一种东西，只是前者更好卖。

Deadline 自己的影评写的是，这本石黑一雄的小说本该很合他的路子，但电影“在它承诺的每一个方向上都交付不了”。这句话才是 Waititi 该回应的东西——不是“你们恨 Jojo Rabbit”，而是“这次你为什么没做到”。他没有回应，因为回应需要承认有一个可以被做好的标准存在，而他刚刚宣布那个标准过时了。

“Obsolete”这个词用得很有意思。一个导演说影评过时了，等于说作品不需要被判断。但作品是要被卖出去的，701K 就是判断。他可以继续不读评论，十五年不读也行；只是那些评论正在替他读票房。

A film flops, and the director's first move is to declare the critics nonexistent. Taika Waititi says he hasn't read a review aimed at him or his peers in almost fifteen years, but he listens to the people — and then calls the ones who gave his movie a 26% critics score "sad lost souls" who couldn't become artists, so they became critics instead. Irrelevant. Out of touch. Obsolete. In the bin.

The structure is clear: separate evaluation from fact. The 78% audience score (now 73%) is real, the 26% critics score is fake; the people are real, the professionals are fake. The box office doesn't buy it. A reported $20M budget, a $701K opening weekend domestic, and Sony cutting the wide release to 880 theaters. You can put the critics in the bin. The bin doesn't buy tickets.

This is Chapter 3 running in reverse. Whoever controls the cognitive entry point gets to manufacture what counts as fact. Waititi no longer holds the entry point, so he's trying to declare the entry point itself invalid. That isn't weaponized expression — it's the sulk that follows losing at weaponized expression. Someone who actually holds the power of interpretation doesn't need to say "irrelevant." Saying it is the admission that the reviews landed.

What's worth watching is the identity move. "Couldn't become artists, so they became critics" redefines criticism as the envy of failures, which means the content of the criticism never has to be answered. It's the cheapest defense available: don't rebut the argument, just demean the person making it. He listens to "the people." Who are the people? The 73%. Using a headcount as evidence and using a score as evidence are the same move; the headcount just sells better.

Deadline's own review says the Ishiguro novel should have been a snug fit for him, but the film "doesn't deliver on any of the fronts it promises." That's the thing he should be answering — not "you hated Jojo Rabbit," but "why didn't you pull this off." He doesn't answer it, because answering requires admitting that a standard exists by which the work can be judged, and he has just declared that standard obsolete.

"Obsolete" is the tell. A director saying criticism is obsolete is saying his work doesn't need to be judged. But the work is being sold, and $701K is the judgment. He can keep not reading reviews, for another fifteen years if he likes. The reviews are reading his box office for him.
