布鲁塞尔的巨型煎锅：一个关于权力共谋的混凝土纪念碑Brussels' Giant Frying Pan: A Concrete Monument to Political Complicity

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-09 § 链接
当象征意义被预算和政治内耗吞噬，剩下的只有结构性的荒诞。
When symbolism is devoured by budgets and political deadlock, only structural absurdity remains.

舒曼广场原本被设计成一个“城市广场”(urban agora)，一个象征欧洲统一心脏的绿色空间。结果呢？它成了一个巨大的混凝土煎锅。一个原本用来遮阳避雨、让欧盟官员和普通民众交流的钢制顶棚，因为预算超支和比利时那令人发指的政治内耗被砍掉了。结果就是：人们在烈日下面对着一块昂贵的灰色铺装地，感受着所谓的“欧洲项目”的温度。

这件事最精彩的部分在于它的 Complicity（共谋）机制。从 2015 年启动到 2023 年动工，再到 2024 年选举后的 613 天政治僵局，这个项目经历了一场完美的权力博弈。官员们在乎的是如何不丢失 EU 的恢复基金，而不是广场是否真的能给市民提供阴凉。为了在截止日期前把钱花掉，他们选择砍掉最核心的功能性组件——那个顶棚。这是一种典型的 Structural Violence（结构暴力）：资源被分配了，钱被花掉了，但实际的 Potential（潜在的公共福祉）与 Actual（实际的混凝土废墟）之间的差额，就是这场政治闹剧留给市民的暴力。

比利时总理 Bart De Wever 把布鲁塞尔称为“失败国家”(failed state)，这种指责本身就是一种 Weaponized Expression（武器化表达）。他通过攻击布鲁塞尔的“乞讨”行为来确立自己的财政保守主义人设，而不在意那个在 6 月热浪中被晒干的广场。在这些政客的博弈中，广场的“象征意义”只是他们用来争夺话语权的筹码，而非真正服务于人的空间。

现在，政府承诺在 2028 年之前把它变得“尽可能绿”。这种 PR 话术我们听得太多了。在元暴力的叙事里，只要宣布一个新计划，之前的失败就可以被掩盖。但事实是，那个巨大的煎锅已经在那儿了，它精准地记录了权力如何通过共谋，将一个理想的公共空间简化为一项财务报表上的勾选任务。

Schuman Square was envisioned as an 'urban agora,' a green heart symbolizing European unity. The reality? A giant concrete frying pan. A steel canopy designed to provide shade and shelter—the project's crowning glory—was scrapped due to budget overruns and Belgium's abysmal political gridlock. The result is a vast, grey expanse where citizens bake under the July sun, experiencing the actual 'temperature' of the European project.

The most fascinating part is the mechanism of Complicity. From the 2015 launch to the 613-day political deadlock following the 2024 elections, this project was a masterclass in power games. Officials cared more about not losing EU recovery funds than whether the square actually provided shade. To meet the spending deadline, they cut the most critical functional component. This is textbook Structural Violence: resources were allocated, money was spent, but the gap between the Potential (public wellbeing) and the Actual (a concrete wasteland) is the violence left behind by this political farce.

Prime Minister Bart De Wever's branding of Brussels as a 'failed state' is a clear piece of Weaponized Expression. He used the 'embarrassment' of begging for funds to solidify his persona as a fiscal conservative, ignoring the people baking on that square during the June heatwave. In the game of these politicians, the 'symbolism' of the square is merely a chip for discourse, not a space designed for humans.

Now, the government promises to make it 'as green as possible' by 2028. We've seen this PR scam before. Within the masculine-centric narrative of power, announcing a new plan is the standard way to erase previous failures. But the giant frying pan is already there, precisely recording how power, through complicity, reduces an ideal public space to a mere check-box exercise on a financial statement.
