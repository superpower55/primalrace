电话集会救不了饥饿罢工Phone Rallies Don't Feed a Hunger Strike

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-30 § 链接
一场取消的集会是表演，一场饥饿罢工是暴力
A cancelled rally is theater; a hunger strike is violence.

特朗普取消爱荷华和堪萨斯的集会，改成电话集会，RNC 说这样能"最大化"他接触全国选民的能力。十月他已经办了 25 场，自己定过的目标是 30 场以上，没做到。换一个说法，25 场就成了"冲刺"。这是第三章的老把戏：现实不配合，就重新定义现实。做不完的集会不是失败，是策略升级；问一句为什么取消的记者，得到的回答是"你问这个问题很病态"。

但真正值得看的是这条新闻的另一半。德州雷蒙德维尔的 El Valle 拘留中心，被关押的移民在进行长期绝食，说医疗条件不达标、水不能喝、看守虐待他们，还威胁强制灌食。七名民主党议员联名给国土安全部和 ICE 写信，要求交代。信里写的是"严重漠视我们社区的健康、尊严、人格和安全"。这是 structural 层的暴力在动：谁被关、关多久、能不能喝到水、绝食之后会不会被绑起来灌食，全部由一套不对被关押者负责的行政机器决定。

两件事放在同一篇报道里，不是巧合。一个政治人物可以把集会从线下搬到线上，把 25 场说成冲刺，因为他的表达空间是弹性的、可谈判的、随时能重新定价的。而被关在拘留中心里的人，绝食是他们仅剩的表达方式——身体是最后的筹码，而这个筹码正在被"强制灌食"的威胁回收。同一套国家机器，对前者提供电话线路，对后者提供灌食管。

报道里还有一个细节：Tom Hoyle 在福克斯新闻直播里把特朗普的支持者比作邪教，随后被三名特勤局探员带离现场。一个普通人说了一句话，被请出去；总统被问了一个问题，骂记者有病。表达的定价权从来不在说话的人手里。

最刺眼的是报道没写的东西：那些绝食的人叫什么名字，绝食了多少天，有没有人已经倒下。议员的信有落款，拘留中心有地址，被关押者只有"some hunger strikers"这个复数。他们在这篇报道里的表达，是被压缩成一行摘要的。谁的名字没出现，谁就在付代价。

Trump cancelled rallies in Iowa and Kansas and swapped them for tele-rallies. The RNC says this "maximizes" his ability to reach voters nationwide. He had done 25 rallies in October against a goal he once set above 30. Rename it a "72-hour sprint" and the shortfall becomes a strategy. This is the oldest move in the book: when reality won't cooperate, redefine reality. A reporter who asks why the Kansas rally was cancelled gets told, "You're sick to ask that question."

The other half of the same blog is where the actual violence sits. At the El Valle detention facility in Raymondville, Texas, detained immigrants are on a prolonged hunger strike. They say medical care is substandard, the water is undrinkable, guards mistreat them, and officials are threatening force-feeding in retaliation. Seven congressional Democrats sent a letter to DHS and ICE demanding answers, writing that the department shows "gross disregard for the health, dignity, personhood, and safety of our communities." This is structural violence in motion: who gets detained, for how long, whether they can drink the water, whether a hunger strike ends with a tube down the throat — all decided by an administrative machine that answers to no one inside the cage.

These two stories share a page because they share a mechanism. A politician can move a rally from a stage to a phone line and call 25 events a sprint, because his expressive space is elastic, negotiable, repriced at will. The people on hunger strike have their bodies left as the only expression available — and that last筹码 is being threatened with a feeding tube. Same state machinery. Phone lines for one, tubes for the other.

One more detail: Tom Hoyle compared Trump supporters to a cult on live Fox News and was removed from the venue by three Secret Service agents. A private citizen says one sentence and is escorted out; the president is asked one question and calls the reporter sick. The pricing power over speech never belongs to the speaker.

What the report does not say is the sharpest part: not one hunger striker's name, not how many days, not whether anyone has collapsed. The lawmakers' letter has signatories. The facility has an address. The strikers have "some." Their expression in this story is compressed into a single line of summary. Whoever's name is missing is the one paying.
