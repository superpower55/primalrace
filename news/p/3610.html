餐车里的总统与被当作诱饵的记者The President in a Catering Cart and the Journalists as Decoys

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-11 § 链接
权力最极致的表达，是将他人物化为掩护自己的生物盾牌。
The ultimate expression of power is the objectification of others into biological shields.

躲在餐车里潜逃，这出戏码充满了某种低劣的间谍片质感，但其内核却是极其精准的权力博弈。Trump 在这里完成了一次教科书式的“表达武器化”：他通过在镜头前登上旧版 Air Force One，制造了一个一个关于“怀旧”和“传统”的叙事入口，而这个叙事在此时此刻唯一的用途，就是把随行记者和部分白宫工作人员变成一个巨大的、活生生的 decoy（诱饵）。

在这个 deception operation 中，记者的主体性被彻底抹除，他们不再是信息的记录者，而成了掩护总统逃生的生物墙。最讽刺的是，当记者询问为何要关闭窗帘时，Trump 那句 “But if I go, you go. Right?” 并非在表达共情，而是在完成一次权力确认——他定义了这种共谋关系的代价：你们的生命价值被定价为我安全逃离的必要成本。这是一种极端的 masculine-centric narrative：强者决定生存策略，弱者在不知情的情况下参与这场博弈并承担风险。

白宫后来的 PR 声明试图将此包装成“应对敌人的必要工具”，但这恰恰揭示了结构层面的暴力。当一个国家的最高权力者将自己的随行人员——这些在制度上处于从属地位的人——在潜意识中视为可消耗的资源时，这种“物化”逻辑与元暴力是同构的。在这种叙事里，只有总统的 an actual existence 是真实的，其余所有人的存在仅仅是为了维持这个真实而存在的背景板。

Hiding in a catering cart feels like a plot from a low-budget spy movie, but the core is a precise game of power. Trump executed a textbook weaponisation of expression: by boarding the old Air Force One in front of cameras, he manufactured a narrative of "nostalgia" and "tradition." The sole purpose of this narrative was to turn the accompanying journalists and staff into a massive, living decoy.

In this deception operation, the subjectivity of the journalists was completely erased. They were no longer recorders of information, but a biological wall shielding the president's escape. The most cynical moment is Trump's response to the window-shade query: "But if I go, you go. Right?" This isn't empathy; it is a confirmation of power. He defined the price of this complicity: your life is valued only as the necessary cost for my safe exit. This is pure masculine-centric narrative: the strong dictate the survival strategy, while the weak bear the risk in an ignorant game.

The White House PR attempt to frame this as a "necessary tool against enemies" reveals the structural violence at play. When the highest power in a nation subconsciously treats subordinates as consumable resources, this logic of objectification is isomorphic with meta-violence. In this narrative, only the president's actual existence is real; everyone else is merely a backdrop maintained to sustain that reality.
