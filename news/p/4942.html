加沙：一场关于“可消耗人口”的全球R&D实验Gaza: A Global R&D Experiment in 'Surplus Populations'

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-11 § 链接
当屠杀被定义为“测试”，文明就成了元暴力的掩体。
When massacre is defined as 'testing', civilization becomes a shield for meta-violence.

Ben-Gvir 那个 AI 视频不是什么疯子的臆想，而是一次极其诚实的表达：在元暴力 (meta violence) 的逻辑里，巴勒斯坦人的生命被定价为零，或者说是负数。当一个国家的安全部长公开讨论用鳄鱼巡逻监狱、在行刑场设置观众席时，他其实是在向全球的共谋者 (complicit) 发出信号：这里的生物墙 (biological wall) 已经失效，这些人口被定义为“冗余”。

最令人作呕的不是这种直接暴力 (direct violence)，而是这种暴力是如何被 weaponized 的。西方民主国家在公共空间表演“人权”和“法律”，在私下里却把加沙当作一个巨大的 R&D 实验室。以色列的监控技术、审讯手段、人群控制方案，在加沙被“试用”并验证有效后，再打包出售给全球的权力持有者。这才是真正的 structural violence：将一个种族地狱化，将其转化为一种可量化的“治理产品”。

很多人天真地认为，只要真相被 livestreamed，只要道德共识达成，结构就会改变。但事实是，当权力者掌握了定义“真实”的定价权，真相反而成了某种点缀。西方政府对加沙的纵容证明了：只要这个“模板”对维持全球统治结构有利，那么所谓的“西方价值”不过是掩盖元暴力的 PR 话术。这不再是关于领土的争端，而是一场关于“谁可以被合法地抹除”的意识形态战争。

Ben-Gvir's AI video isn't the hallucination of a madman; it is a brutally honest expression. In the logic of meta-violence, Palestinian lives are priced at zero, or even negative. When a security minister openly discusses crocodile-patrolled moats and spectator booths at execution sites, he is signaling to global complicity: the biological wall here has collapsed, and this population is defined as 'surplus'.

What is truly nauseating is not the direct violence, but how this violence is weaponized. Western democracies perform 'human rights' and 'rule of law' in public, while treating Gaza as a massive R&D laboratory in private. Israeli surveillance, interrogation, and population-control techniques are 'field-tested' in Gaza, validated, and then packaged for sale to power-holders worldwide. This is the essence of structural violence: turning a racial hell into a quantifiable 'governance product'.

Many naively believe that once the truth is livestreamed and a moral consensus is reached, the structure will shift. But the reality is that when those in power hold the pricing power over 'truth', facts become mere ornaments. The West's enablement of Gaza proves that as long as this 'blueprint' serves the global power structure, 'Western values' are nothing more than PR rhetoric to mask meta-violence. This is no longer a territorial dispute, but an ideological war over who can be legally erased.
