换一张脸，换不掉那套叙事A New Face Doesn't Change the Story

文娱 文化层 A.V. Club ↗ 2026-11-02 § 链接
新闻摘要漫威影业正在为重启版《X战警》组建新阵容，本·王（Ben Wang）将出演冰人（Iceman）。此前已公布的人选包括萨迪·辛克饰演琴·葛蕾、基特·康纳饰演镭射眼、克里斯托弗·阿博特饰演X教授、亚当·德赖弗饰演凶兆先生等。
The newsMarvel Studios is casting its X-Men reboot, with Ben Wang set to play Iceman. Already announced: Sadie Sink as Jean Grey, Kit Connor as Cyclops, Christopher Abbott as Professor X, and Adam Driver as Mister Sinister.
选角新闻不是没有政治，是政治被包装成了没有。
Casting news isn't apolitical. The politics is just packaged as none.

这条新闻里没有人在付代价，所以我本来该跳过它。但「选角」这件事本身，是表达武器化的一个标准入口，值得说两句。

漫威这次重启《X战警》，公布了一串名字：萨迪·辛克演琴·葛蕾，基特·康纳演镭射眼，萨玛拉·维文演白皇后，亚当·德赖弗演凶兆先生，本·王演冰人。报道把这些称为「集中轰炸式的选角公告」，语气是兴奋的。

兴奋是对的，但兴奋的对象值得追问。这一整套公告在做什么？它在制造「我们听到了」的幻觉。变种人这个IP从诞生起就在讲被排斥的族群，X教授和万磁王的分歧是马丁·路德·金和马尔科姆·X的漫画版。现在迪士尼把这个故事重新打包，用一串多元的演员名单告诉观众：看，我们很进步。

问题在于，名单是表达，不是结构。换掉几张脸，不改变谁在写剧本、谁在定档期、谁在分票房。报道里提到《复仇者联盟：末日》会是福克斯宇宙的「最后一次亮相」，伊恩·麦克莱恩、帕特里克·斯图尔特这些老面孔回来告别——这是一场精心设计的怀旧仪式，目的是让观众在情感上完成交接，然后继续买票。

第三章讲武器化，最常见的入口就是「制造可能性」。迪士尼不是在拍一部关于被排斥者的电影，它是在用被排斥者的符号做营销。变种人的痛苦被提炼成一个卖点，就像钻石被提炼成「恒久远」。

真正的问题不是本·王演不演冰人。是这套叙事里，谁在定义「多元」，谁在决定「进步」长什么样，以及当这些定义被写进合同和财报的时候，观众拿到的是表达，还是被表达。

报道没写的是：这些演员签的是什么合约，片酬结构如何，创作团队里有多少人真正参与决策。选角公告只给你看脸，不给你看权力。

No one is paying a price in this story, so I should skip it. But casting itself is a standard entry point for weaponized expression, and it's worth two minutes.

Marvel is rebooting X-Men, and the announcement rolls out a list of names: Sadie Sink as Jean Grey, Kit Connor as Cyclops, Samara Weaving as Emma Frost, Adam Driver as Mister Sinister, Ben Wang as Iceman. The report calls it a "concentrated blast of X-Men casting announcements." The tone is excitement.

Excitement is fine. But what exactly is being excited about? The whole rollout is manufacturing the feeling of being heard. The X-Men have always been about excluded peoples—the Xavier/Magneto split is the comic-book version of Martin Luther King and Malcolm X. Now Disney repackages that story and uses a diverse cast list to tell audiences: look, we're progressive.

The problem is that a cast list is expression, not structure. Swapping faces doesn't change who writes the scripts, who sets the release dates, who splits the box office. The report notes that Avengers: Doomsday will be the Fox universe's "final appearance," with Ian McKellen and Patrick Stewart returning for one last goodbye—a carefully staged nostalgia ritual designed to move the audience emotionally through the handoff, then keep them buying tickets.

Chapter Three is about weaponization, and the most common entry point is manufacturing possibility. Disney isn't making a film about the excluded. It's using the symbols of the excluded as marketing. The pain of mutants gets distilled into a selling point, the way diamonds get distilled into "forever."

The real question isn't whether Ben Wang plays Iceman. It's who defines "diverse" in this narrative, who decides what "progress" looks like, and when those definitions get written into contracts and earnings reports, whether the audience is getting expression or being expressed.

What the report doesn't say: what contracts these actors signed, how the pay structure works, how many people on the creative team actually make decisions. A casting announcement shows you faces. It doesn't show you power.
