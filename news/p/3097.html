用“战争”叙事掩盖的结构性怠政Structural Negligence Masked by the Narrative of 'War'

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-01 § 链接
将治理失败包装成“英雄之战”，是典型的文化暴力掩体。
Packaging governance failure as a 'heroic battle' is a classic shield of cultural violence.

这篇报道在视觉上极其成功地构建了一场“英雄主义”叙事：疲惫的消防员、战壕般的防火带、以及在绝望中凝视深渊的男性群体。但只要你把目光从那些“被烟灰覆盖的脸”移开，就能看到一个典型的结构性暴力现场。所谓的“megafire”并非不可抗力的天灾，而是一场由电力设备短路诱发、由资源配置失效加剧的治理危机。

最荒谬的共谋发生在权力顶层。马克龙宣布现在不是“分析教训”的时候，因为正处于“全面战斗”中。这种将行政失职（如2017年起就被警告的飞机更新问题）定义为“战争”的手段，就是典型的武器化表达。通过引入“战争”这个认知入口，统治者成功地将讨论从“为什么资源不足”转移到了“消防员多么英勇”的感性层面。当人们在为英雄流泪时，谁还记得那些被忽视的预警？

而这场“战争”的底色依然是深沉的男性中心叙事。200个男人和“少数几个女人”在体育中心吃香肠，年轻成员在长辈的“小鸡”玩笑中完成权力交接。这种兄弟会式的结盟，在直接暴力（火灾）面前提供了情绪价值，却在结构层面上维持了某种古老的、基于体能和层级压制的秩序。这种秩序让人们习惯于用“不可预测”来掩盖“本可避免”。

人们像金鱼一样遗忘，因为这种“英雄-灾难-遗忘”的循环本身就是一种文化暴力。它让受害者认为唯一的救赎是等待下一个英雄出现，而不是要求一个公正的资源分配系统。这场火烧掉了森林，也烧掉了人们对结构性责任的追究心。

This report succeeds visually in constructing a 'heroic' narrative: exhausted firefighters, trench-like firebreaks, and a masculine collective staring into the abyss. But once you shift your gaze from those 'soot-covered faces,' you see a textbook scene of structural violence. The so-called 'megafire' was not an inevitable natural disaster, but a governance crisis triggered by faulty electrical equipment and exacerbated by the failure of resource allocation.

The most absurd complicity occurs at the top. Macron declares that now is not the time to 'analyse lessons' because France is in 'full battle.' This tactic of defining administrative negligence—such as the failure to update the firefighting fleet despite warnings since 2017—as a 'war' is a clear example of weaponized expression. By using 'war' as a cognitive entry point, the rulers successfully shift the discourse from 'why were resources insufficient' to the emotional realm of 'how brave the firefighters are.' While the public weeps for heroes, who remembers the ignored warnings?

Furthermore, the backdrop of this 'war' remains a deeply masculine-centric narrative. Two hundred men and a 'handful of women' eating sausages at a sports centre, with younger members receiving a rite of passage through 'little chicken' jokes from their superiors. This fraternity-style alliance provides emotional value in the face of direct violence, yet structurally maintains an ancient order of physical and hierarchical dominance. It encourages the belief that events are 'unpredictable' rather than 'preventable.'

People forget like goldfish because this cycle of 'hero-disaster-oblivion' is itself a form of cultural violence. It conditions victims to believe that the only salvation is waiting for the next hero, rather than demanding a just system of resource distribution. The fire consumed the forests, and in the process, it incinerated the public's will to hold structural power accountable.
