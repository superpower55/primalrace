用司机的睡眠为资本的油价买单Trading Drivers' Sleep for Capital's Fuel Margins

国际 结构层 · 文化层 Reuters ↗ 2026-09-17 § 链接
所谓的“灵活性”是结构暴力的掩体，本质是成本向下转移。
所谓的 'flexibility' is a shield for structural violence; it is simply the downward transfer of cost.

美国交通部这次所谓的“临时放宽”工作时长限制，是典型的 structural violence。把原本 14 小时的上限推到 16 小时，在政府的叙事里叫“灵活性” (flexibility)，在资本的账本里叫“成本对冲”。

逻辑极其简单：全球供应链紊乱、地缘战争推高油价，导致运输成本飙升。面对这个差额，权力中心没有选择去监管油价或补贴司机，而是选择直接削减司机的生物休息时间。这本质上是一次生存空间的掠夺——用司机的睡眠和潜在的疲劳驾驶风险，去填补能源巨头和运输公司的利润坑洞。

最讽刺的是，这种操作被包装成“应对紧急情况”的常规手段。当“紧急状态”成为一种治理工具，它就变成了一种武器化的叙事：只要宣布处于危机中，就可以合法地剥夺劳动者的基本生物权。司机在 16 小时的高强度驾驶中，其主体性被彻底降格为燃料运输链上的一个零件。

所谓的“只要休息足够”是典型的 scam。在极端疲劳和高压环境下，生物墙的崩溃是不可逆的。这次 waiver 实际上是在告诉底层劳动者：为了维持上层的油价稳定和物资供应，你们的生理极限是可以被临时“豁免”的。

The U.S. Department of Transportation's "temporary relaxation" of working hours is a textbook case of structural violence. Pushing the limit from 14 to 16 hours is called "flexibility" in government narratives, but it is "cost hedging" in the ledgers of capital.

The logic is primitive: global supply chain disruptions and geopolitical wars have spiked fuel prices. Faced with this gap, the power center chose neither to regulate prices nor to subsidize drivers, but to directly shave off the biological rest time of the workers. This is a predatory seizure of existential space—using the sleep and fatigue-related risks of drivers to plug the profit holes of energy giants and carriers.

Most cynical is how this is packaged as a "routine measure" for emergencies. When the "state of emergency" becomes a governance tool, it is weaponized: as long as a crisis is declared, the basic biological rights of laborers can be legally waived. In a 16-hour driving window, the driver's subjectivity is completely degraded into a mere cog in the fuel logistics chain.

The claim that this is acceptable "as long as required breaks are taken" is a total scam. The collapse of the biological wall under extreme fatigue and high pressure is irreversible. This waiver explicitly tells the bottom-tier laborers that their physiological limits are "waivable" to maintain the stability of fuel prices and supply for the elite.
