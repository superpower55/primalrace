AI Slop Cleanup: The New Proletarianization of CreativityAI Slop Cleanup: The New Proletarianization of Creativity

科技 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-02 § 链接
AI didn't replace the creator; it turned the creator into a janitor for its hallucinations.
AI didn't replace the creator; it turned the creator into a janitor for its hallucinations.

这是一场极其阴险的结构性剥削。AI 并没有像资本承诺的那样“解放”创意工作者，而是通过制造大量低质的 AI slop，将设计师和编辑从“创造者”降格为“数字清洁工”。

在这种博弈中，创意人的主体性被彻底解构。原本的 creative work 是一个从 0 到 1 的表达过程，而现在的 cleanup 则是对一个错误答案的修补。当 90% 的需求变成“修复 AI 错误”时，这意味着表达的定价权已经从人类手中转移到了算法手中。人类不再是定义“什么是好设计”的人，而成了确保“AI 的错误不那么明显”的共谋者。

最讽刺的是，这种工作被包装成一种“补救性机会”。客户在享受 AI 带来的低成本初稿后，试图用 lowball 的价格雇佣专业人士来弥补 AI 的无能。这是一种典型的结构性暴力：资本通过 AI 压低了创意劳动的准入门槛，同时又通过要求“人类润色”来榨取最后一点专业价值，且拒绝为此支付公正的对价。

Lisa 决定回归水彩和画布，这不仅是对“无灵魂”工作的反抗，更是一次生存策略的物理迁移。在数字空间被 AI 幻觉填满后，物理世界的生物墙——真实触感、不可复制的笔触——成了最后一块能维持主体性的阵地。当数字表达被武器化为一种高效的垃圾制造机，真实的、低效的、有温度的 human-made 表达，反而成了对抗元暴力的唯一最优解。

This is a sinister form of structural violence. AI hasn't "liberated" creative workers as capital promised; instead, by churning out massive amounts of AI slop, it has downgraded designers and editors from "creators" to "digital janitors."

In this existential game, the subjectivity of the creative professional is being completely dismantled. Original creative work was an expression from 0 to 1; cleanup is merely the repair of a wrong answer. When 90% of requests become "fixing AI errors," it means the pricing power of expression has shifted from humans to algorithms. Humans are no longer the ones defining "what is good design," but have become complicit agents ensuring that "AI's failures aren't too obvious."

The irony is that this work is packaged as a "remedial opportunity." Clients, enjoying the low-cost first drafts provided by AI, attempt to hire professionals to fix the AI's incompetence using lowball rates. This is a textbook example of structural violence: capital uses AI to lower the entry barrier for creative labor, while simultaneously extracting the last bit of professional value through "human polishing," all while refusing to pay a just price.

Lisa's decision to return to canvases and watercolors is not just a rebellion against "soulless" work, but a physical migration of survival strategy. As the digital space is saturated with AI hallucinations, the biological wall of the physical world—real touch, irreplaceable brushstrokes—becomes the last stronghold for maintaining subjectivity. When digital expression is weaponized into an efficient garbage-making machine, true, inefficient, and warm human-made expression becomes the only true optimal expression to counter meta-violence.
