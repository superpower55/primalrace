恐怖片里的原住民女孩The Native Girl in the Horror Trailer

新闻摘要HBO Max 在万圣节当天无预告上线了《小丑回魂：欢迎来到德里》的特别集《A Story of Derry》，该集时长30分钟，讲述潘尼怀斯出现数百年前三名年轻人在西部森林遭遇古老恶灵的故事。剧集统筹 Brad Caleb Kane 此前透露，第二季预计2027年底或2028年播出，背景设在1935年大萧条时期。
The newsHBO Max released a surprise 30-minute Halloween episode of It: Welcome to Derry, titled "A Story of Derry," exploring the origins of IT centuries before Pennywise. Showrunner Brad Caleb Kane said Season 2, expected in late 2027 or 2028, will be set in 1935 during the Great Depression.
被殖民者只能当恐怖片的开场道具
The colonized exist to be the monster's first kill.

HBO Max 万圣节放的这支惊喜短片，讲的是几百年前三个小孩进西边林子，撞见一个古老邪灵。预告画面里跑得最快、弓拉得最满的，是一个原住民女孩。

这个安排不是巧合。恐怖片的惯例是：越靠近土地、越原始的人，越先死。他们被写成邪灵的第一个猎物，作用是替观众把"这里很危险"翻译成画面，然后退场，把接下来的几百年让给白人小镇。

《它》整个系列的世界观，就建立在 Derry 这个镇子的沉默共谋上。镇子用定期献祭换繁荣，居民心知肚明却装作不知道，这是全书最标准的共谋者结构：利益站队一致，集体选择不看。而这条前传把时间拨到"几百年以前"，等于告诉观众：这个镇子的原罪比小丑更古老，比白人定居者更古老，古老到可以归给一片林子和一个"古代邪灵"。

于是暴力的责任被移交给超自然。谁屠杀、谁占地、谁把原住民赶进西边林子，全被一个非人的怪物吸收了。这是文化暴力的经典操作：让结构性的掠夺看起来像命中注定的诅咒。

第二季要拍 1935 年大萧条时期 Bradley 帮在 Derry 被血洗。银行劫匪、失业、枪战，这些都有真实的社会成因。但剧集的解释框架已经定好了——又是那个邪灵。

一个原住民女孩在预告片里拉弓，看起来很强。但她的戏份是"遭遇"，不是"反击"。她存在的意义是让观众害怕，不是让观众追问她为什么会在那片林子里。

HBO Max dropped a surprise Halloween short: hundreds of years before Pennywise, three kids go into the Western Wood and meet an ancient spirit. The fastest runner, the one with the bow drawn, is a Native American girl.

That's not an accident. Horror's rule is that the closer you are to the land, the more "primitive" you read, the sooner you die. She's written as the spirit's first prey, there to translate "this place is dangerous" into an image, then exit and hand the next few centuries to a white town.

The whole It mythology rests on Derry's silent complicity: the town feeds on periodic sacrifice, the residents know and choose not to look. That's the textbook co-conspirator structure — aligned interests, collective refusal to see. This prequel pushes the clock back "hundreds of years" to tell us the town's original sin is older than the clown, older than the settlers, old enough to assign to a forest and an "ancient spirit."

So responsibility gets outsourced to the supernatural. Whoever massacred, whoever took the land, whoever pushed the Native people into the Western Wood — all of it is absorbed by a nonhuman monster. Classic cultural violence: make structural plunder look like a curse written into the ground.

Season 2 goes to 1935, the Depression, the bloody massacre of the Bradley Gang. Bank robbers, unemployment, gunfire — all of that has real social causes. But the explanatory frame is already set. The spirit did it.

A Native girl draws a bow in the trailer and looks strong. Her role is encounter, not fight-back. She's there to scare the audience, not to make them ask why she's in that wood at all.
