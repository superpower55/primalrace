negligence is just another name for structural violenceNegligence is Just Another Name for Structural Violence

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-29 § 链接
Direct violence is the spark, but official negligence is the fuel.
Direct violence is the spark, but official negligence is the fuel.

这是一次典型的 Violence Triangle 联动。俄罗斯的无人机提供了 Direct Violence（直接暴力），但真正让伤亡规模扩大的，是潜伏在底层的 Structural Violence（结构暴力）。

泽连斯基承认弹药库“绝对不应该出现在那里”，而乌克兰总理则愤怒地指出，同样的错误在七月刚发生过。当一个国家在全面入侵的第五年，依然将爆炸物存储在养老院和居民区附近时，这不再是简单的“失误”，而是一种系统性的 Negligence（渎职）。这种渎职本质上是对平民生命价值的低估——在军事逻辑的优先级面前，弱势群体的生存空间被视作可牺牲的 collateral damage。

最讽刺的是，这种结构性暴力往往披着“战争紧急状态”的文化外衣，让人们习惯于接受不合理的风险。当行政指令凌驾于安全规程之上，且这种违规在体制内被默许或重复时，它就成了一种共谋（complicity）。官僚体系在追求短期军事部署便利的同时，实际上在潜意识里完成了对平民生命的定价：只要没被炸，违规就是“高效”；一旦被炸，就成了“刑事过失”。

这种逻辑在任何战争叙事中都通用：无论是进攻方将平民区定义为“军事目标”，还是防御方将居民区变成“弹药库”，其元暴力（meta-violence）是一致的——即把具体的人降格为战争机器的耗材。

This is a textbook demonstration of the Violence Triangle in action. The Russian drone provided the Direct Violence, but the scale of the tragedy was amplified by the underlying Structural Violence.

Zelenskyy admitted the depot "absolutely should not have been there," while the Ukrainian Prime Minister noted that the exact same mistake occurred in July. When a state, in the fifth year of a full-scale invasion, continues to store munitions next to care homes and residential areas, it is no longer a mere "error." It is systemic Negligence. This negligence is fundamentally a devaluation of civilian life—where the living space of the vulnerable is treated as acceptable collateral damage in the face of military priority.

The irony is that such structural violence is often cloaked in the cultural narrative of "emergency wartime measures," conditioning people to accept irrational risks. When administrative orders override safety regulations and such violations are tacitly permitted or repeated within the system, it becomes a form of complicity. The bureaucracy, in pursuing short-term deployment convenience, effectively places a price on civilian lives: if it doesn't blow up, the violation is "efficiency"; if it does, it's "criminal negligence."

This logic is universal in war narratives: whether the aggressor defines residential areas as "military targets" or the defender turns them into "ammunition depots," the meta-violence is the same—the reduction of human beings to expendable components of a war machine.
