程序正义是权力者的最后一块遮羞布Procedural Justice as the Final Fig Leaf for the Powerful

性别 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-17 § 链接
当法律在讨论“程序是否公正”时，受害者的存在性已被彻底抹除。
When the law debates 'procedural fairness,' the victim's existence is effectively erased.

这是一场典型的结构性暴力演出。一个 69 岁的行业资深权力者，面对两名 7-8 岁儿童的指控，最终获释的原因竟然是检方在挑选证人时“没有公正地指导”大陪审团。请注意这个荒诞的逻辑：法官关心的不是孩子是否被 groping，而是检察官在操作程序时是否足够 an impartial manner。

在加尔通的暴力三角里，这属于典型的 structural violence。法律被异化为一套复杂的术语游戏，只要在程序入口处制造一点瑕疵，就能将直接暴力 (direct violence) 转化为一场关于“法律流程”的学术讨论。当法官在裁决书中写下“检察官未能公正行事”时，他实际上是在通过维护一套所谓的“程序正义”，来掩盖一个更深层的元暴力 (meta violence)：一个拥有导演、制片人双重身份的成年男性，在封闭的剧组权力结构中，对幼童身体的绝对支配权被视为可以被“程序瑕疵”抵消的成本。

这种 dismiss 绝非正义的胜利，而是权力共谋 (complicity) 的结果。剧组、法律体系、以及那个被定义为“谎言”的指控，共同构成了一道生物墙。孩子们的表达在这一刻被定义为“lies”，而权力者的表达则被定义为“innocence”。最令人作呕的是，法官还大方地告诉 Busfield，检方可以“重新起诉”——这就像是在说：这次你赢了，因为对方没填好表格，下次如果你运气不好，我们再来玩这场博弈。

这场博弈的 Actual 状态是：施暴者重获自由，受害者在法律的羞辱中再次被客体化。Potential 与 Actual 之间的差额，就是这起案件被 dismiss 的真正代价。

This is a textbook performance of structural violence. A 69-year-old industry power player, facing allegations from two children aged 7 and 8, is set free because the prosecution failed to 'fairly instruct' the grand jury on witness requests. Note the absurdity: the judge isn't concerned with whether the children were groped, but whether the prosecutor acted in an 'impartial manner' regarding the paperwork.

In Galtung's Violence Triangle, this is structural violence at its peak. The law is weaponized into a complex terminology game; a minor procedural flaw is used to convert direct violence into a sterile debate over 'legal process.' When the judge writes that the prosecutor 'failed to act in a fair manner,' he is using 'procedural justice' as a shield to mask a deeper meta-violence: the absolute dominance of an adult male director/producer over the bodies of children in a closed set hierarchy.

This dismissal is not a victory for justice, but a result of systemic complicity. The production set, the legal system, and the label of 'lies' attached to the accusations form a biological wall. The children's expression is dismissed as a lie, while the power player's expression is validated as 'innocence.' The most cynical part is the judge's note that the state could 'pursue new charges'—essentially telling Busfield: you won this round because the other side messed up the forms; we can gamble again next time.

The Actual state of this game: the predator is free, and the victims are objectified once more by the law. The gap between Potential and Actual is the true price of this dismissal.
