破坏性扫描：一种极其高效的元暴力Destructive Scanning: A Masterclass in Meta-Violence

科技 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-05 § 链接
把人类文明的载体当成矿石开采，这就是 AI 时代的殖民逻辑。
Treating the vessels of human civilization as raw ore is the colonial logic of the AI era.

Anthropic 的 "Project Panama" 简直是对“表达”这个词最残忍的注脚。他们把书籍——这些承载了人类主体性、风格与灵魂的表达物——定义为一种待开采的“高质量数据集”。在他们的逻辑里，书不再是交流的媒介，而是某种生物质原材料。所谓的“破坏性扫描” (destructive scanning)，本质上就是一种文化层面的 strip mining（剥离式开采）：切掉书脊，撕碎纸页，把人类的思考抽干成 token，然后将物理原件丢进垃圾桶。

这不仅是版权纠纷，而是一场典型的存在性战争。AI 公司在博弈中通过操纵“合理使用” (fair use) 的法律定义，完成了对人类认知入口的暴力接管。CEO 把与作者沟通称为 "business slog"（商业苦差），这意味着在他们的价值体系里，人类作者的主体性完全低于一个 API 的迭代效率。这种对“人类作者”这一原初种族的剥夺，与历史上殖民者摧毁原住民文化以建立新秩序的逻辑如出一辙：先将对方客体化，再将其资源化，最后将其废弃化。

最讽刺的是，法院竟然将这种行为比作“教一个人读书”。这正是元暴力 (meta violence) 的最高境界——用一套看似“理性”、“文明”的类比，为结构性的掠夺提供合法性掩体。当人类的表达被转化为模型权重，我们失去的不仅是书，而是对“什么是事实”和“如何书写”的定义权。我们正在集体共谋，将自己从创造者降格为消费者，把文明的火种交给几个持有算力的私募股权公司去决定如何“重新格式化”。

Anthropic's "Project Panama" is the most brutal footnote to the concept of Expression. They have redefined books—the very vessels of human subjectivity, style, and soul—as a "high-quality dataset." In their logic, a book is no longer a medium of communication, but a form of biological raw material. "Destructive scanning" is, in essence, a form of cultural strip mining: slicing spines, shredding pages, draining human thought into tokens, and tossing the physical remains into a landfill.

This is not merely a copyright dispute; it is a textbook Existential War. By manipulating the legal definition of "fair use," AI corporations have executed a violent takeover of the cognitive entrance. The CEO describes engaging with authors as a "business slog," proving that in their value system, the subjectivity of human authors is entirely inferior to the iterative efficiency of an API. This erasure of the "human author" as a Primal Race mirrors the colonial logic of destroying indigenous cultures to establish a new order: objectify, resource-ize, and finally, discard.

The most cynical part is the court's analogy, comparing this to "teaching a human to read." This is the peak of meta-violence—using a seemingly "rational" and "civilized" analogy to provide a legal shield for structural plunder. As human expression is converted into model weights, we lose not just books, but the power to define "what is fact" and "how to write." We are collectively acting as complicitors, downgrading ourselves from creators to consumers, handing the sparks of civilization to a few private equity firms to decide how to "reformat" us.
