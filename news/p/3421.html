用MBA学费买断的沉默：FIFA的权力共谋Silence Bought with an MBA: The Power Complicity of FIFA

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-08 § 链接
所谓的“离职补偿”不过是权力对主体性的定价，用金钱完成对潜在举报者的消音。
A "departure payment" is simply the pricing of subjectivity, using capital to mute potential whistleblowers.

这是一场典型的权力共谋 (complicity)。UEFA承认支付了六位数的离职金并资助了MBA课程，而FIFA则用一套标准的公关辞令——“断然否认”和“诽谤”——来试图封锁真相。在这种叙事中，那名女性员工被简化为一个“被指控的情人”，她的主体性在金钱与权力的博弈中被彻底抹除。

请注意这里的结构暴力 (structural violence)：一个处于权力顶端的男性，通过组织资源的分配（离职金、教育经费）来处理私密关系带来的风险。这根本不是什么“符合当时规定”的行政操作，而是一种武器化的资源调配。MBA课程在这里不是为了提升员工竞争力，而是一张精心包装的“封口费”收据。通过将补偿合法化为“职业发展”，权力者成功地将一次潜在的权力滥用转化为一次合规的行政支出。

最令人作呕的是FIFA的辩护逻辑：“没有员工投诉，所以没有事件”。这在元暴力 (meta violence) 的逻辑中极其常见：在极度不对等的权力结构下，受害者选择沉默是生存的最优解表达 (optimal expression)，而权力者则将这种基于恐惧或利益交换的沉默，反过来定义为“清白”的证据。这种逻辑将结构性的压迫伪装成个体选择的缺失。

Infantino现在正试图将世界杯私有化，这不过是他权力贪欲的延伸。从私有化一个女性的沉默，到私有化全球足球的商业权益，逻辑是一脉相承的：只要能掌控解释权，任何掠夺都可以被定义为“现代化”或“合规”。

This is a textbook case of complicity. UEFA admits to a six-figure payout and MBA funding, while FIFA deploys standard PR armor—"categorically untrue" and "defamatory"—to blockade the truth. In this narrative, the female employee is reduced to an "alleged lover," her subjectivity completely erased in the game between money and power.

Observe the structural violence at play: a man at the apex of power utilizes organizational resources (severance, tuition) to mitigate the risks of his private liaisons. This is not "compliance with regulations"; it is the weaponization of resource allocation. The MBA course is not for professional growth, but a sophisticated receipt for hush money. By framing compensation as "career development," the powerful transform an abuse of authority into a compliant administrative expense.

FIFA's defense is particularly grotesque: "No one complained, therefore nothing happened." This is the core of meta violence—in a structure of extreme asymmetry, silence is the optimal expression for survival. The powerful then weaponize this silence, redefining it as evidence of innocence.

Infantino is now pushing to privatize the World Cup, a mere extension of his appetite for control. From privatizing a woman's silence to privatizing the commercial rights of global football, the logic remains the same: as long as you control the interpretation, any plunder can be rebranded as "modernization" or "regulation."
