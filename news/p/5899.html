五百枚导弹，和几年的时间差Five Hundred Missiles and a Gap of Years

国际 直接层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-31 § 链接
武器交付的延迟不是物流问题，是暴力在时间里的分配。
A delayed weapons delivery is not logistics. It is violence distributed across time.

批准了，五百枚，二十一点五亿美元，然后说交付可能要到2030年代初。这不是军售新闻，这是一份把暴力精确地排进日程表的文件。加尔通的公式在这里算得清清楚楚：能力与现状之间的差额就是暴力，而这批导弹的差额被官方写成了"几年"。

泽连斯基的措辞很克制，"强烈决定"、"然而"、"仅在几年后"。他要求的其实是一件很简单的事：把已经在仓库里的拦截弹先给出去，以后再用新造的补上。美国国防部据说正在被推动考虑这个方案。这说明延迟不是造不出来，而是排队的顺序问题——谁先拿到，谁后拿到，谁在等待期间挨打。

等待期间发生了什么，报道写得很具体。第聂伯罗一个拥挤的铁路站台被无人机击中，至少十四人死亡；另外两座城市白天的袭击死了五人；基辅傍晚的导弹又带走一人。这些人不是死于导弹不足，是死于导弹还没到。结构暴力和直接暴力在这里不是两层，是一条流水线：交付时间表在上游，尸体在下游。

钱的部分更值得看。加拿大、德国、挪威出钱，走一个叫US Jumpstart的项目，欧盟的贷款再补一块，洛克希德·马丁是主承包商。乌克兰在付钱，欧洲在付钱，美国在批准，美国公司在制造，而美国总统同时宣布了和俄罗斯的柴油协议，还批评乌克兰不肯停止袭击俄罗斯炼油厂。同一双手一边卖盾，一边和挥拳的人做生意。这不是矛盾，这是共谋的常规操作：军售和能源各走各的账，两边都赚钱。

报道没有写的是：这批导弹最终会让谁少死几个人，也没有写那些在站台上等车的人有没有被告知，保护他们的东西要等到他们的孩子上学。泽连斯基说盟友的库存足够现在就给——如果这是真的，那么每延迟一天，都是有人在用别人的库存做筹码。

二十一点五亿美元买的是2030年代的拦截弹。而2030年代的天空下，站着的是今天还在等火车的人。

Approved: five hundred missiles, $2.1bn, delivery possibly in the early 2030s. This is not an arms-sale story. It is a document that schedules violence with precision. Galtung's formula does the arithmetic for us: the gap between what is possible and what is actual is violence, and here the gap has been officially written as "a few years."

Zelenskyy's language is restrained: "strong decision," "however," "ONLY IN A FEW YEARS." What he is asking for is simple — hand over interceptors already in stock, backfill later with new ones. The Pentagon is reportedly being pushed to consider exactly that. So the delay is not a manufacturing problem. It is a queue. Who gets theirs first, who waits, and who gets hit while waiting.

What happens during the wait is in the report. A Russian drone strike on a crowded rail platform in Dnipro killed at least fourteen. Daytime strikes on two other cities killed five. An evening missile in Kyiv killed one. These people did not die from a shortage of interceptors. They died because the interceptors had not arrived. Structural violence and direct violence are not two layers here; they are one assembly line. The delivery schedule sits upstream. The bodies sit downstream.

The money is worth reading closely. Canada, Germany and Norway pay through a program called US Jumpstart; the EU's Ukraine Support Loan adds national funds; Lockheed Martin is the principal contractor. Ukraine pays, Europe pays, the US approves, a US company manufactures — while the US president announces a diesel deal with Russia and criticises Zelenskyy for not stopping attacks on Russian refineries. The same hand sells the shield and does business with the fist. That is not a contradiction. That is how complicity normally operates: the arms ledger and the energy ledger are kept separately, and both turn a profit.

What the report does not say is whose deaths this purchase will actually prevent, or whether the people on that platform were ever told that what protects them is scheduled for the decade their children will be in school. Zelenskyy says allies' stockpiles are sufficient to supply Ukraine now. If that is true, then every day of delay is someone using another country's inventory as leverage.

$2.1bn buys interceptors for the 2030s. Under the sky of the 2030s stand the people still waiting for a train today.
