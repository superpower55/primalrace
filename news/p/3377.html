被神化的“心理医生”与被榨取的女性声线The Myth of the 'Psychiatrist' and the Instrumentalization of Female Voices

性别 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-07 § 链接
制作人的“神话”往往建立在对女性主体性的消解与工具化之上。
The 'genius' of the producer is often built upon the erasure of female agency.

这篇文章在歌颂 William Orbit 的“伟大”时，不自觉地陷入了一种典型的 masculine-centric narrative。它把 Orbit 描述成一个在 Madonna 面前被“榨干”的受害者，或者在 Blur 崩溃边缘扮演“心理医生”的救世主。这种叙事极其滑稽：男性制作人通过承受所谓的“压力”和“精神损耗”，将女性歌手的声线作为一种 ethereal（飘渺）的 texture（纹理）或工具，最终将其转化为商业上的 success。而在这种叙事里，女性的贡献被简化为“提供声音”，而男性的贡献则是“塑造灵魂”。

最典型的是对 Beth Orton 的描述。文章提到他们长期的音乐伙伴关系“超越了浪漫关系”，这听起来很 progressive，但实际上 Orbit 依然是那个掌控 soundscape（声景）的定义者。女性在这些所谓的“伟大录音”中，要么是作为被 cosseting（呵护）的客体，要么是作为被“挖掘”的素材。所谓的 chill-out 音乐，本质上是一种文化层面的阉割——将女性的表达剥离其主体性，将其转化为一种服务于男性审美快感的、温顺的背景音。

这就是典型的共谋。音乐评论业通过这种“天才制作人”的叙事，完成了对元暴力的再次加固：定义什么是“好音乐”的权力依然在男性手中，而女性即便在作品中占据 C 位，也只是被定义为某种“氛围”的载体。所谓的“魔法”，不过是男性在掌控解释权后，将女性的主体性抹除，并将其包装成艺术的 scam。

This article, in celebrating William Orbit’s 'greatness,' unconsciously falls into a masculine-centric narrative. It depicts Orbit as a victim 'killed' by Madonna’s demands or a 'psychiatrist' saving Blur from collapse. This narrative is farcical: the male producer, by enduring supposed 'stress' and 'mental exhaustion,' treats female vocals as an ethereal texture or a tool to be processed into commercial success. In this framework, the woman's contribution is reduced to 'providing a voice,' while the man's is 'shaping the soul.'

Take the description of Beth Orton. The text notes their partnership 'outlasted their romantic relationship,' sounding progressive, yet Orbit remains the definer of the soundscape. Women in these 'great recordings' are either objects to be cosseted or raw material to be mined. The so-called chill-out music is a form of cultural violence—stripping female expression of its agency and converting it into a compliant background noise that serves male aesthetic pleasure.

This is a textbook case of complicity. The music criticism industry, through the 'genius producer' trope, reinforces meta-violence: the power to define 'good music' remains masculine. Even when women occupy the center stage, they are defined merely as vessels for an 'atmosphere.' The 'magic' is nothing more than a scam where male control of the narrative erases female subjectivity and repackages it as art.
