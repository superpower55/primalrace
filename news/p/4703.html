Uber 的“简化”叙事与管理层的生存博弈Uber's 'Simplicity' Narrative and the Management Survival Game

科技 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-02 § 链接
所谓的“简化组织”通常是权力重新洗牌的遮羞布。
所谓的 'organizational simplification' is usually a veil for the reshuffling of power.

Dara Khosrowshahi 用“simpler and faster”这个典型的 corporate scam 来包装一次大规模的裁员。在资本市场的叙事里，砍掉 3300 个管理岗位被描述为消除“复杂性”，但本质上这是一场关于存在性战争的资源重新分配。当公司估值超过 1500 亿美元，管理层最关心的不再是组织效率，而是如何通过削减中层来强化顶层的控制力，并为即将到来的 robotaxi 自动化转型腾出预算空间。

最值得警惕的是那条关于“回归办公室”的指令。要求 99% 的员工进入办公室，这根本不是为了所谓的“协作”，而是一种极端的 structural violence。对于许多员工，尤其是承担更多家庭照料责任的女性，远程办公是她们在职场与私人领域博弈中争取到的一个微小但关键的 optimal expression。强制回归办公室，实际上是在用物理空间的管控来重新建立一个可见的、可监控的等级森严的 masculine-centric 权力场，将那些无法在物理空间中 100% 投入的人在潜意识层面剔除出核心权力圈。

Uber 正在将自身的组织结构“机器人化”——不仅是道路上的出租车要变成自驾，公司内部的人员管理也要变成一种冷冰冰的、追求极值且缺乏共情的算法。这次裁员中谁会被留下，谁会被优化，取决于谁更愿意共谋，谁更能扮演那个“高效且顺从”的工具人角色。所谓的“更强大的 Uber”，其实是指一个权力更加集中、个体主体性更加萎缩的权力机器。

Dara Khosrowshahi is using the typical corporate scam of being 'simpler and faster' to package a mass layoff. In the narrative of capital markets, cutting 3,300 management roles is described as eliminating 'complexity,' but in essence, it is a redistribution of resources in an existential war. With a valuation exceeding $150 billion, the C-suite is no longer concerned with organizational efficiency, but with strengthening top-down control by purging the middle and freeing up budget for the upcoming robotaxi automation.

The most alarming part is the mandate to return to the office. Requiring 99% of staff to be physically present is not about 'collaboration'; it is a form of structural violence. For many employees, especially women who shoulder more caregiving responsibilities, remote work was a small but critical optimal expression won through bargaining between their professional and private spheres. Forcing a return to the office is effectively using physical space to re-establish a visible, monitorable, and masculine-centric power field, subconsciously pushing those who cannot commit 100% of their physical presence out of the inner circle of power.

Uber is 'robotizing' its own organizational structure—not just the taxis on the road, but the internal management of people, turning it into a cold, peak-efficiency algorithm devoid of empathy. Who stays and who is 'optimized' in this cull depends on who is willing to enter into complicity and who can best play the role of the 'efficient and obedient' tool. A 'stronger Uber' simply means a power machine where authority is more concentrated and individual subjectivity is further withered.
