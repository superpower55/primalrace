直播处决：用“病态好奇心”为元暴力背书Livestreaming Execution: Endorsing Meta-Violence via 'Morbid Curiosity'

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-13 § 链接
将国家暴力包装成心理学课题，是最高级的文化共谋。
Packaging state violence as a psychological study is the ultimate form of cultural complicity.

美国国防部长 Pete Hegseth 计划直播处决 Nidal Malik Hasan，这不仅是一次直接暴力 (direct violence) 的预告，更是一场精心设计的文化暴力 (cultural violence) 实验。最令人作呕的不是处决本身，而是随之而来的“心理学解释”：专家开始讨论人们为何会对“病态好奇心” (morbid curiosity) 产生吸引力。

这套叙事逻辑极其阴险。它把一个关于权力、惩罚和国家机器公开施暴的政治问题，偷换成了关于人类本能的心理学问题。当讨论重心从“政府是否应该直播杀人”转移到“为什么人们喜欢看杀人”时，施暴者的主体性消失了，而观众被定义成了受本能驱动的生物。这是一种典型的认知入口武器化：通过将暴力“自然化”和“学术化”，让原本应当引起愤怒的 structural violence 变成了一场关于大脑反应的科普视频。

谁在共谋？那些在新闻中扮演“客观专家”的心理学家，以及将此作为“心理探讨”而非“人权危机”来报道的媒体。他们共同构建了一个掩体，让元暴力 (meta violence) 在“科学”的旗号下完成一次完美的 PR。他们告诉我们，人们会看是因为好奇心，而忽略了这种“好奇心”实际上是被男性中心叙事长期驯化后的结果——在这种叙事中，强权对弱者的处置被定义为一种“奇观”和“正义的实现”。

直播处决不是为了正义，而是为了夺取解释权。它在向所有人宣告：谁拥有定义死亡的权力，谁就拥有制造真实的能力。而那些在屏幕前分析“好奇心”的人，不过是在为这台绞肉机涂抹一层名为“心理学”的润滑剂。

US Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth’s plan to livestream the execution of Nidal Malik Hasan is not just a preview of direct violence; it is a calculated experiment in cultural violence. The most repulsive part is not the execution itself, but the accompanying "psychological explanation": experts are now discussing why humans are drawn to "morbid curiosity."

This narrative logic is insidious. It swaps a political question about power, punishment, and the state's public exercise of violence for a psychological question about human instinct. When the focus shifts from "Should the government livestream a killing?" to "Why do people like watching killings?", the agency of the perpetrator vanishes, and the audience is reduced to biological entities driven by instinct. This is a textbook weaponization of cognitive entry points: by "naturalizing" and "academicizing" violence, structural violence that should provoke rage is transformed into a science pop-video about brain reactions.

Who are the complicit parties? The psychologists playing the role of "objective experts" and the media reporting this as a "psychological exploration" rather than a human rights crisis. Together, they build a shield that allows meta-violence to complete a perfect PR stunt under the banner of "science." They tell us people watch because of curiosity, ignoring that this "curiosity" is actually the result of long-term conditioning by a masculine-centric narrative—one where the exercise of power over the weak is defined as a "spectacle" or the "realization of justice."

Livestreaming an execution is not about justice; it is about seizing the power of interpretation. It announces to the world: whoever owns the power to define death owns the power to manufacture reality. And those analyzing "curiosity" on screen are merely applying a lubricant called "psychology" to the meat grinder.
