换尺子的人又来量身高了The Man Who Changes the Ruler Is Back to Measure Height

文娱 结构层 · 文化层 IndieWire ↗ 2026-11-02 § 链接
新闻摘要加州州长纽森提议将影视税收抵免从每年7.5亿美元扩大到至少10亿美元，并延长至2040年。此前FilmLA报告显示，尽管加州去年刚把抵免从3.3亿提高到7.5亿，第三季度影视制作量仍下降3.7%。纽森还呼吁国会通过联邦层面的影视税收抵免。
The newsCalifornia Governor Gavin Newsom is proposing to expand the state's film and TV tax credit from $750 million to at least $1 billion annually, extending it through 2040. FilmLA reported that production fell 3.7 percent in the third quarter even after last year's increase from $330 million to $750 million. Newsom also urged Congress to pass a federal film and TV incentive.
抵免翻倍，产量照跌——这不是激励，是给一个正在搬走的行业付赎金。
Doubling the credit while production keeps falling isn't an incentive. It's ransom paid to an industry already packing up.

去年把抵免从3.3亿抬到7.5亿，申请量先涨了一波，然后产量接着跌。今年第三季度又跌3.7%。纽森的反应是把7.5亿抬到10亿，延长到2040年。这个动作很熟悉：现实不配合，就重新定义配合的标准。τ law 换了个行业，照样成立。

税收抵免本身不是问题，问题是它被当成了一台自动售货机——投币，出货。可投进去的币是公共财政，出来的是不是就业，没人验证。报道里没有一个字提到这7.5亿换回了多少岗位、这些岗位的工资中位数是多少、有多少流向了本就总部在洛杉矶的几大厂牌。FilmLA 只给了一个产量跌幅，州长办公室只给了一个更大的数字。两套数字之间没有桥。

真正在动的是结构层。影视制作搬去新泽西、纽约、佐治亚，搬去英国、匈牙利，不是因为加州不够爱它，是因为那些地方更便宜。这是一场州与州、国与国之间的补贴竞标，每一方都在加价，最后赢的是可以拿着报价单到处比价的制片方。它们总部就在洛杉矶，报道自己写了这一点——它们不需要被吸引回来，它们只需要被留住议价的筹码。

纽森说「加州创造了现代娱乐产业，我们是娱乐之都，我们要把它留在这里」。这句话的语法是主权者的语法，不是就业政策的语法。他把产业当成一件可以「留」在家里的财产，而不是一群具体的人每天在通勤、在等通告、在算这个月能不能交上房租。那些人是这条新闻里唯一付代价的人，也是唯一没有出现在任何数字里的人。

他还呼吁国会通过联邦抵免，理由是「各州无法独自承担这个成本」。翻译一下：这场竞标我加不动了，请上级财政来接盘。补贴竞赛的终点从来不是某一方赢，是所有人一起把公共的钱转成私人的确定性，然后宣布这是产业政策。

10亿买的是「势头」这个词。报道里纽森自己也用了：维持去年历史性扩张的势头。势头不是产出，势头是势头的公关版本。等到明年第三季度产量再跌几个点，需要的不是一份就业审计，是12.5亿。

Last year the credit went from $330 million to $750 million. Applications rose briefly, then production kept falling. This year's third quarter: down another 3.7 percent. Newsom's answer is to raise $750 million to $1 billion and extend it to 2040. The move is familiar: when reality won't cooperate, redefine what cooperation means. The τ law travels well. It works in any industry.

A tax credit isn't the problem. The problem is treating it as a vending machine: insert coin, receive jobs. The coin is public money. Whether jobs come out is never checked. Nowhere in this report is there a number for how many positions the $750 million bought, what those positions pay, or how much flowed to the major studios and streamers that, as the report itself notes, are already headquartered in Los Angeles. FilmLA gives a production decline. The governor's office gives a bigger number. There is no bridge between the two sets of figures.

What's actually moving is the structural layer. Production leaves for New Jersey, New York, Georgia, the UK, Hungary, because those places are cheaper. This is a bidding war between states and between countries, and everyone keeps raising. The winner is the party that can carry a quote sheet from one bidder to the next. They don't need to be lured back to California. They need California to keep bidding, so the quote sheet stays useful.

「California created the modern entertainment industry; we're the capital of entertainment and we're going to keep it here,」 Newsom said. That is the grammar of a sovereign, not of an employment policy. It treats an industry as property to be kept in the house, not as a crowd of people commuting, waiting for calls, calculating whether this month's rent gets paid. Those people are the only ones paying in this story, and they are the only ones missing from every number in it.

He also wants Congress to pass a federal credit, because 「states can't shoulder the cost alone.」 Translation: I can't keep raising. Send money from above. A subsidy race never ends with one side winning. It ends with everyone converting public money into private certainty and calling it industrial policy.

A billion dollars buys the word 「momentum.」 Newsom uses it himself: maintaining the momentum of last year's historic expansion. Momentum is not output. Momentum is the PR version of output. When next year's third quarter drops a few more points, the answer won't be an audit of jobs. It will be $1.25 billion.
