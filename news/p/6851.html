八五折卖的是游戏，八五折卖的是人The 85% Off Is on Games, and on You

文娱 文化层 Polygon.com ↗ 2026-11-02 § 链接
新闻摘要史克威尔艾尼克斯在任天堂 eShop 开启 Switch 2 大型促销，超过 90 款游戏参与，折扣最高达 85%，活动 11 月 5 日结束。多款《最终幻想》《勇者斗恶龙》作品创下历史最低价，例如《最终幻想 7 重生》7.49 美元、《最终幻想 7 重制版 Intergrade》7.99 美元。
The newsSquare Enix is running a large Nintendo eShop sale for Switch 2, with more than 90 games discounted up to 85 percent off, ending November 5. Several Final Fantasy and Dragon Quest titles hit their lowest prices ever, including Final Fantasy 7 Rebirth at $7.49 and Final Fantasy 7 Remake Intergrade at $7.99.
定价权在谁手里，谁就定义什么是「值得拥有」
Whoever sets the price also decides what counts as worth having.

一篇促销清单，最该看的是那个 85%。《勇者斗恶龙怪兽篇：暗黑王子》5.99 美元，《最终幻想 7 重生》7.49 美元，都是历史最低。一件商品能被打到原价的零头，说明它的价格从来不是由它值多少决定的，是由卖的人需要它此刻值多少决定的。定价权是一种解释权：谁有权力说这个数字是「划算」，谁就同时有权力说这个数字原本是「合理」的。

促销的叙事入口很老，也很有效：错过就没了，11 月 5 日截止，历史最低，不买就是亏。这套话术不靠骗，靠的是让你把「买」当成一次判断力的表现，而不是一次消费。你以为自己在挑游戏，其实是「限时」「最低价」这几个词在替你挑。

真正值得看一眼的是这些 IP 本身。《最终幻想 7》从 1997 年卖到今天，重制、再重制、Intergrade、Rebirth，同一段故事被切成几份，反复上架，反复降价，反复「历史最低」。这不是打折，这是一条把同一份表达反复变现的流水线，每一次降价都是在给下一次原价重新定锚。玩家觉得占了便宜，是因为锚早就被卖的人插好了。

清单里 90 多款游戏，报道只列了 27 款，剩下的「还有更多值得发掘」。谁被列出来，谁被藏起来，谁配得上「亮点」这个词，是发行方和媒体一起完成的筛选。曝光位置本身就是一种分配，和折扣一样是资源。

至于这些游戏里在演什么、谁被塑造成救世主、谁被塑造成不入世的女神、谁的身体被当成封面，报道一个字没提。促销清单不负责这个，它只负责让你在 11 月 5 日之前按下购买。清单越整齐，越没人问那 90 多个世界里，女性角色加起来说过几句和男人无关的话。

A sale list, and the number to look at is the 85 percent. Dragon Quest Monsters: The Dark Prince at $5.99, Final Fantasy 7 Rebirth at $7.49, both lowest prices ever. When a product can be cut to a fraction of its list price, the price was never set by what it is worth. It was set by what the seller needs it to be worth right now. Pricing power is a kind of interpretive power: whoever gets to call a number a deal also got to call the original number reasonable.

The sales pitch is old and it works: gone soon, ends November 5, lowest ever, not buying is losing. It doesn't lie to you. It gets you to treat buying as a display of judgment rather than an act of consumption. You think you are choosing games. The words limited time and lowest price are choosing for you.

What deserves a second look is the IP itself. Final Fantasy 7 has been on sale since 1997: remade, remastered, Intergrade, Rebirth, the same story sliced into portions, relisted, re-cut, lowest price ever, again. This is not a discount. It is an assembly line that monetizes the same expression on repeat, and every price cut re-anchors the next full price. Players feel they got a bargain because the anchor was planted long before they arrived.

More than 90 games are in the promotion; the report lists 27 and says there is plenty more to discover. Who gets listed, who gets buried, who earns the word highlight, is a selection made jointly by the publisher and the outlet. Placement is itself an allocation, the same kind of resource as the discount.

As for what happens inside these games, who is cast as the savior, who as the unworldly goddess, whose body goes on the cover, the report says nothing. A sale list isn't built for that. It is built to get you to press buy before November 5. The tidier the list, the fewer people ask how many lines, across those 90 worlds, the women get that aren't about a man.
