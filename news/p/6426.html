反弹是假的，钱是真的The Backlash Is Fake, the Money Is Real

科技 文化层 · 元暴力 404 Media ↗ 2026-11-01 § 链接
新闻摘要据404 Media获得的泄露音频，车牌识别公司Flock首席执行官Garrett Langley周日在一次私人警察会议上对警长们称，全国针对该公司的抗议并非真实存在。他还表示妻子建议他永远不要道歉，并称自己不愿再接受媒体采访，还无证据地声称社交媒体上对Flock的反对主要来自俄罗斯机器人。
The newsAccording to leaked audio obtained by 404 Media, Flock CEO Garrett Langley told police chiefs at a private conference on Sunday that nationwide protests against his license plate reader company are not real. He also said his wife advised him never to apologize, that he would prefer not to do press interviews again, and claimed without evidence that social media opposition to Flock is mostly driven by Russian bots.
把抗议说成俄罗斯水军，是把解释权当执法权用。
Calling protest Russian bots is using the power to define as the power to police.

Flock 的 CEO 对着一屋子警察局长说，全国的反 Flock 抗议不是真的。这句话本身就是这次泄露里最重要的证据：一个卖监控基础设施的人，已经不觉得需要向公众解释自己了，他只需要向买家解释。

他的论据是社交媒体讨论量从七月掉了九成五，上周乌克兰无人机炸了俄罗斯数据中心，一夜之间又掉了五成。这是把“网上骂我的人变少了”直接等同于“骂我的人本来就不存在”。讨论量下降可以有一百种原因，他挑了唯一一种让自己不必回应的解释。这不是分析，这是先定结论再找数据，和 τ law 是同一套手艺：现实不配合，就重新定义现实。

更值得看的是他的听众。警察局长们坐在下面，不是来质询的，是来采购的。Flock 的商业模式是向执法机构卖车牌识别，公众的反对在这条交易链里没有席位。所以他能把抗议定性为“有人付钱”，把批评者定性为机器人，把媒体定性为不值得再打交道的东西——他在向客户演示：这些噪音我来处理，你们不用管。

“我妻子说永远不要道歉。”这句话比俄罗斯水军那段更诚实。道歉意味着承认对方有资格被回应，而他给出的答案是：不道歉，不接受采访，不承认反对存在。这是把解释权当成执法权在用——我说抗议是假的，抗议就是假的。

抗议是真的还是假的，不由他宣布。报道里没写那些抗议者是谁、要求什么，这恰恰是这类叙事的效果：当一方垄断了“什么算真实”的定义，另一方的诉求连被记录的机会都没有。

Flock's CEO told a room of police chiefs that the nationwide backlash against his company isn't real. That sentence is the most important thing in the leak: a man selling surveillance infrastructure no longer feels he owes the public an explanation. He only owes one to his buyers.

His evidence is that social media chatter about Flock dropped 95 percent from July, and fell 50 percent overnight last week when Ukraine bombed a Russian data center. This treats "fewer people are posting about me" as "the people posting about me never existed." Chatter can fall for a hundred reasons; he picked the one that lets him not respond. That isn't analysis, it's deciding the conclusion first and then finding a number. Same craft as the τ law: when reality won't cooperate, redefine reality.

Look at the audience. The police chiefs aren't there to interrogate him, they're there to buy. Flock's business is selling license plate readers to law enforcement, and public objection has no seat in that transaction. So he can call the protests paid-for, the critics bots, the press not worth talking to again — he's demonstrating to his customers that he'll handle the noise so they don't have to.

"My wife said never apologize." That line is more honest than the Russian bot bit. An apology admits the other side has standing to be answered. His answer is: no apology, no interviews, no acknowledgment that opposition exists. This is the power to define deployed as the power to police — I say the protest is fake, so it is fake.

Whether the protests are real isn't his to announce. The report doesn't say who the protesters are or what they want, which is exactly the effect of this kind of narrative: when one side monopolizes the definition of what counts as real, the other side's demands don't even get recorded.
