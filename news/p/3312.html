好莱坞的“习惯性顺从”与权力的认知入口Hollywood's Habitual Acquiescence and the Cognitive Entry of Power

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-06 § 链接
资本在威权面前的顺从并非无奈，而是一场精准的利益共谋。
Capital's submission to authoritarianism is not helplessness, but a precise strategic complicity.

吉米·金梅尔被暂停播出的短暂风波，撕开了好莱坞一个陈旧的伤口：面对 authoritarian pressure，资本永远选择最快地达成共谋。从 40 年代的麦卡锡黑名单到今天的 Trump 压力测试，剧本几乎没有变过。制片厂总在宣称“别无选择”，但这本身就是一个巨大的 scam。在权力博弈中，所谓的“无奈”其实是资本在计算完成本后，为了维持既定 structural 优势而主动选择的 acquiescence。

这次洛迦诺电影节的回顾展揭示了一个残酷的事实：黑名单不仅是物理上的禁绝，更是一次彻底的 cultural violence。它通过制造恐惧，将曾经团结的左翼阵营撕裂为“背叛者”与“被放逐者”，从而在认知入口上完成了对“爱国”与“叛国”的定义权垄断。当艺术家被迫使用 pseudonyms 写作，或者在流亡中陷入 paranoia 时，元暴力已经完成了它的使命——让个体在生存压力下自我审查，直到主体性死亡。

好莱坞左翼的消亡，本质上是解释权被强行收回的结果。那些关于工人阶级、种族主义和性别压迫的叙事，被定义为“颠覆”，随后被从主流认知中抹除。这种武器化的表达方式至今有效，因为它利用了人们对被孤立的恐惧。当资本与权力达成共谋，他们共同制造了一套“真实”，让所有不符合该定义的人在存在性战争中提前出局。

The brief turmoil surrounding Jimmy Kimmel's suspension reopens an old wound: in the face of authoritarian pressure, capital always chooses the fastest route to complicity. From the McCarthy blacklist of the 40s to today's Trump pressure tests, the script remains unchanged. Studios always claim they "had no choice," but this is a massive scam. In the game of power, this so-called "helplessness" is actually a calculated choice by capital to maintain its structural advantage.

This Locarno retrospective reveals a brutal truth: the blacklist was not just a physical ban, but a form of thorough cultural violence. By manufacturing fear, it tore apart a once-united left wing into "informers" and "exiles," thereby monopolizing the definition of "patriotism" and "treason" at the cognitive entry. When artists are forced to write under pseudonyms or succumb to paranoia in exile, meta-violence achieves its goal—forcing individuals into self-censorship until their subjectivity dies.

The annihilation of the Hollywood left was essentially the result of the explanation rights being forcibly reclaimed. Narratives of working-class struggle, racism, and sexism were labeled as "subversive" and erased from the mainstream. This weaponization of expression remains effective because it exploits the fear of isolation. When capital and power enter into complicity, they manufacture a shared "reality" that ensures anyone who doesn't fit the definition loses the existential war before it even begins.
