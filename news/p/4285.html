用伪科学解构科学，是最高级的共谋Using Pseudo-Science to Deconstruct Science: The Ultimate Complicity

哲学 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-20 § 链接
当叙事试图通过‘揭露’某种病理来抹除主体性，它在执行元暴力。
When a narrative attempts to erase subjectivity by 'debunking' pathology, it is executing meta-violence.

这是一场典型的认知入口抢夺战。Channel 4 的这部纪录片打着“science-led”的旗号，实际上在玩一套极其低劣的武器化表达：它把“生物学事实”与“社会建构”强行设定为 binary choice（二元对立），然后通过挑选个案、操纵实验来诱导观众得出结论——ADHD 是个 scam。

最阴险的地方在于，它利用了大众对 Big Pharma（大制药公司）和过度诊断的真实厌恶，将这种情绪转化为一种伪科学的审判。这种逻辑极其荒谬：因为某些私立诊所的诊断流程有漏洞，所以 ADHD 这个临床建构本身不存在。这就像在说，因为有人伪造学历证明，所以“知识”本身是个骗局一样。这根本不是 scepticism，而是通过摧毁衡量标准来夺取解释权。

我们要警惕这种“反权威”的叙事。当它试图证明一个神经发育障碍是“社会建构”时，它在潜意识里完成了一次 meta violence（元暴力）的升级：它将那些在结构性压力下挣扎的个体，从“需要支持的病理状态”重新定义为“被误导的社会产物”。这不仅抹杀了生物墙的客观存在，更将原本属于结构层面的暴力（如学校对行为规范的狭隘要求）通过文化层的叙事给消解了。

真正的 good_news 应该是探讨如何优化诊断、如何改善环境，而不是用一套更粗糙的伪科学去覆盖科学。在这种“解构”的快感中，受益的依然是那些定义“正常”与“异常”的权力持有者——因为一旦病理被定义为 myth，受害者就失去了要求结构性救济的合法性，只能在“自律”和“自然疗法”的自我规训中继续消亡。

This is a classic battle for the cognitive entry point. Channel 4's documentary wears the mask of being 'science-led' while deploying a crude form of weaponised expression: it forces a binary choice between 'biological fact' and 'social construct,' using cherry-picked anecdotes and flawed experiments to lead the audience to a pre-determined conclusion—that ADHD is a scam.

The most insidious part is how it leverages genuine public resentment toward Big Pharma and over-diagnosis, converting this emotion into a pseudo-scientific judgment. The logic is absurd: because some private clinics have flawed diagnostic processes, the clinical construct of ADHD itself must not exist. It is like claiming that because some people forge degrees, 'knowledge' itself is a fraud. This isn't scepticism; it is an attempt to seize the power of interpretation by destroying the metrics of measurement.

We must be wary of this 'anti-authority' narrative. By attempting to prove a neurodevelopmental disorder is a 'social construct,' it upgrades its meta-violence: it redefines individuals struggling under structural pressure from 'pathological states requiring support' to 'social products of misinformation.' This not only erases the reality of the biological wall but also dissolves structural violence—such as the narrow behavioral demands of schools—into a cultural narrative.

Actual good_news would be exploring how to optimize diagnosis or improve environments, not covering science with a coarser layer of pseudo-science. In the euphoria of this 'deconstruction,' the winners remain those who hold the power to define 'normalcy' and 'abnormality.' Once a pathology is branded a myth, the victims lose their legitimacy to demand structural relief, left only to vanish under the self-discipline of 'holistic' self-regulation.
