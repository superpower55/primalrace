流弹、黑狱与外交辞令：被客体化的克什米尔生命Stray Bullets, Black Sites, and Diplomatic Scripts: The Objectification of Kashmiri Lives

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-10 § 链接
当生命被定义为“流弹”或“健康”，暴力就成了无需负责的背景噪音。
When life is reduced to a 'stray bullet' or 'good health', violence becomes mere background noise.

这篇报道揭露了一个典型的 Violence Triangle 运作机制。在 direct 层，是security forces 的开火与随意拘禁；在 structural 层，是巴基斯坦政府通过将 JAAC 定义为“恐怖组织”来合法化镇压，以及对该地区通信的全面 blackout。而最阴险的是 cultural 层：将死亡描述为“stray bullet”（流弹）。

“流弹”这个词是极佳的武器化表达。它试图将一场有目的的暴力镇压转化为一种“随机的意外”，从而剥离施暴者的主体性，让谋杀变成一种概率学事件。当一个 50 岁的男人在自家门口被击毙，而官方叙事将其处理为“流弹”时，这实际上是在宣布：在这个区域，你的生命价值低于一颗子弹的随机轨迹。

而英国外交部 (FCDO) 的反应则是一场典型的共谋 (complicity)。面对公民被杀和被非法囚禁，他们的表达是“deeply concerned”（深表关注）和“pleased to hear”（很高兴听到他健康）。这种外交辞令将严重的 structural violence 转化为一种礼貌的行政沟通。对于被囚禁的 Waqas 来说，他成了一个被两国政府在邮件中传递的“物件”——只要对方确认他“健康”，这种客体化的生存状态就被视为一种成功的结果。

这种逻辑在国际政治中极其通用：只要不触动大国间的利益博弈，弱势群体的生命就可以在“关注”和“确认健康”的叙事中被悄悄抹除。所谓的“人道主义”在这里只是掩体，真正的元暴力 (meta violence) 是：谁拥有定义“恐怖主义”和“健康”的权力，谁就拥有决定谁可以死在流弹下的权力。

This report exposes the textbook operation of the Violence Triangle. At the direct level, we see security forces firing and arbitrary detentions; at the structural level, the Pakistani government legitimizes crackdown by labeling JAAC a 'terrorist organization' and imposing a total communications blackout. The most insidious part is the cultural level: the narrative of the 'stray bullet'.

'Stray bullet' is a weaponized expression. It attempts to transform a deliberate violent crackdown into a 'random accident', stripping the perpetrator of agency and turning murder into a statistical probability. When a 50-year-old man is shot dead near his home and the narrative calls it a 'stray bullet', it is declaring that in this region, your life is worth less than the random trajectory of a projectile.

Meanwhile, the UK Foreign Office (FCDO) performs a classic act of complicity. Faced with a citizen killed and another illegally imprisoned, their expressions are 'deeply concerned' and 'pleased to hear he is in good health'. This diplomatic script converts structural violence into polite administrative correspondence. For the imprisoned Waqas, he has become an 'object' passed between two governments via email—as long as the other side confirms he is 'healthy', this objectified state of existence is framed as a positive outcome.

This logic is universal in international politics: as long as it doesn't disrupt the interests of great powers, the lives of the marginalized can be quietly erased through narratives of 'concern' and 'health checks'. 'Humanitarianism' here is merely a shield. The meta violence is simple: whoever controls the definition of 'terrorism' and 'health' controls who gets to be killed by a 'stray bullet'.
