金狮奖的例外与AI杀戮的缩写The Golden Lion's Exception and the Acronym of AI Slaughter

好消息 元暴力 · 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-12 § 链接
当叙事权开始让渡，真相才从被定义的事实中浮现。
Truth emerges from defined facts only when the narrative power begins to shift.

威尼斯电影节这次的奖项分布，是一次典型的 meta 层面的权力位移。May el-Toukhy 的《Woman Unknown》拿金狮，最值得关注的不是电影本身，而是它作为竞赛单元中“唯一由女性独立执导”的身份。这种极端的 underrepresentation 说明了电影工业在 structural 层面的暴力：女性导演在认知入口的竞争中被系统性地排挤，直到评委会决定通过“打破惯例”的 unanimous vote 来进行一次补偿性正义。

电影讲述一个女佣在战后试图掩盖与德国士兵的私情，这本质上是一场关于“表达”与“生存”的博弈。女主角 Marie 面对的是一个典型的男性中心叙事环境，她的过去被定义为“shameful secret”，因为在父权结构的评价体系里，女性的身体经验如果脱离了男性的掌控或认可，就是一种污点。这种对女性历史的抹除，正是 cultural violence 的核心：让女性在历史中保持“未知” (Unknown)，从而维持一个干净的、由男性书写的文明叙事。

而另一部获奖片《NAZA》则直接揭露了现代战争中最高级的 weaponized expression。将平民死亡预期简化为一个缩写（NAZA），是典型的元暴力操作——通过语言的去人性化，将大规模杀戮转化为一个技术指标，从而让执行者在心理上完成共谋。当以色列军方在面对真实证词时选择“断然否认”，他们争夺的不是事实，而是对“现实”的解释权。

这次颁奖礼像是一次小规模的共谋倒戈。从给女性导演正名，到揭露 AI 驱动的屠杀，Actual 正在向 Potential 靠近。但别忘了，当一个女性导演需要通过成为“唯一”来获得关注时，这本身就是一种结构性暴力的反向证明。

The awards at the Venice Film Festival represent a classic shift in meta-violence. The Golden Lion for May el-Toukhy's *Woman Unknown* is less about the film itself and more about its status as the 'only film solely directed by a woman' in competition. This extreme underrepresentation exposes the structural violence of the film industry: female directors are systematically pushed out of the cognitive entry points, until a jury decides to perform a compensatory act of justice via a unanimous vote that breaks tradition.

The story of a housemaid hiding an intimate past with a German soldier is essentially a game of expression and survival. Marie faces a masculine-centric narrative where her experience is labeled a 'shameful secret.' In a patriarchal evaluation system, a woman's bodily experience is a stain if it falls outside male control or approval. This erasure of women's history is the core of cultural violence: keeping women 'Unknown' to maintain a 'civilized' narrative written by men.

Meanwhile, *NAZA* reveals the most advanced weaponisation of expression in modern warfare. Reducing civilian death tolls to an acronym (NAZA) is a meta-violence operation—dehumanizing victims through language to transform mass killing into a technical metric, allowing the perpetrators to achieve complicity. When the Israeli military 'categorically denies' the testimonies, they are not fighting facts; they are fighting for the power to define reality.

This ceremony feels like a small-scale defection of the conspirators. From validating female directors to exposing AI-driven slaughter, the Actual is moving closer to the Potential. But remember: when a female director must be the 'only one' to be noticed, it is, in itself, a reverse proof of structural violence.
