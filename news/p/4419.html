用女性身体为新球杆做注脚，这很“Good Good”Using Female Bodies as Footnotes for New Drivers: Very "Good Good"

当暴力被包装成幽默，它就在完成一次对权力关系的确认。
When violence is packaged as humor, it is confirming a power dynamic.

一个男共创者从背后将女员工推倒在地，然后威胁地宣布“不要碰我的新球杆”。这段被删掉的广告精准地展示了什么叫 Meta Violence：在男本位叙事里，女性的身体只是一个可以被随意推搡的道具，用来反衬男性对财产（新球杆）的绝对所有权。这种暴力被设计成一种“幽默”的表达，其本质是让观众在笑声中内化一个事实——男性的欲望高于女性的身体完整性。

最讽刺的是 Good Good Golf 的公关辞令。他们谈论“inclusive”（包容），谈论“values”（价值观），但这种 PR 话术是典型的 cultural violence。真正的包容不是在出事后删帖道歉，而是在创作之初就意识到，将“推倒女性”作为笑点，本身就是一种结构性暴力的共谋。Callaway 和 PGA Tour 的表态更是标准模板，在不影响商业利益的前提下，通过谴责一个“不小心”的视频来维持其文明的假象。

这种广告之所以能通过审核并发布，是因为在制作团队的共谋场域中，这种表达被认为是“安全”且“好玩”的。这意味着在他们的认知入口里，女性员工的主体性是缺失的。他们没在拍广告，他们在拍一场关于支配权的微型演习。

A male co-founder shoves a female employee to the ground to menacingly protect his new driver. This deleted ad is a textbook example of Meta Violence: in a masculine-centric narrative, the female body is merely a prop to be pushed around, serving only to highlight the male's absolute ownership of property. By framing this as "humor," the ad attempts to make the audience internalize a specific fact—that male desire supersedes female physical integrity.

The PR response from Good Good Golf is equally telling. They pivot to keywords like "inclusive" and "values," which is pure cultural violence. True inclusivity isn't about deleting a clip after a backlash; it's recognizing that using the assault of a woman as a punchline is a form of complicity in structural violence. The statements from Callaway and the PGA Tour are mere templates, maintaining a facade of civility without disrupting the commercial flow.

That this ad even made it to the upload stage proves that within the company's field of complicity, this expression was viewed as "safe" and "funny." In their cognitive entry point, the female employee has no subjectivity. They weren't filming an ad; they were filming a miniature exercise in dominance.
