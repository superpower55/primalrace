被剔除的名单与被垄断的定义权The Excluded List and the Monopoly of Definition

哲学 文化层 · 结构层 The Athletic ↗ 2026-08-08 § 链接
历史不是事实的堆砌，而是定义权者的筛选结果。
History is not a pile of facts, but a filtered result of those holding the power to define.

西雅图水手队在庆祝 50 周年时，由官方和所谓的“传奇”投票选出 50 位最伟大球员。这本质上是一次典型的权力运作：通过设定一个“伟大”的评价标准，将不符合该标准的个体剔除在正史之外。在体育叙事中，这种筛选权被垄断在管理层和成功者手中，形成了一套关于“价值”的元暴力——只有贡献了冠军、数据或商业价值的人才配被定义为“伟大”。

而这篇文章提供了一份“替代名单”。这里有因为打率太低而留下名字的 Mario Mendoza，有因为一次滑垒摔得最惨的 Butch Huskey，甚至有一只试图攻击场地的猫。这份名单揭示了体育作为一种存在性战争的另一面：那些在博弈中输掉的、被系统判定为“失败”的、甚至只是纯粹古怪的个体，才是真实历史的血肉。官方名单是 PR 版本的神庙，而这份名单才是真实的街道。

更有趣的是文中提到的 MLBPA 播客。那些 60 年代在工厂打工、在服装店兼职的球员，在没有工会保护的年代，他们的主体性被俱乐部完全剥夺，被视为可随时替换的工具。如今的球员享受着高薪，但这种进步并非来自管理层的良心发现，而是通过长期的组织化抗争，将原有的共谋关系强行拆解，夺回了部分解释权和定价权。所谓的“传奇”，如果脱离了这种结构性的斗争，就只是被豢养在历史叙事里的宠物。

As the Seattle Mariners celebrate their 50th anniversary, the official list of the '50 Greatest Players'—voted on by officials and 'legends'—is a textbook exercise in power. By establishing a specific metric for 'greatness,' the system systematically excludes anyone who doesn't fit the mold. In sports narratives, this power of definition is monopolized by management and the winners, creating a form of meta-violence where only those who delivered championships, stats, or commercial value are deemed 'great.'

This article offers an 'alternate list.' It features Mario Mendoza, remembered for a low batting average; Butch Huskey, for the most epic face-plant; and even a kitten that tried to kill a groundskeeper. This list exposes the other side of the existential war in sports: those who lost the game, those labeled as 'failures' by the system, or those who were simply weird, are the actual flesh and blood of history. The official list is a PR temple; this list is the actual street.

Most telling is the mention of the MLBPA podcast. Players from the 60s who worked in factories and clothing stores had their subjectivity entirely stripped by clubs, treated as replaceable tools. Today's players enjoy wealth, but this progress didn't come from the benevolence of owners. It came from organized struggle, breaking the existing complicity to reclaim a portion of the power to define and price their own value. A 'legend,' stripped of this structural struggle, is merely a pet kept in the narrative of history.
