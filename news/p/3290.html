用“韧性”掩盖的结构性谋杀Structural Murder Masked as 'Resilience'

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-05 § 链接
将制度性匮乏定义为“新挑战”，是典型的用叙事掩盖结构暴力的共谋。
Defining systemic failure as a 'new challenge' is a complicit act of masking structural violence through narrative.

Yvette Cooper 将 NHS 无法应对高温导致的死亡定义为“新挑战” (a new thing)，这在叙事上是一个极其阴险的 scam。当 MRI 机器在热浪中失效、病房变成蒸笼、近三千人死亡时，这根本不是什么“新挑战”，而是长期结构性暴力 (structural violence) 的延迟爆发。英国的医疗基础设施不是由于气候突然变坏而失效，而是因为它在男本位的行政逻辑中被长期预算削减、维护缺失，最终在气候危机的压力测试下现了原形。

最荒谬的共谋在于，政府在谈论“韧性” (resilience) 和“公共意识活动” (awareness campaign)。要求民众“关注 elderly relatives 和 young babies”，本质上是将结构性失效的救济责任，通过 cultural violence 转移给个体的私人情感。当国家无法提供基本的空调基础设施时，它要求你用“爱”和“关心”去填补这个致命的缺口。这种叙事将制度的渎职包装成邻里互助的温情，让受害者在共谋的温床中等待死亡。

所谓的“资本计划” (capital programme) 投资，在没有具体时间表和强制执行机制前，只是一个 PR 版本的承诺。在 Potential（一个能应对 40C 高温的医疗系统）与 Actual（机器宕机、病房过热）之间巨大的差额，就是由权力阶层通过定义“新挑战”而合法化的暴力。这种“现实不配合就重新定义现实”的机制，与科技公司宣布新定律来掩盖芯片短缺如出一辙。

Yvette Cooper defining the NHS's inability to handle heat-related deaths as a "new thing" is a sinister narrative scam. When MRI machines fail and nearly 3,000 people die, this isn't a "new challenge"—it is the delayed eruption of long-term structural violence. The UK's healthcare infrastructure didn't fail because the climate suddenly changed; it failed because it was systematically gutted by budget cuts and neglect under a masculine-centric administrative logic, finally exposed by the stress test of the climate crisis.

The most absurd complicity lies in the rhetoric of "resilience" and "awareness campaigns." Asking the public to "look out for elderly relatives and young babies" is essentially transferring the responsibility of systemic failure onto private emotional labor via cultural violence. When the state fails to provide basic air-conditioning infrastructure, it asks you to fill that lethal gap with "love" and "care." This narrative packages institutional dereliction as neighborhood warmth, leaving victims to die in a bed of complicity.

The promised "capital programme" investment is nothing more than a PR version of a promise without a concrete timeline or enforcement mechanism. The massive gap between the Potential (a healthcare system capable of handling 40C) and the Actual (crashing machines and overheating wards) is the violence legitimized by the ruling class through the definition of "new challenges." This mechanism of "redefining reality when reality doesn't cooperate" is identical to tech companies announcing new laws to mask chip shortages.
