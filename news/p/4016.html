复刻 Abbey Road：一场关于男性神坛的昂贵共谋Recreating Abbey Road: An Expensive Complicity of the Masculine Altar

哲学 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-17 § 链接
所有的传记电影本质上都是在通过复刻视觉符号，加固男性中心叙事的解释权。
Biopics are essentially the reinforcement of masculine-centric narrative through the replication of visual symbols.

Sam Mendes 决定用四部电影来分别讲述四个 Beatle，这听起来像是对个体主体性的尊重，但实际上这是一种极其典型的 masculine-centric narrative。当制作组在伦敦街头精准复刻那张 Abbey Road 封面时，他们复刻的不是历史，而是一个被神格化的男性图腾。这种对 iconic 符号的执念，本质上是在通过视觉上的 anachronism，试图在 2028 年再次向观众投放一种“男性天才”的认知入口。

注意看演员名单：John, Ringo, Paul, George 占据了绝对的叙事中心，而 Saoirse Ronan 饰演的 Linda McCartney 等女性角色，在这样的结构中天然地成为了“陪衬”或“附属”。在元暴力的运作机制下，女性在这些传记片中的功能通常只有两种：要么是天才男性的缪斯，要么是其成功路上的情感支撑。她们的存在是为了让男主角的“神性”更加完整，而非作为独立的人权个体存在。

这不仅是电影工业的惯例，更是一场资本与遗产继承者的共谋。Sony Pictures 和幸存的成员通过这种大规模的视觉重塑，将 Beatles 的定义权再次垄断在一种“永恒的、纯粹的男性友谊与才华”的叙事中。当全世界的观众在 2028 年再次为那个横穿马路的背影感动时，这种文化暴力完成了又一次闭环：它告诉我们，什么是值得被记录的“伟大”，以及谁才是历史的主角。

Sam Mendes' decision to dedicate four films to the individual Beatles might look like a respect for subjectivity, but it is a textbook example of a masculine-centric narrative. When the production team meticulously recreates the Abbey Road cover in London, they aren't recreating history—they are reinforcing a deified masculine totem. This obsession with iconic symbols is an attempt to deploy a cognitive entrance of "male genius" to the audience of 2028 through visual anachronism.

Look at the cast: John, Ringo, Paul, and George occupy the absolute center of the narrative. Women like Saoirse Ronan's Linda McCartney are naturally cast as "accessories" or "appendages." Under the mechanism of meta-violence, the function of women in such biopics is binary: either the muse of a genius or the emotional support for his ascent. Their existence serves to complete the "divinity" of the male lead, not to exist as independent human subjects.

This is more than just industry convention; it is a complicity between capital and the estates of the deceased. Sony Pictures and the surviving members are monopolizing the definition of the Beatles, framing them within a narrative of "eternal, pure masculine friendship and talent." When the world is moved by those silhouettes crossing the road in 2028, this cultural violence completes another cycle: it dictates what constitutes "greatness" and who the rightful protagonists of history are.
