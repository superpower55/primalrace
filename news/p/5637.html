被抹除的档案与被重启的“政治黑人”Erased Archives and the Reboot of 'Political Blackness'

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-18 § 链接
暴力是 Potential 与 Actual 的差额，而档案是缩小这个差额的武器。
Violence is the gap between Potential and Actual; archives are the weapons to close it.

一个 13 岁孩子在操场上被杀，这在 direct 层是血淋淋的暴力。但真正令人心惊的是 structural 层：当时的教育大臣 Kenneth Baker 拒绝接受调查报告，拒绝将其放入议会图书馆。这种“拒绝记录”的行为就是典型的 structural violence——通过抹除事实来维持一个“文明”的假象。当权力决定什么不该被记录时，它在定义谁不配作为人类存在。

这正是 AIU 档案中心的意义。档案不是故纸堆，而是对 meta violence 的反击。元暴力通过垄断解释权来制造“事实”，而一个记录了黑人历史、泛非主义运动的档案中心，是在夺回解释权。它在告诉后代：你们的痛苦不是个案，而是一场系统性的殖民延续。这种从 cultural 层发起的反击，是将被掩盖的 Potential（本应获得的公正与认同）转化为 Actual 的唯一路径。

有趣的是“政治黑人” (political Blackness) 概念的回归。在 90 年代，人们追求精确的身份分类，这在某种程度上是主体性的觉醒；但在极右翼 resurgent 的今天，这种“战略性结盟”重新变得最优。因为在 far-right 的认知入口里，所有非白人、所有被视为“他者”的人都被打包成同一个靶子。既然对方在元暴力层面将我们共谋为“敌人”，那么弱势群体通过身份政治进行反向结盟，就是目前最公正的表达。

但不要 naive 地庆祝纪念活动的温情。Home Office 的数据显示仇恨犯罪在 2026 年创下新高。这意味着，尽管档案在积累，但结构性的暴力在加速。如果教育依然被权力把持，如果档案中心只能在纪念日被提及而不能进入课本，那么这种纪念就仅仅是一场表演性的救赎，而非结构性的胜利。

A 13-year-old murdered on a playground is direct violence. But the real horror lies in the structural layer: Education Secretary Kenneth Baker's refusal to accept the inquiry report or place it in the House of Commons library. This 'refusal to record' is classic structural violence—using the erasure of facts to maintain a facade of 'civilization.' When power decides what should not be recorded, it defines who is unworthy of existing as human.

This is precisely why the AIU archive center matters. Archives are not dusty piles of paper; they are a counter-attack against meta-violence. Meta-violence monopolizes the right to interpret and manufacture 'facts.' An archive documenting Black history and Pan-Africanism reclaims that right. It tells future generations that their pain is not an isolated incident, but a systemic continuation of colonialism. This cultural-layer resistance is the only path to transform suppressed Potential (the justice and recognition they deserved) into Actual reality.

Interestingly, the concept of 'political Blackness' is returning. In the 90s, the push for precise identity categories was an awakening of subjectivity; however, in the face of a resurgent far-right, this 'strategic alliance' has once again become the optimal expression. In the cognitive entry points of the far-right, all non-whites and 'others' are packaged into a single target. Since the oppressor uses meta-violence to conspire against them as a collective 'enemy,' it is only just for the marginalized to use identity politics for a reverse alliance.

But let's not be naive about the warmth of memorial events. Home Office data shows hate crimes hit record highs in 2026. This means while archives grow, structural violence is accelerating. If education remains controlled by power, and if archives are only mentioned on anniversaries rather than in textbooks, then this commemoration is merely a performative redemption, not a structural victory.
