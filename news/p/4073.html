用“友善度”量化贫困：一场关于社会割裂的叙事 scamQuantifying Poverty as 'Unfriendliness': A Narrative Scam on Social Division

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-18 § 链接
所谓的“不友善”，不过是结构性暴力在文化层面的投射。
So-called 'unfriendliness' is merely the cultural projection of structural violence.

把“友善度”量化成一个 index，然后告诉人们西利群岛最友善、巴金和达格纳姆最不友善，这是一次典型的 cultural violence。这种量化方式极其傲慢：它把“经常与邻居聊天”和“社区自豪感”定义为友善，却选择性地忽略了这些指标背后的生物墙与阶级壁垒。

西利群岛这种地方之所以“友善”，是因为它在生物学和文化上高度同质化。当一个社区由相似的表型、背景和文化组成时，这种所谓的“友善”本质上是一种基于相似性的结盟，是排他性的舒适区。而在巴金和达格纳姆这种被制度性忽视、充满移民、承受生活成本压力的底层区域，人们在 survival mode 下没有余力去 perform 某种中产式的“邻里社交”。

最讽刺的是，这个 index 的制定者——前保守党和工党的高官们——试图通过一个“国家对话”来解决“社会割裂”。这简直是个巨大的 scam。他们先用一套定义“友善”的权力话语把底层区域标记为“不友善”，然后邀请这些被标记的人来讨论如何“更好地生活在一起”。

这种操作的本质是 meta violence：由掌握解释权的男性政治精英定义什么是“凝聚力”，并将结构性剥削导致的社交匮乏，转化为当地居民“性格”或“文化”上的缺陷。当 Potential（本可达到的社会支持）与 Actual（破碎的社区现状）之间存在巨大差额时，这个差额就是 structural violence，但他们却将其包装成了一个关于“友善”的排名。

Quantifying 'friendliness' into an index and ranking the Isles of Scilly as the friendliest while Barking and Dagenham hit rock bottom is a classic act of cultural violence. This methodology is profoundly arrogant: it defines friendliness through 'chatting with neighbours' and 'local pride,' while conveniently ignoring the biological walls and class barriers underlying these metrics.

The 'friendliness' of the Isles of Scilly is rooted in extreme homogeneity of phenotype, background, and culture. In such a space, friendliness is not a virtue but a form of alliance based on resemblance—an exclusive comfort zone. Conversely, in places like Barking and Dagenham, where people are trapped in survival mode under chronic systemic neglect and cost-of-living pressures, there is no surplus energy to perform the bourgeois ritual of 'neighbourly socialising.'

The peak of the irony lies in the architects of this index—former Tory and Labour elites—who seek to fix 'social division' through a 'National Conversation.' This is a complete scam. They first use their power to define 'friendliness,' marking marginalized areas as 'unfriendly,' and then invite the victims of that labeling to discuss how to 'live well together.'

This is meta violence in action: male political elites monopolizing the interpretation of 'cohesion,' transforming the social fragmentation caused by structural exploitation into a perceived cultural or character flaw of the residents. When the gap between Potential and Actual social support is wide, that gap is structural violence—but they've rebranded it as a friendliness ranking.
