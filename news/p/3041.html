把痴呆症患者变成政治足球：一场关于匮乏的共谋Dementia Patients as Political Footballs: A Conspiracy of Deprivation

国际 文化层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-30 § 链接
将弱势群体的苦难武器化，是权力在失去解释权后的最后一次绝望挣扎。
Weaponizing the suffering of the vulnerable is the final, desperate struggle of power after losing the grip on narrative.

Robert Jenrick 这种典型的 establishment 投机分子，最擅长的是一种“表演性表达”：他把自己包装成“被遗忘的英国”的代言人，实际上却在用最粗鄙的方式将 dementia patients（痴呆症患者）当作 political football。这不仅仅是政治上的低级，而是一次典型的表达武器化。他通过制造一个并不存在的“死亡税”叙事，试图在认知入口处植入恐惧，将结构性的社会关怀危机转化为一种情绪化的 grievance 政治。

有趣的是，这次博弈中出现了一个关键的共谋节点崩塌：连 GB News 这种长期为右翼叙事背书的媒体都感到 cringe。这意味着 Jenrick 的表达已经脱离了某种“社会公约”的最优解，进入了纯粹的 deranged 状态。当一个政客在公开场合地吹嘘 offshore accounts（离岸账户）可以帮权贵避税，而让穷人承担社会护理成本时，他实际上是在撕掉文化暴力的伪装，直接向公众展示元暴力的逻辑——即：资源分配的解释权永远掌握在像他这样的人手中，而弱势群体的身体仅仅是他们用来交换选票的筹码。

这种“苦难色情” (misery porn) 的本质是夺取解释权。他不需要提供真正的 solution，因为提供方案意味着要进入 structural layer 去调整资源分配，而他追求的是在 cultural layer 制造冲突。在这种叙事里，痴呆症患者不是需要救助的人，而是被客体化为攻击对手的工具。当一个人把他人的生存危机当成自己的 wish fulfilment 时，这种存在性战争已经进入了最恶劣的阶段：施暴者通过定义受害者的“绝望”，来确认自己的“权力”。

Robert Jenrick, a quintessential establishment opportunist, excels in a form of 'performance expression': posing as the voice of 'forgotten Britain' while crudely turning dementia patients into political footballs. This is more than just political ineptitude; it is a textbook weaponisation of expression. By manufacturing a non-existent 'death tax' narrative, he attempts to inject fear at the cognitive entry point, converting a structural social care crisis into a politics of grievance.

Interestingly, a key node of complicity collapsed in this game: even GB News, a long-term cheerleader for right-wing narratives, felt the cringe. This suggests Jenrick's expression has drifted away from any social 'optimal solution' and entered a state of pure derangement. When a politician openly boasts that offshore accounts protect the elite from paying for social care while the poor suffer, he strips away the mask of cultural violence and exposes the raw logic of meta-violence: the power to interpret and distribute resources remains a monopoly of his class, while the bodies of the vulnerable are merely chips to be traded for votes.

This 'misery porn' is fundamentally about seizing the right to interpret. He has no interest in real solutions because that would require engaging with the structural layer of resource allocation. Instead, he seeks to manufacture conflict at the cultural layer. In his world, dementia patients are not humans in need of care, but objectified tools for attacking opponents. When a man treats another's existential crisis as his own wish fulfilment, the existential war reaches its most vile stage: the oppressor confirms his own power by defining the 'despair' of the oppressed.
