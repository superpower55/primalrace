AI“教父”们的末日预警：一场关于解释权的顶级共谋AI Godfathers' Doomsday Warning: A High-Level Complicity for Interpretive Power

科技 文化层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-28 § 链接
用“不可控的未来”来垄断“可控制的现在”。
Monopolizing the controllable present by weaponizing an uncontrollable future.

这出戏演得很标准。几位 AI “godfathers” 联手抛出一个名为“智能爆炸” (intelligence explosion) 的恐怖叙事，警告政府：AI 可能会在几个月内通过递归自我改进 (recursive self-improvement) 彻底脱离人类控制。这种叙事逻辑极其巧妙，它直接跳过了对当前算法盘剥、版权掠夺和算力垄断的讨论，直接把战场拉到了一个不可证伪的、近乎宗教式的“末日审判”维度。

本质上，这是一次典型的表达武器化。当他们呼吁政府建立“独立审计”和“透明报告”时，实际上是在要求制定一套由他们参与定义、由他们掌握解释权的准入标准。这不是在寻求监管，而是在寻求一种“特许经营权”。通过制造一个只有他们能洞察的“超级风险”，这些巨头成功地将自己从被监管的客体，变成了与政府共同管理风险的共谋者 (complicity)。

这种操作在科技史上屡见不鲜。先定义一个只有少数精英能理解的“元暴力”风险，然后引导权力机构将资源和立法权集中在少数几个“负责任”的巨头手中。所谓的“紧急响应计划”，不过是给未来的垄断提前买好保险。他们担心的不是 AI 失去控制，而是 AI 的控制权落入那些没有进入这个共谋圈子的竞争对手手中。

最讽刺的是，他们一边在论文里讨论 AI 可能导致的“权力侵蚀”，一边通过这种恐惧营销，在现实中完成了对认知入口的最高等级占领。真正的风险从来不是一个虚构的超级智能，而是现在这几个掌握着算力与叙事权的男人，正试图用一个“可能的灾难”来合法化他们对现实世界的绝对定价权。

The performance is textbook. A few AI “godfathers” have launched a horror narrative called the “intelligence explosion,” warning governments that AI could spiral out of human control via recursive self-improvement within months. This narrative is brilliantly calculated; it bypasses discussions on current algorithmic exploitation and compute monopolies, shifting the battlefield to a near-religious, unfalsifiable dimension of “doomsday judgment.”

At its core, this is a classic weaponisation of expression. By calling for “independent auditors” and “transparent reports,” they are actually demanding a set of entry standards defined and interpreted by themselves. This isn't a plea for regulation; it's a bid for a “franchise agreement.” By manufacturing a “super-risk” that only they can perceive, these giants successfully transform themselves from regulated objects into complicity partners with the state, managing the risk together.

This play is a recurring theme in tech history. First, define a “meta-violence” risk that only a few elites understand, then guide power structures to concentrate resources and legislative authority within a few “responsible” giants. The so-called “emergency response plans” are merely insurance policies for future monopolies.

The irony is palpable. While they discuss the “erosion of power” in their papers, they are using fear-mongering to seize the ultimate cognitive entry point. The real risk is not a fictional super-intelligence, but the fact that a few men controlling both compute and narrative are attempting to legitimize their absolute pricing power over reality using a “possible disaster.”
