特朗普及他的“婚礼新娘”表演艺术Trump and the Art of the 'Wedding Bride' Pageantry

国际 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-07 § 链接
当权力演变为纯粹的个人崇拜，政党就成了偶像的陪衬物。
When power evolves into pure personality cult, the party becomes a mere prop for the idol.

这场所谓的“中期大会”根本不是政治活动，而是一场典型的 weaponized expression。它不是为了讨论政策或制定纲领，而是一次精心包装的 Trump show。在政治逻辑中，大会应该是为了达成共识并分配资源，但这次的认知入口被完全垄断：一切为了特朗普及他的存在感。

这种“婚礼新娘”式的表达方式，揭示了共和党内部深刻的共谋关系。那些在官网上悄悄删除特朗普及相关标签的候选人们，正在进行一场关于生存的博弈。他们很清楚，在 approval rating 跌至 33% 的现实面前，过度绑定特朗普及他的 narcissism 是一个极差的最优解。但与此同时，他们又必须在大会上扮演忠诚的追随者，这种“表演性忠诚”就是典型的假.最优解表达——通过扮演他者认可的角色换取短期政治生存，代价是主体性的彻底死亡。

最讽刺的是，共和党试图用“民主社会主义者”和“共产主义”这种过时的文化暴力叙事来转移注意力，试图将选举变成一场关于“美国方式”的 existential war。但这不过是掩盖其 structural violence 的 PR 掩体：他们避谈不受欢迎的伊朗战争，避谈生活成本，只剩下空洞的 pageantry。当一个政党将自己的命运押注在一个人对聚光灯的渴求上时，它已经不再是一个政治组织，而是一个大型的、由共谋者支撑的偶像产业。

This so-called 'midterm convention' is not a political event, but a classic case of weaponized expression. It is not about debating policy or drafting platforms; it is a meticulously packaged Trump show. In political logic, a convention should be about consensus and resource allocation, but here, the cognitive entry point is completely monopolized: everything serves Trump's sense of existence.

This 'bride at every wedding' style of expression reveals a deep complicity within the Republican Party. The candidates quietly scrubbing Trump's likeness from their websites are engaged in a game of survival. They realize that with approval ratings at 33%, hitching their wagon to Trump's narcissism is a terrible optimal expression. Yet, they must still perform loyalty at the convention. This 'performative loyalty' is a textbook fake optimal expression—playing a role recognized by others for short-term gain at the cost of the death of their own subjectivity.

Most ironic is the GOP's attempt to pivot toward outdated cultural violence narratives, casting Democrats as 'godless communists' to frame the election as an existential war for the 'American way.' This is merely a PR screen to hide structural violence: they avoid the unpopular war in Iran and the cost of living, leaving only empty pageantry. When a party bets its destiny on one man's craving for the spotlight, it ceases to be a political organization and becomes a massive idol industry sustained by co-conspirators.
