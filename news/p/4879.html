文明的掩体与深渊的共谋The Shelter of Civilization and the Complicity of the Abyss

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-09 § 链接
所谓的“自由主义危机”其实是元暴力在换皮重启。
The so-called 'crisis of liberalism' is merely meta-violence rebooting with a new skin.

这篇文章试图用一种典型的 liberal idealism 叙事来警示欧洲，把极右翼的崛起描述成一次意外的“坠落”。但如果用加尔通的暴力三角来看，这根本不是坠落，而是 structural violence 在文化层面的延迟兑现。所谓的“宽容”与“和解”，在很多时候只是男性中心叙事（masculine-centric narrative）构建的一套文明掩体，用来掩盖其内部从未真正消弭的排他性。

AfD 的崛起、Musk 的助推以及 Farage 的表演，本质上是一场关于“认知入口”的武器化争夺。他们不再伪装成文明的守护者，而是直接通过生物学定义（biologically improper）来重新划定身份的政治性。这其实就是一种极其粗暴的 meta violence：通过垄断对“国民”和“文明”的解释权，将不符合其标准的人口定义为“非人”或“异类”。

最讽刺的是，这些极右翼政客在博弈中的“最优解表达”正在发生异化。Farage 这种老牌玩家发现，在 MAGA 和克里姆林宫的全球化叙事面前，传统的“温和伪装”失效了。现在的游戏规则是：谁能更极端地地定义“敌我”，谁就能在数字空间的注意力博弈中获得更高的票值。这是一种典型的存在性战争，只不过这次的武器是种族主义和阴谋论。

文章结尾试图用“宽容的力量”来提供希望，这太 naive 了。在这种结构性暴力面前，简单的“呼吁”只是表演性的让步。如果不能拆掉那个由男性中心叙事构建的、定义谁才是“文明人”的元暴力基座，所谓的“避开深渊”不过是给深渊换了一层更体面的地毯。

The article attempts to warn Europe through a typical liberal idealism narrative, framing the far-right rise as an accidental 'fall.' But through the lens of the Violence Triangle, this isn't a fall; it is the delayed manifestation of structural violence at the cultural level. The so-called 'tolerance' and 'conciliation' have often been nothing more than a civilized shelter built by a masculine-centric narrative to mask an inherent and unextinguished exclusivity.

The rise of the AfD, Musk's amplification, and Farage's performance are essentially a weaponization of 'cognitive entry points.' They no longer pretend to be guardians of civilization; instead, they redefine the political nature of identity through biological definitions (biologically improper). This is pure meta-violence: monopolizing the power to define 'citizen' and 'civilization' to categorize non-conforming populations as 'non-human' or 'others.'

Most ironic is how the 'optimal expression' of these far-right politicians is mutating. Old players like Farage find that traditional 'moderate camouflage' fails in the face of the globalized narratives of MAGA and the Kremlin. The current rule of the game is: whoever can more extremely define 'friend vs. foe' wins more value in the attention game of digital spaces. This is an existential war where the weapons are racism and conspiracy theories.

The article's conclusion, hoping in the 'power of tolerance,' is too naive. In the face of such structural violence, simple 'appeals' are merely performative concessions. Unless we dismantle the base of meta-violence—the masculine-centric narrative that defines who is 'civilized'—'avoiding the abyss' is just putting a fancier carpet over the hole.
