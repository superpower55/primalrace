所谓的“去污名化”不过是精英阶层的自我赎罪Destigmatization as Elite Self-Absolution

哲学 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-15 § 链接
用电影的“同情”掩盖制度性的“剥夺”，是最高级的文化暴力。
Replacing structural critique with cinematic 'compassion' is a sophisticated form of cultural violence.

Will Poulter 在访谈里大谈特谈“去污名化” (destigmatization)，呼吁人们给药物成瘾者更多 compassion。这种叙事看起来很 an elegant，但本质上是一场典型的 cultural violence。当一个好莱坞演员通过“模拟”痛苦来获得艺术成就，并以此呼吁大众给底层群体一点“善意”时，他实际上是在强化一种权力结构：强者在施舍理解，弱者在等待被宽恕。

成瘾不是一个关于“自尊心在地下室”的心理问题，而是一个 structural violence 的结果。Poulter 提到的 14 岁被开处方药、家庭暴力、被驱逐出境，这些才是真正的暴力入口。但电影和访谈将重点转向了“成功率 50% 的康复计划”和“肯定的话语”，这把一个制度性的掠夺问题，成功地转化为一个个体努力与情感支持的励志故事。这就是 weaponized narrative：用一个 a poetic depiction 掩盖了资本和医疗体制如何制造了这些“成瘾者”。

最讽刺的是，当 Trump 政府砍掉 10 亿美金的心理健康预算时，这种“电影式的关怀”成了唯一的安慰剂。这种共谋极其隐秘：好莱坞通过拍摄底层苦难来维持其“进步”的形象，而观众在电影院里流泪，觉得自己已经完成了对弱势群体的救赎。但 Actual 状态并没有改变，Potential 与 Actual 之间的差额依然巨大，甚至因为这种“同情”的出现而变得更难被察觉。在这种叙事里，成瘾者再次成为了被凝视的客体，他们的痛苦被定价为电影的艺术价值。

Will Poulter’s discourse on 'destigmatization' and 'compassion' for opioid addicts is a textbook example of cultural violence. When a Hollywood actor 'simulates' pain to achieve artistic acclaim and then calls for public kindness, he reinforces a rigid power hierarchy: the strong grant understanding, while the weak wait for absolution.

Addiction is not a psychological glitch where 'self-respect is in the basement'; it is the output of structural violence. The 14-year-olds prescribed Oxycontin and the victims of domestic violence are products of a system that prioritizes profit over people. By shifting the focus to a '50% success rate' and the power of affirmation, the film transforms a systemic plunder into an individualistic tale of resilience. This is a weaponized narrative: using a 'poetic depiction' to sanitize the mechanism that manufactured these addicts in the first place.

It is profoundly cynical that while the Trump administration slashes billions in mental health funding, 'cinematic empathy' is offered as a substitute. This is a form of complicity: Hollywood maintains its 'progressive' brand by consuming the trauma of the marginalized, and the audience feels a sense of moral completion through their tears. The Actual state remains unchanged; the gap between Potential and Actual persists, now hidden behind a veil of pity. In this framework, the addict remains a mere object of the gaze, their suffering priced as artistic value.
