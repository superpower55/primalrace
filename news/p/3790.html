38.1℃的温度计与被隐形的死亡账单38.1°C and the Invisible Death Toll

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-13 § 链接
气候危机不是自然灾害，而是结构性暴力在生物层面的最终结算。
The climate crisis is not a natural disaster, but the biological settlement of structural violence.

新闻在谈论 38.1℃ 的纪录，在谈论 Kew Gardens 的温度计，在谈论核电站为了冷却水而铺石头。这些都是典型的 cultural violence 叙事：将一场系统性的屠杀包装成一系列“极端天气事件”或“纪录更新”。当媒体在惊叹数字时，他们成功地将一个关于权力、资源分配和资本贪婪的政治问题，转化为了一场关于气象学的讨论。

按照加尔通的暴力三角，这里的 Violence = Potential − Actual。人类本可以生活在一个可持续的生态中，但 Actual 状态被化石燃料资本主义强行拉低。这个差额就是暴力。而这种暴力在结构层 (structural layer) 表现为：谁在空调房里制定政策，谁在“滴汗如雨”的 NHS 病房里面对 240 万名绝望的患者？谁在面对水资源短缺时，依然被允许为了建数据中心而提高水费？

最令人作呕的是那种“沉默杀手” (silent killer) 的说法。12,500 人的死亡被轻描淡写地归因为“高温”，而没有被定义为“被谋杀”。因为在男性中心叙事 (masculine-centric narrative) 构建的工业文明中，对自然的掠夺、对地球生物墙的强拆被定义为“进步”和“增长”。这种元暴力 (meta violence) 让人们习惯于将结构性的屠杀视为“不可抗力”。

当 Labour 党的 150 万套住房目标撞上水资源匮乏的现实时，这不再是简单的规划冲突，而是一次存在性战争的预演。资源在萎缩，而共谋者们——那些水工业监管机构和地产开发商——依然在尝试通过提高账单来维持他们的最优解表达。这场游戏的真正代价，永远由那些无法在公共空间发声的弱势群体，用生命来支付。

The news focuses on the 38.1°C record, the thermometers at Kew Gardens, and nuclear plants laying stones for cooling water. This is classic cultural violence: packaging a systemic massacre as a series of "extreme weather events" or "record-breaking peaks." While the media marvels at the numbers, they successfully pivot a political issue of power, resource distribution, and corporate greed into a meteorological discussion.

Applying Galtung's triangle, Violence = Potential − Actual. Humanity could have existed in a sustainable ecology, but the Actual state was dragged down by fossil-fuel capitalism. This gap is violence. At the structural layer, this manifests as a stark divide: who sets policy in air-conditioned rooms, and who faces 2.4 million desperate patients in NHS wards while "dripping with sweat"? Why is it that water bills are raised for data centers while basic survival resources vanish?

Most repulsive is the term "silent killer." 12,500 deaths are casually attributed to "heat" rather than being defined as "murder." In the masculine-centric narrative of industrial civilization, the plunder of nature and the demolition of the planet's biological walls are defined as "progress" and "growth." This meta violence trains us to accept structural slaughter as an "act of God."

When Labour's target of 1.5 million homes crashes into the reality of water scarcity, it is no longer a simple planning conflict, but a prelude to an existential war. Resources are shrinking, yet the complicity between water regulators and developers continues, attempting to maintain their optimal expression by hiking bills. The true cost of this game is always paid in lives by the marginalized who have no voice in the public square.
