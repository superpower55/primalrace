从模因到刀刃：国家机器如何为暴力定价From Memes to Blades: How the State Prices Violence

国际 文化层 · 结构层 · 直接层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-18 § 链接
官方叙事的‘玩笑’是暴力的前置指令，文化暴力直接导向肉体屠杀。
Official narratives are pre-orders for violence; cultural violence leads directly to physical slaughter.

被捅了17刀的 Singh 先生在问‘为什么这会发生’，但答案就写在国土安全部（DHS）那张被删除的海报里。‘Self deport or Find Out’，配合一个带有种族刻板印象的锡克教司机形象，这根本不是什么‘对个案的指涉’，而是一次极其精准的 weaponized expression。当国家机器通过官方账号将特定族群定义为‘不合格的驾驶者’、‘应当被驱逐的异类’时，它实际上是在公共空间里为针对该族群的暴力‘定价’——它告诉潜在的施暴者：攻击这些人是正义的，甚至是幽默的。

这就是典型的加尔通暴力三角联动：DHS 的海报是 cultural violence（文化暴力），它通过制造‘他者’的低劣感，将结构性的排外情绪合法化；这种文化暴力为 structural violence（结构暴力）铺路，让该群体在法律和行政保护中处于边缘；最后，这种共识在现实中坍塌为 direct violence（直接暴力），变成了 17 刀的血迹。

最令人作呕的是 DHS 的回应：称将袭击归咎于其言论是‘轻率且虚假的’。这种掩体叙事试图将‘官方模因’与‘个人犯罪’切割开来，但他们忘了，权力本身就是认知入口的掌控者。当政府在 X 上发布种族漫画时，它已经完成了从‘执法者’到‘共谋者’的身份切换。它不仅在制造仇恨，它在为杀戮提供剧本。

Mr. Singh, stabbed 17 times, asks 'why this happened,' but the answer is written plainly in that deleted DHS poster. 'Self deport or Find Out,' paired with a racist caricature of a Sikh driver, is not a 'reference to a case'—it is a precisely weaponized expression. When the state machinery defines a specific group as 'incompetent drivers' or 'aliens to be deported' via official channels, it is effectively 'pricing' violence against that group. It signals to potential assailants that attacking these people is just, or even humorous.

This is the Galtung Violence Triangle in full motion: the DHS poster is cultural violence, creating a sense of inferiority and legitimizing xenophobia. This cultural layer reinforces structural violence, pushing the group to the margins of legal and administrative protection. Finally, this consensus collapses into direct violence—17 stab wounds in a rest stop.

Most repulsive is the DHS response, calling the link between their rhetoric and the attack 'RECKLESS and FALSE.' This cover narrative attempts to decouple 'official memes' from 'individual crime,' ignoring that power is the ultimate controller of cognitive entry points. The moment the government posts a racist caricature, it switches identities from 'law enforcer' to complicity. It is not just spreading hate; it is writing the script for murder.
