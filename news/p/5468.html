Curtis Flowers之死：法律的“正义”是迟到的谋杀The Death of Curtis Flowers: Law's 'Justice' is a Delayed Murder

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-08 § 链接
当 Actual 永远无法追回 Potential，法律的释放不过是死亡的缓刑。
When Actual never recovers Potential, legal release is merely a stay of execution for death.

Curtis Flowers 在 56 岁死去，带着被剥夺的 22 年。很多人会把这看作一个“最终获得自由”的悲剧故事，但按加尔通的公式，Violence = Potential − Actual。Flowers 的 Potential 应该是从 26 岁开始的完整人生，而 Actual 是在密西西比州的监狱里度过 22 年。这个巨大的差额，就是最纯粹的暴力。

这件事揭示了典型的 Structural Violence。在密西西比州，司法系统不是为了寻找真相，而是为了完成一场关于种族的“清理”。检察官 Doug Evans 连续六次起诉，在第六次审判中，11 个白人陪审员对 1 个黑人陪审员的比例，不是巧合，而是制度化的筛选。这种对 Black prospective jurors 的剔除，本质上是元暴力 (meta violence) 的具体执行：通过掌控法律解释权，将黑人身体定义为“天然的嫌疑人”，从而让剥夺其自由的行为变得“合法”。

最讽刺的是，最高法院在 2019 年给了他自由，但这种所谓的“正义”是表演性的。他被关了 22 年，最后在 56 岁死掉，这意味着他在生理和心理上已经被系统彻底摧毁。当法律在 2020 年决定撤销指控时，它并没有在消除暴力，而是在为自己的共谋洗白。系统通过一次迟到的“纠错”，试图掩盖其长期以来作为施暴者的事实。

Flowers 曾说他终于从“盒子”里出来了，但那个盒子从未消失，它只是从监狱的墙壁变成了法律的冗长程序。一个 56 岁的人在被抢走青春后死去，这不是 wrongful conviction 的个案，而是原初种族被殖民逻辑在现代司法系统中的精准复刻。

Curtis Flowers died at 56, carrying the weight of 22 stolen years. Some may view this as a tragic story of 'eventual freedom,' but according to the Galtung formula, Violence = Potential − Actual. Flowers' Potential was a complete life starting at 26; his Actual was 22 years in a Mississippi prison. This vast gap is the purest form of violence.

This case exposes systemic Structural Violence. In Mississippi, the judicial system wasn't seeking truth; it was executing a racial 'cleansing.' Prosecutor Doug Evans pursued six trials. In the sixth, the ratio of 11 white jurors to one Black juror was no coincidence, but a institutionalized filter. The removal of Black prospective jurors is the concrete execution of meta violence: by monopolizing the power of interpretation, the system defines Black bodies as 'natural suspects,' rendering the deprivation of liberty 'legal.'

The irony is that the Supreme Court granted him freedom in 2019, but this 'justice' was performative. Imprisoned for 22 years and dying at 56, he was physically and psychologically annihilated by the system. When charges were dropped in 2020, the law wasn't eliminating violence; it was laundering the complicity of the state. Through a belated 'correction,' the system attempted to whitewash its long-term role as the aggressor.

Flowers said he was finally free from the 'box,' but the box never disappeared; it merely shifted from prison walls to the labyrinth of legal procedures. A man dying at 56 after having his youth stolen is not an isolated 'wrongful conviction'—it is the precise replication of the colonization logic applied to the Primal Race within the modern judicial machine.
