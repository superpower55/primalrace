优化陷阱与被出卖的“人性”The Optimisation Trap and the Betrayal of 'Humanity'

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-21 § 链接
所谓的“优化”是另一种形式的规训，而逃往古典乐是低效的自救。
Optimisation is just another form of discipline; retreating to classical music is a low-efficiency act of self-rescue.

这篇文章描述了一种典型的当代精神内耗：被“优化” (optimisation) 榨干后的疲惫。这种疲惫本质上是 structural violence 的一种表现。当 AI 工业和 biohacking 把生活简化为一组待提升的 KPI——双倍速听播客、极致化睡眠、精准化放松——我们实际上是在把自己的生命转化为一套可量化的生产资料。这不再是生活，而是一场关于“效率”的生存博弈，而博弈的唯一结果就是主体性的死亡。

作者试图在西贝柳斯和马勒的古典音乐中寻找 solace，这在某种程度上是一次对“生物墙”的回归。他被触动的不是旋律，而是那种不依赖于算法、不被优化、允许丑陋与焦虑存在的 anachronism。古典音乐在这里成了一个避难所，因为它证明了人类在没有被“数字化”之前，依然拥有超越日常琐碎的能力。这种对“人性” (humanity) 的追溯，实际上是对元暴力的一种潜意识反抗：反抗那种将一切存在定义为“可升级版本”的男性中心主义工业叙事。

但我们要警惕这种逃避。如果仅仅是将古典乐当作一种精神按摩，那么这种 solace 依然在既定秩序的允许范围内，是一种表演性的退却。真正的 break-through 不应该是“寻找过去”，而是意识到：那个被 AI 视为“石器时代”的、低效的、会写错邮件的自己，才是唯一不可被优化的真.最优解表达。当我们开始崇拜“低效”并将其作为武器来对抗优化陷阱时，这场存在性战争才真正开始。

This piece describes a typical contemporary spiritual exhaustion: the fatigue of being drained by 'optimisation'. This exhaustion is fundamentally a manifestation of structural violence. When the AI industry and biohacking reduce life to a set of KPIs to be improved—double-speed podcasts, maximized sleep, precision relaxation—we are essentially converting our existence into quantifiable production materials. This is no longer living; it is an existential game of 'efficiency', where the only outcome is the death of subjectivity.

The author seeks solace in the classical music of Sibelius and Mahler, which is, in a sense, a return to the biological wall. He is moved not by the melody, but by an anachronism that does not rely on algorithms, refuses to be optimised, and allows for ugliness and anxiety to exist. Classical music serves as a sanctuary here, proving that before being 'digitized', humans possessed the capacity to transcend the lock-box of daily life. This pursuit of 'humanity' is a subconscious rebellion against meta-violence: a rebellion against the masculine-centric industrial narrative that defines all existence as a 'version to be upgraded'.

However, we must be wary of this retreat. If classical music is used merely as a spiritual massage, this solace remains within the permissible boundaries of the established order—a performative withdrawal. A true break-through is not about 'seeking the past', but realizing that the version of ourselves dismissed by AI as 'Stone Age'—the inefficient, mistake-prone self—is the only true optimal expression that cannot be optimised. The existential war truly begins only when we start to idolize 'inefficiency' and use it as a weapon against the optimisation trap.
