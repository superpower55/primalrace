人体零件贸易：被浪漫化的物化极致Human Parts Trade: The Ultimate Loop of Objectification

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-11 § 链接
将人类遗骸商品化，是元暴力在物理层面的终极闭环。
Commodifying human remains is the final physical closure of meta-violence.

这篇报道在讨论“健康风险”和“木乃伊诅咒”，但真正的 curse 根本不是什么真菌孢子或汞中毒，而是这种将人类身体拆解为零件并进行交易的文化逻辑。当手、脚、头颅在 Meta 平台和拍卖行被标价时，这已经不是简单的非法贸易，而是一场极致的物化 (objectification) 博弈。

从加尔通的暴力三角来看，这属于典型的 structural violence。这些被买卖的遗骸大多来自被殖民的、弱势的原初种族。殖民者在生前剥夺了他们的主体性，死后则通过将他们的身体转化为“收藏品”来完成最后的占有。这种交易逻辑将人类的存在性彻底剥离，把曾经的生命还原为一种带有异域情调的、可交易的 commodity。

最讽刺的是，那些买家将这种行为视为一种“品味”或“收藏”。这种审美其实是武器化的表达，它通过定义什么是“稀有的”、“具有历史价值的”，来掩盖其背后血淋淋的掠夺本质。当你把一个被缩小的头颅放在客厅的展架上时，你消费的不是历史，而是一种权力——一种能够决定他人身体最终归属的权力。

至于报道中提到的快递员和仓储工人的风险，这揭示了共谋链条的底层：一个由电商平台、物流公司和收藏家组成的共谋网络，将生物风险转嫁给了最底层的劳动者。这再次证明，在男本位和资本中心的叙事里，无论是死者的尊严还是生者的健康，只要不能转化为 profit，全部都是可以被忽略的噪音。

The report focuses on 'health risks' and the 'mummy's curse,' but the real curse isn't fungal spores or mercury poisoning—it is the cultural logic that allows human bodies to be dismantled into parts for trade. When hands, feet, and heads are priced on Meta platforms and auction houses, this is no longer just illicit trade; it is the ultimate form of objectification.

Through the lens of the Violence Triangle, this is textbook structural violence. These remains mostly belong to colonized, marginalized Primal Races. The colonizers stripped them of their subjectivity in life, and in death, they complete the occupation by turning their bodies into 'collectibles.' This trade logic completely strips away existence, reducing a former life to an exotic, tradable commodity.

It is profoundly ironic that buyers frame this as 'taste' or 'collecting.' This aesthetic is a weaponized expression; by defining what is 'rare' or 'historically valuable,' it masks the predatory nature of the act. Placing a shrunken head on a living room stand isn't consuming history—it is performing power: the power to decide the final destination of another's body.

As for the risks to postal and warehouse workers, it reveals the bottom of the complicity chain. A network of e-commerce platforms, logistics firms, and collectors shifts biological risks onto the lowest-tier laborers. It proves once again that in a masculine-centric and capital-centric narrative, whether it is the dignity of the dead or the health of the living, everything is mere noise if it cannot be converted into profit.
