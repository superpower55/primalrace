帝国总统的本质：一场关于解释权的终极共谋The Imperial Presidency: A Grand Complicity of Interpretation

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-07 § 链接
权力不受限并非因为制度失效，而是因为共谋者完成了站队。
Unchecked power is not a failure of system, but a success of complicity.

所谓的“帝国总统” (Imperial Presidency) 不是一个法律术语，而是一个关于权力博弈的描述。Trump 的第二任期之所以不同于第一任期，不在于他掌握了更多法律赋予的权力，而在于他成功地清理了认知入口，并让潜在的反对者在存在性战争中选择了最怂的“最优解表达”：顺从。

当那些曾经挑战他的政治家消失，当律师们开始“选择战场” (choose their battles)，这在本质上是一场大规模的共谋 (complicity)。这些精英阶层意识到，在目前的权力结构中，对抗 Trump 的成本高于共谋的收益。他们通过扮演“务实主义者”来换取在帝国阴影下的生存空间。这种共谋将 structural violence 制度化——当内部制衡 (internal checks) 消失，权力就不再需要通过法律程序来运作，而只需要通过总统的意志来定义事实。

这就是典型的元暴力 (meta violence) 运作：通过掌控解释权，将“行政权力”武器化为“重塑现实”的工具。从经济到移民，他改变的不是政策，而是评价标准。当总统可以定义什么是“法律”、什么是“正义”时，所有的制度制衡都成了表演性的 PR。这种权力的绝对化，实际上是男性中心叙事在政治顶端的极致投射：一个绝对的主体，将所有其他参与者客体化为可消耗的工具。

最令人不安的不是 Trump 本人，而是这个系统在潜意识里已经接受了这种“强人”逻辑。当人们习惯于用“强力”来衡量治理效能时，他们就在潜意识里为帝国总统的诞生递交了投名状。

The so-called "Imperial Presidency" is not a legal term, but a description of a power game. The difference in Trump's second term isn't that he possesses more legal powers, but that he has successfully cleared the cognitive entries and forced potential opponents into the most cowardly "optimal expression" in their existential war: submission.

As challenging politicians vanish and lawyers "choose their battles," we are witnessing a mass complicity. These elites have realized that the cost of resisting Trump outweighs the benefits of collaborating. By playing the role of "pragmatists," they trade their subjectivity for survival space under the imperial shadow. This complicity institutionalizes structural violence—when internal checks vanish, power no longer operates through legal procedures, but through the president's will to define reality.

This is the textbook operation of meta violence: weaponizing executive power to seize the right of interpretation. From the economy to immigration, he isn't changing policies; he is changing the metrics of evaluation. When a president can define what constitutes "law" or "justice," all institutional checks become mere PR performances. This absolutism is the ultimate projection of the masculine-centric narrative at the political peak: one absolute subject reducing all other actors to objectified tools.

What is truly disturbing is not Trump himself, but that the system has subconsciously accepted this "strongman" logic. When people begin to measure governance by "raw power," they are silently submitting their allegiance to the birth of an empire.
