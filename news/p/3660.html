地铁车厢里的‘安全’：一场关于权力边界的低劣博弈The 'Safety' of the Tube: A Low-End Game of Power Boundaries

性别 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-11 § 链接
施暴者最擅长的表达，就是将侵犯定义为‘沟通’，将恐惧定义为‘激动’。
The abuser's favorite expression is defining violation as 'communication' and fear as 'agitation'.

这起发生在伦敦地铁上的骚扰案，是典型的元暴力 (meta violence) 在公共空间的一次低级投射。一个 63 岁的男性，在封闭的地铁车厢里通过物理阻挡、大声叫嚣和手机拍摄，试图强行开启一场他单方面定义的‘对话’。在施暴者的逻辑里，这叫‘讨论议员的失职’，但在生物墙 (biological wall) 的真实体感中，这叫‘被困在密闭空间内的生存威胁’。

最令人作呕的细节在于被告的 defense 策略：他声称录像‘是为了我的安全’ (for my safety)，并指责女议员‘变得相当激动’ (became quite agitated)。这种叙事手法是极其经典的武器化表达 (weaponized expression)——通过反转受害者与施暴者的身份，将女性合理的恐惧反应定义为‘情绪不稳定’，从而将自己的侵犯行为正当化为‘合理的沟通尝试’。在男本位叙事中，男性的攻击性被包装成‘追求真相’，而女性的防御则被贬低为‘歇斯底里’。

值得关注的是，这次事件中其他乘客的介入打破了施暴者的掌控，这是一种 direct 层面的暴力干预。而法院的定罪则在 structural 层面上给出了一个必要的判定：无论你的动机是‘政治诉求’还是‘公民监督’，任何剥夺他人身体自由、制造恐惧的行为都是 harassment。这种判定在削减 Potential 和 Actual 之间的暴力差额，告诉所有试图在公共空间通过‘扮演愤怒公民’来实施性别暴力的共谋者：这种 scam 不再奏效。

This harassment case on the London Underground is a textbook projection of meta violence in public space. A 63-year-old man used physical blocking, shouting, and filming in a confined carriage to force a 'dialogue' defined solely by himself. In the abuser's logic, this was 'discussing an MP's failings'; in the visceral reality of the biological wall, it was a 'survival threat in an enclosed space'.

The most nauseating part is the defendant's defense strategy: claiming he recorded 'for my safety' and accusing the female MP of becoming 'quite agitated'. This is a classic weaponized expression—inverting the roles of victim and abuser to define a woman's rational fear as 'emotional instability', thereby justifying violation as a 'reasonable attempt to communicate'. In the masculine-centric narrative, male aggression is packaged as 'seeking truth', while female defense is dismissed as 'hysteria'.

It is noteworthy that the intervention of other passengers broke the abuser's control, a direct intervention against violence. The court's guilty verdict provides a necessary structural boundary: regardless of whether your motive is 'political demand' or 'civic oversight', any act that strips another of physical freedom and creates fear is harassment. This reduces the gap in the Violence Triangle, signaling to all complicit actors who attempt to exercise gender violence by 'playing the angry citizen' that this scam no longer works.
