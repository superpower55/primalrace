以“文明”之名，行全球收割之实Civilization as a Cover for Global Predation

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-12 § 链接
所谓的“文明价值”是最高级的武器化叙事，旨在为权力共谋提供合法性掩体。
The narrative of "civilizational values" is a weaponized tool used to provide legitimacy for power complicity.

这是一场教科书级别的认知入口争夺战。特朗普政府用“美国优先”作为直接层的烟雾弹，通过关闭 USAID 这种人道主义机构，在文化层制造一个“纳税人的钱被浪费在境外避孕套上”的 scam 叙事，从而在底层支持者中建立起一种虚假的“资源回归”幻觉。

但真正的资金流向揭露了 structural 层的真相：钱并没有回到美国学校，而是流向了欧洲的右翼 think tanks。这些机构（如 878）的成立时间之巧、目标之模糊，恰恰证明了它们不是研究机构，而是权力共谋的节点。通过资助这些所谓的“文明伙伴”，MAGA 正在将一种男性中心叙事（masculine-centric narrative）全球化——将“主权”、“传统遗产”和“文明自信”武器化，用来掩盖其本质上的政治干预和资源掠夺。

最讽刺的是这种 meta-violence 的运作方式：它利用底层民众对“文明被抹除”的恐惧，诱导他们为那些身价上亿的亿万富翁（如 Jacob Rees-Mogg）买单。这是一种典型的共谋机制：顶层的权力精英通过定义什么是“西方文明”来掌握解释权，而底层的支持者在 Fox News 的认知闭环中，被引导去攻击跨性别者和科学家，从而在潜意识中完成了对自身利益被剥夺的自我规训。

当“文明”成为一种可以定价的 grant，它就不再是文化，而是一种殖民工具。这场战争的目的不是拯救文明，而是确保全球范围内的解释权都掌握在同一群共谋者手中。

This is a textbook case of seizing the cognitive entry point. The Trump administration uses "America First" as a smoke screen at the direct level, shuttering USAID to manufacture a cultural scam: the lie that taxpayers' money is being wasted on condoms abroad. This creates a false illusion of "resource reclamation" among the base.

However, the actual flow of funds reveals the structural violence: the money isn't returning to American schools, but flowing into right-wing European think tanks. The suspicious timing and vague goals of groups like '878' prove they aren't research institutes, but nodes of complicity. By funding these "civilizational partners," MAGA is globalizing a masculine-centric narrative—weaponizing "sovereignty" and "heritage" to mask blatant political meddling.

The most cynical part of this meta-violence is its mechanism: it leverages the fear of "civilizational erasure" to trick the working class into subsidizing multimillionaires. This is the essence of complicity: the elite define what "Western Civilization" means to monopolize the right to interpret reality, while the base, trapped in the Fox News echo chamber, is conditioned to attack trans people and scientists, internalizing their own dispossession.

When "civilization" becomes a grant with a price tag, it ceases to be culture and becomes a colonial tool. This crusade isn't about saving civilization; it's about ensuring the power to define "truth" remains in the hands of the same circle of co-conspirators.
