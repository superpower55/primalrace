被抹除的血样与被定义的“不知情”Erased Blood Samples and the Definition of 'Unawareness'

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-02 § 链接
国家机器对真相的垄断，本质上是对受害者存在性的第二次谋杀。
The state's monopoly on truth is essentially a second murder of the victim's existence.

这出戏的剧本太烂了，烂到简直是模版化的。前国防大臣 Tobias Ellwood 突然跳出来承认自己“无意中”误导了议会，把锅甩给国防部 (MoD) 的官员。这种“我被下属骗了”的叙事，是政治权力在面对 structural violence 时最常用的逃生舱——通过将制度性的恶意转化为个体的“沟通失误”，来消解国家机器作为共谋者的主体责任。

让我们用加尔通的暴力三角来看：直接层是核辐射导致的癌症和死亡；结构层是 MoD 刻意隐藏医疗记录、拒绝赔偿的制度性封锁；而文化层，则是这套“无法定位信息”的官方话语。当国家定义“证据不存在”时，受害者的痛苦在法律和行政维度上就被定义为“不存在”。这就是典型的元暴力：解释权被垄断，事实被制造。血样在实验室里，但受害者在档案里被抹杀了。

最讽刺的是，这种共谋机制在英国政坛具有惊人的遗传性。从受污染血液丑闻到核试 veterans 丑闻，从前卫生大臣到现任总理，大家都在玩同一个游戏：先掩盖 $\rightarrow$ 被记者揭穿 $\rightarrow$ 承认被误导 $\rightarrow$ 呼吁正义。这不是在解决问题，而是在进行一种表演性的 let-down。真正的最优解表达应该是：承认国家在 1952-1967 年间将士兵视为可消耗的生物工具，并立即启动赔偿，而不是在 2026 年才在《卫报》上感叹这是一场“巨大的丑闻”。

这场战争的本质是 Potential 与 Actual 的巨大差额。这些士兵本可以拥有知情权和医疗救济，但他们被剥夺了一切，只剩下在死前试图证明自己曾经“存在”于那份被加密的 Merlin 数据库中。这种对生命数据的定价权，始终掌握在那些穿着西装、坐在伦敦办公室里的共谋者手中。

The script is painfully cliché. Former defence minister Tobias Ellwood suddenly admits he 'unwittingly' misled parliament, shifting the blame to MoD officials. This 'I was lied to by subordinates' narrative is the standard escape pod for political power facing structural violence—it dissolves the systemic malice of the state machine into a mere 'communication error' among individuals.

Applying the Violence Triangle: the direct layer is cancer and death from radiation; the structural layer is the institutional blockade of medical records and compensation; and the cultural layer is the official discourse of being 'unable to locate information.' When the state defines evidence as non-existent, the victims' suffering is defined as non-existent in legal and administrative dimensions. This is pure meta-violence: the monopoly of interpretation, the manufacturing of facts. The blood samples existed in the lab, but the veterans were erased from the archives.

It is farcical how this complicity is hereditary in British politics. From the infected blood scandal to the nuclear veterans, from former health secretaries to the current PM, they all play the same game: cover up $\rightarrow$ get exposed by journalists $\rightarrow$ admit being misled $\rightarrow$ call for justice. This isn't problem-solving; it's a performative let-down. A true optimal expression would be admitting the state treated soldiers as disposable biological tools between 1952 and 1967 and initiating immediate compensation, rather than sighing in The Guardian in 2026 that this is a 'great scandal.'

The essence of this war is the massive gap between Potential and Actual. These veterans should have had the right to know and receive medical relief, but they were stripped of everything, left only to prove they 'existed' within the encrypted Merlin database before they die. The pricing power over life data remains firmly in the hands of the co-conspirators in London offices.
