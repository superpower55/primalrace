片场和气，账算在别处Set Harmony, Books Elsewhere

文娱 文化层 · 元暴力 Entertainment Weekly ↗ 2026-11-01 § 链接
把受害者说成施暴者，是最好用的叙事武器。
Calling the victim the perpetrator is the oldest narrative weapon there is.

Feldshuh 说"tensions are NOT RISING"，说"profund differences of opinion"不等于关系紧张，说这些都不会带进片场。她把一场关于种族灭绝的争论，降级成两个同事口味不同。这就是元暴力的操作方式：把解释权从"谁在死"挪到"我们剧组和不和气"。

她对《纽约邮报》说，指控犹太民族搞种族灭绝"wild"，"right out of Hitler's playbook"，"你把受害者变成施暴者"。这句话本身才是那套 playbook：把国家的军事行动和整个民族绑定，再把对这个国家的批评定义成对这个民族的攻击。于是 Brody 引用联合国调查委员会的判断说"put me down as thinking it's a genocide"，就变成了他在迫害犹太人。谁在加沙被炸死，在这套叙事里一个字都不用提。

她接着说，"我的工作，作为 Bina 也作为 Tovah，是爱片场的人，是善良。"善良在这里的功能是封口。一个人可以一边说爱同事，一边把同事对一场正在进行的杀戮的表态叫作希特勒剧本，然后宣布这不影响工作。这不叫克制，这叫把冲突挪到对方无法回应的地方：你继续在片场演我儿子，我继续在采访里说你是迫害者。

报道里没有 Erin Foster 和 Brody 的回应，只有"EW 联系了他们的代表"。谁被问了、谁选择不说、谁抢在所有人之前先定义这件事的性质——这个顺序本身就是材料。先开口的人拿走解释权，沉默的人被写成"被指控的一方"。

这部剧叫《Nobody Wants This》，讲的是拉比和 non-Jewish 女孩的恋爱。现在它的宣传周期被一场真实的战争穿过，而处理方式是把战争翻译成"剧组气氛"。气氛可以澄清，死掉的人不能。Feldshuh 说希望拍到第四季。第四季会有的；加沙的账，不会因为一句"tensions are NOT RISING"就平掉。

Feldshuh says "tensions are NOT RISING." She says there are "profound differences of opinion" and that none of it will be brought to the set. A disagreement about genocide gets downgraded to two colleagues with different tastes. That is how meta-violence works: move the interpretive frame from who is dying to whether the cast gets along.

She told the New York Post that accusing the Jewish people of genocide is "wild," "right out of Hitler's playbook," that you "take the victim and you make the victim the perpetrator." That sentence is the playbook. Bind a state's military campaign to an entire people, then define criticism of the state as an attack on the people. So Brody citing a UN commission of inquiry and saying "put me down as thinking it's a genocide" becomes him persecuting Jews. Who is being killed in Gaza does not appear in the sentence at all.

Then: "My job, as Bina but also as Tovah, is to love the people on set at my place of work, to be kind." Kindness here functions as a gag order. You can say you love your colleague while calling his statement about an ongoing slaughter a Hitler script, and then declare it does not affect the work. That is not restraint. It is relocating the conflict somewhere the other side cannot answer: he keeps playing her son on camera, she keeps calling him a persecutor off it.

The report has no response from Erin Foster or Brody, only that EW reached out to their reps. Who was asked, who chose silence, who got to define the nature of the thing first — that order is the material. Whoever speaks first takes the interpretive ground; whoever stays silent gets written as the accused.

The show is called Nobody Wants This, a rabbi and a non-Jewish girl falling in love. Now its press cycle is pierced by an actual war, and the handling is to translate the war into "set atmosphere." Atmosphere can be clarified. The dead cannot. Feldshuh hopes for a fourth season. There will be one. Gaza's ledger will not balance because someone posted that tensions are NOT RISING.
