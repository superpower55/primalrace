钻石恒久远，是门老生意A Diamond Is Forever, and So Is the Pitch

文娱 文化层 The Hollywood Reporter ↗ 2026-11-03 § 链接
新闻摘要大卫·雅曼（David Yurman）于11月2日发布2026年假日广告《Share the Warmth》，由迈克尔·B·乔丹、因德·纳瓦雷特和《布里奇顿》演员耶琳·哈出镜，在纽约雪景中拍摄。品牌同步上线按价格、风格和赠送对象分类的节日礼品指南，并列出多款戒指、耳环、手链等产品及价格。
The newsDavid Yurman released its 2026 holiday campaign, Share the Warmth, on Nov. 2, starring Michael B. Jordan, Inde Navarrette and Bridgerton's Yerin Ha in snowy New York vignettes. The brand paired the campaign with holiday gift guides sorted by price, style and recipient, alongside rings, earrings and bracelets with listed prices.
把商品说成自我表达，是最老的定价术
Selling self-expression is the oldest pricing trick there is

大卫·雅曼的假日广告文案里写着，两位女演员「体现了品牌对个体性与自我表达坚定不移的承诺」。一颗标价三千四百五十美元的实金耳环，被说成自我表达。这不是新鲜事，新鲜的是它每次都能奏效。

第三章讲过，浪漫爱叙事是商业最持久的武器之一，钻石恒久远一颗永流传是它的样板工程：把世界上最常见的石头之一定价成爱情的必需品，再让收礼的人相信拒绝它就是拒绝爱。假日礼品指南按「给她的」「给他的」分类，这个分类本身就是定价机制——同一块金属，进了「给她的」那一栏，就多了一层「你值得被爱」的溢价。

真正值得看的是这套广告怎么使用人。迈克尔·B·乔丹的头衔是奥斯卡得主、品牌全球大使，两位女演员的资历被写成「2026年具有文化定义意义的表演」。三个人的脸被借来给金属和石头背书，品牌借的是他们身上已经积累的表达资本，还回去的是一张支票。这笔交易对明星本人未必亏，但「个体性」这个词在这里被彻底掏空了：它不指向任何一个人的选择，只指向货架上的四个款式、六个宝石变体。

这不是说买首饰的人都是被骗的。人可以用任何东西表达自己，包括一枚戒指。问题在于，当表达的唯一入口被商业垄断，当「我是谁」的答案被预先写进礼品指南的收件人分类里，剩下的选择就不是表达，是挑选。挑选是消费，表达是存在，两者被混为一谈的地方，就是定价权生效的地方。

广告叫《分享温暖》。温暖分享出去了，账单留给了买的人。而那个被定价的「个体性」，从来没有问过收礼的人想不想要。

David Yurman's campaign copy says the two actresses embody the brand's unwavering commitment to individuality and self-expression. A pair of earrings listed at $3,450 in solid gold is called self-expression. This is not new. What is new is that it keeps working.

Romantic love is one of the most durable weapons in the commercial arsenal, and diamonds are its showcase project: take one of the most common stones on earth, price it as a necessity of love, then let the recipient believe that refusing it means refusing love. The gift guides sort by for her, for him. That sorting is itself the pricing mechanism. The same metal, placed in the for-her column, acquires a surcharge called you deserve to be loved.

What deserves a closer look is how the campaign uses people. Michael B. Jordan is billed as an Oscar winner and global brand ambassador; the two actresses are described as having delivered culture-defining performances in 2026. Three faces are borrowed to endorse metal and stone. The brand borrows the expressive capital they have already accumulated and pays back with a check. The stars may not be losing out, but the word individuality has been hollowed out entirely: it points to no one's actual choice, only to four variations and six stone options on a shelf.

This is not to say that anyone buying jewelry is a dupe. People can express themselves through anything, a ring included. The problem is when the only entrance to expression is commercially monopolized, when the answer to who am I is pre-written into the recipient categories of a gift guide. What remains is not expression but selection. Selection is consumption; expression is existence. Where the two get conflated is exactly where the power to price takes effect.

The campaign is called Share the Warmth. The warmth gets shared. The bill goes to the buyer. And the individuality being priced never asked the recipient whether she wanted it.
