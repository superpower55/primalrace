AI 的“耳聋”与阶级表达的生物墙AI's Deafness and the Biological Wall of Class Expression

科技 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-20 § 链接
技术中立是最大的 scam，AI 的认知入口由权力定义。
Technological neutrality is a scam; AI's cognitive entry is defined by power.

一个不能听懂约克郡口音的 AI 接诊员，绝不是什么“技术迭代中的小 bug”，而是一次典型的 structural violence。当 QuantumLoopAI 宣称其系统被设计为能理解“广泛的口音”时，他们所谓的“广泛”其实是一个被权力定义的标准集——通常是伦敦腔或标准美英音。在这种叙事里，非标准口音被定义为“噪音”或“异常值”，而不再是表达。

口音是背景 (Background) 最直观的表达，它承载着地缘、阶级与身份。当医疗资源这个生存底线被交由一个只认同“精英表达”的 AI 掌控时，约克郡的底层民众在生物学意义上并没有改变，但他们在系统中的“票”贬值了。这就是典型的 Potential − Actual：本可以瞬间预约的医疗服务，因为口音被 AI 过滤，变成了必须亲自奔波的体力成本。差额部分，就是暴力。

最讽刺的是，这种 AI 被包装成“消除排队”的 good_news。但对于那些被系统拒之门外的老人和残障人士，这种“效率”实际上是在通过技术手段完成一次高效的筛选与排斥。这是一种数字时代的元暴力：定义什么是“正常”的沟通，然后让不符合定义的人在沉默中自我怀疑，甚至像新闻中那位患者一样，直接选择“放弃预约”。

当一个 AI 决定你是否能获得医疗服务时，它不再是一个工具，而是一个拥有解释权的共谋者。它在潜意识里告诉这些患者：你的表达方式是不合格的，因此你的需求是不优先的。这不仅是技术的失败，更是权力对边缘人群表达权的再一次殖民。

An AI receptionist that cannot understand a Yorkshire accent is not a 'minor bug' in technical iteration, but a textbook case of structural violence. When QuantumLoopAI claims their system is designed for a 'wide range of accents,' that 'range' is actually a standard set defined by power—typically Received Pronunciation or standard American English. In this narrative, non-standard accents are categorized as 'noise' or 'outliers' rather than valid expression.

Accents are the most direct expression of Background, carrying geography, class, and identity. When the baseline of survival—healthcare access—is handed over to an AI that only recognizes 'elite expressions,' the working class of Yorkshire hasn't changed biologically, but the value of their 'vote' in the system has plummeted. This is the essence of the Violence Triangle: Potential − Actual. A medical appointment that should be instant becomes a physical burden of traveling in person because of an accent filter. That gap is the violence.

The irony is that this AI is packaged as a good_news story about 'eliminating queues.' But for the elderly and disabled who are locked out, this 'efficiency' is actually a high-tech mechanism for screening and exclusion. It is a form of meta-violence in the digital age: defining what constitutes 'normal' communication and forcing those who don't fit the definition into silence or self-doubt, as seen in the patient who simply 'gave up' on booking.

When an AI decides whether you deserve healthcare, it ceases to be a tool and becomes a complicit agent with the power of interpretation. It subconsciously signals to these patients: your mode of expression is invalid, therefore your needs are non-priority. This is not just a technical failure; it is a re-colonization of the expression rights of marginalized people by power.
