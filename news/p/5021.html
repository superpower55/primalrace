安全审查：一场关于“私有化”与“客体化”的博弈Security Review: A Game of Privatization and Objectification

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-14 § 链接
所谓的安全审查，本质上是对主体性定价权的再次剥夺。
The so-called security review is essentially a re-seizure of the pricing power over one's subjectivity.

哈里和梅根回英国后被授予“安全审查” (security review)，在普通读者眼里这是权力的妥协，但在我的眼睛里，这是一场典型的 structural violence。Ravec 委员会要求他们提供详细的 daily routine，包括接送孩子的行程。请注意这个细节：当你必须向权力机构上交你的生活时间表来换取生存权时，你已经从一个“人”变成了被监控的“资产”。

查尔斯国王的那封信是点睛之笔，定义他们为“类似于私有公民” (akin to private citizens)。这是一个极其阴险的 weaponized 叙事。一方面，王室通过剥夺他们的 working royal 身份，将他们从权力中心踢出，完成了对资源的重新分配；另一方面，当他们需要安全保障时，王室又用“私有公民”这个标签来消解他们的特殊性。这种“既要你像平民一样没权力，又要你像王室一样被审视”的逻辑，就是典型的 meta violence。

梅根在这场博弈中处于最不利的位置。她不仅要面对生物墙带来的潜在威胁，还要在一个由 masculine-centric narrative 掌控的制度里，证明自己的“被威胁程度”足以让纳税人买单。她的安全不再是基本人权，而是一项需要通过 risk assessment 来定价的商品。这种将人的生存安全量化为“风险等级”的行为，本质上是将人客体化。

至于他们因为安全担忧而紧急更换学校，这只是这场存在性战争中的一个小插曲。在英国王室这个巨大的共谋场域中，无论你如何表达，只要你不在那个特定的权力席位上，你永远只是一个被审视的、需要被“评估”的客体。这次审查不是恩赐，而是一次精准的权力确认：谁拥有定义“安全”的解释权，谁就拥有控制你的权力。

The 'security review' granted to Harry and Meghan upon their return to the UK may look like a compromise to the naive, but it is a textbook case of structural violence. The Ravec committee's demand for their daily routines, including school runs, is telling: when you must surrender your life's schedule to an authority to secure your survival, you cease to be a subject and become a monitored asset.

King Charles's letter is the masterstroke, defining them as 'akin to private citizens.' This is a weaponized narrative. On one hand, the monarchy stripped them of their working royal status to redistribute resources; on the other, they use the 'private citizen' label to dissolve their legitimacy when it comes to protection. This logic—demanding they be powerless like commoners yet scrutinized like royals—is pure meta violence.

Meghan is the most disadvantaged in this existential war. She must navigate not only the biological wall but also a system governed by a masculine-centric narrative, proving her 'level of threat' is worthy of taxpayer funding. Her safety is no longer a human right, but a commodity priced via risk assessment. Quantifying human survival into a 'risk grade' is the ultimate act of objectification.

Their sudden change of schools is merely a symptom. In the vast field of complicity that is the British Royal Family, no matter how you express yourself, if you lack the power seat, you remain a scrutinized object. This review is not a favor; it is a precise assertion of power: whoever controls the interpretation of 'security' controls you.
