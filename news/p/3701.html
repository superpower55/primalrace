别把生物墙的‘说明书’当成解放宣言Don't Mistake the 'Manual' of the Biological Wall for a Manifesto of Liberation

性别 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-12 § 链接
掌握生物学词汇不等于夺回主体性，警惕被资本和叙事重新定义的‘觉醒’。
Mastering biological vocabulary is not the same as reclaiming subjectivity; beware of 'awakening' redefined by capital.

这篇报道在庆祝年轻女性变得‘懂’激素了，把‘luteal uglies’（黄体期丑陋感）这种社交媒体流行语当成某种 emancipatory（解放）的标志。但从 Violence Triangle 来看，这不过是 cultural layer 的一次微调。知道自己为什么感到‘丑’，并不意味着你摆脱了‘丑’的评价体系，反而可能是在通过精准的生物学标签，完成一次更深层的自我规训。

生物墙（Biological Wall）是客观存在的，但对生物墙的‘解释权’一直被男性中心叙事垄断。过去是让女性‘忽略’身体，现在是让女性‘追踪’身体。这种从‘无视’到‘数字化监控’的转变，本质上是认知入口的迁移。当女性开始在 App 里精确记录排卵期和黄体期时，她们并没有在打赢一场存在性战争，而是在为资本主义提供最精准的生物数据。正如文中所警示的，一旦‘luteal uglies’被定义为一个需要被‘fix’的问题，资本会迅速推出针对性的药丸或产品。这不是在消除暴力，而是在给 structural violence 换一套‘科学’的包装。

真正的最优解表达，不应该是‘我知道我因为激素而感到丑，所以我接受它’，而应该是‘谁定义了这种状态叫丑？为什么这种生理波动被赋予了负面价值？’。如果讨论的终点依然是‘为了更好地照顾自己而避免离婚’，或者‘为了在职场中更好地掩盖情绪’，那么这种所谓的‘觉醒’，其实是共谋者在既定秩序下的自我安抚。她们学习了生物学词汇，却依然在扮演一个‘被管理得更好’的客体。

知识确实是力量，但只有当知识被用来拆解元暴力，而不是用来给枷锁贴标签时，它才具有政治意义。

The article celebrates young women becoming 'literate' about hormones, treating social media buzzwords like 'luteal uglies' as a sign of emancipation. But through the lens of the Violence Triangle, this is merely a tweak at the cultural layer. Knowing why you feel 'ugly' doesn't mean you've escaped the evaluative system of 'ugliness'; rather, it may be a deeper form of self-discipline through precise biological labeling.

The Biological Wall is an objective fact, but the power to interpret it has long been monopolized by masculine-centric narratives. In the past, women were told to 'ignore' their bodies; now, they are encouraged to 'track' them. This shift from 'ignorance' to 'digital surveillance' is simply a migration of cognitive entry points. When women precisely log their ovulation and luteal phases in apps, they aren't winning an existential war—they are providing the most precise biological data for capitalism. As the author warns, once 'luteal uglies' is defined as a problem to be 'fixed,' capital will swiftly launch targeted pills or products. This isn't eliminating violence; it's repackaging structural violence in a 'scientific' wrapper.

A true optimal expression isn't 'I know I feel ugly because of hormones, so I accept it,' but rather 'Who defined this state as ugly? Why is this physiological fluctuation assigned a negative value?' If the discussion ends at 'avoiding divorce by managing my mood' or 'better masking emotions at work,' then this so-called 'awakening' is just the complicity of those seeking comfort within the existing order. They've learned the vocabulary, but they continue to play the role of a 'better-managed' object.

Knowledge is power, but only when that knowledge is used to dismantle meta-violence, not to put labels on the shackles, does it hold political significance.
