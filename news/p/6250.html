推土机碾平的证据The Bulldozed Evidence

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 Democracy Now! ↗ 2026-11-01 § 链接
新闻摘要以色列建筑师埃亚尔·魏兹曼在新书《Ungrounding: The Architecture of Genocide》中，基于法证建筑团队的研究，指出以色列在加沙使用推土机进行「ungrounding」，即抹除破坏痕迹的毁坏方式。自2025年10月停火以来，以色列沿黄线扩张控制，将加沙约54%的土地划入其控制区，并建造军事基地和土墙，压缩巴勒斯坦人生存空间。
The newsIsraeli architect Eyal Weizman's new book "Ungrounding: The Architecture of Genocide," based on Forensic Architecture research, says Israel uses bulldozers in Gaza for "ungrounding," a destruction that erases traces of destruction. Since the October 2025 ceasefire, Israel has expanded control along the Yellow Line, holding about 54% of Gaza and building bases and berms, shrinking space for Palestinians.
推土机翻的不是土，是证据。
The bulldozer isn't turning soil. It's turning evidence.

Weizman 说的那句话是全篇最重的一句：以色列正在违反 ICJ 的命令，销毁它自己实施种族灭绝的证据。推土机把整片地形翻过来，把被炸毁的楼、楼底下的尸体、尸体所在的街区一起铲进六米高的土墙里。这已经不是附带损伤，这是把犯罪现场重新施工成另一种东西。

Ungrounding 这个词的精确之处在于，它描述的不是「毁掉」，而是「毁掉毁掉的痕迹」。空袭之后你还能看见废墟，还能指认这里曾经有一栋楼。推土机把废墟也铲平、翻搅、堆成无法辨认的土丘，连「这里死过人」这个事实都失去了物质载体。Weizman 说加沙约有一万名失踪者，很多在自家楼下的瓦砾里，而以色列在把这些瓦砾挪去修路、修军事基地、堆土墙。挪动瓦砾就是挪动遗骸。

这是暴力三角里最赤裸的一层联动：直接暴力杀人，结构暴力把幸存者赶进帐篷和废墟，然后文化暴力——不，比文化暴力更早——元层的那一步，是让这一切不再可被指认为暴力。法庭需要证据，证据需要现场，现场被推土机推成路基。谁掌握「什么是事实」的制造权，在这里表现为谁掌握铲平事实的铲斗。

而铲斗是 Caterpillar 造的。Weizman 翻出一张 1950 年的档案照片：把加沙地带从巴勒斯坦其余部分切出去的那道沟，是一台二战时期的 Caterpillar 拖拉机拉着单铧犁犁出来的。他用了「就像罗马的建城传说，边界是用犁划出来的」这个类比，不是修辞。加沙地带本身就是一台美国机器犁出来的。七十五年后，同一家公司的机器在把这块地方犁回去。停火之后，私营公司能进场了，保险费降了，地形改造加速了，加沙成了一个巨大的工地。

停火这个词在这里是 scam。黄线被说成撤军第一阶段的标记，Forensic Architecture 测绘了一百多个黄色水泥块，多数放在约定的撤离线之外，还在往里推。每隔 1.8 公里一座军事基地，记录了 34 座，很多建在巴勒斯坦人的房子废墟上。再加一条橙线，有的地方比黄线又深入一公里多。四十公里长、六米高的土墙，材料是成千上万个巴勒斯坦人的家。剩下的百分之三十几，是给这场种族灭绝的幸存者留的地方。

Weizman 有两个判断值得单独拎出来。一个是「以重建为手段的种族灭绝」——特朗普说的「中东的里维埃拉」，库什纳说的「很有价值的海滨地产」，是同一套逻辑的两种口音：先把人从工地上清走，理由是太危险、要施工、要围挡，然后再也不让他们回来。另一个是隧道。他说那可能是最伟大的解放建筑之一：地表 85% 的建筑被毁，隧道的大部分还完好。巴勒斯坦人失去了土地，但完整控制着地下。而以色列把隧道叫作「地铁」，制造一个「地下处处连通」的整体印象，用它给「把整片地表全部毁掉」背书。这个谎言的功能不是描述隧道，是授权铲平一切。

Hind Rajab 那一段是全篇唯一一个被完整重建的个案：五岁的女孩，车里的家人，两名去救她的红新月会医护，一辆 Kia，一辆 Merkava 坦克，13 到 23 米的射击距离，车尾右侧约三十发子弹的一轮扫射。Forensic Architecture 把坦克放进那个环形范围里试了数百个位置，得出最可能的射击点，并指出坦克的光学设备在几公里外就能看清车里是谁。个案的意义不在于个案：Weizman 说，他们被命令离开加沙城的时候，医院正在被炸，撤离路线上有人被狙击。单起枪击和整场种族灭绝之间，是「加起来等于什么」的问题，而以色列的诉讼策略恰恰是把它拆成一桩一桩「针对哈马斯战士的军事行动」。

这就是为什么 250 多名巴勒斯坦记者被杀，Albanese 管它叫 mediacide。杀记者不是战争的杂音，是战争的一部分：让现场无法被记录，让记录无法被传播，让传播无法被相信。推土机毁掉物证，狙击手和空袭毁掉人证。两条线合起来，就是一套完整的证据销毁工程。

Weizman 最后说，加沙的土壤被释放的毒素污染，那片肥沃的冲积土几代人都无法耕种。他还说了一句容易被略过的话：巴勒斯坦人是难民，他们的家在现在的以色列境内，联合国承认他们的回归权，所以在加沙的任何重建都不是对这场罪行的完整清算。

这句话是这篇访谈里唯一没有被推土机碰过的东西：一个还没被缩小的差额。房子可以在原地再盖，土可以换，但回归权不在推土机的作业范围里，也不在任何一份停火协议的条款里。

The heaviest line in the whole interview is Weizman's: Israel is actively destroying the evidence of the genocide it committed, against an ICJ order. The bulldozer flips the terrain over, and the bombed buildings, the bodies underneath them, and the neighborhoods those bodies were in all go into a six-meter berm. This is not collateral damage. This is a crime scene being redeveloped into something else.

What "ungrounding" gets exactly right is that it doesn't describe destruction. It describes the destruction of the traces of destruction. After an air campaign you can still see ruins, still point and say a building stood here. The bulldozer flattens the ruins too, churns them, piles them into mounds no one can identify. Even the fact that people died here loses its physical carrier. Weizman says about 10,000 Palestinians are missing, many under the rubble of their own homes, and Israel is moving that rubble into roads, military bases, earth berms. Moving rubble is moving human remains.

This is the Violence Triangle at its most naked: direct violence kills, structural violence drives the survivors into tents and ruins, and then comes the step before cultural violence — the meta layer — making the whole thing no longer identifiable as violence. A court needs evidence, evidence needs a site, and the site gets pushed into a roadbed. Who controls the manufacture of "what is fact" shows up here as who controls the bucket that levels the facts.

And the bucket is made by Caterpillar. Weizman dug up a 1950 archival photograph: the ditch that cut the Gaza Strip off from the rest of Palestine was carved by a single-blade plough pulled by a Second World War-era Caterpillar. He compares it to the legend of Rome, whose border was carved by a plough. That's not a metaphor. The Gaza Strip itself was ploughed out by an American machine. Seventy-five years later, the same company's machines are ploughing it back in. Since the so-called ceasefire, private companies can enter, insurance costs dropped, terraforming accelerated, and Gaza has turned into an enormous construction site.

"Ceasefire" is a scam here. The Yellow Line was presented as phase one of withdrawal. Forensic Architecture mapped more than 100 yellow concrete blocks, most placed well beyond the agreed line, still moving inward. A fortified base every 1.8 kilometers, 34 documented, many built on the ruins of Palestinian homes. Then an Orange Line, more than a kilometer deeper in places. A 40-kilometer earth berm, six meters high, made of thousands and thousands of Palestinian homes. What's left for the survivors is some 30-something percent.

Two of Weizman's judgments deserve to be pulled out. One: genocide by reconstruction. Trump's "riviera of the Middle East" and Kushner's "very valuable waterfront property" are the same logic in two accents — clear the people off the construction site because it's too dangerous, because we need room to rebuild, because the site must be fenced, and then never let them return. Two: the tunnels. He calls them possibly one of the greatest pieces of architecture of liberation. About 85% of the buildings standing before October 7, 2023 are destroyed; most of the tunnel project is intact. Palestinians lost the land but controlled the subsoil entirely. Israel calls the tunnels "the metro," manufacturing an image of total underground connectivity — and that lie's function isn't to describe tunnels. It's to license flattening everything above them.

The Hind Rajab segment is the only incident reconstructed in full: a five-year-old girl, her family in the car, two Palestine Red Crescent paramedics who came to rescue her, a Kia, a Merkava tank, a firing distance of 13 to 23 meters, a volley of about 30 bullets into the rear right-hand side. Forensic Architecture placed the tank in hundreds of positions within that ring and found the most likely firing point, noting that tanks carry optics that see for kilometers. The point of the single case isn't the single case: when the family was ordered out of Gaza City, the hospitals were being destroyed and people on the evacuation routes were being sniped at. Between one shooting and a genocide lies the question of what it all adds up to — and Israel's litigation strategy is precisely to disaggregate it into one military maneuver at a time.

This is why more than 250 Palestinian journalists and media workers have been killed, and why Albanese calls it mediacide. Killing journalists isn't noise in a war. It's part of the war: make the site unrecordable, the record untransmittable, the transmission unbelievable. The bulldozer destroys the physical evidence; the sniper and the airstrike destroy the witnesses. Together they form a complete evidence-destruction program.

Weizman ends on the soil: the toxins released into Gaza's fertile alluvial earth will make it uncultivatable for generations. And he says one thing that's easy to skip past: Palestinians are refugees, their homes are in what is now Israel, the UN recognizes their right of return, and whatever reconstruction happens in Gaza is not the full undoing of the crime.

That sentence is the one thing in this interview the bulldozer hasn't touched: a gap that hasn't been narrowed. Houses can be rebuilt on the same spot. Soil can be replaced. But the right of return isn't in the bulldozer's work area, and it isn't in any clause of any ceasefire.
