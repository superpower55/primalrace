谁在定义这场“追逐”的代价Who Defines the Cost of the 'Pursuit'

性别 直接层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-25 § 链接
直接暴力是结果，而将个体降格为消耗品的叙事才是元暴力。
Direct violence is the result; the narrative that degrades individuals into consumables is the meta-violence.

这是一场典型的、由男性主导的暴力闭环。从警方的 pursuit 到失控的碰撞，整个过程是纯粹的 masculine-centric narrative：一方是代表国家暴力的执法机器，另一方是拒绝服从的驾驶者。在这个博弈场中，双方都陷入了某种“男性化”的权力冲动——追逐、对抗、速度与强制。而结果是，一名男性死亡，一名女性在毫无关联的情况下被卷入，陷入 critical condition。

请注意这篇报道中极具欺骗性的“中立”叙事。警方的 mandatory referral 和 IOPC 的调查被呈现为一种制度性的公正。但这种 structural violence 的伪装在于：它将一次导致平民重伤的追逐定义为“警察行为”的结果，而非一种由于权力傲慢导致的必然悲剧。在这种叙事里，女性 driver 只是一个被动地接收暴力的 object，她的身体成为了这场男性权力博弈的 collateral damage（附带损害）。

在这种元暴力的逻辑下，男性身体被降格为可消耗的工具——无论是死在车里的司机，还是在追逐中执行命令的警员。而女性则被预设为需要被“提供 specialist support”的弱势客体。这种 support 是一种表演性的温柔，它掩盖了最核心的事实：一个女性的生命安全，在男性权力者们关于“必须拦截”的执念面前，价值几乎为零。

This is a textbook closed-loop of masculine violence. From the police pursuit to the uncontrolled collision, the entire event is a pure masculine-centric narrative: on one side, the state's violence machine; on the other, a driver refusing to submit. Both parties were locked in a 'masculinized' power impulse—pursuit, confrontation, speed, and coercion. The result: one man dead, and a woman, entirely unrelated, swept into the wreckage in critical condition.

Note the deceptive 'neutrality' of this report. The mandatory referral and the IOPC investigation are framed as institutional justice. But the scam of this structural violence lies in defining a pursuit that critically injured a civilian as a result of 'police conduct,' rather than an inevitable tragedy born of power arrogance. In this narrative, the female driver is merely a passive object receiving violence, her body serving as the collateral damage of a masculine power game.

Under the logic of meta-violence, male bodies are degraded into consumables—whether the driver who died in the car or the officers executing orders. Meanwhile, the woman is preset as a vulnerable object in need of 'specialist support.' This support is a performative kindness that masks the core fact: the safety of a woman's life is valued at nearly zero when weighed against the obsession of masculine power-holders over the need to 'intercept.'
