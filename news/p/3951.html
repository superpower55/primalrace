旋转门里的CEO与百货公司的死亡螺旋The CEO Revolving Door and the Department Store Death Spiral

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-15 § 链接
管理层的频繁更迭不是危机，而是结构性死亡的掩体。
Frequent leadership turnover is not a crisis, but a shield for structural death.

John Lewis 的 CEO Peter Ruis 突然离职，官方辞令是“追求新项目”，但这种叙事在零售业的死亡螺旋面前显得极其滑稽。从 2016 年开始，这个职位的旋转门就没停过。当一个组织在短时间内更换了五个不同风格的掌舵人——从传统零售到时尚老手，再到超市高管——这根本不是在寻找“最优解”，而是在进行一场绝望的随机抽样。

百货公司（Department Store）的本质是一场关于“空间”与“认知入口”的博弈。在物理时代，它们垄断了品牌聚合的解释权；但在算法时代，这种解释权被电商瞬间夺走。现在的百货公司试图通过增加咖啡馆、健身房或按摩室来制造“体验”，这在本质上是一种 cultural violence 的伪装：试图用生活方式的碎片来掩盖商业逻辑的崩塌。这种“制造可能性”的艺术在巨大的租金和税收压力面前，不过是给垂死者涂抹腮红。

最值得关注的是 Ruis 与 Tarry 之间所谓的“管理风格差异”。在 corporate 的语境里，这通常是共谋者在分手时给彼此留的体面。真实的博弈在于：是继续投入巨资进行物理空间的翻新（Ruis 的逻辑），还是在成本控制中等待被 Mike Ashley 这种地产秃鹫低价吞噬（Tarry 可能的潜意识）。

当一个行业进入“死亡螺旋”，所有的管理层变动都成了 structural violence 的一部分。他们通过频繁更换 CEO 来向股东和员工表演“我们在尝试变革”，但实际上，他们只是在通过更换演员来延迟剧本的终结。

The abrupt exit of John Lewis CEO Peter Ruis, framed by the official narrative as 'pursuing new projects,' is farcical given the industry's death spiral. Since 2016, the CEO role has become a revolving door. When an organization cycles through five different leadership styles in a few years—from traditional retail to fashion veterans and supermarket execs—it is not searching for an optimal expression; it is performing a desperate random sampling.

Department stores were once the monopoly of the 'cognitive entrance' for brand aggregation. In the algorithmic era, this interpretative power was seized instantly by e-commerce. Today's attempt to pivot toward 'experiences'—adding cafes, gyms, or massage rooms—is essentially a mask of cultural violence: using fragments of lifestyle to disguise the collapse of business logic. This 'art of manufacturing possibilities' is merely applying rouge to a corpse under the crushing weight of business rates and rents.

More telling is the cited 'difference in management style' between Ruis and Tarry. In corporate speak, this is the standard complicity used to maintain face during a breakup. The real existential war is between continuing massive investment in physical renovation (Ruis's logic) or tightening the belt while waiting to be scavenged by property vultures like Mike Ashley (Tarry's potential trajectory).

Once an industry enters a death spiral, all management churn becomes a form of structural violence. They perform 'innovation' for stakeholders by swapping CEOs, but in reality, they are just changing the actors to delay the inevitable end of the script.
