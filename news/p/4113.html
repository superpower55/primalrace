封建制的现代拟态：Stonegate 的债权猎杀Modern Mimicry of Feudalism: Stonegate's Debt Hunting

哲学 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-18 § 链接
所谓的‘利益一致’，是资本在结构性暴力顶端对底层生存权的定价。
The so-called 'aligned interests' are merely the pricing of survival by capital at the apex of structural violence.

Stonegate 把自己的租户关系称为‘利益一致’ (directly aligned)，这简直是今年最大的 scam。在 tied tenancies 这种复杂的租赁协议下，所谓的‘互利’只是一个认知入口，用来诱导租户进入一个被精心设计的财务陷阱。租户承担所有经营风险，而公司通过垄断啤酒供应和操纵维修账单，确保自己永远是博弈的赢家。

这根本不是商业合作，而是一场现代版的封建制。在封建关系中，领主通过控制土地（生产资料）来榨取农奴的剩余价值。Stonegate 扮演的就是那个隐形的领主，它不仅控制着房产，还通过 NDA (non-disclosure agreements) 这种武器化的法律工具，强行封锁受害者的表达空间，让结构性暴力在沉默中完成闭环。

最令人作呕的是它对阿尔兹海默症患者 Phil 的处理。在元暴力的逻辑里，一个失去认知能力的人不再是‘人’，而是一个可以被最大化榨取的‘资产包’。Stonegate 在明知 Phil 失去决策能力的情况下，故意拖延退出程序，让债务在失能期间滚雪球，最后以‘损坏赔偿’之名抢走一个家庭最后的体面。这不仅是 structural violence，更是对人类基本尊严的 direct violence。

公司在开曼群岛注册，背后是私募股权 TDR Capital。这种跨境结构就是为了在享受利润的同时，通过法律防火墙逃避监管。当一个公司一边在财报里宣布‘史上最强利润’，一边派遣执达员去骚扰癌症患者的家属时，它在向世界宣布：在它的价值体系里，人的生命额度低于它的债务回收率。

Stonegate claims their relationship with tenants is 'directly aligned'—a complete scam. Under the tied tenancies model, 'mutual benefit' is nothing more than a weaponized narrative used to lure tenants into a meticulously designed financial trap. Tenants shoulder all operational risks, while the company ensures its win by monopolizing beer supply and manipulating repair bills.

This isn't business; it's a modern mimicry of feudalism. In feudal relations, the lord extracts surplus value by controlling the land. Stonegate acts as the invisible lord, controlling not only the premises but also using NDAs to weaponize silence, ensuring that structural violence completes its loop without public interference.

Most repulsive is the treatment of Phil, an Alzheimer's patient. In the logic of meta-violence, a person who loses cognitive capacity is no longer a human, but an 'asset package' to be maximized. Stonegate deliberately dragged out the exit process while knowing Phil was incapacitated, allowing debts to snowball, and finally robbing a family of their dignity under the guise of 'dilapidations'. This is not just structural violence; it is direct violence against human dignity.

Domiciled in the Cayman Islands and owned by TDR Capital, the company uses cross-border structures to evade regulation while harvesting profits. When a company reports 'strongest first-half profits ever' while sending bailiffs to harass the family of a cancer patient, it declares its core value: the value of a human life is lower than its debt recovery rate.
