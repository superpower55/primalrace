集体掩埋：被抹除的个体与结构性弃民Mass Burials: The Erasure of Individuals and Structural Abandonment

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 The New York Times ↗ 2026-08-31 § 链接
当死者被简化为编号，暴力就从自然灾害升级为结构性抹除。
When the dead are reduced to numbers, violence evolves from natural disaster to structural erasure.

一场洪水，两千五百个失踪者，以及在森林里排队等待被掩埋的无名尸体。很多人会把这看作是一场自然灾害的悲剧，但如果套用加尔通的暴力三角，你会发现这不仅是 direct violence（溺亡），而是一场典型的 structural violence（结构性暴力）。

从洪水爆发到尸体在下游城市 Bharatpur 堆积，政府在整个过程中表现出的不是“无能”，而是一种极其精准的“弃民逻辑”。没有尸体保存能力、没有有效的身份识别机制、只能通过电视幻灯片让家属在沉默中寻找碎片——这套流程的本质是：系统在告知受害者及其家属，你们的个体性在面对行政成本时毫无价值。

最令人作呕的细节是那些“插在水泥盆里的金属编号牌”。当一个活生生的人被简化为一个数字，并被集体掩埋在森林里时，这种表达方式完成了从“死亡”到“抹除”的跃迁。这是一种文化层面的暴力（cultural violence）：它通过一种名为“紧急处置”的叙事，将对死者尊严的剥夺正当化。在这种叙事里，效率高于认同，行政便利高于个体存在。

这种 mass burials 的逻辑在很多所谓的“文明”国家中通用——只要受害者足够底层，只要他们处于地理或政治的边缘，他们的死亡就可以被处理成一种“统计学上的必要”。在这种共谋下，安全部队的防护服、记录过程的摄像机，不过是给这场结构性抹除穿上的 PR 外衣。

A flood, 2,500 missing, and nameless corpses queuing for burial in a forest. Many view this as a natural tragedy, but through Galtung's Violence Triangle, it's clear this isn't just direct violence—it's a textbook case of structural violence.

From the initial deluge to the accumulation of bodies in Bharatpur, the government's response isn't mere 'incompetence'; it's a precise logic of abandonment. No preservation capacity, no effective identification—only a slideshow of remains for families to stare at in silence. This process tells the victims and their families: your individuality is worthless when weighed against administrative costs.

The most sickening detail is the 'metal numbered signs in concrete pots.' When a human being is simplified into a digit and buried in a mass grave, the expression shifts from 'death' to 'erasure.' This is cultural violence: using the narrative of 'emergency disposal' to legitimize the stripping of human dignity. In this narrative, efficiency overrides identity, and administrative convenience overrides existence.

This logic of mass burials is a global standard for the marginalized—as long as the victims are low-class or geographically peripheral, their deaths can be processed as a 'statistical necessity.' Under this complicity, the protective gear of security forces and the documenting cameras are nothing more than a PR cloak for structural erasure.
