Apple 的新家长控制：一套数字化的规训礼包Apple's New Parental Controls: A Digital Discipline Bundle

科技 结构层 · 文化层 Wirecutter: Reviews for the Real World ↗ 2026-09-20 § 链接
所谓的“保护”是控制的入口，而技术让这种控制变得无缝且隐形。
所謂 'protection' is the entry point for control; technology simply makes it seamless and invisible.

Apple 在 iOS 27 中升级的 parental controls 根本不是什么“功能优化”，而是一套极其高效的数字化规训工具。从决定孩子能安装什么 App，到实时审批每一个访问的网址（Ask to Browse），再到管控社交联系人的准入，这已经不是在管理设备，而是在构建一个数字化的全景监狱。当一个孩子的认知入口被父母通过一个 tap 完全掌控时，他失去的不仅是好奇心，更是主体性的萌芽。

最讽刺的是，Apple 引入了基于“专家建议”的 Screen Time 限制。这种将权力外包给所谓“专家”的行为，实际上是 structural violence 的一种高级伪装：它用一种“科学的”、“客观的”标准，掩盖了权力不对等的事实。在这种叙事下，控制不再是父母的私欲，而变成了“为了孩子好”的文明实践。这种 cultural violence 让被控制者在潜意识中内化这种压迫，认为被剥夺探索权是成长的必然。

这种机制在家庭内部的投射极其危险。正如我一直强调的，The Personal is Political。家庭是第一个博弈场，而 Apple 为权力上位者提供了最完美的武器。当一个孩子在数字世界中必须通过“申请”才能获得信息时，他学习到的第一课就是：我的存在是附属于他人的许可之上的。这种从幼年开始的共谋与顺从，为未来进入更宏大的元暴力结构做好了最完美的铺垫。

Apple's updated parental controls in iOS 27 are not 'feature optimizations'—they are a highly efficient toolkit for digital discipline. From deciding which apps are installed to real-time approval of every website via 'Ask to Browse' and vetting every single contact, this is no longer device management. It is the construction of a digital Panopticon. When a child's cognitive entry points are entirely controlled by a parent's tap, they lose more than curiosity; they lose the very seeds of subjectivity.

Most ironic is the introduction of Screen Time limits based on 'expert recommendations.' Outsourcing authority to so-called experts is a sophisticated disguise for structural violence: it uses a 'scientific' and 'objective' veneer to mask the raw reality of power imbalance. In this narrative, control is no longer a parental whim but a 'civilized practice' for the child's own good. This cultural violence encourages the controlled to internalize the oppression, accepting the deprivation of exploration as a necessity of growth.

The projection of this mechanism within the family is perilous. As I have always maintained, The Personal is Political. The home is the primary battlefield of existential war, and Apple has provided the dominant party with the perfect weapon. When a child must 'request' permission to access information, the first lesson they learn is that their existence is contingent upon the permission of another. This early training in complicity and submission prepares them perfectly for the broader meta-violence of the adult world.
