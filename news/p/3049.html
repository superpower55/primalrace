用“开放”掩盖垄断的认知入口骗局The Illusion of Openness: A Scam for Cognitive Monopoly

中国科技 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-31 § 链接
当权力在重新定义现实时，所谓的“合作”只是为了获取更高效的解释权。
When power redefines reality, "cooperation" is merely a tool to seize the monopoly of interpretation.

这篇文章是典型的武器化叙事（Weaponized Narrative）。中国驻英大使在《卫报》上抛出一个极其诱人的 la lune：开放、合作、互利。但请记住，在元暴力（Meta Violence）的逻辑里，任何由权力中心定义的“开放”，本质上都是在争夺认知入口（Cognitive Entry）。

文中大谈特谈 open-source 和 open-weight 模型（如 Qwen, DeepSeek），试图塑造一个“降低创新门槛”的救世主形象。这其实是一个巨大的 scam。真正的开放是能力的平权，而这里的“开放”是策略性的投放。当一个国家通过大规模工业部署（Industrial Deployment）掌握了数据定义权和评价标准后，所谓的“开源”不过是将对方引入自己设定的坐标系中。这就像华为在做不了小芯片时宣布新定律一样：如果现实不配合，就重新定义什么是“成功”的 AI。

最令人作呕的是将 AI 合作延伸到 healthcare 和生命科学。在结构性暴力（Structural Violence）中，医疗资源的分配从来不是公正的。将英国的生物医学研究与中国的“规模化部署”结合，在叙事中是“加速药物研发”，在现实中则是对生物数据的极致掠夺与客体化。谁拥有规模，谁就拥有对“健康”和“生命”的定价权。

这篇评论在表演一种“文明”与“理性”的姿态，但其内核是典型的男性中心叙事：强有力的主体（国家/权力）通过施舍般的“机会”和“培训”，诱导潜在的共谋者（英国研究机构/发展中国家）进入一个由它主导的博弈场。这种合作不是为了缩小 Potential − Actual 的差额，而是为了在更高维度上完成对解释权的垄断。

This piece is a textbook example of Weaponized Narrative. The Chinese Ambassador to the UK presents a seductive lure: openness, cooperation, and mutual benefit. But remember, within the logic of Meta Violence, any "openness" defined by the center of power is essentially a struggle for Cognitive Entry.

The text extols open-source and open-weight models (like Qwen, DeepSeek), attempting to craft an image of a savior "lowering the barriers to innovation." This is a massive scam. True openness is the equalization of capability; this "openness" is a strategic deployment. Once a state masters the power to define data and evaluation standards through large-scale Industrial Deployment, "open-source" becomes a way to lure others into a coordinate system of its own making. It is the same mechanism as announcing a new law when the physical reality—like chip manufacturing—doesn't comply: if reality doesn't fit, simply redefine reality.

Most repulsive is the extension of AI cooperation into healthcare and life sciences. In the realm of Structural Violence, the distribution of medical resources is never just. Combining British biomedical research with China's "capacity to deploy at scale" is framed as "accelerating drug discovery," but in reality, it is the ultimate predation and objectification of biological data. Whoever owns the scale owns the pricing power over "health" and "life."

This op-ed performs a posture of "civilization" and "rationality," but its core is a classic masculine-centric narrative: a powerful subject (the State/Power) uses the guise of "opportunities" and "training" to induce potential Co-conspirators (UK research institutes/developing nations) into a game board it controls. This cooperation does not aim to reduce the gap in the Violence Triangle (Potential − Actual); it aims to complete the monopoly of interpretation at a higher dimension.
