谁在指认主谋？一场关于共谋者的权力甩锅秀Who is the Mastermind? A Power-Shifting Show of Complicity

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-26 § 链接
当权力的共谋者开始互咬，这不叫正义，叫生存博弈。
When powerful co-conspirators start biting each other, it's not justice—it's an existential game of survival.

这场审判最荒诞的地方不在于凶手是谁，而在于这种典型的 masculine-centric narrative：几个掌握资源和解释权的男人，在法庭上通过指认对方是“真主谋”来完成自己的生存博弈。Yorgen Fenech 放弃沉默权，把 Keith Schembri 顶上去，这绝不是良心发现，而是在意识到原有的共谋结构崩塌后，试图通过出卖同类来寻找新的 optimal expression。

看这套叙事：烧烤聚会、秘密文件、WhatsApp 泄密、以及那个被描述成“被操纵”的中间人。这简直是权力的标准剧本。在马耳他的权力结构里， businessman, police leader, 和 prime minister's adviser 构成了一个封闭的共谋场域。他们共享信息，操纵法律，把一个敢于书写事实的女性记者 Daphne Caruana Galizia 像处理垃圾一样用炸弹清除。这种 direct violence 的背后，是整个国家机器在 structural layer 上的深度共谋——警察在掩盖，官员在授意，而执行者在分赃。

最讽刺的是，Fenech 试图用“毒瘾”和“家庭破碎”来包装自己的受害者形象。这又是典型的武器化表达：试图通过展示自身的脆弱来换取某种情感上的豁免。但别忘了，在这种权力博弈中，脆弱是伪装，而权力才是底色。 prosecution 问得最精准：谁才是真正拥有 power 的人？

Daphne 的死亡是这个系统为了维护其 meta-violence 而支付的代价。现在这些共谋者在法庭上表演互咬，试图定义谁是“真正的恶魔”，但这并不改变一个事实：只要这个由男性权力网络定义的“解释权”不被彻底瓦解，无论谁被定罪，这个制造暴力的结构依然在运转。他们争论的是谁在开车，而忽略了这辆车本身就是为了碾碎异见者而造的。

The most absurd part of this trial isn't the identity of the killer, but the classic masculine-centric narrative: a group of men with resources and interpretive power fighting in court, pointing fingers at each other as the 'real mastermind' to win their own survival game. Yorgen Fenech waiving his right to silence to pin everything on Keith Schembri isn't a sudden burst of conscience; it's a desperate search for a new optimal expression after the old structure of complicity collapsed.

Look at the narrative: barbecue parties, leaked files, WhatsApp messages, and a middleman described as 'manipulated.' It's a textbook script of power. In Malta's power structure, businessmen, police leaders, and the prime minister's adviser formed a closed circle of complicity. They shared intel, manipulated laws, and used a car bomb to erase Daphne Caruana Galizia, a woman who dared to write the facts. Behind this direct violence lies a deep structural violence where the state machinery—police covering up, officials authorizing—acted as one.

It is peak irony that Fenech attempts to wrap himself in the image of a victim, citing 'drug addiction' and 'family breakdown.' This is a weaponized expression: attempting to trade vulnerability for emotional leniency. But in this game, vulnerability is a mask; power is the only reality. The prosecution hit the mark: who was the one actually holding the power?

Daphne's murder was the price paid by the system to maintain its meta-violence. Now, these co-conspirators perform a theatrical betrayal in court, arguing over who the 'real demon' is. But this doesn't change the fact that as long as the interpretive power remains within this masculine power network, the structure that produces such violence remains intact. They are arguing over who was driving, while ignoring that the car itself was built specifically to crush dissent.
