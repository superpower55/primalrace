换把尺子，裁掉量不出的A New Ruler, Not a New Skill

科技 结构层 · 文化层 404 Media ↗ 2026-11-02 § 链接
新闻摘要隐私公司 Proton 的 CEO Andy Yen 上月宣布，将对几乎所有现有员工进行「重新面试」，考察他们使用 AI 是否足够，不用就可能被解雇。404 Media 披露他在内部安全频道称要求先测试新代码的同事是「loser thinking」后，Yen 于周五取消了这批面试，用户仍在社交平台上要求他辞职。
The newsProton CEO Andy Yen had planned near company-wide 're-interviews' to check whether employees use AI enough, with jobs at risk if they didn't. After 404 Media reported he called a colleague's call to test new code 'loser thinking,' Yen cancelled the re-interviews on Friday; users are still calling for him to resign.
考核从来不是量能力，是量服从。
The audit was never measuring skill. It was measuring obedience.

Proton 的 CEO 取消的不是一场面试，是一次服从度体检。

让几乎全体员工重新坐进面试间，交代自己怎么用 AI、用得够不够，用不够就可能被解雇——这不是在评估能力，是在评估谁愿意把「快」当成信仰背下来。真正的能力没法用「你用了几次 AI」来量，能这么量的只有一件事：你有没有照我说的做。

这套机制我在别处见过。做不出小芯片，就宣布一条新定律；量不出真本事，就换一把尺子。尺子一换，能力没变，评价变了，人就被重新排了座次。Proton 这一把尺子上刻的是 AI 使用率，刻度是 CEO 亲手划的。

最露骨的一句在安全频道里。有员工请求至少花几天给新代码做渗透测试再上线，Yen 回的是「That's bullshit and loser thinking」，还补了一句，别再「lovingly handcrafting」代码，多用 AI。

一家卖隐私的公司，把「上线前先测一测」叫作 loser thinking。这句话本身就是一份自白：安全不是产品，是营销素材；工程师的专业判断不是资产，是拖慢速度的负债。

所以取消面试根本不算让步。面试取消了，尺子还在，说那句话的人还在，宣布「快和安全可以兼得」的人还在。压力从 HR 的会议室转移到了 Slack 频道，从流程变成了文化——这比一场面试更难拒绝，因为它不再需要通知你。

用户看得很清楚。他们在社交媒体上骂领导层把安全降了优先级，直接要求 Yen 辞职。用户买的是「你的数据不会被拿去卖」，现在发现公司内部连「代码上线前先测几天」都算落后思想，这笔账他们算得比谁都明白。

但用户能要求的只有辞职，要求不了那把尺子消失。换一个 CEO，董事会照样会问：我们的 AI 采用率是多少？

真正的问题从来不是 Yen 一个人。是那种把「快」包装成进步、把「谨慎」贬成 loser 的叙事，它在这家公司里找到了一个愿意替它开口的嘴。嘴可以换，叙事不用。

What Proton's CEO cancelled was not a round of interviews. It was a loyalty check.

Making nearly every employee sit down and explain how much AI they use, with their job on the line if the answer wasn't enough, is not an assessment of ability. It's an assessment of who will internalize 「fast」 as doctrine. Real ability can't be measured in AI usage counts. Only one thing can: whether you do what you're told.

I've seen this machine before. Can't build the small chip, so you announce a new law. Can't measure actual competence, so you swap the ruler. Swap the ruler and the ability is unchanged, the rating is not, and everyone gets re-ranked. Proton's ruler is marked in AI adoption, and the CEO drew the ticks himself.

The most naked line came in the security channel. An employee asked for at least a few days to pentest new code before shipping it. Yen's reply: 「That's bullshit and loser thinking.」 Then: stop 「lovingly handcrafting」 code, use AI more.

A privacy company calling 「test it before you ship it」 loser thinking is a confession. Security isn't the product; it's the marketing copy. An engineer's judgment isn't an asset; it's the drag on velocity.

So cancelling the interviews is not a concession. The interviews are gone, the ruler is not, the man who said it is not, the man who declared speed and security compatible is not. The pressure moved from the HR conference room into the Slack channel, from process into culture, which is harder to refuse because it no longer has to notify you.

Users see it plainly. They've spent the last day blasting leadership on social media for deprioritizing security and calling for Yen to resign. They bought 「your data won't be sold.」 Now they learn that inside the company, 「test the code for a few days first」 counts as backward thinking. They can do that math.

But what users can demand is a resignation. What they can't demand is the disappearance of the ruler. Replace the CEO and the board will still ask: what's our AI adoption rate?

The problem was never one man. It's the narrative that dresses speed as progress and demotes caution to loser, and it found a mouth willing to speak for it inside this company. The mouth can be replaced. The narrative doesn't need to be.
