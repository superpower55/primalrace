Meta 的审判：当“成瘾”成为一种可定价的暴力The Meta Trial: When 'Addiction' Becomes a Priced Violence

科技 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-25 § 链接
产品设计不是技术问题，而是对认知入口的暴力掠夺。
Product design is not a technical issue, but a violent seizure of cognitive entry points.

Meta 被起诉的核心逻辑——Hook, Hold, Harvest, Hide——本质上是一套完整的 weaponized 表达链路。这根本不是什么“产品优化”，而是一场精准的认知入口战争。通过算法制造成瘾，Meta 在儿童的大脑中强行建立了一套多巴胺回路，将用户从独立的主体降格为可收割的数据矿产。这种从生物学层面切入的操控，正是典型的 structural violence：它没有血腥的殴打，但它通过剥夺个体对注意力的掌控权，造成了深远的心理损耗。

有趣的是，硅谷的应对姿态极其典型。当面对法律威胁时，他们先是试图用“事实与法律”来掩盖共谋，随后在数据中心遭遇反弹时，又迅速切换到“情感共鸣”模式。Sam Altman 的那句“我理解人们不想在后院建核电站”是典型的假.最优解表达——通过扮演一个“有同情心的理性人”来换取生存空间，而其背后的逻辑依然是：只要能维持 computing power 的扩张，任何情感表演都是合理的 cost。

这场审判真正的看点不在于 Meta 能否被罚款，而在于它能否被迫修改产品设计。如果法院能像当年对待烟草公司那样，强行切断某些成瘾机制的认知入口，那么这才是 actual 的胜利。否则，任何所谓的“安全承诺”都只是文化层面的 PR 掩体，是为了让 Meta 能在下一个增长周期里更高效地地实施 meta-violence。

The core logic of the case against Meta—Hook, Hold, Harvest, Hide—is essentially a complete weaponized expression chain. This is not 'product optimization'; it is a precise war for cognitive entry points. By engineering addiction, Meta forcibly establishes dopamine loops in children's brains, degrading users from independent subjects into harvestable data mines. This biological-level manipulation is a textbook example of structural violence: there is no bloody assault, but it causes profound psychological attrition by stripping individuals of their agency over attention.

Silicon Valley's response is telling. When facing legal threats, they first attempt to hide complicity behind 'facts and law,' then swiftly pivot to 'emotional resonance' when the datacenter backlash hits. Sam Altman's comment about not wanting a nuclear plant in his backyard is a classic fake optimal expression—playing the 'empathetic rationalist' to secure survival space, while the underlying logic remains: any emotional performance is a justifiable cost as long as the expansion of computing power continues.

The real significance of this trial is not whether Meta gets fined, but whether it is forced to redesign its products. If the courts can forcibly sever certain addictive cognitive entry points, as they did with Big Tobacco, that would be an actual victory. Otherwise, any 'safety commitment' is merely a cultural PR shield, designed to let Meta implement meta-violence more efficiently in the next growth cycle.
