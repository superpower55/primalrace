用暴力刷出的“成功”统计学The Statistics of Violence: How 'Control' is Manufactured

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-24 § 链接
当 Actual 被暴力压低，统计数据的下降就是 Structural Violence 的 PR 版本。
When Actual is suppressed by force, a drop in statistics is merely the PR version of Structural Violence.

英国内政部在庆祝跨境人数下降，而 Utopia 56 在记录被打断的腿和被撕裂的家庭。这就是典型的用统计学掩盖暴力：当 Potential（人们寻求生存的权利）与 Actual（实际抵达）之间的差额被警察的警棍和催泪弹强行扩大时，政府将其定义为“边境管控的成功”。

这场博弈的本质是极其卑劣的共谋。英国出钱，法国出人，双方通过一个 $\text{£}660 ext{m}$ 的“按结果付费”协议，将难民的生存权量化为 KPI。在这种机制下，法国警察的 riot squad 成了刷数据的工具。没收救生衣不能阻止走私者，但能提高溺水率；在沙丘上发射催泪弹不能消除绝望，但能制造恐慌。对于制定规则的人来说，溺水的人不再是“跨境者”，而成了统计表之外的消失者，从而在数据上实现了“成功”。

最令人作呕的是这种 Meta Violence 的叙事：将一个充满血腥和混乱的海岸线包装成“恢复控制”的文明之举。在这种男性中心主义的权力逻辑中，弱势者被彻底客体化为需要被“拦截”的货物。所谓的“成功”，不过是把暴力从直接层（殴打）延伸到结构层（制度性驱逐），最后用文化层（边境安全叙事）进行合法化。只要不出现大规模的公开屠杀，这种缓慢的、弥散的暴力就被允许在“文明”的掩体下继续运行。

The UK Home Office is celebrating a decline in Channel crossings, while Utopia 56 is documenting broken legs and torn-apart families. This is a textbook case of using statistics to mask violence: when the gap between Potential (the right to survive) and Actual (successful arrival) is forcibly widened by batons and tear gas, the government labels it as 'successful border control.'

At its core, this is a vile act of complicity. The UK provides the funding, France provides the boots, and both parties quantify the survival of refugees into KPIs via a $\text{£}660 ext{m}$ 'payment-by-results' deal. Under this mechanism, the French riot squads become tools for data mining. Seizing lifejackets doesn't deter smugglers, but it increases the drowning rate; firing tear gas into sand dunes doesn't erase despair, but it manufactures panic. To the architects of this system, those who drown cease to be 'crossers' and become 'disappeared' from the spreadsheet, thus achieving 'success' on paper.

Most sickening is the Meta Violence of the narrative: packaging a blood-stained coastline as a civilized act of 'restoring control.' In this masculine-centric power logic, the vulnerable are completely objectified into 'cargo' to be intercepted. This so-called 'success' is simply the extension of violence from the direct layer (beatings) to the structural layer (institutional expulsion), finally legitimized by the cultural layer (border security narrative). As long as there is no televised massacre, this slow, diffuse violence is permitted to operate under the cover of 'civilization.'
