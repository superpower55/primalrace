二十九万人的差额，算在谁头上290,000 Short of What Should Be

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 Al Jazeera ↗ 2026-10-31 § 链接
战争叙事里，女性身体既是开战借口，也是统计脚注
In war narratives, women's bodies are both the pretext and the footnote.

六万一千名孕妇被流离失所，其中六百人将在两周内分娩。这串数字被放在报道第二段，作为人道主义危机的注脚。但它其实是一句更冷的话：一场由男人发动的战争，正在向另一群人的身体里收取利息。

加尔通的公式是 Violence = Potential − Actual。这二十九万人本可以不被赶出家门，这六万一千名孕妇本可以在有助产士的地方生产。差额就是暴力。这里三层同时动：直接层是导弹和无人机，也有一名女性被枪杀；结构层是道路被封锁，两百万人的食物、药品、燃料和水被切断；文化层是交战双方都在用“合法机构”“公民利益”“神圣城市”这类词，把人的死亡包装成正当性叙事。

报道里有一句话值得停一下：胡塞武装否认向沙特领土发射无人机，称之为“弥天大谎”；同时他们声称对沙特首都、机场和石油设施的攻击负责。同一方，一边否认，一边认领。这不是说谎说漏了，这是解释权的操作——哪些事实可以承认，哪些必须否认，由谁定义，取决于哪一种叙事对当下更有利。谁的机场被炸、死了十三人伤三百余人，被写进新闻；谁在塔伊兹被枪杀，只配出现在政府通讯社的一段里。

再看谁在共谋。沙特领导的联军在利雅得上空拦截无人机，外交大臣在亚特兰大参加 G20 外长会，与美俄欧同行讨论也门局势，并与加拿大外长发表联合谴责。会议桌上谈的是局势，谈判桌下是二百九十万人离家。也门政府的发言人报出的战果是“中和”约三百三十六名胡塞武装人员、摧毁或损坏一百一十二辆车辆和装备。人被换算成数字，数字被换算成战报，战报被换算成谈判筹码。这条链条上每一环都有人得利，而付出身体代价的，是那六百个两周内要生产的女人。

联合国的人道事务厅派了流动医疗队和助产士，援助组织给约二十四万九千人送去了食物、卫生用品和应急现金——四分之三的流离失所者拿到了东西。这是真的，不是表演。但它是止血，不是缝合。差额没有被缩小，只是被暂时盖住。

报道没有写的是：这六百名孕妇将在哪里生产，有没有产床，有没有血，脐带剪断之后那间屋子还在不在。这些名字没有出现，不是因为不重要，是因为战争的账本上，她们被记在“人道主义影响”一栏，而不是“伤亡”一栏。

Sixty-one hundred pregnant women displaced. Six hundred due to give birth within two weeks. The report puts these numbers in its second paragraph, as a humanitarian footnote. They are something colder: interest collected, inside another group's bodies, on a war launched by men.

Galtung's formula is Violence = Potential − Actual. These 290,000 did not have to leave their homes. These 6,100 pregnant women did not have to give birth where no midwife stands. The gap is the violence. Three layers move at once here: the direct layer is missiles and drones, and a woman shot dead; the structural layer is roads closed, cutting food, medicine, fuel and water to two million people; the cultural layer is both sides wrapping death in "legitimate institutions," "the interest of citizens," "the holy cities."

The report contains one line worth stopping on: the Houthis deny launching drones toward Saudi territory, calling the claims "egregious lies" — while claiming responsibility for attacks on the Saudi capital, airports and oil infrastructure. The same side denies and claims in the same breath. That is not a lie slipping out. That is the management of explanation: which facts get admitted, which get denied, and who decides, depending on which narrative serves the moment. Whose airport was hit, thirteen dead and more than three hundred wounded, makes the news; who was shot dead in Taiz appears in one paragraph of a government wire.

Now the co-conspirators. The Saudi-led coalition intercepts a drone over Riyadh. The foreign minister attends a G20 meeting in Atlanta, discusses Yemen with US, Russian and European counterparts, and issues a joint condemnation with Canada. At the table, the situation. Under the table, 290,000 people gone from their homes. Yemen's government spokesman reports "neutralising" about 336 Houthi fighters and commanders and destroying or damaging 112 vehicles and weapons. People become numbers, numbers become battle reports, battle reports become bargaining chips. Every link in that chain profits. The bodies paying are the six hundred women giving birth within two weeks.

OCHA sent mobile teams and midwives. Aid groups delivered food, hygiene supplies and emergency cash to about 249,000 people — three in four of the displaced. That is real, not performance. It is also a tourniquet, not a suture. The gap is not closed, only covered.

What the report does not say: where those six hundred women will give birth, whether there is a bed, whether there is blood, whether the room still stands after the cord is cut. Their names are absent not because they don't matter, but because the war's ledger files them under "humanitarian impact," not under "casualties."
