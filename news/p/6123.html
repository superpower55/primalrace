三百七十六人开会，二百七十九人到场376 Were Named. 279 Showed Up.

中国科技 结构层 · 文化层 NPR ↗ 2026-10-31 § 链接
缺席的不是人，是忠诚的计量单位
The absences are not people. They are units of loyalty.

376 个中央委员和候补委员，到场的只有 279 个。NPR 说超过四分之一缺席，其中很多据信正在被调查。这个数字本身就是全部结论：一场以纪律为主题的会议，用缺席率来证明纪律的执行力度。

习近平的反腐运动从 2012 年开始，据中纪委副书记张福海在会后对记者说，至今已处理 790 万人，包括 960 多名高级官员。790 万这个量级已经不是反腐了，是一次持续的、制度化的清洗——被清洗的对象不是贪腐，是任何可能不“和习近平保持一致”的人。Torigian 说得直白：这是在为明年的党代会做准备，确保所有人跟他一个口径。

张又侠和刘建超被开除，前者曾是最高级别的军官，后者曾被看好接任外长。Thomas 说中国军队的领导层“在过去三四年里几乎被完全清洗”。而张升民在这轮清洗里不但活下来，还升进了政治局——因为他是反腐运动的关键执行者。这就是清洗的逻辑闭环：被清洗的人证明清洗有效，执行清洗的人获得奖赏。

这里要看清的是哪一层暴力在动。直接层没有血，但结构层在高速运转：一个政党把“忠诚”变成可计量的指标，缺席率、开除名单、晋升名单，全是这个指标的读数。文化层同步跟上——会议的主题被包装成“纪律”“治理”“纯洁性”，Torigian 转述习近平的关切是让党员把党“放在生活的中心，而不是自己的私利”。这句话的功能是把结构暴力翻译成道德要求：你不忠诚，不是因为你反对什么，是因为你“自私”。

Thomas 提了一个值得注意的副作用：地方官员为了不引来纪检系统的注意，会“把事情办得极其稳妥”，结果是更多的惰性、更少的试验，可能“产生与习近平意图相反的效果”。这是清洗的代价，但代价不由清洗者承担。

NPR 这篇报道里最该被追问的地方，是它没有追问的地方。790 万人被处理，没有一个人名、没有一个案由、没有一句辩护被记录。报道引用了两位美国学者来解释这场会议对美国意味着什么——Thomas 说这影响美国对中国在台湾、南海问题上动武意愿的判断——但整篇报道里没有一个被清洗者的声音，也没有一个普通党员的处境。缺席的那 97 人，报道只说“据信正在被调查”。“据信”是这篇报道对 97 个人全部的信息量。

一场关于纪律的会议，最诚实的报道方式可能就是只报出席人数。剩下的都是解读，而解读权在谁手里，报道自己已经演示了一遍。

376 full and alternate central committee members were named in 2022. 279 attended this week's plenum. NPR says more than a quarter were absent, many believed to be under investigation. That number is the whole story: a meeting about discipline, proving the reach of discipline through its absence rate.

Xi's anti-corruption campaign began in late 2012. Since then, according to Zhang Fuhai, deputy secretary of the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection, 7.9 million individuals have been punished, including over 960 senior officials. At that scale it is no longer anti-corruption. It is a permanent, institutionalized purge, and what gets purged is not corruption but anyone who might not be "on the same page" as Xi. Torigian says it plainly: this is Xi setting up for next year's party congress.

Zhang Youxia, formerly the most senior military general, and Liu Jianchao, once tipped as the next foreign minister, are among the 33 expelled. Thomas says China's military leadership has been "almost completely purged in the last three or four years." General Zhang Shengmin not only survived the purges but was promoted to the Politburo, because he ran them. That is the closed loop: the purged prove the purge works, and the purger is rewarded.

Watch which layer of violence is moving. There is no blood at the direct level, but the structural level is running at full speed: a party turning loyalty into a measurable index, with attendance rates, expulsion lists and promotion lists as its readings. The cultural layer keeps pace. The meeting is packaged as discipline, governance, purity. Torigian relays Xi's concern that party members put the CCP "at the center of their lives and not their own selfish interests." That sentence translates structural violence into a moral demand: you are not disloyal because you oppose something, you are disloyal because you are selfish.

Thomas flags a side effect worth noting: local officials, avoiding the attention of the discipline system, may play things really safe, producing more inertia and less experimentation, possibly "the opposite effect to what Xi intended." That is the cost of the purge, and the cost is not borne by the purger.

What NPR's report does not ask is the thing to ask. 7.9 million punished, and not one name, not one charge, not one defense on record. Two American scholars explain what the meeting means for the United States, whether Xi would risk force over Taiwan or the South China Sea. But no purged official speaks, and no ordinary party member appears. Of the 97 absent, the report says only that they are believed to be under investigation. "Believed" is the entire information content about 97 people.

A meeting about discipline may be most honestly covered by reporting only the attendance. Everything else is interpretation, and the report has already demonstrated who holds the interpretive power.
