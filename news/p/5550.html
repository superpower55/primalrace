多样性叙事是结构暴力的止痛药Diversity Narratives as Painkillers for Structural Violence

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-14 § 链接
用个别顶端艺术家的成功，掩盖底层文化空间的系统性坍塌。
Using a few top-tier artists to mask the systemic collapse of grassroots cultural spaces.

五十年前，Naseem Khan 揭露了英国艺术建制对少数族裔的集体无视。五十年后，我们看到 Anish Kapoor 这样的艺术家被推上神坛，多元文化被包装成一种国家名片。但这正是典型的 cultural violence：通过在顶端安置几个“成功样本”，让整个结构性的排斥看起来像是一个已经解决的历史问题。

真正的暴力不在于是否承认多样性，而在于谁掌握了定义“成功”的权力。当 Arts Council England 的新主席在会议上发表感言，用“责任”和“行动”这些词汇进行叙事时，这本质上是一种 weaponized expression。它将结构性的掠夺转化为一种道德上的自我救赎。而与此同时，基层文化空间正面临资金匮乏和 gentrification（士绅化）的挤压。这说明了什么？说明系统并不在乎多元文化本身，它只在乎如何将多元文化“产品化”并纳入其掌控的 canon（经典）之中。

这是一种极其阴险的共谋。建制派通过给予少数族裔艺术家名誉，换取了对底层社区文化生存权的合法剥夺。当一个黑人画家在顶级画廊展出时，无数个社区艺术中心在被房地产开发商拆除。这种“进步”是表演性的让步，它缩小的不是 Potential 和 Actual 之间的差额，而是用一种可见的、点状的成功，掩盖了面状的、结构性的衰败。

所谓的“多元英国”叙事，如果不能解决 sustainability（可持续性）和 growth（增长）的问题，就只是一个巨大的 PR scam。它让受害者相信自己已经进入了系统，而实际上，他们只是成了系统用来证明自己“公正”的装饰品。

Fifty years ago, Naseem Khan exposed the British arts establishment's collective ignorance of ethnic minorities. Today, we see artists like Anish Kapoor canonized, and multiculturalism packaged as a national brand. This is textbook cultural violence: by installing a few 'success stories' at the top, the system makes systemic exclusion look like a solved historical problem.

The real violence isn't about whether diversity is recognized, but who holds the power to define 'success.' When the new chair of Arts Council England speaks of 'responsibility' and 'action,' it is a form of weaponized expression. It transforms structural plunder into a narrative of moral self-redemption. Meanwhile, grassroots cultural spaces are being crushed by financial pressure and gentrification. This proves the system doesn't care about diversity itself; it only cares about how to 'productize' it and absorb it into its own canon.

This is a sinister complicity. The establishment grants fame to a few artists of color in exchange for the legitimate right to strip away the survival of community-based culture. While a Black painter exhibits in a top gallery, countless community arts centers are demolished by developers. This 'progress' is a performative concession; it doesn't shrink the gap between Potential and Actual, but uses visible, point-like success to hide a planar, structural decay.

The 'Diverse Britain' narrative is a massive PR scam unless it addresses sustainability and growth. It tricks the marginalized into believing they have entered the system, when in reality, they have merely become ornaments used by the system to prove its own 'justice.'
