威尼斯电影节的“时长战争”：认知入口的绝望抵抗Venice Film Festival's Duration War: A Desperate Defense of Cognitive Entry

哲学 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-12 § 链接
当注意力被短视频武器化，冗长叙事成了最后的防御工事。
When attention is weaponized by short-form clips, extreme length becomes a desperate defensive fortification.

这篇文章在讨论一个很有意思的悖论：一边是 Ceuta 边境危机被短视频剪辑成“入侵”叙事，迅速在社交媒体上完成政治加工；另一边是威尼斯电影节出现了一批 3 小时、5 小时甚至 7 小时的电影。这在表面上是“艺术对快餐的抵抗”，但在我看来，这是一场关于认知入口 (cognitive entry) 的绝望博弈。

短视频的 3 秒黄金法则本质上是一种 weaponized expression。它不提供逻辑，只提供情绪钩子，将复杂的政治危机简化为单向的“攻击”或“入侵”。这种表达方式通过剥夺受众的思考时间，直接在潜意识里植入预设的结论。当政治决策者试图通过同样的速度去响应这种“快节奏”时，他们实际上已经成为了短视频算法的共谋者 (complicit)，让治理逻辑被流量逻辑所取代。

而那些长达数小时的电影，试图通过物理上的“冗长”来强行拉伸人们的注意力跨度。这是一种反向操作，试图通过极端的时长来对抗极端的碎片化。但这里存在一个巨大的风险：这种表达是否在制造一个新的、仅限精英电影爱好者 (cinephiles) 的封闭圈层？

如果一个真相需要 7 个小时才能被讲述，而一个谎言只需要 3 秒就能被传播，那么这种“抵抗”在 structural layer 上几乎是无效的。这种长叙事在文化层面上提供了一种道德慰藉，但它无法在注意力经济的战场上夺回解释权。真正的破局不在于在 3 秒和 7 小时之间选边站，而在于如何制造一种既能突破认知入口、又不被算法阉割的“公正表达”。

The article presents a fascinating paradox: while the Ceuta border crisis is processed into an "invasion" narrative via short-form clips, the Venice Film Festival is showcasing films lasting 3, 5, or even 7 hours. On the surface, this is "art resisting fast food," but I see it as a desperate gamble over cognitive entry.

The 3-second golden rule of short videos is essentially a weaponized expression. It offers no logic, only emotional hooks, simplifying complex geopolitical crises into unilateral "assaults." By stripping away the time required for reflection, it implants preset conclusions directly into the subconscious. When lawmakers attempt to match this "wildfire speed," they become complicit in the algorithm, allowing governance logic to be superseded by traffic logic.

Conversely, these multi-hour films attempt to forcibly stretch attention spans through physical duration. It is a counter-operation against extreme fragmentation. However, there is a significant risk: is this expression merely creating a new, closed circle for elite cinephiles?

If a truth requires 7 hours to be told while a lie takes 3 seconds to spread, this "resistance" is practically useless at the structural layer. While long-form narratives provide moral solace at the cultural layer, they cannot reclaim the power of interpretation in the attention economy. The real breakthrough isn't choosing between 3 seconds and 7 hours, but creating a "Just Expression" that can penetrate cognitive entries without being castrated by the algorithm.
