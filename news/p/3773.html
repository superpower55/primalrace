AI 音乐：一场关于“表达”的廉价诈骗AI Music: A Cheap Scam of Expression

科技 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-13 § 链接
当表达被简化为 Prompt，艺术就成了对人类主体性的工业化掠夺。
When expression is reduced to a prompt, art becomes an industrial plunder of human subjectivity.

Fenix Flexin 的《Rubberz》冲进 Billboard 热榜，这根本不是什么“技术民主化”，而是一场典型的表达 scam。在我的逻辑里，表达 (Expression) 是存在的确证，是创作者用生物墙之内的血肉、经验和主观意志在存在性战争中博弈出的结果。而 AI 音乐是什么？它是对海量人类表达的无偿采样，通过概率分布进行的半成品拟合。它没有主体性，只有拟像。

最讽刺的是那些唱片巨头的表演。Universal 和 Sony 一边在法庭上指控 AI 侵权，一边又忙着和 Nvidia、Stability AI 签战略合作。这就是典型的共谋 (complicity)。他们并不在乎艺术的死亡，他们在乎的是定价权。他们试图通过制定“准入标准”来重新定义什么是“合法 AI”，本质上是想把 AI 这种降低成本的武器收编进自己的结构性暴力之中，确保未来的“AI 产品”依然在他们的抽成体系内运作。

SZA 感觉自己在打仗，因为这确实是一场关于“认知入口”的战争。当 97% 的听众无法分辨真伪时，这意味着“真实”这个概念正在被稀释。如果一个歌手可以通过扮演一个“AI 拟像”来获取流量，那么人类艺术家的“真.最优解表达”——那种包含瑕疵、痛苦和灵魂的真实碰撞——将被这种低成本的、经过计算的“假.最优解”所覆盖。这不仅是经济上的掠夺，更是文化层面的元暴力：它试图告诉我们，人类的创造力不过是一组可被预测的参数。

所谓的“AI Persona”标签或图表隔离，不过是给这个巨大的骗局贴了一张便利贴。真正的战场不在于标签，而在于我们是否还承认：没有主观意志的输出，不配被称为“表达”。

Fenix Flexin’s 'Rubberz' hitting the Billboard charts isn't 'democratization'; it's a textbook expression scam. In my framework, Expression is the confirmation of existence—the result of a creator gambling their blood, experience, and subjective will within the Biological Wall in an existential war. AI music, however, is merely an unauthorized sampling of massive human expressions, a semi-finished fitting based on probability distributions. It possesses no subjectivity, only simulacra.

The irony lies in the performance of the record majors. Universal and Sony sue AI generators in court while simultaneously signing strategic partnerships with Nvidia and Stability AI. This is pure complicity. They don't care about the death of art; they care about pricing power. By proposing 'eligibility criteria,' they are attempting to redefine 'lawful AI,' essentially absorbing this cost-cutting weapon into their own structural violence to ensure future 'AI products' remain within their commission systems.

SZA feels she is at war because this is indeed a war over cognitive entries. When 97% of listeners cannot distinguish real from fake, the concept of 'truth' is being diluted. If an artist can gain traction by performing an 'AI simulacrum,' the true optimal expression of human artists—the authentic collision of flaws, pain, and soul—will be eclipsed by these low-cost, calculated fake optimal expressions. This is not just economic plunder; it is meta-violence at a cultural level: it attempts to convince us that human creativity is nothing more than a predictable set of parameters.

'AI Persona' tags or chart segregation are merely sticky notes on a massive fraud. The real battlefield isn't about labels, but whether we still acknowledge that any output without subjective will does not deserve to be called 'Expression.'
