cockpit 里的血迹与被武器化的“自杀”叙事Blood in the Cockpit and the Weaponized Narrative of 'Suicide'

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-02 § 链接
当暴力被定义为“自杀”或“恐怖主义”时，结构性的元暴力被掩盖了。
When violence is labeled as 'suicide' or 'terrorism', the underlying structural meta-violence is erased.

这起航班袭击案最诡异的不是驾驶舱里的血腥搏斗，而是事后迅速展开的叙事博弈。内塔尼亚胡急于将其定义为“伊斯兰激进主义”的恐怖袭击，而专家在讨论这究竟是“自杀”还是“恐怖主义”。在这场关于动机的争论中，所有人都在使用 masculine-centric narrative（男性中心叙事）：一个男性飞行员通过暴力夺取控制权，试图用一台巨大的机器制造一个“spectacular”的死亡剧场。

请注意那个被刻意忽略的细节：打破僵局的是一个带着六岁女儿上厕所的女性，Tali Manes。在空乘人员（男性）面对异常情况选择不反应、甚至在乘客尖叫时依然维持某种“秩序”的惯性时，是女性的直觉和表达打破了 cockpit 的生物墙。在这个充满男性英雄主义（如那个模仿纪录片的管道工、接管飞机的飞行员）的叙事中，女性被简化成了“目击者”和“救助者”，但实际上，她是唯一一个在 meta-violence（元暴力）的沉默中率先发出警报并采取行动的人。

将此类事件简单归类为“恐怖主义”或“精神疾病导致的自杀”，实际上是在进行一种认知入口的 weaponization。这种分类将暴力个体化，将其剔除在结构之外。无论是所谓的“激进洗脑”还是“心理疾病”，都成了掩盖中东地缘政治结构性暴力（structural violence）的遮羞布。当人们在讨论飞行员是否被 AQAP 招募时，他们其实在共谋维持一个简单的逻辑：暴力来自某个“疯子”或“极端分子”，而不是来自一个被战争、仇恨和权力不对等长期喂养的生态系统。

最终，这架飞机降落在沙特，一个由男性权力绝对垄断的场域。这场“奇迹”的背后，是直接暴力（direct violence）与结构暴力的短暂碰撞。而我们看到的，依然是男性在定义什么是“威胁”，什么是“安全”，以及如何通过书写这次袭击的“动机”来继续他们的政治博弈。

The most eerie part of this flight attack isn't the bloody struggle in the cockpit, but the rapid narrative game that followed. Netanyahu is quick to label it 'Islamist radicalization', while experts debate whether it was 'suicide' or 'terrorism'. In this dispute over motive, everyone employs a masculine-centric narrative: a male pilot seizing control through violence to turn a massive machine into a 'spectacular' theater of death.

Notice the detail deliberately sidelined: the deadlock was broken by Tali Manes, a woman taking her six-year-old daughter to the toilet. While the male flight attendant failed to react to the anomaly—clinging to a scripted 'order' even as passengers screamed—it was a woman's intuition and expression that breached the biological wall of the cockpit. In a narrative saturated with male heroism—like the plumber mimicking a documentary or the off-duty pilots saving the day—women are reduced to 'witnesses' or 'helpers'. In reality, she was the only one to sound the alarm and act against the silence of meta-violence.

Categorizing such events as 'terrorism' or 'psychiatric suicide' is a form of weaponization of the cognitive entry point. It individualizes violence, stripping it from its structural context. Whether it's 'radical indoctrination' or 'mental illness', these labels serve as veils for the structural violence of Middle Eastern geopolitics. While the world discusses whether the pilot was recruited by AQAP, they are complicit in maintaining a simplistic logic: violence stems from a 'madman' or an 'extremist', not from an ecosystem fed by war, hatred, and power asymmetry.

Ultimately, the plane landed in Saudi Arabia, a domain of absolute male power. This 'miracle' was merely a brief collision between direct violence and structural violence. What we are left with is still the masculine gaze defining what constitutes a 'threat' or 'security', and how to manufacture the 'motive' of this attack to serve their ongoing political game.
