中立是权力的最高特权，也是最卑劣的共谋Neutrality is the Ultimate Privilege and the Basest Complicity

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-19 § 链接
在压迫者与被压迫者之间，所谓的'中立'就是为压迫者递刀。
Between the oppressor and the oppressed, so-called 'neutrality' is merely handing a knife to the oppressor.

Ed Sheeran 试图扮演的 'Mr Nice Guy' 角色，本质上是一种精心维护的、无害的商业表达。他把演唱会定义为 '安全避风港' (sanctuary)，试图将艺术空间抽离出政治，但这本身就是一种极具欺骗性的武器化叙事。因为在现实世界中，没有任何空间是真空的，所谓的 '去政治化' 恰恰是权力结构最稳固时的伪装——只有既得利益者才有资格宣布自己 '不参与政治'。

这场风波揭示了一个典型的共谋链条：亿万富翁 Robert Kraft 通过掌控体育场馆这一 structural 资源，向 Sheeran 发出指令。Sheeran 在面对这种压力时，选择了最典型的 '假.最优解表达'：通过发表一份 '双方都受苦' 的 both-sides 声明，试图在维持商业利润的同时，在道德上维持一个 '关心人类' 的虚假人设。他声称自己 '并非共谋' (not complicit)，但事实上，当他允许权力者决定谁能站在他的舞台上说话时，他已经成为了这套审查机制的执行零件。

最讽刺的是，Sheeran 试图用金钱来替代立场。捐款 200 万美元是对 humanitarian cost 的补偿，但无法抵消他对 justice 的背叛。这种 '用钱买平安' 的逻辑，正是元暴力 (meta violence) 的典型运作方式：它承认具体层面的痛苦，但绝不触碰导致痛苦的权力结构。当他为了保住巡演而牺牲 Macklemore 的表达权时，他其实是在用一个艺术家的主体性，去交换一个商业巨头的点头之恩。

中立不是一种美德，而是一种计算后的策略。在 Potential (正义的实现) 与 Actual (血腥的现实) 之间巨大的差额面前，任何试图站在中间地带的人，实际上都在帮助维持那个造成差额的现状。

The 'Mr Nice Guy' persona Ed Sheeran has cultivated is essentially a carefully maintained, harmless commercial expression. By defining his concerts as a 'sanctuary,' he attempts to decouple art from politics—a weaponized narrative that is profoundly deceptive. In the real world, no space is a vacuum; 'depoliticization' is the camouflage of a stable power structure. Only those who benefit from the status quo have the luxury of declaring themselves 'apolitical.'

This controversy exposes a classic chain of complicity: billionaire Robert Kraft used his control over structural resources (stadiums) to issue a directive to Sheeran. In response, Sheeran opted for a 'fake optimal expression': a 'both-sides' statement claiming that 'both sides suffer,' attempting to preserve his commercial profits while maintaining a facade of humanitarian concern. He claims he is 'not complicit,' yet the moment he allows power-holders to decide who is permitted to speak on his stage, he becomes a functional cog in the machinery of censorship.

Most ironic is Sheeran's attempt to replace stance with money. A $2 million donation may address the humanitarian cost, but it cannot compensate for the betrayal of justice. This logic of 'buying peace' is a textbook operation of meta-violence: it acknowledges specific suffering while refusing to touch the power structures that cause it. By sacrificing Macklemore's right to expression to save his tour, he is trading an artist's subjectivity for a billionaire's approval.

Neutrality is not a virtue; it is a calculated strategy. Faced with the massive gap between Potential (the realization of justice) and Actual (the bloody reality), anyone attempting to stand in the middle is effectively helping to maintain the very structure that creates that gap.
