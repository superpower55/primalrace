葬礼要靠众筹的粘合剂The Glue Who Needed a GoFundMe for His Funeral

文娱 结构层 · 文化层 The Hollywood Reporter ↗ 2026-11-03 § 链接
新闻摘要《艾伦秀》前执行制片人 Matthew Wright 于周五在洛杉矶远程工作时去世，终年52岁。他和丈夫今夏都被裁员，家属发起 GoFundMe 募款以支付遗体运回、葬礼和追悼会费用，目标5万美元，周一已超额完成。Ellen DeGeneres 发布含泪视频悼念，称他是「我们整个家庭的粘合剂」。
The newsMatthew Wright, a former executive producer of The Ellen DeGeneres Show, died Friday at 52 while working remotely in Los Angeles. He and his husband were both laid off this summer, and a GoFundMe for funeral and memorial costs has surpassed its $50,000 goal. DeGeneres posted a tearful video calling him "the glue to our entire family."
他被叫做粘合剂，粘合的是别人的家庭
They called him the glue. He was holding up someone else's family.

一个把节目组称作「我们整个家庭」的人，死后家里拿不出钱把他从洛杉矶运回纽约。GoFundMe 上写得很清楚：他和丈夫今年夏天都被裁了，运费、葬礼、追悼会的开销「这个家庭无法承受」。目标五万，周一就超了。

这就是「家庭」这个词在娱乐工业里的定价。Wright 从 2006 年干到这档节目 2022 年收尾，十六年，被老板在悼词里认证为「粘合剂」。粘合剂是耗材，粘的时候没人看见，撕下来才发现整面墙靠它撑着。撑着的时候它没有合同保障，没有遣散缓冲，没有「我们整个家庭」的医保延续——只有一句死后才说出口的定语。

DeGeneres 的视频是真诚的，我不怀疑眼泪。但眼泪和结构是两回事。同一档节目在 2020 年被曝出工作环境问题，那时被讨论的是「有毒」，被讨论的人里有没有 Wright 我不知道，报道没写；我只知道十六年的忠诚换来的结局，是家属在众筹页面上写「如果你曾被他逗笑过，现在是回报的时候」。回报这个词用得准确——它承认了此前一直是单向的。

更锋利的一层在这里：悼词里反复出现的是「他照顾妈妈，几乎每个周末飞洛杉矶纽约」「他把同样的爱给了友谊」「我们本该有更多时间」。这些话都对，都动人。可它们描述的是一个把情感劳动做到极致的人，而情感劳动从来不被计价，只在人死后被折算成形容词。他丈夫 Justin 的名字在每一段悼念里出现，作为被慰问的对象；没人问这对伴侣失去一半收入之后怎么过。裁员发生在夏天，死亡发生在周五，众筹发生在之后——这条时间线本身就是一份结构暴力的账单，只是被包装成了「无法想象的悲剧」。

tWitch 2022 年走了，现在轮到 Wright，DeGeneres 说他们在另一个世界跳舞大笑。两个被这档节目称作家人的人，先后以这种方式被提起。家人这个词在好莱坞的意思是：我们爱你，但我们不雇你一辈子，也不负责你死后的事。

五万美元筹到了，超了。下一个被叫做粘合剂的人，还在等他的众筹页面。

A man his boss called "the glue to our entire family" died and his family could not afford to bring his body home. The GoFundMe says it plainly: he and his husband were both laid off this summer, and the cost of getting him from Los Angeles to New York, a funeral, a memorial, was "impossible for the family to bear." The goal was $50,000. It was surpassed by Monday.

That is the price of the word "family" in the entertainment industry. Wright worked on that show from 2006 to its finale in 2022. Sixteen years, and the tribute that certifies him as glue comes only after he is gone. Glue is a consumable. Nobody sees it while it holds, and only when it is torn away does the wall reveal what was carrying it. While it was carrying, there was no contract, no severance buffer, no continuation of the "family" health plan — only an epithet spoken too late.

DeGeneres's tears are real; I don't doubt them. But tears and structure are different things. The same show faced reports of a toxic workplace in 2020. Whether Wright was part of what was discussed, the report doesn't say. What it does say is that sixteen years of loyalty ended with his family writing on a fundraising page that if he ever made you laugh, now is the time to repay the favor. Repay is the right word. It admits the flow had been one-directional all along.

The sharper layer: every tribute describes a man who did emotional labor to an extreme — flying LA to New York nearly every weekend to care for his mother, giving "that same depth of love" to his friendships, "we should have had so much more time." All true, all moving. And none of it is ever priced. It is converted into adjectives after death. His husband Justin appears in every tribute as the object of condolence. Nobody asks how a household survives losing half its income and then the person. Layoffs in the summer, death on a Friday, the fundraiser after — that timeline is a structural violence invoice, dressed up as an "unfathomable tragedy."

tWitch died in 2022. Now Wright. DeGeneres says they are together somewhere, dancing and laughing. Two people this show called family, remembered in this order, in this way. Family in Hollywood means: we love you, we will not employ you forever, and we are not responsible for what happens after.

The $50,000 came in. It went over. The next person they call glue is still waiting for his page.
