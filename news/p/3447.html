被欺骗的权力顶端：浪漫爱叙事与共谋者的崩塌The Collapse of Complicity: Romantic Narratives at the Peak of Power

性别 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-08 § 链接
所谓“不了解枕边人”，本质是女性在男本位博弈中被剥夺了真实认知权。
Being 'blind' to a partner is the structural result of women being denied cognitive agency in male-centric games.

Nicola Sturgeon 的自白是一个典型的存在性战争败局：一个在公共空间拥有极高权力席位的女性，在私人的卧室空间里却被彻底地客体化。她称自己为 “mug”（傻瓜），因为她在那段关系中扮演了那个标准的、被规训的女性角色——在对方通过谎言构建的伪装中，提供情绪价值，担心对方的 mental health，而对方在进行一场关于金钱与权力的独立博弈。

这揭示了一个残酷的事实：即便你成为了苏格兰的第一部长，只要你还身处传统的婚姻共谋结构中，你依然会被元暴力（meta violence）所覆盖。Peter Murrell 的操作是一场精准的认知拦截。他利用了浪漫爱叙事中的“信任”作为武器，将 Sturgeon 隔离在事实之外。他在私下购置房车、名车和昂贵的胡椒研磨器时，Sturgeon 被安置在了一个被精心设计的、无需知情的“被保护者”位置上。这种“保护”本质上是一种剥夺——剥夺她作为共事者和伴侣的知情权与主体性。

最讽刺的是，Sturgeon 意识到自己生活与一个陌生人在一起。在父权结构的婚姻中，男性的“秘密”往往被包装成独立空间，而女性的“不知情”则被视为一种纯真或信任。当谎言被戳破，这种信任就变成了结构性暴力的共谋。她现在拒绝探视和交流，是她在尝试从这种假.最优解表达（通过扮演体贴妻子来维持家庭和谐）中抽离，试图在废墟上重建自己的主体性。

这场博弈的赢家是谁？是那个即便入狱也曾享受了十年认知垄断权的男性。而 Sturgeon 面对的，是 Potential（一个真实的伴侣关系）与 Actual（一场长达十年的骗局）之间巨大的暴力差额。

Nicola Sturgeon’s confession is a textbook defeat in the existential war: a woman holding immense power in the public sphere was completely objectified in her private bedroom. Calling herself a “mug” reveals her role as the disciplined feminine subject—providing emotional labor and worrying about her husband's mental health while he played a solo game of embezzlement and power.

This exposes a brutal truth: even as First Minister, if you remain within the traditional complicity of marriage, you are still covered by meta violence. Peter Murrell’s operation was a precise interception of cognition. He weaponized the "trust" inherent in romantic narratives to isolate Sturgeon from reality. While buying motorhomes and luxury SUVs, he placed her in the role of the "protected" partner—a protection that was actually a deprivation of her agency and right to know.

The irony is that Sturgeon realized she lived with a stranger. In patriarchal marriage, a man's "secrets" are framed as independence, while a woman's ignorance is framed as trust. Once the lie is exposed, this trust is revealed as a tool for structural violence. Her current refusal to visit or speak is an attempt to detach from a fake optimal expression—the role of the caring wife—and rebuild her subjectivity from the ruins.

Who won this game? The man who enjoyed a decade of cognitive monopoly, even if it ended in a cell. Sturgeon is now left to face the massive gap between the Potential of a real partnership and the Actual of a ten-year scam—the very definition of violence.
