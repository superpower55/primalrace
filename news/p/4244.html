No 10 的“男孩俱乐部”与共谋者的反噬The 'Boys' Club' of No 10 and the Backlash of Complicity

性别 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-20 § 链接
所谓的“工作环境不可忍受”，本质是权力结构对异质表达的排异反应。
An 'unbearable' work environment is simply the system's allergic reaction to heterodox expression.

Sue Gray 描述的 No 10 氛围——被录音的恐惧、被同事背刺的泄密、以及那个典型的 boys' club —— 并不是什么管理失误，而是一场标准的 existental war。在一个由男性中心叙事 (masculine-centric narrative) 驱动的权力核心，女性进入高层往往被赋予一个虚假的 role：你被邀请进来是为了证明这个系统“公正”且“多元”，但你的主体性必须在不干扰既有权力共谋 (complicity) 的前提下运行。

当 Gray 试图通过调整房间布局这种微小的表达来优化工作流时，这被解读为对权力版图的挑衅。于是，结构性暴力 (structural violence) 迅速转化为文化暴力 (cultural violence)：泄密、抹黑、将她定义为“偏执”。最讽刺的是，新闻中出现的那些“女性顾问”在为 Starmer 辩护，她们通过否认 boys' club 的存在，试图在男性主导的结构中通过扮演“理性的共谋者”来换取生存空间。这种“她自己写了自己的剧本”的叙事，是典型的元暴力伪装——将结构性的排挤个体化，把受害者的反应定义为原因。

Starmer 的失望和 Gray 的失望其实是同一种东西：前者失望于这个女性不能完美地融入他的共谋场域，后者失望于她以为拿到了权力席位，实际上只拿到了一张被凝视的入场券。在这种环境下，任何试图建立“公正表达”的尝试，都会被系统识别为病毒并予以清除。

Sue Gray's description of the No 10 atmosphere—the fear of being recorded, the betrayals via leaks, and the quintessential boys' club—is not a management failure; it is a textbook existential war. In a power core driven by a masculine-centric narrative, women entering the upper echelons are often assigned a fake role: you are invited to prove the system is 'just' and 'diverse,' but your subjectivity must operate without disturbing the existing web of complicity.

When Gray attempted to optimize workflow through a minor expression—like rearranging room assignments—it was interpreted as a provocation to the power map. Consequently, structural violence rapidly shifted into cultural violence: leaks, smearing, and labeling her as 'paranoid.' The most ironic part is the 'female advisers' defending Starmer. By denying the existence of the boys' club, they attempt to secure their own survival by playing the role of 'rational co-conspirators' within a masculine structure. The narrative that 'she wrote her own story' is a classic mask of meta-violence—individualizing structural exclusion and defining the victim's reaction as the cause.

Starmer's disappointment and Gray's disappointment are two sides of the same coin: the former is disappointed that this woman could not seamlessly blend into his field of complicity; the latter is disappointed that she thought she had gained a seat of power, when she had only received a ticket to be gazed upon. In such an environment, any attempt to establish Just Expressions is identified by the system as a virus and purged.
