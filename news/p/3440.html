Harvey Nichols 的死亡螺旋：当审美定价权失效The Death Spiral of Harvey Nichols: When the Power to Define Taste Vanishes

哲学 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-08 § 链接
失去定义“酷”的权力，奢侈品店就只剩下一栋昂贵的烂房子。
Once a brand loses the power to define 'cool', it's just an expensive building selling clothes.

Harvey Nichols 的困境不是什么“数字化转型”失败，而是一次典型的审美定价权（Pricing Power of Taste）的崩塌。在 90 年代，它不仅是卖衣服的，它是认知入口。它定义了什么叫 Absolutely Fabulous，定义了什么样的表达才算进入了时尚的权力中心。那时候，买东西是次要的，获得“被定义为时尚”的身份确认才是真.最优解表达。

现在的情况是，它失去了这种定义权。当 Dover Street Market 成了新兴品牌抢着进入的圣地，Harvey Nichols 就从“定义者”变成了“跟随者”。在存在性战争中，最惨的不是规模小，而是变成了他者的子集——如果你在 Selfridges 能买到一样的东西，那么 Harvey Nichols 的存在就失去了政治意义。它不再是一个能够筛选人群、划分阶级的武器，而变成了一个纯粹的零售空间。

现在的管理层试图通过重新装修、请时尚编辑来挽回“体验感”，这不过是文化层面的 PR 补丁。真正的危机在于，它已经无法在公共空间的注意力博弈中制造“真实”了。当一个品牌失去了制造“非它不可”的可能性，它就陷入了 Mike Ashley 所说的 death spiral。这不是商业危机，而是主体性的死亡。

The crisis at Harvey Nichols isn't about a failure in 'digital transformation'; it's a collapse of the Pricing Power of Taste. In the 90s, it wasn't just selling clothes; it was a cognitive entry point. It defined what was 'Absolutely Fabulous' and who belonged at the center of fashion power. Back then, shopping was secondary; the true optimal expression was the confirmation of identity as 'fashionable'.

Now, that power to define is gone. With Dover Street Market becoming the sanctuary for emerging brands, Harvey Nichols has shifted from a 'definer' to a 'follower'. In the existential war, the worst position is becoming a subset of another—if you can buy the same thing at Selfridges, Harvey Nichols loses its political significance. It is no longer a weapon for screening classes or identities, but merely a retail space.

Management's attempt to revive 'experience' through redesigns and fashion editors is just a cultural PR patch. The real crisis is that it can no longer manufacture 'reality' in the battle for public attention. When a brand can no longer create the possibility of being 'irreplaceable', it enters the death spiral Mike Ashley described. This isn't a business crisis; it's the death of subjectivity.
