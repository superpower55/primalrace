太阳能债券：一场关于能源定价权的结构性博弈Solar Bonds: A Structural Game of Energy Pricing Power

哲学 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-21 § 链接
能源贫困是结构性暴力，而金融工具的武器化让贫困者在节能中被二次收割。
Energy poverty is structural violence; the weaponisation of finance ensures the poor are harvested twice while saving energy.

这则新闻表面在讨论如何降低太阳能板的贷款成本，但其核心揭示的是一种典型的 structural violence：低收入群体在追求 Potential（更低的能源账单）的过程中，被商业贷款的高利率（9%-10%）拦截在了 Actual 之外。这是一种极其阴险的 scam——你为了省钱而借钱，结果省下的钱在十年内全部变成了银行的利息。在这种机制下，节能不再是权利，而成了金融机构针对底层的一种精准收割工具。

Common Wealth 提出的 "solar bonds" 试图通过政府背书将这种金融逻辑从 commercial 转向 universal。这在本质上是试图在 structural 层面上削减暴力差额。如果能将利息砍半并与房产绑定，这意味着能源独立（energy independence）不再是富人的特权，而成了普世的 entitlement。这不仅仅是环保问题，更是关于谁拥有能源定价权、谁在能源转型中获利的权力博弈。

但我们需要警惕政府所谓的 "zero and low-interest loans" 这种 PR 话术。如果缺乏像 "solar bonds" 这样透明的零售投资模型，而仅仅是某种模糊的补贴或行政指令，那么它很可能演变成另一种形式的共谋：政府通过补贴维持安装商的利润，而底层家庭依然在承担隐形的金融风险。真正的 good_news 不在于政府 "促进" 了什么，而在于是否建立了一套让低收入者不再被金融利息掠夺的公正表达机制。

On the surface, this news is about lowering loan costs for solar panels. In reality, it exposes a classic structural violence: low-income households are intercepted by high commercial interest rates (9%-10%) while trying to move from their current state toward a Potential of lower energy bills. It is a cynical scam—borrowing to save, only for those savings to be wiped out by bank interest for a decade. Here, energy efficiency is not a right, but a tool for financial institutions to harvest the bottom tier.

The "solar bonds" proposed by Common Wealth attempt to shift this financial logic from commercial to universal. This is an effort to reduce the violence gap at the structural layer. By halving interest rates and tying loans to the property, energy independence ceases to be a luxury for the rich and becomes a universal entitlement. This is not merely an environmental issue, but a power struggle over who controls energy pricing and who profits from the transition.

However, we must remain skeptical of government PR phrases like "zero and low-interest loans." Without a transparent retail investment model like solar bonds, such promises often devolve into another form of complicity: the state subsidises installers' profits while low-income families still bear hidden financial risks. True good_news is not found in government "promotion," but in the establishment of a Just Expression that prevents the poor from being preyed upon by financial interest.
