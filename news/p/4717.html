阴谋论是权力对真实的一种低端拟态Conspiracy Theories as a Low-End Mimicry of Power

哲学 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-03 § 链接
用虚构的禁忌掩盖真实的结构性暴力，是典型的认知入口陷阱。
Using fictional taboos to mask structural violence is a classic cognitive entrance trap.

这篇关于《Eyes Wide Shut》被删减 20 分钟的阴谋论，本质上是一场关于“认知入口”的低端博弈。阴谋论者试图通过制造一个“被掩盖的禁忌事实”——比如精英阶层的儿童性虐待环路——来诱导受众相信这个世界是由少数阴暗精英操纵的。这种叙事最狡猾的地方在于，它给受众提供了一种“我掌握了真相”的虚假主体性，让他们在 Reddit 的讨论中获得某种存在性的快感。

但如果我们用加尔通的暴力三角来看，这种阴谋论其实是在实施文化层面的暴力。它把真实的 structural violence（结构性暴力）——即全球精英阶层如何通过资本、法律和权力网络合法地剥削和物化弱势群体——简化成了几个“秘密集会”或“被删减的片段”。当人们在争论库布里克是否预言了爱泼斯坦时，真正的权力运作机制反而被这种戏剧化的叙事给掩盖了。阴谋论成了真实暴力的烟雾弹。

至于 Vivian Kubrick 和剪辑师的辟谣，这只是在 direct 层面上地毯式地清理噪音。但无论这 20 分钟是否存在，真正值得追问的是：为什么大众如此渴望相信一个关于“精英邪教”的故事？因为在元暴力的男性中心叙事中，权力的运作总是被描述为某种不可见的、神谕般的意志。人们习惯于寻找一个具体的“反派”来投射恐惧，而不是去审视那个让他们感到无力的、由共谋者构建的社会结构本身。

最讽刺的是，这种阴谋论往往被包装成“觉醒”，但它实际上是另一种形式的自我规训：它让你在崇拜“真相”的过程中，彻底放弃了对现实结构进行理性解剖的能力。

The conspiracy theory regarding the 'missing 20 minutes' of Eyes Wide Shut is essentially a low-end game of cognitive entrances. Conspiracy theorists attempt to manufacture a 'hidden taboo'—such as elite child abuse rings—to convince the audience that the world is orchestrated by a few dark elites. The cunning part of this narrative is that it grants the audience a false sense of subjectivity, a feeling of 'possessing the truth,' providing an existential thrill within Reddit threads.

Analyzing this through Galtung's Violence Triangle, such conspiracy theories actually exert cultural violence. They reduce structural violence—the way global elites legally exploit and objectify the marginalized through capital, law, and power networks—into a few 'secret meetings' or 'deleted scenes.' While the public debates whether Kubrick predicted Epstein, the actual mechanisms of power are masked by this dramatized narrative. The conspiracy becomes a smokescreen for real violence.

Vivian Kubrick's and the editor's denials are merely clearing noise at the direct level. Regardless of whether those 20 minutes exist, the real question is: why is the public so desperate to believe a story about 'elite cults'? In the meta-violence of masculine-centric narratives, the operation of power is always depicted as some invisible, oracular will. People are conditioned to seek a specific 'villain' to project their fears upon, rather than examining the social structure built by complicity that renders them powerless.

The irony is that these theories are often packaged as 'awakening,' yet they are another form of self-discipline: they lead you to abandon the capacity for rational structural analysis in favor of worshipping a curated 'truth.'
