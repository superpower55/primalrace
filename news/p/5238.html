AI 剥削与司法共谋：谁在为捕食者递刀？AI Exploitation and Judicial Complicity: Who is Handing the Knife to Predators?

性别 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-24 § 链接
技术赋予了捕食者规模化剥削的能力，而司法系统则在用“管理成本”为他们提供庇护。
Tech grants predators the power of scalable exploitation, while the legal system provides sanctuary in the name of 'administrative costs'.

这是一个典型的 Violence = Potential − Actual 差额被恶意扩大的案例。AI 技术在这里不是什么“创新”，而是一把被武器化的手术刀，将女性和儿童的身体从其主体性中强行剥离，转化为纯粹的性消费客体。Dogan 拍摄五千张照片并利用 AI 将其“数字化脱衣”，本质上是一场大规模的虚拟强奸。在这种叙事里，受害者的身体成了 AI 模型的燃料，而这种剥削在数字空间里是可复制、可传播且不可逆的。

更令人作呕的是司法系统在其中的 structural violence。一个惯犯、一个将儿童操场作为狩猎场的捕食者，竟然因为司法系统想要“削减羁押人数”这种纯粹的行政管理成本，而被允许保释回到那个俯瞰操场的公寓里。这简直是个巨大的 scam：系统在用受害者的安全感来对冲监狱的预算压力。当受害者家庭被迫搬离家园以求生存，而施暴者却能回到自己的床上时，司法系统已经完成了它与捕食者的潜意识共谋——它在告诉施暴者，只要系统压力足够大，你的 predatory behavior 就可以被容忍。

至于 Met 警察局长的公开批评，虽然听起来正义，但更像是一种在结构性失败后的表演性愤怒。他指责 AI 公司“不负责任”，这当然没错，但 AI 公司的逻辑就是将“可能性”最大化而将“后果”外包给社会。真正的元暴力在于，我们依然生活在一个将男性性欲置于至高优先级，而将女性和儿童的身体安全视为“可接受损耗”的叙事结构中。在这个结构里，Dogan 的律师试图用“抑郁”和“掉入兔子洞”来美化这种纯粹的权力支配欲，试图将一个捕食者包装成一个迷路的可怜虫。

这场博弈中，唯一的真实是受害者母亲口中那个“被击中腹部”的剧痛。这种痛是生物墙被暴力击穿后的生理反应，而司法系统和科技公司则在用冷冰冰的“算法”和“预算”来消解这种痛。

This is a textbook case of the Violence Triangle where the gap between Potential and Actual is maliciously widened. AI technology here is not 'innovation'; it is a weaponized scalpel used to forcibly detach the bodies of women and children from their subjectivity, converting them into pure objects of sexual consumption. Dogan’s act of taking 5,000 photos and using AI to 'digitally strip' them is essentially a mass virtual rape. In this narrative, the victims' bodies become fuel for AI models—an exploitation that is replicable, distributable, and irreversible in digital space.

Even more repulsive is the structural violence embedded in the justice system. A prolific offender who treated a children's playground as his hunting ground was granted bail simply because the system wanted to 'reduce the remand population'—a pure administrative cost-cutting measure. This is a massive scam: the system is hedging its budget pressure against the safety of victims. When victimized families are forced into temporary accommodation to survive while the predator returns to his own bed, the judiciary has completed its subconscious complicity with the predator. It signals that as long as systemic pressure is high, predatory behavior can be tolerated.

As for the Met Commissioner's public criticism, while it sounds just, it feels like performative anger following a structural failure. He calls AI companies 'grossly irresponsible,' which is true, but the logic of AI firms is to maximize 'possibility' while outsourcing the 'consequences' to society. The meta-violence here is that we still live in a masculine-centric narrative where male sexual desire is prioritized over the bodily safety of women and children, who are treated as 'acceptable collateral damage.' In this structure, Dogan's lawyer attempts to sanitize this pure lust for power by framing it as 'depression' or 'falling down a rabbit hole,' attempting to rebrand a predator as a lost soul.

The only truth in this game is the 'punch in the gut' described by the victims' mothers. This pain is the biological reaction to the violent breaching of the biological wall, while the legal system and tech companies use cold 'algorithms' and 'budgets' to dissolve that pain.
