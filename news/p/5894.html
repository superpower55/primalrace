全家福取消了，家还在The Family Photo Is Cancelled. The Family Isn't.

国际 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-31 § 链接
拒绝合影不是反战，是重新分配谁有资格站进画面。
Refusing a photo isn't anti-war. It's redistributing who gets to stand in the frame.

G20外长会开场那张“全家福”被取消，因为三分之一的与会国拒绝和拉夫罗夫同框。这不是反战，这是一次画面成员的重新审核。合影从来不是礼节，是表达：谁站在谁旁边，谁被框进同一张照片，等于宣布谁的存在被这个体系承认。乌克兰不是G20成员，今年没被邀请；南非是创始成员、G20里唯一的非洲国家，也没被邀请。这两件事和“不和俄罗斯合影”发生在同一场会里，报道写得很清楚，但很少有人把它们放在一起看。

德国外长说得很直白：欧洲伙伴和拉夫罗夫之间“没有正常状态”。这句话是对的。但“正常状态”的定义权在谁手里，报道没写。俄罗斯对乌克兰的战争还在打，拉夫罗夫照样坐在桌边，照样反对波兰加入G20——波兰这次是第一次以客人身份到场。战争的一方在会场里，被战争波及的一方在会场外。合影取消只是把这个结构摆到了台面上：不是有人被赶出去，是有人拒绝承认这张桌子还算一张桌子。

鲁比奥在同一个会场呼吁团结，说我们正处在一个关键时刻，面临一系列相互关联的经济和地缘政治危机。同一场会里，特朗普政府正在推动把俄罗斯重新拉回国际社会，同时没有邀请南非。报道说，这可能导致会议结束时拿不出一份联合公报。公报拿不出来，合影也取消了，剩下的是各国外长在亚特兰大坐了一整天，各自表述。

这里真正在动的是文化层：一张照片、一个“正常”的说法、一份“联合公报”的仪式感，这些是让结构暴力看起来像秩序的东西。取消合影是一次表态，但表态的代价落在谁身上？报道里的外交官说会谈“友好”，说俄罗斯的立场“不妥协”。友好和不妥协可以同时成立，因为不妥协的那一方不需要让步，需要让步的是想拍那张照片的人。

南非外交部发言人说得很准确：我们的不参与不是自愿的。这句话应该被印在每一张没拍成的合影上。缺席和拒绝出席是两回事，报道把它们放在同一篇里，但它们的性质完全不同。一个是被排除在画面外，一个是主动走出画面。前者是结构暴力，后者是表达。把两者混为一谈，就会得出“大家都在抵制俄罗斯”的结论，而实际上，有人是在抵制俄罗斯，有人是根本没被通知来拍照。

合影取消了，家还在。只是这个家里谁有椅子、谁有门票、谁只能站在门外看别人拒绝入镜，报道写到了，但没有写完。

The traditional G20 "family photo" was cancelled because a third of the countries at the table refused to stand beside Sergei Lavrov. This is not anti-war. This is a re-audit of who belongs in the picture. A group photo was never ceremony; it is expression: who stands next to whom, who gets framed together, is an announcement that this system recognizes your existence. Ukraine is not a G20 member and was not invited this year. South Africa is a founding member and the only African country in the group, and was also not invited. Both facts sit in the same report as the photo boycott, and almost nobody reads them together.

Germany's foreign minister said it plainly: there is "no normality with Russia." That is correct. But who defines "normality" is not in the report. Russia's war on Ukraine is ongoing, Lavrov still sits at the table, still opposes Poland's G20 membership — Poland attended this meeting as a guest for the first time. The party to the war is inside the room. The party affected by the war is outside it. Cancelling the photo just puts this structure on the table: nobody was expelled. Some people refused to pretend the table was still a table.

Rubio, in the same room, appealed for unity, saying we are in a critical moment facing interconnected economic and geopolitical crises. In the same meeting, the Trump administration is trying to reintegrate Russia into the international community while not inviting South Africa. The report says this will probably prevent a joint communique. No communique, no photo. What's left is a room full of foreign ministers in Atlanta, each making their own statement.

What is actually moving here is the cultural layer: a photograph, a word like "normality," the ritual of a joint communique — these are the things that make structural violence look like order. Cancelling the photo is a statement. But who pays for the statement? Diplomats in the report describe the session as cordial and Russia's position as unyielding. Cordial and unyielding can coexist, because the unyielding side does not need to yield. The side that wanted the photo does.

South Africa's foreign ministry spokesperson put it precisely: our non-participation is not voluntary. That sentence should be printed on every photo that never got taken. Absence and refusal are two different things, and the report places them side by side. One is being excluded from the frame. The other is walking out of it. The first is structural violence. The second is expression. Conflating them produces the conclusion that everyone was boycotting Russia, when in fact some were boycotting Russia and some were simply never told to show up.

The photo is cancelled. The family is still there. Who has a chair, who has a ticket, and who stands outside watching others refuse to be photographed — the report gets that far and stops.
