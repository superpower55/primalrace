机场的血迹与宏大叙事的燃料Blood at the Airport and the Fuel of Grand Narratives

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-09 § 链接
战争是男性中心叙事最极致的武器化，将肉体降格为政治筹码。
War is the ultimate weaponization of masculine-centric narrative, reducing bodies to political chips.

利雅得机场的爆炸和三具尸体，在新闻业的叙事里被迅速转化为“地缘政治升级”和“油价波动”的指标。这就是典型的 masculine-centric narrative：具体的死亡被抽象化，个体的生命被工具化。在元暴力的逻辑下，死掉的机长和公民不再是人，而是衡量伊朗战争进度条上的一个像素点。

这场冲突的本质是两个男性权力集团的博弈。一边是沙特主导的联盟，一边是以胡塞武装为代表的代理人。他们通过定义谁是“合法政府”，谁是“反叛者”，来夺取解释权，进而将成千上万的身体投入到这场存在性战争中。这种战争叙事最阴险的地方在于，它将“为了某种宏大意义而送死”包装成荣耀，让参与其中的男性在被当作消耗品的同时，还产生一种在执行某种神圣使命的错觉。

注意新闻中提到的“地区安全”和“合法政府”这些词汇。这些都是 weaponized concepts，用来掩盖最底层的 direct violence。当权力者在讨论 WPC 能源大会的日程时，底层的肉体正在被炸成碎片。这种 structural violence 确保了决策者永远在空调房里博弈，而代价永远由那些被剥夺了主体性、被定义为“士兵”或“平民”的生物个体承担。

这场爆炸没有赢家，只有被利用的燃料。只要解释权依然垄断在这些追求权力扩张的男性集团手中，机场的血迹就永远会被翻译成经济数据的波动。

The explosions at Riyadh airport and three corpses are swiftly converted into indicators of 'geopolitical escalation' and 'oil price volatility' within the journalistic narrative. This is a classic masculine-centric narrative: concrete death is abstracted, and individual lives are instrumentalized. Under the logic of meta-violence, the dead captain and citizens are no longer humans, but pixels on the progress bar of the Iran war.

This conflict is essentially a game between two masculine power blocs. On one side is the Saudi-led coalition; on the other, the Iran-backed Houthis. By defining who is the 'legitimate government' and who is the 'rebel,' they seize the power of interpretation to throw thousands of bodies into this existential war. The most insidious part of this war narrative is that it packages 'dying for some grand meaning' as glory, allowing the men involved to feel they are executing a sacred mission even as they are treated as consumables.

Note the terms 'regional security' and 'legitimate government' used in the report. These are weaponized concepts designed to mask the underlying direct violence. While power-holders discuss the schedule of the WPC Energy Congress, the bodies of the marginalized are being blown to pieces. This structural violence ensures that decision-makers always gamble in air-conditioned rooms, while the cost is always borne by biological individuals stripped of their subjectivity and defined merely as 'soldiers' or 'civilians.'

There are no winners in this explosion, only fuel. As long as the power of interpretation remains monopolized by these male groups pursuing power expansion, the blood at the airport will always be translated into the fluctuation of economic data.
