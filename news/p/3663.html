把女性监狱腾给男人：一种极其典型的元暴力逻辑Emptying Women's Prisons for Men: A Textbook Case of Meta-Violence

性别 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-11 § 链接
将女性监狱转化为男性监狱，是典型的通过客体化女性来解决男性危机。
Converting women's prisons to hold men is the ultimate objectification of women to solve a masculine crisis.

Andy Burnham 提出的所谓“增加监狱容量”方案，精准地展示了什么叫男性中心叙事 (masculine-centric narrative)。在面临监狱过载的结构性危机时，他的最优解表达竟然包括：加速驱逐外籍囚犯、释放女性囚犯，并将女性监狱改为男性监狱。

请注意这个逻辑的残酷性：在权力持有者的认知入口里，女性囚犯的存在被定义为“可被轻易挪动”或“可被牺牲”的次要项。当男性囚犯因为数量过多而导致系统崩溃时，解决办法不是反思导致监禁率上升的结构性暴力，而是直接把女性的物理生存空间——监狱——给抢走，腾给男人。这在本质上是一次空间掠夺，将女性再次客体化为一种“可调节的资源”来服务于男性的系统稳定。

最讽刺的是，方案中提到的“将女性囚犯分流”听起来像是一种人道主义的 good_news，但结合上下文看，这不过是文化暴力 (cultural violence) 的伪装。其真实目的不是为了女性的权益，而是为了腾出 HMP Bronzefield 这种大监狱来关男人。这种“为了让你自由而把你赶走”的叙事，是典型的 meta violence：它不承认女性作为主体的独立需求，只承认女性在男性危机面前的工具价值。

谁在共谋？司法部、政客和那些讨论“可行性”的专家。他们讨论的是 security measures（安保标准）是否能满足男性囚犯，而从未讨论过女性囚犯被分流后在社区中是否面临更严重的结构性暴力。在这一套博弈中，女性的生存状态被简化成了一个数字，用来对冲男性的监狱压力。这不仅是structural violence，更是对女性作为“原初种族”被殖民逻辑的现代复刻。

Andy Burnham's proposed solutions to the prison overcrowding crisis are a masterclass in masculine-centric narrative. Facing a structural collapse, his "optimal expression" includes accelerating deportations and, most tellingly, releasing female prisoners to convert their facilities into men's jails.

Observe the brutality of this logic: in the cognitive entry point of power, female prisoners are defined as "disposable" or "adjustable" variables. When the system crashes due to the sheer volume of male prisoners, the solution isn't to dismantle the structural violence driving incarceration, but to physically seize the space reserved for women to stabilize the masculine system. This is a spatial raid, reducing women to a resource to be managed for the benefit of men.

Most cynical is the framing of "diverting female offenders" as a progressive move. This is merely cultural violence in disguise. The goal isn't female empowerment; it's to vacate HMP Bronzefield to make room for men. This narrative of "freeing you to make room for him" is pure meta-violence: it ignores the female subject's needs and validates only her utility in solving a male crisis.

Who are the complicit parties? The Ministry of Justice, politicians, and the experts debating "feasibility." They obsess over whether security standards meet the requirements for male prisoners, while remaining silent on the structural violence women face once diverted into the community. In this game, female existence is reduced to a digit used to hedge against male prison pressure. It is a modern iteration of the colonial logic applied to the Primal Race.
