被定价的“叛逆”与右翼媒体的收割Priced Rebellion and the Right-Wing Harvest

国际 文化层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-06 § 链接
所谓的“反建制”勇敢，往往只是在精准地攻击结构性弱势者。
So-called "anti-establishment" bravery is often just a precise attack on the structurally marginalized.

Rod Liddle 的死引发的一系列讣告，完美演绎了什么叫 cultural violence。那些赞美他“勇敢”、“敢于挑战建制”的措辞，本质上是一种 sanitized 的共谋。真正的勇敢是挑战权力，而 Liddle 做的却是把认知入口对准穆斯林、跨性别者和移民——这些在结构层 (structural layer) 处于绝对劣势的群体。攻击弱者不仅不勇敢，反而是一种极其廉价的、被资本定价的表演。

从 90 年代的左翼研究员到右翼媒体的“攻击犬”，Liddle 的路径揭示了一个残酷的真相：在 21 世纪，种族主义成为了最高效的 transgression (违规/叛逆) 形式。当传统的反叛失去了市场，资本通过 GB News 这样的渠道，为那些能够制造对立、煽动仇恨的叙事支付 top dollar。这不再是意识形态之争，而是 hegemonic capture (霸权捕获) —— 通过掌控定义“什么是事实”的权力，将社会撕裂转化为资本利润。

最令人作呕的共谋在于，即便在 Liddle 死亡后，主流叙事依然试图用“内心善良”或“特立独行”来稀释他对他人的真实伤害。这种对暴力的美化，让文化层面的元暴力 (meta violence) 持续生效：它告诉世界，只要你足够“有趣”或“有个性”，那么对边缘群体的践踏就可以被视为一种“风格”。

Liddle 最终在 COPD 的窒息中死去，而他留给世界的，正是那种让无数弱势群体在呼吸之间感到窒息的文化氛围。这种共谋的闭环直到现在依然在运转，只要人们还把“冒犯弱者”误认为“挑战权威”。

The obituaries for Rod Liddle are a masterclass in cultural violence. The praise for him being "brave" or "challenging the establishment" is a sanitized form of complicity. True bravery is challenging power; Liddle, however, aimed his cognitive entry points at Muslims, transgender people, and immigrants—groups already at the bottom of the structural layer. Attacking the marginalized is not brave; it is a cheap, priced performance.

Liddle's trajectory from a 90s left-wing researcher to a right-wing "attack dog" reveals a grim reality: in the 21st century, racism has become the most efficient form of transgression. When traditional rebellion lost its market, capital—through outlets like GB News—began paying top dollar for narratives that manufacture division and hate. This is not an ideological war, but hegemonic capture: seizing the power to define "fact" to convert social rupture into profit.

The most disgusting complicity lies in the attempt to dilute his real-life harm with narratives of a "heart of gold" or "eccentricity." This beautification of violence ensures that meta violence continues to operate: it suggests that as long as you are "interesting" or "edgy," the trampling of marginalized bodies can be rebranded as a "style."

Liddle died suffocating from COPD, but the legacy he left is the very atmosphere that makes countless marginalized people feel they are drowning in air. This cycle of complicity persists as long as the world continues to mistake "offending the weak" for "challenging authority."
