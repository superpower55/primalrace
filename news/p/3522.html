激素补丁与被定义的“女性感”Hormone Patches and the Defined 'Femininity'

性别 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-09 § 链接
当女性需要通过男性激素来找回“女性感”时，这本身就是一场文化暴力的胜利。
When a woman needs male hormones to feel 'like a woman,' it is a victory for cultural violence.

这是一个典型的关于“生物墙”与“文化暴力”共谋的样本。Antonia 在更年期通过补充睾酮（Testosterone）找回了性欲和所谓的“女性感”。请注意这个悖论：在我们的文化叙事中，欲望、主动权和进攻性被定义为 Masculine，而温顺、被动和衰退被定义为 Feminine。当她失去了这些“男性化”的特质时，她感到自己不再像个女人；而当她通过药物重新获得这些特质时，她才觉得“回到了自己”。

这揭示了一个残酷的 Meta Violence：社会对“女性”的定义，竟然是建立在对女性生物性欲望的剥夺之上的。女性必须通过习得或模拟某种程度的“男性化”能量，才能在存在性战争中获得主体性。在这种叙事下，真正的“女性感”不是一种自然的生理状态，而是一套被武器化的评价体系，它要求女性在“被动的客体”和“模拟主体的进攻者”之间进行危险的跳跃。

而 Rob 的视角则暴露了结构性的 Complicity。在他看来，性生活曾是“chore”（苦差事），而现在的 revival 让他确认对方“still likes you”。对他而言，女性的身体首先是“peachy fruit salad”这样的视觉客体，其次才是情感主体。即便在性生活的复苏中，他依然在依赖 Porn 这种典型的、物化女性的文化产品来维持自己的 Arousal。这场所谓的“角色切换”，本质上依然在男本位的性快感逻辑里打转，并没有触及权力关系的真正重构。

This is a textbook sample of the complicity between the Biological Wall and Cultural Violence. Antonia finds her 'femininity' again through Testosterone HRT. Note the paradox: in our narrative, desire, agency, and aggression are defined as Masculine, while passivity and decay are Feminine. When she lost these 'masculine' traits, she felt she ceased to be a woman; when she regained them via medication, she felt she 'returned' to herself.

This reveals a cruel Meta Violence: the social definition of 'woman' is built upon the deprivation of female biological desire. Women must acquire or simulate a certain level of 'masculine' energy to achieve agency in the existential war. In this framework, true 'femininity' is not a natural physiological state, but a weaponized evaluation system that forces women to leap between being a 'passive object' and a 'simulated aggressive subject.'

Rob's perspective exposes the structural Complicity. To him, sex was once a 'chore,' and the current revival is merely a helpful indication that she 'still likes' him. For him, the female body is first a visual object—a 'peachy fruit salad'—and only secondarily an emotional subject. Even in this revival, he relies on Porn, a classic product of cultural violence that objectifies women, to maintain his arousal. This so-called 'role switch' is still spinning within the masculine-centric logic of sexual pleasure; it never touches the actual reconstruction of power.
