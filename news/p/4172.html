以“务实”之名，向资本投诚Pragmatism as a Cloak for Capitalist Complicity

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-19 § 链接
所谓的“务实”通常是权力在面对资本共谋时，用来掩盖背叛的遮羞布。
So-called 'pragmatism' is usually a fig leaf used by power to mask its betrayal in favor of capital.

工党在北海钻井问题上的犹豫，是一场典型的存在性博弈。Burnham 提到的“pragmatic”（务实），在政治语境中几乎等同于“向既得利益集团低头”。当他讨论油气来源而非是否继续依赖化石燃料时，他实际上是在试图通过重新定义问题，来掩盖一个事实：权力正在被石油巨头的 record profits 绑架。

这不仅是政策分歧，更是 structural violence 的延续。气候危机带来的热浪和干旱是 Actual 状态，而人类本可达到的 Potential 状态是迅速脱碳。这两者之间的差额，就是由化石燃料资本驱动的暴力。工党如果批准新钻井，就是选择成为这套暴力机制的共谋者 (complicit)，用选票的短期波动去交换资本的政治入场券。

绿党抛出的“50个席位”威胁，本质上是在尝试夺回认知入口。他们试图告诉选民：气候行动不是一个可以被“务实”掉的选项，而是一个关于生存的 moral imperative。而工党的反应——将其称为“fantasy”——则是典型的元暴力逻辑：将弱势阵营的生存诉求定义为不切实际的幻想，从而维持男性中心叙事下那种“理性、稳健、掌控全局”的统治假象。

这场博弈的真正最优解应该是彻底切断与化石燃料资本的共谋。但对于习惯了在权力结构中通过扮演“成年人”来获利的政客来说，承认资本是 scam 显然比背叛资本要困难得多。

Labour's hesitation over North Sea drilling is a classic existential game. The 'pragmatic' approach mentioned by Burnham is, in political terms, almost synonymous with bowing to vested interests. By shifting the conversation to where oil and gas come from rather than whether we should rely on them, he is attempting to redefine the reality to hide a stark truth: power is being hijacked by the record profits of oil giants.

This is not merely a policy dispute; it is the continuation of structural violence. The heatwaves and droughts of the climate crisis represent our Actual state, while the Potential state is a rapid decarbonization. The gap between them is the violence driven by fossil fuel capital. If Labour approves new drilling, they are choosing to become complicit in this mechanism, trading short-term electoral volatility for the political patronage of capital.

The Green Party's threat of '50 seats' is an attempt to seize the cognitive entry point. They are arguing that climate action is not an option to be 'pragmatized' away, but a moral imperative for survival. Labour's dismissal of this as 'fantasy' is a textbook application of meta-violence: defining the survival demands of a marginalized faction as unrealistic fantasies to maintain the illusion of 'rational, steady, and in-control' governance typical of masculine-centric narratives.

The true optimal expression in this game would be a total rupture from the complicity of fossil fuel capital. However, for politicians accustomed to winning the existential war by playing the 'adult in the room,' admitting that this capital is a scam is far more difficult than betraying their own constituents.
