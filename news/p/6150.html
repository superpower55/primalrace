万圣节片单里的女人The Women in the Halloween Watchlist

文娱 文化层 · 元暴力 Vulture ↗ 2026-10-31 § 链接
恐怖片单列了十几部，女人的处境只出现在一句黄腔里
A dozen horror picks, and a woman's situation appears only as a dirty joke.

万圣节看什么，Vulture 列了一串片单。整篇读下来，唯一一处真正写到女人处境的地方，是《Wicker》那条下面的一行小字：跟一个柳条做的鸡巴做爱会怎样，我们咨询了专家。

这就是全部。一部浪漫喜剧，卖点是 Olivia Colman 向 Peter Dinklage 订一个柳条编的丈夫，Alexander Skarsgård 从里面走出来。女人对现实里的浪漫选项不满意，于是去定做一个。这个设定本身已经把浪漫爱叙事的底牌翻开了——所谓 The One，是可以按需生产、按需交付的。可报道不聊这个，报道聊的是那根柳条的物理性能。女人的欲望在这里被处理成一个玩笑的入口，一个可以拿去做实验的装置。

再看整张单子。《Carrie》重拍，核心还是那个 abusive mother 和那个被羞辱的女孩；《The Gilded Age》第四季，Grover Cleveland 来吃午饭，百老汇的角儿们轮番登场，评论员自己都说，看这剧像腿上没摊一本 Playbill 就有点不对劲。这些作品里的女性表达是精致的、考究的、被服装置景包裹好的，但她们要解决的问题，还是嫁谁、被谁看、被谁的母亲毁掉。

片单里真正给女人留了位置的地方，是恐怖。恐怖片是少数允许女性发疯、流血、复仇的类型，所以它年年被推荐，年年被消费。但推荐语写的是导演们分享自己最爱的 gory movie，是 Edgar Wright 选《American Werewolf in London》，是某个导演回忆自己在镜头前被砍头。血浆是主角，恐惧是主角，那个被追、被砍、被附身的女人的身体，是道具。

Patrick Stewart 的讣闻插在中间，写得很动情，说他的声音让企业号显得更大。这话没错，但同一份刊物在同一个页面里，用一整段怀念一个男人的嗓音，用一行字调侃一根柳条鸡巴。版面本身就是一次投票，投给谁值得被认真谈论。

万圣节的恐怖是被允许的，因为它有出口，看完就关掉。真正没有出口的那部分，是片单里那些女人——被定做的丈夫、被毁掉的女儿、被砍头时尖叫的配角——她们的身体一直在被当作素材使用，而使用她们的人，从来不需要咨询专家。

Vulture's Halloween watchlist runs long. The one place it actually touches a woman's situation is a single line under Wicker: what would happen if you had sex with a wicker dick? We consulted an expert.

That's it. A romantic comedy whose premise is Olivia Colman commissioning Peter Dinklage to weave her a husband, and Alexander Skarsgård stepping out of it. A woman finds the real options for romance so limited that she orders one custom-made. The setup already flips over the romance narrative and shows its cards: The One can be manufactured and delivered to spec. The piece doesn't talk about that. It talks about the physics of the wicker.

Then look at the rest of the list. Carrie gets remade, and the core is still the abusive mother and the humiliated girl. The Gilded Age season four has Grover Cleveland coming to lunch and Broadway names filing through, and the critic admits it feels odd to watch without a Playbill in your lap. The feminine expression in these shows is exquisite, costumed, upholstered — but the problems the women are solving are still whom to marry, who is looking, and which mother ruins them.

Where the list genuinely makes room for women is horror. Horror is one of the few genres that lets women go mad, bleed, and take revenge, which is why it gets recommended every year and consumed every year. But the recommendations are directors sharing their favorite gory movies — Edgar Wright picking An American Werewolf in London, a director reminiscing about getting decapitated on camera. The blood is the subject. The fear is the subject. The body of the woman being chased, cut, and possessed is the prop.

Patrick Stewart's obituary sits in the middle, lovingly written, about how his voice made the Enterprise feel bigger. True enough. But the same publication, on the same page, spends a full passage mourning a man's voice and one line joking about a wicker dick. The layout is itself a vote on who gets taken seriously.

Halloween horror is permitted because it has an exit — you turn it off. What has no exit is the women inside the list: the husband made to order, the daughter destroyed, the extra screaming as she's cut down. Their bodies keep being used as material, and whoever is using them never needs to consult an expert.
