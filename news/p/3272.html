霍尔穆兹海峡的“希望”：一场关于定价权的男性博弈The 'Hope' of Hormuz: A Masculine Game of Pricing Power

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-05 § 链接
所谓的外交希望，只是两个男性权力中心在争夺资源定价权的口头博弈。
Diplomatic 'hope' is merely a verbal game between two masculine power centers fighting for resource pricing power.

看到“希望”这个词就得警惕。特朗普和伊朗政权在霍尔穆兹海峡问题上释放的所谓“积极信号”，本质上是一场关于定价权的 Existential War。一方想通过控制航道来收服务费，另一方则在宣称“我不让你们收，我们要收”。这种对话的底色是纯粹的 masculine-centric narrative：把全球能源命脉当成筹码，在两个权力中心之间进行切割。至于那些因为油价飙升而苦恼的家庭，在他们的叙事里只是背景板，是用来衡量博弈筹码的度量衡。

更讽刺的是，在同一份报道里，以色列军队正命令黎巴嫩村民撤离。这就是典型的元暴力 (meta violence) 运作方式：在最高层的“文明”外交辞令（如“外交努力”、“积极进展”）掩盖下，底层在承受最直接的 direct violence。这种结构极其稳定——权力中心在罗马谈协议，而士兵和平民在村庄里搬家。这种叙事上的脱节，让战争变成了某种权力者的“可能性艺术”，而受害者则被简化为统计数据。

所谓的 deal 只要还没落地，就只是另一种 weaponized expression。它通过制造短暂的“希望”来操纵全球市场，让油价在波动中完成一次资本的洗牌。这场博弈里没有公正的表达，只有谁能定义“事实”，谁能掌控“入口”。

Whenever you see the word 'hope' in these reports, be wary. The 'positive signals' from Trump and the Iranian regime regarding the Strait of Hormuz are essentially an Existential War over pricing power. One side wants to control the route to collect service fees; the other claims, 'I won't let you charge, we'll charge.' The backdrop here is a pure masculine-centric narrative: treating global energy arteries as bargaining chips to be carved up between two power centers. The families suffering from skyrocketing oil prices are mere background noise—metrics used to measure the value of the chips.

Even more cynical is the juxtaposition in the report: while the elites signal hope, the IDF orders Lebanese villagers to flee. This is the textbook operation of meta violence: direct violence at the bottom is masked by 'civilized' diplomatic rhetoric ('diplomatic efforts', 'positive progress') at the top. The structure is disturbingly stable—power centers negotiate in Rome while civilians pack their bags in villages. This narrative disconnect turns war into an 'art of manufacturing possibilities' for the powerful, while the victims are reduced to statistics.

Until a deal is actually implemented, it remains another form of weaponized expression. By manufacturing temporary 'hope', they manipulate global markets and allow capital to reshuffle through price volatility. There is no Just Expression here, only a struggle over who defines 'fact' and who controls the 'entry point'.
