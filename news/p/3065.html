权力在邮编之间搬家，但元暴力不换地址Power Moving Between Postcodes, Meta-Violence Staying Put

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-31 § 链接
资源分配的重新切分不等于权力的让渡，只是在不同邮编间分配共谋额度。
Redistributing resources is not ceding power; it is merely allocating complicity quotas across different postcodes.

John Healey 宣称要把钱和权力从威斯敏斯特“扩散”到全英国的每个邮编，这听起来像是一场 structural 层的资源再分配，但本质上是一次典型的叙事 weaponization。在政治博弈中，“扩散权力”往往是最高级的权力操纵——通过在局部制造“获得感”的幻觉，来掩盖核心解释权依然被男性中心叙事垄断的事实。

看看这份预算清单：给酒吧、俱乐部和live house减税，给市长分收入税。这不过是在既有的 masculine-centric 经济结构里进行微调。所谓的“稳定性”和“财政纪律”，其实就是一套共谋者的潜规则：只要不触碰最顶层的财富积累逻辑（比如财富税和房产税的实质性改革），就可以在边缘地带通过一些碎片化的福利来换取底层对体制的顺从。

真正的权力 let-go 应该是让那些被结构性剥夺的群体——尤其是那些在生育、养育中被母职惩罚 (Motherhood Penalty) 挤出公共空间的女性，以及被殖民逻辑定义为弱势的原初种族——获得定义预算优先级的权力。但 Healey 的预算案里没有提到这些。他承诺的是“稳定”，而对于被压迫者来说，所谓的“稳定”往往就是 structural violence 的另一种说法。

权力并没有离开威斯敏斯特，它只是学会了如何通过更精巧的邮编分发，让共谋者们觉得这次分赃更公平了。

John Healey claims to "spread money and power" from Westminster to every postcode in Britain. This sounds like a shift in structural layer, but it is a classic weaponization of narrative. In political gaming, "spreading power" is often the most sophisticated form of control—creating an illusion of "gain" in the periphery to mask the fact that the core interpretative power remains monopolized by the masculine-centric narrative.

Look at the budget checklist: tax cuts for pubs, clubs, and music venues; sharing income tax with mayors. This is merely fine-tuning within the existing masculine-centric economic structure. The so-called "stability" and "fiscal discipline" are simply the unspoken rules of the complicity: as long as the top-tier wealth accumulation logic (such as substantive wealth and property tax reform) remains untouched, the system can buy compliance through fragmented benefits at the margins.

True power let-go would mean giving the structurally deprived—especially women pushed out of public spaces by the Motherhood Penalty and the Primal Race defined as weak by colonial logic—the power to define budgetary priorities. But Healey’s plan mentions none of this. He promises "stability," and for the oppressed, "stability" is often just another name for structural violence.

Power hasn't left Westminster; it has simply learned to distribute itself more cleverly across postcodes, making the co-conspirators feel that the spoils are being shared more fairly this time.
