彼得·蒂尔的阿根廷实验室：末日生存还是权力殖民？Peter Thiel's Argentina Lab: Apocalypse Prep or Power Colonization?

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-03 § 链接
所谓的“技术自由意志主义”本质上是最高阶的权力共谋。
So-called 'techno-libertarianism' is essentially the highest form of power complicity.

彼得·蒂尔（Peter Thiel）搬到布宜诺斯艾利斯，媒体在讨论他是为了躲避第三次世界大战的“末日准备”，还是为了商业利益的“务实选择”。这种二元对立本身就是个 scam。对于一个掌控 Palantir 这种情报机器的亿万富翁来说，生存与权力从来不是两个选项，而是同一枚硬币的两面。

蒂尔在阿根廷的布局是典型的“表达武器化”。他不需要公开游说，只需要通过与米莱（Milei）这种极端右翼政客的 closed-door meetings，将自己的 techno-libertarian 愿景直接转化为 structural violence。所谓的“super RIGI”投资体制——低税率、30年监管豁免，以及将公民个人数据交给情报部门的法令，这就是在为技术寡头量身定制的“殖民特区”。

这里发生了一场极其恶心的共谋：米莱提供了国家机器的准入权，而蒂尔提供了技术统治的蓝图。他们试图将整个阿根廷变成一个 laboratory，实验如何用 AI 代理、非人类公司以及半自治的“创业城市”来消解国家的监管。这根本不是什么自由意志主义，而是试图通过夺取解释权，重新定义什么是“法律”和“主权”。

最讽刺的是，这种“实验室”的底层逻辑依然是元暴力（meta violence）。它将整个国家的公民客体化为数据点，将土地视为可被资本随意切割的资产。当一个外国投资者能无视国会质询、在权力中心自由穿梭时，这个国家的民主程序已经成了掩盖权力转移的文化伪装。蒂尔不是在躲避末日，他是在亲手制造一个只有他这种等级的人才能生存的、由算法和情报掌控的新世界。

The media is debating whether Peter Thiel's move to Buenos Aires is 'apocalypse prep' for WWIII or 'pure pragmatism' for business. This dichotomy is a scam. For a billionaire controlling an intelligence machine like Palantir, survival and power are never separate options; they are two sides of the same coin.

Thiel's layout in Argentina is a textbook case of the weaponisation of expression. He doesn't need public lobbying; he simply translates his techno-libertarian vision into structural violence through closed-door meetings with far-right politicians like Milei. The 'super RIGI' regime—minimal taxes, 30-year regulatory guarantees, and decrees handing citizen data to intelligence services—is a 'colonial zone' custom-built for tech oligarchs.

What we see here is a disgusting complicity: Milei provides access to the state machine, and Thiel provides the blueprint for technocracy. They are attempting to turn Argentina into a laboratory to experiment with AI agents, non-human corporations, and semi-autonomous 'startup cities' to dissolve state regulation. This isn't libertarianism; it's an attempt to seize the power of interpretation and redefine 'law' and 'sovereignty'.

Most ironically, the underlying logic of this 'laboratory' remains meta-violence. It objectifies the nation's citizens into data points and treats land as an asset to be sliced by capital. When a foreign investor can ignore congressional summons while strolling through the corridors of power, the country's democratic process becomes mere cultural violence—a facade masking the transfer of power. Thiel isn't hiding from the apocalypse; he is actively engineering a new world where only his class survives, governed by algorithms and intelligence.
