特朗普及普京：两个自恋者的共谋与互骗Trump and Putin: The Complicity and Mutual Scam of Two Narcissists

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-24 § 链接
所有的“强人政治”本质上都是一种自恋的 scam，而权力则是其唯一的通货。
All 'Strongman Politics' is essentially a narcissist's scam, with power as its only currency.

这篇文章讨论的是 Trump 是否是俄罗斯的 asset，但这个问题本身就落入了对方的叙事陷阱。在元暴力（meta violence）的逻辑里，我们习惯于在“忠诚”或“背叛”这种道德词汇中寻找答案，但对于 Trump 这种生物来说，不存在所谓的国家忠诚，他唯一的忠诚对象是自己的存在感。他与 Putin 的关系不是特工与上线，而是一场关于“强人”人设的共谋（complicity）。

Trump 认为自己可以通过所谓的 rapport 操纵 Putin，这是一种典型的自恋错觉。他将国际政治简化为两个 alpha male 的私人博弈，试图通过扮演对方认可的“强人”角色来换取短期利益。而 Putin 则利用这种认知入口，将 Trump 视为一个可以被轻易操纵的工具。这种博弈没有公正的表达（just expressions），只有谁更能把对方当成客体（object）来消费。

最令人作呕的 structural violence 在于，这场两个男人的权力游戏，其代价是由乌克兰的儿童诊所和像 Olena Palsh 这样的个体来支付的。当 Trump 在椭圆形办公室里通过羞辱 Zelenskyy 来确立自己的权力位阶时，他实际上是在执行一种典型的男性中心叙事：弱者必须服从强者的定义，而事实（fact）在权力面前毫无价值。这就是 weaponized expression 的极致——将他国的生存权转化为自己讨价还价的筹码，甚至要求以矿产资源作为“还款”。

所谓的“背叛”其实是这套逻辑的必然结果。当两个自恋者发现对方无法提供预期的情绪价值或权力回报时，他们会迅速从共谋转向厌恶。这场战争之所以在 Putin 那里成了“无法赢但不能停”的死循环，是因为在元暴力的世界里，承认失败意味着主体性的死亡。对他而言，继续消耗生命比承认错误更符合他的“最优解表达”。

The article asks if Trump is a Russian asset, but this question falls into a narrative trap. In the logic of meta violence, we tend to seek answers in moral terms like 'loyalty' or 'betrayal.' But for a creature like Trump, national loyalty is non-existent; his only loyalty is to his own sense of existence. His relationship with Putin is not that of an agent and a handler, but a complicity built on the 'strongman' persona.

Trump believes he can manipulate Putin through 'rapport,' a classic narcissistic delusion. He simplifies international politics into a private game between two alpha males, attempting to trade in a role the other recognizes for short-term gains. Putin, meanwhile, uses this cognitive entry point to treat Trump as a tool to be manipulated. There are no just expressions here, only a competition over who can better objectify the other.

The most visceral structural violence is that the cost of this power game between two men is paid by Ukrainian children's clinics and individuals like Olena Palsh. When Trump establishes his power hierarchy by humiliating Zelenskyy in the Oval Office, he is executing a masculine-centric narrative: the weak must submit to the strong's definition, and facts are worthless in the face of power. This is the peak of weaponized expression—converting another nation's right to exist into a bargaining chip, even demanding mineral wealth as 'repayment.'

This 'betrayal' is the inevitable outcome. When two narcissists find that the other cannot provide the expected emotional value or power return, they pivot from complicity to disgust. The war remains a futile loop for Putin because, in the world of meta violence, admitting defeat is the death of subjectivity. For him, consuming lives is a more viable 'optimal expression' than admitting a mistake.
