道歉是廉价的，共谋才是昂贵的Apologies are Cheap, Complicity is Expensive

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-06 § 链接
权力者的道歉不是为了反思，而是为了在共谋圈内完成一次低成本的续约。
A powerful man's apology is not for reflection, but a low-cost contract renewal within the circle of complicity.

这是一场典型的、极其拙劣的权力表演。Gianni Infantino 试图通过几封书面道歉信来抹平他私自出卖世界杯商业权益的傲慢，但真正的戏剧点不在于他是否错了，而在于那群所谓的“领导团队”如何迅速地在拉巴特达成共识，宣布对他保持“全力支持”。

在 FIFA 这种组织里，不存在真正的“错误”，只存在“被泄露的计划”。Infantino 所谓的道歉，本质上是对其操作流程不够隐秘的懊悔，而非对权力滥用的反省。这种叙事通过将结构性危机简化为“沟通不足”和“流程错误”，成功地将一场治理危机降格为一次管理上的小瑕疵。这就是典型的文化暴力：用一套官僚主义的术语，让权力的掠夺看起来像是一次由于“效率追求”而产生的误会。

最讽刺的细节是，为了展示团结，他们选择在女足非洲杯的比赛现场共同亮相。这种安排极其恶心——他们把女性运动员的身体和比赛作为政治掩体，利用“发展足球”这种正确且温情的叙事来掩盖高层权力分赃的丑陋。女性在这里再次成为了被武器化的工具，被用来为一个男性中心叙事的权力集团刷存在感和正当性。

所谓的“不再容忍对治理的攻击”，其实就是一份权力者的通牒：我已经给了你们面子（道歉），现在请你们闭嘴并继续共谋。只要这个共谋环不被打破，Infantino 依然是那个定义事实的人。

This is a textbook, clumsy performance of power. Gianni Infantino attempts to smooth over his arrogance in selling off World Cup commercial rights with a few written apologies, but the real drama isn't whether he was wrong—it's how his 'leadership team' rapidly converged in Rabat to declare their 'full support.'

Within FIFA, there are no true 'mistakes,' only 'leaked plans.' Infantino's apology is essentially a regret that his process wasn't discreet enough, not a reflection on the abuse of power. By framing a structural crisis as 'lack of consultation' and 'procedural errors,' they successfully downgrade a governance failure into a managerial glitch. This is a classic manifestation of cultural violence: using bureaucratic jargon to make the predation of power look like a misunderstanding driven by 'efficiency.'

The most cynical detail is their decision to appear together at a Women's Africa Cup of Nations match to show solidarity. It is repulsive—they use the bodies and games of female athletes as political shields, weaponizing the 'development of the game' narrative to mask the ugliness of a power-sharing deal among men. Once again, women are treated as tools for a masculine-centric narrative to perform legitimacy.

The claim that FIFA 'will no longer tolerate attacks' on its governance is simply an ultimatum from the powerful: I have given you the courtesy of an apology; now shut up and resume your complicity. As long as this circle of complicity remains intact, Infantino remains the one who defines what 'truth' is.
