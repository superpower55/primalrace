纯洁的小丑与消失的受害者The Pure Clown and the Vanishing Victim

哲学 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-07 § 链接
没有受害者的喜剧是最高级的共谋，它通过维持现状来换取安全感。
Comedy without victims is the ultimate complicity, trading safety for the maintenance of the status quo.

Jimmy Cricket 的讣告读起来像是一篇关于“好人”的圣徒传。一个穿着破旧礼服、戴着歪帽子、毫无攻击性且“没有受害者” (no victim) 的小丑。在现代喜剧追求解构、讽刺和政治正确地挖掘结构性暴力时，这种“纯洁”的幽默被包装成了一种稀缺的温情。

但从加尔通的暴力三角来看，这种“没有受害者”的表达本身就是一种极其高效的 cultural violence。当一个喜剧演员宣布自己没有 agenda，只为了让人们发笑时，他实际上是在执行一个巨大的共谋 (complicity)：他默认了当前这个世界的秩序是无需被质疑的，默认了所有观众在笑声中处于一个和谐的共识之中。这种“纯洁”本质上是对元暴力 (meta violence) 的绝对顺从——它不触碰权力，不挑战结构，不揭露那些在笑声之外被噤声的痛苦。

一个“毫无野心”的小丑，其实是这个系统最完美的润滑剂。他用一种 an-cynical 的姿态，给观众提供了一种廉价的心理按摩，让人们相信世界依然可以如此单纯。这种表达的最优解是：通过扮演一个无害的客体，换取在主流电视荧幕和皇家演出 (Royal Variety Performance) 上的入场券。他赢得了名声和教皇的爵位，而代价是主体性的完全让渡给那个维持现状的权力结构。

我们不需要一个完美的、纯洁的小丑，我们需要的是那些敢于让观众在笑声中感到不安的人。因为真正的公正表达，从来不会在没有任何人感到被冒犯的情况下达成。

The obituary of Jimmy Cricket reads like a hagiography of a 'good man.' A clown in tattered evening dress and a crooked hat, entirely non-aggressive and, crucially, with 'no victim.' While modern comedy strives for deconstruction, satire, and the political correctness of exposing structural violence, this 'pure' humor is framed as a rare form of warmth.

However, through the lens of the Violence Triangle, this 'victimless' expression is itself a highly efficient form of cultural violence. When a comedian declares they have no agenda and only wish to make people laugh, they are engaging in a massive complicity: they assume the current world order is beyond question and that the audience exists in a harmonious consensus. This 'purity' is essentially an absolute submission to meta violence—it does not touch power, challenge structures, or reveal the agonies silenced beyond the laughter.

An 'ambitionless' clown is the perfect lubricant for the system. By performing as a harmless object, he secures an entry ticket to mainstream television and the Royal Variety Performance. He won fame and a papal knighthood, but the price was the complete surrender of his subjectivity to the power structure that maintains the status quo.

We do not need a perfect, pure clown; we need those who dare to make the audience feel uneasy through laughter. Because a truly Just Expression is never achieved without someone feeling offended.
