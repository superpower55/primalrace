减肥药的快感与肉体的账单The Euphoria of Weight-Loss Drugs and the Biological Bill

性别 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-11 § 链接
用生物黑客手段强行修改欲望，最终会由肉体支付利息。
Using bio-hacking to force-modify desire eventually leads to a debt paid by the flesh.

Ozempic 这类 GLP-1 药物的流行，本质上是一场关于“表达”的生物性作弊。它通过模拟激素，在生物墙上强行开了一道口子，把原本复杂的食欲博弈简化成了单一的化学指令：不饿。这种“快捷方式”让无数人以为拿到了身体的定价权，但实际上，这只是在透支未来的生命额度。

文章里提到的掉发、皮肤松弛、肌肉流失，其实就是 Violence = Potential − Actual 公式在肉体上的体现。当你用药物强行压制食欲，你的身体并没有在“健康地减重”，而是在经历一场人为制造的饥荒。这种 Structural Violence 发生在细胞层面：身体在缺乏营养支撑的情况下，不得不拆东墙补西墙，优先牺牲肌肉和胶原蛋白来维持基本运转。结果就是，你赢得了体重秤上的数字，却输掉了主体性的生命质量。

最讽刺的共谋发生在审美工业与医药资本之间。药物让脸塌了，于是医美产业接手，通过填充剂把失去的脂肪“补”回来。这是一个完美的商业闭环：医药公司负责摧毁你的自然支撑系统，医美公司负责向你售卖昂贵的替代品。在这个过程中，女性的身体再次成为了被定义、被消费的客体——先被要求“瘦”，再被要求“紧致”，最后在药物和针剂的循环中，彻底失去了对自身生物节奏的掌控。

不要被“长期用药就像高血压药一样正常”这种叙事给骗了。高血压药是在修复一个失衡的系统，而减肥药在很多时候是在强行覆盖一个生物信号。当你停止用药，身体会以更猛烈的反弹来夺回它失去的能量。这种博弈没有真正的最优解，除非你意识到：试图通过化学手段抹除欲望，本身就是一种对主体性的自我阉割。

The popularity of GLP-1 drugs like Ozempic is essentially a biological cheat code for 'expression'. By mimicking hormones, they force a breach in the Biological Wall, simplifying the complex game of appetite into a single chemical command: no hunger. This 'shortcut' makes many believe they've gained pricing power over their bodies, but in reality, they are just spending their future biological credit.

The hair loss, sagging skin, and muscle wasting mentioned in the article are the physical manifestations of the formula Violence = Potential − Actual. When you forcibly suppress appetite, your body isn't 'losing weight healthily'; it's experiencing a man-made famine. This is Structural Violence at the cellular level: without nutritional support, the body must cannibalize itself, sacrificing muscle and collagen to maintain basic functions. The result is a victory on the scale, but a loss in the quality of existential existence.

The most cynical complicity occurs between the aesthetic industry and pharmaceutical capital. The drugs make the face sag, and the aesthetic industry steps in to 'fill' the lost fat with injectables. It's a perfect commercial loop: pharma destroys your natural support system, and med-spas sell you expensive replacements. In this process, the female body is once again a colonized object—first demanded to be 'thin', then demanded to be 'firm', eventually losing all control over its own biological rhythm in a cycle of injections.

Don't be fooled by the narrative that 'long-term use is as normal as blood pressure medication'. Blood pressure meds repair an imbalanced system; weight-loss drugs often just overwrite a biological signal. When you stop, the body reclaims its energy with a violent rebound. There is no true optimal expression here, unless you realize that attempting to erase desire through chemistry is, in itself, a form of self-castration of subjectivity.
