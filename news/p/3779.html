资本的食尸鬼与被吞噬的“品味”The Capital Ghoul and the Devoured 'Taste'

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-13 § 链接
奢侈品被低端资本收购，本质是定价权的暴力洗牌。
The acquisition of luxury by low-end capital is a violent reshuffling of pricing power.

Mike Ashley 的 Frasers Group 再次上演了资本市场的“食尸鬼”戏码：在 Harvey Nichols 陷入 administration 的至暗时刻精准入场。这不是什么救世主降临，而是一次典型的对认知入口的暴力接管。Harvey Nichols 曾是 90 年代 chic 的旗手，代表的是一种被精心构建的、具有排他性的 luxury 审美。而 Ashley 的底色是 Sports Direct——一个靠极致性价比和低端扩张起家的商业机器。

这场收购最残酷的部分在于所谓的“rationalisation”（合理化）。Ashley 打算将大部分门店重塑为 House of Fraser 或 Flannels。这意味着，原本作为阶级筛选器的“品味”被直接降维打击，转化为一个规模化的、可复制的商业 ecosystem。当一个定义 luxury 的符号被一个定义 mass-market 的资本掌控，原本的审美定价权就消失了。这是一种 structural violence：通过操纵资本流向，强行抹除那些不符合其“可持续”逻辑的文化表达。

有趣的是，CEO Julia Goddard 还在用“operational efficiency”这种典型的 corporate-speak 进行自我催眠。在资本的博弈中，这种表达是典型的“假.最优解”：通过扮演一个配合的管理者来换取短期的职位存续，代价是主体性的死亡。对于 Harvey Nichols 来说，它不再是一个文化地标，而成了 Ashley 扩充奢侈品版图的一块血肉。这场战争没有赢家，只有被吞噬的符号和变得更加臃肿的资本怪物。

Mike Ashley’s Frasers Group has once again played the 'ghoul' of the capital market, swooping in exactly when Harvey Nichols hit the bottom of administration. This is no rescue mission; it is a textbook violent takeover of a cognitive entry point. Harvey Nichols was once the flag-bearer of 90s chic, representing a meticulously constructed, exclusive luxury aesthetic. Ashley’s DNA, however, is Sports Direct—a commercial machine built on extreme cost-efficiency and low-end expansion.

The most brutal part of this deal is the so-called 'rationalisation.' Ashley intends to rebrand most stores as House of Fraser or Flannels. This means 'taste,' which once functioned as a class filter, is being downgraded and converted into a scalable, replicable commercial ecosystem. When the symbol that defines luxury is controlled by capital that defines the mass-market, the original pricing power of aesthetics vanishes. This is structural violence: using capital flows to forcibly erase cultural expressions that don't fit a specific 'sustainable' logic.

It is amusing to see CEO Julia Goddard still using corporate-speak like 'operational efficiency' to hypnotize herself. In this game of power, such expression is a 'fake optimal solution': playing the role of a compliant manager to secure short-term survival at the cost of the death of subjectivity. For Harvey Nichols, it is no longer a cultural landmark, but merely a piece of flesh for Ashley to expand his luxury portfolio. There are no winners here, only devoured symbols and an ever-bloating capital monster.
