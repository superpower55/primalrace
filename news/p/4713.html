反文化是骗局，而“知识分子”是最好的掩体Counterculture is a Scam, and 'Intellectualism' is the Perfect Cover

哲学 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-02 § 链接
当表达被量化为指标，所有的“地下”最终都只是主流叙事的预备役。
When expression is quantified by metrics, the 'underground' is merely a reserve army for the mainstream.

Sofie Royer 是一个典型的、被高度美化了的“知识分子”艺人。她的表达充满了 Jstor 数据库、塔可夫斯基和达达主义。在主流媒体看来，这叫 erudition（博学），但在我看来，这不过是另一种形式的 identity politics——一种通过堆砌文化符号来建立阶级壁垒的表达。当她把“引用”称为最酷的事情时，她实际上是在通过定义一套只有特定人群才能解码的“密语”，来获得一种精神上的定价权。

有趣的是，她对“女性专属 DJ 之夜”的反感，以及她对“非政治化结构”的向往，恰恰暴露了她的认知盲区。她试图在艺术中追求一种 a-political 的纯粹，但这本身就是一种极大的特权。只有那些在结构层（structural layer）不被压迫的人，才有资格要求“去政治化”。对于原初种族而言，存在本身就是政治。她认为性别和血统不应成为 USP（独特销售主张），但她忘了，她能够如此轻盈地讨论这个问题，正是因为她已经内化了某种精英主义的男性中心叙事——即认为“才华”可以脱离“权力结构”而独立存在。

她感叹 countercultural（反文化）注定失败，看着 Boiler Room 被资本规模化而感到 bummer。这太天真了。反文化从来就不是为了赢，而是一场被精心包装的认知入口游戏。资本并不害怕反文化，资本最擅长的是将反文化 weaponized（武器化），将其转化为一种可消费的“酷”标签。当她唱着自己“太 pop 无法进入艺术圈，太 indie 无法进入主流”时，她其实已经找到了她的最优解表达：扮演一个“被低估的才女”。

这种扮演是安全的。因为在这种叙事里，她不需要面对真正的存在性战争，只需要在维也纳的公寓里抽着细烟，在“艺术”与“商业”的缝隙中维持一种优雅的挫败感。真正的反文化应该是对元暴力的拆解，而不是在 35mm 胶片和 4K 修复的隐喻里，给自己的心找一个 audition 的机会。

Sofie Royer is a textbook example of the glamorized 'intellectual' artist. Her expression is a dense collage of Jstor databases, Tarkovsky, and Dadaism. To the mainstream media, this is erudition; to me, it is simply another form of identity politics—using cultural symbols to construct class barriers. By calling 'referencing' the coolest thing one can do, she is essentially establishing a pricing power over meaning through a 'secret code' accessible only to a specific elite.

Her distaste for female-only DJ nights and her longing for an 'apolitical structure' reveal a profound cognitive blind spot. The desire for a-political purity is a luxury. Only those who are not oppressed at the structural layer have the privilege to demand the removal of politics from art. For the Primal Race, existence itself is political. She argues that gender and heritage shouldn't be a USP, forgetting that her ability to make this argument stems from an internalized masculine-centric narrative—the delusion that 'talent' can exist independently of power structures.

She laments that everything countercultural is doomed, feeling a 'bummer' as Boiler Room scales up via capital. This is naive. Counterculture was never meant to 'win'; it is a weaponized cognitive entry point. Capital doesn't fear the underground; it consumes it, turning rebellion into a consumable label of 'cool.' When she sings about being 'too pop for the art world, too indie for the mainstream,' she has found her optimal expression: playing the role of the 'underrated genius.'

This performance is safe. It allows her to avoid the actual existential war, opting instead to maintain an elegant sense of failure while smoking slim cigarettes in a Vienna apartment, treating her heart as a script for an audition. True counterculture is the dismantling of meta-violence, not searching for a leading part within the metaphors of 35mm film and 4K restoration.
