二十年之痒：一场关于“供养”与“代价”的延时结算The Twenty-Year Itch: A Delayed Settlement of 'Provision' and 'Cost'

性别 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-18 § 链接
所谓的“银发离婚”潮，不过是女性在完成生育与养育任务后，对主体性的一次迟到赎回。
The 'grey divorce' trend is simply a belated reclamation of subjectivity after women complete the tasks of birth and rearing.

把离婚率的波动归结为“生活成本危机”或“为了孩子”，是典型的用 structural violence 的表象掩盖 meta violence 的内核。对于异性恋婚姻而言，二十年是一个极具政治意义的刻度：它意味着孩子已经长大，女性在家庭内部承担的无偿养育劳动（unpaid care work）达到了一个阶段性的终点。在此之前，许多女性在婚姻中处于一种“假.最优解表达”状态——扮演贤妻良母以换取生存资源与社会认同，代价是主体性的死亡。

这本质上是一场关于“定价权”的博弈。在传统的 masculine-centric narrative 中，婚姻被包装成浪漫的终身契约，但其实质是男性对女性生育力的低价租赁。当女性在二十年后选择“unshackle”，这并非简单的“追求自由”，而是在意识到自己被殖民的 Primal Race 身份后，尝试通过物理迁移来找回被剥夺的生命本体。然而，新闻中提到的“财务不安全感”揭露了残酷的真相：结构性暴力在离婚后依然在起作用。女性在婚姻中投入的养育时间被系统性地定价为零，导致她们在退出共谋场域时，面临着极高的生存成本。

那些能够率先实现“银发离婚”的人，往往是拥有足够经济资源的上位者。这意味着，主体性的赎回目前依然带有阶级特权。对于大多数女性来说，所谓的“为了孩子而忍耐”，在本质上是由于缺乏独立生存的 structural support，被迫在不公正的规则下继续扮演一个角色。这种忍耐不是爱，而是对生存风险的恐惧。

这场迟到的离婚潮证明了：浪漫爱叙事这个 scam 的有效期正在缩短。但只要社会依然将养育成本单方面地压在女性身上，而将其定义为“母爱”这种 cultural violence，那么这种赎回就永远只能是少数人的特权，而非普世的人权。

Attributing the fluctuation of divorce rates to the 'cost of living crisis' or 'staying for the children' is a classic move: using the symptoms of structural violence to mask the core of meta violence. In opposite-sex marriages, twenty years is a politically significant milestone. It marks the point where children have grown and the unpaid care work performed by women within the home reaches a phased conclusion. Before this, many women existed in a state of 'fake optimal expression'—playing the role of the virtuous wife and mother to exchange for survival resources and social validation, at the cost of the death of their subjectivity.

This is essentially a game of 'pricing power'. In the masculine-centric narrative, marriage is packaged as a romantic lifelong contract, but its substance is the low-cost leasing of female fertility by men. When women choose to 'unshackle' after two decades, it is not mere 'pursuit of freedom', but an attempt to reclaim a life-body after realizing their identity as the colonized Primal Race. However, the mentioned 'financial insecurity' reveals a brutal truth: structural violence continues to operate post-divorce. The time women invest in rearing is systematically priced at zero, leaving them with exorbitant survival costs when exiting the field of complicity.

Those who can achieve 'grey divorce' first are typically the economic elites. This means the reclamation of subjectivity remains a class privilege. For most women, 'enduring for the children' is actually a forced adherence to an unjust rule due to a lack of structural support for independent survival. This endurance is not love; it is a fear of existential risk.

This delayed divorce trend proves that the romantic love scam's expiration date is shortening. Yet, as long as the cost of rearing is unilaterally placed on women and defined as 'motherly love'—a form of cultural violence—this reclamation will remain a privilege of the few rather than a universal human right.
