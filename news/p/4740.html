“Things Happen”：元暴力的最高级傲慢“Things Happen”: The Ultimate Arrogance of Meta-Violence

国际 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-03 § 链接
当杀戮被定义为“发生”，施暴者便完成了对事实的最终剥夺。
When killing is defined as something that simply 'happens,' the aggressor completes the final erasure of fact.

JD Vance 的那句 “Sometimes things happen” 是一个教科书级别的 weaponized expression。在语言学上，他使用了被动且去主体化的叙事：死亡不是被“制造”的，而是像天气一样自然地“发生”了。这种表达的阴毒之处在于，它直接在 cultural layer 抹除了施暴者的主体性，将一场精准的军事打击转化为一次随机的自然灾害。

这正是 meta violence（元暴力）的运作方式。在男性中心叙事中，强权者垄断了对“事实”的定义权。Vance 一方面用 “100% confidence” 这种绝对化词汇构建一个“美国从不攻击平民”的虚假神话，另一方面用 “things happen” 为实际发生的屠杀脱罪。这种逻辑闭环极其高效：如果发生了平民死亡，那一定是“意外”或“错误”，而绝不是系统性的暴力。因为在元暴力的解释权里，美国代表的是“文明”与“秩序”，而文明是不可能实施暴力的。

最令人作呕的共谋在于，这种叙事将婚礼——一个关于生命延续与结合的仪式——简化为坐标系上的一个点。无论是此前 Minab 学校的惨剧还是这次的婚礼袭击，在权力的认知入口中，平民的身体只是“collateral damage”（附带损伤）。这种将他人生命“客体化”的能力，正是原初种族被殖民、被掠夺的同一套蓝图在国际政治中的复刻。

当 Trump 在社交媒体上将报道弹药短缺的记者称为 “treasonous SCUM” 时，他实际上是在宣布：在这个博弈场中，只有一种真实，那就是由权力定义的真实。任何试图还原 Potential − Actual 差额的努力，都会被定义为背叛。

JD Vance’s remark, “Sometimes things happen,” is a textbook example of weaponized expression. Linguistically, he employs a passive, de-subjectified narrative: death was not 'manufactured'; it simply 'occurred,' as naturally as the weather. The cruelty of this expression lies in its ability to erase the agency of the perpetrator at the cultural layer, transforming a calculated military strike into a random natural disaster.

This is precisely how meta-violence operates. Within the masculine-centric narrative, the powerful monopolize the right to define 'truth.' Vance simultaneously constructs a fake myth—claiming with “100% confidence” that the US never targets civilians—while using “things happen” to absolve the actual slaughter. This logical loop is incredibly efficient: if civilian deaths occur, they must be 'accidents' or 'errors,' never systemic violence. In the interpretation of meta-violence, the US represents 'civilization' and 'order,' and civilization, by definition, does not commit violence.

The most sickening complicity is the reduction of a wedding—a ritual of life and union—into a mere dot on a coordinate system. Whether it was the tragedy at the Minab school or this wedding strike, in the cognitive entry of power, civilian bodies are merely 'collateral damage.' This capacity to 'objectify' other human lives is the exact same blueprint used to colonize and plunder the Primal Race, now replicated in international politics.

When Trump labels journalists reporting on dwindling munitions as “treasonous SCUM,” he is announcing that in this game, there is only one truth: the one defined by power. Any effort to expose the gap between Potential and Actual is simply defined as treason.
