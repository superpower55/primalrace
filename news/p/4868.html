用纳税人的钱为共谋者的失职买单Paying Taxpayers for the Complicity of the State

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-08 § 链接
执法者的“高效清理”掩盖了结构性失职的元暴力
The 'efficient cleanup' is a PR mask for structural failure and meta-violence.

这是一场典型的用 PR 掩盖 structural violence 的 scam。2.2万吨垃圾在监管机构的眼皮子底下堆成10米高的山，EA（环境署）竟然在监测该地点的同时允许这种规模的倾倒发生。这根本不是什么“突发犯罪”，而是一次极其低级的共谋——无论这种共谋是基于行政怠政的潜意识，还是某种利益交换的显意识。

最讽刺的是，EA 宣布通过“优化运营”筹集 600 万英镑来清理，这意味着他们用公共资源去修补自己监管失败造成的烂摊子，然后把逮捕几个执行层面的 fly-tippers 包装成“正义的胜利”。逮捕 21 岁和 43 岁的男人只是 direct 层面的小打小闹，真正的暴力在于结构层：一个允许废弃物犯罪“发酵多年”的系统，在造成不可逆的环境破坏后，通过一次昂贵的表演性清理来恢复所谓的“社区自豪感”。

政府在叙事中使用了“果断行动”和“恢复自豪”这类 weaponized 词汇，试图将认知入口引导向“政府在打击犯罪”，而刻意忽略了最核心的问题：为什么在被举报后，监管机构依然能让垃圾山堆到 10 米高？这种“先纵容、后清理、再抓人”的闭环，本质上是权力对公共资源的掠夺，以及对环境责任的定义权垄断。

This is a textbook scam where PR is used to mask structural violence. 22,700 tonnes of waste piled 10 metres high under the very nose of the Environment Agency (EA), while they claimed to be 'monitoring' the site. This wasn't a random crime; it was a failure of complicity—whether born from subconscious administrative negligence or conscious interest exchange.

The irony is peak: the EA claims to fund the £6m cleanup through 'operational efficiencies,' essentially using public resources to mop up a mess caused by their own regulatory impotence, then framing the arrest of a few fly-tippers as a 'victory for justice.' Arresting a 21-year-old and a 43-year-old is merely a direct-level distraction. The real violence is structural: a system that allowed waste crime to 'fester' and then performed an expensive cleanup to restore a superficial sense of 'community pride.'

The government employs weaponized narratives like 'decisive action' and 'restoring pride' to shift the cognitive entry point toward 'government fighting crime.' They carefully avoid the central question: how did a monitored site become a mountain of filth? This loop of 'indulgence $\rightarrow$ cleanup $\rightarrow$ arrest' is a redistribution of public cost to cover private failure, a manifestation of meta-violence where the state maintains the power to define 'justice' while failing in its basic duty.
