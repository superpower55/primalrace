边境色情：当棕色肉体被加工成右翼的数字养料Border Porn: When Brown Bodies are Processed into Right-Wing Digital Feed

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-04 § 链接
边境不仅是地理分界，更是将人类客体化为“内容”的武器化剧场。
Borders are not just geographical lines, but weaponised theaters that objectify humans into 'content'.

Ceuta 的这场闹剧，本质上是一次精准的表达武器化 (Weaponisation of Expression) 演习。那些所谓的“公民记者”和右翼政客，并不是在报道危机，而是在制造一种“边境色情” (Border Porn)。他们通过远距离拍摄，将成千上万个具体的个体抹除，将其加工成一个面目模糊的、棕色的“肉体大块”。在这个认知入口里，会计师、外科医生与难民没有区别，他们都被简化为一种威胁符号，用来喂养白人至上主义的末日幻想。

这正是典型的文化暴力 (Cultural Violence) 在运作：通过定义什么是“野蛮的他者”，让随后的结构性暴力——比如加强边境围栏、吊销申根资格、甚至未来的合法屠杀——在观众眼中显得“自然”且“正义”。当特朗普和马斯克在社交媒体上转发这些片段时，他们不是在讨论政策，而是在通过掌控解释权来夺取“什么是事实”的定义权。在这种叙事中，棕色身体不再是人，而是一个可被消费的数字资产，是用来巩固白人基督教欧洲认同的祭品。

最令人作呕的共谋 (Complicity) 来自于那些感到“庆幸”的左翼。当他们因为移民们“回去了”而松一口气时，他们已经潜意识里接受了右翼设定的博弈规则：即“移民本身就是一个问题”。这种 tacit acceptance 是元暴力 (Meta Violence) 的胜利——它让人们忘记了，真正的暴力不是人们在移动，而是那个将人类分为“纯血”与“野蛮”的等级系统。如果左翼不能在存在性战争中拿回定义权，那么下一次 Ceuta 就不再是直播秀，而会变成一场真实的、被数字化美化的屠杀。

The farce at Ceuta is essentially a precision exercise in the Weaponisation of Expression. Those self-styled 'citizen journalists' and right-wing politicians aren't reporting a crisis; they are producing 'Border Porn'. By filming from a distance, they erase individual identities and process thousands of humans into a singular, blurred mass of 'brown bodies'. Within this cognitive entry point, accountants and surgeons are indistinguishable from refugees; all are reduced to a symbol of threat to feed the apocalyptic fantasies of white supremacy.

This is Cultural Violence in its purest form: by defining the 'savage other', the subsequent structural violence—reinforced fences, Schengen suspensions, or even future legalized massacres—appears 'natural' and 'just' to the audience. When Trump and Musk amplify these clips, they aren't debating policy; they are seizing the power of interpretation to manufacture 'what is fact'. In this narrative, brown bodies are no longer humans, but digital assets used to consolidate the imagined identity of a white Christian Europe.

The most sickening complicity comes from the left, who feel 'relieved' that the migrants returned. In that relief, they have subconsciously accepted the right-wing's game: the trope that migration itself is a prima facie problem. This tacit acceptance is a victory for Meta Violence—it makes people forget that the real violence is not the movement of people, but the hierarchical system that divides humanity into 'pure blood' and 'savages'. Unless the left reclaims the power of definition in this existential war, the next Ceuta will cease to be a livestream and become a real, digitally-glamorized massacre.
