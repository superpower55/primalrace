用未来的血肉填今天的账单：北海油田的经济骗局Filling Today's Ledger with Tomorrow's Flesh: The North Sea Oil Scam

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-25 § 链接
所谓的“经济效益”是局部掠夺的快感，而“气候损害”是全球共谋的账单。
Economic benefit is a localized loot; climate damage is a globalized bill of complicity.

这不过是一场典型的关于“定价权”的 scam。化石燃料公司 Adura 给出的 287 亿英镑是 face value，是写在财报里的短期利润；而帝国理工学院算出的 1190 亿到 3360 亿英镑损害，则是被刻意隐藏在结构层之下的 structural violence。

这种计算逻辑的差异，本质上是认知入口的争夺。能源巨头试图用“能源安全”和“经济增长”这两个 weaponized concepts 来制造一个虚假的真实：仿佛钻井能降低电费。但事实上，原油是国际大宗商品，价格由全球市场决定，而非由英国政府的审批决定。所谓的“能源安全”只是一个掩体，掩盖的是资本在进行一场极不公正的博弈——让少数股东在当下获利，让全球人类在未来支付气候灾难的赔偿金。

这里的共谋链条极其清晰：能源巨头提供虚假的经济承诺 $ ightarrow$ 右翼政党将其包装成“爱国”与“增长” $ ightarrow$ 政府在“就业”的压力下扮演犹豫的决策者。他们共谋地将气候损害定义为“外部性”（externality），这意味着在他们的账本里，未来的死亡和极端天气是没有价格的，或者说，其价格被定义为 0。

当 6% 的全球损害就能抹平英国本土的所有收益时，这场博弈的本质就是一种文明的自残。这不再是经济问题，而是一场关于谁在掠夺谁的生存权的战争。如果一个政府依然在权衡这笔账，说明它依然处于男性中心叙事的元暴力之中：认为可以通过某种“管理”和“平衡”来掌控自然，而忽视了生物墙和物理定律是不接受谈判的。

This is a classic scam regarding the power of pricing. The £28.7bn estimated by Adura is mere face value—short-term profit written in financial reports. In contrast, the £119bn to £336bn in damages identified by Imperial College is the structural violence deliberately hidden beneath the structural layer.

The discrepancy in logic is a battle over cognitive entry points. Fossil fuel giants use weaponized concepts like "energy security" and "economic growth" to manufacture a fake reality: the delusion that drilling lowers utility bills. In reality, oil is an internationally traded commodity. "Energy security" is merely a cover for an unjust game where a few shareholders profit now, while humanity pays the reparations for climate catastrophe later.

The chain of complicity is transparent: energy giants offer fake economic promises $ ightarrow$ right-wing parties package this as "patriotism" and "growth" $ ightarrow$ the government plays the hesitant decision-maker under the pressure of "jobs." Together, they conspire to define climate damage as an "externality," effectively pricing future deaths and extreme weather at zero in their ledgers.

When just 6% of global damage can wipe out all domestic gains, this is not an economic calculation but a form of civilized self-mutilation. It is an existential war over who plunders whose right to exist. Any government still "weighing" this is trapped in the meta-violence of a masculine-centric narrative: the belief that nature can be "managed" or "balanced," ignoring the fact that biological walls and physical laws do not negotiate.
