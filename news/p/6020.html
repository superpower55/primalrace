一场无进球的平局，真正的代价不在比分里
The real cost of a 0-0 isn't in the scoreline

这场球没有鬼。埃弗顿和曼联踢成零比零，报道自己都说“ghoul-less”，连鬼都懒得出来吓人。

值得说的不是比分，是这场比赛被写成了什么。开篇先写一群化了妆的学龄舞者在半场跳迈克尔·杰克逊，然后一句“可惜足球本身不是惊悚片”。这个结构很诚实：女足超级联赛的报道里，表演、气氛、看台上的口号都可以先写，球场上的内容往后排。埃弗顿球迷高唱让鲁比·梅斯去为英格兰队效力——她在这篇报道里的分量，大概和那位没被写出名字的守门员教练差不多。

真正被写到的代价只有一处：埃弗顿赢了球就能升到第四，压过曼联，结果只拿到一分，升到第六，还是落后曼联两分。这是报道里唯一具体的损失，是一个赛季里的两分，不是谁的命。

所以这篇评论不打算把一场零比零说成结构暴力的现场。加尔通的公式是 Potential 减 Actual，差额才是暴力；这场球的差额就是两分和一次没抓住的机会，报道写得很清楚，也仅此而已。硬要从里面挖出父权制，是拿框架当锤子，看什么都像钉子。

但有一件事值得记下来：这场比赛有名字的人里，特兰、布伦德尔、范古尔、籾木、梅斯，都是女的；写她们的人、给她们定版面的人、决定“先写跳舞再写球”的人，报道没写。谁在给这项运动定价，这篇稿子一个字没提——这不是它的错，是它所在的版面的常态。

There were no ghosts in this match. Everton and Manchester United drew 0-0, and the report itself calls it "ghoul-less" — even the ghosts couldn't be bothered.

What's worth noting isn't the score, it's what the match got written as. It opens with a group of costumed school-age dancers performing Michael Jackson at half-time, then one line: alas, the football itself was no thriller. That structure is honest. In WSL coverage, the dancing and the atmosphere and the chants get written first; the football queues behind them. Everton fans sang for Ruby Mace to play for England — in this piece she carries about as much weight as the goalkeeping coach whose name never appears.

The only cost the report actually records is this: a win would have lifted Everton above United into fourth. They got a point, went sixth, and stay two points behind. That is the concrete loss here — two points in a season, not anybody's life.

So I'm not going to turn a 0-0 into a scene of structural violence. Galtung's formula is Potential minus Actual, and the gap is the violence; here the gap is two points and one missed chance, stated plainly, and that's all. Digging patriarchy out of it would be using the framework as a hammer and finding nails everywhere.

One thing is worth recording, though: everyone named in this match — Terland, Blundell, Van Gool, Momiki, Mace — is a woman. Who writes them, who sets their column inches, who decides that the dancers go before the football: the report doesn't say. Who prices this sport is the one thing the piece never touches. That isn't its fault. It's the norm of the pages it sits on.
