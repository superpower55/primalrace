用“家庭价值”包装的跨国暴力出口Transnational Violence Exported as 'Family Values'

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-02 § 链接
所谓的“主权”与“价值”，不过是权力者用以合法化性别暴力的叙事武器。
So-called 'sovereignty' and 'values' are merely narrative weapons used to legitimize gender violence.

西非多国密集出台反 LGBTQ+ 法律，这根本不是什么“本土文化”的觉醒，而是一场典型的表达武器化 (weaponization of expression) 协同行动。当军政府需要通过制造敌对者来维持权力，当宗教团体需要通过定义“家庭价值”来垄断解释权时，LGBTQ+ 群体成了最廉价的 scapegoats。

这件事最讽刺的共谋 (complicity) 在于，这些法律在口头上反西方、谈主权，实际上却在接收来自美国亚利桑那州等西方保守基督教团体的资金和模版。这种“跨国强化”揭示了一个事实：只要是能通过剥夺他者主体性来巩固自身权力结构的叙事，无论它是左还是右，无论它在哪个地缘，其底层逻辑完全一致——这就是元暴力 (meta violence) 的全球分发。

从结构层来看，这些法律通过将“性别”强行定义为二元对立，完成了对非标准表达者的生物墙加固。当一个人的表达被定义为犯罪，他便在存在性战争中被剥夺了所有投票权。而那些被拘捕、殴打的跨性别女性，则是这种 structural violence 最终通过 direct violence 呈现的血腥结果。

所谓的“家庭主权”不过是一个巨大的 scam。它通过定义什么是“正常的家庭”，将所有不符合该定义的人定义为“异类”，从而让权力者在“保护文明”的掩体下，心安理得地实施最原始的种族化压迫。在这种叙事里，人权被切割成了碎片，而碎片被扔进了名为“传统”的垃圾桶。

The surge of anti-LGBTQ+ laws across West Africa is not a 'local cultural awakening,' but a textbook case of the weaponization of expression. When military juntas need to manufacture enemies to maintain power and religious groups seek to monopolize the interpretation of 'family values,' LGBTQ+ communities become the cheapest scapegoats.

The most cynical complicity here is that while these regimes preach anti-Western rhetoric and 'sovereignty,' they are actively importing funding and legislative templates from conservative Christian groups in the US. This 'transnational reinforcement' proves that any narrative designed to consolidate power by stripping others of their subjectivity follows the same logic—this is the global distribution of meta-violence.

Structurally, these laws reinforce the biological wall by forcibly defining gender as a binary. Once an individual's expression is criminalized, they are stripped of all voting power in the existential war. The detention and beating of transgender women are simply the direct violence resulting from this structural violence.

'Family sovereignty' is a massive scam. By defining what a 'normal family' is, it labels everyone else as 'deviant,' allowing power-holders to exercise primal, racialized oppression under the cover of 'protecting civilization.' In this narrative, human rights are fragmented and discarded in the trash bin of 'tradition.'
