囤罐头的人不背锅The Man Stockpiling Beans Won't Pay for Them

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-31 § 链接
防务大臣囤豆子，财相砍预算，账单落在谁头上
The defence secretary hoards cans while the chancellor cuts budgets. Guess who gets the bill.

国防大臣韦斯·斯特里廷开始囤罐头了。烤豆、甜玉米、红腰豆、金枪鱼、蛋白棒、坚果，外加瓶装水、急救包、手电筒。他说这是为了"以身作则"，让英国人想想自家的"家庭韧性"。

同一天，财政大臣约翰·希利宣布防务开支要冲到 GDP 的 3%，为此已经要求白厅各部门为长期投资找出 1% 的削减。看懂这个顺序：先定一个数字，再让别的部门去凑钱。凑钱的部门是医院、学校、地方服务，囤罐头的部门是国防部。斯特里廷的母亲打电话问他发薪日前该买多少东西，他把这个当成好消息讲给《泰晤士报》听。一个国家的国防大臣，把国民对断供的恐惧当成政策已经"传达到位"的证据。

这是结构暴力最标准的一次重新分配：风险被宣布为全民共担，成本被切给最没有议价权的那一端。能囤一柜子罐头的人，和"预算到月底"的人，不是同一批人。前者囤货是常识，后者囤货是奢侈。斯特里廷自己说得很清楚，这些都是"应对日常挑战的常识"——日常挑战，不是战争。

然后是那套叙事。威胁上升，所以要威慑；威慑失败，所以要能"击败他们"；所以要为"未来的战争"做好准备。战争叙事从来是这样运作的：先制造一个必须回应的紧迫性，再让紧迫性去合法化一切原本会被追问的分配。加尔通的公式在这里是现成的——现状低于本可达到的状态，差额就是暴力。现在这个差额的名字叫"国防"，于是它不再被算作暴力，而被算作责任。

更有意思的是斯特里廷对司法系统的说法。他说法院正在对特种部队的"声誉与诚信"发动一场"积极的运动"。也就是说，追究战争罪行的程序，被他描述成对军队的抹黑。这一句比罐头重要得多：它在提前给未来的问责定性，把监督翻译成敌意。解释权在这里被调转了一次方向——谁在调查，谁就成了攻击者。

希利的预算被批评把达到 3% 的细节推到了明年的支出审查。马克·吕特说英国走在"可信的路径"上。可信的路径，不可信的细节。数字先立起来，钱后找，账最后落在谁的头上，报道没写。没写的地方就是问题所在：那 1% 的削减从哪些部门出，哪些服务会先消失，哪些人在等一个不会来的预约。这些名字在这篇报道里一个都没有出现。

斯特里廷说"把钱乱花不是美德"。这句话是对的，也是这篇报道里唯一一句可以被拿去追问的话——追问的对象是他自己，和他刚刚宣布的那个还没算清楚的数字。

The defence secretary, Wes Streeting, has started stockpiling. Baked beans, sweetcorn, red kidney beans, tuna, protein bars, nuts, plus bottled water, a first aid kit and a battery-powered torch. He says he is doing it to "lead by example," so that Britons think about their own "household resilience."

On the same day, the chancellor, John Healey, said defence spending should reach 3% of GDP, and that Whitehall departments have already been asked to find 1% cuts to long-term investment to fund it. Note the order: first the number, then everyone else pays for it. The departments finding the money are hospitals, schools, local services. The department hoarding cans is the Ministry of Defence. Streeting's mother called to ask how much she should buy before payday, and he told the Times this as good news. A defence secretary treating public fear of shortage as proof the message has landed.

This is structural violence doing its most standard trick: risk is declared universal, cost is sliced off and handed to whoever has the least bargaining power. The person who can fill a cupboard with cans and the person "budgeting to the end of the month" are not the same person. For one, stockpiling is common sense. For the other, it is a luxury. Streeting says as much himself: these are "commonsense things for the everyday challenges." Everyday challenges, not war.

Then the narrative. Threats are rising, so we must deter; if deterrence fails, we must be ready to "defeat them"; so we must prepare for "the wars ahead." War narratives always work this way: manufacture an urgency that must be answered, then let the urgency legitimise every distribution that would otherwise be questioned. Galtung's formula is sitting right there. Violence equals Potential minus Actual. The gap now has a name, "defence," and once it has that name it stops counting as violence and starts counting as duty.

More interesting is what Streeting says about the courts. He claims the courts are in the middle of an "active campaign" to "smear the reputation and integrity of our special forces." That sentence matters far more than the beans. It pre-labels future accountability as hostility. Someone is investigating; therefore someone is attacking. The power to explain has been flipped, and the investigator becomes the aggressor.

Healey's budget was criticised for pushing the details of how to reach 3% into next year's spending review. Mark Rutte says the UK is on "a credible path." A credible path with incredible details. The number comes first, the money comes later, and who pays is left for later still. The report doesn't say. What it doesn't say is the point: which departments absorb the 1%, which services vanish first, who waits for an appointment that never comes. None of those names appear here.

Streeting says "spending more money badly is not virtuous." True, and the only line in this report worth turning back on him, along with the number he has just announced and not yet counted.
