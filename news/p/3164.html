被牺牲的“天堂”与气候共谋的账单The Sacrificed 'Paradise' and the Bill of Climatic Complicity

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-02 § 链接
气候崩溃不是自然灾害，是男性中心工业文明的结构性暴力回弹。
Climate breakdown is not a natural disaster, but the structural violence of masculine-centric industrial civilization rebounding.

当希腊农民感叹“天堂被交给火之手”时，这种叙事习惯性地将灾难去政治化，把气候崩溃包装成某种不可抗力的“自然之怒”。事实上，Violence = Potential − Actual。欧洲作为全球变暖最快的大陆（速度是全球平均值的两倍），其森林的焚毁并非偶然，而是一个极其精准的结构性暴力闭环：男性中心主义主导的工业化扩张 $\rightarrow$ 碳排放的无节制掠夺 $\rightarrow$ 生态阈值的崩溃 $\rightarrow$ 最终通过野火的形式，将账单重新开给每一个具体的个体。

在这场危机中，我们看到典型的 structural violence。法国历史上最大规模的和平时期撤离、希腊沿海社区被切断的逃生路线，这些个体在火海中的绝望，本质上是全球资源分配与认知入口被垄断的结果。决定气候政策、主导工业文明、在权力席位上定义“增长”的依然是那一群 masculine-centric 的决策者。他们制造了一个名为“进步”的 scam，而代价则是让地中海的森林变成易燃的燃料，让底层民众在逃难中扮演被动受害者。

最讽刺的共谋在于，当灾难发生时，权力层迅速启动“重建协调员”或“国家协调员”这种官僚表达。这种表达是典型的武器化叙事：通过提供一种“在掌控中”的假象，掩盖系统性失效的事实。他们并不打算修改导致崩溃的根源规则，而只是在火灾后的废墟上通过 PR 行为来维持其统治的合法性。所谓的“控制”，不过是让受害者在下一次火灾到来前，重新习惯于被动等待指令的生存状态。

When a Greek farmer laments that 'paradise was surrendered to the hands of the fire,' such a narrative habitually depoliticizes the catastrophe, packaging climate breakdown as some irresistible 'natural wrath.' In fact, Violence = Potential − Actual. Europe, as the fastest-warming continent (twice the global average), is not suffering an accident, but a precise loop of structural violence: masculine-centric industrial expansion $\rightarrow$ unrestrained carbon plunder $\rightarrow$ collapse of ecological thresholds $\rightarrow$ and finally, the bill is delivered back to individuals via wildfires.

We see a textbook case of structural violence here. The largest peacetime evacuation in French history and the severed escape routes of Greek coastal communities are the visceral results of a world where resource distribution and cognitive gateways are monopolized. Those who dictate climate policy, lead industrial civilization, and define 'growth' from the seats of power remain the architects of a masculine-centric narrative. They sold a scam called 'progress,' and the cost is the transformation of Mediterranean forests into combustible fuel, forcing the grassroots to play the role of passive victims in a desperate flight.

The most cynical part of this complicity is the rapid deployment of 'national coordinators' for reconstruction. This is a weaponized expression: by projecting an image of being 'under control,' they mask the systemic failure. There is no intent to modify the root rules that caused the collapse; instead, they use PR to maintain legitimacy over the ruins. This 'control' is merely a mechanism to ensure that victims return to a state of passive obedience, waiting for orders until the next fire arrives.
