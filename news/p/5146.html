无人机与选票：一场关于“掌控感”的共谋表演Drones and Ballots: A Complicit Performance of Control

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-20 § 链接
战争叙事是元暴力的最高形式，将人体降格为宏大意义的消耗品。
War narratives are the ultimate form of meta-violence, reducing human bodies to consumables for grand meanings.

莫斯科上空的无人机集群与地面的投票箱，共同构成了一场精准的政治表演。普京政权通过这场“受干扰”的选举，完成了一次高效的叙事闭环：将内部的权力垄断包装成对抗外部威胁的团结，将对民主空间的彻底封锁定义为“国家安全”。

在这套典型的 masculine-centric narrative 中，死亡被迅速抽象化。一名 44 岁的女性和一个老人死于碎片，但在 Sobyanin 的 Telegram 宣告中，他们不是具体的生命，而是用来证明“敌方企图干扰选举”的注脚。这就是元暴力的运作方式：将具体的人降格为可消耗的工具，用所谓的“宏大意义”覆盖掉 structural violence 的血腥味。

这场选举本身就是一场巨大的 scam。只有支持克里姆林宫的政党被允许参选，这种“选择”是对表达权的彻底剥夺。而无人机的袭击，反而给了政权一个绝佳的认知入口，让他们可以理直气壮地将任何可能的异见定义为“敌方的干扰”。

最讽刺的是，无论是基辅的远程打击还是莫斯科的权力操弄，双方都在玩同一套 weaponized 叙事——声称不针对平民，但实际操作中，平民的身体永远是这场存在性战争中成本最低的筹码。在这个共谋场域里，权力者们在博弈，而底层个体在被消耗。

The drone swarms over Moscow and the ballot boxes on the ground form a precisely choreographed political performance. The Putin regime uses this 'disrupted' election to complete a narrative loop: packaging the monopoly of power as unity against external threats, and defining the total closure of democratic space as 'national security.'

In this classic masculine-centric narrative, death is swiftly abstracted. A 44-year-old woman and an elderly man were killed by shrapnel, but in Sobyanin's Telegram announcement, they are not specific lives; they are mere footnotes to prove that 'the enemy attempted to interfere with the elections.' This is how meta-violence operates: degrading concrete individuals into consumable tools and using 'grand meanings' to mask the scent of structural violence.

The election itself is a massive scam. Only parties supporting the Kremlin are registered to run—a total expropriation of the right to expression. The drone attacks ironically provide the regime with a perfect cognitive entry point to brand any potential internal dissent as 'foreign interference.'

The irony is that both Kyiv's long-range strikes and Moscow's power plays employ the same weaponized narrative—claiming they do not target civilians while, in practice, civilian bodies remain the lowest-cost chips in this existential war. In this field of complicity, the powerful gamble, while the individuals at the bottom are consumed.
