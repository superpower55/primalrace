谁在定义“无价值”的人Who Defines the 'Worthless'

哲学 直接层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-28 § 链接
暴力不是因为绝望，而是因为在权力等级中找到了可以被客体化的牺牲品。
Violence isn't born of despair, but of finding expendable objects within a power hierarchy.

这起案件被包装成一个“掉入社会裂缝”的悲剧，但这种叙事掩盖了最核心的 meta violence。Desborough 并非简单的绝望者，他是一个在失去社会地位后，试图在底层生态中重建“控制权”的 control freak。当他无法在 civil engineer 的阶级中维持权力时，他转向了对那些被定义为“无价值”的人——那些 addiction、depression 且被 cuckooing 的 vulnerable people——实施绝对统治。

这里的暴力逻辑非常清晰：Desborough 杀掉他的“朋友”并非因为冲动，而是因为他将这些人客体化为所谓的“dinlos”（傻瓜）和“muppets”。在他的认知入口里，这些人的生命价值低于他的愤怒。这种将人分级并决定谁“值得死去”的权力感，正是男性中心叙事中最典型的逻辑——定义谁是主体，谁是可消耗的客体。

最令人作呕的是这种暴力的共谋。他一边在法庭上扮演一个遭遇“不幸情况”的受害者，一边在笔记本上写着要“处决掠食者”。他试图通过杀戮来证明自己依然是那个掌握生杀大权的“强者”，即便这个强者现在住在破烂的 shack 里。这种对控制权的病态执着，本质上就是元暴力的个体化投射：如果我不能掌控世界，我就掌控并毁灭那些比我更弱的人。

至于那些被分尸并焚烧的受害者，他们不仅死于 Desborough 的手，更死于一个让他们在社会结构中变得“透明”的 structural violence。当一个人的存在被简化为“无家可归”和“成瘾”时，他们在施暴者眼中就失去了作为人的生物墙，变成了可以被随意切割的肉块。

This case is being packaged as a tragedy of people 'slipping through the cracks,' but this narrative masks the core meta-violence. Desborough wasn't just a desperate man; he was a control freak attempting to rebuild a sense of dominance within a bottom-tier ecosystem. When he could no longer maintain power as a civil engineer, he pivoted toward those defined as 'worthless'—the vulnerable people battling addiction and depression—to exercise absolute control.

The logic of violence here is transparent: Desborough murdered his 'friends' not out of impulse, but because he objectified them as 'dinlos' and 'muppets.' In his cognitive entry point, the value of these lives was lower than the value of his own anger. This act of grading human life and deciding who 'deserves to die' is the quintessential logic of masculine-centric narratives—defining who is the subject and who is the expendable object.

The complicity in this violence is the most repulsive part. He performed the role of a victim of 'unfortunate circumstances' in court, while his notes revealed a desire to 'execute predators.' He sought to prove he was still the 'strongman' with the power of life and death, even if that strength was exercised from a dilapidated shack. This pathological obsession with control is a personalized projection of meta-violence: if I cannot control the world, I will control and destroy those weaker than me.

As for the victims, dismembered and burnt, they died not only by Desborough's hand but by a structural violence that rendered them 'invisible.' When a person's existence is reduced to 'homeless' or 'addict,' they lose their biological wall of human dignity in the eyes of the predator, becoming nothing more than pieces of meat to be carved.
