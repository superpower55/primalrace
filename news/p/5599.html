用“定义权”代替事实：特朗普及共谋者的认知闭环Replacing Fact with Definition: The Cognitive Loop of Trump and His Complicitors

国际 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-17 § 链接
当现实不配合时，就重新定义现实——这是权力武器化的最高形式。
When reality doesn't cooperate, redefine reality—the ultimate form of weaponized expression.

特朗普在佐治亚州的这场集会，是一次教科书级别的“表达武器化”演习。他不再试图通过证据来证明选举舞弊，而是直接通过宣布“他从未被选举”来夺取解释权。在元暴力 (meta violence) 的逻辑里，事实不重要，重要的是谁拥有定义事实的权力。当他把“可负担性” (affordability) 定义为民主党的 propaganda 时，他实际上是在尝试关闭支持者的认知入口，将所有不符合其叙事的现实直接剔除出“真实”的范畴。

这场博弈中最令人不安的不是特朗普的谎言，而是现场那些共谋者 (co-conspirators) 的状态。那个在烈日下等待 80 分钟仍坚称他是“史上最伟大总统”的女性，展现了典型的内化规训。她通过将批评者定义为“不懂”的人，在潜意识中完成了与权力的利益站队。这种共谋的回报是某种虚幻的身份认同感：只要我站在“正确”的一边，我就成了这个强大叙事的一部分，从而在存在性战争中获得一种伪装的优越感。

从对 DEI 政策的攻击到对委内瑞拉战争的美化，特朗普构建的是一个完全由他定义的平行世界。在这个世界里， merit (功绩) 被重新定义为对他的忠诚，而任何反对声音都被迅速地、物理性地清除（如被驱逐的抗议者）。这不仅仅是政治操弄，而是一种结构性暴力：通过垄断解释权，让受众在不知不觉中让渡主体性，最终变成权力机器的生物插件。

Trump's rally in Georgia is a textbook exercise in the weaponisation of expression. He is no longer attempting to prove election fraud with evidence; instead, he seizes the power of interpretation by simply declaring that Ossoff "was never elected." In the logic of meta violence, facts are irrelevant; what matters is who holds the power to define them. By labeling "affordability" as Democratic propaganda, he is attempting to shut down the cognitive entry points of his supporters, erasing any reality that contradicts his narrative from the category of "truth."

What is more disturbing than Trump's lies is the state of the co-conspirators. The woman waiting 80 minutes in the scorching sun, insisting he is the "greatest president of all time," exemplifies internalized discipline. By framing critics as people who "don't know better," she completes a subconscious alignment with power. The reward for this complicity is a phantom sense of identity: as long as she stands on the "right" side, she becomes part of a powerful narrative, gaining a fake advantage in the existential war.

From attacking DEI policies to glorifying the war in Venezuela, Trump is constructing a parallel universe. In this world, "merit" is redefined as loyalty to him, and any dissent is swiftly and physically purged—as seen with the protesters. This is not mere political maneuvering, but structural violence: by monopolizing the right to interpret, he encourages the audience to surrender their subjectivity and become biological plugins for the power machine.
