Meta的算法监狱：一场关于认知入口的殖民战争Meta's Algorithmic Prison: A Colonial War for Cognitive Entry

科技 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-18 § 链接
成瘾设计不是技术失误，而是对人类认知入口的精准武器化。
Addictive design is not a technical glitch, but the precise weaponisation of cognitive entry.

Meta被29个州起诉，核心在于它将“表达”变成了一个精心设计的陷阱。无限滚动、点赞、视觉滤镜，这些不是什么“良性功能”，而是典型的武器化表达（Weaponized Expression）。它们通过精准操控多巴胺分泌，在青少年的大脑中建立一套虚假的评价体系，夺取了他们定义“什么是真实”的解释权。

这本质上是一场认知入口的殖民。Meta利用算法制造了一个闭环的文化暴力（Cultural Violence）场域：滤镜定义了审美，点赞定义了价值，而FOMO（恐惧错过）则成了维持这个监狱运转的电子枷锁。对于处于生物墙发育期的青少年来说，这种对主体性的剥夺是毁灭性的——他们被诱导扮演算法认可的角色，以换取短期的社交认同，代价是主体性的死亡。

最讽刺的是Meta的辩护：称这些是“行业普遍挑战”。这种话术就是典型的共谋者逻辑。当所有社交巨头都默认将用户时长作为唯一KPI时，它们就形成了一个庞大的共谋网络，共同维护一个以榨取注意力为核心的结构性暴力（Structural Violence）系统。赔偿金无论多少，只要算法的底层逻辑不被强行拆除，这依然是一场表演性的正义。

这场审判真正的看点不在于那2000亿美元，而在于它能否像当年反烟草诉讼一样，撕开文化暴力的伪装，让大众意识到：当你觉得自己在“社交”时，你其实只是在被某个CEO定义的现实中被动地消费。

Meta is being sued by 29 states, and the core of the issue is that it has transformed 'expression' into a meticulously engineered trap. Infinite scrolling, likes, and visual filters are not 'benign features'—they are classic Weaponized Expressions. By precisely manipulating dopamine, Meta establishes a fraudulent evaluation system in young minds, seizing the power to define 'what is real.'

This is essentially a colonization of cognitive entry. Meta utilizes algorithms to create a closed loop of Cultural Violence: filters define aesthetics, likes define value, and FOMO serves as the electronic shackle keeping this prison operational. For adolescents still navigating their Biological Walls, this deprivation of subjectivity is devastating—they are lured into performing roles approved by the algorithm for short-term validation, at the cost of the death of their own subjectivity.

Meta's defense is the most cynical part: calling these 'industry-wide challenges.' This is textbook complicity logic. When all social giants accept user-time as the sole KPI, they form a massive network of complicity, maintaining a system of Structural Violence centered on the extraction of attention. No matter the amount of damages, as long as the underlying logic of the algorithm remains intact, this is merely performative justice.

The true significance of this trial isn't the $200 billion, but whether it can, like the tobacco lawsuits of the 90s, strip away the mask of Cultural Violence and force the public to realize: when you think you are 'socializing,' you are actually being passively consumed within a reality defined by a CEO.
