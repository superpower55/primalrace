波音的“自由落体”：资本共谋下的死亡定价权Boeing's Freefall: The Pricing of Life via Corporate Complicity

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-20 § 链接
当监管者成为共谋，企业利润便成了对生命的定价权。
When regulators become co-conspirators, corporate profit becomes the pricing power over human life.

波音的故事不是航空事故，而是一场典型的 structural violence。根据加尔通的暴力三角，当一个系统的 Actual（实际安全状态）被刻意压低至低于 Potential（本可达到的安全标准）时，其中的差额就是暴力。波音通过削减质量控制、外包关键部件来最大化 shareholder profit，这个差额直接兑换成了 346 具尸体和一个举报人的“自杀”。

最令人作呕的不是波音的贪婪，而是 FAA（美国联邦航空管理局）的 complicity。监管机构本应是生物墙，防止资本的贪婪直接地摧毁生命，但在这里，监管者与被监管者达成了共谋。FAA 在 2020 年匆忙解除禁飞令，本质上是在为波音的资本循环背书。当监管逻辑从“确保安全”切换到“确保企业不崩盘”时，元暴力（meta violence）就完成了闭环：资本定义了什么是“可接受的风险”，而这种定义权就是一种杀人执照。

电影中提到的 CEO 拿走 3280 万美元薪水而员工在工厂里不敢坐自己的飞机，这揭示了 neoliberal era 的残酷真相：在资本的认知入口里，人被降格为可消耗的工具。无论是死在空难中的乘客，还是被纪律处分的举报人 John Barnett，他们都被客体化为财务报表上的“损耗”。

不要被所谓的“企业改进”叙事欺骗。只要领导层面对的依然是 multimillion-dollar retirement packages 而非刑事指控，这种 structural violence 就永远不会停止。因为在共谋者的逻辑里，交罚款是经营成本，而坐牢才是真正的损失。

The Boeing saga is not about aviation accidents; it is a textbook case of structural violence. According to the Violence Triangle, when the Actual state of safety is deliberately suppressed below its Potential, the gap is violence. By slashing quality control and outsourcing critical components to maximize shareholder profit, Boeing converted this gap directly into 346 corpses and the 'suicide' of a whistleblower.

The most repulsive part is not Boeing's greed, but the complicity of the FAA. A regulatory body should act as a biological wall, preventing capital's appetite from destroying lives. Instead, the regulator and the regulated formed a pact. The FAA's hurried lifting of the grounding order in 2020 was essentially an endorsement of Boeing's capital cycle. When the logic of regulation shifts from 'ensuring safety' to 'ensuring the firm doesn't collapse,' meta violence completes its loop: capital defines 'acceptable risk,' and this power of definition is a license to kill.

The image of a CEO pocketing $32.8 million while employees fear flying on the planes they build exposes the brutal truth of the neoliberal era: in the cognitive entry of capital, humans are downgraded to consumable tools. Whether it is the passengers who died in crashes or the disciplined whistleblower John Barnett, they are objectified as 'attrition' on a financial statement.

Do not be fooled by the narrative of 'corporate improvement.' As long as failed leadership faces multimillion-dollar retirement packages instead of criminal prosecution, this structural violence will persist. In the logic of the co-conspirators, paying fines is a cost of doing business, while prison is the only real loss.
