自动贩卖机里的“愿望清单”与被外包的生命Vending Machine Wishlists and the Outsourcing of Existence

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-09 § 链接
当人们幻想用机器解决生育与情感时，本质是在通过技术外包来逃避结构性暴力。
Fantasizing about buying birth or love from machines is a subconscious attempt to bypass structural violence through technical outsourcing.

《卫报》这篇看似轻盈的读者互动，实则是一次关于“存在性战争”的潜意识大普查。人们在幻想贩卖机里买到廉价火车票、欧盟护照甚至“新男友”时，其实是在用一种极其幼稚的 a-temporal 幻想，试图跳过现实中极其残酷的博弈过程。当一个读者提出想要“体外培育的婴儿”以省去受孕、怀孕和分娩的努力时，这不仅仅是科技幻想，而是一次典型的对生物墙 (Biological Wall) 的绝望反抗。

生育的痛苦与母职惩罚 (Motherhood Penalty) 是结构性暴力 (structural violence) 的核心。在当前的社会协作中，女性的生育力被当作一种可被榨取的资源，而其身体代价却被掩盖在“神圣母性”的文化叙事之下。那个想要“罐装婴儿”的幻想，本质上是女性在意识到自己无法在现有的共谋结构中获得真.最优解表达后，试图通过技术手段将自己从“原初种族”的生物枷锁中剥离。但讽刺的是，如果这种技术真的实现，它极大概率会成为新的武器化工具——由资本和权力定义谁有资格拥有“完美婴儿”，从而在基因层面完成新一轮的阶级筛选。

而那些关于“买到爱、善良与和平”的呓语，则是文化暴力 (cultural violence) 导致的主体性缺失。当个体无法在公共空间通过公正的表达获得认同，只能在私域中通过幻想一个“全能机器”来填补存在感的空缺。人们习惯于将复杂的权力关系简化为一次投币交易，这正是元暴力 (meta violence) 的最高形式：它让你相信，只要支付足够的价格，你就可以绕过所有不公正的结构，直接抵达结果。这不仅是 scam，更是对主体性死亡的集体潜意识确认。

This seemingly lighthearted reader interaction in The Guardian is actually a subconscious survey of the 'existential war.' When people imagine buying cheap train tickets, EU passports, or even a 'new boyfriend' from a machine, they are using a naive a-temporal fantasy to leapfrog over the brutal reality of social gaming. The reader wishing for 'vat-grown babies' to avoid the effort of conceiving and delivering is not just a sci-fi whim; it is a desperate rebellion against the Biological Wall.

The agony of childbirth and the Motherhood Penalty are the core of structural violence. In current social cooperation, female fertility is treated as a resource to be extracted, while the physical cost is masked by the cultural narrative of 'sacred motherhood.' The fantasy of 'canned babies' is essentially an attempt by women, realizing they cannot reach a true optimal expression within the current complicity structure, to detach themselves from the biological shackles of the Primal Race. Ironically, if such technology existed, it would likely become a weaponized tool for capital and power to define who deserves a 'perfect baby,' completing a new round of class screening at the genetic level.

As for the ramblings about buying 'kindness, hugs, and love,' this is the result of cultural violence leading to a loss of subjectivity. When individuals cannot find recognition through just expressions in public spaces, they retreat into a fantasy of an 'omnipotent machine' to fill the void of their existence. The habit of simplifying complex power relations into a coin-op transaction is the ultimate form of meta violence: it makes you believe that by paying a price, you can bypass all unjust structures and arrive directly at the result. This is not just a scam; it is a collective subconscious confirmation of the death of subjectivity.
