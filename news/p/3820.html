谁在定义“恐怖分子”：一个15岁女孩的生物墙与结构性谋杀Defining the 'Terrorist': The Biological Wall and Structural Murder of a 15-Year-Old

性别 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-14 § 链接
当权力垄断了“恐怖主义”的解释权，弱势个体的生存即成了某种犯罪。
When power monopolizes the definition of 'terrorism,' the existence of the vulnerable becomes a crime.

Rhianan Rudd 的死不是一场意外，而是一次典型的 structural violence。一个15岁、患有未确诊自闭症、处于精神崩溃边缘的女孩，在被新纳粹主义者 online grooming 之后，被国家机器迅速地贴上了“恐怖分子”的标签。这里最令人作呕的不是那个新纳粹分子的恶意，而是国家机器在面对一个生物学上的弱者时，展现出的那种令人惊叹的、机械的冷漠。

从第一章的视角看，Rhianan 的表达——那些所谓的“仇恨言论”和“炸弹手册”——其实是一个破碎灵魂在绝望中寻找认同的拙劣尝试。但对于 MI5 和 Prevent 这种权力机构来说，她们不需要看到一个活生生的人，只需要一个可以被归类、被处理的 case。她们直接跳过了对一个未成年女孩心理状态的审计，直接启动了最暴力的认知入口：将她定义为“恐怖分子”。

这就是典型的 meta violence。男性中心叙事下的国家机器，习惯于将“安全”定义为对潜在威胁的绝对掌控，而这种掌控的代价就是抹杀个体的复杂性。在权力者的逻辑里，一个15岁女孩的心理危机被简化为一种“安全风险”。这种解释权的垄断，让 Rhianan 从一个需要救助的受害者，瞬间变成了需要被监禁的罪犯。即使后来指控被撤销，但结构性的暴力已经完成了它的闭环：从逮捕到监禁，再到在毫无专业支撑的儿童之家中自杀。

最讽刺的是那些机构的“权利回复”。MI5 和 Prevent 用法律上的“免责”来掩盖道德上的共谋。他们通过一套文明的、程序化的叙事，将一个女孩的死亡消解为“策略的优化”。这种用 PR 话术包装的 structural violence 才是最深层的恐怖。当一个系统能够理直气壮地对一个死掉的孩子说“你的死 informing 了我们的策略”时，这个系统本身就是最大的恐怖分子。

The death of Rhianan Rudd was not an accident; it was a textbook case of structural violence. A 15-year-old girl with undiagnosed autism and a collapsing mental state was rapidly labeled a 'terrorist' after being online groomed by a neo-fascist. The most repulsive part is not the malice of the groomer, but the mechanical coldness of the state machine when facing a biological underdog.

From the perspective of Expression, Rhianan’s output—the so-called 'hate speech' and 'bomb manuals'—was a clumsy attempt by a broken soul to find belonging. But for agencies like MI5 and Prevent, they didn't need to see a human being; they needed a case to be categorized and processed. They bypassed the audit of a minor's psychological state and activated the most violent cognitive entry: defining her as a 'terrorist.'

This is pure meta violence. The state machine, driven by a masculine-centric narrative, defines 'security' as the absolute control of potential threats. The cost of this control is the erasure of individual complexity. In the logic of power, a 15-year-old's mental crisis was simplified into a 'security risk.' This monopoly over the right to interpret transformed Rhianan from a victim in need of rescue into a criminal in need of incarceration. Even after charges were dropped, the structural violence had closed its loop: from arrest to custody, and finally to suicide in an ill-equipped children's home.

Most ironic are the 'rights of reply.' MI5 and Prevent use legal 'absolution' to mask their complicity. Through a civilized, procedural narrative, they dissolve a girl's death into 'strategy optimization.' This version of structural violence, wrapped in PR speak, is the deepest horror. When a system can tell a dead child that her death 'informed' their strategy, the system itself is the true terrorist.
