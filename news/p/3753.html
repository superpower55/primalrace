日式咖喱：一场关于殖民洗白与认知入口的烹饪秀Japanese Curry: A Culinary Show of Colonial Whitewashing

哲学 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-13 § 链接
所谓的“国民料理”，往往是殖民暴力被文化层抹除后的甜点化叙事。
A "national dish" is often just a dessert-like narrative after colonial violence is erased by the cultural layer.

这篇食谱在教你如何做出浓郁的咖喱，但它在开头随口提到的历史，恰恰揭露了一个典型的 cultural violence 样本。日式咖喱（Kare Raisu）的路径极其荒诞：它不是来自印度，而是通过大英帝国海军，以“英国咖喱粉”的形式进入日本。这意味着，一种源自被殖民地的文化产物，在经过殖民者的“过滤”和“重新定义”后，被日本军队和学校接纳，最终变成了所谓的“国民料理”。

这是一个标准的 weaponized 认知入口操作。原产地的印度被彻底剔除出叙事，取而代之的是“西式料理”（Yoshoku）的标签。当一个文化符号被剥离了它的原生地和被殖民的血泪，它就变成了一个纯粹的审美对象。这种“迂回路线”不仅是食材的迁移，更是解释权的篡改。殖民者定义了什么是“文明的咖喱”，而接收者则心安理得地将其内化为自身的身份认同。

最讽刺的是，这篇食谱在推崇这种“国民味”的同时，将其简化为巧克力、蜂蜜等“秘密配方”的叠加。当暴力的结构层（colonial rule）被文化层（recipe/national dish）完全覆盖，这种食物就成了一种温情的 scam。我们吃下的不仅是浓稠的淀粉糊，更是被精心修剪过的历史真相。

This recipe teaches you how to make a thick curry, but the history it casually mentions at the start reveals a textbook case of cultural violence. The path of Japanese curry (Kare Raisu) is absurd: it didn't come from India, but via the British Royal Navy as "British curry powder." This means a cultural product from a colonized land was "filtered" and "redefined" by the colonizer before being adopted by the Japanese military and schools, eventually becoming a "national dish."

This is a standard operation of weaponized cognitive entry. The origin—India—is completely excised from the narrative, replaced by the label of "Western-influenced cuisine" (Yoshoku). When a cultural symbol is stripped of its origin and the blood of colonization, it becomes a mere object of aesthetic consumption. This "roundabout route" is not just a migration of ingredients, but a hijacking of the right to interpret.

The irony is that while this recipe celebrates this "national taste," it reduces it to a layering of "secret ingredients" like chocolate and honey. When the structural layer of violence (colonial rule) is fully covered by the cultural layer (recipe/national dish), the food becomes a cozy scam. We are consuming not just a starchy slurry, but a carefully pruned version of historical truth.
