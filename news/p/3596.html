用“破坏者”掩盖“烂尾楼”：特朗普的叙事拼贴术Masking a Botched Job with 'Vandals': Trump's Narrative Patchwork

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-10 § 链接
当结构性崩塌发生时，权力习惯于制造一个具体的“敌人”来承接愤怒。
When structural collapse occurs, power habitually manufactures a specific 'enemy' to absorb the public's rage.

这是一场典型的 weaponized expression。面对一个预算从 200 万飙升至 1600 万、且由于 rushed 导致质量低劣的烂尾工程，特朗普的反应不是审计 contractor，而是迅速在认知入口建立一个“破坏者” (vandals) 的叙事。将 structural violence（制度性的资源浪费与低劣工程）转化为 direct violence（具体的破坏行为），是权力最廉价的脱身之术。

在这个博弈中，David Hearn 被推出来扮演那个被定义为“敌人”的角色。特朗普通过引用一个所谓“高度可信”但匿名的证人，试图制造一个事实。这种操作的本质是夺取解释权：只要能把损害定义为“蓄意破坏”，那么工程本身的 shoddy 就可以被稀释为“相对无关紧要”的小瑕疵。这是一种极其恶劣的叙事拼贴，用个体的“罪行”来掩盖体制的“无能”。

最讽刺的共谋场域在于 Jeanine Pirro 的处境。作为总统的 loyalist，她本应是这套叙事的执行者，但当证据指向工程本身是 scam 时，她选择了法律事实。而特朗普对她的愤怒——称其“像廉价雨伞一样折叠”——揭示了这种共谋的潜规则：在元暴力的逻辑里，忠诚不意味着追求真相，而意味着必须无条件地维护那个“制造真实”的人。当共谋者试图回归公正表达时，她便成了被清洗的对象。

从 200 万到 1600 万的预算跳跃，以及无需竞争的 bidding 过程，本身就是一种 structural violence。这笔钱本可以转化为更好的公共资源，现在却成了掩盖在“美国旗蓝色”之下的烂摊子。这种“现实不配合就重新定义现实”的机制，与他定义芯片定律或 GDP 的逻辑如出一辙。

This is a textbook case of weaponized expression. Faced with a botched project that ballooned from $2M to $16M due to being rushed and shoddy, Trump's reflex isn't to audit the contractor, but to swiftly establish a 'vandalism' narrative at the cognitive entry point. By transforming structural violence—institutional waste and substandard engineering—into direct violence (specific acts of sabotage), power finds the cheapest way to evade accountability.

In this game, David Hearn is cast as the 'enemy.' Trump attempts to manufacture a fact by citing a so-called 'highly credible' yet anonymous witness. The essence of this maneuver is the seizure of the right to interpret: as long as the damage is defined as 'intentional sabotage,' the inherent shoddiness of the project can be diluted into 'relatively inconsequential' flaws. It is a malicious narrative patchwork, using an individual's alleged 'crime' to cloak systemic incompetence.

The most ironic site of complicity is Jeanine Pirro's predicament. As a loyalist, she was expected to be the executor of this narrative, but when evidence pointed to the installation being a scam, she deferred to legal facts. Trump's rage—claiming she 'folded like a cheap umbrella'—reveals the unspoken rule of this complicity: under the logic of meta-violence, loyalty does not mean pursuing the truth, but unconditionally upholding the one who 'manufactures reality.' When a co-conspirator attempts a Just Expression, she becomes a target for removal.

The budget jump from $2M to $16M and the non-competitive bidding process are themselves forms of structural violence. These resources could have been transformed into genuine public goods; instead, they are a mess hidden under 'American flag blue.' This mechanism of 'redefining reality when reality refuses to cooperate' is identical to how he handles chip laws or GDP figures.
