没有犯罪的死亡，依然是暴力的结果Death Without Crime is Still the Result of Violence

性别 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-20 § 链接
当三名女性在深夜被出租车丢在海边并集体溺亡，'无犯罪'不等于'无暴力'。
When three women are dropped by taxi at the beach at night and drown, 'no crime' does not mean 'no violence'.

警方用 'no criminal involvement' 试图给这起悲剧画上句号。在法律的逻辑里，只要没有第三者施加物理伤害，这就是个单纯的意外或自杀。但如果用加尔通的暴力三角来看，这简直是 structural violence 的教科书案例：当 Actual（实际生存状态）远低于 Potential（本可达到的生命状态），其中的差额就是暴力。

三姐妹在深夜被出租车丢在海边，穿着日常服装，没有去过任何俱乐部或商店，然后集体进入大海。这种叙事极其诡异，它在 cultural 层面上被包装成一个 '悲剧'，但在 meta 层面上，它揭示了某种深层的绝望。一个家庭的三名女性在同一时间、同一地点选择或被迫走向死亡，这绝不是个案，而是某种结构性压抑的临界点。当黑人女性在现代社会面对的不仅是种族歧视，还有原初种族被殖民的深层潜意识时，大海成了她们唯一的出口。

警方提到的 'black communities' 的担忧是对的，但他们的关注点太窄，只在 direct 层面的 '是否被谋杀' 上打转。真正的暴力不在于有没有凶手，而在于什么样的社会结构能让三个成年女性在深夜集体走向深海。这种‘无犯罪’的结论，实际上是共谋者们在用法律的‘客观’来掩盖结构的残暴。只要没有证据证明有人推她们下去，系统就可以心安理得地宣布：这里没有暴力。

Police attempt to close the case with 'no criminal involvement'. In legal logic, if no third party inflicted physical harm, it is a mere accident or suicide. But through the lens of the Violence Triangle, this is a textbook case of structural violence: when Actual existence falls far below Potential, the gap is violence.

Three sisters, dropped off by taxi at the beach in everyday clothing, visiting no businesses, then collectively entering the sea. This narrative is eerie. While culturally packaged as a 'tragedy', at the meta level, it reveals a profound existential despair. The simultaneous death of three women from one family is not an isolated incident, but a tipping point of structural oppression. For Black women, facing both racial discrimination and the historical trauma of the Primal Race, the ocean became the only exit.

The police mention 'black communities' concerns, but their focus is too narrow, trapped in the direct layer of 'was there a murderer?'. The real violence isn't about the presence of a killer, but about what kind of social structure drives three adult women into the deep sea at midnight. This 'no crime' conclusion is a form of complicity, using legal 'objectivity' to mask structural brutality. As long as there is no evidence of a physical push, the system can comfortably declare: there was no violence here.
