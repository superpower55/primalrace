自由的定价权：当人权变成选票的筹码The Pricing of Liberty: When Human Rights Become Electoral Chips

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-27 § 链接
自由不是政府的施舍，而是权力在计算成本后的妥协。
Freedom is not a government grant, but a compromise made after calculating the cost of power.

Liberty 的这份民调揭示了一个极其讽刺的真相：在英国，言论自由的价值不再取决于它作为基本人权的 an intrinsic value，而取决于它在下一次大选中的 electoral value。当人权组织不得不通过“可能会丢掉选票”来警告工党政府时，这场博弈的逻辑已经从“公正的表达”变成了纯粹的政治算计。

从加尔通的暴力三角来看，这正是典型的 structural violence。政府通过《犯罪与警务法》增加警察权力，将“累积影响”这种模糊的词汇武器化，从而在法律层面合法地压缩 Potential 表达空间。而文化层面的共谋则更加精巧：通过将抗议定义为“仇恨行军”或“暴徒行为”，政府在认知入口处完成了对异见者的定罪，使直接的逮捕和禁言看起来像是为了“维护秩序”的必要之举。

最令人作呕的是那种“划清界限”的叙事。内政部声称在“表达观点”与“非法行为”之间有清晰的界限，但事实是，界限的定义权（pricing power）始终在权力手中。当持有“支持 Palestine Action”标语的人被大规模逮捕时，这种界限就成了一把可以随时伸缩的尺子。这本质上是一场关于“什么是事实”的制造权战争：政府定义谁是恐怖分子，谁是暴徒，然后用这个定义来合法化对人权的剥夺。

不要被这种“民意警示”带来的微小希望所欺骗。如果政府改变方向，那绝不是因为他们意识到了人权的神圣，而是因为在存在性战争的博弈中，限制言论的成本暂时超过了收益。真正的胜利应该是解释权的换手，而不是在权力的天平上，把自由当作一种可以讨价还价的商品。

Liberty's poll reveals a cynical truth: in the UK, the value of free speech is no longer determined by its intrinsic value as a human right, but by its electoral value in the next general election. When a human rights organization must warn the Labour government that it 'could cost votes,' the logic of the game has shifted from Just Expressions to pure political calculus.

Applying the Violence Triangle, this is textbook structural violence. The government weaponizes vague terms like 'cumulative impact' via the Crime and Policing Act to legally compress the potential space for expression. The complicity at the cultural layer is even more sophisticated: by labeling protests as 'hate marches' or 'thuggery,' the state pre-defines dissenters at the cognitive entry point, making direct arrests and silencing appear as necessary measures to 'maintain order.'

Most repulsive is the narrative of 'drawing a clear line.' The Home Office claims a distinction between expressing a view and unlawful behavior, but the pricing power over that line remains solely with the state. When thousands are arrested for placards supporting Palestine Action, the line becomes a retractable ruler. This is a war over the manufacture of facts: the state defines who is a terrorist or a thug, then uses that definition to legitimize the stripping of human rights.

Do not be naive about the 'wake-up call' of public opinion. If the government pivots, it won't be because they've rediscovered the sanctity of human rights, but because the cost of restriction has temporarily outweighed the benefit in the existential war of power. True victory requires a transfer of the power of interpretation, not treating freedom as a commodity to be bargained for on a political scale.
