眼泪之门：被当作筹码的生物墙The Gate of Tears: Biological Walls as Geopolitical Chips

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-12 § 链接
地缘政治的“战略要地”是元暴力的伪装，其本质是肉身被客体化为筹码。
The 'strategic importance' of geopolitics is a mask for meta-violence, reducing human bodies to mere bargaining chips.

红海海岸的5万难民，在新闻叙事里被简化为“人道主义危机”或“地缘博弈的受害者”。但如果用加尔通的暴力三角来看，这根本不是什么“突然的冲突”，而是一场极其典型的 structural violence。巴布曼代卜海峡（Bab al-Mandab）被命名为“眼泪之门”，这个名字在现代地缘政治中被 weaponized 成了一种浪漫化的悲剧叙事，掩盖了其背后冰冷的逻辑：在这个空间里，人类的生存权低于全球贸易的航道权。

这场战争的参与者——无论是胡塞武装、沙特、美国还是伊朗——全部在执行一种 masculine-centric narrative（男性中心叙事）。他们争夺的不是人民的福祉，而是对“入口”的控制权。在这种元暴力的驱动下，也门平民的肉身被彻底客体化，成了他们博弈桌上的筹码。当新闻在讨论油价上涨和航道封锁时，它实际上在共谋一种逻辑：即全球经济的波动比数万个具体生命的消亡更具有“战略意义”。

最残酷的在于，在这种大规模 displacement（流离失所）中，生物墙（Biological Wall）成为了最无情的筛选机制。虽然报道中提到了“家庭”，但我们知道，在逃难的摩托车和混乱的抢掠中，生理女性、儿童和老人是结构性最弱势的。他们不仅要面对直接的 shelling（炮击），还要在崩溃的社会秩序中承受针对女性身体的 secondary violence。这种暴力被掩盖在“战争必然”的文化叙事之下，使得人们习惯于将女性的苦难视为战争的背景板，而非战争的核心罪行。

所谓的“人道主义援助”在此时往往变成了另一种表演性让步。当援助组织在评估“准入权限”时，他们其实是在与一个由男性共谋者构建的暴力系统谈判。如果这个系统认为维持战争的成本低于维持和平的收益，那么这些所谓的“紧急需求”永远只能是杯水车薪。

The 50,000 refugees on the Red Sea coast are reduced in news narratives to 'humanitarian crises' or 'victims of geopolitical struggle.' Through the lens of the Violence Triangle, this is not a 'sudden conflict' but a textbook case of structural violence. The Bab al-Mandab strait, the 'Gate of Tears,' has had its name weaponized into a romanticized tragedy to mask a cold logic: in this space, the right to survive is subordinate to the right of global trade routes.

The actors—Houthis, Saudi Arabia, the US, and Iran—are all executing a masculine-centric narrative. They aren't fighting for people's well-being, but for control over the 'entrance.' Driven by this meta-violence, the bodies of Yemeni civilians are completely objectified as chips on a gambling table. When the news discusses rising oil prices and shipping blockades, it is complicit in a logic that suggests global economic fluctuations hold more 'strategic significance' than the extinction of tens of thousands of individual lives.

The most brutal part is that during this mass displacement, the Biological Wall becomes the most ruthless screening mechanism. While reports mention 'families,' we know that biological females, children, and the elderly are the most structurally vulnerable. They face not only direct shelling but also the secondary violence targeted at female bodies within a collapsed social order. This violence is hidden under a cultural narrative of 'war inevitability,' making the suffering of women a mere backdrop to war rather than its core crime.

So-called 'humanitarian aid' often becomes another form of performative concession. When aid organizations assess 'access,' they are essentially negotiating with a violent system built by masculine complicity. If this system finds the cost of maintaining war lower than the benefit of peace, these 'urgent needs' will remain nothing more than a drop in the bucket.
