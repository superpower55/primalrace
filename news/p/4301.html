以‘实用’之名，行‘剥夺’之实The 'Practicality' Scam: Stripping the Power to Think

哲学 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-20 § 链接
所谓的‘实用主义’教育，本质上是为底层制造廉价劳动力，为上位者垄断认知入口。
So-called 'vocationalism' is a weapon to manufacture cheap labor and monopolize the entry points of cognition.

Reform UK 这场闹剧精准地展示了什么是文化暴力的武器化。当 Richard Tice 和 Suella Braverman 攻击那些所谓的 “Mickey Mouse” 学位，尤其是对着 “Gender Studies” 啐口水时，他们并不是在讨论教育质量，而是在进行一场关于认知入口的清理行动。

在他们的叙事里，历史、语言和性别研究被定义为 “废话”，而挖掘战壕或从事 crypto 投机被定义为 “实用”。这套逻辑的本质是：剥夺结构性弱势者 “学习如何思考” 的能力。因为一旦你学会了分析结构、质疑权力、理解性别政治，你就不再是一个好用的、顺从的 apprentice，而是一个具有主体性的威胁。他们想把牛津大学变成技术学院，实际上是想把所有潜在的批判者变成高效的零件。

最讽刺的是这场表演的共谋者。Tice 标榜自己的 merit，却刻意隐瞒了 family firm 的背景，这种典型的 nepo baby 行为揭示了一个真相：他们定义的 “实用” 仅适用于底层。对于上位者，教育是用来构建阶级品味和权力网络的；而对于底层，教育应该是某种快速变现的技能培训。这种对教育定义的垄断，正是元暴力的体现——由男性中心叙事主导的权力阶层，决定谁有权思考，谁只能干活。

Suella Braverman 的表演则更像是一场记忆缺失的 scam。一个曾经的内阁大臣，现在跳出来指责前任政府让学生失望，这种毫无底线的 flip-flop 恰恰证明了他们并不在乎学生，只在乎如何通过制造敌对叙事来抢夺注意力空间。他们不是在提供教育方案，而是在制造一种名为 “常识” 的武器，用来合法化对知识阶层的清洗。

Reform UK's latest circus is a textbook case of the weaponisation of cultural violence. When Richard Tice and Suella Braverman attack 'Mickey Mouse' degrees and spit on 'Gender Studies,' they aren't debating educational quality; they are conducting a clearance operation of cognitive entry points.

In their narrative, history, languages, and gender studies are dismissed as 'bullshit,' while digging trenches or chasing dodgy crypto is hailed as 'practical.' The core of this logic is the deprivation of the ability to 'learn how to think' for the structurally disadvantaged. Once you learn to analyse structures, question power, and understand gender politics, you cease to be a compliant apprentice and become a threat with subjectivity. Their desire to turn Oxford into a technical college is an attempt to turn potential critics into efficient spare parts.

The complicity here is peak irony. Tice boasts of his 'merit' while erasing his family firm's legacy—a classic nepo baby move that reveals the truth: their definition of 'practicality' only applies to the bottom. For the elite, education is for building class taste and power networks; for the masses, it's mere skill-training for immediate monetization. This monopoly over the definition of education is the embodiment of meta-violence—a masculine-centric power structure deciding who gets to think and who must simply toil.

Suella Braverman's performance is a total scam of selective memory. A former cabinet minister now blaming the very government she served for 'letting students down' shows they don't care about students at all. They only care about seizing attention through hostile narratives. They aren't offering an educational alternative; they are manufacturing a weapon called 'common sense' to legitimise the purging of the intellectual class.
