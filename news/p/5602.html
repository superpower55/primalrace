Frieze 艺术展：把结构性暴力做成昂贵的景观Frieze: Turning Structural Violence into Expensive Spectacles

哲学 文化层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-17 § 链接
当暴力被转化为审美客体，它就成了权力的最高级共谋。
When violence is converted into aesthetic objects, it becomes the ultimate complicity of power.

Frieze 这种顶级的艺术博览会，本质上是一场关于“定义权”的博弈。这次展出的作品很有意思，它们精准地捕捉了当代社会的各种 violence，但这种捕捉方式本身就是一种 weaponization。艺术家们把 OnlyFans 的工业化性剥削、美国医疗系统的资本操纵、以及女性身体的被动客体化，全部打包成一个个精美的 installation。这在文化层面上完成了一次极其危险的置换：将 structural violence 转化为审美体验。

最讽刺的是 Yuli Serfaty 那些无头金属狗。它试图通过一种 surreal 的方式探讨女性身体被 devaluation 和 exploited 的极端状态，但当这个“被剥削的身体”被放置在昂贵的画廊展位里，成为收藏家们品评的“satirical edge”时，它就陷入了一个死循环：艺术在批判剥削，但艺术市场本身就在通过这种批判来完成资本增值。这就是典型的共谋 (complicity) —— 权力阶层通过消费“对权力的批判”来确认自己的文明与宽容。

至于那些被设计成“贞操锁”形状的 urinal cakes，这种把排泄与情色、禁锢与消费结合的表达，不过是利用了认知入口里的 erotic 叙事来制造噱头。它在试图挑衅，但这种挑衅在 Frieze 这种资本密集的场域里，最终只会变成一种高级的、带有阶级区分色彩的“品味”游戏。在这种语境下，真实的痛苦被抽离，只剩下一种名为“深刻”的表演性快感。

好在 Luce Turnier 这样被艺术史遗忘的女性主义叙事被重新挖掘，这在 meta 层面上是一次微小的解释权换手。但除此之外，大多数作品只是在重复一个逻辑：把暴力作为素材，将其 commodification，然后卖给那些制造暴力的人。这不是在拆穿枷锁，而是在给枷锁镀金。

Frieze, as a top-tier art fair, is essentially a game of defining the narrative. The artworks this year are fascinating; they precisely capture various forms of violence in contemporary society, but the act of capturing is itself a form of weaponization. Artists have packaged the industrial sexual exploitation of OnlyFans, the corporate manipulation of the US healthcare system, and the passive objectification of the female body into polished installations. Culturally, this achieves a dangerous substitution: converting structural violence into an aesthetic experience.

Most ironic are Yuli Serfaty’s headless metallic dogs. They attempt to explore the extreme state of the female body being devalued and exploited through a surreal lens. However, when this "exploited body" is placed in an expensive gallery booth to be critiqued as a "satirical edge" by collectors, it enters a death loop: art critiques exploitation, while the art market itself leverages that critique for capital appreciation. This is a textbook case of complicity—the power class confirms its own civility and tolerance by consuming the "critique of power."

As for the urinal cakes designed like chastity devices, this expression merely utilizes the erotic narrative as a cognitive entry point to create a gimmick. It attempts to provoke, but in a capital-dense environment like Frieze, such provocation inevitably devolves into a high-brow "taste" game used for class distinction. In this context, real suffering is stripped away, leaving only a performative pleasure labeled as "profundity."

Fortunately, the rediscovery of forgotten female narratives like Luce Turnier represents a minor shift in the meta-level of interpretation. Beyond that, however, most works repeat a single logic: use violence as raw material, commodify it, and sell it back to the very people who manufacture that violence. This is not breaking the chains; it is gilding them.
