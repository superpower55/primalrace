用AI防御AI：一场关于“可能性”的权力操弄AI for Defense: A Power Play in the Realm of Possibility

科技 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-27 § 链接
防御性AI并非救赎，而是权力在生物学层面的新一次共谋。
Defensive AI is not salvation, but a new complicity of power over biological existence.

这篇文章试图把AI在生物学领域的应用包装成一场“进攻”与“防御”的赛跑，但这种叙事本身就是一种典型的武器化表达。它通过制造一个名为“生物武器”的恐怖客体，将人们的注意力从“谁在掌控AI”转移到“AI能做什么”上。这种逻辑极其阴险：它承认AI能制造灾难，但随即提供一个由WHO等权力机构掌控的“防御性AI”作为唯一解。这本质上是在通过制造恐惧，来合法化对全球生物数据和认知入口的进一步垄断。

所谓的 Eios 系统，通过抓取社交媒体、公民闲谈和非正式报告来“提取信号”，这在结构层面上就是一次大规模的监控升级。它将全球个体的生物状态变成了可被算法筛选的“噪音”或“信号”。在这个过程中，个体的生物隐私被抹除，取而代之的是一个由权力中心定义的“公共卫生安全”叙事。这种“防御”的代价是，所有人的生物性表达都被纳入了一个全球性的监视网，而解释权依然死死地握在那些制定规则的人手中。

最荒谬的共谋在于，这篇文章的作者通过宣传“生物武器门槛降低”来制造紧迫感，从而为那些掌握最高端AI资源的权力阶层争取更多资源分配的优先权。这又是典型的元暴力逻辑：定义什么是“危险”，然后宣布自己是唯一的“救世主”。在这种叙事下，生物学不再是探索生命的科学，而变成了权力的延伸。所谓的“人道主义防御”不过是给生物殖民穿上的一件白大褂，而真正的生物墙，正在被这些所谓的“防御系统”重新定义和加固。

This piece attempts to frame AI's role in biological sciences as a race between "offense" and "defense," but this narrative is a textbook example of weaponized expression. By manufacturing the terror of "biological weapons," it shifts the focus from "who controls the AI" to "what AI can do." The logic is insidious: it acknowledges the potential for catastrophe only to position "defensive AI"—controlled by power centers like the WHO—as the sole solution. This is essentially using fear to legitimize a further monopoly over global biological data and cognitive entry points.

The so-called Eios system, which scrapes social media and "citizen chatter" to "pull signal from noise," is structurally a massive upgrade in surveillance. It transforms the biological state of global individuals into "noise" or "signals" to be filtered by an algorithm. In this process, individual biological privacy is erased, replaced by a "public health security" narrative defined by the center of power. The cost of this "defense" is that all biological expressions are absorbed into a global surveillance net, while the power of interpretation remains firmly with the rule-makers.

The ultimate complicity lies in how the author uses the "lowered barrier to biological weapons" to create urgency, thereby securing priority resource allocation for the power elite who own the most advanced AI. This is the core of meta-violence: defining what is "dangerous" and then declaring oneself the only "savior." In this narrative, biology is no longer a science of life, but an extension of power. This "humanitarian defense" is merely a white lab coat draped over biological colonialism, while the actual biological walls are being redefined and reinforced by these very "defense systems."
