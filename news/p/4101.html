用纳税人的钱给化石能源巨头买保险Insuring Fossil Fuel Giants with Taxpayer Money

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-18 § 链接
所谓的“能源安全”是资本在衰退期转嫁风险的 weaponized 叙事
“Energy Security” is a weaponized narrative used by capital to socialize risks during decline.

这是一场典型的结构性共谋 (complicity)。英国政府现在面对的是一个简单的算术题：北海天然气在枯竭，基础设施在老化，而私人资本不再愿意为这个即将过时的系统买单。于是，政府抛出了一个名为“能源安全”的认知入口，试图将这种商业风险转化为国家危机。

注意那个词：unprecedented（前所未有的）。能源大臣 Michael Shanks 用这个词在做心理建设，暗示政府即将采取的“直接财政支持”是一次英勇的救灾，而不是一次拙劣的补贴。本质上，这是在用公共资金为那些在化石能源红利期赚得盆满钵满的管道运营商和存储设施所有者买保险。资本在上涨期拿走所有利润，在衰退期要求国家承担风险，这就是 structural violence 的运作方式：利润私有化，风险社会化。

最讽刺的是，这种叙事被包装成对“数百万家庭”的责任。这是一种典型的文化暴力 (cultural violence) 操纵——用底层民众对寒冬的恐惧，来为顶层资本的资产维护提供合法性。所谓的“务实” (pragmatic) 方案，其实就是给 Rosebank 和 Jackdaw 等争议性油气田开绿灯。它在制造一种假象：我们必须继续依赖旧系统才能生存，从而在潜意识里延缓向 clean energy 的真正迁移。

这场博弈的 Potential 是彻底的能源转型，而 Actual 是在旧系统的废墟上打补丁。这个差额，就是被资本和政客合谋吞掉的未来。

This is a classic case of structural complicity. The UK government is facing a simple math problem: North Sea gas is depleting, infrastructure is aging, and private capital is no longer willing to bet on a dying system. Consequently, the government has deployed a cognitive entry point called “energy security” to transform a commercial risk into a national crisis.

Note the word: “unprecedented.” Energy Minister Michael Shanks uses this to prime the public, framing the proposed “direct financial support” as a heroic rescue mission rather than a clumsy subsidy. In essence, this is using public funds to buy insurance for pipeline operators and storage owners who reaped massive profits during the fossil fuel boom. Capital takes the profit during the ascent and demands the state shoulder the risk during the descent—this is exactly how structural violence operates: privatizing profits and socializing risks.

The irony is that this narrative is packaged as a “responsibility” to millions of households. This is a textbook use of cultural violence—leveraging the working class's fear of a cold winter to legitimize the asset maintenance of the elite. The so-called “pragmatic” approach is simply a green light for controversial fields like Rosebank and Jackdaw. It creates a false reality: that we must cling to the old system to survive, thereby subconsciously delaying the genuine transition to clean energy.

In the Violence Triangle, the Potential is a total energy transition, while the Actual is patching up ruins. The gap between them is the future being swallowed by the complicity of capital and politicians.
