Su Pollard 的亮片裙与存在性战争的胜利Su Pollard's Sequins and the Victory in Existential War

性别 文化层 · 结构层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-17 § 链接
当一个人决定不再扮演被定义的角色，表达就成了夺回主体性的武器。
When one stops playing the assigned role, expression becomes a weapon to reclaim subjectivity.

这篇关于 Su Pollard 的访谈，表面上是典型的名人回忆录式温情叙事，但如果用存在性战争的视角去看，这其实是一个结构性弱势者通过“最优解表达”赢得主体性回归的绝佳样本。

Su 从一个诺丁汉工人阶级家庭出身，面对的是一个极其刻板的阶级与性别共谋场域。在那个环境下，女性的表达通常被限制在“温柔”、“得体”或“辅助者”的角色中。但 Su 选择了最激进的路径：她通过亮片裙、夸张的眼镜、甚至在包里装番茄和派对喇叭，构建了一套完全独立于主流审美之外的符号系统。这不仅是古灵精怪，而是一种对“生物墙”和“阶级墙”的暴力拆除。她拒绝被定义为“工人阶级演员”或“中年女性”，而是通过这种高度可见的、近乎挑衅的表达，强行在公共空间为自己夺取了定义权。

最值得关注的是她对生育的拒绝。在 80 年代的父权结构下，女性的价值被高度绑定在生育能力上。Su 直截了当地说“我为什么要做母亲？”，这在本质上是对一种结构性暴力的拒绝。她意识到，一旦进入母亲这个角色，她的主体性将被“养育”这个巨大的黑盒吞噬，导致她在公共空间（事业）的消失。她选择将激情投射给 showbiz 而非男人，这是她在博弈中找到的真.最优解——通过切断与传统性别角色的绑定，确保自己的存在性不被让渡。

即便在面对前夫的性倾向被小报攻击时，她依然能保持一种近乎残酷的坦然。因为她已经完成了从“被定义者”到“定义者”的转变。当一个女性不再恐惧被视为“怪异”或“不正常”时，外界所有的文化暴力（cultural violence）都失去了着力点。Su Pollard 的生活就是一场长达 52 年的演出，而这场演出的最高成就，就是她让自己的“真.最优解”与她的生活表达完全重合了。

This interview with Su Pollard looks like a typical warm celebrity memoir, but through the lens of Existential War, it is a prime example of a structurally disadvantaged individual winning back their subjectivity through Optimal Expression.

Coming from a working-class family in Nottingham, Su faced a rigid field of class and gender complicity. In that environment, feminine expression was typically restricted to roles like "gentle," "proper," or "assistant." Su chose the most radical path: she built a symbolic system entirely independent of mainstream aesthetics—sequined dresses, oversized glasses, and carrying tomatoes and party blowers in her bag. This isn't just quirkiness; it is a violent demolition of the biological and class walls. By being highly visible and almost provocative, she forcibly seized the right to define herself in the public sphere.

Most notable is her rejection of motherhood. In the patriarchal structure of the 80s, a woman's value was heavily tied to her fertility. Su's blunt question, "Why would I want to be a mother?", is essentially a refusal of structural violence. She realized that entering the role of a mother would allow her subjectivity to be swallowed by the black box of "caregiving," leading to her disappearance from the public sphere. Choosing passion for showbiz over men was her True Optimal Expression—ensuring her existence was not surrendered by binding herself to traditional gender roles.

Even when tabloids attacked her ex-husband's sexuality, she remained remarkably unfazed. She had already completed the transition from the "defined" to the "definer." When a woman no longer fears being seen as "weird" or "abnormal," all external cultural violence loses its grip. Su Pollard's life is a 52-year performance, and the greatest achievement of this show is that her True Optimal Expression has completely merged with her lived expression.
