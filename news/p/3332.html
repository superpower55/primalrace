当“自杀”成为一种制度性掩体When 'Suicide' Becomes a Institutional Shield

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-07 § 链接
法律对暴力的上限封顶，是对谋杀的官方授权。
Capping the punishment for violence is an official authorization for murder.

Liza Shams 的脸被击碎了，但法医报告却写着“服用鼠药”。在阿富汗，这种叙事置换不是医疗失误，而是一种精准的 structural violence。当一个国家的司法系统将家暴的惩罚上限封顶为 15 天监禁，而将女性寻求庇护的行为定义为犯罪时，法律已经不再是保护机制，而是一本关于如何高效且合法地通过肉体消灭来解决“家庭问题”的说明书。

这是一个典型的 meta violence 闭环：塔利班通过废除女性事务部和女性法官，垄断了对“事实”的解释权。在这种叙事中，女性的身体被彻底客体化为男性的私产，而“自杀”则成了最完美的 weaponized 概念——它将结构性的屠杀伪装成个体的心理崩溃，让施暴者在共谋者的掩护下获得绝对的 impunity。

最令人作呕的共谋发生在法律细节里：母亲不能直接为女儿提起诉讼，必须将代理权交给丈夫。这意味着，在这个系统里，受害者必须通过施暴者才能触达正义。这根本不是什么宗教传统，而是一场关于生存空间的彻底殖民。女性在这个国家不仅被剥夺了公共空间，甚至被剥夺了作为“人”的法律主体性。

Liza 在录音里说她感到“窒息”，这种窒息感来自生物墙之外的、由权力编织的巨网。当真相被掩盖在“自杀”的标签下，每一个被定义为自杀的女性，其实都是被这个结构性暴力机器谋杀的。

Liza Shams had her face beaten in, yet the forensic report cited 'rat poison.' In Afghanistan, this narrative displacement is not a medical error; it is a precise form of structural violence. When a state's judicial system caps the punishment for domestic abuse at 15 days while criminalizing a woman's search for refuge, the law ceases to be a protection mechanism. It becomes a manual on how to efficiently and legally eliminate 'domestic problems' through physical erasure.

This is a classic meta-violence loop: the Taliban, by dissolving the Ministry of Women's Affairs and removing female judges, has monopolized the power to define 'fact.' In this narrative, the female body is completely objectified as male property, and 'suicide' becomes a weaponized concept—masking structural massacre as individual psychological collapse, granting the perpetrator absolute impunity under the cover of complicity.

The most sickening complicity lies in the legal minutiae: a mother cannot directly pursue justice for her daughter but must transfer power of attorney to the husband. In this system, the victim must pass through the perpetrator to reach justice. This is not 'religious tradition'; it is a total colonization of existential space. Women in this country are stripped not only of public space but of their legal subjectivity as human beings.

Liza spoke of 'suffocating' in her recordings. This suffocation comes from a web of power woven far beyond the biological wall. When truth is buried under the label of 'suicide,' every woman so defined is, in fact, murdered by the machinery of structural violence.
