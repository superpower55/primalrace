马耳他的“电影天堂”：一场关于主体性出卖的税务 scamMalta's Movie Paradise: A Tax Scam of Subjectivity

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-10 § 链接
当一个国家被工程化为好莱坞的布景板，它就成了文化殖民的共谋者。
When a nation is engineered into a Hollywood backdrop, it becomes a complicit agent of cultural colonization.

马耳他把自己的国土变成了一个巨大的、可租赁的“视觉插件”。在好莱坞的叙事里，马耳他可以是罗马，可以是西西里，可以是加勒比海，也可以是任何一个被定义为“异域”的地方。这种“多样性”其实是一种极端的抹除：马耳他的真实地理与历史被 weaponized 成了一种廉价的审美资源，用来服务于外部的认知入口。

这本质上是一场关于主体性的博弈。马耳他政府通过极其慷慨的 tax incentives（现金返还）诱导好莱坞入驻，试图在经济维度上获得“最优解”。但这个最优解是假的。当一个国家的电影工业被定义为“服务业”而非“创作业”时，它就陷入了 structural violence。好莱坞拿走了故事的解释权，只给马耳他留下了一些临时工的薪水和被损坏的百年雕像。

最讽刺的是这种内部的共谋：当地的专业技术人员因为习惯了好莱坞的定价权，导致本土导演再也雇不起自己的摄影师。这是一种典型的元暴力——外部的霸权叙事不仅垄断了屏幕，还通过改变当地的经济生态，物理性地扼杀了本土叙事产生的可能性。本土创作者在自己的土地上变成了异乡人，因为他们的“表达”在好莱坞的资金量面前毫无竞争力。

马耳他以为自己在玩一场“军备竞赛”，但实际上它只是在为全球文化霸权提供一个更便宜的仓库。当一个地方只剩下“被拍摄”的价值，而失去了“讲述”的能力，它就从一个主权国家降格为了一个巨大的、没有灵魂的 movie set。

Malta has effectively turned its entire territory into a giant, rentable "visual plugin." In Hollywood's narrative, Malta can be Rome, Sicily, the Caribbean, or any place defined as "exotic." This supposed "versatility" is actually an extreme erasure: Malta's actual geography and history are weaponized as cheap aesthetic resources to serve external cognitive entry points.

This is essentially a game of subjectivity. The Maltese government seeks a "optimal expression" in economic terms through aggressive tax incentives. But this is a fake optimal. When a nation's film industry is defined as a "service sector" rather than a "creative sector," it falls into structural violence. Hollywood retains the power of interpretation, leaving Malta with nothing but temporary wages and damaged century-old statues.

The most ironic part is the internal complicity: local crews, accustomed to Hollywood's pricing power, have become unaffordable for domestic directors. This is a textbook example of meta-violence—external hegemonic narratives not only monopolize the screen but physically stifle the possibility of local narratives by altering the economic ecosystem. Local creators become aliens in their own land because their expressions have no competitiveness against Hollywood's capital.

Malta believes it is winning an "arms race," but it is merely providing a cheaper warehouse for global cultural hegemony. When a place retains only the value of being "shot" and loses the capacity to "tell," it is downgraded from a sovereign state to a giant, soulless movie set.
