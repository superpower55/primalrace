用战争威胁兜售的“和平”scamThe 'Peace' Scam Sold Through War Threats

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-03 § 链接
外交谈判不是为了公正，而是强者在博弈中通过制造恐慌来定价。
Diplomacy is not about justice, but about the powerful pricing their terms through manufactured panic.

特朗普的表演逻辑极其简单：先制造一个随时会爆炸的 direct violence 预期，然后通过“决定暂缓攻击”来扮演救世主。这种叙事不是为了和平，而是将“不被攻击”包装成一种恩赐，从而在随后的谈判中夺取更高的定价权。这是一种典型的 weaponized expression，通过操纵认知入口，让全球市场和地缘政治参与者在恐惧中接受他定义的“ deal”。

在这场存在性战争中，霍尔木兹海峡的通航权被异化成了筹码。伊朗通过关闭海峡制造结构性压力，而特朗普则利用这种压力来换取对核计划的控制权。双方在博弈，但真正被当作耗材的是全球能源价格和那些在红海、波斯湾附近战战兢兢的船员。这种“外交”本质上是两个权力中心在进行一次关于谁能更有效地威胁他人的共谋。

最讽刺的是，这种叙事习惯性地将“安全”与“强权”绑定。无论是特朗普的社交媒体宣言，还是以色列能源部那句“无论是否有协议，我们都会打击”，都在强化一个元暴力逻辑：只有通过更强大的暴力机器，才能换取所谓的秩序。在这种 masculine-centric narrative 下，所谓的“外交突破”不过是暴力机器在按下快门前的短暂停顿，而一个公正的、互不掠夺的共识，在这些权力玩家的字典里根本不存在。

Trump's performance logic is elementary: create an imminent expectation of direct violence, then play the savior by 'deciding to hold off' on attacks. This narrative isn't about peace; it's about packaging 'not being attacked' as a gift to seize higher pricing power in subsequent negotiations. It is a textbook weaponisation of expression, manipulating the cognitive entry point to force global markets and geopolitical actors to accept his definition of a 'deal' under duress.

In this existential war, the accessibility of the Strait of Hormuz has been alienated into a bargaining chip. Iran creates structural pressure by closing the strait, while Trump leverages that pressure to trade for control over nuclear capabilities. Both sides are gaming the system, but the actual consumables are global energy prices and the crews of tankers trembling in the Red Sea and the Gulf. This 'diplomacy' is essentially a complicity between two power centers over who can more effectively threaten the other.

The most cynical part is how this narrative habitually binds 'security' to 'hegemony.' From Trump's social media proclamations to the Israeli Energy Minister's vow that 'we will strike' regardless of agreements, they reinforce a meta-violence logic: only through a more powerful violence machine can 'order' be achieved. In this masculine-centric narrative, a 'diplomatic breakthrough' is merely a brief pause before the shutter clicks. A just expression—a consensus of mutual non-predation—simply does not exist in the lexicon of these power players.
