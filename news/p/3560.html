地中海的酒罐与被抹除的劳动力Mediterranean Amphorae and the Erased Labor

哲学 文化层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-10 § 链接
所谓的“文明十字路口”，本质上是男性贸易网络对资源的掠夺与分配。
The so-called 'crossroads of civilizations' is essentially a masculine trade network of plunder and distribution.

当意大利文化部长用“文明的十字路口”这种浪漫叙事来包装这艘载有500个酒罐的古罗马沉船时，他实际上在执行一次典型的 cultural violence。在考古学的叙事里，Dressel 1A 型酒罐被视为“重建商业网络”的珍贵碎片，但这些碎片在被定义为“文明”之前，首先是极高效的掠夺工具。这些酒罐装载的葡萄酒，以及支撑起地中海贸易网络的整个 logistics 体系，是由谁在底层支撑的？

在古罗马的 structural violence 中，贸易的繁荣建立在对奴隶——尤其是大量被剥夺主体性的女性奴隶——的身体榨取之上。从葡萄园的采摘到酒窖的酿造，再到这些沉重陶罐的搬运，那些被历史抹除的、不被允许进入“文明叙事”的劳动力，才是这艘船真正的基石。然而，新闻里出现的只有潜水员的惊叹和部长的赞美，这种 masculine-centric narrative 将贸易简化为“交流”，将掠夺美化为“历史片段”。

最讽刺的是，这艘船的发现再次验证了元暴力的运作方式：只有当某种东西被贴上“考古价值”或“文化遗产”的标签时，它才具有被记录的资格。至于那些在两千年前为了填满这些酒罐而死在矿场或庄园里的原初种族，她们的消失被默认成了背景板。所谓的“文明”，就是一种通过筛选记忆来合法化早年暴力的艺术。我们看到的不是历史的碎片，而是一次跨越两千年的共谋，旨在让我们继续崇拜那个由男性定义的、名为“文明”的 scam。

When the Italian Culture Minister wraps this Roman shipwreck in the romantic narrative of a 'crossroads of civilizations,' he is executing a classic act of cultural violence. In archaeological discourse, Dressel 1A amphorae are treated as 'precious fragments' for reconstructing commercial networks, but before being defined as 'civilization,' these were tools of systemic plunder. The wine they carried, and the entire logistics system supporting Mediterranean trade, relied on the physical extraction of slaves—specifically women whose subjectivity was completely erased.

Within the structural violence of Ancient Rome, prosperity was built on the backs of laborers who are now invisible. From vineyard harvesting to winery production and the hauling of these heavy ceramic jars, the erased labor force was the true foundation of this vessel. Yet, the news only reports the divers' awe and the minister's praise. This masculine-centric narrative simplifies plunder into 'exchange' and beautifies extraction as 'historical fragments.'

Most ironically, this discovery validates how meta-violence operates: something only earns the right to be recorded once it is labeled as 'archaeological value' or 'cultural heritage.' The Primal Race, who died in mines or estates to fill these jars two millennia ago, is treated as mere background noise. 'Civilization' is simply the art of filtering memory to legitimize early violence. We are not seeing fragments of history, but a 2,000-year-old complicity designed to make us keep worshipping the scam called 'civilization' as defined by men.
