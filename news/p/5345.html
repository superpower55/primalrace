用失效的法律报警：权力的傲慢与叙事陷阱Reporting as a 'Crime': The Hubris of Power and the Narrative Trap

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-30 § 链接
当权力试图通过定义“犯罪”来掩盖事实，法律就成了权力的PR工具。
When power defines 'crime' to mask facts, law becomes a PR tool for the elite.

一个法官试图用一条已经废除的法律去起诉记者，这简直是今年最精彩的权力幽默。Kimya Holmes 法官的行为揭示了一个残酷的事实：在很多掌握权力的人眼中，法律不是用来约束自己的准绳，而是用来恐吓异见者的 weaponized tool。她并不在乎法律是否真实有效，她在乎的是通过“刑事投诉”这个动作，在文化层制造一种“记者在犯罪”的叙事，从而将公众的注意力从那栋价值 76.5 万美元的捐赠房转移到对“隐私”的讨论上。

这不仅是个案，而是一场典型的 structural violence。从法庭工作人员给记者戴上手铐，到最高法院法官通过诽谤诉讼赢取 30 万美元，路易斯安那州的司法系统正在构建一个共谋场域 (complicity field)。在这个场域里，法官、司法行政官和部分执法部门共同协作，试图重新定义什么是“事实”，什么是“合法”。他们试图通过制造恐惧，让媒体在报道权力腐败之前先进行自我审查 (self-censorship)。

最讽刺的是，这位法官依赖的“法律依据”竟然来自一个未更新的政府网站。这揭示了元暴力 (meta violence) 的一个细节：权力者习惯于垄断解释权，甚至当他们面对的是过时的、错误的解释时，只要这个解释能服务于他们的利益，他们就会将其视作真理。这种“现实不配合就重新定义现实”的逻辑，与某些科技巨头宣布新定律来掩盖能力不足如出一辙。这本质上是一场关于“谁拥有定义权”的存在性战争。

A judge filing a police complaint based on a repealed law is the year's most exquisite piece of power-humor. Judge Kimya Holmes's actions reveal a brutal truth: for many in power, the law is not a yardstick for self-restraint, but a weaponized tool to intimidate dissenters. She doesn't care if the law is actually in effect; she cares about the act of 'filing a criminal complaint' to manufacture a cultural narrative that the journalist is a criminal, shifting public attention from a $765,000 donated house to a debate over 'privacy.'

This is no isolated incident, but a manifestation of structural violence. From courthouse deputies handcuffing journalists to a Supreme Court justice winning $300,000 in a libel suit, Louisiana's judicial system is constructing a complicity field. In this field, judges, administrators, and law enforcement collaborate to redefine 'fact' and 'legality.' They aim to instill fear, forcing the media into self-censorship before they dare report on systemic corruption.

The peak of irony is that the judge relied on an outdated government website. This exposes a nuance of meta violence: the powerful are accustomed to monopolizing the right of interpretation. Even when faced with an obsolete or erroneous interpretation, they embrace it as truth as long as it serves their interests. This logic of 'redefining reality when reality doesn't cooperate' mirrors how some tech giants announce new 'laws' to mask their lack of actual capability. At its core, this is an existential war over who owns the power to define.
