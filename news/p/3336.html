反恐名单上的生物墙与元暴力的循环The Biological Wall on Terror Lists and the Cycle of Meta-Violence

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-07 § 链接
反恐机制是对‘异常’的定义权，而定义权本身就是一种元暴力。
Anti-terror mechanisms are the power to define 'abnormality', and that power is itself a form of meta-violence.

Prevent 计划的数据再次证明，国家机器在定义‘极端主义’时，本质上是在进行一场关于‘正常’与‘异常’的认知战争。42% 的极右翼举报增长，表面上是针对仇恨犯罪的防御，但深层结构揭示的是一种危险的叙事闭环：当权力机构掌握了‘激进化’ (radicalisation) 的定义权，任何不符合主流中产叙事的表达，都有可能被武器化为‘恐怖威胁’。

最令人不安的细节在于那些被标记为‘involuntary celibate’ (incel) 的群体。incel 并不是一种简单的政治意识形态，而是一种典型的男性中心叙事 (masculine-centric narrative) 的崩塌产物。当这群男性发现自己无法通过扮演‘强者’或‘供养者’来兑现父权制承诺的性资源与权力时，他们将这种挫败感转化为对女性的仇恨。这正是元暴力的逻辑：他们不认为自己是施暴者，而认为自己是‘被剥夺者’。这种将自身客体化的扭曲心理，最终通过直接暴力 (direct violence) 作用于女性身体。

而 Prevent 计划中 18% 的自闭症患者比例，则揭示了 structural violence 的另一面。神经多样性 (neurodiversity) 带来的表达差异，在缺乏共情的官僚体系中被简化为‘可疑迹象’。这是一种典型的共谋：安全部门与社会规范共同协作，将‘不善于社交’或‘思维异质’的人群标记为潜在威胁。在这种机制下，生物墙不仅是身体的，更是认知的——如果你不具备某种‘标准’的社交表达，你就在安全审查的雷达上成了个红点。

所谓的‘保护公众安全’，往往是 cultural violence 的伪装。当 56% 的被举报者根本没有明确的意识形态时，这个计划实际上变成了一个巨大的‘异类收集箱’。它不旨在消除暴力，而旨在通过建立一份秘密名单，将那些无法被现有权力结构消化的人员进行分类和管控。

The Prevent program data proves once again that when the state defines 'extremism', it is engaging in a cognitive war over 'normalcy' versus 'abnormality'. The 42% rise in far-right referrals appears to be a defense against hate crime, but the structural layer reveals a dangerous narrative loop: when authorities monopolize the definition of 'radicalisation', any expression deviating from the mainstream middle-class narrative can be weaponized as a 'terror threat'.

The most disturbing detail is the inclusion of 'incel' extremism. Inceldom is not a simple political ideology, but a product of the collapse of the masculine-centric narrative. When these men find they can no longer redeem the sexual resources and power promised by the patriarchy by playing the 'strong man' or 'provider', they transform this frustration into misogyny. This is the core logic of meta-violence: they do not see themselves as perpetrators, but as 'deprived' victims. This distorted psychology of self-objectification eventually manifests as direct violence against female bodies.

Furthermore, the fact that 18% of referrals are autistic reveals another facet of structural violence. The expressive differences brought by neurodiversity are simplified into 'suspicious signs' by an unempathetic bureaucratic system. This is a clear case of complicity: security agencies and social norms collaborate to mark those with non-standard social expressions as potential threats. Here, the biological wall is not just physical, but cognitive—if you lack 'standard' social expression, you become a red dot on the security radar.

Claims of 'keeping the public safe' are often a camouflage for cultural violence. With 56% of referrals having no identified ideology, the program has effectively become a giant 'outcast collection box'. It does not aim to eliminate violence, but to categorize and control those who cannot be digested by the existing power structure through a secretive list.
