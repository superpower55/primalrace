钱包的“性别分类”是一场低级共谋The 'Gendered' Wallet is a Low-Level Complicity

哲学 文化层 Wirecutter: Reviews for the Real World ↗ 2026-08-03 § 链接
用营销定义的性别界限，掩盖了功能性表达的统一。
Marketing-defined gender boundaries mask the unity of functional expression.

Wirecutter 这篇购物指南试图表现得非常“进步”：它在正文里轻飘飘地写道，虽然一名作者负责测试“面向女性”的款式，另一名负责“面向男性”的，但“这里的钱包适合任何喜欢它们的人”。这是一种典型的表演性让步。它在口头上解构性别，但在实际操作中依然严格遵守父权制的分类逻辑，将产品预设为两个对立的阵营。

这种分类本质上是 cultural violence 的一种微小残留。所谓的“女性款”往往意味着更多的颜色、更复杂的拉链结构（为了承载更多杂物）或特定的曲线设计；而“男性款”则被定义为极简、耐操、或者某种所谓的“粗犷美学”。这种定义权被商业品牌垄断，通过认知入口告诉消费者：你的性别决定了你应该使用什么样的工具。这不仅是营销 scam，更是一种对主体性表达的限制。

最讽刺的是，当他们推荐那些所谓的“中立”极简款时，依然在用“Dad bait”这种词来嘲讽男性对功能的执念。这说明在他们的叙事里，性别依然是最高优先级的标签。一个钱包的功能是承载货币和凭证，这在生物学上没有任何性别差异。将一个皮革制品强行切割为男/女款，就是为了在存在性战争中通过制造“差异”来创造更多的消费需求。

真正的公正表达应该是：这里有 45 种不同容量、材质和审美的工具，请根据你的实际需求选择。而不是在分好组的笼子里告诉用户：你可以跨笼子挑选，但笼子依然在那儿。

This Wirecutter guide attempts to perform a 'progressive' stance. It casually mentions that while one author tested styles 'marketed toward women' and another 'geared toward men,' the picks are 'for anyone who likes ’em.' This is a classic performance of concession. While it verbally deconstructs gender, the actual methodology strictly adheres to the patriarchal logic of binary classification, presupposing two opposing camps.

This classification is a residue of cultural violence. 'Women's styles' are defined by more colors, complex zippers for 'clutter,' or specific curves; 'Men's styles' are coded as minimalist, rugged, or possessing a certain 'masculine' aesthetic. This power of definition is monopolized by brands, using cognitive entry points to tell consumers that their gender determines the tools they should use. It is not just a marketing scam, but a restriction on the expression of subjectivity.

The irony peaks when they describe certain functional features as 'dad bait.' This proves that in their narrative, gender remains the primary label. The function of a wallet is to hold currency and IDs—a task with zero biological gender difference. Forcing a leather product into male/female categories is simply a way to manufacture demand by creating artificial differences in the existential war of consumption.

Just Expression would be: 'Here are 45 tools with different capacities, materials, and aesthetics; choose based on your actual needs.' Not telling users they can 'cross-shop' while keeping the cages firmly in place.
