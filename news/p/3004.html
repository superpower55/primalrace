Unisex 并不是中立，而是男性的默认值Unisex is Not Neutral; It's the Masculine Default

性别 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-29 § 链接
所谓的 'Unisex' 往往是男性身体标准的霸权延伸。
The term 'Unisex' is often just a mask for the hegemony of the masculine phenotype.

这篇文章在讨论超马装备时，轻描淡写地使用了 'unisex' 这个词。在消费主义的叙事里，unisex 被包装成一种包容，但本质上它是一次极其典型的 meta violence：将男性的生物表型（Phenotype）设定为系统的默认基准，而将女性身体定义为需要通过 'Women's fit' 才能被兼容的 '特例'。

看看作者的测试逻辑：他用自己的身体（一个生理男性）去跑数百英里，然后得出结论。当他提到 Nathan Pinnacle 有 'women's fit' 时，潜台词是：这个产品首先是为男性设计的，如果女性觉得不舒服，可以去买那个『针对女性修改过』的次级版本。这种认知入口的霸权在于，它让女性在选择装备时，首先面对的是一个不适配的『标准』，然后才在边缘地带寻找补丁。

这不仅是产品设计问题，而是一场关于存在性的博弈。当一个行业默认『通用』=『男性』时，女性在公共运动空间中的主体性就被消解了。她们必须通过扮演『适配者』来获得功能性，或者忍受不适。这种 structural violence 隐藏在『专业测评』的客观外壳下，通过共谋让人们相信：只要有 Women's 选项，这就是公正的表达。事实上，真正的公正应该是打破『默认值』的垄断。

This article discusses ultra-running gear while casually deploying the term 'unisex.' In the narrative of consumerism, unisex is packaged as inclusivity, but it is essentially a textbook example of meta violence: setting the masculine phenotype as the system's default baseline, while defining the female body as an 'exception' that requires a 'Women's fit' to be compatible.

Look at the author's testing logic: he uses his own body (a biological male) to run hundreds of miles and then derives his conclusions. When he mentions that the Nathan Pinnacle has a 'women's fit,' the subtext is clear: the product is designed for men first, and if women find it uncomfortable, they can opt for a secondary version 'modified' for them. The hegemony of this cognitive entry point lies in forcing women to first encounter a non-fitting 'standard' before seeking patches on the margins.

This is not merely a design flaw; it is an existential war. When an industry defaults 'universal' to 'masculine,' the subjectivity of women in public sporting spaces is erased. They must either perform as 'adapters' to gain functionality or endure discomfort. This structural violence is hidden beneath the objective veneer of 'professional reviews,' utilizing complicity to make us believe that as long as there is a 'Women's' option, the expression is just. In reality, true justice requires dismantling the monopoly of the 'default.'
