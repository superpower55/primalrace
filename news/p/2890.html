所谓的“战争策略”不过是结构性失职的遮羞布The 'War Strategy' is Merely a Shroud for Structural Failure

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-27 § 链接
将制度性缺失包装成“战争”，是典型的用叙事掩盖结构暴力的共谋。
Packaging systemic failure as 'war' is a classic complicity of using narrative to mask structural violence.

当森林管理人员用“战争策略” (war strategy) 来形容这次灭火行动时，他实际上在完成一次潜意识的共谋：通过将自然灾害“军事化”，把原本属于政府的制度性失职，转化为一种英雄主义的叙事。这种叙事最阴险的地方在于，它让人们关注于消防员的“疲惫”和志愿者的“忙碌”，而忽略了最核心的 structural violence —— 为什么在2026年，一个国家公园的生存竟然要依赖于“运气好”地雇佣私人直升机？

这正是典型的 Potential − Actual 的差额。Potential 是一个现代国家应有的公共应急机制（如当地人要求的政府合同直升机），而 Actual 是一个依赖私人承包商、在风起时迅速失控的补丁系统。这个差额就是暴力，它直接作用于那些无法逃离的小型哺乳动物、昆虫，以及被告知“已控制”却在深夜被紧急疏散的村民。

英国政府在事后提供军事支持，却声称没有专项灭火直升机，这种表演性让步 (performative concession) 毫无意义。它试图通过“提供帮助”的姿态来掩盖其在资源分配上的长期傲慢。当公共安全被碎片化为“私人运营商的拼凑”时，所谓的“协同合作”不过是在一个崩塌的结构里互助搬砖，而定义这个结构的人，依然在享受着不作为的特权。

When forestry managers describe the firefighting effort as a 'war strategy,' they are performing a subconscious act of complicity: by 'militarizing' a natural disaster, they transform systemic institutional failure into a narrative of heroism. The most insidious part of this narrative is that it directs public attention toward the 'exhaustion' of firefighters and the 'bustle' of volunteers, while erasing the core structural violence —— why, in 2026, does the survival of a national park depend on the 'luck' of hiring private helicopters?

This is a textbook case of the Violence Triangle: Violence = Potential − Actual. The Potential is a modern state's public emergency mechanism (such as the government-contracted helicopters locals demanded), while the Actual is a patched-up system reliant on private contractors that collapses the moment the wind shifts. This gap is the violence, exerted directly upon the small mammals and insects that cannot escape, and the villagers who were told the fire was 'contained' only to be evacuated in the dead of night.

The UK government's offer of military support, while claiming no access to specialized firefighting aircraft, is a performative concession. It attempts to mask long-term arrogance in resource allocation through a gesture of 'help.' When public safety is fragmented into a 'patchwork of private operators,' the celebrated 'coordinated effort' is nothing more than mutual aid while moving bricks within a collapsing structure, while those who define the structure continue to enjoy the privilege of inaction.
