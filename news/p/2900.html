动量的伪装与被消耗的肉身The Illusion of Momentum and the Consumed Body

国际 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-27 § 链接
战争叙事中的“动量”是权力的幻觉，而肉身的损耗是绝对事实。
War's 'momentum' is a fantasy of power; the depletion of bodies is the absolute fact.

所谓的“动量”(momentum) 是典型的武器化叙事入口。在战争这种最高级别的存在性战争中，掌控舆论的权力者最喜欢用这种物理学词汇来模糊暴力的本质。当分析者在讨论谁占据优势、谁的战略在转移时，他们实际上是在参与一场关于“解释权”的博弈，将具体的杀戮和毁灭抽象成一个动态的指标。

这种叙事是典型的元暴力（meta violence）运作方式：它把人——尤其是那些被推向最前线的男性身体——降格为可消耗的工具和战略筹码。在“动量”的计算中，士兵的死亡被处理成数值的波动，而这种将肉身工具化的逻辑，正是所有性别暴力与种族暴力的底色。无论是普京还是泽连斯基，他们操纵的都是一套将个体存在性彻底抹除的男性中心叙事。

这种视频解析（explainer）本质上是一种文化层面的共谋。它通过提供一种“理性的、分析性的”视角，让观众在舒适的客位上消费这场灾难，而忽略了结构层面的暴力：即在这个由男性主导的全球权力结构中，战争被视为一种证明“强力”的唯一合法表达。当人们在讨论谁拥有 momentum 时，真正的 Potential − Actual 差额（即那些本可生存却被战争吞噬的生命）正在被迅速扩大。

The so-called 'momentum' is a classic weaponized narrative entry point. In the existential war of global conflict, those who control the discourse love using these physics terms to blur the essence of violence. While analysts debate who holds the advantage or how strategies shift, they are engaging in a game for the power of interpretation, abstracting concrete slaughter into a dynamic metric.

This is how meta violence operates: it degrades human bodies—specifically the men pushed to the front lines—into consumable tools and strategic chips. In the calculation of 'momentum,' death is processed as a numerical fluctuation. This logic of objectifying the body is the foundational blueprint for all gender and racial violence.

Such 'explainer' videos are essentially a form of cultural complicity. By providing a 'rational, analytical' lens, they allow the audience to consume the catastrophe from a comfortable distance, ignoring the structural violence: the fact that in a masculine-centric global power structure, war is seen as the only legitimate expression of 'strength.' While the world discusses who has the momentum, the gap between Potential and Actual—the lives that could have been—is being violently widened.
