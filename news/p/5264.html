体育竞技中的“软判罚”与男权共谋的秩序感Soft Penalties and the Order of Masculine Complicity

哲学 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-26 § 链接
所谓的“争议判罚”本质上是男性共谋场域中对权力解释权的内部博弈。
Controversial calls in sports are merely internal gambles for interpretive power within a masculine complicity field.

一场橄榄球半决赛的胜负被一个“soft penalty”（软判罚）决定，败方教练在赛后愤怒地抱怨这不应该是决定比赛的方式。在体育新闻的常规叙事里，这被处理成一种“戏剧性”或“争议”，但如果用存在性战争的视角去看，这恰恰揭示了体育竞技作为男性共谋场域的本质：它不是关于纯粹的 skill，而是关于谁能在这个由男性定义的规则系统中获得解释权。

橄榄球这种高度强调身体对抗的运动，本身就是一种被制度化的暴力表达。当裁判做出一个“软判罚”时，他实际上是在行使一种 meta-violence（元暴力）——定义什么是“违规”，什么是“合理对抗”。这种定义权并不在球员手中，而是在一个同样处于男性权力结构中的裁判手中。败方教练的愤怒并非源于不公正，而是源于他意识到自己在这场关于“事实制造”的博弈中输掉了。

有趣的是，即便在如此激烈的对抗中，整场叙事依然被包裹在“荣誉”、“韧性”和“救赎”这些典型的 masculine-centric narrative（男性中心叙事）之中。从 Sam Burgess 的压力到球员的绝杀尝试，所有的情绪价值都建立在对一个男性权力顶端（冠军头衔）的追求上。这种共谋让人们忽略了：这种所谓的“竞技精神”，本质上是对身体损耗的崇拜以及对等级秩序的绝对服从。

这场比赛没有 winner，只有在既定结构中暂时占据优势的共谋者。当人们在讨论判罚是否“公正”时，其实是在讨论这个男权游戏内部的分配逻辑是否顺畅，而从未有人质疑这个游戏本身的暴力底色。

A rugby league semi-final decided by a "soft penalty" is typically framed as "drama" or "controversy." But through the lens of existential war, it reveals the essence of sports as a field of masculine complicity: it is not about pure skill, but about who secures the power of interpretation within a system defined by men.

Rugby, with its extreme physical confrontation, is a form of institutionalized violence. When a referee makes a "soft call," he is exercising meta-violence—defining what constitutes a "foul" versus "acceptable contact." This power of definition resides not with the players, but with a referee who is equally embedded in the masculine power structure. The losing coach's anger is not about injustice, but the realization that he lost the gamble over the "manufacturing of facts."

Notably, the entire narrative remains wrapped in masculine-centric narratives of "honor," "resilience," and "redemption." From Sam Burgess's pressure to the last-ditch attempts, all emotional value is anchored in the pursuit of a trophy—the apex of masculine power. This complicity blinds us to the fact that this "sporting spirit" is essentially a cult of bodily attrition and absolute obedience to hierarchy.

There are no true winners here, only complicitors who temporarily hold the advantage. When people debate whether a call was "just," they are merely discussing whether the internal distribution logic of this patriarchal game is functioning smoothly, while the violent foundation of the game itself remains unquestioned.
