万圣节没有鬼，只有打分板No Ghosts Tonight, Only Scoreboards

文娱 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-31 § 链接
把淘汰包装成恐怖节目，是娱乐业最熟练的一层暴力
Selling elimination as horror is entertainment's most practiced violence.

《Strictly Come Dancing》今晚的万圣节特辑，最诚实的部分是那个"triple-off"：三对选手第一次同时站进淘汰区，评委不再投票救谁，只投票淘汰谁。这是节目组自己承认了——这个舞台的本质不是跳舞，是让人掉下去，而且掉得越有戏剧性越好。

整篇直播稿从头到尾都在教你怎么看这场秀：南瓜形状的打分板、从棺材里爬出来的开场、被涂成绿色的脸、"frightening footwork"、"zombified hips"、片尾字幕把制作人员的名字改成万圣节谐音梗。这是一份观看向导，也是一份规训说明书——告诉你哪些东西值得笑，哪些人今晚要被牺牲掉当节目效果。

注意那个词："a poor celebrity has their face painted green"。写稿的人自己都用了 poor。被涂绿脸的那个人，是这场秀里唯一真的在付代价的人，而代价的内容是：你的脸不属于你，今晚它属于节目的主题。

再看淘汰区。Bethany Antonia 是博彩公司眼里的下一个出局热门，Lawrence Robb 第二，Jaime Winstone 第三。博彩赔率已经先于评委和观众把这三个人排好了序——观众投票还没开始，"谁该走"这件事已经被定价了。这是最干净的一层结构暴力：没有人在使坏，只是市场先把结果写好了，然后所有人照着演。

稿子里最值得读的一句，是关于往季选手的：Katie Piper 2018 年第三个被淘汰，Claire Sweeney 2004 年第一季第四个被淘汰。十几年过去了，她们被请回来，身份仍然是"第几个被淘汰的"。这个节目对女性的记忆方式，就是记住她在哪一轮掉下去。

万圣节是个好借口。鬼是假的，棺材是道具，血是糖浆，唯一真实的东西是那三对要争生存的选手，和一份已经算好的赔率。

The most honest thing about tonight's Strictly Halloween special is the triple-off: for the first time, three couples stand in the elimination zone at once, and the judges vote to eliminate rather than to save. That's the show admitting what it is. The stage isn't for dancing. It's for dropping people, and the more theatrically they drop, the better.

The whole liveblog is a viewing manual: pumpkin-shaped scoring paddles, celebrities descending from the ceiling or climbing out of coffins, a face painted green, "frightening footwork," "zombified hips," production crew names spookified in the end credits. It tells you what to laugh at and, more quietly, who gets sacrificed tonight for the bit.

Look at the phrase the writer reaches for: "a poor celebrity has their face painted green." Poor. The one person actually paying a cost in this show is the one whose face stops belonging to them for the evening and starts belonging to the theme.

Then the betting. Bethany Antonia is the bookies' favourite for elimination, Lawrence Robb second, Jaime Winstone third. The odds have already ranked the bottom three before a single public vote is counted. Nobody is doing anything wrong; the market simply wrote the ending first and everyone performs it. That is structural violence at its cleanest.

The sharpest line in the piece is about the alumni: Katie Piper, eliminated third in 2018; Claire Sweeney, eliminated fourth in the very first series in 2004. Years later they're invited back, and their identity is still which round they fell in. This is how the show remembers women: by the week they went out.

Halloween is a good alibi. The ghosts are fake, the coffin is a prop, the blood is syrup. The only real things are the three couples fighting to survive and an odds sheet that already decided how it ends.
