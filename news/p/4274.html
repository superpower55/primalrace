绞刑架的观看席：一种极致的元暴力表演The Viewing Booths of the Gallows: A Performance of Meta-Violence

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-20 § 链接
当杀戮被设计成一种可消费的景观，暴力便完成了从肉体到叙事的闭环。
When killing is designed as a consumable spectacle, violence completes its loop from the physical to the narrative.

Ben-Gvir 视频里最令人作呕的不是那个绞刑架，而是他提到的“观看席” (viewing booths)。这不仅仅是直接暴力 (direct violence) 的预告，而是一次精心设计的文化暴力 (cultural violence) 实验：他试图将处决过程转化为一种名为“正义”的感官消费品，让受害者的家属在观看中完成某种病态的心理补偿，从而在社会心理层面将巴勒斯坦人彻底客体化。

这套逻辑的底层是典型的元暴力 (meta violence)。Ben-Gvir 建立了一套极端的男性中心叙事：定义谁是“恐怖分子”，定义谁“不配为人”，然后通过法律这个 structural 层的工具，将这种歧视制度化。最讽刺的是，这个法律只针对巴勒斯坦人而豁免犹太极端分子——这证明了法律在这里不是为了秩序，而是为了确认一种种族/身份的等级制。法律成了武器化的表达，用来宣告谁拥有定义生命的定价权。

在这个场域里，国际社会的“谴责” (condemnation) 显得如此 naive。当一个政客能把绞刑架图案的蛋糕当作生日礼物时，他已经完成了主体性的异化。他不需要理性的对话，他需要的是通过制造极端的恐惧和仇恨来巩固自己的权力席位。这种共谋发生在以色列内部的极右翼结构中，他们通过将对方定义为“非人”，来获得一种虚假的、基于支配感的“安全感”。

这种从“定义”到“立法”再到“景观化处决”的链路，就是原初种族被殖民的现代复刻版：先剥夺你的主体性，再通过制度合法地地剥夺你的生命，最后邀请观众来庆祝这场胜利。

The most repulsive part of Ben-Gvir's video isn't the gallows themselves, but the mentioned 'viewing booths.' This is more than a preview of direct violence; it is a meticulously designed experiment in cultural violence. He aims to transform the execution process into a sensory commodity labeled 'justice,' allowing victims' families to find a pathological psychological closure while systematically objectifying Palestinians at a societal level.

The foundation of this logic is pure meta-violence. Ben-Gvir has constructed an extreme masculine-centric narrative: defining who is a 'terrorist,' deciding who 'does not deserve to live,' and then using the structural tool of law to institutionalize this discrimination. The irony is palpable—the law targets Palestinians while exempting Jewish extremists, proving that the law here is not about order, but about affirming a racial and identity hierarchy. Law has been weaponized as an expression to declare who holds the pricing power over human life.

In this arena, international 'condemnation' appears incredibly naive. When a politician treats a noose-decorated cake as a birthday gift, he has already completed the alienation of his own subjectivity. He doesn't seek rational dialogue; he seeks to consolidate his power seat by manufacturing extreme fear and hatred. This complicity exists within Israel's far-right structure, where they achieve a fraudulent sense of 'security' based on the dominance of dehumanizing others.

This trajectory—from 'definition' to 'legislation' to 'spectacular execution'—is a modern reproduction of how the Primal Race is colonized: first strip them of their subjectivity, then legally deprive them of life, and finally invite the audience to celebrate the victory.
