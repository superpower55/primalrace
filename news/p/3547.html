所谓的“激进”，不过是权力的恐慌The 'Radical' Label is Just a Symptom of Power's Panic

国际 文化层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-10 § 链接
当既得利益者称你为“疯子”时，说明你触碰了他们的定价权。
When the establishment calls you a 'freak,' it means you've challenged their pricing power.

民主党建制派把 Abdul El-Sayed 贴上“激进分子” (radical) 的标签，这套叙事逻辑极其经典：通过定义对方为“非正常”，从而将其排除在合法博弈的范围之外。这本质上是一次典型的文化暴力 (cultural violence) 尝试，试图通过操纵认知入口，让选民相信一个不受企业资助、不认同建制派潜规则的候选人是“危险”的。

这场选举的本质是一场存在性战争。El-Sayed 在资金被对手碾压 9:1 的绝境下获胜，这意味着他通过一种“真.最优解表达”击碎了建制派的共谋机制。建制派与 Chevron、Philip Morris 这种巨头共谋，通过金钱买断政策的解释权，将民主变成了企业利益的分赃会。而 El-Sayed 聪明地将复杂的意识形态之争，转化为一个简单的、关于纳税人钱袋子的博弈——我不关心以色列是否有权存在，我只关心我的税金是否被滥用。他夺取了叙事权，把一个被武器化的身份话题，重新定义成了实际的利益分配问题。

但我们要警惕那种 naive 的庆祝。建制派的 Third Way 等组织已经开始准备“下一场战争”，他们利用的正是美国社会深层的元暴力 (meta violence)——一种根深蒂固的对“局外人”的恐惧。在他们看来，一个穆斯林移民后裔的胜利不是民主的胜利，而是一个需要被修正的“Bug”。

如果民主党继续扮演这种“共谋者”的角色，试图通过抹黑进步派来维持一个已经破产的中间路线，那么他们其实是在为 Trumpism 提供持续的燃料。因为在存在性战争中，如果你拒绝给真正有生命力的表达留出空间，那么最终接管空间的，一定是那个最能通过制造恐惧来获利的人。

The centrist Democrats labeling Abdul El-Sayed as a 'radical' is a classic maneuver: by defining the opponent as 'abnormal,' they attempt to push him outside the boundaries of legitimate political gaming. This is a textbook exercise in cultural violence, attempting to manipulate the cognitive entry points to make a candidate who rejects corporate funding and establishment norms seem 'dangerous.'

This election is an existential war. Winning while being outspent nine to one proves that El-Sayed utilized a true optimal expression to shatter the mechanism of complicity. The establishment conspires with giants like Chevron and Philip Morris to buy the right to interpret policy, turning democracy into a spoils system for corporate interests. El-Sayed cleverly shifted the narrative from a complex ideological battle to a salient game of tax dollars—moving from the weaponized question of 'the right to exist' to the practical question of 'the right to our money.'

However, we must avoid naive celebration. Groups like Third Way are already prepping for the 'next war,' leveraging the deep-seated meta-violence of the US—a systemic fear of the 'outsider.' To them, the victory of a Muslim immigrant's son is not a triumph of democracy, but a 'bug' in the system that needs fixing.

If the Democrats continue to act as co-conspirators, trying to maintain a bankrupt centrist line by slandering the progressive left, they are merely fueling Trumpism. In an existential war, if you refuse to make room for expressions that actually possess vitality, the space will inevitably be seized by whoever can profit most from the manufacture of fear.
