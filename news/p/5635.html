把公共资源变成私人神龛：特朗普的“征服欲”叙事Turning Public Assets into Private Shrines: Trump's Narrative of Conquest

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-18 § 链接
权力的极致快感不在于拥有，而在于将他人的资产强行定义为自己的意志。
The ultimate pleasure of power lies not in possession, but in forcibly redefining others' assets as one's own will.

砍掉430棵树，在物理层面上是 direct violence，但在结构层面上，这是一次典型的 asset seizure。特朗普试图将东波托马克高尔夫球场从一个 public good 转化为一个 private enclave。这里的关键不在于高尔夫球本身，而在于“定义权”的武器化。对于一个习惯于 masculine-centric narrative 的权力者来说，公共资产不是用来维护的，而是用来“征服”的。

这是一种极具攻击性的表达方式：通过无视法律程序（oversight）和法院指令，将国家公园管理局的资源强行揉入自己的 brand ventures。他追求的不是一个更好的球场，而是一个印着自己名字的 shrine。当一个公共空间被要求围绕一个人的 vanity project 运转时，这个空间的 a-priori 属性就被抹杀了，取而代之的是一种元暴力（meta violence）——即“我的意志高于所有既定规则”。

这种行为逻辑与殖民者的逻辑同构：将不属于自己的土地标记为自己的领地，从中获得存在性的快感。如果这个项目成功，DC 将变成一个巨型的 Graceland，一个由粉丝和共谋者维护的个人崇拜场域。这种从“公共历史”到“个人纪念馆”的迁移，本质上是夺取解释权的战争。他不在乎球场是否专业，他在乎的是：这本不属于我，但我可以通过权力让它听我的。

Felling 430 trees is direct violence at the physical level, but at the structural level, it is a classic asset seizure. Trump is attempting to pivot the East Potomac Golf Links from a public good to a private enclave. The core issue here isn't golf; it's the weaponisation of the 'right to define.' For a power-player steeped in masculine-centric narrative, public assets are not for maintenance—they are for conquest.

This is an aggressive form of expression: by bypassing oversight and court orders, he seeks to fold National Park Service resources into his own brand ventures. He isn't pursuing a better course, but a shrine bearing his name. When a public space is forced to revolve around one man's vanity project, its a-priori nature is erased, replaced by meta violence—the assertion that 'my will supersedes all established rules.'

This logic is isomorphic to that of the colonizer: marking land that isn't theirs as their own to derive existential pleasure. If successful, DC becomes a giant Graceland, a field of personal worship maintained by fans and complicity. This migration from 'public history' to 'personal monument' is essentially a war over the right to interpretation. He doesn't care if the course is championship-level; he cares that it wasn't his, but he can make it so through power.
