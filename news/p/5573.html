Eurotunnel 的高效与被抹除的 HGV 司机The Efficiency of Eurotunnel and the Erased HGV Drivers

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-10-15 § 链接
当 Eurostar 忙着声明服务不受影响时，被撞碎的才是真实的人。
While Eurostar rushes to claim services are unaffected, the crushed bodies are the only truth.

这是一场典型的 structural violence 现场，尽管它被包装成一次单纯的“交通意外”。新闻的叙事重心在极短的篇幅内完成了迅速的位移：从 19 个受伤的司机，转移到 Eurostar 的服务是否受影响。在资本的认知入口里，乘客的通勤时间是核心事实，而那些在 loading platform 上被撞得血肉模糊的 HGV（重型货物车辆）司机，只是维持这个物流系统运转的生物插件。

请注意这里的共谋逻辑。Eurostar 迅速发布 status update，急于向公众证明其“高效”与“正常”，这种对“秩序”的执着实际上是在定义什么是重要的人。在元暴力的叙事中，坐在头等舱里的乘客是主体，而开着大卡车、在深夜被塞进 minibus 运往隧道的司机则是被物化的工具。他们的受伤被简化为“an incident”，而服务的延迟则被视为一种“危机”。

这种对人命的钝感，正是因为 HGV 司机在整个跨境贸易链条中处于结构性弱势。他们被生物墙和阶级墙双重禁锢，在深夜的 loading platform 上，他们的存在感低到只有在发生事故时才会被 Prefect 的声明提及一次。这种“不影响服务”的傲慢，正是文化层暴力对结构性剥削的完美掩盖。

This is a textbook scene of structural violence, thinly veiled as a simple 'traffic accident.' The narrative center of the news shifts violently within a few paragraphs: from 19 injured drivers to whether Eurostar's services are impacted. In the cognitive entry of capital, the commute time of passengers is the core fact, while the HGV drivers crushed on the loading platform are merely biological plugins maintaining the logistics system.

Observe the logic of complicity here. Eurostar's rapid status update is a desperate attempt to prove its 'efficiency' and 'normality' to the public. This obsession with 'order' effectively defines who the 'important' people are. In the masculine-centric narrative of the meta-violence, the first-class passenger is the subject; the driver crammed into a minibus at midnight is a commodified tool. Their injuries are reduced to 'an incident,' while a service delay would be a 'crisis.'

This insensitivity toward human life stems from the structural weakness of HGV drivers in the cross-border trade chain. Trapped by both biological and class walls, their existence on the loading platform is so marginal that they are only mentioned in a Prefect's statement when blood is spilled. The arrogance of 'not affecting service' is the ultimate cultural violence, masking the systemic exploitation of the working body.
