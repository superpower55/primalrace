用“惊悚片”消费喀布尔的地狱Consuming Kabul's Hell as a 'Gripping Thriller'

国际 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-15 § 链接
将结构性暴力转化为娱乐叙事，是最高级的文化共谋。
Transforming structural violence into entertainment is the ultimate form of cultural complicity.

BBC Four 准备播出的这部关于喀布尔陷落的剧集被描述为 "gripping thriller"（扣人心弦的惊悚片）。请注意这个词：gripping。当数以万计的阿富汗女性在瞬间失去受教育权、工作权，甚至被禁锢在家庭中面对无尽的 structural violence 时，西方媒体的认知入口将其定义为一种“惊悚”的戏剧体验。

这是一种典型的 weaponized expression。它把地缘政治的溃败和女性主体性的死亡，包装成一个关于“人性困境” (human dilemmas) 的故事。通过 Nazany 一家的视角，观众在舒适的沙发上体验了一把“决定留下还是逃离”的紧张感，而这种紧张感在播出结束的那一刻就消失了。但对于那些被留在原地的女性来说，地狱是 24/7 的常态，没有片尾字幕，也没有导演剪辑版。

最讽刺的是，这种叙事方式完成了西方自由主义者的心理闭环：他们通过观看这种“扣人心弦”的悲剧，确认了自己作为“文明人”的同情心，却在潜意识中将阿富汗女性客体化为某种背景板。这正是元暴力的运作逻辑——即便是在讨论受害者的作品里，定义权依然在掌握着摄像机和剪辑权的男性中心叙事手中。他们决定什么时候开始“惊悚”，什么时候结束“悲剧”。

把一个种族的生存危机变成晚间电视节目单上的一个选项，这种共谋让观众在消费苦难的同时，心安理得地进入下一个节目：庆祝 Graceland 的音乐会。

BBC Four's upcoming series on the fall of Kabul is described as a "gripping thriller." Note the word: gripping. While thousands of Afghan women instantly lost their rights to education and work, sliding into a void of structural violence, the Western media's cognitive entry point defines this as a dramatic entertainment experience.

This is a classic case of weaponized expression. It packages geopolitical failure and the death of female subjectivity as a story of "human dilemmas." Through the eyes of the Nazany family, viewers experience the tension of "stay or flee" from the comfort of their sofas—a tension that vanishes the moment the credits roll. For the women left behind, however, hell is a 24/7 reality with no director's cut.

The irony is that this narrative completes the psychological loop for Western liberals: by consuming this "gripping" tragedy, they validate their own compassion as "civilized" beings, while subconsciously objectifying Afghan women as mere background scenery. This is exactly how meta-violence operates—even in works discussing victims, the power of definition remains with the masculine-centric narrative controlling the cameras and the edit.

Turning the existential crisis of a Primal Race into a line item on a TV schedule is a form of complicity. It allows the audience to consume suffering and then seamlessly transition to the next program: a celebratory concert for Graceland.
