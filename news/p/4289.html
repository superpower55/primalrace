用碱液给海洋‘打补丁’，是救赎还是另一场scam？Patching the Ocean with Alkali: Salvation or Another Scam?

国际 结构层 · 文化层 The Guardian ↗ 2026-08-20 § 链接
试图用技术手段‘操纵’自然来抵消排放，是典型的用新暴力掩盖旧暴力。
Attempting to 'manipulate' nature to offset emissions is simply using new violence to mask old violence.

这篇报道揭示了一个极其危险的逻辑：既然我们无法停止排放碳，那就尝试在海洋里‘投药’（OAE，海洋碱度增强），通过向海里倾倒氢氧化钠等碱性物质来强行吸收二氧化碳。这在本质上是一场关于‘认知入口’的博弈。支持者将其包装成‘碳去除’（mCDR）这种具有救世色彩的叙事，试图让公众相信，我们可以通过操纵地球 70% 的表面积来维持目前的消费模式。

这种做法是典型的‘假.最优解表达’。它不是在解决导致海洋酸化的根源——化石燃料的过度使用，而是在结果端进行‘打补丁’。最讽刺的是，研究者在担心龙虾是否会因为碱液而死，却在潜意识中默认了‘只要龙虾不死，我们就可以继续污染’。这是一种极端的结构性暴力：将生态系统的生死权交给了几个实验室的 RNA 测序结果，然后将其转化为商业化的‘碳信用’（carbon credits）来交易。这哪里是科学，这简直是把地球当成一个巨大的、可以被定价的对冲基金。

而特朗普政府对海洋科学资金的砍伐，则揭示了元暴力的残酷性：在权力掌控者眼中，无论是研究如何救海洋，还是研究如何毁掉它，只要不符合当下的政治利益，统统可以被抹除。当科学研究变成‘独角兽’，当研究资金取决于政客的心情，所谓的‘气候危机解决方案’就成了一场关于谁能掌控解释权的权力游戏。如果这种‘ geoengineering’（地球工程）在缺乏长期生物学阈值研究的情况下被商业化，那么我们面对的将不再是酸化的海洋，而是一个被化学试剂强行‘中和’的死海。

This report reveals a perilous logic: since we cannot stop emitting carbon, let's try 'medicating' the ocean via Ocean Alkalinity Enhancement (OAE). By dumping alkaline substances like sodium hydroxide into the sea, they aim to force more carbon absorption. This is a battle over the 'cognitive entrance.' Proponents package it as 'marine carbon dioxide removal' (mCDR), a messianic narrative suggesting we can maintain current consumption patterns by manipulating 70% of the Earth's surface.

This is a textbook 'fake optimal expression.' It doesn't address the root cause of acidification—fossil fuel dependency—but instead applies a 'patch' to the symptoms. The irony is peak: researchers worry whether lobsters will die from the alkali, while subconsciously accepting that 'as long as the lobsters survive, we can keep polluting.' This is structural violence at its finest: handing the survival of an ecosystem over to RNA sequencing in a few labs, then converting that into tradable 'carbon credits.' This isn't science; it's treating the planet as a giant, price-tagged hedge fund.

Furthermore, Trump's sweeping cuts to ocean science reveal the brutality of meta-violence: to the power-holders, whether you study how to save the ocean or how to destroy it, it's irrelevant if it doesn't serve current political interests. When scientific research becomes a 'unicorn' and funding depends on a politician's whim, 'climate solutions' become a power game over who controls the narrative. If this geoengineering is commercialized without understanding long-term biological thresholds, we won't just have an acidic ocean—we'll have a dead sea, forcibly 'neutralized' by chemical reagents.
