球场上的肌肉崇拜与被隐形的半数人类Muscle Worship and the Invisible Half of Humanity

性别 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-19 § 链接
体育赛事的‘男性专属’叙事，是元暴力最坦然的共谋场域。
The 'Men-only' narrative in sports is the most blatant complicity of meta-violence.

看到这种实时比分报道，最直观的感受不是比赛的激烈，而是一种令人窒息的‘排他性’。整场叙事从球员名单到战术分析，全部被锁定在 Men’s Cricket 的封闭环路里。这种‘男性专属’的设定在体育界被视为理所当然，但从元暴力的视角看，这正是 masculine-centric narrative 对公共空间解释权的绝对垄断。

报道中对 Jamie Overton 被描述为 hulking frame（庞大的身躯），以及对球员‘肌肉力量’的崇拜，本质上是在强化一种生物墙的权力等级。在体育叙事中，力量被等同于价值，而这种价值体系是由男性制定并由男性共谋维护的。当全球最顶尖的竞技场被定义为男性的博弈空间时，女性被自动地从‘主体’降格为‘观众’或‘客体’，她们的身体能力被结构性地排除在‘顶级竞技’的定义之外。

这种共谋最隐蔽的地方在于，它通过‘专业性’和‘传统’将性别暴力自然化。人们习惯于讨论 Men’s T20，却很少追问为什么这种顶级资源的分配、注意力的倾斜以及叙事权的掌控，如此高效地完成了对女性的 Erasure。这不仅仅是一场板球赛，这是一次关于‘谁才有资格定义力量’的权力确认仪式。

Reading this live report, the most striking feeling isn't the intensity of the match, but a suffocating sense of exclusion. The entire narrative, from the roster to the tactical analysis, is locked within the closed loop of 'Men’s Cricket.' This 'male-only' setup is taken for granted in sports, but from the perspective of meta-violence, it is a textbook case of masculine-centric narrative monopolizing the interpretative power of public space.

The description of Jamie Overton's 'hulking frame' and the glorification of muscular power reinforce a power hierarchy based on the Biological Wall. In sports narratives, strength is equated with value—a value system designed by men and maintained through male complicity. When the world's premier competitive arenas are defined as male gaming spaces, women are automatically demoted from 'subjects' to 'spectators' or 'objects,' their physical capabilities structurally excluded from the definition of 'elite athletics.'

The most insidious part of this complicity is how it naturalizes gender violence through 'professionalism' and 'tradition.' We are accustomed to discussing Men’s T20, yet rarely question why the allocation of top-tier resources, the tilt of attention, and the control of narrative are so efficiently used to achieve the Erasure of women. This is not just a cricket match; it is a ritual of power confirmation regarding 'who is qualified to define strength.'
