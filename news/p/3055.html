当“正常”成为武器：寡头时代的叙事闭环The Weaponization of 'Normal': Narrative Closure in the Age of Oligarchs

国际 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-07-31 § 链接
定义“正常”的权力，就是定义谁该被清除的权力。
The power to define 'normal' is the power to define who must be eliminated.

这篇文章揭露了一个极其典型的 weaponized 叙事过程：权力者不再仅仅通过 direct violence（如逮捕、监禁）来统治，而是先在 cultural layer 建立一套关于“正常” (normal) 的定义权。Stephen Miller 对抗议者“外貌畸形”的攻击，本质上是在通过表型 (phenotype) 的污名化，将异见者直接剔除出“人”的范畴。这和当年纳粹定义 untermenschen 的逻辑完全一致：先定义你是不正常的，那么接下来的结构性剥夺和直接暴力就变成了“清理”而非“屠杀”。

更阴险的是这种暴力的 structural 传导。ALEC 这种 junktanks 制造的“模版法律” (model legislation)，实际上是在全球范围内复制一套有利于资本的共谋机制。当法律被修改到“只有无用的抗议才是合法的”时，Potential − Actual 的差额被制度性地锁死了。这不再是简单的政治分歧，而是一场关于存在性的战争：寡头们试图通过垄断解释权，让所有不符合其利益的表达在定义上就成为“恐怖主义”。

最令人不寒而栗的是这种 meta violence 的闭环。当法官可以悄悄地将普通刑事案件标记为“恐怖主义相关”并加刑时，司法系统已经变成了元暴力的执行末梢。在这种共谋下，法律不再是公正的表达，而成了掩盖 structural violence 的 PR 包装。如果你认为只要不被定义为“恐怖分子”就是安全的，那你就低估了这套叙事武器的升级速度——它会不断地通过重新定义“正常”和“合法”，直到把你变成一个在法律意义上不具备主体性的客体。

This piece exposes a textbook process of weaponized expression: power no longer rules solely through direct violence, but by first establishing a monopoly over the definition of 'normal' at the cultural layer. Stephen Miller’s attack on the 'deformed' appearance of protesters is a strategic stigmatization of phenotype, effectively stripping dissidents of their humanity. This is the exact logic of the untermenschen: once you are defined as abnormal, structural deprivation and direct violence are rebranded as 'cleansing' rather than slaughter.

Even more sinister is the structural transmission of this violence. The 'model legislation' produced by junktanks like ALEC is essentially a global replication of a complicity mechanism favoring capital. When laws are engineered so that 'only useless protests are legal,' the gap between Potential and Actual is institutionally locked. This is no longer a mere political disagreement, but an existential war: oligarchs are attempting to use their monopoly on interpretation to define all non-compliant expression as 'terrorism.'

What is most chilling is the closure of this meta-violence. When judges can quietly mark ordinary criminal cases as 'terrorist-linked' to increase sentencing, the judicial system becomes the terminal executor of meta-violence. Under this complicity, the law ceases to be a just expression and becomes a PR facade for structural violence. If you believe you are safe as long as you aren't labeled a 'terrorist,' you underestimate the speed of this narrative weapon—it will continuously redefine 'normal' and 'legal' until you are rendered an object without agency in the eyes of the law.
