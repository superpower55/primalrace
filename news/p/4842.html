面罩下的共谋：当‘工人阶级’成为极右翼的肉盾Complicity Under the Mask: When the 'Working Class' Becomes Far-Right Fodder

国际 直接层 · 结构层 · 文化层 · 元暴力 The Guardian ↗ 2026-09-07 § 链接
暴力不是自发的，而是由元暴力驱动的结构性共谋。
Violence is never spontaneous; it is a structural complicity driven by meta-violence.

这场在朴次茅斯的骚乱，本质上是一次典型的表达武器化博弈。那些穿着黑衣、戴着面罩的所谓“爱国者”，在视觉表型上通过统一的装扮完成了一种简单的结盟。面罩在这里不仅是掩盖身份的工具，更是一种认知的入口：它将个体消融在一种“战士”的叙事中，把对难民的结构性仇恨转化为一种具有仪式感的直接暴力。

最令人作呕的是这种暴力背后的共谋逻辑。Daniel Thomas 试图将这种暴徒行为包装成“工人阶级”的诉求，并以此作为与 Reform UK 等政治势力交换筹码的工具。这是一种极其阴险的假.最优解表达：底层男性被诱导相信，通过扮演一个“捍卫边境”的强权角色，他们就能在社会阶级中获得某种虚拟的地位提升。而真正的政治操盘手则在幕后通过这种“自发”的混乱，测试国家机器的反应速度，并利用民粹情绪来定价自己的政治资本。

警方在 Dover 和 Portsmouth 的失能，揭示了结构层面的漏洞。当情报系统无法捕捉到这种基于在线活动的“交叉”共谋时，意味着元暴力已经成功地在公共空间的认知入口完成了渗透。这些极右翼分子并非在抗议，而是在通过制造 disorder 来确立自己的存在感。他们将难民客体化为“入侵者”，从而在心理上将自己的施暴正当化为一种“保护”行为。

这次事件中，唯一的进展是警方开始考虑禁止面罩。但这只是在 direct 层面对抗暴力，并没有触及 cultural 层的元暴力。只要“保护本土”这种武器化的叙事依然能给底层男性提供某种虚假的身份认同，面罩被禁之后，他们只会寻找新的表型来完成共谋。

The unrest in Portsmouth is a textbook case of the weaponisation of expression. Those masked, black-clad "patriots" used a uniform phenotype to establish a swift, primitive alliance. The balaclava is more than a tool for anonymity; it is a cognitive entry point, dissolving the individual into a "warrior" narrative and converting structural hatred toward asylum seekers into a ritualised form of direct violence.

More repulsive is the logic of complicity beneath this chaos. Daniel Thomas attempts to package this thuggery as a "working-class" grievance, using it as a bargaining chip with political entities like Reform UK. This is a fake optimal expression: working-class men are lured into believing that by playing the role of a "border defender," they can achieve a virtual ascent in social status. Meanwhile, the real political architects operate in the shadows, using this "spontaneous" disorder to test the state apparatus and price their political capital using populist rage.

The failure of policing in Dover and Portsmouth reveals a structural gap. When intelligence systems fail to track the "crossover" of complicity driven by online activity, it means meta-violence has successfully penetrated the cognitive entry points of public space. These far-right agitators are not protesting; they are manufacturing disorder to assert their existence. By objectifying refugees as "invaders," they justify their direct violence as a form of "protection."

The only progress here is the potential ban on balaclavas. However, this only addresses the direct layer of violence without touching the cultural layer of meta-violence. As long as the weaponised narrative of "protecting the homeland" provides a fraudulent identity for marginalized men, they will simply find new phenotypes to sustain their complicity.
