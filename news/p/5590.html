Violence is not a 'senseless tragedy', but the gap between potential safety and actual failure.

再一次，我们被告知这是一场“毫无意义的悲剧” (senseless tragedy)。这种叙事是典型的 cultural violence，它通过将暴力定义为“随机”和“不可理解”，成功地掩盖了 structural violence 的真实面目。当一个 16 岁的少年能轻易用母亲购买的散弹枪射穿学校的大门，杀掉三个孩子和一个老师时，这绝不是什么“无意义”，而是一个极其精准的系统性结果。

最令人作呕的是叙事中对那个“英勇干预” (heroically intervened) 的旁观者的赞美。州长和市长在烛光晚餐般的仪式感中，将个体暴力的对冲包装成英雄主义。这是一种极其危险的共谋：通过赞美个体的“枪战胜利”，来掩盖制度在保护儿童方面的彻底瘫痪。如果安全需要依赖于一个恰好在场且携带武器的随机路人，那么这个社会的安全底线其实就是零。

这起事件揭示了美国校园暴力的元暴力逻辑：一种极端的 masculine-centric narrative。在这种叙事中，枪支被视为权力的延伸，而杀戮被简化为个体的心理崩溃。但事实是，这种暴力是结构性的——从宽松的枪支管控到对青少年男性攻击性的纵容，再到事后用“祈祷”和“心碎”来填补的公共话语。这种 loop 保证了下一次屠杀依然具有“可能性”。

Potential − Actual = Violence。孩子们本应在学校获得 100% 的安全，但 Actual 却是被散弹枪撕碎。这个巨大的差额，就是这个体制每天在制造的暴力。

Once again, we are told this is a "senseless tragedy." This narrative is a textbook example of cultural violence; by defining violence as "random" and "incomprehensible," it successfully masks the reality of structural violence. When a 16-year-old can effortlessly blast through school doors with a shotgun purchased by his mother to kill three children and a teacher, it is not "senseless"—it is a precise systemic output.

The most nauseating part is the praise for the bystander who "heroically intervened." The governor and mayor, amidst the ritualism of a candlelit vigil, package the clash of individual violence as heroism. This is a dangerous complicity: by glorifying a random citizen's "gunfight victory," they erase the total paralysis of the system in protecting children. If safety depends on a random armed passerby happening to be in the right place, the actual safety baseline of this society is zero.

This event exposes the meta-violence logic of US school shootings: an extreme masculine-centric narrative. In this framework, firearms are extensions of power, and massacres are reduced to individual psychological breakdowns. In reality, this is structural—from lax gun laws to the tolerance of adolescent male aggression, and finally to a public discourse filled with "prayers" and "broken hearts." This loop ensures that the "possibility" of the next massacre remains intact.

Potential − Actual = Violence. Children should have had 100% safety in school, but the Actual was being torn apart by a shotgun. This massive gap is the violence this system manufactures every single day.
